Meet the Dentists

Dr. Kirsten was born and raised in Waukesha, Wisconsin. She attended Whittier Elementary School, Heyer Elementary School, Central Middle School, and graduated from Waukesha South High School in 1991. Dr. Kirsten is happy to be practicing dentistry in the city where she grew up. Waukesha is a fun, family-friendly community, and Dr. Kirsten and her family enjoy many community events.

Dr. Kirsten earned her undergraduate degree from Valparaiso University in Indiana and graduated from Marquette University School of Dentistry in 1997. She completed her pediatric dental residency at the Children’s Hospital Medical Center in Cincinnati, Ohio in 1999.

Dr. Kirsten is active in many professional organizations and she is committed to remaining current on the latest advances in dentistry. She is a member of the American Dental Association, Wisconsin Dental Association, and the Waukesha County Dental Society.

Dr. Kirsten is a volunteer at the Hausmann Nature Center in Lapham Peak State Park.

Dr. Kirsten and her husband Brian Udovich have two children, son Bryce and daughter Brynn. She enjoys spending time with her family. They enjoy all things outdoors including hiking, kayaking, running, and biking. Dr. Kirsten also enjoys cooking, reading, and traveling. Someday she hopes to visit all of our National Parks.

Dr. Robert E. Davies

Dr. Robert is a life-long resident of Waukesha. He attended Randall Elementary School and transferred to Whittier Elementary School when it was first built. He graduated from Waukesha High School in 1961 and in 2001 was selected to be on its Wall of Fame.

Dr. Robert has grown up in a dental family. His grandfather, mother, two aunts, and two uncles were all dentists. He was able to see first-hand what a wonderful profession dentistry can be. Dr. Robert has eight children, four of them are dentists and one is a dental hygienist. He also has two daughters-in-law who are dentists.

Dr. Robert received his pre-dental education at Carroll College. He graduated from Marquette University School of Dentistry. He served 2 years as a dentist for the U. S. Coast Guard in Cape May, New Jersey. Upon returning to Waukesha, he went back to Marquette and finished his post graduate training in pediatric dentistry in 1971. While at Marquette he was elected into Omicron Kappa Upsilon (Honorary Dental Society) and Alpha Sigma Nu (National Jesuit Honor Society).

Dr. Robert has many interests in the community. He was a member of the Waukesha School Board for 24 years and served as its president for 7 years. He co-founded the St. Joseph’s Dental Clinic in 1979. He is a member and elder of the Southminster Presbyterian Church. Dr. Robert is a member of several dental organizations.
