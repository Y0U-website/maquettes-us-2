  • Gather information about your medical and dental history while forming a treatment plan. Because we only provide the highest possible standards of care, our treatment planning involves taking X-rays and photographs of your mouth. In addition, each patient receives a CBCT.
  • The CBCT emits a minimal amount of radiation, just like traditional X-rays, and takes hundreds of images in seconds to create a 3D replica of your facial structures. This guide ensures that the implant post is inserted not only in the right place but also at the right angle and depth. This ensures a safe procedure with optimal results for your unique smile.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Dental Implants in Durham, NC

Can I get same-day dental implants in Durham, NC?

For full-arch cases, yes. All-on-4 and All-on-X treatment is planned so that your implants are placed and a temporary restoration is fitted in a single day — each arch takes about two hours and is performed under sedation, so you do not leave without teeth. Single-tooth cases work differently and usually involve a healing period before the final crown is attached.

Are implant-supported dentures in Durham, NC the same as All-on-4?

Patients use "implant-supported dentures" and "implant-retained dentures" for any replacement teeth held in place by implants rather than by suction or adhesive. All-on-4 is the fixed version: the teeth are secured to the implants and you do not take them out. Other designs attach to the implants but can still be removed for cleaning. Dr. Erfan Imeni will explain which design suits your bone volume, bite, and daily routine after reviewing your CBCT scan.

Is All-on-4 the same as All-on-X?

All-on-X is the umbrella term for full-mouth dental implant treatment that restores an entire arch on a small number of implants. All-on-4 uses four implants per arch and All-on-6 uses six. Dr. Erfan Imeni recommends the number that suits your bone volume and bite after reviewing your CBCT scan.

Who can receive All-on-4 or All-on-X permanent teeth?

Dr. Erfan Imeni can review your case and determine whether you are a good candidate for these dental implants. In most cases, the best candidates are those who have lost all of their teeth, or who currently wear dentures.

I’ve been told that I cannot receive a dental implant or permanent bridge due to bone loss. Could I be a candidate for All on X / Permanent Teeth?

In most cases, patients who are not good candidates for traditional dental implants or fixed bridges are candidates for All on X / Permanent Teeth because it inserts implants into the frontal area of the jaw, thus eliminating the need to perform a bone graft or sinus lift.

What makes All on X / Permanent Teeth so different?

Instead of inserting the implant posts directly into the jawbone, All on X / Permanent Teeth insert the post into the frontal part of the jaw, which is denser. The main difference, however, is that All on X / Permanent Teeth are inserted at an angle. This allows us to insert a longer implant into the denser part of your bone, creating a less invasive treatment and avoiding the sinus cavity when inserting implants into the upper arch.

How long does the procedure take?

Each arch takes 2 hours to complete. The procedure is performed under sedation, and patients will receive a temporary restoration in only one day!

Is it painful?

The All on X / Permanent Teeth procedure results in minimal discomfort or pain. This is because the procedure is less invasive and performed under sedation. Patients will also receive anti-inflammatory medicine before their surgery, resulting in less post-surgery pain.

How long is the healing time?

There is no healing period between placing your implants and receiving your final restoration. With traditional implants, healing time takes anywhere between 6 and 9 months. With All on X / Permanent Teeth, however, there is no required healing time due to the increased stability of specialized implants and advanced procedures.

I need to remove all of my remaining teeth before receiving implants. Isn’t there healing time for tooth extraction before receiving a fixed bridge?

With the All on X / Permanent Teeth process, there is no required healing time after extracting the remaining teeth. This is because the implant can be placed immediately after tooth extraction, and the bridge will be resting on your implant, not your gums. This allows the gums to completely heal and close up after the procedure; there is no dry or exposed socket.
