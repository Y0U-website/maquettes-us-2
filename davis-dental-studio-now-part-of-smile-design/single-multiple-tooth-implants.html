Frequently Asked Questions About Single & Multiple Implants

What is a dental implant made of?

A dental implant is a small titanium screw that acts as an anchor to a dental restoration. The implant is inserted into your jawbone to replace the roots of your missing teeth, and your restoration is attached to the top of the implant.

Will my restoration look natural?

Absolutely! Dental implants are designed to feel, look, and function like your natural teeth.

Am I a good candidate for a dental implant?

You may be a good candidate for dental implants if you have good oral health and sufficient bone density. Anyone who is missing a tooth, whether from decay, injury, or other factors, may be a good candidate.

How successful are dental implants?

Dental implants have carried a high success rate for more than three decades. While our dentist can successfully insert your implant, maintaining good oral hygiene is essential for your implant to last.

Are dental implants painful?

All dental implant procedures are performed under anesthesia to help minimize any discomfort. After surgery, Dr. Erfan Imeni may prescribe pain medication; however, most patient experience minimal discomfort during their recovery at home. Generally, patients feel great the following day and can return to work and other daily activities.

How much does the treatment cost?

Treatment cost varies, depending on how many implants you have placed. Cost is also affected by the type of restoration you receive, and other factors that your personal case may involve. After a thorough examination from our dentist, we can provide an estimate of your treatment cost.

Does insurance cover dental implant procedures?

Your insurance may cover your implant procedure, depending on your specific insurance plan. If your insurance plan does not currently cover implants, it may be possible to receive additional coverage. We encourage you to talk with your insurance provider to discuss your coverage options.

Can I replace several missing teeth with multiple dental implants in Durham, NC?

Yes. Multiple dental implants can support an implant bridge that replaces several teeth in a row without placing one implant per tooth, and separate single implants can restore gaps in different parts of the mouth. If every tooth in an arch is missing, full-mouth All-on-4 treatment is usually the better option.

How do I choose a dental implant dentist in Durham, NC?

Look for a Durham dentist who takes a CBCT 3D scan before planning your implant rather than a flat X-ray, uses a surgical guide to place it, explains why a particular restoration was chosen for your case, and gives you a written estimate before you commit. How long does the procedure take?

Completing a dental implant procedure involves several steps, including a healing period, which varies by patient. Generally, treatment can last several months; however, there are some options where implants can be placed and restorations completed immediately.
