About Dr. Davis

While he is a lover of music, campfires, and trout streams, for Dr. Davis, family comes first! When not taking care of patients, he spends as much time as possible with his four amazing children, sharing his interests with them and watching them grow and discover things that resonate with who they are, and who they are becoming.

Dr. Davis came to dentistry from a background in the visual arts. After earning a bachelor’s degree in art education and a master’s degree in sculpture, Dr. Davis worked as an educator and a professional sculptor for several years.

As the family grew, however, it became apparent a new direction was needed and Dr. Davis headed back to school. While attending The Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry at the University of Southern California, Dr. Davis came to realize that the skills he had developed as an artist provided a solid foundation for his future of sculpting smiles. He also realized that he was not made for life in the big city!

Since graduation, Dr. Davis has worked as an associate dentist in a nearby private practice where he has strengthened his skills and established many meaningful relationships with both patients and coworkers. While change is never easy, Dr. Davis is very excited to finally be opening his own private practice here in West Bend! Dr. Davis and his dedicated team here at Davis Family Dental look forward to meeting you and your family and helping you maintain your bright and healthy smiles!
