The practice

Dr. Davis loves seeing the changes in both aesthetics and confidence in patients as they go through their treatment plan. We offer a wide array of orthodontic services , including ceramic braces , Invisalign , and Invisalign for Teens .

All of our team members are committed to maintaining the highest standards in orthodontic care. We look forward to working with you through this important life journey!

With warm welcomes and genuine smiles, Dr. Davis and the team are here to assist throughout your orthodontic journey. From initial paperwork to insurance, financials, and scheduling appointments at times that work with your busy life, they are happy to help!

A clear, convenient, and comfortable option to achieve the smile you’ve always wanted.

Dr. Laura Davis is a native of upstate New York and graduated with honors from Oakwood College in Huntsville, Alabama in 1983. She had completed most of her didactic courses for the Ph.D. program in Neurophysiology at Howard University in Washington, D.C., when she decided to go to dental school.

She received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Georgetown University in 1990, and completed her orthodontic residency from Howard University in 1992. Dr. Davis is a member of the American Dental Association and the American Association of Orthodontists .

Dr. DeMont Davis, or as he is affectionately known in the office as “Dr. DJ”, is an Atlanta native, receiving his Bachelor’s of Science degree in Biology from Oakwood University in 2017. Upon completion, he was accepted into the historic Meharry Medical College School of Dentistry in Nashville, TN.
