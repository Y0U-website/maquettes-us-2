Paying for Preventive Dental Care Saves Money

Preventive dental care can actually help you keep more of your money. When you follow a six-month checkup plan and take care of your teeth and gums between visits to the general dentist in Shawnee, OK, you will be less likely to develop costly problems. Plus, if issues or conditions do arise, we can treat them in their earliest stages. This can save you thousands – if not tens of thousands – of dollars over your lifetime.

Understanding How Dental and Health Insurance Works

When your dental or health insurance agrees to pay for a portion of your dental bill, we will be happy to submit paperwork on your behalf. However, please note that you will still be responsible for any co-pays or unpaid balances at the time of your appointment.

Special Payment Plans for Extensive Treatment

If you are in need of extensive treatment, we may arrange to set up a payment schedule that is a private arrangement between Shawnee Dental and you. This is done on a case-by-case basis.
