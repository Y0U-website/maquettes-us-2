The practice

Dr. Edward Davis is a board-certified orthodontist who serves the families of Lexington and Richland counties by offering high-quality, affordable orthodontic care. Born and raised in West Columbia, Dr. Davis is excited to be practicing in his hometown where he is a community leader.

Braces are orthodontic devices that gradually straighten teeth, correct jaw alignment, and improve oral health by applying controlled pressure using brackets, wires, and sometimes elastic bands.

Clear aligners are a system of removable trays that straighten teeth discreetly. Custom-made trays gradually shift teeth into place, offering a comfortable and nearly invisible alternative to traditional braces.

By taking weekly scans of your teeth using your mobile device, DentalMonitoring helps our office keep your treatment on track, decrease in-office appointments, and offer constant communication and support.
