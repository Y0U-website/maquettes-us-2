Not at all. Orthodontic treatment isn’t just for kids and teens—more adults than ever are choosing to straighten their teeth and improve their bite later in life. Whether you’re in your 30s, 40s, 50s, or beyond, it’s never too late to benefit from healthier alignment, improved function, and a smile you love. Our orthodontic options

Ceramic braces Custom braces Traditional metal braces Clear aligners Clear, comfortable, and virtually invisible, clear aligners are a top choice for adults looking to straighten their smile with minimal disruption. The aligners are removable for meals, meetings, and brushing—making them an easy fit for busy schedules. At Davis Orthodontics, we use a variety of clear aligner systems, including Invisalign, and pair treatment with iTero digital scanning and Dental Monitoring so you can track your progress and check in with your doctor from anywhere.

Ceramic braces

For adults who want a more subtle alternative to traditional braces but still need a fixed appliance, ceramic braces are a great option. They blend in with your natural tooth color and offer reliable, efficient results for mild to moderate alignment issues. These are especially popular with professionals who want effective treatment without a flashy appearance.

Custom braces

Custom braces use advanced 3D imaging to create brackets designed specifically for your teeth. This allows for more precise movement, fewer appointments, and potentially faster treatment. As we expand this system across our practice, more adults are benefiting from a highly personalized approach to braces.

Traditional metal braces

Traditional metal braces are a time-tested approach to orthodontics that are versatile and effective. Modern brackets are slimmer and more comfortable than ever, and can be used to correct even the most complex misalignments.
