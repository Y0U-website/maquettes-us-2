What is early interceptive orthodontics?

The benefits of early interceptive orthodontics

Phase I treatment helps us address alignment and jaw development concerns while your child is still growing, potentially reducing the need for more invasive treatment later on.

Supports healthy jaw growth Early care can help guide jaw development and bite alignment while bones are still forming.

Creates space for permanent teeth By addressing crowding early, we can make room for incoming adult teeth and reduce the need for extractions later.

Builds confidence and comfort Starting early helps kids feel more comfortable with orthodontic care and confident in their smiles from the beginning.

Our early orthodontic treatments

Palatal expanders Partial braces or aligners Space maintainers Space maintainers are simple devices that help preserve room for permanent teeth when a baby tooth is lost early. They prevent nearby teeth from shifting out of place and help guide proper eruption. At Davis Orthodontics, we often use these as part of early treatment plans to avoid crowding and ensure a smoother path to a balanced smile.

Palatal expanders

Palatal expanders are used to gently widen the upper jaw, making more space for teeth and improving bite alignment. This treatment is most effective while a child’s bones are still developing. Our team uses digital scans to customize each appliance for comfort and effectiveness, helping kids breathe easier, chew better, and smile brighter.

Partial braces or aligners

In some cases, early treatment may include a limited set of braces or clear aligners to correct specific issues. This can help guide teeth into better positions and prepare for Phase II treatment later. We keep things light, positive, and kid-friendly—so your child can feel proud of their progress.
