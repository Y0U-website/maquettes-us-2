Common orthodontic issues & emergencies

Broken bracket Lost or damaged aligner Sore teeth or jaw Swallowed or displaced appliance Poking wire Wires can sometimes shift and irritate the inside of the mouth. If this happens, try using orthodontic wax over the end of the wire to protect your cheek or gums. You can also try using a clean pencil eraser to gently bend the wire back into place. If discomfort continues, give us a call and we’ll guide you on next steps or get you in for a quick fix.

Broken bracket

A bracket that becomes loose or breaks away from the tooth can usually wait a few days, but it should still be addressed. If it’s still attached to the wire, use wax to keep it in place and prevent irritation. Don’t try to remove it yourself. Contact our office so we can schedule a repair and keep your treatment plan on track.

Lost or damaged aligner

If you damage or misplace your clear aligner, don’t panic. Depending on where you are in your wear cycle, we may recommend moving ahead to your next aligner or going back to the previous one to maintain progress. Call our office, and we’ll advise you on what to do and when to come in for a replacement if needed.

Sore teeth or jaw

Mild soreness is normal after braces are adjusted or when starting a new aligner tray. You can manage this discomfort with over-the-counter pain relievers and soft foods for a day or two. If pain is severe or doesn’t improve, give us a call. There may be something we can adjust to make you more comfortable.

Swallowed or displaced appliance

If a piece of your appliance breaks off and is swallowed or inhaled, seek medical attention immediately and then call our office. Though rare, this is considered a true emergency. We’ll work with your medical provider to coordinate follow-up care and replace the appliance safely.
