Dr. Marc Dager

Dr. Marc Dager brings nearly two decades of experience to Davis Orthodontics, where he leads the Spartanburg office with expertise and kindness. A native of Indiana, he completed his undergraduate degree at the University of Notre Dame, followed by dental school at the University of Louisville and a Master of Science in Orthodontics at the University of Michigan. He’s a diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics and an active member of several national dental organizations.

Dr. Dager is passionate about creating life-changing smiles and helping patients go from hiding their teeth to smiling with pride. He lives in Inman, SC with his wife, Kelly, and enjoys lake life, college football (Go Irish!), playing guitar, and restoring classic cars. Patients appreciate his calm demeanor, detailed approach, and genuine care.
