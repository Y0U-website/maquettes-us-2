From school and sports to selfies and social events, today’s teens need orthodontic options that fit their lives. At Davis Orthodontics, we offer flexible, discreet, and effective treatments that help teens feel confident every step of the way.

What is teen orthodontics?

The benefits of orthodontic care for teens

Teen years are a powerful time for personal growth, and orthodontics can support that by improving function, self-esteem, and overall health.

Improves alignment and bite Treatment corrects common issues like crowding, spacing, underbites, and overbites, setting teens up for a lifetime of better oral health.

Boosts confidence A straighter smile helps teens feel more confident in social situations, photos, and face-to-face interactions.

Fits busy schedules With technology like virtual monitoring and flexible options like clear aligners, treatment works around school, sports, and everything in between.

Our orthodontic options for teens

Traditional metal braces Ceramic braces Custom braces Invisalign for teens Invisalign for teens uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gently move teeth into place. It’s perfect for active teens who want to straighten their teeth without drawing attention to treatment. The aligners come with compliance indicators so parents and doctors can track usage, and because they’re removable, brushing, flossing, and eating stay simple. At Davis Orthodontics, we pair Invisalign with Dental Monitoring, so teens can check in remotely and spend less time in the office.

Traditional metal braces

Traditional metal braces remain a reliable and effective option for teen orthodontics—especially for more complex cases. Today’s brackets are smaller, sleeker, and more comfortable than ever, and teens can personalize their look with colorful elastics. Our team is great at making braces feel fun, not frustrating—and we’re always available to answer questions or offer support along the way.

Ceramic braces

For teens who want a more subtle version of traditional braces, ceramic braces are a great option. These tooth-colored brackets blend in with the natural smile while delivering the same powerful results. We often recommend ceramic braces for older teens who want a less visible treatment option but still need the precision of fixed appliances.

Custom braces

Custom braces are designed specifically for each patient’s teeth using digital scans and 3D planning. This advanced approach often shortens treatment time and reduces the number of adjustments needed. As this becomes a standard part of our care model, more teens are enjoying a truly personalized path to a confident smile.
