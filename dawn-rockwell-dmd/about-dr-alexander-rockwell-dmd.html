About Dr. Alexander Rockwell

Originally from Kiev, Ukraine, Dr. Alex Rockwell, immigrated to the United States with his parents when he was 12 years old. By age 15, already working as an assistant in his mother’s dental practice, Dr. Rockwell knew that he wanted to follow in her footsteps. Today, he and his wife Dr. Dawn Rockwell, are proud to provide comprehensive dental care to patients in Middletown and Hamilton, New Jersey, at Rockwell Dentistry. Doctors Alex and Dawn are looking forward to having their twin children Eric and Natasha, who are in Dental School now, to join them.

Dr. Alex Rockwell attended Brooklyn College in New York City and then earned his doctorate in dental medicine from Fairleigh Dickinson University in Teaneck, New Jersey. After dental school, Dr. Rockwell spent the following years studying and gaining experience in Oral Surgery, and Endodontics. He also pursued advanced training in Periodontics, Implantology, and Acute Care.

Dr. Alex Rockwell’s expertise and experience allowed him the honor of being selected as one of the leaders in the dental field as an author of “Consequences of missing teeth?”in Smile Creating Healthy and Beautiful Smiles With Cosmetic Dentistry.

Dr. Rockwell maintains affiliations in several professional organizations, including the American Dental Association, American Academy of Implant Dentistry, and American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

Outside of the office Dr. Rockwell enjoys spending time with his wife, Dr. Dawn, and their twin children, Eric and Natasha, who are currently in Dental School studying to follow in their parents footsteps. Dr. Rockwell also enjoys playing and coaching hockey with Eric, skiing, golf and auto racing.

Dr. Dawn Rockwell

A self-proclaimed “Jersey Girl,” Dr. Dawn enjoys spending time with her extended family at the shore, yoga, and educating herself about health and wellness. She is also an avid animal lover, and she loves playing and taking care of her family dog, Bella.

Dr. Dawn’s late dog, Fergie, has passed away recently. Dr. Dawn always made sure Fergie had the prettiest teeth at the dog park. Check out the video of Dr. Dawn cleaning Fergie’s teeth!

Dr. Natasha Rockwell

Dr. Natasha believes in personalization of dental care, and does not take a “one size fits all” approach to treatment. She believes in empowering her patients to become educated and informed about their dental decisions and brings a thoughtful and thorough approach to all aspects of her patient’s care.

Dr. Natasha is now living in Jersey City, NJ even though she will always have a soft place in her heart for Philadelphia. In her spare time, you will find her exploring new coffee shops and living an active lifestyle- practicing various styles of yoga and fitness, and skiing in the winter. Dr. Natasha was formerly a competitive figure skater and has most recently became 200 Hour certified Yoga Instructor.

Dr. Natasha is passionate about health and being an advocate for all aspects of women’s health and wellbeing.
