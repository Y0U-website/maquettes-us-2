The Cosmetic Dentistry Experience at Rockwell Dentistry — Step by Step

Whether your goal is a single whitening session or a full smile makeover, the process at Rockwell Dentistry is the same: unhurried, transparent, and built entirely around your goals. Here’s how it works:

Free Cosmetic Consultation

Call (732) 706-1100 or request your consultation online . Dr. Rockwell listens to your concerns, examines your smile, and discusses your goals — without any judgment or pressure. You can come in with photos of smiles you admire, or simply describe what bothers you. There’s no obligation.

Digital Smile Preview

For most cosmetic cases, we use Snap Instant Digital Imaging to show you a realistic preview of your potential results before any treatment is planned. You see the proposed outcome — and can request adjustments to shape, shade, and proportions — before committing to a single procedure.

Personalized Treatment Plan

Dr. Rockwell presents a complete written treatment plan — outlining each procedure recommended, the sequence, the timeline, and the full cost. If your plan involves multiple treatments, he explains the order and the reasoning. You review and approve every element before anything begins.

Treatment — Entirely In-House

Every cosmetic procedure at Rockwell Dentistry is performed in-house by Dr. Rockwell — whether it’s a single whitening session or a multi-treatment smile makeover combining veneers, Invisalign, whitening, and Botox. You never need to coordinate between separate specialists or offices. One doctor, one practice, complete care.

Your Final Result

When treatment is complete, you see your finished smile — and you’ll know immediately whether it matches the preview you approved at the start of the process. At Rockwell Dentistry, the goal is always a result you’re genuinely proud of, not just satisfied with. And we don’t consider the case closed until you feel that way too.
