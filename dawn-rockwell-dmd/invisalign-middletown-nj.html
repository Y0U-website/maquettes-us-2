The Real Benefits of Invisalign Clear Aligners

Beyond straightening teeth, Invisalign improves oral health, restores confidence, and fits into real life in a way that traditional braces simply can’t match. Here’s what patients consistently tell us they love most:

Virtually Invisible Treatment

SmartTrack® clear plastic is nearly undetectable. Work, attend events, appear in photos — most people won’t know you’re in treatment unless you tell them.

No Food Restrictions — Ever

Remove aligners, eat whatever you want, brush, replace. No forbidden food lists, no awkward moments with chewy or crunchy meals.

Easier Oral Hygiene

Brush and floss normally. No threading under wires or working around brackets. Easier hygiene means lower risk of cavities and gum issues during treatment.

See Results Before You Start

Your 3D digital treatment plan shows a simulation of how your teeth will move through each aligner stage — from your current smile to your final result — before you begin.

More Comfortable Than Braces

No sharp wires or metal brackets to cut your cheeks. No emergency visits for broken brackets. Smooth, custom-fitted plastic that gently moves teeth over time.

Fewer Office Visits

Most Invisalign patients return for progress checks every 6–8 weeks — less frequently than typical braces adjustments. Less time in the chair, more time in your life.

Your Invisalign Journey at Rockwell Dentistry — Step by Step

Starting Invisalign at Rockwell Dentistry is a fully guided, no-guesswork process. Here’s exactly what happens from your first call to your final retainer:

Free Invisalign Consultation

Call (732) 706-1100 or request your consultation online . Dr. Rockwell examines your bite and alignment, discusses your goals, and we verify your insurance benefits before you arrive.

3D Digital Scan & Treatment Preview

An intraoral scanner takes a precise 3D digital scan of your teeth — no messy impression trays. Your scan generates a ClinCheck® digital treatment plan showing exactly how your teeth will move through each stage, including your final result.

Treatment Plan Review & Cost Confirmation

Dr. Rockwell presents your complete digital treatment plan — you see every aligner stage, the projected timeline, and the full cost. You approve the plan before we submit to Invisalign for fabrication. No surprises.

Aligner Delivery — Treatment Begins

Your custom aligner trays arrive. We fit your first tray, walk you through insertion and removal, and review home care instructions. Small tooth-colored attachments may be placed on certain teeth to guide movement.

Progress Checkups

Return every six to eight weeks for a brief progress check. Dr. Rockwell confirms your teeth are tracking as planned, provides your next aligner sets, and makes any refinements needed. Appointments are typically 20-30 minutes.

Treatment Complete — Retainer Fitted

When you’ve completed your aligner series and Dr. Rockwell confirms optimal tooth position, treatment is complete. A custom retainer is fitted — wearing it as directed maintains your new smile for life.
