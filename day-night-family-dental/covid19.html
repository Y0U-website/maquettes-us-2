COVID-19 Statement

At Day & Night Family Dental, ensuring the health and safety of our patients and employees is a top priority. While these added steps may take a bit more time, Day & Night Family Dental feels the additional caution is worth the effort and peace of mind.

If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact Day & Night Family Dental at (910) 354-2680 or carecenter@dayandnightdental.com .

  • COVID-19 Statement - Day & Night Family Dental Day & Night Family Dental remains open and ready to serve patients. However, given recent events, you might notice a few changes on your next visit. You should call Day & Night Family Dental before heading to our facility to let us know you are coming. The phone number for Day & Night Family Dental is (910) 354-2680. Currently, Day & Night Family Dental is only seeing emergency cases.
  • You should approach the patient entrance on arrival. You will be greeted by a dental assistant who will start the patient screening and registration process. If there is no dental assistant available to meet you, please be patient. The dental assistant is most likely helping another patient inside our facility. The bulk of the patient screening and registration process will be conducted by mobile phone with patients completing the screening and registration process from their car. Patients will be asked to wait in their vehicle until Day & Night Family Dental is ready to see them.
  • Day & Night Family Dental has implemented enhanced screening protocols for patients and employees. For patients and those accompanying patients, this involves taking your temperature, a discussion of your recent travel history as well as interactions you might have with those not feeling well. For convenience and safety, Day & Night Family Dental asks the number of family members, friends and associates accompanying patients are kept to a minimum.
  • Day & Night Family Dental has robust cleaning and sterilization protocols in place since the clinic’s inception in 2011. These protocols comply with CDC and NIOSH guidelines and are continually updated in response to any changes in those guidelines. In response to the Coronavirus threat, we will require all patients to wash their hands in the presence of an employee and rinse with a specially formulated mouth wash before being treated.
