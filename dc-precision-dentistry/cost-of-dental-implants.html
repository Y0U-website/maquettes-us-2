Comparing Costs of Types of Dental Implant Restorations

When you’re thinking about the cost of dental implants in Washington, DC, one of the biggest factors is what type of implant restoration you need. Different options are designed to fit different needs, and each comes with its own price range.

A Dental Implant

A single dental implant to replace one tooth typically costs $4,500. This price typically includes the implant, the connector piece (called an abutment), and the crown. In the DC area, many patients pay around the national average for this type of implant.

Implant Bridge

If you’re replacing several teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge will cost more than a single implant because it uses more implant posts and a larger restoration. Implant bridges can cost $5,000 based on the number of teeth you replace.

Implant Dentures

For patients who are missing most or all of their teeth, an implant-supported denture or an All-on-4/All-on-6 solution is a great option. Because full-arch restorations are more complex and require more implants and lab work, the overall cost is higher.

All-on-4 can cost $12,000 or more, but this depends on if you get a full arch or both arches. All-on-6 will also cost higher because it uses more implants. However, many patients with bone loss don’t need bone grafting if they get All-on-4, which lessens the cost of pretreatments.

The Value of Dental Implants

Although dental implants cost more upfront, they deliver a better quality of life. Implants are the most natural solution to replacing missing teeth . This is because they replace the tooth roots.

Dental implant posts are also permanent. If you brush, floss, and visit the dentist regularly, you will never need new implant posts. Dental implants ensure restorations last longer than if they were used with crowns or cement for support.

Implants don’t require regular maintenance or special care. Brushing and flossing between implant restorations will keep them clean. Dental implants are an investment that improves bite function, aesthetics, and overall health.

Why Choose Implants over Removable Restorations?

If you choose a traditional dental bridge or denture, you will most likely suffer from some bone loss over the years. Bone loss can lead to many issues, including making you look much older than you are, diet restrictions, and further tooth loss.

Even though they cost less upfront, dentures and dental bridges may need to be replaced, realigned, or rebased over time. Dentures also require daily care and cleaning that can be more time-consuming. These regular maintenance and replacement costs will add up over time and may even exceed the cost of implants.
