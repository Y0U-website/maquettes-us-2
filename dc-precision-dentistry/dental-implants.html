The Dental Implant Process in Washington, D.C.

Getting dental implants at DC Precision Dentistry is a multi-step process. We’ll ensure you feel comfortable and confident during each step. Our office also welcomes you to ask specific questions so we can provide clarification when needed.

Treatment Planning with the Sirona CBCT

We’re committed to providing our patients with the highest standard of dental care. With the Sirona CBCT, Dr. Deckard can produce high-quality images of both the soft and hard tissue. Utilizing Sirona CBCT technology gives patients a less invasive, more precise solution for placing dental implants.

Dr. Deckard uses CBCT technology to construct and plan the dental implant procedure. He’ll assess bone density and quality, plan the entire procedure, and take accurate molds and impressions. This means you won’t have to visit separate specialists, and you can save money by getting each step of implant treatment in one office.

Implant Surgery

Once Dr. Deckard determines you’re a good implant candidate, he’ll help you schedule a surgery date.

To prepare for the procedure, Dr. Deckard will first numb the implant site so you’re comfortable throughout the visit. During the surgery, Dr. Deckard will insert the titanium post into the jawbone. However, you may need a bone graft before implant placement if you don’t have enough bone density to support implants. We can coordinate pretreatments if needed.

Healing

During osseointegration, the titanium post fuses with the jawbone to create a stable foundation for the final dental prosthesis. Osseointegration typically takes 3 to 6 months to complete.

The Success Rate of Dental Implants

Patients who are thinking about replacing missing teeth with dental implants have some anxiety and fears. Some worry that the dental implant will fall out of their mouth. An implant will never fall out, but you must take care of it to keep it healthy.

The success rate of dental implants is typically very high. Dental implants have been around for decades and are a common procedure. One of the biggest fears surrounding dental implants is the fear that they’ll fall out of your mouth. Millions of people have had dental implants, but no data supports claims that they fall out.

A recent study was done on 5,200 patients who got dental implants between June 2008 and April 2015. It found that less than 3 % had an implant failure. The results prove that dental implants have a success rate of 95 % or higher. With proper care, your implants should last a lifetime and provide solid and natural-looking teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions

We welcome you to read answers to common questions about dental implants in Washington, DC. Please let us know if you have any more questions or concerns, and we’ll be happy to help.

Can I Get Dental Implants in One Day?

You can get a dental implant in one day after the surgeon places it in the jawbone, but it will take weeks for the implant to heal. The process of osseointegration, or bone growth around the implant, takes several weeks to fully complete. Dr. Deckard will discuss the expected timeframe between the placement of the dental implants and their restoration.

How Many Dental Implants Can I Get at Once?

Getting a single implant in one day is normal, but the healing can take weeks or even months. Usually, you’ll have to visit many times over a period of weeks or months to finish getting all your dental implants. Some dentists also offer all-on-four dental implants, which can hold dentures in place with just one appointment and the right sedation.

Will my Dental Implants Look Natural?

Yes, your dental implants will look and feel natural in most cases. Dr. Deckard can customize the crowns’ size, shape, and color on top of the implants to match your existing teeth perfectly. You shouldn’t be able to tell that they aren’t real teeth. These implants also work like natural teeth, making them an ideal alternative to dental bridges or partial dentures.

Are Dental Implants a Permanent Solution for Tooth Loss?

Yes, dental implants are a reliable and long-lasting solution for tooth replacement. They can last a lifetime with proper attention and maintenance. Dr. Deckard can provide detailed instructions on how to best care for them. Regular check-ups and cleanings can keep them looking and functioning properly.

How Painful is Getting an Implant?

Most patients don’t have significant pain when getting a dental implant. Your dentist will provide you with local anesthetics and sedation to ensure your comfort. Some mild discomfort is possible after the procedure, but you can manage this with over-the-counter pain medications. Dr. Deckard will discuss any potential side effects or complications so you fully understand the process of implantation and restoration.

Can Anyone Get Dental Implants?

Most patients are good candidates for dental implants, but there are a few factors to think about. You need to have enough healthy jawbone to support the implant, and your gums should be free of disease. Conditions like diabetes or smoke heavily can affect the healing process. Dr. Deckard will evaluate your oral and overall health to decide if implants are the right choice for you.
