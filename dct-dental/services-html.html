Services

Every smile, every stage

Nine areas of care under one roof — so you’re never referred across town for treatment we can do beautifully ourselves.

General Dentistry Comprehensive exams, fillings, and honest guidance for your whole family. We treat the small things early so they stay small.

Restorative Dentistry Tooth-colored fillings, crowns, and bridges that disappear into your natural smile. Most crowns are milled and placed in a single visit.

Cosmetic Dentistry Professional (in-office and take-home) whitening, porcelain veneers, and subtle reshaping designed around YOUR face — not a one-size-fits-all solution. The goal is a smile that looks like the best you.

Dental Implants Permanent, natural-feeling replacements for missing teeth, planned with 3D imaging for precision placement. Eat, speak, and smile with total confidence again.

Orthodontics Clear aligners for teens and adults, monitored with digital scans at every step. Straighter teeth are healthier teeth — and easier to keep clean.

Oral Surgery Wisdom teeth, extractions, and bone grafting performed in-house with sedation options for every comfort level. You’ll go home the same day with a clear recovery plan.

Botox & TMJ Therapy Relief for jaw pain, clenching, grinding, and tension headaches through targeted therapeutic Botox and custom night guards. Wake up without the ache.
