Care you can put a face to

Two doctors, one philosophy: listen first, explain everything, and never rush a patient through the chair.

Dr. Dean Trubschenck

Dr. Trubschenck was born and raised in Sacramento, California, where growing up with a father in dentistry first sparked his interest in the profession. After high school, he spent two years as a missionary in Monterrey, Mexico, becoming fluent in Spanish while assisting healthcare professionals with humanitarian care — an experience that cemented his passion for dentistry and serving others.

He earned a B.S. in Microbiology from Brigham Young University before receiving his DDS from the University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in 2017. After practicing in Austin, Texas, he relocated to Rhode Island, where he continues advanced training — including specialized study in Spain and Japan — and embraces modern digital dentistry, from digital scanning to 3D printing, to deliver precise, comfortable care.

Outside the office, he enjoys hiking and paddleboarding with his two Shiba Inus, riding motorcycles, playing recreational soccer, and exploring Rhode Island’s food and live music scene.

Dr. Matthew Wilks

Dr. Wilks is a Warwick native and graduate of Bishop Hendricken High School. He completed his bachelor’s degree at the University of Notre Dame before continuing his studies at the Marquette School of Dentistry, where he trained in the preventative, restorative, prosthodontic, endodontic, periodontal, and surgical disciplines of dentistry. He returned to our office in July 2024 as an associate general dentist to help patients in the community achieve their dental goals for healthy teeth and confident smiles.

In his free time, Dr. Wilks can be found running, golfing, playing guitar, and cheering on the Notre Dame Fighting Irish.
