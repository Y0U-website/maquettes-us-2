Frequently Asked Questions How often should I visit the dentist for a check-up?

Answer: It is recommended to visit your dentist every six months for a routine check-up and cleaning. Regular visits help detect potential issues early and keep your smile healthy.

  • What should I do if I have a dental emergency? Answer: If you’re experiencing a dental emergency, such as a knocked-out tooth, severe pain, or a broken tooth, contact our office immediately. We offer emergency appointments to address urgent dental concerns and relieve discomfort.
  • What are the options for teeth whitening? Answer: We offer several teeth whitening options, including in-office treatments and take-home kits. Dr. Mort will help you choose the best option based on your needs and the condition of your teeth.
  • Is dental work covered by insurance? Answer: Many dental procedures are covered by insurance, but coverage varies depending on your plan. Our office works with most major insurance providers, and we can assist in verifying your benefits and explaining out-of-pocket costs to the best of our abilities.
  • What should I do if I have sensitive teeth? Answer: Tooth sensitivity can be caused by various factors. It’s important to schedule an appointment with us to determine the cause and discuss treatment options. In the meantime, using a toothpaste designed for sensitive teeth may help alleviate discomfort.
