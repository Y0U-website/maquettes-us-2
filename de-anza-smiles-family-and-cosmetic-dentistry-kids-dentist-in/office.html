Comforts

Patient comfort is a top priority for everyone at De Anza Smiles Family Dentistry. We offer a variety of comforts to ensure our patients are relaxed and at ease throughout the entire treatment.

Chair Comfort

You will relax into our comfortable top of the line adjustable chairs that are upholstered with the softest of leather. All patients are offered protective darkened eyewear and warm blankets. At the end of the procedure, patients are offered warm cotton towelettes (disposable) to freshen themselves up.

Audiovisual Entertainment

Apple iPads and Bose wireless headsets in each treatment room allow you to browse and enjoy from a selection of entertainment choices a perfect distraction while completing your dental treatment.

Nitrous Oxide Anxiety and Pain Management

For anxious patients, especially those getting more extensive treatment, Dr. Saboowala provides mild oral sedative medication with Nitrous oxide/oxygen gas inhalation to help patients relax during the procedure.

Lobby Comfort

We offer fresh coffee and water bottles, along with TV entertainment in the lobby. Our kids nook is stocked with toys and books to keep children occupied.
