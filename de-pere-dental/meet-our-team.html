Meet Our Team

Carrie

Carrie has been in the dental field for over 25 years. Behind her friendly smile is someone who’s passionate about getting to know all our patients and making sure they receive quality service and attention from our dental office. Carrie was born and raised in Ashwaubenon and now lives in Kimberly with her fiancé and her puppy, Blueberry. Away from the dental office, she loves spending time with her family, especially her son, and reading, walking and shopping.

Trisha

Trisha joined the dental field after completing the Dental Assisting program at NWTC. She has been in the dental field her entire career and truly loves all the aspects of dentistry, especially getting to know the patients along with their lives and stories they share. Raised in Ashwaubenon, and a current De Pere resident, she loves being close to home and having her workplace be a part of her community. She and her husband, John, and their three daughters loves spending time as a family, especially up north at the cabin.

Alyssa

Alyssa began her career in dental in 2023 when she made a career change from the education field to join her husband at the dental office. Her favorite part of her job is working alongside the team to achieve a common goal of providing a pleasant environment and experience for patients. Alyssa was born and raised in Two Rivers and currently resides in De Pere with Dr. Mike and their 3 children and dogs. In her free time, Alyssa enjoys traveling, being with family and friends, being outdoors, and going up north to the cabin.

Jenny

Jenny has been a dedicated dental hygienist for over 15 years and is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. She earned her dental hygiene degree from NWTC and has loved building relationships with patients and providing compassionate care throughout her career. Originally from the area, Jenny lives in De Pere with her two children. When not caring for patients, she enjoys spending quality time with her family and making the most of everything the community has to offer.
