Jack Orzepowski, DDS

Why did Dr. Orzepowski choose dentistry?

I love both the art and science of dentistry, as well as working with patients to build their perfect smile! I also love getting to help patients improve their daily lives—whether it’s fixing a broken tooth or alleviating that nagging tooth pain. I value each relationship that I make with my patients and building trust together through their care.

“De Pere Smiles is a family, and we’re all here with the same goal in mind: do the right thing for our patients. I love going to work in such a warm and welcoming environment, as well as working together with a team that is always ready to go the extra mile!

I’m happy to be back in the Fox Valley and serving my community! I grew up down the road in Appleton, WI, and then attended Marquette University, receiving both my undergraduate and dental degrees. After graduation, I moved to Philadelphia, PA, and completed a one-year General Practice Residency at Albert Einstein Medical Center, where I gained training in surgery, endodontics, and implant placement. I believe strongly in keeping up with the constantly-changing dental field and am eager to chat with you about the latest and greatest.

I am married to my best friend, Taylor, who is a dentist in nearby Hortonville, WI. When we’re not talking about teeth, we both love to travel, golf, ski, hike, and catch our favorite bands on tour. We both couldn’t be happier to be settling down in the Fox Valley!
