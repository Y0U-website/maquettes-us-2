Our Implant Process

At Deakyne Dental Associates, we take a comprehensive approach to dental implants, ensuring each step is tailored to meet your needs. Here’s what you can expect during your implant journey:

Initial Consultation

Your journey begins with a personal consultation where we discuss your dental goals and perform a thorough examination. We’ll utilize state-of-the-art imaging technology to assess the condition of your jawbone and create a customized treatment plan.

Surgical Placement

During the surgical phase, a small titanium post will be inserted into your jawbone. This “root” is a stable, secure anchor for the artificial tooth and is allowed to heal and integrate with your jawbone over several months.

Abutment and Restoration

Once healing is complete, an abutment is attached to the implant post, which serves as the base for your custom-made dental crown. This final step restores your tooth’s appearance and function, leaving you with a seamless, natural-looking smile.
