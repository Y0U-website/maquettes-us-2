Common Orthodontic Issues We Treat

Orthodontic treatment addresses a variety of structural concerns that can impact oral health:

  • Crooked or misaligned teeth: Teeth that are not properly aligned can make cleaning more difficult and increase the risk of plaque buildup, cavities, and gum disease.
  • Overbite, underbite, and crossbite: Bite issues affect how the upper and lower teeth come together, which can lead to chewing difficulties, jaw strain, and uneven tooth wear.
  • Gaps between teeth: Excess spacing can trap food particles and affect both function and appearance, often requiring correction for proper alignment.
  • Jaw alignment issues: Improper jaw positioning can cause discomfort, difficulty chewing, and long-term stress on the temporomandibular joint (TMJ).
  • Uneven wear caused by improper bite: When teeth do not align correctly, certain areas experience excessive pressure, leading to accelerated wear and potential damage over time.

Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

Choosing orthodontic care provides both functional and long-term health advantages:

  • Improved Oral Health: Aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of decay and gum disease.
  • Better Bite Function: Correct alignment improves chewing efficiency and reduces strain on the jaw.
  • Reduced Risk of Damage: Proper positioning lowers the chance of uneven wear and tooth damage.
  • Enhanced Appearance: Straighter teeth contribute to a more balanced and natural smile.
  • Long-Term Stability: Well-aligned teeth are more likely to remain stable with proper maintenance.
