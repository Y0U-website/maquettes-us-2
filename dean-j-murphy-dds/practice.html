Welcome to Orthodontics Specialties

When you first arrive at Orthodontic Specialties you will be greeted by one of our friendly team. Committed to your orthodontic needs, our team will ensure you feel comfortable and at ease throughout your treatment.

The Orthodontic Specialties team has extensive experience in their specific duties and is dedicated to providing you with the best in orthodontic care. They will also help to schedule your future appointments for times which best suit your needs. During your consultation, one of our support team will accompany you into the clinic and be by your side throughout your consultation.

Within Orthodontic Specialties, we focus on the relationship we have with our patients and their families. We don’t work for an insurance company here. We work for you. The team at Orthodontic Specialties looks forward to caring for you.

Meet Dr. Murphy

Dr. Murphy was educated as a dentist and orthodontist at Chicago’s Loyola University. A native of the Chicago suburbs, he has lived in the Rockford area for over 25 years. Early arch development, cleft palate, TMJ treatment have been areas of particular interest in his 27 years as a practicing orthodontist. Continuing education to learn new techniques and apply those ideas to help patients has been a passion throughout his career.

As the father of six children, Dr. Murphy understands children’s wishes, feelings and concerns. He works with them to turn problems and obstacles into goals and achievements. Close attention and the desire to listen help carry over into his professional work. Solving problems becomes the motto for the day’ as each patient presents unique challenges. The latest technologies and diagnostic techniques are applied to help with a patient’s many needs.
