Services Offered in westchester

We’re proud to offer comprehensive services at our three convenient locations, meaning that no matter which office you visit, you can expect the same exceptional level of care. Services offered at our Westchester location include:

EndoDontics Endodontics is a branch of dentistry that focuses on the diagnosis and treatment of dental diseases, disorders, and abnormalities of the pulp tissue inside teeth.

Cracked Teeth Cracked teeth is just one type of traumatic dental injury. Others tend to include a sudden physical trauma, such as falls, sports injuries, car accidents, and other types of impact.

Root canal therapy Root canal therapy is a treatment used to repair and save a tooth that is severely decayed or infected. The procedure helps to prevent further infection by eliminating bacteria from the affected area.

Endodontic Surgery Endodontic microsurgeries are advanced procedures that involve the use of tiny instruments and microscopes to repair or replace damaged tooth structures with durable, long-lasting solutions.

Emergency Endodontics Endodontic emergencies can come in many forms. Common endodontic emergencies include severe tooth pain, a broken dental restoration, an abscessed or infected tooth, or a knocked-out tooth.

Sedation Dentistry Sedation dentistry can be beneficial for those who experience high levels of stress when visiting the endodontist, are afraid of needles, or have difficulty sitting still for an extended period of time.
