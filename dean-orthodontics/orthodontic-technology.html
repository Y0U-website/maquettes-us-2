Orthodontic Technology

  • Digital X-rays: Similar to a digital camera, a digital X-ray is connected to a computer, and its sensors are typically cleaner, so your orthodontist doesn’t have to make guesses.
  • Diode laser treatment : This laser is powerful enough to remove extra tissue from the gums and cheeks but gentle enough to reduce recovery time. It’s an excellent option for people who want a fast, effective procedure that ends in a more attractive smile.
  • uLab Systems aligners: There are a variety of brands of clear aligners on the market today, but our staff was impressed with the versatility and features of this manufacturer’s products. For instance, we can take a hybrid approach to our patients who can use aligners for the top arch and fixed appliances for the bottom.
