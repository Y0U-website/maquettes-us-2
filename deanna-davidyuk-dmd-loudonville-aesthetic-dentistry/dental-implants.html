Dental implants are one of the most advanced and natural-looking solutions for replacing missing teeth. Designed to function like a natural tooth, an implant replaces both the missing tooth root and the visible crown above the gumline. By integrating with your jawbone, dental implants provide a stable, long-lasting restoration that helps restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence.

What Are Dental Implants ?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots typically made from biocompatible titanium that are placed into the jawbone. Over time, the implant integrates with the surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a strong foundation for a custom dental restoration. Unlike a dental bridge, implants do not require altering the neighboring teeth. This allows you to replace a missing tooth while preserving the health and structure of the surrounding teeth.

The Dental Implant Restoration Has Three Parts :

1. Implant Post A small, screw-like titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone to replace the missing tooth root. 2. Abutment A connector piece attached to the implant post that supports the final restoration above the gumline. 3. Custom Crown A natural-looking tooth replacement that is custom designed to match your surrounding teeth in shape, size, and color.
