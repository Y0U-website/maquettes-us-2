Preventive dental care is the foundation of a healthy smile. Regular cleanings, comprehensive exams, digital X-rays, and oral cancer screenings help detect problems early—often before they become painful or require extensive treatment. By staying proactive with routine dental visits, you can protect your teeth, gums, and overall health for years to come.

What Is Preventive Treatment ?

Preventive treatment focuses on maintaining your oral health and preventing dental problems before they develop or worsen. Routine cleanings remove plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing alone cannot, while regular exams and digital X-rays allow us to identify cavities, infections, bone loss, and other concerns in their earliest stages. Every preventive visit also includes an oral cancer screening to help detect abnormalities as early as possible.

When Is Preventive Treatment Necessary ?

Dr. Davidyuk recommends preventive dental care for patients of all ages. Regular visits help maintain healthy teeth and gums, reduce the need for complex procedures, and allow small issues to be treated before they become larger, more costly problems.
