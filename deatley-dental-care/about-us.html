About Us

General & Cosmetic Dentistry in Mountain Home, AR

At DeAtley Dental Care, we offer compassionate, high-quality general and cosmetic dentistry for patients of all ages! We make sure that each and every patient feels comfortable and relaxed while in our care, and that we do everything we can to help you reach your dental goals. Our experienced, knowledgeable team offers a wide range of services, from routine cleanings to dental implants and teeth whitening .

We have been proudly serving patients in Mountain Home, AR and the surrounding areas since 2006!

Dr. Irvin DeAtley

Dr. Irvin is a native of the Twin Lakes area, growing up on the banks of the White River and graduating from Norfork High School in 1993. He received an Associates of Applied Science in Radiology at North Arkansas College in 1996, where he received the first Mallinckrodt Medical Excellence Award. Dr. DeAtley worked in Radiology at Baxter Regional Medical Center for several years before deciding to continue his education and pursue a degree in Dentistry. He attended the University of Oklahoma and attained his Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree in 2006.

Dr. Melvin Benson Jr.

Dr. Melvin Benson Jr., DDS, FAGD, is a graduate of the University of Oklahoma Health Sciences Center College of Dentistry (2006), which is among the top clinical education programs in the nation. Dr. Benson has authored a book titled Changing the Way You Go to the Dentist, and in 2018 he became one of the elite 6 % of general dentists to earn a Fellowship from the Academy of General Dentistry (FAGD). He has been practicing dentistry for 20 years and is licensed in Florida, Arkansas, Colorado, and Wyoming.

Although new to DeAtley Dental Care, Dr. Benson is not new to the Mountain Home area. Dr. Benson and Dr. Irvin DeAtley attended dental school at OU together from 2002-2006 and became fast friends. Dr. Benson has been visiting Mountain Home for the past 24 years and he is very excited to become a more permanent fixture in the community and to be an additional dentist at DeAtley Dental Care.
