FINANCIAL

We understand that dental insurance can be a highly complex area that creates confusion in understanding the highest level of benefits to which you and your child are entitled to receive. For the convenience of our patients, we accept most dental insurance plans, and we are in-network with most major dental insurances.

  • Read More If you have a dental benefit plan, please bring along your dental insurance card and current information with you at each of your child’s dental adventures. We will be more than happy to file all claim forms electronically provided we receive your insurance information before or on the day of your appointment. Our staff will always be available to help answer any questions you have with insurance claims or insurance coverage.
