Dental Implant Options

Dental implants can replace one or more missing teeth. We can use single dental implants to replace one or all of your missing teeth. These are standalone prosthetics that do not require any special tools or products for regular care.

If you have multiple missing teeth in a row, we can use a fixed dental bridge in combination with dental implants to replace your missing teeth. Dental implants provide stability for bridges and can help our patients avoid the need for a partial denture. Because dental bridges do require extra care to stay clean, your implant-supported bridge will too.

Implant-supported dentures combine the benefits of dental implants with dentures for additional confidence and security. Placing several (usually two to six) dental implants per arch that lock in your dentures can offer the best of both worlds!

Dental Implant Treatment

Dental implant treatment requires a couple of steps.

1. A comprehensive exam will help us determine if you are a good candidate for dental implants. If you need bone grafting to increase your bone density prior to surgery, we will let you know and provide you with options! Most patients are good candidates for dental implants, but some medical conditions or medications may prevent you from dental surgery.

2. If dental implants are a good option for you, we will place your dental implant . This requires surgery, but most patients report it was much easier than they imagined! As you heal, your dental implant will begin to fuse with your bone, creating a stable foundation for your crown. Complete healing usually takes a few months. In some cases, we can restore a dental implant right away.

3. Once you heal, we will create a custom dental crown that matches your existing teeth. This is the part of the implant that will be visible when you smile. Because we take great care creating dental implants for our patients, your smile will look seamless and natural.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do dental implants cost more than other replacement options? Dental implants are the best and most permanent replacement option that involved more time and specialized parts. Up front dental implants may cost more, but typically cost far less over the span of a lifetime compared to constantly replacing bridgework or partial dentures.

Why is it important to replace missing teeth?

Missing teeth can cause many health problems. Replacing missing teeth can prevent problems like bone loss in the jaws, premature wrinkles, gaps in the smile, problems with speech and nutrition/eating, nearby teeth shifting and break down in a short amount of time.

What are the advantages of dental implants?

Dental implants have many advantages. They look just like real teeth. They are strong and long-lasting. They are much easier to clean around than bridges. They allow you to eat and talk normally. They keep the bone from wearing away where the tooth was lost.

I’m missing some teeth. What is the best way to replace them?

The best way to replace missing teeth is often with dental implants. They are the best replacement of lost teeth available today because they look great and last a long time. Dental implants can last for years or even decades when properly cared for and they blend into your smile so they are not noticeable.
