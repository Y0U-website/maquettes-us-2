Stabilize Your Dentures With Dental Implants

These dentures rest on your gums but won’t slip or slide during eating and speaking. This option provides excellent stability and is often more affordable than other implant dentures.

Implant-supported dentures use at least four dental implants to keep your replacement teeth securely fixed in place. They are fully supported by the implants rather than your gums. This provides maximum stability and chewing power. You simply brush and floss to clean them.

While they typically cost more initially, they offer the closest match to natural teeth and can last many years with proper care.

We’ll Take Care of Your Entire Treatment Before getting your implant dentures, we’ll ensure your mouth is healthy and ready for treatment. If needed, your dentist will remove damaged or diseased teeth. During this process, we’ll take careful steps to preserve as much bone as possible for your future implants.

If you’ve lost bone density over time, we can rebuild your jaw through bone grafting or ridge augmentation procedures. These treatments create a strong foundation for your implants.
