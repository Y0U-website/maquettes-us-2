Our current office was newly constructed in 2017. We utilize the latest in dental technology, including low-radiation digital x-rays (which emit up to 90 % less radiation than conventional x-ray machines) and intraoral cameras to help you achieve and maintain optimum oral health. We offer a wide range of services including all aspects of modern dentistry. Let us put our facilities to work for you!

Our office maintains paperless medical and dental records and is in compliance with the latest digital record keeping laws as well as HIPPA compliance to assure patient privacy and confidentiality.

The members of our team undergo extensive training in sterilization procedures and follow all current guidelines established by the CDC and OSHA to ensure patient safety. We also participate in regular continuing education courses to stay on the cutting edge of available methods and materials.

We strive to create the individual care and attention that each patient deserves, and to ensure maximum comfort and convenience for you as part of our services. We are focused on increasing your quality of life and restoring confidence in your healthy beautiful smile!

In her efforts to know each patient, Dr. Sobocinski performs a comprehensive review of your dental as well as medical history. Sometimes seemingly unrelated illnesses or medications can directly or indirectly impact your dental health, so Dr. Sobocinski takes the time to review your history carefully.

During the first visit expect to have a full mouth set of x-rays taken and reviewed as well as getting a comprehensive oral exam. You will be presented with different options for treatment plans and financial arrangements which will help you make decisions about which treatment best suits your needs.
