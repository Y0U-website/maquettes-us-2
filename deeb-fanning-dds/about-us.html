Calabash, NC Dental Practice

At Deeb & Fanning DDS, we believe that everyone deserves the chance to keep their natural teeth for a lifetime. This belief drives us to provide comprehensive preventive care, patient education, and the finest restorative treatments available.

We’re not here to treat you as just another patient, but as a guest and a friend. With over 35 years of dedicated service to the communities of Brunswick and Horry County, we’ve earned a reputation for providing excellent, personalized dental care.

Dr. Deeb, Dr. Fanning, Dr. Wolf, and Dr. Graham are also proud members of the American Dental Association (ADA), the North Carolina Dental Society, and the International Association for Orthodontics.

What Makes Our Practice Unique?

Care and Commitment

From the moment you walk in, you’ll notice that our entire team is dedicated to your health and comfort. We use the latest technology and state-of-the-art equipment to provide the best possible care.

Our office is designed to feel clean, comfortable, and thoroughly sterilized, so you can rest assured that your safety is our top priority.

Honesty and Affordability

We provide clear, upfront information about the cost of your treatment and offer a range of payment options to fit your budget. We also assist with dental insurance and alternative payment solutions, ensuring that financial concerns never get in the way of your care.

Easy Access to Dental Care and Information

We know your time is valuable, which is why we offer flexible office hours and provide reminders for your appointments. Whether you prefer to speak with us in person, over the phone, or via email, we’re always here to answer your questions and address any concerns you may have.

Comprehensive Care in One Location

At Deeb & Fanning DDS, we offer a full range of dental services right here in our office. From routine checkups to complex restorative work, we’re equipped to handle it all.

If we need to refer you to a specialist, we’ll send you to trusted professionals who share our commitment to quality care.

Personalized Service

We take the time to get to know you and your oral health goals. Our team works with you to create a treatment plan that’s tailored to your needs, and we’re always here to offer guidance and support every step of the way.
