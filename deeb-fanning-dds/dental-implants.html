Dental Implants in Calabash, NC

A missing tooth changes the way your mouth works. Biting into food can feel uneven, speaking may shift slightly, and smiling can feel uncomfortable. Dental implants fill the gaps in your smile with strong, natural-looking roots anchored in your jawbone.

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium screws that act as sturdy foundations for replacement teeth. Once placed in the jaw, the bone gradually bonds with the implant. This process creates strong, lasting support for crowns, bridges, or dentures.

With implants, you can smile naturally, eat almost anything, and feel confident in your appearance.

How Are Dental Implants Placed?

The dental implant process begins with a thorough evaluation, including a dental exam and X-rays. Depending on your needs, either our dentists or a referred oral and maxillofacial surgeon will guide your treatment.

Implants are surgically placed into the jawbone, sometimes at the same time as extractions and bone grafting. Over the next 8–12 weeks, the implants integrate with the bone while you may use a temporary prosthesis and eat a soft diet.

Once integration is complete, healing collars are placed, impressions are taken, and your final restoration— crown , bridge , or denture —is attached. Most patients complete the process in four to six months without major disruption to daily life.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Good candidates for dental implants are generally healthy and have enough jawbone to support the implant. If bone has diminished, grafting can rebuild the area for a successful placement. During your consultation, our team will go over your health history, answer your questions, and create a plan that fits your smile and your needs.

Are Dental Implants Permanent?

With regular care, dental implants can last for decades. Once they’ve bonded with your jawbone, they stay firmly in place, supporting teeth that look and function like your own. Implants help maintain your facial structure and let you eat, speak, and smile without worry for years.
