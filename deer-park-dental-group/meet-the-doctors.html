MEET YOUR DEER PARK DENTAL GROUP DENTIST

Rushi Dave, DMD, FAGD, FICOI

Through his education and experiences, Dr. Dave aims to provide personalized patient care while striving for clinical excellence. He believes in transparency, strong communication, and building a trustworthy relationship with all his patients. Dr. Dave is excited to provide quality and comforting care to his patients using his knowledge, experience, passion, and enthusiasm for dentistry!

Dr. Dave is a proud member of the Academy of General Dentistry, International Congress of Oral Implantologists, Academy of Osseointegration. He recently received his fellowship from ICOI and Academy of General Dentistry.

When not saving lives one tooth at a time, Dr. Dave is either spending time with his wife, catching a Houston sporting event or playing basketball and tennis with his buddies. He recently developed an interest in baking desserts and making pizzas from scratch!

MEET YOUR DEER PARK DENTAL GROUP DENTIST

Mili Shah, DMD

I feel honored that my patients place their trust in me, and I take my responsibility and their health seriously. I try to put myself in the shoes of the patient and believe in the importance of education to help them make the best choices for their oral health.

Dr. Mili Shah’s passion for dentistry also began at an early age! As the younger sister, she often followed her sister around and found herself at her dental appointments. It was these visits that sparked her interest in dentistry, and she began shadowing a dentist in high school. She followed her childhood dream and earned her doctorate degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, MA.

As a member of American Academy of General Dentistry, Texas Academy of General Dentistry and Spear Study Club, Dr. Shah is continually learning more about general dentistry, technology and facial esthetics. Since the field constantly evolves, Dr. Shah finds it important to stay on top of the latest developments through continuing education. Patient comfort and satisfaction are her top priorities.

In her free time, Dr. Shah enjoys spending time with her husband Dr. Dave, checking out the latest restaurants, cooking, traveling and DIY projects.

About Our Dental Philosophy

At Deer Park Dental Group, we believe that a strong dental practice is built on trust, transparency, and patient-centered care. Our dentists prioritize open communication with patients to ensure they feel comfortable and informed about their dental health.

We focus on creating a welcoming environment where every patient can voice their concerns and preferences. This philosophy not only fosters a strong relationship between our dentists and patients but also enhances the overall dental experience, leading to better health outcomes.

Comprehensive Dental Services Offered

Deer Park Dental Group offers a wide range of dental services tailored to meet the diverse needs of our patients. From routine check-ups to advanced cosmetic procedures, we ensure that every aspect of dental health is covered.

Our services include general dentistry, cosmetic enhancements, orthodontics, and emergency care. Each service is designed to maintain and improve your oral health, ensuring that you leave our office with a confident smile.

Meet Our Team of Experts

Our team at Deer Park Dental Group is comprised of highly qualified and experienced dental professionals dedicated to providing exceptional care. Each dentist brings a unique skill set and perspective to the practice, ensuring comprehensive treatment options for our patients.

In addition to Dr. Rushi Dave and Dr. Mili Shah, our support staff is trained to assist in every aspect of your visit, from scheduling appointments to providing aftercare instructions. Together, we create a cohesive team focused on your dental health and satisfaction.
