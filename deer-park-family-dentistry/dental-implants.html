Cost of Dental Implants

Each stage of the dental implant process will incur a specific cost, which is why it’s hard to give a precise estimate of the price of your treatment. The surgical placement, possible addition of an abutment, and custom restoration will each have a different cost associated with them. Additionally, preexisting health issues as well as bone grafting may add to the price of your treatment. For patients with weaker jawbones, we may have to perform bone grafting to strengthen it, allowing for the placement of implants.

Preliminary Treatments & Dental Implant Surgery

When meeting with your implant dentist in Deer Park, you’ll find out if you need preliminary treatments before undergoing the surgical placement of your new teeth. If bone grafting, gum disease treatment, tooth extraction, a sinus lift, or any other similar procedure is required, this will be factored into your overall cost.

Additionally, the placement of your dental implants will also be incorporated. Since our dentists partner with local specialists in the area, you can expect to receive a separate bill from their office.

The Parts of Your Dental Implant

Apart from any preliminary procedures and dental implant surgery you’ll require, there are additional factors that must also be considered:

  • Number of Implants: The more teeth you are missing, the more you can expect to pay. A single titanium post will cost much less than the four or six you will need for a full implant denture.
  • Type of Restoration: Fabricating an implant crown is not as expensive as an implant denture, so this will be incorporated into the cost of your treatment.
  • Material: Most dental implants are crafted out of titanium or zirconia, each with its own price tag.
  • Manufacturer: The company that manufactures your dental implants will come with a price; however, you can expect our team to make sure you receive high-quality implants that last.

FAQs

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to replace the roots of missing teeth, providing a permanent foundation for replacement crowns, bridges, or dentures. The implant integrates with your natural bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable base that functions like a natural tooth root. This comprehensive replacement system restores both the visible tooth structure and the essential root support that maintains jawbone health and facial structure.

How many teeth can dental implants replace?

Whether you’re missing one, several, or all your natural teeth, dental implants can help you regain your smile! A dental crown paired with a single implant post can easily replace a single tooth. An implant-retained bridge or partial denture can replace several teeth at a time, usually with one dental implant post on either side of the gap. If you’re missing an entire arch of teeth, a full denture can be anchored in place with a few strategically placed implant posts.

How successful are dental implants?

Dental implants boast an astonishing success rate of over 95 %. However, their success or failure depends heavily on how well you take care of them. As mentioned above, issues like tooth decay and gum disease can threaten the stability and functionality of your dental implant, so it’s imperative that you continue to practice great oral hygiene to keep your newly rebuilt smile sparkling and strong.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants have the potential to last a lifetime with proper care and maintenance, with success rates exceeding 95 % over many years of clinical research. The titanium implant post itself is designed to be permanent, while the crown or restoration may need replacement after 15-20 years due to normal wear. With excellent oral hygiene and regular dental checkups, many patients enjoy their implants for 30 years or more without any complications.

Do dental implants hurt?

The implant placement procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won’t feel pain during the surgery, though some pressure sensations are normal. Most patients report that post-operative discomfort is less than they expected and can be managed effectively with over-the-counter pain medications for a few days. The healing process is generally comfortable, with most patients returning to normal activities within a few days of the procedure.

How are dental implants cared for?

Not only do dental implants look, act, and feel like natural teeth, but they can be cared for just as easily as well! You won’t need to use any special products or make any changes to your daily dental routine. Brushing for two minutes twice a day, flossing daily, and rinsing with antiseptic mouthwash regularly can help them last for decades to come. In fact, with the proper care, you can easily expect your dental implants to last a lifetime!

How do dental implants work?

Dental implants work by replacing the natural tooth root with a biocompatible titanium post that integrates with your jawbone over several months. Once osseointegration is complete, an abutment is attached to the implant to connect it to a custom crown, bridge, or denture restoration. This creates a complete tooth replacement that functions, feels, and looks like a natural tooth while providing the stimulation needed to maintain a healthy jawbone.

Are dental implants safe?

Dental implants are considered one of the safest and most predictable procedures in dentistry, with decades of research supporting their safety and effectiveness. The biocompatible titanium material is well-tolerated by the human body and has been used successfully in medical applications for many years. When placed by experienced professionals and maintained with good oral hygiene, implants have minimal risk of complications and provide excellent long-term outcomes.

Will my dental insurance cover the cost of dental implants?

Every dental insurance policy is different, so you’ll need to review the specifics of your plan to see what it covers. Feel free to bring your insurance paperwork with you to your consultation and we’ll be happy to go over it with you. In most cases, dental insurance companies will pay for portions or percentages of a dental implant procedure. At Deer Park Family Dental, we’re proud to also offer affordable financing options through CareCredit. This will allow you to break up the cost of your treatment into easy payments that fit into your monthly budget.
