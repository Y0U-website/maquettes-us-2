Complete Smile Evaluation

Dr. Jane Reeves and Dr. Jason Peck will examine your teeth, discuss your smile goals, and determine if dental Invisalign is the right choice for your specific orthodontic needs. During this thorough evaluation, we’ll assess your current bite and tooth alignment to ensure you’re a good candidate for this type of dental service. You’ll learn about the treatment process, expected timeline, and investment required for your customized clear aligner therapy.

Custom Aligner Creation

Using precise digital impressions and measurements, we work with Invisalign to create your personalized set of clear aligners that fit your teeth perfectly. Each aligner in the series is designed to move your teeth gradually toward their ideal positions while maintaining your comfort throughout treatment. Your custom aligners are manufactured specifically for your unique dental structure and orthodontic needs.

Aligner Fitting and Care Instructions

You’ll receive your first set of custom aligners along with detailed instructions for proper wear and maintenance during your dental Invisalign treatment. Each aligner should be worn for 20-22 hours per day, removing them only for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing to ensure optimal results. Our team will provide comprehensive guidance on caring for your aligners and maintaining excellent oral hygiene throughout the process.

Treatment Monitoring Visits

Regular check-up appointments every 6-8 weeks allow us to monitor your progress and ensure your teeth are moving according to plan. Dr. Jane Reeves and Dr. Jason Peck will assess your tooth movement, provide your next set of aligners, and address any concerns you may have about your treatment progress. These monitoring visits are essential for keeping your dental Invisalign treatment on track and achieving your desired results.

Retention and Smile Maintenance

Upon completion of your clear aligner treatment, you’ll receive custom retainers to maintain your beautiful new smile and protect your investment in orthodontic care. Dr. Jane Reeves and Dr. Jason Peck will provide detailed instructions on retainer wear and care to ensure your teeth remain in their new positions long-term. Regular follow-up appointments help us monitor your smile stability and address any maintenance needs for lasting results.

FAQs

How long does Invisalign take?

The duration of Invisalign treatment typically ranges from 12 to 18 months for most patients, though simple cases may be completed in as little as 6 months, while more complex alignments can take up to 24 months. Your specific timeline depends on the severity of your misalignment, how consistently you wear your aligners, and your individual response to treatment. Most patients change to a new set of aligners every one to two weeks throughout the treatment process.

How does Invisalign work?

Invisalign works by using a series of custom-made, clear plastic aligners that gradually shift your teeth into proper alignment. Each aligner applies gentle, controlled pressure to specific teeth, moving them incrementally toward their final positions. You’ll wear each set of aligners for about two weeks before progressing to the next set in the series, with each aligner designed to continue the precise movement planned by your orthodontist using advanced 3D imaging technology.

What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is a modern orthodontic treatment system that straightens teeth using clear, removable aligners instead of traditional metal braces. The aligners are virtually invisible when worn and are custom-designed for each patient using digital impressions and 3D computer modeling. This innovative approach allows you to achieve a straighter smile discreetly while maintaining the ability to eat, drink, and clean your teeth normally by removing the aligners as needed.

Does Invisalign hurt?

Invisalign typically causes mild discomfort rather than significant pain, especially during the first few days of wearing a new set of aligners as your teeth adjust to the pressure. Most patients describe the sensation as a feeling of tightness or pressure that gradually subsides as their teeth begin to move. Any discomfort is generally much less than with traditional braces and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers if needed.

How to get Invisalign?

To get Invisalign, start by scheduling a consultation with an orthodontist or certified Invisalign provider who will evaluate your teeth and determine if you’re a good candidate for treatment. During this appointment, they’ll take digital impressions or scans of your teeth to create a personalized treatment plan. Once approved, your custom aligners will be manufactured, and you’ll receive detailed instructions on wearing and caring for them throughout your treatment journey.
