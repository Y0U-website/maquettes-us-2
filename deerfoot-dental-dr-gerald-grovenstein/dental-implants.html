Dental Implants

  • Natural look and feel: They blend seamlessly with your smile and function like real teeth.
  • Secure and comfortable: Unlike removable dentures, implants remain firmly in place, improving comfort and speech.
  • Support oral health: Implants help prevent bone loss and preserve your jaw structure.
  • Full function restored: Enjoy eating your favorite foods again with natural chewing strength.
  • What is the procedure for dental implants? A dental implant is placed by inserting a titanium post into the jawbone, creating a strong foundation for a replacement tooth. Our experienced dentists make the process comfortable and ensure long-lasting, successful results.
  • What are dental implants made of? Most dental implants are made from titanium, a biocompatible material that safely integrates with the jawbone. Chosen for its strength and durability, titanium fuses naturally with bone, creating a stable and long-lasting base for replacement teeth.
  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are a dependable, long-term solution for missing teeth and can last a lifetime with proper care. Maintaining good oral hygiene, visiting your dentist regularly, and avoiding habits like smoking are key to ensuring their durability.
  • Is the dental implant procedure painful? Dental implant procedures are generally done with local anesthesia, and most patients experience only mild discomfort. Any soreness afterward is usually manageable with over-the-counter pain medication and improves within a few days. At Deerfoot Dental, our team provides clear aftercare guidance to support a comfortable recovery.
  • How do I care for my dental implants? Caring for dental implants is much like caring for natural teeth—daily brushing, flossing, and routine checkups are essential. To protect them long-term, avoid biting hard objects and maintain healthy gums, as regular dental visits help preserve both the health and lifespan of your implants.
  • Can anyone get dental implants? Many patients qualify for dental implants, but a consultation is needed to confirm candidacy. Factors such as bone density, oral health, and overall wellness are considered, and our team will perform a detailed evaluation to design a treatment plan tailored to your needs.
