The practice

We work as a team, dedicated to improving and maintaining your oral health. Whatever your dental needs, you will be in great hands.

Our team has the professional experience to realize that there is no such thing as one-size-fits-all approach to treatment. Whether you need a cleaning or crowns, each patient will be treat as an individual.

Although we can handle most cases in office, we may feel it’s in your best interest to see a specialist for highest quality of care. We have developed a referral network composed of only the very best doctors southern Maine has to offer, and are confident our collaborative approach to your care will be of the highest quality.

We understand that the dental office is not everyone’s favorite place to be, that is why we take the time to make sure you are completely comfortable at all times in our chair.
