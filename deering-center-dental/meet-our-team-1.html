Our Team

Lisa Mathews, RDH/ Office Manager Lisa is going on 35+ years as a hygienist and has been with the office since 2002. She has recently transitioned to front desk duties but helps out in all aspects of the office. She is originally from Connecticut but has been a Mainah since college, getting her hygiene degree from Westbrook College (Now UNE) in 1984. She and her husband Steve have 4 wonderful adult children and an adorable family pup. When she’s away from her desk, she enjoys gardening, boating at her camp on Little Sebago and taking cruises!

Molly Fitzgerald, RDH The daughter of Dr. Rich, Molly grew up in New Gloucester, ME and has been with the practice for 14+ years. She graduated from Forsyth School of Dental Hygiene in Boston in 2008 and cherishes relationships with her patients, who greatly enjoy visits because they are comfortable and full of laughs. Molly previously worked as a clinical instructor at UNE School of Dental Hygiene and an EMT for over 10 years. Outside of dentistry, Molly has a precious son, Tommy, with her husband, Hale and adorable Frenchies, Ellie & Arlee. She enjoys riding horses and downhill skiing.
