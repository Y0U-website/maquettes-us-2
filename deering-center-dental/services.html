Our Services

Emergency Care & Extractions All teeth are different and accidents happen. Whether your teeth are chipped, damaged or if you’ve had mouth trauma, our team can treat emergent situations and get you back to comfort in a quick and efficient manner. We perform most extractions in house on a case-by-case basis. If you require additional medical attention or have a particularly tricky tooth, we may refer you to an oral surgeon.

Restorative Whether its fillings or crowns, we can easily handle all of your restorative needs. Cavities and cracks are what we do every day, so you can feel comfortable that we will competently address those needs and return you to optimal dental health.

Dentures and Implants Dentures and implants are a great way to restore function and esthetics to those teeth that have retired from years of chewing. We offer all types of dentures and work with the finest specialists in the area to place and restore implants. If you require removal of your remaining teeth, immediate dentures are a great option to leave with a beautiful bright smile!
