All memberships are set up on auto-pay and auto-renewal, renewing on your enrollment date. You have 14 days from renewal to cancel for a full refund, provided no treatment has been rendered (including cleanings and exams). After 6 months, you’re responsible for the full unpaid annual balance, with payments credited toward it.

If a treatment plan is presented while your membership is active and the membership lapses before treatment moves forward or completes, membership discounts are removed from the remaining fees.
