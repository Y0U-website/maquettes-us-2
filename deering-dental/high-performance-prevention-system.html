What Is High Performance Prevention?

At its core, High Performance Prevention is a smarter, more effective approach to dental care. It’s the system behind our 86.5 % reduction in root canals and the reason our patients need less dental work, not more.

This isn’t just about hygiene. It’s about biology, behavior, biomechanics, and breathing.

By addressing the root causes of decay and disease and helping your body work the way it was meant to, we’re able to keep your teeth stronger, longer, and healthier for life.

The 3 Pillars of True Prevention

1. Biology: Work with your body, not against it

We focus on restoring balance in the oral microbiome, supporting remineralization, and reducing inflammation. Key elements backed by emerging research linking oral health to systemic wellness.

Imbalances of the oral microbiota exert relevant influence in systemic health. ( PMC9405223 )

2. Behavior: Replace damaging habits with smarter ones

Habits like frequent snacking, poor sleep, or using harsh oral care products disrupt your body’s natural defenses. Our system corrects these patterns to protect against decay and gum disease.

3. Biomechanics: Fix the structure to stop the stress

Your bite, jaw alignment, and airway can all affect tooth health. Structural issues like grinding, clenching, or mouth breathing often lead to premature wear and our system actively addresses those before they cause damage.

How Our Patients Need 86.5 % Fewer Root Canals

Instead, we intervene early with tools like hydroxyapatite remineralization, dental probiotics, and non-invasive techniques that restore teeth before they reach the point of needing a root canal.

Hydroxyapatite toothpastes have been shown to deposit and restore demineralized enamel surfaces. ( PMC8930857 )

Biomimetic dentistry is the art and science of repairing damaged teeth with restorations that imitate living tissues in terms of appearance, function, and strength. ( PMC9936671 )

These methods strengthen natural tooth structure and drastically reduce the retreatment cycle that comes with traditional “drill-and-fill” approaches.

Why Most Dentists Keep You Stuck in the Cycle

Traditional dentistry often waits for problems to develop. Then it drills, cuts, and replaces.

Each procedure removes more healthy structure and makes the tooth more fragile. Fillings lead to crowns, crowns lead to root canals, and eventually, teeth are lost. Prevention gets buried under damage control.

The High Performance Prevention system breaks this cycle, focusing instead on preserving what’s still strong, fixing what’s vulnerable, and stopping decay before it starts.

What Makes This System Different

The Oral-Systemic Advantage

We now know that oral health isn’t just about teeth.

People who have poor oral health have higher rates of cardiovascular problems such as heart attack or stroke than people with good oral health. ( Harvard Health )

Oral microbiome dysbiosis may contribute to cardiometabolic diseases. ( IFM )

That’s why our system isn’t just cosmetic or surface-level. It can have serious implications for heart health, brain health, and metabolic resilience.

When you treat the mouth as part of the whole body, everything changes.
