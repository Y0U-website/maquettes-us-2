Dental Implants are a viable option for most adults with tooth loss. Although a thorough consultation must be completed before moving forward with treatment, we can determine how best to proceed so that your Dental Implant placement is most successful.

When you lose teeth, the bone in your jaw that supported them starts to shrink. The sockets recede, and over time the bone gets smaller and smaller. For many people, this results in a sunken-faced look that could easily have been avoided.

Not only does the bone shrink, but as that process continues, it can result in the shifting of your teeth, leading to a domino effect of tooth loss. While dental restorations like bridges can slow the shifting of teeth, they don’t prevent bone loss and eventually can still result in the same troubles.

Dental Implants are able to stop bone loss by replacing the lost roots of your teeth. This means that bone won’t shrink, and you’ll be saving the rest of your teeth as well!

By providing a lasting, positive result, Dental Implants can improve your self-esteem and confidence in your smile and your oral health.

Completing Your Smile With Dental Implants

Before Dental Implant Treatment After Dental Implant Treatment Let’s answer some of your Dental Implant questions… What Is A Dental Implant? Dental Implants are small titanium screws that are about the size of the roots of your natural teeth. The Dental Implant is placed in your jaw and fills the role of your missing root. Implants are threaded on the inside and outside, so once your implant is placed, we are able to finish it with a variety of dental restorations.

Dental Implants act a lot like any other permanent surgical material – they integrate with your bone to create an incredibly strong and permanent bond. Just like screws used in other surgeries, titanium is the material of choice due to its excellent ability to integrate with bone.

Dental Implants are a good option for anyone in good health. In order to place the implant, you must have healthy gums and enough bone below the implant to support it. If either of these are an issue for you, there is a way to treat either of these conditions first before placing an implant.

Many do not experience any pain at all throughout the process. Most people are able to work the next day after each step in the treatment process.

One of the biggest benefits of Dental Implants is that, in most cases, they prevent future pain and complications. Dental Implants are a permanent solution that allow you to turn back the clock on your teeth. They’re just like having your real, healthy teeth back.

Here at our Deering Dental we offer various options for Dental Implants:

Single Tooth Implants

Single tooth implants simply replace one tooth. A tiny titanium post is gently placed into your mouth with a crown fitted on top. Dental Implants are proven to be the most beneficial way to replace either a single, multiple or full set of teeth.

Multiple Teeth Implants

If you have a few missing teeth, whether this is from an accident or illness or poor oral care, then don’t worry we can fix these! Multiple Dental Implants can be placed with a permanent fixed bridge which enables you to eat and chew whatever you like. The replacements will look realistic, natural and function well.

Implant Retained Dentures

These offer a greater support to your existing dentures using a series of implants with a bar or clasp system to snap your dentures onto. You can still remove them at night but they will give you a higher level of stability during the day.
