The Process of Getting Dental Implants in Troy, MI

If you’re considering implants to restore your smile, Drs. Ted and Chris Degenhardt would love to talk with you. Our highly skilled dentists have helped hundreds of patients restore their smiles with dental implants and can help you, too.

Better yet, we can complete implant dentistry from start to finish here at Degenhardt Dental so you can work with the dentists and team you trust. Here’s how the process works:

If you’re considering tooth implants in Troy, MI, contact Degenhardt Dental at (248) 643-6551 to book an evaluation with one of our dentists.

  • Initial Evaluation The first step is booking an evaluation with one of our dentists to discuss your options. We’ll use 3-D imaging to pre-plan the precise placement of your implant for optimal results on the day of your surgery.
  • Tooth Extraction and Implant Placement Dental implant placement occurs through a simple surgical procedure completed at our Troy dental office. Sometimes, this procedure is combined with tooth extraction if we re able to place the implant at the same time. If not, implant placement may take place after healing from the extraction is completed.
  • Healing and Integration Next, we’ll give the implant time to heal and integrate with your bone through osseointegration, which can take three to six months. Osseointegration sets the foundation for your new restoration.
  • Restoration Once healing and integration are completed, our dentists will restore your smile with a ceramic porcelain crown for a single tooth or a bridge or denture to replace multiple teeth.
