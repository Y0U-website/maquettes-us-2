What Are the Advantages of All-on-X Dentures?

Choosing implant dentures in Troy offers our patients several excellent benefits. Many patients wearing ill-fitting, removable dentures have switched or are considering all-on-x for their smiles.

Implant-supported dentures are held securely in place, so you won’t have to worry about embarrassing movement or slippage when you talk or laugh.

Dental implants provide the added benefit of keeping your jawbone stimulated, so you don’t have to worry about the bone loss or deterioration associated with traditional denture wear.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the all-on-x system different from a standard removable denture?

All-on-x refers to implant dentures that may use two, four, or six dental implants to support an upper or lower denture. Our dentists customize the denture to "snap" into the implants, which hold it securely in place.

The implants provide the additional benefit of keeping your jawbone healthy and stimulated, so you don t experience the bone loss and deterioration that happens with traditional denture wear and tooth loss.
