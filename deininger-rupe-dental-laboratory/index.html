Denture Care (Facts to Know) Getting used to dentures, sleep with them for seven to ten days. For soreness, rinse with warm, salt water, three to five times per day. For cleaning, use a soft, denture brush. Soak in a denture cleaning solution at least 20 minutes per day.

Immediate Dentures We offer immediate dentures right after teeth are extracted. Your denture is inserted at that time; it is then relined after eight weeks, for the final process.

We are proud to serve our community. You can rest assured that your dentures are created locally, without the added expense of traveling far away or waiting for dentures to be shipped. We are known for exceptional value in quality dental care and denture services.
