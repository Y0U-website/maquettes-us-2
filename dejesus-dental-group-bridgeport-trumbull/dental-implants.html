Dental Implants in Bridgeport &…

Mini-implants are smaller, yet equally effective alternatives to traditional dental implants. These innovative dental devices provide enhanced stability and support for dentures or dental prosthetics. With their minimally invasive nature, mini-implants offer a comfortable and efficient solution for those seeking to improve their oral function and confidence.

When it comes to restoring an entire arch of missing teeth, a full arch prosthesis is the ultimate answer. This comprehensive dental solution involves replacing all of your teeth in either the upper or lower jaw with a customized prosthesis that replicates the look, feel, and function of natural teeth.

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution for missing teeth, offering exceptional durability and stability. Made from biocompatible materials like titanium, implants fuse with the jawbone, forming a strong foundation for prosthetic teeth. With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants have the potential to last a lifetime.

The cost associated with dental implants is something we are asked frequently. The cost of dental implants can vary depending on factors such as the number of implants needed, the complexity of the case, and any additional procedures required. It’s always best to have a consultation with an implant dentist before committing to any work.

FAQs

What are the advantages of dental implants over other tooth replacement options?

Dental implants provide a permanent solution that mimics natural teeth’ function and appearance, preserves bone health, and does not affect adjacent teeth.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown attached to the implant typically lasts up to 10-15 years before it may need replacement due to wear.

What is the success rate of dental implants?

The success rate of dental implants is typically above 90 %, depending on the location of the jaw and adherence to care and maintenance.

Are there any restrictions after getting dental implants?

After implant surgery, eating soft foods, avoiding smoking, and following oral hygiene instructions are advised to ensure proper healing and implant success.
