FAQs

At what age can someone start orthodontic treatment?

Orthodontic treatment can begin as early as age 7, as the American Association of Orthodontists recommends when the first adult molars and incisors have usually erupted. However, adults of any age can undergo orthodontic treatment.

What are the benefits of orthodontic treatment beyond aesthetics?

Beyond improving the appearance of your smile, orthodontic treatment can help correct bite issues, reduce wear and tear on teeth, and improve oral health by making teeth easier to clean.

How long does orthodontic treatment typically take?

The duration of orthodontic treatment can vary significantly depending on the individual’s needs, ranging from a few months to several years, with an average of around two years.

What types of braces are available?

Several types of braces are available, including traditional metal braces, ceramic braces that match the color of your teeth, lingual braces that are placed behind the teeth, and clear aligners like Invisalign®.
