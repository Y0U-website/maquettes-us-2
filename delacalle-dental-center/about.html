About Us

Our Great Story

Family Business

Founded by Dr. Gilda M. DeLaCalle in 2004, DeLaCalle Medical Center has been in business for 15 years under the general management of her son David. In 2014 her daughter Karina joined the team as a General Dentist Dental Plan coordinator.

All Services Under One Roof

Medicine has evolved throughout the years, we have been able to adapt provide innovative modalities the newest technology within our facilities. We provide preventive medicine and meet your needs as patient all under one roof. With Laboratory Analysis, Diagnostic Ultrasound, Medical/Dental X-Ray Imaging, Panoramic Imaging, Pain Management, Allergy Management, Cytological Testing more.

Transportation Services

Under our facility, our medicare advantage patients can benefit from the free transportation services we have to offer to them. We make sure that our patients are picked up from their home, brought safely to any of our centers for consultation or any service we provide, and then taken home with a scheduled plan.
