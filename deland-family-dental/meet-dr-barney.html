Meet Dr Barney

Craig Barney, DMD

Dr. Barney received his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) Degree at Case School of Dental Medicine in Cleveland, OH, in 2005. Prior to attending dental school, he received his Bachelor’s Degree in Zoology from Brigham Young University in Provo, UT, in 2001.

For the past 17 years, Dr. Barney has worked as a practice owner in Kennewick, WA. His focus has been on delivering quality, comprehensive dental care to his patients with a gentle, kind touch. He has also worked to expand his skill set to include implant and oral surgery , root canal treatments , and Invisalign clear braces . He is very experienced in each of these areas and has many hundreds of successful cases.

Dr. Barney is happily married to his wife, Brooke, and has 4 children: Logan (21), Addison (19), Sawyer (16), and Chase (9). In his spare time, Dr. Barney enjoys watersports, running, fly fishing, snow skiing, and playing the piano. He also enjoys serving in his Church and doing humanitarian work to help those in need.
