Community Involvement

We’re proud to endorse the “Communities in Schools” program, dedicated to helping at-risk students graduate and achieve their potential. Dr. Shih frequently contributes his time to reading sessions with kids at Wood Acres Elementary School, tutors children in STEM-related classes like robotics camps at DuPont Middle School, and provides free dental care at the Sulzbacher dental clinic, embodying our passion for community engagement.

With every set of braces , we also donate $40 to the cause, which goes toward purchasing graduation gowns and covering fees for students who may otherwise be unable to afford to walk across the stage.

Our commitment to community engagement drives us to make a positive impact on the lives of those we serve.
