Our Team

Dr. Jacqueline Delash

During her formative adolescent years, Dr. Delash spent countless hours in a dental laboratory learning the mechanics of dental materials, developing a critical eye for tooth esthetics, and honing clinical skills. While this experience is atypical for most, these early years were spent under the tutelage of her mother, a celebrated and highly skilled dental technologist. These influential times are what led Dr. Delash to pursue a career in dentistry.

Dr. Zach Wilson

Dr. Wilson first developed an interest in dentistry during high school after experiencing how orthodontic treatment transformed his confidence and quality of life. That experience sparked an appreciation for the impact dentistry can have on both oral health and self-esteem. His passion for patient care deepened during college through years of volunteer service at the Hearts and Hands Clinic, where he worked as a dental assistant providing care to underserved patients.

Dr. Ronald C. Wilcox

Dr. Wilcox has practiced dentistry in Peachtree City for over thirty years. He is a graduate of the Emory University School of Dentistry where he won top clinical honors, and regularly attends continuing education courses to stay at the forefront of his field.
