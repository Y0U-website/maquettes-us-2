ABOUT OUR PRACTICE

We understand that choosing a pediatric dentist for your child is an important decision. From the moment you walk in the door, you will be greeted by a caring staff in a warm stress-free environment. As a child-focused dental practice, our dentists have specialized training dedicated to infants, children, and adolescents. The procedures are devoted to children’s needs and are conducted with efficiency while giving attention to each patient. Our focus is on preventative care to support each child’s oral health to sustain a healthy smile that will last a lifetime.

Don’t put off a visit to the dentist any longer. Make an appointment today knowing your child will receive care in a comfortable and friendly environment. Our little patients love coming in for their routine check-ups. Walk-ins and emergencies are welcomed. As the only pediatric dentist in Milford, DE, we also serve the surrounding cities of Georgetown, Harrington, Dover, Millsboro and ohers.

Dr. Martinez

Dr. Gerardo Martinez is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist. His academic career started at the University of South Florida (USF) where he completed his B.A. and B.S. degrees. He completed his dental training at the College of Dental Medicine at Nova South Eastern University (NSU), where he obtained his D.M.D. degree. After dental school, he completed a one year residency program in Advance Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) from the University of Florida.
