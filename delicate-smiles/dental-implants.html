Signs You May Need Dental Implants

Notice any of these? Schedule a visit — early care is easier care.

Missing Tooth

A single missing tooth can be replaced with an implant that looks and works just like the one you lost.

Several Gaps

Multiple missing teeth can be restored with implant-supported bridges for a stable, natural result.

Loose Dentures

If your dentures slip or feel uncomfortable, implants can anchor them securely for a confident, stable fit.

Failing Tooth

A tooth too damaged to save can be replaced with an implant, often in coordination with a gentle extraction.
