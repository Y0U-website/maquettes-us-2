The practice

We have a strong team of caring, energetic, dedicated professional and fun individuals who are passionate about dentistry and are committed to our patients.

When a patient calls or walks into our clinic, they are provided with the kind of care and service that will exceed their expectation. We provide detailed explanations of treatment options and discuss what is to be expected during future visits.

Dr. Delphine I. George completed her undergraduate education at UCLA in Los Angeles with a B.S. in Biology. She then worked in a dental office, before gaining admission to Howard University College of Dentistry in Washington D.C. where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

She keeps up with constantly evolving dentistry by taking a lot or training and continued education classes. She enjoys dancing, playing scrabble, solving puzzles, short stories and movies.
