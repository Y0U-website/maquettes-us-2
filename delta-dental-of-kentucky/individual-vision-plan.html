DeltaVision

DeltaVision®, a partnership between Delta Dental of Kentucky and VSP Vision Services, provides a comprehensive vision benefit plan to individuals and families.

Why choose DeltaVision?

  • Large nationwide network. VSP Choice Network has over 38,000 preferred providers at over 100,000 access points nationwide. Members are free to see any provider they choose. In-network VSP Choice providers will provide the largest savings to the member.
  • Value-packed plans. You get a fully-covered WellVision Exam (after copay), and an average savings of 30 % off the most popular lens enhancements through VSP Doctors and participating retail chain locations. You will also have access to the widest selection of eyewear at the lowest out-of-pocket cost.
  • Easy online enrollment. DeltaVision is available to bundle with your existing Delta Dental individual and family plan. Don’t have a dental plan? Enrolling is fast and seamless!

You ll see the difference with DeltaVision.

Busy Schedule? No Problem

91 % of VSP doctors offer early morning, evening, and weekend appointments, and 24-hour access to emergency care.

Eyewear That You ll Love

Choose eyewear that’s right for you and your budget. From classic styles to the latest designer fashions, there are hundreds of options for DeltaVision members.

Personalized Care

DeltaVision members receive quality care that focuses on their eyes and overall wellness. Our eye care provider will look for vision problems and signs of other health conditions.

DeltaVision utilizes VSP Choice Network

Delta Dental is committed to your health and happiness. That’s why we’ve partnered with VSP to offer DeltaVision. You have the freedom to choose the provider that’s right for you - from 100,000 access points, including the largest national network of independent doctors and over 5,000 retail chain locations.
