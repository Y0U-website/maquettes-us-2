The practice

We believe you deserve the highest quality of care in an atmosphere of compassion and trust. Our dental care philosophy revolves around creating a long-term strategy to ensure optimal dental health for you, your family, and friends.

Dr. Salhi is not your ordinary dentist. He will take the time to get to know you and make sure you’re completely comfortable with your care. Your procedures will always be thoroughly explained before and during treatment, in language you can understand. After all, the more you understand, the more excited you’ll be about your beautiful new smile and well-functioning bite.

Call Dr. Salhi at our Eugene dental office to experience life-changing dentistry today!

Dr. Firas Salhi believes in building long-term, trusting relationships with his patients, his staff, and the Lane County community he has served for more than 12 years. Dr. Salhi has a special talent and vision for cosmetic dentistry, implants, and full-mouth reconstruction.

After getting his doctoral degree in dentistry from New York University , Dr. Salhi moved to Oregon in 1999 to join a prosthodontic specialty program at the Veteran’s Administration Medical Center in Portland. He is an active member of the American College of Prosthodontists and annually attends leading advanced dental education institutions in order to provide the very best in dentistry.

The passion Dr. Salhi has for health care and the total wellness of his patients is displayed in the family of patients and friendships that have been built over the years. It has always been his focus to make each patient feel at home when he or she comes to our office for treatment, and he works hard to give his patients the attention they deserve.

For most people, achieving a healthy, beautiful smile is the reason for choosing to receive orthodontic care. If you’re self-conscious about your smile, having braces will greatly improve the way you look and feel.

When your treatment is complete, you will have the smile you’ve always dreamed of as well as the confidence to show it off! We also want you to feel comfortable and confident throughout your entire orthodontic experience, so our practice is dedicated to making sure that you receive the quality care you deserve.
