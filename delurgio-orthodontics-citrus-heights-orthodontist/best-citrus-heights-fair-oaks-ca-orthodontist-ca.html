Flexible Financing for Braces & Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontic treatment is an investment in your and your loved ones’ overall dental, physical and psychological well being. Our team is sensitive to the fact that people have different needs in fulfilling their financial obligations. For this reason, we provide several flexible payment options, including extended payment options.

We work with the majority of insurance plans and depending on the coverage, payments from most insurance plans can be used toward treatment. Orthodontic insurance covers a single lifetime orthodontic benefit, as opposed to your general dental insurance benefits that are renewed annually. At your first visit with us, we will sort out your insurance benefits and explain how your benefits will work for you. As a courtesy to you, we will also be happy to work with your insurance company and assist you in utilizing your benefit coverage via proper documentation and benefit claims.

In addition, we offer in-house financing with no credit check needed. We have flexible financing for all including 0 % financing for any balance not covered by your insurance, courtesies for those who prefer to pay in full and even extended financing to offer a variety of options that will work with any budget. To make monthly payments convenient and save you time, there is an option for automatic debit from your bank account or charge to your credit card.
