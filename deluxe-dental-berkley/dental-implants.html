Dental Implants

A dental implant is a titanium post placed in the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots, topped with a custom crown.

The Implant Process

  • Consultation – Treatment planning with a dentist.
  • Tooth Extraction & Bone Graft (if needed).
  • Implant Placement – A titanium post is inserted into the bone.
  • Crown Placement – A permanent crown is placed after 3–6 months of healing.

Benefits of Dental Implants

✔ Long-lasting (30+ years with proper care)

✔ No slipping or discomfort like dentures

✔ Easy maintenance (brush and floss like natural teeth)
