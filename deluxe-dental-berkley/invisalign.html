What is Invisalign?

Invisalign uses clear tray aligners to straighten teeth discreetly. Aligners are custom-made and worn 20-22 hours per day .

Invisalign Steps

🦷 Consultation: Dentist evaluates and recommends.

🦷 First Appointment: Impressions taken for custom

🦷 Follow-Ups: Trays replaced every 1-2 weeks for gradual tooth

🦷 Final Appointment: Treatment completed, retainers provided to maintain
