🦷 Scaling and Root Planing

Scaling and root planing (also called a deep cleaning) is a non-surgical periodontal treatment that removes plaque and tartar buildup below the gumline. It helps treat gum disease and prevents further infection.

🔹 How It Works

✔️ Scaling – Removes plaque and tartar from the tooth surfaces and below the gumline. ✔️ Root Planing – Smooths the tooth roots to encourage gum reattachment and prevent bacteria buildup.

🔹 Importance of Treatment

● Prevents gum disease from worsening ● Reduces inflammation and bleeding gums ● Protects against tooth loss ● Promotes gum reattachment to teeth
