Procedure for Dental Implants

The dental implant procedure at Deluxe Dental in Nacogdoches, TX is a carefully planned treatment designed to replace missing teeth with strong, natural-looking restorations. Here’s what patients can typically expect during the dental implant process :

Dental implants provide a durable and permanent solution for missing teeth, helping restore chewing ability, speech, facial structure, and confidence with a natural-looking smile.

  • Initial Consultation & Examination The dentist begins with a comprehensive oral examination, digital X-rays, and sometimes 3D imaging to evaluate bone density and determine whether dental implants are the right solution for you.
  • Customized Treatment Planning Based on your oral health and smile goals, a personalized treatment plan is created. The dentist will discuss the number of implants needed, treatment timeline, and any preparatory procedures such as bone grafting if required.
  • Preparation of the Implant Site If the jawbone is not strong enough to support an implant, bone grafting may be recommended to strengthen the area before implant placement.
  • Implant Placement Surgery During the procedure, a titanium implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone beneath the gums. Local anesthesia is used to ensure patient comfort throughout the treatment.
  • Healing & Osseointegration Over the next few months, the implant naturally fuses with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. This creates a strong and stable foundation for the replacement tooth.
  • Abutment Placement Once healing is complete, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached to the implant. This piece will hold the final dental restoration securely in place.
  • Custom Crown or Restoration Placement A custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the abutment, completing the smile restoration with a natural appearance and comfortable fit.
  • Aftercare & Follow-Up Visits Patients receive instructions on maintaining their dental implants through proper oral hygiene, regular dental visits, and healthy lifestyle habits to ensure long-term success.
