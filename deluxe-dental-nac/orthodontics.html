Procedure for Orthodontics

The orthodontic treatment process at Deluxe Dental in Nacogdoches, TX is designed to gradually straighten teeth, improve bite alignment, and enhance overall oral health. Here’s what patients can typically expect during the orthodontic procedure :

Orthodontic treatment not only creates a straighter smile but also improves oral function, bite balance, and long-term dental health, helping patients enjoy greater comfort and confidence.

  • Initial Consultation & Examination The dentist or orthodontic provider begins with a comprehensive examination of your teeth, jaw, and bite. Digital X-rays, photographs, and impressions or 3D scans may be taken to evaluate alignment issues and create a personalized treatment plan.
  • Customized Treatment Planning Based on the examination results, the dental team discusses the best orthodontic option for your needs. This may include traditional braces or clear aligners like Invisalign® depending on the severity of misalignment and your smile goals.
  • Placement of Orthodontic Appliances For Braces: Brackets are bonded to the teeth and connected with wires that gradually guide teeth into proper alignment.
  • For Clear Aligners: Patients receive a series of custom-made transparent trays designed to gently shift teeth over time.
  • Regular Adjustment Appointments Patients visit the dental office periodically for adjustments and progress monitoring. For braces, wires may be tightened or changed. For clear aligners, new trays are provided every few weeks as the teeth gradually move.
  • Gradual Teeth Alignment Over several months or years, controlled pressure slowly repositions the teeth and improves bite alignment. Treatment duration varies based on individual dental needs.
  • Monitoring Oral Health Throughout treatment, the dental team monitors oral health to ensure teeth and gums remain healthy while alignment progresses.
  • Removal of Braces or Completion of Aligners Once the teeth reach the desired position, braces are removed or aligner treatment is completed. The smile is evaluated to ensure proper alignment and bite function.
  • Retainer Placement A custom retainer is provided to help maintain the new tooth positions and prevent shifting after treatment.
