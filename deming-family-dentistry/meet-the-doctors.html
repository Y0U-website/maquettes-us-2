Dr. Andie Deming

Doctor of Dental Medicine

Dr. Andie Deming was born and raised in London, Kentucky where she graduated from South Laurel High School. She then spent eight years at the University of Kentucky, where she earned her Bachelor of Science in Human Nutrition, followed by her Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD) at UK College of Dentistry.

During her time at UK College of Dentistry, Dr. Andie served as her Class President. She also coordinated Saturday Morning Clinic, a monthly clinic providing dental care to underserved children ages 4 to 12. Dr. Andie met her husband, Dr. Jacob Deming, in her first few days of dental school.

Dr. Andie knew she wanted to be a dentist since she was in the 5th grade. She is passionate about encouraging and educating others so that they can achieve a healthy smile they are proud of. She loves helping patients develop positive dental health habits they can stick to. Dr. Andie focuses on providing quality care, long-lasting solutions, and a comfortable dental experience.

Dr. Andie loves a variety of procedures, and has taken additional courses on cosmetics and esthetics while in dental school, as well as continuing education courses in Endodontics (root canal procedures).

Outside of work, Dr. Andie enjoys spending time with her husband, Dr. Jacob Deming. Together, they love to travel, play board games and video games, and host Bible study at their house. Dr. Andie also enjoys cooking, running, spending time with friends and family, and making mini sculptures out of clay.

Dr. Jacob Deming

Doctor of Dental Medicine

Dr. Jacob Deming grew up in Paw Paw, Michigan a small town in the southwest part of the state. After graduation from Paw Paw High School, he attended Grand Valley State University in Grand Rapids, Michigan for four years. There he received a Bachelor of Science double majoring in Biology and Biomedical Sciences.

Following his undergraduate studies, Dr. Deming attended the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry, where he received his Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD), and where he met his wife, Dr. Andie Deming. While at UKCD, Dr. Deming earned the American Academy of Implant Dentistry award for placing the most implants of anyone in his class while in dental school. Dr. Deming also served as the Vice President of his class.

Dr. Deming takes great pride in providing quality, patient-centered care and enjoys getting to know each person that walks through the door. He is highly motivated to work through any problem a patient may face, and he is determined to provide a solution that best benefits them. Dr. Deming is passionate about providing resolutions that transform a person’s smile, confidence, and overall oral health. He prioritizes transparency throughout all processes.

Dr. Deming loves a variety of procedures. He has a special interest in implant dentistry and has taken additional courses both in school and in continuing education after graduation. He enjoys being able to provide this transformative care from start to finish, allowing patients to have their comfort, chewing, and smile again.

In his free time, Dr. Deming enjoys spending time with his wife, Dr. Andie. Together, they love hiking, traveling, and being active in their church and community. Dr. Deming also enjoys basketball, fishing, playing video games, all things Star Wars, and learning new things.
