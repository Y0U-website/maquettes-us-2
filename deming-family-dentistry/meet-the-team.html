Staff That Will Make You Smile

At Deming Family Dentistry, our professional staff is dedicated to providing each and every one of our patients with top-notch care. We believe in treating our patients like family and providing a welcoming and comfortable environment where you can receive the best care.

Jessica

Office Manager

Amy H.

Registered Dental Hygienist

Amy has been making patients feel welcome and cared for in the dental chair for more than 25 years. Originally from Corbin, she loves meeting new people and building lasting relationships with her patients. Her friendly personality and positive attitude help create a relaxed, comfortable experience for everyone she treats. Outside of work, Amy enjoys traveling, spending time with her kids, attending Bible study, and staying active with regular workouts.

Sky

Registered Dental Hygienist

Originally from St. Petersburg, Florida, Sky has three years of experience as a hygienist providing preventive dental care in general dentistry. Sky is passionate about educating her patients and believes that teaching is one of the most important parts of her role. She enjoys helping people feel more confident in their smiles while providing gentle, thorough care in a comfortable environment. When not at work, Sky enjoys hiking, paddleboarding, reading, and painting.

Madison

Registered Dental Hygienist

Madison is from Williamsburg, Kentucky. She brings four years of experience as an orthodontic assistant and is a recent graduate of Lincoln Memorial University’s Dental Hygiene program. Madison is passionate about educating patients and helping them understand the connection between oral health and overall wellness, with a special interest in airway health. She loves building lasting relationships with her patients and creating a comfortable, positive experience at every visit. Outside of the office, Madison enjoys baking, attending church, and spending time with her husband and their pets.

Amy G.

Expanded Duties Dental Assistant

Amy has been helping patients smile proudly for 19 years. A Barbourville, Kentucky native, she’s skilled in preventive, restorative, and surgical procedures and loves seeing patients leave with confidence and a healthy smile. Amy enjoys connecting with families and making a lasting impact on their lives. When she’s not at work, she loves spending time with her husband and three children- two of which are twins! She also enjoys traveling and relaxing at the lake on weekends.

Brenda

Expanded Duties Dental Assistant

Brenda has been a dental assistant for over 35 years and loves helping patients achieve their perfect smiles. A Barbourville, Kentucky native, she earned her Expanded Duties Dental Assistant certification from the University of Kentucky. Outside the office, Brenda enjoys studying her Bible, journaling, crafting, and spending time with her family- especially her grandkids!

Linda

Expanded Duties Dental Assistant

With 30 years of experience in general, cosmetic, and prosthodontic dentistry, Linda loves helping create new and beautiful smiles. Originally from Lexington, Kentucky, she enjoys making patients feel comfortable and confident in their care. Linda received her EDDA certification from the University of Kentucky and is a Certified National Dental Assistant. Outside of work, she loves spending time at the beach and going shopping.

Virginia

Dental Assistant

With over 20 years of experience in dentistry, Virginia loves building lasting relationships with her patients and teammates. Originally from Corbin, Kentucky, she’s known for making everyone feel like part of the family. When she’s not at the office, Virginia enjoys hunting, fishing, and spending time with her loved ones.

Deon

Expanded Duties Dental Assistant

Deon joined the team with a heart for helping others and a genuine love for patient care. Originally from London, Kentucky, she completed a dental assisting program with expanded duties certifications, coronal polishing, and radiography. Though newer to the field, Deon’s compassion and calm presence make a lasting impression on everyone she meets. She believes in being there for patients- whether it’s offering a kind word, a prayer, or simply a hand to hold. Outside the office, Deon enjoys creating art and collecting vinyl records.

Angie

Treatment Coordinator

Originally from Harlan County and now a longtime Corbin resident, Angie has been in dentistry for the past two years. She loves seeing how happy patients feel after their treatment and enjoys being part of a caring, supportive team. Outside of the office, Angie is passionate about fitness and nutrition- holding numerous fitness certifications and even some state records!
