Services for Your Smile

Services that Will Make You Smile

At Deming Family Dentistry, we offer a wide range of services in our office. We are proud to provide our customers with top-notch care supported by the latest technology. Contact us today! We guarantee you will be smiling by the end of your visit.

Crowns

Get a crown to protect your damaged tooth. A dental crown is a custom-made cap that covers and protects a damaged, weakened, or heavily filled tooth, restoring its strength, function, and natural appearance. Designed to match the shape and color of your natural teeth, crowns blend in beautifully with your smile while providing long-lasting protection. You may need a crown after a root canal, to repair a cracked or broken tooth, to support a large filling, or to restore a worn or misshapen tooth.

Root Canals

When a cavity has reached the nerve, a root canal can often save the tooth. A root canal is a dental procedure that removes infected or damaged tissue from inside a tooth to relieve pain and save the tooth from needing to be pulled. Instead of losing the tooth, the inside is cleaned, sealed, and restored with a crown, allowing you to keep your natural smile. Root canals are a safe and effective way to stop pain, prevent further infection, and preserve your tooth for years to come.

Implants

Replace your missing teeth with the next best thing. A dental implant is a long-lasting, natural-looking replacement for a missing tooth, consisting of a small titanium post placed in the jawbone to support a crown, bridge, or denture. At our practice, we use advanced CBCT imaging, intraoral scanners, and custom 3D-printed surgical guides to ensure precise placement and optimal results. By using premium Straumann implants, known for their quality and durability, we provide patients with a highly predictable solution to restore their smile.

Bridges

Get a Bridge to fill the gaps in your smile. A dental bridge is a custom-made restoration that fills the gap left by one or more missing teeth by anchoring to the natural teeth or implants on either side. Designed to match the color and shape of your surrounding teeth, bridges look natural and blend seamlessly with your smile. You might need a bridge to restore your ability to chew and speak properly, prevent remaining teeth from shifting, and maintain the overall shape of your face.

Clear Aligners

Straighten your smile without the hassle and appearance of metal braces. Clear aligners are custom-made, removable trays that gently straighten your teeth over time without the need for metal brackets or wires. They are a more comfortable, discreet, and convenient alternative to traditional braces, allowing you to eat, brush, and smile with ease throughout treatment.

Veneers

Get a picture perfect smile with a layer of highly esthetic porcelain. A dental veneer is a thin, custom-made shell that is carefully placed over the front surface of your teeth to improve their appearance. Made from high-quality materials, veneers are designed to look natural, brightening your smile by covering imperfections like stains, chips, or gaps. They’re a great option for achieving a beautiful, confident smile with a durable and long-lasting solution.

Whitening

Brighten your smile with one of our many options to whiten, in the office or at home. Custom-made bleaching trays are created specifically for your teeth, allowing you to whiten gradually and comfortably at home, while in-office ZOOM whitening is a faster, professional treatment that lightens your teeth dramatically in just one appointment using a special light-activated gel. Both methods are safe and effective, with the choice depending on your lifestyle and desired results.

Fillings

When bacteria cause cavities, a filling is needed to stop the spread of decay. A dental filling is a treatment used to repair a tooth damaged by decay or a small crack by filling the cleaned-out area with a tooth-colored material. You would need a filling to restore your tooth’s strength, prevent further decay, and relieve any sensitivity or discomfort. Fillings help keep your natural teeth healthy and functioning properly for years to come.

Cleanings

A professional dental cleaning is a thorough cleaning performed by your hygienist that removes plaque, tartar, and bacteria from your teeth and gums—areas that regular brushing and flossing can’t fully reach. Getting cleaned every six months helps prevent cavities, gum disease, and bad breath, keeping your smile healthy and your mouth feeling fresh. Regular cleanings also allow your dentist to spot any issues early, so you can avoid more extensive treatments down the road.

Dentures

Get a set of dentures made with the latest digital technology for a great look and fit. Dentures are removable dental appliances that replace missing teeth and restore your smile, helping you eat, speak, and feel confident again. Our dentures are fully customizable in both shape and color to look natural and match your unique preferences—we take the time to listen carefully to what you want so you can enjoy a comfortable, beautiful smile tailored just for you.

Overdentures

Implant overdentures are dentures that snap securely onto dental implants placed in your jaw, offering much greater stability and comfort than traditional dentures. They help prevent slipping and improve your ability to chew and speak confidently, while also preserving bone health by stimulating the jawbone. This makes implant overdentures a durable, comfortable, and natural-feeling solution for restoring your smile and oral function.
