Replace Missing Teeth

Dr. Dempsey and the staff of Westbrooke Family Dentistry in West Des Moines, IA , use various methods to restore missing teeth and tooth structures that are missing due to tooth decay, tooth fractures, or deterioration of previous dental restorations. Some of the dental restorations we provide include:

Dental Crowns

Crowns are best used on teeth that are damaged. They restore the size, strength, and appearance of teeth. Crowns can also hold a bridge in place or cover a dental implant.

Fillings

To treat a cavity, the dentist removes the decayed part of the tooth and then fills it in with a special material. At Westbrooke Family Dentistry, we use composite, or tooth-colored, fillings. Composite fillings can also be used for cosmetic purposes to change the color of teeth, reshape disfigured teeth, and to fix cracked or broken teeth.

Implant Crowns

One option for replacing a missing tooth is with a dental implant. Implants have been used for more than a quarter of a century. They are surgically placed in the jaw where they function as a sturdy anchor for replacement teeth. A single tooth implant replaces the missing tooth’s roots. A single crown is then placed over the implant system to restore function and appearance.

Bridges

A gap caused by one or more missing teeth can make you look awkward. Bridges are two or more crowns that fill in the gaps and are anchored to healthy teeth. They restore the patient’s smile, the ability to chew and speak, and improve his appearance.

Partials

Partial dentures are replacement teeth attached to a gum colored plastic base that holds the dentures in place in the mouth. Partials are perfect for patients with gaps at different areas of the mouth.

Veneers

Dental veneers are very thin shells of ceramic or composite resin that are bonded to the front of the teeth. They are used to improve the appearance of teeth, and require little or no anesthesia.
