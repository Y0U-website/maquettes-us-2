Patient Centered Care Services

Highly skilled physicians, pharmacists, dentists, and mental health professionals deliver progressive, patient-centered healthcare with a personal team of White House health coaches, educators, nutritionists and wellness.

Medical Primary Care Provider

Your primary care provider will lead a team of highly skilled health coaches, educators, nurses and health professionals. This provider will know your history, medications and family situation and will make sure that you and your children are healthy and up to date on screenings or immunizations. Behavioral Health

White House Clinics offers Behavioral Health Services that are integrated into your primary care services. Behavioral Health providers in each of our clinics will work with your medical provider and other members of your care team to help you identify goals related to your physical and mental health and develop a plan to help you achieve those goals. Clinical Social Workers can assist with:

Find a provider or see locations and hours .

  • Trauma or abuse
  • Stress
  • Depression & feelings of guilt/hopelessness
  • Alcohol and/or substance abuse
  • Anxiety or panic attacks
  • Difficulty concentrating or paying attention
  • Anger issues
  • Smoking cessation

On-Site Pharmacy

White House Clinics is a full service pharmacy that works to provide patients with the necessary medicines needed at the lowest possible cost. White House Clinics can also deliver your prescriptions right to your door in certain locations (call for more info).

Non-Clinical Support Enabling Services’

HIV Outreach

To make HIV testing and prevention easily accessed by members of our communities, we’re bringing services out of the clinic and into the community. Our Primary Care HIV Prevention team can offer community-based HIV testing, access to PrEP for HIV prevention, and other primary care treatment and screenings.
