Working with us.

  • Integrated Practice. The facility is a community health center offering medical, dental, pharmacy, and behavioral health services in an integrated setting. White House Clinics proudly practices team based care embedding health educators in the clinical care teams to support patient education, self-management support, and case management needs. Additionally, we’ve modified a process used by Kaiser to support our physicians in completing patients’ preventative health needs. The practice has used an electronic health record since 2005.
  • Flexible Work Schedule. Work schedules are flexible to support a work life balance. Most physicians work either four 10-hour or five 8-hour shifts; however, the practice offers other options in order to support each provider’s personal goals.
  • Salary and Benefits. White House Clinics offers employees at all levels a highly competitive salary and a comprehensive benefit package. Physicians are paid a guaranteed base salary with opportunity for incentive bonuses. We are proud to offer all full-time employees health, dental, and short and long-term disability insurance as well as a generous paid time off benefits. Professional development funds offer both additional time off and reimbursement for educational endeavors. Malpractice provided with no need for tail coverage upon departure. Loan repayment available at select locations.
