Dr. Dennis Eguchi

Dr. Dennis Eguchi was born and raised in Mentor, Ohio, outside of Cleveland.

He earned his bachelors degree in Zoology from Ohio University. He then earned his D.D.S from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry.

Upon graduation from dental school, he entered the U.S. Army where he spent 9 1/2 years in the United States Army Dental Corps as a general dentist and endodontist.

Dr. Eguchi has been practicing in his wife’s hometown of Watsonville, California since 1984.

Dr. Eguchi has been happily married to his wife, Linda, for over 30 years. They are the proud parents of three sons; Matthew, Michael, and Mark, as well as their daughter, Melissa. He enjoys spending time with his family, reading, creating art, and playing raquetball.
