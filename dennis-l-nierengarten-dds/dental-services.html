EMERGENCY DENTAL SERVICES

Our team proudly provides emergency dental services. Any situation that poses a threat to the health of your teeth and gums can be considered a dental emergency. This includes impacted or infected teeth, accidents that may have broken a tooth, and more. Our emergency services can typically be provided same day for most conditions. Over-the-counter pain medications (not aspirin), ice packs, and heating pads can be used for pain management.
