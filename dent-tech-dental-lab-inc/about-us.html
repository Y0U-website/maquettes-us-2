About Us

Mike Abraham, Master CDT, has been in the dental laboratory technology industry for over 40 years. Mike earned his Associate Degree in Dental Technology at Texas State Technical College in Waco Texas where he became a Master CDT in all six specialties earning his first CDT certification in 1994. Less than 1 % of CDTs can call themselves a master CDT and far fewer have mastered all six specialties.

Mike Abraham, Master CDT, named 2017 CDT of the YearAbraham is actively involved in the Dental Technology Community and frequently travels nationwide as an examiner administering practical CDT examinations. Mike served as president of Associates Dental lab of Arkansas for two years and is actively involved in his community. Mike has worked with both Donated Dental Services of Arkansas and Donated Dental Services of Mississippi and is an avid supporter of VFW 3030 a foundation supporting Veterans.

Our profession is lucky to have such a hard working, caring, gifted technician and lab owner. He exemplifies the best in your organization and is deserving of this outstanding honor. – Alvin W. Neff, DDS, FAGD, FICD
