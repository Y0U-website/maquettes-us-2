Will Denta-Gard® Kid’s Comfort Pro sports mouthguards fit my bigger child?

Yes. Kid’s Comfort Pro is a one-size, self-adjusting sports mouthguard designed to flex and adapt to different mouth sizes. It works well for growing kids, larger teens, and adults - so you don’t need to guess between youth and adult sizes.

Can I wear a Denta-Gard® with braces?

Yes. Denta-Gard® was designed by an Orthodontist to fit comfortably over orthodontic appliances. You will need to make a minor adjustment to use with a Forsus appliance or Herbst appliance. We can make that adjustment for you if you mention it in your notes during checkout of call our office.

There are many different types of mouth guards. Which is best for me?

We feel the best mouth guard is one that protects both upper and lower teeth and is comfortable to wear.

Where are Denta-Gard® Kid’s Comfort Pro mouthguards made?

They are made in the United Sates, in central Texas. We take immense pride in emphasizing that every aspect of our mouthguards, from the raw materials to molding, assembly, and any other associated process, is exclusively produced and fully processed within the United States.

Do I need to boil or mold it to fit it to my mouth?

No. Just fold it in half and place in the mouth. The soft-flex material is designed to adjust naturally, with no boiling or molding required.

What materials are the Denta-Gard® Kid’s Comfort Pro made from?

They are made in the USA using surgical-grade, FDA-approved synthetic materials similar to rubber. It is both latex-free and BPA-free.
