The Denta-Gard® Kid’s Comfort Pro™ Sports Mouthguard offers protection for upper and lower teeth as well as soft tissue. Its natural spring hinge is designed to move with the mouth, helping provide continuous protection while allowing natural breathing and speech. The soft-flex material creates a comfortable fit, including for athletes with orthodontic braces.

Built for active athletes, the Kid’s Comfort Pro™ is soft, pliable, and comfortable. Orthodontists value its protective design and its compatibility with orthodontic treatment. It fits over most braces and appliances, and it complies with National Federation of High School (NFHS) and National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) mouthguard guidelines.

The Kid’s Comfort Pro™ sports mouthguard is ready to wear immediately—no boiling, molding, or impressions are required for a comfortable fit. Its soft-flex universal design accommodates both children and adults. Simply fold the mouthguard and place it in the mouth; the innovative soft-flex materials enable it to self-adjust to each wearer’s unique mouth shape. You can even be worn over braces!

While we were attending an athletic conference, many coaches and trainers shared their observation that athletes often chew on the ends of traditional hard plastic mouthguards, a habit that compromises their effectiveness. Once damaged, these traditional mouthguards lose their impact-softening benefits, yet the damage can be difficult for coaches to detect when the guard is in place.

In contrast, the Kid’s Comfort Pro™ is designed to resist this kind of wear and tear. Its tough yet soft and durable construction prevents athletes from chewing it away. The mouthguard’s comfortable fit also eliminates the urge to remove it for chewing, ensuring it stays in place during use.

Do I need to boil the Denta-Gard® Kid’s Comfort Pro to fit it to my mouth?

No. There is no need to boil or mold. Its’ durable, yet soft material will fit comfortably and securely in your mouth.

What materials are the Denta-Gard® Kid’s Comfort Pro™ made from?

Our sports mouthguards are made in the USA using surgical-grade, FDA-approved synthetic materials similar to rubber. They are both latex-free and BPA-free.

Can I wear Denta-Gard® at night when I sleep?

Yes. Most people scissor it in half and wear over their lower teeth. To avoid the possibility of choking, attach the strap to a button on your pajamas. Consult your dentist before using a Denta-Gard® as a night guard.
