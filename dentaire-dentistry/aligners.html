questions

How does Invisalign work to straighten teeth?

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, clear plastic aligners that gradually move your teeth into their ideal positions. Each set is worn for about 1-2 weeks before moving to the next set. Our team uses advanced technology to plan your entire treatment sequence, showing you exactly how your teeth will move.

How long does Invisalign treatment typically take?

Most Invisalign cases are completed in 12-18 months, though some simple cases may take less time and complex cases may take longer. Our team will provide a specific timeline during your consultation based on your individual needs and treatment goals, using digital technology to optimize your treatment efficiency.

Will Invisalign affect my daily activities?

Invisalign is designed to fit seamlessly into your lifestyle. You can remove the aligners for eating, drinking anything other than water, brushing, flossing, and special occasions. Most patients find that aligners don’t significantly impact their speech, work, or social activities after a brief adjustment period.

How often do I need to wear my Invisalign aligners?

For optimal results, you should wear your aligners 22 hours a day, removing them only for eating, drinking, and oral hygiene. Our team will monitor your progress throughout treatment and ensure you’re on track to achieve your desired results within the planned timeframe, and maintain them with retainers .

Why does Our team recommend Invisalign before cosmetic treatments?

Invisalign optimizes tooth position before cosmetic procedures like veneers or bonding, ensuring better aesthetics and longer-lasting results. When teeth are properly aligned first, cosmetic work looks more natural, functions better, and is less likely to experience problems like chipping or debonding over time.

Can I see what my results will look like before starting treatment?

Yes, our team uses advanced iTero scanning technology to create a digital treatment plan that shows you exactly how your teeth will move and what your final smile will look like. This preview helps you understand your treatment plan and feel confident about your decision to proceed with Invisalign.
