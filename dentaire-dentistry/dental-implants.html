benefits

Permanent Solution

Dental implants can last a lifetime with proper care, making them the most cost-effective long-term tooth replacement option. Unlike bridges or dentures that may need replacement, implants become a permanent part of your mouth, functioning exactly like natural teeth.

Bone Preservation

Implants are the only tooth replacement option that preserves and stimulates jawbone tissue, preventing the bone loss that occurs with missing teeth. This preservation maintains your facial structure and prevents the sunken appearance that can develop over time.

Natural Function

Dental implants provide the same chewing force as natural teeth, allowing you to eat all your favorite foods without restriction. They feel completely natural and don’t require special care beyond regular brushing and flossing, making them incredibly convenient.

questions

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with adequate bone structure are good candidates for dental implants. Our team will perform a comprehensive evaluation including x-rays to assess your bone density and overall oral health. Factors like gum disease, smoking, or certain medical conditions may affect candidacy, but many of these can be addressed before implant placement.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The complete implant process typically takes 3-6 months, allowing time for the implant to integrate with your jawbone. Our team may be able to place a temporary restoration immediately in some cases. The timeline varies based on individual healing and whether additional procedures like bone grafting are needed.

Is dental implant surgery painful or uncomfortable?

Most patients are surprised by how comfortable implant surgery is. Our team uses advanced techniques and appropriate numbing to ensure your comfort during the procedure. Post-surgical discomfort is typically mild and easily managed with over-the-counter pain medication. Many patients return to work the next day.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Dental implants are cared for just like natural teeth with regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings. They don’t require special maintenance products or procedures. Good oral hygiene and regular dental visits with our team will help ensure your implants last a lifetime.

How do dental implants compare to bridges or dentures?

Dental implants are superior because they don’t affect adjacent teeth, preserve jawbone structure, provide natural chewing force, and can last a lifetime. Bridges require grinding down neighboring teeth, while dentures can be unstable and don’t prevent bone loss. Implants are the only tooth replacement that truly replaces both the tooth and root.

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have a success rate of over 95 % when placed by experienced practitioners like our team. Factors that contribute to success include proper patient selection, precise placement techniques, good oral hygiene, and regular follow-up care. Our team’s experience and attention to detail help ensure optimal outcomes for our implant patients.
