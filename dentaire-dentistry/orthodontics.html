questions

Why does Our team often recommend orthodontics before cosmetic treatments?

Orthodontic treatment optimizes tooth position and bite alignment before cosmetic procedures, ensuring better results that last longer. When teeth are properly aligned before veneers or bonding, the cosmetic work looks more natural, functions better, and is less likely to experience problems like chipping or debonding over time.

Am I too old for orthodontic treatment?

It’s never too late for orthodontic treatment. Our team treats many adult patients who want to improve their smile’s function and appearance. Adult orthodontics can address crowding, spacing, bite issues, and other concerns while considering your lifestyle and aesthetic preferences throughout treatment.

How does Invisalign compare to traditional braces?

Invisalign clear aligners are nearly invisible, removable for eating and cleaning, and generally more comfortable than traditional braces. They’re ideal for adults and teens who want discreet treatment. Our team will evaluate your specific case and recommend the best option based on your needs and treatment goals.

How long does orthodontic treatment typically take?

Treatment time varies based on the complexity of your case, but most adult orthodontic treatment takes 12-24 months. Our team uses advanced technology to create efficient treatment plans, and Invisalign cases often progress more quickly than traditional braces. We’ll provide a specific timeline during your consultation.

Can orthodontic treatment improve my oral health?

Yes, properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing your risk of cavities and gum disease. Correcting bite problems can also reduce tooth wear, jaw strain, and related issues. Our team considers both the health and aesthetic benefits when planning your orthodontic treatment.

What technology do you use for orthodontic treatment planning?

Our team uses advanced iTero digital scanning technology to create precise treatment plans and show you your expected results before treatment begins. This technology eliminates messy impressions while providing highly accurate treatment planning that optimizes your results and treatment efficiency.
