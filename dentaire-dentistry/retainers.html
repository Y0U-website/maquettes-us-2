questions

Why do I need to wear retainers after orthodontic treatment?

Retainers are essential because teeth have a natural tendency to shift back toward their original positions after orthodontic treatment. The tissues and bone around your teeth need time to stabilize in their new positions. Without retainers, your teeth will gradually move, undoing the results of your orthodontic treatment.

What types of retainers are available?

Our team offers several retainer options including clear removable retainers similar to Invisalign aligners, traditional wire retainers, and fixed retainers that are bonded to the back of your teeth. We’ll recommend the best option based on your specific case, lifestyle, and retention needs.

How long do I need to wear my retainers?

Initially, you’ll typically wear retainers full-time for several months, then transition to nighttime-only wear. However, to maintain your results permanently, some level of retainer wear is recommended for life. Our team will provide specific instructions based on your individual treatment and goals.

How do I care for my retainers?

Clean your retainers daily with a soft toothbrush and mild soap or specialized retainer cleaner. Avoid hot water which can warp the material. Store them in their protective case when not wearing them, and bring them to your dental appointments for professional cleaning and evaluation.

What happens if I lose or break my retainer?

Contact our team immediately if your retainer is lost, broken, or no longer fits properly. Even a few days without retention can allow teeth to begin shifting. We can quickly create a replacement retainer to prevent any loss of your orthodontic results.

How often should I have my retainers checked?

Our team recommends bringing your retainers to regular dental checkups so we can evaluate their fit and condition. As your mouth changes over time, retainers may need adjustment or replacement to continue providing effective retention of your orthodontic results.

What are Vivera retainers?

Vivera retainers, created by Invisalign®️, are crafted from strong, transparent plastic. Each retainer is designed to maintain the patient’s smile with a precise, comfortable fit. Many patients appreciate that Vivera retainers are discreet and blend in easily during daytime wear. They’re also often described as slimmer and less bulky compared to other removable retainer options. Vivera retainers are FDA-registered medical devices designed for retention but also offer protection against mild teeth grinding (bruxism) by acting as a durable barrier.
