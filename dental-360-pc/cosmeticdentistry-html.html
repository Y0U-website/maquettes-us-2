Cosmetic Dentistry

Because who doesn’t want a white smile? Dental 360 offers a safe and effective “at home” custom whitening system to give you the results you can’t achieve from over the counter whitening systems or toothpastes alone.

A popular option is Opalescence Take Home Whitening System which gives patients control over their desired shade. A mold of your teeth is taken in the office and a whitening tray is then fabricated from that mold. The patient is then sent home with whitening gel, trays, and instructions on how to start the whitening process.

There are many different teeth whitening options based on patient’s desired outcome. It is important to us for our patients to be confident in their smile; maybe whitening is your simple solution.

Custom whitening trays are fabricated just for your mouth and you are sent home with our prescription whitening solution so you can whiten your teeth on your own schedule. Each application takes approximately 30 mins and you should see results in less than two weeks.
