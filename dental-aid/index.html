At our non-profit dental practice, we are proud to serve our community by offering quality dentistry at affordable prices. Our rates are 30-40 % lower than other dental offices, ensuring that everyone has access to the care they need. Make Dental Aid your dental home, where compassionate care and affordability go hand in hand.

Visiting your dentist once a year and a dental hygienist twice a year is important for maintaining oral health. At Dental Aid, we can provide comprehensive cleanings and exams, X-rays, fluoride treatment, fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, root canals, veneers, oral surgery and pain and infection relief. Our team of professionals is dedicated to helping you maintain your best smile.

At Dental Aid, we specialize in the latest dentistry care and treatments to help our patients maintain their smiles. Some of our specialty dental services include extractions, and orthodontics. Our team of professionals are dedicated to helping you achieve the best possible result for your dental care needs.

Dental Aid is committed to providing the best care with the latest technologies available. We employ a same day CEREC machine for quick, accurate dental restorations and other advanced techniques to ensure our patients’ needs are met. Our team is dedicated to staying up-to-date on the latest advancements in dentistry so our patients can benefit from modern treatments.
