About Dental American Group

Dental American Group has been at the forefront of dental care in South Florida for over 30 years. Founded by Dr. Lillian Gonzalez and now being led by Dr. Gonzalez’s son, our practice is a testament to the family legacy and innovation in dentistry. We are proud of our journey from a humble family practice to a leading dental provider with three locations in the region Hialeah, Miami, and Pembroke Pines, FL.
