Financing

Payment Options

We accept all major credit cards, ATM cards, and cash. We offer our patients a 10 % courtesy discount when the entire treatment is pre-paid in full using cash or a credit card or a 5 % courtesy discount when the entire treatment is pre-paid in 2 installments using cash or a credit card.

For your convenience, we offer a payment plan through a third party, which helps you receive your treatment in a timely manner.

Financial Partners

As a service to our patients, we offer several financing options for you to pay for your elective treatments from $100.00 and up. No interest plans for 3, 6, 12, and 24 months or extended payment plans with low monthly payments are available.

Insurance

Our offices accept most dental insurance, PPO, HMO, and discount plans offered in South Florida. When calling for your appointment, please provide us with your insurance information, and we will be glad to verify coverage for you. Our staff will estimate your insurance benefits at each visit and ask you to pay the difference each time. Please note that this is an estimate only. We will bill the insurance company as a courtesy to you.
