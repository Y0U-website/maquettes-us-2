Dr. Sarit Kaplan

Dr. Kaplan is one of a selected group of specialists in Greater DC who have extensive experience, education and training in the diagnosis and treatment of dental restoration to patients with challenging cosmetic dentistry, temporomandibular disorders, better known to most as TMJ and/or TMD, and in the area of Obstructive Sleep Apnea appliances.

Dr. Kaplan is a Fellow of the American College of Prosthodontists and a Diplomate of the American Board of Prosthodontics. She currently serves as the Immediate Past President (2023) of the prestigious dental organization, The American Academy of Fixed Prosthodontics.

As a Clinical Assistant Professor in the Prosthodontic Residency Program at the University of Maryland Dental School, Dr. Kaplan instructed future prosthodontists about the challenges of treating highly complex dental and esthetic procedures.

As an educator, Dr. Kaplan has published case studies in the Journal of Prosthetic Dentistry and the Journal of the American Dental Association. She is the Past President of the American College of Prosthodontists (ACP) – Maryland Section. Before elected to be the president of the American Academy of Fixed Prosthodontics, Dr. Kaplan was selected by her peers to serve on the Board of Directors of this prestige national organization and she served as the chairperson for the Poster Program of the Academy for many years.

Dr. Kaplan always strives to provide each and every patient with the best personal care, the way she would like to have her family and herself cared for.
