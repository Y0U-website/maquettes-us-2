Restorative Dentistry

Crowns also known as caps, are fixed prosthesis shaped tooth and cover a tooth to re-establish its size and appearance as well as improve its strength. Bridges are a restorative treatment for the missing teeth, which involves creating a dental prosthesis made of one or two dental crowns.
