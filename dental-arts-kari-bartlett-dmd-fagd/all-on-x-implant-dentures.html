How All-On-X Implant Dentures Works

  • The doctors use state-of-the-art 3D imaging to identify the optimal locations for placing at least four implants, providing a stable foundation for a full set of upper or lower teeth.
  • The teeth are made into one unit so the load is evenly distributed and chewing power is strong.
  • The new teeth are typically made from zirconia—one of the strongest and most natural-looking materials available.

Efficient & Seamless Procedure for All-On-X Implant Dentures

Implants look and feel like natural teeth and have many advantages over other forms of tooth replacement. Our doctors perform comprehensive, detailed treatment planning and will make sure that every step of your treatment is done right.

Detailed diagnostics

Our dentists will start by performing a thorough examination of your mouth. We will take X-rays and, if necessary, we will also obtain a 3D CT scan of your teeth, jaw and skull, so that we can have a complete overview of all your oral structures. We will determine if you are a candidate for dental implants and go over your treatment goals with you so that we can make sure that dental implants are the right option for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

All-On-X dental implants could be as high as $30,000 per arch, depending on several factors including the number of implants needed, your bone density, and whether additional procedures like bone grafting are required. While this represents a significant investment, All-On-X implants are a permanent solution that can last 20+ years with proper care, unlike traditional dentures that require replacement every 5-7 years. After an initial consultation and evaluation with our doctors we will be able to give you an All-Inclusive-Price tailored around your specific needs.

We recommend scheduling a consultation to receive a personalized treatment plan and accurate cost estimate based on your specific needs.

At Dental Arts our doctors and staff will provide an appropriate sedation option to ensure the patient is completely comfortable. Most patients report feeling pressure but no discomfort during the procedure.

After the procedure, mild to moderate discomfort is normal and typically manageable with prescribed pain medication. Most patients describe the recovery as less painful than having multiple tooth extractions. You can expect:

Days 1-3: Peak discomfort, manageable with medication Days 4-7: Significant improvement, reduced pain medication needed Week 2+: Most discomfort resolved

Swelling and bruising are common but subside within the first week. Following post-operative instructions carefully—including cold compresses, soft foods, and proper oral hygiene—significantly reduces discomfort and promotes faster healing.

The All-On-X procedure typically takes 3-4 hours per arch to complete. If you’re replacing both upper and lower teeth, the full procedure can be completed in a single day, though many patients prefer to schedule them separately.

Timeline breakdown: Day of surgery: You’ll receive your temporary prosthesis immediately after implant placement, allowing you to leave with a functional smile the same day.

Recovery period: 3-6 months for full osseointegration (implant fusion with bone) Final restoration: After complete healing, we’ll replace your temporary teeth with your permanent, custom-crafted prosthesis.

The immediate teeth placement means you won’t go without teeth during the healing process—a significant advantage over traditional implant approaches that require months of healing before receiving replacement teeth.

All-On-4® is a specific dental implant treatment trademarked by Nobel Biocare that uses exactly four implants per arch. All-On-X is a broader term referring to full-arch implant-supported dentures using a variable number of implants (typically 4-6) based on individual patient needs. Key differences:

Which is better? The ideal solution depends on your specific bone structure and oral health. Patients with excellent bone density may only need 4 implants, while those with bone loss might benefit from 5-6 implants for better support and longevity.

During your consultation, we’ll perform a CT scan to determine the optimal number and placement of implants for your situation, ensuring the best long-term outcome for your investment.
