Quality Treatment for Dental Implants Providing A Lifetime of Functionality

Dr. Bartlett performs extensive preparation for your implant treatment, including 3D cone beam CT scans of your teeth, jaw and skull, when necessary. She will make study models of your teeth to work with in planning. When more than one implant is being placed, she will fabricate a surgical guide that fits over your dental arch and allows for efficient, precise insertion.

Dr. Bartlett and staff will make sure that every step of your implant treatment is performed with the greatest accuracy, so that your implants are stable, durable and will last you for the rest of your life.

Individual Dental Implants

To replace an individual missing tooth, our doctors can place an implant and top it with a porcelain crown.

Implant-Supported Porcelain Bridge

Our doctors provide beautiful and durable all-porcelain bridges to replace multiple teeth in a row.

Implant-Retained, Snap-On Removable Dentures

Your dentures will snap onto implants using special attachments, so that they are stable in your mouth. You will still be able to remove your dentures for cleaning. We can provide implant-retained dentures with as little as two implants in the bottom arch and four implants in the top arch.

Denture Stabilization of your current dentures

Dr. Bartlett can retrofit your current dentures with implant attachments so that they are retained by implants in your jaw, turning your dentures into implant-retained, snap-on removable dentures as above.

All-on-X Implant Dentures

To provide maximum stability and lifelike functionality, the doctors provide fixed-in dentures attached to dental implants, called All-on-X Implant Dentures . Because of the support from the dental implants, the new teeth do not need the upper false palate and are less bulky than conventional dentures. The All-on-X Implant Dentures procedure provides functional teeth that are attached the same day that implants are placed. Learn more about the All-on-X Implant Dentures procedure.

Implant-Supported, Porcelain Bridges to replace an entire arch of teeth

Dr. Bartlett can use a series of implant-supported, porcelain bridges to replace an entire arch of missing teeth. This creates the most stable, aesthetic and lifelike result.

Our Procedure for Dental Implants

Detailed diagnostics

Study model and meticulous treatment planning

Using high-quality impression material, Dr. Bartlett will make a detailed study model of your mouth, which she will use to plan out your procedure. She will plan your treatment in detail, including any necessary extractions, bone grafting or prior orthodontic work that might be needed.

Surgical guide

When your treatment involves two or more implants, our doctors will fabricate a custom surgical guide to assist them in their placement. The surgical guide fits over your dental arch and has guide marks that indicate the exact position and angle of your implants, making placement precise and efficient.

Extractions

If you are using implants to replace teeth that still need to be extracted, Dr. Bartlett can perform the necessary extractions at the same time as placing your implants, if possible in your situation.

Bone grafting

Implants require a certain amount of bone to be present to be stable in your jaw and carry the load of your replacement teeth. If you have insufficient bone in your jaw for your implants to be stable, Dr. Bartlett can often perform a small bone graft at the same time as placing your implants to solve this problem.

Sinus lifts

When bone loss occurs in the upper jaw, it can result in the lowering of the floor of one or both of your sinus cavities, leaving too little bone for an implant to be placed. A special type of bone graft, known as a sinus lift, may be the solution in such circumstances. Dr. Bartlett can perform most sinus lifts in-house, and she partners with trusted local bone graft specialists for complex sinus lifts, should they be necessary.

Immediate replacement teeth

We understand that missing teeth can make you feel self-conscious and embarrassed about smiling, especially right after any extractions. We will help you preserve the appearance of your smile at all times during your dental implant treatment, so that you do not have to run into any awkward situations.

If you are getting a single implant to replace a tooth in a visible area of your mouth, Dr. Bartlett may place a temporary replacement tooth immediately after implant placement, so that the appearance of your smile is not affected. The temporary will be crafted and placed in such a way that it will not put pressure on your implant, allowing for proper healing. Dr. Bartlett will give you full instructions on how to chew, eat and take care of your implant while your implant heals and your temporary is in place.

If you are having multiple teeth or an entire arch replaced, we can craft you a temporary denture or bridge so that you never have to be without teeth. When your implants have fully healed, you will receive your permanent, final denture or bridge.

High-quality final new teeth

We use high-quality materials and excellent dental labs to ensure that your final teeth are strong and durable, and look beautiful and natural.

The dentists at Dental Arts want you to like the way you look and love the way you feel, and will take great care to make your new teeth look just the way you want them. They will also make sure they are both very stable and comfortable. It’s very important to our doctors that you are happy, and they will take the time to get it just right so that you can walk out of our office with a new smile you absolutely love.

Who Are Candidates for Dental Implants?

In the past, not everyone was a candidate for dental implants. Some patients had too much bone loss in their jaw for implants to be stable, and patients with serious health conditions such as diabetes were also often not considered candidates. Thanks to recent developments in surgical techniques and implant dentistry in general, most patients can now enjoy the many benefits dental implants have to offer.
