Meet our Doctor

Dr. Yurixander Gonzalez is originally from Cuba , were he studies and achieved his degree in General Dentistry in 2012. He completed two years of Advanced Education in General Dentistry at Florida Institute for Advanced Dental Education (FLIADE) obtaining his certificate in 2022 . Dr. Gonzalez has advanced training in oral surgery and cosmetic dentistry. He believes in making a difference in the patient’s life by providing the highest standard of care. He is also fluent in Spanish and English.
