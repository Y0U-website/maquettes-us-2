Appointments

Easily Book Your Appointment

Step 1 Contact Us: Reach out to Dental Arts of Broward by phone or email to schedule your appointment.

Step 2 Provide Information: Have your contact details, insurance information, and any relevant medical history ready.

Step 3 Select Services: Choose the dental services you need, whether it’s a routine check-up, a specific procedure, or a consultation.

Step 4 Preferred Date & Time: Indicate your preferred appointment date and time, or let us know your availability for us to find a suitable slot.

Step 5 Confirmation: We’ll confirm your appointment and send you a reminder. If you need to reschedule or have any questions, feel free to contact us.
