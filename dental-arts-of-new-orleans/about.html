History Of Dental Arts Of New Orleans

More than four decades of shaping smiles & changing lives.

In 2016, the practice became the only mother-daughter dental team in the Greater New Orleans Area with the addition of Dr. Gabrielle Richard.

Dr. Gizelle Richard opened her doors in 1986. After four decades of caring for generations of patients in Algiers, the practice is proud to continue its legacy of exceptional dental care in its new home in Old Metairie.

In 2018 , Dr. Gizelle Richard welcomed her son in law to the practice, Dr. Brent Benoit. He is a board certified orthodontist who is trained in both traditional braces and clear aligners for adults and children.
