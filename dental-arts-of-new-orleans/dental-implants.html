All-on-four dental implants and all-on-six dental implants are two different techniques. Both options involve replacing all of the teeth in one or both jaws with a fixed, permanent prosthesis that is anchored to dental implants.

The primary difference between the two techniques is the number of implants used to support the restoration. With the all-on-four technique, four implants are used to anchor the prosthesis in place, while with the all-on-six technique, six implants are used.
