Dr. Duc Pham

Meet the Doctor

Dr. Pham is originally from Grand Rapids, MI. He earned his Bachelor of Science and Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degrees at the University of Michigan—Go Blue! After graduation, Dr. Pham completed a residency in General Practice Dentistry, further enhancing his skills in a wide variety of dental services. He is also Invisalign certified and provides a wide range of treatments in restorative dentistry, crown and bridge work, dentures, cosmetic dentistry, endodontics, and both simple and surgical extractions.

Dr. Pham’s interest in dentistry was sparked by his uncle’s dedication to offering pro bono dental care to underserved communities. This, along with his leadership role in STEM outreach programs during his college years, inspired Dr. Pham to combine his passion for helping others with his creativity in problem-solving, making dentistry the perfect fit for his career.

Dr. Pham’s approach to patient care is rooted in providing personalized, patient-centered treatment, blending clinical expertise with empathy and respect. He is committed to fostering an environment where patients feel heard and empowered in managing their oral health.

When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Pham enjoys traveling, cooking, hiking, playing music, and spending time with friends and family. Now residing in New Hampshire, he is excited to explore the state’s beautiful outdoor hiking trails. Dr. Pham remains passionate about community involvement and professional development, always striving to provide the best care for his diverse patient base.
