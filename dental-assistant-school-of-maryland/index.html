The practice

If so, then a career as a Dental Assistant might be right for you. Available jobs in this field are expected to grow faster than most other occupations until 2030 according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics.

DAC prepares students for entry level, X-ray certified positions as a chair-side assistant in a dental office. The dental assistant training program is designed to teach the students the skills and knowledge necessary to assist dentists and hygienists in all chair-side procedures, and prepares students for DANB Radiation Health and Safety (RHS) Exam.

Starts September 9, 2026 Enrollment closes September 1, 2026

DRC Provided opportunity to learn the various routines of radiographic techniques and processing. In addition, this program prepares students for DANB Radiation Health and Safety (RHS) Exam, a requirement to obtain a Radiology License in State of Maryland.

3 months of experience and employment in a dental office is required.
