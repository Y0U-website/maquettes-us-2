ABOUT DAWC

Our Facility

The school is operated out of an active, 2800 square foot dental office. Our office has seven dental and treatment rooms all outfit with modern, state-of-the-art equipment. The office reception room also serves as our class room, seating up to 20 students.

  • Adec dental units
  • Periapical X-ray units
  • Panoramic X-ray unit
  • Model trimmer
  • Dental molds and tooth models
  • Dental instruments
  • Computers
  • Various dental materials and supplies

Dr. Mark Ortinau

A native of St. Louis, Dr. Ortinau practiced family dentistry in St. Louis for over 40 years. Dr. Ortinau is a member of the American Dental Association, Missouri Dental Association, Greater St. Louis Dental Society, American Orthodontic Society, International Association for Orthodontics, Acadamy of Computerized Dentistry and several other professional organizations.

  • 1995 Received the prestigious Fellowship Award from the Academy of General Dentistry
  • 2004 Received award of merit from Greater St. Louis Dental Society
  • 2006 Inducted into the International College of Dentists

Steven Ortinau

Steve Ortinau is a native of St. Louis. After graduating from Lafayette High School, he attended East Central College on a baseball scholarship. He completed his undergraduate education at Rockhurst University.

In 2000, Steve began working as a dental assistant with aspirations to continue on to dental school. In continuing his education, he completed classes to become certified in expanded functions for a dental assistant. Always with a desire to become a dentist, he continues to strive to be the best dental assistant in St. Louis. In 2007, Steve was blessed with an opportunity to branch out in the field of dentistry as a Cad/Cam expert trainer and designer.

Steve volunteers his time with Give Kids A Smile and Service International in efforts to bless children and people in need of dental care.
