COURSES

Get Started Today

Our intensive 10-week training course prepares dental assistants for an actual, practicing dental office. All classes held in our office are taught by dental professionals who practice and teach the most current, up-to-date methods. The course is designed to give the student maximum training and experience, including lots of hands-on training.

Our facility is equipped with the very latest state-of-the-art dental equipment and audio-visual teaching aids. You will be taught the most current techniques using the very best technology available.

The teaching is fast paced, and relies heavily on individual participation. Each group is no larger than one instructor per ten students so we can deliver personal attention and a worthwhile learning experience to you.
