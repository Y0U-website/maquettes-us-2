About

WELCOME TO DASSP

Dental Assisting School of San Pablo & Vacaville, DASSP , was started by Ms. Kinkin Sandhu and Dr. Birender Sandhu on August 2016. The school is certified from Dental Board of California.

With more than 30 years in dental practice, teaching and instruction experience between the two, the experience at DASSP will be extraordinary.

DASSP offers certification classes required in a dental assistant, DA, or registered dental assistant, RDA, to acquire prior to executing such dental procedures in a dental office such as Infection Control, Coronal Polishing, Pits and Fissure Sealants and Radiation Safety.

DASSP class schedules vary that meet once or twice a week, during the day or night time so students who are working can still avail of the classes. The short length of each session can vary from 6-9 weeks. We believe in recruiting a small number of students per session so that each of you gets undivided attention.

We offer an extension of actual dental office experience at Dr. Sandhu’s private dental practice for students who want real hands-on experience in the dental field.

Flexible financing options are available. Please refer COURSES for further details.
