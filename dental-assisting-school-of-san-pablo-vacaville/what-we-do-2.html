How are we different from a traditional school?

You do not need to attend an extensive full time course to acquire the skills needed for Dental Assisting. The same set of skills could be received at a part time school where a dentist and a trained dental instructor with credentials teach you with actual patients.

Either path allows you to become a dental assistant. We offers fast track training without the financial liability involved. Our dental assistant programs are designed to quickly train our students so that they can immediately & successfully acquire an employment in the dental field with the dentist.

You can get your education fast, while you keep your existing job and after graduating, start working in your new job right away!

What do I need to become a Dental Assistant?

You are required to have the following to become a Dental Assistant:

  • Clinical Training
  • X ray License & CPR Certification (Basic Life Support i.e. First Aid)
