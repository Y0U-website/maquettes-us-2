Our Experience and Expertise Make All the Difference

Whether you choose traditional braces or Invisalign, we are the premier choice for adults and children.

Invisalign has designated Dental Associates of Connecticut as a Diamond Plus Provider. This means that we treat the highest amount of Invisalign patients, adults and teens, each year as a private practice. Dr. Gilman and Dr. Gund are members of the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) and the American Dental Association (ADA) and are among the most experienced orthodontists in the country. Together with our team of specially trained general dentists we can provide Invisalign to patients in all our locations.

Benefits beyond what you see

Correcting the position of your teeth not only improves your smile, it also benefits your health by reducing the chance of gum disease, lowering stress on your jaw from a misaligned bite, and reducing excessive or uneven wear on your teeth.

Cutting-Edge Technology for the Best Results

We offer precise, state-of-the-art Itero digital impression scanning that completely eliminates the need for time-consuming and messy traditional dental impressions.

These scanners provide faster modeling of your bite and improved accuracy, which means better results.
