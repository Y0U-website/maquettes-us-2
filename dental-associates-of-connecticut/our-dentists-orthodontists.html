You’ll notice something very different about Dental Associates of Connecticut the moment you walk in the door. Whether it’s our Danbury, Fairfield, New Milford, Newtown or Shelton office our dentists will welcome you as a member of the family. Their care and devotion go far beyond the latest procedures, technologies, and conveniences offered. Our dentists go the extra mile to make sure each and every patient has a uniquely satisfying and comfortable visit. Let us show you the Dental Associates difference.
