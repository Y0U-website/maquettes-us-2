Dr. Bhavin Gupta, DMD

Dr. Bhavin Gupta is a compassionate pediatric dentist dedicated to creating positive, educational, and fun dental experiences for children. A Connecticut native, Dr. Gupta is a proud Triple Husky, having earned his undergraduate degree, Doctor of Dental Medicine, and specialized pediatric training all from the University of Connecticut. He also completed a General Practice Residency at Montefiore Hospital, gaining advanced experience in managing a wide range of complex dental needs.

Dr. Gupta’s practice is rooted in the belief that early, comfortable visits build a lifetime of healthy habits. He specializes in comprehensive care for infants, children, and adolescents, with a particular passion for preventive care and patient education. His gentle approach and calm demeanor are designed to put young patients at ease while providing parents with total peace of mind.

An active member of the American Association of Pediatric Dentistry and the CSDA, Dr. Gupta stays at the forefront of the latest clinical advancements. Outside of the office, he is a true sports enthusiast, especially when it comes to soccer and basketball, and loves recharging in nature by exploring America’s national parks with friends and family.

Dr. Suren Swampillai, DDS

Dr. Suren Swampillai earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from New York University College of Dentistry. He is committed to delivering high-quality dental care with a strong focus on patient comfort and satisfaction.

Dedicated to excellence, Dr. Swampillai invests countless hours in continuing education to ensure his patients benefit from the latest advancements in dentistry.

Outside of the office, Dr. Swampillai enjoys spending time with his wife and is an avid reader, particularly of business-oriented books. One of his personal favorites is Principles by Ray Dalio.

Dr. Dawn Weldon, DDS

Originally from North Carolina, Dr. Weldon graduated from UNC-Chapel Hill with a BA degree in Psychology and a minor in Human Nutrition. She then pursued her passion for healthcare at NYU College of Dentistry, earning a DDS degree in dental surgery.

Excelling in esthetics, Dr. Weldon explored various specialties but found her true calling in Endodontics. She completed a general practice residency in dentistry at the VA Hospital in Manhattan, followed by a specialty residency in Endodontics at the same hospital. Her relatable and kind nature, along with her precision in delivering high-quality care, made her a favorite dentist among many veterans she treats.

Dr. Erol Gund, DDS

Dr. Genel Giuliano - General Dentist

Dr. Genel Giuliano specializes in Implantology, the modern method for replacing missing teeth. She has practiced at Dental Associates’ New Milford office since 2000.

Dr. Giuliano received a Bachelor of Arts degree in Psychology at Boston College, and Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) at Temple University in Philadelphia.

She served her residency in Advanced Education in General Dentistry at Temple University, with a strong emphasis on Implantology.

Dr. Giuliano belongs to the American Dental Association and the Connecticut Dental Association.

When not working with patients to improve their smiles, Dr. Giuliano loves to travel, garden, paint, being outdoors and most importantly, spending time with her family. She is fluent in Spanish and proficient in Italian.

Dr. Brian Fusillo - General Dentist

Dr. Fusillo, who is also fluent in Spanish and proficient in Italian, was on the Dean’s List at UMDMJ and served as an officer of the HDA. He is currently a member of the UMDNJ Alumni society, the American Dental Association (ADA), and the Connecticut Dental Association. Throughout his career he has received multiple awards and honors including an Award for Outstanding Community Service from the American Legion.

Dr. Fusillo also participates in numerous continuing education courses on all the latest forms of cosmetic dentistry. His commitment to improving dental treatment has motivated him to continue his ongoing research into improving patient’s aesthetic concerns as well as addressing their dental anxieties.
