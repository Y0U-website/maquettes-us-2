What are my orthodontic treatment options?

Whether you want to straighten your teeth for cosmetic or functional reasons, you have plenty of options to achieve your orthodontic goals.

Orthodontics can correct numerous dental issues, including gapped, misaligned, and overcrowded teeth. Teeth straightening also corrects malocclusions like overbites, underbites, open bites, and cross bites.

  • Traditional braces: Conventional braces are the most commonly recognized because they have been used for decades. They use metal wires, brackets, and elastics to apply controlled pressure to your teeth to move them to the ideal position. Traditional braces are permanently attached to your teeth; only a dentist can remove/adjust them. Unlike metal braces of yesteryears, modern braces are created from lightweight, tooth-like materials for more comfortable and discreet treatment.
  • Invisalign aligners: Teeth straightening isn’t just for children. While adult braces exist, there is yet another discreet and convenient method. Invisalign uses clear, removable aligners to perfect smiles, making it a popular alternative for teens, adults, and anyone self-conscious about metal braces. Furthermore, Invisalign trays offer removable convenience, allowing you to eat your favorite foods and clean your teeth without limitations.
