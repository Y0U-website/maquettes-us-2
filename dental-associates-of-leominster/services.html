Opalescence

This system of teeth whitening is wonderful, because you can do it at home at your own pace. Results are normally seen in as little as three to five days, and can be maintained for as long as you like. The gel is usable with other trays made through a dentist and can be purchased through our offices. We offer gels ranging from 10 % to 35 % carbamide peroxide mix.

Implants

These are great because they replace missing teeth, help to prevent bone loss and help to maintain the general health of the patient.

Some denture patients even use implants to help secure their dentures better when relines and adhesives just won’t work. Dr. Chang surgically places these implants and restores them to like natural tooth function and aesthetics.

Vizilite Plus Exam

This is an early oral cancer exam, best defense against one of the deadliest forms of cancer that has been linked to the human papilloma virus.

Botox and Juvederm Filler

Dr. Chang has advanced training is facial aesthetics using Botox and Dermal Fillers.

Surgical and Tooth Removal Procedures

Dr. Chang offers tooth removal and bone grafting procedures also soft tissue and biopsies in both locations. Our offices offer these special procedures and all General Dental needs in two locations for the entire family.
