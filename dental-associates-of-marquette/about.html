About Dental Associates

Meet our Management

TAMARA MCCLELLAN

Business Manager Tammy joined Dental Associates in September of 1978. She is a graduate of Marquette Senior High School and Northern Michigan University. Tammy started as a "girl Friday" during her high school years. Upon graduating from Northern Michigan University,she then continued her work at the front desk, Treatment Assistant and the Preventive Hygiene Program. She became the Office Manager in 2000 and was promoted to the Business Manager in 2010. She has been married to David since 1989. Tammy and her husband enjoy their time with family, friends, entertaining and traveling.

MARCY MUELLE

Office Manager Marcy is a native of the Upper Peninsula. She has lived in the Marquette area since 1991 and has been with Dental Associates since 2008. She enjoys being part of a team that makes patients feel welcomed and valued. She is happy to answer any question and ensure a positive visit for all patients.
