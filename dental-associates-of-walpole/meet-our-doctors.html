Meet Our Doctors

Pediatrics

Scott F. Petrie, D.M.D.

Dr. Petrie received his B.A. in Biology from Boston University and his D.M.D. degree from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. He completed training in pediatric dentistry at Children’s Hospital of Boston and Harvard School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Lee is a faculty member at Boston Children’s Hospital and Harvard School of Dental Medicine, where she treats patients with special healthcare needs and mentors dental students. She is an active member of the Massachusetts Dental Society, American Dental Association, and American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, and volunteers with Give Kids a Smile to provide free dental care to underserved children.

In addition to her clinical training, Dr. Cooperman has been actively involved in state and federal advocacy initiatives, working to improve access to dental care for children and for patients with special healthcare needs.

Dr. Mahdavi became an Invisalign® certified orthodontist in 2000, and is now an Elite Invisalign® Provider. Dr. Mahdavi is a proud partner of Smiles Change Lives , a national non-profit organization that helps kids from low-income families get braces.

Dr. Kane’s greatest personal interest outside of dentistry is technology. He has lectured at Yankee Dental Congress on integrating the modern dental office with contemporary digital equipment. In addition, he is participating in research that tests the accuracy of digital equipment used in pediatric and orthodontic practices.

Dr. Marcks lives on the South Shore with her husband and daughter. In her free time, she enjoys distance running and has completed 23 half marathons and 3 full marathons (and counting!). She also loves spending time in her kitchen experimenting with new recipes.

Adult Dentistry

Christopher R. Page, D.M.D.

Dr. Christopher Page graduated from the University of Pennsylvania in Philadelphia and went on to receive his D.M.D. from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. While in dental school he completed an externship at Wrentham Development Center, working with special needs patients. Dr. Page also became a trained CEREC dentist in 2006, taking great interest in innovative restorative procedures and technology in dentistry. In addition to his work at Dental Associates of Walpole, he maintains a practice at Page Family Dental in Revere, MA.

As a member of the American Dental Society and Massachusetts Dental Society, Dr. Adams is looking forward to working to improve access to care in this area.

Dr. Palys has published several scientific articles on periodontics, and has worked with numerous research teams at Harvard University School of Dental Medicine and Forsyth Dental Center. He was a Research Fellow in the Department of Periodontology at Harvard, and a clinical research investigator at the Boston Oral Health Clinical Resource Center. In addition, he was a clinical instructor at the Forsyth School of Dental Hygiene and in Forsyth Dental Center’s Periodontal Residency Program.

Dr. Palys was also a research volunteer at Dana-Farber Cancer Institute’s Department of Cellular and Molecular Biology. When not dazzling the patients of Dental Associates of Walpole, he works as a periodontist at Harvard Dental Center Cambridge, which serves the entire Harvard University community. He is also a predoctoral faculty instructor at the Harvard School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Sabra obtained his residency training and received his certificate in Periodontics and Dental Implants from the University of Texas Health Science Center in San Antonio. While in Texas, he completed his master’s degree in biomedical sciences for his research on soft tissue healing after implant surgery in patients with diabetes.

In September 2017, at the annual conference of the American Academy of Periodontology, Dr. Sabra was selected as the recipient of the Dr. and Mrs. Gerald M. Kramer Scholar Award for Excellence, given to the nation’s top overall graduating resident.

Dr. Sabra is a member of the American Board of Periodontology, American Academy of Periodontology, American Dental Association, and the Massachusetts Dental Society.

Dr. Bateman also attended the University of Texas Health Science Center, for additional training in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. In his Chief Residency year, he scored 100 on his in-service exam, which was the highest score in the nation. On completion of his residency program, Dr. Bateman received his Medical Doctorate, Certificate in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, and a one year Certificate in General Surgery.

Dr. Bateman is a Fellow of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, is a member of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Utah Association of Oral and Maxillofacial surgeons, and the American Medical Association.

Dr. Bateman loves to spend time with his wife and 6 kids, and says the greatest accomplishment in his life is getting his wife to marry him.
