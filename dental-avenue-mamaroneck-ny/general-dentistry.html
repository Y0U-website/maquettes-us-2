Dental Exam

Comprehensive assessment of oral health.

A dental exam is a comprehensive evaluation of a patient’s oral health conducted by a dental professional. It involves a thorough examination of the teeth, gums, and other oral structures. During the exam, the dentist assesses the overall oral health, identifies any existing dental problems, and determines the need for further treatments or preventive measures.

Mouth Guards

Protective devices for teeth during sports.

A mouth guard is a protective device designed to cover and cushion the teeth, gums, and surrounding structures during physical activities or sports. It is typically made of a flexible material, such as silicone or laminate, and is custom-fitted to your mouth. The custom fit ensures maximum comfort and effectiveness in providing protection against dental injuries.

Night Guards

Custom-fitted guards to prevent teeth grinding..

A night guard is a dental appliance made of a soft, flexible material that is worn over the teeth during sleep. It is custom-fitted to your mouth to provide a comfortable and secure fit. Night guards are designed to prevent the upper and lower teeth from coming into direct contact, reducing the damaging effects of teeth grinding or clenching.

Oral Cancer Screening

Examination for early detection of oral cancer.

Oral cancer screening is a thorough examination of the oral cavity, including the lips, tongue, gums, cheeks, throat, and other oral tissues. It is a proactive approach to identify any early signs or suspicious lesions that may indicate the presence of oral cancer.

Retainers

Custom devices to maintain tooth alignment.

Retainers are dental appliances made of plastic or metal that are custom-fitted to your mouth. They are typically worn after the completion of orthodontic treatment to prevent your teeth from shifting back to their original positions. Retainers can be removable or fixed, depending on your specific needs and treatment plan.

Sleep Apnea Treatment

Solutions for breathing-related sleep disorders.

Sleep apnea is a condition in which a person’s breathing repeatedly stops and starts during sleep. The most common type of sleep apnea is obstructive sleep apnea (OSA), which occurs when the muscles at the back of the throat fail to keep the airway open. This results in brief pauses in breathing, leading to disrupted sleep patterns and potential health consequences.

TMJ Disorder (TMJD) Treatment

Therapies for jaw joint dysfunction.

TMJ disorder, also known as TMJD or TMD, is a condition that affects the temporomandibular joint, which allows for the smooth movement of the jaw. TMJD can result from various factors, including jaw misalignment, teeth grinding or clenching (bruxism), arthritis, stress, or injury to the jaw joint. The exact cause of TMJD can vary among individuals.

Teeth Cleaning (Deep)

Thorough cleaning to address gum disease.

Deep teeth cleaning, or scaling and root planing, is a non-surgical procedure performed by our dental professionals to treat gum disease and remove plaque and tartar buildup that extends below the gumline. It involves two main steps: scaling and root planing. Scaling is the removal of plaque and tartar from the tooth surfaces and along the gumline, including the pockets formed between the gums and teeth.

Teeth Cleaning (Regular)

Routine cleaning for oral hygiene.

Regular teeth cleaning, or prophylaxis, is a professional dental procedure performed by our skilled dental hygienists at Dental Avenue. It involves the thorough removal of plaque, tartar, and stains from the tooth surfaces, especially in areas that are difficult to reach with regular brushing and flossing. Regular teeth cleaning is typically recommended every 6 months as part of routine dental care.
