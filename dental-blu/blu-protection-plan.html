Membership plans.

When you join the Blu Smiles Protection Plan, our dentists will conduct a comprehensive dental exam to assess your overall oral health. Based on the findings, you’ll fall into one of two categories:

Healthy Plan

You have no signs of periodontal disease (gum disease or bone loss).

Child Complete (Ages 0–13) ‍

‍ Affordable preventive care tailored for kids.

Adult Complete (Ages 14+)

‍ Comprehensive routine care for teens and adults.

The Blu Smiles Protection Plan offers exceptional value with the following benefits included in your membership:

Plus, members save over 40 % on these essential services and gain access to a reduced fee schedule for restorative and other treatments as needed.

  • Routine Dental Exams
  • Professional Cleanings
  • Fluoride Treatments
  • Oral Cancer Screenings
  • Full-Mouth Panoramic X-Rays
  • Cavity-Detecting X-Rays

Perio Plan

You show signs of active periodontal disease or have a history of periodontal therapy. This plan includes specialized care to manage and maintain gum health.

  • Routine Dental Exams
  • Professional Cleanings
  • Fluoride Treatments
  • Oral Cancer Screenings
  • Full-Mouth Panoramic X-Rays
  • Cavity-Detecting X-Rays
