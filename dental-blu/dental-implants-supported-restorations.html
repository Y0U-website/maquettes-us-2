Dental Implants

  • Long-Lasting Durability: With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.
  • Enhanced Comfort: Unlike removable dentures, implants are secure and feel like natural teeth.
  • Improved Oral Health: Implants preserve jawbone structure and prevent bone loss.
  • Restored Confidence: Enjoy a complete, beautiful smile with implants that stay in place.

Types of Implant-Supported Restorations

Single-Tooth Implants

Replace one missing tooth with a dental implant and a custom-made crown for a seamless restoration.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Ideal for replacing multiple missing teeth, implant-supported bridges use implants to anchor a fixed restoration without relying on adjacent natural teeth.

All-on-X Implants

For patients missing an entire arch of teeth, All-on-X Implants provide a secure and natural-looking solution using fewer implants to support a full set of teeth.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Upgrade from traditional dentures with Implant-Supported Dentures, which are securely fixed to implants, eliminating the need for adhesives and improving stability.

The Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation and Planning: During your initial visit, we’ll evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and determine if you’re a candidate for implants.
  • Preparation: For some patients, additional procedures like Bone Grafting may be necessary to ensure a strong foundation.
  • Implant Placement: The implant is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or Sedation Dentistry for added comfort.
  • Healing Period: Over several months, the implant integrates with the bone to provide a stable foundation.
  • Restoration: Once healed, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to complete your restoration.

Are Dental Implants Right for You?

Dental implants are a great solution for most adults with healthy gums and sufficient jawbone density. However, if you’ve experienced bone loss, Bone Grafting or other preparatory treatments can make implants a viable option.

Caring for Your Implants

Dental implants require the same care as natural teeth. Follow these tips to maintain their longevity:

  • Brush and floss daily to keep the surrounding gums and bone healthy.
  • Use a water flosser or special interdental brushes for hard-to-reach areas.
  • Schedule regular Dental Exams and Cleanings to monitor your implants.
  • Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol consumption, which can affect healing and long-term success.

FAQs About Dental Implants

Are dental implants painful?

The implant procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, and most patients report minimal discomfort during recovery.

How long does the implant process take?

The timeline varies but generally takes several months, including the healing period. Temporary solutions are available to maintain your smile in the meantime.

Are dental implants expensive?

While the initial cost is higher than other options, implants are a long-term investment that often lasts a lifetime, reducing the need for replacements.

Can smokers get dental implants?

Smoking can affect the success rate of implants, but quitting before and after the procedure can improve outcomes. Discuss your lifestyle during your consultation.

Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Some insurance plans cover parts of the implant process, such as crowns. Contact us to review your coverage and financing options.
