Need Help With a Dental Emergency?

Dental pain or an unexpected dental problem can make it difficult to focus on anything else. Significant tooth pain, swelling, dental trauma, a damaged tooth, a lost filling or crown, or signs of infection may need prompt attention.

If you are unsure what to do, call us and explain what is happening. Our team can ask a few basic questions and help you understand the next available option. Same-day emergency visits may be available depending on the office schedule and the nature of the concern.

If you are experiencing significant dental pain, swelling, trauma, or another urgent concern, speaking with our team may help you understand what to do next.

If you have uncontrolled bleeding, major facial trauma, or swelling that makes it difficult to breathe or swallow, seek emergency medical care immediately or call 911.

You can also learn more about urgent dental concerns on our Emergency Dentist in Miami page.

Questions About Insurance, Financing, or Payment?

Dental insurance and treatment costs can sometimes be difficult to understand. You do not need to figure everything out before contacting us.

Dental Blush works with many PPO dental insurance plans, and our team can help verify available benefits and estimated patient responsibility based on information provided by the insurance carrier. We can also help verify Dental Medicare Advantage benefits when applicable.

Because every insurance plan is different, actual coverage depends on the individual policy, deductible, annual maximum, limitations, and dental services received.

If additional payment flexibility would be helpful, you can learn about available options on our Dental Financing page.

Patients looking for an alternative to traditional dental insurance can also review our Dental Discount Plans .

If you are not sure which option applies to you, ask us. Our team can help explain the available information before you decide how to move forward.

Frequently Asked Questions About Contacting Dental Blush

How can I contact Dental Blush?

You can call (305) 553-0666 , email dentist@dentalblush.com , or complete the contact form on this page. If your concern is urgent, calling is usually the best option.

What should I include in the contact form?

Tell us your name, preferred way to contact you, and a brief description of how we can help. You do not need to know exactly which dental treatment you need before reaching out.

Can I contact your team in Spanish?

Yes. Our team provides assistance in English and Spanish. You can ask questions about appointments, dental care, insurance, financing, office information, and other concerns in the language you feel more comfortable using.

Can your team help with insurance and financing questions?

Yes. We can provide general information about many PPO plans, applicable Dental Medicare Advantage benefits, available financing options, and dental discount plans. Final insurance coverage and financing approval depend on the individual plan or financing provider.

What should I do if I have an urgent dental problem?

Call our office and describe what is happening. Our team can review the information you provide and help explain the next available option. For serious medical emergencies involving uncontrolled bleeding, major facial trauma, or difficulty breathing or swallowing, seek emergency medical care immediately.
