Les soins

Braces require careful cleaning around brackets and wires because plaque and food can collect around the appliance. Your dental team will explain how to adjust brushing and cleaning between the teeth during treatment.

Dental implants and dentures both replace missing teeth, but they work differently. Traditional dentures are removable, while implants can provide a fixed foundation in the jaw for certain restorations.

Implant treatment requires enough bone and healthy surrounding tissues to support the planned restoration. Your general health, dental health, medical history, oral hygiene, and other factors can also affect treatment planning and healing.

Patients exploring Orthodontics in Miami may have more than one treatment option. Braces and clear aligners both move teeth gradually, but they use different systems and require different types of daily care.
