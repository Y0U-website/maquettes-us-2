Braces and Clear Aligner Options

Patients exploring Orthodontics in Miami may have more than one treatment option. Braces and clear aligners both move teeth gradually, but they use different systems and require different types of daily care.

Traditional Braces

Traditional braces use brackets attached to the teeth and wires that guide tooth movement over time. Because the appliance remains fixed during active treatment, the dental team can use it to manage many types of alignment and bite concerns.

Braces require careful brushing and cleaning around brackets and wires. Your dental provider will also explain which foods or habits may damage the appliance and what to do if a bracket or wire causes a problem.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners use removable trays that apply planned pressure to the teeth in stages. They can be removed for eating, brushing, and flossing, but treatment depends on wearing them consistently according to your provider’s instructions.

Clear aligners are not the right approach for every orthodontic problem. If you want more detailed information about removable aligner treatment, visit our Invisalign in Miami page.

How Do We Choose Between Braces and Clear Aligners?

The best appliance is the one that can accomplish the movements your teeth and bite require while fitting the overall treatment plan. Appearance is only one part of that decision.

When planning Orthodontic Treatment in Miami , the dental team considers tooth position, bite relationships, oral health, treatment complexity, and how well the patient can follow the instructions required by the selected system.

Some cases may be suitable for either braces or clear aligners, while others may benefit more from one approach. Your consultation is the place to compare those options based on your actual dental condition.

What Happens During an Orthodontic Consultation?

An orthodontic consultation focuses on understanding your alignment, bite, oral health, and treatment goals before recommending a plan.

Every smile is different, so Orthodontics in Miami should begin with an individualized evaluation rather than a one-size-fits-all treatment plan.

  • Initial evaluation: The dental team reviews your teeth, gums, bite, spacing, crowding, and other alignment concerns.
  • Diagnostic records: Dental imaging, photographs, scans, or other records may be used when appropriate for treatment planning.
  • Treatment discussion: The provider explains whether braces, clear aligners, or another approach may fit your needs.
  • Timing and cost review: You receive information about the expected treatment process, estimated costs, and available payment options.
  • Next steps: If you decide to move forward, the team explains what happens before active treatment begins.

How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Take?

There is no single timeline for braces or clear aligners. Treatment length depends on your starting tooth positions, bite, complexity of movement, treatment goals, appliance type, and how your teeth respond.

Regular progress visits are part of Orthodontic Treatment in Miami . These appointments allow the dental team to monitor movement, evaluate the appliances, and make changes when necessary.

Your provider can give you a more useful estimate after evaluating your individual case.

Keeping Teeth and Gums Healthy During Orthodontics

Good oral hygiene remains important throughout orthodontic treatment. Brackets, wires, and removable trays can change the way you clean your teeth, so your routine may need some adjustments.

With braces, plaque and food can collect around brackets and wires. Clear aligners require cleaning of both the trays and teeth before the aligners go back into the mouth.

Professional cleanings continue to play an important role while you receive Orthodontic Treatment in Miami . To learn more about preventive visits, see our Teeth Cleaning in Miami page.

What If a Bracket, Wire, or Aligner Causes a Problem?

A loose bracket, damaged wire, lost aligner, or appliance that no longer fits correctly can interfere with treatment. Contact the office so the dental team can tell you whether the problem needs an earlier visit or can wait until your scheduled appointment.

Strong tooth pain, significant swelling, trauma, or another urgent dental problem may require a separate evaluation. For urgent dental concerns, visit our Emergency Dentist in Miami page or call (305) 553-0666 .

What Happens After Braces or Clear Aligners?

Active tooth movement is only one phase of orthodontic care. After braces or aligners finish moving the teeth, retainers help maintain the new positions.

Your provider will recommend a retainer based on your treatment and explain how to wear and care for it. Following those instructions is an important part of protecting the result of Orthodontic Treatment in Miami .

If a retainer breaks, is lost, or stops fitting correctly, contact the office rather than waiting for the teeth to noticeably shift.
