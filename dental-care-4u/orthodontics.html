Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the best age to start orthodontic treatment?

A: While there’s no best age, most orthodontic treatment begins between ages 8 and 14, during the peak growth period. However, adults can also benefit from orthodontics.

Q: What conditions can orthodontics treat?

A: Orthodontics can treat a variety of conditions, including overcrowded teeth, gaps, overbites, underbites, and crossbites.
