New Technology With a Gentle Touch

We are dedicated to delivering outstanding dental care and utilizing advanced dental technology to ensure you receive the highest quality treatment. Our cutting-edge tools enable us to quickly and accurately detect dental issues, providing efficient treatment while prioritizing your comfort. When you choose our practice, you can trust that you are receiving the latest technology and treatments available.

Digital X-Rays

Our digital X-rays emit less radiation, take images faster without any need for processing, and allow us to optimize the images so we can provide a more precise diagnosis.

Digital Intraoral Camera

By using this camera, we can zoom in closely on specific teeth and capture a high-quality image of them. This gives us a better look at problem areas and the healing process.

iTero Scanner

iTero scanners are used to create digital dental impressions through a much less invasive process than traditional dental impressions—which require mold trays and impression material (alginate) to sit in your mouth.

3D Printer

3D Printing technology has revolutionized the dental industry, allowing for the manufacture of dentures, whitening trays, and implants in-office. This enables dental professionals to create high-precision apparatuses with a faster turn-around time than traditional outsourcing.
