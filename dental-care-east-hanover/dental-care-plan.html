Dental Savings Plan

  • NON-REFUNDABLE. If the patient decides not to use the plan, we will not issue any refunds or premiums
  • The full annual fee is due at the time of enrollment
  • If the plan lapses, a $50 reactivation fee will be charged
  • Benefit coverage is subject to annual review and change
  • Cannot be used in conjunction with another dental plan

Important Notes

Please note that this is a discount plan rather than a dental insurance plan and therefore cannot be used:

  • In conjunction with another dental plan
  • For specialists’ referrals
  • For services and injuries covered by workman’s compensation
  • For any charges related to hospitals or hospitalizations
  • For dental care costs covered under a medical, automobile, or any other claims
  • For any procedure or treatment that lies in what the treating dentist/doctor believes is outside the realm of their capability
