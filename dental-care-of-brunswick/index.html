Looking for a family dentist near you in Brunswick, Ohio? We offer comprehensive dental care for the whole family. Our services include:

Implant Dentistry – Dental implants are the best replacement for missing teeth because they are the only solution that addresses tooth loss both above and below the gum line. Our doctors have specialized dental implant training to provide restorations that look natural and feel comfortable.

Restorative Dentistry – Restorative dentistry is a term used to describe a number of different procedures that restore strength, function, and appearance to damaged teeth. We provide durable restorations that blend in naturally with the rest of your smile.

Bridges – Bridges are used to replace one or more missing teeth. We offer a number of different bridge options and each of them is made with careful attention to detail to ensure a realistic appearance and comfort.

Root Canals – Root canals allow us to save a tooth that would otherwise need to be extracted. Our advanced techniques make the procedure comfortable and highly effective.

Dental Extractions – There are times when a tooth cannot be saved and an extraction is needed. Instead of referring patients to another practice, we provide dental extractions right here at Bellissimi Dental.

Periodontal Treatment – Without healthy gums, you can’t have healthy teeth. We provide effective treatments for every stage of gum disease.
