Our Mission

Trusted Dental Care in Cerritos for Over 25 Years

Since 1998, we have proudly served Cerritos and the surrounding communities by providing comprehensive general and cosmetic dentistry in a welcoming, family-friendly environment. For more than 25 years, our team has been committed to helping patients achieve healthier, brighter smiles through personalized care and lasting relationships built on trust.

We believe every smile tells a story. That’s why we take the time to listen, educate, and care for each patient like family. Whether you’re visiting for preventive care, restorative treatment, cosmetic services, or simply reassurance, we’re honored to be part of your smile journey.

Because to us, you’re more than just a patient—we truly think the world of your smile.

Meet Dr. Dazo, DDS

Founder of Dental Care of Cerritos

Dr. Marbea S. Dazo, DDS

Founder & General Dentist Dr. Marbea Dazo is the heart of Dental Care of Cerritos and has proudly served the Cerritos community for more than 20 years. She earned her dental degree from the University of Baguio College of Dentistry in the Philippines and continued her advanced education in cosmetic dentistry at the University of Southern California (USC).

As a general dentist, Dr. Dazo provides comprehensive dental care with a special interest in cosmetic dentistry, including aesthetic crowns and smile-enhancing treatments. She takes great pride in creating a welcoming environment where patients of all ages feel comfortable, respected, and confident in their care.

Known for her upbeat personality and gentle approach, Dr. Dazo is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles while building lasting relationships with the families she serves. She also enjoys giving back to the community by teaching children throughout the ABC Unified School District about the importance of good oral health and lifelong dental habits.

Serving others extends far beyond the walls of her practice. Dr. Dazo has participated in dental mission trips to provide care to underserved communities and is a proud financial supporter of Doctors Without Borders, as well as missionaries serving communities around the world. Her commitment to service reflects her belief that compassionate care has the power to make a lasting difference, both locally and globally.

Outside the office, Dr. Dazo enjoys traveling, spending time with her family, and making memories with her beloved French bulldog, Alex, who proudly serves as the office mascot.

Dr. Dazo is an active member of the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association, and the National Association of Filipino Dentists in America.
