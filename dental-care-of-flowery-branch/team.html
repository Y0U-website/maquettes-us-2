0 Our Dentists Dr. Jason Lue Yen, DDS Dr. Lue Yen completed his dental education at the University of Maryland, where he obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree. Dr. Lina Warda, DDS

Dr. Lina Warda is a graduate of The University of Southern California. Following her dental education, Dr. Warda embarked on a mission to provide top-notch dental care to individuals of all ages. Through her warm and approachable demeanor, she quickly establishes a strong rapport with her patients, ensuring that they feel comfortable and at ease during their dental visits.

Dr. Jina Kang, DDS

Dr. Jina Kang practiced in Seattle, WA before moving to Georgia to join Dental Care of Flowery Branch. She received her Bachelor of Science from Georgia Institute of Technology. She then attended the University of Detroit Mercy, where she obtained her Doctor of Dental Surgery. She completed a general practice residency at Veterans Affairs and the University of Michigan Health System in Ann Arbor, MI.

Dr. Kang is a lifelong learner and loves to take continuing education courses in order to keep up with the latest technology. She has had her fair share of dental experiences so she understands how intimidating a dental visit can be for some patients and makes it her personal goal to provide a comfortable atmosphere. She finds her job very rewarding because she gets to help patients smile beautiful again while getting them out of pain and discomfort.

While not in the office, Dr. Kang is a foodie and likes to travel and try new restaurants and desserts with her husband and their son, Daniel.

Dr. Eric Becker, DDS

Dr. Becker graduated from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry and completed a General Practice Residency In Knoxville, Tennessee. He was honored to be inducted into the Gamma Pi Delta Prosthodontic Honor Society for his dedication and skill. Beyond dentistry, Dr. Becker is passionate about giving back. He has volunteered with organizations like Mission of Mercy Dental and other networks, showing his deep commitment to both quality and care to the community.
