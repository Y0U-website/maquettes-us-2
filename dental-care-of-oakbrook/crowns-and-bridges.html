Crowns & Bridges

If you are experiencing a missing tooth or teeth, let the experts at Dental Care of Oak Brook bring that full smile back. Dr. Kimet can fit you for a custom-made, natural looking bridge to restore the contour of your teeth.

Often referred to as fixed partial dentures, bridges are semi-permanent and bonded to existing teeth or implants. Dental Care of Oak Brook also offers several permanent bridges including, conventional fixed bridges, cantilever bridges and resin-bonded bridges. Using porcelain, gold alloys and other combinations of materials, Dr. Kimet will ensure that your new teeth perfectly match your existing ones.
