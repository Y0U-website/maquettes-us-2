Dr. Gina Gill, DMD

Harvard and Tufts-Trained Orthodontist Delivering Personalized Care

Dr. Gina Gill is a board-certified orthodontist at Dental Care Orthodontics in Stamford, CT, known for her warm, patient-centered approach and her exceptional training pedigree. With degrees from NYU, Harvard, and Tufts, Dr. Gill combines academic excellence with a genuine gift for connection — the kind that turns orthodontic treatment into a trusted, personal journey.

Undergraduate Degree New York University — graduated with honors Dental Degree Harvard School of Dental Medicine Orthodontic Specialty Training Tufts University School of Dental Medicine Harvard and Tufts both rank among the country’s most selective and academically rigorous dental programs, and Dr. Gill has brought that same standard of care and continuous learning into her Stamford practice.

Treatment Philosophy

Personalized care is her signature

Every patient has a unique bite, timeline, budget, and comfort level — and Dr. Gill builds treatment plans that reflect all of it. She spends extra time at consultations making sure patients understand what’s happening and why.

Strong relationships produce better outcomes

Orthodontic treatment often spans a year or two. Dr. Gill believes consistent, trusting relationships between doctor and patient lead to better compliance, better results, and a better experience overall.

Education empowers decisions

She’s known for explaining complex orthodontic concepts in plain language — whether to a 10-year-old getting their first set of braces or an adult weighing clear aligners vs. braces for the first time.
