Dr. Peter Feibish is a board-certified orthodontist at Dental Care Orthodontics in Stamford, CT, where he has dedicated his career to helping patients of all ages achieve healthier, more confident smiles. With advanced training from Columbia University and an approach built on long-term oral health, function, and aesthetics, Dr. Feibish is a trusted name in orthodontic care across Fairfield County.

Clinical Specialties

Dr. Feibish offers the full spectrum of modern orthodontic treatment options:

Conventional metal braces — time-tested, highly effective, and often the best choice for complex movements

Clear ceramic braces — discreet alternative to metal for image-conscious patients

Damon self-ligating braces — efficient system using specialized brackets for reduced friction

Invisalign clear aligners — removable, nearly invisible treatment option

Comprehensive orthodontic evaluation and diagnosis

Complex bite correction including overbite, underbite, crossbite, and open bite

Compassionate orthodontic care for all ages

Treatment Philosophy

Long-term oral health comes first

Straight teeth matter, but so does the stability of your bite, the health of your jaw joints, and the positioning of your teeth for decades to come. Dr. Feibish treats the whole system.

Function and aesthetics work together

A beautifully aligned smile should also function properly — chewing, speaking, and wearing naturally over time. He doesn’t compromise one for the other.

Treatment should fit the patient

He builds plans that respect his patients’ schedules, budgets, and lifestyles while still delivering excellent clinical outcomes.
