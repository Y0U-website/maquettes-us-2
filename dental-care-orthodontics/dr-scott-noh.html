Dr. Scott Noh, DDS, MS

Evidence-Based Orthodontics with Columbia-Trained Precision

Dr. Scott Noh is a board-certified orthodontist at Dental Care Orthodontics in Stamford, CT, known for his rigorous, evidence-based approach to orthodontic care and his dedication to patient education. With a background in biochemistry, a dental degree from Columbia, and a Master of Science in Orthodontics, Dr. Noh brings scientific precision and clinical excellence to every case.

Undergraduate Degree UCLA — Biochemistry Dental Degree Columbia University College of Dental Medicine Orthodontic Specialty Training Orthodontic Residency and Master of Science (MS) in Orthodontics The combination of biochemistry, dentistry, and a research-based MS in Orthodontics gives Dr. Noh a uniquely analytical foundation. He approaches every case with the mindset of a clinician-scientist — grounded in evidence, refined through experience.

A Rich, Culturally Fluent Background

Dr. Noh was raised internationally with time spent in Seoul, Sydney, and Los Angeles. That global background shapes his approach to patient care: he’s culturally adaptable, communicates easily across backgrounds, and brings a broad perspective to a field that benefits from openness to diverse patient needs.

Clinical Specialties

Dr. Noh offers the complete range of modern orthodontic services:

Comprehensive braces treatment including Damon self-ligating systems

Clear ceramic braces for patients wanting a more discreet option

Invisalign clear aligner therapy for mild to complex cases

Advanced bite correction and complex malocclusion cases

Collaboration with dental and medical specialists for interdisciplinary care

What Patients Appreciate About Dr. Noh

Thoughtful, precise treatment planning — every case is mapped out with extensive diagnostic details

Calm, measured demeanor — highly reassuring for anxious patients of any age

Multilingual and multicultural fluency — connects easily with Stamford’s diverse populations

Treatment Philosophy

Evidence first

Dr. Noh relies on current peer-reviewed research and diagnostic data — not habit or convention — to guide treatment decisions. He’s committed to ongoing continuing education.

Precision matters

Small details in bracket placement, force application, and treatment sequencing make significant differences in outcomes. Dr. Noh applies exacting standards to every step of treatment.

Patients deserve to understand their care

Dr. Noh takes time to explain diagnoses, treatment mechanics, expected timelines, and potential alternatives. Patients leave consultations with a genuine understanding of what’s happening.
