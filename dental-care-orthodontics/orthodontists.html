Meet the Doctors

Dr. Peter Feibish, DDS

Columbia-trained orthodontist specializing in comprehensive care

Dr. Peter Feibish brings an Ivy League education and decades of clinical experience to every patient relationship. After completing his undergraduate studies at Rutgers University, he earned his dental degree with honors from Temple University School of Dentistry before pursuing his orthodontic specialty training at Columbia University.

Dr. Feibish’s clinical interests span the full range of modern orthodontic treatment — conventional braces, clear ceramic braces, Damon self-ligating systems, and Invisalign. His approach emphasizes long-term oral health, functional occlusion, and aesthetic outcomes that hold up over time.

02 Dr. Gina Gill, DMD Harvard and Tufts-trained orthodontist focused on relationship-driven care

Dr. Gina Gill earned her undergraduate degree with honors from NYU before attending Harvard School of Dental Medicine. She then completed her orthodontic residency at Tufts University, a program widely recognized for its rigor and research contributions to the specialty.

Patients describe Dr. Gill as warm, patient, and exceptionally thorough. Her treatment focus includes traditional braces, clear braces, and Invisalign — but her real signature is the depth of connection she builds with each patient. She treats everyone, from nervous 8-year-olds to busy professionals, as an individual with unique goals.

03 Dr. Scott Noh, DDS, MS Columbia-trained orthodontist with a precision-focused approach

Dr. Scott Noh’s international upbringing shapes his perspective on patient care. He completed his undergraduate studies at UCLA before earning his dental degree at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine. He then completed both his orthodontic residency and a Master of Science in Orthodontics.

Dr. Noh brings a scientist’s rigor to clinical practice. He’s deeply committed to evidence-based orthodontics, precision diagnostics, and patient education. His patients often comment on how clearly he explains the “why” behind every treatment decision.
