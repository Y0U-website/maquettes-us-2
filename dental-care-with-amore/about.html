Meet Dr. Amit Patel

Dr. Amit Patel, D.D.S. brings a calm, caring approach to patient care at Dental Care with Amore&#x27;. He attended Marquette University for both his undergraduate education and dental school.

After practicing dentistry in both Wisconsin and Colorado for close to a decade, he wanted to start his own practice. He appreciates autonomy and the ability to shape a patient experience that feels more personal, more thorough, and more centered on doing the right thing.

Dr. Patel enjoys being able to make a difference in people&#x27;s lives, whether that is through cosmetics or function. He also values the role technology plays in modern dentistry and appreciates how the right tools can support more precise, efficient care.

Patients and team members describe Dr. Patel as warm, patient, incredibly intelligent, heartfelt, genuine, caring, thorough, gentle, personable, and friendly. He is also known as a great patient educator who takes time to explain treatment options clearly and help patients feel confident in their decisions.

That focus on education and connection is a big part of what patients can expect in his care. Dr. Patel believes in detailed conversations, long-term relationships, and helping patients stay engaged in their dental health over time.

Comfort and technology designed to support a better visit

Dental Care with Amore&#x27; combines a cozy, inviting atmosphere with state-of-the-art equipment and machines. This balance helps support patient comfort while also allowing for a more modern approach to care. From the waiting area to the technology used during treatment, the goal is to make visits feel easier, more efficient, and more reassuring.

Prime Mill Intraoral Scanner

Our Prime Scan intraoral scanner creates a precise digital impression of your teeth in minutes, eliminating the need for messy traditional molds and giving our team an accurate, detailed map of your smile to work from.

CEREC Milling Unit

Our CEREC milling unit allows us to design and craft high quality ceramic restorations right here in our office, so you can walk out the same day with a permanent crown, inlay, or onlay without the wait of a traditional lab.

CBCT Cone Beam Tomography

Our CBCT cone beam scanner produces a detailed 3D image of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures, giving us a complete picture of your oral health that allows for more precise diagnoses and better treatment planning.

Digital X-ray Sensors

Our digital X-ray sensors capture detailed images of your teeth and gums instantly, with significantly less radiation than traditional X-rays, so we can spot concerns early and walk you through exactly what we&#x27;re seeing in real time.

Stain Air Polisher

Our stain air polisher gently removes surface stains and plaque using a fine mist of air, water, and powder — leaving your teeth feeling noticeably cleaner and smoother in just minutes.

Entertainment

Relax a little and enjoy your favorite show before your appointment or during a break in your restorative procedure.

Kids Play Area

Our kids play area is designed to make little ones feel right at home, with a fun and welcoming space that helps ease any nerves and keeps children comfortable while they wait.
