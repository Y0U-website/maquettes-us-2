Titanium Implant

Our Philosophy

A Dental Implant procedure is one that is being able to replace your lost tooth, not with removable dentures, but with an implant that looks and feels just like a normal, secure tooth. With dental implants from your Dental Center Group specialist, you can accomplish a tooth for a lifetime. This procedure has become a popular option in dental reconstruction, with over half a million implants placed every year in the U.S. With their natural appearance and strong fit, dental implants can be your answer to bring confidence to your smile.

Our 8k Technology We use the latest technologies and techniques to ensure that our patients receive the best possible care. From digital x-rays to 3D imaging, we have invested in the tools and equipment necessary to provide accurate diagnoses and effective treatments.

A secure, comfortable fit that feels natural, without the slipping of normal dentures No other adjacent teeth have to be ground down or disturbed to hold an implant in place You can talk and eat normally without the inconvenience You will no longer feel self-conscious about missing teeth
