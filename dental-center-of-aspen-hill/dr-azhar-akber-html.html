Get to Know Dr. Azhar Akber

Born and raised in Central Asia, Dr. Azhar Akber is passionate about having the ability to help others with their oral health while getting to see the immediate positive impact of his work. He’s quickly become an incredible asset to our team with both his skill and enthusiasm in dentistry, and he looks forward to meeting new patients whenever possible. Feel free to read more about his background and education before coming in for your visit!

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Akber attended the University of Maryland, College Park for his undergraduate degree before studying at the University of California, San Francisco for his dental doctorate. He then continued his training in general practice residency at the West Los Angeles VA Medical Center where he helped provide comprehensive dental care, including restoratives, endodontics, removables (dentures), and oral surgeries to veterans with complex medical histories.

Dr. Akber has taken numerous hours of continuing education through the Veterans Affairs Dental Services and the Academy of General Dentistry. He plans to advance his knowledge and skills with more courses so that he can continue to improve while providing patients with the best care possible. He is also an active member of the American Dental Association.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Whenever he isn’t at the office, Dr. Akber enjoys playing and watching soccer. He also likes traveling, hiking, and exploring local and national parks. Being able to visit familiar and new places is very rewarding for him, and he hopes to go to more locations in the future.
