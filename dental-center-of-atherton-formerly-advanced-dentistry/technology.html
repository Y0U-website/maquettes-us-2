Comfortable, Drill-Free Care with Solea Laser Dentistry

Solea® is a breakthrough technology that enables us to treat cavities without anesthesia, delivering a dental experience patients prefer without needles or drilling.

This laser allows for selective tissue ablation, which results in the preservation of healthy tooth structure and provides a bactericidal effect. With precise control over soft tissue procedures that are blood-free and suture-free, Solea® also enables minimal post-op discomfort and remarkably rapid healing with better outcomes than using traditional instruments.

Medical-Grade 3D Printing

Primeprint Solution offers a streamlined, fully automated 3D printing solution for creating precise, custom biocompatible applications.

This technology enables us to design and manufacture medical-grade dental appliances, such as nightguards, splints, surgical guides, and more, within an hour. It eliminates the need to send impressions to an external lab, reduces production wait times, and eliminates the need for an extra dental appointment for appliance delivery.

Preventive Care & Lifelong Oral Health

Prevention is one of the most important investments you can make in your oral health. Our advanced hygiene technologies combine evidence-based care with personalized treatment to support more comfortable cleanings, healthier gums, and long-term oral health.

More Comfortable, Precise Hygiene Experience

We’ve enhanced our professional teeth cleaning experience with Guided Biofilm Therapy (GBT), an evidence-based approach that combines advanced technology with proven clinical protocols to gently and thoroughly remove harmful biofilm. The result is a cleaner, more comfortable hygiene visit that supports healthier teeth, gums, implants, and long-term oral health.

Advanced Care for Healthier Gums

Preventive care goes beyond professional cleanings. For patients with periodontal concerns, the Picasso Clario Hygiene Laser provides a gentle, minimally invasive approach to managing harmful bacteria, supporting healthy gum tissue, and promoting long-term oral health.
