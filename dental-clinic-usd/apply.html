Dual Credit

Use the dual credit application if you are a high school student who wants to enroll in USD college courses and earn credits towards your high school diploma while starting a college degree.

We invite veterans, current military members and families of military members to view the admissions resources we have designed for you. Learn more about the admissions process for military and veteran students, and then apply using one of the applications above.
