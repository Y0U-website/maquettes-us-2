Welcome to the Department of Dental Hygiene

Department Opportunities

USD is home to more than 180 student organizations, including a student chapter of the American Dental Hygienists’ Association (ADHA) housed in the Department of Dental Hygiene and the USD Dental Club. Through your involvement outside the classroom, you’ll be able to find your community, network with other professionals and experience personal and professional growth.

A unique part of your USD education is access to undergraduate research opportunities. As a dental hygiene student at USD, you will have the chance to explore your interests and conduct innovative research with the support of professors who care about your success. Grants and travel funding are available through the Office of Research and Sponsored Programs.

Our department offers several in-person and at-home courses throughout the year for dental professionals.

Mentorship and Advising

In the Department of Dental Hygiene, our faculty, staff and students work closely together to help students identify their career paths and achieve their goals. Our class sizes are small, allowing students and faculty members the opportunity to pursue mentorship connections and work together on research projects.

USD is home to the only comprehensive health sciences school and medical school in the state. This advantage offers our students the unique opportunity to learn and train alongside peers in a variety of disciplines, equipping them with the collaborative experiences they need to be effective members of their future patients’ care teams.

We are also proud to have strong relationships with the region’s leading health care providers. These partnerships offer our students invaluable opportunities for developing professional connections, getting hands-on experiences and serving their communities early in their education.
