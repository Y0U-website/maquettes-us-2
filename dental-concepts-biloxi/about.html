MEET THE DOCTOR

DR. KIONO BARNES, DMD

Experience

With 16 years of experience, Kiono Barnes has been redefining what it means to have an amazing and healthy smile.

Schooling

Dr. Barnes graduated from Biloxi High School. He earned a bachelors degree in science at Florida A & M University. He then went on to earn a Doctor of Medical Dentistry degree at the University of Florida College of Dentistry.

Local

Dr. Kiono L Barnes was born in Lansing, MI. He was raised in Diberville on the Mississippi Gulf Coast.

Practice

Dr. Barnes completed a postgraduate general practice residency at the Biloxi VA in 2005. After being displaced by Hurricane Katrina, he managed a practice in Atlanta, GA for one year. In 2006 he relocated back to the coast where he practiced as a contract general dentist for a year at Keesler Air Force Base. In early 2007 he began Dental Concepts, where he has been practicing ever since.
