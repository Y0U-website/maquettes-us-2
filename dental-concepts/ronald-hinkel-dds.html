Mansimran Sandhu

Dr Sandhu started her dental career in Dentistry in 2010 after completing Bachelor’s in Dental Surgery from India. She got her degree in Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from University of Illinois Chicago (UIC). She also completed the Advanced Clinical Training Program in Endodontics from UCLA. She is originally from India but has found Indiana to be her new home, as she and her family love the people and Midwestern values.

Dr Sandhu practices all aspects of dentistry and believes in providing the highest quality care, which is not just limited to teeth. She is very active in updating herself with new technology, techniques by staying up to date with Continuing Education and implementing them into the practice. She feels very blessed and proud of her experienced, caring and well-trained staff who help to make patients at ease and make their visit comfortable.

She lives in Lafayette with her husband and 2-year-old son. She enjoys dancing, traveling, cooking, and spending time with her family.
