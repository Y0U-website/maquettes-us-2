Dental Implants

A dental implant is used to support one or more false teeth. It is a titanium screw that can replace the root of a tooth when it fails. Just like a tooth root, it is placed into the jawbone.Titanium is a metal whose main characteristic is its ability to osseointegrate with the bone, i.e., accept this tissue permanently.

Implants are a safe, well-established treatment. It s probably true to say that implants, much like natural teeth, will last for as long as you care for them.

How to Care for Your Implants

How well you look after your implants - and whether you go for your regular maintenance appointments - will have the biggest impact on how long they will last. If you don t look after your implants, they will develop a coating similar to what you get on neglected natural teeth. Left untreated, this can lead to gum infection, bleeding, soreness, and general discomfort. You could get all these problems with natural teeth as well.

If your implants are well looked after, and if the bone they are fitted to is strong and healthy, you can expect them to last for many years. However, just as with other surgical implants (such as a hip replacement), there is no lifetime guarantee.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Natural look, feel, and function
  • Long-lasting solution for missing teeth
  • Maintain natural facial features
  • Improved oral health
