Emergency Dental Care

  • Chipped, cracked, or broken teeth: Damaged teeth need immediate care to prevent further breakage and restore the tooth’s structure.
  • Knocked-out teeth: If a tooth has been knocked out due to trauma, acting quickly can increase the chances of saving it.
  • Lost fillings or crowns: A missing filling or crown can lead to discomfort and further damage if not addressed immediately.
  • Infections or abscesses: Infections in the gums or tooth roots can cause serious health issues and need urgent attention.

Emergency Dental Services We Offer

At Dental Defenders, we are equipped to handle a wide range of dental emergencies quickly and efficiently. Some of the emergency services we provide include:

Severe tooth pain is one of the most common dental emergencies. We’ll diagnose the underlying cause of the pain and provide immediate relief through treatments such as fillings, root canals, or extractions, if necessary.

If you’ve chipped, cracked, or broken a tooth, our dentists can quickly repair the damage using methods such as bonding, veneers, or crowns. Prompt treatment can prevent further breakage and restore the function and appearance of your tooth.

If you’ve knocked out a tooth, it’s essential to seek treatment as soon as possible. In many cases, we can reinsert the tooth and secure it back into place if you act quickly. Be sure to bring the tooth with you, and avoid touching the root.

Dental infections and abscesses can cause significant pain and swelling. Left untreated, they can spread and lead to more serious health problems. We provide antibiotics, drain abscesses, and perform root canals to treat infections.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) About Emergency Dental Care

  • 1. What should I do if I knock out a tooth? If possible, locate the tooth, hold it by the crown (the part that’s usually visible), and rinse it with water. Place the tooth back into the socket, if you can, and bite down gently on a clean cloth. If reinsertion isn’t possible, store the tooth in milk or saliva and come to our office immediately.
  • 2. How do I know if my toothache is a dental emergency? If your toothache is severe, persistent, and accompanied by swelling, fever, or a bad taste in your mouth, it’s likely a dental emergency. Contact us as soon as possible for an evaluation.
  • 3. Can Medicaid cover emergency dental treatments? The cost of teeth whitening at Dental Defenders depends on the type of treatment you choose (in-office or take-home kits). We offer competitive pricing, and our team will provide a personalized quote during your consultation.
  • 4. What should I do if I lose a filling or crown? A lost filling or crown can expose sensitive parts of your tooth, causing pain and making the tooth more vulnerable to damage. Call us immediately for a replacement, and in the meantime, avoid chewing on that side of your mouth.
  • 5. How can I prevent dental emergencies? You can reduce the risk of dental emergencies by practicing good oral hygiene, avoiding hard or sticky foods, wearing a mouthguard during sports, and visiting us regularly for checkups.
