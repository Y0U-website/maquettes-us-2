Our In-Office Whitening Process:

  • Consultation: We begin with a comprehensive consultation to discuss your goals and assess your dental health.
  • Preparation: Your gums and surrounding areas are protected to ensure the whitening gel only affects your teeth.
  • Application: A professional-strength whitening gel is applied to your teeth, and an advanced LED light may be used to activate and enhance the whitening process.

Is Teeth Whitening Right for You?

Teeth whitening is a highly effective treatment for most people, but it may not be suitable for everyone. For instance, if you have severe tooth sensitivity, gum disease, or existing dental restorations (such as crowns or veneers), our dentists will evaluate your oral health and recommend the best course of action.

Who Can Benefit from Teeth Whitening?

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) About Teeth Whitening

  • 1. How long do the results of professional teeth whitening last? The longevity of teeth whitening results depends on your lifestyle habits and oral hygiene routine. Typically, professional teeth whitening can last anywhere from 6 months to 2 years. Avoiding staining foods and drinks, practicing good oral hygiene, and scheduling regular dental cleanings can help maintain your results.
  • 2. Is teeth whitening safe for everyone? Yes, our professional teeth whitening treatments are safe and effective for most patients. However, if you have sensitive teeth or certain dental conditions, our team will customize the treatment to ensure your comfort and safety.
  • 3. How much does teeth whitening cost at Dental Defenders in Lewisville? The cost of teeth whitening at Dental Defenders depends on the type of treatment you choose (in-office or take-home kits). We offer competitive pricing, and our team will provide a personalized quote during your consultation.
  • 4. Can I whiten my teeth if I have fillings or crowns? Teeth whitening only works on natural teeth. If you have fillings, crowns, or veneers, these will not be affected by the whitening process. Our Lewisville dentist can discuss other options to ensure your smile is evenly brightened.
  • 5. Will teeth whitening be covered by Medicaid? Medicaid generally does not cover cosmetic dental treatments such as teeth whitening. However, our team will work with you to discuss affordable payment options or financing plans to make your whitening treatment accessible.
