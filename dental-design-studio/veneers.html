Am I a Candidate for Veneers?

Dr. Whalen will begin with an in-depth consultation and evaluation of your teeth, gums, and jaw. We will utilize X-rays and advanced imaging technology to get a perfect picture of your teeth, which allows us to determine whether you are a good Candidate for dental veneers. As a general rule, your teeth will have to be structurally healthy to accommodate them safely. If your tooth is too damaged, we may recommend a crown or a dental implant with a customized crown instead.

If Dr. Whalen determines veneers are a good fit, we will continue on to designing your smile. We use an advanced smile design system which allows us along with our superior dental lab to create a mock up of your final smile. Taking into account your skin tone, facial symmetry and size of your facial features a talented ceramic artist will design and handcraft your custom porcelain veneers. Placement usually requires just one visit. Your new shapelier, stain-resistant smile will allow you to enjoy life without worrying about your image! Your dream smile is only 3 appointments away.
