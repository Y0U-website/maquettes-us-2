Meet Your Dentist

A Skilled and Compassionate Professional Committed to Your Optimal Dental Health

Dr. Michael Reyes

Background

Dr. Reyes completed his undergraduate studies at the University of Illinois in Chicago and later earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) at the University of Illinois Chicago College of Dentistry. As a young child, Dr. Reyes enjoyed visiting the dentist, which inspired him to become a dentist himself.

Dental Style

Dr. Reyes values building relationships with his patients and works with them on treatment plans, so patients have a solid understanding of options and are comfortable with their care. His clinical philosophy emphasizes early prevention with proper oral hygiene and regular maintenance to avoid dental health problems.

Fun Facts

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Reyes enjoys spending time with his wife and three children. He is also a big sports fan who loves watching and attending games, especially the Chicago Cubs, Chicago Bulls, and Denver Broncos. In addition, he enjoys playing board games, attending concerts, and playing tennis
