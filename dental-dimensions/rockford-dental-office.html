What Sets Our Rockford Dental Office Apart?

We know what it’s like to sit in a dental chair, and we are committed to making every visit to our dental office a comfortable, stress-free experience. When you visit our practice, have peace of mind knowing our passion resides in your smile and ensuring you reach a state of optimum dental health. Have questions about your teeth? Dental Dimensions is a general dental practice located in Rockford, Illinois. Our dental office specializes in comprehensive preventative dentistry, restorative services, surgical procedures, and cosmetic dentistry. We believe that making your smile the best it can be, will go a long way to improving your quality of life. A beautiful smile builds confidence, and healthy teeth and gums improve your overall health. It is our goal to be your partner in achieving both! Change your smile…change your life! Meet Your Dentist

Skilled and Compassionate Professional Committed to Your Optimal Dental Health

Dr. Maria Hamidani

Background

Dr. Hamidani is an active member of the Illinois State Dental Society, Academy of General Dentistry, American Dental Association, and the Chicago Dental Society. She is committed to continuing education in order to provide her patients with state-of-the-art technology, procedures, and materials in dentistry. She also enjoys attending the Chicago Dental Society’s Midwinter Meeting every year – this is a conference dedicated to networking with dental professionals from over 16 different countries and learning about the latest professional products and instruments in dentistry.

Fun Facts

When not practicing dentistry, she enjoys hiking, traveling, and spending quality time with her family. She has been married to her husband, Azhar for 10 years, and they are proud parents to two amazing little boys, Hamid and Hasin. You can also find her volunteering at local dental events, as well as abroad for Mission Trips.
