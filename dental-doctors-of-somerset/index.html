The practice

Our commitment to you and your family’s well-being is reflected in our comprehensive general and family dental services in Glastonbury, Connecticut. Whether you are seeking routine check-ups or more specialized treatments, our experienced dentists and team are here to ensure your smile remains vibrant and healthy.

Our dentists and team are dedicated to enhancing both the functionality and aesthetics of your smile through advanced restorative dental techniques.

Cutting-edge dental technology meets compassionate care at Dental Doctors of Somerset. We understand that your oral health is of utmost importance, and we are committed to providing you with the best possible treatment experience.

We are committed to providing high-quality dental care in a warm, welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable, respected and valued. Through honest communication , we build long lasting relationships and empower patients to make informed decisions about their oral health.

A brighter smile is one of the simplest ways to refresh your appearance and your confidence. We offer professional teeth whitening that removes years of staining from coffee, tea, and everyday life, delivering noticeably brighter results in a single comfortable visit.

Our cone beam 3D imaging gives Dr. Fernandez and our team a level of diagnostic detail that traditional X-rays can’t match. The result is more accurate treatment planning, fewer surprises, and a safer, more efficient experience for you.

At Dental Doctors of Somerset, we take your care seriously from the moment you walk in. Our team is focused on making your visit as comfortable as possible so you leave feeling informed and well cared for. Get to know Dr. Fernandez, Dr. Hyman, and the rest of our team before your first visit.
