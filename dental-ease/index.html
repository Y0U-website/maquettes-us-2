The practice

Past 🛒 Trader Joe’s and 🏦 Citibank on the intersection of Broadway and Meadow Glen

D ental Ease was started by Dr. Sahar Jabr over ten years ago, with a mission to deliver high-quality patient centered dentistry with patient comfort being the highest priority.

Joined by Dr. Joshua Ng, Dental Ease combines the latest in dental technology, from digital x-rays which utilize 90 % less radiation, to intra-oral cameras and patient education systems that allow the patient to fully understand all dental procedures.

Les soins

In addition to providing general, implant and cosmetic dentistry, Dental Ease is fortunate to be joined by a talented group of in-house specialists, whose services range from root canal treatment and oral surgery to full-mouth reconstruction.
