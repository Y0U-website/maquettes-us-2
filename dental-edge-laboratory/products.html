Partial, Denture Repair & Reline

Partial, Denture Repair & Reline

A broken denture or partial can cause physical and mental discomfort, while also being a major inconvenience in your life. They may become damaged in a variety of ways including impact, biting a hard object, natural wear and tear and more. When you have a damaged denture or partial, you need immediate care to prevent further damage. We are capable of restoring your denture or partial and if it cannot be completely fixed, we will advise you of the condition and will recommend steps needed in preventing further breakage and, if necessary, getting a new denture or partial created.
