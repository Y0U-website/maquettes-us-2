What Is Routine Exam & Cleaning?

A routine visit includes a comprehensive examination of your teeth, gums, and overall oral health, a professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar, digital X-rays if needed, and a personalized at-home care plan. We recommend a checkup every six months for most patients.

Key Benefits

  • Catches cavities, gum disease, and oral issues early
  • Removes hardened plaque (tartar) that brushing alone can’t
  • Brighter, fresher-feeling teeth after every visit
  • Personalized advice on brushing, flossing, and diet
  • Includes oral cancer screening as part of every exam
