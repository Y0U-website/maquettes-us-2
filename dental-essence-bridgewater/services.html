What We Offer

Everything Your Smile Needs — Under One Roof.

Emergency Dentistry Broken tooth? Sudden pain? We offer same-day emergency appointments to get you out of discomfort fast. Call (774) 251-9113 anytime.

Routine Exam & Cleaning Comprehensive exams, professional cleanings, and X-rays to keep your smile healthy year-round. Recommended every six months for optimal oral health.

Dental Fillings (Composite) Modern tooth-colored composite fillings restore cavities discreetly. No silver, no metallic look — virtually invisible.

Root Canal Treatment Modern root canals are nothing like the horror stories — most feel no different than a filling. Save your natural tooth and eliminate pain in one visit.

Tooth Extraction Gentle, comfortable tooth removal when a tooth can’t be saved. We prioritize your comfort with modern anesthesia and clear aftercare guidance.

Complete Dentures

Comfortable, natural-looking complete dentures crafted to fit your mouth precisely — restoring your smile and confidence.

Dental Crowns Protect and strengthen damaged teeth with custom porcelain crowns designed to match your natural smile — usually done in two visits.

Invisalign for Adults

Straighten your teeth discreetly with Invisalign clear aligners. Dr. Sopariwala is a Certified Invisalign Provider and offers complimentary consultations.

Single Tooth Dental Implant Replace a single missing tooth with a permanent, natural-feeling dental implant. Implants preserve your jaw bone and never need replacement.

White Spot Lesion Treatment Remove those chalky white marks on your teeth (often left after braces) with modern minimally-invasive treatments — no drilling required.

Preventive Dentistry Regular exams, cleanings, and screenings that stop dental problems before they start. Prevention is the most affordable and comfortable dentistry there is.

Bruxism Treatment / Night Guards Grinding your teeth at night? Custom-fit night guards protect enamel and relieve jaw tension while you sleep.

Dental Bridges Bridge the gap left by missing teeth with a durable, natural-looking dental bridge that blends with your existing teeth.

Dental Veneers Transform stained, chipped, or uneven teeth with custom porcelain veneers. A minimally invasive way to achieve the smile you’ve always wanted.

Fluoride Treatment Strengthen enamel and prevent cavities with a professional fluoride treatment — a simple, effective step during your routine cleaning.

Gum Reconstruction Restore healthy gum tissue after gum disease or recession. Dr. Sopariwala uses gentle, modern techniques to rebuild your gum line and protect your teeth.

Implant-Supported Dentures A stable, life-changing alternative to traditional dentures. Implant-supported dentures snap into place — no slipping, no adhesives, and they feel like natural teeth.

Removable Partial Dentures Replace multiple missing teeth with comfortable, natural-looking partial dentures. A cost-effective solution when implants aren’t the right fit.

Retainers Custom retainers that keep your teeth straight after Invisalign or braces. Wearing them nightly protects your investment for years to come.

Scaling & Root Planing (Deep Cleaning) A specialized deep cleaning that treats early gum disease by removing plaque and bacteria below the gum line. Often prevents the need for more serious periodontal work.

Teeth Whitening Brighten your smile by several shades with our professional whitening treatments — safer, faster, and more effective than any store-bought kit.

Not Sure Which Service You Need?

Schedule a consultation and Dr. Sopariwala will guide you through your options — no pressure, just honest advice.
