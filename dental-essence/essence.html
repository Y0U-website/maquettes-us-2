Essence

“ESSENCE” is the intrinsic nature or indispensable quality of something, especially something abstract, that determines its character. We take this to heart and adopted it as our name. We know that your impression is the sum of all the details you encounter here, both visible and intangible.

Your experience at Dental Essence is our highest priority. First, we’ve built our team to ensure every interaction with us makes you feel right at home. Additionally, we have recently completed a massive renovation in hopes of creating a unique medical environment that you probably don’t expect and may have never felt before.

Our office combines a spacious, open airy feel with modern design and visual delight, featuring artwork from Franklin Arts . Beyond the visual sensations, we are ready to charm your ears with your choice of audio, so make sure to bring your headphones. So far, our patients have loved the new look and the feeling you get inside Dental Essence. We look forward to you experiencing it yourself.

From the moment you walk in to the second you exit our doors, we want you to feel right at home. We want to make sure you look forward to your next visit just as much as we do.
