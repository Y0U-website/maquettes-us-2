Meet Our Team

Whether you’re on the phone or visiting us, our team focuses on you and your individual needs. We take pride in offering the best service and care, and we are committed to make you feel welcome-- right at home with us.

Meaghan A. Neuberger, D.D.S.

If Meaghan could have any super power she’d want it to be X-Ray vision. This would certainly make her job easier. If you find her doing something she likes, it’s probably eating Phish Food ice cream or perfecting her next craft project. If there’s one thing you won’t find her doing, it’s talking in front of a large group of people.

She would like to (strongly) encourage you to always use your blinker and put your garbage in the proper receptacle.

Meaghan’s latest obsession is military novels and movies and has immense admiration for those who serve our country. If you see her moving her hands wildly (hopefully without grasping sharp instruments) it is because she is learning American Sign Language.

Dr. Neuberger earned her Bachelors Degree at the University of South Dakota and her D.D.S. at the University of Nebraska Medical Center in 2007.

She is a member of the American Dental Association, the South Dakota Dental Association, and the Southeastern District Dental Society.

Stephanie Schmitz, D.D.S.

"FORE!" is something you’ll never hear from Dr. Stephanie’s mouth. Her soaring, straight-down-the-fairway tee shot is the result from years of practice playing for the golf team at Kansas State.

Dr. Stephanie served at Falls Community Health in Sioux Falls for the past nine years, most recently as Dental Director. She received her D.D.S. from the University of Nebraska Medical Center, just like Dr. Meaghan. You’ll find them reminiscing about the good ol’ times in Huskerland. When the Huskers play Kansas State, however, that’s when things get a little bit awkward around the office.

She mentioned her obsession with "European style board games." What, no Monopoly or Candyland? I have no idea what this "style" of board game means. If you really want to know this mystery, ask her.

What does Dr. Stephanie take the most pride in? "Treating people fairly and kindly." I think she’s going to fit right in around here. Minus the dancing in the hall part. That will take a little getting used to.

We take responsibility to improve your oral health and well being. We will fully explain all treatment options and will always help set and achieve your goals. We can’t wait to see you!
