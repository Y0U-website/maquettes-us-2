Questions about Orthodontics? We’ve Got Answers

  • How long does orthodontic treatment take? Everyone’s different, but most people start seeing results in a few months. Dr. Esposito will lay out your timeline clearly, so you know exactly what to expect.
  • How do I know if I need braces or clear aligners? Both aligners and braces offer a solution to give you a straighter, healthier, and more beautiful smile. Braces and each kind of clear aligner have advantages for different needs, and a consultation with our team will help you understand what is best for you.
  • Will braces or clear aligners hurt? Some pressure is normal as your teeth move, but most people don’t find it painful. And if you do, just give us a shout – we’ll make adjustments as needed.
  • Can I afford clear aligners? With flexible payment options and our team ready to walk you through costs, we’ll make sure you get quality care without the financial headache.
  • What if I forget to wear my aligners? It happens! Missing a day here and there is okay. Just get back on track when you remember, and keep up with regular check-ins to stay on course.
  • Are clear aligners as effective as traditional braces? Clear aligners like Invisalign® and SureSmile can be just as effective for mild to moderate alignment issues. For more complex cases, traditional braces might be a better choice. We’ll help you decide what’s best for your needs.
  • Are there age limits for orthodontic treatment? No! Orthodontics can be effective at almost any age, and many adults choose braces or aligners to improve their smile. We offer options for children, teens, and adults.
