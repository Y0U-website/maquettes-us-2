The team behind Dental Experts of Florida

Dr. Zakariya Salhan

Dr. Zakariya Salhan earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry, the world’s first dental school, and completed advanced residency training through the VA Palo Alto Health Care System in affiliation with Stanford Hospital.With a passion for implant and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Salhan is committed to lifelong learning and regularly pursues advanced continuing education to stay at the forefront of modern dental techniques. He is dedicated to providing personalized, compassionate care and helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles.

Dr. Adil Ibrahim

Dr. Adil Ibrahim is specialized in full mouth reconstructions, crowns and bridges, root canals, veneers, dentures, extractions, and composite fillings. Born and raised in Florida, most of his childhood was spent in Tampa. He attended the University of South Florida, where he graduated with honors and received a Bachelor of Science in Biomedical Sciences. He attained his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry. He is a member of the American Dental Association and a volunteer at the Red Crescent Clinic in Tampa.

Dr. Waleed Ibrahim

Dr. Ibrahim was born and raised in Florida, where he spent most of his time in Tampa, FL. Dr. Hamed El-Jabali

Dr. Hamed El-Jabali, a Tampa-raised dentist with an educational background from the University of South Florida and the University of New England, combines his expertise in implantology and Botox certification with a passion for outdoor activities, making him a distinguished and personable leader in dental care.

Frequently asked questions

Got questions? We&#x27;ve got answers. Explore our FAQs to find helpful information about our services, procedures, and what to expect at Dental Experts of Florida.

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your initial appointment will typically involve a thorough examination, dental cleaning, and a discussion about your oral health history and goals. We ensure each step is comfortable and fully explained.

What options do I have for improving the appearance of my teeth?

We offer a range of cosmetic services including teeth whitening, veneers, and Invisalign®. During your consultation, we’ll discuss the best options to achieve your desired results.

How do you handle dental emergencies?

For dental emergencies, we strive to provide prompt care. If you&#x27;re experiencing severe pain, swelling, or have suffered dental trauma, please contact us immediately for guidance and to arrange an urgent visit.
