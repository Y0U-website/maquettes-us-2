A Team You Can Trust

Each member of our team is dedicated to providing attentive care, clear communication, and a positive dental experience for every patient.

Dr. Sonal Bhoot, DMD

Dr. Sonal Bhoot, DMD is the owner dentist at Dental Expressions and brings over 20 years of experience providing comprehensive, patient-centered dental care in Lee’s Summit and surrounding communities. She earned her Doctorate in Dental Medicine from the New Jersey Dental School in 2003 and is committed to delivering high-quality care for patients of all ages.

Dr. Bhoot’s clinical expertise includes cosmetic dentistry, CEREC same-day restorations, Invisalign, endodontics, oral surgery, TMJ treatment, restorative dentistry, and laser-assisted procedures. She is deeply committed to continuing education and stays current with modern techniques to ensure safe, effective, and comfortable care.

An active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), Kansas Dental Association, Missouri Dental Association, and the Lee’s Summit Chamber of Commerce, Dr. Bhoot is also involved in local study clubs and community initiatives that support dental education and access to care.
