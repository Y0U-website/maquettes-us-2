Premium Aligners: Invisalign® & ClearCorrect®

We offer the world’s two most prestigious systems for discreetly straightening your teeth. Both are doctor-led, meaning every stage of your treatment is supervised by a DFC specialist.

Invisalign® | The Global Standard

As the most recognized name in aligner , Invisalign uses proprietary SmartTrack® material to provide highly predictable tooth movements. This system is world-renowned for its comfortable, nearly invisible fit and its ability to handle a wide range of complex dental alignments with clinically proven success.

ClearCorrect® | Medical Engineering

ClearCorrect aligners are engineered for maximum clarity and superior stain resistance. This medical-grade system offers a high-performance alternative for aligner therapy , focusing on a tight, secure fit that provides the constant pressure needed for efficient results.

Why Choose Specialist-Led Orthodontics?

When you choose DFC for your aligners, you aren’t just buying plastic trays; you are investing in a specialist’s expertise. We ensure your journey is safe, predictable, and medically sound through four key pillars:

Precision Bite Correction

We don’t just straighten the front teeth for aesthetics; we ensure your upper and lower jaws fit together in perfect "functional harmony." This prevents long-term issues like uneven tooth wear and chronic jaw pain (TMJ).

Bone Health & Root Preservation

Moving teeth is a biological process that involves remodeling bone. Our specialists use advanced imaging to monitor your roots, ensuring teeth move safely without causing recession, instability, or permanent root damage.

Advanced Custom Attachments

Unlike "one-size-fits-all" home kits, we use discreet, tooth-colored attachments. these act as "anchors" that allow our Invisalign and ClearCorrect systems to achieve complex movements that basic aligners simply cannot perform.

Continuous Specialist Supervision

Your treatment is never on "autopilot." Every stage of your progress is physically verified by our doctors, allowing us to make real-time adjustments to your plan, ensuring the most efficient and stable result possible.
