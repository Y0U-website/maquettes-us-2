Our Advanced Implant Solutions

We tailor our restoration techniques to your specific needs, ensuring a seamless aesthetic and functional result.

Single Tooth Replacements

The gold standard for missing individual teeth. We use high-precision implants that mimic the natural root structure.

The Result: Seamless integration with your natural teeth and preservation of the surrounding bone and facial structure.

All-on-4® Total Restoration

A life-changing, non-removable solution for patients missing an entire arch of teeth.

Maximum Stability: A full set of teeth supported by only four strategically placed implants.

Immediate Results: Often referred to as Teeth in a Day, this procedure provides a fixed, functional smile with minimal recovery time.

Implant-Supported Overdentures

Eliminate the frustration, social anxiety, and discomfort of loose dentures.

The Fix: We anchor your dentures directly to implants.

The Benefit: Perfect fit, zero slipping, and the ability to eat and speak with total confidence.

Why Choose Specialist-Led Implantology?

When you choose DFC for your dental implants, you aren’t just receiving a prosthetic; you are investing in a sophisticated surgical procedure. We ensure your restoration is permanent, safe, and biocompatible through four key pillars:

Elite Bio-Material Selection

We strictly reject low-cost, generic implants that risk rejection or bone loss. We use only the world’s highest-grade, titanium or zirconia systems, ensuring 99 % osseointegration success and a lifetime of biological compatibility.

Prosthetic & Functional Harmony

An implant must do more than look good; it must function perfectly with your bite. Our specialists design your crown to distribute chewing forces evenly, protecting your jaw joint (TMJ) and preventing damage to surrounding natural teeth.

3D Guided Surgical Precision

Using advanced CT scans and digital planning, we map your bone density and nerve locations before the first incision. This "Guided Surgery" approach allows for sub-millimeter accuracy, faster healing, and virtually no margin for error.

Board-Certified Clinical Protocols

Every implant procedure follows the strict safety and sterilization protocols of our Medical Board. From the initial bone assessment to the final restoration, your care is managed by experts who specialize in complex oral reconstruction.
