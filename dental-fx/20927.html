Our Commitment to Patient Comfort

Dr. Judy Yu is a compassionate and gentle dentist who puts patients at ease. For those who need a little more reassurance, we offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) to help you get the dental care you need despite any anxiety. Also, ask a member of our team about valium-based dentistry in Pasadena.

However, we want your entire dental visit to be memorable and relaxing, which is why we offer a movie lounge. This theater includes reclining seats for your viewing pleasure.

We also offer umbrella services to your car in the event of rain or snow.

Whether you’re looking for a heated towel, a cozy blanket, or even a neck pillow, we have you covered.

And don’t forget to enjoy a refreshment. Generally, we offer bottled water and juice.
