Our Accessibility Statement

If you are looking for mouse and keyboard alternatives, speech recognition software such as Dragon Naturally Speaking may help you navigate web pages and online services. This software allows the user to move focus around a web page or application screen through voice controls.

We are committed to ensuring we meet all of your needs. If you require assistance with any of the content on our website, please call our office at (203) 235-5588 and we will do everything in our power to assist you. Thank you!

  • Our Accessibility Statement - Dental Group of Meriden-Wallingford Accessibility Statement We are continuously working to improve the accessibility of content on our website. Below, you’ll find a few recommendations to help make your browsing experience more accessible: If you have trouble seeing web pages, the US Social Security Administration offers these tips for optimizing your computer and browser to improve your online experience. Use your computer to read web pages out loud
  • Use the keyboard to navigate screens
  • Increase text size
  • Magnify your screen
  • Change background and text colors
  • Make your mouse pointer more visible (Windows only)
