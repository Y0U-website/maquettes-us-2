Regular dental visits are only one small part of maintaining healthy teeth. What you do at home between these visits is more important. Good oral health also includes:

● Brushing twice daily ● Daily flossing ● Healthy dietary habits ● Routine professional cleanings ● Early treatment of dental problems

Preventive care is not only the healthiest approach - it’s often the most affordable. Catching problems early can help preserve more natural tooth structure and reduce the need for more extensive treatment in the future.

Most patients should have a dental exam every six months. Some patients with a higher risk of dental disease may benefit from more frequent visits based on the doctor’s recommendation.

  • How often should I have my teeth cleaned? For most patients, a professional cleaning every six months is recommended. If you have periodontal (gum) disease or other risk factors, more frequent cleanings may be needed to maintain your oral health.
  • Do I need X-rays at every visit? Not usually. We only take X-rays when they are clinically necessary to diagnose or monitor your oral health. If you have recent diagnostic-quality X-rays from another office, we may be able to use those instead.
  • At what age should children first see a dentist? The American Dental Association recommends a child’s first dental visit by their first birthday or within six months of the first tooth erupting. Early visits help establish healthy habits and monitor development.
  • What happens during a comprehensive exam? Your comprehensive exam includes a thorough evaluation of your teeth, gums, bite, existing dental work, and overall oral health. If needed, diagnostic X-rays are taken, and Dr. Matos will review the findings, discuss your options, and answer any questions.
  • What is the difference between a routine cleaning and a deep cleaning? A routine cleaning is for patients with healthy gums and focuses on removing plaque and tartar above the gumline. A deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) is recommended when gum disease is present and removes bacteria and buildup below the gumline to help restore gum health.
