Dr. Gehring

Retired Dentist

After many years of dedicated service to our patients and community, Dr. Timothy Gehring officially retired from clinical practice in September 2025 . Dr. Gehring is deeply grateful for the trust and relationships built over the years and is confident in the continued care of his patients.

Dr. Gehring has entrusted the future of the practice to Dr. Tanner Gustafson and Dr. Scott Schmidt , whose clinical excellence, integrity, and patient-centered philosophy reflect the same values that have always guided this office.
