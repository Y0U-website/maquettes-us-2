The All-On-4® Dental Implant Procedure at Dental Health Boutique

Our experienced team will guide you through every step of the All-On-4® process:

  • Free Consultation: Discuss your goals and determine if All-On-4® is right for you.
  • Planning and Preparation: We’ll create a personalized treatment plan using advanced technology.
  • All-On-4® Procedure Step-by-Step: Day 1: Implant Placement Prepare your mouth for implants (tooth removal, cleaning, jawbone preparation)
  • Place four new implants
  • Stitch the wounds with dissolvable stitches
  • Administer additional anesthetic for post-operative comfort
  • Day 2: Attaching the Bridge Test the placement and screw the bridge into place
  • Check bite and communication for adjustments
  • Day 3: Permanent Attachment Secure your permanent prosthetic teeth
  • Simple appointment with no adjustments needed
