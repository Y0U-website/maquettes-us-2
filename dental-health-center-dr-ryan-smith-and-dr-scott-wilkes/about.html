Dr. Ryan Smith

Dr. Ryan Smith is a family dentist who grew up in Rexburg, Idaho, and has a love for dentistry and farming. He earned his bachelor’s degree at Utah State University and then continued to the University of Kentucky for dental school. Dr. Smith loves helping patients of all ages and needs. He is known to have a gentle touch and a caring demeanor with all he encounters.

Read More

His expertise extends to various dental procedures, with a particular fondness for the unique challenges presented by implant dentistry. Proficient in areas such as root canals, bridges, wisdom tooth removal, and orthodontics, Dr. Smith is dedicated to staying abreast of the latest advancements in the field. He holds certification in I.V. sedation and is trained in administering botox and fillers, contributing to his patient’s overall well-being.

Beyond his role at the Dental Health Center in Rexburg, Dr. Smith collaborates with his father Doug at West Yellowstone Dentistry in Montana, enhancing dental care accessibility for the community. Balancing his professional commitments, he cherishes quality time with his wife Kristi and their four children. An enthusiast of snowmobiling, ATV riding, gardening, and video games, Dr. Smith embodies a multifaceted approach to life both inside and outside the dental practice.
