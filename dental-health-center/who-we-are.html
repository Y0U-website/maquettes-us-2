We Are Dental Health Centers

We are the dental office with the "family-feel" you have been looking for! We have 4 locations to provide convenient, comfortable, and high-quality dental care. Visit our main office in Eau Claire or one of our other locations in Cadott, Stanley, or Thorp!

Dr. John Tessendorf started Dental Health Center in 1979. He has since expanded his practice to other towns throughout the Chippewa Valley, each clinic offering a close-knit, small-town feel! We provide general dentistry for all ages, including composite fillings, crowns, implants, surgical and non-surgical periodontics, root canal treatment, extractions, oral conscious sedation, orthodontics (traditional and Invisalign), and TMD treatment. Our Eau Claire clinic also offers oral appliance therapy for Sleep Apnea.

We are in-network with most insurance plans and we also offer cash and senior discounts for those without insurance. Emergencies services are available and NEW PATIENT S of all ages are welcome!
