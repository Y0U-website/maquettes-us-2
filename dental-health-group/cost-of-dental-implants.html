The Dental Implant Treatment Process

Initial Consultation and Planning

During your first visit, Dr. Dhanoa will conduct a detailed exam including digital imaging to assess your bone health and create a personalized treatment plan. This visit is a great opportunity to ask questions, voice any concerns (especially if you’ve had dental anxiety in the past), and learn about your financing and insurance options. Our d entist in Bonney lake wa makes your comfort our priority, offering education without pressure.

Implant Placement Surgery

Once the treatment plan is finalized, we schedule the implant placement procedure. A titanium post is precisely positioned into the jawbone under local anesthesia. This creates a stable foundation for your future crown and starts the process of osseointegration, where the implant fuses to the bone. Our office is designed for a calm experience, and our compassionate, multilingual staff ensure every patient feels at ease from the moment they walk in.

Healing and Final Restoration

After the implant has fully integrated with the bone, usually over a 3-6 month period, we complete your smile by attaching a custom-made crown or bridge. This is crafted to match your existing teeth in shape and color. Patients love the natural look and feel of their restored smiles! We take extra care to ensure a seamless fit and long-lasting comfort, supporting both function and appearance for years to come.
