The Dental Implants Treatment Process

Initial Consultation

During your first visit, Dr. Dhanoa will conduct a thorough examination of your oral health. This includes taking X-rays and discussing your medical history to determine if you’re a suitable candidate for dental implants. Dentist Bonney lake wa will also explain the procedure in detail, addressing any questions or concerns you may have.

Implant Placement

Once you’re deemed a candidate, the next step involves surgically placing the titanium implant into your jawbone. This procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia for your comfort. The implant serves as a replacement tooth root and requires time to integrate with the bone, a process known as osseointegration, which takes 3-6 months for most patients.

Crown Attachment

After the implant has successfully fused with the jawbone, an abutment will be placed onto your implant, and then a custom-made crown will be built and attached to the abutment. This crown is designed to match your natural teeth in color and shape, ensuring a harmonious and natural-looking smile. The final result is a fully restored tooth that functions and feels like a natural one.
