Meet Our Team

At Dental Health Group, our friendly team is the heart of everything we do. From our dentists to our front-office staff, every team member is dedicated to making your visit comfortable, personalized, and stress-free. As your trusted dentist in Bonney Lake, we take the time to get to know you and help you feel at ease at every appointment.

Dr. Dhanoa

Dr. Dhanoa is a general dentist with 10 years of experience providing comprehensive dental care. He earned his DDS (Doctor of Dental Surgery) from the University of Washington Dental School and focuses on preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry.

Born and raised in the South Seattle area, Dr. Dhanoa believes quality dental care begins with listening. He takes a patient-focused, judgment-free approach, taking time to understand each patient’s concerns, explain their options clearly, and build a treatment plan that fits their needs. From routine cleanings to cosmetic dentistry in Bonney Lake , his goal is to help every patient leave with better oral health and a more confident smile.

Outside the office, Dr. Dhanoa is a devoted family man who is married with one child and enjoys connecting with the local community.

Dr. Patel

Dr. Patel is a general dentist with several years of experience caring for patients of all ages. With a focus on general dentistry, she is committed to helping each patient maintain a healthy, comfortable smile.

Dr. Patel takes a gentle, patient-first approach to care. She listens closely to each patient’s concerns, explains treatment options in clear terms, and works to make every visit relaxed and stress-free. Whether you are coming in for a routine check-up or addressing a specific concern, she is here to support your oral health every step of the way.

Outside the practice, Dr. Patel is married with one child. She values building lasting relationships with her patients and is proud to be among the dentists in Bonney Lake who care for local families.

Technology & Amenities

Our boutique-style office is designed to feel calm and comfortable, with cozy seating, bright finishes, and a light, soothing scent throughout the space. We offer a beverage bar with coffee, tea, and a selection of flavored waters. Each treatment room has a 50 television, so you can view detailed images of your mouth and then relax with a pillow, blanket, and your favorite show while we care for your smile.

We’re glad to offer modern dental technology that helps make treatment more efficient and more comfortable. Our digital iTero scanner gives our dentists precise, high-definition images of your mouth that we can show you in real time, so you can see exactly what we see. Our CBCT scanner captures a full view of your mouth in seconds for accurate treatment planning. Our Picasso soft-tissue laser supports minimally invasive procedures, often with less anesthesia and more comfortable healing.
