Dental Implants

Do you dread going to the dentist? You deserve a dental experience that is relaxing and informative. Dental Health International is different. We focus on making your visit enjoyable and positive. We want you to feel comfortable asking questions and reviewing any concerns. Education is integral to dental health, so we take the time to explain our recommendations carefully. We will take care of all your dental needs, whether they involve dental implants or a simple teeth cleaning. We’ll help you gain a healthy smile that you are proud of.

Quality Dental Implants with DHI

Missing a tooth or multiple teeth can be discouraging and have an overall negative effect on your self-esteem. At Dental Health International , we want our patients to love their smiles and feel confident with their appearance. Through the use of dental implants, we can offer multiple restorative appliances that replace missing teeth.

A dental implant is a titanium piece that we can surgically place into your jawbone. Depending on the patient, a sedative can be prescribed prior to treatment, and during the procedure, we use local anesthesia, maximizing your comfort. Additionally, the doctor will send you home with appropriate medications to help ease the healing process. Below are three examples of restorations that can be attached to implants:

Single Crown Implant

One option for patients missing a single tooth is a single crown implant. Dental crowns are artificial tooth caps fabricated to fit into your smile perfectly in size, shape, and shade. Implant crowns are attached to the implant through abutments, which serve as connecting pieces that join the crown to the implants.

Bridge Implants

An Implant bridge is a row of two or more artificial teeth secured onto two or more dental implants. Implants help maintain jawbone health because of the stimulation caused by chewing. Patients who are missing two or more teeth in a row should consider bridge implants.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Dentures allow patients to regain a complete and beautiful smile if they have lost most or all their teeth. Traditional dentures are created to sit on the gum tissue, resting on the jawbone, and they can be easily dislodged. We use implants to secure dentures in the mouth to avoid slippage. The density of your jawbone will indicate how many implants will be needed on each dental arch to support the denture fully.

Benefits of Dental Implants

We surgically place implants into the jawbone as the new tooth root(s) for the chosen restoration. Not only do they provide a strong foundation for the restoration, but they also maintain the jawbone’s health through stimulation from chewing and other functions.

Dental implants are built to last a lifetime! If taken care of properly, your implant and the restoration will stay firm and steady, limiting the need for follow-ups. Keeping implants clean is essential to ensure that it lasts for many years. Brushing two or three times per day with a soft-bristle brush, flossing between all teeth and around the base of each implant, and rinsing with an antiseptic mouthwash should all become part of your daily routine. Regular dental checkups at least twice a year are vital to ensure any problems are caught early on and your implant stays healthy.
