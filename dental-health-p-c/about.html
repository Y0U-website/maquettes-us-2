About Us

General Dentistry, Oral Surgery & Cosmetic Dentistry located in BRONX, Bronx and New York, NY

Our Mission

New York Dental Health P.C. was founded in January of 2015 with one mission, to unite dental offices in our Bronx community that are committed to providing the same great experience across all of our locations.

Luckily, regardless of the youth of our name, our individual offices have been up and running in the West Bronx area for over 40 years. These locations are so embedded in this community that the majority of the staff in our offices live and work here and many of them have done so for well over a decade.

At any location of New York Dental Health P.C. you will find your neighbors and friends, and they’re ready to make sure that your dental health is at its best.

Our Values

We pride ourselves on making your experience the best that it can be. That means we aren’t picky about how you pay. We accept medicaid and nearly all insurance plans.

That means our doctors and staff will not only perform your dental care gently and kindly, but also quickly so you can get on with the rest of your day. And it also means that we can’t wait to start a relationship with you, your family, and all those teeth that could last for years.

About Us

Our New York Dental Health team provides exceptional dental care to patients of all ages in the Inwood neighborhood of Manhattan and the Morris Heights, Fordham Heights, Mount Hope, Mott Haven, and Morrisania neighborhoods in the Bronx, New York City.

Our practice was founded in 2015 with a mission: To unite dental offices in the Bronx community committed to providing superior dental experiences across all locations. Our New York Dental Health offices have provided outstanding dental care in the West Bronx area for more than 40 years. The majority of our staff have lived in the area for over a decade.

Patients can meet friends and neighbors ready to optimize their dental health at any of our New York Dental Health locations. We offer preventive care, such as teeth cleanings, sealants, and fluoride treatments, as well as extractions, dental implants, dental bridges, crowns, root canals, dentures, and other restorative dental services.

Our office also provides cosmetic dental treatments, including Invisalign , teeth whitening, and dental veneers. We perform dental care kindly, gently, and quickly so patients can resume their typical everyday activities.

Our New York Dental Health team prides itself on making each patient’s experience the best it can be, and we aren’t picky about how they pay. Our office accepts Medicaid, cash pay, and nearly all insurance plans. We look forward to maintaining long-lasting relationships with patients, their families, and their teeth that can last many decades when cared for properly.
