About Traditional Braces

Traditional metal braces are what you probably think of when you hear the word "Braces". They have changed a lot over the years.

These days, they are quite comfortable and continue to be a favorite among young patients because they are able to accessorize their metal braces with colored rubber bands.

Invisible braces , on the other hand, are made of clear materials that allow for teenagers or adults to correct their orthodontic health without the aesthetic concerns of the more traditional metal braces.

Invisalign & Clear Aligners

Invisalign is an excellent option if you’d like to address your orthodontic concerns, but would prefer not to wear metal braces. Invisible aligners are comfortable and easy to wear.

The best part about Invisalign is that it’s removable. If you have a business meeting or interview, you can easily pop it out.

As long as you wear it for the recommended amount of time per day, treatment will go on uninterrupted.
