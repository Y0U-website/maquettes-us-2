Innovative Materials and Surface Treatments

There are many types of implants available. Titanium remains popular, thanks to its high biocompatibility and good osseointegration properties. However, in recent years, other biocompatible materials have become more common.

Most recently, new surface technologies have been able to combine Zirconia and Titanium together into a new patented material that combines the strength and biocompatible properties of these materials that have resulted in higher success rates. The Dental Implant Aesthetic Center has begun using implants with this improved surface technology for all of our patients.

Digital Imaging and 3D Scanning in Implantology

Our service starts with sophisticated digital imaging and 3D scanning to determine the health of the existing teeth and bones. We’ll use the information gathered from our 3D imaging to identify the procedure and placement for the highest level of success.

CBCT Scanning

Our CBCT scanning system helps us get a detailed, 360-degree view of the jaw, nerves, gums, and soft tissue. The information we can collect through computed tomography goes far beyond that of a traditional X-ray, helping our dental team properly plan and place dental implants.

Using 3D imaging, we can check the bone density and jaw health and plan the installation to the finest detail, including predicting what sort of aftercare might be necessary during the healing process. The scan includes the sinuses, nasal cavity, and nerve canals, so you can feel confident that the prosthodontist working with you has a full overview of any nerves that might be affected by the installation of dental implants.

Our dentists and dental technicians take a patient-centered approach to care, starting with CBCT scanning. With this type of diagnostic, you’ll receive a lower dose of radiation than you would from a traditional dental scan, and you’ll get more detailed results.
