Who Should Get Dental Implants?

Dental implants are ideal for patients who are missing teeth or have teeth that need to be removed due to conditions that deem them no longer restorable. Dental Implant restorations can be used to replace a single tooth or multiple teeth. At the Dental Implant Aesthetic Center, we understand how important diagnosis and planning is when it comes to delivering the best dental implant restorations to our patients.

What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant restoration consists of 3 pieces, the implant, the abutment, and the crown/bridge. The dental implant is a device that replaces the root of a tooth. The dental implant heals to your jaw bone below your soft tissue. Once the implant has fully healed to the bone, an abutment is placed on the implant. The abutment can then be seen above the soft tissue and is used to connect a crown/bridge to the implant.
