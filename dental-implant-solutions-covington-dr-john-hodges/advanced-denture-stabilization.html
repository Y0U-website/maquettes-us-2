Snap On Dentures

For patients on a budget or who want the easiest implant option to clean, we offer Snap On Dentures, which offer greater comfort, stability, and chewing efficiency than traditional dentures. Snap On Dentures are retained by as little as two dental implants, minimizing tipping, rocking, and dislodging of your denture. Throw those adhesive pastes away! Adding more implants improves the stability of your denture even more, and if planned for properly , Snap On Dentures can be upgraded to a Fixed Full Arch Restoration in the future.
