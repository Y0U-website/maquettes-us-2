Welcome to Noza Dental! Dr. Dilnoza Sobirova is not just a highly skilled dentist; she is someone who genuinely cares about making a positive difference in her patients’ lives. With a warm, compassionate approach to dentistry, Dr. Sobirova works to ensure that every patient feels comfortable and confident in their care.

Educational Background

Dr. Sobirova earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (D.M.D.) degree from Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry in 2019. Prior to dental school, she completed a Bachelor’s Degree in Chemistry at Temple University, where she laid the groundwork for her deep understanding of the science behind dentistry.

To further enhance her skills, Dr. Sobirova completed a one-year Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at NYU Langone from 2019 to 2020. During her residency, she gained advanced training and practical experience in various aspects of general dentistry, with an emphasis on more complex cases and providing comprehensive care. This residency allowed her to further refine her expertise in preventive dental care, restorative treatments, and cosmetic procedures.

Throughout her academic career, Dr. Sobirova was dedicated to not only mastering the technical aspects of dentistry but also learning the importance of personalized patient care. Her education provided her with the foundation she needed to offer her patients the best possible treatment with a focus on holistic, long-term oral health.

Professional Experience

Dr. Sobirova has been practicing dentistry since graduating from Temple University in 2019. After completing her education, she began her professional career, where she has continued to refine her skills in general dentistry and cosmetic treatments. Her one-year residency at NYU Langone provided valuable hands-on experience, equipping her with the tools necessary to handle a wide variety of complex dental cases.

Over the years, she has developed a strong expertise in preventive dental care, root canal therapy, smile makeover treatments, and cosmetic bonding, making her a well-rounded provider capable of addressing a wide range of dental concerns.

Dr. Sobirova takes great pride in helping her patients feel at ease during their visits. Whether performing routine teeth cleanings or more advanced dental procedures, her calm and reassuring presence ensures that each patient feels heard and comfortable.

Approach to Dentistry

Dr. Sobirova’s approach to dentistry is grounded in compassion, education, and the use of advanced technology. She firmly believes that great dentistry goes beyond just treating teeth; it’s about helping patients achieve better overall health and well-being.

Her care philosophy is rooted in preventive dentistry, focusing on early detection and proactive care. She takes the time to explain each procedure thoroughly, ensuring that her patients understand their treatment options and feel confident in the care they are receiving. Her goal is always to make every patient feel comfortable, whether it’s their first visit or a follow-up appointment.

Why Dr. Sobirova Loves Dentistry

For Dr. Sobirova, dentistry is more than a profession; it’s a passion. She has always been fascinated by the intersection of science and art that dentistry provides. What excites her most about the profession is the ability to have a direct, positive impact on a patient’s life. Helping someone regain their smile or restore their confidence is what drives her to give every patient the best care possible.

Building Relationships with Patients

Dr. Sobirova takes a holistic approach to patient care. She believes that every patient deserves individualized attention and that building long-term relationships based on trust is key to success in dentistry. She listens closely to each patient’s concerns, offering treatments that are tailored to their unique needs and goals.

Many patients appreciate Dr. Sobirova’s warm and welcoming demeanor, which helps create a stress-free, comfortable environment. Whether you are visiting for a simple dental exam or a more complex smile makeover, Dr. Sobirova’s goal is always to make your experience as pleasant and reassuring as possible.

Continuing Education and Professional Development

Dr. Sobirova is committed to lifelong learning and professional development. She participates in continuing education courses and stays current with the latest research in dental hygiene and oral health. This commitment allows her to provide the highest level of care using the most advanced techniques available in the field.

In addition to her formal education, Dr. Sobirova is fluent in several languages, including Tajik, Uzbek, Russian, and basic Turkish, allowing her to communicate with a diverse group of patients and offer a more personalized care experience.
