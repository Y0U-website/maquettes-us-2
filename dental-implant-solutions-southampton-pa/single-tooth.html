The Single Tooth Implant Procedure

The single tooth implant procedure is typically performed in two stages:

The entire process typically takes several months to complete, but the result is a durable and natural-looking replacement tooth.

  • Placement of the Implant Post : In the first stage, the titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone. Over time, the bone integrates with the implant in a process called osseointegration, ensuring a stable foundation for the replacement tooth.
  • Placement of the Crown : Once the implant post has fully integrated with the jawbone, a custom crown is placed on top of the post. This crown is designed to match the size, shape, and color of your natural teeth for a seamless result.

Single Tooth Implant vs. Dental Bridge

When considering tooth replacement options, many patients ask about the difference between single tooth implants and dental bridges. Both options have their benefits, but the key difference is that dental implants are a permanent, standalone solution, while dental bridges require the support of adjacent teeth.

With a dental bridge, healthy teeth on either side of the gap must be altered to support the bridge. This can compromise the structure of your healthy teeth over time. On the other hand, single tooth implants do not affect adjacent teeth and offer a more natural, long-term solution.

Recovery After a Single Tooth Implant Procedure

After the single tooth implant procedure, you may experience some swelling or discomfort, but this is typically manageable with over-the-counter pain medication. The healing process takes time, as the implant must integrate with the jawbone. Most patients can resume normal activities within a few days, although it can take a few months for the implant to fully fuse with the bone.

Your dentist will provide specific aftercare instructions to ensure the best results, including recommendations for managing swelling and caring for the surgical site. A follow-up appointment will be scheduled to monitor your progress and ensure the implant is healing properly.
