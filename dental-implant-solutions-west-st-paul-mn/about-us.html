Meet the Editor

Daniel Editor, SmileLocator

Daniel oversees the editorial direction of SmileLocator and ensures that every guide delivers accurate, unbiased, and practical information for patients seeking dental implant solutions they can trust . His work reflects a deep belief in patient education, transparency, and community-driven care.

Through his collaboration and ongoing inspiration with the professional network at Affordable Dentures & Implants , Daniel continues to learn from more than four decades of experience serving over eight million patients across the U.S. The organization’s focus on accessibility, continuing education, and ethical care aligns with SmileLocator’s mission to help patients find affordable, high-quality implant options.

Editorial Standards

Our guides are built on reputable sources, clinical data, and professional insight. Each article is reviewed for clarity and factual accuracy. We do not replace medical advice — instead, we empower patients to speak confidently with their dentists and make the best decision for their oral health.

Transparency & Independence

SmileLocator is an independent educational resource. We are not a clinic and we do not provide treatments. Limited advertising revenue supports our platform and never influences our content. Our commitment is — and will always remain — to the people we serve.
