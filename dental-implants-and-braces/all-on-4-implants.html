What Are The Benefits Of All-On-4 Dentures?

Choosing All-on-4 dental implants is a transformative decision, and we want you to feel confident about your choice. All-on-4 offers a range of benefits that set it apart from traditional dentures and other tooth replacement options.

  • Streamlined Process: Most patients can skip tissue grafts, significantly reducing treatment time and making the process faster and more convenient.
  • Secure Fit: All-on-4 implants provide a stable foundation for your dentures, ensuring they stay in place while you eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
  • Long-Lasting Results: With proper care, All-on-4 implants can last for decades, unlike traditional dentures that may need to be replaced every 10 years.
  • Enhanced Functionality: While traditional dentures restore only about 20 % of your chewing ability, All-on-4 can restore 70 % or more, allowing you to enjoy a wider variety of foods.
