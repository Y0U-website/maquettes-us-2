Benefits of Dental Implants

The titanium post is biocompatible and integrates with your jawbone, creating a strong foundation for your new teeth. This process not only ensures excellent stability and chewing power but also stimulates the bone, preventing deterioration over time. Dental implants uniquely replace both the tooth root and the visible tooth, offering unparalleled benefits.

How Long Does the Process Take? The timeline for dental implants varies depending on your individual needs. If a tooth extraction is required, implant placement may sometimes happen immediately after. However, in cases where bone grafting is necessary, healing could take around six months before the implant can be placed. After the posts are surgically implanted, a period of osseointegration (where the post fuses with the jawbone) usually follows, lasting several months. Once complete, the final prosthetic teeth can be attached.
