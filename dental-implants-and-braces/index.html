A single tooth implant is a modern solution designed to replicate the entire tooth structure, including the root. The implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone, mimicking the natural root and providing a stable foundation. Over several months, the bone integrates with the implant, securing it permanently. Once the bone has healed, a custom-made crown is attached to the implant post…

Benefits Of An Implant-Supported Crown Dental implants are the only prosthetic solution that replaces the entire tooth, from root to crown. This comprehensive approach offers numerous advantages that are not achievable with traditional fixed bridges. These benefits include: A success rate of over 95 %, ensuring a durable and lasting solution for decades.

A natural appearance, thanks to the use of high-quality ceramic materials that restore your smile with confidence.

The ability to regain up to 70 % of your natural biting strength, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods comfortably.

Stimulation of new bone growth to prevent bone loss and maintain jaw health.
