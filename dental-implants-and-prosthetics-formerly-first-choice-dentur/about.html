About Us

Dr Paul Romriell DMD

Dentistry is in Dr. Paul Romriell’s blood. Born and raised in Pocatello, Idaho, he grew up with a deep connection to the field—his mother was a dental hygienist, and his father is a dentist. Dr. Romriell met his wife in high school and went on to pursue his passion for dentistry at the University of Louisville in Kentucky, where he earned his dental degree. He then completed a three-year prosthodontic residency at Indiana University, followed by a fellowship in maxillofacial prosthetics.

Dr. Romriell’s passion lies in restoring smiles and solving complex dental challenges, no matter how difficult they may seem. His patients benefit from his advanced training, expertise, and compassionate approach to care.

When he’s not in the office, he enjoys golf, skiing and watching movies. Most of all, he loves spending time with his wife and their four children.
