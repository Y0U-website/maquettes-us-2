Thousands of people in Idaho and across the country are missing teeth. Whether you are missing one or several teeth in your mouth, you know that it hurts more than just your smile.

The practice

Implants are ideal at replacing a single missing tooth or even multiple teeth, making it possible to smile and eat as normal. In many cases, you’ll brush and floss like normal.

Implants take the place of the roots of your lost teeth. That creates a stable, direct connection between your replacement teeth and your jaw.

Les soins

Implants can be just as valuable if you need a dental crown or bridge as they are for someone who needs full-mouth reconstruction. Implants stop teeth from moving, and by replacing your lost roots, you are protecting the teeth you still have.

Implant supported dentures make it so you can better bite and chew practically. You can eat a variety of foods, which means meals are more nutritious and enjoyable, too. Plus, you won’t need any adhesives when you have implants to hold your dentures in…
