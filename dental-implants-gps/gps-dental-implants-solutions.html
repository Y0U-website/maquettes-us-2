Dental Implants GPS 101 A dental implant is a titanium screw anchored into your jawbone. The screw takes the place of the missing root of your tooth. Bone grows tightly around the screw, much like a post to concrete. A tooth or bridge of teeth is then attached to the dental implant(s).
