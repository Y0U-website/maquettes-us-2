The Step-by-Step Procedure

Getting All-on-X dental implants is a multi-step process that includes:

  • Initial Consultation – One of the Feasterville All-on-X implants specialists will examine your mouth to determine if all-on-X implants are suitable for you and how many implants will be required for a full arch restoration. This is also a good opportunity to discuss costs.
  • Implant Surgical Procedure – On the day of your surgery, local or oral anesthesia options will be administered, and the dentist will make a gum incision in each place an implant is to be installed. Multiple implants will be inserted into your jawbone with screw placement designed to have each abutment of a dental Implant in Feasterville placed in a manner to best hold a prosthesis.
  • Bone Grafting – after the surgery, the bone grafting process, known as osseointegration, will fuse the implants to the bone over a few months. During this healing time, temporary crowns will be installed to allow you to continue with your daily life.
  • Permanent Teeth Replacement – After the implant healing process is complete, a permanent bridge crown will be installed, and you will have a beautiful smile that will make you proud.

Who Is a Candidate for All-on-X Dental Implants in Feasterville?

Anyone who is suffering from severe tooth loss, is in generally good health, and has sufficient jawbone density can be a good candidate for All-on-X implant surgery in Feasterville. Be sure to discuss the process with your International Congress of Oral Implantologists dentist and understand the recovery process to determine if you are a good candidate.

Cost of All-on-X Dental Implants in Feasterville
