Frequently Asked Questions

What should I look for when choosing a new dentist?

When looking for a new dentist, you should evaluate their qualifications and education. Find a dentist who can help you feel comfortable during your appointment. It’s essential to find dentists that have gone through extensive training and continue their education to stay current with the newest and safest dental practices.

What can I expect at my first dental appointment?

Your first dental appointment begins with an initial consultation and a professional cleaning. Your dentist will review your previous medical history as well as address any concerns that you may have about your smile. This will also be the perfect time to ask questions you may have about their dental services, credentials, and personal smile goals.

Which dental procedures are right for my smile?

Your dentist will let you know which procedures are right for you during your initial consultation. They’ll conduct a complete evaluation of your teeth and gums to check for any underlying issues or problems that may need addressing. Your dentist will then customize a treatment plan based on your oral health needs so you can achieve your desired smile.

What are your tips for better oral hygiene at home?

Generally, a good at-home oral hygiene routine consists of brushing your teeth twice a day, flossing daily, and rinsing with suitable mouthwash. We recommend using a soft-bristled toothbrush because hard bristles can cause damage to your teeth.

If you’d like personal recommendations on the kinds of products that would be best for you, your dentist can help. At your next appointment, simply ask for tips on your at-home oral hygiene.
