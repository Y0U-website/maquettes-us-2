The practice

Dr Hicks has been practicing dentistry for over 12 years, and is proud to call the upstate home. Dr Hicks obtained a DMD degree from the Medical College of Georgia School of Dentistry in 2012. Dr Hicks has extensive experience in General Dentistry, serving patients of all ages and backgrounds.

Dr Hicks is passionate about oral health and beautiful smiles, in addition to treating each patient with gentleness, kindness and respect. He is committed to building long term relationships, and ensuring all patients a positive, beneficial experience in their oral health journey.

Dr Hicks enjoys spending time with his wife and 2 children, and being an active member of Covenant United Methodist Church. He also enjoys gardening, college football, classic cars, and serving the community.

Dr Hicks is an active member of the American Dental Association and the South Carolina Dental Association.
