FAQ About Dental Implants

What are whole mouth dental implants and how do they work?

Whole mouth dental implants also called full mouth dental implants or full arch dental implants permanently replace all missing teeth using titanium posts placed into the jawbone. The posts fuse with the bone and support permanent crowns, bridges, or dentures for a natural-looking, lifelong smile.

What is the difference between full mouth reconstruction and full mouth dental implants?

Full mouth reconstruction is the overall process of rebuilding all teeth using multiple procedures implants, crowns, bone grafting, and more. Full mouth dental implants are the core treatment within that plan. Our implant dentistry team combines both to deliver the best long-term result for each patient.

Is All-on-4 or All-on-6 better for full mouth dental implants?

All-on-4 uses four implants per arch and suits patients with minimal bone loss. All-on-6 uses six implants for greater stability and is better for patients with moderate bone loss. Dr. Boruchov will review your scans at your Astoria or Forest Hills consultation and recommend the right option for you.
