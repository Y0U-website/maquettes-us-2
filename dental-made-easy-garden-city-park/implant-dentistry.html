Why Choose Dental Implants Over Bridges or Dentures?

Bridges and dentures have limitations. Bridges require altering healthy teeth, which weakens them over time. Dentures can slip, causing discomfort and making eating or speaking difficult. Dental implants offer a steady, long-lasting option.

Dental implants protect the jawbone. When a tooth is lost, the surrounding bone may shrink. Implants work like natural tooth roots. They stimulate the bone and stop further loss. This process maintains facial structure and prevents a sunken look.

Dental implants also improve chewing. They attach firmly to the jawbone, allowing you to enjoy a variety of foods without pain. Unlike dentures that need adhesives and frequent adjustments, implants remain fixed and need only regular brushing and flossing. With proper care, implants can last a lifetime and reduce long-term costs.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants work best for people with healthy gums and enough jawbone density. A strong jawbone lets the implant fuse properly and remain stable. Certain conditions affect implant eligibility. Gum disease or infections can slow healing. Significant bone loss may require a bone graft. Smoking and conditions like diabetes may hinder recovery.

Good candidates have one or more missing teeth, healthy gums, and sufficient bone density. Patients with healing issues may need other treatment options. A bone graft can rebuild the jawbone to support the implant securely.

FAQs on Implant Dentistry

What Are Implants in Dentistry?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to permanently replace missing tooth roots. They are considered the gold standard in restorative dentistry, providing a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or implant-supported dentures. Once the implant integrates with the bone through osseointegration, patients regain full chewing function and a natural-looking smile. At Dental Made Easy, our clinicians deliver comprehensive implant dentistry across Brooklyn, Forest Hills, Astoria, and Garden City Park.

How Long Does a Dental Implant Last?

Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime. The titanium post permanently integrates with the jawbone, while the crown typically requires replacement after 10–15 years due to normal wear. Routine professional check-ups at Dental Made Easy combined with consistent oral hygiene are the most important factors in maximising long-term implant success.

What Is Implant Dentistry and Periodontics?

Implant dentistry and periodontics are two closely connected dental specialities that together determine the long-term success of dental implants. Periodontics addresses the health of the gums and supporting bone — the critical foundation upon which implants are placed. At Dental Made Easy, a full periodontal assessment is conducted prior to every implant procedure to ensure optimal tissue health and lasting implant outcomes.

Can You Get Dental Implants If You Have Osteopenia?

Osteopenia requires careful evaluation before proceeding with dental implant treatment. Reduced bone density can affect the success of osseointegration, so a thorough assessment — including 3D imaging — is conducted at Dental Made Easy to determine candidacy and whether bone grafting may be recommended prior to implant placement.
