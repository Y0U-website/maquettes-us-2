Gentle Dental Cleaning & Exams

Routine teeth cleanings are one of the most important steps you can take to protect your oral health. Even with excellent brushing and flossing habits at home, plaque and tartar can build up in hard-to-reach areas. Professional teeth cleanings in Toledo remove these deposits, helping prevent cavities, gum disease, and other long-term dental issues.

Most patients benefit from visiting the dentist every six months for a dental cleaning and exam, although we may recommend more frequent visits based on your individual needs. Regular preventive care not only keeps your smile looking great, but also helps catch small problems before they become bigger, more costly concerns. We also include a complimentary oral cancer screening, checking for lumps, lesions, or abnormalities in the mouth, head, and neck, because early detection matters.

What’s Included in Your Dental Cleaning

When you come in for a dental cleaning , our team focuses on both prevention and early detection. Your visit typically includes a thorough evaluation of your teeth, gums, and overall oral health. We begin with a gentle but detailed exam, checking for signs of tooth decay, gum disease, and other concerns. Digital X-rays may be taken if needed to detect issues that are not visible to the eye.

Next, your hygienist will carefully remove plaque and tartar buildup using specialized instruments. This process, called scaling, eliminates bacteria that can lead to cavities and gum disease. Once your teeth are clean, we polish them to remove surface stains and leave your smile feeling smooth and refreshed. To finish, we floss between your teeth and may apply a fluoride treatment to strengthen enamel and help prevent future decay. Fluoride plays an important role in protecting teeth and reducing the risk of cavities over time.

Before you leave, the dentist will complete a full exam and discuss any findings, along with personalized recommendations to keep your smile healthy between visits.
