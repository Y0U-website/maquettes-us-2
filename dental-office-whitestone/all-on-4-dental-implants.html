Why would All-on-4 Implants be needed?

You may want or even need all-on-4 implants if your natural teeth are not-salvageable and/or your current prosthesis is constantly slipping or just uncomfortable and ill-fitting, causing you to feel pain or discomfort whenever you put it in. The implants easily keep the restoration in place so that it won&#x27;t slip or budge. This allows for a more natural and realistic-looking smile and restores comfort as well as confidence.

Who&#x27;s a good candidate for All-on-4 Implants?

The majority of patients who want all-on-4 implants can safely have the procedure done.

What happens during the procedure for All-on-4 Implants?

Once pre-surgical planning is completed, surgical guides and your immediate(temporary) prosthesis are made by and received from the laboratory, you can book the procedure, keeping in mind that you will spend most of that day with us and will need roughly about a week of recovery.
