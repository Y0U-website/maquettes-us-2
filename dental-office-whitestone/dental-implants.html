Implant Options We Offer

Single Tooth Implants

Multiple Tooth Implants

Several implants can support a bridge to replace multiple missing teeth in a row.

Full-Arch and All-on-4

For patients missing most or all of their teeth, All-on-4 dental implants and full-arch restoration replace a complete arch on as few as four implants, a secure and permanent alternative to removable dentures.

The Dental Implant Process

Consultation and 3D Imaging

Treatment begins with an exam and a cone beam CT scan, which lets Dr. Rosa evaluate your bone and plan placement with precision.

Building the Foundation, If Needed

If you have lost bone in the upper jaw, a sinus lift or bone grafting may be needed first to create a stable base for the implant.

Implant Placement

The implant is placed during a focused, comfortable procedure. Our surgeons use X-Guide dynamic navigation for exceptional accuracy, and IV sedation is available for patients who prefer to be fully relaxed.

Healing and Restoration

Over the following months the implant fuses with your bone. Once healed, your final crown, bridge, or arch is secured in place, completing your restored smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do dental implants cost in NYC?

The cost depends on how many implants you need and whether procedures like a sinus lift or bone graft are involved. We provide a clear estimate at your consultation and help you understand your insurance coverage before treatment begins.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and routine checkups, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown on top may eventually need replacement, but the implant itself is designed to be permanent.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth are candidates. If you have experienced bone loss, a sinus lift or bone graft can often rebuild the foundation so implants become possible. A consultation and 3D scan give you a definitive answer.

Can I get implants the same day as my extraction?

In many cases, yes. When conditions allow, an implant can be placed at the time a tooth is removed, and sometimes with a temporary tooth attached. Dr. Rosa will let you know if you are a candidate.

Are dental implants safe?

Yes. Implants are a well-established, highly successful treatment. Placed by a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon using 3D planning, they have an excellent long-term success rate.
