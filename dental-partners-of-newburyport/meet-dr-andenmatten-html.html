Meet Thalita Andenmatten, DMD

Dr. Thalita Andenmatten’s career in dentistry began in 2006, when she first graduated from dental school in Brazil. She then went on to obtain her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) with honors at the Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, and today, she specializes in all aspects of general and implant dentistry.

Dr. Andenmatten actively participates in continuing education courses so that she can provide exceptional dental experiences with the latest techniques and technologies. A staunch advocate for dental public health, she regularly volunteers at numerous community outreach events.

Fluent in Portuguese, Spanish, and English, away from the office, Dr. Andenmatten loves the outdoors, traveling, and spending time with her loving family.
