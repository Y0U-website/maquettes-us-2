Services

Prevention

Have you had your 2 cleanings this year? Regular preventive care, early diagnosis and treatment can help you avoid more costly dental problems that may develop or be worsened by lack of basic preventive care. To promote preventive care, many dental plans pay all or most of the cost for routine dental checkups, including cleanings and exams. Take advantage of those benefits and schedule your appointment now!

Periodontal Care

What is periodontal disease you may ask? It is an infection that affects the tissues and bone that support teeth. If you have been diagnosed with periodontal disease, you are not alone. Many others have the same problem. Periodontal disease has no cure but must be managed. Here at Dental Permanente of Stamford we do just that! We will provide you with easy to manage treatment options depending upon the type and severity of the disease. Don’t wait until it hurts, schedule your periodontal evaluation now!

Digital X-Rays

Dental X-rays are taken yearly to check for cavities, bone loss or any infections. Dental X-rays are necessary part of your check up and are very safe . They help reveal any issues that are not visible during a standard exam. This allows for early diagnosis and treatment. Click on learn more from the American Dental Association.

Children Dental Care

Once your children learn how to care for their smile, they carry it with them for life! Whether it’s your little one’s first dental visit or a family checkup, we’re here to make dentistry a positive, stress-free experience. We start seeing children as early as 1 years old.

Invisalign

Transform your smile with Invisalign, the clear alternative to braces. By using a series of clear, removable aligners, Invisalign straightens your teeth with results you’ll notice sooner than you think. The course of treatment involves changing aligners approximately every two weeks, moving your teeth into a straighter position step by step, until you have a more beautiful smile. Schedule a consultation now!

Other Services

Restorations&#x2f;Filling

If you have a cavity, getting a filling is one of the most important steps you can take to protect your oral health. Our compassionate doctor prioritizes your comfort, using gentle techniques and local anesthetic to ensure a stress-free experience. A filling will not only relieve any sensitivity but also restore your tooth’s integrity , preventing further damage and protecting it from future infection.

Endodontics &#x28;Root Canal&#x29;

Millions of teeth are treated and saved each year with endodontic treatment. Endodontic treatment is necessary when the pulp becomes inflamed or infected. The pulp contains blood vessels, nerves and connective tissue, and helps to grow the root of your tooth during development. Endodontic treatment will help you maintain your natural smile, continue eating the foods you love and limits the need for ongoing dental work. With proper care, most teeth that have had root canal treatment can last forever.

Crown &#x28;Cap&#x29;

Teeth that cannot be saved with a simple filling need a dental crown. A dental crown is a cap which fits snugly over an existing tooth, providing aesthetic beauty while protecting the remaining tooth material from decay. Most dental crowns are made from high-quality porcelain, which gives your teeth a healthy glow, mirroring how your natural teeth react to sunlight. In fact, most people won’t even know you’ve had work done.

Dental Bridge

A dental bridge is a permanent, natural-looking solution for replacing one or more missing teeth . By "bridging" the gap left by a missing tooth, this fixed restoration restores your smile’s appearance and helps maintain your oral health. Made from high-quality materials like porcelain, dental bridges are custom-designed to blend seamlessly with your existing teeth.

Removable Prosthesis

Partial dentures or complete dentures are a removable option to replace missing teeth. Dentures are the most common removable prosthesis in the market and are, by far, the most common way that most patients choose to replace their missing teeth. The reason behind this is that, despite all the advancements in dental technology, removable prosthesis are noninvasive and will not require any surgical incisions or painful recovery.

TMJ&#x3a; Temporomandibular Joint Disorder

Do you suffer from headaches, earaches, neck or back pain? This may be because your upper and lower teeth don’t fit together quite right. This creates a conflict between your teeth and jaw joints that leads to teeth clenching and grinding (bruxism) and ultimately pain and damage. Get relief now! Get fitted for a personalized night guard . A night guard, also known as a dental mouth guard or a bite guard, is an appliance that protects the teeth and other vital structures by offering a protective barrier between the upper and lower teeth.

Insurance and Billing

In-Network Insurances

Out-of-Network Insurances

An “out-of-network” provider means that although we take your insurance in our practice we are not contracted for reimbursement on the specific fee schedule provided by your insurance company instead they will pay “reasonable and customary” fee’s. A “Reasonable and customary” fee is the amount that your insurance determines is the normal range of payment for a specific service/procedure. This will create a difference in fee’s which the patient is ultimately responsible for. The best way to find out this information is by calling your insurance company and asking them.
