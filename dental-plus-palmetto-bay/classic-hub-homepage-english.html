Our Specialties

Our Dental and Cosmetic Services

At Dental Plus, we offer a wide range of dental treatments designed to care for your oral health and give you the best smile. As well as cosmetic treatments that provide a more youthful appearance. We have highly trained specialists and cutting-edge technology to ensure optimal results in every procedure.

Dental Implants A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone to replace a missing tooth. Harvard University highlights that implants provide a durable and natural solution for tooth loss.

Cosmetic Botox An innovative technique aimed at optimizing muscle function to improve appearance naturally, without the need for invasive procedures. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has approved Botox for both aesthetic and therapeutic purposes.

Smile Design Smile design is a dental procedure that enhances the patient’s dental aesthetics and facial harmony. According to El Bosque University in Colombia, this treatment focuses not only on appearance but also provides numerous benefits for oral health and overall well-being.

Clear Aligners Dental aligners are transparent and removable orthodontic devices designed to correct misalignment and malocclusion problems. The American Association of Orthodontists indicates that clear aligners are an effective and aesthetic option for straightening teeth.

Dental Veneers Dental veneers are thin shells made of porcelain or composite resin that are bonded to the front surface of the teeth to enhance their appearance. They are used to correct discoloration, chips, gaps, worn teeth, and minor imperfections, creating a more balanced and natural-looking smile.

Dental Cleaning A procedure performed by a dentist or dental hygienist to remove plaque, tartar, and surface stains from teeth. The National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research (NIDCR) states that plaque buildup can lead to cavities and gum disease, making its removal crucial for maintaining oral health.

Tooth Extraction Tooth extraction is a recommended procedure when preserving the tooth is not viable or poses a risk to oral health. According to a study published in the journal RCOE by the Complutense University of Madrid, extractions are indicated in various clinical situations.

Dental Fillings A treatment used to repair teeth damaged by cavities, fractures, or wear. It involves removing the affected part of the tooth and replacing it with a special material. Creighton University highlights that fillings restore the function and structural integrity of the tooth.

Deep Cleaning A specialized dental procedure performed to treat gum disease in its early or advanced stages (gingivitis or periodontitis). The NIDCR emphasizes that plaque removal is essential to prevent periodontal disease.

Root Canal Treatment A root canal, or endodontic treatment, is essential to save teeth with inflamed or infected pulp, preventing their extraction. According to MedlinePlus, this procedure removes damaged nerve tissue and bacteria inside the tooth while preserving its functionality.

Crowns & Bridges A dental bridge is a structure used to replace one or more missing teeth. Creighton University states that crowns and bridges restore the function and aesthetics of the smile.

Overdentures Overdentures on implants provide an effective solution for patients with total edentulism, significantly improving quality of life and oral function. A study published in Advances in Periodontology and Oral Implantology highlights that implant-supported overdentures offer excellent biological and functional responses in geriatric patients, improving prosthetic stability and chewing capacity.

Dentures Complete dentures are an economical and widely used solution for patients who have lost all their natural teeth. According to a study published in the Gaceta Médica Estudiantil of Cuba, while dental implants are considered the ideal treatment for complete oral rehabilitation, anatomical and external factors often lead to the use of removable prostheses.

Laser Teeth Whitening Laser teeth whitening is a fast and effective treatment designed to brighten your smile. A study published in The European Journal of Esthetic Dentistry found that professional laser-assisted whitening treatments may provide a slightly greater whitening effect compared to treatments performed without light activation.

Braces (Orthodontics) Fixed appliances made of metal, composite, or ceramic used by orthodontists to improve a patient’s smile, function, and aesthetics. The American Association of Orthodontists emphasizes that orthodontics corrects dental misalignments and improves occlusion.

Dental Emergencies Timely treatment of a dental emergency is crucial to prevent serious complications that could affect a patient’s overall health. According to an article published in Acta Médica del Centro, dental emergencies require immediate attention to reduce severity, pain, or infection risks, as they can impact general health.

Night Guard A night guard is a dental device designed to protect the teeth and jaw from bruxism, which involves clenching or grinding teeth during sleep. The American Academy of Sleep Medicine recommends night guards to prevent tooth wear and relieve jaw tension.

Dental Sealants Dental sealants are an effective preventive measure to reduce the incidence of cavities in children and adolescents. A study published in the journal Atención Primaria found that the application of occlusal sealants in the first permanent molars of high-risk schoolchildren provided significant protection against cavities.

Surgical Extraction Surgical tooth extraction, especially of third molars (wisdom teeth), is indicated in various clinical situations to preserve oral and general health. This is supported by a study published in the journal RCOE.
