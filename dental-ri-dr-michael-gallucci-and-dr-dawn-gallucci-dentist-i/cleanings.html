What is Scaling?

Scaling is part of dental teeth cleaning and it involves removing hard calcified deposits from the surfaces of teeth. Scaling is important to maintain the health of your teeth and mouth and is performed by means of the hand and ultrasonic scalers.

People can develop plaque, stains, and tartar on their teeth, which are removed through professional scaling. Plaque and tartar may hide behind damaged teeth or roots. By removing the tartar and plaque layer, the dentist will be able to examine the health of your teeth. Therefore, the dentist recommends that you have regular six-month cleanings and check-ups. Yearly x-rays are also very important in detecting cavities, evaluating bone health, and diagnosing any potential infections or pathologies.

Scaling is a very safe treatment that can help prevent major oral problems as well as tooth decay. There is no harm done to the tooth by ultrasonic and hand scaling instruments.

If you see bleeding after or during the scaling procedure, you should not panic; it is normal and caused by gum inflammation due to plaque/bacteria. The gums should be much less inflamed the days following cleaning. It is important that patients maintain the results of the cleaning by properly brushing and flossing at least twice a day.

Teeth Cleaning Procedure at Dental RI

A thorough examination of your mouth is performed by the dentist usually after the cleaning. The dentist will review any x-rays that are taken, diagnose any cavities, and develop a comprehensive treatment plan specific to the needs of each patient.

We use Cavitron devices which are ultrasonic scalers used to make the cleaning procedure more comfortable and effective. A Cavitron is a high-frequency sound wave instrument used by our hygienists to clean teeth. The ultrasonic sound waves vibrate the calculus, tarter, and plaque off teeth.

Through the use of our ultrasonic scalers and hand instruments, most plaque and calculus will be removed from the surfaces of the teeth. Some people are more prone to plaque and calculus accumulation than others. Factors include poor oral hygiene by the patient, jaw bone loss, saliva content, and so much more. Our hygienists and dentists will reinforce healthy oral hygiene habits at each visit and educate the patient.

Using a motor-driven brush, our hygienists will polish your teeth using a professional polishing toothpaste after removing calculus and plaque. This polishing procedure effectively removes surface stains from the teeth and leaves a smooth finish. This smooth finish is unfavorable for the regrowth of plaque.

Our hygienists will floss your teeth after polishing them to remove plaque between the teeth and to educate the patient on the proper technique.

Rinsing with mouthwash after flossing will remove all the leftover debris and make your mouth feel extremely fresh and clean.
