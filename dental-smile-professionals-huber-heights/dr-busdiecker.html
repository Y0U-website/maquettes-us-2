Dr. Busdiecker

Dr. Todd Busdiecker has been helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles for nearly three decades. A graduate of The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in 1996, he has been practicing dentistry for over 30 years and continues to enjoy every aspect of caring for his patients.

Dr. Busdiecker’s philosophy is rooted in a genuine desire to help others. I have always wanted to help people, he says. My goal, as a dentist, is to help people retain their teeth for the rest of their lives with maximum comfort, aesthetics, and function. His compassionate approach, combined with years of clinical experience, has earned the trust of generations of patients.

Known for his commitment to excellence, Dr. Busdiecker provides a comprehensive range of advanced restorative and cosmetic dental services. He has extensive experience in crown and bridge dentistry and root canal therapy, while also offering Invisalign® clear aligners, custom porcelain veneers, full smile makeovers, complete smile restorations, and both cosmetic and therapeutic Botox® treatments.

Dr. Busdiecker has completed advanced training with nationally recognized cosmetic dental specialists and earned certification in Advanced Aesthetics. He is also Shatkin certified, allowing him to provide highly aesthetic, minimally invasive restorative solutions tailored to each patient’s goals. Whether restoring a single tooth or completely transforming a smile, he is passionate about creating natural-looking, long-lasting results that improve both confidence and oral health.

Committed to staying at the forefront of modern dentistry, Dr. Busdiecker is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Ohio Dental Association, the Cincinnati Dental Society, and the International Society of Aesthetic Medical Professionals.

Outside the office, Dr. Busdiecker enjoys spending time with his wife, Brittany, and their three boys.
