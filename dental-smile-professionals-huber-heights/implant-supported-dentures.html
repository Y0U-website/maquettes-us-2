FAQ’s

How many implants are needed for implant-supported dentures?

Most full-arch dentures require 2–6 implants for support. We ll evaluate your needs and bone structure to determine the right number for your treatment plan.

Are implant-supported dentures removable?

They can be either fixed (non-removable) or removable, depending on your preference and needs. We’ll guide you in choosing the best option for your lifestyle.
