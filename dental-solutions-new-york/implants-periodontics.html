B.A.B.E GRAFT The B.A.B.E. Graft™ is a bone grafting procedure using the patient’s blood, stem cells, and growth factors to accelerate healing and improve bone regeneration, reducing the risk of rejection and side effects.

Lanap Laser Gum Treatment

LANAP (Laser Assisted New Attachment Procedure) is a minimally invasive laser treatment for gum disease that uses a specialized laser to remove infected tissue, promote healing, and regenerate healthy gum and bone without the need for traditional surgery.

Gum pocket reduction is a dental procedure that involves cleaning and reshaping the gums to reduce deep pockets between the teeth and gums, helping to prevent infection and improve gum health in cases of advanced gum disease.

Dental Implant

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root, providing a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture.

Bone Graft

A bone graft is a surgical procedure where bone tissue is transplanted to a site in the jaw or elsewhere to promote healing, support dental implants, or repair bone loss caused by injury or disease.

Sinus Augmentation

Sinus augmentation, also known as a sinus lift, is a surgical procedure that involves adding bone to the upper jaw in the area of the molars and premolars, typically to prepare for dental implants by raising the sinus floor and allowing for the growth of new bone.

Periodontal (GUM) Surgery

Periodontal (gum) surgery is a dental procedure treating advanced gum disease by repairing damaged tissue, reducing pockets between the gums and teeth, and restoring oral health.

Gum Graft

A gum graft is a dental procedure where tissue is taken from the roof of the mouth or a donor source and grafted onto areas of the gums to cover exposed tooth roots, restore gum health, and prevent further recession.

Crown Lengthening

Crown lengthening is a dental procedure where excess gum tissue or bone is removed to expose more of a tooth’s surface, often done to improve the appearance of a "gummy" smile or to prepare a tooth for a crown restoration.
