Dr. Jeneen Shephard

Dr. Jeneen Shephard grew up in Rockford, Michigan and attended the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor prior to entering dental school. She received her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Detroit Mercy in 2021. Dr. Shephard is a member of the Kent County Dental Society, Michigan Dental Association, and American Dental Association. Dr. Shephard also works with Miles of Smiles to serve the dental needs of children in Ottawa County.

Dr. Shephard enjoys spending time with her husband Patrick and English bulldog Arby, as well as cooking, gardening, reading, and going to the movies.

Our staff always strives to provide you with exceptional service. All of our assistants and hygienists are experienced (some have been with us for over 20 years), which means they are comfortable and confident in delivering quality care. Yet all our staff is committed to continuing education, which means they are able to deliver modern, current dentistry using the latest technologies. The administrative department will be there for all your needs from scheduling to insurance questions. We are a dedicated, dependable team working for you.
