At Dental Spa, we provide comprehensive care for the entire family. Many of our patients have 3 generations receiving treatment; from grandparents to grandchildren. General dentistry includes exam, digital xrays and ultrasonic cleaning. Patients with periodontal issues can receive deep cleanings, Breath Rx products or laser treatment. For more complex cases we can refer you to a specialist.
