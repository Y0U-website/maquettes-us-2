Meet Your Periodontist

Dr. Kelsey Tengan

Dr. Kelsey Tengan is a board-certified periodontist with a passion for clinical precision and whole-patient wellness. Originally from Maui, Hawaii, Dr. Tengan brings a unique combination of Midwest professionalism and island-born warmth to his surgical care. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Creighton University and completed his periodontics residency at the University of Iowa, where he also earned a Master of Science in Oral Biology.

Since beginning his practice in 2017, Dr. Tengan has dedicated himself to advancing periodontal care through evidence-based treatment planning and modern dental technology. His surgical expertise includes dental implants , complex tooth extractions, soft tissue and bone grafting, and therapies for periodontal disease. With each case, he prioritizes long-term outcomes and a collaborative mindset that puts patient comfort and open communication first.

Known for his ability to explain complex treatment options in ways that are easy to understand, Dr. Tengan strives to make periodontal care less intimidating, especially for patients who are nervous about treatment. He takes pride in working closely with referring dentists to ensure continuity of care and a seamless clinical journey for every patient.

Dr. Tengan also believes in the power of technology to enhance treatment accuracy and reduce healing time. That’s why he’s invested in tools such as CBCT scanners, intraoral imaging, and 3D-printed surgical guides—allowing him to deliver accurate, comfortable, and efficient treatment solutions.

Whether it’s placing a life-changing dental implant or managing chronic gum disease, Dr. Tengan treats each patient with empathy, thorough clinical skill, and the individualized attention they deserve.

Your Comfort Comes First

What to Expect at Your First Visit

At Dental Specialists of Milwaukee, your first visit is designed to be thorough, clear, and stress-free. Whether you’ve been referred by your general dentist or are seeking specialized treatment directly, we’ll take time to get to know your dental history, goals, and concerns before discussing your treatment options.

The Experience You’ll be welcomed by our friendly front desk team, then meet with Dr. Tengan for a detailed consultation. During your appointment, we may take advanced diagnostic images and discuss a range of individualized treatment options. We strive to make every step—whether surgical planning or follow-up care—simple, transparent, and centered on your comfort.

What to Bring Please bring the following items to your first visit:

Any referral paperwork or x-rays from your general dentist

A list of medications and medical conditions

Completed new patient forms (or arrive early to fill them out in-office)

Precision Dentistry, Powered by Innovation

At Dental Specialists of Milwaukee, we’ve invested in modern equipment and technology to provide a better, more accurate healthcare experience. Our tools make treatment safer, more predictable, and more comfortable—leading to better long-term results for patients.

CBCT Imaging

Our in-house cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) unit offers high-resolution 3D scans that enhance diagnosis and treatment planning. This allows for safer surgeries, such as implants and extractions, by providing a detailed look at a patient’s bone anatomy, nerves, and sinuses.

Intraoral Scanning

Digital impressions taken with our intraoral scanner are more comfortable than traditional molds and allow for higher precision during implant planning and surgical guide fabrication. The tech also improves case communication with referring dentists.

3D-Printed Surgical Guides

To ensure pinpoint accuracy during implant procedures, we use custom 3D-printed surgical guides. These tools help align implants with incredible precision, reducing surgical time and improving healing outcomes.
