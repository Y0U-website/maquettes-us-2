The practice

From routine to complex extractions, including emergency extractions, we offer compassionate surgical care supported by advanced imaging and sedation options.

At Dental Specialists of Milwaukee, it’s our people, our technology, and our values that make us different. From same-day surgical appointments to seamless communication with your general dentist, we prioritize efficiency, transparency, and comfort—without compromising top-tier clinical outcomes.

Dr. Tengan brings nationally recognized credentials, but stays grounded in local, human-centered care that makes even complex treatment plans feel approachable.

CBCT scanning, intraoral imaging, and 3D-printed surgical guides allow for unparalleled accuracy in diagnosis, planning, and treatment.

We understand that dental issues don’t wait, which is why we offer same-day evaluations and treatments whenever possible.
