Our Dental Implant Process: From Consultation to Final Restoration

At Dental Specialists of Niles, P.C., we follow a carefully planned, step-by-step process to ensure successful and comfortable dental implant placement:

  • Step 1: Initial Consultation Your journey starts with a detailed consultation with Dr. Anasinski at our Niles, IL office. During this visit, we evaluate your oral health, review your medical history, and take 3D imaging to determine your suitability for dental implants. Dr. Anasinski will explain the treatment plan, discuss your options, and answer any questions.
  • Placement of the Titanium Post The next step involves placing the titanium post into your jawbone in a minor oral surgery procedure. This post serves as the root for your new tooth. Over the next few months, osseointegration occurs, in which the post fuses with your bone to provide a secure foundation.
  • Attaching the Abutment Once the post has integrated with the bone, we attach an abutment to the titanium post. The abutment acts as a connector between the implant and the custom crown. This may involve a minor procedure to expose the implant and prepare it for the final restoration.
  • Final Restoration (Custom Crown) Finally, we place your custom crown onto the abutment, completing the dental implant process. Your new tooth will match the size, shape, and color of your natural teeth, restoring both the function and appearance of your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

  • What Are Dental Implants Made Of? Dental implants are made of titanium, which is highly biocompatible and fuses with the jawbone during osseointegration. This ensures a stable, long-lasting foundation for your custom crown.
  • How Long Do Dental Implants Last? With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime . The titanium post fuses with the jawbone, creating a permanent solution for tooth loss. Regular dental visits help ensure the implant stays in good condition
  • Are Dental Implants Painful? The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you should feel little to no pain . After the procedure, some discomfort is common but typically manageable with prescribed pain relievers.
  • Can Dental Implants Be Placed in One Visit? The full implant procedure involves multiple steps and may take several months to complete. However, some patients may be eligible for same-day implants in specific cases.
  • How Much Do Dental Implants Cost in Niles, IL? The cost of dental implants can vary depending on the complexity of your case and the number of implants needed. Dr. Anasinski will provide a detailed cost estimate during your consultation at Dental Specialists of Niles, P.C.
