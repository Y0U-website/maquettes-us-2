About Our Practice

Conveniently located on Main Street in Lumberton, TX, Dental Square focuses on the comfort and convenience of all of our patients. Our bright and sunlight waiting room helps to calm the nerves, and we even have a play area for the little ones to keep them entertained. Our team can offer a variety of dental treatments from general dentistry like checkups, exams, and fillings to cosmetic and restorative treatments like dental crowns, bridges and teeth whitening.
