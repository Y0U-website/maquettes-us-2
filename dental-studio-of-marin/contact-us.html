Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about appointments, visits, and finding us.

How do I book an appointment?

Call our office or use the appointment request form on our website, and our team will help you find a convenient time.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! We’re always glad to welcome new patients and families to the practice.

Where are you located?

We’re located in San Rafael and proudly serve patients across Marin County. Just give us a call and our team will gladly help you find us.

What are your office hours?

Our hours are designed to fit busy schedules. Please contact our office for current hours and availability.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance information (if any), a list of medications, and any recent dental records or X-rays.

Do you offer emergency appointments?

Yes. If you’re in pain or have a dental emergency, call us right away and we’ll do our best to see you promptly.

How can I reach you with an urgent problem?

Give our office a call and follow the instructions in our message for guidance on urgent dental needs.

Do you see children and families?

Yes. We provide comprehensive care for all ages, so your whole family can be seen in one place.

Can you help me find the office or with parking?

Of course. Give us a call and we’ll gladly point you in the right direction and help with directions or parking.

Can I request a specific appointment time?

Absolutely. Let us know what works best for you and we’ll do our best to accommodate your schedule.
