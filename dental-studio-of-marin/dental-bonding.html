Caring for Bonded Teeth

Bonded teeth don’t require any special routine — just regular brushing, flossing, and checkups. Because the resin can chip or stain over time, we recommend avoiding habits like biting nails or chewing ice, and cutting back on stain-heavy foods and drinks. With reasonable care, bonding can look great for years before it needs a touch-up.

Is Bonding Right for You?

Bonding is a great fit for minor cosmetic touch-ups, but for larger changes or more durable results, veneers or crowns may be a better choice. At your visit, we’ll look at your goals and recommend the option that gives you the best long-term value and the smile you’re after.
