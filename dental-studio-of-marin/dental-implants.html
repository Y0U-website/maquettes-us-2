Dental Implants

  • Protect Your Jawbone They stimulate bone and help prevent the sunken look of missing teeth. Preserves facial structure
  • Long-Lasting With good care, implants can last decades or even a lifetime.
  • No Impact on Neighbors Unlike bridges, they don’t rely on altering adjacent teeth.

Implant-Retained Crowns

To replace a single missing tooth, we place one implant and top it with a custom crown — an implant-retained crown . Because the crown attaches directly to the implant rather than the neighboring teeth, your healthy teeth stay untouched, and the result looks and feels completely natural. It’s the most seamless way to fill a single gap for the long term.

Implant-Supported Dentures

For patients missing several or all of their teeth, implant-supported dentures offer a secure, comfortable alternative to traditional removable dentures.

  • Anchored to a few strategically placed implants for stability
  • No slipping, clicking, or messy adhesives
  • Stronger biting power to enjoy the foods you love
  • Available as removable or fixed depending on your needs

The Implant Process

Getting implants is a step-by-step process that unfolds over a few months. After a thorough evaluation and planning, we place the implant and allow it to fuse with your jawbone during a healing period. Once it’s fully integrated, we attach your custom crown, bridge, or denture. Though it takes time, the payoff is a restoration that’s built to last and feels like your own.

Caring for Your Implants

One of the best things about implants is how easy they are to maintain — you care for them just like natural teeth. Brush, floss, and keep up with regular checkups so we can monitor the implant and surrounding gums. With good habits, your implants can stay healthy and functional for many years to come.
