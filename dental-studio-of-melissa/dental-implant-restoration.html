Why Choose Dental Implant Restoration?

Dental implant restoration becomes necessary when you are missing one or more natural teeth. Beyond cosmetic concerns, tooth loss can impact your ability to eat, chew, and speak comfortably. Dental implants provide a long-lasting solution that improves both your smile and quality of life. Ideal candidates have healthy bone structure where the implant posts will be placed, and in cases of bone loss, bone grafting or a sinus lift may be recommended to ensure successful placement.

What Happens During the Restoration Process?

The process involves several steps. Local anesthesia is administered for comfort, then the titanium implant post is securely placed in the jawbone and the gums are sutured closed. Over the next three to six months, the implant undergoes osseointegration, fusing with the bone to create a stable foundation. Once integrated, a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is attached to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. Regular follow-up visits monitor the health and stability of the implant for long-term success.
