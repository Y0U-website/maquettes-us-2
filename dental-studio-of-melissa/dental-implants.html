What to Expect During the Dental Implant Process

Your implant journey begins with a consultation in our Melissa office to discuss your goals and design a personalized treatment plan. When you are ready, Dr. Khan surgically places the implant into the jawbone, followed by a healing period during which osseointegration occurs, fusing the implant securely with the bone. If additional support is needed, a bone graft may be recommended.

Once healing is complete, an abutment is attached to the implant to hold your custom-made crown, matched to the shape, size, and color of your natural teeth. A temporary crown may be used until your permanent restoration is ready.
