The Importance of Orthodontic Treatment

Crooked or crowded teeth can pose several challenges. Beyond affecting your self-esteem and making you hesitant to smile, they can also impact oral hygiene. Difficulty cleaning and flossing properly can lead to issues like tooth decay, gingivitis, and gum disease. There is no age limit for orthodontic treatment. While a child’s first consultation is recommended around ages seven to nine, adults can benefit at any stage of life.

What to Expect During Orthodontic Treatment

The journey begins with a comprehensive examination, including x-rays, followed by a detailed consultation. Throughout treatment, you will have regular visits for adjustments or to receive new aligners. The duration varies but typically ranges from a few months to two or three years, depending on the complexity of your case.
