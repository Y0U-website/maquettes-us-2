We are happy that your Doctor has chosen our surgery center for your upcoming procedure. The following information will provide details regarding your time with us.

The Dental Surgical Center of Medina is a first-class ambulatory surgery center, where surgeons perform dental and oral/facial surgery in an outpatient surgical setting. Patients are cared for with the intent of discharging to home upon surgical and recovery completion.

Established in early 2010 by a Staff Anesthesiologist of a local children’s hospital, the Dental Surgical Center of Medina is designed specifically for dental/oral surgery under general anesthesia, the first of its kind in Northeast Ohio.

Our Surgery Center provides the highest level of care, giving patients an alternative to hospitalization. Licensed by the Ohio Department of Health, the surgery center has gained the Center for Medicare/Medicaid Services (CMS) Deemed Accreditation status, the highest possible accreditation administered by the Accreditation Association for Ambulatory Health Care (AAAHC).
