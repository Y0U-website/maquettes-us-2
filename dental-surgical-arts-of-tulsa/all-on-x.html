The All-On-X Dental Implant Process

Your treatment begins with a detailed evaluation at Dental Surgical Arts of Tulsa. The process may vary depending on your health, remaining teeth, bone structure, and whether additional procedures are needed.

Consultation and Treatment Planning

Your provider will review your medical history, dental history, X-rays or imaging, current teeth, jawbone structure, gum health, and goals. This visit helps determine whether All-On-X dental implants are appropriate or whether another treatment option may be better.

If existing teeth need to be removed, your provider will explain how extractions fit into the treatment plan. Some patients may need tooth extractions before or during implant treatment.

Implant Placement Surgery

During surgery, four or more dental implants are placed into the jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. These implants are positioned to support a full-arch prosthetic and distribute biting forces as evenly as possible.

Local anesthesia is used, and sedation may be available for patients who qualify. Patients who feel anxious about surgery can discuss sedation dentistry during the consultation.

Temporary Prosthetic Placement

In many cases, a temporary set of teeth may be attached after implant placement. This temporary prosthetic allows you to leave with a functional smile while the implants heal. Not every patient qualifies for immediate loading, and your provider will explain whether this is appropriate for your case.

Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next several months, the implants fuse with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. This healing period is important because it helps create the foundation for the final prosthetic.

Final Restoration

After healing is complete, a final customized prosthetic is secured. This restoration is designed to fit your bite, support facial appearance, and provide long-term function when properly maintained.

All-On-X vs. Traditional Dentures

All-On-X and traditional dentures both replace missing teeth, but they function differently.

All-On-X Dental Implants

All-On-X prosthetics are supported by dental implants placed in the jawbone. They are designed to remain fixed in the mouth and provide improved stability compared with removable dentures. This option may be better suited for patients seeking a more secure full-arch replacement.

Traditional Dentures

Traditional dentures rest on the gums and are removable. They may be less invasive and more affordable initially, but some patients experience slipping, sore spots, reduced chewing ability, or difficulty adapting.

FAQs: All-On-X Dental Implants in Tulsa, OK

What does All-On-X mean?

All-On-X refers to a full-arch dental implant treatment where a complete upper or lower set of replacement teeth is supported by four or more implants. The “X” means the exact number of implants varies based on the patient’s anatomy, bone support, and treatment plan.

Are All-On-X dental implants removable?

The prosthetic is typically fixed to dental implants and is not removed like a traditional denture. Your dental team may remove it during maintenance visits when needed. This design helps improve stability compared with many removable denture options.

How long do All-On-X dental implants last?

Dental implants can last many years with proper care, but longevity depends on oral hygiene, bone health, bite forces, medical conditions, tobacco use, and regular dental maintenance. The prosthetic teeth may require repair or replacement over time due to normal wear.
