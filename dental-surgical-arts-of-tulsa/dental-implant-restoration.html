FAQs: Dental Implant Restoration in Tulsa

  • What’s the difference between implant placement and implant restoration?: Placement refers to the surgical insertion of the implant post. Restoration involves the visible part—like crowns, bridges, or dentures—that attach to the implant.
  • Can a damaged implant crown be replaced without removing the implant?: Yes. If the implant post is stable, we can typically replace the crown or abutment without additional surgery.
  • Is implant restoration painful?: No. Most restoration procedures are non-surgical and involve little to no discomfort.
  • What if my implant is loose?: A loose implant may indicate an issue with the crown, abutment, or even the implant itself. We’ll diagnose the source and recommend the appropriate solution.
  • Do you restore implants placed by other dentists?: Yes. We routinely work with patients who had implants placed elsewhere and now need repair, replacement, or evaluation.
