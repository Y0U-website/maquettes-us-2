FAQs: Implant-Supported Dentures in Tulsa

  • Are implant-supported dentures removable or fixed?: Both options are available. Removable overdentures “snap in,” while fixed options stay permanently attached.
  • How many implants do I need?: Most patients need 2–6 implants per arch, depending on whether you want a removable or fixed denture.
  • What if I already wear dentures?: We can often adapt existing dentures to work with implants or craft new ones for a perfect fit.
  • Is the procedure painful?: The surgery is performed with local anesthesia or sedation, and most patients report minimal discomfort.
  • How long do implant-supported dentures last?: With proper care, the implants can last a lifetime. The denture itself may need to be replaced or adjusted every 7–10 years.
