Sidney Sternberger, DMD

Dr. Sidney Sternberger received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the New Jersey Dental School (now Rutgers School of Dental Medicine) in 2008. He then completed a General Practice Residency at Coler–Goldwater Specialty Hospital in New York City, followed by a second year of General Practice Residency at New York-Presbyterian Queens.

He earned a post-doctoral Fellowship Certificate in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and Implantology from New York-Presbyterian Queens, which included advanced dental implant training at NYU College of Dentistry and Columbia University College of Dental Medicine. These extended programs provided comprehensive surgical and prosthetic experience, including implant placement, grafting procedures, and prosthetically guided rehabilitation.

Dr. Sternberger remains committed to academic dentistry and resident education. He currently serves as a Clinical Attending in Prosthodontics in the Department of Dental Medicine at Wyckoff Heights Medical Center, where he teaches advanced prosthodontics and implant treatment planning. He previously served as a Clinical Attending at NYU/Bellevue Hospital and Northwell Long Island Jewish Medical Center, and was a former Assistant/Clinical Professor in Prosthodontics at Stony Brook University School of Dental Medicine.

These academic and hospital roles reflect more than a decade of involvement in dental education, interdisciplinary treatment planning, and advanced prosthetic dentistry—experience he brings directly to private practice.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Sternberger enjoys spending time with his children. He is frequently at the ice skating rink cheering on his son, Jonah, who plays ice hockey, and supporting his daughter, Evelyn, in her recreational activities. Dr. Sternberger is active in his local synagogue and enjoys learning Torah, which provides a meaningful balance to his professional life.

Rebecca Sternberger, DDS, MA, MBA

Doctor of Dental Surgery • Master of Arts • Master of Business Administration

Dr. Rebecca Sternberger received her doctoral degree from New York University College of Dentistry. She excelled in the field of Prosthodontics and Reconstructive Dentistry and was awarded the Best of the Best in this field at graduation.

During her training, Dr. Sternberger placed first and third, respectively, in the national research competition at the American College of Prosthodontists conferences. She completed a year of general practice training at Coler Goldwater Specialty Hospital in New York City and placed and restored dental implants at Bellevue Hospital in New York City.

Following her general practice training, Dr. Sternberger completed an additional three years of Prosthodontic specialty training at Stony Brook University School of Dental Medicine, Department of Prosthodontics and Digital Technology. During that time she completed a Masters in Business Administration, with a concentration in Health Care Management from Stony Brook University College of Business.

Dr. Sternberger currently serves as Site Director at St. Barnabas Hospital, where she teaches dental residents Prosthodontics and Implant Dentistry, bringing teaching hospital expertise to her practice.

Dr. Sternberger is extensively trained in cosmetic dentistry, with a wide-range of experience in veneers, smile makeovers and full mouth rehabilitation. She is committed to helping her patients achieve the highest level of oral health and their dream smile.

When Dr. Sternberger is not in the office, she enjoys biking, traveling and spending time with her family.
