Dr. Rima Modi, DMD

Dr. Rima Modi, DMD is passionate about transforming the dental experience for her patients. She earned her dental degree from Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry in Philadelphia, PA, and brings a wealth of knowledge and compassion to her practice. With a gentle touch and a commitment to continuous learning, Dr. Modi ensures that every patient feels understood and well cared for.

As an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), Illinois State Dental Society (ISDS), and the Algonquin Chamber of Commerce, she stays at the forefront of dental advancements to bring the best care to her community. Outside of work, Dr. Modi enjoys spending time with her family, exploring new hiking trails, and indulging in Bollywood movies.

She looks forward to welcoming you to Dental Symphony and helping you achieve a smile you’ll love.
