Frequently Asked Questions

Considering dental implants in Algonquin? Here are the answers to the questions our patients ask most before starting treatment.

The dental implant process typically takes between 3 and 6 months from placement to final restoration, with most of that time spent in the healing phase as the implant fuses with the jawbone. The timeline varies based on your individual healing rate and whether any preparatory procedures — such as bone grafting — are needed beforehand.

Implant placement is performed under local anesthesia, and oral sedation is often available to help you feel relaxed throughout the procedure. Most patients report that the procedure is far more comfortable than they expected. Some soreness and swelling in the days following surgery is normal and manageable with over-the-counter pain medication and the aftercare instructions Dr. Wang provides.

Caring for a dental implant is virtually identical to caring for a natural tooth. Brush twice daily, floss around the implant, and attend your regular cleaning appointments at Dental Symphony. Unlike natural teeth, implants cannot decay — but the surrounding gum tissue can still develop infection if not properly maintained, so consistent oral hygiene remains essential.

Dental implants have a success rate of approximately 95 to 98 percent, making them one of the most predictable procedures in modern dentistry. Success rates are highest in healthy, non-smoking patients with adequate bone density. Dr. Wang’s advanced training in implant surgery and Dr. Modi’s precision in restorative placement ensure that your implant is given every advantage for long-term success.
