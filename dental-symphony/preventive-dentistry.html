Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about preventive dental care in Algonquin? Here are answers to the questions our patients ask most.

Most patients benefit from a professional cleaning and oral exam every six months. However, patients with a history of gum disease, higher cavity risk, or other oral health concerns may need more frequent visits. Dr. Modi will recommend the right schedule based on your individual needs during your exam.

During your oral exam, Dr. Modi will carefully evaluate your teeth, gums, jaw, and soft tissues for any signs of concern. We also perform an oral cancer screening and review digital X-rays to detect issues early — often before you notice any symptoms — so treatment stays simple and manageable.

Yes. Dental Symphony provides custom-fitted night guards for patients who grind or clench their teeth, as well as athletic mouth guards for those who play contact sports. Custom guards offer far superior comfort, fit, and protection compared to over-the-counter options.

Periodontal management involves ongoing care for patients diagnosed with gum disease. If Dr. Modi or Dr. Wang identifies signs of periodontal disease during your exam, they will create a personalized treatment plan to help stabilize and protect your gum health long-term.

Most dental insurance plans cover preventive services — such as cleanings and exams — at little or no cost to you. For patients without insurance, Dental Symphony offers an in-house membership plan that provides affordable access to routine preventive care. Our team is happy to review your options.

We recommend bringing children in for their first dental visit around age one or when their first tooth appears. Early preventive care helps identify developmental concerns, builds healthy oral habits, and makes future dental visits a positive experience. Dental Symphony warmly welcomes patients of all ages.
