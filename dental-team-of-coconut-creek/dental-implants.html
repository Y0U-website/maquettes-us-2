Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are suitable for individuals of all ages in good oral health who have lost teeth due to accidents, gum disease, or injury. Chronic conditions, heavy smoking, or alcohol use may require special considerations.

What to Expect During Your Dental Implant Procedure:

Initial Procedure: The dentist makes an incision in the gums and drills a hole in the bone for the implant. After suturing, the jawbone heals around the implant over three to six months.

Second Procedure: Once healed, a new incision is made to place the abutment, which supports the gum tissue and secures the replacement tooth. Impressions are taken to create a crown that matches your bite.

In some cases, a one-stage procedure may be performed, placing the implant, abutment, and crown in one visit.

Dental implants do not require alterations to adjacent healthy teeth. Local anesthesia is used to minimize discomfort.

After your procedure, it’s vital to care for your implants as you would your natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and biannual dental checkups are essential.
