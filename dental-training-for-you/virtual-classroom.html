What’s included in your Virtual Classroom

Your Virtual Classroom gives you the video lessons, materials and resources for every program you’re enrolled in.

Expert video lessons Watch clear, step-by-step lessons taught by instructors with 30+ years of experience: pause, rewind and rewatch anytime.

Downloadable resources Guides, worksheets and reference materials you can download, print and keep for your studies and your career.

Program syllabi See exactly what each course covers, module by module, so you always know what’s next on your learning path.

Certificate on completion Finish your coursework and earn a recognized certificate that shows employers you’re ready to work.

Two ways to access your studies

Depending on your program, you will sign in through one of two platforms. Hablamos Español.

Your certificate-program lessons, syllabi and downloadable resources live on Moodle. Sign in with the Moodle username and password provided by our Student Services team.

My Online Classes Your continuing education classes are delivered on our student portal, a platform separate from Moodle. Sign in with the access details we send to your email when you enroll to watch your video classes and download your materials.

A full research library, included with enrollment

Every enrolled student gets access to the Aquinas Library, a professional library of dental and health-science resources to support your coursework, assignments and lifelong learning at no extra cost.

  • Peer-reviewed articles and clinical references
  • Trusted sources for research and assignments
  • Available online, alongside your courses

How it works

From enrollment to your certificate in four simple steps.

1. Enroll in a program Choose your program and complete enrollment with our team, in English or Spanish, whichever you prefer.

2. Receive your access We send your access details: a Moodle login for certificate programs, or your student portal login for continuing education classes.

3. Access lessons & resources Work through your video lessons, syllabi and downloadable materials at your own pace, 24/7.

4. Earn your certificate Complete your coursework and receive a recognized certificate that opens doors in the job market.
