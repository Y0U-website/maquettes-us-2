The Benefits of Dental Implants

Improved Oral Function Dental implants allow you to chew, bite, and speak more easily by mimicking the strength and stability of a natural tooth. This means you can enjoy your favorite meals again without discomfort or embarrassment.

Long-Lasting Solution With proper care, dental implants can last for decades, making them a reliable investment in your oral health. Unlike dentures, they’re fixed in place, so you don’t have to worry about shifting or replacements.

Preserve Jawbone Health Missing teeth can lead to bone loss over time, but dental implants stimulate the jawbone to prevent deterioration. This helps maintain your facial structure and keeps your smile looking youthful and full.

Our Difference

What to Expect From Dental Implants Comprehensive Consultation Your journey begins with a personalized consultation at Dental Transformations in Leland. Dr. Thompson will carefully listen to your concerns and evaluate your oral health using advanced CBCT (3D) imaging. This technology helps map out the exact placement of your implant, ensuring precision and comfort. By the end of this visit, you’ll have a clear understanding of your treatment plan.

Implant Placement

During the procedure, Dr. Thompson will gently position the small titanium post into your jawbone. Thanks to modern techniques and local anesthesia, the process is designed to be as comfortable as possible. Some patients describe it as easier than a tooth extraction. Once the implant is placed, your body will start integrating the post into the bone, which creates a secure foundation for your new tooth.

Restoration and Follow-Up

After a healing period of 3-6 months, a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture will be attached to your implant. This final step completes your restored smile, blending seamlessly with your existing teeth. Our team will support you with guidelines for care and regular follow-ups to ensure the longevity of your implant. The result? A confident, fully functional smile you’ll love sharing with the world.
