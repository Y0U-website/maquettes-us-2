The practice

Dr. Kaleb Thompson is the guiding force behind Dental Transformations. Originally from Utah, he relocated to North Carolina in 2016 and now brings more than a decade of clinical experience to the Leland community. A graduate of Roseman University of Health Sciences, Dr. Thompson is known for his thoughtful approach to care: one that balances technical precision with genuine human connection.

With advanced experience in implants, root canals, extractions, dentures , and pediatric dentistry, he offers comprehensive treatment designed to support long-term oral health. Fluent in Spanish and valued for his calm, approachable manner, Dr. Thompson provides care that feels both highly skilled and deeply personal.

A healthy smile begins with preservation. Preventive dentistry focuses on maintaining balance through regular cleanings, fluoride treatments, and periodontal care, helping your oral health remain strong, steady, and supported over time.

Small refinements can make a meaningful difference. Our cosmetic treatments , including whitening, veneers, and bonding, are thoughtfully tailored to enhance your natural smile—never overdone, always considered.

Dentistry at Dental Transformations is guided by intention rather than routine. Dr. Kaleb Thompson and his team believe that good care begins with listening, taking the time to understand each patient before recommending a path forward. Through thoughtful dialogue, modern tools, and continual learning, we provide care that feels measured, personal, and grounded in trust.

Family-first approach We treat every patient like a member of our own family. Every patient is treated with attention and respect, creating care that feels personal rather than procedural.

Advanced clinical skills Our experience in complex procedures, including implants, root canals, and pediatric dentistry, allows us to provide comprehensive care with confidence and consistency.

Spanish-speaking care Clear communication matters. Dr. Thompson’s fluency in Spanish helps ensure understanding, comfort, and trust for more members of our community.
