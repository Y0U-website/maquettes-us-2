About Us

What Sets Our Weston Dental Office Apart?

We know what it’s like to sit in a dental chair, and we are committed to making every visit to our dental office a comfortable, stress-free experience. When you visit our dental office, have peace of mind knowing our passion resides in your smile and ensuring you reach a state of optimum dental health. Have questions about your teeth? Ask us anything, and we will happily provide expert advice and guidance.

Dental Visions is a general dental practice with a dental office located in Weston, Wisconsin. Our clinic specializes in a variety of dental services , including comprehensive preventative dentistry, restorative services, cosmetic dentistry, and emergency services.

Out Dental Office: Meet Your Dentists

Skilled and Compassionate Professionals Committed to Your Optimal Dental Health

Dr. Addison Dunham

Background

Dr. Addison Dunham is originally from North Carolina and completed his undergraduate studies at University of North Carolina Chapel Hill, majoring in biology. He went on to obtain his dental degree at Indiana University School of Dentistry. Dr. Dunham joined the Dental Visions team in 2026.

Dental Style

Dr. Dunham is passionate about providing patient-centered care and educating patients to empower them to make the best decision for their oral and overall health. Dr. Dunham is committed to continuing education to expand his skillset and provide high-quality care. Dr. Dunham strives to make every dental visit a positive one for his patients.

Fun Facts

In his free time, Dr. Dunham enjoys watching sports, reading science fiction, and spending time outdoors hiking and skiing with his fiancée.

Dr. Jeremy Hoffman

Background

Dr. Hoffman graduated from Southern Illinois University – Carbondale with a B.S. in Chemistry in December 1999 and from Marquette University School of Dentistry in May 2004. He has been practicing with Dental Visions since 2015. The family atmosphere and the commitment to excellence for each patient in the office have impressed Dr. Hoffman since day one.

Dental Style

Dr. Hoffman enjoys the variety of cases that general dentistry brings. Whether it is a broken tooth that needs a crown, a decayed front tooth that needs an esthetic filling, or a painful tooth that needs to be treated with a root canal, those procedures are all part of his daily routine. He has attended many continuing education courses throughout the years to learn new techniques and perspectives on a variety of dental topics.

He is actively involved in organized dentistry and currently serves as Speaker of the House for the Wisconsin Dental Association. In October 2022, he was inducted into the American College of Dentists, the oldest dental honorary society in the country, recognizing ethics, professionalism, and leadership in dentistry.

Fun Facts

Outside of dentistry, you can find Dr. Hoffman training for an ultramarathon (races longer than 26.2 miles) or attending one of his children’s sporting events. He loves listening to history podcasts, reading spy novels and watching an eclectic mix of sports including the NFL, baseball, soccer and Aussie Rules football.

Dr. Alison Mlodik

Background

Dr. Alison Mlodik completed her undergraduate studies at the University of Wisconsin-Eau Claire, while completing her dental degree at the University of Iowa College of Dentistry.

Dental Style

As a dedicated dentist, Dr. Mlodik truly cares about meeting the needs of her patients and values the history of the practice. She feels that it is essential to educate patients, working with each of her patients to help them better understand their options so they can make the best decision for their individual needs and goals. Dr. Mlodik is certified to offer treatment for sleep apnea, dental implants, and laser dentistry.

Fun Facts

Away from the office, you can find Dr. Mlodik spending time with her five boys, enjoying her family’s hobby farm, as well as camping and fishing.
