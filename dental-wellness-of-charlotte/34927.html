What are my options if I’m missing a tooth? Dental implants are considered the gold standard in tooth replacement because they replace both your tooth and root.

This is so important because without a tooth root, your jawbone has nothing to keep it stimulated, and it begins to deteriorate. This can diminish support for neighboring teeth, resulting in more tooth loss and impacting your facial structure.

How Dental Implants Work

A dental implant takes the place of your root and integrates with your bone because it s made of biocompatible titanium. It keeps your bone healthy while supporting your new tooth, a custom porcelain crown. Getting dental implants in South Charlotte is the closest replacement to your natural teeth.

A dental bridge may be a good solution for patients who aren t candidates for tooth implants. This fixed appliance uses two dental crowns as anchors to healthy teeth to support one or more prosthetic teeth in between, which "bridge" the gap left by your missing teeth.

How do dental implants differ from other tooth replacement options?

Dental implants , widely considered the gold standard for long-term tooth loss treatment, differ significantly from alternatives like dentures and bridges . Here are some of the most vital differences:

  • Structural integrity . Unlike dentures, which are removable, and bridges, which call for alterations to adjacent teeth, dental implants are surgically embedded into the jawbone, recreating the form and function of natural teeth and providing a stable and permanent foundation for replacements like dental crowns.
  • Bone health . Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone degradation that often accompanies tooth loss. Dentures and bridges do not address this issue.
  • Longevity . With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Dentures and bridges, meanwhile, typically require replacement every 7-15 years.
  • Superior chewing function . Implants offer superior chewing efficiency to dentures and bridges, allowing you to enjoy a full range of foods without restrictions.
  • Ease of maintenance . Dentures require regular removal for cleaning, while bridges require special tools for flossing. Implants, however, only require normal oral hygiene practices like brushing and flossing.
  • Comfort and confidence . Dental implants are anchored in place, eliminating the discomfort and risk of slipping or speech impediments common with dentures and allowing the wearer to bite, chew, speak, and smile with total confidence!
