Frequently Asked Questions

When should my child have an orthodontic evaluation? As a parent, you want to do everything you can to ensure that your little one s smile is healthy. Children should have their first orthodontic evaluation around seven or eight . While this may seem early, some children can benefit from early or interceptive orthodontics.

Phase One Orthodontic Treatment

Candidates for early orthodontic treatment typically have crowded teeth or severe bite concerns like a pronounced overbite, underbite, crossbite, or open bite. Bite misalignment can cause problems with your child s speech, chewing, dental health, and self-confidence. Phase one orthodontic treatment takes advantage of your child s growth and can correct bite problems and make space to allow future adult teeth to come in with the proper positioning.

Phase Two Ortho Treatment

Then, a future phase two of orthodontic treatment would focus mainly on tooth alignment when your child is 12 or 13 and would be a quicker and more straightforward treatment.

Not every child will benefit from early orthodontic treatment, but having the first evaluation at age seven will give you peace of mind knowing you re on track with creating a healthy and happy smile for your child in the future.

How Does Invisalign in South Charlotte Work?

If you and Dr. Shirman determine that invisible braces are right for you, he ll create your customized treatment plan with 3-D imaging. Then, Invisalign uses that plan to fabricate your clear aligners in sets that you ll change every two to three weeks.
