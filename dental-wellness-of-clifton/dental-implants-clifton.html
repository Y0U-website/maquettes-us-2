Dental Implants

Did we mention you can never get a cavity on an implant?!

  • Tooth Implant Restorations in Clifton, New Jersey If you have a tooth missing, Dr. Meytin may recommend replacing it with a dental implant. The implant is surgically placed into your bone, and a crown is then attached to the implant to replace your missing tooth. Dental implants are often our first choice and our standard of care to replacing missing or damaged teeth. Benefits of Dental Implants There are so many benefits to having an implant: They are predictable,
  • They are strong and built to last,
  • They protect your healthy bone and adjacent teeth,
  • They allow you to speak easily and eat your favorite foods,
  • They allow you retain your natural face shape and smile,
  • They are truly the next best thing to natural teeth!
