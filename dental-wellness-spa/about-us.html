About Dr. Umar Mirza

He wants you and your family to feel at ease in his care, and confident in the guidance he has to offer. Prior to going to dental school in New York, Dr. Mirza was a college professor at many different institutions in New Jersey and Philadelphia. This has taught him how to communicate effectively with patients and educate them properly to help them make decisions about their treatment.

Dr. Mirza was mentored in endodontics from world renowned Dr. Brett Gilbert and routinely does molar root canals. Dr. Mirza can also do orthodontics with 3d printed braces using Lightforce along with Invisalign . When away from dentistry, Dr. Mirza likes to spend time with his wife and two girls. He enjoys playing and watching different sports and took part in the pickleball craze. He’s a lifelong student of knowledge.
