The All-on-X Treatment Process

Evaluation

Dr. Manmode takes 3D CBCT scans and digital impressions, then designs your custom implant placement plan using advanced treatment planning software.

Preparation

On surgery day, Dr. Manmode administers local anesthesia and sedation, then carefully extracts any remaining damaged teeth from the arch.

Implant Placement

Dr. Manmode places 4-6 titanium implants at precisely calculated angles, using tilted posterior posts to maximize bone contact and stability.

Same-Day Prosthesis

Our on-site denture lab fabricates your provisional arch while you rest, and Dr. Manmode secures it to the implants before you leave.

Final Restoration

After 4-6 months of osseointegration, Dr. Manmode places your permanent custom prosthesis crafted from high-strength zirconia or porcelain.

Side Effects and Risks

All-on-X dental implant surgery is a well-established procedure with high success rates exceeding 95 % in clinical studies. However, as with any surgical procedure, patients should understand the potential risks and side effects.

Common side effects (resolve within 1-2 weeks): Swelling and bruising around the jaw and cheeks, mild to moderate discomfort managed with prescribed pain medication, minor bleeding at the surgical sites during the first 24-48 hours, temporary numbness near the implant sites, and difficulty eating hard foods during the initial healing phase.

Less common risks: Implant failure to integrate with the bone (occurs in fewer than 3-5 % of cases), infection at the surgical site requiring antibiotics, temporary or rarely permanent nerve damage causing altered sensation, sinus complications for upper arch implants placed near the sinus cavity, and prosthesis fracture during the provisional phase.

Dr. Manmode minimizes these risks through meticulous 3D-guided surgical planning, sterile technique, and comprehensive post-operative monitoring. Any complications are addressed immediately at our Longwood office.
