Meet Dr. Couret

Dr. Couret

Once Dr. Couret, our dentist in Chicago, IL, had completed dental school, he pledged to never stop learning, and remains committed to the latest cutting-edge technology and advances in the field of dentistry.

The Story

From a very early age, my father taught me the value of hard work. My dad owned Shell gas stations and started bringing me to work when I was five. By age seven, I was pumping gas. I remember when I would run outside to greet a customer, my father would remind me to “look enthusiastic, pick up your feet, and smile!” He taught me that every person who pulled into the station deserved an amazing customer experience.

I loved every minute of working at the gas station. My early experience taught me that I wanted a career working directly with people. One day in college, I needed to see a dentist, so I asked around and got a recommendation to see a dentist named Dr. Frank Munoz. When I walked into his office, the staff were welcoming, friendly, and greeted me by name. They genuinely seemed like they all loved their jobs. For the first time, I felt a healthcare provider truly cared about me.

Then his hygienist gave me the best teeth cleaning that I had ever received in my life! Perhaps because my grandfather had been a dentist and the idea was somehow embedded in my subconscious, but that day I decided to become a dentist.

Since then, I’ve built my dream office. This was not done alone – I am surrounded by the best team. Most importantly, I could not have achieved my dream without the support of my wife, Sarah, who has always been my biggest cheerleader. As the proud owner of Dente Complete Dentistry, I look forward to meeting you and helping you achieve the smile of your dreams.

Education and Credentials:

A 1994 graduate of Indiana University School of Business, I then graduated from University of Iowa College of Dentistry in 2001 with a DDS.

I’m a member of the Chicago Dental Society and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry as well as the Illinois Dental Society and the American Dental Society.

Additionally, I was trained and am a member of DOCS (The Dental Organization for Conscious Sedation).
