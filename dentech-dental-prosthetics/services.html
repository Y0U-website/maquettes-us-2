Service FAQs

Common questions from practices about materials, submissions, and turnaround.

What materials do you offer for crowns and bridges?

We support common restorative materials including zirconia and porcelain options. Material selection is confirmed per your prescription and case requirements.

Do you accept intraoral scans?

Yes. We support digital submissions and can assist with scan review and case planning to help improve consistency.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Turnaround depends on case type and complexity. We provide clear expectations at intake and communicate promptly if any details are needed.

Can you help with shade matching?

Yes. We can advise on shade selection and characterization and coordinate on photos and shade information when provided.

How do you handle adjustments or remakes?

If an adjustment is needed, we document the request and work with your team on next steps, timelines, and any additional information required.

Do you serve practices outside your local area?

Yes. We work with practices nationwide and support shipping and communication workflows for out-of-area cases.
