What is a Root Canal?

A root canal (endodontic therapy) is a treatment used to save a tooth that’s severely infected or damaged . Instead of removing the tooth, the infected nerve and pulp are carefully cleaned out, the inside is disinfected, and the tooth is sealed to prevent future problems. A crown is then placed on top to restore strength and function.
