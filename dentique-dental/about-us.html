Meet Our Team

Dr. Bich Dao

Dr. Bich Dao is a compassionate and ethical dental care provider dedicated to delivering high-quality dentistry while building meaningful, long-lasting relationships with his patients. He believes exceptional care starts with trust, open communication, and ensuring every patient feels comfortable, informed, and respected throughout their visit.

Read More

Dr. Dao earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from LECOM School of Dental Medicine in Bradenton, Florida, and completed his Bachelor of Science in Clinical Professions at Florida State University, where he graduated Summa Cum Laude.

Committed to serving others, Dr. Dao has volunteered with several community outreach programs, including Mission of Mercy, Remote Area Medical, and Give Kids A Smile. These experiences have reinforced his passion for making quality dental care accessible to patients of all backgrounds.

Dr. Dao is fluent in English and conversational in Vietnamese, allowing him to better connect with a diverse patient community.

Outside of the office, Dr. Dao enjoys staying active, traveling to new places, and discovering new foods.
