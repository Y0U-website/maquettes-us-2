Dental Implants in Manhattan

Our doctors and staff are known for being genuinely friendly and caring, and they work hard to ensure you are comfortable at all times. We offer many sedation options, including oral and intravenous (IV) sedation, to help you feel totally at ease.

Your new implant teeth will be created in our in-house dental lab. Throughout the design process, you will be consulted to ensure we make your new teeth to your liking. We use the best materials when making your top-quality new teeth.

We work hard to make our implant treatment affordable so you can enjoy the life-changing benefits implants provide. We also work with Proceed Finance, CareCredit and Cherry to provide no- and low-interest financing options.

State-of-the-Art Technology

We have invested in the latest digital dental technologies to make your treatment efficient, precise and comfortable.

Preparing for implant treatment starts with a dental cone beam CT scan of your mouth and jaw area. Dr. Blum identifies the optimal areas for implant placement and uses special 3D software to simulate the surgery in advance to ensure everything comes out exactly as intended.
