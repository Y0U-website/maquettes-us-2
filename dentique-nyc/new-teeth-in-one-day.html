Yes, All-on-X is far superior to dentures. Many denture wearers feel that their dentures never seem to fit right, no matter how often they get them realigned. The reason for this is that when teeth are missing, the bone that held them in place begins to shrink away. While the jawbone that supports the dentures is constantly receding, the pressure that dentures put on the gum ridge actually accelerates this bone-loss process, causing the slipping problem to just keep getting worse.

All-on-X uses dental implants that preserve the jawbone and prevent the sunken in facial look denture wearers often have. The new teeth never need to come out and stay put for comfortable chewing and confident smiling.

Caption: Bone loss occurs as a result of leaving teeth unreplaced and wearing dentures. Eventually, so much bone is lost that the lower part of the face collapses in on itself. The chin moves toward the nose, and the cheeks sink in, causing deep wrinkles and folds.

The All-on-X procedure is specially designed to help patients who have experienced bone loss in their jaw. The treatment can use long implants that are angled when placed to reach the highest density bone available. This gives the implants maximum bone coverage and support. As a result, even patients with severe bone loss can often receive All-on-X.

Even though it is uncommon with these types of cases, Dr. Blum can provide expert bone grafting when needed to ensure a stable foundation for implant placement.
