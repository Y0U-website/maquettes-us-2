The practice

Are you dealing with missing teeth? At Dentique Smiles , we have the solution you’ve been looking for—dental implants! Dr. Waleed and Dr. Haitham Salman specialize in restoring your teeth and improving your quality of life through dental implants.

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. Here’s why they could be the right option for you:

Stability and Health : The implant post serves as an artificial tooth root, anchoring securely to your jawbone. It not only provides stability for your restoration but also stimulates the bone tissue, keeping it healthy and strong.

Versatility : Implant restorations can be customized to suit your unique needs, whether you need a single crown, a dental bridge, or a denture.

Dental implants are intended to be permanent replacements for missing teeth. To keep them stable and functional, follow these care steps:

Daily Oral Care : Maintain your implants just like your natural teeth with regular brushing and flossing.

Almost anyone who has lost a tooth or teeth can benefit from implant dentistry. If your teeth and gums are healthy, you are likely a suitable candidate. However, if you have pre-existing dental conditions like tooth decay or gum disease, our dentists will recommend treatment for those issues before proceeding with implants.

If you have questions about implant dentistry or want to schedule a consultation with our experienced dentists, please contact our office today. We’re excited to help you regain a healthy, beautiful smile!
