Meet our Team of Dentists .

Dr. Jessica Su - DMD

Dr. Jessica Su is a talented and compassionate dental practitioner who is committed to providing her patients with the best possible care. She has extensive experience in all aspects of adult and pediatric general dentistry, as well as a degree from Temple University School of Dentistry. Dr. Su is dedicated to providing customized care and takes the time to walk her patients through treatment and support them in reaching and keeping a confident, beautiful smile. Visit her in Houston today and receive the outstanding dental care you deserve.

Dr Benjamin Kee.

Dr. Benjamin Kee is experienced in comprehensive dental care services including dental exam, surgical extractions with socket preservation, dental fillings, root canal therapy(including molar endo), and root planning. Fluent in English, Mandarin, and Cantonese.

Dr. Pauline Yang

Dr. Pauline Yang is trained in CEREC CAD/CAM, invisalign, digital photography, and digital radiography. Performed procedures including crown and bridge prosthetics, double-cord PVS impressions, fabrication of acrylic provisionals, and pediatric dentistry. Fluent in speaking Taiwanese, Mandarin, and English.

Dr. Dalton Huynh

Dr. Huynh loves interacting with his patients and teaching them about oral healthcare and helping them practice better oral hygiene. He looks forward to staying up to date with the latest advances in dentistry in order to provide the best possible care for his patients.
