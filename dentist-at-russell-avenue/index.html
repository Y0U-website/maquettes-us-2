Meet Dr. Junhyung Woo DDS and our dedicated team, committed to providing family-oriented dental care in a warm, inclusive environment for our diverse community.

Dr. Woo moved to the United States from South Korea at the age of 14. He went on to earn his degree in Bioengineering from the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), followed by his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Southern California.

Committed to staying at the forefront of dental innovation, Dr. Woo actively pursues continuing education and advanced training. His approach to dentistry is centered on each patient’s needs, blending knowledge, precision, and compassion to provide the highest quality of care.

Outside the office, Dr. Woo enjoys an active family life with his wife, Claire, and their young son, Harry. He also loves hiking, playing tennis, and spending time with loved ones.
