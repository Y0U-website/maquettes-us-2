The practice

Dr. Birch has always had dual passions for art and healthcare. Coming from a family of artists and musicians, he has also always loved helping people and being of service to others. When he first got his taste of the dental field working in an orthodontic lab in college, he discovered that dentistry could be that perfect blend of art and healthcare—and he’s been hooked ever since.

Dr. Birch attended Arizona State University for his undergraduate studies, where he majored in Psychology. He then went on to attend Nova Southeastern College of Dental Medicine where he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine Degree.

In his spare time, Dr. Birch plays keyboard, guitar, and sings in a cover band, playing shows a few times each month in local venues around the valley. He also enjoys wakeboarding, snowboarding, and traveling with his wife and family—especially to places where they can snorkel or scuba dive.
