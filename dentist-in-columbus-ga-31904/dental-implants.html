What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts that serve as artificial tooth roots. They are placed in your jaw to support a crown, bridge, or denture. Because they fuse with your bone, implants offer unmatched strength and stability compared to other teeth replacement options.

Whether you have a single missing tooth or multiple missing teeth, implants help preserve your bone and gums while delivering beautiful smiles.

How Can Dental Implants Save Your Smile?

Missing teeth can deeply affect your oral health and confidence, but dental implants provide a lasting solution. At TLC Dental, Dr. Collier offers advanced implant treatment designed to restore both comfort and beauty to your smile.

A dental implant works as an artificial tooth root, replacing what was lost. During implant placement, a titanium post is inserted into the jawbone. Over a healing period called osseointegration, the implant fuses with your bone, providing a secure foundation.

An abutment is then connected to hold your new tooth—whether that is a single crown, bridge, or implant-supported dentures.

Unlike dentures, dental implants are permanent and virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth. Many patients find the procedure straightforward, and with a personalized treatment plan, you will regain your ability to chew, speak, and smile with full confidence.

Implants FAQs

Our team answers your questions about dental implants in Columbus, GA.

  • How long does the treatment take? Your dental implant treatment can take three (lower jaw) to six (upper jaw) months. The longer time frame is required to give your implant and jawbone time to fuse fully. However, everyone heals differently, so Dr. Collier will monitor your progress closely.
  • How long do implants last? Implants last longer than most restorative solutions but need to be cared for properly. You will need to follow a proper hygiene routine, just like with your natural teeth. When implants are appropriately maintained, and good oral health exists, they can last a lifetime.
  • How much do implants cost? The cost varies depending on the number of implants you need, the condition of your teeth and jawbone, and the type of replacement teeth you chose.
