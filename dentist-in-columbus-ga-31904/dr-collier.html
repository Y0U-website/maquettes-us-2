Away From Dentistry

As a family man, Dr. Collier loves spending time with his wife and kids. He is an avid hunter, fisherman, and enjoys being in the great outdoors. In the Fall, his house is divided rooting for the Alabama Crimson Tide and the Georgia Bulldogs. In the Spring and Summer, he tries to work on his golf game but more often, it works on him!

His Hero

Dr. Collier’s hero is his father. He was a hard-working man who spent 43 years as a railroad worker to support his family and allow his children to get an education. His work ethic and sense of responsibility are at the core of who Dr. Collier tries to be every day.

Interesting Fact

Now, this is interesting Dr. Collier once taught himself to make rope hammocks and sold them as a side business.
