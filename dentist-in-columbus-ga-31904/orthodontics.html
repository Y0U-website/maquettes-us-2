Comfortable and clear aligners

Clear aligners provide nearly invisible treatment, allowing patients to improve their smile without anyone noticing. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign® aligners use smooth trays instead of wires and brackets.

Removable aligner trays

Aligner trays allow normal eating, brushing, and flossing, making preventative care and daily dental care easier. These removable trays support a healthy routine and improve overall wellness while keeping your teeth clean.

Customized for your smile goals

Every set of Invisalign® aligners is custom-made to support your smile goals. At our Columbus office, Dr. Collier creates personalized care solutions so patients can achieve a beautiful smile they feel proud to share.

Comfortable care and effective results

The advanced clear aligners gently shift teeth, offering comfortable care throughout treatment. Many patients expect noticeable improvements within months. Made from SmartTrack® material, these Invisalign® clear aligners may reduce treatment time.

What Is Invisalign®?

Invisalign® uses a series of clear aligners and custom aligner trays to move teeth into better alignment. Unlike braces, Invisalign® offers discreet treatment with removable trays.
