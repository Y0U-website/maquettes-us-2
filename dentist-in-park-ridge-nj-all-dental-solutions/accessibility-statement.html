Accessibility statement

We aim for our website to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, which outlines how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities. While we strive to adhere to these guidelines, some areas of the website may not yet fully comply. We are actively working to address these issues.
