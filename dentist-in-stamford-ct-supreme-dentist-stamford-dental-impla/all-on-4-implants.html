What Are All-On-4 Implants?

A common misconception about dental implants is that they are the same as replacing multiple missing teeth with a single metal rod. While this may be an option for some, many people want their smiles restored by getting all new tooth replacement in one procedure and not spread over time like before! That’s why we offer All On 4 Implant Systems which use less precious metals than traditional full jaw surgeries do so you can enjoy better oral health without spending more money upfront or waiting around on pain medications while our team replaces what needs fixing up right away.

The Benefits of All-On-4 Implants

All-on 4® is a low cost, minimally invasive method that restores function and confidence for many people. At Supreme Dental, the dentist uses computer guided technology in order to see where there are high levels of bone without needing grafted material which reduces costs as well as recovery time on patients while restoring their dental health with this quick procedure.

Your All-On-4 Specialist in Stamford, CT

Permanent Solution

With All-on 4 implants , you replace all your missing teeth with a permanent prosthodontic device that never needs to be removed. Our doctors surgically implant small titanium posts at strategic points along the upper or lower jaw and these implants not only hold dentures in place but also promote healthy new bone tissue growth so there’s less risk for edentulous people who may have sunken facial features after losing their teeth.

Healthier Eating

The loss of a functional bite can be very tough for people who are edentulous. For many, the only thing that is left when it comes to eating well-balanced meals and enjoying healthy foods like meats or vegetables in their raw form is through All On 4 implant surgery which allows them not just one but two permanent teeth back where they were missing before.

Decrease in Gum Disease

Permanent tooth replacement is a great option for those who want to avoid gum disease. With All-on 4, you will be able brush and floss as if your natural teeth where present which goes an extremely long way in maintaining oral health.
