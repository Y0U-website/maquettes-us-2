All On 6 Dental Implants Procedure

A perfect smile starts with a scan. The first step of any dental procedure is to take x-rays and scans, which will help us identify the best course for your unique dental needs as well as show how much bone loss you’ve had over time. We’ll make impressions if necessary after this process so that when it comes time to restore or replace teeth in their rightful place they will fit like never before.

All on 6 dental implants are a type of implant-supported denture that uses only six dental implants to support an entire arch of teeth. This procedure, which offers a more solid and natural-looking alternative to standard dentures, is a successful and popular treatment for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth.

The All on 6 dental implant procedure typically begins with a thorough consultation and examination to establish whether or not the patient is a good candidate for the surgery. Once the patient has been cleared for treatment, the dentist will carefully put six dental implants into the jawbone for maximum support. Over time, the implants will merge with the bone, forming a strong and sturdy basis for the denture.

The dentist will affix a custom-made denture to the implants once they have healed. The denture is designed to fit snugly over the implants, resulting in a set of teeth that is both natural-looking and functional. In addition, the All-on-6 procedure can be utilized on both the upper and lower arches, giving patients who have lost all or most of their teeth a complete set of teeth.

Advantages:

When compared to regular dentures, all six dental implants offer numerous advantages. They provide a more sturdy and secure solution that is less prone to slipping or moving while eating or speaking. They also produce a more natural-looking grin, increasing self-esteem and confidence. Furthermore, the implants help to protect the jawbone by stimulating bone growth, which can occur with regular dentures.

Many dental clinics offer All on 6 dental implants, and choosing a qualified and experienced dentist to perform the procedure is essential. The cost of All on 6 implants can vary depending on factors such as the dentist’s experience, the materials used, and the clinic’s location.

The Benefits of All On 6 Implants

  • It is better than removable dentures : Because your dentures won’t slip or move around in the mouth, you can eat and drink as much as is pleasing for a full hour before needing braces! You’ll still need to brush teeth every day with normal toothpaste.
  • It is a long-lasting dental intervention. Dentures are a pain to wear and require constant maintenance. They only last 3-6 months, which is why most people end up going back for replacements every year or two! Dental implants on the other hand offer you 20 years of service with one procedure instead of needing multiple visits from different professionals.
  • You can bite and chew any foods. Your implants are screwed into the bone of your jaw and they won’t go anywhere! Just like natural teeth, they function normally.
