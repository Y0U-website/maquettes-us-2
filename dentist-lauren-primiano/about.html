What to expect

Your Personalized Care Experience

Once you schedule your appointment at Dr. Primiano at Cornerstone Dentistry, you can fill out your forms online to save some time. Then, just head over to our dental office when it’s time for your first visit.

You’ll meet with our front office team and fill out any necessary forms. Then, you’ll go back to one of our comfortable patient operatories. One of our dental hygienists will gently clean your teeth and perform an initial checkup. We may take x-rays if necessary, too. Then, Dr. Primiano will enter the room and perform an in-depth oral exam. She’ll discuss her findings with you, and answer any questions you may have about your oral health.

If everything looks good, you’ll schedule your next six-month checkup and then go home. But if Dr. Primiano notices any areas of concern, she’ll discuss them with you and go over your treatment options. Then, you can schedule the follow-up care you need to keep your smile healthy.

WHAT TO BRING

Besides your smile, you’ll want to bring your insurance card (if applicable), as well as a method of payment if any co-pays or other costs are required for your checkup. That’s it!
