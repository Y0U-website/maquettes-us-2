Frequently Asked Questions

Check out these frequently asked questions, or call us to speak with our team.

What should I do if an Invisalign button comes off?

Sometimes Invisalign buttons on teeth wear away or pop off. These can be replaced at your next regular appointment. We ask that you call the office in advance to let us know, so the dentist has adequate time to replace the missing buttons.

What if a color ring/donut is missing from my bracket?

Sometimes teeth do not have the color ring/donut on the bracket. Instead, string, wire, springs, or hooks may be in place and can be difficult to see. A missing color ring is not an emergency, but it may delay movement of a single tooth. If your appointment is more than a week away, please call the office to schedule an appointment.

What should I do if a bracket falls off?

Keep the bracket that has fallen off. If your appointment is more than a week away, please call the office to schedule an appointment.

What should I do if a wire is poking me?

First, check to see if the middle of the wire, either a black or red line, is centered between the two front teeth. If not, use tweezers or your fingers to move the line to between the two front teeth. Move the wire in the direction opposite of the side being poked. If both sides are poking, dry the area as best you can and apply wax. If your appointment is more than a week away and you have tried both steps above, please call the office to schedule an appointment.
