THE DENTAL IMPLANT PROCESS

We believe in a measured once, cut once philosophy. At our Livingston office, we use advanced 3D imaging to plan your procedure with extreme precision, ensuring the best possible outcome.

  • Initial Consultation: We use 3D CBCT scans to assess your bone density and map out the exact placement of the implant.
  • Precise Placement: The implant post is gently placed into the jawbone. We offer various sedation options to ensure you are completely comfortable.
  • Osseointegration: Over a few months, the post fuses with your bone, creating a permanent bond.
  • The Final Crown: Once healed, we place a custom-crafted Honest Crown on top that perfectly matches your surrounding teeth.

RECOVERY & LONG-TERM CARE

One of the biggest benefits of dental implants is that they are incredibly easy to maintain. Once the healing process is complete, you care for them just like your natural teeth.

  • Initial Healing: Most patients report only minor discomfort for a few days following placement, easily managed with over-the-counter medications.
  • Simple Hygiene: No special cleaners are required, just regular brushing and flossing.
  • Routine Checkups: We monitor your implant during your regular teeth cleanings at our Livingston office to ensure it remains healthy and stable.
