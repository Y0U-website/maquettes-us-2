Our Orthodontic Services in Livingston, New Jersey

At The Honest Crown, we offer a full range of orthodontic treatments, from traditional braces to clear aligner options.

Traditional Braces

Metal braces remain the most effective option for complex cases. We use modern brackets that are smaller and more comfortable than older designs, without compromising on results.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners straighten your teeth with a series of custom-fitted, nearly invisible trays. They are removable, easy to clean around, and a popular choice for teens and adults who want a discreet option.

Retainers

After active treatment ends, retainers keep your teeth in their new position. We provide fixed and removable options depending on what works best for your case.

Early Orthodontic Evaluation

We recommend a first orthodontic evaluation around age seven. Catching issues early gives us more options and often leads to shorter, simpler treatment later on.

Orthodontic Care for the Whole Family

Orthodontic treatment is not just for teenagers. We see patients across all age groups and tailor every plan to where they are in life.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Most cases take between 12 and 24 months. Simple cases can be shorter. Your treatment timeline depends on the complexity of your alignment issues.

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

For mild-to-moderate cases, yes. Complex bite issues may still require traditional braces. We will recommend the right option after a full assessment.

Does orthodontic treatment hurt?

Soreness after adjustments is normal and usually fades within a day or two. Modern braces and aligners are far more comfortable than older systems.

Does insurance cover orthodontics?

Many plans include partial orthodontic coverage, especially for patients under 18. We will check your benefits before your consultation so you know what to expect.
