General & Preventive Dentistry

How often should I visit a dentist in Culver City for a cleaning?

Patients should visit the dentist every 3, 4, or 6 months for professional cleanings and exams to prevent cavities, gum disease, and other oral health issues. Frequency is dependent on existing oral health conditions and home care.

What happens during a dental exam near Culver City?

A comprehensive dental exam is scheduled for 90 minutes and typically includes digital X-rays, oral cancer screening, gum evaluation, photos and a full assessment of your teeth, TMJ and bite to detect issues early. The doctor does a thorough review of your medical history and recommends hygiene tools and products specific to your conditions and lifestyle. The doctor will also review your oral health goals to ensure we are on the right path attain them.

What is a deep cleaning and do I need one?

A deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) is recommended if you have signs of gum disease. It removes plaque and bacteria below the gumline to restore gum health. Due to the aggressive nature of the disease, a deep cleaning appointment will involve some method of bacterial decontamination either involving a laser treatment or antibiotics topically placed within the infected areas.

Are dental sealants worth it for adults and kids?

Yes, dental sealants are a preventive treatment that protects molars from cavities, especially beneficial for children and cavity-prone adults.

Are dental implants the best option for missing teeth?

Dental implants are considered one of the best solutions for missing teeth because they look, feel, and function like natural teeth while preserving jawbone health.

What is All-on-4 or All-on-X dental implants?

All-on-4 (or All-on-X) is a full-arch restoration technique that replaces an entire set of teeth using a small number of strategically placed implants.

Invisalign & Orthodontics

How does Invisalign work?

Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gradually straighten teeth without the need for traditional metal braces. It is more hygienic and esthetic than traditional braces but 100 % based on patient compliance.

Do I need to align my teeth?

Teeth alignment isn’t just about appearance—it also affects your oral health and function. You may benefit from aligning your teeth if you have crowding, gaps, bite issues (overbite, underbite, crossbite), or difficulty cleaning between teeth.

Proper alignment makes it easier to maintain good oral hygiene, reduces the risk of cavities and gum disease, and helps prevent uneven wear, jaw strain, and discomfort over time.

How long does Invisalign take?

Treatment typically takes 6 to 18 months depending on the complexity of your case.

Is Invisalign better than braces?

Invisalign is a popular alternative to braces for adults and teens who want a more discreet and comfortable orthodontic solution.

Gum Disease & Periodontal Care

What are the signs of gum disease?

Common signs include bleeding gums, bad breath, gum recession, and loose teeth. Early treatment is key to preventing tooth loss.

How serious is gum disease?

Gum disease can range from mild to very serious if left untreated. In its early stage (gingivitis), it may cause red, swollen, or bleeding gums. However, it can progress into periodontitis, a more advanced condition that can lead to gum recession, bone loss, and even tooth loss.

Beyond your mouth, gum disease has been linked to overall health concerns such as heart disease, diabetes complications, and inflammation in the body.

How is gum disease treated?

Treatment may include deep cleanings, laser therapy, and at-home care to eliminate bacteria and restore gum health.

What is TMJ treatment in Culver City?

TMJ treatment focuses on relieving jaw pain, headaches, and bite issues through custom appliances and bite alignment therapies. Dr G uses a nonsurgical approach via physiologic dentistry.

Can a dentist help with sleep apnea?

Yes, dentists offer oral sleep appliances that help keep the airway open, reducing snoring and symptoms of mild to moderate sleep apnea.

What is a nightguard and do I need one?

A nightguard is a custom device worn during sleep to protect teeth from grinding (bruxism) and reduce jaw pain.
