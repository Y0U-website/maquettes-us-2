SCULPTING SMILES: THE PROCESS OF COSMETIC DENTISTRY

The cosmetic dentistry process typically begins with a consultation with our best cosmetic dentist Dr. Maya Roshan in Washington, DC, where your goals and concerns are discussed, and a personalized treatment plan is developed. Here’s a breakdown of the process:

  • Initial Consultation: During the initial consultation, you’ll discuss your desired outcomes and undergo a comprehensive examination to evaluate your oral health and identify any issues that need addressing.
  • Treatment Planning: Based on the examination findings and your goals, the cosmetic dentist will create a tailored treatment plan outlining the recommended procedures and their sequence.
  • Procedure Selection: Depending on your specific needs, various cosmetic dentistry procedures may be recommended. These can include teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, orthodontics, gum contouring, dental implants, or a combination of treatments.
  • Preparation: Before certain procedures, such as veneers or dental crowns, the dentist may need to prepare your teeth by removing a small amount of enamel to ensure a proper fit and optimal results.
