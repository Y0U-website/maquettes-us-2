Aesthetic dentistry begins with design. We will take digital images of your face and teeth from several angles to create a platform for the design of your future smile. We design using a digital clone of the patient made up of photos, x-rays, and three-dimensional imagery. We then digitally alter the image to explore with you what will be your perfect smile.

Once you have decided on how you want your smile to look, we will provide you with a treatment plan that will show you the number and types of procedures that you will need to achieve it. We will map out a plan and a timeframe that we can use to achieve your dream smile.
