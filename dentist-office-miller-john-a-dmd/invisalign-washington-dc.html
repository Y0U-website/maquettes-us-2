Invisalign

Invisalign, also known as clear aligners, are removable orthodontic appliances designed to gradually shift teeth into the proper position. The virtually invisible design is perfect for adults looking to align their smiles without the look of metal brackets or wires.

These clear plastic trays are designed with a thermoplastic material that fits over your teeth. Each set of trays is worn for about two weeks before moving on to the next set in the series. Gradually, as the aligner is replaced every two weeks, the teeth will move closer to their final positions.

The Benefits of Invisalign

Since there are no metal parts or brackets, the aligners are much more comfortable than traditional braces. In addition, they are removable and can be taken out to eat and clean your teeth. This also helps straighten teeth faster than conventional braces because you aren’t tempted to eat sticky foods that can get stuck in your braces and make straightening take longer.

The Invisalign treatment process involves taking a series of custom-made, clear aligners called “trays.” Each tray is designed to fit over the teeth and gradually move the teeth into the desired position. Every few weeks, you will switch to the next set of trays in the sequence and continue this process until your teeth are as straight as they can be!

At your initial consultation, we will discuss the entire process with you and answer any questions you may have. We also take impressions to ensure that we fabricate the correct size of trays for you to wear throughout your entire treatment.

Not everyone is comfortable wearing the metal braces often associated with orthodontic treatments. The clear plastic aligners are virtually invisible, so no one needs to know that you are undergoing treatment unless you tell them. Benefits of Invisalign®

Unlike traditional metal braces, Invisalign® aligners are completely removable and virtually invisible. Aside from being a convenient option for busy professionals, this can also reduce a patient’s risk of experiencing demineralization or decay.

Another benefit of Invisalign® is that they’re custom-made for each set of teeth. This consideration prevents irritation around the sensitive gums and reduces the risk of tooth trauma that could occur due to metal brackets. The smooth plastic of the aligners also prevents abrasions on the cheeks that can cause soreness. This can be especially helpful when it comes to patients who are dealing with anxiety about their treatment.

Invisalign FAQs

Invisalign is a type of clear aligner that uses clear, removable aligners to straighten teeth discreetly. Unlike braces that use brackets and wires to reposition the teeth, Invisalign uses a series of custom-made aligners that are worn over the teeth to straighten them gradually. Each set of aligners is worn for about two weeks and replaced with the next set in the series.

If you need to straighten your teeth before a big event that can be spoiled by crooked teeth, then Invisalign is the better choice. It’s also a great option for teens who don’t want to wear traditional metal braces during their formative years.

An adult with mild to moderately severe misalignment may also choose Invisalign over metal braces. No matter which option you choose, we will make sure you still get the beautiful smile of dreams!

The aligners are made from a clear plastic material, so they’re hardly visible to other people when you’re wearing them. In fact, people may not even notice you’re wearing them at all!

The entire process typically takes nine to fifteen months to complete. But the treatment time depends on the severity of your condition.

Patients should be wearing their aligners for at least 22 hours a day for the best results. If patients are not doing so, they may not see optimal results with their treatment. However, it’s up to the patient to decide how much they want to wear their aligners and how often they change them out. Your dentist or orthodontist will give you specific instructions on how to care for your aligners to achieve the best results.

Invisalign is a discreet orthodontic solution that corrects a wide variety of dental issues. It can be used to correct crooked teeth, overbites, underbites, and crossbites.
