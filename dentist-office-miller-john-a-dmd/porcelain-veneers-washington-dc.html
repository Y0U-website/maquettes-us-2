The Benefits of Veneers

Veneers are thin, tooth-shaped shells that fit over the front of your teeth to correct chips, gaps, stains, and other imperfections. Unlike crowns, veneers don’t require extensive shaping or preparation on your tooth before placement. They’re also stain-resistant and can last for years before needing replacement. For these reasons, veneers are often considered one of the most conservative cosmetic dentistry treatments.

Comparing Porcelain Veneers and Prepless Veneers

Both veneers and prepless veneers are made from incredibly thin, tough porcelain, and when properly cared for and placed, they will offer you a stunning smile you may enjoy for many years. Besides, both kinds of veneers can fix the same aesthetic issues, such as chipped, gaps, long or short teeth, misaligned teeth, stains, unevenly proportioned teeth, and worn enamel.

And every veneer treatment begins with the dentist taking impressions of the patient’s teeth.

The patient was dissatisfied with her initial set of 4 veneers. We switched the original four veneers for smaller alternatives that flawlessly blended in to result in a natural smile.

Then comes the difference between the procedures.

The process of getting a prepless veneer involves cleaning and sanding the teeth to get rid of any shine, surface stains, and small enamel flaws. This is all that has to be done to your enamel to prepare it for your personalized veneers. Prepless veneers are also extremely thin; hence, the shade of each veneer is chosen carefully to complement the natural underlying tooth enamel.

On the other hand, your natural teeth undergo a small amount of permanent enamel removal during the placement of porcelain veneers so that each tooth can fit the veneer well.
