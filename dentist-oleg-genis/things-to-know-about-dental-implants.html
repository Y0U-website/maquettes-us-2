The Truth About Are Dental Implants Safe?

Dental implant surgery is safe for most patients except in rare cases because of specific medical conditions. Overall, it is a widespread and successful dental procedure.

Your Philadelphia D entist will go over your medical records and explain all the steps and details of implant surgery.

  • There is little to no risk associated with dental implants for a healthy person. However, if someone is already suffering from another dental problem or gum disease or has another unrelated health issue, such as diabetes, implant surgery may not be ideal.
  • It is essential to check for other dental problems and get the dental implant only when oral health is good.
  • Dental implant surgery is also safe for healthy older adults who want a more permanent alternative to dentures.

Bottom Line Can Dental Implants Be Removed?

Yes, dental implants can be removed. In most cases, dental implant surgery is successful, but there are some rare situations in which the surgery needs adjusting. Complications may develop in rare cases when the implant may need to be removed.

Without complications, your dental implant should last a lifetime.

  • Your dentist may do a revision implant surgery to remove a misaligned dental implant or adjust the position.
  • There is a need to remove the implant if an infection has caused failure in the implant properly healing and attaching into place.
  • Sometimes, the patient’s body rejects the implant; in this case, it must be removed.
  • An implant may need to be removed if it has become loose because of injury, trauma, or general gum complications.
