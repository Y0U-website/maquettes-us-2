Dental Office Forms

Manage your practice safely with our benchmark office forms.

As a member of the National Society of Dental Practitioners and a Dentist s Advantage client, you have access to a library of dental consent and record keeping forms. Each are available in English and Spanish and available as PDFs for download.

We recommend downloading, saving, printing and making copies of the forms you wish to use in your practice. Be sure to always keep the signed original of a completed form on file, and release only copies of that form as necessary.

  • Effectively and efficiently organize patient care,
  • Keep fully informed about the patient s health status, and
  • Provide the necessary documentation in the event of a malpractice lawsuit.
