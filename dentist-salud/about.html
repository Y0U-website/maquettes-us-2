Dr. Carlos Baquero Nunez

Dr. Baquero continues the legacy his mother began, carrying forward the tradition of family-first, bilingual dentistry while incorporating state-of-the-art technology.

He is highly trained in implant dentistry and GentleWave® root canal therapy, helping patients access minimally invasive, comfortable solutions that protect their natural teeth. Dr. Carlos is also a leader in using digital technology, including CBCT scans and Trios® digital impressions, to deliver accurate diagnoses and efficient treatment planning.

Beyond dentistry, Dr. Baquero loves racing go-karts, playing video games, and staying active by playing tennis with his dad. On weekends, he and his partner enjoy exploring new restaurants around Charlotte, often with their dog, Scooby Doo, by their side.

Dr. Sara Nunez Baquero

Dr. Sara brings a modern perspective and advanced training to Dentist Salud, carrying on her family’s mission while guiding the practice into new areas of dentistry. She is certified in GentleWave root canal therapy and Invisalign, allowing her to pair clinical precision with a natural ability to put patients at ease. With a special passion for aesthetics and restorative care, Dr. Sara often leads full-mouth rehabilitation cases, working closely with the practice’s in-house lab technician to deliver beautiful, long-lasting results.

What patients love most about Dr. Sara is her approachable style. She takes time to explain treatments in clear, simple terms, in Spanish or English, so care feels easy to understand and never intimidating. For Charlotte’s bilingual community, that clarity and compassion make all the difference.

Outside the office, Dr. Sara is full of energy and loves staying active. She enjoys kickboxing, running, yoga, and long walks with her doodle, Scrappy Doo. She’s also an avid traveler and loves to dance, discover new restaurants, and spend time exploring Charlotte’s vibrant food and culture scene.

Meet our team

Many of our team members have been with Dentist Salud for more than a decade. They are bilingual, approachable, and dedicated to making every visit feel welcoming, comfortable, and stress-free.

Mr. Carlos serves as the practice manager of this family-owned dental office, where he takes great pride in building a stable, dedicated team focused on delivering high-quality, personalized care.

Working alongside his wife and children—all dentists—he helps create a truly unique environment where patients experience consistent, honest, and compassionate care at every visit. What he enjoys most is seeing the lasting relationships that are built between the team and patients over time. For him, it’s not just about managing a practice—it’s about caring for people. In his free time, Mr. Carlos enjoys playing tennis, watching comedy shows, and eating.
