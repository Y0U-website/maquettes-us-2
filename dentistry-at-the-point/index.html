The practice

Our practice has been serving the residents of Point Edward, Sarnia-Lambton and communities beyond since 2014.

With no steps or stairs, our street level office is easily accessible. During the winter months our building ice-melt system works to keep our sidewalk bare. When inside, our reception area(s) for both adults and children separately feature TV s and sofas to relax prior to your appointment complete with a refreshment area.

Our patients also enjoy the relaxing comforts offered by our clinical space. If you so desire, escape while treatments are being performed lying in chairs equipped with massaging features with the additional option for those seeking even further distraction, the ability to watch and listen privately to your favorite Netflix program using wireless headphones.

Our clinic operatories are also equipped with a centrally installed Amaircare Air Wash HEPA filtration system and an integrated vanEE HRV to continually evacuate and replenish our clinical operatories with purified air.

Beyond everything else, our friendly staff is ready to greet you with a smile and will make every effort to ensure your stay with us is as comfortable as possible!
