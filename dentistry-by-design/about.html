Where Your Smile Feels at Home

Welcome to Dentistry By Design

At Dentistry By Design, we’re redefining what it means to visit the dentist. Rooted in Columbia, MO, and powered by advanced technology, our practice was founded by locals who believe great dentistry starts with great relationships. Our mission is simple: to build trust through compassionate, personalized care that supports your smile at every stage of life.

With a full suite of services — from same-day crowns and clear aligners to emergency care and cosmetic enhancements — our team is dedicated to combining comfort, clinical excellence, and convenience all under one roof. Whether you’re a busy parent or a working professional, we’re here to deliver the care that fits your life.

Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Kayla White

Born and raised right here in Columbia, Dr. Kayla White is proud to bring heartfelt, high-quality dentistry to the community she calls home. A graduate with honors and years of clinical experience, Dr. White has developed a special passion for esthetic dentistry, restorative treatments, and building long-term connections with the families she serves. Her dedication to continued education and patient-centered care ensures every treatment plan is thoughtful, modern, and tailored to your goals.

Dr. White’s skillset is backed by national recognition in Esthetic Dentistry and Orofacial Pain, and she consistently stays ahead of advancements in the field with continued education in multiple facets of dentistry. Patients appreciate her gentle touch, transparent guidance, and eye for detail — all of which are especially evident in services like veneers, cosmetic restorations, and same-day crowns using the latest CEREC technology.

More than a dentist, Dr. White is a trusted partner in her patients’ oral health. Her commitment to establishing comfort, confidence, and consistency in dental care makes her someone families return to year after year — and generation after generation.

Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Houston Rash

Also a Columbia native, Dr. Houston Rash brings a blend of expertise and compassionate care to every patient interaction. A graduate of the Missouri School of Dentistry and Oral Health, Dr. Rash is known for his pursuit of advanced education — including clinical training at the prestigious Dawson Academy — and his Invisalign certification, which allows him to provide subtle, effective orthodontic solutions for teens and adults alike.

Dr. Rash is also a published researcher and an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Missouri Dental Association (MDA), and Dental Success Network. His focus on functional, esthetic, and outcome-driven dentistry ensures patients receive solutions that restore both form and confidence.

From emergency care to full smile transformations, Dr. Rash is passionate about making dentistry accessible, understandable, and transformative. His precision and empathy help each patient feel calm and cared for — and he’s excited to grow with the community he serves.

In their free time, they love making memories with their son, Beckham and their two dogs, Archie and Kona. As Columbia natives and proud Mizzou graduates, they enjoy cheering on Mizzou sports, spending time with friends and family at local favorites and biking the MKT/Katy trail!

First Visit

Comfortable Care and Clear Communication

Your first visit to Dentistry By Design offers so much more than a quick check-up. From the moment you walk in, our goal is to make you feel relaxed and confident about your dental health. During this visit, we’ll take the time to review your medical history, listen to your concerns, and create a personalized treatment plan that works with your lifestyle and goals.

The Experience We believe in a proactive, patient-first approach. Your first appointment includes a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays, a preventive cleaning (if applicable), and a professional evaluation from one of our experienced doctors. We’ll walk you through every step of the process and answer all your questions so you leave feeling educated and empowered.

What to Bring To make your visit seamless, please be sure to bring:

Completed new patient forms (if filled out in advance)

Modern Technology & Comforts

From digital tools that improve your care to thoughtful amenities like TVs in treatment rooms and complimentary coffee and water in the reception area, everything we do is designed to make your experience better.

CEREC Same-Day Crowns

Skip the temporary crown and save time. With CEREC technology, we design, craft, and place high-quality ceramic crowns in a single visit — no impressions, no waiting weeks.

Intraoral Scanners

Say goodbye to goopy impressions. Our digital intraoral scanners create highly accurate 3D models of your mouth, leading to better fitting restorations and a more comfortable experience.

Digital X-Rays

Digital radiography provides faster, clearer images with significantly reduced radiation exposure. This allows for more accurate diagnosis and quicker treatment planning, all while keeping patients safer.

TVs in Treatment Rooms

Whether you&#x27;re in for a routine cleaning or a more involved procedure, enjoy your favorite show or movie while we work. It’s just one of the ways we help you feel at ease in the chair.

Coffee & Water at Reception

Feel at home the moment you walk in. Help yourself to a cup of coffee or a bottle of water while you wait — our way of saying we’re glad you’re here.

Giving Back

Strengthening Our Community

At Dentistry By Design, our commitment to Columbia goes beyond dental care. As lifelong residents, Dr. White and Dr. Rash are deeply invested in giving back to the community that helped shape who they are today.

We’re proud members of the Columbia Chamber of Commerce, partners of Melius, and active supporters of both the American Dental Association and the Missouri Dental Association. Our practice also sponsors local schools and youth programs, helping us contribute directly to the wellbeing and growth of the next generation.

Community involvement is at the heart of our mission — and as we continue to grow, so will our efforts to support the people, organizations, and neighborhoods that make Columbia such a special place to call home.
