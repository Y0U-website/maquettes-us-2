What To Expect at Your First Visit

Being well-prepared for your appointment will ensure that the doctor has all of the needed information to provide the best possible care for you. It also will help relieve any unnecessary anxiety you may be feeling. We look forward to your first visit.

  • 1 Download and Complete New Patient Forms Download the New Patient Form (PDF) from our patient portal and fill it out before your visit to save time in the office.
  • 2 Gather Your Insurance and ID Information Bring your dental insurance card, a valid photo ID, and a list of any current medications so we can update your health history accurately.
  • 3 Arrive at Our Taylor, MI Office We are located at 25721 Goddard Rd, Taylor, MI 48180 . Plan to arrive a few minutes early to get settled and meet our front desk team.
  • 4 Meet Your Care Team and Review Your Health History A team member will review your health history, medications, and any concerns you have before your exam begins.
  • 5 Receive Your Comprehensive Exam Dr. Siddiqui or Dr. Dhillon will perform a thorough dental exam, including X-rays if needed, to assess your oral health and identify any areas of concern.
  • 6 Review Your Treatment Plan Your doctor will walk you through any findings and present a clear treatment plan — with no pressure and no surprises. Questions about insurance or financing are always welcome.
