Dr. Bumann is the Local Board Certified Pediatric Dentist Helping Kids Build Healthy Smiles

Dr. Aaron Bumann is a board certified pediatric dentist dedicated to helping children develop healthy smiles through compassionate care and patient education. At Dentistry for Children, he focuses on creating positive dental experiences while helping families understand how to support their child’s oral health.

Through prevention focused pediatric dentistry and clear communication with parents, Dr. Bumann works to help children feel comfortable during dental visits and confident about caring for their teeth as they grow.

Dr. Bumann’s Story Dr. Aaron Bumann has dedicated his career to helping children develop healthy smiles through compassionate pediatric dentistry. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry in 2013.

After completing dental school, Dr. Bumann continued his education through the Pediatric Dental Residency Program at Geisinger Medical Center in Danville, Pennsylvania. He completed this specialized pediatric dentistry training in 2016, preparing him to provide dental care designed specifically for infants, children, and teens.

Dr. Bumann joined Dentistry for Children in 2017 and now provides pediatric dentistry for families at the Liberty location. Before joining the practice, he lived in Ann Arbor, Michigan where he worked in private practice and also taught pediatric dentistry at the University of Michigan.

In addition to caring for patients, Dr. Bumann is passionate about children’s health advocacy. He works with organizations such as the Missouri Coalition for Oral Health and the Missouri Dental Association to help improve access to high quality oral health care for children.

Outside of the dental office, Dr. Bumann enjoys spending time with his family. He and his wife have two children and enjoy many activities together as a family. In his free time, Dr. Bumann enjoys reading, cooking with his family, watching college football, and playing with his kids.

“Children are likely to live up to what you believe of them.” — Lady Bird Johnson

Dr. Bumann’s Dental Team Dr. Bumann works with a dedicated team of dental assistants, hygienists, and patient coordinators who help create a welcoming and supportive pediatric dental office for every child.

Together, this team focuses on helping children feel comfortable during their dental visits while providing parents with clear guidance about their child’s oral health. Their goal is to create positive dental experiences that help children build confidence about visiting the dentist.
