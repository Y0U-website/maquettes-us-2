Dr. Roeber Provides Compassionate Pediatric Dentistry that Makes Kids Feel Confident

Dr. Brandi Roeber is a board certified pediatric dentist who is passionate about helping children feel confident and comfortable during dental visits. At Dentistry for Children, she focuses on creating positive dental experiences while helping families protect their child’s oral health.

Through compassionate pediatric dentistry and patient education, Dr. Roeber helps children develop healthy habits that support strong smiles as they grow.

Dr. Roeber’s Story Dr. Brandi Roeber has dedicated her career to helping children develop healthy smiles through compassionate pediatric dentistry. She started by earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Nebraska College of Dentistry in 2007.

After dental school, Dr. Roeber continued her training through the Pediatric Dentistry Program at the University of Nebraska Medical Center. She completed her postgraduate specialization in pediatric dentistry in 2009, preparing her to provide dental care specifically designed for infants, children, teens, and special needs patients.

Dr. Roeber later became board certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry in 2010. Board certification is a voluntary credential that demonstrates advanced training and expertise in pediatric dental care.

As a pediatric dentist, Dr. Roeber focuses on helping children feel comfortable while building lifelong relationships with families. She enjoys educating children and parents about oral health and helping each child develop confidence about visiting the dentist.

Outside the office Dr. Roeber enjoys spending time with her husband and their four children: Iliana, Neva, Lucia, and John. In her free time, Dr. Roeber enjoys cooking with her husband, visiting the park with her children, and spending time with family and friends. She is also a passionate sports fan who enjoys cheering for the Kansas City Royals and Husker football.

Dr. Roeber’s Dental Team Dr. Roeber works alongside a team of dental assistants, hygienists, and patient coordinators who help create a welcoming and supportive pediatric dental office for every child.

Together, the team focuses on helping children feel comfortable during dental visits while providing parents with clear information about their child’s oral health. Their goal is to create positive dental experiences that help children feel confident about visiting the dentist.
