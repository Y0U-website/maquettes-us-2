Dr. William Bauman

Owner of Dentistry For the Entire Family since 2024.

About

Dr. Bauman approaches dental health treatment comprehensively, focusing on early detection, prevention and education. He is trained to identify signs of dental problems before symptoms and damage manifest. In many instances, this will save you from discomfort, repeated treatments and reducing your overall time in the dental chair. He is also very sensitive to fears of dentistry and has options to assist patients with unneeded pain and anxiety. Dr. Bauman insists on being available to his patients and staff and will cater to their individual needs.

"I’m excited by the ever-progressing technology that continues to emerge in dentistry—enabling better treatment outcomes for patients".

Education

Dr. William Bauman received his dental degree from Creighton University and his BS through St. John’s University.

Dr. Thyra Jagger

Joined Dentistry for the Entire Family in 2014

About

A smile is the first thing someone often notices about you. "At the age of 15, I recall how embarrassed and self-conscious I was about my smile that I’d actually cover my mouth when I’d laugh to avoid showing my funky smile".

My smile was restored. "It was so liberating to be able to smile once again with confidence and not have to cover my mouth when I’d laugh. That experience is why I chose to become a dentist. "To help others smile with confidence."

Dr. Jagger and her husband live in Maple Grove and are busy parents of three active children. In her free time, she enjoys running, yoga, skiing, and sailing, and she is a foodie–she enjoys cooking and dining out at local restaurants. Dr. Jagger also enjoys ubering her children to all of their sporting events!

Education

Dr. Thyra Jagger received her dental degree from the University of Minnesota dental school in 2009 and has a BA in Biology from Hamline University.

Post graduation, Dr. Jagger has completed advanced training in cosmetic dentistry. Advanced training certifications include: Contemporary and Esthetic Dentistry Certificate Invisalign Certification Botox FACES Certification

Dr. Thyra Jagger has extensive experience in restorative, therapeutic, and cosmetic dentistry and is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Anne Vetrano

Joined Dentistry for the Entire Family in 2023

About

Dr. Vetrano’s goal is to put each patient’s needs first, prioritizing the highest levels of care, comfort and communication.

In high school, Dr. Vetrano went on a medical mission trip to Costa Rica, working alongside doctors and a dentist. She was so moved to see people, fearful and in pain, who had never been to a dentist before—then leave with a smile on their face. It showed her how impactful dentistry could be and how important someone’s smile really is.

Outside the practice, Dr. Vetrano enjoys traveling, hiking, reading, and trying new restaurants with her fiancé, Nick.

She has enjoyed exploring all around the Twin Cities. In her free time, she loves cooking, reading, and traveling.

Education

Dr. Anne Vetrano is originally from the Chicago area. She graduated from Marquette University School of Dentistry in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, where she earned her degree as a Doctor of Dental Surgery.

Prior to dental school, she attended Villanova University near Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, where she studied psychology and Spanish for health professionals. In addition to her academic successes, she was a Division I track and field athlete at Villanova for all four years and is a three-time Big East champion in triple jump.

After dental school, she moved to Minneapolis where she had practiced for few years before joining Dentistry For the Entire Family. She is a member of the Delta Sigma Delta Dental Fraternity (Chapter President 2019-2020), American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, and the Seattle Study Club.
