About Us Your Dentist in Sand Springs, OK

Richard L. Brown, Jr., DDS, FACD, FAGD, FPFA, FIADFE

Dr. Richard Brown Jr. was born in Henryetta, Oklahoma, and raised in Barstow, California, where he graduated from high school. After high school he returned to Oklahoma to attend Northeastern State University, where he was a college golfer and attained a B.S. degree, and then the University of Oklahoma where he received a DDS degree.

Upon graduation, Dr. Brown attended a one-year residency in General Dentistry and served five years as a member of the United States Army.
