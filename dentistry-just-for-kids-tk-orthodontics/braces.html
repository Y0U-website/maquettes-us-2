FAQs About Braces

  • What’s the best age to get braces? The American Association of Orthodontists Opens a new window to the AAO website recommends all children get an evaluation with an orthodontic specialist no later than age seven. Early treatment may prevent or intercept more serious problems from developing as well as make treatment at a later age shorter and less complicated. We also believe you’re never too old for orthodontics . Dr. Kula treats patients of all ages!
  • How long is the treatment? The exact treatment time depends on each patient’s specific needs and treatment goals. Most treatments last approximately from 12 to 30 months.
  • Do braces hurt? You should never feel extreme pain while wearing braces, but you may feel mild discomfort when you first get your braces put on and after adjustments. This pain should naturally disappear but, if not, Dr. Kula Meet our orthodontist is on standby to provide relief.
  • How can I take care of my braces? Good oral hygiene is always important, so never forget to brush and floss after every meal. Try to avoid hard and sticky foods because they can damage the brackets. Also, make sure you attend all of your orthodontic appointments and follow Dr. Kula’s instructions and recommendations.
  • Is there a problem if my kid plays sports while wearing braces? Not at all! Let your kid be a kid and enjoy the physical activity. You just need to make sure they use a mouthguard when participating in contact sports.
