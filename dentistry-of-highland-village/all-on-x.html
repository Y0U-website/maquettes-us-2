What We Offer at Dentistry of Highland Village

Tooth loss affects far more people than many realize. According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, tooth loss can lead to poor diet, difficulty speaking, and a lower quality of life overall , and the leading causes are largely preventable with the right care and attention. Our team helps patients understand where they stand and what steps make sense next.

We provide thorough consultations and evaluations for patients exploring dental implants as a way to replace one or more missing teeth. During your visit, we assess your oral health, review your bone structure, and discuss your goals so we can help determine whether implants are a suitable option for your smile.

How Our Consultation Process Works

A consultation with our team starts with a conversation, not a sales pitch. We want to understand what brought you in, whether that is a single missing tooth, several teeth lost over time, or general concerns about your long-term oral health.

From there, we conduct a full clinical evaluation, including digital X-rays and an intraoral camera exam, to get a clear picture of your jawbone density and the health of your surrounding teeth and gums. We walk you through what we find in plain language, answering questions along the way so you never feel rushed or left in the dark. If implants appear to be a good fit, we outline what the general implant procedure looks like and what to expect at each stage.

Understanding Candidacy for Dental Implants

Not every patient is at the same starting point when they come in to discuss implants, and that is exactly why the evaluation matters so much. Some patients have been missing a tooth for years and have already experienced some bone loss in that area, while others come in shortly after an extraction and want to understand their replacement options right away.

Overall oral health plays a significant role in candidacy as well. Healthy gums, adequate bone density, and well-managed conditions like diabetes all factor into whether implants are likely to succeed long-term, which is part of why we never skip a thorough clinical review. Patients who smoke or who have significant bone loss are not automatically ruled out, but these factors often shape the recommended approach and timeline.

We also take the time to talk through your daily habits, your bite, and your expectations for the finished result. Two patients missing the same tooth can have very different treatment plans once we account for their unique anatomy and goals, which is why we avoid a one-size-fits-all conversation from the start.

Our Coordinated Referral Partnership

While we handle the full evaluation and planning conversation in our Houston office, we do not perform implant placement surgery here at Dentistry of Highland Village. Instead, we work closely with Dr. Rushi Dave, who performs implant placement, including full arch and All-on-X procedures, at his Pearland location roughly 25 minutes away.

This arrangement allows us to combine local, familiar care with specialized surgical experience.
