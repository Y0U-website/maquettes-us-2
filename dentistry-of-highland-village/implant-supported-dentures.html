Implant-Supported Dentures in Houston, TX

Losing most or all of your teeth doesn’t mean settling for a smile that slips, clicks, or holds you back from the foods you love. Implant-supported dentures offer a way to anchor a full or partial denture securely in place, giving you back the stability and confidence that traditional dentures often can’t provide.

What Are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures combine two proven approaches to tooth replacement: a denture base custom-fitted to your gums and a small number of implants placed in the jawbone that anchor the denture firmly in place. Rather than relying on suction or adhesive to stay put, the denture snaps or locks onto the implants, so it stays secure while you eat, speak, and smile.

Depending on your needs, this can mean a removable overdenture that clips onto implants for easy nightly removal, or a more permanent fixed option that stays in your mouth full-time. Both approaches solve the same basic problem: a traditional denture rests directly on the gum tissue and can shift or cause sore spots over time, while an implant-supported version transfers that pressure to the jawbone instead.

Our Role in Your Treatment

We start with a thorough exam, a review of your jawbone health and remaining teeth, and an honest conversation about whether implant-supported dentures make sense for your situation. If they do, we coordinate your referral to Dr. Dave, who performs implant placement at our Pearland location, so the surgical piece of your treatment is handled by someone who focuses specifically on that work.

What to Expect Financially

Cost is often the first question patients ask, and it’s a fair one given that implant-supported dentures typically cost more upfront than traditional removable dentures. The exact price depends on how many implants you need, the type of denture you choose, and whether any preparatory work like bone grafting is required first. We go over detailed cost information during your consultation so there are no surprises once treatment begins.

We accept all PPO insurance plans, and our team can help you understand what portion of treatment your plan may cover. For patients without insurance or with a plan that doesn’t fully cover implant-related treatment, we also discuss financing options so cost doesn’t stand between you and a denture that actually fits the way it should.

Understanding the Full Process

Because implant-supported dentures involve both a surgical phase and a restorative phase, the full procedure typically spans several months from initial placement to final fitting. Healing time varies by patient, and we build your timeline around what your jawbone actually needs rather than rushing the process. Patients who ask about a faster alternative sometimes consider a traditional dental bridge instead, and we’re happy to walk through how that compares during your visit.
