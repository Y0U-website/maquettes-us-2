Why Choose an In-House Membership Plan Over Traditional Insurance?

Traditional dental insurance comes with a well-known set of frustrations: waiting periods before major work is covered, annual maximums that can run out quickly, and a list of exclusions that can leave patients surprised by out-of-pocket costs even when they’re technically covered. An in-house membership plan eliminates most of that complexity. There are no third-party intermediaries, no claims to file, and no waiting to find out whether a procedure is approved.

For self-employed individuals, small business owners, and anyone between jobs or employer-sponsored plans, the VIP Discount Plan offers a practical solution. It provides real, immediate savings on dental care without requiring patients to navigate a confusing benefits landscape on their own. The team at Dentistry of Highland Village is happy to walk new patients through how the plan works and help determine whether it’s the right fit for their situation.
