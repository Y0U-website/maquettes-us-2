How Long Does Aligner Treatment Take?

Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of your case, but most patients complete their aligner treatment in 6 to 18 months . Mild to moderate cases often wrap up on the shorter end, while more involved corrections may take longer.

Compare that to traditional braces, which can take 2-3 years for similar results. It’s easy to see why aligners have become such a popular choice.

Dr. Hernandez will give you a personalized timeline during your consultation so you know exactly what to expect from day one.

Caring for Your Aligners: Simple Tips for Success

Getting the most out of your aligners comes down to a few easy habits:

  • Wear them consistently. 20-22 hours per day is the sweet spot. Wearing them less slows down your progress.
  • Remove them before eating or drinking anything other than water. Colored drinks like coffee, tea, and juice can stain the trays, and food can get trapped underneath.
  • Clean your aligners daily. Use a soft toothbrush and mild soap or a dedicated aligner cleaner. Avoid hot water because it can warp the plastic.
  • Store them safely. When you’re not wearing them, keep your aligners in their case. They’re easy to lose (and accidentally throw away) if you leave them on a table or napkin.
  • Keep up with your dental hygiene. Clean teeth go back into clean trays. Brush after meals before reinserting your aligners whenever possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will orthodontic aligners affect how I speak?

Most people notice a very slight adjustment period when they first start wearing aligners. You might notice a minor lisp for the first few days. This is completely normal and typically goes away within a week as your tongue adjusts. Because the trays are thin and custom-fitted, the adjustment is much quicker and less noticeable than with traditional braces. The vast majority of our patients tell us they had no issue speaking clearly after the first few days of wearing them.

Are orthodontic aligners painful?

Painful is a strong word. Most patients describe it as mild pressure or a slight soreness when they switch to a new tray. This is actually a positive sign that the aligners are actively shifting your teeth. The discomfort is usually manageable and fades within 24-48 hours of switching trays. If you’re ever experiencing significant pain, don’t hesitate to reach out to us at Dentistry of North Port. Dr. Hernandez will make sure everything is on track and comfortable.

How do I know if aligners are right for me, or if I need traditional braces instead?

The only real way to know is through a professional evaluation, and that’s exactly what Dr. Rosa Hernandez offers at our North Port office. Aligners are highly effective for mild to moderate orthodontic issues, but some cases (like significant bite problems or severely rotated teeth) may still be better treated with traditional orthodontics. Dr. Hernandez will review your dental X-rays, examine your bite, and give you a clear, honest recommendation. There’s no pressure, just a straightforward conversation about what’s best for your smile.

What happens after I finish aligner treatment? Will my teeth stay straight?

Yes, as long as you wear your retainer! After you complete your aligner series, your teeth need time to stabilize in their new positions. Without a retainer, teeth have a natural tendency to drift back toward where they started (it’s frustrating, but true for both aligners and braces). Dr. Hernandez will provide you with a custom retainer and a wear schedule to protect your investment. Most patients wear their retainer nightly long-term to keep their smile looking exactly the way they want.
