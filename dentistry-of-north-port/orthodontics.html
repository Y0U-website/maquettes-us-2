Orthodontic Services Offered at Our North Port Office

Traditional Braces

Traditional braces remain one of the most trusted orthodontic treatments for patients of all ages. Using brackets and wires, braces gently move teeth into proper alignment over time. They are highly effective for complex cases involving severe crowding, spacing, or bite problems. With today’s smaller, more comfortable brackets, braces are less noticeable than ever and provide predictable results.

SureSmile®

SureSmile® takes orthodontics to the next level. This system uses 3D imaging and robotic wire-bending technology to create a highly precise, customized treatment plan. Because of this precision, many patients experience shorter treatment times and fewer office visits compared to traditional braces. SureSmile® is an excellent option for patients seeking accuracy, efficiency, and comfort in their orthodontic journey.

Orthodontic Aligners

Clear orthodontic aligners are an increasingly popular choice for both teens and adults. These custom-fit trays gradually move teeth into alignment while remaining virtually invisible. Aligners are removable, making it easier to brush, floss, and enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions. For patients with mild to moderate misalignment, aligners offer a discreet and flexible alternative to braces—perfect for busy lifestyles or those who prefer a more natural look during treatment.

How Do I Know If I Need Orthodontic Treatment?

Not every orthodontic issue is obvious, and many people wonder whether they truly need treatment. In general, orthodontics can benefit both children and adults who experience problems with tooth alignment or bite function.

You may be a good candidate for orthodontic care if you notice any of the following:

Even if your teeth look relatively straight, underlying bite issues can still affect your oral health over time. If you’re still unsure whether or not you need orthodontic treatment, contact our North Port dentist by calling (941) 200-5812.

  • Crowded or crooked teeth that overlap or twist out of position.
  • Gaps or spaces between your teeth that affect appearance or function.
  • Bite issues , such as an overbite, underbite, open bite, or crossbite.
  • Difficulty chewing or biting food comfortably.
  • Speech problems that may be linked to misaligned teeth.
  • Jaw pain or clicking , which can sometimes result from improper alignment.

How to Know Which Orthodontic Treatment Is Right for You

With so many orthodontic options available, it’s natural to wonder which treatment best fits your needs. The right choice depends on your dental concerns, lifestyle, and personal goals.

During your consultation at Dentistry of North Port, Dr. Hernandez will take digital scans, assess your bite, and discuss your smile goals to recommend the most effective option. Together, you’ll choose the treatment that not only aligns your teeth but also fits comfortably into your lifestyle.

  • Traditional Braces may be the best option if you have more complex alignment or bite issues. They’re effective for correcting severe crowding, rotations, and jaw alignment problems.
  • SureSmile® is ideal if you want the precision of advanced technology and potentially shorter treatment times. This treatment can address a wide range of orthodontic concerns with remarkable accuracy.
  • Clear Aligners are a great choice if you’re looking for a discreet solution and have mild to moderate misalignment. They’re removable, making it easier to maintain your oral hygiene and enjoy your favorite foods.

Why Orthodontics Is More Than Just Straight Teeth

Orthodontics goes far beyond creating a beautiful smile. Properly aligned teeth and jaws contribute to long-term dental and overall health. Misaligned teeth can make it difficult to chew, speak clearly, or keep your mouth clean. Without treatment, these issues can lead to tooth decay, gum disease, jaw pain, and even uneven tooth wear.

Straight teeth not only make daily oral hygiene easier but also give you confidence in social and professional interactions. At Dentistry of North Port, we believe everyone deserves a smile they can feel proud of, and orthodontic treatment is one of the most effective ways to achieve that.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment length depends on the severity of your case and the type of treatment you choose. On average, braces may take 18–24 months, while SureSmile and clear aligners can sometimes achieve results in a shorter timeframe. Dr. Hernandez will provide a customized timeline during your consultation.

Are orthodontic aligners as effective as braces?

Yes, aligners are highly effective for many orthodontic cases, particularly mild to moderate alignment issues. However, traditional braces may be recommended for more complex corrections. During your evaluation, Dr. Hernandez will recommend the best option to give you the results you want.

Is orthodontic treatment just for kids?

Not at all. While many patients start orthodontic care in their teens, more adults are now choosing treatment than ever before. Clear aligners and modern braces make it easier for adults to discreetly achieve the smile they’ve always wanted.

Do braces or clear aligners hurt?

You may feel some mild discomfort when you first start treatment or after adjustments, but it’s usually temporary. This soreness is a sign that your teeth are moving as planned. Most patients describe it as pressure rather than pain, and it typically fades within a few days. Over-the-counter pain relief and soft foods can help you stay comfortable during the adjustment period.
