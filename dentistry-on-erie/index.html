The practice

Originally starting out with Dr. Bill Black on Delta Avenue in Mount Lookout, this practice has been serving Cincinnati since the early 1970s. After Dr. Black’s retirement, Dr. Richard Jackson purchased the practice and moved it to its current location on Erie Ave in East Hyde Park. Dr. Jackson faithfully served the people of this practice for 42 years before his retirement in July 2025.

Dr. Rachel Bailey is the new face of the practice and, with her, brings a new name — Dentistry on Erie. Despite the new name and new face, she keeps the same dental team and office environment you know and love. She hopes to continue the legacy of this practice built by Drs. Black and Jackson.

After earning a Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry degree from the University of Louisville, Dr. Bailey has continued to expand her expertise through a variety of continuing education courses. By staying up to date with the latest techniques and technology, she ensures patients receive modern, high-quality care in a relaxed environment.

Patients often describe Dr. Bailey as approachable, compassionate, and detail-oriented. She truly values building long-lasting relationships and enjoys getting to know the families she serves—sometimes caring for multiple generations within the same household.
