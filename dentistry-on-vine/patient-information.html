What to Expect at Your First Visit*

Your first visit to the office will include a comprehensive dental exam, including a screening for cavities, gum disease, oral cancer, and other problems. We will also take a series of digital x-rays.

Next, with your help, we will prioritize treatment and create a plan.

*Your first visit does not include a dental cleaning. Before you leave we will schedule a dental cleaning appointment that will best suit your needs.

What should I expect during my first visit to Dentistry on Vine?

During your first visit, we’ll conduct a comprehensive dental exam including cavity screening, gum, and oral cancer checks, plus digital X-rays. Treatment priorities are discussed, and a cleaning will be scheduled separately. The new patient exam does not include the cleaning.

How do I complete patient intake forms before my appointment?

We send our new patient forms via text or email. Completing forms in advance helps us prepare for your visit efficiently.

Which insurance plans do you accept, and how can I submit my insurance information?

We accept most dental insurance plans and are in‑network with Delta Dental Premier. Please bring your insurance card or email photos of both sides to info@dentistryonvine.com before your appointment.

What payment options and discounts are available if I don’t have insurance?

We accept all major credit/debit cards and offer CareCredit financing for low‑interest payment plans. Seniors (60+) can also ask about our discount plan for affordable dental health care.

Are you currently accepting new patients at Dentistry on Vine?

Yes—we are actively welcoming new patients at our Hudson, WI location. Feel free to call or request an appointment online.

How can I pay my bill or view statements online?

We offer an online bill payment portal that allows you to view and pay your dental bill securely at your convenience.
