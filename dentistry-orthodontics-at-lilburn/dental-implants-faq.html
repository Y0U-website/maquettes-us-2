Dental Implants FAQ

Dental implants have become a popular solution for replacing missing teeth, offering both durability and a natural look. If you’re considering this option, you likely have a few questions about the procedure, benefits, and aftercare. To help you make an informed decision, our dental implant dentist in Kennesaw and Lilburn, GA , has answered some of the most frequently asked questions about dental implants.

What Are Dental Implants? Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of titanium that are surgically placed into your jawbone. Over time, the implants fuse with the bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation for a replacement tooth or crown. This provides a permanent and secure solution for missing teeth, allowing you to restore both functionality and aesthetics.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most individuals with good oral health are candidates for dental implant treatment . Ideal candidates should have healthy gums and enough bone density to support the implant. However, even if you have experienced bone loss, treatments like bone grafting can often make implants possible. It’s essential to consult with our dental implant dentist to determine if you’re a good candidate based on your oral health and overall medical history.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants are designed to be a long-lasting solution, often lasting a lifetime with proper care. The implant itself, which is anchored into the jawbone, can last for decades. However, the crown or prosthetic tooth may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear and tear. Maintaining good oral hygiene and regular visits to our dentist are essential to prolong the life of your implants.

What Is the Procedure Like?

The dental implant procedure typically involves multiple steps. First, our dentist will evaluate your oral health and take scans of your jawbone. If you’re a candidate for implants, the surgical placement of the titanium post will be scheduled. After the implant is placed, there’s a healing period where the implant fuses with the bone. Once healed, a custom crown will be attached to the implant, completing the restoration.

Does the Procedure Hurt?

The dental implant surgery itself is usually performed under local anesthesia, so you shouldn’t feel any pain during the procedure. Some patients may experience mild discomfort or swelling afterward, which can be managed with over-the-counter pain medication. The recovery process is generally smooth, and any postoperative discomfort typically subsides within a few days.

How Should I Care for My Dental Implants?

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for your natural teeth. Daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental checkups are essential. While the implants themselves won’t decay, maintaining healthy gums and surrounding teeth is critical for the longevity of your implants. Good oral hygiene also helps prevent complications like gum disease, which could affect the stability of the implant.

Are Dental Implants Expensive?

The cost of dental implant treatment can vary depending on several factors, including the number of implants needed, the type of restoration, and any additional treatments like bone grafting. While they may have a higher upfront cost compared to other tooth replacement options, their durability and long-term benefits make dental implants a worthwhile investment.
