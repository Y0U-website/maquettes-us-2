The Adult Orthodontics Process

Consultation And Records: A comprehensive exam, digital scans, photos, and X-rays help evaluate tooth position, jaw relationships, and gum health. Goals are discussed, and options are reviewed.

Customized Plan: A plan outlines which teeth will move, estimated timeframes, and the appliance type. Choices may include metal braces, ceramic braces, or clear aligners.

Active Treatment: Braces use brackets and wires that are adjusted at visits. Clear aligners are worn 20 to 22 hours per day and changed on a set schedule. Small attachments, elastics, or interproximal reduction may be used to guide precise movement.

Progress Checks: Visits are typically every 6 to 10 weeks for braces and every 6 to 12 weeks for aligners. Good oral hygiene and appliance care are essential to stay on track.

Retention: After active movement, retainers hold teeth in their new positions. Nightly wear is common long term to prevent relapse.

What To Expect

Timeline: Many adult orthodontic cases take 6 to 24 months, depending on complexity and appliance type. Mild spacing or crowding may finish sooner, while significant bite changes can take longer.

Comfort: Temporary pressure or soreness is common after adjustments or when starting new aligners. Over-the-counter pain relief and a soft diet for a day or two can help.

Diet And Care: With braces, it is best to avoid hard or sticky foods and to clean carefully around brackets and wires. With aligners, remove trays when eating or drinking anything other than water, and brush before putting them back in.

Oral Hygiene: Brushing twice daily and cleaning between teeth is crucial. Professional cleanings support gum health during treatment.

After Treatment: Retainers are essential for maintaining results. Wearing retainers as directed preserves alignment and protects your investment.
