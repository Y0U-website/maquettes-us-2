Dental Implant FAQs

Dental implants are titanium posts that act as artificial tooth roots. They are placed in the jawbone and topped with a custom crown, bridge, or denture to restore missing teeth.

Most healthy adults with missing teeth are good candidates. Ideal candidates have healthy gums, sufficient bone density, and no uncontrolled medical conditions. We’ll evaluate your health and oral structure during your consultation.

With proper care including daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental visits, implants can last 20+ years or even a lifetime.

Most patients report minimal discomfort. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia or sedation, and post-op soreness is usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.

The full process can take a few months, depending on healing time and whether bone grafting is needed. However, immediate solutions like Teeth-in-a-Day are available in certain cases.

If you’ve experienced bone loss, we may recommend a bone graft to build up the jawbone. This ensures a stable foundation for your implant.

Yes! We use custom-crafted restorations designed to match your natural teeth in shape, size, and color for a seamless, natural-looking smile.

Caring for implants is similar to caring for natural teeth, brush twice daily, floss once a day, and schedule regular checkups. No special tools or products are needed.

Coverage varies depending on your plan. Our team will help you understand your benefits and explore financing options if needed.

We combine advanced technology, personalized care, and extensive implant experience to deliver precise, long-lasting results in a comfortable and supportive environment.
