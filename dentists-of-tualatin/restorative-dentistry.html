Restorative Dentistry

Bridges Fixed restorations that replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to neighboring teeth or implants.

Implant restorations: A strong, long‑term solution for single or multiple missing teeth, or to secure dentures. Implants look and feel close to natural teeth, support facial structure, and don’t require adhesives.

  • Restorative Dentistry Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing and replacing teeth to bring back their full strength, function, and natural appearance, so you can enjoy a healthy, confident smile. Crowns Full‑coverage “caps” that restore weakened or damaged teeth to proper form and function. Porcelain options are highly durable and color‑matched for a natural smile. Common reasons for a crown: Restore a tooth with insufficient structure for a large filling
  • Protect a cracked or weakened tooth
  • Restore a fractured tooth
  • Cover a discolored or misshapen tooth
  • Complete a dental implant
  • Restore your smile and confidence
  • Improve chewing and speech
  • Maintain facial shape
  • Distribute bite forces properly
  • Prevent neighboring teeth from drifting
