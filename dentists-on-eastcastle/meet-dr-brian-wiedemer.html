Meet Dr. Brian Wiedemer- Dentist…

I love being busy and constantly challenged to think of new approaches to care. I enjoy meeting new people and building long-term relationships with them. I enjoy learning and expanding my abilities. I find it fulfilling to make a difference in people’s lives. Dentistry is an amazing profession, because I am able to do all these things, and many more, each day.

I respect the individuality of my patients, and enjoy getting to know them as we work together to find the dental solutions that work for their specific needs and situation. Whether I’m restoring function, relieving pain, or creating a healthy smile, I’m ensuring a better life for the person, and that’s extremely rewarding. - Dr. Brian A. Wiedemer

EDUCATION AND CONTINUING EDUCATION

Dr. Wiedemer received his undergraduate degree at DePauw University in Greencastle, IN and then was awarded a Doctorate in Dental Surgery from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry . Following graduation, Dr. Wiedemer resolved to pursue continuing education courses, knowing they are essential to providing patients with the highest quality treatment. He believes his patients deserve to be aware of all current, proven treatment options available, and to have a doctor who is capable of providing those treatments.

Through his active memberships in the Michigan Dental Association and West Michigan Dental Association, Dr. Wiedemer has access to the latest research findings, as well as information about emerging technologies, new dental materials, and changes in treatment methodologies.

PERSONAL LIFE

Dr. Wiedemer grew up in East Lansing, MI and now lives in Grand Rapids with his wife, Dr. Sarah Webb. He loves fishing for salmon and steelhead on Lake Michigan, and is an avid kayaker, too; sometimes he attempts to fish from his kayak. During the summer, Dr. Wiedemer can often be found hiking or camping with his wife. As Dr. Webb is an excellent cook, Dr. Wiedemer enjoys being her taste tester for all new recipes.
