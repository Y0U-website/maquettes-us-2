Botox (botulinum toxin) works by temporarily relaxing the overused muscles responsible for jaw tension and grinding. When injected into specific areas of the jaw (such as the masseter and temporalis muscles), it reduces muscle contraction, easing pain and protecting your teeth from the damaging effects of clenching and bruxism. Botox for TMJ is: Minimally invasive
  • Quick and virtually painless
  • FDA-approved for medical use
  • Effective for many patients within days
  • Long-lasting (typically 3–4 months per session)

Symptoms Botox Can Help Address

Botox for TMJ can be effective in reducing or eliminating:

Many patients report improved sleep, fewer flare-ups, and an overall improvement in daily comfort.

  • Jaw tension and soreness
  • Clenching and grinding (bruxism)
  • Tension headaches and migraines
  • Clicking or popping of the jaw
  • Facial and neck pain related to muscle strain
  • Wear on teeth from chronic grinding
  • Discomfort while chewing, speaking, or yawning
