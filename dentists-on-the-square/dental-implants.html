A dental implant is a titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth. It is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it fuses naturally through a process called osseointegration. Once healed, the implant is topped with a custom crown, bridge, or denture — fully restoring both appearance and function. Dental implants are considered the gold standard in modern tooth replacement because they: Mimic the structure and strength of a natural tooth
  • Prevent bone loss and maintain facial volume
  • Allow for confident chewing and speaking
  • Do not rely on adjacent teeth for support
  • Are permanent and easy to maintain

Am I a Candidate for Implants?

You may be a good candidate for dental implants if you:

Even if you’ve been told in the past that implants aren’t an option, advancements in treatment may make you eligible today.

  • Are missing one or more teeth
  • Have sufficient bone density (or are willing to undergo bone grafting)
  • Are in generally good health
  • Don’t want a removable denture or bridge
  • Want the most natural-feeling tooth replacement available
