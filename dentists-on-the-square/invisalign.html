Our Invisalign Process

At Dentists on the Square, your Invisalign journey is guided by experienced doctors trained in cosmetic and functional alignment. Here&#x27;s what to expect:

We evaluate your bite, discuss your goals, and take a digital iTero scan of your teeth — no messy impressions required.

Your scan is used to create a fully digital 3D simulation of how your teeth will move over time, including your projected final result.

Once your aligners arrive, we make sure they fit perfectly and show you how to wear and care for them. You’ll change to a new set every 1–2 weeks.

We’ll see you every 6–8 weeks for brief check-ins to ensure everything’s moving correctly.

Once treatment is complete, you’ll receive retainers to maintain your results long-term.

Most treatments take 6–18 months, depending on the complexity of your case.
