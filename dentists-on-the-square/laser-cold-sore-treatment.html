Cold sores, also called fever blisters, are small fluid-filled lesions that typically appear on or around the lips. They’re caused by the herpes simplex virus (HSV-1), which lies dormant in the body and can reactivate due to stress, illness, sun exposure, or a weakened immune system. Common symptoms include: Tingling or burning sensations
  • Painful blisters or open sores
  • Crusting and scabbing during healing
  • Self-consciousness due to visibility

How Cold Sore Laser Treatment Works

At Dentists on the Square, we use a dental soft tissue laser to safely treat the affected area. The laser light destroys the virus on the surface and in the surrounding tissue, reduces inflammation, and stimulates your body’s natural healing response.

The treatment is fast, comfortable, and requires no anesthesia or downtime.

  • Prevent a cold sore from fully developing when treated at the tingling stage
  • Reduce pain and inflammation
  • Speed up healing (often within 2–3 days)
  • Decrease the severity and duration of active outbreaks
  • Reduce the frequency of future outbreaks in the same area

When to Schedule Treatment

Laser treatment is most effective when performed at the first sign of a cold sore — often during the tingling, itching, or burning stage before a blister appears.

However, it can also provide relief if the sore is already visible by:

  • Reducing its lifespan
  • Limiting the risk of spreading
  • Helping prevent recurrence in the same area
