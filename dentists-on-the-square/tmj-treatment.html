Temporomandibular Joint Disorder (often abbreviated as TMJ or TMD) occurs when the jaw joints and surrounding muscles become inflamed, overworked, or misaligned. Common TMJ symptoms include: Jaw pain or tightness
  • Clicking, popping, or locking when opening the mouth
  • Headaches or migraines
  • Neck and shoulder tension
  • Earaches or a feeling of fullness in the ears
  • Pain when chewing or talking
  • Tooth wear or sensitivity from clenching or grinding

How We Treat TMJ at Dentists on the Square

Our team begins every TMJ treatment with a detailed consultation, imaging, and muscle/joint evaluation to identify the root cause of your discomfort. Your treatment plan may include:

Protective appliances that reduce strain on the jaw and prevent damage caused by clenching and grinding (bruxism).

Selective reshaping of the teeth to improve how they come together and reduce joint stress.

When alignment contributes to jaw tension, Invisalign may be recommended to balance the bite long-term.

FDA-approved use of Botox injections to relax overactive jaw muscles, ease tension, and provide lasting pain relief.

For more complex cases, we coordinate with trusted local specialists in TMJ-focused therapy and rehabilitation.

We believe in conservative, effective treatment that gets to the root of the issue — not just masking symptoms.

When to Seek TMJ Treatment

If any of the following apply to you, it may be time to speak with a TMJ-trained dentist:

Early intervention can prevent more serious damage and preserve joint health over time.

  • You wake up with jaw soreness or headaches
  • Your jaw locks open or closed
  • You clench or grind your teeth (even unknowingly)
  • You hear clicking or popping during jaw movement
  • Your bite feels “off” or uneven
  • You&#x27;ve noticed wear on your teeth without decay
  • You’ve had limited success with medications or self-care
