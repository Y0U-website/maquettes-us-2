Treat Gum Disease Before It Progresses

Periodontal disease often develops silently without noticeable pain. By the time symptoms appear, the gums and supporting bone may already be damaged.

At Dentists on Washington, we diagnose and treat gum disease early to help prevent tooth loss, infection, and long-term oral health complications. During your dental exam, we carefully evaluate your gums to determine the best treatment plan for your needs.

Your Periodontal Disease Treatment Process

Diagnosis

Your dentist examines your gums and customizes treatment recommendations based on the severity of the condition and your oral health needs.

Scaling & Root Planing

Scaling and root planing is a deep cleaning procedure used to remove plaque and tartar beneath the gumline. The tooth roots are then smoothed to help the gums heal and reduce future bacterial buildup.

Healing & Follow-Up Care

After treatment, your dentist monitors healing and checks if periodontal pockets have improved. Ongoing maintenance and good oral hygiene are essential to prevent gum disease from returning.

Common Signs of Periodontal Disease

Bleeding Gums Gums that bleed while brushing or flossing may be an early warning sign.

Persistent Bad Breath Bacteria trapped below the gumline can lead to chronic bad breath.

Gum Swelling Inflamed or sensitive gums often indicate infection or irritation.

Receding Gums Gums pulling away from the teeth may expose roots and increase sensitivity.

Why Early Treatment Matters

Treating periodontal disease early helps protect your gums, jawbone, and teeth from permanent damage. Delaying treatment can allow infection to spread deeper below the gumline, increasing the risk of tooth loss and more complex dental procedures.

Benefits of Treatment

  • Reduces gum inflammation
  • Removes harmful bacteria
  • Helps prevent tooth loss
  • Improves gum health
  • Supports long-term oral wellness
