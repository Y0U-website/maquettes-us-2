Dr. Neda Oromchian

Dr. Neda Oromchian is the Dental Director of Dentists on Wheels. She graduated from the University of

the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry. She practiced pediatric

dentistry due to her abundant love for children. As a doctor, she has

consistently been involved with volunteering her time and service to

patients and organizations in need, such as providing free dental work for local schools.

Dr. David Rainero

Dr. Neda Oromchian

Dr. Neda Oromchian

Dr. Rainero is a Board Certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and is a Fellow of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons. He is a member of numerous professional societies including the American Dental Association and the Contra Costa Dental Society. He has

been in private practice in Walnut Creek for over 25 years. Dr. Rainero is on the Active Medical Staff of John Muir Medical Center in Walnut Creek and has helped develop, establish and coordinates the Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Trauma Service.

Dr. Kian Farzaneh

Dr. John Swearingen

Dr. Farzaneh is a volunteer Oral Surgeon. He graduated from the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry. He then completed a four-year oral and maxillofacial residency program at Highland General Hospital/ University of the Pacific. He is also currently on staff at John Muir Medical Center, a major trauma center in the Bay Area. He volunteers abroad in Haiti and Guatemala by providing extractions of kid’s infected baby teeth. Bringing his volunteerism effort locally, he has joined DOW to heal as many as he can in his community.

Dr. Brian Hathcoat

Dr. Brian Hathcoat served in the U.S. Navy before attending U.C. Berkeley. He later earned his DDS from the University of the Pacific in San Francisco. Dr. Hathcoat is an active member of the Contra Costa County Dental Society and gives back to the community as a volunteer dentist. Dr. Yass Dastmalchi

Dr. Yass Dastmalchi brings specialized endodontic expertise to our team. After earning her dental degree from the University of Minnesota, she completed advanced endodontic training at Boston University Goldman School of Dentistry. With over 25 years of practice experience Dr. Dastmalchi is committed to providing high-quality care to underserved communities. When not volunteering with us, she enjoys spending time with her husband and two daughters, traveling, reading, and Zumba.

Dr. Procopio

Dr. Procopio brings international expertise to our team, having earned her dental degree with honors from Italy’s University of Messina in 1992, with additional training at the University of Bologna. After relocating to California in 1998, she graduated from the University of Pacific School of Dentistry in 2001. Following two years of practice in San Francisco, she established herself in Walnut Creek in 2003, where she lives with her husband and two daughters. Her commitment to community service has inspired the next generation, as one of her daughters also volunteers with Dentists on Wheels.
