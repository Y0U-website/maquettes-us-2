Am I a good candidate for All-on-4 Dental Implants?

Why not go with a hybrid that combines the best aspects of each? The All-on-4® implant treatment concept is an innovative solution for individuals with significant tooth loss that might be right for you.

– Restore Natural Speech and Function For patients with removable dentures or a dental bridge, there are significant benefits to replacing your current device with an All-on-4. The biggest advantage is that All-on-4 implant-supported dentures permanently anchor to the jawbone, enabling natural speech and function. You can also return to a restriction-free diet and never again deal with embarrassing slippage.

– Stimulate Healthy Jawbone Growth The most significant disadvantage to removable dentures and bridges is that they do not stimulate growth in your jawbone. Removable dentures place bite pressure directly onto the gums. Without stimulation, the jaw slowly loses bone mass. Implants act just like your natural teeth by transferring bite pressure on the jawbone and stimulating healthy growth.

The All-on-4 procedure takes place in a single surgical appointment, which usually lasts only a few hours. You can use your new teeth like usual on the very same day, and full recovery often only takes a week or two. So, in a single day, experience the complete transformation of your smile and oral function.

– Provide a Reliable and Lasting Prosthetic

All-on-4 is very reliable, showing a 94 % success rate after 10 years. An all-on-4 prosthetic is designed to last for decades, and with proper care, can last a lifetime.
