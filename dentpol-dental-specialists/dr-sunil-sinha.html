Dr Sunil Sinha

In 2010, Dr. Sinha became board-certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. Dr. Sinha’s training at the University of Cincinnati brought to light his passion for orthognathic and trauma surgery. Dr. Sinha has privileges at Advocate Christ Hospital which is the busiest Level 1 trauma center in Illinois as well as various other local hospitals.

Dr. Sinha sees patients of all ages and volunteers his services for the severely mentally and physically challenged as part of Easter Seals. His office provides anxious patients with various options for sedation including IV sedation, Nitrous oxide and oral sedation. He has extensive experience in implantology, including the All-on-4 technique.

Dr. Sinha is married to his wife Dr. Gunjan Jain and has 3 sons Armaan, Arjun, and Avi. When he is not practicing medicine Dr. Sinha enjoys spending time with his family and being involved with his boys’ baseball team.
