Implant Denture Repair by Mail

Implant-supported dentures (also called overdentures) place additional stress on the denture base because they connect to dental implants through attachment systems. Over time, this stress can cause cracks, fractures, or breakage in the acrylic denture base , especially around the attachment areas. Denture Repair Lab provides professional implant denture base repair by mail for customers nationwide.

Once your implant denture arrives at our lab, it is evaluated by a professional dental technician to determine how the denture base can be repaired and reinforced while preserving the existing implant attachment components.

This service applies to implant-retained dentures that use locator, bar, or similar attachment systems.

What Implant Denture Repair Covers

This service does not include replacing implant parts, housings, locators, or bars. Those components require separate services.

  • Cracks or fractures in the acrylic implant denture base
  • Breaks in the overdenture near implant attachment housings
  • Acrylic damage surrounding attachment areas
  • Structural weaknesses caused by repeated stress
  • Base reinforcement of overdenture when needed

How Implant Denture Repair Works

  • Ship your implant denture to our lab using the carrier and speed you prefer.
  • Inspection and confirmation — we examine the denture base and confirm the repair method and price before any work begins.
  • Professional base repair — the acrylic base is repaired and reinforced as needed around the existing implant attachment areas.
  • Return shipping with tracking — your denture is shipped back with tracking. Express return shipping is available.

How Long Does Implant Denture Repair Take?

Most implant denture base repairs are completed after inspection and approval and shipped back within 24–48 hours of arrival at our lab. Final delivery time depends on the shipping method selected and how quickly the repair is approved.
