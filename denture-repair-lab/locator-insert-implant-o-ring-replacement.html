Locator Insert & Implant O-Ring Replacement by Mail

If your implant overdenture has worn, broken, or missing O-rings or locator inserts, it may not fit securely or function comfortably. Denture Repair Lab provides professional locator insert and implant overdenture O-ring replacement by mail for customers nationwide. Once your overdenture arrives at our lab, it is evaluated by a professional dental technician so the correct components can be selected and replaced.

This service is specifically for implant-retained dentures (overdentures) that use locator inserts, O-rings, or similar attachment housings.

What Locator Insert / O-Ring Replacement Covers

This service is for attachment replacement only. Denture base cracks, broken teeth, or structural damage are handled on separate repair pages.

  • Implant O-rings
  • Locator inserts
  • Hader bar ryders
  • Retentive components for implant overdentures
  • Worn or broken attachment inserts

How Locator Insert & O-Ring Replacement Works

  • Ship your overdenture to our lab using the shipping method you prefer.
  • Inspection and confirmation — we identify the correct attachment system and confirm the final price before any work begins.
  • Professional replacement — worn or damaged O-rings and inserts are replaced with new components.
  • Return shipping with tracking — your overdenture is shipped back with tracking. Express return shipping is available.

How Long Does O-Ring & Locator Replacement Take?

Most locator insert and O-ring replacements are completed the same day they are received and shipped back within 24 hours of arrival at our lab. Final delivery time depends on the shipping method selected and how quickly the replacement is confirmed after inspection.

How Locator & O-Ring Replacement Pricing Works

  • The number of O-rings or inserts being replaced
  • The type and brand of the attachment system
  • Whether additional attachment components are required
