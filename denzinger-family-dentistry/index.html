Early orthodontic evaluation provides both timely detection of problems and greater opportunity for more effective treatment with optimal results. Prudent intervention guides growth and development, preventing serious problems or complications later. When orthodontic intervention is not necessary, our team can carefully monitor growth and development and begin treatment when it is ideal.

By the age of 7, the first adult molars erupt, establishing the back bite. During this time, the Denzinger Family Dentistry team can evaluate front-to-back and side-to-side tooth relationships. For example, the presence of erupting incisors can indicate possible overbite, open bite, crowding or gummy smiles. Timely screening increases the chances for an incredible smile.
