New Patient Examinations

For our adult patients, the first visit begins with a comprehensive exam and X-rays. This gives us the time to get to know you, answer your questions, and ensure you feel comfortable with our team. During the exam, we carefully check your teeth, gums, and overall oral health.

We want to be clear: we never pressure our patients to complete treatment. Our role is to educate and provide guidance, while respecting that every patient has their own priorities and goals for their oral health.
