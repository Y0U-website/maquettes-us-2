Frequently Asked Questions

Where is your Anacortes orthodontic office located?

Our office is located at 1220 22nd St Ste A, Anacortes, WA 98221, on 22nd Street. We’re easy to reach for patients throughout Anacortes, Fidalgo Island, and the surrounding Skagit County area. Call us at (360) 293-2888 to schedule a visit or ask for directions.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. Your first consultation is free. During this visit, we’ll review your smile, discuss your goals, and recommend the best treatment options for you. It’s a no-pressure way to learn what’s possible and get your questions answered. Call (360) 293-2888 to book.

What types of braces and aligners do you offer in Anacortes?

We offer Damon System braces, including Damon Q metal and discreet Damon Clear brackets, plus clear aligners for a nearly invisible option. Dr. Damon will help you choose the treatment that best fits your smile, lifestyle, and budget during your consultation.

At what age should my child see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first orthodontic evaluation around age seven. Early visits let us monitor your child’s growth and address issues like crowding or crossbite at the ideal time. Many children won’t need treatment right away, but early evaluation helps us plan ahead.

Do you serve patients from the San Juan Islands and greater Skagit County?

Absolutely. Our Anacortes office welcomes patients from Fidalgo Island, the San Juan Islands, and communities across Skagit County. We schedule appointments efficiently and use virtual monitoring where possible, which helps reduce travel and office visits for families coming from farther away.
