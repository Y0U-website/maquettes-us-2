The practice

Dr. Derek Damon has been creating confident smiles in Anacortes since 2001. We pair advanced technology with personalized care, so you enjoy faster treatment, fewer appointments, and results you’ll be proud to share.

Whether you’re a parent booking your child’s first evaluation or an adult finally ready to straighten your teeth, we make every visit comfortable and welcoming. Your smile is our priority, and we’re here to guide you every step of the way.

Dr. Derek R. Damon , DDS, has served the Anacortes community for more than two decades. As an accredited orthodontist, he completed advanced post-doctoral training in orthodontics through a program recognized by the American Dental Association.

Orthodontics runs deep in his family. Dr. Damon is the sixth orthodontist across generations of the Damon family, so you can feel confident you’re in expert hands.

He earned his dental degree at the University of Washington and completed his orthodontic residency there in 2001. Dr. Damon is a proud member of the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO), the Pacific Coast Society of Orthodontists, and the Washington State Dental Association.

He also gives back locally, volunteering with the Mount Baker District Dental Society to support dental access programs across our region.

We offer a full range of treatments for every age and stage of life. Here are the services patients rely on most at our Anacortes office.

The Damon System uses tie-less, self-ligating brackets and gentle memory-shape wires. This means greater comfort, fewer adjustments, and faster overall treatment than traditional braces. Choose from Damon Q metal brackets or discreet Damon Clear brackets.

Les soins

We offer Damon System braces, including Damon Q metal and discreet Damon Clear brackets, plus clear aligners for a nearly invisible option. Dr. Damon will help you choose the treatment that best fits your smile, lifestyle, and budget during your consultation.
