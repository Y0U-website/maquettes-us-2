What to Expect During Invisalign Treatment

Your Invisalign treatment begins with a consultation at our office in Bellingham or Anacortes. We use modern imaging and digital scanning technology to evaluate your smile and create your treatment plan.

  • Consultation and Evaluation: We begin by discussing your concerns, goals, and orthodontic needs. Then we evaluate whether Invisalign is the right treatment option for your smile.
  • Digital Smile Scanning: Digital scans allow us to view your teeth in detail and plan your treatment with greater precision.
  • Custom Aligner Series: Your aligners are made specifically for your teeth and designed to move them gradually over time.
  • Daily Wear: For the best results, aligners should be worn as directed each day and removed only for eating, brushing, and flossing.
  • Progress Appointments: We monitor your progress throughout treatment to make sure everything is moving as planned.
  • Retainers After Treatment: After Invisalign treatment is complete, retainers help maintain your new smile and protect your results.
