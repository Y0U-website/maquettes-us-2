Meet the Doctors at Derhake Dental

At Derhake Dental, we pride ourselves on providing exceptional customer service and treating our patients like family. We always ask how we would want our loved ones to be cared for and provide that experience for your family.

Our experienced dental team is proud of our warm and welcoming atmosphere and wants all your visits to be pleasant and stress-free. We love going the extra mile, knowing that an exceptional experience helps motivate you to stay on top of your oral health needs. We are dedicated to ensuring that your dental needs are met and that you leave our office with a healthy and beautiful smile.

Dr. Spring, Dr. Nasresfahani, and our team of hygienists, assistants, and support staff look forward to welcoming you to Derhake Dental and getting to know you and your dental needs. Please take a moment to get to know our wonderful team members, and don t hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns.

Dr Claire Waldman

As a lifelong crafter, Dr. Waldman was drawn to dentistry for the unique opportunity to combine art, science, and education. In her free time she continues to quilt and loves biking. She has a sweet tooth, and her favorite dessert is frozen custard!

Dr. Sarah Spring

Dr. Sarah Spring graduated with honors and various accolades from the University of Missouri Kansas City School of Dentistry. Upon graduation, she completed a fellowship program in which she trained and mentored dental students as a part-time faculty member. Since then, Dr. Spring has served the patients in the Kansas City area.

Dr. Spring’s interest in dentistry was first sparked as a high school senior participating in healthcare rotations while living in New Haven, Missouri. She brings to her practice a background in health sciences and dental hygiene from Missouri Southern State University and years of experience as a dental hygienist.

When seeing patients in our office Monday through Thursday, Dr. Spring uses the latest technology and treatment techniques to maintain and restore her patients’ smiles and confidence. Patients often compliment Dr. Spring on her compassionate approach and warm, friendly personality. She enjoys bonding with patients of all ages and performing a wide variety of procedures.

Outside of the office, she serves as a Board Member of the Missouri Academy of General Dentistry. The Academy of General Dentistry is focused on continuing education. Dr. Spring is also a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry, a prestigious award that only 6 percent of dentists obtain.

Not one to sit idly, Dr. Spring often volunteers at community events and dental outreach programs such as Team Smile and Mission of Mercy. In her free time, Dr. Spring loves spending time with her husband, Gordon, two children, Isaac and Edith, and their Goldendoodle, Max. Other interests include traveling and gardening.
