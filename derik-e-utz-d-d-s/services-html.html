Gum Disease

Did you know that over 85 % of the population has some form of gum disease? Periodontal (gum) disease can affect your overall health and has been linked to other systemic diseases of the body. Unfortunately, symptoms of gum disease are not always easy to identify and may linger for months without notice. Our hygienists are highly skilled to recognize gum disease. Once diagnosed, they will assess the level of the disease to determine the appropriate care plan, utilizing the following services:

  • Charting and Diagnostic Services
  • Soft Tissue Management
  • Laser Conditioning of Affected Tissue
