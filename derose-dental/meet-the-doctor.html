Maria DeRose Barnes D.D.S.

Dr. Barnes enjoys continuing the 3rd generation of DeRose Dental in Racine, Wisconsin. She feels extremely grateful and excited to be back providing dental care to the communities where she grew up.

Francesca DeRose D.D.S.

Dr. DeRose is a lifelong resident of the Racine/Kenosha area. She is a graduate of Marquette University School of Dentistry, and has extensive experience in restorative and cosmetic dentistry - much to the acclaim of many happy patients. Dr. DeRose is past President of the Racine Dental Association and has served in representative positions with both the Wisconsin Dental Association and the American Dental Association. She served on the ADA’s Council on Dental Benefits and is a part-time faculty member at Marquette University School of Dentistry.

She enjoys working with her daughter, Dr. Maria DeRose Barnes at DeRose Dental in Racine, Wisconsin and is very proud that her daughter continues to carry on the family tradition of providing excellent dental care services.
