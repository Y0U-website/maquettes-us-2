Maria

Maria was born and raised right here in Racine, Wisconsin. Maria began her career in healthcare as certified nursing assistant before switching over to the field of dentistry in 1990. In her free time, Maria enjoys cooking and baking delicious Lithuanian food for her many friends and family.

Maria’s area of expertise in our office is the front reception area. She will greet you when you arrive for your appointment and take care of all of your scheduling needs!

Julie

Julie is a Racine native who, prior to having children, worked at Ascension and North Shore Bank. She took a 20 year hiatus to be a stay at home Mom and now enjoys working at the front desk of DeRose Dental. In her free time, she enjoys spending time with family, experimenting in the kitchen with new recipes and spending time outdoors.

Jocelyn

Jocelyn graduated from Nazarene High School in Belize and subsequently earned her Associate’s Degree in Tourism Management in El Salvador. She is fluent in English and Spanish. Jocelyn is the founder of a small shipping company based in Belize and has extensive experience working remotely as a virtual assistant. For several years, she has provided professional services to doctors and offices in the U.S. Jocelyn is delighted to be a part of DeRose Dental and looks forward to speaking with you as a part of our Receptionist team.

Mia

Mia joined the DeRose Dental team in 2022 and has been enjoying learning all about the various aspects of dentistry. She is a UW Parkside graduate and current Masters Degree student who is also working towards applying to dental school. Outside of the office, Mia enjoys volunteering in the community, spending time with family and friends and going to the gym.

Maureen

Maureen has been a Dental Hygienist for 27 years working mostly in specialty practices focusing on maintaining periodontal disease and treating complex dental matters. Being passionate about oral health, Maureen takes pride in educating all of her patients. She strives to give the absolute best care to everyone she sees. Maureen has more than 37 years in the dental field. Prior to becoming a hygienist, she was a dental assistant. Dentistry has been a vital part of her life. Outside the office, she enjoys traveling, biking and spending time with her husband, two children and grandchildren.

Emily

Emily started her dental career in 2008 with a Dental Hygiene degree from MATC. At the dental office, she enjoys helping patients achieve a health smile. In her free time, she loves to bake and spend time with her husband, 3 girls and hermit crab Rainbow.

Blanca

Blanca started her dental career in 2017 when she joined the DeRose Dental team after graduating from the Dental Assisting Program at Gateway Technical College. She was Dr.Barnes’ assistant before moving to her current role. Currently, her main focus is helping our patients understand the financial aspect of their treatment and dental insurance. She is passionate about ensuring our patients receive the best service and attention. In her free time you will find her involved in community projects and spending time with her family and her cat Miauski.

Denise

Denise has been in dentistry since 1984. She has enjoyed her many varied experiences in the dental field, including 10 years working with complex pediatric cases at Children’s Hospital of Wisconsin. Denise has spent the last 25 years in private dental practice and maintains her passion for dental assisting, patient education and relationship building. in her free time, Denise enjoys spending time with her family, outdoor activities including biking, caring for her vegetable garden and 2 cats.

Angela

Angela is a graduate of Marquette University. She has 29 years of experience in the dental field, 4 as a dental assistant and 25 as a dental hygienist in private practice. Angela enjoys getting to know her patients and providing the education and tools needed to maintain a healthy smile. Outside the office, Angela enjoys camping and traveling with her husband and two children.
