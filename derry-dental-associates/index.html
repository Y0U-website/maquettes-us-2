Dr. Erik grew up in the nearby town of West Newbury, MA. He attended Pentucket Regional High School and was also involved with a local swim team where he met his wife, Dr. Karen. He attended the University of Massachusetts and earned his bachelor’s degree in Exercise Physiology. After working as a phlebotomist at MGH in Boston, he ultimately decided on the field of dentistry.

He then attended Temple University’s Kornberg School of Dentistry and obtained his doctorate of medical dentistry (DMD) alongside Dr. Karen. While at Temple, Dr. Erik excelled in the clinical aspect of dentistry and received the Dentsply International Merit award for Removable Prosthodontics. He was also the president of the Stomatognathic Honor Society which focuses on total mouth reconstruction…

Dr. Erik has gone on to complete a Maxicourse certification. This is an advanced surgical training for Dental Implantology, which consists of over 300 continuing education units focusing on the placement and restoration of dental implants, bone grafting, and prosthetic reconstruction.

Dr. Erik is passionate about his patient’s well-being and focuses on treating the patient as a whole. He is an advocate for total body health and wellness. Dr. Erik himself enjoys and participates in all activities such as biking, swimming, running, hiking and just being outdoors.
