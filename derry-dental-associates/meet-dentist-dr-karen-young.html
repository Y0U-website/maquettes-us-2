Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Erik Young As an advocate for total body wellness, Dr. Erik doesn’t only consider a patient’s oral health when treating them, he treats every patient as a whole. Dr. Erik grew up in nearby West Newbury, MA where he was involved with a local swim team and where he met his wife, Dr. Karen Young.

Dr. Karen Young A Salem, NH native, Dr. Karen, along with Dr. Erik, attended Temple Kornberg University School of Dentistry in Philadelphia for their doctorates of dental medicine. She believes that every patient, no matter how small, deserves high-quality and customized dental care to improve their oral health and their lives.
