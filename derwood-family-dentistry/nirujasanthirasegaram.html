Dr. Niruja Santhirasegaram or fondly called by her patients as Dr. Niru has been serving the DC Metro area since 2012.

Dr. Niru graduated from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry with a Doctor in Dental Surgery Degree. Dr. Niru has been serving the Washington Metro area and she is known for her attention to detail and excellent care towards her patients.

Her Sri Lankan and East African background helps her to treat her patients with compassion and utmost unique care. As your family dental provider Dr. Niru routinely seeks out advanced certifications and training courses to expand her knowledge and skills related to family dental care.
