Casey Gremore, DDS

General Dentist

Dr. Gremore began practicing at Des Moines Dental Care’s current location in 2016, formerly known as Sloan Family Dentistry. Prior to practicing in Des Moines and following his graduation in 2012, Dr. Gremore practiced dentistry at the Captain James A. Lovell Federal Health Care Center in North Chicago. As a lieutenant in the United States Navy, he received additional training in areas of operative dentistry, oral surgery, endodontics, CAD/CAM dentistry, periodontics and oral diagnosis.

He is currently licensed to practice dentistry in Iowa and is a member of the American Dental Association , Iowa Dental Association and Des Moines District Dental Society .

Dr. Gremore is originally from Dubuque, Iowa. After graduating from Hempstead High School , he earned his undergraduate degree at the University of Northern Iowa with a major in Biology and minors in Chemistry and Politics & Law.

Following his undergraduate education, Dr. Gremore attended the University of Iowa College of Dentistry , earning the degree Doctor of Dental Surgery.

Outside of practicing dentistry, Dr. Gremore enjoys golfing, gaming, music, playing guitar and spending time with his family.
