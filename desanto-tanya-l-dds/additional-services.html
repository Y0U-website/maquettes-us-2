Comprehensive Dental Excellence at Prairie Dental Group

At Prairie Dental Group, we pride ourselves on offering a broad spectrum of top-notch dental services. Beyond our cosmetic dentistry expertise, we provide a variety of treatments, including children’s dentistry, precision porcelain crown work, effective topical fluoride treatments, and protective Night Guard appliances.

We’re equipped to handle all your dental needs, from routine fillings to advanced digital X-rays, and even solutions for sleep apnea or snoring issues. Our range extends to include six-month smile adjustments and comprehensive TMJ therapy, ideal for those suffering from migraines or chronic tooth grinding and jaw clenching. To explore these options, call us for a detailed consultation.

Our dedication to pediatric dental care ensures that your children receive thorough cleaning and general dentistry in a nurturing, enjoyable atmosphere. We’re committed to teaching them valuable oral health habits while ensuring they have a positive experience. To make each visit special, we offer a delightful treasure box from which children can choose a prize for their good behavior.

Understanding that dental visits can be anxiety-inducing, we at Prairie Dental Group provide calming solutions like medication or nitrous oxide to alleviate any discomfort or fear during procedures. We encourage open communication about any procedure-related concerns, ensuring that we’re always ready to assist and provide comfort.

In the event of dental emergencies, our team is prepared to respond swiftly. We offer flexible scheduling for urgent cases, catering to situations like toothaches, infections, broken teeth, or injuries. Our goal is to provide prompt and efficient care in your time of need.

Our practice, led by an all-female team of attentive dentists, has been serving the community since 2000 and has accumulated over 70 years of experience. We’ve been honored as the Best of Springfield for 10 years in a row, a testament to our commitment to patient-first care and the use of state-of-the-art technology.
