Same-Day Crown Treatments

Effortless Smile Enhancements with CEREC Crowns

At Prairie Dental Group, we understand the impact of a damaged tooth on your confidence and smile. To address this, we offer an efficient, long-lasting remedy with our same-day CEREC crowns. These ceramic crowns are meticulously crafted using a CEREC intraoral camera, ensuring a perfect match in color, texture, and translucency to your natural teeth. This technology not only restores your smile but also enhances its natural beauty.

CEREC, standing for Ceramic Reconstruction, is a revolutionary method in dental restoration. When you opt for a CEREC crown at our clinic, you receive a custom-made ceramic crown that seamlessly blends with your existing teeth, improving your smile both aesthetically and functionally. These crowns are notable for their durability and affordability, making them an excellent choice for dental restoration.

For those interested in a swift and effective restorative solution, our same-day CEREC crowns are an ideal choice. Should you have any queries about CEREC crowns or wish to schedule a consultation, feel free to contact our office.
