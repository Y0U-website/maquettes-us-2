Porcelain Veneers

Thin custom shells bonded to the front of your teeth. Fixes chips, gaps, severe discoloration, or misalignment.

  • Best for: Multiple cosmetic issues at once
  • Durability: 10 to 15+ years
  • Custom designed for your face

Dental Bonding

Tooth-colored composite resin sculpted onto your tooth. Fixes chips, gaps, and small cosmetic issues quickly.

  • Best for: Single tooth fixes
  • Timeline: One visit, immediate results
  • Cost: Much less than veneers
  • Reversible: No tooth structure removed

Crowns & Bridges

Custom crowns cap damaged or weakened teeth, and bridges replace missing teeth by anchoring to the ones beside them. Both restore strength and a natural look.

  • Best for: Damaged, weak, or missing teeth
  • Custom-matched to your natural teeth
  • Restores full chewing strength
  • Long-lasting with good care

How a Cosmetic Consultation Works

A great cosmetic result starts with great planning. Here’s how we approach it.

Step 1. Listen First

We start by understanding what you’d change about your smile if you could. There are no wrong answers and no judgment. Just an honest conversation.

Step 2. Examine & Photograph

We look closely at your teeth, gums, bite, and overall facial harmony. Digital photos help us plan and let you see what we see.

Step 3. Present Options

You’ll get multiple solutions ranging from minimal to involved, each with clear pricing and timeline. No high-pressure upsell. Just real options.

Step 4. Preview Your Result

For larger cases, we can show you a digital preview of your new smile before committing to treatment. You see the result before you decide.

Step 5. Phased or Complete

Many patients spread cosmetic work over time to fit their budget. Others do it all at once. Both work. We make a plan around your life.
