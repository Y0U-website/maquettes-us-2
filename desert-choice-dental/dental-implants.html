Implant-Supported Bridge

Replace 2 to 4 teeth with implants anchoring a bridge, without grinding down healthy teeth.

  • Best for: Several missing teeth in a row
  • Timeline: 3 to 6 months
  • Preserves surrounding teeth
  • More stable than traditional bridges

All-on-4 / All-on-6

A full set of replacement teeth supported by just 4 or 6 implants per arch. Often placed in a single day.

  • Best for: Full upper or lower replacement
  • Timeline: Same-day teeth possible
  • Fewer implants = lower cost
  • Permanent , not a denture

Implant-Retained Dentures

Keep your existing dentures from slipping by anchoring them to a few strategically placed implants.

  • Best for: Loose, ill-fitting dentures
  • Timeline: 3 to 4 months
  • Removable for easy cleaning
  • No more denture adhesive

Your Implant Journey, Start to Finish

Implants take time. But every step is planned upfront so you always know what’s next and what it’ll cost.

Step 1. Consultation & 3D Scan

We examine your mouth, take a 3D scan of your jaw, and discuss your goals. You’ll get a written treatment plan with full pricing before any work begins.

Step 2. Implant Placement

Dr. Nivin places the titanium implant into your jawbone under local anesthesia, with sedation available if you’d prefer. The procedure typically takes 30 to 60 minutes per implant.

Step 3. Healing Period

Your jawbone bonds to the implant over 3 to 6 months, a process called osseointegration. During this time, you wear a temporary tooth so you never go without a smile.

Step 4. Crown Placement

Once healed, we attach your custom-made permanent crown. Color-matched to your other teeth, shaped to look natural. The result is indistinguishable from a real tooth.

Step 5. Follow-Up Care

Implants are easy to maintain, brush and floss like normal teeth. We’ll see you at routine cleanings to make sure everything stays healthy long-term.
