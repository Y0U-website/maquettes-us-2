Sealants & Fluoride

Simple preventive treatments that dramatically reduce a child’s risk of cavities.

  • Sealants: thin protective coating on molars
  • Fluoride: strengthens enamel against decay
  • Painless , no needles, no drilling
  • Most effective in ages 6 to 14

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Modern composite fillings that match your natural tooth color. Stronger, safer, and better-looking than old silver fillings.

  • Mercury-free composite materials
  • Matched to your tooth shade
  • Bonds directly to your tooth
  • Long-lasting when properly placed

Crowns, Bridges & Extractions

For damaged or missing teeth. We handle the full range of restorative work in-house.

  • Crowns rebuild badly damaged teeth
  • Bridges replace missing teeth
  • Extractions done gently, sedation available
  • Wisdom teeth handled in-house

Your First Family Visit

If you’ve been searching for a long-term family dental home, here’s how we start the relationship.

Step 1. New Patient Paperwork

Quick online forms before your visit so you can spend your appointment time getting care, not filling out paperwork in a waiting room.

Step 2. Full Exam

Dr. Nivin personally examines each family member. We take digital X-rays only if needed, screen for oral cancer, and check overall mouth health.

Step 3. Cleaning & Polish

Our hygienists do a gentle, thorough cleaning. For kids’ first visits, we go slow and let them ask questions throughout.

Step 4. Plan & Discuss

At the end of the visit, Dr. Nivin sits down with you and walks through what she found, what needs attention, and what can wait. No high-pressure pitch.

Step 5. Schedule Next Steps

Whatever your family needs next, whether that’s a cleaning in 6 months or treatment planning, we set it up before you leave.
