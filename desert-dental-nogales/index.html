The practice

All-on-4 dental implants work by utilizing four strategically placed implants to support a full set of replacement teeth. This approach offers immediate functionality, minimizes the need for bone grafting, and provides a stable, permanent, and natural-looking smile. It’s a transformative procedure that can enhance a patient’s quality of life by restoring their ability to eat, speak, and smile…

The prosthetic teeth are carefully crafted to appear remarkably natural. They are customized to match the shape, size, and color of your existing teeth, ensuring a seamless blend with your facial features. The goal is to create a smile that not only looks beautiful but also appears entirely genuine. We offer different materials for these prosthetic teeth.
