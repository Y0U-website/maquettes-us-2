Coming from a family deeply rooted in the medical field, Gerson was influenced from a young age by his relatives. Growing up in Mexico and later in Utah, Gerson was inspired by his uncle, a respected dentist, who played a significant role in sparking his interest in dentistry.

Driven by his family’s influence and his own curiosity, Gerson embarked on a journey to pursue a career in dentistry by enrolling in American Tech College to become a lab technician. Throughout his education path, he delved into various courses focusing on esthetic techniques, honing his skills and expertise in the field.
