Maria Romaniak-Kumik, DDS

Maria Romaniak-Kumik, DDS

Design Dentistry and Smile is a LLC owned by Dr. Romaniak-Kumik.

Dr.Romaniak-Kumik has been practicing dentistry for the past 29 years. She graduated from New York University Collage of Dentistry in 1995.

From January 1996 till January 1997 Dr. Romaniak-Kumik did her Residency in General Dentistry working for US Army Dental at Fort Knox, KY .

From January-1997 till May-2000 Dr.Romaniak-Kumik worked as an associate in private practice of Dr.Michael Malka in the state of New Jersey.

From May 2000 till December 2008 she has been employed as a general dentist by Community Health Center providing dental services for Community Population for 8 years.

In December 2008 Dr.Romaniak-Kumik opened Design Dentistry and Smile, LLC. When it comes to practice success, Dr.Romaniak-Kumik says that everybody on staff, herself included, is an equal player on the field. When I hire people, I tell them that I don’t think I’m more important than anyone else when it comes to practice success.

There’s a chain of command, of course, but when it comes to succeeding we all play a very equal role.
