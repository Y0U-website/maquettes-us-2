Your Step-by-Step Plan

Understand your goals

We start with photos, a 3D scan, and a bite assessment — plus a conversation about your priorities: discreet treatment, speed, comfort, or long-term stability.

Map your digital plan

Your teeth and bite are analyzed digitally, and we design a phased plan that predicts movement and explains why aligners or braces are recommended.

Begin gradual, guided movement

Aligners or braces apply controlled forces to shift teeth safely. We monitor comfort, hygiene, and progress so the pace stays healthy and predictable.

Fine-tune your result

As movement progresses, we make micro-adjustments to aligners, attachments, or wires to ensure symmetry, balance, and a comfortable bite.

Lock in long-term stability

Your retainer plan is customized to how your teeth behave. We help you maintain your result so your smile stays straight — not just today, but for years.
