Dental Implants

  • Implant-supported restorations are very stable. Unlike dentures, they do not require adhesive.
  • Thanks to their great stability, when you have implants, you do not need to worry about dietary restrictions. Even difficult-to-chew foods like whole fruits and steaks are options!
  • Dental implants are durable. When you care for them properly - with routine hygiene and dental visits - they can last for decades.
