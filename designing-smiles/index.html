Losing teeth can be devastating, whether you lose a single tooth or you need to wear full dentures. You may feel embarrassed about your teeth, and you may worry about how well you will be able to eat and speak. Fortunately, dental implants can replace missing teeth in a way that is virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth.

At Designing Smiles in Roseville, MN, Dr. Deborah Fung performs all aspects of implant placement, including planning, surgery, and restoration. With her clinical precision and eye for design, you will enjoy a quick recovery and a beautiful smile that you can feel great about.

Dr. Fung will begin your treatment with a thorough consultation and planning session. After determining your candidacy for implants, she will evaluate your jawbone structure and look at the best positions for implant posts. The number of implants you need will depend on the size of your prosthetic. You could receive a single implant for a crown or multiple implants to support a full denture.

While you are waiting for the implants to integrate, Dr. Fung will design your final prosthetic. She will consider the natural shape and color of your teeth to provide a lifelike match. After your jaw has healed, she will attach the prosthetic to the tops of the implants.

Since implants sit in the jawbone, it is essential that they have enough tissue for support. If you have sustained jaw recession as a result of your tooth loss, you may not be eligible for implants. In many cases, however, a bone graft can rebuild the lost structure and reinstate your candidacy for treatment.
