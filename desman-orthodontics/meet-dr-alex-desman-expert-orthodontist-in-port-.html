Meet Your Port St. Lucie Orthodontist

Dr. Desman believes successful orthodontic treatment starts with understanding and education. He makes time to address questions, outline each phase of treatment, and ensure patients know what to expect along the way. With a strong attention to detail , he provides precise, effective orthodontic solutions in an environment where patients and families feel respected and appreciated. His focus is on building long-term relationships through honesty, careful planning, and treatment strategies designed around each patient’s orthodontic needs.
