Meet Our Team

Your Braces and Clear Aligner Team in Port St. Lucie

Diane

Diane is our office manager and a Certified Orthodontic Assistant. She has been in the orthodontic field since 1985 and has been part of Desman Orthodontics since 1999. Married to her high school sweetheart, she’s a proud mom of three daughters and grandmother to four. Diane loves spending time with family, friends, and serving in her church ministry.

Elizabeth

Elizabeth joined our practice as an orthodontic assistant in 2024 and quickly became a patient favorite. She loves learning new aspects of orthodontics and is committed to making every visit comfortable and stress-free. Outside the office, she enjoys exercising, watching rom-coms, and spending time with loved ones.

Sheyla

Sheyla became an Orthodontic Assistant in September of 2020. She has joined Desman Orthodontics along with her years of experience to ensure the journey to your dream smile is smooth sailing! Outside of the office she enjoys spending time with family, horseback riding through trails or catching a wave at the beach.

Tiffaney

As our scheduling coordinator, Tiffaney brings years of valuable experience to the front desk. She supports everything from scheduling to insurance to social media, and she’s excited to welcome new patients into the practice. On weekends, you’ll find her cheering on her kids at volleyball and baseball tournaments. Her favorite treat? Chocolate chip cookies.
