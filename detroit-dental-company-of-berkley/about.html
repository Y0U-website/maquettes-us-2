MEET OUR DENTISTS

Meet

DR. DIORA KASHAT, DDS

Originally from Clinton Township, Dr. Kashat received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the prestigious University of Michigan Dental School and completed her dental residency at Jacobi Medical Center in New York.

After 10 years in practice, her favorite aspect of dentistry is not only enhancing the health and quality of life for patients, but seeing them smile confidently after care. As the owner of Detroit Dental Company, she looks forward to bringing focused, effective treatment plan to patients of all needs and ages.

Dr. Kashat specializes in dental implants, but is skilled in all aspects of dentistry, including cosmetic, restorative and orthodontics. Whether you’re seeking a straighter, whiter smile or correcting missing and cracked teeth, she’s got you covered.

OUTSIDE OF THE PRACTICE:

Outside of work, she enjoys taking various workout classes and spending time with her husband, her son Ari, daughter Kaia, and family.

Learn more about Dr. Kashat when you visit us for your next appointment!

Meet

DR. CHRISTINA FLUEGGE

Dr. Fluegge earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry and her bachelor’s degree in neuroscience from the University of Michigan. She has a passion for encouraging and educating her patients on preventative care and actively serves the community by volunteering at a local dental clinic. She provides high quality care and takes pride in making her patients of all ages feel comfortable and confident!

OUTSIDE OF THE PRACTICE:

Outside of work, she enjoys spending time with her fiancé and their families. She enjoys going on morning walks, taking workout classes and baking sourdough bread!

Meet

DR. ALAN CIRILLI, DDS

Dr. Cirilli has been practicing dentistry for over 40 years, having graduated from Detroit Mercy Dental School before opening his own practice.

Dr. Cirilli enjoys all aspects of dentistry especially helping patients restore their smile through advanced surgical procedures. He regularly participates in continuing education courses in order to provide patients with the best possible care.

After transitioning his practice this year to new ownership, he’s pleased to call Detroit Dental Company home and is thrilled to work alongside the incredibly talented Dr. Diora Kashat.

OUTSIDE OF THE PRACTICE:

Outside of work, he enjoys golfing, spending time with his three grown children, Alan, Tony, and Ashley, daughter-in-law Ashley, and with his adorable grandsons, Tyson, Levi, Arrow and Atlas.

Learn more about Dr. Cirilli when you visit us for your next appointment!
