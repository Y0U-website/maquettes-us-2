PAPERLESS AND CHARTLESS CONVENIENCE

At Detroit Dental Company, we’ve embraced a fully digital, paperless, and chartless approach to streamline your dental care experience. By eliminating paper records, all patient information is securely stored and easily accessible through our digital system, improving accuracy and efficiency.

Our chartless system allows us to maintain and update your dental records electronically, ensuring quick access to vital information and seamless collaboration when needed. This modern approach helps us provide a more organized, eco-friendly, and convenient service for you.

CONE BEAM TECHNOLOGY

At Detroit Dental Company, we use advanced Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) to provide precise 3D images of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures. This state-of-the-art technology offers a comprehensive view, allowing us to diagnose and plan treatments with greater accuracy.

Unlike traditional 2D X-rays, Cone Beam imaging captures detailed, 360-degree scans, which are especially useful for procedures like dental implants, root canal therapy, and assessing complex cases. With CBCT, we can tailor treatments to your unique anatomy, improving outcomes and minimizing risks.

DIGITAL SCANNING AND IMPRESSIONS

Say goodbye to messy, uncomfortable impression trays! At Detroit Dental Company, we use advanced digital scanning technology to capture precise 3D images of your teeth. This modern approach allows us to create highly accurate digital impressions for crowns, bridges, Invisalign , and other treatments.

With digital scanning, the process is faster, more comfortable, and more accurate than traditional methods. The result? A better-fitting restoration and a more streamlined experience for you no putty, no hassle.

CEREC SAME-DAY CROWNS

At Detroit Dental Company, we offer CEREC milling technology, allowing us to create custom, high-quality dental restorations in just one visit. With CEREC, we can design, mill, and place crowns, inlays, and onlays right in our office eliminating the need for temporary crowns or multiple appointments.

Using 3D digital scanning, we capture precise measurements of your tooth, then the CEREC machine mills your restoration from a durable ceramic block. This technology ensures a perfect fit, natural appearance, and a faster, more convenient experience for you.

3D PRINTING

At Detroit Dental Company, we harness the power of 3D printing to enhance precision and efficiency in our dental treatments. This cutting-edge technology allows us to create custom dental appliances, such as surgical guides, clear aligners, and even temporary crowns, right in our office.

By using 3D printing, we can deliver highly accurate, tailor-made solutions faster than ever before. Whether for orthodontic care or complex procedures, this technology helps us provide more personalized, precise, and comfortable treatments for our patients.
