Dr. Ban Nacy was born and raised in Baghdad Iraq. She graduated from the University of Baghdad College of Dentistry . From there she moved to New York where she studied and graduated from NYU College of Dentistry .

Ban continued on to perform one year of post grad training in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at Boston’s Mass General Hospital . This is the oldest and largest teaching hospital of Harvard Medical School .

She went on to perform an additional year of training for Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at Woodhull Medical Center an affiliate of New York University .

She has a decade of patient care and continues to advance her knowledge at every opportunity. Ban has and continues to provide care to the underprivileged.
