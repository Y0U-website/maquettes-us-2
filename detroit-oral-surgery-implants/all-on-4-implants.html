What Are All-on-X / Full Arch Implants?

Benefits of All-on-X / Full Arch Implants

Fewer Appointments

Fewer appointments and a faster timeline compared to conventional, multi-implant cases.

Immediate Confidence

Immediate confidence, with many patients receiving provisional teeth the same day.

Stable Function

Stable chewing and clear speech without the slipping common to removable dentures.

Jawbone Stimulation

Jawbone stimulation that supports facial structure and long-term oral health.

Natural Look

A fixed, natural look designed for daily comfort and durability.

How the All-on-X Implant Procedure Works

Consultation and Planning

A thorough exam and imaging inform a precise, customized plan using digital on-X workflows for accuracy and efficiency.

Implant Placement

Implants are strategically positioned to maximize stability and support a full-arch restoration.

Provisional Teeth

In many cases, a provisional set of fixed teeth can be attached the same day so you leave with a complete smile.

Healing and Final Restoration

Over the following months, the implants integrate with the bone while you use your provisional teeth. Once healing is complete, the final, durable bridge is placed to deliver long-term function and aesthetics.

Pros and Cons

Pro: Fewer Visits

Fewer implants and visits compared to traditional approaches.

Pro: Same-Day Teeth

Often provides same-day provisional teeth for immediate confidence.

Pro: Fixed Stability

Fixed stability without adhesives or daily removal.

Pro: Reduced Grafting

May reduce the need for grafting in select cases.

Con: Health Requirements

Requires healthy gums and sufficient bone for stable support.

Con: Provisional Phase

Provisional phase requires mindful eating and care while healing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Many patients receive provisional teeth the same day implants are placed. Final teeth are delivered after healing, which typically takes a few months. The overall timeline depends on your specific needs and healing response.

Comfort measures are used throughout treatment to minimize discomfort. Some soreness is common after surgery and generally improves over several days. Clear home-care instructions help support a smooth recovery.

All-on-X is designed to use available bone efficiently, which may reduce the need for grafting in select cases. An exam determines whether this approach or another full-arch tooth replacement is best for you.

Daily brushing, cleaning under the bridge as directed, and routine follow-ups are essential. Consistent care helps protect your implants and keep your restoration looking and functioning its best.

All-on-X provides a fixed, nonremovable bridge on strategically placed implants for a full arch.
