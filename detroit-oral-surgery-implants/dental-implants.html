The Dental Implants Treatment Process

Comprehensive Evaluation

The visit includes a full review of your oral health, medical history, and goals. Digital planning helps visualize bone volume, identify ideal implant sites, and map the final tooth positions for a predictable outcome.

Precise Surgical Placement

Implants are placed using minimally invasive methods whenever possible. Guided surgery helps ensure accuracy and can reduce chair time and post-operative discomfort.

Healing and Integration

Over the next several weeks or months, the implant and bone fuse together. During this phase, you may wear a temporary restoration to maintain appearance and function as you heal.

Final Restoration

Once integration is complete, the final crown, bridge, or full-arch prosthesis is placed. The result is a confident, stable smile that looks and feels natural.
