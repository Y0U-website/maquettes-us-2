Most adults with adequate bone and reasonable oral health are candidates. A clinical exam and imaging help determine whether implants are a good fit or if preparatory steps like bone grafting are needed.

With proper care and regular check-ups, implants can last many years. The implant post itself integrates with bone and is designed for long-term stability. Restorations on top may eventually need replacement.

Initial healing takes days to a few weeks. Full integration with the jawbone typically requires several months before the final restoration is placed. Guidelines for diet, activity, and hygiene support smooth recovery.
