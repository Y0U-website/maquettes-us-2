How do dental implants help preserve jawbone health and prevent bone loss?

A missing tooth can cause the jawbone to deteriorate over time. Dental implants stimulate the bone just like natural tooth roots, maintaining strength and preventing the sunken appearance often seen with long-term tooth loss. This helps protect facial structure and long-term oral health.
