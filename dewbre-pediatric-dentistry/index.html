The practice

Following dental school, Dr. Dewbre completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry, where he quickly fell in love with the Oklahoma City area. He then returned to Lubbock to practice alongside his childhood dentist for four years in a family practice setting.

Today, Dr. Dewbre is proud to serve families in Edmond and the greater Oklahoma City area, combining high-quality care with a fun, welcoming environment for children.

Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with his wife, Cori, and their two children, Quinn and Nash. Being back in Oklahoma is especially meaningful, as it allows him to be close to his brother, who also happens to be his best friend and a local orthodontist.
