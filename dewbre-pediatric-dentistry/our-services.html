Our Services

O U R S E R V I C E S

Our priority is to create a positive dental experience for your child. This shapes their willingness to continue seeking oral health care for a lifetime. Our focus is on teaching healthy habits that will allow overall health with an emphasis on healthy teeth and gums. We offer simple, individualized plans to help caregivers keep their children s teeth healthy.

CLEANINGS

Gentle, thorough cleanings designed to keep your child’s smile healthy, bright, and cavity-free. We focus on prevention while making every visit comfortable and stress-free.

COMPOSITE FILLINGS

Tooth-colored fillings that blend seamlessly with your child’s natural smile while restoring strength and function. A safe, durable solution for treating cavities.

DENTAL EMERGENCIES

Fast, compassionate care when your child needs it most. From toothaches to injuries, we’re here to provide prompt treatment and relief.

TOOTH EXTRACTIONS

When a tooth needs to be removed, we ensure a smooth, gentle experience with your child’s comfort as our top priority.

INFANT DENTAL CARE

Early dental visits to support healthy development from the very beginning. We guide parents on proper care, feeding habits, and prevention. We believe in everyone by ONE!

TONGUE TIE REVISIONS

A quick, effective procedure to improve mobility and function, supporting better feeding, speech, and overall oral development. Visit our tongue tie page to learn more.
