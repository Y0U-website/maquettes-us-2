Meet Your Trusted Willow Park, TX Pediatric Dentist

Get to Know Dr. Michael Ball

As an associate dentist here at Stroud Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Ball goes above and beyond each day to provide high-quality care to children here in our local community. He loves working with such fun patients, as well as amazing staff members! If you’d like to learn more about him, just keep reading below.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Pediatric Dentist?

Dr. Ball needed quite a bit of complex dental care as a child, including several years of braces and an early dental implant! These experiences led him to explore the dental field more thoroughly as he grew up. Several local dentists mentored him during college and ignited his passion for this type of work. He’s always wanted his career to involve children in some way, and becoming a pediatric dentist gave him the opportunity to help little ones in need and keep them smiling brightly!

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?
