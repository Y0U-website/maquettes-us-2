Vaccines

Get your COVID-19 vaccine today!

The COVID-19 vaccine and Pfizer-BioNTech, Moderna and Johnson & Johnson boosters are available at select Marshall Health clinics . Individuals ages 5 and older are eligible to get vaccinated.

Vaccination protects you and those around you. At Marshall Health, we are committed to making the COVID-19 vaccine accessible to our patients and community. We will continue to provide the latest information you want to know about the safety and science behind the vaccine and, of course, how and where to get it.
