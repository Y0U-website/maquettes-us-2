Who Qualifies for Dental Implants?

While dental implants are an excellent solution for most patients, these tooth replacements are not for everyone. Patients with significant bone density loss in the jawbone due to gum disease or other chronic conditions may not qualify for dental implants. This is because the anchor posts necessary for dental implants require strong jawbones for safe implantation.

What to Expect During the Implant Procedure

The dental implant procedure is a multi-step process. Our staff at Diadem Family Dental will visually inspect the teeth, gum, and jaw structures for any issues. Next, technicians will take images and X-rays to determine the patient’s dental health. If the patient is a good candidate for dental implants, the next steps involve implanting titanium posts.

These posts will be inserted into the gums and jaw as an anchor for the tooth replacement. After the healing process finishes, our dentist will install a custom crown on the titanium post, finishing the implant procedure and completing your smile.
