Accessibility Statement

Please note that older systems or outdated browsers may not provide the best experience or full accessibility support.

  • Screen magnification tools
  • Speech recognition software
  • Built-in text-to-speech tools in operating systems

Compatibility Limitations

DiamondBraces.com is optimized for use with the latest versions of mainstream web browsers, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. We also ensure compatibility with widely used assistive technologies such as JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, and TalkBack.

However, it is important to note that older systems or outdated browsers may not support all features of the site or may present inconsistent performance. We strongly recommend that visitors update their browsers and operating systems to the latest supported versions to ensure the best possible experience.

Ongoing Accessibility Commitment

Maintaining and improving accessibility on diamondbraces.com is an ongoing process. Diamond Braces conducts periodic testing, both automated and manual, to monitor compliance and identify potential barriers to access. We work with accessibility consultants and industry resources to stay current with evolving best practices and standards.

Feedback from users is an essential part of this process. We welcome input from patients and visitors regarding the accessibility of our website and are committed to making reasonable adjustments when accessibility issues are identified.

Feedback and Support

If you encounter any difficulty in accessing content on diamondbraces.com , or if you have questions or suggestions regarding our site’s accessibility, we encourage you to contact us. Diamond Braces will make every effort to respond to accessibility feedback promptly and effectively.
