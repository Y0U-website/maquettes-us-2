The practice

Missing teeth can affect your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence. While bridges and dentures provide solutions, dental implants offer the most durable, long-lasting replacement by mimicking natural teeth. These titanium posts fuse with the jawbone, creating a stable foundation for crowns, bridges , or full-arch restorations.

At Diamond Dental Service in Lake Zurich, we place implants ourselves rather than sending you to an outside surgeon. Your evaluation, your 3D CBCT scan, the placement, and the final crown all happen in our office at 1289 S Rand Rd. One team, one chart, one set of records.

With good care, dental implants can last decades or even a lifetime. The crown on top may need replacement after 10–15 years.

Coverage varies widely by plan. We accept most PPO plans and Medicare (not Medicaid), and we verify your benefits before treatment so you know what yours pays before you start. If you’re uninsured, financing through CareCredit and Cherry and our membership plan can bring the cost down.

If you have healthy gums and sufficient jawbone, you’re likely a candidate for our Lake Zurich dental implant treatment. If needed, we also offer bone grafting in-office.

Most dental implant cases take about 3 to 6 months, including healing time and final crown placement for full restoration.

am I a candidate for dental implants ? Two things matter most: healthy gums and enough jawbone to hold the post. The ADA’s general guidance is that implants are an option for patients in good overall health. Untreated gum disease, active infection, or bone that has resorbed after a tooth has been missing for years will all need to be addressed first.

Thin bone is not a dead end. We perform bone grafting in-office, so if your scan shows you need more volume before placement, you are not sent elsewhere for it. The only way to know where you stand is a scan and an exam.
