our lake zurich dental services

general dentistry

crowns & bridges

Crowns strengthen teeth that are cracked, worn, or heavily filled with natural-looking porcelain. Bridges replace missing teeth by restoring bite function and keeping surrounding teeth from shifting.

emergency dental care

Same-day appointments help relieve pain fast for toothaches, broken teeth, infections, or lost fillings. We use digital diagnostics to quickly pinpoint the issue and recommend the right next step.

dentures

Custom full or partial dentures restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. We also offer implant-supported options for a more secure feel and improved stability.

root canals

Root canal therapy removes infection to stop pain and save your natural tooth. With modern techniques and digital imaging, treatment is precise, efficient, and far more comfortable than most patients expect.

bonding & white fillings

Composite bonding and white fillings use the same natural-looking material to fix chips, cracks, and cavities, restoring strength and blending seamlessly with your smile in a comfortable, efficient visit.

cleaning & exams

tmj & tmd treatment

TMJ/TMD care can reduce jaw pain, tension headaches, and clenching-related soreness. Custom bite solutions and supportive therapies help protect your teeth and improve everyday comfort.

dental sealants

family dentistry

sleep apnea

Custom oral appliances can help keep the airway open to reduce snoring and improve sleep quality. We’ll evaluate your symptoms and fit a device designed for comfort and nightly use.

cosmetic dentistry

invisalign

Invisalign straightens teeth with clear, removable aligners designed for comfort and convenience. We use iTero digital scanning and 3D planning to create a precise roadmap and predictable results

veneers

Porcelain veneers reshape and brighten teeth for a natural-looking transformation. We can preview your new smile digitally and may offer no-prep veneers for a more conservative option.

whitening

Zoom whitening lifts deep stains quickly for a noticeably brighter smile in a single visit. It’s a fast, professional option when you want results you can see right away.
