Cosmetic Dentistry Services

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening can lift stains and brighten your smile more predictably than store-bought products. We help you choose an approach that fits your goals and sensitivity level.

Veneers

Veneers cover the front surface of selected teeth to improve color, shape, and alignment. They can be a good option for teeth with deeper discoloration or noticeable wear.

Dental Bonding

Bonding uses tooth-colored material to repair chips, refine edges, or close small gaps. It is often a conservative way to make small but meaningful improvements.

Dental Crowns

In some cases, a crown can also support cosmetic goals by restoring the shape and appearance of a tooth that is worn, cracked, or heavily restored, while also strengthening it for everyday use.

We offer cosmetic dentistry services that focus on practical, natural-looking changes. Whether you have a special event coming up or simply want to feel more confident when you smile, we can talk through what makes sense for you and your teeth.

Our Approach at Diamond Fisher Dental

From your first appointment, we set aside time to hear your story and understand your goals. Your initial visit often includes up to an hour with the doctor, so you never feel rushed through questions or decisions. Together, you and your dentist create a plan that considers your overall health, not just a single tooth. Our team then helps schedule visits that work with your calendar and comfort level.

We also keep an eye on technology and update when it offers real benefits for patients, such as digital radiographs, secure online forms, and text-friendly communication. Our goal is to keep care practical, easy, and modern.

As a Scarborough dentist, we are committed to long-term oral health and to helping you understand your options.
