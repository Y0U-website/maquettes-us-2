Restorative Dentistry Services

We offer restorative dentistry services that focus on comfort, function, and long-term health. Whether you have a tooth that hurts, a broken filling, or a space where a tooth is missing, we can talk through options and build a plan that feels manageable.

Fillings

Fillings repair areas of decay or small chips so you can chew more comfortably and protect the tooth from further damage. We focus on conservative treatment and clear explanations of what each filling is meant to do.

Dental Crowns

Crowns cover and protect teeth that are cracked, worn, or heavily restored. They help restore strength and shape so the tooth can handle everyday use more reliably.

Dental Bridges

Bridges replace missing teeth by anchoring to nearby teeth. They can improve chewing, support your bite, and help prevent neighboring teeth from shifting into empty spaces.

Implant Retained Dentures and Implant Supported Hybrid Prosthesis

When all of the teeth in a dental arch are missing, we have several options to restore function, comfort, and confidence. Dental implants can be placed and restored with either a removable implant-retained denture or a fixed hybrid prosthesis that does not need to be removed at night. We can explain which choices fit your situation.

Implant Crowns & Bridges

When one or a few teeth are missing, we can replace the teeth with implant(s) and restore the space with fixed, natural-feeling implant-supported crowns and bridges.
