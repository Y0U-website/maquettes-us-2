The practice

Dentistry gives me an amazing platform to engage with a broad group of people from my community, and allows me to change their lives for the better.

I am passionate about all aspects of dentistry and take pride in providing comprehensive care to my patients.

Visiting your dentist every six months for professional cleanings ensures that your teeth stay healthy and your smile stays beautiful.

Fillings, available in amalgam or tooth-colored composite, repair a tooth after a cavity has been removed.

Veneers correct minor cosmetic tooth issues, including chips, irregular spacing, misshapen teeth, and discolored teeth.

Custom dentures replace a full arch of teeth or several teeth in a row with natural-looking, removable replacement teeth.

We take time to make your child’s visits, from preventive care to restorative treatments, as stress free and pleasant as possible.

At Diamond Smiles Dentistry, Dr. Aisha Akpabio , Dr. Bridget Tinsley and our team strive to provide dentistry in the Detroit, Grosse Pointe, and Southfield communities that is comprehensive, high quality, and comfortable.
