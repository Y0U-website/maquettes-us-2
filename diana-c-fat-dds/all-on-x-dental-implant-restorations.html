The Custom Restoration Design Process

Creating an All-on-X restoration involves much more than simply replacing missing teeth. Dr. Fat considers your facial proportions, lip support, speech patterns, and aesthetic preferences to design a restoration that enhances your overall appearance.

Digital planning technology allows precise visualization of the final result before treatment begins. This advanced planning ensures that the restoration will fit perfectly with the implant positions while providing optimal function and appearance.

The design process includes careful consideration of bite relationships and jaw function. Dr. Diana Fat ensures that your new teeth align properly for comfortable chewing and speaking while distributing forces evenly across all implants to promote long-term success.

Color and shape selection receives equal attention to ensure your restoration blends naturally with your complexion and facial features. The goal is to create teeth that look like they’ve always belonged in your smile.

Materials and Fabrication Excellence

Modern All-on-X restorations utilize advanced materials that provide exceptional strength, durability, and aesthetics. Dr. Fat selects materials based on your specific needs, lifestyle, and aesthetic goals.

Zirconia and high-grade ceramics offer outstanding strength while maintaining the translucency and color variation found in natural teeth. These materials resist staining and wear, ensuring your restoration maintains its appearance for many years.

The fabrication process involves skilled dental technicians who craft each restoration by hand, paying attention to details that make the difference between an obvious prosthetic and a natural-looking smile. Dr. Diana Fat oversees every step to ensure the final product meets her exacting standards.

Caring for Your All-on-X Restoration

One of the greatest advantages of All-on-X treatment lies in how simple maintenance becomes compared to traditional dentures. Your new teeth require the same basic care as natural teeth – regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings.

Dr. Fat provides detailed instructions for cleaning around implant sites and maintaining optimal gum health. Special flossing techniques and water irrigation help keep the area where the restoration meets your gums clean and healthy.

Regular checkups allow monitoring of both the restoration and the underlying implants. Dr. Diana Fat watches for any signs of wear or complications while ensuring your bite remains properly balanced as natural changes occur over time.

Professional cleanings remove buildup that can accumulate around implant sites, just as they do around natural teeth. These appointments also provide opportunities to address any concerns or make minor adjustments if needed.

Planning Your All-on-X Journey

The All-on-X treatment process requires careful coordination between multiple phases, and Dr. Fat guides patients through each step while managing expectations and ensuring optimal outcomes.

Timeline planning helps patients understand what to expect throughout treatment, from initial consultation through final restoration placement. Most patients complete their All-on-X journey within several months, though individual timelines vary based on healing and specific treatment needs.
