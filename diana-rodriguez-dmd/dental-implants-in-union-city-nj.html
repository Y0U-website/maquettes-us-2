Mini Dental Implants

The basic function of the mini implant is the same as the traditional version. The real difference between the two lies in the design of each.

Mini implants have the same functionality and appearance above the gumline as traditional implants but are half the size. The screw that is placed in the jawbone is much more narrow.

Other Differences Between Mini Dental Implants and Traditional Dental Implants

Other than the difference in size, there are additional differences you should be aware of when you’re considering which type of treatment is best for you.

The Need for a Bone Graft

Because of the large size of the traditional implant screws, a larger area of the jawbone is needed to secure the implants. Patients often need bone grafting to correct a lack of bone density at the implant site.

However, with mini dental implants and their smaller screws, this large area of the jawbone is not needed (which means that a bone graft usually isn’t needed, either).

Abutments vs. End Balls

While traditional dental implants require that an abutment be placed over the screw before the crown goes on, mini dental implants do not require an abutment at all.

Instead, the mini implant has a small ball on the end of the screw. This means there are fewer steps involved in the placement of mini dental implants and fewer parts to deal with.

The Actual Procedures

Traditional implant procedures are generally more invasive due to the fact that bone grafting is often needed. This also means that the time it takes for traditional implant procedures is longer.

Due to the more invasive nature of traditional implants, patient discomfort can be greater, and recovery time is often longer than with mini implants.

After a patient requires a bone graft, they need to wait a significant amount of time before they have the prosthetic tooth placed. This time is required for the graft area to heal and varies between patients according to several different factors.

In contrast, with smaller screws and no bone grafting, prosthetic teeth can be placed right away during a mini dental implant procedure.

The Costs

Because mini dental implants usually don’t require a dental bone graft, the cost is less than that of a traditional implant procedure. The smaller size of the screws and the lack of an abutment also reduce the costs. This makes them a convenient solution for patients with limited funds available for their dental work.

Why Consider Mini Dental Implants?

To summarize, below are some benefits of mini dental implants:

The Process Is Less Stressful Overall

The process of placing traditional dental implants is more invasive, more time-consuming, and more costly. Smaller incisions are required for mini implants, which means a faster procedure and a quicker healing process.

There Is Usually No Bone Grafting Needed

Standard dental implants often require bone grafting, but mini implants can be used with much less jawbone space. The overall diameter of a mini implant is half the size of a standard implant.

Enjoy Your New Teeth After Just One Visit

Mini dental implants can often be placed in just one visit to the dentist, whereas typical dental implants can require at least two visits, if not more.

They Improve Oral Health, Appearance, and Function

Mini dental implants promote a healthy jawbone by encouraging stimulation of the tissues and preventing jawbone loss. The prosthetic teeth look exactly like beautiful, natural teeth.

Mini dental implants will allow you to enjoy all of your favorite foods again. You’ll also be more self-confident in your smile, the appearance of your jaw, and your ability to communicate verbally.
