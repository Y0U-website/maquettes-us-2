The Fix-on-Six® procedure is currently the very best solution in the field of dentistry for patients who are missing full arches of teeth. Consider the above benefits more closely in the sections below.

  • Regaining the ability to speak and chew correctly
  • Rediscovering the confidence to laugh and smile
  • Not needing messy, painful, ill-fitting dentures
  • Enjoying a solution for missing teeth that will last for many decades

Jawbone Stabilization

Having mini dental implants in the jaw does more than secure your new prosthetic teeth in place. They also stabilize the jawbone and help stop the deterioration that results from a lack of teeth.

When a jawbone loses density and volume, a person’s facial appearance changes and can take on a sunken-in look. The implants stimulate the jawbone just like tooth roots would, preventing bone loss so that the patient retains a full, healthy jaw.

The Ability to Speak and Chew Correctly

Teeth are critical to a person’s ability to pronounce words correctly and chew many different foods. When teeth are missing, it invariably degrades a person’s ability to do both.

The full arch dental implants of Fix-on-Six® give patients the ability to speak clearly, and with up to 80 % of the bite power of natural teeth, the prosthetic teeth can handle even the toughest foods.

The Confidence to Laugh and Smile

Many people are embarrassed about the condition of their teeth or the fact that they are missing them. At Union City Mini Dental Implants, we completely understand that, and our ultimate goal is always to give our patients the self-confidence to smile and laugh again.

Fix-on-Six® dental implants can give patients a renewed sense of pride in their appearance. Many patients who get Fix-on-Six® implants regain a sense of joy in life, knowing that their oral appearance is no longer a cause of embarrassment and frustration.

No Need for Messy, Ill-Fitting Dentures

The hassles that often come with traditional dentures can chip away at a person’s zest for life: soaking them in cleaner every night, worrying about them shifting or even falling out when speaking or eating, mouth and gum irritation, and the list goes on.

There is no risk of full arch dental implants shifting, moving, falling out, or causing sores in the mouth.

A Solution for Missing Teeth that Will Last for Many Decades

Traditional dentures don’t last nearly as long as Fix-on-Six® implants do. As a person with dentures ages, they must have those dentures refitted and remade many times over as the shape of their face changes.

Fix-on-Six® dental implants are designed to be permanent solutions that do not require endless refittings or follow-up appointments with your dentist to maintain. The implants only require that you brush, floss, and go to your regular dental checkups twice each year, just as you would with natural teeth.
