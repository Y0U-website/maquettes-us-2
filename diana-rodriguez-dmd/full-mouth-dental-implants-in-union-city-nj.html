Benefits of Full Mouth Dental Implants

If you’re still unsure whether you’d like to use mini dental implants for your full mouth reconstruction, check out the advantages they offer.

Mini dental implants are highly effective and are significantly more likely than other options, like bridges or dentures, to make your full mouth reconstruction a success. Although they will require a bit of surgery, once you’ve gone through with the procedure, you’re highly likely to be satisfied with the results.

Other methods of full mouth reconstruction can’t offer the necessary stimulation to induce bone growth that full mouth dental implants can. They’ll help you avoid the various issues listed in the section above.

It’s not all about gaining function back, although that is a big deal. Your full mouth reconstruction is also supposed to restore your smile. Dentures are significantly less natural looking than mini dental implants, which means you’ll feel much more confident showing off your new teeth with full-mouth dental implants.

You’ll have more strength to your bite with full-mouth dental implants than you would with dentures. That means you’ll be much closer to the feel of natural teeth when you choose them for your full mouth reconstruction.
