What Are Mini Dental Implants?

In order to understand what mini dental implants are, it’s important to first know about traditional dental implants.

Traditional Dental Implants

Traditional implants are titanium or zirconium posts that are surgically placed within the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. This is a permanent solution to missing or damaged teeth that also helps the jawbone avoid deterioration.

A dental implant consists of three parts:

The root is placed into the jawbone, the abutment is placed on top and secured in place, and the crown (the prosthetic tooth) is secured to the abutment.

One of the best features of dental implants is the titanium or zirconium posts, which actually integrate with the jawbone over time.

  • The titanium or zirconium root
  • The crown
  • The abutment

The Importance of Avoiding Jawbone Deterioration

Why would a jawbone deteriorate due to missing teeth? A jawbone receives stimulation from the roots of teeth and the teeth themselves, keeping the bone purposeful and active. When teeth and their roots are missing, there is a lack of stimulation. Without this stimulation, the jawbone slowly deteriorates.

Jawbone deterioration affects the facial characteristics of a person and often causes the face to have a sunken-in appearance. This hollow-looking appearance can make people feel self-conscious. Dental implants continue the stimulation of the jawbone, mitigating or eliminating the concerns of deterioration and facial structure changes.

Mini Dental Implants

Mini dental implants are fast, convenient, and safe alternatives to traditional dental implants. While traditional implants will remain an excellent solution for some patients, mini implants are often a better solution.

This type of dental implant is a single unit with three parts that differ from the three pieces of a traditional implant. These parts are:

An artificial tooth is attached to the post using a rubber O-ring, which means that no abutments need to be attached before the tooth is placed. This little change makes a big difference because it eliminates an entire step involved in traditional dental implants.

  • The socket
  • The ball
  • The O-ring

The Benefits of Mini Dental Implants

There are many reasons why Dr. Diana Rodriguez, DMD, recommends mini dental implants for patients whenever possible. Mini dental implants offer the same permanent results as traditional implants, and the results look and feel very natural. Some of the other benefits of mini dental implants include the following:

The Implants Themselves Are Smaller

The crown structure of mini implants is exactly the same as the structure of traditional ones. However, the implanted portion of mini versions is smaller. Traditional implants can be anywhere from 3-6mm in diameter, while mini implants are just 2-3mm in diameter.

Bone Grafting Is Usually Not Necessary

When a person’s natural tooth is lost, if it is not replaced right away with an implant, the jaw begins to deteriorate. This means that oftentimes, a bone grafting procedure is needed before a traditional dental implant can be placed.

Mini implants, however, can be placed without bone grafting, even when insufficient bone density in the jaw is present for traditional implants. Thanks to their smaller size, mini implants do not require as much “jawbone room” as traditional ones do.

The Implant Procedure Is Easier

The bone grafting procedure that is necessary with many traditional implants is a tedious task that requires significant time. However, with mini dental implants, since bone grafting isn’t required and neither are abutments, the procedure is faster and easier for patients.

Mini Dental Implants Are Less Expensive

As you can imagine, since abutments are not needed, and neither are bone grafts, mini dental implants cost much less than traditional implants. This makes them a good option for many people who have limited funds available for their dental procedures.
