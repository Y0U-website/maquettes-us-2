Insurance & Payment Options

We work with most major dental insurance providers and will help you maximize your benefits. Our team is happy to verify your coverage before your appointment so there are no unexpected surprises.

For patients without insurance or with out-of-pocket costs, we offer flexible payment options. Please contact our office we’ll always work with you to find a solution that fits.

Dental Anxiety? You’re in the Right Place.

If you’ve been avoiding the dentist because of fear or anxiety, we want you to know: you are not alone, and we are not here to judge.

Our team is experienced in helping anxious patients feel calm and in control. We’ll move at your pace, explain everything clearly, and check in throughout your visit. Many of our most nervous first-time patients become our most loyal long-term patients because they discover that dentistry doesn’t have to be scary.
