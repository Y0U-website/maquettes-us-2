Our Practice

A dental home built on compassion, expertise, and a genuine love for the people we serve.

Dr. Sandu, DMD

Dr. Sandu founded this practice on a simple but powerful belief: that exceptional dental care and a truly comfortable experience are not mutually exclusive they are inseparable.

With advanced training in general, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Sandu brings both clinical excellence and genuine warmth to every patient interaction. Whether you’re coming in for a routine visit or a complex procedure, you’ll always feel heard, respected, and well cared for.

Beyond the clinical expertise, Dr. Sandu is deeply invested in the Queensbury community and takes pride in building long-term relationships with patients and their families. Many patients have been coming here for years and bringing their own children now too.

A Space Designed for Comfort

From the moment you step inside, you’ll notice that our practice was built with intention. Every detail the warm tones, the natural light, the thoughtful layout was chosen to feel more like a welcoming home than a clinical office.

Our hallways are bright and spacious, guiding you from one area to the next with ease and calm. Treatment rooms are designed to be serene and comfortable, with large windows that bring the outside world in.

Several rooms even overlook natural surroundings, giving patients something beautiful to focus on. Some even enjoy bird watching during their cleanings.
