Dental Services

Our Dental Services

From your first check-up to a complete smile transformation, we provide thoughtful care at every stage of life.

Care for Every Need

Preventive & Diagnostic Care

Regular cleanings, comprehensive examinations, digital X-rays, and oral cancer screenings form the foundation of lifelong dental health. We recommend checkups every six months.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Professional teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, tooth bonding, and complete smile makeovers. Whether you want a subtle refresh or a dramatic transformation, we’ll help you achieve the smile you deserve.

Restorative Dentistry

Tooth-colored fillings, porcelain crowns and bridges, full and partial dentures, and dental implants. We restore both the function and aesthetics of your smile with natural-looking results.

Children’s Dentistry

We love seeing young patients and building positive dental habits from an early age. Our gentle approach helps children feel comfortable and excited about caring for their smiles for life.

Periodontal (Gum) Care

Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile. We provide gum evaluations, scaling and root planing for gum disease, and ongoing periodontal maintenance to protect your long-term health.

Night Guards & Oral Appliances

Custom-fitted night guards for teeth grinding and oral appliance therapy for snoring and mild sleep apnea. Protect your teeth and improve your sleep quality with solutions tailored to your needs.

Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies don’t wait for convenient moments. We make every effort to see emergency patients promptly and provide swift, compassionate care. Call us at (518) 792-6756 .

Comfort & Anxiety Management

Dental anxiety is real and valid. Our team is trained to work with nervous patients using a calm, gentle approach, clear communication, and comfort options to make every visit manageable and even enjoyable.

Comfortable from Start to Finish

Our treatment rooms overlook natural surroundings some patients enjoy bird watching during their cleanings.
