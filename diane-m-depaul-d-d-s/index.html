If your tooth has a large filling, is root-treated, or otherwise damaged, a crown can protect it from further harm. A bridge can be used when you have one or more missing teeth in a row.

Dental implants are small titanium posts that replace gaps in a person’s smile with natural-looking teeth.the implant is a small post-surgically embedded into the bone beneath the gums.

Invisalign is an orthodontic treatment that uses clear aligners instead of braces to shift your teeth into position gradually.These aligners are custom-made and fit snugly over for your teeth.
