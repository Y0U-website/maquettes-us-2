Since 1954, Riley Dental Group has been dedicated to caring for our patients’ dental health by providing exceptional customer service and treating our patients like family. We care for families from San Fernando, CA, and the surrounding communities of Santa Clarita, Los Angeles, and San Fernando Valley.

We enjoy helping people, and it shows! Our patients appreciate our relaxed atmosphere and often find us smiling and sharing a laugh.

Our skilled, compassionate team manages the dental needs of your whole family under one roof. From cleanings and exams for patients of all ages to fillings, root canals, dental implants, and cosmetic dentistry, we can do it all in-house without a referral elsewhere.
