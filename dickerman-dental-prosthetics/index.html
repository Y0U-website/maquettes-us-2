Since 1965, Dickerman Dental Prosthetics has been crafting high-quality dental restorations in-house at our Sharon, Massachusetts-based dental laboratory.

Our dental laboratory team uses high-quality materials, including Lava™ Plus and Ultimate, IPS e.max®, Lucitone, and Noritake ceramics to fabricate our outstanding dental restorations.

We have embraced new technology through constant education. Our dental laboratory is experienced in CAD/CAM manufacturing, full-contour zirconia, implant abutments and digital impression systems. Consistent, quality work has earned three of our technicians the Massachusetts Dental Society Technician of the Year Award.

At Dickerman Dental Prosthetics, we provide you with dental solutions, not just products. We want to be a resource for our dentists—an experienced asset—to deliver more than just dental restorations. Our solutions are the result of leading-edge education, which allows us to provide you with the implants and prosthetics you demand. We look forward to working with you.
