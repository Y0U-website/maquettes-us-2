Treatment Planning

Our goal is to be a partner and resource to you. We offer assistance with treatment planning in order to facilitate successful treatment outcomes. Click here to email us or call 781-828-2808 with any questions you may have.

Digital Imaging

With our digital imaging service, there is no need to just imagine the possibilities. Send us photographs or digital files of your patients and we’ll send back three 8.5 X 11 prints with before and after views, as well as the digital files. This allows you to show smile design options to your patients and visually demonstrate what they can expect.

Diagnostic Wax-Ups

Diagnostic wax-ups are an excellent tool to allow your patients to preview the smile design for an optimal result. Our diagnostic wax-ups are artistically created for you and your patient on hard white stone duplicate casts. These delicate casts are returned to you in plastic cast “safes”. These ensure safe transport and storage of your delicate diagnostic wax-ups. Your original casts are routinely preserved for use as a reference point in patient treatment.

After patient and doctor approval diagnostic wax-ups may be converted to temporaries or templates.
