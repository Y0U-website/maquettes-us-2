Our Children’s Dental Services in Tulsa

We offer a full range of pediatric dental services, including:

Infant Oral Exams

Early evaluation to establish a dental home and provide guidance on oral hygiene for babies and toddlers.

Preventative Care

Regular checkups, cleanings, fluoride treatments, and dental sealants for children to prevent cavities and other issues related to early childhood caries and protect young teeth.

Restorative Care

Treating cavities and other dental issues with child-friendly techniques and materials.

Orthodontic Evaluation

Early assessment for potential orthodontic needs to ensure proper jaw development and alignment of teeth.

A Positive Dental Experience for Your Child

We strive to make every visit to our Tulsa office a positive and enjoyable experience for your child. Our friendly team is skilled at building trust with young patients, making dental care for children fun and engaging. We recommend scheduling your child’s first dental visit within six months of their first tooth erupting or by their first birthday, whichever comes first. This early introduction to dental care helps establish healthy habits and allows us to monitor your child’s oral development from the start.
