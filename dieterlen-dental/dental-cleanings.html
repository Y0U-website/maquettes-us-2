Dental Cleanings

Maintaining a healthy smile goes beyond brushing and flossing at home. Regular professional dental cleanings or dental prophylaxis are essential for preventing dental problems and keeping your teeth and gums in their best condition. At Dieterlen Dental, we offer comprehensive teeth cleaning and oral prophylaxis services tailored to your unique needs.

REQUEST Appointment What Happens During a Dental Cleaning at Dieterlen Dental ? Our experienced dental hygienists will provide a thorough examination and dental hygiene cleaning of your teeth and gums:

Examine Your Mouth

We’ll carefully assess your teeth, gums, and overall oral health for any signs of concern, such as cavities or gum disease.

Scaling and Polishing

We’ll use specialized tools to gently and effectively remove plaque and tartar (calculus) – deposits that can only be removed by a dental professional.

Polish Your Teeth

To remove surface stains and leave your teeth smooth and shiny, we’ll polish them with a professional-grade paste.

Floss Your Teeth

Flossing helps remove debris from between your teeth and along the gumline, contributing to a cleaner, healthier mouth.

Apply Fluoride

Fluoride treatments strengthen your tooth enamel and help protect against tooth decay.

Why Are Dental Cleanings Important?

Prevent Gum Disease

Gum disease (periodontal disease) is a common infection that can damage your gums and the bones supporting your teeth. Regular cleanings, combined with good oral hygiene practices at home, help prevent and manage this condition and can contribute to your overall periodontal maintenance .

Detect Early Problems

Early detection of dental issues like cavities allows for prompt treatment, preventing them from worsening.

Freshen Your Breath

Dental cleanings can remove bacteria and food particles that cause bad breath, leaving your mouth feeling refreshed.

Brighten Your Smile

Removing surface stains during cleanings can enhance the appearance of your teeth, giving you a brighter smile.

How Often Should You Get a Dental Cleaning?

We recommend most patients have a professional dental cleaning every six months. However, some individuals may require more frequent cleanings depending on their specific needs and oral health conditions.
