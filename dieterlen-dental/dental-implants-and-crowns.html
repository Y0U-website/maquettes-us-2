Dental Implants and Crowns in Tulsa

At Dieterlen Dental in Tulsa, OK, we offer state-of-the-art dental implant and crown solutions to restore your smile’s function, health, and beauty. If you’re missing one or more teeth, dental implants, also known as endosteal implants , provide a permanent, natural-looking replacement for your missing tooth roots, while dental crowns, sometimes called dental caps , serve as artificial teeth that are placed on top of the implants or damaged teeth.

REQUEST Appointment What are Dental Implants? Implant dentistry involves surgically placing small titanium posts (implants) into your jawbone to serve as artificial tooth roots. They provide a strong and stable foundation for tooth replacement options, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures.

Dental crowns are custom-made restorations that cover the entire visible portion of a tooth. They are used to protect and strengthen damaged teeth, improve their appearance, or serve as the final restoration for a dental implant. Crowns can be made from various materials, including zirconia , porcelain fused to metal (PFM) , or all-ceramic .

Why Choose Dental Implants and Crowns ?

Permanent Solution

Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime, offering a permanent solution for missing teeth.

Natural Look and Feel

Implants and crowns mimic the appearance and function of natural teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Improved Oral Health

Unlike traditional bridges, dental implants don’t require altering adjacent teeth. They also help preserve jawbone density and prevent bone loss.

Enhanced Confidence

Restoring your smile with implants and crowns can boost your self-esteem and improve your overall quality of life.
