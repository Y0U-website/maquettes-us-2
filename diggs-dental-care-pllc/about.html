Our mission

Delivering high-quality, comprehensive dental care

with a personalized patient experience in a comfortable environment.

At Diggs Dental Care, we want to get to know you. We value our patients and their families, and we believe in treating every person as if they were a member of our own family—with dignity, fairness, and respect. Dr. Tanner and Dr. Kate strive to achieve these goals with each individual patient, and feel inspired to use their skills and knowledge as experienced dentists to positively impact the community every day.

Providing personalized care

Your Trusted Missoula Dentist

Dr. Tanner Diggs

Dr. Tanner Diggs is a third-generation resident of Missoula and a fourth-generation dentist. Growing up with dentistry on both sides of his family, he formed a passion for the art of dentistry early in life. Dr. Tanner is thrilled to be in Missoula providing quality and patient-focused dental care to the community that he so dearly loves.

Dr. Kate Diggs

Dr. Kate Diggs grew up in Rochester, Minnesota, and attended the University of Minnesota to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree, graduating in 2015. Dr. Kate is so happy to be in Missoula, and is very devoted to her patients at Diggs Dental Care.
