Preparing for Your First Visit

What to expect

If you&#x27;re visiting us for the first time, there’s no need to be nervous! Your first visit will be focused on getting to know you and your needs, lifestyle, and goals. You’ll also have plenty of time to meet the team and get settled in our office.

WHAT TO BRING

For your first visit, bring along your insurance information or ID card if you are planning to use dental insurance.

We accept most major insurance plans and file claims for you. Out-of-network? We’ll still file, but full payment is required, and your insurer will reimburse you.

If you haven’t already, fill out your new patient paperwork to save time during your appointment. Other than that, just sit back, relax, and let us take it from there!
