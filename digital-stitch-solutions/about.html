Digital Stitch Solutions

With over a decades worth of experience in 3shape, Exocad, InLab, multiple mills, as well printers and their respective CAM software’s; Digital Stitch Solutions is ready to take on your hypotheticals!

Are you ready to take a step back from designing and allow another craftsmen to design your restorations? From single unit restorations to full mouth and All-on-X restorations, Digital Stitch Solutions is ready to design anything you may need to take your ideas in to reality.

Our Approach IS Hypothetical

It all begins with an idea, and space to put it. Digital Stitch Solutions believes that all designs are initially hypothetical, and there should never be reason for a poor fitting crown or restoration that is not on par with what our clients are wanting. With the technology available, Digital Stitch Solutions wants to make the perfect restoration and consult You on your desired idea, your hypothetical tooth, that we can “Stitch” together and return to you to make reality.

We Design it

Send your scans through your preferred method (ie. IO scanner, 3Shape, Email, Dropbox), and let’s stitch together with a relationship that benefits not only us creators, but the patient as well. After receiving your initial preferences, we can begin the process of Stitching together the best hypothetical restorations for you to process into reality!

You Manufacture it

From any form of milling technology to printing technologies, I will deliver designs that are compatible and have parameters ideal for your method of manufacturing. Let me remove the hassle and stress of figuring out what’s right for your team!

Digital Stitch Solutions will offer complete workflows on most all manufacturer designs, and offer solutions for sending the designs to the manufacturers to take the hassle out of all the back-end work a lab or office would usually have to go through. Abutments should arrive at the office from the manufacture within a timely fashion. The specific time frame on abutment delivery is dependent on the customers’ shipping preferences and the manufacture production time.
