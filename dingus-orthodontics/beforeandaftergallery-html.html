OVERBITE

Details: This patient had crowding in her upper and lower teeth and complained that her lower teeth were touching the roof of her mouth when she chewed. Dr. Bill used orthodontic braces, bite blocks, and rubber bands to correct her bite and achieve a beautiful result.

IMPACTED CUSPID

Details: This patient had a baby tooth that would not fall out on its own, due to its position wedged between two teeth. In addition, he had spacing between his upper teeth and crowding between his lower teeth. Dr. Bill used orthodontic braces, coil springs, and elastic chain to eliminate the crowding, close the spaces, and bring the impacted cuspid into its appropriate position.

OPEN BITE

SPACING

OVERBITE AND OVERJET
