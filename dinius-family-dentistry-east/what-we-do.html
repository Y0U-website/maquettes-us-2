Dental Services - What We Do

What We Do

Our approach is a bit different than the other guys.

Dr. Mike, Dr. Jon and staff are proud to offer the full range of high-quality, restorative dental services from simple fillings to full mouth reconstruction. We treat patients of all ages, beginning from the cleaning and exam of their very first teeth, with application of sealants, to all stages of life, including crowns, implants and dentures as needed.

Both doctors work closely with the best specialists in the North State for comprehensive lifetime care of their patients’ teeth and gums.

Here are some of the dental services that we offer:

Preventative

Through regular exams, cleanings and x-rays, our team promotes a proactive approach to your dental care, identifying potential problems before they arise and maintaining excellence in oral health.

Pediatric

We love our pediatric patients and treat them like we treat our own kids. Our team’s goal is to create a positive experience for these little patients, so they will be committed to a lifetime dedicated to healthy teeth and gums.

Restorative

Throughout our lives, various dental needs do arise that require restorative action. Whether that involves root canals, extractions, crowns, or other services, our team is well equipped to manage these and other restorative treatments.

Specialized Care

Sometimes dental solutions are complex and require more advanced procedures. Our dental team has extensive experience working with the best specialists in the area to treat the full spectrum of needs, from simple crowns to full mouth reconstruction cases.

We treat you like family.

We are committed to giving you the best service available. Our patients are never just a name or a number. They are individuals. They have lives and families, hopes and dreams. We want to get to know you, visit by visit. We want to build a long-term relationship with you and your family, even to the next generation.
