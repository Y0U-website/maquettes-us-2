Dental Milestones By Ages

This is the beginning of your child’s dental journey. The first tooth usually appears around six months, and we recommend scheduling their first dental visit by age one. Early care helps monitor tooth development, establish healthy habits and prevent future issues before they start. Reach out to schedule your child’s appointment!

Toddlers are full of energy — and new teeth! By age three, most children have a full set of baby teeth. This stage is ideal for teaching brushing routines, monitoring thumb-sucking and introducing professional cleanings. Regular dental visits support speech development, cavity prevention and create a fun, stress-free experience for future appointments.

As baby teeth fall out and adult teeth come in, this stage is key for monitoring dental growth and alignment. Children begin learning responsibility for their own oral care. Routine visits allow us to catch concerns early, encourage healthy habits and keep developing smiles strong, clean and ready for the teen years.

Teen years bring unique challenges like braces, wisdom teeth and increasing independence. It is important to reinforce consistent brushing, healthy eating and regular checkups. We help teens take ownership of their oral health while addressing changes that come with growth. Our team offers friendly, age-appropriate support to keep teen smiles confident and healthy.

Why Pediatric Dentistry Matters

Pediatric dentistry reduces fear by creating positive early experiences, which helps children become confident, cooperative and calm during dental visits as they grow older.

Early care allows our dentist to monitor tooth development, prevent decay and manage habits like thumb-sucking or pacifier use before they cause long-term issues.

Education on proper brushing, flossing and nutrition empowers kids to take ownership of their oral health and encourages smart habits that last into adulthood.
