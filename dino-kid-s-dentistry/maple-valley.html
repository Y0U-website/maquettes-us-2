Dental Milestones By Ages

Dental care starts early — even before the first tooth erupts. Wiping gums after feedings and introducing a soft toothbrush at the first sign of teeth sets the foundation. A dental visit by age one helps detect early concerns and allows parents to learn proper techniques for keeping their infant’s mouth healthy.

Toddlers develop quickly, and so do their teeth. This stage focuses on building daily routines and addressing habits like pacifier use or thumb-sucking. First cleanings and fluoride treatments may begin now. Visiting our dentist regularly creates positive associations, encourages cooperation and helps toddlers feel confident and safe in the dental chair.

With the transition from baby teeth to permanent ones, this is a time of change. Children become more aware of their smile, so it is essential to reinforce proper brushing and flossing. Regular exams help detect alignment concerns, tooth decay or hygiene struggles early while supporting a strong, healthy oral foundation.

Teenagers face new dental needs including orthodontics, wisdom teeth and lifestyle choices that affect oral health. They may also become more self-conscious about their smile. We provide guidance tailored to teens, encouraging responsibility and healthy routines. Consistent care helps manage changes and keeps their smile healthy through this important life stage.

Why Pediatric Dentistry Matters

We understand child behavior and tailor appointments to be calming, fun and interactive, making it easier for children to enjoy visiting the dentist.
