Dr. Slavik was born and raised in the southern part of Russia near the Black Sea. It is one of the very few areas of the country with warm summers, mild winters, and a very culturally/ethnically diverse population. During his childhood, he enjoyed spending time with his older brother, cousins, and often spent summers with his grandmother at her country home. After high school, he was accepted into the State University of Medicine and Dentistry, in Russia for dental education.

In dental school, Dr. Slavik completed a comprehensive medical and dental education. Dr.Slavik began to find that he especially enjoyed spending time in the pediatric dental department, which was the beginning of his journey to becoming a pediatric dental specialist. After graduating from his dental school in Russia, he moved to the United States to further his education.

Dr. Slavik applied and was accepted to the New York University College of Dentistry Advanced Standing Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) program for internationally trained dentists seeking to practice dentistry in the US. After obtaining his second dental degree through NYU,

Dr. Slavik sought to become a Pediatric Dentist. He applied and was accepted to the residency specialty training at NYU Langone Hospitals. This two-year training solely focused on providing care for infants and children through adolescence, including those with special health needs.

Dr.Slavik loves working with children and their families! He always enjoys learning from his patients and especially focuses on making their dental visit a fun experience. With the support of his awesome team, Dr. Slavik uses compassion and empathy to help children get over their dental fears and anxiety.

Dr. Slavik enjoys staying active, spending time with friends and family, and exploring coastal destinations.

Dr. Sonia Jefferies “I love helping children (and their parents) become less fearful of the dentist while also helping them have beautiful, healthy smiles!”

Dr. Ngandu Sonia Jefferies was born in Belgium but has spent most of her life in Alabama. Her parents are from the Democratic Republic of Congo. Growing up, Dr. Jefferies lived in many different places in Europe and the U.S.

She earned her Bachelor of Science degree in Biochemistry with a minor in French from Rhodes College in Memphis, TN. Dr. Jefferies attended the University of Alabama at Birmingham School of Dentistry and received her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in 2011. After working as a general dentist for 3 years, she returned to school and earned her specialty certificate in Pediatric Dentistry in 2016. During her residency in pediatric dentistry, she was also appointed chief resident.

Dr. Jefferies moved from Alabama to the Ithaca area in the fall of 2016 where her husband is studying at Cornell. At that time she also joined the Dinosaur Dental team.

When Dr. Jefferies isn’t working, she enjoys cooking, trying new recipes, and eating food from around the world.

Dr. Janis Pinto Pediatric Dentistry Specialist

Dr. Janis Pinto was born and raised in Bogota, Colombia, where she earned a Bachelor of Science in Business administration from Politécnico Grancolombiano. Dr. Pinto moved permanently to Orlando, Florida 15 years ago and attended University of Central Florida to receive her second Bachelor of Science in Molecular Biology and Microbiology. She obtained her dental training at University of Florida College Of Dentistry.

Upon graduation, she continued her education and received a certificate in pediatric dentistry at NYU Lutheran Medical Center where she was appointed Chief Resident.

Dr. Pinto enjoys running, music, traveling and spending time with family and friends; she is also a follower of college football …Go Gators!

Dr. Cai Zhang Pediatric Dentistry Specialist

Dr. Cai Zhang was born in China and moved to the U.S. at the age of four. She was raised in San Diego, CA where she attended San Diego State and then obtained her D.D.S degree from University California San Francisco in 2013.

She continued her studies with specialty training in pediatric dentistry at NYU-Lutheran Medical Center. During her residency she received extensive training treating children under different behavior management techniques (Nitrous oxide, Oral and I.V sedation and cases under general anesthesia).

Her passion to travel and commitment to service led her to volunteer for many public health organizations and events. She educated children and parents in outreach programs teaching prevention practices and implementing hygiene programs throughout San Francisco where she did her dental training. Also, she has volunteered abroad in humanitarian trips teaching English in rural China and providing free dental care to underserved communities of Jamaica and Mexico. She plans to continue partaking in further outreach programs.

When she isn’t at work, Dr. Cai enjoys spending time with family and friends, cooking, tennis, hiking, yoga and running.
