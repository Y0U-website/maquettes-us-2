Frequently Asked Questions

What can cosmetic dentistry help with?

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on how teeth look, not on treating disease. It can help with stains, chips, small gaps, uneven edges, and dental work that no longer looks natural. If there are cavities or gum concerns, those should be addressed first so cosmetic care is built on a healthy foundation.

Cosmetic Dentistry: Purpose, Procedures & Benefits – Cleveland Clinic

How long do cosmetic results last?

Results vary based on the treatment and daily habits. Good oral hygiene, regular cleanings, and avoiding heavy staining or grinding can help results last longer. Before any treatment begins, we talk through realistic expectations and maintenance.

What are the cost considerations for cosmetic dentistry in Rexburg?

The cost of cosmetic dentistry in Rexburg depends on the type of treatment, how many teeth are involved, and the condition of your teeth before treatment begins. Simple options like whitening or bonding are usually more affordable, while veneers or more involved cosmetic work require more planning, materials, and time. Because cosmetic dentistry focuses on appearance, insurance coverage is often limited, which is why understanding costs upfront matters.

At Strobel Family Dental, cosmetic costs are discussed early and clearly so there are no surprises. Dr. Dirk Strobel and Dr. Heber Strobel explain which options fit your goals, what can wait, and what level of investment makes sense for long-term satisfaction. The goal is to help you choose an approach that feels comfortable for your budget and your expectations, not to push unnecessary treatment.

How do I know which option fits me?

The right option depends on what you want to change and the condition of your teeth. Some concerns are simple and can be addressed with whitening or bonding, while others benefit from aligners or veneers. A consultation helps you compare options without committing to treatment.

How is cosmetic cost decided?

Cost depends on the type of treatment, materials used, and number of visits. Insurance often has limited cosmetic coverage, which is why cost conversations happen early. You will understand your options and expected costs before making any decisions.
