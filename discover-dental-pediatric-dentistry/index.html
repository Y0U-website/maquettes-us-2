The practice

Our goal is to help children establish a strong foundation for a lifetime of good oral health. With that in mind, we do our very best to make every visit to our office a positive and engaging experience. Dr. Perez, along with every member of the office team, are sensitive to the unique needs of children and understand just how to help them feel comfortable and put them most at ease.

Dental care can pose unique challenges for some children. Our office not only provides a compassionate and welcoming environment, but we are also well equipped to provide care for young patients with special needs or conditions that may make it difficult for them to sit in a dental chair.

Remember, excellent oral health begins with proper oral care. At the office of Discover Dental, we focus on preventing dental disease and providing gentle, compassionate care to keep your child’s smile healthy and bright. We welcome new patients and offer care during and after hours for dental emergencies. Call us today to learn more or schedule an appointment!
