Financial Information

We accept Mastercard, Visa, American Express, and Discover credit cards, as well as cash and personal checks. For uninsured patients, we offer discount plans and flexible payment plan options—because cost should never stand in the way of great dental care. Our billing coordinator will be happy to discuss any insurance or billing questions you may have.

Our office provides dedicated care to help children get the dental care they need and establish a solid foundation for good oral health. We understand the financial considerations involved in dental care and remain mindful of your pocketbook.

Every treatment plan gets tailored to address individual needs. In all cases, our staff will review the treatment plan with you and discuss all the financial options we offer to make getting care the least stressful and most accessible for your family.

Insurance Plans

We accept most PPO plans including Aetna, Ameritas, Anthem, Careington, Cigna, CSEA, Delta Dental, Guardian, Healthplex, MetLife, Unum, and United insurance. Please call us with your insurance information prior to your visit, and we will be happy to explain your benefits to you.
