Membership Plan

Once you are in our office, our doctors and team will confirm the correct membership plan for your needs and goals.

  • Fluoride treatment with cleaning
  • Regular exam with cleaning
  • Oral cancer screening with cleaning
  • 1 set of routine X-rays per year
  • 1 emergency exam with X-ray per year
  • Up to 10 % off additional treatment
  • Most HSA/FSA cards accepted
  • Total plan valued at $545.00

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay for membership?

Our secure online portal accepts all major credit/debit cards, most HSA and FSA cards, and you’ll always have access to a receipt. Additional options may be available in our office.

When does my membership become effective?

After signing up, your membership is effective immediately. No waiting period! Simply call our office to schedule an appointment when you are ready.

Once I am a member, what’s next?

Call our office to schedule an appointment for yourself and your family members. It’s that simple!

Tell me more about these treatment discounts – how does it all work?

With insurance, patients are responsible for a certain percentage of restorative procedures (fillings, crowns, cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, and other treatments). Our plan works similarly – the membership covers preventive care, and we discount restorative treatments – but it’s better. There are no caps, no maximums, and no insurance company getting in the way of your oral health.

Can I add family members to my account?

Yes! You can add family members when you enroll in the membership plan, or at any time after.

What information is required to sign up?

Your full name, phone number, email address, and date of birth are required when signing up for our membership plan.

**This Dental Membership Plan is NOT dental insurance; it is a membership program for prepaid dental services offered by your dentist. The Plan and any discounts included do not cover procedures performed outside of our practice. Your membership may include a combination of the following dental codes: D1110, D1120, D0150, D0120, D0272, D0274, D0210, D0330, D0140, D0220, D1206, D1208, D4910
