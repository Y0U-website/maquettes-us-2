About Us

Modern-thinking dentistry with small-town heart and a commitment to exceptional care—for everyone, every single day.

With advanced technology, complete in-house dental care, and a team that’s always learning, our focus is to make you feel comfortable and respected.

When something better exists, we find it, learn it, and bring it to you. Drs. Dave, Tom, and Bob attend continuing education and sharpen their skills every year to provide you with today’s best care. That same mindset drives every technology investment we make to bring you a more comfortable and efficient experience.

Not everyone is happy about the thought of going to the dentist. We want you to feel safe and among friends when you visit our office. Whatever your anxiety may be, we strive to alleviate it, whether that’s talking things through or offering safe sedation options when you need them. Our doctors have no problem taking their time to make you feel at ease.

Our doctors and team are woven into the fabric of this community. From visiting local schools and teaching good oral habits to sponsoring youth programs and supporting local organizations and LGBTQ+ initiatives, we stay involved and give back. The people who visit our office aren’t just patients. They’re our neighbors, and that changes everything about how we care for them.

We offer a warm, cozy atmosphere you’ll feel the moment you walk in. We designed every room around your comfort, with headphones, pillows, blankets, and Netflix overhead. With advanced care done right here in our office, your visits are more efficient, more comfortable, and easier to fit into your life—so you can get the quality care you need close to home.

We Celebrate Diversity

We’re proud to care for people of all identities and backgrounds, creating a space where you can feel safe and supported.

Our patients and team come from all walks of life—different backgrounds, cultures, belief systems, and stories—and that’s something we genuinely celebrate. We take the time to get to know each person individually, and the connections we build here often go well beyond the dental chair.

We honor chosen pronouns and identities, partner with local LGBTQ+ organizations, and show up for community events. Everyone who walks through our door deserves to feel respected, seen, and completely at ease.
