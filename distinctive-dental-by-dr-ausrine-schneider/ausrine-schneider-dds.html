Meet Dr. Ausrine Schneider

Restorative, Cosmetic & Implant Dentistry in Willowbrook, IL

Dr. Schneider is a passionate dentist who was born and raised in Lithuania. Her love for dentistry and the desire to help people have happier and healthier lives inspired her to pursue and earn a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree in 1990 and Orthodontics Certification in 1992 from Kaunas Medical Academy in Kaunas, Lithuania.

In her pursuit of excellence, Dr. Schneider moved to the United States to learn more about dentistry and oral health. She earned her second DDS degree from the University of Illinois at Chicago in 2000 and eventually implant fellowship at New York University in 2010.

In July of 2001, she opened her practice in Willowbrook, Illinois, and has been serving patients ever since.

Throughout her career, Dr. Schneider has been actively involved in many dental associations, including the American Dental Association, Chicago Dental Society, American Academy of General Dentistry, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry and International Congress on Oral Implantology. Her dedication and commitment to the field of dentistry are evidenced by her board membership and former presidency of the Chicago Component of the American Academy of General Dentistry.

With over 20 years of professional dental experience Dr. Schneider has clinical expertise on wide range of cosmetic and surgical dental procedures which helped her become a trusted and successful dentist. She thoroughly enjoys building relationships with each patient. Her goal is to provide high-quality dental care, personalized attention, and a comfortable environment to make every visit to the dentist a positive experience.

When she is not at work, Dr. Schneider is a devoted mother of two daughters. She enjoys spending time with her family and friends, as well as traveling, sailing, exercising, and cooking. Her passion for life and dentistry is infectious and her patients appreciate her kind and caring nature.
