Nervous?

No matter how long it has been since you last saw a dentist, we won’t judge and will will never shame you! Approximately one in six patients are nervous about dental appointments.

If you’re anxious or tense about your appointment, please tell us. It will help us adapt the treatment to your needs.

We are proud to offer IV Moderate Sedation for all dental procedures. Only a few offices in Dane County offer this service and the outcome has been amazing for those individuals who are nervous and need options.
