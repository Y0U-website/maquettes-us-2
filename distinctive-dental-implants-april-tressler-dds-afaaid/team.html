Dr. April Tressler

DDS, AFAAID

My engineering background led me to the world of dental implants . I began my implant journey in 2019 by learning the basics through a variety of courses. From there I was hooked! In 2021 I joined the American Academy of Implant Dentistry and enrolled in the Maxicourse, which is a year long course for implant mastery. I am currently completing a mini-residency for advanced surgical techniques with the Dental Implant Learning Center in New York City. I am currently an Associate Fellow of the AAID with plans to advance my credentials to diplomat status.

In 2016 I trained with the American Academy of Facial Cosmetics. During my years in New Jersey I owned and operated an esthetics practice called “Facial Esthetics @ The Shore” which specialized in botox and dermal fillers. I have continued with my esthetics training and have worked in several medical spas in the Madison area. I am very excited to have my own dedicated esthetics practice to continue to help patients enhance their smiles! Stay tuned as I continue to add more esthetic options in the future!
