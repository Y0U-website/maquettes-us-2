Why Dental Exams Matter

Think of a dental examination as a preventative health measure, like getting a yearly physical, but for your mouth. Regular checkups help spot problems early, when they’re usually easier and less expensive to treat. Beyond cavities, dental exams can reveal early signs of gum disease, jaw misalignment, and even certain health conditions that affect the whole body. This is why many families rely on dentist offices in New Brighton, MN, for ongoing preventive care.

  • Checking the bite to ensure proper alignment

Signs You’re Due for an Exam

Even if your teeth feel fine, certain signs might mean it’s time to schedule an appointment. Consider booking an exam if you notice:

If these symptoms appear, visiting dentist offices in New Brighton, MN can help you address issues before they become more serious.

  • Persistent bad breath or a bad taste in the mouth
  • Bleeding gums when brushing or flossing
  • Tooth sensitivity to hot, cold, or sweet foods
  • A change in how your teeth fit together when biting
  • Any sores or spots in the mouth that don’t heal quickly
