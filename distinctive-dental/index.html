The practice

Dr. Davidson pursued a Physiology major at Michigan State University in the Lyman Briggs College. He then went on to receive a DDS degree from the University Of Detroit Mercy School Of Dentistry in 1997.

Dentistry is Dr. Davidson’s passion. He refers to his patients as guests and feels and inner reward every time he helps his patients maintain or create a new natural smile. You can be rest assured that maintaining healthy teeth and gums is always an important concern here. Truly an artist Dr. Davidson is especially proud of the beautiful and natural looking smiles he and his team have created.

When Dr. Davidson is away from the office he enjoys spending time with his wife Kristen and their three children Isabella, Jack, and Dominic. He also enjoys skiing, golfing and boating in northern Michigan with his family.
