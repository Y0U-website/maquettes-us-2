OUR MISSION

Our mission is to be a leader of dental innovation in the New Orleans metro area & to provide world-class dental services in a tranquil, state of the art dental facility while prioritizing comfortable & quality patient care.

OUR STORY

District Dental Spa, LLC, is the signature general and cosmetic dentistry clinic of Dr. Douglas A. Rodrigue, Jr., DDS. Dr. Doug had long aspired to establish his premier practice in the New Orleans area. After careful consideration, he was pleased to break ground in the heart of the historic Arts & Warehouse District in October of 2018. Numerous unique design elements were incorporated into the creation of our state-of-the-art facility, including live-edge Louisiana cypress countertops, European white-oak flooring, spacious 16-foot ceilings, and premium leather massage chairs.

After opening in May of 2019, Dr. Doug and his team at District Dental Spa, have strived to offer the residents of New Orleans a superior option when it comes to dental care & spa services. Patient comfort, quality of care, and a harmonious environment are our top priorities at our contemporary new location.
