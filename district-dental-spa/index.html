After graduating from Marquette University, Dr. Cash began his career in dentistry as a serviceman in the Army. In 2014, he was commissioned as a Captain and served soldiers in Georgia, Texas, and the Middle East. After his commitment ended, he moved with his wife and dogs to Indianapolis, where he enjoys cycling, running, and spending time with family and friends. He also began teaching…

In 2022, he opened District Dental Spa in the Bates-Hendricks neighborhood of Indianapolis. Dr. Cash is excited to bring his practice closer to home and welcome new patients to the growing District Dental Spa family.

Twice a year, Dr. Cash is proud to participate in Give Kids A Smile, an event that offers free dental screenings, treatments, and preventative services to underserved kids in the Indianapolis area. This valuable service gives kids the tools and information they need to maintain their oral hygiene, prevent painful cavities, and tooth decay, and grow into a healthy smile.
