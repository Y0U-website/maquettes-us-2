Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are the best option in modern dentistry for replacing missing or damaged teeth. Unlike bridges or dentures, which only replace the visible part of the tooth, implants replace both the root and crown portions of natural teeth. The implant post, which acts as the root, integrates with the jawbone to provide a stable, supportive base. It also stimulates healthy bone formation and reduces bone loss.

They are a durable solution for tooth loss, as they firmly attach to the bone. Moreover, implants are biocompatible and adjust well to the surrounding soft tissues. This screw-like post is surgically inserted into the jawbone, where it fuses with the surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration. An abutment is then placed over the post to connect it to your final restoration, which may be a crown, bridge, or denture.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

During your initial evaluation, our dentists inspect your oral health, bone density, and other factors to determine your eligibility. Adequate jawbone is essential, since the post must fuse with the bone to provide support. If you have significant bone loss, we can discuss options to prepare the site.

Why are implants better than a bridge or denture?

Bridges and dentures only replace the visible part of the tooth. Implants replace both the root and the crown. Because the post integrates with the jawbone, it provides a stable base, stimulates healthy bone formation, and reduces bone loss. Implants are also biocompatible and adjust well to the surrounding soft tissues.

What types of dental implants do you offer in Ceres, CA?

We offer single tooth implants to permanently replace one or more lost teeth, and overdenture implants for full-arch restoration when most or all natural teeth are missing. Overdentures use 2 to 4 implant posts as anchors to support a custom denture on the upper arch, lower arch, or both.

How long does dental implant treatment take?

Implant treatment takes place over several months. After the posts are surgically placed, they need time to fuse with your jawbone before the final restoration is attached. Dr. Popat will walk you through a timeline for your specific case at your consultation.
