The Benefits of Dentures for Your Oral Health and Quality of Life

Dentures offer a wide range of oral and aesthetic benefits to improve the quality of life and overall health:

What is the difference between partial and full dentures?

A full denture substitutes for a whole missing arch and is supported by your gums and jaw ridge or palate. A partial denture is a plate with one or more artificial teeth that relies on the support of your gums and underlying bone, plus special clasps that grip your surviving natural teeth for extra stability.

Why are implant-supported dentures more stable?

Because they snap onto implants inserted into your jaw rather than relying on suction against your gums, implant-supported dentures typically provide considerably more stability than regular dentures while remaining removable for cleaning.
