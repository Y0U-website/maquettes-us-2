Underbite

In case of an underbite, the lower teeth close ahead of the upper teeth. This is the opposite of normal biting, in which your upper teeth slightly overlap your lower teeth. An underbite can result from abnormal jaw growth.

Crowding

Crowding is a common problem that occurs when your jaw does not have enough room to fit all the teeth, causing them to overlap. It can be caused by a narrow jaw space, abnormal eruption pattern, or irregular tooth-to-jaw ratio.

Spacing

Spacing presents with gaps between two or more teeth. This can be due to frequent thumb sucking, prolonged use of pacifiers, or small-sized teeth. It can affect the aesthetic appeal of your smile and increase the risk of periodontal disease.
