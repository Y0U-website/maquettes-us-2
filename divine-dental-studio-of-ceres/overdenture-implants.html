Are Overdentures Different From Regular Dentures?

The main difference between overdentures and dentures is that of stability. Removable dentures rely on the suction between the denture and the bone ridges to hold them in place. However, the suction force becomes weak and unreliable with time. On the other hand, our overdenture implants in Ceres, CA, use metal implant posts to secure the denture over the jawbone, which provides a stronger and more reliable attachment. This factor makes them stronger and more reliable than conventional dentures. They also cover a lesser surface area of the oral cavity, providing greater ease and comfort.

Overdentures use metal implant posts to secure the denture to the jawbone, which creates a stronger, more reliable attachment while covering less of your mouth.

Can I still remove my overdenture for cleaning?

Yes. We attach your custom denture using special abutments that allow you to easily remove and reattach it as needed. Our team will show you how to clean and care for the appliance so it lasts as long as possible.

Am I a good candidate for overdenture implants?

At your first visit, we clinically examine your mouth to confirm you qualify. You must have sufficient jawbone and good oral hygiene for implants to succeed. We also use advanced digital scanning to plan precise implant placement.

What does the overdenture implant process involve?

After your evaluation and digital scans, our dentists place the metal implant posts in your jawbone under local anesthesia. It is a minimally invasive procedure. You will then wait a few months for the implants to bond with the bone before we attach your custom-made denture over the implants.
