What if I do not have enough jawbone?

We may recommend bone grafting for patients with extensive bone loss so there is a strong, stable base to hold the implant in place.

What are the benefits of single tooth implants?

They are permanent and provide long-term retention, replacing both the crown and root of the missing tooth. You can eat your favorite foods comfortably, they look natural, they support your facial structure and help prevent sagging, they promote healthy bone formation and prevent bone loss, and you can brush and floss them just like natural teeth.

What happens during the implant procedure?

The treatment takes more than one visit. We start with a clinical exam and 3D oral scans to plan your case. We then numb the area and place the implant post, a tiny metal screw that acts as the root of the tooth. The post needs 3 to 6 months to heal and fuse with your bone. Once healing is complete, we connect an abutment and place your final crown, bridge, or denture.

Can single tooth implants replace more than one missing tooth?

Yes. We use single tooth implants to replace one or more missing teeth. Dr. Popat will evaluate your remaining teeth and design a customized plan that restores both the health and appearance of your smile.
