Real Relationships. Dedicated Dentistry.

Dr. Kjersten Heron is passionate about serving the people of the Mount Vernon community. Her parents began Division Street Dental with a vision of compassionate, quality care, with genuine relationships. She is honored to carry on their legacy and continue building meaningful relationships while holding herself to the highest of standards. With her patients in the driver’s seat, Dr. Heron believes in educating and empowering patients to make the best decisions for their oral health. She is right by their side to support them on every step of their journey.

Education and Expertise

Born and raised in Mount Vernon, Dr. Heron graduated from Mount Vernon High school. She attended Lewis & Clark College and Pacific Lutheran University where she earned her undergrad degree. Earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery, she completed her degree at the University of Washington School of Dentistry. She is passionate about expanding her knowledge and perfecting her skills with continuing education.
