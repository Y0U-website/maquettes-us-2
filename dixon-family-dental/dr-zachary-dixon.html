Zachary Dixon, D.D.S.

Welcome to Dixon Family Dental, your family dentist in Morrilton, AR.

Dr. Zachary Dixon is a Morrilton High School graduate, Class of 2003. He attended Arkansas Tech University, earning a Bachelor of Science in Biology in 2007, and went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Tennessee Health Science Center in 2011. Dr. Dixon is a proud member of the American Dental Association, Arkansas State Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, and the Central District Dental Society.

“I love every aspect of dentistry and the rewarding opportunity to help others. I feel truly blessed to serve the community I’ve called home my entire life. Our amazing staff makes everyday fun, meaningful, and fulfilling.” – Dr. Dixon

Outside the office, Dr. Dixon enjoys spending time with his wife, Natalie, a first-grade teacher at Morrilton Primary School, and their three children — Zachary, Brody, and Blakely. He’s an avid outdoorsman who loves hunting, fishing, canoeing, and making memories with his family in the Great Arkansas outdoors.
