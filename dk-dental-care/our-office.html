Our story

Originally, Dr. Weis opened the practice, in Hales Corners, over 30 years ago. As Dr. Weis prepared to retire from dentistry, Dr. David keeling joined him in 2017. A Hales Corners resident himself, Dr. David Keeling was excited for this great opportunity to practice in his own community and is honored to continue the legacy of quality, patient centered care! In February 2019, Dr. David Keeling moved the practice to it&#x27;s new location, just a few blocks north of the prior office, in order to expand patient services and care.

What to Expect

If it’s your first time visiting DK Dental Care, you can expect a warm welcome and a comfortable experience. You’ll never feel rushed or out-of-the-loop because our communicative approach ensures that every patient is well-informed about their diagnosis and treatment options. Initial visits typically include a comprehensive exam, cleaning, and x-rays to give you everything you need to start your journey with us.
