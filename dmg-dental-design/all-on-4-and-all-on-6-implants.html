What Is All-On-4?

All-on-4 Implants is a revolutionary dental implant procedure designed to replace a full arch of teeth using just four implants. This method anchors a complete set of teeth to a minimal number of implants, offering a stable foundation for your new smile. The All-on-4 procedure is popular for its efficiency, as most patients can have their implants placed and their new teeth attached in a single day.

What Is All-On-6?

All-on-6 implants in Bothell are similar to All-on-4 but use six implants instead of four, providing additional support and stability for patients with less bone density or who need extra reinforcement. This option is particularly beneficial for patients with more extensive bone loss. The All-on-6 procedure offers the same life-changing benefits as All-on-4, with the added advantage of a stronger and more durable foundation for the replacement teeth.

All-on-4 vs. All-on-6: Which One is Right for You?

While All-on-4 and All-on-6 Implants near you offer similar benefits, the number of implants used makes a difference in stability and suitability for different bone structures. All-on-6 may be recommended for patients with significant bone loss or those who want added support for their new teeth. Your dentist will assess your oral health and help you determine which procedure best meets your needs.
