What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants in Bothell are artificial tooth roots, typically made from titanium, that are surgically placed into the jawbone to support replacement teeth. These implants serve as a foundation for crowns (artificial teeth) permanently secured to the implants. The implant&#x27;s screw-like shape helps it stay in place and provides ample surface area for the bone to grow into, a process called osseointegration.

Patients with bone loss or those not eligible for standard implants may consider mini implants. Mini implants in Bothell are often used to secure advanced or partial dentures and are less expensive than full standard dental implants.
