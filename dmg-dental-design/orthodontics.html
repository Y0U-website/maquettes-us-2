When is the Best Time for Orthodontic Treatment?

The ideal time for orthodontic treatment is typically between ages 10 and 14, as the teeth are still shifting into their final positions. However, orthodontics isn&#x27;t just for kids. Adults are increasingly opting for treatment to improve the appearance and functionality of their teeth, especially with the rise of discreet treatment options.

Maintaining Oral Hygiene During Orthodontic Treatment

Maintaining excellent oral hygiene is crucial regardless of the orthodontic method you choose. Traditional braces can trap food and plaque, making daily flossing even more important. Your dentist will provide guidance on how to clean around your braces and may recommend using special threader tools for effective flossing. With clear aligners, cleaning is simpler, as you can remove the aligners to brush and floss as usual. But no matter your choice of treatment, regular dental visits and diligent cleaning habits will help ensure the success of your orthodontic treatment.
