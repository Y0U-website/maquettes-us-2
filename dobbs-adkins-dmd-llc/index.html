The practice

From general exams and cleanings, to cosmetic dentistry and endodontic treatments, Dobbs & Adkins, DMD, LLC provides the full gamut of dental services for their patients. Along with their caring and professional staff, both doctors work with their patients to help them achieve their best, brightest, and healthiest smile .

The founder of the practice grew up in Trussville and graduated from Hewitt-Trussville High School. He earned his undergraduate degree from Auburn University and his dental degree from UAB. He is married to Sharon (Pair) and has one daughter, Dori, who along with her husband Tyler Weldon are employed by Auburn University. He is an avid fly fisherman and enjoys golf and Auburn football.

Dr. Adkins joined the practice in 2011 and is also a lifelong resident of Trussville. He graduated from the University of Alabama and got his dental degree from UAB. He is married to Emily (Walsh) and they have four children.

Dr. Zane Hood grew up in Trussville and went to the University of Alabama. He earned his DMD from UAB School of Dentistry in Spring 2024, graduating with research honors. He’s been married to his wife Cayla for four years. They have a dog named Cooper and are expecting a baby boy in March 2025.
