Patient Information

Providing Dental Care for All Ages for 40 Years

For more than 40 years, Dobbs & Adkins, DMD, LLC has offered professional, modern, and family-friendly dental care for the Trussville area. Our dentists provide each patient with specialized general and cosmetic dental care catered to their specific needs. We are dedicated to providing you and your family with the preventive dental care you need in order to achieve and maintain a healthy smile .

We are committed to welcoming all of our patients with care and compassion, and provide them with a warm, inviting environment. Our team is dedicated to helping our patients establish their dental needs and goals, and then develop and implement a personalized treatment plan.

In order for us to provide you with prompt service, please arrive 15 minutes prior to your appointment and bring your insurance card, social security number, and a filled in Patient Information form with you. We’ll also need to know if you have any medical conditions, such as artificial joint replacement or heart valve replacement, that require antibiotic premedication. If you are taking any blood thinner medications, please consult with your doctor before your appointment.
