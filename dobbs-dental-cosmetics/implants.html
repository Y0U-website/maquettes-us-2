The Advantages of Dental Implants

​Dental implants replace the tooth and the crown portion of the tooth. This makes them the ideal solution for tooth replacement. Also, they do not rely on the adjacent teeth for support. They are the only replacement option that promotes bone regeneration in the jaw.

Convenience

Implants are the only permanent option for missing teeth. You should care for them as you would your natural teeth.

Beauty

Implant restorations are often created using dental porcelain, which can be molded to resemble your natural teeth.

Comfort

Your implants are designed especially for you. A custom restoration will fit comfortably over your implants, allowing you to make the most out of your new smile.

Stability

Dental implants will help the final restoration remain stable. An implant secured restoration will not slip or loosen.

Many patients assume they are not candidates for dental implants. In reality, most adults with missing teeth can qualify.
