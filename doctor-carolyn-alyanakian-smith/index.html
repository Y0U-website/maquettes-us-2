The practice

Diagnostic and preventative services include: • Routine cleaning and deep cleaning procedures • Sealants and fluoride treatments • Tooth colored fillings • Metal free crowns and bridges • Night guards • Sports mouth guards • Removable and implant retained dentures • Pediatric dentistry Keep your smile healthy and bright with these procedures.

Dental Implants are artificial teeth surgically placed where the missing teeth used to be. There are many benefits to implants such as:

• A more youthful appearance • No slurred speech or clicking dentures • The appearance and feel of natural teeth • A natural chewing motion • No more ill-fitting dentures • No more nightly soaking, denture adhesives or daily maintenance

We work closely with our specialists who place the implants. We restore implants with crowns that allow them to function as a tooth in your mouth, once they are determined by the specialist to be ready for restoration.

We will take impressions and make customized trays for you. You place the whitening material in trays and wear them for the recommended time per day. Within a few days you will be on the road to a whiter and brighter smile.

For ongoing whitening we offer a touch up kit of whitening material for those of you who have good fitting custom trays and want to maintain your sparkling smile!
