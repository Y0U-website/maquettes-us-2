The practice

We are dedicated to your comfort when you visit our office. We strive to give you a safe place to talk about your health. Our entire staff can help answer any questions you may have, no matter how silly you think they may be.

Our friendly dental team is dedicated to creating a welcoming, stress-free experience for every patient.

ClearCorrect is a discreet and effective orthodontic treatment that uses custom-made, clear aligners to gradually straighten your teeth without the need for traditional braces.

Our technology helps us detect issues early and provide effective solutions.

We listen, explain every option clearly, and work with you to create treatment plans that match your goals, budget, and lifestyle.

Dental fillings are used to fill cavities and repair cracked or broken teeth. Fillings can be done with gold, porcelain, or silver amalgam, but is most commonly done with materials called composite resin fillings that are designed to match the color of your teeth.

A tooth can lose its normal shape and size for a variety of reasons. A crown covers your tooth to help restore its original shape and size. Crowns can also be used to make teeth stronger and look better.

X-rays are used to examine the inside of your teeth. We are hoping to be able to catch any problems that we can’t see with the naked eye! Using radiology, we are able to see which teeth are developing cavities.
