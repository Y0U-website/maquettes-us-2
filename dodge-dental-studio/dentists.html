Meet The Team Dr. Emma & Ryan Dodge, Hartford’s Extraordinary Dentists Call Us at 262-673-6050 To Schedule Call Us 262-673-6050 Your Partners in Dental Health Purchased by Drs. Emma and Ryan Dodge in 2021, Dodge Dental Studio was originally owned by Dr. Paul Scheunemann.

The practice has a long history in the Harford community, having been opened in 1985 and serving as a dental home for many years. Dr. Emma first visited the practice as a 3 year old, and her experiences with Dr. Scheunemann inspired her to become a dentist. As an inspiration to her and later a mentor, Drs. Emma and Ryan are honored to work with the amazing team Dr. Scheunemann has created and look forward to continuing his tradition of excellent care.

A year and a half after our office officially became Dodge Dental Studio, Drs. Emma and Ryan Dodge acquired Dr. Doubleday’s patient base, welcoming many smiles through our door, where we are able to ensure his patients receive the same excellent standard of care that Dr. Doubleday provided.

Emma C. Dodge, DDS

“I love to see improvement in health throughout my time getting to know patients.”

Dr. Emma Dodge’s dentistry journey began by completing a Bachelors of Science and shortly after her Doctorate of Dental Surgery at Marquette University. Dr. Emma has been able to take her expertise in dentistry into the Navy where she was a United States Navy Dental Officer. Wanting to return to her roots in the Hartford community, she took over for her childhood dentist, Dr. Scheunemann in 2021 with her husband, Dr. Ryan, to provide a genuinely focused and compassionate dental experience to her neighbors.

Dr. Emma’s passion lies in getting to know patients throughout their lives, understanding their dreams and goals all while getting to be part of their health journey. She takes the time to know what motivates each patient and helps with understanding the why behind their dental care.

In her personal time, she is an avid reader and stays active outdoors by running, hiking, biking, and travelling. She plays piano at church and loves spending time with her husband and daughter Lucille, enjoying baking, cooking, and being part of their community together.

Ryan C. Dodge, DDS “It’s very rewarding to hear from patients after we have established their dental home that they feel healthier, and have more confidence in themselves.”

Having received a Bachelors of Science and a Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Marquette University, Dr. Ryan Dodge brings a wealth of dental education and experience to our practice. Working as an Associate Dentist in Virginia while Dr. Emma was on Active Duty, Dr. Ryan gathered knowledge on how to best serve his patients. Having founded Dodge Dental Studio in 2021 with his wife, Dr. Emma, Dr. Ryan is able to serve his community and educate his patients to achieve dental health.

Dr. Ryan feels deeply rewarded when he is able to restore his patient’s health and see their comfort and confidence grow in every visit. His passion lies in building genuine relationships with his patients and being able to explain the perio-systemic connection to better develop tailored treatment plans and help people love their smiles.

In his personal time, Dr. Ryan loves spending time with his daughter, Lucille, and his wife. His passion is singing and playing trumpet at church. He spends plenty of time outdoors by hiking, running, biking, and kayaking. Cooking, baking, and finding new roasts of coffee beans to try are hobbies he enjoys and shares with his family.
