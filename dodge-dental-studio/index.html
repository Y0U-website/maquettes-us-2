The practice

The Latin phrase Cura Personalis is at the core of our values and competencies. We focus every aspect of our care and our team work through our approach, taking care of you beyond your dental health. We want to make a difference in your life by caring for your oral health, your overall health, and emotional wellbeing.

We want to achieve health, not just fix what’s broken. We take the time to understand your health and ease your anxieties, tailoring your care for long lasting health.

We spend extra time with every patient to make sure you understand your oral health, your treatment options, and how it all connects to your overall well-being.

The tools we use, the knowledge we apply, and the care you receive are always evolving. We dedicate the time to improve our technology, systems, and education to ensure we are using all avenues to improve your oral health.

We are committed to giving back, building trust, and being a steady presence for our neighbors across generations.
