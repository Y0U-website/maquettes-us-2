Are dental implants right for me?

Dental implants are a long-lasting, natural-looking solution for replacing missing teeth. Getting a dental implant involves having a titanium post surgically installed into your jawbone. This acts as a secure foundation for a custom crown, bridge, or dentures. Unlike other tooth replacement options, implants restore both the visible tooth and the underlying root. Providing unparalleled stability and durability.

Beyond improving the appearance of your smile, dental implants offer many functional benefits. They prevent bone loss in the jaw, maintain your facial structure, and make it easier to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. We design our dental implants in Waxhaw to look and feel just like your natural teeth, blending seamlessly into your beautiful smile.

Are restorative treatments permanent?

These types of treatments are meant to be long-lasting and durable, but their longevity depends on the type of restoration and how well you care for your teeth.

For example, dental implants can last decades or even a lifetime with proper care, as they integrate with your jawbone to provide a stable, permanent solution. On the other hand, crowns, fillings, and other restorations may need replacement after several years because of natural wear and tear.

At Dogwood Dental Spa, we only use high-quality materials and advanced techniques to maximize the lifespan of every restoration. To keep your treatments in top condition, maintaining good oral hygiene is essential. Our team is here to help you get the most out of your restorative care, ensuring your smile stays healthy, gorgeous, and functional for years to come.

How do I care for my dental restorations to make them last?

Caring for your dental restorations is straightforward and closely mirrors routine care for natural teeth. We recommend brushing at least twice daily using a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste to keep your teeth and restorations clean and free from plaque buildup.

Flossing regularly helps remove debris and prevents decay around the edges of your restorations. Also, visiting us for routine cleanings and exams every 6 months is essential to monitor the health of your teeth and the conditions of your restorations.
