Should I go to the ER or call 911 for a dental emergency?

If your dental emergency is life-threatening—such as severe jaw injury, uncontrollable bleeding, or difficulty breathing—call 911 or visit the ER immediately. These situations require urgent medical attention to ensure your safety.

For non-life-threatening issues, like tooth pain, broken teeth, infections, or lost crowns, it’s best to contact our emergency dentist in Waxhaw. Our team specializes in treating these types of dental emergencies and can provide the care you need.
