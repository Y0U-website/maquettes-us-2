Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most patients benefit from visiting the dentist every 6 months for a professional cleaning and comprehensive checkup. These regular appointments are key to maintaining optimal oral health by preventing issues like tooth decay, gum diseases, and bad breath.

For some patients, such as those with a history of gum disease, frequent cavities, or orthodontic appliances, more frequent visits may be necessary. These additional checkups allow us to monitor your oral health closely and address any potential concerns before they progress into more complex issues.

Regular dental care isn’t just about keeping your teeth clean—it’s about supporting your overall well-being and helping you to enjoy a confident, healthy smile for years to come.

What happens during a dental checkup?

A dental checkup is more than just a routine visit—it’s an essential step in keeping your smile healthy. At Dogwood Dental Spa, we tailor every appointment to your needs, ensuring you feel comfortable, informed, and cared for.

We believe dental care should be a partnership, which is why we take the time to explain each step of the process and answer any questions you may have. A checkup at Dogwood Dental Spa isn’t just about treating your teeth—it’s about empowering you with the knowledge and tools you need to care for your smile confidently.

  • Professional Cleaning: We’ll gently remove plaque, tartar, and surface stains, leaving your teeth clean and smooth. With teeth cleaning in Waxhaw, we can help you prevent cavities, gum diseases, and more.
  • Comprehensive Exam: Your dentist will thoroughly examine your teeth, gums, and mouth to check for any early signs of potential concerns.
  • X-Rays (If Needed): For a complete picture of your oral health, we may take X-rays to detect problems that aren’t visible to the naked eye, like decay between teeth or issues below the gum line.
  • Personalized Advice: You and your dentist will discuss your oral hygiene habits and give you tips to enhance your routine at home. This can help ensure sustainable oral health for years to come.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

Dental emergencies can happen unexpectedly, and knowing how to respond can make all the difference in protecting your oral health. If you’re experiencing severe tooth pain, a broken or knocked-out tooth, swelling, or any other urgent issue, please call our office immediately. We prioritize same-day emergency dental appointments to provide quick relief for urgent concerns.

For potentially life-threatening emergencies, such as uncontrollable bleeding, facial trauma, or difficulty breathing, it’s essential to call 911 or visit the ER as soon as possible. These situations require urgent medical attention that goes beyond dental care.
