How do I know if I have TMJ?

TMJ refers to the temporomandibular joint, the vital connection between your jawbone and skull that allows you to chew, speak, yawn, and more. When this joint experiences dysfunction or strain, it can lead to a condition known as TMJ disorder, often causing pain and discomfort that can disrupt everyday life.

What symptoms does TMJ disorder cause?

Living with TMJ disorder can be frustrating, especially when the symptoms interfere with your life. These issues often go beyond simple jaw discomfort, impacting other areas of your body and overall well-being. Recognizing the signs early can help you get the relief you need.

If you’re experiencing any of these symptoms, it’s important to seek care. We specialize in identifying the root cause of TMJ disorders and providing effective relief. Our goal is to restore your comfort and improve your quality of life with our TMJ treatment in Waxhaw.

  • Jaw pain or tenderness
  • Clicking, popping, or locking of the jaw
  • Difficulty opening or closing your mouth fully
  • Headaches or migraines
  • Ear pain
  • Facial or neck muscle tension
  • Pain that radiates to your shoulders or back

How does Botox help with TMJ pain?

Botox is a transformative solution for managing TMJ pain and tension. This FDA-approved treatment works by relaxing overactive muscles in the jaw and face that contribute to clenching, grinding, and discomfort. By temporarily blocking the nerve signals that cause these muscles to contract, Botox provides relief from pain and helps reduce the strain on your temporomandibular joint. We carefully target the specific muscles that are responsible for your discomfort. Our goal is not only to alleviate tension but to improve your jaw mobility and prevent long-term issues.

The procedure itself is minimally invasive and quick, often completed in less than 15 minutes with minimal downtime. Most patients begin to feel relief within a few days, with results typically lasting 3 to 4 months. If you’ve been living with the frustrating symptoms of TMJ, Botox can be a game-changer, relieving pain and helping you regain comfort in your daily life.

What are occlusal guards?

Occlusal guards, commonly referred to as night guards, are custom-made dental devices that protect your teeth and ease strain on your jaw. They are especially beneficial for people who clench or grind their teeth, a condition known as bruxism. Bruxism often contributes to TMJ pain, tooth wear, and even headaches.

Worn primarily during sleep, occlusal guards act as a cushion between your upper and lower teeth, distributing pressure evenly and preventing the excessive force that can cause damage over time. By protecting your teeth as you sleep, these guards help promote better alignment, reduce tension, and minimize the risk of long-term complications like cracked teeth or joint pain.

If you wake up with jaw pain, morning headaches, or notice signs of tooth wear, an occlusal guard might be the solution you need. With our TMJ treatment in Waxhaw, we can help you find relief and protect your smile.

How do I know if I’m a good candidate for TMJ treatments?

If you experience jaw pain, headaches, or teeth clenching or grinding, you’re likely a good candidate for treatments like Botox or occlusal guards. Ideal candidates should be in good overall health and have no underlying medical conditions that may interfere with treatment.
