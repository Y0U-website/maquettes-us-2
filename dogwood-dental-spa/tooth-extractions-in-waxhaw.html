How does surgical dentistry differ from general dentistry?

Surgical dentistry goes beyond routine care to address complex dental concerns that require specialized expertise and techniques. While general dentistry focuses on preventive treatments like cleanings, exams, and fillings, surgical dentistry involves procedures such as tooth extractions, dental implants, and oral surgeries. These treatments help resolve more advanced concerns, such as severe decay, impacted teeth, or missing teeth.

Do wisdom teeth always require surgical extraction?

Not all wisdom teeth require removal. If they are healthy, fully erupted, and properly aligned, extraction may not be necessary. However, wisdom teeth often become problematic when they grow in at odd angles, remain partially or fully impacted, or cause overcrowding in the mouth. These concerns can lead to discomfort, infections, or damage to neighboring teeth, making surgical removal the best course of action.

Does a tooth extraction or implant procedure hurt?

At Dogwood Dental Spa, your comfort is our top priority. Our team uses local anesthesia to numb the treatment area, ensuring you don’t feel pain during the procedure itself. For patients with dental anxiety or those undergoing more complex procedures, we also offer prescription options to help you feel calm and relaxed throughout the process.

While you won’t feel pain during the procedure, it’s normal to experience mild discomfort or swelling afterward. This is typically short-lived and can be managed effectively with over-the-counter pain relievers, cold compresses, and the aftercare instructions we provide. Our team will also check in with you after your procedure to ensure you’re healing well and address any concerns you may have.

How can I manage discomfort after surgery?

Managing pain and discomfort after getting surgical procedures like tooth extractions in Waxhaw is simple with the proper care and guidance. We will ensure you know exactly how to promote healing and stay comfortable with comprehensive after-care guidelines.
