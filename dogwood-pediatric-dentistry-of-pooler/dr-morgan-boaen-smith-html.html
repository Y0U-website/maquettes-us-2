Dr. Morgan Boaen Smith

Dr. Morgan Boaen Smith was born and raised in Savannah, attending Saint Vincent Academy for high school, where she played volleyball, basketball, and soccer. She went on to complete her undergraduate training at The University of Georgia, graduating, magna cum laude, in 2011 with a Bachelor of Science.

Dr. Morgan received her Doctor of Dental Medicine from The Dental College of Georgia at Augusta University (formerly the Medical College of Georgia School of Dentistry). She then continued her training and completed a residency in pediatric dentistry, during which she trained at Children’s Healthcare of Atlanta. Dr. Morgan loves working with and providing dental care to all pediatric patients, including those with special healthcare needs.

Dr. Morgan stays up-to-date on the latest research and information in pediatric dentistry by participating in continuing education through the many professional organizations of which she is a member, including the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the Southeastern Society of Pediatric Dentistry.

In her free time, Dr. Morgan enjoys spending time with her husband, Pate, who is also a dentist in Savannah. They enjoy relaxing at the beach, cheering on the Dawgs, and spending time with family and friends (including their two dogs, Baker and Fletcher). Dr. Morgan is happy to – once again – call Savannah home.
