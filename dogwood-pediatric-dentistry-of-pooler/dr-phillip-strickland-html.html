Dr. Phillip Strickland

A Georgia native, Dr. Phillip Strickland graduated from The University of Georgia, cum laude, with a Bachelor of Science in Biology and a Spanish minor. He went on to attend the Medical College of Georgia School of Dentistry (now Augusta University), where he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine in 2011. Dr. Phillip completed his residency in Pediatric Dentistry in 2013. During residency, Dr. Phillip specialized in oral health care for infants and children through adolescence, including those with special healthcare needs.

Prior to joining the Dogwood team, Dr. Phillip was a dentist at Children’s Healthcare of Atlanta where he treated children with a variety of special healthcare needs and craniofacial anomalies. Dr. Phillip has also served as adjunct faculty at Augusta University College of Dental Medicine, supervising and mentoring pediatric dental residents.

Dr. Phillip started working part-time with our Dogwood team in the summer of 2018 until he and his family relocated to Savannah in May 2019. Dr. Phillip is excited to now be full-time with our team and grateful to join the Dogwood family!

Dr. Phillip became board certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry in 2014, and his professional memberships include the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the Southeastern Society of Pediatric Dentistry, and the Georgia Dental Association.

Outside of the office, Dr. Phillip enjoys traveling, playing tennis, sitting poolside, and watching UGA football. Dr. Phillip is also an Eagle Scout and enjoys being outdoors with his two dogs exploring what low country living has to offer!
