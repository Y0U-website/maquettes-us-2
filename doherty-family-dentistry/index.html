Les soins

0 Our Services Cleanings/Exams Prevention is always advocated in dentistry. A routine cleaning and exam is key to prevention. Whether it be 3, 4, or 6 months between cleanings and exams, our goal is to catch decay, gum disease, and other pathologies early.

Dentures include both partial and complete dentures. Our process for making dentures is designed to have the best fit and function we can achieve with a prosthetic. Your input during the process is vital in achieving those goals.

Dental implants are a wonderful way to replace missing teeth. They act as an artificial root that can anchor restorations ranging from a single tooth to adding stability to a complete denture.

Invisalign® and Candid are the clear braces alternative. The teeth are moved through a sequence of clear aligners that gradually bring the patient to a healthier mouth and straighter smile.

We are happy to consult with you on any cosmetic concerns. Our wish is that every person is comfortable with their smile and we would love to discuss options if there is anything we can do to achieve that goal.
