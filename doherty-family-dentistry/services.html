Cosmetic Dentistry

We are happy to consult with you on any cosmetic concerns. Our wish is that every person is comfortable with their smile and we would love to discuss options if there is anything we can do to achieve that goal.

Restorative

Dental Implants

Dental implants are the current ideal for replacing a missing tooth. Simply put, it is an artificial root that we can use as a foundation for a number of restorative options. An implant is the next best thing to having a real tooth.

Whitening

Our office offers in office Zoom whitening and take home whitening kits that can potentially brighten the teeth by 3-4 shades or more. Call the office today in order to set up a consult regarding whitening possibilities.

Dentures

Dentures include both partial and complete dentures. Our process for making dentures is designed to have the best fit and function we can achieve with a prosthetic. Your input during the process is vital in achieving those goals.

Clear Aligners

Invisalign® and Candid are clear braces options. They are designed to move teeth through a series of clear aligners that gradually bring the teeth to a healthy and esthetic smile.
