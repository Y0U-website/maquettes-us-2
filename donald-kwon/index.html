0 Meet Dr. Donald Kwon Dr. Donald Kwon is a dedicated orthodontist with deep roots in Southern California. Born in Los Angeles and raised in the close-knit community of La Cañada, he grew up surrounded by the values of care and community. For over a decade, Dr. Donald Kwon had the privilege of practicing alongside his father, serving patients at their iconic Koreatown office and their second location in his beloved hometown of La Cañada. His experience working as part of a father-son duo shaped his philosophy of blending innovation with a personal touch to create exceptional patient experiences.

Dr. Kwon now lives in La Cañada with his wife, three children, and their beloved dog. He appreciates his short commute to work, allowing him to remain closely connected to the community he cherishes. Outside the office, Dr. Kwon is passionate about staying active. Whether swimming, playing golf, or enjoying soccer games with childhood friends, he finds joy in movement and camaraderie.

A self-proclaimed foodie, Dr. Kwon balances his active lifestyle with a love for discovering delicious meals and sharing recommendations about his favorite restaurants. His kind nature and easy going personality ensures that every patient feels comfortable and cared for, making him not just a skilled orthodontist but also a trusted community member.

Dr. Kwon is honored to serve the families of La Cañada and beyond, bringing smiles to his community one patient at a time.
