The practice

In July 2024, Dr. Kate Shadid joined the practice, continuing our tradition of compassionate, patient-focused dentistry.

Today, our team brings over 100 years of combined experience among our dentists and hygienists, allowing us to provide trusted, knowledgeable care for every patient we serve.

For more than 60 years, Donlan Sudholt Dental has been built on trusted relationships and personalized care. Our experienced doctors and dedicated staff are committed to making every visit comfortable, welcoming, and focused on your individual needs.

We combine advanced dental technology with a compassionate, patient-first approach to ensure you feel confident in your care whether you’re visiting for a routine cleaning or a more advanced treatment.

A Peoria native, Dr. John has proudly served his hometown community for decades. With extensive experience in esthetic and restorative dentistry, he is dedicated to providing personalized, high-quality care for every patient.

A Peoria native, Dr. Nicole is proud to care for patients in her hometown community. A Cum Laude graduate and former President of the Peoria District Dental Society, she combines advanced training, leadership, and compassionate care to deliver exceptional results for her patients.

A Magna Cum Laude graduate and second in his dental school class, Dr. Eric brings advanced training and precision to every patient he treats. As a former President of the Peoria District Dental Society, he is committed to delivering comprehensive, high-quality care with a patient-first approach.

Dr. Kate Shadid is a Peoria native who is proud to serve patients in her hometown alongside her father, Dr. John. She earned her DMD from the University of Illinois Chicago College of Dentistry and is passionate about providing compassionate, high-quality care for patients of all ages.
