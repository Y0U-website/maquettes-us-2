Our Team

At Donlan Sudholt Dental, our team is more than a group of professionals we’re neighbors, parents, and members of the Peoria community. Together, we combine experience, advanced technology, and compassionate care to help every patient feel confident and cared for.

Dr. John Donlan

Dr. Nicole Sudholt

Dr. Eric Sudholt

Dr. Eric Sudholt is a native of the St. Louis metro area. He grew up in Belleville, Illinois, where he attended St. Teresa Grade School and Althoff Catholic High School. Dr. Sudholt did his undergraduate studies at The University of Missouri earning a Bachelor of Science in Biology in 2006, graduating Magna Cum Laude.

He earned his D.M.D. degree from Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine in 2010, graduating Magna Cum Laude and second in his class. Dr. Sudholt splits his work week at his practice in Eureka, IL, Eureka Family Dental. Dr. Sudholt is a former President of the Peoria District Dental Society. He is also a member of the Illinois State Dental Society, the Chicago Dental Society, International Team for Implantology study club member and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Eric resides in Dunlap with his wife, Dr. Nicole, their two children, Nolan and Nora and Irish Doodle Penny. In his free time you ll find Eric with family at Oak Run, tinkering on his Bronco and doing work around his yard.

Dr. Kate Shadid

As a Peoria native, Dr. Kate Shadid (formerly Donlan) is excited to be back in her hometown to provide dental care alongside her dad, Dr. John in the practice. Dr. Kate is a graduate of Peoria Notre Dame High School and earned her Bachelor of Science in Biology from Bradley University. She went on to earn her Doctorate degree (DMD) from the University of Illinois Chicago College of Dentistry. Dr. Kate is a member of the Peoria District Dental Society, American Dental Association, and Illinois State Dental Society.

Outside of the office, Kate enjoys reading, spending time with her husband, Luke and daughter, Eve, and going for walks with their dog, Jerry.
