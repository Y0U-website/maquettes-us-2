For over 40 Years, we have been serving local dentists in the St. Louis Metro Area and southern Illinois communities. Tom Donnell, CDT, FNBC, started Donnell Dental Laboratory in 1970 as a small one-person laboratory serving a few clients. Through maintaining consistency, service, and technological advances, the laboratory grew to a full-service facility serving clients nationwide.

After graduating in 1982 with a degree in Dental Technology, Bart Donnell, CDT went on to become certified in two specialties and worked alongside his father for 17 years. Subsequently, Bart purchased the laboratory in 2003, continuing the tradition of quality and exceptional customer service.

With more Certified Dental Technicians than any other lab, Donnell’s industry-leading, full-service catalog and timely service give dentists more options for meeting their patients’ diverse needs.

Donnell’s model for function is a collaborative yet systemized approach, ensuring that restorations seat fast and take up less chair time in your office.

By using FDA-compliant materials, we offer peace of mind to dentists and patients alike. We are proud to have established and enforced a quality system to ensure that the restorations we manufacture exceed our clients’ expectations.
