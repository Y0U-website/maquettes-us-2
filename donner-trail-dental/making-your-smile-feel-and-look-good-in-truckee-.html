Making Your Smile Feel and Look Good in Truckee and Tahoe City

14 Dec Making Your Smile Feel and Look Good in Truckee and Tahoe City

Many people work hard to maintain their health and appearance by exercising and trying to eat right. Another fantastic way to maintain health and build self-confidence is to invest in your smile. At North Tahoe Dental, Dr. Fligor DDS has helped hundreds of people both feel and look good in their smile.

For some people, their smile is simply improved through our in-office teeth whitening and Invisalign invisible braces to straighten a few crooked teeth. Other patients may require removing old, discolored, and/or leaking fillings to achieve the desired results. The most dramatic improvements come from a comprehensive approach that may use porcelain crowns and veneers to completely change the look and shape of the teeth. Porcelain restorations not only look good, but they can also strengthen teeth. A comprehensive rehabilitation will also address proper function and promote jaw joint health.

If you are concerned about the appearance and function of your smile for either social or professional reasons, contact us at North Tahoe Dental for a comprehensive dental analysis to review your options. With your goals and oral health at hand, we’ll help you feel confidence and relief through enhancing your smile with advanced technology, quality materials, and exceptional care.
