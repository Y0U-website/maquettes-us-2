Accessibility

Accessibility measures

The website uses semantic headings and landmarks, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus indicators, text alternatives for meaningful images, labeled form controls, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, and automated accessibility testing.

Automated checks are supplemented by manual review because no automated tool can establish complete conformance.

Current review status

Chromium, Firefox, WebKit, Android Chrome emulation, iOS Safari emulation, and Microsoft Edge are included in the automated compatibility plan. A physical Android review is recorded. Broader physical-device, assistive-technology, and supported-PDF application reviews remain ongoing, and this page does not claim complete conformance.

Documents and third-party destinations

Some patient documents are provided as PDFs and may be opened by the visitor’s browser or PDF application. External directions and other third-party destinations are controlled by their respective providers.

Report an accessibility barrier

Call the office at (843) 525-6866 and describe the page, control, or document that is difficult to use.

Do not include symptoms, diagnoses, medications, insurance identifiers, or other protected health information in a general website message.
