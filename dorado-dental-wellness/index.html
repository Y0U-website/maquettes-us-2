The practice

My work integrates functional medicine, airway-focused orthodontics, biologic dentistry, and whole-body wellness, guided by science, education, and longevity research.

I believe dentistry should be calm, compassionate, science-driven, and deeply personalized. Every patient deserves to feel safe, understood, and supported on their path to long-term health.

My mission is simple: healthy smiles, empowered individuals and families, and a community that lives longer and better.
