Our Philosophy

  • Traditional Dentists: They primarily focus on diagnosing and treating dental issues such as cavities, gum disease, and tooth decay, often without considering the broader health context.

Materials and Methods:

  • We use biocompatible, non-toxic materials for dental work, avoiding substances like mercury amalgam fillings. We employ hydroxyapatite both in the clinic and at home, and we recommend treatment methods such as safe mercury amalgam removal treatment, ozone therapy, and herbal remedies.
  • Traditional Dentists: They commonly use conventional materials and methods, including amalgam fillings, fluoride treatments, and standard pharmaceuticals.

Preventive Care:

  • We emphasize prevention through education about nutrition, sleep, breathing, hydration, supplementation, oral hygiene, and lifestyle habits that support dental and overall health.
  • Traditional Dentists: While prevention is also important, the focus is more on regular cleanings, check-ups, and the use of fluoride.

Treatment Philosophy:

  • As often as possible, we use a minimally invasive approach, aiming to preserve as much of the natural tooth structure as possible. We also integrate other health practices and work alongside other healthcare providers to ensure a coordinated approach to health. These other providers include functional medicine practitioners, nutritionists, naturopaths, myofunctional and speech therapists, chiropractors, otorhinolaryngologists, psychiatrists, among others.
  • Traditional Dentists: The treatment approach may be more interventionist or "sick care"-centric, involving procedures like drilling and filling cavities, root canals, and extractions when necessary and giving the patient little insight into how their dental health may relate to other chronic conditions from which they suffer.

Patient-Centered Care:

By choosing a holistic and functional dentist, patients can expect a dental care approach that looks beyond the immediate dental problem to consider long-term health and wellness. This approach is especially beneficial for those who are sensitive to conventional dental materials or who prefer natural and integrative health practices.

  • We focus on creating a personalized treatment plan that considers the patient’s unique health history, needs, and goals. We often spend more time with each patient to understand their overall health and wellness.
  • Traditional Dentists: The care is often standardized, with a primary focus on treating specific dental issues rather than a holistic assessment of the patient’s health.
