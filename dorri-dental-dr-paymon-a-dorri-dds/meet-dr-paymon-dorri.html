Personal Background & Interests

I was born and raised in Los Angeles, California, where my beloved wife, Shiva, and I began our life together before moving to Michigan to pursue my dental education and career. Today, we are blessed to be raising our three wonderful daughters here in the Midwest—a place we have come to love for its welcoming spirit, sense of community, and the beauty of its seasons.

My journey in dentistry has been shaped by a passion for service. From providing care to underserved populations through nonprofit work to building lasting relationships with families in our community, I see every patient interaction as an opportunity to give back and make a positive impact.

I am grateful every day for the opportunity to do what I love—and for the privilege of caring for each person who walks through our doors.

I want to thank each of you—our patients, our community, and our dedicated team—for the trust and kindness you have extended to my family and me. I look forward to walking this path with you, continuing the legacy of excellence established here, and helping you achieve a lifetime of oral fitness and wellness.
