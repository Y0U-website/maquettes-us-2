Our Practice

Thank you for using DosSantos Family Dentistry. We are a husband and wife team with practices located in Huntington, NY and Port Jefferson Station, NY.

With every patient, the goal of our dental practice is to provide exceptional cosmetic, restorative, and implant dentistry in state of the art, modern facilities.

You will receive personalized, quality treatment in a gentle and caring environment.

We take the time to answer all of your questions and work hard to build a relationship based on trust and respect.

Our ultimate goal is to make you a part of our dental family that we have proudly built on Long Island for the past 18 years.
