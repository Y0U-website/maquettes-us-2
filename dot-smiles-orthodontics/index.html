Get started on treatment without taking a trip to the practice! SmileSnap is an online assessment software that allows you to send us your treatment preferences and pictures of your smile. Prefer an in-office exam? Request one here .

Did you know that Dot Smiles offers virtual consultations? Using SmileSnap, we can review your smile and recommend a treatment option that is right for you, all while offering the same great care you expect from a top Boston orthodontist! With virtual consultations, there is no need to take time off of work or school, find childcare, or deal with traffic in order to meet with your orthodontist.…

We began offering SmileSnap in accordance with social distancing guidelines during 2020 but quickly discovered that some patients love the option to skip the trip–pandemic or not. We’re still quite happy to welcome patients into our office, and we’re also proud to offer this high-tech convenience!

It’s so easy! Click here to get started with SmileSnap, and the widget will walk you through all of the steps. See below for tips on getting good, clear photos that we can easily work from.

Check out the video to learn how to take clear, beautiful photos of your teeth from the angles that best help us to assess your smile. You will need to take five different photos of your smile to help us prepare the best treatment plan for your unique orthodontic goals.

SmileSnap is both convenient and super easy to use! Plus, it’s endorsed by the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO), so you can feel confident in using it.
