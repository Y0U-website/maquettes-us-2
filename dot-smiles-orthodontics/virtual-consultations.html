SmileSnap is both convenient and super easy to use! Plus, it’s endorsed by the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO), so you can feel confident in using it.

  • Have a friend take the photos (although selfies work, too).
  • Use a flash when taking photos, or make sure you are in an area with good lighting.
  • Capture your mouth only. Make sure you are close enough to your mouth to get a clear shot.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?

The path towards your new smile begins after you send in the five photos of your teeth! There then four simple steps to achieve your perfect smile. These include:

PHOTO REVIEW

Our team will review the pictures that you sent us, and get back to you with our treatment recommendations within three business days.

RECOMMENDATIONS

Based on Dr. Joffre’s assessment of the photos you provided to us, we will recommend a treatment that will work for you, as well as an estimate on treatment length and costs.

SCHEDULING

If you choose to proceed with the recommended treatment, we can schedule an in-person appointment at a time that works for you. We understand that you have a busy schedule, and we will do everything we can to accommodate you.

TREATMENT

At your initial appointment, we will take X-rays, photos, and scans of your teeth. You will also have the opportunity to discuss and settle on your treatment plans with Dr. Joffre. Please, do not hesitate to ask any questions that you may have during this time. Our team wants you to feel informed about your decision, as well as excited about your new smile!

Interested in learning more about SmileSnap? Contact our office today for answers to any of your questions! We want to make sure that your SmileSnap experience goes as smoothly as possible, as it is meant to be a convenient and easy alternative to in-person consultations.
