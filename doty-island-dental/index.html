The practice

We recommend routine cleanings to prevent tooth decay and maintain a healhy, bright smile.

We can do most other procedures such as root canals, dentures, extractions and grafting in house. If not, we know great specialists to refer you to.

We are located on Green Bay Road in the Fox Cites in Northeast Wisconsin. We are near downtown Neenah and downtown Menasha.

At Doty Island Dental we believe that we do things a bit differently. We believe in treating the whole person, and this means treating your mouth as part of your overall health. We are discovering that many illnesses speak through your mouth and treating oral conditions can improve your overall health.

So What Does This Mean? It means that we need to take a systematic approach to oral health. It means that from children to adults our quality of life can be improved through education and preventative care. It means that we need to evaluate your mouth for conditions that can impact your heart, your children and your confidence.

We highly integrate technology to improve your experience. We offer digital scanning of your teeth, which allows us to take away less of your tooth, achieve a better fit and avoids uncomfortable, old-fashioned impressions. We use technology to help diagnose your teeth, and use the latest in technology to treat you in the least invasive most comfortable ways possible.
