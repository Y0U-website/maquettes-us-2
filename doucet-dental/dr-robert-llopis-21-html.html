Dr. Robert Llopis

Dr. Robert Llopis is the dentist and creator of Urgent Dental Care, a full service dental office caring for all general and family dental needs with a concentration on implants and extractions. Urgent Dental Care currently has locations in both Lafayette and Morgan City Louisiana.

Dr. Llopis then went on to teach at the Hospital de Escuela in Tegucigalpa, Honduras. Dr. Llopis moved his family back to Louisiana after his mother’s diagnosis with Alzheimer’s disease and after she went to be with the Lord, he made both he and his mother’s dream come true by opening Urgent Dental Care. Dr. Llopis and his family feel extremely blessed to be a part of the friendly, culturally rich community of Acadiana.

Dr. Llopis has been married for 19 years, has two children and the family is very active in the Lafayette community. Dr. Llopis is fluent in both English and Spanish and welcomes all to visit his dental facility.
