What Are Dental Implants?

Implants are considered the gold standard in tooth replacement because, unlike any other option, they replace both the root and tooth. A dental implant is the closest you can get to having a natural tooth again.

The implant relies on three parts to complete your new tooth:

Dr. Booth can also use dental implants to replace multiple missing teeth . Two to six implants can support a custom denture or a bridge to restore a few missing teeth and even an entire arch.

  • The Implant: This is a biocompatible post that a trusted local specialist places into your jawbone. The post naturally fuses and integrates with your bone over time, taking the place of your root.
  • The Abutment: This piece connects the implant to its restoration after it’s completely healed.
  • The Restoration: A custom porcelain crown is the finishing touch for your new tooth, and it’s designed to blend flawlessly into your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do dental implants require any special care? Dental implants are designed to function like natural teeth, and caring for them is just as straightforward. In most cases, maintaining your implant involves the same good habits you use for natural teeth daily brushing and flossing, regular dental checkups, and avoiding habits that could damage your teeth, like chewing on ice or biting your nails.

At Doug Booth, D.D.S. in Huntsville, we use state-of-the-art technology to ensure your dental implant is placed with precision and properly integrated with your jawbone. Once healed and restored with a custom crown, your implant should feel and function like a natural tooth.

While implants are not susceptible to decay, the surrounding gum tissue and bone still require healthy maintenance. Routine cleanings and checkups allow us to monitor your implant and surrounding structures using high-resolution imaging and digital diagnostics.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, dental implants can last many years, often a lifetime. We’ll provide personalized instructions to help you maintain your implant and overall oral health in optimal condition.

The Dental Implant Process

If you’re considering tooth implants in Huntsville, the first step is to schedule an evaluation with Dr. Booth to discuss your options. He uses x-rays, photos, 3-D imaging combined with a thorough exam to determine if dental implants are the right treatment for your needs.

If you move forward with implants, you’ll work with a trusted local specialist for implant placement. Dr. Booth will then oversee the healing process, which takes place over three to six months.

Dr. Booth will design and fabricate your implant restoration, and once it’s fully healed and integrated with your bone, he’ll permanently place it.

Dental Implants Technology

CBCT Cone Beam Technology

CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography) allows Dr. Booth to capture 3-D images of your teeth, jaw, nerves, and sinuses with remarkable clarity. This advanced imaging tool is a crucial component in planning complex procedures, such as dental implants, root canals, and TMJ treatments .

Unlike traditional x-rays, CBCT scans provide a comprehensive, three-dimensional view, enabling us to detect issues that might be missed in two-dimensional images. The process is quick, non-invasive, and performed right here in our Huntsville office.

With this modern image technology, we can diagnose with greater accuracy and plan treatments that are safer, more efficient, and tailored to your needs. It also helps us explain your condition more clearly, so you feel informed and confident about your care.
