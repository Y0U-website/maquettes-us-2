Meet Dr. Doug Booth

Dr. Booth is happiest when he sees a patient light up, especially someone who’s finally proud of their smile or no longer anxious about visiting the dentist. Whether he s easing a patient’s fears or celebrating a treatment milestone, he values every moment of connection. He also loves working with a team that shares his patient-first philosophy and makes coming to the office every day feel like second nature.

His love of science and desire to work hands-on with people drew him to dentistry. That same creativity and curiosity extend into his personal life; when he’s not at the office, you might find him fishing, playing tennis, reading, painting, or building something unique in his woodshop.

If life had taken a different path, Dr. Booth might have chased dreams of being a rock star, an NBA standout, or a bestselling author. While the stage, court, and writing desk had their appeal, he’s grateful that dentistry became his calling, and so are his patients.

From the moment you walk in, the atmosphere at Dr. Booth’s office is warm, welcoming, and genuinely hospitable it feels more like visiting friends than a dental appointment. Dr. Booth is an outstanding dentist who combines expertise with a kind, calming presence. The entire staff is friendly, professional, and clearly cares about you as a person, not just a patient. They go above and beyond to make sure you’re comfortable and informed every step of the way. I can’t recommend Dr. Booth and his team enough!
