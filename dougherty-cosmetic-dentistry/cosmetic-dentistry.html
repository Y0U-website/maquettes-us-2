We love porcelain veneers because of their versatility in correcting numerous smile imperfections, including stained, chipped, uneven, and gapped teeth, in just two appointments. Porcelain veneers are also durable and stain-resistant, ensuring your smile keeps looking bright for 10-plus years.

Custom Crowns

A dental crown is a tooth-shaped appliance that covers your entire tooth to enhance its function and appearance. Our office uses porcelain crowns to create a life-like and fully functioning smile. Like porcelain veneers, porcelain crowns are long-lasting and stain-resistant to help achieve durable aesthetics and oral function.

Crowns aren’t just for fixing broken teeth; rather they are where strength meets aesthetics. Crowns play a big role in cosmetic dentistry. Maybe you’re replacing old metal crowns or restoring a front tooth; either way, we use top-tier materials to match your smile’s natural look and feel.

Dental crowns are restorations that cover and protect damaged, weakened, or heavily restored teeth. They restore function, structure, and appearance, especially in cases where fillings or other methods are not sufficient. Our office offers three types of high-quality materials to suit different needs and preferences.

E.max, Zirconia, and Feldspathic Porcelain

E.max crowns are crafted from lithium disilicate ceramic, offering exceptional strength and natural translucency. They’re ideal for anterior (front) teeth where aesthetics matter most, but durable enough for posterior teeth in many cases.

Zirconia crowns are known for their unmatched strength and resistance to wear. They’re perfect for patients who grind their teeth or need long-lasting support in molars or back teeth. Zirconia also offers excellent biocompatibility, making it a smart choice for those with metal sensitivities.

Feldspathic porcelain crowns are hand-layered and artistically crafted to provide the most lifelike appearance. Often chosen for high-end smile designs, they deliver a delicate, translucent look that mimics natural enamel and is perfect for the most visible areas of the smile.

We’ll help you choose the right option based on your bite, location of the tooth, and cosmetic goals. All of our crowns are designed digitally for precision, fit, and longevity.

If you want to know which of these treatments and materials is right for you, call and schedule a consultation with our dental office today and get started on your personalized smile.

Invisalign

Invisalign aligners are removable, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods and maintain optimal oral hygiene. Each set of aligners is worn for about two weeks before switching to the next, and most treatment plans range from 6–18 months. The clear, nearly invisible design makes it a favorite for patients who want to improve their smile without drawing attention to the process.

We correct misaligned smiles in our office using this cosmetic treatment. Many patients love Invisalign because it’s discreet and convenient. Besides, it’s usually faster than traditional braces for most cases when used as directed by Dr. Richard D’Avanzo.

If you’ve been putting off straightening your teeth because braces aren’t appealing, Invisalign may be the answer. This personalized aligner system gently moves teeth into better alignment, without brackets, wires, or frequent office visits.

  • Bite misalignment
  • Shifting teeth
