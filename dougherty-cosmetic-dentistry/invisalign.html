What is Invisalign? Invisalign is an alternative to traditional metal braces that uses a series of clear aligners (also called trays) to gently move teeth into their correct position. The aligners are made of a clear plastic material, which makes them virtually invisible! The trays fit snugly over your teeth, so no one will ever know you’re wearing them.

Invisalign is the most sophisticated clear aligner system on the market. Patients should accept no substitute. In the skilled hands of an experienced orthodontist, we can achieve the results you are looking for.

If you have any of these dental or cosmetic issues then Invisalign might be the right choice for your smile.

  • Crooked Teeth: Teeth that are turned or misaligned.
  • Crowded Teeth: Not enough space in the mouth, causing teeth to overlap.
  • Gaps Between Teeth: Spaces between teeth that can affect your bite or appearance.
  • Overbite: Top front teeth that sit over the bottom teeth.
  • Underbite: Bottom front teeth placement juts out over the top teeth.
  • Crossbite: Upper and lower jaws do not line up.
  • Open Bite: Upper front teeth and bottom front teeth don’t meet at all.

Risks of Delaying Invisalign Treatment

Putting off orthodontic treatment may seem harmless, but misaligned teeth can lead to long-term oral health issues, including:

  • Increased Wear and Tear: Uneven bites put extra stress on certain teeth, leading to chips, cracks, or premature wear.
  • Jaw Pain and TMJ Problems: Bite misalignment can strain jaw joints and muscles, causing discomfort or headaches.
  • Higher Risk of Gum Disease: Crowded or overlapping teeth are harder to clean, allowing plaque and bacteria to accumulate.
  • Tooth Shifting: Gaps or crowding often worsen with time, making future treatment more complex.
  • Reduced Confidence: Misaligned teeth may affect the way you smile, speak, and feel in social or professional settings.
