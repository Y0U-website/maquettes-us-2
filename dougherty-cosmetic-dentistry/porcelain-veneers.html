Play Experience the Difference of Prepless and Minimal Prep Veneers On top of traditional veneers, we also offer prepless and minimal prep veneers. Prepless veneers are similar to porcelain veneers because they are shells bonded to the front of your teeth to improve their appearance, but they are ultra-thin and require less reshaping of the enamel. They often help patients achieve a more natural look.

Prepless and minimal prep veneers require very slight shaping of the tooth surface, typically less than 0.3mm. This approach maintains the integrity of your natural teeth while still providing beautiful, lasting results.

Benefits of Prepless and Minimal Prep Veneers

Choosing a more conservative veneer option offers a number of advantages, such as:

No-prep veneers can usually be done faster and with less discomfort than traditional veneers.

  • Preservation of Natural Tooth Structure: Your natural teeth remain largely untouched underneath.
  • No Need for Anesthesia: In most cases, these veneers can be placed without numbing.
  • Reversible in Some Cases: Because there’s minimal change to your tooth structure, certain prepless veneer cases may be reversible.
  • Natural Aesthetics: Modern materials closely mimic the translucency and luster of natural enamel, and they can even cover the darkest of stains.
  • Durable and Long-Lasting: With proper care, your veneers can last 10 years or more.
  • Stain-Resistant: Veneers resist coffee and tea stains, as well as cigarette smoke.

What to Expect During Treatment

This entire process takes place in-house at our advanced dental facility, allowing for quick turnaround and precise control over every detail. Our goal is to make your veneer journey smooth, personalized, and predictable, so you can enjoy your new smile with confidence as soon as possible.

Veneers aren’t about a quick fix. It’s about designing a smile that’s as remarkable as the life you’re living.

1. Consultation and Smile Evaluation:

Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation where we listen to your smile goals and cosmetic desires. We assess the current condition of your teeth, gums, and bite, taking into account not only aesthetics but also function and airway health. During this evaluation, we discuss all veneer options available to you, including prepless veneers, minimal-prep veneers, traditional porcelain veneers, or a combination tailored to your unique smile. This ensures your treatment plan respects your oral health and provides the most natural, comfortable, and durable outcome.

2. Impressions and Smile Design:

Next, we capture highly detailed digital impressions using our state-of-the-art iTero scanner, along with photographs of your teeth and face. These images serve as the foundation for designing veneers that complement your facial structure, skin tone, and smile line. Using advanced software and artistic expertise, we map out the ideal size, shape, and shade of each veneer. This digital planning allows us to preview your potential results and make any adjustments before any work begins, giving you confidence that your new smile will be on par with what you envisioned.

3. Prep:

For traditional porcelain veneers, a very small amount of enamel, usually less than a millimeter, is gently removed from the front surface of your teeth. This creates the necessary space for the veneer to fit seamlessly without adding bulk or altering your bite. Many patients find this process comfortable, often requiring only local anesthesia. Alternatively, if you are a candidate for prepless or no-prep veneers, this step may be completely skipped, preserving your natural enamel while still achieving a remarkable cosmetic improvement.

4. Bonding:

Once your veneers are custom-crafted and ready, the next step is bonding. This critical phase involves carefully cleaning and sanding your tooth’s surface to create a strong foundation. Then, using a specially formulated adhesive resin, the porcelain veneer is precisely positioned and secured to your tooth. A curing light is applied to harden the resin quickly and create a durable, lasting bond. This technique restores your tooth’s natural function while enhancing its appearance, giving you a smile that looks and feels natural.

5. Final Results:

After bonding, we thoroughly check your new veneers for fit, bite, and aesthetics. Any minor adjustments needed to ensure comfort and a flawless look are made at this time. We want you to feel completely confident in your smile, from the way it looks to the way it feels every time you bite or speak.

Our team will provide guidance on caring for your veneers to keep them beautiful and lasting for many years.
