Dr. Douglas D. Wright

Douglas Wright graduated from the University of Maryland Pharmacy School in 1981. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland in 1985. While in school he completed externship training at John Hopkins Hospital, the University of Maryland Hospital, and the National Institutes of Health, Baltimore Cancer Research Center.

Today, Dr. Wright has been working in dentistry for over 35 years! He has published articles in the Journal of Academy of General Dentistry, Navy Medicine Magazine, Implant Practice US, Proceedings of the Naval Institute and Dental Economics magazine. Dr. Wright and his wife Karen live in Harrisonburg, Virginia. They have three children named Nicholas, Julia, and Rosemary. Dr. Wright enjoys playing in a soft rock band, where he handles rhythm guitar and vocals. He also enjoys shooting sporting clays.

Get to Know The Dentist

Dr. Kennedy graduated from the University of California, San Francisco School of Dentistry in 1988. Prior to dental school, he served in the US Coast Guard as a hospital corpsman and attended the University of Florida for his undergraduate studies in microbiology.

Dr. Kennedy has been in private practice in Yuba City, California for 24 years, enjoying all facets of general dentistry and serving his patients from ages 2 to 99. His particular field of interest is advanced restorative techniques involving porcelain crowns and veneers. Cavity prevention through analysis of each patient’s unique risk factors, including bacterial “bio challenge”, is an important aspect of his treatment style.

Dr. Kennedy was past president and editor of the local Dental Society in California and received the California Dental Association Political and Grass Roots award for community water fluoridation, as well as the Distinguished Volunteer Award. Mike has enjoyed serving on a number of dental mission trips with his family, locally, as well as in Mexico and the Amazon Valley in Peru.

Dr. Kennedy’s wife, Pam, is originally from northern Virginia and they moved back home to Richmond in 2015. They have three grown children. Mike and Pam enjoy traveling, hiking, reading, and running. He has completed over 30 marathons including the Boston Marathon on three occasions, and 2 ultra marathons around Lake Tahoe.

Dr. Kennedy and Dr. Wright have worked together for over 10 years. Dr. Kennedy practices in our office about 6 days a month, the other days he’s teaching at VCU Dental School in Richmond, VA.
