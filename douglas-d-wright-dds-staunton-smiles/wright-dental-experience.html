Non-Invasive Sleep Apnea Relief

Sleep apnea doesn’t just disrupt your rest — it impacts your overall health and quality of life.

At Staunton Smiles, Dr. Kennedy offers custom dental appliances as a simple, non-surgical solution for managing obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) and snoring.

If CPAP isn’t working for you or you’ve never been diagnosed, we’re here to help .

Preventive Dentistry

Preventive dentistry helps maintain good oral health and wellness. It’s a combination of routine dental check-ups and developing good dental habits like brushing and flossing. The most effective way to ensure the health of your teeth and gums is to come in for your dental cleanings.

We offer routine dental cleanings as well as non-surgical periodontics. Our highly skilled Dental Hygienist, Brandy, is very thorough in accessing patient’s gum health to determine the type of dental cleaning needed.

General and Implant Dentistry

We offer general services such as, composite fillings, dental extractions, root canals and cosmetic procedures. With our on site CEREC Mill, we can design and customize dental restorations same day. This allows us to provide faster, high-quality results in fewer appointments.

Dental implants are changing the way people live! With them, people are rediscovering the comfort and confidence to eat, speak, laugh and enjoy life . If you’re missing teeth dental implants may be right for you! Dr. Wright uses this minimally invasive approach to help patients in Valley regain their smiles.

If, like many others, you feel implant dentistry is the choice for you, we’re here to help.
