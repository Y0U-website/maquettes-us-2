About Us

  • Intraoral Cameras: Get a clear, real-time view of what we see. Intraoral cameras allow us to walk you through your treatment plan with full visual transparency.
  • Transillumination Cavity Detection (CariVu™): Safe, radiation-free technology that uses gentle light to identify hidden decay and cracks before they become larger issues.
  • Digital X-Rays: Fast, high-resolution imaging that produces up to 80 % less radiation than traditional film X-rays for safe and precise diagnostics.
  • AI-Supported Diagnostics: Advanced artificial intelligence tools that assist our clinical team in analyzing images for early detection and enhanced diagnostic accuracy.
  • Digital Workflow for Full Mouth Implants: Comprehensive digital planning for complex full-mouth implant treatments, ensuring optimal placement, function, and aesthetics.
  • Digitally Supported Custom Implant Restorations: Custom-crafted crowns and prosthetics designed using digital precision for a perfect fit and natural look.
  • High-End Dental Laboratory Support: We partner with top-tier dental laboratories using premium materials to craft durable, beautifully natural restorations.
  • Recently Updated Operatories: Fully modern treatment suites equipped with the latest dental chairs and ergonomic tools designed for your maximum comfort and safety.

What Sets Us Apart

It is very important to us to create and sustain an office environment that is at once welcoming and comfortable. We utilize modern and trusted systems and equipment for the safe and expert delivery of dental care. You should know that we are compliant with the state of the art mandates for sterilization and disinfection control practices. In fact, we surpass those standards with our system of waterline disinfection and hazardous wast e and recycling operations.

Our digital records are easily retrievable and transferrable between our office and referring dentists, while edging us closer and closer to a paperless, environmentally friendly administrative practice.

We maintain a hipaa compliant practice, ensuring that your privacy and protection against identity theft are safe and secure. The use of digital xray equipment, both intraoral and panoramic, provides for the minimum of radiation exposure while using no hazardous chemicals.

The use of intraoral cameras heightens the patient experience and enables us to share treatment plans and successes in vivid digital color.

Resin bonded restorations are used to provide the most conservative reduction of tooth surfaces to minimize current and future trauma while providing lifelike, tooth shaded restorations.

Ultrasonic scalers, prophy jets and surface anesthetics contribute to comfortable, thorough cleaning visits.

All manner and form of implant restorations, crown and bridgework, complete and partial dentures and basic restorative services are our expertise. Thorough, complete oral exams, basic periodontaltherapies and preventive services are also the skills we offer for our patients.
