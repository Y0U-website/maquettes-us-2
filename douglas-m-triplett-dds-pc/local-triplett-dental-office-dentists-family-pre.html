Oral Hygiene Cleanings & Cancer Screenings

Regular oral hygiene cleanings help keep your teeth clean and it helps us detect potential problems sooner. During our semi-annual cleanings, we not only sterilize your mouth, and address any of your concerns, but we also provide comprehensive examinations, including; x-rays and cancer screenings.

Periodontal (Gum) Disease

Periodontal, or gum, disease is when plaque, tartar, and bacteria build-up around your gums and teeth. This build-up causes your teeth and jawbone to deteriorate, which leads to decreased support. We offer periodontal exams, treatment, and prevention.

Digital X-Rays

Our preventative care goes beyond cleaning the surface level. Digital x-rays are a detailed way to analyze your entire mouth. We offer in-office x-rays for comfortable, timely, and accurate results to help us achieve whole mouth health.

Mouth Guards

Mouth guards, also known as mouth protectors, sports guards, and many other names, are designed to proactively help protect your mouth, teeth, and jaw from injuries. We offer custom mouth guard fittings and creations to ensure a comfortable and protective solution.

Night & Snore Guards

Night and snore mouthguards are custom fitted dental devices used to enhance sleep and protect your teeth. Some people need a night guards for teeth grinding, others need one for snoring. Either way, our team is here to help you find a solution.
