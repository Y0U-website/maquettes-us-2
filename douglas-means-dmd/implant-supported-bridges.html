The surgical process to place the implants may be done in the office or in a hospital setting depending on several factors. Once all steps have been taken, which can take a few appointments to complete, the dentist will monitor your healing process to ensure the bridge seats properly and does not need to be adjusted.

  • Impressions will be taken to measure the proper placement and size of the bridge
  • The bridge will be created to match the rest of your smile
  • Implant surgery will be performed to place the anchoring mechanisms in the jaw bone
  • The bridge will be installed on the implants
