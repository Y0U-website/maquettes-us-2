Dr. McArthur majored in Spanish, minored in Chemistry, with an unofficial emphasis in outdoor recreation at BYU. He returned to his home state to complete his doctor degree at West Virginia School of Dentistry. To further his education and serve his country, he joined the US Army and ran a remote clinic during his deployment to Afghanistan. He has over ten years of dental experience.
