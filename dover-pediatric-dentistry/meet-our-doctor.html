Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Nadarajah Ganeshkumar , fondly referred to as “Dr. Ganesh,” is a graduate of the Boston University School of Dental Medicine’s Pediatric program and also holds MSc and PhD degrees in microbiology from the University of British Columbia in Vancouver, Canada. Prior to owning Dover Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Ganesh was the Division Chief of Pediatric Dentistry at Nemours/ A.I. Dupont Hospital.
