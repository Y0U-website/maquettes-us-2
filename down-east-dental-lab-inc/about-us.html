About us

Down East Dental Lab opened for business in April 1984 as a one man removable laboratory. Since then, we ve grown to a 3 technician laboratory with over 80 years experience. We are very proud to be a family owned and operated business.

It is our desire to provide our clients with the highest quality restorations possible with the utmost respect & customer service available. We take great pride in being family operated, and strive to treat those we do business with as part of our family as well.

Mike Grubb began his career in dental laboratory technology in 1971. He spent 13 years learning the trade prior to opening Down East Dental Lab in 1984. He specializes in both complete and partial removable dental prosthetics, implant removable prosthetics, flexible partial restorations, and many other applications.

In 2004, after graduating from East Carolina University, Michael Grubb began his career in dental laboratory technology. Michael worked off and on at the laboratory through high school and college, as time allowed. In 2008, he earned recognition as a Certified Dental Technician (CDT). Michael specializes in complete and partial removable dental prosthetics, as well as flexible partial restorations.
