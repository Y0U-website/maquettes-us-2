Dr. Chad

Dr. Jackson’s goal is to set up his patients for a lifetime of healthy smiles through prevention of dental disease. He believes in using current technology and evidence based dentistry to provide excellent care and deliver a world-class dental experience. When Dr. Jackson is not helping create healthy smiles, he likes to live an active lifestyle. His hobbies include fitness, basketball, and spending time with his wife, Brittaney, and their dog, Scooter.

He enjoys an active lifestyle with his family. His weekends are full of home improvement projects, tinkering with computers, spending time on the water and camping. He is a member of Pima Dental Study Club, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association and the Southern Arizona Dental Society.
