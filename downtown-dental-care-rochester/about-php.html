About Dr. Rainville

Dr. Rainville was born and raised in a small town located in Southwestern Montana. With the beautiful Rocky Mountains surrounding the ranching and logging community of Deer Lodge, Dr. Rainville developed --- early on --- a love for the outdoors . When he is not seeing patients, he enjoys hiking, biking, tending to his yard, and downhill alpine skiing . He and his wife have raised their son here in Rochester along with their energetic miniature schnauzer, Ollie.

"I have personally assembled a consistent, long-term support team of professionals who are experienced, knowledgeable, and conscientiously focused on providing our patients with an unparalleled degree of calm professionalism, warmth, dignity, and peace of mind for their dental treatment. I genuinely appreciate the trust my patients place in me as well as in my support team...and together, we are committed to doing all we can to merit such a precious privilege."
