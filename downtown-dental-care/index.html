The practice

Dr. Stafford worked as an associate dentist for three years. In October 2008, he became owner operator of Downtown Dental Care .

Dr. Caleb Crow is a dedicated general dentist with a passion for providing high-quality, compassionate care tailored to each patient’s unique needs. A Conway native, Dr. Crow graduated from Ouachita Baptist University in 2021 and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Tennessee in 2025.

Dr. Crow takes pride in helping patients feel comfortable and confident in their dental care. His clinical interests include preventive dentistry, restorative procedures, and guiding patients toward healthy, lasting smiles through a gentle and detail-oriented approach.

Outside of the office, Dr. Crow enjoys spending time outdoors with his wife and their dog, camping, going to the gym, and game nights. He’s also an avid traveler and big fan of the Arkansas Razorbacks.

Dr. Crow looks forward to building lasting relationships with his patients and serving the community with integrity and care.
