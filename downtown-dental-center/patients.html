What to Expect on Your First Visit

Here at Downtown Dental Center, we pride ourselves on taking all the anxiety and discomfort away from your dental experiences. When you call us for the first time, we will talk through what you need and set up an appointment that works for you.

When you visit, we ask you to bring important pieces of information with you, like your dental records, recent x-rays, dental insurance card, your patient forms (available to print on this page), and your photo ID. With this information, we can ensure that you get care that takes your specific requirements into account. After we sit down and talk to you about your needs and examine your information and the state of your teeth in person, we can discuss a treatment plan that makes sense for your schedule and budget.

Whether you need simple and quick services like teeth whitening or something as complex as root canal therapy, we will make sure you have the knowledge you need to feel comfortable and make an informed decision for your health.
