What are Dental Bridges?

Another tooth replacement option is a dental bridge. This generally involves one or two abutments placed over healthy teeth to hold a pontic in place. A pontic is a false tooth or row of teeth that bridges the gap where a tooth or teeth are missing.

Should I Choose an Implant or a Bridge?

Whether you get an implant or a bridge is based on a few different factors. One of the most important factors is whether or not your jawbone can support a dental implant. The process of placing the titanium rod involves drilling a screw into the jawbone, and if the jawbone has suffered too much bone loss or is otherwise not able to support the rod, then a dental implant is not an option. If this is the case, bridges may be the best alternative.

Another consideration is cost. While dental implants generally last longer, they are more expensive upfront. How long an implant will last when compared with a dental bridge should be taken into consideration when considering the costs of the procedures.

Speak with your dentist about which option would be best for you and for your budget.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Implants can last a long time, especially if well cared for. They can actually last decades! It’s important to have the implant placed by a quality dentist, though, to ensure they are installed correctly.

How Do I Ensure the Health of My Implant?

One of the main factors when it comes to the longevity of an implant is how a patient cares for the implant. A dental implant should be treated like a natural tooth, and should be properly brushed and flossed. This will help to ensure the health of the gums and the surrounding teeth.

Make sure to brush, floss, and rinse out your mouth at least twice a day to ensure proper overall oral health.

In order to ensure the longevity of your implants, it’s important to allow them to heal properly once they’ve been installed. This includes eating a softer diet for the first couple of weeks, then slowly introducing denser foods. It will take your jawbone time to acclimate to the new force being applied to it.

If a tooth or teeth are missing as a result of a sports injury, it’s extremely important to have a proper, secure mouth guard fitted. From there, make sure to wear it when playing (and even practicing) the sports you enjoy.

Many patients find a dental implant to be a wonderful long-term solution for the replacement of missing teeth. If you have a missing tooth or are in need of an extraction, speak with your dentist about a dental implant as a replacement option.
