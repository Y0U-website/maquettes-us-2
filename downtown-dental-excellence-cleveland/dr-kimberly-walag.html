Dr. Kimberly Walag D.D.S.

Dr. Kimberly Walag was born and raised in the Midwest, where she attended Indiana University in Bloomington and completed a B.S. in Biology in 2010. Following undergrad, she moved to Tampa, Florida, to complete her Master’s degree in Medical Sciences at the University of South Florida Morsani College of Medicine. In 2015, Dr. Walag returned to Indianapolis, Indiana, where she completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at Indiana University School of Dentistry. While in dental school, she was actively involved in the American Student Dental Association at local and national levels.

In her free time, she enjoys exploring new restaurants in Houston with her husband, Dan, and hanging out with their rescue pup, Nico. If you have any good restaurant recommendations, she would love to hear them!
