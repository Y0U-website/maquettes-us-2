Dr. Lanette Sikes - Dentist in Cleveland, TX

Dr. Lanette Sikes D.D.S.

Experience and Professional Dedication

Dr. Lanette Sikes brings over 25 years of experience in general dentistry to her practice, Downtown Dental Excellence. A 1999 graduate of the University of Texas Dental Branch in Houston, Dr. Sikes has dedicated herself to ongoing education, completing over 500 hours of continuing education courses and earning the prestigious Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry award. She is a past president of the American Association of Women Dentists and an active member of the ADA, TDA, and GHDS.

Holistic Approach to Patient Care

Commitment to Excellence and Innovation

Driven by a passion for her profession, Dr. Sikes stays current with the latest advancements in dentistry through continuing education and by embracing new technologies. She is particularly interested in restorative and implant dentistry, finding great satisfaction in creating beautiful smiles and restoring her patients’ ability to eat and enjoy life to the fullest.

A Patient-Focused Practice

Dr. Sikes is proud to lead a compassionate and dedicated team at Downtown Dental Excellence. She believes in building lasting relationships with her patients, fostering a welcoming and supportive environment where patients feel comfortable and confident in their care. Her commitment to patient-focused dentistry extends to the community, where she provides free or discounted dental work for those in need.
