What to Expect: Your Dental Implant Journey

Step 1: Consultation & Planning

We begin with a detailed exam, digital imaging, and a discussion of your goals. If bone loss is present, we’ll discuss bone grafting or alternative solutions.

Step 2: Implant Placement

The titanium post is placed in the jawbone under local anesthesia. Most patients report minimal discomfort.

Step 3: Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next few months, your implant naturally fuses with the bone, creating a stable foundation.

Step 4: Restoration

Once healed, we place your custom-designed crown, bridge, or denture — matched in color and shape for a seamless look.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last 20+ years , often for life. The crown may need replacement after 10–15 years due to wear.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort , thanks to local anesthesia and Dr. Schuldt’s gentle technique. Sedation options are also available for anxious patients.

How much do dental implants cost in Grand Island, NE?

Costs vary based on the number of implants, the need for bone grafting, and restoration type. We’ll provide a detailed estimate at your consultation.

Will I need a bone graft?

If you’ve been missing teeth for a long time, bone density may be low. Bone grafting helps build a stable base for your implant.

Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Some insurance plans contribute to implant-related costs, especially for the crown or denture portion. Our team will help you maximize your benefits.
