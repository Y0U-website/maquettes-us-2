Dr. Kenna & Dr. Martin combine advanced training with a passion for patient care. They take the time to listen, explain your options and create personalized treatment plans for their patients.

Dr. Trey Kenna DMD, Doctor of Dental Medicine

Dr. Kenna attended the Medical College of Georgia earning a Doctor of Dental Medicine and completing a General Practice Residency (GPR). Within the GPR, Dr. Kenna received additional training in dental implant placement, restorative dentistry, and IV sedation.

Dr. Matt Martin, DMD, Doctor of Dental Medicine

Dr. Martin attended the Medical College of Georgia earning a Doctor of Dental Medicine in 2013. Dr. Martin has been in private practice since then. Dr. Martin has active memberships with the South Carolina Dental Association and the American Dental Association.
