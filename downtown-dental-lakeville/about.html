Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Brian Harrison

Dr. Harrison comes from a long line of dentists, but he&#x27;ll be the first to tell you he didn&#x27;t always plan to carry on the family tradition. In fact, as a child, he once told his father (also a dentist) that dentistry was "boring". That changed quickly after hands-on experience in healthcare, including time as an EMT, revealed how dentistry can blend science, art, human connection, and hands-on problem solving in a way that fuels him. Dentistry is in his blood afterall.

Dr. Harrison earned his Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry and his Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Minnesota. With a background that stretches beyond the dental chair, Dr. Harrison brings a broader perspective to patient care. His focus is not just on teeth, but the full experience of health, trust, and comfort.

For Dr. Harrison, dentistry is equal parts precision and genuine connection. Whether it&#x27;s restoring function, aesthetics, or confidence, he&#x27;s most inspired when patients leave his office feeling more like themselves.

Beyond technical skills, Dr. Harrison believes in a philosophical approach to dentistry built on three core principles: First, knowledge empowers patients to make informed decisions about their care. Second, quality dentistry is an essential health investment rather than a quick fix. Third, treatment planning should be collaborative, putting patients in control while providing expert guidance.

When he&#x27;s not crafting beautiful smiles, you&#x27;ll find Dr. Harrison teeing off on the golf course with his wife and toddler. When the weather isn&#x27;t cooperating, he&#x27;s searching for the perfect wine pairing to go alonside a new recipe in the kitchen, reading a great book, or playing with his puppy that is no longer a puppy, but always will be to him.

Life is too short to not indulge in the things that make you smile, on and off the clock!

Your First Visit Experience

Your first appointment at Downtown Dental sets the foundation for our partnership in your oral health journey. Unlike traditional dental visits that rush through procedures, we take time to understand your history, goals, and concerns. You&#x27;ll leave not just with cleaner teeth, but with a clear understanding of your current oral health and a collaborative plan for the future.

Modern Solutions for Better Dental Health

While our historic brick building in downtown Lakeville maintains its charm, we&#x27;re committed to providing modern dental solutions.

Digital X-Rays

Our digital radiography system delivers detailed images with significantly less radiation than traditional film X-rays. This technology allows for immediate viewing, better diagnosis, and more informed treatment discussions with patients.

Detailed Photography

Our dental photography system captures high-resolution images of your teeth and gums, providing a clear visual record of your oral health. These photos are invaluable for monitoring changes over time, enhancing treatment planning, and improving patient communication by helping you see exactly what your dentist sees.
