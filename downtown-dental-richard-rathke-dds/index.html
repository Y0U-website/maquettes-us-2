The practice

Dr. Rathke has a highly skilled mix of experience, expertise, and compassion that his patients appreciate. We take pride in our consistent and reliable exceptional dental care coupled with gentle and kind patient service.

Dental anxiety is a common fear among patients who have had a bad experience as a child or with a different dentist. At Downtown Dental, our patients have come to expect our kind and gentle manner. We work hard to make you feel comfortable and secure during each dental visit; in fact, you may find yourself looking forward to visiting with us!

Everyone is busy these days, so we are open during the lunch hour to accommodate our working patients. We try to make your time with us as efficient as possible, which is why we are pleased to offer some of the latest in dental technology. Not only does it enable us to make more accurate diagnoses, but it often means less time in the dental chair.

We are also available to treat dental emergencies , whether or not you are a patient of record. We know that dental emergencies can strike at any time even on vacation! Don’t try to wait until you fly back to treat your dental pain; the pain will only get worse and may ruin your vacation.

Frequently, we can see you on the same day you call us. We know that dental emergencies can make people feel stressed out and unsure of how to proceed. Depending on the type of dental emergency you have, we can provide tips on how to take action and minimize your discomfort while preventing further damage.

Depending on how many teeth we replace, Dr. Rathke will attach a crown, bridge, or denture to the implant so you can chew on the tooth again. Since we craft beautiful, high-quality tooth restorations, you can expect yours to look natural and blend seamlessly…

The implant post we place in your jaw is made of medical-grade titanium and replaces the lost tooth root. This is crucial because when a tooth is lost, bone in the jaw around it immediately starts to break down.
