Warm, Personalized Dentistry In Downtown Fallbrook, CA

From preventive visits to advanced restorative care, Downtown Dental Studio offers comprehensive dentistry for the whole family in one convenient Fallbrook location.

The practice

At Downtown Dental Studio, Dr. Richard J Gill and our team provide warm, judgment-free dental care in the heart of Fallbrook. We take the time to listen, explain your options clearly, and help you feel comfortable every step of the way.

Whether you are here for a routine cleaning, a dental emergency, or a complete smile transformation, our goal is to make your visit feel calm, personalized, and easy to understand.

Personalized Treatment Plans: Your smile, goals, and budget matter. We tailor care to what you actually need no one-size-fits-all plans.

Transparent Pricing: Know your costs upfront, with VIP Memberships, financing, & private pay options available.

Gentle, Judgment-Free Visits: Nervous about the dentist? You’re not alone. We provide a calm, welcoming space where you feel comfortable and in control.

Same-Day Emergency Care: Dental pain shouldn’t wait. We offer same-day emergency appointments whenever possible.

Whether you have dental insurance, need financing, or want a simple membership plan, Downtown Dental Studio offers options to help you plan for your care with confidence.

Insurance Info We work with many PPO dental insurance plans and can help verify your benefits, review estimated coverage, and explain any out-of-pocket costs before treatment.

Les soins

General Dentistry Routine exams, cleanings, X-rays, fillings, gum care, and preventive treatments to keep your smile healthy.

Crowns & Root Canals Restorative care to repair damaged teeth, relieve pain, and protect your natural smile whenever possible.

Dental Implants Strong, natural-looking tooth replacement options for one missing tooth, several teeth, or a full smile.

Invisalign & Clear Aligners Clear aligners to straighten your teeth comfortably and discreetly without traditional braces.

Cosmetic Dentistry Whitening, veneers, bonding, and personalized smile enhancements designed around your goals.
