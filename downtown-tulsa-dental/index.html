The practice

Now located in the historic Chamber of Commerce building at 616 S Boston Ave, Downtown Tulsa Dental has a long-standing tradition of serving downtown Tulsa. Dr. Stevens is the fourth caretaker of a practice family that is more than 100 years old. We still have active patients in our practice with records from the 1950’s!

We provide comprehensive general dentistry services, including root canals, dental implants, initial TMJ and airway evaluation and treatment, and same day emergency care. Thanks to the use of modern technology, we are often able to provide treatments in a single appointment that traditionally take two or more visits to complete.
