The practice

Don’t wait for a small ache to become a major issue. Whether it’s a chipped tooth or sensitive gums, early intervention keeps your mouth healthy and your treatments simple.

Dr. Dylan Fogel brings a modern approach to care while continuing the trust this practice has built in Fond du Lac. He believes good dentistry starts with listening, clear communication, and making patients feel at ease.

Dr. Dylan earned his undergraduate degree from Lawrence University in Appleton, attended Northwestern University, and received his dental degree from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine in Illinois. After five years practicing in the Fox Valley, he and his wife Brianna were excited to put down roots in Fond du Lac and continue building a practice where patients feel informed…

From preventive visits to cosmetic and restorative treatment, we care for everyday needs and more complex concerns. You will understand your options, why they matter, and what the next step looks like.

Enhance the appearance of your smile with cosmetic dental treatments designed to improve color, shape, balance, and overall confidence while still keeping your results natural-looking.

When dental pain or an unexpected issue happens, prompt care matters. Emergency dental treatment helps address discomfort quickly and protect your oral health before the problem gets worse.

Oral cancer screenings are an important part of preventive care. Regular screenings help identify unusual changes early and support timely treatment when needed.

Biomimetic dentistry focuses on restoring teeth in a way that preserves as much healthy natural structure as possible while supporting long-term strength and function.
