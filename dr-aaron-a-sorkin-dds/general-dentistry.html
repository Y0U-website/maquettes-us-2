Frequently Asked Questions On Local Children’s Dentistry

What Is a General Dentist? A general dentist is a healthcare professional who manages your general oral health care at general dentistry centers. This includes diagnosing and treating various tooth and gum complaints.

When the What Do General Dentists Do? A general dentist offers you primary oral health care. Some of the most frequent procedures and services they perform are: Preventive services – general checkups for the entire family Restorative services – crowns, implants, bridges, dentures Cosmetic procedures – veneers, dental contouring, professional teeth whitening Overall health concerns – general practice dentistry specialists can answer any questions you have about your teeth and gums’ health

When Should I See A General Dentist? You should have preventive, general practice dentistry checkup appointments with your general dentist at least once every six months. Also, you should schedule an appointment as soon as you experience tooth and gum pain or other problems.

What Can You Tell Your General Dentist? You can discuss any concern about your oral health with your general dentist and ask them for tips for preventive care, including teaching you how to brush and floss your teeth correctly.
