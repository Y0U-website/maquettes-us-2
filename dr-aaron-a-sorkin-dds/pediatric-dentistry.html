Frequently Asked Questions On Local Children’s Dentistry

Why Choose A Kids Dentist? Pediatric dentistry focuses on special training, education, and providing a comfortable environment. A dentist has two to three additional years of special training to cater to the unique needs of children. Therefore, choosing the right local kids’ dentist to trust with the health and beauty of your child’s smile is essential.

What Is A Board Certified Child Dentist? When the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry (ABPD) certifies a dentist that the local children’s dentistry services being provided are as per the standards of excellence then a doctor is said to be a board-certified child dentist. It means that your dentist, like Dr. Megan Magargal, DMD, has been accredited with complete training to deliver quality patient care.

When Do I Begin Bringing My Kid To A Child Dentist? According to the American Dental Association, the first tooth appears at 6 months. So as soon as you see a tooth, bring your infant to your trusted local pediatric dentist in King of Prussia for a dental checkup.
