Composite Fillings

By addressing decay early with a composite filling, you can avoid more extensive dental treatments in the future.

  • Prevent further damage or infection
  • Restore the natural shape and strength of the tooth
  • Improve the appearance of chipped or worn teeth
  • Maintain long-term oral health

What to Expect During the Procedure

Your composite filling appointment at Christiana Family Dental Care is typically quick and comfortable:

Most patients can return to their normal routine the same day.

  • The affected area is gently numbed to ensure a pain-free experience
  • Decay or damaged tooth structure is carefully removed
  • The composite material is applied in layers and shaped to fit your tooth
  • A special light is used to harden the filling
  • The filling is polished for a smooth, natural finish

After Your Filling

After receiving a composite filling, you may notice mild, temporary sensitivity. Here’s what to keep in mind:

  • Avoid eating until the numbness wears off
  • Stick to soft foods for a few hours if needed
  • Mild sensitivity to hot, cold, or pressure is normal for a few days
  • Call us if you experience severe pain, swelling, or bite issues
  • Following these guidelines helps ensure proper healing and long-lasting results.
