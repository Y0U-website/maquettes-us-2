Implant Crown

Unlike removable options, implant crowns offer stability, durability, and long-term comfort.

  • Replace a missing tooth with a natural-looking, permanent solution
  • Maintain jawbone health by stimulating the bone
  • Prevent surrounding teeth from shifting out of place
  • Improve overall smile appearance and confidence

What to Expect

The dental implant itself is placed at another dental office. Once we receive the report confirming that the implant has healed and is ready for restoration, our team begins the implant crown process.

The restoration process usually takes two to three appointments, depending on individual needs.

  • Ordering the necessary implant components
  • Taking impressions or digital scans for precise crown fabrication
  • Fitting and adjusting your custom crown for comfort and bite alignment
  • Securing the crown in place for a natural, long-lasting result

After Your Procedure

Proper care is essential to protect your implant crown and ensure long-term success:

With proper care, implant crowns can last many years.

  • Maintain excellent oral hygiene with daily brushing and flossing
  • Attend regular dental check-ups and cleanings
  • Avoid chewing extremely hard objects (such as ice or pens)
  • Contact us if you notice pain, swelling, or any loosening of the crown
