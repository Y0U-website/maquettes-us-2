If you already have a dental implant placed and are ready for the final restoration, contact us today to schedule your implant crown consultation at our dental office in Newark, DE.

An implant crown is a custom-made artificial tooth that attaches securely to a dental implant. The implant, which is placed into the jawbone by a specialist, acts as an artificial tooth root and provides strong, stable support for the crown.

Once the implant has fully healed and integrated with the jawbone, the implant crown is placed on top to restore the appearance and function of a missing tooth. Implant crowns are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Unlike removable options, implant crowns offer stability, durability, and long-term comfort.
