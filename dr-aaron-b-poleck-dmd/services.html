Why Choose Us

You Can Expect Nothing Short of Fantastic Dental Services

Cleaning & Preventive Care At Christiana Family Dental Care, we believe routine cleanings and preventive care are essential for long-term oral health. Our team helps patients in Newark, DE maintain healthy teeth and gums by catching dental issues early and preventing future problems. Regular visits also reduce plaque buildup, lower the risk of gum disease, and support a confident, healthy smile.

Composite Fillings Composite fillings are an effective way to restore cavities and minor tooth damage while maintaining a natural-looking smile. These tooth-colored restorations strengthen the tooth and blend seamlessly with your existing teeth, allowing you to protect both function and appearance with a lasting solution.

Extractions When a tooth cannot be saved, a simple extraction may be the best option for protecting your overall oral health. At Christiana Family Dental Care, we provide gentle extractions for patients in Newark, DE, focusing on comfort, safety, and clear aftercare guidance to support smooth healing.

Crowns & Bridges Crowns and bridges are restorative solutions designed to repair damaged teeth or replace missing ones. These custom restorations help restore chewing ability, improve smile appearance, and prevent surrounding teeth from shifting out of place, supporting long-term oral stability.

Root Canal Root canal treatment relieves pain and removes infection while preserving your natural tooth. This procedure restores comfort and prevents the need for extraction, allowing the treated tooth to function normally for many years with proper care and restoration.

Dentures Dentures provide a reliable solution for replacing missing teeth and restoring everyday function. Patients at Christiana Family Dental Care in Newark, DE receive custom partial and complete dentures designed to improve chewing, speech, facial support, and overall confidence.

Implant Crowns Implant crowns offer a permanent, natural-looking replacement for missing teeth by attaching securely to a dental implant. This restorative option provides excellent stability, restores bite function, and helps maintain jawbone health for long-term oral wellness.

Emergency Care Dental emergencies require prompt attention to relieve pain and prevent further complications. Christiana Family Dental Care provides timely emergency dental care for patients in Newark, DE, helping address urgent issues such as severe tooth pain, broken teeth, or dental injuries as quickly as possible.
