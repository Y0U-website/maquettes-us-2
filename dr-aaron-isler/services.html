Specialist endodontic care at every office.

Every service is focused on saving your natural tooth and relieving pain, with technology and training general dentists don’t have.

  • Root Canal Therapy Root canal therapy is how a tooth with an infected nerve is saved instead of pulled. Your endodontist treats it under a surgical microscope with 3D CBCT imaging and rubber dam isolation, seeing every canal clearly. Your dentist can refer you, or you can call any of our offices directly. Learn more
  • Emergency Root Canal Treatment Emergency root canal treatment means being seen today rather than waiting days for an appointment. Call your nearest office, and an endodontic specialist will numb the tooth, find the source of the ache, and treat it in one visit. Most patients feel steady relief within 24 to 48 hours. Learn more
  • Root Canal Retreatment Root canal retreatment is the second look a tooth gets when an earlier treatment did not fully settle it. Your endodontist works under a surgical microscope with 3D CBCT imaging, looking for what the first treatment missed: an extra canal, a hidden crack, or a seal that broke down. Your natural tooth stays in place. Learn more
  • Apicoectomy (Periapical Surgery) An apicoectomy is the option that keeps a treated tooth in place when infection at the root has not cleared. Your endodontist plans it from a 3D CBCT scan and works under a surgical microscope. You feel pressure but no pain under local anesthesia, and you are home resting within a couple of hours. Learn more
  • Dental Trauma Treatment Same-day care for knocked-out teeth, root fractures, and dental injuries. We work fast because every minute matters, especially for avulsed teeth. Learn more
  • 3D CBCT Imaging in Endodontics 3D CBCT imaging happens during your appointment, without a separate visit and with no preparation on your part. You sit or stand while the scanner rotates once around your head; nothing touches you, and there is no tunnel to go inside. Your endodontist then reviews the images with you before treatment begins. Learn more

Technology at every location

Every MFE office is equipped to the same specialist standard. Hover to pause and read.

  • Surgical Operating Microscope Magnifies the tooth up to 25× well beyond what dental loupes can show so we can see canals and cracks general dentists miss.
  • 3D CBCT Cone-Beam CT Low-dose 3D scan that maps your tooth and bone from every angle before treatment begins.
  • The Wand® Anesthesia Computer-controlled injection that releases numbing medicine slowly and gently.
  • Electric Handpieces Smoother, quieter, and more precise than air-driven drills, less vibration, better control.
  • Ultrasonic Instruments High-frequency vibration that activates rinses and cleans tiny crevices other tools miss.
  • Digital Radiography Up to 90 % less radiation than film X-rays, with instant images and zoom for better diagnosis.
  • Nickel-Titanium Files Flexible instruments that bend with the natural curves of your tooth for cleaner shaping.
  • Paperless Records Your treatment history follows you to any MFE location, secure and instantly accessible.

Same-day emergency appointments are available at every office. If you have a knocked-out tooth, severe swelling, or uncontrolled pain, call your nearest office directly. After hours, our answering service will connect you with the on-call doctor.
