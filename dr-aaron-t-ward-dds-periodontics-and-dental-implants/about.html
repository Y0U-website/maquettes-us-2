What To Expect

Your First Visit

Office Comforts and Amenities

Our goal is always to keep patients comfortable, happy, and even relaxed! That’s why we’ve made our office a warm and inviting place to visit, from our massaging dental chairs to other patient-centered amenities.

Massaging Dental Chairs

Who said a trip to the dentist couldn’t be relaxing? We’ve added a spa-like touch to our office with massage chairs that keep you comfortable during your treatments.

Sedation Options

We offer a range of sedation options for our patients to ensure you&#x27;re comfortable and anxiety free during your treatment, whatever your needs may be.

Warm Blankets

We want you to feel cared for and relaxed at each visit. We&#x27;ve found that small comforts like cozy blankets go a long way in helping our patients feel right at home throughout their visits.

Advanced Periodontal Technology

CBCT Imaging

Using cone beam technology, we can provide patients with 3D images that display much more detailed information than traditional x-rays alone.

Intraoral Scanner

Our intraoral scanner provides mess-free impressions for patients instead of the goopy, uncomfortable putty-based impressions of the past.

In-Office Lab

Enjoy the convenience of our practice’s in-office lab. Because we craft your prosthetics in-house, you won’t be sent to a secondary location to get your implants or restorations created.

Soft Tissue Laser

Our state-of-the-art soft tissue laser can be used to reshape the gum line or remove excess gum tissue in one quick, simple, and more comfortable procedure.
