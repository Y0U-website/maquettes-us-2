What are Dental Implants?

Implant Dentistry

Dental implants provide a permanent solution for missing teeth by providing a solid foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth. With implant dentistry, our office can transform your smile in as little as a single day! We offer both single tooth implants and All-on-4 implant-supported bridges to best meet the needs of your smile. We’re determined to help you feel and look your best with natural-looking implants that give you the grin you deserve.

Single Tooth Implants

Just missing one tooth? Naturally, we’ll treat you with a single tooth implant! This is a simple dental implant that’s typically topped with a dental crown restoration for the most natural look and straightforward treatment.

Transformation Through Restoration

Implant-Supported Bridges

If you’re missing two or more consecutive teeth, implant supported bridges may be the right option. Unlike traditional bridges, which are typically supported by crowns placed over adjacent teeth, implant supported bridges are attached to titanium posts in your jaw for a more stable foundation.

The Best Bridge For You

Implant-Retained Dentures (Snaps)

Instead of relying on friction or adhesives to remain in place, implant-retained dentures literally “snap” into dental implants, offering more of a secure foundation than traditional dentures alone. Patients also remark that they’re more comfortable than traditional dentures.

Added Comfort. More Stability.

Full Arch Implants

Full arch implants are also known as All-on-4 Implants , and are the best treatment option to replace a full set of missing teeth. With this procedure, the upper or lower set of teeth can be replaced with four implants that serve as a permanent foundation for your new teeth. It’s a comprehensive solution for natural, comfortable teeth that can last a lifetime.
