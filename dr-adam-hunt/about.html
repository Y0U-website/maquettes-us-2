Meet Our Team

Brandon Dentist, Dr. Adam C. Hunt, is dedicated to providing you with a pleasant visit and results that you’re proud to show off. Call our Brandon Dentist Office at (813) 689-4949 with any questions or to make an appointment.

Dr. Hunt is currently a member of the American Dental Association, Florida Dental Association, West Coast District Dental Association, & the Brandon Chamber of Commerce.

Prior to his settling in Florida Dr. Hunt lived in Utah, Ohio, and Portugal. He now resides in the Brandon community with his wife and three beautiful little girls. With their family time the Hunt’s like to soak up the Florida sun and take advantage of the great opportunities the area offers. The family also enjoys taking part in their church activities.

She was born in Albania and spent her adolescent years in Greece. She loves to spend quality time with her family, traveling especially for missionary work, exercising such as yoga and pilates, walk or run with her rescue puppy.

She became a dental hygienist to help people achieve a beautiful, healthy smile. Her main concern is the comfort of her patients and her ability to educate patients on Periodontal disease and other various dental issues.
