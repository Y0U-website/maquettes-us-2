We manage the dental needs of your whole family under one roof. From Invisalign and implants to cosmetic dentistry, Botox, and laser dentistry, we do it all!

We know your time is important, so we offer evening and seasonal Saturday appointments.

Our staff welcomes patients to our office in the same way we welcome someone to our home. We believe your first encounter here should be a comfortable one. From the time you first call to the time we say good-bye we are here to serve you and acquaint ourselves with your needs, your problems, and your fears.

The Silverdale Dentistry team is fully versed in the latest technology and procedures, and all our dental assistants are fully certified. Our managerial staff is well versed in all dental business matters, including insurance procedures, and will accommodate you in all areas related to treatment.

We strive, whether interacting with patients or co-workers, to be 100 % positive and we always remember that you, the patient, is our reason for being here!
