Testimonials

A collection of dental practices now 30+ locations strong, Light Dental Studios began its humble journey as the second-ever dentist in Puyallup , Washington all the way back in 1971.

In the spirit of many other revolutionary start-ups, Dr. James P. Zanner established the company by renovating his own mobile home into a dental office, the original location of which sits just across from the current South Hill Mall . Known for his exceedingly gentle touch and commitment to the latest dental innovations, Dr. Zanner built relationships that have managed to last entire lifetimes, not the least of which because he gave shots that didn’t hurt, a testament to his expertise and compassion both.

Welcome to Light Dental Studios, where the magic of excellent dental healthcare is more than just a fairytale.
