What to Expect at Your First Visit

During your first visit, our team will get to know you and have a conversation with you about your dental health. Then, we’ll examine your mouth, professionally clean your teeth and gums, and your dentist will address any issues, if necessary. If you don’t have any dental issues, we’ll simply schedule your next preventive appointment at the end of your cleaning. If we do notice a cavity or other oral issue, we’ll address it with a customized treatment plan.

All the Comforts of Home

We’ve designed our office and amenities with your comfort in mind. Excellent dentistry should be paired with stellar service and a warm environment. That’s why we offer TVs in each room and in the waiting waiting room, a beverage station to keep you hydrated, and a kids play area. If there’s anything else we can do to make your visit more enjoyable, let us know!
