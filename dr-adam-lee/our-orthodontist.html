Meet the Artists Behind Your Smile Masterpiece

Your smile is the first thing people notice about you and our mission is to help you achieve a healthy, beautiful smile. We know you have an important choice to make when it comes to orthodontic care. That’s why we are committed to offering high-quality treatment options to help you achieve your most perfect smile. Visit our offices today to discuss a personalized treatment plan with our dental teams in Payson and Spanish Fork.

Dr. Kimberly Doucette, Orthodontist

I am so excited to join the team at Red Rock Orthodontics! I can’t wait to meet all of you!

A little about me: after graduating from dental school at Midwestern University in Glendale, AZ where I received the awards for “Most Compassionate” and “Family Woman of the Year”, I furthered my education at the University of Nevada Las Vegas where I earned a Certificate in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics along with a Master of Science in Oral Biology. I am an active member of the American Association of Orthodontists, American Dental Association and I am now Board Certified.

Outside of my professional life, I am happily married to my high school sweetheart and am a proud parent of three children. In my free time, I enjoy spending quality time with my family, and staying active through working out and playing volleyball.

I take great pride in building strong relationships with my patients and being a part of their smile journey. My goal is to provide exceptional care and support to all those I have the pleasure of treating.

Dr. Ryan Morris, Orthodontist

While growing up in Eagle, Idaho, Dr. Morris developed an appreciation for the great outdoors and has a passion for fly tying and fishing. His love of the outdoors also helped attribute to his achievement as an Eagle Scout.

In addition to his interest in outdoor activities, Dr. Morris enjoys music and sports. He is an accomplished guitar player, composes his own music, and plays in numerous bands. He also plays basketball and enjoys watching sporting events.

Dr. Morris resides in Spanish Fork, Utah with his wife and three daughters. In his free time, you can find Dr. Morris spending time with his family, teaching his daughters to fly fish, and sharing his love of music with them.

Before arriving in Utah, Dr. Morris practiced in Illinois for the past four years as co-owner of an orthodontic practice. We are so happy to welcome Dr. Morris to the practice and are confident you will enjoy getting to know him better.
