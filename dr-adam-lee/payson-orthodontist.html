Orthodontic Treatments

Our team offers efficient, customized orthodontic care built around your needs, whether that means braces for kids and teens or adult orthodontics. Explore your options below.

Braces

From traditional metal braces to Damon and Micro Braces, our braces options apply steady, gentle pressure to move teeth into place, including Phase I and Phase II care for younger patients.

Clear Aligners

Prefer a more discreet option? Our clear aligners straighten teeth without brackets or wires, making them a popular choice for teens and adults who want a flexible, low-visibility treatment.

Orthognathic Surgery & TADs

For more complex bite or jaw concerns, we offer surgical options and temporary anchorage devices (TADs) to support treatment that braces or aligners alone can’t fully address, along with tools like AcceleDent and Propel, as well as airway and sleep diagnostics when indicated.
