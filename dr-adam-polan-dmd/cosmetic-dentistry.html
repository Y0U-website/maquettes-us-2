Frequently Asked Questions

Cosmetic Dentistry FAQs

  • How do I know which cosmetic treatment is right for me? Schedule a consultation and we’ll discuss your goals, examine your teeth and gums, and recommend options based on your needs. Some patients only need whitening, while others benefit from veneers, bonding, aligners, crowns, or a combination of treatments.
  • How long do cosmetic dental results last? Longevity depends on the treatment and how well you care for your teeth. Veneers and crowns can last many years with proper care. Whitening results may need periodic touch-ups. Good oral hygiene and regular checkups help extend all results.
  • Is cosmetic dental work painful? Most cosmetic treatments are minimally invasive with little to no discomfort. Local anesthesia is available when needed, and any minor soreness afterward can typically be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.
  • Will insurance cover my cosmetic treatment? Many cosmetic treatments are considered elective and may not be fully covered, but procedures that also restore function may have partial coverage. We can help review your options and discuss payment or financing solutions during your consultation.
  • What is gum contouring and who is a good candidate? Gum contouring reshapes the gum tissue around your teeth to correct a gummy smile, fix an uneven gumline, or expose more of the tooth surface. You may be a candidate if you have excess gum tissue, teeth that appear too short, or cosmetic concerns about the balance between your teeth and gums. A consultation is needed to confirm your gums are healthy and the treatment is appropriate.
  • Does gum contouring show results immediately? Yes — you can usually see a visible difference right after the procedure. Your gums will need time to heal fully, and the final appearance will become clearer as swelling and tenderness resolve.
  • Is dental bonding completed in one visit? In many cases, yes. Dental bonding is often completed in a single appointment, making it a convenient option for patients who want quick cosmetic improvement.
  • What are the benefits of dental bonding? Dental bonding is natural-looking, minimally invasive, versatile, and often more affordable than veneers or crowns. It can improve minor cosmetic concerns while preserving much of the natural tooth structure.
  • What are the drawbacks of dental bonding? Bonding may not last as long as porcelain veneers or crowns. The resin can also chip or stain over time, so it may require touch-ups or replacement in the future.
  • How do I care for dental bonding? Brush and floss regularly, visit your dentist for checkups, avoid biting hard objects, and limit staining foods and drinks such as coffee, tea, red wine, and tobacco products.
  • Can cosmetic treatments be combined? Absolutely. Gum contouring is often combined with veneers, crowns, whitening, bonding, or clear aligners as part of a complete smile makeover. In fact, combining treatments often delivers the most balanced and natural-looking final result.
