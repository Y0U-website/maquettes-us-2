The implant placement procedure typically takes 30-60 minutes per implant, depending on the complexity of your case and the number of implants being placed.

Following implant placement, a healing period of 3-6 months allows the implant to integrate with your jawbone through osseointegration. During this time, temporary restorations may be provided to maintain aesthetics and function while your implants heal.

We monitor your healing progress through regular follow-up appointments, ensuring proper integration before proceeding to the restoration phase. This careful approach maximizes the long-term success of your implant treatment.

Once integration is complete, your custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant, completing your tooth replacement. Dr. Rudin’s attention to detail ensures your restoration matches your natural teeth perfectly while providing optimal function and comfort.

The restoration process involves taking precise impressions and working with skilled laboratory technicians to create restorations that meet the highest standards for aesthetics and function, integrating seamlessly with our cosmetic dentistry approach. Multiple fittings may be required to ensure a perfect fit and bite alignment before final placement.

Life-Changing Benefits

Dental implants provide benefits that extend far beyond simple tooth replacement, improving your quality of life in ways that other treatment options cannot match. The permanent nature of implants also eliminates the ongoing maintenance and replacement costs associated with dentures and bridges.

Missing teeth can significantly impact your self-confidence and willingness to smile, laugh, or speak in social and professional situations. Implants restore your natural smile and eliminate the embarrassment associated with missing teeth or ill-fitting dentures.

The stability and strength of implant-supported teeth allow you to eat all your favorite foods without restriction, improving your nutrition and overall health. Unlike dentures, which may limit your food choices, implants provide the chewing efficiency of natural teeth.

Missing teeth or ill-fitting dentures can affect your speech clarity and confidence. Implants provide the stability necessary for clear speech while eliminating the clicking or slipping sounds that can occur with removable appliances.

Implants help maintain the health of surrounding teeth and gums by preserving bone structure and eliminating the need to alter adjacent healthy teeth, which is why periodontal treatment is essential before implant placement. This preservation of your natural oral anatomy promotes better long-term oral health outcomes.

Candidacy and Success Factors

Most patients who have lost one or more teeth are candidates for dental implant treatment. Individual factors such as bone quality, overall health, and existing oral health status all influence the treatment plan — which is why a thorough evaluation is the essential first step. Dr. Rudin’s comprehensive exam includes advanced 3D imaging to assess bone volume and anatomy, identifying any factors that need to be addressed before implant placement can proceed.

Modern implant materials and techniques have significantly improved success rates, and many conditions that previously ruled out implants — such as moderate bone loss — can now be addressed through preparatory procedures like bone grafting . For patients with gum disease , treatment to restore gum health is completed before implant placement to protect long-term outcomes.

In cases where insufficient bone is available for implant placement, bone grafting procedures can often create the necessary foundation for successful implant treatment. Dr. Rudin’s training includes advanced bone grafting techniques that can restore bone volume and quality in most situations.
