Does Your Child Have a Tongue- or Lip-Tie? Learn How We Can Help

At Sunshine Pediatric Dentistry in Evansville, IN, Dr. Aditi Jindal provides expert care for children with tongue- and lip-ties. These common conditions can make breastfeeding, speaking, and even smiling more difficult for your child. Left untreated, they can lead to long-term issues like dental decay, speech delays, and even sleep problems.

Crowns (Caps)

A crown (often called a cap) covers the tooth and restores it to its original shape and size. Decay is removed and cleaned from the tooth and a preformed crown is placed over the tooth.

Composite Fillings

The common silver filling contains mercury. Dr. Aditi Jindal’s practice uses the highest-quality biocompatible, tooth-colored restorations to create healthy, beautiful smiles.

Preventive Care

Much of your preventive dental care starts with you. Developing healthy dental habits early in life can help reduce cavities, gum disease, and gingivitis.
