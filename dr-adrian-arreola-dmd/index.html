The practice

Hazlet • Aberdeen • Matawan • Cliffwood • Middletown • Holmdel • Lincroft • Red Bank • Marlboro

My wife and I were married here. We’ve built our life here, and we look forward to raising our future family in this same community. We’ve planted roots because we believe in the families, the schools, the neighborhoods, and the sense of connection that makes this area special.

For years, I’ve had the privilege of caring for children and families throughout Monmouth County as a pediatric dentist. Watching kids grow, gain confidence, and return year after year has been one of the most meaningful parts of my career.

Now, I’m excited to take the next step — opening my own pediatric dental practice.

I believe one of the most powerful parts of pediatric dentistry is relationship-building over time.

My goal is to be the consistent doctor your child sees from their very first visit through adolescence — celebrating milestones, tracking growth, and building trust year after year.

Children thrive when they feel safe and familiar. Parents appreciate knowing who is caring for their child — not just today, but long-term.

This practice is built around stability, long-term relationships, and being a steady presence for families in our community, because children deserve the comfort of growing up with a doctor they know and trust.
