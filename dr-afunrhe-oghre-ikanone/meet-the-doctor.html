Meet The Team

Dr. Afunrhe Oghre-Ikanone Passionate, Experienced, and Committed to Exceptional Care Dr. Oghre is a board-certified general dentist with over eleven years of experience in dental medicine, known not only for his clinical expertise but also for his genuine care and commitment to his patients and community.

He earned his Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences from the University of Missouri–St. Louis and went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery from Howard University in Washington, D.C. Dr. Oghre then completed an intensive residency at Bronx Lebanon Hospital in New York, sharpening his skills in comprehensive and advanced dental care.

After years of practicing in Buffalo, New York, Dr. Oghre found his home in Mount Pleasant, Texas, where he continues to serve his patients with compassion, precision, and integrity.

Beyond the dental chair, Dr. Oghre is passionate about giving back through community service and outreach. He believes in dentistry that not only transforms smiles but also builds trust and long-term relationships. His approach to care is rooted in honesty, patient education, and a deep respect for each individual’s unique needs.

When he’s not creating beautiful, healthy smiles, Dr. Oghre enjoys reading, traveling, and staying active by playing soccer. He also expresses his creativity through music—playing the bass guitar and drums in his free time.

Dr. Oghre’s dedication to excellence, paired with his approachable and caring nature, makes him a trusted provider and valued member of the Mount Pleasant community. He looks forward to welcoming you and your family to the practice!
