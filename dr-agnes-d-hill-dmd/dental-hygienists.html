Suzie

Bio Suzie is from Minneapolis, MN. Her career began in 1976 working in private practice as a Certified and Registered Dental Assistant. She worked in the General Practice Residency program at the University of Minnesota Dental School. From 1986-1992, she taught Dental Assisting at Moorhead Technical College in Moorhead MN. She attended Century College in White Bear Lake, MN, completing her degree in Dental Hygiene (1996). She joined Bassett Creek Dental in 1996.

Suzie is married to Kevin and has two children Calvin and Erin. She enjoys running, biking, walking her dog, skiing, yoga, gardening, and volunteering.

Kelly

Bio Kelly is a Minnesota native from Golden Valley and earned her Associate of Science degree in Dental Hygiene from Argosy University. She has been practicing dental hygiene since 2011, bringing more than fifteen years of experience caring for patients in both the Twin Cities area and Boston, Massachusetts.

Kelly joined Bassett Creek Dental in 2016 and is dedicated to delivering high-quality, preventive dental care in a comfortable and supportive environment. She is known for her gentle chairside manner, thorough approach to care, and commitment to patient education, helping individuals and families understand the connection between oral health and overall wellness. Kelly values building long-term relationships with her patients and striving to make every visit a positive experience.

She currently resides in the Twin Cities area with her husband and their three children. Outside of the office, Kelly enjoys being an active mom, staying physically active, and spending meaningful time with her family and friends.
