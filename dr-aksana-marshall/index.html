Dr. Luke is thrilled to be a part of community events and involved in all that happens in Tifton. From events hosted at Pediatric Dental Center of Georgia like a hair donation event or candy give-back, to coaching sports, home is where his heart is!

Dr. Luke and Dr. Marshall are both honored to be a part of professional associations that extend their skills through professional learning and events, and benefit their patients by making connections that help improve the quality of care provided by Pediatric Dental Center of Georgia.

Dr. Luke was raised in Tifton, Georgia and is proud to have returned to his hometown to practice pediatric dentistry. He graduated with honors from Tift County High School in 2000. During high school, Dr. Luke enjoyed playing varsity baseball, being with family and spending time at Lake Blackshear.

After high school, Dr. Luke continued his education at Mercer University and graduated Magna Cum Laude in 2004 with a Bachelor s of Science in Biology. He then began Dental School at the Medical College of Georgia in the fall of 2004. Dr. Luke earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree in 2008 from MCG.

Following dental school Dr. Luke trained in the speciality of pediatric dentistry for two years from 2008-2010 at the Medical College of Georgia School of Dentistry. He also trained at Children s Healthcare of Atlanta furthering his specialist training treating children with special needs.
