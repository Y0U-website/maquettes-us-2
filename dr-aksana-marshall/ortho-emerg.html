If you are experiencing a medical emergency, please call 911.

We will provide emergency dental care for all individuals during regular business hours. After regular business hours emergency dental care is provided exclusively for patients of record.

During regular business hours emergencies:

Should your child suffer a dental emergency during regular business hours, please immediately contact our office at ( 229) 238-3787 and we will promptly triage the injury. Once Dr. Luke or Dr. Marshall is able to assess your child’s condition, depending upon your child’s specific symptoms, we will either provide over-the-phone recommendations or schedule an appointment. We truly appreciate your understanding and flexibility as we address your child’s dental emergency.

Occasionally treating a dental emergency will cause us to run slightly late with our regularly scheduled patients. When this situation occurs, we apologize to all our patients, request your understanding, and will do our best to keep you informed.
