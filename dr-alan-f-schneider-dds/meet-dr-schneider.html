Meet Dr. Alan F. Schneider

Alan F. Schneider, D.D.S

Providing his patients with quality orthodontic care since 1999.

Highly educated, he obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree and orthodontic specialty certificate from the University of Minnesota. He also completed a hospital-based residency in general practice at Meriter Hospital in Madison, Wisconsin.

Dr. Schneider feels it is important to stay on the cutting-edge of orthodontics. Thus, he maintains memberships in many prestigious professional organization, such as the American Association of Orthodontists, College of Diplomates of the American Board of Orthodontics, Minnesota Association of Orthodontists, Midwestern Society of Orthodontists, American Dental Association, Minnesota Dental Association, West Central District Dental Society and the St. Cloud Dental Society.

Along with running his own practice, Dr. Schneider is a consultant at the St. Cloud Centra Care Cleft & Craniofacial Clinic and the University of Minnesota Cleft & Craniofacial Clinic. He also volunteers his time with the American Dental Association’s annual Give Kids a Smile program.

Away from the office, Dr. Schneider enjoys music, sports, outdoor activities, traveling and spending time with his wife, Heidi; son, Isaac; and daughter, Maggie.
