Dental Implants Guilford CT

  • Enhanced Comfort : Because they become part of your jawbone, dental implants eliminate the discomfort of removable dentures.
  • Better Functionality : Implants allow you to speak, chew, and bite with ease, as they don’t slip or shift like traditional dentures.
  • Durability : With proper care, dental implants are designed to last a lifetime. Unlike bridges, which may need to be replaced over time, implants are a permanent solution.
  • Jawbone Health : When a tooth is lost, the bone in the area may begin to deteriorate. Dental implants help preserve the jawbone by stimulating bone growth and preventing further bone loss.
  • Improved Oral Health : Dental implants don’t require altering nearby teeth, as a tooth-supported bridge might. This helps preserve the integrity of your remaining natural teeth.

Can Anyone Get Dental Implants?

In most cases, dental implants are a viable option for adults who are in good overall health and have sufficient bone density in their jaw to support the implant. However, certain factors can affect a person’s eligibility for implants. These factors include:

A thorough examination and consultation at Friedler Dental Group will help determine if dental implants are the right solution for you.

  • Bone Health: Sufficient healthy bone in your jaw is required to secure the implant. If significant bone loss is present, a bone graft may be needed before implant surgery.
  • Gum Health: Healthy gums are crucial for successful dental implants. Patients with untreated gum disease may need to resolve their condition before proceeding with implants.
  • Medical Conditions: Chronic conditions, such as diabetes or heart disease, can impact the healing process after surgery. Your dentist will review your medical history to determine if implants are a safe option for you.
  • Age: Dental implants are generally recommended for adults, as younger individuals may still be growing, which can impact implant placement and longevity.

How to Care for Dental Implants

Just like natural teeth, dental implants require daily brushing and flossing to prevent plaque buildup and maintain gum health. It’s important to use a soft-bristled toothbrush to clean around the implant area and be gentle on your gums. Regular dental check-ups are also essential, as they allow your dentist to monitor your implants and detect any potential issues early.
