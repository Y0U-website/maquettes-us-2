Albert Zak, DDS

Dr. Zak earned his DDS from the University at Buffalo and completed his advanced training in general dentistry at Rochester General Hospital. After his tenure as a clinical instructor at RGH and private practice in Newark, he joined Perinton Dental Group in 2008. Dr. Zak is dedicated to providing the best care for his patients, and actively pursues every opportunity to further his knowledge of dentistry.

Svetlana (Lana) Yurovskiy, DDS

A graduate of the University at Buffalo’s School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Yurovskiy spent her post-residency time in practice in Canandaigua and at Hill Haven nursing home in Webster as well as teaching at Rochester General. She joined Perinton Dental Group in 2010. Dr. Y continues to stay abreast of the industry’s latest improvements to provide her patients with the best care available.

Peter D’Elia, DDS

Outside of his professional life, Dr. D’Elia finds joy in various activities. He is an avid Mets fan and enjoys following their games. Playing the drums allows him to express his creativity and unwind. In the kitchen, Dr. D’Elia explores his culinary skills, enjoying the process of cooking. He also enjoys discovering new restaurants and engaging in the latest activities in the vibrant Rochester area, sharing these experiences with his wife.

Alessandro Geminiani, DDS

Dr. Geminiani is board-certified, holding the prestigious title of Diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology. Dr. Geminiani received his D.D.S. degree from the University of Siena (Italy) in 2004, summa cum laude. He then pursued a Master of Science in Fixed Prosthodontics and Prosthetic Dental Materials in 2006 with highest honors. Dr. Geminiani also completed 2 certificates and a Master of Science in Clinical Investigation degree at Eastman Dental Center. He is fluent in English, Spanish and Italian.
