Dental Implants

If you’re missing one tooth or several, or if you’re tired of hassling with dentures, implants can offer a lasting, stable, comfortable solution. They are small, biocompatible posts that act as artificial tooth roots. Surgically placed into your jawbone, implants fuse naturally with your bone over several months and provide a strong, stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. Unlike removable appliances like dentures, dental implants are fixed in place, helping you eat, speak, and smile with renewed confidence and peace of mind.

What to Expect: The Dental Implant Process

We want you to feel confident every step of the way. Here’s how the dental implant process works at Perinton Dental Group:

We’ll begin with a thorough exam and digital imaging to evaluate your oral health and bone structure. Together, we’ll discuss your goals and recommend the best implant option for your needs.

Once you’re ready, we’ll place the implant post into your jawbone. This surgical procedure is done with precision and care under anesthesia, and most patients experience minimal post-op discomfort.

Over the next few months, the implant naturally fuses with your bone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a stable foundation for your new tooth or teeth.

Once healing is complete, we’ll attach a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture to your implant. The result is a secure, functional, and natural-looking smile.

We’re here to support you at every step, from your first consultation to your final restoration. Our goal is to keep you informed and comfortable through every stage of your treatment.

All-On-4® Dental Implants

If you’ve experienced extensive tooth loss, wear full dentures, or have reduced jawbone density, All-On-4® dental implants may be a life-changing solution. This treatment allows us to anchor a full arch of prosthetic teeth using just four strategically placed implants, two of which are angled to take advantage of your existing bone structure. It’s an effective option designed for maximum bone contact, long-term success, and restoration with fewer implants.

Teeth-In-A-Day

If you are looking to replace an entire arch of teeth quickly, Teeth-In-A-Day offers a streamlined, immediate solution. This innovative procedure combines precise implant placement and custom prosthetic fabrication, all in a single day. Using advanced digital imaging and guided implant technology, we can place your implants and secure a full set of beautiful, functional teeth the very same day, with no need to wait months for your new teeth.
