Orthodontics Isn’t All About Looks

Wanting a straight smile isn’t vain. It’s a key part of your overall health and confidence. At Perinton Dental Group, we help patients of all ages improve their oral health and feel great about their smiles with advanced orthodontic treatments. We proudly offer both Invisalign® clear aligners and Powerprox® 6-Month Braces—two effective solutions designed to fit your lifestyle. Whether you’re looking for nearly invisible aligners or a fast track to straighter teeth, our treatments deliver results without the discomfort or appearance of traditional metal braces.

As one of the world’s most advanced clear aligner systems, Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, removable trays to gradually shift your teeth into alignment. With this smooth, nearly invisible solution, there is no need for brackets and wires, so straightening your teeth can fit seamlessly into your daily routine. Most patients complete treatment in less time than traditional braces, often with fewer office visits.

Powerprox offers a unique orthodontic solution that can straighten your teeth quickly, often in just six months. These clear braces have tooth-colored wires and brackets for an understated look during treatment. When you’re done with your teeth transformation process, they can come off easily!
