With a distinguished background and more than 25 years of experience, Dr. Smolyar elevates the level of care provided to his patients. Meet Your Team

Dr. Jeffrey Reitzel completed his undergraduate studies at the University of Georgia, where he earned a Bachelors of Fine Arts. A native South Carolinian, he received his Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry at the Medical University of South Carolina, where he also completed residency in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. Dr. Reitzel is a member of AAOMS and MSOMS. Dr. Reitzel’s clinical focus is patient-centered care, he is known for being thoughtful about each case and putting the patient’s needs and wishes first. Dr. Reitzel lives in Rhode Island with his wife and daughters.
