The practice

At Dr. Alex Smolyar DMD Dentistry, you can expect more than just exceptional dental care; you will receive personalized attention from a distinguished professional you can trust.

Dr. Alex Smolyar DMD, a boutique dental practice serving New England’s elite for over 25 years, uses state-of-the-art technology to provide the highest quality multi-specialty care. Offering a full range of integrated services all in one place, we ensure seamless coordination for each of our patients’ unique dental needs.

Through our patient-centered approach, we work alongside our patients to deliver advanced, comprehensive solutions that ensure long-term, stress-free, and comfortable dental care.

Graduating with honors in clinical achievements, Dr. Alex Smolyar earned his DMD degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in 1998. Following graduation, he became Pankey Institute-trained, where he received elite training and education for advanced and comprehensive Dentistry.

For schedule availability, simply call our office or submit your inquiry with our contact form and one of our team members will contact you to schedule your consultation.
