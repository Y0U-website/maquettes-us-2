Patient Info Dr. Alex Smolyar DMD

Patient Forms Completing patient information forms prior to your first visit helps us greatly expedite the new patient process. Please take a moment to print, complete and bring in these forms on the day of your first appointment.

Insurance Information We understand that dental insurance is an important resource for our patients. As a service to our valued patients, we submit to all insurance company plans and file all insurance claims for you electronically. We will do our best to guarantee you receive all of the benefits due to you from your insurance provider, however, it is your responsibility to ensure you are reimbursed properly. Please consult with your insurance in advance of your appointment to determine the extent of your benefits.

Payment Information We strive to provide you with the highest quality treatment that fits your budget. In addition to accepting cash, checks and all major credit cards, we offer affordable and flexible payment options to ensure you get the treatment you deserve while focusing on what matters the most keeping your smile healthy.

Your treatment is important to us. When scheduling appointments, we take special care to ensure our patients receive the attention they need each and every time they visit. In an effort to provide all of our patients with access to the highest quality care when they need it, our cancellation policy requires that you give our office 48 hours notice in the event you would like to reschedule.

Are you in pain or have a dental emergency? Dr. Smolyar is committed to helping his patients receive dental care when it matters the most. If you are experiencing pain during office hours we will do our best to schedule you the same day or the following morning. If you need emergency dental services after-hours please call 617-712-2770.
