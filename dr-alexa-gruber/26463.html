Dr. Alexa Gruber

Alexa Gruber, DDS

Dentist

Dr. Alexa Gruber graduated from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry with a Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 2016. Prior to her dental education, she attended the University of Richmond, and graduated in 2012 with cum laude honors. While at VCU, she completed a selective course in Advanced Prosthodontics, a mentorship in Oral Medicine and received awards from the Academy of Oral medicine and the International Congress of Oral Implantologists.

As a member of the Gruber Dental practice since 2017, Dr. Gruber loves being able to make a tangible difference in someone’s life whether it’s getting someone out of pain or enhancing their smile. When she is not at the office looking after patients, Dr. Gruber enjoys the outdoors. Hiking, sailing, and traveling are among her favorite activities. She also loves spending time with her family particularly her husband, Doug, daughters Addison and Mackenzie, and their dog, Tex.
