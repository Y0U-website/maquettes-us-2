How Is a Dental Implant Done?

During your consultation with Dr. Mendelsohn, you will decide on the best treatment options for your dental implants. The dental implants procedure can be done in two visits. On the first visit, we will place the dental implant and then the post with a temporary tooth on top.

In a dental procedure, Dr. Erik Mendelsohn places the implant. The titanium implant is perfectly positioned at the correct angle, which allows for an easy time when placing the permanent tooth. Three months is the typical healing time, but you will be wearing a beautiful provisional tooth at that time.

Osseo-integration occurs during this time, which allows the bone to become one with the dental implant.

Once the bone integration has occurred around the implant, Dr. Erik Mendelsohn will place an abutment or post inside the dental implant so that a dental crown can be held in place over it.

Your new dental crown, which is custom-made by our laboratory, is cemented or screwed to the abutment.
