Meet Dr. Amina Linn Meet Dr. Amina, our newest associate dentist, who brings a vibrant approach to patient care. Her academic journey began at La Salle University, where she graduated Maxima Cum Laude in 2016 with a major in Public Health and a minor in Chemistry.

Dr. Amina earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree with Cum Laude honors from Temple University School of Dentistry in 2022. And notably, she has completed specialized Invisalign training with the highly respected Dr. David Galler.

Growing up in Brigantine, Dr. Amina cultivated a love for music, mastering the violin and trumpet over two decades, inspired by her musician mother. She now makes her home in Galloway with her husband, Mike.

When not at the practice, she enjoys traveling, spending time with friends and family, and experimenting with exciting new recipes in the kitchen.
