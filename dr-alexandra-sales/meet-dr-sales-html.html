Meet the Doctor Smiles of…

Upon graduation from dental school, Dr. Kirk served in the United States Navy Dental Corps from 2012 to 2016, providing dental care to Sailors and Marines in Pensacola, FL, and San Diego, CA. She has also participated in dental missions serving underprivileged patients in Mexico, Belize, the Philippines, and Botswana. Following her time in private practices in Connecticut and North Carolina, she decided to return to her home state and join Smiles of Valparaiso.

Throughout her career, she has continued to enhance her skills through advanced training courses in Invisalign, digital dentistry, pain management, oral pathology, and endodontics.

- American Dental Association - Indiana Dental Association - Northwest Indiana Dental Society

Originally from Indianapolis, Dr. Kirk lives in Ogden Dunes with her husband Michael and her kids Teagan and Connor. When she is away from the office, she enjoys running, hiking, traveling, and cheering for the Fighting Irish.
