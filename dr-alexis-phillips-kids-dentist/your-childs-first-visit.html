Your Child’s First Visit

Your child’s first dental appointment

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends that your child see the dentist by their first birthday to ensure things are progressing well. We design an enjoyable experience with a personalized diet, hygiene, and habit plan to equip your child for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

Kids have fun We create fun moments that lead to positive memories to inspire a love for the dentist. From choosing their favorite movie, colorful sunglasses, toothpaste flavor, and prize at the end, they will feel like they got the special treatment they deserve.

Parents have fun We create enjoyable moments for our parents, too. You are the reason your child will not only have a blast here, but have a healthy smile for life. We make things as easy as possible for you while we partner with you on your child’s smile journey.

Finances and insurance Because we care about your finances, we offer payment options to fit you and your family. Our friendly team will be happy to file with your insurance, whether you are in or out of network, to ensure you are receiving your maximum benefits.
