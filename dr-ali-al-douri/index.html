Preventive dentistry is the modern approach to helping you keep a healthy mouth. It helps you keep your teeth, and means you need to have less dental treatment. The two main causes of tooth loss are decay and gum disease. The better we deal with these two problems, the more chance people have of keeping their teeth for life.

At Reform Dentistry, our preventive care program is a cooperative effort by the patient, dentist, and dental staff to preserve the natural dentition and supporting structures by preventing the onset, progress, and recurrence of dental diseases and conditions.
