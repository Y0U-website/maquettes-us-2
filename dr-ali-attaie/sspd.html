The hellosmile “WOW” experience

  • Connect with us Give us a call to make an appointment .
  • Kid Friendly Environment Our offices are beautiful and created for children to feel safe, calm, and carefree. From watching their favorite movie during treatment, to health activities in the waiting room, and to prizes. We have strived to create an atmosphere that leaves a lasting, positive memory.
  • Incentive Program We have designed an incentive program you and your child will love. Every hellosmile member gets a smile, passport, and is encouraged to stay cavity free with prizes for parents and kids.
  • Pediatric Team We have handpicked a pediatric dental team that is passionate about your child’s comfort and care.
  • Teaching Conversation At every visit Kids get specific coaching on how to better brush their smile and are motivated to be cavity free by receiving congratulatory stickers, balloons, and special prizes.
  • Stay Connected - Stay Healthy Stay connected with us through Facebook to get special announcements or follow us on twitter for health tips. We are also on Instagram posting pics to keep you smiling.
  • Baby Oral Health Check Up The Academy of Pediatrics recommends that in order to prevent dental problems, your child should have the proper oral evaluation when the first tooth appears, or no later than his/her first birthday. Your child’s oral health is very important. Primary, or “baby” teeth not only help children speak clearly and chew naturally, they also aid in forming a path that permanent teeth can follow when they are ready to come in.
  • Special Needs Pediatric Care At hellosmile we are fortunate to work with families that have a wide range of needs. For our patients with special needs we have specially trained staff that work with Autism, Downs Syndrome, and ADHD. We have developed special programs to serve all children.
  • Pediatric Dental Treatment We are here to serve as your child’s primary pediatric dental home from birth through adolescence. Through our SmileSchool program we are committed to preventive care and offer an array of tools to keep children engaged in their oral health.
  • Sedation and General Anesthesia We provide a comprehensive array of options from minimal sedation and nitrous oxide (laughing gas) to office or hospital based general anesthesia. We have extensive experience in this area and are here to help your child with the most appropriate option when such treatment is needed.
  • Emergency Visits When accidents happen, or when your child is in pain our Pediatric Dental team are here to help you and your family during emergencies. We accept patients on short notice at times of emergency and have Specialists on call to help save your child’s teeth and restore his/her level of comfort quickly.
  • Community Health Workshops Our SmileSchool program was designed to help children to understand about the effects of diet and the oral health care habits that are needed to keep their teeth, gums, and bodies healthy.
  • FREE Oral Health Kit At hellosmile our extensive community outreach efforts include free oral health screenings, education, and prevention at local daycares, schools, nurseries, and community agencies.
  • Z3 Mommy & Me Program Most parents are not aware of how important good dental care is to the health of the mother and baby. We have created a SmileSchool program to not only educate moms but also give them tools and resources to grow healthy babies.
