The practice

Endodontic treatments save patients their natural teeth and in the long run much expense, provided an experienced and knowledgeable clinician performs these procedures. Due to complexity of the procedure, certain cases require a referral to the specialist with advanced training in endodontics, such as complex molar root canals, re treatments and microsurgeries.

Utilizing microscope in endodontics has reduced some inherent problems in performing root canal treatments, such as finding hidden or missed canals. Therefore, it has increased the success rate and eliminated chances of error and/or incomplete treatment.

Having had the experience of practicing general dentistry has made Dr. Vaziri a much better clinician as far as treatment planning, predicting the final outcome, and certainly patient management is concerned.
