Medical Care

The goal of CHC/SEIA is to provide healthcare that focuses on prevention and early identification of common medical problems such as diabetes, high blood pressure, obesity and heart disease.

We provide a variety of services for our adults which includes:

Since 2003, we have provided a medical and dental home to patients that are unable to access health care elsewhere in the community. We have a team of providers that offers excellent, quality care for our patients, regardless of their financial situation.

What to bring to your first appointment:

Proof of income (or no income)

If you are going to sign-up for our Sliding Fee Program.

Health records

Advanced directive

Only applicable if you have an advanced directive.

Insurance information

Only if applicable. This includes private insurance, Medicare, and Medicaid.

Picture ID

Medications and/or supplements

Bring in current bottles of prescribed medications and/or supplements.

Social security number

Minor patients must be accompanied by parent at first appointment

Step-parent is not allowed to bring to first appointment unless consent to treat is previously signed and individual is listed as authorized person.
