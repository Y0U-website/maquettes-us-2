About Mini Miners

  • Comfort During Treatment: Children can choose their movie to watch on ceiling-mounted TVs and select their toothpaste and fluoride flavors.
  • Parent Involvement: Unlike some practices, parents are not just allowed but welcomed into treatment rooms. We work in partnership with parents to help kids have healthy teeth.
  • ​ ​ Modern Techniques : Some of the modern techniques we offer are Hall Crowns (a technique that doesn t require numbing or drilling), White Crowns for front and molar teeth, all levels of sedation , digital x-rays, ICON to bleach out white spots on front teeth, the latest and best tooth materials, Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF), and gentle approaches.​
  • Special Needs Expertise : We take pride in our ability to provide compassionate, adapted care for children with special needs , creating individualized approaches for each child.
