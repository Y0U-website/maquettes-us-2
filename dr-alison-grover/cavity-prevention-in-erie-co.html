Healthy Habits at Home: Your Family s Cavity Prevention Toolkit

Cavity prevention doesn t stop at our office. Here are the daily habits our pediatric dentists recommend to keep your child s smile healthy between visits:

  • Brush Twice a Day Use a soft-bristled, age-appropriate toothbrush with a rice-grain (under 3) or pea-sized (3+) amount of fluoride toothpaste. Parents should help with brushing until around age 7 8, when kids develop the dexterity to brush well on their own.
  • Floss Daily Start flossing as soon as two teeth touch. Floss picks or flossers designed for kids can make it easier and more fun. This removes plaque and food from between teeth where toothbrushes can t reach.
  • Watch the Sugar Frequent snacking on sugary or starchy foods feeds the bacteria that cause cavities. Choose water over juice or soda, and limit sticky or sugary treats - especially between meals. When treats happen, rinse with water afterward!
  • Don t Skip Checkups Regular 6-month visits to Mini Miners allow us to catch early signs of decay, apply protective treatments, and reinforce healthy habits. Plus, kids get to pick a prize from our toy tower - making the dentist something to look forward to!

Water is your child s best drink for cavity prevention - it rinses away food particles and keeps saliva flowing all day long.
