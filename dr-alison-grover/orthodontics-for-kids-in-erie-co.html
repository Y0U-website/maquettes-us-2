FAQs: INVISALIGN & BRACES FOR KIDS

Will my child still need braces as a teenager? Many children who receive Phase 1 treatment will still need some orthodontic work as teenagers (Phase 2), but it’s typically shorter and less complex than it would have been without early intervention.

Is Phase 1 treatment painful? Most children experience minimal discomfort with Phase 1 appliances. Any soreness is typically mild and temporary.

How long does Phase 1 treatment take? Treatment length varies based on the specific issues being addressed, but typically ranges from 9-18 months.
