What Early Detection Catches

A small cavity now, not a root canal late

Early gum trouble reversed, before teeth get loose

Oral cancer found when it is still very treatable

Grinding damage caught before you need crowns

Bite issues fixed before they turn into jaw pain

Old fillings replaced before decay grows underneath

What Your Visit Looks Like

Here is how it goes from the moment you walk in:

We Say Hello

You get greeted, seated, and given time to settle in. Dr. Khan talks with you first what brings you in, any concerns, what has changed since your last visit.

Quick X-Rays

Digital X-rays are fast and use up to 90 % less radiation than the old film kind. Images pop up on the screen right away.

The Full Look

Dr. Khan carefully checks every tooth, all your gums, and your soft tissues. She looks for cavities, cracks, gum issues, oral cancer signs, and how your bite lines up.

The Conversation

Dr Khan will walk you through everything . X-rays, findings, and her recommendations. So that you can leave with a clear plan and a clear cost.

Why It Is Worth It

Saves You Money

Simple math: a filling costs less than a crown, and a crown costs less than an implant. In other words, finding things early is almost always the cheapest path.

Keeps Your Teeth

Regular exams are the single biggest thing you can do to keep your own natural teeth for life.

Good for the Rest of You

Gum disease has been linked to heart trouble, diabetes, and stroke risk. Taking care of your mouth helps take care of you.
