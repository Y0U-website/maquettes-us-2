Digital X-Rays

X-rays are pictures that show what is going on inside and between your teeth. They catch things we cannot see just by looking like small cavities, bone loss, or infections. We only take them when they are truly needed.

  • Show hidden cavities
  • Check bone under gums
  • Very low radiation
  • You wear a protective apron

💡 Why we take digital X-rays instead of the old kind

Digital X-rays use about 90 % less radiation than the film ones from years ago. The pictures come up right away on a screen, so Dr. Khan can show you exactly what she sees. And you wear a lead apron the whole time for extra safety.

Signs It Is Time for a Cleaning

You are probably due for a cleaning if any of these sound like you:

You have not been to the dentist in over 6 months

You see yellow or brown near your gum line

Your teeth feel sensitive to hot or cold

You have a family history of gum problems

You just want that fresh, smooth-teeth feeling

What Happens During Your Visit

Here is exactly what to expect from start to finish:

You Get Comfortable

You are greeted and seated in a comfortable chair. Our team asks how you have been and if anything is bothering you.

Quick X-Rays (If Needed)

If it has been a while since your last set, we take quick digital X-rays. Fast, safe, and you wear a protective apron the whole time.

Gentle Cleaning

Our hygienist carefully removes plaque and tartar. Then your teeth get polished until they feel smooth and shiny. Flossing finishes the job.

A Quick Exam

Dr. Khan takes a look at your teeth and gums, checks the X-rays, and talks with you about anything she noticed. If everything looks good, we see you in six months.

Why Regular Visits Are Worth It

Saves You Money

Cleanings are cheap. Fixing a big cavity or gum problem later is not. A little prevention now saves a lot of cost down the road.

Keeps Your Teeth

Regular visits are the biggest reason people keep their own teeth for life. It is the simplest habit with the best payoff.

Less Pain, Less Worry

Catching things early means you avoid tooth pain and the trips to the drug store for pain relievers. You also skip a lot of stress.

⚠ What happens if you skip cleanings for years

Tartar keeps building up. Gums get sore and start to pull away from teeth. Small cavities grow into big ones. Small gum problems turn into gum disease that can even cause tooth loss. The longer you wait, the more work it takes to get things back on track. If it has been a while, just come in. We are gentle, and we will not make you feel bad about it.

How to Take Care of Your Teeth Between Visits

Cleanings help, but what you do at home matters too. Here is what makes the biggest difference:

Brush twice a day for 2 minutes use a soft-bristled brush and fluoride toothpaste. Do not scrub hard gentle circles work best

Floss once a day your brush cannot reach between teeth. Flossing gets the stuff your brush misses

Change your brush every 3 months when the bristles bend, they stop cleaning well

Drink water after sugary drinks water washes away the sugar that feeds cavity-causing germs

Come in every 6 months do not wait for pain. Two visits a year keeps small problems small
