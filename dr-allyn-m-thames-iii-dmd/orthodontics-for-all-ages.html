Signs to Look for in Your Child’s Smile

Here are a few key indicators to look for to know if your child needs orthodontic treatment:

  • Crowded or missing teeth
  • Premature or delayed loss of baby teeth
  • Challenges in biting or chewing
  • Speech difficulties
  • Prolonged pacifier use
  • Narrow palette
  • Underbite
  • Crossbite
  • Open bite

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a discreet option for teens who want a low-profile way to straighten their teeth. These aligners are custom-made for your teen’s smile and can be removed for meals, brushing, and special occasions.

Teen-specific features may include small tabs to allow for growing molars and blue wear indicators to help track daily use.

For teens who had Phase 1 treatment, this stage (Phase 2) fine-tunes alignment and bite so their results last a lifetime.
