Our Services

Do you want to straighten your teeth without having to wear metal braces? Aligners are the perfect way to straighten your teeth without the hassle of traditional braces. Our clear aligners are custom-made for each patient and are comfortable, removable, and virtually invisible. With aligners, you can enjoy eating, brushing, and flossing with ease, all while transforming your smile. Get started now and join thousands of happy patients who have chosen aligners for their orthodontic needs!

Feeling self-conscious about your smile? Let us help you reveal a brighter, whiter smile and restore your confidence! We offer a safe, effective and non-invasive whitening treatment that will leave you showing off pearly whites in no time.

With Zoom Whitening, It is a simple, non-invasive procedure that usually takes between 45 minutes to an hour . The process combines a special lamp, referred to as a Zoom light or Zoom laser, with a hydrogen peroxide-based whitening gel. Using both, the dentist will bleach each tooth and produce a brighter smile by the end of the treatment.
