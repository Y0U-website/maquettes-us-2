The practice

It is my greatest pleasure to have the opportunity to provide dental services for you and your family. I am Dr. Amanda Schwartz and I look forward to meeting you and building a relationship with the hope of addressing all your dental needs.

I am originally from the Twin Cities area, growing up in the southeast suburb of Apple Valley. I attended the University of North Dakota for my undergraduate degree and received my dental degree from Midwestern University in Arizona.

Upon graduation from dental school, my husband and I moved to Duluth, MN where I practiced family and cosmetic dentistry for more than 8 years. In 2022 we decided to move closer to where we both grew up and establish long–term roots with our children. We have three incredible daughters, Ava, Stella and Preslee.

I, along with the phenomenal staff from Dr. Brad Dodds look forward to continuing to provide you and your family with the same excellent care you have always received. My passion is your smile and I look forward to caring for you and your family.

Moving forward, you won’t notice too many changes, however our name has changed to Grayce Dental - the name has special meaning for my family and I am honored to continue serving the Chanhassen community. Please reach out with any questions or concerns, I look forward to meeting you and your family at your next dental visit.
