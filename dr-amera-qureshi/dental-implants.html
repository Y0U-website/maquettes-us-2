Nolensville, TN - Dental Implants

At Inspire Dental

A Dental implant is an artificial tooth root. Their shape can be compared to a screw. Implants bond with your natural jaw bone and become a strong base or root for an artificial tooth. Inspire Dental offers dental implant procedures at our practice in Nolensville, TN

WHAT SHOULD I EXPECT?

Our trusted staff at Inspire Dental will carefully examine your mouth in order to personalize a procedure specifically for you. Using advanced technology, your dentist will implant the artificial root into your jaw. After a few weeks of waiting for the implant to bond to the jawbone, your dentist will attach a crown to complete the procedure.

HOW LONG WILL THE IMPLANT LAST?

Given the proper care and hygiene, an implant can last up to a lifetime.

WILL MY INSURANCE COVER MY DENTAL IMPLANTS?

Dental implants involve an advanced procedure that restores lost or damaged teeth and affects your overall oral health, meaning most insurance companies will cover some or all of the cost. Our Experts at Inspire Dental are happy to discuss your insurance coverage with you during your visit.
