During these appointments, our experienced hygienists will carefully remove plaque and tartar buildup that can’t be eliminated with regular brushing and flossing alone. This is the first step in keeping your teeth and gums healthy and your smile bright.

WHY IS A ROUTINE CLEANING IMPORTANT?

Everyday habits such as eating and drinking cause plaque buildup throughout the day. Plaque is a sticky, colorless, or pale-yellow film that forms on and in between your teeth. Without proper treatment, plaque turns into tartar, which can only be removed by dental professionals. Tartar attacks tooth enamel and can cause cavities and gum disease. At Inspire Dental we are committed to preventing these oral health issues before they begin with regular teeth cleanings and oral examinations.

WILL IT HURT?

Slight sensitivity is typically normal, however pain during routine dental cleanings is not normal and generally is an indicator that there may be underlying inflammation or bone loss. We offer light sedation or anesthesia to keep your visit with us as comfortable as possible.

WHEN IS IT TIME FOR A DENTAL CLEANING?

Experts recommend a routine cleaning and examination no less than twice a year. However, there are more ways to determine if you’re due for a visit. Is flossing painful? Do you notice bleeding from your gums? Do you frequently notice odors or unpleasant tastes in your mouth? These symptoms might mean it’s time for a teeth cleaning.
