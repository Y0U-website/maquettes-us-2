Follow Keke’s journey as she explores her options for her future and discover how she comes to a conclusion about her vocation. This is a captivating and heartwarming story about how parental encouragement can unlock a child’s potential to dream the biggest dreams possible!

Keke’s smile and determination are a lesson to believe in yourself and each other because your dreams and possibilities are limitless.

Philanthropy Dr. Ami’s Vision for Bonne Foi

Bonne Foi means good faith in the Togolese Republic and is the name of the orphanage in Togo that Dr. Ami co-founded. Unlike orphanages that focus solely on immediate needs like food and shelter, Bonne Foi provides children a rich opportunity for a quality education, leading to college or careers and a fair chance for a bright future.
