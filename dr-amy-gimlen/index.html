Dr. Amy Gimlen is a diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics, an alum of both UCLA and USC, a professor at UCLA Orthodontics, on the craniofacial team at St Johns and a mom of two.

Dr. Gimlen’s mission is to provide excellent, cutting-edge orthodontic care in a personal and comfortable environment. She believes that orthodontics positively impacts patients personal growth and self-confidence in addition to beautiful smiles.

Dr. Gimlen grew up in the South Bay and has also lived in Hawaii and in Northern California. She and her husband, Scott, reside in Manhattan Beach with their kids, Kiele and Kai and dog, Stevie. In addition to her commitment to serving her patients and her community, Dr. Gimlen enjoys traveling and the beach.
