About New Image Advanced Dental

New Image Advanced Dental provides compassionate, modern dentistry grounded in integrity and comfort. Led by Dr. Amy Wilson, our team focuses on personalized care, clear communication, and gentle techniques. The mission is simple: create healthy, confident smiles through attentive service, thorough education, and treatment plans tailored to each patient&#x27;s goals. Patients choose our practice for a welcoming atmosphere, efficient visits, and a collaborative approach that respects time, budget, and preferences.

Located in the heart of Trinidad, Colorado, our practice has proudly served the community and surrounding areas since 1993. Over more than three decades, Dr. Wilson has built lasting relationships with patients of all ages, from young children receiving their first dental exams to adults seeking advanced restorative and cosmetic treatments. We believe that exceptional dental care begins with listening — understanding your concerns, answering your questions, and involving you in every step of your treatment plan.

Our office is equipped with the latest dental technology, including digital imaging, same-day crowns, and advanced diagnostic tools that allow us to deliver faster, more accurate, and more comfortable care. Whether you need a routine cleaning, dental implants, orthodontic treatment, or emergency care, our team is prepared to help you achieve optimal oral health in a relaxed and supportive environment.

Beyond traditional dentistry, Dr. Wilson expanded her practice to include wellness and aesthetic services through New Image Health and Wellness . This sister business offers Functional Medicine, ozone therapy, massage therapy, and facial aesthetic treatments such as Botox and Sculptra. This holistic approach reflects Dr. Wilson&#x27;s commitment to caring for the whole person, not just their teeth.

We welcome new and returning patients and look forward to becoming your trusted partner in health. Call New Image Advanced Dental to schedule your appointment today: 719-846-7387 .
