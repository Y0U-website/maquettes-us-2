Welcome to New Image Advanced Dental

We believe your surroundings matter as much as your care. Our office at 417 University St., Suite 1 in Trinidad, CO was designed with patient comfort, safety, and efficiency in mind. From the moment you walk through the door, you will find a clean, welcoming environment equipped with modern technology and thoughtful touches that make every visit a positive experience.

What You Will Find Inside

Comfortable Reception Area

Our front lobby provides a relaxed, inviting space where you can check in and settle in before your appointment. Our front desk team is always available to answer questions, verify insurance, and help you feel at ease from the start.

Modern Treatment Rooms

Each operatory is equipped with digital X-ray sensors, intra-oral cameras, and chairside monitors so you can see exactly what Dr. Wilson sees during your exam. Our treatment rooms are designed for both comfort and precision, whether you are in for a routine cleaning or an advanced procedure.

Advanced Dental Technology

Our investment in modern equipment, including digital imaging, panoramic X-rays, and cavity detection technology, allows us to diagnose and treat conditions faster, more accurately, and with less discomfort. You can learn more about our imaging capabilities on our dental imaging page .

Wellness and Med Spa Suite

In addition to our dental operatories, our facility includes dedicated rooms for wellness services such as IV infusion therapy and massage, as well as med spa treatments including Botox, dermal fillers, Morpheus8, and facials. Everything is conveniently located under one roof.

Sterilization and Safety

Patient safety is a top priority. Our sterilization area follows strict infection control protocols that meet or exceed OSHA and ADA guidelines. All instruments are thoroughly cleaned, sterilized, and stored in sealed packaging until use.
