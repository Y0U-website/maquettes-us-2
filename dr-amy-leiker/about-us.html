About Today’s Dentistry

Who We Are

Your Topeka Dental Office

We’re a team of caring, compassionate individuals who are dedicated to serving smiles throughout Topeka and surrounding communities. With years of combined experience, our dentists are knowledgeable, skilled, and accurate in every consultation and treatment they deliver.

We don’t sell dentistry. That means you’ll never receive treatment you don’t need, and we’ll never pressure you to make more expensive decisions regarding your oral health. Instead, we work closely with you to find solutions that work for you. We believe in patient education and engagement, and that’s why many of our current patients have been returning for years!

We deliver modern general and cosmetic dentistry to families, as we begin seeing children at age 3. Our office provides a relaxing, comforting dental experience for children, and allows them to feel welcome. Set your children on the right path to lifelong oral health by having them seen by one of our dentists, and teach them the importance of great dental care.

We’ve been practicing dentistry in Topeka since 1978, and we love our community and our patients. It’s like one large carefree family in our office, and we’re happy to have you join! From general and preventive dentistry , including routine cleanings, to cosmetic dentistry like smile whitening, we have every service you need to make your smile sparkle. And we do it all with a patient-focused attitude, which keeps you and your dental goals at the forefront. We’ll help your entire family develop great dental habits and deliver great smiles that last a lifetime.

We’re ready to help you achieve your dental goals! Call us today!
