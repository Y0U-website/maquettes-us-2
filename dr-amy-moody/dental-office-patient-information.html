Provided we have received all of your insurance information and that it remains updated, we are happy to file your insurance claims and make every effort to assist you in obtaining reimbursement from your insurance company. We accept all major dental insurance plans and are considered “in network” with BCBS, Delta Dental, and Humana.

We file electronically the day of services and insurance payments are usually received within 30 days. You will be billed for any remaining balance after 30 days. Please let us know if the statement does not reflect an insurance payment or if there are any questions concerning the balance. You are responsible for any remaining balance either paid or unpaid by insurance and it is due upon receipt.

We do not have a contract with your insurance company, only you do, nor are your benefits determined by our office. We are here to help you understand your insurance benefits, limits and deductibles; however, it is your responsibility to provide us with your plan package and information required in aiding in your understanding. Additionally, this is necessary to appropriately estimate your portion, which is always due at time of service. We cannot ever guarantee insurance payment for services.

It is important to remember that insurances are meant to aid in paying for dental care and that no insurance pays for all services at 100 %.

Payment is expected and due at time dental services are rendered.

For your convenience we accept:

Cash, Personal Checks, Visa, Master Card, and Discover.
