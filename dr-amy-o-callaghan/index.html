The practice

Dr. Amy O’Callaghan grew up in New Canaan, CT. She obtained her BS in Biology at Virginia Tech where she graduated Magna Cum Laude. She returned to Connecticut to obtain her Doctorate of Dental Medicine at the University of Connecticut. Dr. O’Callaghan went on to complete her post-graduate training in pediatric dentistry at the University of Connecticut and Connecticut Children’s Medical Center.…

Dr. O’Callaghan is committed to organized dentistry and community involvement. She serves on the board of the Connecticut Society of Pediatric Dentists and is President of the Shoreline Dental Society. Dr. O’Callaghan was voted by her peers as one of Connecticut’s Top Pediatric Dentists for Connecticut Magazine every year since 2014.

Dr. Amy currently resides in Guilford with her husband, two children, and one fur baby. When she isn’t at the office, you can find her running, baking, and dancing in the kitchen with her children.

Dr. Amy’s goal is for every child to have a positive and fun experience at the dentist.

Dr. Michael Pawlowski is originally from Long Island, NY. He graduated from Stony Brook University School of Dental Medicine where he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. Following this, he completed his pediatric dentistry residency at Yale-New Haven Hospital, where he served as chief resident.

While in college, Dr. Pawlowski double majored in biology and education and frequently uses these skills to teach patients and parents about the importance of oral health.

Usually the first person in the room to crack a joke, Dr. Pawlowski believes humor is an important part of making patients feel comfortable. He is always ready to share a laugh while keeping little smiles bright.
