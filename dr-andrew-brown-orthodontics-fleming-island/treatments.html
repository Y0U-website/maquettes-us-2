Braces Metal Braces Ceramic Braces Damon Braces Traditional metal braces have stood the test of time and are very effective for correcting many orthodontic issues. Our practice uses a surgical-grade stainless steel bracket that is the smallest on the market to ensure a comfortable feel. You even have the ability to customize colors so you can express your personality while undergoing treatment.

For those that prefer a more clandestine option, ceramic braces are the go-to. Made of tooth-colored materials, these braces blend in with the teeth, making them a good fit for those who wouldn’t like to draw attention to their braces. They are just as effective as metal braces, but with the added benefit of being less noticeable.

Damon braces are more advanced and adapt a unique self-ligating technique to align teeth. They require fewer adjustments than traditional braces, which means fewer appointments and less time in the chair. And with their streamlined appearance, they are a popular choice for patients who want both discrete and more effective braces..

Invisalign ®

Looking for a discreet and convenient way to straighten your teeth? Invisalign might be the perfect solution for you. Invisalign uses a series of clear aligners that gradually shift your teeth into the desired position. Unlike traditional metal braces, Invisalign aligners are virtually invisible, allowing you to straighten your teeth without anyone noticing.

Vivera Retainers Vivera retainers are a kind of clear, removable retainer made from a patented thermoplastic material that provides superior strength and durability. They are custom-made to fit the teeth and are designed to maintain the results of orthodontic treatment. Unlike other types of retainers, they’re made using the same technology as Invisalign clear aligners and guarantee optimal retention. Contact us today to learn more about our Vivera retainer options.
