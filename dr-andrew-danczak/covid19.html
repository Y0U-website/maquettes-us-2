Our Safety Commitment

At our practice, we have always placed great importance on infection control and prevention. Our infection control processes are designed to ensure that you receive the best possible care in a safe and comfortable environment.

We follow the guidelines and recommendations of various leading health authorities such as the American Dental Association (ADA), the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), and the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA). We stay up-to-date with the latest developments and guidance issued by these organizations to ensure that our infection control procedures are always current and effective.

We are always here to answer any questions you may have about the steps we take to keep you and every patient safe in our practice. If you need to schedule an appointment, please call our office at 440-888-4545.

Thank you for your trust and loyalty as our patient. We look forward to treating you and your loved ones, friends, and neighbors with the same level of care and attention to safety that we have always provided.
