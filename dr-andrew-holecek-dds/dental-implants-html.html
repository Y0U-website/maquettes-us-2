Are Dental Implants the Right Choice For Replacing Missing Teeth?

How Do Dental Implants Work?

Dental implants are the only reconstructive option today that replicates the entire structure of your natural tooth, root and all. The implant itself serves as the root and is surgically inserted by our doctors (no outside specialist required) into the original tooth’s socket so that it can form a bond with your natural tissue and bone. Once the patient has healed, a custom restoration is placed on top of the implant, recompleting your confident smile in a healthy and beautifully natural fashion.

How Can I Get My Denture to Fit Better?

If you’re struggling with a denture that’s constantly slipping, dental implants could be the right solution for you. At Sage Dental Care, our doctors can design an implant-retained restoration that attaches to placed implants for much greater stability and permanence. We may be able to alter your existing denture, or a new prosthetic could be necessary. In any case, the benefits are numerous your oral health will be improved, your oral function will flourish (allowing you to eat and speak confidently again), and there’s no longer any frustrating slippage to worry about.
