We restore your teeth and your smile.

We help patients maintain strong, healthy teeth and gums. Utilizing state-of-the-art prosthodontic care, we restore natural teeth and replace lost teeth with natural-looking teeth. We provide a variety of services to enhance and restore smiles:

  • Implant Dentistry
  • Crowns
  • Bridges
  • Partial and Complete Dentures
  • Porcelain Veneers
  • Cosmetic Dentistry

Implant Dentistry

Dental implants can give patients a confident and radiant smile. Implants enhance your ability to eat comfortably, conserve bone structure, and maintain a clean, healthy mouth.

Implants provide support where teeth are missing without putting pressure on your remaining natural teeth. They can replace a single missing tooth, multiple teeth, or a complete arch.

Dental implants are small prosthetic posts placed into the bone of the upper or lower jaw. The posts serve as an anchor for replacement teeth, just as the root anchored in the lost natural teeth. Dental implants are appropriate for almost anyone who is currently missing teeth or is at risk of losing teeth.

Crowns

We replace large, worn-out fillings with natural-looking ceramic crowns. These state-of-the-art, high-strength, metal-free crowns will restore teeth and eliminate lines at the gum line to provide a completely natural-looking appearance.

Bridges

Have you lost teeth? Missing teeth can result in the remaining teeth drifting out of position and causing problems with chewing. A fixed bridge can replace one tooth or several teeth that have been lost, preventing these problems, and eliminating unsightly gaps.

Partial and Complete Dentures

Several denture types include complete dentures, special clasp-less (wireless) removable partial dentures using precision attachments, and overdentures supported by either natural teeth or implants.

  • Teeth for chewing and breaking down food
  • Structure and shape to facial tissues
  • A beautiful smile
  • Improved quality of speech

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a great way to repair or enhance the appearance of front teeth, giving you the bright smile you always wanted.

  • Reduce or Eliminate Spaces Between Teeth (Diastemas)
  • Repair Broken or Chipped Teeth
  • Whiten Dark, Yellow, Permanently Stained or Discolored Teeth
  • Correct Misshapen or Crooked Teeth
  • Perform Cosmetic Services (Bleaching, Bonding, Sealants)
