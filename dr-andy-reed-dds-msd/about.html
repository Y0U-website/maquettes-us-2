What Sets Us Apart

Welcome to our practice, where you’re treated like family! Our commitment to cutting-edge care and thoughtfully tailored scheduling sets us apart from the rest. Our team is dedicated to keeping your child’s smile healthy and making every visit enjoyable. We will work closely with you to care for your child at the most suitable appointment times, ensuring a seamless and delightful experience for everyone.

To help us provide the best care and cooperation during your child’s visit, we’ve designed specific time slots for different age groups. We appreciate your flexibility in scheduling to support a smoother experience for everyone!

ABPD Certification

Dr. Andy and Dr. Crystal both hold American Board of Pediatric Dentistry (ABPD) certifications. This prestigious certification emphasizes our commitment to providing high standards of dental care tailored specifically for children. It reflects our dedication to continuous learning and excellence in pediatric dental health. In the fall of 2024, Dr. Reed renewed his Diplomate status, marking his second decade of specialized pediatric dental practice.

Dr. Andy

Welcome to the friendly world of Sumner Pediatric Dentistry, led by Dr. Andy – the fun-loving dentist who always puts your child’s health and comfort first. With a passion for engaging kids and making dental visits enjoyable, Dr. Andy brings expertise and warmth to every appointment. His dedication to pediatric dentistry ensures that every child leaves with a brighter, healthier smile.

Dr. Crystal

Originally hailing from California with a wealth of experience from across the globe, Dr. Crystal is the heart of our practice. She pursued her biochemistry degree at UC Riverside before earning her dentistry credentials with honors from Harvard School of Dental Medicine. Dr. Crystal’s passion for pediatric dentistry is matched only by her dedication to creating a fun and engaging dental environment for kids. She’s also known for her green thumb in gardening, her baking experiments, and cheering on her children at their many activities.
