Frequently Asked Questions About Implant Dentistry

What makes dental implants different from other tooth replacement options?

Dental implants are unique because they fuse with the jawbone, creating a stable foundation that feels and functions like natural teeth. Unlike bridges or dentures, they do not rely on adjacent teeth for support and help preserve bone structure, providing both aesthetic and long-term functional benefits.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure. Local anesthesia is used to ensure comfort, and post-operative symptoms are usually mild and manageable with over-the-counter medication. Many individuals compare the experience to a routine tooth extraction and are surprised by how quickly they recover.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The complete process typically spans several months, depending on individual healing time and any additional treatments needed. After the implant post is placed, a healing period of a few months allows it to integrate with the bone. Once healed, the permanent restoration is attached to complete the process.

Can smokers get dental implants?

Yes, but smoking may increase the risk of complications such as delayed healing or implant failure. Smoking affects blood flow and bone health, which are critical for successful integration. Patients who smoke are encouraged to quit or reduce tobacco use to improve outcomes and ensure the longevity of the implant.
