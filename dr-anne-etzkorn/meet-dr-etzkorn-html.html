Westminster Family Dentistry

Dr. Etzkorn is also trained as a fine artist, having exhibited and sold artwork at the Delaplane Center for the Arts. She is a native of Frederick, Maryland and graduated Magna Cum Laude from her undergraduate studies at James Madison University. She has received over 180 hours in additional specialized training in areas of advanced dentistry techniques including laser dentistry, sedation, root canal therapy, and Invisalign.

Dr. Etzkorn started this practice in 2013 and has been in practice for over 10 years. Before opening this office, she was practicing with another general dentist who focused on implant dentistry in Solomon’s Island, MD. Dr. Etzkorn provides the full range of dental services including fillings, crowns, cosmetic procedures, pediatric care, root canals, etc.

Dr. Etzkorn has two boys, Wesley and Oliver, with her husband, Jeremy, who is a pilot and owns the local flight school, Dream Aviation. In their free time, they enjoy travelling, spending time with extended family and generally keeping their active boys busy!
