Orthodontic care aims to improve both the function and appearance of the teeth and jaw, contributing to better oral health and overall well-being. It can include traditional braces, clear aligners, retainers, and other appliances to gradually move teeth into their ideal positions. These treatments benefit both children and adults, helping maintain a healthy smile that lasts a lifetime.

Dental implants are often recommended when you have one or more missing teeth. They provide several benefits and can be a preferable option over other tooth replacement methods like dentures or bridges.

There are various advantages of dental implants including the preservation of adjacent teeth and longevity. Unlike traditional fixed bridges, which require the reduction of adjacent healthy teeth, implants do not affect surrounding teeth.

Implants also have a high success rate and typically last longer than bridges or removable dentures, which may need replacement over time.
