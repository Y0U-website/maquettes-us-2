Meet the Doctors

Dr. Thomas K. Turner

Dr. Thomas K. Turner graduated from the Ohio State University School of Dentistry in 1994. After graduating, he completed a General Practice Residency at the Medical College of Ohio. Since then, Dr. Turner has been enjoying private practice in Toledo. He provides high quality dental care while keeping up with the latest technology and education. Dr. Turner is also a board member of Toledo Dental Society

Even though dentistry is a major focus for Dr. Thomas, he shares many other passions in life. First and foremost is his family. He married his high school sweetheart, and is a father of four. The family enjoys a wide range of sports, outdoor activities, traveling and seeing new places. Dr. Thomas keeps an eventful schedule, but always has time to put your dental needs first when needed.

Dr. Anthony C. Turner

Dr. Anthony C. Turner graduated from the University of Toledo in 1991 with a bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, specializing in marketing. He then earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the Ohio State University in 1997. Upon returning to Toledo, he joined his brother in private practice.

Dr. Tony married his high school sweetheart and is a father of four. Dr. Tony enjoys all kinds of sports, whether it is coaching his children, watching sports on television (go Bucks!), or playing them himself. The family loves to share their fun with friends and family by having them over for cookouts and fun times.

While at the office, you can expect honesty and integrity from Drs. Turner and their staff. Even though the doctors can treat most of your dental needs, they have a passion for cosmetic dentistry, including porcelain veneers, crowns, bridgework, and tooth-colored fillings.

You can bet that the office wants you to have a pleasant and relaxing experience while getting superior care. Drs. Tony and Tom are proud members of the Toledo Dental Society, the Ohio Dental Association, the American Dental Association, and the Great Black Swamp Study Club.

Dr. Hannah C. Becker

Dr. Hannah C. Becker graduated from Miami University with a bachelor’s degree in biochemistry, minoring in French. She obtained a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Indiana University. One of her favorite extracurricular programs in which she participated during dental school was the Shelter Sealant program—a program that provides free preventive dental care, such as oral health education, exams, fluoride treatments, and sealants, to children of homeless shelters and shelters for victims of domestic violence in the Indianapolis area.

Dr. Hannah grew up in northwest Ohio with two younger brothers and one younger sister. She enjoys spending time with her family, playing soccer, traveling, and drinking coffee.
