Learn more here: Dental Foundation of Oregon

The Ochoco Dental team has helped create, organize, and sponsor the fun run/walk since its inception in 2013. This single event has raised hundreds of thousands of dollars for distribution through local community grants as well as support for The Tooth Taxi, CCF Crook County on the Move, school sports teams and PE programs, and CCPRD Happy Healthy Hearts Program (sports scholarships).

  • Since the Tooth Taxi launched in late fall 2008, it has visited over 254 schools/sites all over the State of Oregon, and served over 14,000 children while delivering over $4,053,000 in donated dental services.
