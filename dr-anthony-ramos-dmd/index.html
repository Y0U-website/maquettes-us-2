The practice

Dedicated to lifelong learning, Dr. Ramos has completed advanced training through Kois Center, Spear Education, Seattle Study Club, Healthy Start, and CEREC Doctors. He also served as an Associate Faculty Member at OHSU School of Dentistry and remains actively involved in professional organizations and continuing education to ensure his patients receive the highest standard of care.

The balance of science and art is of upmost importance to Dr. Ramos, both in dental practice and daily life. He is especially proud of the Prineville Paddy Pint Run and its relationship between wellness and community involvement.

Dr. Mark Eilers has been providing exceptional general and cosmetic dental care since 2005. A graduate of Oregon Health & Science University School of Dentistry, he is committed to delivering high-quality, patient-centered care while building lasting relationships founded on trust and compassion.

In addition to private practice, Dr. Eilers has advanced expertise in forensic odontology, completing a fellowship at the University of Texas Health Science Center. He is a Diplomate of the American Board of Forensic Odontology, serves as an odontology consultant for medical examiners, and has contributed as an educator and expert in forensic dentistry.

Dedicated to lifelong learning, Dr. Eilers has completed advanced continuing education through the Kois Center , one of the nation’s premier programs in restorative and interdisciplinary dentistry, and remains committed to providing patients with the highest standard of care.
