Saddle Brook, NJ Dentist

Dr. Anthony Randazzo

Dr. Anthony Randazzo is a Bergen county native who was born and raised in Elmwood Park, NJ. He attended St. Leo’s School in Elmwood Park for 9 years prior to beginning his pre-dental / pre-medical journey. He attended the Academy of Medical Science Technology (AMST) at the Bergen County Academies in Hackensack. NJ. While undergoing this rigorous curriculum, he completed various internship and externship opportunities at local hospitals, such as Hackensack University Medical Center, Bergen Regional Medical Center, and Thomas Jefferson University.

He graduated Magna Cum Laude at Rutgers University in New Brunswick, NJ with degrees in Psychology and Biology. He underwent extensive research in psychology and continually uses his past psychology training to help alleviate the stresses of dental anxiety and phobia in patients.

He continued his academics at Rutgers University by graduating from the Rutgers School of Dental Medicine (formally known as the UMDNJ) in Newark, NJ. Afterwards, he furthered his clinical and educational training by completing a General Practice Residency at the Hackensack University Medical Center. He now serves as a faculty advisor at the Hackensack University Medical Center dental program.

Dr. Randazzo is very involved in our community, frequently visiting local elementary schools to educate young students about the importance of oral health. He has also been invited to speak at the local AARP to talk about common topics in their age group, such as dry mouth, diabetes and its connection to periodontal disease, and osteoporosis. He also enjoys volunteering his time by traveling to other countries on surgical mission trips.

During his free time, Dr. Randazzo loves spending time with his wife, Melissa, and his two young children, Layla and Vinny. His wife is a Pediatrician and you may see them together during his volunteer opportunities with young students. Dr. Randazzo is an adventure seeker and enjoys skiing, hiking, boating, grilling, and traveling.

Dr. Randazzo is proficient in all facets of general dentistry, but takes special interest in cosmetic dentistry, oral surgery, and implantology.

Dr. Frank Feraco

Dr. Feraco was born in Nutley, NJ, graduating from Nutley High School in 2010. He received his undergraduate degree in biology from Rowan University in 2013, graduating Magna Cum Laude and with Honors. He received his DMD from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in 2019 and completed 2 years of GPR residency at the East Orange VA Medical Center in 2021.
