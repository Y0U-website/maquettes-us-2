Web Accessibility

Arrow Watkin Dental Associates is committed to making our website easy to use for everyone, including people with disabilities. We want all visitors to be able to find information clearly and navigate our site comfortably.

Accessibility Standards Our website is partially conformant with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. This means we work to meet recognized accessibility standards, but some parts of the site, such as certain third-party videos or media tools, may not be fully accessible.

Browser and Technology Support WatkinDental.com works best with the latest versions of popular web browsers, including Chrome, Firefox, and Safari. The site is designed to support assistive technologies such as screen readers (including JAWS, NVDA, and VoiceOver), speech recognition software, and alternative input devices. Some older browsers, including Internet Explorer, may not display the site as intended.

Need Help or Have Feedback? If you have trouble using our website or need information in a different format, we’re here to help.

We appreciate your feedback and are continually working to improve the accessibility of our website for all patients.
