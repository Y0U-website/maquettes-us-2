What’s the secret to having healthy and strong teeth? Regular checkups and exams with an experienced dentist. That’s why patients in the Jackson, TN area have relied on Dr. Art Luckman, DDS since 1985 for all their dental needs.

Do you have chipped or broken teeth? Are you missing a tooth or multiple teeth? We have cosmetic dentistry options to make your smile perfect again. Hide broken or discolored teeth with our crowns, or replace missing teeth with our bridges and dentures.

We work with most insurance companies to make your restorative treatments affordable (please note: we do not accept Medicare or TennCare).
