Frequently Asked Questions About Accelerated Orthodontics

How much faster is accelerated orthodontics compared to traditional braces?

Accelerated orthodontics can reduce treatment time by 30-50 % compared to traditional methods. While conventional braces typically require 18-36 months, accelerated techniques often achieve similar results in 6-18 months, depending on the complexity of your case and your compliance with treatment protocols.

Is accelerated orthodontics safe for children and teenagers?

Yes, accelerated orthodontics is safe for patients whose permanent teeth have fully erupted, typically around age 12-14. The micro-vibration technology and gentle acceleration techniques are designed to work with your body’s natural bone remodeling processes, making them safe for younger patients when properly supervised.

Will accelerated orthodontics cost more than traditional treatment?

While the initial investment may be slightly higher, accelerated orthodontics often provides better value due to shorter treatment time, fewer appointments, and faster results. Many patients find the time savings and convenience justify any additional cost, especially when considering the professional and personal benefits of achieving results sooner.

Can I eat normally with removable accelerated orthodontic devices?

Absolutely! One of the major advantages of removable accelerated orthodontic systems is the ability to eat whatever you want. Simply remove the device during meals, enjoy your food without restrictions, clean your teeth, and reinsert the device to continue your treatment.

How often do I need to wear the accelerated orthodontic device?

Most accelerated orthodontic devices require 20-22 hours of daily wear for optimal results. You can remove them for meals, brushing, flossing, and special occasions, but consistent wear is crucial for achieving the faster timeline that makes this treatment so appealing.

Will I experience pain with accelerated orthodontics?

Most patients experience significantly less discomfort with accelerated orthodontics compared to traditional braces. The gentle, consistent micro-vibrations actually help reduce inflammation and improve blood flow, leading to less pain and faster healing throughout the treatment process.

How do I clean and maintain my accelerated orthodontic device?

Cleaning is simple and straightforward. Remove the device, brush it gently with a soft toothbrush and mild soap or specialized cleaning solution, rinse thoroughly, and store in the provided case. Your regular oral hygiene routine remains unchanged since you can brush and floss normally without the device.

Can accelerated orthodontics fix severe orthodontic problems?

Accelerated orthodontics can address many orthodontic issues, from mild crowding to more complex cases. During your consultation, your orthodontist will evaluate your specific situation and determine if you’re a good candidate for accelerated treatment or if traditional methods would be more appropriate for your needs.

What happens if I lose or break my accelerated orthodontic device?

Don’t worry! Contact your orthodontist immediately to arrange for a replacement. Most practices can create new devices quickly, and temporary measures can often prevent treatment delays. Many practices offer replacement warranties or insurance to protect your investment.

What are the benefits of accelerated orthodontics?

The main benefits of fast braces are the reduction in orthodontic treatment times, enhanced patient comfort, and beautiful results. Speak with your dental professional to determine if you’re a good candidate for accelerated orthodontics.

What is accelerated orthodontics tooth movement

Accelerated ortho works by combining orthodontic appliances with additional special techniques to enhance tooth movement. Non-surgical accelerated orthodontic systems typically stimulate faster tooth movement through micro-vibrations delivered through a removable mouthpiece.

How soon will I see results with accelerated orthodontics?

Many patients notice initial improvements within 4-6 weeks of starting accelerated orthodontic treatment. Visible changes continue throughout the treatment process, with significant improvements often apparent within 3-4 months, much faster than traditional orthodontic methods.
