Dr. Petrie, DMD

Born and raised in Des Moines, WA, to an engineer father and artist mother, I grew up with two brothers and developed expertise in woodworking, mechanical engineering, and home construction. I earned a BS in Biology and Physiology from the University of Washington (2005), graduated from the University of Pittsburgh Dental School (2010), and completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) at Lutheran Medical Center in Spokane, WA (2010-2011).

My passion for dentistry, particularly orthodontics, emerged in elementary school. I began my career in pediatrics before managing a satellite office as an associate and purchasing my first practice in tacoma in 2013 at age 29. In 2015, I was diagnosed with stage II cancer and underwent surgery and chemotherapy—a profoundly humbling experience that reshaped my perspective.

I introduced clear aligner orthodontics to my practice in 2013 and served as president of the Northwest Aligners study club from 2015-2022. In 2013, I helped establish the board for the American Academy of Clear Aligners (AACA), the world’s largest clear aligner education organization focused on Invisalign. I became a Key Opinion Leader (KOL) for Propel Orthodontics in 2018 and began lecturing nationally. In 2023, I joined Candid Clear Aligners as a KOL and clinical educator faculty member.

As a national speaker and lecturer for Candid Pro and Glidewell, I have completed over 1,000 aligner cases and mentor doctors across the United States. My extensive continuing education encompasses hundreds of hours in advanced implantology, cosmetic dentistry, full-mouth reconstruction, and airway/sleep medicine. I am recognized as an expert in clear aligner therapy, which I view as a holistic approach to oral health that reduces future dental needs while improving function and airway management.

In 2019, my focus shifted toward personal development through extensive coaching and men’s leadership work, fostering my interest (and my wife’s) in health and wellness. This evolution influences how I mentor young dentists, giving them "permission to thrive" and providing the guidance I once needed.

I aim to expand our aligner services to promote overall health and wellness while delivering more conservative, longer-lasting dental care. Both offices now utilize laser dentistry for enhanced hygiene and soft tissue procedures. We plan to add Fotona laser technology to our tacoma location in 2026 to enhance our procedures, airway treatment, and aesthetic capabilities.
