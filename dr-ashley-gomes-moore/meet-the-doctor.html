Meet The Doctor

Ashley Gomes-Moore, DMD

Since opening Stencil Dental in Swansea, MA in 2014, Dr. Gomes-Moore has built a reputation throughout the South Coast for her gentle touch, meticulous attention to detail, and outstanding aesthetic results . Patients from Swansea, Somerset, Seekonk, Dighton, Rehoboth, and beyond trust her with everything from routine cleanings to full smile makeovers.

“My goal is to make every visit comfortable and help patients feel confident in their smile.”

A Dentist Who Combines Art & Science

What sets Dr. Gomes-Moore apart is her belief that great dentistry is both a science and an art form. Whether she is placing a dental implant, designing a smile with porcelain crowns and veneers, or straightening teeth with Invisalign, she approaches every case with the same careful eye for proportion, symmetry, and natural beauty. Her artistic background gives her an intuitive sense of how a smile fits a person’s face — and her scientific training ensures that every result is built to last.

"My goal is to make every visit comfortable and help patients feel confident in their smile." — Dr. Ashley Gomes-Moore, DMD
