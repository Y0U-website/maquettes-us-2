Additional Benefits of All on 4 Dental Implants

As already mentioned, unlike conventional dentures, All on 4 dental implants and dentures won’t slip or interfere with speech. Due to the open palette that exists with the All on 4 denture patient has greater ability to taste foods. Patient will have teeth that resemble natural teeth. The dental implants also prevent bone deterioration in the jaw and even help restore facial features by fighting resorption.

Alternatives and Potential Drawbacks to All on 4 Dental Implants

Though four implants provide support, it may well be that five to eight will provide significantly more, and this may be the option that Guzaitis Dental Group recommends. If the jawbone has atrophied, it is possible a bone graft can restore lost tissue. Patients should also understand that All on 4 dental implants replace what were fourteen natural root systems with just four points of support.
