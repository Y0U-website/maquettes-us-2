Making smiles in Delaware and Pennsylvania wider and brighter since 1972

Welcome to the Saltz Dental Center, conveniently located in Chester, PA. We are a complete family dental office whose goal is to provide the best possible care for our patients, in a fast and comfortable manner.

To do this, we have 4 dentists on staff. This allows us to take care of your immediate dental needs right away, often on your very first visit.

The Saltz Dental Center has a complete denture lab on its premises, which allows us to repair dentures while you wait, and make new dentures quickly and efficiently.

Other services offered are budget plans and a senior citizen discount. We are conveniently located off exit 6 of I-95 (close to Delaware), and only minutes south of the Philadelphia International Airport.
