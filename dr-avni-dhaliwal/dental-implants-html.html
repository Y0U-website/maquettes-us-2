What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the only form of tooth replacement that allows you to feel completely confident in the stability and longevity of your new smile. These titanium posts, which mimic the natural root structure of teeth, anchor in the jawbone to create a firm foundation. After some time, they provide the necessary support to keep your new implant crown, bridge, or denture sturdy and comfortably in place.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

The dental implant process can take up to a year to complete. The reason is that four distinct steps must be taken to ensure success. From the initial consultation to the dental implant surgery , the recovery time spent at home, and finally, the placement of your new restoration , each stage is essential.

You can expect your journey to look different than another patient’s, but these same four steps must be completed by everyone seeking a more permanent form of tooth replacement.

Benefits of Dental Implants

The benefits of dental implants are far greater than those of dental bridges and traditional dentures. Not only are they affixed to the jawbone for optimal stability, but they’re also designed to last decades. You don’t need to worry about your teeth slipping while eating, and you can enjoy a smile that looks as close to the real thing as possible. These are just a few of the advantages of this superior treatment.
