Pediatric Dental Services We offer a comprehensive range of pediatric dental services to meet the unique needs of children from infancy through adolescence. Our services include routine cleanings and exams, fluoride treatments, sealants, cavity fillings, and more.

Parents love Dr. Blen Pediatric Dentistry because we go above and beyond to make dental care fun. Our office is designed with kids in mind. Our commitment to personalized care means we take the time to get to know your child and create a dental plan tailored to their needs.
