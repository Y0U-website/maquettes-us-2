Regain Your Confident Smile with Our All-On-4 Dental Implants

I f you have lost teeth in a whole arch, the all-on-4 technique in Pasadena, California, offers a stable and natural teeth replacement. This revolutionary method only uses four implants per jaw to support a full arch dental replacement. Also known as full mouth implants, all-on-4 implants are ideal for anyone with extensive tooth loss when conventional dentures aren’t an option.

At Pasadena Prosthodontics, Drs. Christopher Marchack and Eugenia Chan offer full-mouth implants created with high-quality materials and the latest technologies. Visit our office today to learn more about our all-on-4 implants.

What are all-on-4 implants?

In the past, restoring an entire arch of teeth involved replacing every tooth lost with an implant. This route isn’t only expensive but extensive, requiring several months to heal. Today, the all-on-4 technique allows dentists to replace teeth in a whole arch with as few as four implants. Unlike conventional dentures saddled on your gums, implant-supported dentures are firmly anchored in the jawbone, providing ultimate stability.

The small number of implants significantly reduces treatment time and makes the treatment affordable. The disruptions to your everyday life are minimal as you leave our office with new teeth and require only a few follow-up appointments as your gums heal.

What does the all-on-4 treatment involve?

Your initial appointment involves treatment planning, taking into account your individual needs, implant placement location, and oral health. Traditional implants require a dense jawbone to support the osseointegration process. For full-mouth implants, the doctor strategically utilizes the available bone, often reducing the need for grafting.

On the day of surgery, the dentist will surgically place four implants at optimal positions in the jawbone. Then, we’ll attach a temporary denture to ensure you live a normal life as your implants heal. During healing, observe optimal oral hygiene, quit smoking, and honor follow-up appointments to support successful implantation.

After 4-6 months, you’ll return to our office to restore your implants with a permanent, custom-fitted denture. Now, you can enjoy a stable, beautiful smile that feels and looks like natural teeth!

Why choose all-on-4 implants?

The all-on-4 technique is the ultimate solution for those with extensive tooth loss after advanced gum disease, dental trauma, or widespread tooth decay. Unlike traditional dentures , the all-on-4 implants offer the following benefits.

  • They restore oral function to nearly 100 %.
  • Optimal stability when chewing, talking, or sneezing.
  • Implants preserve the jawbone in the area placed.
  • The all-on-4 technique is more affordable than conventional implants .
  • Long-lasting solution.
