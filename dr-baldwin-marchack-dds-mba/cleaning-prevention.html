Dental hygiene: Essential care for Pasadena, CA smiles

Dr. Baldwin Marchack and Dr. Christopher Marchack are happy to provide preventive care a few times annually, but you have the opportunity to maintain oral health 365 days a year. The dental hygiene team at Pasadena Prosthodontics and General Dentistry in Pasadena, CA is here to help!

Regular checkups

A hygiene visit begins with professional cleaning, called dental prophylaxis or “prophy.” Our hygienists are experienced clinicians, so skilled that they train others in the field. They use special instruments to gently scale away calculus – plaque that hardens into tartar above and below the gum line. They remove the sticky film of bacterial plaque that coats teeth, producing toxins that irritate gum tissue and initiate periodontal disease . Then remaining traces of plaque and surface stains are removed with a final polish.

The dentist performs a comprehensive examination, including:

  • Analysis of gum health and bone around teeth.
  • Checking each tooth for decay, fractures, chips, and wear.
  • Inspection of fillings, crowns , bridges , and other dental restorations.
  • Evaluation of fit and wear of dentures or other dental appliances.
  • Oral cancer screening. This painless check of soft tissues in the mouth, throat, lips, neck, and face takes only a few minutes, and it could ultimately save your life.
  • Periodic x-rays, with frequency depending on your age, oral condition, and risk factors.

Importance of x-rays

Our practice utilizes digital radiography – which creates high resolution images with minimal radiation exposure. X-rays give the dentist a look “inside,” to spot problems with oral development, hidden dental caries, bone loss, cysts, and tumors. Early detection allows the most efficient and economical treatment, with the least discomfort. X-rays are also critical to precise treatment planning.

Help with home care

Our hygiene team provides instruction and hands-on demonstration to help you keep your mouth healthy between visits. The dentist talks with you about nutrition and lifestyle habits that impact oral health. Aftercare and ongoing home hygiene is crucial to an uneventful recovery after a prosthodontic procedure and to preserve results.

Frequently Asked Questions about Dental Hygiene

Why is regular dental hygiene important for maintaining my oral health?

Consistent dental hygiene is the foundation of long-term oral health. Daily brushing and flossing help control plaque buildup, while professional cleanings remove hardened deposits that cannot be removed at home. Routine checkups help identify problems such as decay, gum inflammation, or wear early, when treatment is simpler and more conservative. Preventive care supports healthy teeth and gums year-round, not just on the day of your appointment.

What can I expect during a dental checkup at Pasadena Prosthodontics?

A dental checkup begins with a professional cleaning to remove plaque, tartar, and surface stains. The dentist then performs a thorough examination, evaluating gum health, bone support, and each tooth for damage or decay. Existing restorations and dental appliances are carefully assessed for fit and function. An oral cancer screening is included, and digital X-rays may be taken when needed to evaluate areas not visible during the exam.

How often should I get dental X-rays? Are they safe?

The frequency of dental X-rays depends on factors such as age, oral health history, and risk for dental disease. Digital radiography captures high-resolution images while minimizing radiation exposure. These images help detect hidden concerns like bone loss, decay between teeth, or changes beneath restorations. X-rays play an important role in early diagnosis and accurate treatment planning.

What home care tips can help me maintain oral health between visits?

Brushing twice daily with proper technique and flossing once a day are essential habits. Nutrition and lifestyle choices also impact oral health, particularly sugar intake and tobacco use. The hygiene team provides personalized instruction and demonstrations to improve daily care. Following recommended home care is especially important after prosthodontic procedures to protect results and promote healing.

How does Pasadena Prosthodontics support patients in preventing periodontal disease?

Periodontal disease develops when plaque and tartar irritate the gum tissue over time. During hygiene visits, buildup is carefully removed above and below the gumline. Patients receive education on effective home care techniques to reduce future risk. Prevention is a collaborative effort that combines professional care with daily habits to maintain healthy gums.

Why are professional cleanings important even if I brush and floss daily?

Even excellent home care cannot remove all plaque and tartar, especially below the gumline. Professional cleanings reach areas that are difficult to access at home. These visits help prevent gum disease , tooth decay, and complications around restorations. Cleanings also provide an opportunity to monitor changes over time.

How does preventive care protect dental restorations and prosthodontic work?

Crowns , bridges , implants , and dentures rely on healthy surrounding tissues for long-term success. Preventive care helps reduce inflammation, decay, and wear that can compromise restorations. Regular evaluations allow early adjustments or maintenance, extending the life of dental work and preserving comfort and function.

How does preventive dentistry support long-term oral and overall health?

Healthy teeth and gums contribute to better nutrition, comfort, and confidence. Preventive dentistry reduces the risk of advanced dental problems and the need for extensive treatment. By maintaining oral health, patients also help limit chronic inflammation that may affect overall wellness.
