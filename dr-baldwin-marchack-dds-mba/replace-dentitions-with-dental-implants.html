Replace dentitions with beautiful dental implants

Losing a tooth as a child is an exciting time, but it can be devastating as an adult. Millions of Americans across the country are currently living with some form of tooth loss. Luckily, with advancements like dental implants, it is easier than ever to restore your smile and confidence. At Pasadena Prosthodontics in Pasadena, California, we customize teeth replacement treatment to help you achieve a beautiful, functional smile. Our team works hard to prioritize your needs to give you the best experience possible.

There are many reasons individuals lose teeth – decay, trauma, or periodontal disease are some of the most common. Regardless of the reason, dental implants are a permanent tooth-replacement option that will look and function naturally.

Dental implants are tiny posts that are placed into the jawbone to act as a replacement for the missing root. Over time, the post will integrate with the jawbone to provide structural stability for the final restoration.

Once the implant is fully integrated with the jawbone, we can finish the restoration with a dental crown , fixed bridge , or overdenture, depending on how many teeth are missing. We even offer full arch implants for individuals missing all the teeth within either the upper or lower arch. Full arch implants involve the placement of several strategic implants that will be used to support an entire arch dental bridge.

Benefits of dental implants

We know you have choices for tooth replacement, so it’s essential to understand the many advantages of dental implants. One of the most important things to consider is the longevity of your restoration. Dental implants behave like a natural tooth root, stimulating the bone surrounding it preventing bone loss and premature facial aging. Moreover, the implant’s stability makes them easier to speak and eat with than with other tooth replacement options.

Replace Missing Dentition with Dental Implants FAQs

Can dental implants replace all of my missing teeth?

Yes. Dental implants can replace a single missing tooth, several missing teeth, or an entire upper or lower dentition. Depending on your needs, Drs. Christopher Marchack and Eugenia Chan may recommend implant-supported crowns , bridges , or full-arch restorations . Each solution is customized to restore a complete, functional smile that looks natural and allows you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

What is the best option for replacing an entire dentition?

For patients missing all of the teeth in one or both arches, implant-supported full-arch restorations are often an excellent long-term solution. Instead of replacing every tooth with an individual implant, a strategically placed number of dental implants can support a complete arch of replacement teeth. This approach provides outstanding stability while restoring appearance, chewing ability, and everyday comfort.

Will replacing missing dentition with dental implants improve my chewing ability?

Yes. Missing multiple teeth can make it difficult to chew many everyday foods comfortably. Implant-supported restorations provide a secure foundation that allows for stronger, more efficient biting and chewing than many traditional removable options. Restoring your dentition with dental implants helps you enjoy a wider variety of foods while supporting better nutrition and overall quality of life.

Can replacing missing teeth with dental implants improve my facial appearance?

Absolutely. Missing teeth and the underlying jawbone changes that follow tooth loss can contribute to a sunken facial appearance over time. Because dental implants stimulate the jawbone much like natural tooth roots, they help preserve bone and support your facial structure. Replacing missing dentition with implants can create a fuller, healthier-looking smile and facial profile.

What if I have been missing teeth for many years?

Even if you have lived with missing teeth for a long time, you may still be a candidate for dental implant treatment. During your consultation, your prosthodontist will evaluate your jawbone, gums, and overall oral health to determine the most appropriate solution. Many patients who have experienced long-term tooth loss can successfully restore their dentition with customized implant treatment.

How long do implant-supported replacement teeth last?

Dental implants are designed to provide a long-lasting foundation for replacement teeth . With excellent oral hygiene and routine professional care, the implants themselves can remain successful for many years. The attached crowns, bridges, or full-arch restorations are also built from durable materials and can provide dependable function while restoring your complete dentition for the long term.
