Dr. Steven McEwan is skilled dentist who is passionate about building long lasting patient relationships based on trust and respect. He loves working with patients to better understand their oral health and how to achieve their dental goals. Dr. McEwan completed his undergraduate studies at Marquette University where he would go on to earn his doctor of dental surgery from the school of dentistry.

Dedicated to learning and growing as a dentist, Dr. McEwan completes advanced training and education each year, and hold memberships in the American Dental Association, the Wisconsin Dental Association, and Greater Milwaukee Dental Association.

Born and raised in a large Milwaukee family, Dr. McEwan was the seventh of eight children. He loves spending his free time with friends and family, biking, golfing, playing basketball, and rooting for the Packers, Brewers, Bucks, and Marquette basketball.
