Personalized Care is a Priority

Dr. Bessler strives to maintain a low volume of patients in order to devote ample attention during every office visit. Under Dr. Bessler’s care, you can expect that your oral health will be thoroughly assessed in order to identify your needs, note your goals, and recommend the right treatments.

Our office features experienced team members who understand the importance of putting our patients first. Dr. Bessler has been practicing at our office for nine years, and both his hygienist and assistant have been with Bessler & Kang for more than 15 years. Our team will happily accommodate all of your needs with their expertise and friendly demeanor, because our mission is to make your dental appointment something to look forward to.

Our recently renovated facility is inviting and cozy, featuring warm colors, comfortable seating, and a homey ambiance no other dentists’ office can meet. Our waiting area is designed to gently calm your nerves prior to your appointment, and we offer free Wi­Fi so our patients can stay connected and entertained. We also have comfortable pillows which are available to patients who are undergoing lengthier treatments.

We understand that communicating with your insurance provider can be difficult and even confusing, so we handle correspondence in order to ensure you receive maximum benefits. For out­-of­-pocket costs, we offer financing plans so you can afford the treatment you want and need on any budget.

If you need to establish ongoing dental care, contact Dr. Bessler’s office today. We are focused on helping you achieve excellent overall health and a great quality of life through exceptional dentistry. We look forward to meeting you and your family, and helping you to experience all the benefits of a healthy and beautiful smile.
