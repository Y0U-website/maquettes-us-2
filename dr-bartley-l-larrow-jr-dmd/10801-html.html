B.L. Larrow Sr., D.D.S. (Retired) A native Vermonter, Dr. Larrow, Sr. graduated with a Bachelor of Arts degree from the University of Vermont in 1958. He then graduated from McGill University in Montreal, Quebec with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 1963. Following a two year tour in the United States Navy, Dr. Larrow, Sr. returned to Vermont to join his father, Dr. Jesse Larrow, in private practice.

Bart Larrow, Jr., D.M.D. Also a native Vermonter, Dr. Larrow, Jr. graduated from the University of Pennsylvania with a Bachelor of Arts degree in 1995, and a Doctor of Dental Medicine in 1996. Following a one year General Practice Residency at the New York Hospital/ Cornell Medical Center in New York City, Dr. Larrow, Jr. practiced for several years in New Jersey. In the year 2000, he returned to join Dr. Larrow, Sr. in private practice in Middlebury.
