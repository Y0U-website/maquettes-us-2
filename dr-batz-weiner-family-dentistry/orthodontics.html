The Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontic treatment is not only effective in restoring your self-esteem, but it also contributes to a sustainably healthy smile. The benefits of orthodontics include:

  • Amplified confidence
  • Achieve an aligned bite
  • Alleviate jaw discomfort and TMJ symptoms
  • Enhance your oral health
  • Care for your teeth and gums more effectively
  • Combat tooth wear and damage

Impact of Orthodontics on Your Oral Health

Orthodontic concerns can take a toll on your oral health. Crowded teeth make it more challenging to adequately clean your teeth and gums, increasing the risk of decay and periodontal disease. Additionally, misaligned smiles are notorious for triggering TMJ symptoms , jaw pain, and headaches. Fortunately, these issues can be mitigated with orthodontic care.

Orthodontics for Teens

Once a child reaches their teenage years, their facial bones and oral cavities begin to develop. This impacts the alignment of the teeth and jaw. Orthodontics for teens is highly recommended to eliminate worsening discomfort and more complex dental concerns. With Invisalign or clear braces treatments, your teen can enhance their smile and uphold their confidence.

Orthodontic Treatment for Children

The precise age to begin orthodontic treatment varies depending on the child’s oral development. On average, orthodontics should be considered once a child reaches the age of seven. Pediatric orthodontic care allows our specialists to detect, diagnose, and remedy issues with the teeth and jaw before symptoms become more severe, eliminating the need for surgical intervention.

Advantages of Orthodontic Treatment for Children

Early orthodontic treatment helps children avoid problematic dental concerns later in life. The advantages of orthodontics for children include:

  • Prevent future tooth extractions
  • Create space to avoid crowding
  • Greater control over the upper and lower arch width
  • Adjust the jaw to encourage adult teeth to emerge straight
  • Correct speech concerns and thumb sucking

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Invisalign clear aligners are a revolutionary advancement in the world of orthodontics. Invisalign involves a series of clear aligner trays custom-fitted to the patient’s mouth. Each tray must be worn for approximately two weeks before upgrading to the next set in the series. This process gradually shifts the teeth into alignment for a seamless smile.

Modern Braces

For patients who are not eligible for Invisalign, we offer clear braces. In contrast to bulky metal braces, our modern braces feature smaller, clear, or ceramic brackets and a lower profile. We also offer lingual braces, which serve the same purpose but are placed behind the teeth rather than on the surface like standard braces.
