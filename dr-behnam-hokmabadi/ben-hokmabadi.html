Ben hokmabadi

Dr. Ben’s educational journey began at Santa Clara University where he obtained a bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Math, with a minor in Biology, in 1997. After spending a few years in the tech industry, he was drawn to the dental field, which perfectly married his interests in hands-on creative and scientific processes. He completed his dental education at the University of the Pacific Dental School in San Francisco, and began his practice in Belmont, focusing on delivering exceptional dental care.

An active individual, Dr. Ben enjoys hiking, biking, and playing tennis. He also has a unique talent for solving the Rubik’s cube in under two minutes! At home, Dr. Ben loves to spend time with his wife Azi and their three boys, often hosting BBQs and enjoying time with friends and family.

Dr. Ben’s dental philosophy is centered on creating trust with his patients and providing the best possible care by attentively listening to their needs. His approach ensures that every patient feels safe, comfortable, and heard, fostering a long-lasting relationship.

Having recently joined Total Health Dental Care as an affiliate office, Dr. Ben is excited about the camaraderie and the advanced technological environment, which aligns with his commitment to dental excellence. Whether you’re visiting for a routine check-up or a complex procedure, Dr. Ben Hokmabadi looks forward to meeting you and enhancing your dental health experience.
