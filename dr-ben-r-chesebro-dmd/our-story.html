Our Story

As your family’s medical home, HealthWorks is a place you’ll always feel welcome, seen, and cared for.

In addition to primary medical care for all ages, we provide mental and behavioral health services, and affordable prescriptions —all from one convenient location.

Additionally, our mobile clinic brings services like sick visits, minor injury treatment, and well-child exams right to your student’s school.

To make health care affordable, we offer a sliding scale fee structure for our income-eligible patients. We also accept most insurance!

Our Mission

HealthWorks delivers high quality, culturally sensitive, comprehensive primary care.

Our patients and their families are at the center of everything we do. We work to promote health in body and mind.

HealthWorks is building the health of our community by serving everyone, regardless of ability to pay.

We partner with other community services to meet the long-term needs of our patients.

HealthWorks employees are engaged, caring and empathetic.

We seek to bring our best to our patients and our community every day.

We do more than care for you; we care about you. Getting to know you creates a unique opportunity for us to work together and connect you with resources (including community partners and/or our care and case management services) to improve your quality of life.

We’re proud to meet you right where you are, walk with you on your journey, and be part of celebrating your successes along the way.

Together with our leadership team and board of directors , HealthWorks’ physicians and staff work to bring their best to our patients—and our community—every day.
