To make health care affordable, we offer a sliding scale fee structure for our income-eligible patients.

At HealthWorks, we welcome and care for everyone, regardless of insurance status or ability to pay.

If you have health care insurance, but it doesn’t cover or only partially covers fees for certain services at HealthWorks, you may be eligible for the sliding fee discount based on your income and family size.

If you qualify for the sliding fee scale, you will need to pay a minimum established fee (see chart in the document linked below) when checking in for your appointment.

If I have insurance, can I still qualify for the sliding fee scale?

Yes! If you have health care insurance, but it doesn’t cover or only partially covers fees for certain services at HealthWorks, you may be eligible for the sliding fee discount based on your income and family size .

What documents do I need to provide for proof of income?

When you apply for the sliding fee scale, we’ll ask you for information from your most recent tax return (signed and filed within the last 12 months, if filed) and/or the past 30 days of current income via pay stubs, plus other documentation as needed to confirm other income types.
