The practice

Our office provides a variety of treatment options for children and adults. Optimal treatment plans will be discussed at your initial consultation.

Developed by Dr. Cassalia, Wirleign®, also known as C 5 Hidden Orthodontics, is a completely invisible and comfortable treatment option for minor orthodontic issues.

There are many types of braces available than can be tailored to your orthodontic preferences.

Phase I, or early interceptive treatment, is orthodontic treatment that is done before all of the permanent teeth have erupted, and often occurs between the ages of six and ten. Phase 1 orthodontic treatment, or interceptive orthodontics, typically occurs between 6 and 9 years of age, during the mixed dentition phase, when patients begin to lose their baby teeth.

Lingual braces are attached to the inside of the tooth surface and are not visible to one’s smile.

Accelerated orthodontics can speed up tooth movement throughout your orthodontic treatment.

Soft tissue or gum management may become necessary with orthodontic treatment. Soft tissue lasers can enhance the overall aestehtics.

Retention is an important part of your orthodontic treatment. Dr. Cassalia recommends a combination or fixed and removable retainers to maintain your life long smile.
