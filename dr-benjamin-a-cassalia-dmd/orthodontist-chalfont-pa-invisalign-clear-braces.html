Payment & Insurance

When it comes to the financial arrangement. We will work with you to create an affordable payment option for your family’s orthodontic treatment. An estimated Insurance coverage can be determined to provide an accurate payment schedule.

Diagnostics & Treatment

Once it is determined that you or your child is ready for orthodontic treatment, an appointment is scheduled to obtain diagnostic information with digital x-rays, intra-oral photographs and digital scan of the dental arches. Dr. Cassalia will review the diagnostic information with you and/or your child to confirm the initial visit treatment recommendations. After the appliances have been placed on your teeth, you will be seen approximately once every six to eight weeks, and these visits usually last twenty to thirty minutes.
