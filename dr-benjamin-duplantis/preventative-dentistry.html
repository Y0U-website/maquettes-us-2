What is Preventative Dentistry?

Preventive dental care is a powerful and effective strategy for keeping your teeth and gums healthy before larger problems develop. Most people benefit from six-month cleanings and exams, in addition to their good home hygiene habits. When problems cannot be avoided, your care shifts to therapeutic or restorative treatments. Preventative dentistry—like sealants, fluoride, and regular cleanings—helps our Lake Charles dentist reduce your need for avoidable treatments in the future.

Why Preventative Dental Care is Important

Even if you brush and floss your teeth every day, plaque will accumulate in certain areas of your mouth. Once it does, it calcifies into tartar, which cannot be removed using standard home hygiene methods. It is critical to schedule professional cleanings and preventive dental visits to keep your smile healthy.

During these preventive appointments, our dentist in Lake Charles or hygienist will alert you to any issues that require additional attention or intervention. Even if a problem is just beginning, it may be possible to fully reverse the symptoms before they progress into disease or a cavity. However, if you do not plan for those checkups, you may have infections in your mouth that will progress into a non-reversible state.

The Importance of Good Oral Hygiene

Thousands of microbes live in our mouths. Every time you eat, the foods you ingest feed the bacteria, creating acid byproducts. These byproducts are what contribute to the imbalance of plaque biofilm and tartar on your tooth surfaces and under your gums.

You can prevent bacteria from damaging your supporting gum and bone structures by practicing good dental hygiene and scheduling professional cleanings. Chronic dental infections, on the other hand, would ultimately result in gum loss, bone loss, loose teeth, and tooth loss.
