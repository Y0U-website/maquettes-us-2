Restorative Options at Renewed Family Dental

Teeth that are in good condition can last a lifetime. However, when infection or a cavity strikes, prompt restorative treatment is critical to preserving your smile. Dental emergencies such as severe toothaches, abscesses, broken teeth, lost fillings, and damaged crowns should be treated as soon as possible to avoid further complications. Depending on your situation, our office provides a complete menu of restorations to choose from, including:

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Cavities are bacterial infections that weaken the tooth’s outer and inner structures. Untreated tooth decay will keep growing and spread to other teeth once the enamel has decomposed. To break the cycle of tooth decay, we will remove any damaged areas of enamel, clean the underlying tooth structure, and fill in the cavity with a tooth-colored filling. Composite fillings are less invasive to tooth enamel and more attractive, eliminating the need for silver amalgam fillings that require additional tooth alteration.

Dental Bridges

Missing teeth can have a big impact on how your bite functions and looks overall. Fortunately, a bridge can preserve healthy tooth spacing and oral function. Bridges are constructed with two functional crowns anchored to neighboring teeth and an artificial crown suspended between them to fill in the open space. Used with dental implants, a bridge can also replace numerous teeth at one time, doing away with the need for a partial denture.

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns, also known as “caps,” protect and encase the surface of your tooth above the gums. They are required when there has been excessive structural damage (such as large cavities and fractures) or after a root canal treatment. Each crown is made of durable ceramics that we match to your adjacent tooth enamel. That way they blend in (and not stand out) when you speak or smile. We can also place dental crowns on top of implants for individual tooth replacement.
