We welcome patients of all ages to schedule a first visit to Hoelscher Orthodontics. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that an initial orthodontic evaluation is performed at the first sign of orthodontic problems or no later than age 7.

While the majority of children will not require treatment at this age, early examination allows for early detection of a developing problem. Early detection allows us to correct some problems more effectively and less invasively at a younger age. The first appointment is the “get acquainted” visit. One of our assistants will welcome you back to our treatment area for a brief tour of the office. After a brief examination, Dr. Hoelscher will provide an explanation of the preliminary findings and discuss the treatment options available to correct any problems that may exist. Any questions you have will be answered by the doctor at this time.

If treatment is indicated, a complete set of orthodontic records (diagnostic photos, x-rays, comprehensive exam) will be needed. In some cases, all of the necessary information can be obtained at the initial visit and a formal consultation appointment will be scheduled.

If you have scheduled your first visit to Hoelscher Orthodontics, thank you! We look forward to meeting you!
