Get to Know Dr. Ben Youel

Dr. Ben is proud to lead the team at Grayslake Orthodontics. He loves helping children and adults smile more confidently and achieve healthier bites. He can’t wait to give you a smile “Youel” love.

Where Did You Study orthodontics?

Dr. Ben grew up in northern Illinois. He attended North Central College in Naperville, where he graduated Summa Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Science in Chemistry. During his college years, he was a three-time national qualifier and two-time NCAA Division 3 All-American wrestler.

His educational journey then brought him to the University of Illinois at Chicago (UIC) where he earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery. Prior to graduation, he was selected for induction into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon honor society, a recognition awarded to fewer than 10 % of his dental school classmates. Following dental school, Dr. Ben completed a 12-month hospital-based residency at Advocate Illinois Masonic Medical Center in Chicago. While there he learned advanced surgical techniques and how to manage medically complex and special needs patients.

After practicing as a general dentist for a few years, he headed back to UIC to complete a three-year orthodontic residency where he was elected class president by his co-residents. While attaining his specialist credentials as an orthodontist, Dr. Ben also earned a Master of Science degree in Oral Sciences.

Dr. Ben is bilingual (English and Spanish), Invisalign certified and experienced in complex/surgical orthodontics. He spends one day each month providing free care as a volunteer at the Chicago Dental Society Foundation Clinic in Wheaton, IL.

Which Dental Organizations Are You A Member Of?

Dr. Ben is very active in organized dentistry. He serves as a volunteer leader with the American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society, Chicago Dental Society and the Illinois Society of Orthodontists. He is an accomplished speaker and has given lectures around the country on the subjects of personal development, professional development, and leadership.

Outside of the Orthodontic Office, What Do You Like to Do?

In his free time, Dr. Ben likes spending quality time with his wife, Maggie, and their daughter, Genevieve. He also enjoys playing with their dog, Ivy, and their cat, Anders. Dr. Ben also loves watching movie marathons and running real marathons. His favorite post-marathon treat is a big mug of moose tracks ice cream.
