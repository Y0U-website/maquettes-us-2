Humanitarian & Educational Work

– Founder and President of the Fialkoff Dental Study Club. Founded in the early ‘90’s, Dr. Fialkoff has produced hundreds of continuing education seminars that meet monthly and provide Tristate dental professionals with continuing education credits.

– Founding Sponsor and President of the Foundation for a Drug Free World of The Americas Chapter, a federal 501c3 non-profit organization. Since 2006, under Dr. Fialkoff’s leadership, the chapter has conducted more than 1500 events and training workshops, many in which Dr. Fialkoff presented. The chapter has educated more than 100,000 youth throughout the Eastern Seaboard of the USA and in Central America.

– Founder and President of the Implant Certainty Training Program, training Tristate dental professionals on the latest implant placement and restoration techniques. The training program has been meeting bi-annually for more than a decade and has been recognized by Zimmer / Biomet 3i Implant Innovations among other dental-focused companies.

– Founded and directed two US-Cuba Humanitarian Trips bringing dentists from around the USA on a mission and exchange to Havana, providing thousands of dollars of donations to the dental community in Cuba.

– Created an oral health care program and brochure on how to implement the oral health community education programs: “Healthy Smiles, Healthy Lives” “Give Kids a Smile” and “Fall for Smiles” for each dentist’s local community.
