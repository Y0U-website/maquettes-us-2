Great dental health starts with regular checkups to discover any problems before they arise or worsen. Here at Glassboro Dentistry we focus on prevention and giving you the tools to take care of your smile. Your visit will include a thorough examination of your teeth, gums and other soft tissues. Provide an oral cancer screening and a routine cleaning.

Over half of Americans have gum or periodontal disease. There are several approaches for treatment but the best is prevention. If you have bad breath, bleeding from your gums when you brush chances are you have gum disease. Don’t worry we can help you. Treatments such as a deep cleaning, more regular cleaning and better home care can help maintain those pearly white. New technologies such as the use of Lasers and targeted chemotherapeutics have made treatment of this condition more predictable and comfortable.

Cavities can be deceiving. Sometimes you can tell if you have one, but without proper exploration and radiographs it’s hard to tell. Dr. Sheth can take care of it. He will remove the diseased tissue; place a white filling and blend it to make it look natural.

We use the latest technology in white fillings.

Teeth need to be extracted for a variety of reasons from a toothache to fracture, or simply not enough space. Dr. Sheth will make sure you’re extremely numb before he removes the tooth. Prior to extracting any tooth we will also discuss all your available options for replacement and discuss what may be the best choice for you.

Wisdom teeth (3rd molars) need to evaluated for cleanliness, cavities and space in the arch. Usually, wisdom teeth need to be removed as their eruption impacts the teeth and tissues in front of them. Typically, wisdom teeth are extracted before the Age of 25 as risk of complications increases over time.

Root canals are necessary when nerve of the tooth is injured by a deep cavity or trauma to the tooth. This procedure involves getting the tooth numb and removing the nerve of the tooth and any diseased tooth structure. The tooth is then filled and a crown is placed to protect the tooth from fracturing.

Crowns or caps are used to cover the part of the tooth above the gum. They can be made of a variety of materials such as porcelain, gold or a combination. Crowns are typically used to protect teeth from fracture, protect root canal treated teeth, or improve the appearance of the tooth.

If a crown/cap has come loose or you feel as though it is coming loose. Bring in all the pieces you have as we may be able to re-cement or fix it. Typically, crowns come loose as the tooth underneath becomes weakened or decayed.

If you feel like you have either swallowed or inhaled the crown, please seek medical attention as inhaled objects can potentially be life threatening.

Bridges are simply crowns fused together to help replace single or multiple missing teeth. We use the teeth either side of the missing tooth as anchors to “bridge” the space. Bridges can excellent alternative for patients who are not candidates for dental implants.

If you feel like your teeth are stained or discolored. We have solutions for you! We offer both take home and in office whitening products. Ask us about our specials! Regain your bright, youthful smile with a whitening treatment. Please call our office to schedule a consult to find out which whitening choice best suites you.

Loose dentures can make eating and talking very challenging. This is a very real problem and one that can be solved in a variety of ways with treatments such as a reline, implants or mini implants. Call Dr. Sheth to see how he can help you and discuss what option is right for you.

Dentures are removable prosthetic devices that are used when all of teeth on the upper and or bottom are missing. Partial dentures only replace a few missing teeth and clasp onto existing teeth. Partial can be made of various materials such as a flexible plastic or metal and acrylic. Both can be used in conjunction with crowns and dental implants to help keep them stable.

Broken/Cracked dentures can sometimes be repaired but this decision can only be made on a case by case basis.

Please Do Not use superglue, or pharmacy repair kits or make adjustments yourself, instead contact us at (856) 956-3625 to schedule an evaluation and remember to bring all the pieces you have available to you to your visit. Many times we can repair dentures and return them to you the same day.

Dental implants are placed in the jaws where the roots were, this replace a single tooth or multiple missing teeth. If taken care of properly they can last a lifetime. Dr. Sheth received his training Montefiore Medical Center and constantly takes educational courses and would be more than happy to discuss your treatment options regarding dental implants and if you are a suitable candidate.

Veneers are Porcelain coverings that are places over the teeth to change the color and shape of the teeth. Call Dr. Sheth For a Consultation at (856) 956-3625.
