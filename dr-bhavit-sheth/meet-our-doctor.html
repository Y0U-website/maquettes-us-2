Dedicated Care from Dr. Sheth and Dr. Kapoor

Compassionate, personalized dentistry backed by years of experience and advanced training.

Bhavit Sheth, DMD

Dr. Sheth grew up in Coventry, England, before moving to Washington, D.C. to complete his undergraduate studies at American University. He began his career working with the National Academies of Sciences on the Board of Life Sciences, where he gained valuable professional experience before pursuing his true calling—dentistry.

He went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the prestigious Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine, followed by a comprehensive residency program at Montefiore Medical Center in New York. There, he received advanced training across multiple dental disciplines, broadening both his clinical skills and patient-focused approach to care.

For over 12 years, Dr. Sheth has proudly served the Glassboro community, where he has built lasting relationships with his patients and their families. Dedicated to lifelong learning, he consistently exceeds state requirements for continuing education, ensuring his patients benefit from the latest advancements in dentistry.

Outside the office, Dr. Sheth is a passionate sports fan who enjoys following the Premier League and Formula One racing. Most of all, he values quality time with his family—his wife, his daughter Ava, and his two stepchildren. He treasures these moments and hopes that one day Ava may share his passion for dentistry.

Aleesha Kapoor, D.M.D.

Dr. Kapoor was born and raised in New Jersey with her parents and sister. She received her Bachelor’s Degree from Villanova University, followed by her Doctorate Degree in Dental Medicine from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, MA. She went on the complete her general practice residency at Mountainside Medical Center in Montclair, NJ.

Dr. Kapoor is committed to providing a calm and stress-free environment for all of her patients. She wants her patients to not only feel comfortable, but also confident and happy with their smiles and overall dental health. Dr. Kapoor is also passionate about treating and educating children in order to provide them with the care and oral hygiene skills necessary for a healthy smile.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Kapoor enjoys baking, learning languages, trying new foods , watching movies, and spending time with family and friends. She is excited to join the Glassboro Dentistry team and be a part of this community.
