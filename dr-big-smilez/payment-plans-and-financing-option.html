Frequently Asked Questions

How much does dental treatment cost with financing?

Costs vary depending on the treatment, but financing allows you to break the total into affordable monthly payments. Our treatment plan coordinator provides a clear cost breakdown before you commit to anything.

Do I need good credit to qualify?

Each financing partner has different approval criteria, and some options are available for a wide range of credit scores. Our team can help you find the plan that gives you the best chance of approval.

Can I use financing and insurance together?

After your insurance covers its portion, financing can be applied to whatever balance remains. Many of our patients use both to keep their out-of-pocket costs as low as possible.
