Binni Patel

Dr. Binni Patel grew up in Galloway, New Jersey spending time at the shore. She graduated Cum Laude from Rutgers University in New Brunswick, New Jersey where she obtained her Bachelor of the Arts degree in Biological Sciences with minors in Physiology and Religion. Continuing further at Rutgers University in Newark, New Jersey she obtained her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree. After graduating from dental school, she came back towards the shore and completed a General Practice Residency as Chief Resident at Monmouth Medical Center in Long Branch, New Jersey.

After one year in private practice she went onto Montefiore Medical Center in Bronx, New York to complete her specialty training in Pediatric Residency. During this time she was able to gain knowledge in the treatment of pediatrics and medically compromised patients. Her experience in the treatment of special needs patients was enhanced at the Rose F. Kennedy Center for Excellence in Developmental Disabilities. She is actively learning in her field as a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD), American Dental Associated (ADA) and Monmouth County Dental Society.

Dr. Binni lives in Edison, NJ with her husband. In her free time she loves to travel the world with her husband and go hiking in the most amazing places. She loves treating children and being part of a wonderful team at Shore Children’s Dental Care.

Uchenna Ndukwe Nwosu

Dr. Uchenna Ndukwe Nwosu is proud to be back at the Jersey Shore, serving the very community she grew up in. She earned her Bachelor’s degree in Biological Sciences from Rutgers University–New Brunswick, where her passion for science and healthcare first took root.

She went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. During her time at Penn, Dr. Uchenna was a leader on campus, serving as President of the Student National Dental Association and excelling as an honors student in the school’s Vulnerable Populations Clinic—an experience that deepened her commitment to compassionate, community-focused care.

Dr. Uchenna is known for her warm, patient-centered approach and her ability to make children feel safe and confident in the dental chair. She strives to build positive dental experiences that set the foundation for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

When she’s not seeing patients, Dr. Uchenna enjoys watching reality TV and never misses a $5 movie night—she’s a proud AMC Stubs member and a regular at the theater every Tuesday!

She is excited to be part of the team at Shore Children’s Dental Care and looks forward to welcoming your family to the office.

Martin Rabinovich

Dr. Rabinovich is a board-certified orthodontist of the American Board of Orthodontics. Dr. Rabinovich is an active member of the American Dental Association, New Jersey Dental Association, Monmouth Ocean County Dental Society, and the American Association of Orthodontists.

While in residency, Dr. Rabinovich represented the Rocky Mountain Society of Orthodontists on the AAO Council on New and Younger Members. Dr. Rabinovich is committed in providing the highest level of orthodontic care and is passionate in helping create more healthy, beautiful and functional smiles that you deserve. Outside the office, Dr. Rabinovich loves to travel, stay active hiking and skiing the outdoors, playing ice hockey, and spending time with his friends and family.
