Our Values & Commitment to Care

At Dr. Blake Brown Dentistry, we’re committed to exceptional, patient-centered care focused on long-term health, comfort, and aesthetics. Trusted by families across Durango, Bayfield, Cortez, and Pagosa Springs, your smile and well-being are always our priority.

Comprehensive, High-Quality Care From cleanings to restorations and cosmetic care, we provide personalized treatments tailored to you.

Cosmetic & Restorative Expertise We specialize in veneers, teeth whitening, dental implants, and full-mouth restorations to enhance both function and aesthetics.

Professional Botox® Treatments Beyond dentistry, we offer Botox treatments for both cosmetic enhancement and TMJ pain relief, helping patients achieve a smoother, more youthful look while also addressing muscle tension and jaw discomfort.

A Private, Comfort-Driven Approach Our office is designed for patients who value high-quality, long-lasting dental work in a calm, stress-free environment. We also provide sedation dentistry for those with dental anxiety or complex treatment needs.

State-of-the-Art Technology We invest in advanced dental technology, including 3D imaging, digital impressions, and intraoral cameras, to ensure precise, efficient, and comfortable treatments.

Emergency Dental Care When You Need It If you’re in pain, call us right away, and we’ll do our best to see you as soon as possible.

A Community-Focused Practice As a locally owned and operated dental office, we’re deeply connected to our community and committed to building lasting relationships with our patients.
