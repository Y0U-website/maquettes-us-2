Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed in the jawbone, acting as artificial tooth roots. They provide a strong foundation for custom restorations such as: Single dental crowns – Ideal for replacing one missing tooth.
  • Dental bridges – Restores multiple missing teeth in a row.
  • Full and partial dentures – Secured with implants for improved stability.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Permanent solution – Designed to last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Prevents bone loss – Stimulates the jawbone to maintain facial structure.
  • Restores full chewing function – Eat all your favorite foods without restrictions.
  • No impact on surrounding teeth – Unlike traditional bridges, implants don’t require modifying healthy teeth.
  • Improves confidence – No slipping, discomfort, or adhesives like traditional dentures.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

You may be a good candidate for dental implants if you:

If your jawbone is too weak for implants, we may recommend bone grafting to strengthen the area before placement.

  • Have one or more missing teeth and want a permanent solution.
  • Have a healthy jawbone with enough density to support an implant.
  • Are in good oral health, free of gum disease or infection.
  • Want a more secure and natural-feeling alternative to dentures.

Our Dental Implant Process

Throughout the process, we ensure your comfort and long-term success with expert care.

  • Consultation & Exam – We assess your oral health, take digital X-rays, and create a customized implant treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement – The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia.
  • Healing & Osseointegration – Over 3-6 months, the implant fuses with the bone for maximum stability.
  • Abutment Placement – A small connector piece is attached to the implant.
  • Final Restoration – A custom crown, bridge, or denture is securely placed for a natural-looking result.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

With proper care, dental implants can last decades or even a lifetime.

  • Brush and floss daily – Just like natural teeth.
  • Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol – These can slow healing and affect implant stability.
  • Schedule regular dental checkups – Professional cleanings help maintain your implant for life.

FAQs About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, implants can last a lifetime, though the crown may need replacement after 10-15 years.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, ensuring a comfortable experience. Mild soreness afterward is manageable with medication.

How soon can I eat after implant surgery?

Soft foods are recommended for the first few days, but you can gradually return to a normal diet as you heal.

Are dental implants better than dentures?

Yes! Implants provide permanent stability, no slipping, and better chewing function compared to traditional dentures.
