Dr. Matt Roberts

Dr. Matthew Roberts is proud to call Joplin home and is passionate about serving the community where he was raised. After graduating from College Heights Christian School, he earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology from Missouri Southern State University before going on to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) degree from the University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Dentistry.

Dr. Roberts is committed to providing high-quality, patient-centered dental care in a welcoming environment. He enjoys helping patients improve their oral health and confidence through personalized treatment and believes in continually expanding his knowledge through advanced education and training to provide the best care possible.

Outside the office, Dr. Roberts enjoys spending time with his wife, Brenecke, and their daughter.
