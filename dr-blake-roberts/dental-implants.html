Questions

Do you offer All-on-4 dental implants?

Yes! We offer All-on-4 implants for patients who need a full arch replacement. This advanced technique provides a stable foundation for a denture or a bridge with as little as 4 implants..

Do you do same-day implants?

In some cases, we offer immediate implant placement after extraction. This can help to shorten treatment time and reduce the number of appointments needed during the restoration process.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Basically, anyone who is missing one or more teeth and has healthy gums and enough jawbone density. Age is not a factor, and implants can be placed in young and older patients alike. If you need a tooth replacement, Dr. Roberts will evaluate your case to determine if dental implants are the best option.

How long does the dental implant process take?

This can vary from patient to patient. The implant placement procedure usually takes a few hours, and the healing process can take months. Once the implant has integrated with the jawbone, the abutment and the final restoration can be placed. While it may take several months to complete the process, the end result is a beautiful and durable restoration that can enhance your smile for years to come.
