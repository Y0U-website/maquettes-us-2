Questions

How is this plan different from dental insurance?

Traditional dental insurance often comes with restrictions, waiting periods, deductibles, and yearly maximums — all of which can limit your access to the care you need. Our in-house savings plan is designed to be simple, transparent, and focused on high-quality care, not insurance company policies.

Can I use this plan with my dental insurance?

No. Our savings plan cannot be combined with insurance or third-party coverage.

Is this plan right for me?

If you don’t have dental insurance or want a more affordable, hassle-free way to manage dental costs, our savings plan is an excellent option.
