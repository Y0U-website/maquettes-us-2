Our Services

What Services We Offer

Preventative Care

Routine cleanings and gum treatments keep your smile healthy and help prevent costly issues down the line. We provide: • Professional teeth cleaning • Periodontal maintenance It is recommended to schedule a cleaning every 6 months. This includes an X-ray image, cleaning, and an exam.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance the beauty of your natural smile. We provide: • Professional teeth whitening • Cosmetic bondings • Porcelain and composite veneers • Smile makeover consultations Our services correct discoloration, chips, or gaps to give you a confident, radiant smile.

Restorative Dentistry

Repair and protect your natural teeth. We provide: • Tooth-colored dental fillings • Custom dental crowns • Root Canal Therapy (to save infected teeth) • Repairs for cracked or worn teeth We restore strength, comfort, and appearance.

Teeth Replacement

Personalized options to replace missing teeth. We offer multiple solutions to restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence, such as: • Implant restorations • Permanent dental bridges • Full and partial dentures We also restore previous implants.

Orthodontics

Straighten your smile effectively. We offer modern solutions to align your teeth and bite, such as: • Invisalign® or clear aligners • Braces • Retainers and maintenance

Extractions

When a tooth can’t be saved, we make removal as smooth and comfortable as possible. Our gentle extraction services help remove problematic teeth and prepare you for the next step in your dental care journey.

Non-Surgical Periodontal Therapy

Restore the health of your gums with gentle, nonsurgical care. We provide: • Scaling and root planing to treat and stabilize periodontal disease • Ongoing periodontal maintenance to support long-term gum health Our services target inflammation and infection below the gumline to preserve your natural teeth and protect your overall oral wellness.
