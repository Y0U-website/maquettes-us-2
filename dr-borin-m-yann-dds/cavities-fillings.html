What Causes Cavities?

Cavities occur as results of tooth decay. The process takes place slowly, requiring time to develop and is often exacerbated by a variety of risk factors, such as:

No matter how excellent the care you take of your teeth may be, most people get at least one cavity, requiring a filling, in their lifetimes.

  • Snacking between meals
  • Sipping on sugary drinks throughout the day
  • Eating and drinking sugary items
  • Improper dental care and oral hygiene
  • Dry mouth
  • Failing to get adequate fluoride
  • Heartburn
  • Eating disorders
