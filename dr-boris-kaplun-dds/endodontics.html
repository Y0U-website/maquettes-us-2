Advanced Endodontic Procedures and Treatments

North Dental General & Cosmetic Dentistry in Mamaroneck, NY, specializes in the effective treatment of root canals. Root canal therapy is a precise procedure that involves a series of steps aimed at removing infected pulp (nerves and blood vessels) from the tooth’s roots.

After a thorough evacuation, the tooth is meticulously disinfected using both instrumental and chemical methods. To preserve the tooth and guard against future infection, the root canals are then sealed with a specialized rubber-like material.

Our dedicated team ensures optimal care to save your tooth and promote long-term dental health. Call us for an appointment.
