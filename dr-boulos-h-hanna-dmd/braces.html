Dental Braces

Metal braces are often best for complex cases, while ceramic braces and clear aligners provide more discreet treatment options. Our team will help you choose the right solution for your smile goals, budget, and lifestyle.

Suitable candidates for braces include children, teenagers, and adults with misaligned, crowded, or crooked teeth, as well as those with bite issues. They are ideal for individuals looking to fix tooth spacing issues, jaw discomfort, or improve oral function and aesthetics at any age.

Your braces fitting begins with a consultation and digital x-rays to evaluate your teeth and bite. We’ll then take impressions or digital scans to create your personalized treatment plan. During your fitting appointment, brackets are carefully placed on your teeth and connected with a wire to begin the alignment process.

Before your appointment, we recommend thoroughly brushing and flossing and eating beforehand, since your teeth may feel sore afterward. Most fittings take 1–2 hours, and you may experience mild discomfort for a few days as your teeth adjust to the braces.

Treatment with metal braces typically takes 18 to 24 months, while treatment with clear aligners ranges from 6 to 18 months. The duration depends on your age, the amount of alignment needed, and your level of cooperation.

The cost of dental braces depends on the type of treatment you choose and the complexity of your case. Traditional metal braces, ceramic braces, lingual braces, and clear aligners all vary in cost, and we’ll provide a personalized estimate during your consultation.

Many dental insurance plans may cover a portion of orthodontic treatment, and we offer flexible payment plans and financing options, including CareCredit ® , to help make treatment more affordable. We recommend avoiding sticky/hard foods, brushing after meals, and flossing daily. If you notice soreness, a poking wire, or a loose bracket, contact us. After treatment, wear your retainer and attend follow-up appointments.

Braces are orthodontic devices that typically consist of metal or ceramic brackets, wires, and elastic bands used to correct misaligned teeth, gaps, and bite issues. They work by applying continuous, gentle pressure to teeth over 1–3 years.

Frequently Asked Questions

At what age should someone get dental braces?

The ideal age for getting braces is generally between 10 and 14 years old, while the jaw and teeth are still developing. However, an initial orthodontic evaluation by age 7 is recommended to identify potential issues early.

How long do you have to wear dental braces?

Most braces are worn for about 18 to 24 months, but it depends on your bite and how your teeth respond.

Do dental braces hurt?

Braces generally do not cause pain. They cause temporary discomfort, soreness, or a dull, aching pressure for 3–5 days.

What foods should I avoid with dental braces?

Avoid hard, sticky, or crunchy foods that can break brackets and bend wires, including popcorn, nuts, caramel, chewing gum, hard candy, and pretzels. Also, avoid biting directly into hard fruits, vegetables, and bread.

How do I brush and floss with dental braces?

Use a soft brush and fluoride toothpaste, cleaning around brackets and along the gumline. Use floss threaders daily to prevent plaque accumulation.

Will I need to wear a retainer after dental braces?

Yes, you will need to wear a retainer after braces to prevent your teeth from shifting back to their original positions. Retainers hold teeth in their new alignment while the surrounding bone and soft tissues settle into place.

Can teeth shift back after dental braces treatment?

They can, especially if the retainer is not worn as directed.

Are clear aligners as effective as traditional braces at Gentle Dental?

Clear aligners are highly effective for mild to moderate tooth straightening, often working faster (6 to 18 months) than traditional braces (18 to 24 months). While they are effective, traditional braces are more reliable due to their continuous, high-precision pressure.
