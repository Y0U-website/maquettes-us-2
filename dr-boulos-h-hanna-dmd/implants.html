Frequently Asked Questions

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are small titanium posts that we place in your jaw to replace missing tooth roots. We attach a custom crown on top to restore your tooth. When patients ask what dental implants are, we explain that they look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

What is the process for getting a dental implant?

For dental implants, our dentists start with an exam and imaging to check your jawbone. We then place the implant, allow time for healing, and attach your crown. A dental implants specialist on our team will ensure proper fit and long-term stability

How much does a full set of dental implants typically cost?

The cost of a full set depends on how many teeth need to be replaced and your bone condition. We review the dental implant cost at Gentle Dental during your visit so you know exactly what to expect. Click here to check your coverage online before your visit. We are also happy to go over insurance coverage and financing options with you.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, the implant post can last for decades or even a lifetime. The crown on dental implants may need to be replaced after several years due to normal wear. Brushing, flossing, and regular checkups help extend the life of your implant.

Does Gentle Dental accept insurance for dental implants?

Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans and are happy to review your benefits with you before treatment.

What are the benefits of dental implants over dentures?

Dental implants stay fixed in place; they do not move when you eat or speak. They feel more natural than dentures and do not require adhesives or removal. With All-on-X dental implants, you also protect your jawbone from shrinking over time.

How do dental implants improve oral health?

Replacing a missing tooth with dental implants helps prevent nearby teeth from shifting. The implant also supports your jawbone and reduces bone loss. This helps maintain your facial structure and keeps your bite aligned over time.

What is the recovery time after getting dental implants?

After placement, mild soreness usually lasts a few days. Full healing for dental implants takes a few months as the implant bonds with your jawbone. We provide clear aftercare instructions to help you stay comfortable during the healing period.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

A good candidate has healthy gums and enough bone to support the implant. We often provide a stable option for missing teeth. Before proceeding with dental implants, dentists confirm your eligibility with imaging during your visit.

How painful is one tooth implant?

We numb the area completely to avoid pain while placing a single implant. Dental implants are similar to a routine dental procedure. Any mild discomfort after the procedure can be managed with standard pain medication.

How long does the entire process take?

The full timeline depends on your healing, but most cases take 3-6 months. Dental implants need time to bond with your jawbone before we place the crown. We provide a clear treatment plan during your initial consultation.

What are the potential risks of dental implants?

Dental implant surgery has a low risk of complications when done properly. Our experienced dental implant dentists work to reduce the risk of infection or implant failure. We monitor your healing closely to ensure the implant integrates correctly.

What are the benefits of dental implants compared to dentures?

Unlike removable options, dental implants stay in place and function like natural teeth. They help maintain your jawbone and allow you to eat comfortably. You also care for them the same way you care for your natural teeth.

How many teeth can one implant replace?

One implant is typically used to replace a single missing tooth. However, full mouth dental implants can support multiple teeth or a full arch. We customize your treatment plan based on how many teeth need to be replaced.
