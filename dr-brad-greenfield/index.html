Throughout his career, Dr. Greenfield has completed hundreds of hours of continuing education, learning from many different leaders in dentistry. Dr. Greenfield’s interest in CAD/CAM dentistry has made him proficient in caring for patients using technology that yields convenient and dependable same-day restorations. As a result, our patients have been able to receive care in the form of single and multiple crowns, bridges, onlays, and veneers that are provided in just one appointment!
