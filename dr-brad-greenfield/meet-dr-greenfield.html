Meet Dr. Greenfield

Brad Greenfield, DDS

Dr. Brad Greenfield received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Detroit Mercy in 1999. After beginning his career in Livonia, MI at a private practice specializing in cosmetic and family dentistry, He achieved his dream of owning his own practice in 2010 upon becoming the owner of Lake Orion Family Dentistry.

Throughout his career, Dr. Greenfield has completed hundreds of hours of continuing education, learning from many different leaders in dentistry. His studies have covered a range of specialties including clear aligner therapy, bruxism, children’s dentistry, laser therapy, holistic dentistry, CAD/CAM dentistry, and more.

Dr. Greenfield’s interest in CAD/CAM dentistry has made him proficient in caring for patients using technology that yields convenient and dependable same-day restorations. As a result, our patients have been able to receive care in the form of single and multiple crowns, bridges, onlays, and veneers that are provided in just one appointment! In addition to studying with some of the most experienced and skilled clinicians in the country, he has used his skills and understanding of technology to place thousands of restorations.

Originally from Waterford, Michigan and a graduate of Waterford Kettering High School, he now resides in Oxford, Michigan. There he lives with his wife, three children, dog, goats and chickens. Other interests Dr. Greenfield has include boating, skiing, mountain biking, and pickleball.
