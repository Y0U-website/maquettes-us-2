Insurance And Financing

In addition to accepting checks, cash, and most major credit cards for payment, we also accept most major dental health insurance plans. We can even file your claim on your behalf! There are also layaway plans for certain forms of care, and we work closely with CareCredit, which provides low- and no-interest dental health financing for qualifying patients.
