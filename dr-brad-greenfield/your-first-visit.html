Your First Visit

Next steps are determined by your dental history and evaluation. Some patients will have their teeth cleaned as part of their first appointment, but you may require something different. We will recommend any needed services and are happy to answer questions during your appointment.

When you schedule your appointment, we will ask that your dental records be transferred so that we can provide a timely review. Please provide all relevant information so that we can reach out to your previous caregiver.

  • A review of your existing radiographs and scans (we will take new radiographs if you have no images, or if there are issues with the ones you have)
  • Periodontal charting, including tissue measurements, to evaluate gum health
  • Oral cancer screening with VELscope
  • Digital dental scans using CEREC technology
