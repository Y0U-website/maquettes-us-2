0 Our Team We are fortunate to have a longstanding, experienced team. We focus on comprehensive dentistry with an emphasis on prevention so that you have the tools to maintain your smile for a lifetime. We offer cosmetic solutions including Invisalign, veneers, crowns, dental implants and cosmetic bonding. Together, we will find the right solution for you.

Dr. Brad Rand graduated from Brewer High School and then attended BYU where he graduated Magna Cum Laude in Human Biology. He completed his doctorate training at the University of Iowa College of Dentistry where he graduated Omicron Kappa Upsilon (highest honors). He has been practicing in Maine since 2006.

Since 2009, he has been a participating member of "Donated Dental Services" which provides dental services for patents with disabilities and special needs. In 2016, Dr. Rand was awarded a Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry, which only 7 % of dentists will achieve by the end of their career.
