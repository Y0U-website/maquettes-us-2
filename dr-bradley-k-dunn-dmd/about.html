Our Core Purpose

The purpose of our practice is to provide our patients with the care they deserve while improving their oral health. Developing and maintaining comprehensive dental care in our area without the need for patients to travel great distances for routine and elective services is vital to the health of our practice. We believe in presenting a practice that acts as an example of how a business can and should be run in the community.

Our Core Values

Our Team

Respects our patients and each other. We treat every patient, colleague, and partner with dignity, empathy, and kindness listening carefully, valuing different perspectives, and fostering a supportive, respectful environment at all times.

Believes in cultivating a fun and caring environment. We create a positive, welcoming atmosphere where teamwork, compassion, and a sense of fun go hand in hand making every interaction enjoyable and meaningful for both patients and team members.

Strives for excellence while maintaining integrity. We are committed to delivering the highest quality care and service, guided by honesty, accountability, and strong ethical principles in everything we do.

Creates growth in our community through profitability and generosity. By operating responsibly and successfully, we’re able to give back investing in our team, supporting local initiatives, and sharing our success to strengthen the community we serve.

About Us

Giving Back to the Community

We want to be a good neighbor to the communities we serve. For us, that means finding activities that make our neighborhood a better, healthier place. One day each spring, we offer free dental care to about 75 of those in our area to those who can’t afford regular care. We also sponsor a number of community sports teams and provide mouthguards to all the team members who need them.
