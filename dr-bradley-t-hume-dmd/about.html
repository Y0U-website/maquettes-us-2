About Our Clinic

We at Palmetto Dental Center believe your dental health care is personal. We understand that your dental concerns are unique and we provide quality oral healthcare tailored to meet your needs. Dr. Bradley T. Hume, Dr. Benjamin D. Curtis and Dr. J. Scott Maloney are focused on you as an individual and we will develop a unique plan that addresses both your clinical and personal goals.

As a family-friendly dental office, our doctors and team members believe in treating you the way we would want to be treated ourselves. We offer modern, gentle care that will improve your oral and overall health, along with your smile. We serve the dental needs of everyone in the family from children to great grandparents.

Our Team

Benjamin D. Curtis, DDS

Dr. Curtis is a native of Minnesota and comes from a family of dentists. Both his father and his grandfather practiced oral surgery and his uncle is a general dentist. Dr. Curtis is a graduate of Bethel University with a B.A. in Biology, and he earned his dental degree from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry in 2013.

Bradley T. Hume, DMD

Dr. Hume is a fifth generation Palmetto native and 1995 graduate of Palmetto High School. Dr. Hume is also a graduate of Auburn University, with both a B.S. in Finance and earned a MBA. He earned his dental degree from the Medical College of Georgia (MCG) School of Dentistry (now Dental College of Georgia).

J. Scott Maloney, DDS

Dr. Maloney is a native of Covington, Georgia, where his father was a general dentist and former President of the Thomas P. Hinman Society. Dr. Maloney is a graduate of Emory University, with a B.A. in Biology. He earned his dental degree from the former Emory University School of Dentistry in 1983.
