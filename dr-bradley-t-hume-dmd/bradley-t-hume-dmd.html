Bradley T. Hume, DMD

Dr. Hume is a fifth generation Palmetto native and 1995 graduate of Palmetto High School. Dr. Hume is also a graduate of Auburn University, with both a B.S. in Finance and earned a MBA. He earned his dental degree from the Medical College of Georgia (MCG) School of Dentistry (now Dental College of Georgia at Augusta University) in 2008, followed by a one-year General Practice Residency (GPR) also at MCG. After one year practicing in South Georgia, Dr. Hume found himself back home in Palmetto.

Dr. Hume met his wife, Elizabeth, at Auburn and they have four children: Madeline, Amelia, Holland and Luke. Dr. Hume is a lifelong and avid outdoorsman, and he enjoys continuing that tradition with his family. He also enjoys spending time with his large extended family, watching baseball and rooting for his Auburn Tigers! Dr. Hume is a member of the First Baptist Church of Palmetto and serves on the board of directors of Agape Flights, a large non-profit that serves Christian missionary affiliates through the transportation of needed supplies in the Caribbean.
