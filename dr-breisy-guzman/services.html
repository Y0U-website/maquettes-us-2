Placing the Veneers

Once the case is returned from the dental laboratory the permanent veneers are placed, removing your temporary ones. After your dentist evaluates the fitting and results, veneers are cemented. The dentist will check how your teeth bite to ensure you are not biting incorrectly onto the veneers. Small reductions of the opposing teeth may be necessary if the bite is not correct. Be ready for a beautiful smile.
