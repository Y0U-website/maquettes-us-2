Meet the Team - Fremont Dental Group

Claire Dental Hygienist Being a dental hygienist allows me to be able to educate and treat our dental patients to ensure that they achieve optimal dental health, all while creating a positive and fun environment. I have been practicing dental hygiene since 2015 and enjoy being part of the quality team at Fremont Dental Group.

Outside of my career, I enjoy spending time with my family, staying active, playing softball, and fishing.

Danelle Dental Hygienist My number-one goal as a dental hygienist is gentle patient care. I am passionate about my job because it gives me a chance to interact one on one with patients not only about dentistry, but on a personal level, which builds long-lasting relationships. I am proud of our team’s ability to listen to our patients, educate them about their options, and then build a treatment plan that best suits that particular patient’s needs.

My husband, Jason, and I have three boys named Trey, Maverik, and Laker who keep us on our toes. I enjoy spending time with family and friends, going to the kids’ activities, water skiing, running, baking, and entertaining.

When not working, I am very busy with my husband, Dan, and our sons, Ryker and Owen. I enjoy spending time with my family and friends, love to watch and play sports, and do my fair share of shopping and scrapbooking.

Kelsey Dental Hygienist I’m excited to be part of a team that is enthusiastic about dental health. Here at Fremont Dental Group, we work together to provide our patients with great care and a positive dental experience.

When I’m not cleaning teeth I’m busy chasing my son, Pierce, and daughters, Raya and Sylvia. We love being outside, going on walks, fishing, and kayaking. I also enjoy a good book if I can find the time.

Marcy Dental Assistant I have more than 20 years of experience as a dental assistant and have worked at Fremont Dental Group since 2012. I love working in an office with the friendliest office staff and the greatest patients who appreciate our services. I take great pride in providing a good dental experience. It is my job to inform patients of any needed treatment and the reasons why it’s important so that they can improve their dental health. My goal is to make each patient comfortable and ensure that they receive the best care.

I am originally from Fremont and live with my husband of 27 years, Reggie. We have two children. Mariah is currently a junior in college and our son, Riley, is a senior in high school. We enjoy attending their activities, being outdoors on the patio, riding the motorcycle, RVing, boating, and taking out our old ’72 Blazer.

It is my privilege to make sure that you enjoy being at the dentist as much as one possibly can. I am convinced that patients can "feel" when they are being treated ethically and with true compassion, and that’s what we provide at FDG.

When I am away fro work, you will find me working in the garden, curled up with a good book, traveling, or spending time with my husband and grown children.

My husband, Dennis, and I have a wonderful family which includes my sons, Brayden, Jordan and wife Alicia with Coralyn and Brooks, daughter, Lexie and her husband Nick and grandsons, Henry, Beckett, Carter, and AJ. I love playing tennis and riding bikes. My friends all call me Crazy Jayne because I am all about having fun!
