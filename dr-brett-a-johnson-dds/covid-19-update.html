Covid 19 Update

Prior to your appointment, Robyn or another staff member will call you to go over some screening questions. When you come to your dental appointment, please come alone if possible. We are limiting the number of people allowed in the office at any time. When you arrive, please remain outside. When we are ready for you, Robyn or another staff member will come out for you and will check your temperature before we admit you to the office. There will be some additional preparation and sterilization done during your visit.

Because our schedule will be limited for the foreseeable future, even if you are already scheduled for an appointment after we are open, we may still have to reschedule you to a later date. We will be contacting everyone prior to appointments; if you do not hear from us, please contact us before coming to an appointment. Please be patient if we cannot get you in right away, especially for hygiene.

There will be a charge of $35 per visit which will cover some of the costs of these additional safeguards. This charge will be due regardless of insurance coverage.

The Covid 19 situation is very fluid and things change often, so please bear with us as we do our best to treat patients in the safest way possible. We are very excited to see all of you again!
