The practice

Our advanced dental technology allows for the removal of third molars, commonly referred to as the wisdom teeth, which are particularly prone to problems both above the gumline as well as below.

When it’s time to replace missing teeth, whether it’s one or a full set, several options might be available to you. A long-lasting choice, implant-supported dentistry brings you as close to growing new teeth as possible. The stability and sense of confidence you develop with implants will leave you pleasantly surprised.

Dr. Shirley is a board certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon. He is one of few oral surgeons in our country who is fellowship trained in Microvascular Oncology and Reconstructive Surgery.

Dr. Shirley has contributed to textbooks and publications and has given numerous presentations on various subjects in the field of oral and maxillofacial surgery. He is part time faculty at LSU Shreveport and JPS Hospital in Ft. Worth where he is trusted to train the next generation of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons.
