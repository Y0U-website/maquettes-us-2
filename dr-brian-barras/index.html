The practice

After graduating, Dr. Barras worked for two years as a scientist for Lockheed Martin Space Operations at NASA’s Johnson Space Center. There he assisted with astronaut and cosmonaut training. In 2007, Dr. Barras fulfilled his passion for serving in the healthcare profession and enrolled at Baylor College of Dentistry. He received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree in 2011 and began his…

Dr. Barras is Board Certified in Pediatric Dentistry. He completed his pediatric dental training at Children’s Medical Center Dallas and Texas Scottish Rite Hospital where he worked with many children who had special health care needs. Dr. Barras trained extensively in various behavior management techniques, including oral sedation and dental care in the operating room under general anesthesia.…

He currently has hospital privileges at St. Joseph’s Hospital in Bryan and College Station.

Dr. Barras married his Aggie wife, Courtney, in College Station in 2006. They have three wonderful children; Hannah, Andrew, and Mary. Dr. Barras and his wife enjoy traveling, exercising as a family, and volunteering at their church. Dr. Barras also fulfills his interest in music through playing the saxophone and piano.
