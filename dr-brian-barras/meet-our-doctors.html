Larry Kalke, DMD

Dr. Kalke grew up in Southern Utah. He received his undergraduate degree from the University of Utah, and attended dental school at CASE Western Reserve University in Cleveland, Ohio. After completing an Advance Education in General Dentistry at CASE, he decided that helping children was where his passion was and chose to specialize in pediatric dentistry.

He completed his specialty training in Pediatric Dentistry at Rainbow Babies and Children’s Hospital in Ohio, where he learned to provide comprehensive dental care for well children and those with special needs including emergency care, procedures in dental office using conscious sedation, and treatment in the operating room and office under general anesthesia. Dr. Kalke is Board Certified in Pediatric Dentistry. He also enjoys spending time at CE courses keeping up-to-date with the current technologies that are available.

For children requiring their treatment in the hospital, Dr. Kalke has privileges at The College Station Medical Center (The MED), and at St. Joseph’s Hospital in Bryan.

“I am married to the perfect person, Misty, and we have four great children. Watching our family thrive in Aggieland has been great! We love the community and the spirit that is in this area of Texas. When we are not running from one kids activities to another we enjoy spending time with friends we have made here. Our family could not imagine being anyplace else."

Matthew Ng, DMD, MSD

Upon completion of orthodontic residency in 2004, Dr. Ng returned to Canada to enter practice with the largest pediatric dental group in Vancouver, Canada. There he learned the unique orthodontic skills necessary to properly manage developing bite problems in kids as young as age 4. In 2008, he made the move to Texas, where he has been in practice in Houston and its surrounding areas.

Dr. Ng is married to his Aggie wife, Theresa, and they have three beautiful children, Andrew, Noah, and Ava. He is actively involved in his Church and his hobbies include exploring culinary adventures, Astros Baseball and Aggie Football of course! “College Station is a place I have grown to love through my wife as a visitor for many years. I am excited to join the Brazos Valley Pediatric Dentistry family and look forward to serving the people of Brazos County and surrounding communities.”
