Brian Drew, DDS

Dr. Brian Drew completed a Bachelor of Science degree in biology with a minor in chemistry from Friend’s University in Wichita, Kansas, before attended dental school at the University of Missouri at Kansas City, from which he graduated in 1998. He regularly completes continuing education to stay at the forefront of the dental field, attending several large conventions each year and reviewing numerous journals to keep up-to-date with the latest advances in dentistry. Dr. Drew has practiced in Shawnee since July 1998.

Dr. Drew and his wife Becky have five children, Adam, Noah, Luke, Rachel and Sarah. Outside of the office, his hobbies include soccer and cars.
