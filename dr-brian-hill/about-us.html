Meet the Team at Lake Country Dental Center

For over 35 years, we have been serving the communities of Graham, Jacksboro, Newcastle, Bryson, and Olney, TX, and many of our team members are familiar faces. Dr. Brian Hill leads our dedicated team, and we focus on providing high quality dentistry combined with the personalized care that our patients have come to expect.

Get to know a little more about us here before your first visit so you ll feel right at home when you meet us!

Dr. Dr. Brian Hill

Dr. Hill is passionate about getting to know his patients personally and building a relationship with each one. He believes that comprehensive dentistry is the key to helping his patients improve their overall health.

He is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Dental Association, the Texas Dental Association, and the Thirteenth District Dental Society

Dr. Hill is married to his beautiful wife Meredith and they have two children, Harrison and Cora. He enjoys spending his free time hunting and fishing in the great outdoors. He also enjoys playing golf as well as watching sports of all kinds.

Tammy has watched many of our patients grow up and now enjoys seeing their children come to the practice. She s an expert at coordinating patients schedules and insurance benefits so our patients can receive the care they need in a timely manner. Her goal is to ensure that everyone has an enjoyable visit.

When she s not with patients, Tammy can be found crafting, baking, and shopping.
