Welcome to Our Practice

Dr. Brian Hill and his team would like to welcome you to Lake Country Dental Center!

The practice

For over 35 years, this practice has been serving patients in Graham, TX along with the surrounding communities of Jacksboro, Newcastle, Bryson, and Olney. Dr. Hill is honored to carry on the proud tradition of high quality dentistry and excellent patient care that has been established here over the years.

Dr. Hill spends a great deal of time getting to know his patients and building relationships with them.

We know that your oral health affects your overall health and well being, and we re proud to be part of your health care team. Whether it s providing you with a thorough cleaning and examination, repairing a tooth, or offering nutrition advice, we re here to meet all of your dental needs.

We welcome children to our practice and make it convenient for every member of the family to get the dental care they need. We know that first dental visits are important for children, and our goal is to make your little one s experience as pleasant and stress-free as possible.

Patients who are new to our practice appreciate the personalized attention they receive from our team. We take x-rays and review them with you so you have a clear understanding of your treatment needs. Our hygienist will provide a thorough yet comfortable cleaning for new patients, and Dr. Hill will talk with you about your goals for your oral health during your comprehensive examination.

Many of our new patients are pleasantly surprised at the level of care they receive when they choose Lake Country Dental Center as their dental home.

Dr. Hill has equipped our office with advanced technology in order to make your experience more comfortable.

Patients who feel anxious about their dental visits may benefit from the use of nitrous oxide, or laughing gas, which helps you to relax and feel more at ease during your treatment. It is also safe for you to drive home on your own and continue normal activities after the use of laughing gas.
