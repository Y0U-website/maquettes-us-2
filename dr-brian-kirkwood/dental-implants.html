What to Expect from the Dental Implant Process

Your implant journey begins with a detailed evaluation using advanced digital imaging and diagnostics. Treatment typically includes:

If bone loss is present, we may recommend Bone Grafting or Ridge Augmentation prior to implant placement to ensure long-term success.

  • Consultation and Planning : We assess bone health and overall oral condition to determine candidacy.
  • Implant Placement : A titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone and allowed to heal and integrate.
  • Healing Period : Most patients heal over several months while the implant fuses with the bone.
  • Abutment and Restoration : We attach a connector and place a custom crown, bridge, or denture.
