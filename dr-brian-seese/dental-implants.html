Implant Dentistry

Dental implants are artificial teeth that have the potential to last your entire life. Each implant is a prosthetic root that is anchored into the jaw itself, just like a real tooth. When new bone has fused around this prosthetic root, the implant can support an abutment with a fixed prosthesis such as a crown, bridge or denture.

If your mouth is healthy and without gum disease or bone loss, your dental implant can last for the rest of your life. In fact, dental implants have the highest success rating of any dental therapy; as high as 98 %.

The number and type of implants you need will depend on the type of restoration that you choose.
