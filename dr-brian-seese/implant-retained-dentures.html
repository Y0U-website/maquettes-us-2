Implant Denture Specialist

Who is an implant retained denture candidate? To be a candidate for dental implants, you need to have a healthy amount of bone in your jaw. Each implant is anchored into the bone and then the denture is secured to it. Once Dr. Seese attaches the denture, it never moves out of place. It’s like having an entirely new smile all over again. To assess your bone levels, we will need to take a series of digital x-rays. These images will show us where you have healthy bone levels, require any grafting or have other factors that affect the health of the implants before they are set into your jaw.

If you’ve struggled with bad fitting dentures or want something that offers you more freedom, contact us today to learn more about our implant retained denture option or to schedule a consultation. You won’t regret it!
