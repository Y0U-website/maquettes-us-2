This program provides discounts on everything from cleanings and x-rays to crowns and fillings! Our Wellness Program includes the following: 2 cleaning per year (absence of gum/periodontal disease)
  • 2 exams per year (includes Comprehensive Exam)
  • 1 Emergency Exam
  • 2 Oral Cancer screenings per year
  • 1 set of Bitewing X-rays per year (4 films)
  • Any additional x-rays needs during the year
  • 15 % OFF all procedures in-office including: Additional cleaning (if needed),
  • dental sealants,
  • fillings,
  • core buildups,
  • extractions,
  • root canals,
  • scaling and root planing (deep cleaning),
  • crowns,
  • veneers,
  • periodontal procedures,
  • dentures,
  • partials.

Eligibility

Any new patient or current patient in good standing can join this program. Current patients must have a zero balance at the time of signing. Annual enrollment fee must be paid in full at the start of the anniversary month.

Program Guidelines

  • Patient’s portion of bill due on the day of service.
  • Cannot be used in conjunction with any other form of dental insurance or coverage.
  • There will be a $50 reinstatement fee if your program lapses.
  • No refunds will be issued at any time if a participant decides not to utilize the dental plan.
  • In order to continue in our Wellness Program and take advantage of these amazing savings, a patient must keep with their 6-month cleanings.
  • The yearly fee will be due on your anniversary month.

Program Types

  • Adult (14+)
  • Child (under 14)
  • Family of Two
  • Family of Four
