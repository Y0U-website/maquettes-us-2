Although born in Iowa, Dr. Brian Swift considers himself a North Carolina native, having lived here since the age of two. He is proud to call this community home. He earned both his Bachelor of Science in Biology and Doctor of Dental Surgery degrees from UNC Chapel Hill, where he served as class president all four years.

Dr. Swift is a member of the North Carolina Dental Society, American Dental Association, and Academy of General Dentistry. He is also an active member of the Durham Orange County Dental Society, Pankey Study Club, and John S. Dilday Study Club. He is licensed to practice in both North Carolina and Tennessee.
