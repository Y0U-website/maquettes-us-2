Meet Dr. Alexander Fuller

Dr. Alexander Fuller is a 2016 graduate of University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine where he obtained his specialty certificate in Endodontics. He earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology at Pace University where he was a research assistant at the Memorial Sloan-Kettering Cancer Center. It was here Dr. Fuller began his journey into the dental world.

In 2014 Dr. Fuller earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry. During his time at Temple he was a research assistant in the Department of Restorative Dentistry and the Department of Graduate Endodontics. He received the Temple University School of Dentistry Outstanding Achievement Award as well as placing on the Deans list. In 2014 Dr. Fuller was honored to be a part of Temple’s service trip to Jamaica where he delivered dental care to communities in need.

Dr. Fuller is a board eligible Endodontist, member of the American Dental Association, Pennsylvania Dental Association, and the American Association of Endodontists.

Dr. Fuller grew up in Abington, PA and is proud to be working so close to his hometown. When not in the operatory saving teeth, Dr. Fuller enjoys traveling, hiking, tennis and watching his Philadelphia sports teams.
