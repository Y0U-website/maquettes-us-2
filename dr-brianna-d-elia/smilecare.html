Wrinkle Injections & Fillers

Wrinkle injections are minimally-invasive treatment that does more than smooth face wrinkles, crow’s feet, and frown lines. Dentist Brianna D’Elia DMD at Park Dental Loft administers injectable medication for dental care (such as reduced jaw pain related to TMJ) and cosmetic care. The injections that we use are additive-free and the results last longer than other commercially available injections. Dr. D’Elia understands the nuances of delicate facial anatomy. She is experienced in making skillful injections to the face that are both comfortable and designed to produce natural-looking results.
