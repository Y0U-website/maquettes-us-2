Uncompromised Service

At Kentucky Periodontics and Implant Dentistry, our methods are well researched and only proven materials are used. We utilize one of the leading implant systems and do NOT use generic implants that are not approved by the Food and Drug Administration. We understand that this may cost a little more, but we want to use the best materials because our patients deserve a tested, high-quality product.

Predictable Results

Dr. Camenisch has completed thousands of periodontal procedures with predictable results. Whether you are interested in replacing missing teeth with dental implants or trying to give yourself a healthy, disease-free smile, Kentucky Periodontics and Implant Dentistry will review your options and discuss the typical outcomes of any work performed.
