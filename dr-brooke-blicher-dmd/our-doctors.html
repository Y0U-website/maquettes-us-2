Doctors

Dr. Blicher joined Upper Valley Endodontics in 2009. She grew up in Florida and New Jersey, but was introduced to New England by her husband Gered, a native of northern Vermont.

Dr. Blicher continues to teach at both Harvard and Tufts dental schools, and is on dental staff at Dartmouth-Hitchcock Medical center. An active researcher, Dr. Blicher has several publications in major dental journals, and most recently co-authored the textbook Endodontics Review: A Study Guide . Dr. Blicher completed the process of board certification in 2013, and is a diplomate in the American Board of Endodontics. She is a member of the American College of Dentists.

Dr. Blicher is a co-founder of Pulp Nonfiction , an educational collaborative dedicated to the practical application of evidence-based endodontics techniques. She is active in organized dentistry both locally on the board of the Grafton-Sullivan Dental Society, and on a national level with the American Association of Endodontists.

Dr. Blicher enjoys the full breadth of endodontic care, and she is particularly interested in the diagnosis of painful conditions and dental resorption, an area that she and Dr. Lucier have published on extensively. She is adept in the management of patient anxiety and underlying medical complexities.

In her spare time, Dr. Blicher is an avid runner, hiker and skier. She lives in Norwich with her husband, two children, and their basset hound, Milton

Rebekah Lucier, DMD

Dr. Lucier joined Upper Valley Endodontics in 2013. She grew up in the Blackstone Valley region of central Massachusetts. Dr. Lucier is committed to providing the highest quality of endodontic care in a calm and caring environment. She enjoys both nonsurgical and surgical care, and is adept in providing care to pediatric patients.

Dr. Lucier is a co-founder of Pulp Nonfiction , an educational collaborative dedicated to the practical application of evidence-based endodontics techniques.

In her free time, Dr. Lucier enjoys running, hiking, cooking, nordic skiing, and playing the violin. She lives in Hanover with her husband, two children, and their yellow lab, Marty.

Adrienne Williams, MD

Dr. Adrienne Williams owns and operates Anesthesia Consultants of the Upper Valley and works to provide professional anesthesia services to offices throughout the area. She has provided advanced sedation services at Upper Valley Endodontics since 2017. She works closely with our endodontists to ensure a comfortable treatment experience for patients in need of sedation for endodontic treatment.

Dr. Williams studied Neurology, Physiology, and Behavior at the University of California, Davis, before earning her MD at Dartmouth Medical School. She completed her anesthesiology residency at Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Center, where she is currently an attending physician. She is board-certified in Anesthesiology and Pediatric Anesthesiology.

For more information on sedation dentistry services in our office, please visit our Sedation Dentistry page or contact our office .
