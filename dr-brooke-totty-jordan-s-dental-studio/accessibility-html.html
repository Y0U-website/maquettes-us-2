Accessibility Statement

  • Text alternatives for images and meaningful labels on all controls
  • Full keyboard operability, including image galleries and dialogs, with visible focus indicators and managed focus
  • Color contrast that meets or exceeds WCAG 2.1 AA ratios
  • Animations and auto-advancing content that pause on interaction and respect your system’s reduced-motion preference
  • A responsive layout that supports zoom up to 400 % and small-screen reflow

Known limitations

Some third-party content embedded on our site — such as the Google Maps location viewer and our online appointment scheduling provider — is not under our direct control and may not fully meet the same standards. If you have difficulty with any of these, please contact us and we will gladly assist you directly.

Feedback and assistance

If you experience any difficulty accessing part of this website, or would like to request information in an alternative format, please contact us. We take all feedback seriously and will work to resolve barriers promptly.
