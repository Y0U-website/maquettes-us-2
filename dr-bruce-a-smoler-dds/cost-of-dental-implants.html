The cost of Dental Implants can vary greatly and is dependent on a patient’s specific situation. Below is a few factors that go into determining the costs of dental implants. The number of teeth that need to be replaced – Single Tooth or Multiple Teeth
  • How many implants will be required to support the new teeth
  • The type of procedure (surgery) performed
  • The type of dental implant that will be used
  • The quality, amount, and location of bone. Note: It is common for the bone to be in poor condition or missing altogether. In these cases, bone grafting may be an additional recommended procedure.

Average Cost Of Dental Implants

The average cost of a single tooth implant ranges from $1000 to $3,000, in addition to $500 to $3000 for the abutment and crown. If additional procedures such as bone grafts, extractions, and extensive imaging and/or modeling are required, the price can increase.

Additional Factors That Can Affect The Cost Of Dental Implants

  • Initial procedures such as x-rays and CT scans
  • Brand, material, and type of post, abutment, and crown
  • Complexity of the surgery
  • Creation of the prosthodontics
  • Additional procedures (if required) such as sinus elevation and bone grafting
