Cosmetic Dentistry

  • 0 DENTAL SERVICES Cosmetic Dentistry At Guffee Dental , cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth while maintaining natural function and long-term oral health. Our caring team works with patients across Anderson, SC, who want to brighten, refine, or completely transform their smiles. Teeth Whitening The foods you eat and the passage of time will affect tooth color. A simple and painless process can restore your pearly whites to a brightness they haven’t had in years.
  • Tooth-Colored Restorations Composite fillings are strong resins that bond to your tooth structure, and are matched to the specific color of your teeth. These durable resins add to your natural smile, and stand the test of time.
  • Porcelain Veneers Dental laminates are wafer-thin porcelain shells that are bonded to the front side of teeth. Veneers offer cosmetic enhancement for teeth that are worn, discolored, chipped or misaligned…and they look great.
  • Tooth-Colored Crowns Covering a broken tooth with a metal crown may be functional, but a cap that matches your surrounding teeth is a far more natural appearance. A uniform, shining smile will improve your looks and your self-esteem. We provide same-day restorations.

Proudly Offering CEREC® One-Day Crowns and Restoration

CEREC® is advanced dental technology that allows crowns and veneers to be designed, created, and placed in a single visit —providing patients with fast, precise, and natural-looking results.
