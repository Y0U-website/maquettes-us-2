About Renaissance Dental

Our Story

Renaissance Dental was founded on a simple belief: patients deserve dentistry that puts their needs first. In an era of corporate consolidation and private equity takeovers, we remain proudly independent.

Our team of three experienced doctors—each with complementary areas of focus—allows us to handle 95 % of dental conditions in-house. You won’t be shuffled between multiple offices or referred endlessly to specialists you don’t know.

We’ve invested heavily in advanced technology like the Fotona Lightwalker Laser, TRIOS 3D Scanner, and Carestream Cone Beam—tools that make your visits more comfortable and deliver superior results.

Proud Member

AIDA is a community of independent dental practices committed to preserving patient-first care. Members pledge to resist corporate pressures and maintain clinical autonomy.

What We Stand For

Four core values guide every decision we make

Independent Ownership We’re not owned by private equity or corporate chains. As AIDA members, our decisions prioritize your health, not shareholder profits.

Patient-First Care No quotas, no pressure, no assembly-line dentistry. We take the time needed to do it right and build lasting relationships.

Comprehensive Team Three expert doctors with complementary areas of focus means 95 % of your dental needs handled in-house.

Advanced Technology We invest in cutting-edge equipment that makes your visits more comfortable and delivers better results.
