The practice

Our clinic is designed and constructed to the highest industry standards with the latest technology and progressive design. Our operatories are designed to make your visit quick and comfortable. Each treatment room has its own computer with a screen in easy view of the chair so the doctor can show you your treatment plan and xrays with no waiting.

With a combined 30+ years of experience, doctors Christopher Jones, Camille Secor, and Neil Stebelton are deeply knowledgeable about their chosen field. Likewise, the professionals on staff are all highly skilled and experienced. Their dental practice has a clear mandate of professional, compassionate dentistry.
