Our Team Orland Family Dental

  • Nina Patient Coordinator The friendly face who greets you when you arrive, Nina has been with us since 2011. Whether you have a question about your upcoming appointment or need to reschedule, she makes sure your experience runs smoothly. Nina is always ready with a smile—and an answer in English or Spanish.
  • Sue Chairside Assistant Sue has been a part of our family since 2002. She started assisting Dr. Frank and now works closely with Dr. Gina and Dr. Shannon. Sue is always ready with a humorous story that is sure to put a smile on your face.
  • Yevette Dental Hygienist Our longtime hygienist, Yevette, earned her license at Loyola in 1983 and has worked with the Orland family since 1988. Her calm approach puts even the most nervous patients at ease.
  • Denise Dental Hygienist Denise graduated from Loyola in 1978 and joined Orland Family Dental in 1992. With her hygiene knowledge, she’s helped generations of families keep their smiles healthy.
  • Isamar Chairside Assistant & Receptionist Isamar became part of the Orland Family Dental team in 2020. She primarily assists Dr. Cassie, but you’ll also spot her at the front desk on Fridays and Saturdays. Friendly and knowledgeable, she’s always happy to answer your dental questions — in both English and Spanish.
  • Francine Receptionist Francine joined us in 2019 after a career as a registered nurse. You’ll find her at the front desk during the week, ready to check you out after your visit and help with anything you need. Her calm, caring nature makes every appointment a little brighter.
