Instagram icon Google icon What are All-on-4 Implants? All-on-4 is a full arch tooth replacement technique that uses four to six dental implants per arch to support a complete set of fixed teeth. Unlike removable dentures that sit on top of the gums, All-on-4 implants are permanently anchored into the jawbone. They do not shift, do not require adhesive, and allow you to eat, speak, and smile with the same function and confidence as natural teeth.

The technique is designed for patients who are missing all or most of their teeth in one or both arches, or who have teeth failing beyond repair. One of its key advantages is that the angled placement of the posterior implants often eliminates the need for separate bone grafting, even in patients with moderate bone loss.

How All-on-4 Works

The process begins with a consultation where we take 3D images, evaluate your bone, and develop a digital treatment plan. On the day of surgery, any remaining failing teeth are extracted, the implants are placed, and a temporary set of teeth is attached. You leave the office the same day with a full set of functional teeth. Over the next three to six months, the implants integrate with the jawbone. Once healed, the final permanent prosthesis is fabricated and placed by your restorative dentist.

All procedures are performed under IV sedation or general anesthesia for your comfort.

Why would All-on-4 Implants be needed?

You may want or even need all-on-4 implants if your current prosthesis is constantly slipping and falling out. Likewise, your prosthetic appliance might be uncomfortable or ill-fitting, causing you to feel pain or discomfort whenever you put it in. The implants easily keep the denture in place so that it won&#x27;t slip or budge. This allows for a more natural and realistic-looking smile.

All-on-4 vs. Dentures

Removable dentures rest on the gums and are held with adhesive. They slip during eating and speaking, require nightly removal, and do not prevent jawbone loss. Over time, bone shrinkage causes dentures to fit poorly, requiring relining or replacement every 5 to 10 years.

All-on-4 implants are fixed to the jaw. They function like natural teeth, preserve bone health, and are maintained with regular brushing and professional cleanings. While the upfront cost is higher than dentures, the long-term value is significantly greater.

Who&#x27;s a good candidate for All-on-4 Implants?

The majority of patients who want all-on-4 implants can safely have the procedure done. Keep in mind that implants rely on healthy underlying bone mass to bond fully. If you have bone loss, we may suggest a bone grafting before the surgery can be done. We will go over your health and medical history before placing implants and you should be healthy enough to undergo the procedure and heal from it.
