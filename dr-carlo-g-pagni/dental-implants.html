What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a permanent, natural-looking solution for missing teeth. These titanium posts act as artificial tooth roots, supporting crowns, bridges, or dentures to restore your smile.

  • Long-lasting durability
  • Improved chewing and speech
  • Enhanced facial structure and bone health
  • A confident, natural-looking smile

How Dental Implants Work

Dental implants replace missing teeth with a permanent restoration anchored directly into the jawbone. Each implant consists of a titanium post (the replacement root), an abutment (the connector), and a custom crown, bridge, or prosthesis (the visible tooth). The titanium post fuses with the bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a foundation that is as stable as a natural tooth root. Unlike bridges or removable dentures, implants preserve jawbone health, do not depend on neighboring teeth for support, and can last a lifetime with proper care.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults with adequate bone volume and reasonable overall health are candidates for dental implants. Age is rarely a disqualifying factor. If you have been told you lack sufficient bone, bone grafting or a sinus lift can rebuild what is needed. Patients who smoke or have uncontrolled diabetes may need additional evaluation. The best way to find out is to schedule a consultation at one of our offices.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

  • What Are Dental Implants Made Of? Dental implants are primarily made of titanium, a biocompatible material that integrates seamlessly with your jawbone.
  • How Long Do Dental Implants Last? With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene are key.
  • Is the Dental Implant Procedure Painful? With advanced anesthesia options, most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure.
  • How Do I Care for My Dental Implants? Care for your implants just like natural teeth—brush, floss, and visit your dentist regularly.
  • What’s the Cost of Dental Implants in Tinley Park or Frankfort? The cost varies based on the type of implant and individual needs. Contact us for a personalized consultation.
  • Can I Get Dental Implants if I Have Bone Loss? Yes, in many cases. Procedures like bone grafting can help prepare your jawbone for implants.
  • What’s the Difference Between All-On-4 and Traditional Implants? All-On-4 uses fewer implants to support a full arch of teeth, making it a faster and more affordable option.
  • How Long Is the Recovery Period? Most patients recover within a few days to a week, but full healing for the implant to integrate can take a few months.
