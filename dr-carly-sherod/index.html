The practice

Dr. Sherod returned to school and received a dental assisting degree from Rochester Community Technical College, and she worked as a dental assistant for a short period of time. She continued her dental education at the University of Minnesota, where she graduated with her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2019.

In the summer of 2019, Drs. Brad and Alicia Bullard welcomed Dr. Sherod to their practice here at Walnut Street Dental. She has continued expanding her dental knowledge and technical skills utilizing the latest dental technologies to provide the best care possible to patients.
