The practice

At Tiny Teeth Pediatric Dentistry, our mission is to provide a happy and loving dental home for all children by delivering both high quality and compassionate dental care. By treating each patient as if they were a member of our own family, we hope to inspire not only a love for coming to the dentist, but also healthy smiles and healthy children for the future. We look forward to meeting both you…

We strive to make sure each child has a great experience while their comprehensive dental needs are met. From routine cleanings to complex treatment or emergencies we offer a wide array of services.

Our Patients are our family! We strive to ensure the absolute best care is given and treat your child as if they were one of our own. We will only recommend treatment that is necessary to ensure the health of your child’s beautiful smile!

We are also here for you, the parents. We know how important (yet difficult) it is to take care of your children’s teeth at home. During your appointment, we will go over the care that you need to be providing at home and look for problems as well. If needed, we will work with your child’s pediatrician.
