Steps for Getting a Dental Implant

Like many things worth waiting for, getting a dental implant is a process. Expect several appointments and a 6 8 month wait while the implant post heals and integrates. Here’s how it works:

  • Step 1 During a thorough evaluation, we use advanced imaging technology to assess jawbone density and map out nerve locations and optimal implant positioning.
  • Step 2 When planning is complete, your implant dentist places the titanium post in the jawbone under local anesthesia. Because we numb the area thoroughly, you won’t feel any pain during the procedure.
  • Step 3 Healing takes several months and is crucial to implant success. During this time, the titanium implant post fuses with the jawbone and creates a stable foundation for the tooth restoration.
  • Step 4 The final steps are securing the abutment and connecting the replacement tooth or teeth. We custom-craft the restoration to blend seamlessly with your smile and allow you to eat, speak, and smile comfortably again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a good dental implant candidate?

Most people with missing teeth make excellent candidates for dental implants , but our dental implant dentist in Zachary will evaluate several factors to ensure the best possible outcome for your specific situation.

Ideal Candidates Typically Have

  • Good Overall Health Your body needs to heal properly after implant placement, so conditions like uncontrolled diabetes or heavy smoking can complicate the process.
  • Adequate Bone Density Implants need a strong jawbone to anchor securely. If you ve been missing teeth for a while, some bone loss is normal, but it doesn t necessarily disqualify you.
  • Healthy Gums Active gum disease needs to be treated before implant placement to prevent complications.
  • Realistic Expectations Understanding the timeline and commitment involved helps ensure satisfaction with your results.

Bone Grafting Options

Sometimes, there isn’t enough dense jawbone to place an implant. Or, the position of the sinuses may not leave sufficient space to place implants in the upper arch.

For these cases, we offer bone grafting at Morris Family Dentistry. This process involves adding bone material to the site to supplement existing bone and make implant placement possible in the future.
