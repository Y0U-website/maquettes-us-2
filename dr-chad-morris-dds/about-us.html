Meet the Team at Morris Family Dentistry

At Morris Family Dentistry, our approach to dental care is built on principles that reflect Dr. Morris s professional philosophy and our deep commitment to Zachary, the community we ve served for nearly four decades. These guiding principles shape every interaction, every treatment decision, and every relationship we build with our patients.

Patient-Centered Care

Everything we do revolves around you and your family s unique needs, concerns, and comfort levels. We believe dental care should feel personal, not clinical which is why we take time to know your story, remember your preferences, and treat you like the valued neighbor and friend you are.

Comprehensive Treatment Philosophy

Rather than fixing individual issues, we look at your complete oral health. We believe in addressing underlying causes, not just symptoms, and considering how all aspects of your dental health work together. This whole-person perspective helps us provide care that supports not just healthy teeth and gums, but your total wellness.

Commitment to Lifelong Learning

Dentistry constantly evolves, and we evolve with it. Dr. Morris and our team actively pursue continuing education because we believe you deserve the benefits of the latest techniques, materials, and approaches. Our commitment to staying current means you receive care that reflects today s best practices.

Complete Dentistry Approach

Instead of viewing teeth in isolation, we analyze how your jaw joints, bite, muscles, and teeth function as a cohesive system. With this thorough evaluation, we can develop treatment plans designed for your unique anatomy and needs, helping to prevent future issues while enhancing function and comfort.

Dr. Chad Morris

Dr. Chad Morris received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 2001 from the LSU School of Dentistry, where he was a member of the C. Edmond Kells Honor Society and the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Honor Society. Growing up in Zachary, it was always his desire to return here to establish his dental practice. He graduated from Zachary High School in 1994 and attended Louisiana Tech University for his undergraduate studies.

Advanced Training and Specialties

Dr. Morris has done extensive coursework on the effects of toxins such as mercury on the body and strives to bring a whole-body approach to his dentistry. He has completed the International Academy of Oral Medicine and Toxicology (IAOMT) coursework for safe amalgam removal (SMART Protocol), where he is also a member. He recognizes the relationship between oral health and the overall health of patients.

Personal Life

In his free time, Dr. Morris enjoys hunting, fishing, playing his guitar, and spending time with his family. He and his wife, Ashley, have four children: Kent, Madison, Avery, and Gabrielle. Family and faith in God are both very important to him, and it is his goal to reflect this in his life and his dental practice.
