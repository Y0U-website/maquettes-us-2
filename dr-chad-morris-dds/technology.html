Technology

Nothing will ever replace human connections, but advanced technology at Morris Family Dentistry plays an integral role in improving your oral health, enhancing comfort and efficiency, and ensuring the best possible outcomes. Please take a few moments to familiarize yourself with what we offer and all the benefits our technology offers.

CBCT Cone Beam Technology

3D Printing

Our in-house 3D printer creates custom models, surgical guides, and appliances right in our office. Whether we re making models for treatment planning, precise surgical guides for implant placement, or custom splints for TMJ treatment, 3D printing technology allows us to provide faster, more personalized care while ensuring perfect fit and function.

Digital Impressions

Gone are the days of messy impression putty! Our digital impression system uses advanced scanning technology to create precise 3D models of your teeth in minutes. Used for crowns, bridges, and orthodontic treatment planning, digital impressions are faster, more comfortable, and more accurate than traditional methods, often eliminating the need for retakes.

Digital X-Rays & Sensors

Our digital x-ray system captures detailed images instantly, eliminating the need for traditional film processing. Used for routine checkups, diagnosing cavities, and treatment planning, digital x-rays expose patients to 90 % less radiation than conventional x-rays while providing crystal-clear images that help Dr. Morris detect problems early when they re easier to treat.

Intraoral Cameras

This small handheld camera captures high-resolution images inside your mouth, allowing you and Dr. Morris to see exactly what s happening with your teeth and gums. Used during exams and consultations, the intraoral camera helps you understand your treatment needs by showing you what the doctor sees, making you a more informed partner in your dental care.

Panoramic X-Rays

A panoramic x-ray captures your entire mouth all teeth, jaws, and surrounding structures in one comprehensive image. Perfect for evaluating wisdom teeth, detecting jaw problems, planning orthodontic treatment, and getting a complete overview of your oral health, this technology provides valuable diagnostic information quickly and comfortably, helping Dr. Morris spot issues that might not show up on smaller x-rays.

Noise-Canceling Headphones

Sometimes the most effective technology is about comfort. Our noise-cancelling headphones let you listen to your favorite music or podcasts during treatment, blocking out dental sounds that might cause anxiety. Whether you re having a routine cleaning or a longer procedure, these headphones help you stay relaxed and make your visit more enjoyable.
