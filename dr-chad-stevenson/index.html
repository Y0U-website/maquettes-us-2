The practice

Fixed, full-arch prostheses are supported by at least 4 implants. They go in, and stay in, your mouth, just like natural teeth. This is the most tooth-like replacement option available.

Using a system of o-ring like attachments, overdentures are a removable prosthesis that snaps onto dental implants in a way that greatly increases surety and staying power.

When 3 or more teeth need to be replaced in a row, a bridge can allow you to replace multiple teeth on a smaller number of dental implants, which decreases the overall cost.

A single tooth implant simply puts back a missing tooth, using 3 machined parts to put back a whole tooth- root to crown.
