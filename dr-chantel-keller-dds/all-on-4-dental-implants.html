Get Your New Smile Fast

One of the best parts about All-on-4 and All-on-6 implants is how quickly you can go from your current situation to a brand-new smile.

Here is how the process works in just a few simple steps:

  • Your Consultation: We take a 3D Cone Beam CT scan of your mouth to plan exactly where the implants will go.
  • The Procedure: In a single visit, we handle any necessary extractions and place your new implants.
  • Your New Smile: Our dental lab crafts your new teeth overnight, so you’ll have a beautiful, fully functional set attached very shortly after your procedure. You won’t be walking around with a gap in your smile!
  • The Final Arch: Once your mouth has fully healed — usually a few months later — we’ll bring you back in to attach your permanent, custom-crafted arch. The final result looks and functions just like natural teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4, All-on-6, & Full Arch Dental Implants

What is the difference between All-on-4 and All-on-6 dental implants?

While both implant options give you a beautiful, permanent new set of upper or lower teeth, the difference is the number of implants used. All-on-4 uses four implants and is great for patients who might have a little less bone density, while All-on-6 uses six implants for even more stability. During your visit, Dr. Winegar will take a look at your smile and let you know which one is the best fit for you!

Am I a candidate for All-on-4 dental implants?

If you are missing most or all of your teeth, there’s a very good chance you’re a candidate! Because of the way All-on-4 implants are angled, they’re often a great fit even if you’ve been told in the past that you don’t have enough bone for traditional implants. The easiest way to find out is to schedule a visit at our Temple, TX office for a quick consultation and a Cone Beam CT scan.

How much do All-on-4 dental implants cost?

When you come in for a consultation, we’ll give you a clear, easy-to-understand cost estimate with no surprises. Plus, we offer no-interest financing through CareCredit and work with most major PPO insurance plans to make sure your new smile fits your budget.

What is the recovery time for All-on-4 dental implants?

You might have a little swelling or mild soreness for the first few days, but it’s usually very manageable with normal pain medication. You’ll get to enjoy a soft-food diet for a few weeks while your implants get cozy and fuse with your jawbone. Most folks are back to their normal daily routine within just a few days!

What does teeth in a day mean?

It means exactly what it sounds like! You can walk into our office with failing teeth, and walk out the very same day with a full, temporary set of teeth securely attached to your new implants. You’ll never have to go without a smile while you heal. Once your mouth is fully healed a few months later, we’ll swap the temporary teeth for your permanent, custom-made arch.

How do I care for full arch dental implants?

Taking care of your new smile is super easy. Just brush twice a day with a soft toothbrush, use a water flosser to keep things clean underneath the arch, and keep coming to see us for your regular check-ups. You never have to take them out and can treat them like they’re your natural teeth!

How is All-on-4 different from traditional dental implants?

If you were to replace a whole mouthful of teeth with traditional implants, you’d need an implant post for every single tooth. That means more surgery, more healing time, and a higher cost. All-on-4 and All-on-6 let us support a whole row of teeth on just four or six posts. It’s a much faster, easier, and more affordable way to get your full smile back!
