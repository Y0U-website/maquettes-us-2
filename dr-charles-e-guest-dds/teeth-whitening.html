  • Take-Home Whitening – An effective method to whiten your teeth, although you play the greatest role in the results. Several weeks may be needed to achieve your desired level of whiteness. First, a mold of your teeth is created and two thin, flexible plastic mouth trays are custom made for you. You will fill the trays with whitening gel and position each tray over the top and bottom teeth. The trays are to be left in for roughly an hour.
