All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • What is an implant-supported bridge? An implant-supported bridge is a durable, long-term solution for replacing multiple missing teeth. Instead of relying on adjacent natural teeth for support, this type of bridge is secured by dental implants placed in the jawbone. This provides exceptional stability, helps preserve bone health, and restores natural function and appearance. Implant-supported bridges enable patients to speak, chew, and smile with confidence while maintaining the integrity of their surrounding teeth.
  • What is the aftercare process? Following your All-on-4 procedure, proper aftercare is essential for long-term comfort and stability. Patients should follow all post-operative instructions, including maintaining gentle oral hygiene, adhering to recommended dietary guidelines, and attending scheduled follow-up visits. Temporary swelling or discomfort is normal and typically subsides within a few days. Consistent care and communication with our team help ensure optimal healing and the lasting success of your new implant-supported smile.
