Dental Implants - Procedure & Cost

  • What is the process for dental implants? The initial consultation includes a review of oral health, imaging, and a discussion of your goals.
  • Treatment planning outlines the steps for implant placement and the required preparatory procedures.
  • Any necessary bone grafting is completed to ensure adequate support for the implant.
  • The implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone under precise, guided techniques.
  • The healing period allows the implant to integrate securely with the bone structure.
  • A custom abutment and crown are designed to achieve natural function and appearance.
  • Final review confirms comfort and fit.
  • How much do dental implants cost? Dental implants typically range from $2,442 to $4,442 per implant. Factors such as insurance coverage and case complexity can also impact the overall cost. See All Office Fees
