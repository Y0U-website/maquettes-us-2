Affordable Dental Membership Plans

Patient Discount Plan A Simpler Way to Access Quality Dental Care No dental insurance? The Local Dentist Membership Plan provides a straightforward way to receive high-quality dental care at special member rates .

Administered by Clerri, LLC, the plan allows members to access meaningful savings on eligible dental services provided at The Local Dentist. Enrollment only takes a few minutes, and members pay the practice directly at the applicable member rate.

Important Consumer Disclosures

This plan is a discount plan and is not insurance coverage .

The range of discounts available under the plan will vary depending on the dental service received.

Clerri, LLC, the discount plan organization, does not make payments to providers for dental services received under the plan.

Plan members are responsible for paying for all dental services but will receive the applicable discount from participating providers that have a current agreement with Clerri, LLC.
