What is cosmetic dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry, or smile restoration, consists of dental services with the main goal of improving looks. Our patients often choose to repair damage, cover up imperfections, and correct misaligned or gapped teeth with our cosmetic smile makeover services in Covington, LA.
