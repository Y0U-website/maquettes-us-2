Dr. Charly Rose

Dr. Rose is a proud member of the following societies and associations:

  • She got into dental school a year early.
  • In 2015, she graduated with her degree in Dentistry from Indiana University School of Dentistry .
  • After graduating from dental school, Dr. Rose moved to North Carolina to work in Public Health Dentistry .
  • She worked with and provided dental care to the underserved, uninsured patients and migrant farmworkers in Jackson, Roanoke Rapids, and Henderson, North Carolina.
  • Dr. Rose is committed to mission work. She helped facilitate the first FREE dental clinic in Indianapolis run by dental and medical students. She has also participated in mission trips around the world.
  • North Carolina Dental Society
  • American Dental Association
  • Christian Medical and Dental Association
