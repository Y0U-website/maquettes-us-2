Your First Visit

  • Periodontal Evaluation: Assessment of gum health and early signs of disease.
  • Wellness Scan: A comprehensive digital scan that captures a 3D model of your teeth and bite, allowing us to monitor changes, track wear, and plan treatment with accuracy.
  • Digital X-rays: Low-radiation imaging for a clear view of bone and tooth structure.
  • Intraoral Photos: High-resolution images taken inside your mouth to show details of your teeth and gums — helping you see exactly what we see.
  • Personalized Home Care Guidance: Tips and tools tailored to your needs for maintaining a healthy smile between visits.

Doctor Examination

Following your hygiene visit, Dr. Patel or Dr. Hughes will complete a comprehensive exam focused on your long-term oral health.

Our Doctors approach every exam as a collaborative process — clearly explaining what’s observed and guiding you through each option. Our goal is to help you make confident, informed decisions about your care.

Your Doctor Examination Includes:

  • Detailed evaluation of teeth, gums, and bite alignment
  • Review of intraoral images and wellness scan results
  • Oral cancer and TMJ screening
  • Discussion of findings and customized treatment recommendations
