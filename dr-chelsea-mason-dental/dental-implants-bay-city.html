The Dental Implant Process at Mason Dental

  • Free consultation. We assess your oral health, jawbone density, and goals.
  • 3D treatment planning. We use digital imaging to map the optimal implant placement.
  • Implant placement. Usually a single in-office procedure under local anesthesia (or sedation if you prefer).
  • Healing period. 3–6 months for the bone to fuse to the implant. A temporary tooth is often placed.
  • Abutment + crown. Once healed, we attach the abutment and secure your custom crown, bridge, or denture.
  • Ongoing care. Routine cleanings and checkups to keep your implants healthy for decades.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults in good general health are candidates for dental implants. Ideal candidates:

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Have sufficient jawbone (bone grafting is an option if bone is thin)
  • Have healthy gums
  • Are committed to good oral hygiene
  • Don’t smoke (or are willing to quit during healing)
  • Don’t have uncontrolled medical conditions that affect healing (diabetes, severe gum disease)

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Coverage varies widely. Many insurance plans cover part of the implant cost (often the crown portion). We accept Delta Dental, Blue Cross Blue Shield, MetLife, Cigna, Aetna, Guardian, and most major plans, and we will verify your benefits before starting treatment.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, implants often last 25+ years or a lifetime. The implant (titanium post) is extremely durable; the crown may need replacement after 10–15 years due to normal wear.

Is the implant procedure painful?

The procedure itself is done under local anesthesia and sedation options are available for anxious patients. Most patients report mild discomfort for a day or two, controlled easily with over-the-counter pain relief. Most patients return to normal activities within 1–2 days.

Can I get implants if I have dentures already?

Yes. Many of our full-arch implant patients come to us frustrated with loose, uncomfortable dentures. Implant-supported dentures or All-on-4 offer a dramatic quality-of-life improvement.

What is All-on-4?

All-on-4 is a technique where 4 implants per arch support a full set of fixed teeth. It’s ideal for patients missing most or all of their teeth in one or both arches. In many cases, a temporary set of teeth can be placed the same day as the implant surgery.

How long does the entire implant process take?

From consultation to final crown: typically 4–8 months. The bulk of that is the 3–6 month healing period. Active in-office time is relatively minimal.
