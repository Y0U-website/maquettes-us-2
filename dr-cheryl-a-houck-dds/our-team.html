Cheryl A. Houck, DDS

Dr. Houck has been in the dental industry since she worked as an undergraduate dental assistant. After finishing school, she worked serving low-income families at Marian Clinic in Topeka for two years. Dr. Houck worked for several years as an associate at Topeka Smiles before partnering with Dr. Beckler.

Dr. Houck enjoys sailing, reading, traveling, and hiking in her free time. She maintains a belief that smiles are universal — giving people the happy, healthy smiles they’ve always wanted is one of her top priorities.

  • Doctor of Dental Surgery — University of Nebraska

  • Continuing education in DOCS oral conscious sedation, Invisalign™, and Las Vegas Institute’s Core 1 course

Michael M. Myers, DDS

Dr. Myers is a native of Topeka, graduating from Topeka High School. While Dr. Myers didn’t come from a family with a background in dentistry, many of his good family friends have careers in the dental field. Dr. Myers practiced dentistry for 3 years in Holton, Kansas before joining Drs. Houck and Beckler at Topeka Smiles. Dr. Myers prides himself on being committed to providing comfortable, quality care to his patients at Topeka Smiles.

In his spare time, you will likely find Dr. Myers spending time at the lake, wrangling his puppy Truman, or planning his next trip with his wife, Morgan.

  • Dentist
  • Human Biology, BA — University of Kansas
  • Doctor of Dental Surgery — University of Missouri — Kansas City
