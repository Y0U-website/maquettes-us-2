Patient Information

As a courtesy, our front office will be happy to file your dental insurance claims.

Nearly all dental services qualify for HSA or FSA funds. Some dental products (including prescription toothpastes and Sonicare toothbrushes) can be covered as well, but it would be best to check your plan benefits.

Your dental benefit from your insurance may vary for a number of reasons, such as:

Your dentist diagnoses and provides treatment based on your needs and on his/her professional judgment and not on the cost of that care. Some insurance plans exclude coverage for necessary treatment in order to reduce their costs. Your plan may not include this particular treatment or procedure, although your dentist deemed the treatment necessary.

We are happy to estimate your payment portion. Your payment portion will vary according to your individual plan, your maximum allowable coverage, and other factors. Ultimately, the patient portion is not known until we have received payment from your insurance company.

The time for a dental insurance company to process an insurance claim varies. If your insurance carrier does not pay as anticipated or tries to deny coverage for your treatment that should have been eligible for benefits, an appeal may be filed which can extend the time frame for reimbursement. If you feel that your insurance company is delaying payment, please feel free to contact them or report them to the Kansas insurance commissioner.

Our team will do its best to answer all of your insurance questions. Please keep in mind that there are many insurance plans available and that your employer chooses your plan and your benefits. If you believe your benefits are inadequate, you may want to discuss the matter with your plan administrator and explore appropriate alternatives.
