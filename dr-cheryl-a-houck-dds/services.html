Botox® treatments

In addition to general and cosmetic dentistry, we’re pleased to offer Botox® treatments for both aesthetic enhancement and therapeutic relief. Cosmetic Botox Smooth fine lines and wrinkles around the forehead, eyes, and mouth for a naturally refreshed appearance. Preventive Dentistry

Preventive dentistry is the practice of caring for your teeth and gums to keep them healthy. It stops cavities, gum disease, and enamel wear before they start.

Ask Us About Mouth Guards for Grinding Teeth

At Topeka Smiles, we make custom mouth guards that protect your teeth from nocturnal grinding (sleep bruxism) and clenching. If you have restless nights and wake up with a sore jaw, we can help.

NightGuard is designed to protect your teeth against harmful nighttime grinding and clenching. Your NightGuard mouth guard will be customized to your mouth size and shape and will create a barrier to cushion the force of grinding between your upper and lower teeth. Get relief from clenching, grinding, and TMJ!

NTI is another mouth guard option. An NTI mouth guard will prevent tooth grinding, and it’s designed to break the habit of tooth grinding or help your muscles relax to reduce grinding. Call for your appointment and let us help you determine which of these solutions can help you sleep better and protect your teeth.
