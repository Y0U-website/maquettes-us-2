We are offering the COVID-19 vaccine to patients 6 months or older. Also inquire about vaccines for pneumonia and Influenza (Flu). Call your local clinic to make an appointment.

As we recognize National Immunization Awareness Month , we encourage individuals and families to stay informed about recommended vaccinations and to partner with their healthcare providers to protect themselves and those around them. Immunizations remain one of the most effective ways to prevent serious illness and support healthier communities.
