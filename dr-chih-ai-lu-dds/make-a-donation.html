Make a Donation

Community support is vital to Northern Valley Indian Health’s mission of establishing and maintaining an effective and efficient healthcare system that ensures services are accessible, available, and of high quality for all patients. With the help of generous donors, Northern Valley Indian Health can purchase medical and dental equipment, offer specialty programs and services, and provide care for the underserved. We welcome and deeply appreciate financial support because it allows us to provide quality health care to all members of our communities.

Northern Valley Indian Health is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization and financial contributions to Northern Valley Indian Health are tax-deductible. Contributions can be made online with a link soon to be provided.
