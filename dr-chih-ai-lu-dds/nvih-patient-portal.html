ACCESS YOUR NVIH PORTAL THROUGH HEALOW™

Northern Valley Indian Health’s Portal facilitates better communication with your provider’s office by providing convenient 24/7 access from the comfort and privacy of your own home or office.

NVIH’s healow portal allows you to securely view your medical records, manage your care, pay bills and access telehealth.

Don’t have a healow account yet? Sign-up is easy!

With your healow account, you can view your health record, including:

  • Assessment and treatment information
  • Diagnosis, health concerns and goals
  • Medications and allergies information
  • Vital signs, such as blood pressure
  • Request or view medical appointments
  • Access health information from trusted resources
  • Pay your bill or set up automatic payments
  • Update your insurance and demographic information
  • Communicate with a customer service representative
