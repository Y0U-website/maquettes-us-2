Dr. Chloe Beninati, D.M.D.

Dr. Chloe Beninati, D.M.D.

Dr. Chloe Beninati is a highly-skilled and compassionate endodontist dedicated to providing exceptional care. Born and raised just outside of Philadelphia, PA , Dr. Beninati’s journey into dentistry began with her undergraduate studies in Psychology, inspiring a career focused on helping others and serving her community.

Education and Advanced Training

Dr. Beninati’s commitment to excellence is evident in her extensive academic achievements:

  • She earned dual degrees—her DDS (Doctor of Dental Surgery) and a Master of Public Health (MPH) —from Stony Brook University School of Dental Medicine , where she graduated Valedictorian .
  • She completed a rigorous General Practice Residency at NYP Brooklyn Methodist Hospital.
  • Dr. Beninati returned to Stony Brook to complete two additional years of specialty training in Endodontics , focusing on root canal therapy and complex dental procedures.
  • She was recently honored with the prestigious Foundation for Endodontics & Dentsply Sirona Freedom Scholarship , recognizing her outstanding achievements in the field.

A Focus on Patient-Centered Care

Dr. Beninati understands that nobody looks forward to a root canal . Her philosophy centers on patient education and open communication . She truly cares for each person like family, ensuring you fully understand your treatment options . Her goal is simple: to alleviate your pain and help you maintain your natural teeth with the highest level of comfort and expertise.
