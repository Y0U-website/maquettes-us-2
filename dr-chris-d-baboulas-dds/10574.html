Cosmetic and Orthodontic Dentistry

If you are not happy with the way your smile looks, it’s never too late to consider cosmetic dentistry and orthodontic options. From an appointment for teeth whitening that instantly makes your smile brighter and whiter to orthodontic options such as Invisalign invisible braces, we can work with you, your budget, and your lifestyle to give you a smile you love to show the world.

If you are unsatisfied with your smile because of the look of your teeth, you don’t have to go through life hiding your smile. Talk to us at North Shore Center of Dental Health, and we can go over your options.

  • Tooth whitening , for your whitest and brightest smile
  • DaVinci veneers , for the Hollywood smile you wish you had been born with
  • Invisalign invisible braces , for straight teeth without a mouth full of metal
  • Cosmetic zircona crowns, which look beautiful and natural
  • Lingual braces , which offer many advantages over traditional braces
