Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs): Dental Implants

How can we help you?

The entire team at North Shore Center of Dental Health expects our patients will have questions regarding the treatment they will receive for themselves and their entire family. Our dentists believe strongly that patient education is one of the most important aspects of good oral health, so we welcome questions that will clarify treatment options. We know our patients can make the best decisions on receiving treatment and making sure they maintain good oral health. We are happy to take whatever time is necessary to talk about your questions and concerns regarding your teeth and mouth.

We hear some questions quite frequently, and we’ve included some of the more common questions below. If your question is not answered here, however, please feel free to contact us for more information. We want to hear your questions about any aspect of your oral health care and treatment options. Our dentists at North Shore Center of Dental Health have the expertise to provide you with the accurate, detailed information you need to keep your smile healthy and beautiful.
