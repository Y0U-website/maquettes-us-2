NEW AND FAMILIAR PATIENTS WELCOME

Whether you are a new patient just getting to know your options for dental care in the area, or a patient who has been with Dr. Green since he began his practice nearly 20 years ago, welcome. Dr. Green and his team are excited that you are here and that you are present in your journey to complete dental health. In choosing Dr. Green’s practice, you are promised genuine care, clear communication, and expert guidance along the way.

SCHEDULING AND CONFIRMING APPOINTMENTS

For returning patients to set up hygiene re-care appointments or appointments with Dr. Green, simply call the front desk at 207-829-4444. Once you have made an appointment, our staff will call you a few days ahead of time to confirm your appointment. Please let us know if you would prefer to receive text messages notifications.

For new patients , your first appointment at the office will be a comprehensive exam with Dr. Green. During this appointment, you and Dr. Green will get acquainted, and he will evaluate your overall health, dental history, and oral health concerns and goals. We will take necessary radiographs, and Dr. Green will assess your periodontal health. Then you will make your next hygiene appointment with Catherine.

Your time is important to us. We work hard to accommodate your preferences when offering schedule times with Catherine and Dr. Green. We are a very busy practice and we appreciate that you promptly confirm your appointments with Karen, and arrive on time for your appointments. Please be courteous and let us know at least 24 hours ahead of time if you are unable to make it to your appointment - we are happy to reschedule with you.
