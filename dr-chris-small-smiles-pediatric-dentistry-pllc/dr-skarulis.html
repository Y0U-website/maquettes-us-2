Dr. Skarulis

Small Smiles Pediatric Dentistry

Dr. Catherine Skarulis is a New York native who was born and raised in Brooklyn. Dr. Catherine received her undergraduate degree in Biology from St. John’s University. She received her Doctorate in Dental Medicine at the University of Pennsylvania, School of Dental Medicine. She continued her post-doctorate training from Temple University School of Dentistry in cooperation with St. Christopher’s Children’s Hospital to specialize in pediatric dentistry.

Dr. Catherine is an American Academy board-certified pediatric dentist, specializing in treating children of all ages, including those with special healthcare needs. She is an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, American Dental Association, and New York State Dental Association. Dr. Catherine strives to make every patients visit a fun experience while providing exceptional dental care.

When not caring for patients, Dr. Catherine spends her down time with her husband and two sons. Some of Dr. Catherine’s hobbies are reading, playing platform tennis, and traveling. She is the youngest of eight children and has more than 25 nieces and nephews. Family time is what she enjoys most.
