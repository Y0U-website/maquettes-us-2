Meet Our Dental Team

Each professional at Dr. Christian Family Dentistry contributes to our mission of providing you with the highest level of expertise and service.

Sarah Front Office Coordinator & Scheduling Manager

Sarah joined our team in November 2022 and brings more than 25 years of dental experience to her role as Front Office Coordinator and Scheduling Manager. She is the first welcoming voice and friendly face you will see upon entering our office. She helps our office run smoothly from the moment patients walk in.

She loves connecting with people and takes joy in helping each patient truly love their smile. Sarah is grateful to work with a team she adores and believes that strong relationships both with coworkers and patients make all the difference.

Outside of work, Sarah treasures time with her family and enjoys traveling whenever she gets the chance.

Tracey Accounts Manager

Tracey has been with our practice since 2012 and offers 13 years of experience in the dental field. As our Accounts Manager, she is extremely knowledgeable and dedicated to helping patients understand and maximize their insurance benefits.

She finds genuine fulfillment in assisting families with the financial side of their care so they can feel confident moving forward with their treatment. Her patience and clarity make her a go-to resource for our patients.

Away from the office, Tracy enjoys reading, crafting, and spending quality time with her family.

Angela Hygiene Coordinator

Angela became part of our dental family in January 2024 and has quickly become an important member of our front office team. As our Hygiene Coordinator, she helps patients stay on track with their preventive care and keeps our schedule organized and efficient.

She has a genuine heart for helping others and finds joy in making each patient feel cared for and supported. Angela loves being part of a team that shares her commitment to patient-centered care.

When she isn’t at work, you can usually find her outdoors enjoying a wide variety of activities that keep her active and energized.

Lakin Registered Dental Assistant

Lakin joined our office in 2021 and brings eight years of experience as a Registered Dental Assistant. Known for her kind personality and reassuring presence, she helps patients feel at ease from the moment they sit in the chair.

Her favorite part of dentistry is helping people feel proud of their smile. She loves being part of the transformation process and supporting patients throughout their visit.

Outside the practice, Lakin enjoys gardening, diamond painting, cooking, and making memories with her family. Her creativity and warmth make her an invaluable part of our team.

Ashley Registered Dental Assistant

Ashley joined our team in 2019 and has eight years of experience as a Registered Dental Assistant. She is known for her warm personality, attention to detail, and commitment to making every patient’s visit a positive one.

She loves helping patients improve their smiles and values the strong relationships she has formed with many of them over the years. Ashley also takes great joy in being part of an encouraging and supportive work family.

Outside the office, she enjoys shopping, hiking, traveling, working in her flower beds, and spending cherished time with her two wonderful boys.

Martina Registered Dental Hygienist

Martina joined our practice in 2023 and brings more than 21 years of experience as a Dental Hygienist. With her extensive knowledge and welcoming personality, she helps create a positive, comfortable experience for every patient she meets.

One of Martina’s favorite parts of her work is the people she loves the conversations, the connections, and the opportunity to learn from her patients while also teaching them how to achieve their healthiest, happiest smile. Her ability to blend education with encouragement makes her a trusted partner in each patient’s dental care.

When she’s not in the office, Martina enjoys traveling, spending time on the lake, being outdoors, and exploring her passion for interior decorating.

Her experience, warmth, and genuine interest in others make Martina an exceptional member of our dental hygiene team.

Amanda Registered Dental Hygienist

Amanda became part of our practice in October 2024 and brings seven years of experience as a Registered Dental Hygienist. She has a gentle, calming presence and is passionate about creating a comfortable, stress-free visit for every patient she treats.

What she loves most about her work is educating patients and helping them feel confident about their oral health. Amanda takes great pride in guiding people toward a happy, healthy smile.

When she’s not in the office, you’ll find her creating stained glass artwork, painting, hiking, thrifting, traveling, or cheering as a dedicated baseball mom. Her creativity and warmth brighten our office every day.

Diana Registered Dental Hygienist

Diana joined our practice in May 2025 after beginning her dental career earlier that year. As a newly graduated hygienist, she brings fresh enthusiasm, compassion, and a strong desire to make a difference.

She enjoys the connections she’s building with both patients and fellow professionals. Diana especially loves watching patients leave the office with a confident smile, knowing she played a part in their positive experience.

When she’s not caring for patients, Diana loves traveling, shopping, and hiking hobbies that fuel her sense of adventure and balance.
