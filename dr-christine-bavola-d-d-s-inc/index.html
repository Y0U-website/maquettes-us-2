The practice

Dr. Bavola has been practicing dentistry for 35 years. She grew up in Brookfield, Ohio, graduated first from Brookfield High School in 1976 and went on to receive a B.S. from John Carroll University. Upon her graduation from John Carroll, Dr. Bavola went to Case Western Reserve University to receive her D.D.S. degree in 1985 and has since been practicing general dentistry.
