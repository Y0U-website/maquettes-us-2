  • Bridges Dental bridges are replacement teeth that fill the gap from a missing tooth and keep nearby teeth from shifting into the empty space, which can impact your bite and put more stress on your teeth and jaw.
  • Crowns Crowns are natural-looking porcelain caps that completely cover and protect a tooth while improving its size, shape, and appearance. Crowns can protect weak teeth or teeth with large fillings from breaking.
  • Dental Implants Dental Implants replace missing teeth or teeth that are beyond repair. Dental implants can restore your smile, improve your oral health, and provide a long-term tooth replacement solution.
  • Dentures Dentures are prosthetic devices that resemble natural teeth and gums and are used to replace missing teeth and improve your smile, speech, chewing, and quality of life. Partial dentures are a good replacement for several teeth, while a complete denture replaces all of the natural teeth.
  • Extractions Some teeth are not able to be saved, and extraction is indicated. Once the tooth is removed, your dentist may recommend an option for replacement such as a dental implant, bridge, or removable appliance.
  • Fillings A filling restores a natural tooth after dental decay is removed. We offer tooth colored composite restorations, as well as amalgam and other materials when indicated.
  • Invisalign We offer Invisalign clear aligner therapy to straighten teeth and improve your smile and bite. This is an alternative to traditional braces if complex orthodontic treatment is not required.
  • Preventative Care & Cleanings We provide regular preventative and diagnostic treatment, such as x-rays, exams, fluoride treatments, and home-care instructions to prevent tooth decay and gum disease. We offer traditional cleanings and deep cleanings when needed to eliminate inflammation and plaque. We provide sealants to prevent the development of dental decay.
  • Pediatric Dentistry Establish a lifetime of proper oral hygiene. We examine children’s teeth and gums, provide cleanings and x-rays, and assess the health of developing teeth. We treat children of all ages with dental needs.
  • Root Canal Therapy Some teeth require a root canal to remove infection and deep decay.
  • Whitening Whitening treatments include custom whitening trays and professional-grade whitening gel used to brighten and whiten your smile.
