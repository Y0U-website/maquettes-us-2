Implant placement and restoration

Implant placement and restoration

Dental implants are an option for tooth replacement that are quickly gaining in popularity. Dental implants are as close to a natural tooth as science can get in appearance and function. A titanium post is placed surgically into the jawbone to help restore the relationship between the bone and the root. After healing, the post will be topped with a restoration that will blend with your other teeth, either as a crown, bridge, or even to secure dentures. Dr. Aldrich can often perform the entire process in-house, which keeps costs down and allows for continuity of care.
