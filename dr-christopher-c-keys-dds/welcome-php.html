Welcome

We are happy to welcome you to our dental office and want you to know that we appreciate the chance to take care of you and your family. Our office is focused on providing you with outstanding dental care in a comfortable manner.

Each patient we have the privilege to serve is entitled to and will receive a careful dental examination. You will find our staff capable, courteous and eager to help you maintain your dental health at the highest possible level. We will strive to make your dental health experience as pleasant as possible. We hope you share in our belief that regular preventative dental healthcare is very important.

During your first visit, the doctor will examine your teeth, perform an oral cancer exam, review any relevant imaging (x-rays), and make an assessment of your oral condition. If it is discovered that you need any dental treatment, a treatment plan and estimate will be prepared for you prior to any procedures. You will have the chance to review recommended treatment and ask questions.

Children are very welcome. Should we have to schedule them in the morning for their dental needs, we can provide a school excuse letter for your child. We prefer to have parents wait in the reception area except during consultations.

We hope you will feel comfortable while visiting our office. If you have any questions please feel free to ask any of our staff members. We are here to make your dental experience as pleasant as possible. Thank you for choosing our office.
