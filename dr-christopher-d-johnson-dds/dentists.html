Stephen Welna, DDS

Family Dentistry

Dr. Welna grew up in Rochester, MN. After graduating from North Carolina State University with degrees in Chemistry and Plant Biology he returned to Rochester to work for Mayo Clinic as a lab technician in their Biochemical Genetics Lab. While at Mayo Clinic he also worked in test development, producing new blood tests to help support state newborn screening programs. Dr. Welna’s passion for dentistry began in Rochester while volunteering at the Salvation Army Good Samaritan Dental Clinic.

Dr. Welna graduated from the University of Minnesota Dental School with the highest academic distinction, earning awards in both fixed and removable prosthodontics, and honors in periodontology. He is passionate about family dentistry and providing care to people of all ages. He loves to use modern technology to improve dental experiences and educate patients about their mouth. Dr. Welna also spends time at the Chippewa Valley Free Clinic and is currently the Vice President of the Chippewa Valley Dental Society.

Dr. Welna, his wife Sarah, and their four young children will often be seen at local playgrounds, going for walks, hikes, bike rides, and floating around on the local waterways in their canoe. He loves to hike, camp, hunt, fish, ski, and enjoys teaching his children to do these things as well. If you have a favorite local outdoor spot Dr. Welna would love to hear about it!
