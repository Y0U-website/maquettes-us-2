The practice

Located in West Caldwell, New Jersey , (Essex County) we are committed to providing comprehensive dental care for infants, children, adolescents, adults and special needs patients.

Dr. Olivia Ike a General Dentist offers a full range of dental services for adolescents and adults, including general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, restorative dentistry, and oral surgery.

To help us ensure patients receive the highest quality of care, we utilize state-of-the-art technology including digital X-rays, which are much safer than traditional X-rays and reduce your radiation exposure by 90 percent!
