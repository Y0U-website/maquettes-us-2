Dr. Christopher Juliano

Dr. Chris Juliano was born and raised in Webster, NY. He is a graduate of Webster High School, class of 1989. He attended Alfred State College for a degree in business administration. Dr. Juliano continued his education at St. John Fisher College for a Bachelors degree in Biology. Nova Southeastern University in Ft. Lauderdale, Florida was where he earned a degree in dental medicine. Nova Southeastern is one of the newest and most technologically advanced dental schools in the nation.

Dr. Juliano is proud to serve the community where he grew up and still lives. His wife, Larissa, a Brighton High School and Buffalo State College graduate, has her Masters in Literacy, teaches in the Williamson School District, and is currently a published author, who you can find on Amazon and Instagram. Dr Juliano and Larissa have 3 beautiful children, Bobby, Isabelle, and Lydia, and their lovable and energetic Golden-doodle, Toree.

The name Juliano Family Dental was carefully chosen to reflect Dr. Juliano and his commitment to the community. Juliano Family Dental is not where a group of people go to “get their teeth fixed”. Juliano Family Dental is an office committed to comprehensively diagnosing and treating patients in order to achieve complete oral health and oral function.
