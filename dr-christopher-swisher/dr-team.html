Christopher Swisher DDS

board certified pediatric dentist

In 2012, he found himself on another traveling job, but this time in Hood River, where his father grew up. He worked for two and a half years at A Kidz Dental Zone, before starting his own practice.

Currently, he holds an affiliate faculty position at the University of Washington, Dept. of Pediatric Dentistry. He is the local ABCD (Access to Baby and Child Dentistry) representative for the state of Washington. And in 2011, he became a diplomat of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. When he’s not in the office, you’ll likely find him somewhere outdoors enjoying life.

Noel Larsen DMD

Dr. Noel Larsen received her D.M.D. degree from the Oregon Health and Sciences University and her certificate in pediatric dentistry in 1994 from the Children’s Hospital Medical Center in Cincinnati, Ohio. She is a second generation dentist, following in her father’s footsteps. He was also a pediatric dentist in the Portland area.

During her 30 plus years of practicing dentistry, she has held many positions in organized dentistry, including president of the Oregon Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, board member and treasurer for the Multnomah County Dental Society, secretary-treasurer of a dental fraternity, Delta Sigma Delta, and co-chair, then chair of the American College of Dentists. She is a member of two honorary dental organizations, the American College of Dentists and the International College of Dentists.

Dr. Larsen especially enjoys treating patients with special needs. "It gives me special joy to make coming to the dentist a fun experience for children who need extra time and patience with each visit." Her time away from the office she enjoys spending time with her family, which includes son, Davis, and their family dog Rudolph or "Rudy," he may come to the office to visit sometime. She is an avid gardener, skier, tennis player and runner.
