Meet our Team

Dr. Christopher Weber

Dr. Weber continues his study in dentistry to expand his knowledge of the latest dental techniques. His knowledge also helps our teams provide exceptional dental services in Florissant . He genuinely cares about his patients and embodies our dental practice with his “soft touch” and soothing words .

Dr. Weber and his wonderful wife Meredith had their adorable baby named Victoria in March 2017. When he’s out of the office, Dr. Weber loves spending time with his family . He enjoys rock climbing, watching movies (especially the Star Wars Saga), and reading comic books.

As a trusted dentist in Florissant, MO for ten years , Dr. Weber is the voice of reason and makes everyone around him feel at ease. His patients and the Soft Touch Dental team love him for being a compassionate and caring dentist.

Dr. Azaryas Mandefro

Dr. Azaryas Mandefro is passionate about giving patients the advanced and personalized dental care they deserve. He earned his Bachelor’s Degree in Dentistry from the University of Missouri and his Doctorate from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine .

Dr. Mandefro is an active member of the American Dental Association , Missouri Dental Association , and Greater St. Louis Dental Society .

He lives in Town and Country with his wife Betty and their daughter Ellie. Being a top-rated dentist in Florissant , Dr. Mandefro holds extensive training on dental implants to help change lives by building one smile at a time.

Meet our Soft Touch Dental Staff

Bree

Dental Hygienist

Bree is one of our registered dental hygienists and the team leader for the hygiene department. She has been working as a hygienist since 2000. Our patients can count on Bree to give them pleasant and thorough teeth cleaning experiences. Her patients often praise her for her gentle voice and calming vibe .

Bree is not only a talented and kind hygienist but also a great listener to your dental concerns. She is well-known for going above and beyond to educate our patients about proper oral hygiene. She also ensures that our patients have the information they need to achieve their best oral health.

Bree and her fiance Brett have two beautiful daughters. Outside the dental practice, Bree is fond of having fun family trips to the St. Louis Zoo or Holiday World almost every weekend. She loves to bake, and her colleagues love to try all her recipes.

On weekends, she hops on a motorcycle date with Brett and spends quality time with her family. Bree is well-respected for her professional work and loved by her friends, family, and patients for her genuine desire to care for others.
