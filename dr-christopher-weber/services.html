Preventive Dental Treatments

We want to make sure that our patients are free from any early onset of serious dental conditions like gum disease by providing a thorough oral exam and oral cancer screenings . Oral exams help our dental team to provide the best dental advice and educate our patients about their current oral health condition. Our services include:

  • Teeth cleaning
  • Gum evaluation and treatment
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Oral x-rays
  • Oral assessment
  • Home oral care tips

Cosmetic Dental Treatments

Teeth Whitening

Our professional teeth whitening treatment is a safe and low-cost option to restore your white and beautiful teeth . Patients can expect their teeth to look about two shades whiter after treatment. Let us help you bring back the smile on your face and regain your confidence that’s been lost by having stained teeth.

Crowns

A dental crown helps restore the strength and appearance of your teeth by covering the damaged tooth with a tooth-shaped cap. It is the most suitable solution to cover up severely damaged teeth or serve as a support for bridges.

Veneers

Porcelain veneers are the ultimate teeth makeover that solves multiple dental issues in one solution. Natural teeth that have spaces, stained, crooked, misshaped, or chipped, are the dental concerns that veneers can solve. It consists of extra thin sheets of porcelain attached to the front of the teeth that will upgrade the shape, size, and color of your teeth .

Tooth-Colored fillings

We provide tooth-colored fillings made up of quartz resin for a more natural-looking tooth. Fillings help restore teeth that are damaged by tooth decay and caused by injuries such as fractured or chipped teeth. It covers any cavities that expose the tooth pulp from the disease-causing bacteria that may cause tooth infection, if left untreated.

Restorative Dental Treatments

Soft Touch Dental provides restorative dental services that help relieve pain, repair tooth damage, and improve bite problems to restore the ideal teeth function and aesthetics. Our services include several teeth-replacement options to fill the gap in your smile such as:

  • Dental Implants
  • Root Canal Therapy
  • Bridges
  • Dentures
