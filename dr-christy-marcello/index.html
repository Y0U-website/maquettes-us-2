The practice

Our Specialists at Marcello Pediatric Dentistry & Orthodontics believe that beautiful smiles start at birth. We provide specialized oral healthcare for your infant, child, and adolescent in a happy, welcoming, and caring setting. From a baby’s first visit to complete orthodontic treatment, we offer comprehensive and customized care for your family.

Semi-annual oral exam, dental cleaning, oral hygiene and cancer screening, fluoride therapy, lip and tongue tie evaluation, sealants.

Composite tooth-colored fillings, full crown restorations, silver diamine fluoride treatment, pulp nerve therapy, extractions, space maintenance.

Braces for children and adults, dentofacial orthopedics, clear aligner treatment

Phase I Orthodontics, guidance of eruption, space maintenance, palatal expansion, habit correction, growth modification

Toothache, facial swelling, knocked-out tooth, chipped/fractured tooth, jaw injury, cut/bitten tongue, lip, or cheek

Mildly invasive procedure which releases the muscle attachment to allow proper function and maneuvering of the musculature

Traditional metal brackets, translucent ceramic brackets, clear aligners
