Dr. Dustin Simpson maintains a patient-centered philosophy, which affirms that every patient deserves special and individualized care to meet their dental needs and goals. This patient-focused approach to oral wellness involves listening to each individual patient, responding with honesty and providing excellent dental care.

Dr. Simpson was born in Maryville, IL and raised in Collinsville, IL. He went to college at Webster University in St. Louis, MO where he obtained his Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. After graduating college, he attended Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine in Alton, IL. There Dr. Simpson earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree.

In addition to his degree, Dr. Simpson is a member of the American Dental Association and the Illinois State Dental Society. Outside of practicing dentistry, Dr. Simpson enjoys spending time with family and friends, being active in the community, and playing golf.

Dr. Simpson welcomes the opportunity to provide you and your family lifetime dental care in a welcoming and comfortable environment.
