Dr. Claire (Carrow) Wiedner

DMD

Dr. Claire was born and raised in the Alton/Godfrey area. She attended Marquette Catholic High School. She graduated from the University of Missouri with a bachelor’s degree in biology and minor in Psychology. She attended Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine in Alton, graduating in 2015.

In her free time, Dr. Claire can be found spending time with her husband and their daughter. They enjoy golfing, walking the trails and finding new local businesses and restaurants.

Dr. Dustin Simpson

Dr. Dustin Simpson maintains a patient-centered philosophy, which affirms that every patient deserves special and individualized care to meet their dental needs and goals. This patient-focused approach to oral wellness involves listening to each individual patient, responding with honesty and providing excellent dental care.

Dr. Simpson was born in Maryville, IL and raised in Collinsville, IL. He went to college at Webster University in St. Louis, MO where he obtained his Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. After graduating college, he attended Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine in Alton, IL. There Dr. Simpson earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree.

In addition to his degree, Dr. Simpson is a member of the American Dental Association and the Illinois State Dental Society. Outside of practicing dentistry, Dr. Simpson enjoys spending time with family and friends, being active in the community, and playing golf.

Dr. Simpson welcomes the opportunity to provide you and your family lifetime dental care in a welcoming and comfortable environment.

Dr. James Nativi

Dr. Nativi received his undergraduate degree from Southern Illinois University at Edwardsville, and his Doctorate Degree from Washington University. Dr. Nativi is an active member of the American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society, Madison County Dental Society, Academy of Sports Dentistry, and a Fellow of the American College of Dentists. He also serves as a team Dentist for Southern Illinois University and works part time at Alton Mental Health Hospital.

Dr. Meagan Hanney

Dr. Meagan Hanney’s goal is to provide a comforting and friendly atmosphere where patients can feel as relaxed as possible. She is known for her gentle chairside manner, strong patient communication, and attention to detail. She believes oral health is an essential part of overall health and focuses on preventing and treating dental diseases.

Dr. Hanney joined the practice in 2025 after relocating to the Belleville area. Prior to that, she practiced in the Springfield area, where she was born and raised. She attended Eureka College for her undergraduate education, where she earned a Bachelor’s degree in Chemistry with a minor in Psychology and graduated Magna Cum Laude. She then attended SIU School of Dental Medicine where she also graduated Magna Cum Laude.

Dr. Hanney’s interest in dentistry began in the 7th grade when she had braces. She found it fascinating how teeth aligned with wire and bracket placement and enjoyed observing the tools used during her appointments. Around this time, she also developed a growing passion for science at school, which quickly became her favorite subject. Through job shadowing, she gained a deeper understanding of dentistry and admired how dentists contribute to improving patients’ overall health and help them achieve confidence through a healthy smile.

When not serving patients, Dr. Hanney enjoys spending time with friends and family, baking, strength training, traveling, and being outdoors.
