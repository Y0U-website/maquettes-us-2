Dental Implants

Implants are stronger than natural teeth. They are also a more favorable approach than bridgework because they do not depend on neighboring teeth for support. To receive implants, you need to have healthy gums and adequate bone to support the implant. You must also be committed to excellent oral hygiene and regular dental visits as these are critical to the long-term success of dental implants.
