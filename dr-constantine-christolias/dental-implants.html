What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a permanent solution for replacing a missing tooth, designed to function and look natural. It consists of three parts: the titanium implant post that goes into the jawbone, the abutment that connects the post to the crown, and the crown itself, which mimics the visible tooth.
