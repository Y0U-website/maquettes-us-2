Full Arch Dental Implants vs. Implant Supported Dentures

Full Arch Dental Implants

With full arch dental implants , we place four to six dental implants within strategic areas of your jaw with the most bone, which will support a full arch prosthesis. The prosthesis is securely screwed into the implants and will only be removed by your dentist allowing you to virtually forget about them, besides normal brushing and flossing.

Implant Supported Dentures

Similar to full arch dental implants , by utilizing two or more dental implants, we can attach your denture device to dental implant posts or a bar attachment. When the denture is connected, it will not shift or click or require adhesives, but will provide you with a stronger bite. The denture is also removable for cleaning.
