Les soins

General Dentistry From routine check-ups and cleanings to more comprehensive procedures, our goal is to ensure oral health and beautiful smiles.

Crowns can restore your tooth’s strength and appearance after damage or decay. They fit over the visible portion of your tooth, providing protection after root canals, large fillings, or implants.

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed in the jawbone as artificial roots for missing teeth. They fuse with the bone, offering a stable base for crowns, bridges, or dentures, while preventing bone loss and restoring chewing function.

Dental veneers are thin, custom shells bonded to the front of teeth to enhance appearance. They conceal imperfections with minimal enamel removal. Porcelain veneers are durable and stain-resistant, lasting 10-15+ years.
