Our Services General Dental Hygiene Maintaining optimal oral health is essential for everyone. By participating in regular check-ups that ensure your mouth, teeth, and gums are clean, you are actively promoting your overall well-being.

Endodontics

At times, the dental pulp (the soft tissue within your tooth) can become damaged, and our primary objective is to always protect and preserve natural teeth by using procedures such as root canals.

Extractions

A dental extraction is the removal of a tooth from its socket in the jawbone. This common procedure is performed to address severe decay, infection, overcrowding, or damage and aims to improve oral health.

Fillin gs

Dental fillings serve as restorative substances designed to mend teeth affected by decay, fractures, or erosion. Fillings restore the function and integrity of your teeth using materials that match your natural tooth color.

Cosmetic

Crowns

Crowns can restore your tooth’s strength and appearance after damage or decay. They fit over the visible portion of your tooth, providing protection after root canals, large fillings, or implants.

Veneers

Dental veneers are thin, custom shells bonded to the front of teeth to enhance appearance. They conceal imperfections with minimal enamel removal. Porcelain veneers are durable and stain-resistant, lasting 10-15+ years.

Bonding

Dental bonding is a quick, non-invasive cosmetic procedure that repairs chipped, cracked, discolored, or misaligned teeth using tooth-colored composite resin. Bonding can typically last from 3 to 10 years.

Teeth Whitening

In-office whitening treatment uses high-concentration peroxide gel, activated by light or laser, to quickly brighten teeth several shades. It removes deep enamel stains more safely and quickly than at-home options.

Restorative

Dentures

We offer removable and fixed dentures to restore confidence by replacing missing teeth. Dentures improve your ability to eat and speak comfortably while enhancing your appearance and supporting facial structure.

Partials

A removable partial denture (RPD) replaces missing teeth while protecting your natural ones. It features replacement teeth on a gum-colored base and clasps that fit snugly with your existing teeth .

Implant Restorations

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed in the jawbone as artificial roots for missing teeth. They fuse with the bone, offering a stable base for crowns, bridges, or dentures, while preventing bone loss and restoring chewing function.

Digital Scanning

A digital scanner is an innovative wand that captures detailed 3D images of your teeth and gums. Our Digital devices allow us to create accurate models instantly, aiding in restorative and implant procedures.
