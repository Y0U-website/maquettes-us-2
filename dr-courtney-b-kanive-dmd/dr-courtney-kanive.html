Dr. Courtney Kanive

Dr. Courtney Kanive grew up in Central Illinois. A nearby neighbor and family friend was her dentist growing up. He helped foster her interest in the field of dentistry. Courtney received her B.S. from the University of Illinois in Champaign-Urbana (2000) and went on to graduate from Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine with the honor of cum laude (2004).

She then went on to achieve a M.S. and Endodontic Specialist Certificate from Saint Louis University Center for Advanced Dental Education (2006). In 2006 she opened Wentzville Endodontics. She is a specialist member of the American Association of Endodontists, the American Dental Association, the Missouri Dental Association, and the Greater Saint Louis Dental Society.

Dr. Kanive has been published in the Journal of Endodontics. Click here to see the article.

She enjoys music, golfing, fishing, traveling and spending time with her staff, friends, her parents and in-laws, and husband and two sons. She resides in Lake Saint Louis and can be contacted at drkanive@wentzvilleendo.com
