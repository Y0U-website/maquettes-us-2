About Us

Legacy Smile Co.

At Legacy Smile Co. , we believe exceptional dentistry begins with genuine relationships. Our mission is to provide personalized, comprehensive care in an environment where every patient feels welcomed, valued, and known—not just as a patient, but as part of our practice family.

Founded in 1996 by Dr. Janet Hatcher Rice, the practice became Bristol’s first woman-owned dental practice and earned a reputation for clinical excellence, integrity, and compassionate care. In 2024, Dr. Courtney LaHatte Blankenship proudly joined Rice Dental Arts and continued that legacy, preserving the values patients have trusted for decades while introducing a fresh vision for the future under the name Legacy Smile Co.

Today, Legacy Smile Co. remains a locally owned, independent dental practice . In an era when many dental offices are being purchased by large corporate organizations, we are proud to remain privately owned, allowing every decision we make to be guided by one simple question: What is best for our patients? Our independence gives us the freedom to build lasting relationships, provide individualized treatment, and never compromise the quality of care we deliver.

Dr. Blankenship combines advanced technology, including laser dentistry and modern digital techniques, with a commitment to lifelong learning and personalized treatment. Every recommendation is made with your health, comfort, and long-term goals in mind. We believe informed patients make confident decisions, which is why we take time to explain treatment options, answer questions, and provide detailed treatment plans before care begins.

Education Prevention

The healthiest smiles begin with prevention. Our philosophy is centered on helping you keep your natural teeth healthy for a lifetime through regular examinations, professional cleanings, and patient education.

Every comprehensive exam includes a review of your medical history, appropriate digital radiographs, an evaluation of your teeth and gums, an oral cancer screening, and a conversation about your overall oral health. We believe you deserve to understand not only what we recommend, but why .

Because oral health is closely connected to overall wellness—including heart disease, diabetes, and other systemic conditions—we view dentistry as an important part of helping you live a healthier life.

Uncompromising Safety

Your health and safety are our highest priorities. We follow rigorous infection control protocols that meet or exceed the recommendations of the American Dental Association, the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, and OSHA.

Our team uses advanced sterilization procedures, single-use materials whenever appropriate, and strict quality standards throughout every aspect of your visit. You can feel confident knowing that every precaution is taken to provide safe, comfortable, and exceptional care.

Training Expertise

To give you the best possible service and results, Legacy Smile Co. has made a pledge to commit to lifelong learning. The doctor and staff attend continuing education classes to stay abreast of the latest dental treatment. As your dental health professionals, we want you to be confident in the knowledge that we are a team of highly trained and skilled clinicians.

A Positive Experience

Building a foundation of trust by treating our patients with compassion is vital to our success. We understand how uneasy some patients may feel about their dental visits, and we go out of our way to provide a positive experience by going slowly and carefully while explaining every procedure we do. Our team is dedicated to excellence and strives to exceed expectations by making your visit as comfortable and pleasant as possible.

Designed for Your Comfort

From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll notice that Legacy Smile Co. feels different. We believe your dental experience should feel anything but clinical. Our bright, open reception room, natural light, and comfortable furnishings create a warm and inviting environment from the moment you arrive. Enjoy a cup of coffee or tea, or help yourself to refreshing water while you’re here.

Throughout your visit, thoughtful touches, from a warm neck wrap and cozy blanket to lip balm and dark glasses, are designed with your comfort in mind. Every detail reflects our belief that excellent dentistry and genuine hospitality belong together.
