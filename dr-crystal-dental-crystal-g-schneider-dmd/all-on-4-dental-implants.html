All on Four Dental Implant Aftercare

In most cases, research has shown that placing the implants immediately after tooth extraction can improve the outcomes of the procedure. Dr. Crystal Dental has seen patients resume a normal diet in just six weeks after the implants are placed. The more closely you follow the after-care instructions from us, the better the results you will experience.

During the days and weeks following your more invasive appointments, eating softer foods will keep you comfortable. Once the healing process takes hold, you can gradually include harder foods. Once we have completed your All-on-4 procedure, and you are fully healed, you need to continue to maintain good oral hygiene habits to keep your restoration healthy and your overall health strong.
