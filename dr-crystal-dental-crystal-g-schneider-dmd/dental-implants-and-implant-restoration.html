The Dental Implant and Implant Restoration Procedure

The process of having a dental implant placed will take several months to complete, but the process is streamlined with coordinated appointments with our highly qualified specialists. The first step could include an extraction of a tooth. This may be due to decay, or a level of damage to the tooth that cannot be corrected. After that, Dr. Crystal Dental or our specialists will use state-of-the-art advanced 3D digital x-rays to examine the integrity of your jawbone to confirm that there is enough density and mass to support the implant properly.

Our specialists are highly trained in dental implant placement and work closely with Dr. Crystal Dental to ensure to proper placement for your final restorative goals, using advanced digital technology. The dental implant placement procedure is comfortably completed with our specialists and if necessary, can include dental sedation if preferred. After placement, healing includes a process known as osseointegration where the dental implant fuses with the surrounding bone. A follow up appointment with the surgical specialist will confirm full integration and its readiness for restoration.

Once the dental implant is stable, you are ready to appoint at Dr. Crystal Dental for the restorative steps whether that is a single tooth implant, implant supported bridge, implant supported denture or implant hybrid denture. we will add an extension or abutment to the post to reach the surface of your gumline. Finally, the artificial crown will be added, either using an internal screw or dental cement. Your new tooth has now been placed, and the implant restoration procedure is complete.

Dental Implant and Implant Restoration Aftercare

When it comes to the life expectancy of your dental implant or implant restoration, the primary factor is your personal oral hygiene, bone health, and a stable bite. A dental implant needs to be treated like a natural tooth and should be properly brushed and flossed daily. This will help to maintain the health of your gums, and your surrounding natural teeth.

Continue to brush twice daily, floss and rinse your mouth once daily to ensure proper overall oral health.

It is also essential to understand that, even though dental implants are strong, the healing time is critical for the success of the dental implants after they are initially placed. For the first few weeks, a diet consisting of softer food will be recommended, with a slow introduction of harder foods. Your jawbone needs to acclimate to the new force that the artificial root is applying to it.

If your dental implant is the result of a sports injury, make sure to use a mouth guard in future contests for proper protection.

A dental implant and implant restoration is an excellent, long-term option for tooth replacement. If you are missing a tooth or need a tooth pulled, consult with Dr. Crystal Dental about whether a dental implant might be a viable option for you.
