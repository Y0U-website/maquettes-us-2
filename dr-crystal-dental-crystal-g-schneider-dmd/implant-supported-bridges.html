Implant Supported Bridge Mentor…

An implant supported bridge leaves your adjoining teeth in their natural condition. The two neighboring teeth do not need to undergo any prep work to support the new artificial crowns. The dental implants, acting as artificial roots, will hold your bridge securely so that it feels and functions just like your natural teeth. This artificial tooth root stimulates the jaw to maintain density and prevent jawbone atrophy.

  • No hassle – You will not experience or encounter any disruptions in the appearance and functionality of your healthy smile or how you care for your teeth daily. You can continue to brush and floss as usual, enjoy your favorite foods, and smile and talk with complete confidence.
  • Long lasting – After final placement, your implant supported bridge has the potential to last a lifetime. This is substantially longer than traditional bridges and dentures, which usually need to be replaced every 5 to 10 years.

The Implant Supported Bridge Procedure

An implant-supported bridge solution will require several steps of treatment, which could take from six to nine months for completion. After the initial evaluation at Dr. Crystal Dental has confirmed that we have the necessary bone density to work with, we will surgically place the dental implants into the spot in the jawbone of tooth loss. After this surgery, the bone tissues need sufficient time to bond, grow, and fuse with the implants in a process called osseointegration.

After the osseointegration process is completed, the second step will be to attach abutments, or small extensions, to the implants. After this second step of treatment, the gums will again need about two weeks to heal.

The final stage of your implant supported bridge restoration is the placement of your custom designed bridge to these implant extensions. A special dental adhesive will be used to bond the dental bridge, with the artificial crowns, to the abutment posts. This secures your new implant supported bridge so that it becomes a natural and permanent part of your smile.

With continued preventative oral hygiene care at-home and scheduling your regular dental exams and checkups every six months with Dr. Crystal Dental, your implant supported bridge will last for decades, and you can smile and eat the foods you enjoy. Your healthy and natural smile will be restored, you will have no stability issues with biting, chewing, and speaking, and the artificial root maintains the health of the jawbone so you will not experience any unnecessary sagging with your facial structure.
