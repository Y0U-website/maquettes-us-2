The practice

Dr. Cynthia Noll specializes in keeping smiles healthy, and this means emphasizing prevention offering the dental services you need today to maintain your smile tomorrow. It’s simple. We want you to have healthy teeth for life.

For those looking to enhance the appearance of their smile, we also offer a variety of cosmetic dentistry services to help boost your confidence everything from professional teeth whitening to porcelain veneers.

Also, for our patients with TMJ and bruxism temporomandibular joint dysfunction and grinding and clenching, respectively we offer advanced therapies to ease your pain and restore jaw health.

At Noll Dentistry, you’ll discover a compassionate and knowledgeable dentist in Dr. Cynthia Noll and a team of dental professionals who share her patient-centered focus.

When you’re under our care, it’s all about you, and this includes keeping you relaxed and comfortable, educating you about your dental health options, and allowing you to choose the services that fit your lifestyle and your budget. We pride ourselves on being a no-pressure dental practice.

We are also here for you and your family members in the event of a dental emergency. If you have a toothache, swelling, broken tooth, or damaged restoration, we encourage you to contact a member of our dental team. We can assess your situation over the phone and make the best recommendation for you. At Noll Dentistry, when you’re in pain, we work hard to treat you as soon as possible.

If you suffer from dental anxiety, we are also here to help. Rather than forego needed dental care, ask Dr. Noll about our sedation dentistry. We never want you to compromise your smile healthy because you’re feeling anxious. While we pride ourselves on our gentle and soothing approach to patient care, we also understand when you need a little extra reassurance.

When it comes to your smile, we are always thinking long term. What service can we offer today to help you maintain your smile health in the future?

Les soins

Dr. Noll recommends twice-yearly dental exams and teeth cleanings, which fall in line with best practices put forward by the American Dental Association.

Periodontal disease, or gum disease, is caused by plaque that builds up on the teeth and especially under the gumline. The sticky plaque attracts bacteria and eventually turns into hard tartar. The tartar and bacteria irritate the gums and cause infection.

Whether you’re looking for preventive, general, or cosmetic dentistry, we are here for you. If you need advanced therapies for jaw health or teeth grinding, we also have you covered.
