As our new Dental Assistant you will have an opportunity to help make a child’s smile brighter every day. You will be working side by side with our Pediatric Dentists helping deliver quality care to our young dental community. Performing tasks such as taking radiographs/x-rays, note-taking during exams, sterilization, and eventually chairside assisting working directly with the dentist in providing restorative and surgical care to our patients.

We are a Pediatric Dentistry focused dental practice that has been treating the patients of the Capital Region and Upstate NY for over 40. Our Doctors and team are known for excellence in both clinical treatment& patient experience. We recently expanded our ability to care for children by adding a third facility to our existing offices in Latham and Delmar, NY.

With our constant growth we need more great people to join our team so we continue making smiles grow in the Capital Region for years to come!

We are always looking to add smiling new faces to our team of highly trained group of pediatric dentists, dental hygienists, dental assistants, and business team members.

We understand that some children have anxiety or apprehensions about going to the dentist, and we do everything in our power to make our office a positive and happy place to be. From the moment they walk in the door at Where Smiles Grow, we are completely committed to making sure all our patients, and their families, feel safe and right at home.

Currently, we are looking to fill positions at our 3 convenient locations in Latham, Delmar and Schodack, NY.

We cannot wait to hear from you! Experience is preferred, but we are willing to train the right person.
