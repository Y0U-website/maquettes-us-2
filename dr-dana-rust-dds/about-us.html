Dentist in Oak Ridge TN

Meet the Team at Dana Rust DDS, Jeff Phillips DDS, Brady Hull DMD

Dr. Dana Rust, Dr. Jeff Phillips and Dr. Brady Hull are proud to provide the community of Oak Ridge, TN with exceptional dental care. Our dentists are highly skilled and have participated in advanced continuing education that allows them to take on even the most complicated dental cases. Our goal is to ensure your comfort while helping you achieve the oral health and aesthetics you desire.

Our practice has been serving patients for over 35 years, and many of our team members have worked together for a long time, giving us a family feel that you don’t often find at a dental office. Learn more about our dentists and team members below so you’ll feel right at home as soon as you arrive.

Dana Rust

Dr. Dana Rust takes great pride in the quality care he provides to his patients. Always mindful of each individual s goals and comfort, Dr. Rust provides treatment options and discusses each one in detail to help patients make the best choice for long-term stability, function and aesthetics. Dr. Rust is highly aware of the stress and anxiety that many patients have when visiting the dentist and does his best to ease their apprehension. To ensure the best experience for his patients, Dr. Rust explains the steps and what to expect during and following treatment.

Dr. Rust is an Oak Ridge High School graduate and continued his education at the University of Tennessee in Knoxville where he graduated with highest honors with a degree in Microbiology. He attended dental school at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center in Memphis, where he graduated with high honors and was inducted into several honorary societies for his excellence in the field of dentistry. Dr. Rust enjoys keeping up with the latest materials and technology and makes continuing education a priority in order to provide his patients with the best treatment outcomes.

Dr. Rust s hobbies include raising his three active boys, UT sports, marathon and ultra trail running.

Jeff Phillips

Dr. Jeff Phillips is a well known and respected dentist in Oak Ridge, TN. His primary goals for his patients are excellent oral health, comfort, and aesthetics. With more than 40 years of experience, Dr. Phillips has enhanced and restored many smiles in our area, from general maintenance to complete cosmetic makeovers. Dr. Phillips’ sense of beauty and artistry is recognized by both his patients and colleagues.

Dr Phillips enjoyed science and received a B.S. in Chemistry from Middle Tennessee State University and completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Tennessee , Memphis. He continually strives to enhance his knowledge of current dental procedures by attending continuing education courses and hands-on seminars on a regular basis.

Dr. Phillips is a longtime member of the Oak Ridge Rotary Club and serves on an advisory board for the Dental Hygiene program at Roane State Community College. He is a member of the Second District Dental Society, Tennessee Dental Association and the American Dental Association. He has participated in Remote Area Medical and a mission trips to Uganda and India.

Living and raising his family in Oak Ridge has been a wonderful experience for Dr. Phillips. He has been married to Kathy for over 40 years, has two grown children, Julie and David, and six grandchildren. Dr. Phillips and his family like to travel and be on the lake. Dr. Phillips enjoys hiking, mountain biking, skiing, history, and reading.
