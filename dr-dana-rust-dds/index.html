The practice

Dr. Rust, Dr. Phillips and Dr Hull invest in the most advanced technology to ensure your experience at our office is comfortable and efficient.

An example of innovative technology you’ll find at our practice is CEREC, which allows us to offer same-day dentistry and complete restorations such as porcelain crowns in one visit. Same-day treatment is especially beneficial for patients experiencing dental emergencies, or patients with hectic schedules needing to streamline their time spent at the dentist.

CEREC utilizes CAD-CAM technology to scan a 3-D image of your tooth directly to our milling machine where your crown is crafted precisely to your Oak Ridge dentist’s specifications. CEREC allows you to receive a fully functional and aesthetically pleasing dental restoration in only one visit instead of the two weeks that was required in the past.

Restore Both Your Oral Health and Your Confidence. We Offer Solutions for One or Multiple Missing Teeth.

Unfortunately, the problem of missing teeth goes beyond the cosmetic issue. Missing teeth put additional stress on your remaining teeth and can lead to damage, including fractures and even further tooth loss. When you are missing teeth, you may find it difficult or even painful to enjoy the fresh, healthy foods you need for proper nutrition and nourishment.

Les soins

Dental implants are a permanent solution to the problem of missing teeth.

For many people, missing teeth present a mostly cosmetic problem. This is a fair concern: Missing teeth carry a stigma, and people with missing teeth may find themselves unfairly judged in many social settings.
