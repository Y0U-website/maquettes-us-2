ABOUT DR. MISHLER

Hi, my name is Dane Mishler. I grew up in Crawfordsville, Indiana. I graduated from Southmont High School, went to Ball State University, and graduated from Indiana University Dental School in 2015. I joined Dr. Rucker right after graduation and acquired the practice from her in 2017. I am a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Indiana Dental Association (IDA) of which I am a participant of the Ben Hur Dental society, and I am an associate fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID).

I married my beautiful wife Haley in 2017. We have three children Elin, Isley, and Alistair. I love spending time with my family. We are members at East Baptist Church, Haley and I are part of the music team there. I also am an avid triathlete and compete in multiple events every year including Ironman 70.3 distance.

I truly love being a dentist and getting to take care of my patients. I enjoy getting to know my patients, educating them on their dental health, and getting to treat people throughout their lives. Our team at the office is the best, we enjoy working with each other and want was is best for our patients.
