Veneers

Both types of veneers are performed in two visits. During the first visit, Dr. Green will take impressions of your teeth and send them to a dental laboratory where your veneers will be fabricated. During the second visit, Dr. Green will fit and bond your veneers to your teeth and make any necessary adjustments.

  • Lumineers : These are a brand of veneers that are thinner than traditional veneers and require little or no tooth preparation. They can be placed over your teeth without drilling or shaving off any enamel. They can also correct various dental flaws, such as stains, chips, cracks, gaps, or misalignment. They can also enhance your smile without altering your natural tooth structure.
