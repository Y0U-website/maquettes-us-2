Our Dentists

Dr. John Eberz

Dr. John Eberz grew up in Western New York. He attended John Carroll University in Cleveland, graduating with a Bachelor’s Degree in Biology. Dr. Eberz then returned home to attend SUNY at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine, graduating in 2003. He continued his education at Strong Memorial Hospital in Rochester and completed his Residency in 2004. He started Sheridan Family Dentistry with Dr. Donahue in January 2012.

Dr. Eberz is a member of a collaborative dental community on CDOCS, the American Dental Association , the Eighth District Dental Society , and the New York State Dental Association . He has also spent time on the Alumni Association Board as well as the SUNY at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine Board.

Dr. Eberz enjoys golf, skiing, and rooting on the Bills and Sabres. He loves spending time with his wife Melissa, daughter Grace, son Samuel, and his Jack Russell Terriers, Duke and Duchess.

Dr. Tyler Ferguson

Dr. Ferguson performs all forms of general dentistry including CAD/CAM crowns, implants, and clear aligner orthodontics, with a strong interest in cosmetic dentistry. He loves restoring the smiles of his patients and strives to make every appointment comfortable and enjoyable. He is a member of the American Dental Association, New York State Dental Association, and Eighth District Dental Society. Dr. Ferguson continuously seeks out training and professional development opportunities to stay on top of the latest technologies.

After working for a colleague, he started Sheridan Family Dentistry in 2012 with Dr. John Eberz.

Dr. Donahue has a passion for advanced dental technology. He has loved helping people to put their best smile forward for the past 20 years. His dedication to patient care shines through in his thoroughness in dental work and he hopes to encourage healthy smiles in all of his patients. He performs procedures such as fillings, extractions, implants, CAD/CAM crowns, and permanently restoring teeth with artificial substitutes such as crowns, bridges, inlays, onlays, and veneers along with SureSmile Aligners.

Dr. Donahue is a member of the 8th District Dental Society, Twin City Dental Society, NYS Dental Association, and the American Dental Association. When he is not in the office, Dr. Donahue enjoys spending time with his wife Mary, and their 5 children. He is very involved in all of his kids’ sporting events and has a passion for the outdoors. Go Bills!

Dr. Danielle O’Connell

Dr. O’Connell also does our SureSmile Aligners in office. She wants all her patients to feel like they left fully knowing what treatment was done and why.

When she is not taking care of her patients, Dr. O’Connell and her husband Mike are enjoying every second of their 9-month-old son Harrison and Australian cattle/shepherd Finn. Dr. Larry Whelan Dr. Larry Whelan is a hometown guy, growing up right here In Tonawanda, NY.

He joined Sheridan Family Dentistry in June 2012 after owning his own practice for 29 years! After graduating with his bachelor’s in Biology, he continued his schooling graduating from SUNY Buffalo in 1982. He went on to do his residency in 1983 at Loma Linda in California.

Dr. Whelan has been practicing in the Ken-Ton area for 41 years. Over the years he has developed a passion for building a relationship and connection with his patients. He has seen it all in the dentistry field.
