Emergency

If your child has a dental emergency during normal business hours, please immediately call our office at 435.752.4330 and we will see your child as soon as possible. If the emergency takes place outside of our office hours, Dr. Gehring can be reached on his emergency cell phone at 435.764.5437 .

We do NOT set aside “emergency time” on a daily basis, but your child’s comfort and safety are of primary importance to Dr. Gehring and our staff. Occasionally treating an emergency will cause us to run slightly behind with our regular scheduled patients. When this occurs, we apologize, request your understanding, and we will do our best to keep you informed. We will always strive to see your child in a timely manner.
