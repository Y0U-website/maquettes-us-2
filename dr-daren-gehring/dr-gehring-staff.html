  • CHILDREN’S DENTAL DEVELOPMENT & AIRWAY DENTISTRY Children’s smiles are constantly changing as their teeth, jaws, and oral structures develop. Dr. Gehring monitors growth and development to help identify concerns early and provide personalized care for growing smiles. He also stays current with advancements in airway-focused pediatric dentistry, evaluating how oral development, breathing patterns, and airway health may relate to a child’s overall dental development.
  • BIOLASE LASER DENTISTRY Dr. Gehring utilizes advanced dental technology , including Biolase Waterlase laser dentistry, to provide precise and comfortable treatment options for children when appropriate. Laser technology can help improve certain procedures by allowing for minimally invasive treatment and enhanced patient comfort.
  • RESTORATIVE DENTAL CARE When children experience cavities, dental injuries, or other concerns, Dr. Gehring provides restorative dental care focused on protecting teeth, restoring function, and helping children maintain healthy smiles.
  • EMERGENCY DENTAL CARE Dental emergencies can be stressful for both children and parents. Dr. Gehring provides compassionate emergency dental care to help families navigate unexpected injuries, tooth pain, and urgent dental concerns.

SPECIFICALLY TRAINED TO TREAT CHILDREN AND TEENS

Children are not just small adults. Their teeth, bodies, emotions, and communication styles are constantly changing — and they deserve dental care designed specifically for them.

Our team has specialized training focused on the unique needs of infants, children, and teenagers. We understand childhood dental development, behavior guidance, and how to create comfortable experiences for children at every stage.

We believe parents deserve clear information and honest recommendations when making decisions about their child’s dental health.

Our goal is to educate families, explain what we see, discuss treatment options, and help parents make informed choices that are right for their child.

You can expect our team to explain what we see during your child’s visit, why we recommend certain treatments, and what options are available to you. A trusting relationship starts with open communication.
