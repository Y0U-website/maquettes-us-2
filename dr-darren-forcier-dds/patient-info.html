Patient Info

Patient Info Your First Step to a Confident Smile Your first visit is all about getting to know you and your smile goals. You’ll meet Dr. Forcier, receive a complete orthodontic evaluation, and have time to ask questions about treatment options like braces or clear aligners. We’ll walk you through everything—no pressure, no rush.

We believe in clear communication and personalized care from day one. Whether you’re a parent bringing in your child or exploring treatment for yourself, our goal is to make your first visit comfortable, informative, and the beginning of a great experience.

Office Visits

Regular visits, typically every six to ten weeks, are crucial for tracking and adjusting your treatment. We strive to accommodate your schedule and make these appointments as convenient as possible. Life can be unpredictable— if you’re running late or need to reschedule, just let us know. We’re here to help every step of the way. Call one of our offices to book your next appointment.

Patient Forms

At Forcier Orthodontics, we value your time and strive to make your experience as easy as possible. Financing & Insurance

At Forcier Orthodontics, we believe cost should never stand in the way of a confident, healthy smile. During your first visit, we’ll provide a complimentary insurance benefit check so you’ll know exactly what’s covered and how to plan ahead. Orthodontic coverage can vary by provider, and our team is here to guide you through every step.

We proudly offer flexible payment plans through OrthoFi, giving you the freedom to choose monthly payments that work for your budget. Whether you’re considering braces or clear aligners, we make treatment affordable, transparent, and stress-free. We also accept many insurance plans and will help you make the most of your benefits. Feel free to log in to your OrthoFi account to make a payment or view information about your account.

Join the Kids Club

Our Kids Club is designed for growing smiles who aren’t quite ready for braces—but still deserve to be part of the fun! While we monitor your child’s dental development through free observation visits, they’ll learn about braces, earn rewards, and join in on exclusive giveaways. With regular check-ins, we help ensure teeth are coming in properly and keep you informed every step of the way. Every visit brings a chance to win cool prizes and build excitement for what’s ahead!
