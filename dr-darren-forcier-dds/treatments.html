Treatments Our Commitment to You At Forcier Orthodontics, our mission is to provide expert, personalized orthodontic care that’s comfortable, honest, and designed around you. From your first visit to your final result, we’re here to make your smile journey smooth, rewarding, and built to last.

Orthodontic care for every age

Phase 1 treatment typically starts between ages 7–10 and focuses on guiding jaw growth and creating space for developing teeth. These early steps can prevent bigger issues later and set the foundation for a healthy, balanced smile. We monitor growth closely to ensure the best timing and outcomes for future treatment.

The teen years are an ideal time for orthodontic treatment. With most permanent teeth in place, we use braces or clear aligners to correct bite issues and alignment—boosting confidence right when it matters most. Our goal is to make the process smooth, flexible, and tailored to each teen’s needs.

More adults than ever are choosing orthodontic treatment to improve their health and appearance. With discreet options like clear aligners and ceramic braces, we make it easy to fit treatment into your lifestyle. Whether it’s your first time or you’re revisiting treatment, we’re here to help every step of the way.

Braces

Metal braces remain the most reliable option for correcting bite issues and aligning teeth with precision. Today’s brackets are smaller, smoother, and more comfortable than ever. They’re a great choice for kids, teens, and adults alike. With regular check-ins and expert adjustments, you’ll be on track for a strong, healthy smile.

Clear braces use tooth-colored ceramic brackets for a more low-key look without sacrificing performance. They’re ideal for patients who want the strength of braces with a more aesthetic finish. Designed to be durable and stain-resistant, they’re a popular choice among teens and adults. You get the same reliable results with a softer, less noticeable appearance.

Clear Aligners

Invisalign is one of the most recognized names in clear aligner therapy—trusted by millions of patients worldwide. Using advanced 3D imaging and custom treatment planning, Invisalign aligners are designed to gently and efficiently move teeth without the need for brackets or wires. They’re smooth, nearly invisible, and removable, making them a great choice for adults and teens alike. With consistent wear and expert monitoring, Invisalign can treat a wide range of orthodontic issues while fitting easily into your daily life.

3M Clarity Aligners offers a high-tech, personalized solution for patients looking for clear and comfortable treatment. Powered by digital precision and designed for flexibility, these trays are nearly invisible and tailored to your unique tooth movement. 3M aligners work well for mild to moderate cases and are backed by one of the most trusted brands in dental innovation. They’re a great option for patients who want an effective and discreet alternative to traditional braces—with reliable results.

Retainers

Lock in your smile for life!

Retainers are essential to maintaining your results after braces or aligner treatment. They help keep your teeth in their new position while the surrounding bone and tissue settle. We offer both fixed and removable retainers, customized to fit your needs and lifestyle. Wearing your retainer as directed is the key to keeping your smile straight for the long haul.

Emergency Care

Quick Help When Things Feel Off

Orthodontic emergencies are rare, but if something doesn’t feel right—like a loose bracket, broken wire, or sudden discomfort—we’re here to help. You can often manage minor issues at home by placing wax on poking parts or using tweezers to move a wire. If something comes off, save it and bring it to your visit. For anything painful, give us a call, and we’ll guide you through what to do next. Your comfort and safety are always our priority.
