COVID-19 STAY SAFE

Even though our office was closed most of March to June 2020, we were able to provide essential care for emergency patients. Tele-dentistry became invaluable to us during our closure with patients sending pictures of loose baby teeth, cracked molars and even finger cuts. With the help of online seminars, endless personal research and guidelines from the CDC and local public health agencies, our office re-opened safely in July 2020.

Our doctor and staff are screened daily for symptoms, and tested regularly for the virus. Together with strict patient screening and check-in protocol, we have provided a safe environment for our patients and staff.

Now the we are into our third year of Covid 19 and its many variants, some of us have let down our guards and did away with wearing masks and precautions that we take for granted. But this have expose us more to the common flu and the current RSV virus. We hope that all of you have taken advantage of the Covid vaccine as well as the common flu vaccine. Our office have not taken any of these germs and viruses lightly and ask you to follow the guideline below to keep our patients, doctors and staff safe.

THE MUST DO&#x27;S

IF YOU ARE SICK, DON’T FEEL WELL OR HAVE BEEN AROUND SOMEONE WHO IS SICK, PLEASE STAY HOME. YOU CAN CALL US TO RESCHEDULE UNTIL YOU FEEL BETTER.

FACE MASK : Although not mandatory, we still ask that you wear a face mask when you enter and leave our office. Due to limited supply, you must bring your own. Our staff will also be wearing masks and face shields.

STAY 6 FEET APART FROM OTHER PATIENTS: Our waiting room will be set up for social distancing and additional markings and barriers will be prominent. Unless you are a child, being seen as a family, or need assistance, try to come to your appointment by yourself.

TEMPERATURE CHECK: Your temperature will be checked by our staff when you enter our office.

WASHING HANDS: We will ask you to wash your hands or use hand sanitizer.

MOUTH WASH: We will ask to to rinse with a safe peroxide-based mouthwash before any dental procedures.

KEEPING YOU AND OUR OFFICE SAFE

Infection control and sterilization has always been a top priority at our office. We will be complying with the latest protocol for social distancing and sanitation guidelines to prevent risk to our patients and staff.

We will be wearing more physical barriers (PPE). Our office scheduling will be staggered to minimize crowding in the waiting room. You might be asked to wait in your car until you room is sterilized and prepared for you.

Our waiting room and restrooms will be frequently disinfected and cleaned on a regular basis throughout the day.

There will be more essential equipment such as medical grade air purification systems. Extra oral suction machines during restorative procedures to reduce aerosols. Anti-viral fogging units with EPA approved "Hypo-Chlorous Acid".

We have spent countless hours and on-line seminars evaluating and researching new technology and equipment that we feel will lessen the risk of spreading the virus. As these become more definitive and available, we will implement them in our office.
