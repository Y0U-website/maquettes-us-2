The Benefits of Dental Implants

Versatile

You can replace one tooth, multiple teeth, or an entire arch of teeth. The All-on-4 ® treatment concept uses four implant posts to secure a fixed denture and restore oral function.

Bone health protection and preservation

Dental implants replace the entire tooth with an artificial tooth root, and stimulate the jaw bone to prevent atrophy. Dentures and other removable options only sit on top of the bony ridge and do not prevent bone atrophy, dental shifting, or other problems.

Enjoy your favorite foods

Unlike removable dentures, implants remain in your mouth while eating so you can enjoy all of your favorite foods without restrictions.

Improved self-esteem

A complete smile can improve your self-esteem and quality of life. Dental implants look, feel, and function just like natural teeth.

Types of Dental Implants in Lehi, UT

  • Single Implants. A dental implant can be used to replace one missing tooth anywhere in the mouth, provided you have enough adequate jaw bone to support it.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges. Traditional bridges require adjacent healthy teeth to be shaved down to support the bride. An implant-supported bridge protects your natural teeth and also benefits your jaw bone health.
  • Same-Day Implants. Our surgeons can place an implant post immediately following a tooth extraction, as long as you have enough jaw bone, greatly reducing the number of necessary appointments.
  • All-on-4 ® Treatment Concept. A fixed denture can be secured with as few as four dental implants posts, fully restoring your oral aesthetic and function.

Dental Implant Surgery in Lehi, UT

Your oral maxillofacial surgeon will perform your dental implant procedure at our office in Lehi, UT. We will create detailed surgical guides using the most cutting-edge CBCT scanning technology to ensure your implants are placed with the highest level of precision.

First, your oral surgeon will make an incision into the gum tissue and place the post into the jaw bone. Over the next 3 to 6 months, the post will integrate and become the foundation of your replacement tooth. While your mouth is healing, your restorative dentist will create your custom crown. Once the post has completely fused with the jaw bone beneath the gums, the implant will be exposed during a second surgery to attach the abutment and place your personalized crown.
