Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • What are All-on-4 dental implants? All-on-4 is an advanced treatment that replaces all your upper or lower teeth with a secure, natural-looking bridge supported by just four dental implants. This approach often allows you to leave with a full set of teeth the same day your implants are placed.
  • Am I a candidate for All-on-4 implants? If you have many missing or failing teeth—or are tired of wearing dentures—All-on-4 could be a great solution. Most patients are candidates, even if you’ve been told you have bone loss. Dr. Toler will perform a thorough exam and 3D imaging to create a plan just for you.
  • How long does the process take? In many cases, you’ll have your implants placed and leave with a temporary set of teeth the same day. After a few months of healing, your permanent, custom-made bridge is securely attached.
  • Will it look and feel natural? Absolutely. All-on-4 teeth are designed to look, feel, and function like your own natural teeth. They are fixed in place—no slipping or removing them at night.
  • Does the procedure hurt? Most patients say they feel very little discomfort during the procedure itself. We offer sedation options to keep you relaxed. Some soreness is normal afterward, but it usually improves within a few days.
  • How much does All-on-4 cost? Every case is unique, so pricing can vary based on your specific needs. During your consultation, we’ll provide a clear, detailed estimate with no surprises.
  • Do you offer financing? Yes. We’ve partnered with Cherry to make treatment more affordable. Many patients choose low monthly payment plans with fast approvals. We’ll walk you through all your options.
  • How long do All-on-4 implants last? With good care and regular dental visits, All-on-4 implants can last many years—often decades. We’ll be here to help you maintain your smile for life.
  • Why should I choose Hixson Dental Group and Dr. Toler? Dr. Toler is a third-generation dentist with advanced training in implant dentistry and a passion for restoring smiles. Unlike some practices, we don’t disappear after treatment—we provide all your dental care under one roof, so you can feel confident you’ll always have a team you trust.
