Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • What are Snap-In Dentures? Snap-In Dentures are removable dentures that attach securely to dental implants. They snap into place for added stability and remove for cleaning.
  • How are Snap-In Dentures different from regular dentures? Traditional dentures rest on the gums and can move or slip. Snap-In Dentures connect to implants, which helps them stay in place more securely.
  • Am I a candidate for Snap-In Dentures If you are missing most or all of your teeth and want a more secure option than traditional dentures, you may be a candidate. A consultation and exam can determine what’s best for your smile.
  • How many implants are needed? This depends on your mouth, bone support, and treatment plan, but many Snap-In Denture cases use two to four implants per arch.
  • Will Snap-In Dentures look natural? Yes. They are custom-made to fit your mouth and smile, and they are designed to look natural while improving comfort and function.
  • Do you offer financing? Yes. We’ve partnered with Cherry to make treatment more affordable. Many patients choose low monthly payment plans with fast approvals. We’ll walk you through all your options.
  • Why should I choose Hixson Dental Group and Dr. Toler? Because you deserve a team that combines advanced treatment with compassion, honesty, and long-term care. We’re here to help you find the solution that fits your life, your goals, and your budget.
