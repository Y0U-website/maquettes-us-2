The practice

Our Dental Recognition (DR) Program provides affordable dental care at the highest quality to those without dental insurance.

Once a New Patient Exam is completed, regular dental visits can be scheduled at our DR Program rate. At the Adult New Patient Exam, the health of the gums will be diagnosed and the proper dental treatment will be proposed. Please note that only patients with a healthy mouth qualify for a regular dental cleaning.

Your initial exam will last approximately 90 minutes. Oral hygiene instructions will be provided along with suggestions to help you care for your teeth. In most cases, we will also clean your teeth on this visit and provide a complete dental evaluation. Learn More
