Meet The Team

Dr. Gleaner has been practicing at this location in Bayonne for over 35 years. He started practicing with Dr. Gerard Steinert who had been practicing since 1948. Many of his patients have continued care with Dr. Gleaner, as well as their friends and family. A Rutgers University and Fairleigh Dickinson School of Dentistry graduate, Dr. Gleaner continued his training at Kings County Hospital in Brooklyn, NY.

For many years he was an adjunct clinical professor at New York University School of Dentistry. His professional activities include active membership in the Hudson County Dental Society, New Jersey Dental Association, as well as the American Dental Association.

He has served in numerous positions in these groups, including President of the Hudson County Dental Society, Editor, and for many years chairman of Children’s Dental Health Month, held annually in February.

Dr. Gleaner has participated in "Give Kids A Smile" day, an annual event to treat children at no cost, and has volunteered at the Trudi Birger Children’s Dental Clinic in Jerusalem, Israel, as well as "Team Smile", a national program sponsored by multiple dental corporations to provide free dental care to children in programs throughout the country.

He sponsors a very popular "Halloween Candy Buy Back" program, where excess candy is sent to US military troops throughout the world, with toothbrushes, of course. He was recently honored at the NJ Dental Association annual conference with a member recognition award, given to one member of the county with long time service to the profession and the community.

When not working, Dr. Gleaner is busy enjoying time with his wife Leslie of almost 40 years, as well as his three children and three grandchildren who really don’t brush their teeth enough!

At Dr. Gleaner, we are committed to providing our patients with the highest quality dental care possible. We use only the best materials and equipment, and we adhere to strict sterilization and safety protocols to ensure the health and well-being of our patients.

Maria is our chairside dental assistant. She is an important part of being sure every aspect of your treatment with Dr. Gleaner is comfortable and efficient. She is especially valuable with those "fearful" patients as her gentle manner will help you be put to ease during any procedures. Not only is she helpful with Dr. Gleaner and the staff, but she is great at educating the patient about their care, so don’t hesitate to ask her if you have any questions.

Yelitza is a dedicated dental hygienist committed to providing quality care and ensuring that every patient feels comfortable and well-informed during their visits. Passionate about oral health and overall wellness, Yelitza combines her professional expertise with her desire to help patients achieve healthy and beautiful smiles.

Outside the office, Yelitza enjoys an active lifestyle that includes going to the gym and cooking healthy meals for her family. She loves traveling and creating special memories with her loved ones.

Additionally, she is passionate about continuing education, staying up-to-date with the latest advancements in the dental field to provide the best possible care to her patients.

Yelitza’s warm and professional approach makes her an essential part of the dental team, always ready to help patients feel confident while taking care of their oral health.
