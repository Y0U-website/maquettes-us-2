You deserve the opportunity to have the most cutting-edge and high-quality care possible. Our team has many years of experience in the dental field and is passionate about continuing education and training. We perform a wide variety of services efficiently, timely, and professionally, and we always work with a gentle touch.

Our friendly and well-trained team genuinely cares about helping you and your family have an enjoyable time with us. You’ll find that our excellent chair-side manner and engaging personalities will put you at ease. From the front desk to the treatment chair, we are kind, dependable and focused on meeting your needs.

Tired of running around from practice to practice for your family’s dental care? You won’t have to do that here! We provide orthodontics, pediatrics, cosmetics and general dental services all under one roof. Also, with eight full-time hygienists, we can complete your entire family’s routine cleanings and exams in just an hour! As you can see, we do our best to make it easy and convenient for you and your family to get the care you need.

We understand that it can be difficult to feel comfortable in the treatment chair, which is why we work hard to create a warm and welcoming environment that will put you at ease. We also provide blankets, pillows, and anesthetic warmers so you can feel relaxed and comfortable during your care.

Because we value you, we strive to be accommodating and helpful in every way. For your family’s convenience, we provide appointment text and email reminders. Our genuine care for you also extends beyond our practice doors with personalized phone calls following big procedures. We want you to know that we are always here for you.

Did you know that we are one of the top two offices in the state for the number of CEREC restorations we preform? Patients continue to choose us for this advanced procedure because of our skilled team and advanced technology. We have a new state-of-the-art facility equipped with the most up-to-date dental technology because it helps us better identify, treat and prevent dental problems and, ultimately, better serve you. We will continue to invest with you in mind.
