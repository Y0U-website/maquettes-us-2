What Are Dental Implants?

Implants are small, biocompatible titanium posts that replace missing tooth roots. Over time, they fuse with your jawbone to create a secure foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures.

Because they integrate naturally with your bone, implants not only provide long-term stability but also help prevent the bone loss often associated with missing teeth.

Long-Term Success Starts With Ongoing Care

With proper oral hygiene and regular checkups, dental implants can last a lifetime. Our skilled hygiene team plays a vital role in your long-term success—providing expert cleanings, maintenance, and education to help protect your investment and keep your smile healthy for years to come.
