The practice

As a family-owned and multi-generational practice, Heritage Dental Care is deeply dedicated to providing compassionate and personalized care to all our patients.

We prioritize our patients’ comfort, concerns, and overall well-being, striving to build enduring and trusting relationships.

With a commitment to individualized attention, we ensure that every visit is tailored to meet our patients’ needs, resulting in a comfortable and supportive experience!

Heritage Dental Care offers a comprehensive range of dental services such as routine cleanings, fillings, crowns, bridges, implant restorations, dentures, and teeth whitening appliances. Our practice serves patients of all ages, including children and the elderly community.

Heritage Dental Care possesses an unwavering commitment to patient care.

From the dentists and hygienists to the administrative staff, everyone takes the time to build meaningful connections with patients, understanding their unique personalities and life stories.

This personalized approach ensures that the practice can provide the highest level of care to each individual.
