Dentists at Heritage Dental Care in Northfield, MN

Dr. David Brust

Dr. David Brust is a native of Northfield, MN and a graduate of NHS. He earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in Chemistry from St. Olaf College in 1991. Dr. Brust graduated with a high distinction from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry in 1996. He was awarded an Oral Surgery Fellowship at the Minneapolis Veterans Hospital where he practiced for one year. Upon completion of the fellowship, he returned to Northfield where he joined Division Street Dentists, now Heritage Dental Care.

Dr. Brust is a member of the American Dental Association, the Minnesota Dental Association, and Southeastern District Dental Society. He is also a member of Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Honor Society.

Dave lives in Northfield and has two daughters. In his spare time he enjoys the kids’ activities, outdoor sports and recreation.

Dr. Michael Thompson

Dr. Michael Thompson grew up in Grand Forks, North Dakota and attended St. John’s University where he earned a B.A. in Biological Sciences in 2009.

He then attended the University of Minnesota School Dentistry receiving his D.D.S. degree in 2013. Dr. Thompson is a member of the American Dental Society along with the Minnesota Dental Association.

Dr. Thompson enjoys all aspects of dentistry and particularly working with youngsters in an effort to introduce them to dentistry while minimizing their anxiety.

Dr. Thompson, with his wife, Kaitlin, two daughters and son, reside in Northfield and enjoy spending time outdoors in Northern Minnesota during the summers.

Dr. Nathan McMillon

Dr. McMillon was born and raised in Lakeville, Minnesota. He is an alum of the University of Wisconsin-Madison where he received his Bachelor of Science in Neurobiology. He received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Iowa. He is proud to have acquired a taste of what the Midwest has to offer, and is excited to call Northfield home after having spent his first years of practice in Apple Valley.

One of his biggest motivators is to help patients improve their self-esteem through maintaining oral health, and loving the way they feel and look when they smile. He is motivated to help patients with dental anxiety overcome their fear of dentistry and educate patients about better oral health.

Outside of the office, you will find Dr. McMillon playing sports including pickleball, basketball, softball, flag football, and golf. Dr. McMillon loves to visit and socialize with his friends and family and will spend many weekends traveling for concerts, weddings, Vikings games, and other social activities.

Dr. McMillon is currently involved with the American Dental Association, Minnesota Dental Association, St. Paul District Dental Society, CDOCS, and Southside Comprehensive Study Club (Seattle Study Club). Call and make an appointment with Dr. McMillon today!
