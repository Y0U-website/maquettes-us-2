Same-Day Dental Crowns

The Planmeca PlanScan E4D allows us to create crowns, inlays, onlays, veneers, and some bridges right in the office. The restoration process used to be comprised of messy molds, a waiting period as they were sent to the lab and the restoration was created, and at least one more visit to complete the procedure. Now, we use a digital intra-oral camera with computer-assisted design (CAD) tools and a milling machine right in the office. No messy molds. No long waits. No extra visits. Your comfort is of paramount concern to us.

Digital X-Rays

We use digital dental x-rays to view structures beyond what we can see on the surface. For you, this means less exposure to radiation and the ability for you and your dentist to view the x-rays immediately on the computer screen. The clarity of the images is superior to traditional film, and the dentist can zoom in on areas to see details. This is beneficial for early diagnosis and treatment.

Teeth Whitening

Teeth can become stained from coffee, wine, smoking, tea, and other causes. When your smile isn’t as white as you’d like, we can schedule an appointment for teeth whitening. Whitening offers up to eight shades lighter in one treatment. Take-home kits whiten your smile on your schedule. Teeth whitening is safe and effective with little to no irritation. Next time you’re here, ask if teeth whitening is right for you.
