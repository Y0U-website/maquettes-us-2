Meet Our Team

Dr. Samuel David Rosenbaum

Dr. David Rosenbaum was born and raised in Pasadena, Texas. After graduating from Sam Rayburn High School in 1988, he attended Texas A & M University for his undergraduate degree in Chemistry. Dr. Rosenbaum then obtained his doctorate in dentistry at the University of Texas Health Science Center in San Antonio in 1997. He worked in Tyler as an associate and went on to open his own practice in Decatur, TX, in May 2000.

He met his wife, Jana, while studying at Texas A & M and the two married in 1995. Jana is also the practice’s office manager. The couple has been blessed with three children, twin boys Jakob and Jagger, and a daughter, Lily. Dr. Rosenbaum enjoys traveling, golfing, and spending time with his family.

Jana Hughes Rosenbaum

Jana Rosenbaum is the office manager at Rosenbaum Dental. She is married to Dr. Rosenbaum, and they have three beautiful children together. Most of her free time is spent with her children, Jakob, Jagger, and Lily.

Jana graduated from Decatur High School and went on to study at Texas A & M where she received a Bachelor of Science degree. She met her husband while attending Texas A & M, and they have been married since 1995.

She loves her job because she gets to see many old and new friends every day, and gets to help patients with any questions they have.

Abby Chambless-Williams

Abby is a registered dental hygienist. She has been working in the dental field for 20 years, nine as a hygienist. Abby graduated with honors from Amarillo College School of Dental Hygiene in 2010. Her roles and responsibilities at Rosenbaum Dental are to provide each patient with outstanding dental care, educate them on their oral hygiene status and present the needed tools to help them achieve dental excellence.

Abby resides in Decatur with her husband, Lance. They have two daughters, Quade and Quincy. In her spare time, she enjoys traveling, baking, cooking, and being outdoors. She also enjoys spending time with her friends, family, and 15 nieces and nephews.
