Braces for Teens and Kids

  • Create facial symmetry by influencing jaw growth
  • Reduce the risk of trauma to protruding front teeth
  • Preserve space for unerupted teeth
  • Reduce the need for tooth removal
  • Reduce the treatment time with braces
  • Prevent malocclusions (bad bites)

What We Look For

During your child’s first orthodontic examination we look for:

  • Periodontal (gum) problems related to crooked teeth
  • Jaw growth imbalances such as facial asymmetry
  • Bite problems such as overbite and underbite
  • Crowding and unerupted (impacted) teeth
  • Space loss that requires preserving space for unerupted teeth
  • Narrow arches that can result in a crossbite
  • Jaw joint and jaw dysfunction (TMD)
