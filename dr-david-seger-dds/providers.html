Medical

EMILY VUCHETICH

Dr. Vuchetich joined Midtown Health Center in 2015. She is a board-certified pediatrician with more than 25 years of experience, including 11 years at Children’s Hospital Urgent Care Center in Omaha. She has a special interest in preventative care for children and adolescents and provides pediatric services at the Norfolk Clinic on Mondays, Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays.

ALLISON WALLICK

Dr. Wallick, a South Dakota native, joined Midtown in 2021. She earned her undergraduate degree from South Dakota State University and her medical degree from the University of South Dakota Sanford School of Medicine before completing her family medicine residency at Clarkson Family Medicine in Omaha. She sees patients of all ages with interests in chronic disease management, preventative medicine, psychiatric care, and women’s health. She provides Medical Assisted Treatment services at the Norfolk Clinic on Mondays through Fridays.

JENNIFER STAMAN

Jenny is a Family Nurse Practitioner who joined Midtown’s pediatric department in 2020. She earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from the University of Nebraska College of Nursing and her Master of Science in Nursing from Clarkson College. With 10 years of nursing experience, including four years in Women’s and Children’s Services at Faith Regional Health Services, she provides pediatric care at the Norfolk Clinic on Mondays through Fridays.

KASSIDY HORST

Kassidy joined Midtown Health Center in 2017 as a Women’s Health Nurse Practitioner. She completed the Women’s Health Nurse Practitioner program at the University of Nebraska Medical Center College of Nursing. Kassidy provides comprehensive women’s health services including annual exams, prenatal and reproductive care, and STI testing and treatment for both men and women. She sees patients at Northeast Community College and Wayne clinics during the school year and at the West Point Clinic on Tuesdays.

BRIONE REZAC

Brione is a Nebraska native and a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner. She earned both her Bachelor of Science in Nursing and Doctor of Nursing Practice degrees from the University of Nebraska Medical Center. She joined Midtown in 2022 with over 10 years of nursing experience in acute and long-term care settings, she provides care for patients of all ages with a focus on preventative care and chronic disease management. Brione sees patients at the Norfolk and Xpress Clinics.

JENNIFER WEINANDT

Jennifer is a Northeast Nebraska native who joined Midtown Health Center in 2023 as a Certified Nurse Midwife. She earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from Mount Marty College and her Master of Science in Nursing with a certification in Midwifery from the University of Cincinnati. She provides midwifery services on Tuesday mornings in partnership with Midwest Health Partners.

ASHLEY STRAIN

Ashley has worked in healthcare for more than 18 years and joined Midtown Health Center in 2024 as a Family Nurse Practitioner. She earned her Bachelor of Science in Radiologic Technology from Mount Marty College and her Master of Healthcare Administration and completed her nursing degrees at Central Community College and Chamberlain University. Ashley provides care for patients of all ages at the Norfolk Clinic on Mondays, Tuesdays, and Fridays, and the Madison Clinic on Wednesdays and Thursdays.

MACKENZIE POSPISIL

Mackenzie is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner from Northeast Nebraska. She earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from the University of Nebraska Medical Center and her Doctor of Nursing Practice degree from Clarkson College. With experience in ICU and Cardiac Cath Lab nursing, as of 2025 she now provides care for Midtown patients of all ages at the Norfolk and Xpress Clinics.

LORI BRATETIC

Lori, a Sioux City, Iowa native, joined Midtown Health Center in 2025. She earned her Master’s degree in Family Nurse Practitioner studies from Chamberlain College of Nursing in 2021 and completed the Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner program in 2023. With more than 20 years of experience in hospital and clinical settings, Lori provides care for patients of all ages with specialties in Family Practice, Psychiatry, and Cardiology. MICHELLE SCHRAMM

She provides comprehensive hygiene services with a focus on periodontal disease and works at the Norfolk Clinic and off-site at Norfolk Regional Center and Norfolk Veterans Home on Mondays, Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Fridays.

MOLLIE MILLER

Behavioral Health

He joined Midtown in 2018 and provides behavioral health services including assessment and treatment for child and adolescent developmental and behavioral concerns.

KRISTEN NEUHALFEN

JESSICA SOTELO

Jessica was born and raised in the Norfolk area and is a Licensed Independent Mental Health Practitioner who joined Midtown in 2022. Jessica provides therapy and evaluations for both adults and children, with a focus on supporting growth and recovery in mental health and addiction.

DEVIN WATTS

Since 2023, Devin has provided mental health care for Midtown patients and families, focusing on treatment and support for psychiatric conditions.

LATICIA SUMNER

Laticia, a Columbus, Nebraska native, earned both her Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in agency counseling from Wayne State College. OUR MISSION

To provide high quality medical, behavioral health, and dental services that are affordable, accessible, and patient centered in an atmosphere of trust and respect.

WHO DO WE SERVE

We are a non-profit 501(c3) Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) serving Northeast Nebraska.

Midtown Health Center receives HHS funding and has Federal Public Service deemed status with respect to certain health or health-related claims, including medical malpractice claims, for itself and its covered individuals.
