Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

You might be a suitable candidate for implant dentures if:

Even if you are not a candidate for implant dentures right away, you may become one after some preliminary care. Most adults with missing teeth can qualify for this procedure!

  • You have lost all or most of your teeth throughout one of your dental arches.
  • Your jawbone is healthy enough to support dental implants.
  • You are free of active gum disease.
  • You are healthy enough to undergo minor surgery.
  • You have good habits that will support your implants’ long-term success.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Implant dentures offer some pretty amazing benefits:

  • Improved stability : Implant dentures do not slip and slide around in the mouth.
  • Enhanced comfort : You can enjoy a more natural feel in your mouth compared to traditional removable dentures.
  • Better chewing efficiency : Implant dentures provide an impressive bite force, so you can feel free to enjoy a wide variety of nutritious foods.
  • Preservation of jawbone : Dental implants stimulate the jawbone and can prevent the deterioration that normally occurs after tooth extractions.
  • Natural appearance : Implant dentures are designed to look and feel as natural as possible.
  • Easy maintenance: In many ways, taking good care of implant dentures is similar to caring for natural teeth.
