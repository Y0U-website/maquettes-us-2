She continues her dental education through attendance at the annual Hinman Dental Society meeting in Atlanta, and she also studies materials provided by the Academy of General Dentistry.Dr. Anthony is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry and the Georgia Dental Association. As a dentist, her goal is to provide excellent dental care with kindness and compassion.
