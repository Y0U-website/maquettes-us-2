The Doctors Sherwood Old Town Dental

Dr. Huyssoon

Dr. Debi M. Huyssoon (pronounced Hi-soon) is a general dentist that truly values the patient-doctor relationship. She earned her B.A. in Chemistry from the University of San Diego, where she also served in the U.S. Army Reserves and later in the California National Guard. She continued her education at Portland State University, earning a B.S. in Biology, and went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Medicine (D.M.D.) degree from OHSU School of Dentistry in 2005.

Dr. Huyssoon is a member of the American Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, and the Alpha Omega Study Club, a branch of the Seattle Study Club.

She began her career as an associate dentist in Hillsboro and Salem but always knew that she wanted to own her own practice. In 2007, she purchased a small dental practice in the heart of Old Town Sherwood. In 2010 she expanded the practice by acquiring another nearby Sherwood office that was closing its doors, continuing to grow and serve the community.

Outside of the office Dr. Huyssoon enjoys traveling, gardening, cooking and canning. She also stays active through workouts and target shooting. Most importantly, she loves spending time with her husband, Scott, our Office General Manager, their two teenage children (which you have seen in our local ad’s how they have grown throughout the years), their two dogs, her mom, that also lives with them, and their extended family sister, family and close friends

Dr. Atack

Dr. Atack earned his bachelor’s degrees from BYU and Portland State University and received his Doctor of Dental Medicine (D.M.D.) degree from OHSU in 2008.

He has lived in the Portland area since 2001 and considers himself a true Oregonian. He enjoys all four seasons and takes full advantage of the outdoors. While he was once an avid snowboarder, he now spends his time exploring the Oregon Coast, kayaking local lakes, during the spring and summer, and hiking scenic trails whenever the weather allows.

When he’s feeling adventurous, Dr. Atack enjoys international travel with his long-term partner. They love experiencing new cultures, trying unique foods, and encountering local wildlife. Closer to home, he also enjoys cooking and is known for making yummy treats.

Dr. Atack is a proud father of five children who keep him busy and fulfilled. While three of them are now pursuing their own paths, they still make time to come home and “hang out with Dad”.

Known for his caring and gentle demeanor, Dr. Atack brings a great sense of humor and a warm, personable approach to patient care. His easygoing personality helps patients feel relaxed and comfortable during their visits.

Karen

Karen has over 30 years of experience in the dental field. She began her career as a dental assistant but soon realized she preferred working in the front office, where her playful personality could really shine. Dentistry has always been a part of her life; her dad was a dentist, and she likes to say, “she has blue handled scissors”.

Karen joined our team in the Fall of 2009, and we plan on keeping her here for many years to come.

Her welcoming smile is often the first thing you see when you enter our office. Karen is highly knowledgeable when it comes to insurance and is the go-to person for verifying coverage and answering billing questions. She takes the time to explain recommended treatment clearly and helps patients understand their benefits and estimated costs so there are no surprises.

Karen was raised in the Portland area and attended Oregon State University, making her a loyal OSU sports fan; although she will root for the Ducks if the Beavers aren’t playing. She enjoys watching football and chatting with patients about “last night’s game”.

Outside of work, Karen loves camping with her husband and friends and going scrapbooking with her sister-in-law, mother-in-law and friends. She also keeps in touch with many of her college friends and enjoys spending time with them whenever she can.

Karen has two grown sons, a daughter-in-law, a grandbaby and the most handsome German Shephard ever! She is a true dog lover and will happily invite patients to bring their pups by to say hello! Known for her silly puns and fun-loving jokes, Karen keeps both patients and staff smiling throughout the day.

Marta

Office Manager & Patient Care Coordinator

Marta began her career in the dental field 16 years ago when she started a temporary project with Dr. Huyssoon, working just a couple hours a day. She quickly discovered that she enjoyed working in the dental office environment and was eager to learn all aspects of the role. Within a month, she became a permanent team member at Sherwood Old Town Dental in 2010.

Over the years, Marta has worn many hats while raising her family, always putting her heart and soul into her work. Today we are proud to have her as our Office Manager and Patient Care Coordinator.

Marta is usually the friendly voice you will hear when you call our office. She works diligently to help patients find appointment times that fit their schedules and stays connected through calls, texts, letters, and postcards to ensure patients remain on track with their routine dental care. She strives to make everyone feel welcome and truly part of the “practice family”, you’ll quickly find yourself sharing stories and building a connection with her through your conversations.

With her warm, sincere and outgoing personality, and a laugh that you will hear throughout the office. Marta is consistently on the ball and goes the extra mile for both patients and staff.

Outside of work, Marta enjoys spending time with her husband, their two amazing adult children, and her son-in-law, along with their large extended family. She also loves their adorable, little 15-pound Cheeweenie and their three “grand-dogs”, two Yellow Labs and German Shorthaired Pointer mix.

Marta and her husband care for her father-in-Law daily which keeps them very busy, but when they have time, they enjoy glamping in their travel trailer or heading out to new adventures. She also loves reading, crafting making tie blankets, and running her Mary Kay business.

Kelsey

Kelsey has over 10 years of experience as a dental hygienist and joined the Sherwood Old Town Dental team in March 2021. She has been a wonderful addition to the Practice. Kelsey is highly informative and enjoys sharing helpful tips and guidance to support her patients in maintaining excellent oral health.

She greets every patient with a warm smile and brings a gentle, caring approach to her work. Known for her kind and attentive demeanor, Kelsey helps patients feel comfortable and at ease during their visits.

Kelsey is detail-oriented and plays an important role in keeping the team up to date on protocols and regulatory changes. She is committed to maintaining a safe, organized, and high-quality clinical environment.

Outside the office, Kelsey is an active member of the Sherwood community. She volunteers at local schools where her children attend and enjoys connecting with patients both in and out of the office.

Originally from the Tualatin area, Kelsey moved to Sherwood in 2009 with her husband. They have two children and a Golden Retriever. She enjoys spending time with her family, visiting the zoo and parks, and exploring new places. In her free time, Kelsey also loves crafting, decorating her home, especially during the Fall and holiday season and making memories with family and friends.

natalie

She joined us in January of 2024 after making a big move back to Oregon with her husband, three growing boys, and a lively crew of pets. Allison

Allison has over 15 years of experience in the dental field. She began her career as an assistant at one of our referring orthodontic offices. After two years, she decided to pursue dental hygiene school while continuing to work at the orthodontic office.

After graduating, Allison joined our team in August 2012 and worked with us until early 2016, when she stepped away to focus on raising her family. During that time, she had the opportunity to live abroad for a couple of years and travel throughout Europe. We stayed in touch, and in July 2025, we were thrilled to welcome her back to the team—and we plan to keep her for many years to come. It’s been so fun seeing how much her kids have grown!

Allison has a sweet and welcoming approach. She is funny, engaging, and known for her gentle touch. Outside of work, Allison loves cooking, making most things from scratch, spending time with her family, and hanging out with their Italian Water dog. She enjoys traveling anywhere sunny and warm, with Greece being her favorite destination. With her keen ear for learning different languages, she’s always challenging herself to learn more; her Spanish is especially impressive!
