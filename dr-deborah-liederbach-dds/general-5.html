NCS Services

North Coast Smiles offers a comprehensive range of services to address various oral health needs. From routine dental cleanings and exams to advanced restorative and cosmetic procedures, we are equipped to deliver the highest quality of care. Our team is proficient in handling complex cases and is dedicated to achieving optimal results for every patient.

Experienced Professionals North Coast Smiles is a staff of experienced dental professionals. We play a crucial role in providing comprehensive oral care to patients. We offer a range of tasks aimed at maintaining and improving patients oral health. Here s an overview of what North Coast Smiles does:

TMJ/TMD Treatment

North Coast Smiles provides treatments for temporomandibular joint disorders, which can cause jaw pain and related issues.

Child/Pediatric Dentistry

North Coast Smiles caters to children s dental needs, offering specialized care for kids, including check-ups, cleanings, and education.

Preventive Education

North Coast Smiles educates our patients about proper oral hygiene practices and provide guidance on maintaining good oral health with every service.

Emergency Dental Care

North Coast Smiles is equipped to handle dental emergencies, such as severe toothaches, broken teeth, or other urgent oral issues.

Oral Surgery

North Coast Smiles provides oral surgery services, including tooth extractions, wisdom teeth removal, and dental implant placement.

Dentures

North Coast Smiles provides full or partial dentures to replace missing teeth and restore functionality.

Dental Crowns and Bridges

Crowns are used to cover and protect damaged teeth, while bridges replace missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent teeth.

Periodontal (Gum) Care

Clinics can offer treatments for gum diseases like gingivitis and periodontitis, including scaling and root planing.

Orthodontics

North Coast Smiles provides orthodontic services to address teeth misalignment, using clear aligners, or other corrective appliances.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Services like veneers, dental bonding, and contouring are available to enhance the aesthetics of your smile.

Teeth Whitening

North Coast Smiles teeth whitening procedures help to improve the appearance of stained or discolored teeth.

Fillings and Restorations

Dental clinics can treat cavities by removing decayed portions of teeth and filling the spaces with various materials like amalgam or composite resin.
