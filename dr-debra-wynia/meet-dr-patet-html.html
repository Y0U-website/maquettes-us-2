Meet Dr. Patet

  • Meet Dr. Patet Dentist - Forest Lake, MN Get to Know G. Rankin Patet A graduate of the University of Minnesota with a bachelor’s degree in dental science, Dr. Patet continued his education at the University by obtaining his doctorate in dental surgery in 1986. Dr. Patet also completed a one-year general practice residency program at the University of Minnesota. Professional Achievements Member of the Minnesota Dental Association, St. Paul District Dental Association, and the American Dental Association
  • University of Minnesota School of Dentistry Part-Time Faculty - Clinical Instructor from 1987-1991
  • Completion of Comprehensive Care Study Group on Dental Implants Bicon Dental Implant Course
  • Member of East Metro Study Club

Practice Interests

He provides general dentistry for both adults and children and enjoys working with families and patients of all ages.

Personal Interests

Dr. Patet, a lifelong resident of South St. Paul, Minnesota, still resides in the St. Paul suburb with his wife, Janelle, and his son, Phil. In his free time, he enjoys home remodeling, gardening, cooking, and running.
