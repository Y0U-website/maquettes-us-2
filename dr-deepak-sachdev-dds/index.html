Whether you’re missing one or several teeth, dental implants can help restore the look and function of your smile. When you are missing teeth it can dramatically change how your mouth functions making normal tasks, like chewing, difficult and even painful. Don’t let your missing teeth hold you back any longer.

Crowns are a good cosmetic treatment option as they not only strengthen your teeth but can also improve the appearance of your smile. While there are many different types of crowns, each serves it own, unique purpose . Dr. Sachdev and his team will help you determine if a crown would be beneficial for your dental needs.
