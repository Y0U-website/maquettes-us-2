Educational Background: New York University College of Dentistry, Doctor of Dental Surgery College of Arts and Science, Cornell University B. S. in Biopsychology

After completing dental school, Dr. Gupta was selected for a year-long dental residency program at Lutheran Medical Center in New York. During her time there, Dr. Gupta placed implants and crowns, and she performed many root canals, extractions and complex dental procedures. Additionally she observed and performed many surgical and specialty procedures in this program.

Since her completion from residency, she has worked in Brooklyn, Queens, midtown and uptown Manhattan. Currently she works as an attending at Long Island Jewish Hospital teaching dental residents comprehensive dental procedures. Dr. Gupta has honed her skills in cosmetic dentistry using advanced cosmetic techniques.
