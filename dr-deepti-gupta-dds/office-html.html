Our Office

Essential Dental is located on Willis Avenue off of Hillside Avenue. Our office provides a home like environment with a friendly staff and high quality of care.

We provide all of the latest procedures available in dentistry. Our staff is knoweledgeable in all procedures and we make it a point to explain the procedures to our patients. The doctor always performs a risks and benefits assessment and discusses all possibilities of the treatment plan with the patient. Our staff is always there to answer all the questions that the patients may have.
