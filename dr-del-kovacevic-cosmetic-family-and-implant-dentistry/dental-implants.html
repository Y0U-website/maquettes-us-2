What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a transformative new teeth solution for replacing missing teeth, offering a permanent fix beyond traditional treatments. Comprising three parts titanium posts, abutments, and dental crowns these implants seamlessly integrate with your jawbone and fit between your natural teeth, providing a natural look and feel that mimics your teeth.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Unlock a whole life with a host of advantages with dental implants, the premier solution for replacing a missing tooth and ensuring healthy gums:

  • Natural Look and Feel : Dental implants seamlessly blend with your natural teeth, providing an aesthetically pleasing and authentic smile.
  • Unrestricted Enjoyment : Indulge in your favorite foods without limitations, as dental implants offer the strength and stability to handle culinary delight.
  • Stability in Speech and Smile : Experience confidence in daily activities, as dental implants remain firmly in place, preventing unwanted shifting during speech and smiles.
  • Support for Jawbone and Gums : Dental implants contribute to the overall health of your jawbone and gums, preserving the structural integrity of your facial features.
  • Adhesive-Free Convenience : Bid farewell to the hassles of special adhesives often associated with traditional treatments.
  • Permanent, Lasting Solution : With proper care, dental implants are a lifelong solution, offering enduring functionality and aesthetics.

Supplemental Procedures for Dental Implants

Jawbone Deterioration Treatment

Combatting jawbone deterioration and bone loss, even from a single tooth loss, is crucial for the implanted posts and crowns’ success. Our office offers supplemental care like bone grafting to enhance the front bone foundation, ensuring optimal conditions for dental implant placement.

Gum Recession Solutions

Dental implants require healthy gums, or you run the risk of implant failure or a challenging healing process. For those facing gum recession complications, supplementary procedures are available to address and rectify this condition. A healthy gum line and surrounding teeth are vital for the stability and longevity of dental implants.

Individualized Care Plans

Dr. Kovacevic designs customized patient supplemental care plans, addressing specific challenges like periodontal disease or the health of your other teeth to pave the way for successful dental implant treatments.
