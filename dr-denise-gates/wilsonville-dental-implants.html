As a cosmetic dentist in Wilsonville, OR our team at Gates Family Dentistry has the experience and skill needed to help restore your smile back to health. If you’ve suffered from a chipped, cracked, damaged, or lost tooth, dental implants may be the right solution to restore the form and function back to your oral health.

Dental implants are titanium fixtures surgically placed directly into the jawbone to provide additional stability and support. Once a dental implant is placed into position, Dr. Gates can use the implant to anchor replacement teeth like dentures or a dental bridge that enable her to successfully restore your smile back to health.

How Dental Implants Work

While dental implants offer a great solution for repairing your oral health, you need to have healthy gums and a sufficient amount of remaining bone structure to support your implant. For your dental implant surgery to be a success, it’s absolutely vital that you practice quality oral hygiene at home while also scheduling regular visits to see your cosmetic dentist in Wilsonville, OR. This means that brushing at least twice a day and flossing daily are the key to ensuring your implant health.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants act as the next best thing to your natural, healthy teeth. Both stable and strong, a dental implant can restore a lost tooth so that it functions, fits, and feels just like a natural tooth. Other common options for tooth replacement, such as bridges and dentures, can lead to bone deterioration, and may impact your ability to speak, smile, and eat.

Dental implants are also built to last, making them a far more cost-effective solution for your long-term oral health. Most traditional, tooth-supported dental bridges last for only between 5 to 7 years. Even if you practice the best oral care possible, they may only last 10 years before a replacement is needed. The costs associated with that can really add up over the years. However, while an implant may require the occasional adjustment, they can last a lifetime when properly cared for by you and your cosmetic dentist in Wilsonville, OR.

Most importantly, dental implants allow you to enjoy life without having to worry about the state of your smile. You won’t need to stay home out of embarrassment because of worry that your smile looks different or that missing teeth will interfere with your ability to have fun. You certainly won’t have to worry about a dental bridge or dentures coming loose or falling out when you eat, drink, or laugh. A smile restored with dental implants is one you can feel confident will keep you looking and feeling your best.
