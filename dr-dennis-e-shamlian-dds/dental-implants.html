Dental Implants in Fresno, CA

Losing a tooth can affect far more than your smile it can impact how you chew, how you speak, and how confident you feel day to day. Dental implants offer the most permanent, natural-feeling solution for replacing missing teeth, and our Fresno dental team has extensive experience helping patients restore their smiles with implants that look, feel, and function like real teeth.

A dental implant is a small titanium post that’s surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as an artificial tooth root. Over the course of several months, the implant fuses with the surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a remarkably stable foundation. Once healed, a custom-made crown is attached to the implant, completing the restoration with a result that’s virtually indistinguishable from a natural tooth.

Unlike bridges or dentures, which sit on top of the gums or rely on neighboring teeth for support, implants replace both the root and the visible portion of a missing tooth. This makes them the only tooth-replacement option that also helps preserve your jawbone. When a tooth is lost, the bone beneath it can begin to shrink over time due to lack of stimulation. Because an implant functions like a natural root, it continues to stimulate the bone, helping prevent this deterioration and maintaining your facial structure over the long term.

Implants can be used to replace a single missing tooth, several teeth, or even support a full-arch denture for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth. During your consultation, we’ll take detailed images and scans of your jaw to assess bone density and determine whether you’re a good candidate for implants, or whether a bone graft might be needed first to build up sufficient support.

The implant process typically unfolds in stages. First, the implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone, usually under local anesthesia with sedation options available for patients who feel anxious about the procedure. After placement, a healing period of several months allows the implant to fully integrate with the bone. Once healing is complete, we attach an abutment and take impressions for your custom crown, which is then fabricated and secured to the implant, completing your new tooth.

While the timeline for implants is longer than some other restoration options, most patients find the investment well worth it given the results. With proper care regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups implants can last a lifetime, making them one of the most cost-effective long-term solutions when compared to restorations that may need to be replaced or adjusted every several years.

Many of our Fresno patients are surprised by how natural implants feel once healed. Because the implant is anchored directly into the bone, there’s no shifting or slipping like some patients experience with removable dentures. You’ll be able to eat your favorite foods, speak clearly, and smile with confidence, all without worrying about your replacement tooth moving or coming loose.
