When to Act Fast and Seek Care

Recognizing a dental emergency early can save your smile. Urgent issues such as severe tooth pain, broken or knocked-out teeth, lost crowns or fillings, gum infections, or abscesses often cause bleeding, swelling, or difficulty eating and speaking. These situations require immediate care to stop pain, prevent further complications, and sometimes even save a tooth.
