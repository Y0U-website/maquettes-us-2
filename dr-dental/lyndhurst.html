Dr. Willan Jumbo is an orthodontist at Dr. Dental, a practice which provides affordable and quality dental care in the Tri-state area.

Dentist & Orthodontist in Lyndhurst,NJ

Dr. Jumbo was born and raised in Ecuador. He attended Kean University, where he obtained a Bachelor of Science in Cell and Molecular Biology. He continued his education at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine and earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine. Dr. Jumbo completed his post graduate training in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics at Montefiore Medical Center / Albert Einstein College of Medicine in NYC.

Dr. Jumbo specializes in the diagnosis of tooth and jaw abnormalities, determining the best treatment method for correction and designing individualized appliances to create beautiful and healthy smiles. He is a firm believer that orthodontic therapy can transform a patient’s life by improving their self- esteem and confidence.

His professional affiliations include the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO), the American Dental Association (ADA), and the Northeastern Society of Orthodontists (NESO).

In his free time, Dr. Jumbo enjoys traveling, exploring new cities, trying new restaurants, social dancing, spending time with his family and friends, and exploring new hobbies.
