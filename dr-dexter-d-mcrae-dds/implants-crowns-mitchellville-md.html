How to Take Care of Dental Crowns

Your crowns will last on average 10 years if you follow a good oral hygiene routine and visit the dentist twice yearly. To keep your crowns in top condition, you should follow these tips:

Keep your crown looking good with these simple steps

  • Brush your teeth at least twice daily
  • Daily floss between your teeth
  • Use antibacterial mouthwashes every day
  • Avoid biting your fingernails or hard food, such as ice.
  • Open packaging with your teeth instead

A bridge is a prosthetic device that fills the gap left by your missing teeth. The bridge can be attached to the natural teeth on either side of your gap. A bridge can be used to replace several missing teeth in a line and restore your smile’s function and beauty.

Different Types of Dental Bridges

Your specific needs and oral health will determine the type of bridge you need. There are many different types of bridges:

  • Removable bridge: This is a solution similar to dentures but only replaces some teeth, not an entire arch. The bridge will be attached to your natural teeth and can be removed for cleaning.
  • Traditional (Fixed Bridge): A fixed bridge is permanently attached to the natural teeth adjacent to the gap in your mouth. The bridge will function and be stable like natural teeth.
  • Implant-Supported Bridge: Rather than attaching a dental bridge to your natural tooth, you can place implants on either side of your gap. The bridge can be permanently attached to the implants by our team for a safe restoration.
