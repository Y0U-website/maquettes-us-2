The Dental Implant Process

Your journey to a complete smile begins with a comprehensive consultation at our Golden Isles facility. Our dental implant process is methodical and precise, ensuring optimal results for each patient.

Virtual Care Coordination Session

We begin with a Virtual Care Coordination Session conducted entirely online, saving you an initial trip to our office. During this convenient meeting, we’ll discuss your dental concerns, review your medical history, explain the dental implant process, and address any questions about oral surgery options. This patient-friendly approach respects your time while providing the personalized attention you deserve at the start of your dental implant journey.

Preparatory Procedures

Some patients may require preliminary treatments such as bone grafting or sinus lifts to ensure adequate support for the implants. These procedures create the optimal foundation for successful dental implant surgery.

Implant Placement

During the surgical phase, our specialists precisely position the dental implants within your jawbone. This outpatient procedure is performed with sedation options to ensure your comfort throughout.

Healing Period

The implants integrate with your natural bone through a process called osseointegration, typically taking 3-6 months. This crucial healing phase creates the stable foundation needed for your permanent teeth.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, we attach custom-crafted, natural-looking teeth to your dental implants, completing your smile transformation.

Dental Implant Solutions We Offer

Single Tooth Implants

When you’re missing just one tooth, a single dental implant with a custom crown provides a standalone solution that doesn’t affect neighboring teeth. This approach preserves your natural dental structure while filling the gap with a permanent replacement.

Multiple Tooth Implants

For patients missing several teeth, we can place strategic dental implants to support multiple replacement teeth. This approach provides better stability and bone preservation than traditional bridges.

Full-Arch Replacements (innovaSmile All-on-X)

Our signature innovaSmile All-on-X technique offers a revolutionary solution for patients missing most or all of their teeth. Using just four to six precisely placed dental implants, we can support an entire arch of new teeth, eliminating the need for removable dentures.

Implant-Supported Bridges

When several adjacent teeth are missing, implant-supported bridges offer a secure and comfortable alternative to traditional bridges or partial dentures. These restorations are anchored by dental implants, providing stability without relying on neighboring teeth for support.
