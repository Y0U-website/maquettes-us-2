Dental Implants Solutions for Seniors with Bone Loss

Bone loss is common in elderly patients, but it doesn’t disqualify you from treatment. Dr. Mattox utilizes advanced techniques, including:

  • 3D imaging and surgical planning to maximize existing bone
  • Bone grafting procedures when needed
  • All-on-X techniques that work with available bone anatomy

Medical Coordination

The Senior Implant Experience

Comfortable Treatment Process

The implant placement takes 60-90 minutes using local anesthesia with optional sedation. Most seniors describe the experience as easier than expected, with many watching TV during treatment.

Recovery for Seniors

Recovery typically involves 3-5 days of mild discomfort, manageable with over-the-counter pain medication. Most patients return to normal activities within 48 hours. We provide detailed post-operative instructions including soft food recommendations and gentle cleaning techniques.
