The practice

He then attended the Dental College of Georgia, where he was selected as the MCG Foundation Scholar for the Class of 2016.

After earning his dental degree, Dr. Mattox completed a dual-degree residency program at Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland, Ohio. During this time, he received his Medical Doctorate, completed his Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery residency, and finished a one-year internship in General Surgery.

Prior to starting his own practice, Dr. Mattox worked at Coastal Oral Surgery in Saint Simons Island and Saint Marys, Georgia; Southeastern Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery in Savannah, Georgia; and Nuvia Implant Centers in Columbus, Ohio.

Dr. Mattox is deeply committed to using technology to improve patient outcomes. His areas of expertise include surgical treatment planning using 3D modeling for advanced dental implant surgeries, software development and technology implementation to streamline oral surgery practices, and complex dental implant rehabilitation cases that restore form and function of teeth.

Dr. Mattox’s patient-first approach to healthcare was inspired by his father, who instilled in him the importance of prioritizing patient care. His mother’s compassion and support have fostered his creativity while challenging him to pursue knowledge, enhancing his dedication to providing exceptional care.

Dr. Mattox attributes his success to the unwavering support and encouragement of his family. He is deeply grateful to his father for instilling a patient-first approach to healthcare and to his mother for her compassion, support, and love.
