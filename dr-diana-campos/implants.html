About Implants

FAQ’s

What are dental implants? Dental implants are metal posts that anchor crowns, bridges, or dentures. They replace teeth that are missing or need extraction due to severe damage or decay. Mamkin Dentistry Aesthetic & Restorative Center offers an array of dental implants that match your desired outcome. The implants and the teeth they bond to look and feel like real teeth. Dental implants are strong and long-lasting. They can perfect the look of your smile and reduce the risk of complications. What are the different types of dental implants? Dental implants can replace just one missing permanent tooth, multiple missing teeth, or all your natural teeth. All-on-4® dental implants replace all of your top or bottom teeth using four dental implant posts and dentures.
