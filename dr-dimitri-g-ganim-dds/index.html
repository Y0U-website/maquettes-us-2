The practice

Dr. Ganim has been a member of the Rhode Island, Pawtucket, New England, and American Dental Associations, and the American College of Dentists and Pierre Fauchard Academy dental honor societies.

Our website was designed to inform and entertain you. I expect that this site will continually be a work in progress, adding new information about our office, the dental procedures you may be interested in, and dentistry in general.

From preventive care to dental implants, Dimitri G. Ganim, DDS, offers all the restorative dental services you need to maintain good oral health.

We have attempted to make our office even more comfortable and relaxing for you. From the fireplace in our reception room to the TV’s and murals that are in our treatment rooms, we make the extra effort to make your visit more pleasurable. Even more importanly, our warm and caring staff will all strive to make your visit exceed your expectations.

Our office utilizes intra-oral cameras to actually show you what we seen when examing your mouth. We also have a video patient education system to help inform you of any dental problems that you may have and also of your alternative treatment options. We feel that it is vitally important for you to have the information you need to make informed decisions concerning your care.

Please contact our office for an appointment to see how our dental services can meet your needs in creating a healthy and functional smile that you can be proud of.

During the routine visit, we will do an oral cancer screening, check for tooth decay and ensure overall health of the teeth and gums including dietetic and hygiene counseling. In addition, we attempt to detect orthodontic problems early enough so that it may require less time and money for treatment . For younger children, we will provide you with information regarding your child’s oral health…

Implant dentistry offers us the means of restoring function in ways that would not otherwise be possible. Implants can be used as anchors for both fixed replacements (replace teeth that are not removable) or for removable dentures. In addition to valuable anchorage, implants can allow us to make significant impreovements in both function and appearance of replacement teeth.

Les soins

Come to our dental office to restore your smile with our restorative dental care. We offer restorative an prosthetic procedures that include partial dentures, long-lasting crowns, bridges and veneers to retore the health and appearance of your teeth.

During the initial visit, Dr. Ganim will evaluate your dental needs and suggest the best treatment options for you. We will then try and help you schedule your visits as efficiently as possible.

Tooth whitening/bleaching is a treatment to improve the aesthetic appearance of your teeth. We offer several options for this procedure.
