Malinda Husson, DDS, MSD

I enjoy staying up to date on the most recent dental procedures, dental materials, and advances within dentistry, as well as anesthesia practices, to maintain safe, effective, and high quality care for my patients. It is important for me to offer unique ways of allowing children to conquer their fears and anxiety with dental appointments, including offering sedation and anesthesia options for children and patients with special healthcare needs, as well as providing options for minimally invasive dentistry for those children that may not require anesthesia care.

Outside the Office

I am originally from Kenova, West Virginia and recently moved back to Charleston, West Virginia after living in Virginia for 14 years. My husband David and I have three children, Alex, David Jr., and Farra. We are excited to be back in West Virginia with friends and family, and look forward to meeting new friends and developing new relationships in our home state.

The kids keep us very busy, but when we have time, we enjoy traveling throughout the US and exploring and enjoying this beautiful state we live in. We are members at St. George Antiochian Orthodox Church and enjoy spending time with our church family as well.

Since we are new to the area, I am currently looking into ways that I can become more active within the community, including dental outreach programs and community programs that would benefit Charleston families. Previously, I volunteered during Mission of Mercy and Give Kids a Smile events to provide dental care to those who need it the most.
