Our Purpose

Crafting Bright Smiles for a Lifetime of Health

Your Smile Matters

Summer Smile Dental was built on one belief — every patient deserves to feel heard and cared for. Drs. Shaila and Dona Torbati founded this practice to build lasting relationships with the South Gate community. From your first visit, we slow down, listen to your concerns, and never rush you toward treatment you don’t fully understand. We treat people, not just teeth — and that philosophy shapes everything we do, from how we greet you at the door to how we follow up after your procedure.

Quality Care Guaranteed

Quality at Summer Smile Dental shows up in the details. We use digital X-rays, guided implant placement technology, and same-day crown systems that eliminate second appointments. Every procedure follows strict sterilization protocols so you can trust the care you receive meets the highest clinical standards. We also back our work — if something doesn’t feel right after a procedure, our team is available to address it. That accountability is what separates a practice that values you from one that simply processes appointments.

Personalized Attention

No two patients are the same, and your treatment plan shouldn’t feel like it was written for someone else. We review your full history, understand your goals, and factor in your budget and schedule before recommending anything. For anxious patients, sedation options are available. For families, we coordinate appointments to reduce trips. For those who haven’t visited a dentist in years, we start without judgment. Open 7 days a week with extended hours, we fit around your life — not the other way around.
