How the Process Works

We capture a detailed 3D image of your jaw, bone structure, and surrounding anatomy.

Your surgeon maps out ideal implant placement using advanced software.

A precision guide is created to direct implant placement during surgery.

Implants are placed exactly as planned—down to the millimeter.
