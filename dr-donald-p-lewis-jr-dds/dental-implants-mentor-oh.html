What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are advanced tooth replacement solutions that restore missing teeth by replacing both the tooth root and visible crown. Surgically placed into the jawbone, dental implants function as a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or full-arch restorations designed to look, feel, and function more like natural teeth. Over time, the implant integrates with the surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration, helping preserve jawbone strength and facial structure after tooth loss.

At Ohio’s Center for Oral, Facial & Dental Implant Surgery, our board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeons use advanced 3D imaging, digital surgical planning, and hospital-based surgical expertise to provide precise, long-term implant solutions for patients throughout Mentor and Northeast Ohio. Whether replacing a single missing tooth or rebuilding a full smile, dental implant treatment is carefully customized to support lasting function, stability, comfort, and confidence.

Why Patients Choose Dental Implants

Dental implants are considered one of the most predictable and long-term solutions for tooth replacement because they restore both the visible tooth and the underlying root structure.

Key Benefits of Dental Implants Include:

Restored Strength & Chewing Function

Dental implants are anchored directly into the jawbone, providing the stability needed to eat a wider variety of foods with greater comfort and confidence. Unlike removable appliances, implants are designed to function more like natural teeth during everyday activities.

Preservation of Jawbone & Facial Structure

When a tooth is lost, the surrounding jawbone can gradually deteriorate over time. Dental implants help stimulate and preserve bone in the area, supporting long-term oral health while maintaining natural facial contours and structure.

Natural-Looking, Customized Results

Each implant restoration is designed to complement your smile, bite, and facial anatomy. The result is a replacement tooth or restoration that looks natural, feels secure, and blends seamlessly with surrounding teeth.

Long-Term Comfort & Stability

Because implants integrate with the jawbone, they eliminate many of the frustrations associated with removable dentures, including slipping, movement, and dependence on adhesives. Many patients find implants provide greater comfort, confidence, and day-to-day convenience.

Anesthesia Options

We offer a spectrum of anesthesia options to meet the needs of every patient — from minor procedures to complex surgeries.

Local Anesthesia

Local anesthesia is the foundation of all oral surgery procedures. * You remain **fully awake** * The surgical area is completely numbed * You will **not feel pain**, only slight pressure Best for: * Simple tooth extractions * Minor surgical procedures Local anesthesia is often combined with sedation to improve comfort.

Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas)

Nitrous oxide is a gentle, fast-acting form of sedation that helps you relax during treatment. * Delivered through a **small nasal mask** * You remain **awake and in control** * Effects begin quickly and wear off within minutes ### Why patients choose nitrous oxide: * Reduces anxiety and nervousness * No lingering drowsiness after your visit * Adjustable throughout your procedure * Safe and widely used in dentistry

Twilight Sedation (IV Sedation)

IV sedation, often called “twilight sleep,” is one of the most popular options for oral surgery. * Administered through a **small IV** * You enter a **deep state of relaxation** * Many patients **remember little or nothing** about the procedure You may drift in and out of sleep, but you remain comfortable and responsive. Ideal for: * Wisdom teeth removal * Dental implants * Patients with moderate to high anxiety IV sedation is continuously monitored and can be adjusted at any time for your safety.

General Anesthesia

General anesthesia allows you to be completely asleep during your procedure. * You are **fully unconscious** * You will have **no awareness of the procedure** * Vital signs are closely monitored throughout Recommended for: * Complex surgical procedures * Patients with significant anxiety * Cases requiring deeper levels of sedation Our surgeons are extensively trained and certified to safely administer anesthesia in a controlled, monitored environment.
