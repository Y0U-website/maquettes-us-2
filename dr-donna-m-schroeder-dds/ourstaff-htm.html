Improving Access to Quality Health Care for All

We meet our mission by providing patient-centered health care for the whole family - and the whole community.

Our experienced and friendly staff create a comfortable, safe environment for your medical, dental, and behavioral health care needs.

Our credentialed health care providers are skilled and compassionate.

We are committed to quality care regardless of ability to pay.

We will work with you to find an affordable option for the care you need.

Need Help Paying for Your Services?

If you do not have medical or dental insurance or if your insurance does not cover certain services, you may be eligible to enroll in our Sliding Fee Program.

Land Acknowledgment Statement

We acknowledge that our clinics are located on traditional, ancestral, and contemporary lands of Indigenous people who cared for and called these lands home for generations. Our clinics reside on Ojibwe and Dakota lands, and we recognize that the spirit of the Ojibwe and Dakota permeates these lands. The land we reside on has great spiritual and historical significance for Indigenous peoples past and present. We strive to build tribal relations and support tribal sovereignty in our community.
