The practice

Our goal is to help you achieve the highest level of oral health. We want you to keep your teeth for a lifetime.

We tailor your treatment to meet your personal needs and desires, ensuring that when you leave our office, you will require as little additional dentistry as possible.

I believe that every person is different and special. It important to me that each patient is ready for treatment before we begin.

Dr. Toy and her team provide personalized comfort so you can feel your best during your checkups and confident in your oral hygiene when at home.

We welcome children and encourage preventive dentistry, like sealants and sport guards. Along with our comprehensive gum therapy to ensure that you retain your teeth for a lifetime, we offer a variety of dental services depending on your personal needs.

Yes, we are currently welcoming new patients! If you are a new patient, please print and fill out the Patient Forms and bring them in on your first visit.

We are open Monday through Thursday from 8am–1pm and 2pm–5pm ; Fridays are by appointment only and we are closed on weekends.
