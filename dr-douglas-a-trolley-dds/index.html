The practice

At Trolley Dental Care, we utilize state-of-the-art technology and dental techniques to create beautiful smiles for patients of all ages. By providing innovative care with a compassionate and minimally invasive approach, we help each of our patients realize their best smile.

Located along the Erie Canal in Pittsford, NY, just 15 minutes from downtown Rochester, Trolley Dental Care has been providing quality general and family dentistry to Western New York for almost 100 years.

Our goal is to deliver excellence in dentistry by offering gentle and attentive care so you can stay on course to good oral and overall health. Learn more about our commitment to our patients, as well as the community.

Attain the smile you’ve always wanted with Trolley Dental Care. From in-office whitening to minor tooth movement, a beautiful, healthy smile is always within reach.

Healthy smiles come in all shapes and sizes. As an Alzheimer’s Friendly Business℠ and ADA-compliant practice, we serve people of all ages and abilities.

Les soins

Maintain your oral health with comprehensive, compassionate preventive care. With routine cleanings and general exams, we keep your mouth healthy and your smile happy.

We believe in the importance of identifying and treating gum disease in its earliest stages. Using gentle, minimally invasive techniques, we can get your gums back on track.
