East Hudson Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

  • Dental Implants Dental implants are artificial roots and teeth (usually titanium) that are surgically placed into the upper or lower jawbone. They are very strong, stable, and durable and will last many years, but on occasion, they will have to be re-tightened or replaced due to normal wear. The teeth attached to implants are very natural looking and often enhance or restore a patient’s smile! Dental implants may be for you if: You need to replace one or more missing teeth.
  • You want to resolve joint pain or bite problems caused by teeth shifting into a missing tooth space.
  • You want to feel confident in your smile.
  • You need help to restore chewing, speech, or digestion.
  • You would like to restore or enhance facial tissues.
  • You need support a bridge or denture to make it more secure and comfortable.

Single Tooth Replacement

When both the tooth and root are damaged, the best permanent replacement is a dental implant in conjunction with a ceramic crown. This solution both looks and functions just like a natural tooth. This means that all components are installed as a single unit, resulting in immediately full functioning teeth, shorter treatment time and minimized pain.

You should expect to be able to work the day after having the implant installed.

Multiple Teeth Replacement

Full Arch

When all teeth are missing or in such condition that they need to be replaced, a fixed bridge anchored to dental implants is the best permanent solution. Before dental implants, there were no fixed solution available for people who lost all their teeth. Today, it is possible to replace a full jaw with dental implants and a fixed bridge that results in a permanent, stable, and high-aesthetic solution.

  • Lets you eat and function like having natural teeth
  • A solid, stable solution that will serve you for life
  • Preserves your facial appearance and prevents bone loss

"All-on-4"

“All-on-4” is a cost-effective treatment solution based on four implants secured in the jawbone to obtain optimal positioning and support of a complete denture. This innovative solution by Nobel Biocare provides the patients with a fixed bridge (denture) in a couple of hours in just one visit. The All-on-4 clinical solution has been developed to maximize the use of available bone and to allow for Immediate Function™:

  • Shortened treatment time
  • Better clinical efficiency
  • Go from tooth loss to having functional, aesthetic teeth in one treatment session
  • Beautiful, natural-looking teeth, from the very moment they are placed
