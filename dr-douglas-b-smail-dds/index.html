Dental implants are artificial roots and teeth (usually titanium) that are surgically placed into the upper or lower jawbone. They are very strong, stable, and durable and will last many years, but on occasion, they will have to be re-tightened or replaced due to normal wear. The teeth attached to implants are very natural looking and often enhance or restore a patient’s smile! Dental implants…

When both the tooth and root are damaged, the best permanent replacement is a dental implant in conjunction with a ceramic crown. This solution both looks and functions just like a natural tooth. This means that all components are installed as a single unit, resulting in immediately full functioning teeth, shorter treatment time and minimized pain.
