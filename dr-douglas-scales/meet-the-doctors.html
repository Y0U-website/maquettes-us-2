Meet The Doctors

Dr. Scales is a resident of Eagle Harbor and member of Sacred Heart Catholic Church in Fleming Island. He is a third generation dentist, and honors his late father, Kenneth Scales. Dr. Scales and his wife Jeanine have a daughter, Kathleen who is currently in college.

Dr. Scales attended Franciscan University of Steubenville in Ohio for undergraduate studies. After four years he graduated with a Bachelors of Arts Degree. He then pursued a Dental degree from the Medical University of South Carolina Dental College in Charleston.

Upon graduation from dental school in 1990, Dr. Scales returned to Orange Park and began practicing dentistry with his father in Jacksonville. For the last 20 years he has had a private practice here in Fleming Island. Dr. Scales is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Northeast Dental Association, Clay County Dental Society, and Jacksonville Dental Society.

Dr. Scales has been a member of the Clay County Chamber since 1997. In 2000, he was the first President of the Clay County Chamber’s Southeast Council representing the areas of Fleming Island, Green Cove Springs, and Middleburg.

Dr. Victor Erminy, DDS

Dr. Victor Erminy was born in Caracas, Venezuela, and attended the School of Dentistry at the University Santa Maria where he received his doctorate degree. He completed his residency program at MetroHealth Medical Center–Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland, Ohio. During this time, he underwent extensive training in implant dentistry, oral surgery, and prosthodontics. He has also completed an implant fellowship program at New York University.

Dr. Erminy worked as an attending faculty in the hospital-based residency program at MetroHealth Medical Center in Cleveland for five years before moving to Florida. He is passionate about restorative dentistry and surgical implants, both areas of which he has extensive training. He also takes pride in providing patients with outstanding dental care and comfort.

Dr. Erminy lives in Jacksonville with his wife and two daughters. In his spare time, he enjoys running, playing soccer, golfing and being with his family.
