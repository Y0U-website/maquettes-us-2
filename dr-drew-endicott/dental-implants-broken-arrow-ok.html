Your Implant Options

We match the solution to your smile, lifestyle, and budget.

  • Single-Tooth Implant — Replace one missing tooth without touching healthy neighbors.
  • Implant-Supported Bridge — Close a 2–3 tooth gap with fewer implants.
  • Snap-In (Overdenture) — 2–4 implants stabilize a removable denture (great value and comfort).
  • Fixed Full-Arch (“All-on-X”) — A non-removable set of teeth on 4–6 implants for maximum stability.
  • Stability: From improved (snap-in) to rock-solid (fixed full-arch) chewing confidence
  • Maintenance: Removable options clean outside the mouth; fixed options brush/floss like natural teeth (with specific tools)
  • Timeline: Single implants often finish in a few months; full-arch can be same-day provisional with final teeth after healing
  • Best Fit & Budget: We’ll walk you through pros, cons, and costs so you choose what fits your life

Our Implant Process

From first consult to final tooth — clear, predictable, and organized.

4. Healing & Protection

The implant bonds with bone (osseointegration). We use temporary solutions so you can smile and function meanwhile.

2. Plan & Preview

5. Final Restoration
