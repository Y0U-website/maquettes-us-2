Common Questions About Membership Plans

Endicott Dental is here to answer all of your questions!

What plan should I sign up for?

If you’re unsure which plan is best for you, simply visit with one of our team members. If you are new to our practice, Dr. Endicott will let you know if he recommends a specific plan for you at your first exam .

What if I forget to use my benefits?

Keeping up with regular appointments is the sole responsibility of the patient. We’re sorry, but we do not give refunds for unscheduled or missed appointments.

What payment forms are accepted?

Credit and debit cards are accepted, including MasterCard, Visa, Discover, and American Express. We will need to keep a current card on file for those opting for the month-to-month membership option.

When will my plan start?

You can begin using your plan the day you sign up! Your plan is good for one year of service from the date you sign up. Renewal rates may be subject to change.

Can I use my membership with my Insurance?

This plan is for our patients who do not currently have dental insurance. Unfortunately, we cannot apply any other discounts along with this plan.
