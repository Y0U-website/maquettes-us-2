The dentist behind the dentistry

When I graduated from Boston University and began practicing, I never imagined I would spend more than two decades serving the same community. But year after year, I’ve been drawn to the relationships dentistry makes possible — watching children grow into adults, parents become grandparents, and families place their trust in me generation after generation.

What I enjoy most is helping people feel comfortable, understood, and confident in their decisions — whether they’re seeking relief from pain, rebuilding a worn smile, or simply looking for a dentist they can trust. My goal is always the same: listen carefully, explain honestly, and provide the kind of care I’d feel comfortable recommending to my own family.

Outside the office, I enjoy landscape photography, time with my three children, and volunteering through charitable and mission-based dental programs — caring for people who might otherwise go without. After more than twenty years in Tamarac, I remain grateful for the trust this community has placed in me and my team.
