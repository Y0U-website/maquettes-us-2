The right tool depends on the case .

Every orthodontic case at our office is personally planned. Some patients are good candidates for either method others have a clear preference, and many fall somewhere between.

Clear Aligners

Removable, nearly invisible trays that gradually move teeth into better positions. Worn most of the day, removed for eating and brushing, and swapped on a regular schedule.

  • Patients who prefer a discreet appearance
  • Mild to moderate crowding or spacing
  • Adults balancing professional life
  • Patients who can commit to consistent wear

Traditional Braces

Brackets bonded to each tooth, guided by an archwire adjusted at each visit. One of the most versatile options in orthodontics particularly when precise movement or more significant bite correction is needed.

  • Significant crowding or rotation
  • More significant bite correction (over/under/crossbite)
  • Cases requiring precise tooth-by-tooth control
  • Patients who’d rather not manage removable trays

Two specialized approaches.

Limited Orthodontics

Sometimes one small correction is enough. Limited orthodontics targets specific teeth or a single issue, rather than full-mouth alignment, when that’s the right answer for your case.

Pre-Restorative Orthodontics

Before crowns, bridges, or implants, moving teeth into their proper position can make restorative work more conservative, more durable, and more predictable. Often planned as one step in a larger treatment.

What happens, step by step .

Three phases. Real timing. Here’s what to expect from the first visit through long-term retention.

Your first visit.

We take photographs, digital scans, and a bite analysis. Dr. Dulay reviews everything personally and gives you a clear recommendation aligners, braces, a limited approach, or whether ortho is even the right answer for your case. No pressure to commit on the day. We’d rather you understand your options.

Teeth moving into place.

Typically 18 to 24 months. Limited cases 6 to 12.

Once treatment begins, you’ll come in roughly every 4 to 8 weeks. Each visit takes 15 to 30 minutes for adjustments or tray exchanges. Some soreness or pressure is normal for a day or two after each visit it fades. You’ll be eating normally throughout, with a few common-sense exceptions for braces patients.

Holding your results.

After active treatment, custom retainers maintain your results. Teeth naturally want to drift back toward where they came from retention is what holds them in their new position. Most patients wear retainers full-time for the first few months, then nightly long-term.
