How Long Does Clear Aligner Treatment Take?

Every aligner treatment is unique, and the timeline depends on your specific smile goals and the complexity of your case. On average, most patients complete Invisalign or clear aligner treatment within 12 to 18 months , though some may achieve their desired results in as little as six months. During your consultation, Dr. Duong Ly will provide a personalized estimate and guide you through each stage of your treatment plan.

Following these steps helps ensure your clear aligner treatment stays on track and delivers the beautiful, healthy smile you’ve been waiting for.

  • How Do I Care for My Clear Aligners? Proper care and consistency are key to achieving your best smile: Wear your aligners 22 hours a day. Consistent use is essential for effective tooth movement.
  • Change aligners weekly or biweekly as directed in your personalized treatment plan.
  • Remove aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water to prevent staining or damage.
  • Brush and floss before reinserting your aligners to maintain excellent oral hygiene and prevent decay.
  • Attend regular checkups every 4–6 weeks so Dr. Duong Ly can monitor your progress and provide your next set of trays.

Many patients start to see noticeable improvements within the first few weeks of treatment, with most achieving their final results in 12 to 18 months , depending on their individual needs.
