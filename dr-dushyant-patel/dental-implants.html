Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

Which type of dental implant is right for me?

This depends on how many teeth you are missing and your specific oral health needs, ranging from a single tooth implant to a full-arch solution like All-On-4. Our team will recommend the right option during your consultation based on a thorough evaluation of your teeth and jawbone.

Can I get dental implants if I have been told I do not have enough bone?

Yes, in many cases. Techniques like angled implant placement often allow us to work with your existing bone structure, reducing or eliminating the need for bone grafting that other providers might require.

How long does dental implant treatment take?

Timelines vary significantly by treatment type, from a few months for a single tooth implant to about six months for a full-arch solution like All-On-4 or snap-in dentures. Same-day options like Teeth-In-A-Day allow you to leave with a functional smile immediately, with your final restoration completed after healing.

Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Coverage varies widely by plan, with some providers covering a portion of the cost and others offering little to no coverage. Our team can verify your specific benefits and walk you through financing options to help cover any remaining cost.

With proper care, the implant post itself can last a lifetime, since it fuses directly with the jawbone. The visible restoration, such as a crown or bridge, may need replacement after 10 to 15 years due to normal wear.

What if I am missing all of my teeth in one or both arches?

For patients missing most or all of their teeth, options like snap-in dentures or All-On-4 Teeth-In-A-Day provide a fixed or securely attached full-arch solution. Our team will evaluate your specific situation and recommend the option that best fits your goals and jawbone health.
