What Our Patients’ Say

FAQs Why would someone need bone grafting before dental implants? Bone grafting is often recommended when there is not enough healthy jawbone to support a dental implant. Bone loss can happen after tooth loss, gum disease, or injury. Grafting helps rebuild the bone so an implant can be placed more securely.

What does the bone grafting procedure involve?

Bone grafting involves placing graft material in areas where bone has weakened or thinned. Over time, the body uses this material as a foundation to grow new bone, creating stronger support for future dental treatment.

What causes bone loss in the jaw?

Jawbone loss can occur when a tooth has been missing for a long time, after an extraction, or due to infection or trauma. Without stimulation from a natural tooth or implant, the bone can gradually shrink over time.

Will bone grafting help improve long-term implant success?

Bone grafting helps create a stable foundation for dental implants. By strengthening the jawbone, it supports proper implant placement and helps improve long-term stability once the implant is restored.

How do I care for my mouth after bone grafting?

After bone grafting, patients are given specific care instructions to protect the surgical area while it heals. Following these guidelines is important to support proper bone development and reduce the risk of complications.

How do I know if bone grafting is right for me?

The need for bone grafting is determined through an exam and imaging. During a consultation, the surgeon evaluates bone structure and discusses whether grafting is necessary to support dental implants or other treatments.
