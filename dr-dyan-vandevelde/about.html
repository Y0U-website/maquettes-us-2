Get to Know Dr. Jason Gong

I come from a family of physicians, but it was the hands-on aspect of dentistry that truly captured my interest. It’s the perfect blend of engineering, design, and patient care-- all things I&#x27;m passionate about. After receiving my undergraduate and dental education at UCLA, I completed a rigorous residency at a Level 1 Trauma Center, where I gained invaluable experience with dental emergencies and complex cases.

Today, I’m especially focused on modern restorative techniques, like same-day crowns using advanced milling technology and cosmetic bondings that allow me to rejuvenate smiles without ever putting a drill to the natural tooth! I’ve also brought dental implant placement to the practice, allowing patients to receive all the care they need right here in Lunada Bay.

After spending a decade in West LA, I met my wife-- a lifelong South Bay resident. Once I experienced the quieter, more relaxed pace of life here, it didn’t take long for us to realize this was where we wanted to plant our roots and build a life together. When I’m not talking teeth, you can find me spending time with my wife Michelle and our little boy Eli!

It’s incredibly inspiring to see the trust Dr. Van De Velde has built with her patients here at Lunada Bay Dental. I’m honored and excited to join the team and continue that tradition of excellence, compassion, and community.

Get to Know Dr. Van de Velde

As a child, I hated going to the dentist! You could say I became a dentist as a form of self-defense—but in truth, I chose this profession because I fell in love with its unique blend of science, art, and human connection. There’s something truly special about helping someone regain their function and confidence through a healthy, radiant smile.

I completed my undergraduate studies at UCLA and earned my dental degree from Tufts University. I bring over 40 years of experience to my work and have had the privilege of leading Lunada Bay Dental for the past 15 years. In that time, I’ve built a practice grounded in clinical excellence and compassionate care.

I embrace the latest technologies, including digital intraoral scanners, same-day crown milling, and innovative water laser therapy, to ensure precise, comfortable, and effective treatments. I strive to make every visit gentle, personal, and empowering—because I know from experience how intimidating dentistry can feel.

Outside the office, I love adventure and the outdoors. I spend my free time hiking, scuba diving, skiing, and flying—yes, I’m also a private pilot! Whether in the air or in the operatory, I’m always seeking new ways to explore, grow, and bring joy into people’s lives.

Advanced Technology for You

At Lunada Bay Dental, we harness the power of advanced technology to provide precise, efficient, and comfortable treatments. Our modern tools not only enhance diagnostic accuracy but also ensure that every procedure is performed with the utmost care and speed.

Intraoral Scanners

Our intraoral scanners create detailed digital impressions, offering precise measurements that improve the accuracy of your treatment plan while enhancing your comfort.

CEREC Same-Day Crowns

With advanced CEREC PrimeScan and PrimeMill technology, we offer same-day crowns that are precisely designed and custom-milled to match your natural teeth.

3D CBCT Scanner

Our 3D CBCT scanner delivers comprehensive three‑dimensional imaging, allowing for accurate diagnoses and treatment planning that lead to better outcomes.

Pearl AI

Leveraging advanced machine learning, Pearl AI assists in analyzing dental radiographs for improved diagnostic precision and personalized treatment planning.

Waterlase All‑Tissue Laser

The Waterlase All‑Tissue Laser offers minimally invasive soft tissue treatments, reducing discomfort and accelerating healing while delivering precise, effective results.
