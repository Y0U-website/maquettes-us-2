FAQs

What services are included under general dentistry?

General dentistry at McMillen Dental includes routine cleanings, exams, X-rays, sealants, periodontal evaluations, and other preventive treatments aimed at maintaining long-term oral health. These visits help detect early issues in the teeth and gums, allowing the dentist to recommend personalized care when needed. Most new patients begin with a comprehensive exam that includes charting, periodontal probing, and a full review of their dental history.

Do you provide dental care for children, teens, adults, and seniors?

Yes, McMillen Dental treats patients of all ages, offering age-appropriate care for toddlers, teens, adults, and seniors. The team focuses on gentle, compassionate dentistry that meets the unique needs of each age group, from early childhood cleanings to senior oral-health management. This family-friendly approach makes it easy for households to receive consistent care in the same office.

Do you accept Medicaid or Molina Healthcare at McMillen Dental?

McMillen Dental does not accept Medicaid or Molina, including any Medicaid-managed plans. Instead, the practice participates with many major PPO insurance providers and offers clear communication upfront to help patients understand their coverage. Calling ahead allows the team to verify benefits and ensure there are no surprises before treatment.

Do you offer financing or payment plans for dental treatment?

Yes. The office provides several payment options for patients needing flexibility, including financing programs for larger treatments and self-pay arrangements for those without insurance. These options help make essential dental care more accessible by allowing patients to spread costs over time. The team can walk you through available choices and help determine what works best for your budget.

What restorative dentistry services do you offer?

Restorative services at McMillen Dental include crowns, bridges, implants, dentures, partials, and root canal therapy. These treatments are designed to restore comfort, function, and appearance when teeth are damaged or missing. After an evaluation, the dentist will recommend the most effective treatment plan and explain expected results.

Do you offer emergency dental care?

Yes. McMillen Dental provides prompt emergency appointments for problems like toothaches, injuries, infections, or broken teeth. Calling the office as soon as an issue arises allows the team to help you determine next steps and, when possible, schedule a same-day visit to relieve pain and prevent further complications.

How often should I schedule dental cleanings?

Most patients benefit from cleanings every six months, although some may need more frequent visits depending on their gum health and risk factors. Routine cleanings help remove plaque, monitor early signs of gum disease, and keep your smile healthy long-term. Your dentist or hygienist will recommend a personalized schedule based on your needs.
