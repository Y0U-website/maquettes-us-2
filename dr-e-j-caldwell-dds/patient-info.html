McMillen Dental’s Dental Health Saving Plan

The McMillen Dental team realizes that dental insurance, which provides financial benefits to many, is not available to everyone. Our dental health savings plan was created for those in our community who do not have access to dental insurance.

The McMillen Dental Health Savings Plan has no claims to file, no deductibles, no copays, no waiting periods, and no annual spending caps.

Our Dental Health Savings Plan Includes:

  • Two Hygiene Cleanings
  • Two Doctor Examinations
  • Oral Hygiene Instruction
  • Periodontal Evaluation
  • Oral Cancer Screening (Visual)
  • Check-Up X-Rays (As necessary)
  • Fluoride Treatment
  • Total Value: $587
  • 20 % Discount off Restorative Dental Treatment

Family Dental Health Savings Plan Pricing

  • A single person invests $299 one-time per year to participate in the plan.
  • A couple of two adults would pay $274/ per person each year to participate in the plan.
  • For families of four (2 adults & 2 children under the age of 14), the annual cost per person is $236.50.
