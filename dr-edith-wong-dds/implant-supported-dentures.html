The Process of Getting Implant-Supported Dentures

Your journey towards implant-supported dentures begins with an initial consultation at our Lacey office. During this visit, our team will evaluate your oral health and discuss your specific needs. The process involves several key steps:

1. Consultation and Planning: A comprehensive examination and imaging to create a personalized treatment plan.

2. Surgical Placement: Dental implants are surgically positioned into the jawbone by our experienced [dr_type].

3. Healing Period: A few months are required for osseointegration, where the bone fuses with the implants.

4. Denture Fitting: Once healing is complete, custom dentures are attached to the implants.

Your Experience: What to Expect

The entire process from consultation to final fitting can take several months; however, patients often find the results well worth the wait. During recovery, you might experience minor discomfort which can be managed with prescribed medications.
