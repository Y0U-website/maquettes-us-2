Regular dental checkups allow us to spot signs of nutritional deficiencies, developmental concerns, or improper bite alignment. Your oral health is closely linked to your overall well-being, so it’s essential to inform your dentist of any changes in your health, even if they seem unrelated.

Our experienced team will assess your oral health during each visit, offering tailored treatment options to address any concerns. Prevention is always better than cure, so by addressing small issues early on, we help you avoid more extensive treatments down the road.

Lapeer Family Dental has been serving the Lapeer community with compassion and expertise for years. Our team is dedicated to helping you achieve optimal oral health through routine exams, cleanings, and specialized treatments. Here’s what you can expect during your regular appointments:

Our goal is to establish your current oral health status and work with you to maintain and improve it over time. We’ll ensure that you’re fully informed of your treatment options and feel empowered to make decisions about your care.

For most people, we recommend dental visits every six months. However, certain situations may require more frequent checkups. For example, if you are managing conditions such as gum disease, pregnancy-related gingivitis, cancer, or diabetes, more regular visits can help mitigate additional risks.

During your cleaning, our hygienist will remove plaque and tartar buildup, polish your teeth, and assess any concerns you may have. Our dentist will then evaluate your X-rays, check for cavities, assess your gums, and screen for oral cancer.
