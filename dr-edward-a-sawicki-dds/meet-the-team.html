Meet Our Dental Team!

We are a dental practice devoted to restoring and enhancing the natural beauty of your smile using conservative, state-of-the-art procedures that will result in beautiful, long-lasting smiles!

A standard of excellence in personalized dental care enables us to provide the quality dental services our patients deserve. We provide comprehensive treatment planning and use restorative and cosmetic dentistry to achieve optimal dental health. Should a dental emergency occur, we make every effort to see and care for you as soon as possible.

Building trust by treating our patients as special individuals is vital to our success. We understand how uneasy some patients may feel about their dental visits and how we can make a difference in providing a relaxing and positive experience. Our entire team is dedicated to providing you with excellent, personalized care and service to make your visits as comfortable and pleasant as possible.

Thank you for allowing us to take care of your and your family’s dental needs and look forward to serving you.

Dr. J. Michael Owen, DDS

Dr. Michael Owen graduated from the University of Detroit School of Dentistry in 1985. He has been involved in esthetic and implant dentistry since that time. Dr. Owen did an implant mini-residency through the American Academy of Implant Dentistry.

Dr. Owen has been involved nationally with the Academy of General Dentistry for over 15 years and has been an award-winning Editor and President of the Michigan AGD. He currently serves on the Board of Trustees of the Michigan AGD and has achieved his Fellowship in that organization. He is also a Fellow of the prestigious Pierre Fauchard Society.

Dr. Owen is very involved in youth sports programs where he serves as a coach in basketball and soccer.

Dr. Robert P. O’Bryan, DDS

Robert Patrick O’Bryan, DDS, has been creating beautiful smiles and practicing the art of cosmetic and restorative dentistry in the Lapeer County area since 1975. His mission has always been to provide "innovative dentistry with a caring touch." He is dedicated to his patients’ ultimate comfort and care and to their ultimate smile.

Dr. O’Bryan recently introduced an innovative, new technology he calls PerfectSmile. In fact, PerfectSmile is such an advancement in Dental Care that Dr. O’Bryan has trademarked the name and applied for a patent on his process. According to Dr. O’Bryan, every element of the PerfectSmile system is designed to create smiles that look and feel healthy, beautiful, and natural.

Dr. Edward Sawicki, DDS

An associate doctor at Lapeer Family Dental since 1990, Dr. Edward Sawicki received both his Bachelor’s degree and Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Michigan - Ann Arbor. Dr. Sawicki has been involved in esthetic, endodontic, and implant dentistry.

Dr. Kyle Grindling, DDS

Dr. Kyle Grindling received his Bachelor of Science degree from Michigan State University. He was awarded a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry in 2009.

He has been practicing the art of restorative and removable prosthetic dentistry as a committed and caring provider in Chicago and recently moved back to Michigan. Dr. Grindling brings energy and enthusiasm to our practice and looks to build relationships with patients to create an enjoyable, professional environment and provide excellent quality care to every patient.

Dr. Grindling and his wife currently reside in Royal Oak, Michigan.
