OUR SERVICES

Here at Lakeview Dental Center we offer a variety of services. For more information, please read the descriptions.

Services provided are not limited to what is shown on this page.

Please visit our social media pages to view our treatment photos!

EXAMINATIONS

Close inspection of the teeth and tissues of the mouth using physical assessment, radiographs, and other diagnostic aids. Dental care begins with this assessment, and is followed by diagnosis and ultimately a treatment plan.

X-RAYS

Imaging for accurate diagnoses and treatment planning.

GENERAL AND DEEP CLEANINGS

Most of the times, a checkup is followed by a general cleaning to halt the initiation or progression of gum disease.

In situations where disease is already present, our doctor might recommend a deep cleaning, also called scaling and root planning.

FILLING

Restoration of the function, integrity, and morphology of missing tooth structure resulting from caries or external trauma. Click here for more information.

TOOTH EXTRACTION

Removal of hopeless or badly decayed teeth.

CROWN

Full coverage restoration that restores a tooth to its normal size, shape, and function.

IMPLANT RESTORATION

DENTAL PROSTHETICS - DENTURE APPLIANCES

Removable appliances to replace missing teeth.

ORTHODONTICS

The treatment of misaligned teeth and malocclusion with the use of braces.

ROOT CANAL

Treatment to save and repair a badly decayed or infected tooth.

WHITENING TRAYS

Fabricated custom whitening trays for at home use. They are meant for continuous use as needed.

VENEERS

An ultra-thin shell of tooth-colored porcelain or composite that is cemented over the facial surfaces of your teeth to improve the appearance.
