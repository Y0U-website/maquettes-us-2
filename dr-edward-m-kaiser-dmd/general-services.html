Pediatric Dentistry

Catering to the dental needs of children requires a special touch and a specific skill set. Kaiser Dental are experts in Pediatric dentistry and are specially trained to treat the oral health needs of children, from infancy through their teenage years. This involves in-depth knowledge about children’s behavior, as well as their growth and development. Our office is also designed to make kids feel comfortable and relaxed – with plenty of toys, activities and a warm, inviting and fun décor.

Nitrous Sedation Dentistry

Ever wish you could sail through dental visits without anxiety or fear? Would you rather endure an agonizing toothache than go to the dentist? Answering “yes” to these questions could mean that you’re a perfect candidate for sedation dentistry. With nitrous oral dentistry, you can forget about fear and focus on pure relaxation. Nitrous Oxide has been used for many years to help relieve patients from anxiety and discomfort.
