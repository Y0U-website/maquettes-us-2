The practice

At Kaiser Dental, Dr. Edward Kaiser and our dedicated team combine expertise and compassion to create a comfortable, welcoming environment for every patient. We believe that personalized care, open communication, and the latest in dental technology lead to healthier smiles—and happier patients.

Dr. Edward Kaiser and Dr. Bernard Dolobach have proudly served the Uniontown community for years, combining compassionate care with advanced techniques to help you achieve lasting oral health.

Our dedicated team is committed to making every visit positive and stress-free, ensuring you feel at home from the moment you arrive.

We understand that dental issues can occur unexpectedly. Whenever possible, we offer same-day emergency visits to address urgent concerns.

We know life can be busy. That’s why we provide scheduling options designed to accommodate your needs and keep your treatment on track.

We work with numerous insurance carriers and offer a variety of payment plans. You’ll never face hidden costs or surprises—just honest pricing.

From digital x-rays to advanced cosmetic treatments, our state-of-the-art equipment helps us deliver more precise, comfortable care for every patient.

From preventative check-ups to transformative cosmetic treatments, Kaiser Dental offers a full spectrum of services tailored to your unique needs. Our goal is simple: to help you achieve and maintain a healthy, confident smile.
