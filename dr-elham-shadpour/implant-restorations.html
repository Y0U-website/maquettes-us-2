Who Can Have Dental Implants?

Most adults with good overall health and sufficient jawbone density can be candidates for dental implants. However, patients with conditions like periodontal disease or insufficient bone structure may require additional treatments such as bone grafting or periodontal therapy. If you’re considering Dental Implants in Glendale, Queens, NY, our experts can evaluate your oral health and determine the best treatment plan for you.

The Implant Treatment Process

  • Initial Consultation: Comprehensive dental examination and treatment planning.
  • Implant Placement: Surgical insertion of the titanium posts into the jawbone.
  • Healing Period: Allowing the implants to integrate with the bone, typically taking a few months.
  • Abutment Placement: Attaching a connector to the implant.
  • Restoration Placement: Custom-made crowns, bridges, or dentures are secured to the abutments.
