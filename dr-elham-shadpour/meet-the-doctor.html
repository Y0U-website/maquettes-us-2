Meet The Doctor

Skill and compassion intertwine seamlessly at Gen Z Dentistry, located in the vibrant community of Glendale, Queens, NY. Dr. Elham Shadpour, the driving force behind this practice, embodies these principles, ensuring that each patient receives exceptional care tailored to their unique needs. With a commitment to staying abreast of the latest advancements in dentistry, Dr. Shadpour leads her team in providing cutting-edge treatment options to their valued patients. At Gen Z Dentistry, patients aren’t just numbers; they are individuals deserving of respect, trust, and personalized attention.

Educational Background

Dr. Elham Shadpour’s journey to becoming a trusted dentist began at the renowned New York University College of Dentistry. Following her education, she pursued a residency at Lutheran Medical Center in Brooklyn and Queens, where she dedicated herself to serving underserved communities. This experience instilled in her a deep sense of compassion and empathy, shaping her approach to patient care.

Years of Experience

With over a decade of experience in dentistry, Dr. Shadpour brings a wealth of knowledge and expertise to Gen Z Dentistry. She has remained dedicated to providing caring and personalized dental services to her patients throughout her career. Her commitment to excellence and patient satisfaction is evident in every interaction.

Philosophy of Care

Dr. Shadpour’s philosophy of care centers on providing top-notch dental services in a friendly, down-to-earth manner. At Gen Z Dentistry, patients are treated with respect, compassion, and empathy. Dr. Shadpour takes the time to listen to her patients’ concerns and goals, developing personalized treatment plans to meet their individual needs.

Commitment to Excellence

At Gen Z Dentistry, excellence is non-negotiable. Dr. Shadpour and her team spare no effort in ensuring that each patient receives the highest standard of care. From state-of-the-art technology to the latest treatment techniques, every aspect of the practice is designed to enhance the patient experience and deliver exceptional results.

Community Engagement

Beyond the confines of the practice, Dr. Shadpour is actively involved in giving back to her community. She volunteers her time and expertise to provide dental care to underserved populations, believing that access to quality dental services is a fundamental right. Her dedication to service underscores her commitment to positively impacting the world around her.

Patient Testimonials

In conclusion, Dr. Elham Shadpour at Gen Z Dentistry is dedicated to providing exceptional dental care in Glendale, Queens, NY. With a focus on skill, compassion, and personalized service, Dr. Shadpour and her team are committed to helping their patients achieve optimal oral health and a beautiful smile. Whether you’re due for a check-up or in need of more extensive dental work, you can trust Gen Z Dentistry to provide you with the highest standard of care in a warm and welcoming environment.
