Encore Dental – A Smilist Dental Company of Shrewsbury, NJ now offers IV Sedation (Intravenous Sedation) for patients who undergo oral surgery. Medications are administered through the vein before the procedure. When this happens the patient experiences “twilight anesthesia”, as it is a depressed level of consciousness, with the ability to respond to verbal commands.

Most patients will not remember anything about the procedure that was performed under this type of anesthesia. Supplemental oxygen is delivered through the nose, and the patient’s vital signs are closely monitored during and after the procedure. Many patients find IV Sedation helpful during wisdom teeth extraction procedures.
