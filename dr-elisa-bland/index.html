If you have felt insecure about your smile, cosmetic dentistry might be a great solution for you. Encore Dental – A Smilist Dental Company of Shrewsbury, NJ dental office provides a variety of cosmetic dentistry treatments to help you get the smile that you have always dreamed of.

Cosmetic dentistry includes teeth-whitening to help remove any stains that might have built up from coffee or food. Composite or porcelain veneers are also offered to help alter the shape and color of your teeth. Veneers are thin restorations that are customized to fit your teeth and then placed over the top of them, giving your teeth a complete makeover.

Encore Dental – A Smilist Dental Company of Shrewsbury, NJ now offers IV Sedation (Intravenous Sedation) for patients who undergo oral surgery. Medications are administered through the vein before the procedure. When this happens the patient experiences “twilight anesthesia”, as it is a depressed level of consciousness, with the ability to respond to verbal commands.
