About Van Heest Family Dentistry

0 Who We Are Dr. van Heest is a fourth generation dentist and a third generation Pitt graduate (Class of ‘89). She stays informed of new techniques through continuing Education and consultation with specialists. She enjoys caring for all age groups and has practiced in the Cranberry area since 1981. Dr. van Heest lives in Franklin Park with her husband and two boys. She enjoys her time with her two westies and volunteering at Orchard View Stables.

We are a well educated group that is motivated to make your care, comfort and understanding of dental needs our primary goal. You will always be greeted by a friendly face. Our office manager is well versed in answering insurance questions and dealing with your concerns. Our hygiene and dental assistants are constantly seeking educational opportunities to stay on the cutting edge of dentistry to better serve you.

During your first visit, you can expect a comprehensive and personalized dental experience. Our experienced dentist will conduct a complete diagnostic exam, review your medical history and past dental experiences, perform oral cancer screenings, and recommend a cleaning approach that best meets your individual health needs. We prioritize your comfort and aim to provide you with exceptional care, ensuring you feel confident and well-informed throughout your visit.
