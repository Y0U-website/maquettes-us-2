Anxiety/Sedation

Patients with dental anxiety can feel more relaxed with oral sedation or nitrous oxide sedation during treatment.

Invisible Braces

Krystal Klear Aligners is the clear and simple alternative to braces. It’s a series of clear, custom-made, removable aligners that gradually straighten your teeth as you wear them.

Laser Dentistry

Dr. Billiot is one of only a few dentists in the region to use the Solea dental laser. The laser vaporizes target tissue using light whether it is tooth, bone or gum. This eliminates the typical noise, numbness and pain out of your dental visit. Learn More

Teeth Whitening

Custom-made bleaching trays are made for each patient. They wear them daily for 1 to 2 weeks.

Cleaning/X-rays/Exam

These are typically done the first visit. Depending on the patient’s oral condition, the cleaning may take place during a second visit. CBCT machine for 3D x-rays

Complete/Partial Dentures

We offer various types of dentures to replace missing teeth.

Fillings

We offer tooth colored and amalgam fillings.

Dental Implants

These are a great alternative to a traditional fixed bridge. An implant replaces a missing tooth without having to modify adjacent teeth. The implant functions like the patient’s natural tooth.

Periodontal Therapy

Our hygienists provide scaling/root planing to patients in various stages of periodontal disease. It removes calculus/debris from under the gums to restore the gum tissue to a healthy state.

Crowns

Teeth with large restorations, or fractured teeth, may benefit from a full coverage crown to keep the tooth strong.

Extractions

We perform extractions in our office. Some patients will be referred to local oral surgeons.

Root Canals

When the nerve of the tooth becomes infected, root canal therapy removes the nerve and allows the patient to keep his/her tooth.

Sealants

A thin coating is applied to the chewing surfaces of 6-year and 12-year molars to prevent cavities. They are applied to the deep grooves of children’s teeth as soon as they erupt.

Nightguards

Many people clench and grind their teeth subconsciously while sleeping. This can cause jaw soreness and premature wear on teeth. A nightguard reduces these conditions.
