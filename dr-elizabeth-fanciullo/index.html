The practice

Dentistry is Dr. Elizabeth Fanciullo’s passion; in fact, it has been a part of her family since the late 1800s, when her great-grandfather started his practice. In more than a century since, her grandfather, great uncle, uncle, and cousin have all dedicated their lives to the field.

Born and raised in Louisville, Kentucky, “Dr. Liz” was an award-winning television reporter and producer in her hometown TV market. But, it never felt quite right to her. So, like the generations of relatives before her, she decided to become a dentist – and immediately, she knew she had found her life’s work.

Dr. Liz received her BA in Journalism from the University of Kentucky, where she graduated Summa Cum Laude. She then completed her Doctor of Dental Medicine Degree from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine, where she was on the Dean’s List and a member of the Prosthodontics Honors Society, which recognizes top students in the field of restorative dentistry. She is a member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, American Academy of Facial Esthetics, American Dental Association, New Jersey Dental Association and Monmouth-Ocean Counties Dental Society.

Dr. Liz loves living in Monmouth County and exploring all the area has to offer. In her spare time, she loves working out, cooking delicious meals, playing piano, and hanging out with her husband and their young son, Nicholas, and adorable mini dachshund, Tootsie.
