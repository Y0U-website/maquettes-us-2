Dental Membership Plan

Northpark Dental Membership Plan is very simple to understand, unlike insurance!

  • There are no deductibles . Once you enroll, your benefits start immediately.
  • There is no paperwork . Savings are applied the moment you check out.
  • There is no waiting period . Once you enroll, benefits start immediately.

Two Membership Plans Available.

Why become a member?

By becoming a member, you will receive all your preventive care at no additional charge. You are saving over 45 % on your preventive care! And you will receive a 15 % discount on ALL services at Northpark Dental. Whether you need a crown or cosmetic procedures you can save hundreds by being a member.

Limitations of Dental insurance plans:

  • Yearly maximums. Most insurance caps at $1500. Once you hit this number, care becomes an out of pocket expense.
  • Deductibles. An average deductible is $100. This must be met before your insurance kicks in.
  • Paperwork. Trust us when we say it is not fun submitting paperwork for denied services.
  • Waiting period. The average waiting period is 12 months.
  • Denied services. Just because you want it does not mean your insurance thinks you need it.
  • Pre-existing conditions. Some insurances have a missing tooth clause, meaning that if you have a missing tooth prior to purchasing your dental coverage then insurance will not pay for a replacement.
