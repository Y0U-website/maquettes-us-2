About

Elliot Rand, D.D.S

I have always dreamed of building a dental practice that would be able to provide patients with care at a single office that meets all their dental needs. Patient care entails performing dentistry and understanding the patient&#x27;s needs, from their smile to their inner spirit.

As a dentist, I have been able to help people in many different ways. This means the world to me; I am eternally thankful and blessed. Incredibly, I have had the chance to travel to remote places worldwide and provide healthcare education to children.

Education and Continuing Education

I graduated from Howard University College of Dentistry in 1987. Since graduation, I have completed many continuing education courses and obtained certifications in advanced dental procedures. I am involved in the latest advances in cutting-edge dental technology and materials.

In 2023, Dr. Rand has been speaking throughout the USA on the advancement of AI in Dentistry.

He is recognized as a key speaker with Pearl AI and helping dentists and patients understand the “why “ in their treatment is needed. The practice is advanced from 3-D technology to AI in diagnosing dental issues.
