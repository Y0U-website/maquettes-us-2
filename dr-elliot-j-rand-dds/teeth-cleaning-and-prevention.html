Why Is Preventive Care Essential?

Preventive dental care for your teeth is essential to keep problems like tooth decay, gum disease, and others at bay. When you choose proper preventive dental care, you can keep these dental problems from occurring. When you visit our dental office for a teeth cleaning, we will also give you instructions about caring for your teeth and maintaining your pearly whites at home. Our recommendations include:

  • Brush your teeth regularly, twice a day. Refrain from brushing too rigorously, as it affects your enamel. Brush for at least three minutes per session.
  • Your dental care is not limited to brushing; you must add flossing (if you don’t already) in your routine. Flossing prevents the accumulation of food debris between your teeth, thus keeping bacteria and plaque away. For a bonus, you can floss after every meal to maintain your dental health!
  • Rinse your mouth using an antiseptic mouthwash. Not only does it help with the removal of bacteria, but it also keeps bad breath away.
  • Visit us for dental cleanings and examinations at least twice a year.
