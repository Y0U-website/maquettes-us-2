About Our Pediatric Dental Team…

Flexible Scheduling Made Easy for Busy Families:

  • Pain-free technology: We offer options like laughing gas and gentle tools to make procedures as comfortable as possible.
  • Positive reinforcement: We celebrate every milestone and achievement, building confidence and trust in dental care.
  • Colorful and kid-friendly office: Our waiting room is filled with books and games to keep little ones entertained.
  • Reward system: Kids earn prizes for good behavior and completing their dental checkups.
  • Kid-friendly television and movies: Transform cleanings into mini-adventures with their favorite shows.
  • Early morning appointments: Fit dental care into your busy schedule with flexible hours as early as 7:30 AM.
  • Short wait times: We value your time and prioritize prompt appointments.
  • Emergency appointments available: Unexpected dental problems? We’re here to help, with same-day appointments available.
