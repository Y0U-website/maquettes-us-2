DR. EMILY FRIEDRICH, DDS

Dr. Emily Friedrich grew up in Amanda, Ohio. She received her bachelor’s degree in biology from Hillsdale College and then her doctorate of dentistry from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. She joined the family practice in 2007. She is a member of the American Dental Association, Ohio Dental Association and Columbus Dental Society.

Dr. Friedrich and her husband, David, have five children, Ethan, Andrew, Elizabeth, Caroline and Hudson. She enjoys running, cooking, gardening, participating in outdoor activities and, of course, spending as much time as possible with her husband and children!!

DR. JAMES PHILLIPS, DDS

Dr. James Phillips graduated from Bloom Carroll High School in 1968. He spent two years in the United States Marine Corps from 1971-1973. Dr. Phillips attended undergrad and graduated from Ohio University in 1975 with a degree in zoology. He then received his dental degree from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in 1978.

After graduating, he joined his father, Dr. Edward Phillips, who started the practice in 1946, at this location in Canal Winchester, Ohio. Currently, Dr. Phillips shares the practice with his wife, Dr. Diana Phillips, and daughter, Dr. Emily Friedrich.

He is the proud father of four children and has 16 grandchildren. In his spare time, he enjoys working with his sawmill and spending time with his grandchildren.

AMANDA, Registered Dental Hygienist

Amanda graduated from Raymond Walter’s Dental Hygiene School in 2008. She has been working in our office since 2011. However, as one of Drs. James and Diana Phillips’ daughters, you could say she has always been a part of this office.

One of the things she enjoys most about dentistry is building relationships with the patients she sees. She always remembers her patients and they are like family to her.

Amanda is married to Ben and they have four children, Amelia, Samuel, Lucy and Penny. She enjoys spending time outdoors and with all her family and friends.
