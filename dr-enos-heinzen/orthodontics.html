A straighter smile for a healthier you

Straight teeth do more than just boost your confidence. They also improve your oral health. Misaligned teeth can make it harder to clean your teeth properly, increasing the risk of cavities and gum disease. At Moore, Pascarella & Heinzen, we want to make it as easy as possible to enjoy a healthier, more beautiful smile. That’s why we offer Invisalign clear aligners in the convenience of your regular dental office, and work closely with trusted orthodontists in the Redding and Red Bluff areas for more complex cases.

Why straighten your teeth?

Orthodontic care is about more than looks. Correcting misalignments can help prevent:

By addressing these issues, orthodontic treatment contributes to both your dental health and overall confidence.

  • Difficulty cleaning between teeth, which raises the risk of decay and gum disease.
  • Uneven pressure on certain teeth, leading to wear and damage.
  • Jaw pain or chewing difficulties due to bite problems.
  • Feeling self-conscious about your smile.

How we help you get started

It’s our mission to make your journey to a healthier smile as smooth as possible. Here’s what that looks like:

  • Complimentary Consultation: We’ll assess your smile, bite, and dental history while discussing your smile goals and any issues your misaligned teeth cause in your daily life.
  • Personalized Plan: We’ll outline your treatment options, review your financing options , and determine if a referral to an orthodontist near you is the best choice.
  • Treatment Begins: Whether with clear aligners at our office or with an orthodontist for more complex care, we’ll be with you every step of the way.
  • Protecting Your Smile During Treatment : It’s vital to keep up with excellent dental care while you’re undergoing orthodontics. We’ll recommend a hygiene plan including detailed home care instructions and regular professional cleanings to keep your teeth and gums as healthy as possible during your treatment.
  • Maintaining Your Results: Depending on your specific needs, we may recommend a permanent or removable orthodontic retainer to keep your teeth in their ideal positions long-term.
