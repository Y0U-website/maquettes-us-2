Laser Dentistry

Recent advances in laser technology have allowed select dentists to offer laser treatment . A dental laser is a high-tech device that generates a highly concentrated beam of light, which can be used to treat tooth decay, enamel and gum tissue. Advantages of laser dentistry include less pain, minimal bleeding, reduced risk of infection, minimal or no anesthesia, no noise, faster healing, a more sterilized environment (laser kills bacteria), and treatment of canker sores.

Video Cavity

Maybe if you could actually see those old fillings yourself, you’d feel a lot better about having them fixed. Here’s where an intra-oral camera is just the ticket! An intra-oral camera is about the size of a pen, so it fits easily into your mouth. Once positioned, it sends pictures to a monitor, where your teeth and gums appear in a full-color, usually as a very large image. Want a copy of the image? We can print one for you.

I nvisalign

Invisalign clear aligners are an affordable, doctor-friendly alternative to traditional braces. Invisalign uses a series of clear overlay templates called aligners that have been generated by computer simulation to gradually move the teeth. This brace is available to adult patients with certain orthodontic bite problems. Straighter teeth promote healthier gums, which leads to easier cleanings.

Porcelain Crowns

Do you have a cap or bridge on your front teeth that you are no longer happy with? Most aren’t aware that it is now possible to replace these unattractive teeth with natural looking all-porcelain restorations. Metal-free crowns and bridges get rid of the opaque, unnatural look of traditional metal dentistry and restore the beautiful smile you’ve always hoped for. Porcelain restorations (crowns & onlays) are under a 3-year warranty and can be completed in just 2 visits.

Etch Bonded Fillings

Tired of those ugly silver/mercury fillings in your mouth? At Calian Dental Group, we have had special training in the use of Etch Bonded White Fillings which restore the original strength and appearance of your teeth. This advanced technique eliminates the need for caps or crowns in most cases, and preserves much more tooth structure resulting in teeth that look natural and feel great!
