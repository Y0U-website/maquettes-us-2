Meet Our Talented Team

We have extensive experience in treating both adults and children in all aspects of dentistry including root canals, wisdom teeth extractions, implants, braces, and cosmetic dentistry. Our practices are equipped with the latest state of the art technology and they adhere to the highest sterilization standards.

Dr. Lee is a proud U.S. Army veteran who served overseas in South Korea and Japan. He completed his undergraduate studies at Georgia Tech and earned his dental degree from USC School of Dentistry. He is passionate about community outreach, volunteering on dental mission trips across the U.S., Taiwan, and Sri Lanka, and participating in the ADA Foundation’s Give Kids a Smile program each year.

Originally from Lima, Peru, Dr. Chia graduated from the University of Southern California with her DDS in 2010. During dental school, she traveled to Guatemala and Honduras to provide dental care to underserved communities. She brings a warm, family-oriented approach to every patient interaction.

Dr. Nakamatsu is K&E’s root canal specialist. A U.S. Army veteran who served in Korea, Iraq, and Maryland, he returned to USC after his military service to complete advanced training in Endodontics under the renowned Dr. James Simon. His specialized expertise means patients needing root canal therapy are treated by a dedicated specialist without ever leaving our office.

Narissa keeps K&E Family Dental running smoothly behind the scenes. With years of experience in dental practice management, she oversees the day-to-day operations of the office to ensure every patient has a seamless experience from scheduling to checkout. She’s often the first friendly voice you’ll hear when you call our office.

Memberships

We are active members of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, and Western Los Angeles Dental Society, and maintain a proud affiliation with the Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry of USC. Our commitment to these professional organizations keeps our team connected to the latest research, techniques, and best practices in dentistry. It’s one more way we ensure every patient receives exceptional care.
