K&E Family Dental’s In-House Dental Plan K&E Family Dental’s In-House Dental Plan is a unique service provided for patients without an existing dental insurance plan. It offers low cost, no deductibles, no annual maximum, and is effective immediately.

CareCredit CareCredit is a healthcare credit card that helps you pay for treatments and procedures for you and your family that may not be covered by dental insurance. With special financing options, you can use your CareCredit card for your dental needs as well as with many other healthcare providers.
