FAQs About Braces, Aligner Therapy and TMJ in Beachwood :

Here are some of the most common questions we get from patients and their parents on the East side of Cleveland:

  • 1. What orthodontic services are available near Beachwood? We provide a full range of orthodontic treatments, including braces, Clear Aligners, Early Orthodontic Treatment , Adult Orthodontics, TMJ Treatment, and Sleep Therapy. Each plan is tailored to your age, goals, and oral health needs.
  • 2. How do I know which treatment is right for me? Dr. Lawrence begins every case with a detailed consultation that includes digital scans and a bite analysis. We’ll discuss your smile goals and explain the best treatment options based on your unique needs whether that’s braces, aligners, or a specialized therapy plan.
  • 3. Are clear aligners as effective as traditional braces? Yes, for most cases! Clear aligners use gentle, consistent pressure to move teeth into ideal alignment. They can treat mild to moderate crowding, spacing, and bite issues, all while remaining nearly invisible and removable for meals and brushing.
  • 4. Can orthodontics help with TMJ pain or headaches? Absolutely. Misaligned teeth or bite imbalances can strain jaw muscles and joints, leading to TMJ pain. Our TMJ Treatment options are designed to relieve discomfort by improving alignment and jaw position often reducing tension headaches as well.
  • 5. How long does orthodontic treatment take? Treatment length varies based on your goals and case complexity. Most Beachwood patients complete treatment in 12 24 months, though early or limited cases may finish sooner. Dr. Lawrence will provide an estimated timeline at your first visit.
