What makes our practice unique is that Dr. Eric lives just above his practice on the second floor. Located in Royersford, PA, The Royersford Dentist is your local, comprehensive dental solution for the entire family.

The Royersford Dentist is proud to be your local, family-friendly dental office. We care for families from Royersford, PA, and the surrounding communities of Collegeville, Spring City, Phoenixville, Pottstown, and Limerick.

Our skilled, compassionate team, under the guidance of Dr. Eric Paster, manages everything from regular cleanings and exams for the whole family to sleep apnea and snoring devices, veneers, crowns, Invisalign, and full-mouth rehabilitation.

The Royersford Dentist is a high-quality, low-volume, relationship-based office. We love creating lifelong relationships with our patients, and our team treats each patient with the same concern, respect, and kindness with which we treat members of our own families.

We are sensitive to anyone apprehensive about dental treatment, and we work with them to make their visits with us comfortable.

Our goal is to help each of our patients achieve and maintain long-term dental health and an attractive smile. We believe that early intervention is critical in preventing long-term issues, and we offer a safe, comfortable setting where patients are included in developing a treatment plan that is right for them.
