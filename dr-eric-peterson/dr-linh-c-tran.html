About Dr. Linh C. Tran

Philosophy on Care

Your teeth are often one of the first things people notice about you! Therefore, I promise to be honest and give you the best options. I’ll help you choose the right treatment that suits your wants and needs. Being honest, open and truthful is incredibly important to me, so together, we’ll look at your x-rays, I’ll explain procedures and I promise to listen so that we can work together towards your dental goal.

This might be surprising, but I initially ruled out dentistry because I thought dentists would just breeze in for a few minutes after getting your teeth cleaned… or even worse, buckle down on treatment without any hint of a smile. Just like a dental chicken, I didn’t want any part of that.

My mind changed completely when I shadowed a fantastic dentist who not only took extra time with patients, but also made anxious people feel comfortable even when having dental treatment. Peterson Dental is like that and that’s one of the reasons I chose to practice with Dr. Peterson!

Philosophy on Comfort

The point is that I can easily cater the situation to your needs. Rest assured no matter what environment you need, you’ll always get the same high-quality dentistry you’ve come to expect from Peterson Dental.

BIO

I currently live in Hoover with my husband Habeeb and our dog Mochi. I treasure our family time, so in our free time, we will visit with our parents. We both come from a background of hard working and loving immigrant parents, mine from Vietnam and his from Lebanon. Because of our rich cultures, I love traveling and exploring unique foods. In my spare time, I enjoy dabbling in different arts, such as painting, pottery, and dance. I love visiting with friends, staying active, and working out.

Thanks so much for reading about me! I can’t wait to see you soon.
