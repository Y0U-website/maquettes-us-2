The practice

To maintain healthy teeth and gums, we believe our patients should visit us at least two times per year. Neglecting these visits could lead to major issues for your teeth or gums, requiring more complex and costly treatment to correct. It’s best to catch issues early with routine maintenance appointments.

We work with several dental insurance plans. We ask that you have your insurance information at hand when you contact our office to schedule an appointment, that will allow us to confirm that we participate with your plan.
