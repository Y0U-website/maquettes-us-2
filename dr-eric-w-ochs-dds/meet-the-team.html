Meet the Dentists

Dr. Susan Baker Dr Baker grew up in St. Clair Shores and graduated from Lakeshore High School. She attended Adrian College where she earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology, then went on to the University of Detroit School of Dentistry and graduated in 1989. Dr.’s Baker and Ochs met at dental school and got married in 1990; they reside in Clinton Township. They have two sons, who are now grown. In her spare time, she enjoys working in her garden, biking, and spending time with her family.

Meet Our Staff!

Our Hygienists

Our hygienists Michelle O., Ebonie, Anna, and Michelle S. are an important part of Baker & Ochs Family Dentistry. Whether you need just a routine cleaning or a periodontal (deep) cleaning, all four are very capable of making your treatment effective and comfortable. Feel free to ask them questions about oral care products and techniques for keeping your mouth as healthy as possible, and they will give you personalized instructions to best suit your needs.

Our Assistants

Our skilled and friendly assistants are an important part of Baker & Ochs and will help get you through your appointment smoothly. They are highly trained to operate our microprocessor based sterilization equipment and also help the doctors perform dental procedures efficiently.

Front Office Staff

Our friendly front office staff is available to assist you with scheduling appointments to best suit your needs, and can ensure that your health update and insurance information are up to date. They are knowledgeable in dental insurance matters and can answer your questions confidentially.
