Outside of the Office, What Does Dr. Nickas Like to Do?

A self-professed animal nut, Dr. Nickas and his fiancée, Corey, have a 3,000-gallon koi pond that they maintain year-round. They love to travel to exotic locations and observe local wildlife. In fact, they’ve come face-to-face with giant tortoises in the Gal pagos Islands, and swam with tropical fish while scuba diving on the Belize barrier reef. He says Our planet if so full of interesting life. Our goal is to see as much as possible while we’re here.

Locally, Dr. Nickas enjoys fishing in Illinois and Wisconsin, mountain biking and attending live music concerts.
