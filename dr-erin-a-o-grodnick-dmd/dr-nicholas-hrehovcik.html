Meet Dr. Nicholas Hrehovcik

Dr. Hrehovcik s calm demeanor and gentle ways have quickly earned him a reputation as an exceptional dentist amongst the patients at Signature Smiles of Hunterdon. A New Jersey native, Dr. Hrehovcik s interest and career in dentistry started early as a part-time orthodontist assistant while in high school and college. Educated in New Jersey, Dr. Hrehovcik s undergraduate degree is from Ramapo College of New Jersey, his master s in Biomedical Informatics from Rutgers University Newark, and his Doctor of Dental Medicine from the Rutgers School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Hrehovcik is married to Miranda Dekker, LCSW. A former college athlete, Dr. Hrehovcik loves sports and being outdoors. In his spare time, he enjoys cycling, golfing, and relaxing with his wife, son Everett and family.
