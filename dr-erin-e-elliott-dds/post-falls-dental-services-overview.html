Preventative Dentistry – Protecting Your Oral Health for Life

Good oral health starts with regular preventative care . At Post Falls Family Dental , we focus on early detection and routine maintenance to help you avoid serious dental issues in the future.

Our Preventative Services Include:

✔ New Patient Exams – Comprehensive evaluations, including digital X-rays and oral cancer screenings. ✔ Professional Cleanings – Removes plaque, tartar, and stains for optimal oral health. ✔ Dental Sealants – Protective coatings that help prevent cavities in children and adults. ✔ Healthy Start Program – Early intervention for jaw development and sleep-disordered breathing.

By committing to bi-annual dental visits , you can prevent costly and complex dental treatments in the future.

Sedation Dentistry – Stress-Free, Comfortable Dental Care

Dental anxiety is a common concern , but it shouldn’t prevent you from getting the care you need. Our sedation dentistry options ensure that every patient feels at ease during their treatment.

Our Sedation Options Include:

✔ Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas) – A mild and fast-acting sedative to help you relax. ✔ Oral Conscious Sedation – A deeper level of relaxation for longer procedures or higher anxiety levels.

Even complex treatments can feel quick and stress-free with sedation dentistry , making it easier for nervous patients to maintain their oral health .
