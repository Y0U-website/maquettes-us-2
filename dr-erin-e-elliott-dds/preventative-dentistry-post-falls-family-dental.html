Preventative Dental Services Healthy Start Did you know that 9 out of 10 children suffer from an undiagnosed sleep disorder or breathing issue ? The Healthy Start Program at Post Falls Family Dental is a non-invasive solution that corrects oral habits early, promoting healthy jaw development and proper breathing patterns.

Healthy Start helps correct these issues early , reducing the need for orthodontics in the future. This program promotes: ✅ Proper airway development for better breathing. ✅ Straight teeth without braces in many cases. ✅ Improved focus, sleep, and overall health .
