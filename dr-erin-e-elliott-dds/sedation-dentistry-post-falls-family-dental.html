Sedation Dentistry

🔹 Benefits of Nitrous Oxide: ✅ Reduces anxiety without putting you to sleep . ✅ Completely safe and wears off quickly . ✅ No lasting drowsiness – you can even drive yourself home after treatment. ✅ Effective for both adults and children .

For those who experience mild dental anxiety or require a quick recovery after their procedure , nitrous oxide is an excellent choice .

  • You will feel relaxed and at ease within minutes.
  • The effects wear off quickly, allowing you to resume normal activities after your appointment.

Oral Conscious Sedation

For patients with moderate to severe dental anxiety , oral conscious sedation offers a deeper level of relaxation while still allowing you to remain awake.

🔹 Benefits of Oral Conscious Sedation: ✅ Ideal for patients with higher levels of anxiety . ✅ More procedures can be completed in one visit . ✅ No needles required – taken as a pill. ✅ May cause drowsiness – a caregiver must drive you home after your appointment.

Oral sedation is perfect for those who avoid the dentist due to fear , allowing them to receive necessary treatments comfortably and stress-free .

Who Can Benefit from Sedation Dentistry? Sedation dentistry is an excellent option for patients who:

✔ Experience fear or anxiety about dental visits . ✔ Have sensitive teeth or a strong gag reflex . ✔ Require long or multiple procedures in one visit . ✔ Have difficulty sitting still for extended periods . ✔ Have had negative dental experiences in the past .

At Post Falls Family Dental , we make it our priority to ensure that every patient feels safe and comfortable during their visit. Whether you have mild anxiety or severe dental phobia , we have a sedation solution that fits your needs .

  • A prescribed sedative is taken before your appointment .
  • The medication induces a state of deep relaxation , making you feel at ease and unbothered by the procedure .
  • You remain conscious and responsive but may have little memory of the procedure afterward .
