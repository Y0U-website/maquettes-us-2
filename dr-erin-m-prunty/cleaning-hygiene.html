Why Are Cleaning & Hygiene Appointments Important?

Routine six-month teeth cleanings help remove plaque and tartar that can contribute to cavities and gum disease. Not only that, at Taconic Dental, Dr. Erin Prunty or Dr. Daniel Scialabba will perform a comprehensive oral exam and oral cancer screening during every cleaning & hygiene visit. This helps us catch any potential oral health issues early, while they&#x27;re still easy to treat.

Plus, our team blends expert preventive care with warm, personalized attention. You’ll never feel rushed or like “just another patient.” Our goal is to make you feel at home from the moment you walk in. With the help of high-tech tools like CBCT imaging and intraoral cameras, we ensure nothing gets missed while keeping your visit efficient, comfortable, and stress-free.

Brighter, Fresher Smile Professional cleanings lift surface stains caused by coffee, tea, and everyday foods. You&#x27;ll leave your appointment not only feeling refreshed but also smiling with more confidence thanks to visibly whiter teeth.

Early Problem Detection Regular hygiene visits allow our team to spot potential issues like decay or infection early, before they become painful or costly. With routine screenings, you get peace of mind and proactive care.

What to Expect From Cleaning & Hygiene Appointments

Warm Welcome and Evaluation

Warm Welcome and Evaluation

When you arrive at Taconic Dental, you’ll be greeted by our friendly team, and perhaps our therapy dog, Baloo, who’s always ready to help ease nerves. We’ll begin with a review of your dental and medical history, followed by digital diagnostics like low-radiation X-rays or CBCT imaging, if needed. This helps us get a clear picture of your current oral health and any areas that need extra attention. You’ll have time to ask questions and feel fully informed before we begin.

Personalized Dental Cleaning

Personalized Dental Cleaning

Your cleaning is done by a gentle, experienced hygienist who customizes care based on your gum health, sensitivity, and goals. We use ultrasonic tools and hand instruments to remove built-up plaque and tartar, even in hard-to-reach areas, without discomfort. You&#x27;ll also get a thorough polish to remove stains and a flossing to leave everything feeling squeaky clean. If there’s any discomfort or anxiety, we offer comfort aids and sedation options to make the visit easier on you.

Prevention and Ongoing Care

Prevention and Ongoing Care

Once your teeth are clean, we’ll walk you through any findings and offer tips to keep your smile healthy at home. If needed, Dr. Prunty or Dr. Scialabba may recommend fluoride treatments or discuss any follow-up care. From there, we help you schedule your next routine visit so your smile stays radiant and healthy year-round. Our goal is to support you, not just during your appointment, but every day in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens during a dental cleaning appointment?

During a professional cleaning, a hygienist removes plaque and tartar buildup, especially in areas that are difficult to reach with brushing and flossing alone. Your teeth will be polished to remove surface stains, and your gums will be evaluated to check for signs of gum disease.
