Why Choose Implant Crowns at Taconic Dental?

Choosing where to restore your smile is a big decision, and we’re here to help make it easier. At Taconic Dental, we combine expert care with a calming, family-friendly environment. Our full-service approach means you won’t need outside referrals; everything from planning to placement happens here.

We use CBCT imaging and intraoral scanning to create implant crowns that fit perfectly and look completely natural. And if you feel anxious, you’ll appreciate our comfort-first philosophy, including sedation options and even a friendly therapy dog to ease your nerves. We’re proud to offer implant crowns that not only restore your oral health but transform your everyday life.

Better Jaw Health and Tooth Stability Missing teeth can lead to bone loss, which affects your facial structure and bite. Implant crowns help stimulate your jawbone, preserving its strength and preventing the shifting of nearby teeth. This long-term stability contributes greatly to your oral health.

Confidence That Lasts With implant crowns, you don’t just restore a tooth, you restore your self-esteem. Whether it’s a single tooth or several, our high-tech approach ensures a natural appearance and a secure fit. What to Expect From Implant Crowns

Personalized Consultation and Planning

Personalized Consultation and Planning

Your journey begins with a one-on-one consultation with Dr. Prunty. Using digital imaging, including CBCT scans and intraoral scanning, we assess your mouth and bone health to determine if implants are right for you. This advanced planning allows us to design implant crowns that are both precise and functional, eliminating guesswork and reducing treatment time. You’ll have all your questions answered in a comfortable, pressure-free setting.

Implant Placement and Healing

Implant Placement and Healing

Once you’re ready, the dental implant is surgically placed into your jawbone. This part of the process is done with your comfort in mind, and we offer sedation for those who feel nervous or have dental anxiety. Over the next 3-6 months, the implant naturally bonds with your bone, creating a strong foundation for your crown. During this healing period, we’ll monitor your progress closely to ensure everything is on track.

Custom Crown Placement

Custom Crown Placement

After healing, it’s time for your custom implant crown. Using digital scans and images of your mouth, we will create a crown that fits beautifully and matches your natural teeth in shape and color. It’s then securely attached to the implant, finalizing the process. You’ll leave with a fully restored smile that not only looks great but functions like your original tooth, if not better.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do implant crowns look and feel like natural teeth?

Yes. Implant crowns are designed to closely match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth. They feel secure and function like real teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
