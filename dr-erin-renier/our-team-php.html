Dr. Erin K. Renier, DDS

I love dentistry because it is an ideal blend of art and science, my two greatest passions. Every person has unique dental needs. I strive to educate my patients and partner with them to achieve healthy, beautiful smiles.

In my free time, you will find me watercolor painting. Check out my artwork at erinrenier.com. I am a member of the Lake Superior watercolor society and serve as the secretary for the Artists of Minnesota Board. I love hiking the Superior Hiking Trail with my husband Eric and I am an avid cross-country skier and member of the sailing crew for Wednesday night sailboat races. I love activities that connect me to so many people in our community. I’m glad to call Duluth my home.

Terani Harris, Dental Therapist

I was born and raised in the Duluth area and grew up in Twig, Minnesota. I graduated from Proctor High School and developed an interest in dentistry at a young age. After high school, I proudly served in the Air National Guard with the 148th Fighter Wing for nine years, including a deployment to Afghanistan, while continuing my education.

I graduated from Lake Superior College in 2011 as a Dental Hygienist and went on to earn my bachelor s degree in Dental Hygiene and a Master of Science in Advanced Dental Therapy from Metropolitan State University in St. Paul in 2016. I am an active member of the Minnesota Dental Therapy Association, the Minnesota Oral Health Coalition, and the Minnesota Dental Hygiene Association, and I am committed to advancing oral health and access to care within our communities.

As a mid-level dental practitioner, I provide restorative and preventive dental care with a strong focus on making patients feel comfortable, heard, and at ease. I understand that dental visits can be stressful for many people, and I strive to create a calm, welcoming environment where patients feel safe and confident in their care. Building trusting, long term relationships with my patients is one of the most rewarding parts of my work, and I take pride in providing compassionate, personalized care.

Outside of the dental clinic, I enjoy spending time with my partner and our two children. Much of our time is spent at hockey rinks, baseball fields, and soccer fields cheering on the kids. When I have time to unwind, I love horseback riding and spending time with my horse, Lucky.
