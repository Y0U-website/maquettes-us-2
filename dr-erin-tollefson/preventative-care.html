Teeth Cleanings & Hygiene

Teeth cleanings and oral exams form the backbone of preventive dentistry. We recommend that patients come and see us every six months at Valley Edge Dental in Eau Claire. During your appointment, your teeth will be polished to remove plaque and then flossed to remove any remaining debris. Next, we will examine your mouth to look for signs of gum disease, cavities, and other common oral health issues. After this, you’ll schedule any necessary follow-ups and be sent home.

How Preventive Dentistry Helps

Digital X-Rays

Our advanced imaging technology allows us to see below the surface, and ensures your dentist understands every aspect of your oral health and well-being. X-rays are usually recommended yearly, or whenever our team suspects a hidden oral health issue.

Preventing Common Oral Health Issues

We offer a wide variety of preventive care treatments for gum disease, cavity prevention, and much more.

Gum Disease Treatment

Periodontal Care

We offer expert periodontal treatment for gum disease, including deep cleanings (scaling & root planing) to halt and reverse the spread of gum disease.

Strengthen Teeth

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments use a powerful, fluoride-rich solution that’s painted onto the teeth after a cleaning. This strengthens them and prevents cavities.

Prevent Cavities

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are thin layers of dental resin applied to the rear teeth. They protect the teeth from cavities by ensuring bacteria, acid, and food particles cannot contact the enamel directly.

Peace Of Mind With Oral Cancer Screenings

We offer oral cancer screenings as part of every appointment at Valley Edge Dental. We will look for common signs of oral cancer, providing you with peace of mind and ensuring you have a clean bill of health.
