Meet Dr. Ernest Reeh

Beyond his professional accomplishments, Dr. Reeh is actively involved in his community, including serving as an adult leader in Boy Scout Troop 9132.

03 Personalized Approach Precision You Can Trust A Personalized and Comforting Approach Dr. Reeh understands that endodontic procedures can be intimidating, so he prioritizes patient comfort with humor, clear explanations, and meticulous care—even drawing upside-down illustrations to engage patients.Known as “Canal Man” for his precision in finding and treating hidden canals, he’s respected for his fine motor skills and diagnostic expertise. For him, the greatest reward is seeing patients relieved and surprised at how easy their treatment was.

  • Fellow, American Association of Endodontists
  • Top Dentist Recognition in Mpls.St.Paul Magazine since 2007
  • Consultant for 3M Dental Division and Carestream Dental (Kodak Imaging)
  • Guest Lecturer for the Veterans Administration Hospital Dental Residency Program in Minneapolis

Life Outside the Office

When he&#x27;s not performing endodontic treatments, Dr. Reeh enjoys woodworking, tinkering with his hobby car, and gardening. His passion for hands-on craftsmanship extends beyond dentistry, as he finds relaxation in building, fixing, and creating with the same precision he brings to patient care.

He also values the close-knit environment at River Valley Endodontics, describing his team as family and ensuring that every patient who walks through the door feels welcome, informed, and well cared for.
