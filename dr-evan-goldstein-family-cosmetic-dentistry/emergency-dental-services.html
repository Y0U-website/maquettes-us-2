Is It Time to Seek Emergency Dental Services?

If you’re experiencing dental discomfort or a sudden dental issue, it might be time to contact a professional. Here’s how you can tell:

  • Persistent tooth pain
  • Broken or chipped tooth
  • Swelling and infections
  • Severe gum bleeding
  • Lost dental fillings or crowns
  • Traumatic dental injury
