About Us

All our professionals at Fort Dental Center maintain the highest levels of accreditation and pursue ongoing education to stay abreast of the latest trends in dentistry.

Meet Dr. Samman

Dr. Fadi Samman is a dentist that believes in treating each patient as an individual with dental needs that deserve special attention. He is a compassionate general dentist who performs cosmetic dentistry with great skill and passion. With over eighteen years of experience in dentistry, Dr. Samman is prepared for just about any dental issue you may have.

Dr. Samman will start you on your journey to your beautiful smile by first analyzing your oral health with a personal visual evaluation and consultation. After addressing your dental health needs, goals and expectations, he will custom design your dental health plan to create the perfect smile that enhances your face shape and personal beauty.

Schedule your appointment today with Dr. Samman at Fort Dental Center to begin your journey in achieving the healthiest and most beautiful smile you could ever dream of having.
