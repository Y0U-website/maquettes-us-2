General Dentistry

Teeth cleaning, root canals, and fillings constitute the bulk of a dentist’s practice. Even though we perform these routine procedures almost every day, we realize that for the patient, any trip to the dentist comes with a certain amount of uneasiness. This is why we strive to make every visit as stress-free as possible. We stay up to date on the latest technological advancements in dentistry, which means that we are constantly improving the patient experience.

Cosmetic dentistry

Nothing creates a better first impression than a healthy smile. When your teeth are damaged or stained, you don’t feel as confident about showing them off. Brushing, flossing, and regular checkups are great ways to prevent tooth loss or discoloration, but once the damage is done, the next step is cosmetic dentistry. We perform a number of cosmetic dentistry services, including installing bridges and crowns, dentures, and teeth whitening.

Dental surgery

Impacted wisdom teeth are one of the most common conditions that require a surgical solution. The fact that wisdom teeth are so common doesn’t mean the condition is any less painful. Fortunately, relief is as close as your trusted family dentist. Wisdom teeth removal is a major decision, and our dentists can carefully assess each patient’s individual condition to determine whether or not surgery is necessary.

The advantages of a general dentist

Since we don’t specialize, we are able to offer a broader range of services versus the narrower expertise of a specialist. Many dental problems can be corrected by basic care, which is more cost-effective for the patient. If Dr. Hyneman or Dr. Santiago determines that you need a specialist, we will be happy to refer you to one, but you may find your issues can be addressed by simple preventative measures and treatment options.
