Are There Any Alternatives to Dental Implants?

Yes, there are alternatives if the dental implants cost Lake Orion MI poses a hurdle or if you’re exploring different options. Bridges and dentures are common alternatives, each with distinct advantages and limitations.

Dentures: Another solution, especially for multiple missing teeth, dentures are removable and can offer a quick fix. However, they might not provide the same stability as implants.
