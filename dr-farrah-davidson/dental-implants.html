All-On-4 dental implants represent a revolutionary approach to dental restoration, offering a permanent solution for individuals with significant tooth loss or severe dental issues.

All-On-4 dental implants are a type of full-arch restoration. Unlike traditional dentures, which rest on the gums, All-On-4 implants provide a fixed, non-removable set of teeth anchored by four dental implants in the jawbone. These implants act as the foundation, providing stability and preventing the bone loss often associated with conventional dentures.

1. Stability and Durability: The implants are securely anchored in the jawbone, providing a stable and durable foundation for the prosthetic teeth. This stability helps in maintaining the structure of the jawbone and preventing bone resorption.

2. Improved Aesthetics and Functionality: All-On-4 implants look and function like natural teeth. They allow for normal chewing and speaking capabilities, improving overall quality of life.

3. Quick Procedure: The All-On-4 procedure is typically completed in a single day, often referred to as Teeth in a Day. This means patients can walk out of the dental office with a new set of teeth on the same day as their surgery.

4. Cost-Effective: Compared to placing individual implants for each missing tooth, the All-On-4 approach is more cost-effective. It uses fewer implants and often requires less bone grafting.

5. Minimal Invasiveness: By using only four implants, the All-On-4 technique is less invasive than traditional methods that may require more implants and extensive grafting.

1. Consultation and Planning: The process begins with a thorough consultation, including a detailed examination, X-rays, and 3D imaging. This helps in creating a precise treatment plan tailored to the patient’s needs.

2. Implant Placement: During the procedure, four implants are placed into the jawbone. The two posterior implants are angled to maximize the use of available bone and avoid anatomical structures such as the sinus cavity.

3. Temporary Prosthesis: A temporary set of teeth is attached to the implants immediately after surgery. This temporary prosthesis allows for normal function and aesthetics while the implants integrate with the jawbone.

4. Healing and Osseointegration: Over the next few months, the implants undergo osseointegration, where they fuse with the jawbone, providing a stable base for the permanent prosthesis.

5. Final Prosthesis: After the healing period, the temporary prosthesis is replaced with a custom-made, permanent set of teeth that fit perfectly and function naturally.
