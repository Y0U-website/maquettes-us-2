Fernando L. Oliver

Meet Dr. Fernando L. Oliver. He has over 30 years of experience restoring patient s teeth and creating beautiful and healthy smiles. He is as a cosmetic, family, and preventive dentist, fully bilingual in English and Spanish, and, provides expert dental healthcare with warmth and detailed attention.

Dr. Oliver received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Puerto Rico s School of Odontology. Upon obtaining his degree, he served as an officer in the United States Air Force Dental Corps at Eglin AFB, in Pensacola, Fl. Following his military service, he moved on to work as Chief of Staff at the multi-dentist, multi-location Sarasota Dental Clinic in Sarasota, Fl. until opening his private practice in Orlando, FL.

Dr. Oliver s concern for general dental health led him to serve his community for many years as a volunteer dentist at the Orlando Coalition for the Homeless where he not only helped to develop the services provided by the clinics but also received honors for his contributions and many years of service.

Dr. Oliver loves spending time with his family, golfing and restoring old cars and motorcycles. He is very personable, relaxed and good humored, making his patients feel at ease when providing dental care with the utmost respect. The website section Patients comment is a testament to that!
