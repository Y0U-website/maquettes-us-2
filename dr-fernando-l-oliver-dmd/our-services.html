INVISIBLE BRACES

At Alatmonte Dental Associates, we provide you with a simple alternative to traditional metal braces. We recommend invisible braces and use two brands available in the market - Invisalign or Clear Correct. This gives you an affordable alternative for your budget.

Invisible braces are a series of clear, custom-made, removable aligners that gradually straighten your teeth as you wear them. Each aligner moves your teeth just a little bit at a time and before you know it, your smile is beautiful!

They are transparent and practically invisible aligners, so most people won’t even know you’re in treatment, they fit you perfectly, they’re more comfortable than regular braces, and they are removable, so you can eat whatever you like, yet clean your teeth normally without the hazzle of special tools
