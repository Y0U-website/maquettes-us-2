Dr. Firas Belbeisi

Close Close Dr. Firas Belbeisi Owner & General Dentist

For Dr. Firas Belbeisi—known to most patients as Dr. B—dentistry is in his DNA. Growing up in Ann Arbor, he spent weekends and after-school hours in his father’s dental office, watching what it meant to care for people through relationships, not just procedures. Today, he brings that same spirit to The Dental Experience: where dentistry feels less like an obligation and more like a connection.

After earning his Bachelor of Science in Biological Psychology from the University of Michigan, Dr. B completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Detroit Mercy. He’s been practicing for over 15 years and continues to pursue advanced education, training across the country to stay ahead of evolving clinical techniques and technologies.

Dr. B’s passion lies in oral surgery, implant dentistry , and creating treatment experiences that are efficient, comfortable, and tech-driven. Whether it’s same-day crowns with CEREC, clear aligners, or full-arch implant solutions, he’s always looking for ways to simplify complex care and make it more accessible to the people who need it.

At the heart of his practice is a deep belief in relentless improvement —not just in clinical outcomes, but in the way patients are treated, spoken to, and supported. You’ll often hear him say, “We give a damn”—and it shows in the way he leads, the way he trains his team, and the way he shows up for every patient.

Outside the office, you’ll find Dr. B spending time with family and friends, getting in a CrossFit session, playing soccer, or working on his golf swing. He’s a proud Ann Arbor native (Go Blue!) and a big believer that laughter really is the best medicine.

Experience & Affiliations

I am a board-certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon dedicated to providing advanced surgical care with precision, safety, and compassion.

After completing my undergraduate studies at Michigan State University, I earned my dental degree from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry. Dr. Daryl Frederick

Close Close Dr. Daryl Frederick General Dentist

Dr. Daryl Frederick has been caring for smiles in the Jackson community for more than four decades. Known for his commitment to advanced technology and compassionate care, he’s proud to be part of a dental team that prioritizes continuous learning, personal connection, and doing the right thing for every patient who walks through the door.

A proud graduate of Vandercook Lake High School and the University of Michigan School of Dentistry, Dr. Frederick returned to Jackson early in his career to build a practice rooted in the values of his hometown. Over the years, he has maintained affiliations with the American Dental Association and the Michigan Dental Association , and spent 16 years accredited by the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry .

Dr. Frederick is especially passionate about staying at the forefront of dentistry’s evolving technologies. He was an early adopter of digital imaging and continues to champion modern systems like CEREC same-day crowns, helping patients get efficient, top-tier care without compromise.

Outside the office, Dr. Frederick is a family man through and through. He and his wife of nearly 47 years, Linda, have two children and seven grandchildren—all living close by. Whether he’s at the practice or enjoying time with his family, Dr. Frederick brings joy, gratitude, and a deep sense of purpose to everything he does.
