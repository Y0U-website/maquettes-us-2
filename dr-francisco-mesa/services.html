Digital Dentistry

The Smile Center offers digital dentistry to improve the patient experience through digital scanning, CBCT imaging, 3D printing, CEREC imaging, and CEREC milling.

CEREC Same Day Crowns

With CEREC technology, the Smile Center is able to offer same day crowns. Patients are able to stay in their chair and leave with their new crown the same day.

Dental Implants

Our Fredericksburg and Ashland offices offer dental implants to give you a comfortable, long-lasting, and beautiful smile.

Endodontics

A root canal is a procedure where we will remove the infected pulp and the nerve if necessary. Our dentist will then seal the opening to prevent any future infection.

Tongue and Lip-tie Treatment

Dr. Butterworth utilizes state-of-the-art laser technology to perform a frenectomy procedure which will release a lip and tongue-tie in infants, children, and adults.

Oral Surgery

We have an oral surgeon on staff at our Fredericksburg office so we can take care of your surgical needs right in our office! Schedule a consultation to learn more.

Emergency Services

When emergencies happen, please call our office to schedule an appointment. We will do our best to schedule you as soon as we have availability.

Laser Technology

Our offices utilize laser technology for soft tissue surgeries. It is minimally invasive and leads to a faster recovery time.

Pediatric Dentistry

Pediatric dentistry focuses on the oral health and development of infants and children through their adolescent years.

Sedation Dentistry

Conscious sedation is an alternative for those who are nervous about dental care or those who want to have multiple dental procedures accomplished in one visit.

Invisalign

Invisalign is an orthodontic treatment in which teeth are gently corrected by using a series of custom clear aligners engineered specifically for comfort and effectiveness.

Teeth Whitening

TMJ + Myofascial Pain

TMJ can cause significant pain as the discs wear down and bone becomes exposed. If you have these symptoms, you should see your dentist to discuss treatment.

Dental Veneers

Veneers are custom-made thin coverings for the front side of your teeth. They are an option for correcting stained, chipped, decayed, or crooked teeth.

Dentures and Partials

Restorative dentistry is not just about improving the look of your teeth. Replacing or repairing teeth is vital for your dental health and for preserving your other teeth.
