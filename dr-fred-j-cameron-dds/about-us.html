Paul A. Cameron

DDS

Raised locally in Marin County, California, Paul attended Redwood High School in Larkspur. After finishing his undergraduate studies at San Diego State University, Dr. Paul A. Cameron graduated from the University of the Pacific, Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco, California.

Dr. Paul joined his father Fred’s practice in 1995. He is currently an active member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association and the Marin County Dental Association. Dr. Paul specializes in cosmetic dentistry and has received special training from the Pacific Aesthetic Continuum (PAC-live). With the well being of the patients in mind, Dr. Paul brings to the office the latest technology, ideas and equipment available in the dental fields.

Outside of the office, Dr. Paul’s personal interests include golf, skiing, roller hockey, dirt biking, kite surfing and spending time with his wife, Dr. Kristina Cameron, and their two children.

Dr. Kristina Cameron

DDS

Kristina was born and raised in Glendora, California. A southern California native, she holds ties to the University of the Pacific, Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco, California through her father, Dr. Paul Senise, DDS. Kristina received her Bachelor of Arts degree in Public Relations from from the University of the Pacific, Stockton.

In 1995, she left the field of public relations to follow a path toward dentistry. Kristina graduated from the University of the Pacific, Dugoni School of Dentistry and went on to receive a diploma from the Advanced Education in General Dentistry program from the same institution.

Dr. Kris was awarded for her excellence with the Advanced Education in General Dentistry Special Patient Award for her dedication to the comprehensive care of special needs patients.

After practicing in Novato, California for seven years, Dr. Kristina joined her husband Dr. Paul Cameron, and father-in-law Dr. Fred Cameron in their Corte Madera practice. While Dr. Fred specializes in dental implants and Dr. Paul specializes in cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Kris finds comfort in treating and caring for our most adorable and special visitors: our pediatric patients.

When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Kris can be found caring for and volunteering at the school of her two children, and enjoys golfing, tennis, skiing, and spending time with family and friends.

Dr. Fred J. Cameron

DDS

Dr. Cameron enjoys participating in off-road motocross motorcycle racing and kite boarding. His other interests include off-road bicycling, skiing and hiking the terrain around Marin County, which all keep him physically fit for his competitions.

Fred has been married to Janet, a retired elementary school librarian, since 1992 and they enjoy spending time with their three children and four grandchildren. Dr. Paul A. Cameron, DDS, Fred’s son, joined the practice in 1995 and Dr. Kristina Cameron, DDS, Paul’s wife, joined the practice in 2000.

Bobbie Miller

Office Manager

I graduated from the College of Marin Registered Dental Assisting Program having worked for Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeons for over ten years and organized, restructured and problem solved many dental offices in Marin County. I spent six years working at Marin General Hospital in the Medical Staff office working hand-in-hand with all specialities of physicians and staff. I have my Registered Dental Assisting license, Xray license, California Real Estate license, California State Manicure and massage licenses.

I’m known for my gardening ability, love computers, people, animals, reading and having fun. I have a grown son who will always be my baby - he, like Drs. Fred and Paul, loves motocross riding, mountain bike riding and all water sports.

I have been in the medical/dental world for many years now and love the fact that this office has state-of-the-art dental equipment and software. Our office is light, fun and friendly with the professionalism, knowledge and know-how to get the job done in a timely fashion. Our new dental chairs help relax our patients with countless numbers falling to sleep while being worked on! Come see us!
