What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a prosthetic tooth on a titanium shaft that is surgically attached to the jawbone with a screw. While more involved than dentures, implants don’t need to be removed and can last a lifetime. Each implant is placed individually, so they don’t put any stress on surrounding teeth and can be easily removed and replaced if necessary. They can also be used as support for a bridge, providing an alternative to partial dentures

Mini Dental Implants

At about half the diameter of traditional implants, and half the cost, mini dental implants can be an option if you need an implant to stabilize your lower dentures.
