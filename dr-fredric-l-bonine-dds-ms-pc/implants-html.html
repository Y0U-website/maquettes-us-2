Teeth-In-A-Day

Have you lost your upper and lower teeth? Are you unhappy with your dentures? Perhaps you already have dentures but you are suffering through the common problems that come with ill-fitting dentures. Not being able to eat what you like or want to eat––like a nice juicy steak. Being afraid or too embarrassed to smile? Losing your dentures from sneezing or coughing? If you were thinking that dentures were your only option, think again.

We’ve got a simple, elegant solution for people who have lost their upper and lower teeth...it’s called Teeth-In-A-Day. Teeth-In-A-Day (also referred to as the All-on-4 Technique, or Same Day Dental Implants) provides an affordable solution compared to dentures and bridges. With Teeth-In-A-Day, you’ll literally come out of the dental chair with a new set of teeth. Aftercare will require restricted chewing activity during the healing period. Most patients are able to return to work the following day and report minimal discomfort.

Teeth-In-A-Day uses dental implants that act as anchors to attach to a natural looking and permanent set of replacement teeth. Teeth-In-A-Day has many advantages over dentures. It’s a more natural look. They’re strong and durable, and they will last much longer than dentures, bridges, and partials.
