Dental Implants

  • It gives an alternative for the permanent replacement of missing teeth.
  • It also gives a comfortable and natural-looking tooth that blends in with the surrounding teeth.
  • The aesthetics of your smile will be enhanced.
  • They are strong and sturdy similar to original teeth and can last for 10-15 years with proper care and maintenance.
  • Dental implants are just like natural teeth and do not require dietary restrictions.
  • No additional care is required for the implants other than the normal brushing and flossing on a daily basis.
  • The implants will last for a long duration, and the ceramic crown may wear as long as natural enamel normally does.

How are the dental implants fixed?

The actual procedure involves first numbing the teeth by using local anesthesia. A small hole is drilled into the jawbone in place of the missing tooth, and the implant is inserted in it. The implant is fortified with dental cement. A certain time is allowed for the healing process so that the jawbone will grow around the implant’s grooves and the gums start covering the stud partially.

In the final step, the dentist will place the crown on the implant and check it for a perfect fit. It is permanently bonded using dental adhesives. A bite test is conducted, and the implant crown is polished.

What is the cost of Dental Implants?

The cost of the dental implants will be determined by various factors such as the number of teeth to be replaced, the technique, and the type of material used for the crown. In the long run, the implants’ cost will outweigh the benefits it offers for the patients.

What are the various types of Dental Implants?

  • Immediate Load Dental Implants: Implants that offer temporary tooth replacement during the first consultation with your dentist. This can be done if you have enough natural bone to secure the implant for immediate placement.
  • Mini-dental Implants: This type of implant is economical and easy to afford. It is based on a less invasive technique and is used primarily to stabilize a lower denture. It is useful for patients with bone loss.
  • Fixed Bridges: This type is perfect for patients with multiple missing teeth. It involves using both healthy teeth and artificial anchors as abutments. It is also economical in major replacements.
