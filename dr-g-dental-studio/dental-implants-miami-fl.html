How much does a dental implant cost in Miami?

Our single-tooth package is $2,699, including the implant, healing cap, abutment, and custom crown. Final cost depends on your clinical needs.

How long does the dental implant process take?

Healing and osseointegration can take several weeks to several months before the final crown is placed.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

Placement is done under local anesthesia. Mild soreness afterward is normal, and we provide aftercare guidance.

Can I get an implant if I have bone loss?

Possibly, often after bone grafting or a sinus lift. A CBCT scan and exam determine your options.
