A dental implant is a titanium post placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth’s root. Once it integrates with the bone through osseointegration, it supports a custom crown, bridge, or denture — restoring both function and appearance.

A fixed, long-term solution built around your bite, bone structure, and smile goals.
