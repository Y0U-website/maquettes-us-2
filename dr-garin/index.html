The practice

At Haddon Oral Surgeons PA , we treat each and every one of our patients as an individual, providing you with the personalized level of care you deserve. Whether visiting our oral surgeon for dental implants or to have your wisdom teeth extracted, you’re in good hands at Haddon Oral Surgeons PA .

The primary objective is to perform Oral and Maxillofacial surgery including: Wisdom teeth removal, Dental implants, procedures to aid in Orthodontics and Corrective Jaw surgery. All of these procedures are completed in a comfortable and safe environment
