Dr. Garret Guthrie

Dr. Garret Guthrie is passionate about providing high-quality, patient-centered dental care and continually advancing his skills to better serve his patients. He is deeply committed to lifelong learning and has completed extensive continuing education to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry. He attended UMKC School of Dentistry, graduating in 2015.

Dr. Guthrie has earned a Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry (FAGD ) and a Fellowship in the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (FICOI) distinctions that reflect his dedication to clinical excellence and advanced training, particularly in comprehensive and implant dentistry. He believes in combining precision, innovation, and compassion to help patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. His approach focuses on building lasting relationships and ensuring every patient feels comfortable and informed about their care.

Outside of the office, Dr. Guthrie enjoys spending time with his wonderful wife and their two daughters. As a family, they love being outdoors, and he especially enjoys golfing and making the most of time outside whenever possible.
