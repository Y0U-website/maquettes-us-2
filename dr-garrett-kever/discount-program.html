In-House Dental Membership Plan in Oviedo FL

Bliss Membership Plan

No insurance? No problem.

Lack of insurance shouldn’t dictate the health of your mouth. We have a discount plan that includes routine cleanings, x-rays, exams, and provides discounts on the cost of other dental work. Talk with us to find the best solution for you.

The Bliss Membership Plan has the following tiers and details:

Adult

Benefits

  • + 2 Dental Cleanings (absence of periodontal disease)
  • All Needed X-rays
  • 2 Periodic Exams
  • 20 % off Dental Treatment
  • 20 % off Implants
  • 10 % off Invisalign
  • 10 % off Botox

Adult Periodontal

Benefits

  • +3 Gum Therapy Maintenance Visits
  • All Needed X-rays
  • 2 Periodic Exams
  • 20 % off Dental Treatment
  • 20 % off Implants
  • 10 % off Invisalign
  • 10 % off Botox

Children

Benefits

  • 2 Regular Exams
