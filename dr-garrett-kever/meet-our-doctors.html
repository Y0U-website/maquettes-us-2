Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Garrett Kever

Dr. Garrett Kever was born and raised in Springdale, Arkansas. In 2012, he graduated from the University of Arkansas with a Bachelor of Science in Biology degree. He then continued his education and graduated from the LSU School of Dentistry with his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

Dr. Kever was selected as one of Orlando’s Dentists of the Year in 2019 by Orlando Style Magazine, and was also distinguished as a recipient for RSVP Magazine’s “The List: 40 Under 40” of up and coming professionals. In 2016 he was awarded by the Academy of Osseointegration for outstanding work in implant dentistry. He has continued to expand his expertise by taking courses in many subjects including but not limited to: Invisalign, esthetic dentistry, endodontics (root canals), surgical procedures, and implant restoration.

Dr. Garrett has a conservative approach to dentistry and he is frequently described by patients as having a calming demeanor with a gentle touch. Patient comfort and trustworthiness are of utmost importance as he strives to listen intently and answer any questions regarding treatment or oral health. He is committed to providing the highest level of care and satisfaction.

Outside the clinical setting, Dr. Kever enjoys sports, cooking, working on projects, and spending time with his family and their newborn daughter.

Dr. Brenna Kever

Following graduation from dental school, she was accepted to a position at the Birmingham VA Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency. There she served veterans while furthering her clinical expertise in complex treatment planning, dental surgery, implant placement, and endodontics. Outside of residency, she has continued advancing her skills in a variety of dental disciplines. One of her passions is treating TMJ Disorders, and she is accredited by the American Academy of Facial Esthetics for Botox treatment.

She is passionate about providing the highest quality of care and treatment to her patients. Her patients appreciate her how detail-oriented and thorough she is.

Dr. Brenna is on the board for the Florida Dental Association Public Affair’s Committee, and is active in her local dental association. She enjoys giving back to her community and has been active in the Junior League of Florida, also serving as a chair of their State Public Affairs Committee.

In her spare time, Dr. Brenna enjoys learning new skills and crafts, exercising, going to church, and spending time with her family.
