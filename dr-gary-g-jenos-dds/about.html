Caring for our community, inside and outside the office

Community Impact

At Lakeville Family Dental Center, caring for our community goes far beyond the walls of our office. We believe strong, healthy communities are built through connection, compassion, and service.

We’re proud to support Lakeville and the surrounding areas through meaningful involvement, including:

These efforts reflect our belief that good health is deeply connected to feeling supported, valued, and empowered. By investing in our community, we’re helping create brighter futures, one smile at a time.

  • Sponsoring local sports teams and community programs that help our neighbors thrive
  • Partnering with fellow small businesses as active members of the local Chamber of Commerce
  • Participating in the Giving Tree program each holiday season to support families in need
  • Mentoring future healthcare professionals through the Minnesota Center for Advanced Professional Studies (MNCAPS), where Dr. Matt, Dr. Chad, and Dr. Amanda guide students exploring careers in healthcare and dentistry
