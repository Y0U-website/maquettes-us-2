Restorative Dentistry

If your teeth have been damaged by decay, injury, or wear, our restorative dentistry services can help bring back your comfort, function, and confidence. We’ll focus on repairing damage and restoring your smile’s natural function and beauty.

After a thorough examination, your dentist will explain your diagnosis and recommend personalized treatment options.

When complex treatment is needed, we work closely with trusted local specialists to ensure you receive the highest level of care.

  • Tooth-colored fillings
  • Porcelain crowns
  • Dental bridges
  • Dentures and partials
  • Dental implants

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your smile plays a big role in your confidence. Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your smile while helping you feel more confident in everyday life.

Our skilled doctors create natural-looking results that complement your features.

Whether you’re looking for a subtle refresh or a complete transformation, we’ll work with you to create a smile you love.

Compassionate care when you need it most.

  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Porcelain veneers
  • Dental bonding
  • Smile makeovers

Emergency Dentistry

Dental emergencies can happen when you least expect them. If you’re experiencing tooth pain, swelling, trauma, a broken tooth, or other urgent concerns, our team is here to help.

We offer same-day and urgent appointments to relieve pain, address the problem, and restore your oral health quickly and safely.

If you’re having a dental emergency, please contact our office right away.

Clear Aligner Orthodontics

Misaligned teeth can affect both your appearance and your oral health. Our clear aligner orthodontic services help improve alignment, function, and long-term dental wellness for patients of all ages.

We offer modern clear aligner therapy in partnership with SureSmile .

Additional services to support your oral health.

  • A natural cosmetic makeover using your own teeth
  • Increased confidence and self-esteem
  • Improved chewing function
  • Reduced risk of tooth decay, wear, and gum disease
