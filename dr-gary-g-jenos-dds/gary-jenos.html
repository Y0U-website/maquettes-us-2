Amanda De Los Rios, D.D.S.

Amanda De Los Rios grew up in Plymouth, MN. Amanda attended college at the University of Minnesota, Morris, earning a Bachelor of Arts in biology and Spanish. Having always wanted to be a dentist, Amanda fulfilled her dream and graduated from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry with distinction and as a member of the National Dental Honor Society, Omicron Kappa Upsilon.

Amanda enjoys treating patients of all ages and hopes to help people on their path to a healthy smile, wherever they begin.

Some of Amanda’s favorite activities include baking, weekends at her parents’ cabin, and spending time with her family.

Riley Schulz, D.D.S.

Dr. Riley Schulz was born in Ashland, Wisconsin, and moved to Albert Lea, Minnesota when she was young. She grew up in southern Minnesota and moved to Minneapolis to attend the University of Minnesota. Riley graduated with a bachelor’s degree in 2018. She continued at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry and graduated May (2023).

Riley currently lives in Minneapolis with her partner, Jackson, and their two adorable cats.

Dr. Schulz is passionate about helping her patients achieve and maintain excellent oral health. She strives to create a warm and welcoming environment where everyone can feel comfortable discussing their dental needs. Dr. Schulz believes that building long-term and trusting relationships with patients is the key to providing the best possible care.

In her free time, Riley loves spending moments with family and friends. She enjoys many hobbies and activities such as drawing, reading, hiking, snowshoeing, swimming, and camping. She and Jackson hope to visit all of the U.S. National Parks in the future! You can find Riley training and competing with the Minnesota tug of war team, “DJ’s”, based right here in Lakeville.

Whether you are looking to create a beautiful new smile or simply address concerns you may have, Dr. Schulz will be committed to providing you with the highest level of care and attention.

Lauren Lizardo, D.D.S.

Dr. Lauren Lizardo grew up in Chanhassen, MN. She initially attended Duke University in Durham, NC. After deciding she wanted to pursue a career in dentistry, she transferred to Drake University in Des Moines, IA where she earned her BS in Biology and then graduated from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry with her DDS degree. During dental school, Lauren was a recipient of the competitive NHSC Scholarship. As a part of this program, Dr. Lizardo spent time practicing in a dental public health setting in both Harrisonburg, Virginia and Martinsburg, West Virginia.

Lauren is happy to have returned home to Minnesota with her husband, Will, and daughter, Anja. In her free time, Dr. Lizardo enjoys hiking, reading, seeing new places, and spending time with family and friends. The Lizardo family hopes to someday visit all the US National Parks and US Capitol buildings.
