Frequently Asked Questions

We believe in transparent pricing so you know what to expect when you come in for care.

You can pay your bill securely through our website or contact our office for assistance.

  • Do you accept my dental insurance? We accept most major dental insurance plans, including BCBS, Delta, Guardian, HealthPartners, and United Concordia/Military. We also work with many other plans as an out-of-network provider. If you’re unsure about your coverage, our team is happy to help you verify your benefits before your visit.
  • What will my first visit be like? Your first visit is all about getting to know you and your dental health. It typically includes a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays (if needed), and time with your dentist to review your goals and concerns. We’ll walk you through everything and answer your questions so you always know what to expect.
  • How much will my treatment cost? Treatment costs vary depending on your individual needs and insurance coverage. Before beginning any major treatment, we will review: Your recommended care
  • Your insurance benefits
  • Your estimated out-of-pocket cost
  • Will my insurance cover my treatment? Dental insurance rarely covers 100 % of treatment. Most plans cover a percentage of certain services up to a yearly maximum. Coverage is determined by your insurance provider, not our office. While we help you maximize your benefits, any remaining balance is the patient’s responsibility.
  • Do you offer payment plans or financing? Yes. We offer flexible financing options through our trusted partner, Sunbit, allowing you to spread payments over time. For larger treatment plans, in-office payment arrangements may also be available.
  • What if I don’t have dental insurance? No problem! We welcome patients without insurance and offer several affordable options, including our VIP Membership Plan. This plan provides preventive care and exclusive savings on treatments for a low annual fee making it easier to manage your dental care without insurance.
  • Do you handle insurance claims for me? Yes. Our team submits all insurance claims on your behalf and works directly with your provider whenever possible. If additional information is needed, we’ll guide you through the process and keep you informed along the way. description
  • Can I start treatment before insurance approval? In many cases, yes. Some insurance plans require prior authorization for certain procedures, which can take time. If treatment is needed urgently, we may recommend moving forward before approval. We’ll always discuss fees in advance so you can make an informed decision.
  • How can I pay my bill? We offer several convenient payment options, including: Online bill pay
  • Credit cards
  • Cash and checks
