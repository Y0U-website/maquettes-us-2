Les soins

Professional teeth cleaning removes plaque and tartar to help prevent cavities and gum disease. Regular visits protect enamel, support healthy gums, and reduce the need for complex treatments like a crown or other restorative procedures in the future.

After healing, impressions are taken to create a custom crown that matches the colour and shape of your natural teeth. The design focuses on restoring both appearance and proper function.

A small opening is made in the gum to place the titanium implant into the jawbone. Over time, it bonds naturally with the bone, creating a strong and stable foundation for restoration.

In cases of overcrowding, removing a tooth can create space for proper alignment. This improves bite stability and long-term function. Whether planned or done during a dental emergency, care is provided with comfort and precision.
