Restores Natural Function

A dental implant restores comfortable chewing and clear speech without slipping or movement. Placed into the jawbone, it bonds naturally through osseointegration, creating a strong base for a custom crown that looks and functions like a real tooth.

Preserves Jawbone Health

After tooth loss, the jawbone can shrink due to lack of stimulation. A dental implant acts like a natural root, helping maintain bone strength and supporting long-term oral health.

Protects Surrounding Teeth

Unlike traditional bridges, implants do not require altering neighbouring teeth. This preserves healthy enamel and maintains the natural structure and balance of your smile.

Long-Lasting Solution

With proper brushing, flossing, and regular check-ups, a dental implant can last for many years, making it a reliable option for replacing a missing tooth.

Natural Appearance

Each implant crown is designed to match your surrounding teeth in colour, shape, and size, creating a seamless and stable smile.

Request a Cost Estimate Our 4-Step Single Dental Implant Process At Family Dentistry of Niles, our 4-step Single Dental Implant process is carefully planned to restore your missing tooth with precision, durability, and a natural-looking result.

Comprehensive Examination

Your visit begins with a thorough evaluation of your oral health to identify any concerns that may affect treatment. Digital imaging assesses bone, gums, and surrounding structures to ensure implants can be placed safely and predictably.

Implant Surgery and Placement

A small opening is made in the gum to place the titanium implant into the jawbone. Over time, it bonds naturally with the bone, creating a strong and stable foundation for restoration.

Custom Crown Design

After healing, impressions are taken to create a custom crown that matches the colour and shape of your natural teeth. The design focuses on restoring both appearance and proper function.

Final Restoration & Follow-Up

The finished crown is securely attached to the implant, restoring comfort and chewing ability. Follow-up visits help monitor healing and maintain long-term success with proper care.

Our Advanced Technology and Techniques

CBCT 3D Imaging Our CBCT 3D imaging system provides highly detailed, three-dimensional views of teeth, bone, and surrounding structures. This advanced imaging technology supports precise diagnosis and treatment planning, especially for dental implants, surgical procedures, and complex restorative treatments.

Intraoral Cameras Intraoral cameras capture clear, real-time images inside your mouth. These detailed visuals help patients better understand their oral health and allow for more transparent discussions during exams and treatment planning.

Digital X-Rays Digital X-rays produce high-quality images while significantly reducing radiation exposure compared to traditional X-rays. They help us quickly and accurately detect cavities, bone loss, infections, and other dental concerns.

Digital Scanners We use advanced digital scanners to create accurate 3D images of your teeth and gums. This technology eliminates the need for messy traditional impressions and improves comfort during the scanning process. Digital scanners enhance precision when planning crowns, bridges, clear aligners, and other restorative treatments.

Nitrous Oxide Sedation For patients who experience dental anxiety, we offer nitrous oxide sedation. This safe and gentle option helps patients remain calm and relaxed throughout their treatment.

Sterilization & Infection Control Systems We maintain strict sterilization and infection control protocols using advanced equipment and industry-standard safety procedures. Patient safety, cleanliness, and hygiene remain our highest priorities.
