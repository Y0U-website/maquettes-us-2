Request a Cost Estimate Our 4-Step Tooth Colored Fillings Process At Family Dentistry of Niles, our 4-step Tooth Colored Fillings process is designed to restore cavities with natural-looking results, lasting strength, and patient comfort at every stage.

Comprehensive Examination

Your visit begins with a careful evaluation of your teeth and gums to assess decay or damage. Digital imaging may be used to detect hidden issues. We also discuss whether a composite filling or another option is best for your long-term oral health.

Gentle Preparation

The affected area is cleaned to remove decay while preserving healthy tooth structure. This conservative approach helps maintain strength and prevent further damage.

Placement of Composite Resin

A tooth-colored resin is applied in layers and shaped to match your natural tooth. Each layer is hardened with a special light for strength and durability.

Shaping and Polishing

The filling is polished for a smooth, natural finish. Your bite is adjusted to ensure comfort and proper function.
