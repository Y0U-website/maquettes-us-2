Why Dental Implants Are the Gold Standard for Tooth Replacement

No other tooth replacement option matches the stability, longevity, and natural feel of a dental implant. Bridges require shaping the healthy teeth on either side. Dentures can shift and require ongoing maintenance. Implants do neither. With proper care, dental implants can last for decades, and in some cases, a lifetime, making them the most durable and cost-effective solution available for replacing a missing tooth.

The bone preservation benefit sets implants apart in a way that is easy to overlook. When a tooth root is no longer present, the surrounding jawbone gradually resorbs. An implant replicates the function of a natural root, stimulating the bone and maintaining the facial structure that supports your appearance over time. The investment in an implant today pays dividends in your oral health for decades.

What to Expect During Your Dental Implant Treatment

Here is exactly what you can expect when you visit the best implant dentist in Bedford and Windham:

A Comprehensive Consultation We will review your oral health, take digital X-rays and scans, and assess whether you have sufficient bone density for implant placement. If bone grafting is needed, we will discuss that as part of your treatment plan.

Implant Placement Our oral surgeon places the titanium post into the jawbone in a precise, carefully planned procedure using CT guided technology.

Healing and Osseointegration Over the following weeks, the implant fuses with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This creates the stable foundation your crown will attach to. Your team will monitor your healing throughout this period.

Placement of Your Custom Crown Once healing is complete, a custom crown is fabricated from digital impressions and bonded to the implant. We check the fit, color, and bite carefully before you leave.

Long-Term Care Guidance We will walk you through how to care for your implant and what to expect at future checkups. With routine maintenance, your implant is built to last.
