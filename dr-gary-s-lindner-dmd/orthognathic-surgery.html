Why Orthognathic Surgery Can Be Life-Changing

Jaw misalignment is rarely just a cosmetic concern. Patients who undergo orthognathic surgery frequently report improvement in chewing function, relief from chronic jaw pain and headaches, better breathing, and, in many cases, a meaningful reduction in sleep apnea symptoms. Speech difficulties related to jaw positioning can also improve significantly following surgery.

The aesthetic benefits are equally real. Correcting significant jaw discrepancies often results in a more harmonious facial balance, and many patients find that their overall appearance reflects a confidence they did not have before. For patients who have lived with the functional and emotional weight of a jaw that does not work or look the way it should, surgery can represent a turning point that goes well beyond dentistry.

What to Expect During Your Orthognathic Surgery Treatment

Here is exactly what you can expect when you choose our team for corrective jaw surgery in Bedford and Windham:

A Comprehensive Surgical Consultation We will evaluate your bite, jaw structure, and facial anatomy using digital scans and imaging, and work with our orthodontic team to determine the full scope of your treatment plan.

Pre-Surgical Orthodontics In most cases, braces or aligners are worn for a period before surgery to position the teeth correctly within each jaw in preparation for surgical correction. This phase typically lasts twelve to eighteen months.

Surgical Procedure Surgery is performed under general anesthesia. The jaw bones are carefully repositioned and secured with small plates and screws. Most patients stay overnight for monitoring before returning home.

Initial Recovery Swelling, tenderness, and temporary dietary restrictions are normal in the weeks following surgery. Most patients return to work or school within two to three weeks, though complete healing of the jaw takes nine to twelve months.

Post-Surgical Orthodontics and Follow-Up After healing, orthodontic treatment continues to refine the bite and finalize your result. Our team monitors your progress closely throughout this phase and remains available to you every step of the way.

How long does the full orthognathic surgery process take? From the start of pre-surgical orthodontics through the completion of post-surgical treatment, the full process typically takes two to three years. Surgery itself is one stage within a larger coordinated plan. Your provider will give you a personalized timeline at your consultation based on the specifics of your case.

Is orthognathic surgery covered by insurance? Because orthognathic surgery addresses functional concerns rather than purely cosmetic ones, it is often covered at least in part by medical insurance. Coverage varies significantly by plan and diagnosis. Our team will help you understand your benefits and navigate the process before treatment begins.
