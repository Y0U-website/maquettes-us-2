Before I started working for Dr. Ginger I did not know much about working in a dental office. Ever since I have worked here I have learned so much about my job and dentistry, in general. I have also met many great patients. It is wonderful to form relationships with all of them. I do my very best to make sure that all of our patients are comfortable when they come in for a visit.

Back at home I have a handsome husband and two wonderful young and energetic teenagers. My family is very important to me and I am thankful to be so blessed. Outside of work I love to go for walks with friends and go shopping. In the summertime, I enjoy sitting poolside with family and friends. I also enjoy reading a good book from time to time.

In my life I cherish many people, especially my friends and family, but I would have to say that admire my husband the most. He is an honest and hard working kind of man and always supports me in everything that I do. He continually motivates me and keeps me centered. He is an excellent provider for our family and I am so grateful to have him in my life.
