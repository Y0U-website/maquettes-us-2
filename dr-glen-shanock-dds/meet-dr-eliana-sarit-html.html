Get to Know Dr. Eliana Sarit

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Eliana Sarit is a Board-Certified Orthodontist with a passion for providing high-quality care. She earned her Doctorate in Dental Surgery at Universidad Iberoamericana, graduating Magna Cum Laude. After relocating to New York City, she completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry program at Columbia University, where she had the honor of serving as Chief Resident. Dr. Eliana then moved to Philadelphia to complete her specialty training in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics, alongside a Master of Science in Oral Biology.

Dr. Eliana is deeply committed to lifelong learning, regularly attending continuing education courses, seminars, and conferences to stay up-to-date with the latest advancements in orthodontics. She is an active member of several professional organizations, including the American Association of Orthodontists, the American Board of Orthodontics, and the American Dental Association and the Richmond County Dental Society, where she serves as part of the board.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Outside of the office, Dr. Eliana enjoys spending time outdoors, traveling, work out classes-shoutout to Pure Barre and F45! She also loves quality time with family and friends and trying out creative kitchen recipes from the ‘gram.
