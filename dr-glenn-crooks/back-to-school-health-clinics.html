Appointments for immunizations and health screenings can be scheduled at our Cherokee , Fannin , Gilmer , Murray , Pickens , and Whitfield County Health Departments. Simply click on the county name to view contact information and hours of service.

Parents may also bring their children to special back-to-school events hosted by or featuring our health departments, which will provide services. No appointment is required for these events. Please stay up to date as we continue adding school health clinic events taking place this summer throughout the North Georgia Health District!

  • Rising Eleventh-Grader Immunizations: Children 16 years and older entering the 11th grade must have received a booster dose of the meningococcal conjugate vaccine (MCV4) unless their initial dose was administered after their 16th birthday.
