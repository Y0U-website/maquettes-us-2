Meet Our Doctor

Glenn M. Wilson, DMD Dr. Glenn Wilson completed his undergraduate studies at Trinity College before attending Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, where he graduated with his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in 1989. He continues to complete continuing education courses yearly in order to stay up-to-date with the latest treatment techniques and provide his patients with excellent care. He has also received advanced training in implant restorations. Dr. Wilson has practiced in the New Milford, CT, area for 25 years.

As a dentist, Dr. Wilson is committed to providing state-of-the-art dental care for the entire family in a small, private setting. He takes the time to provide care for each patient’s individual needs and wants his patients to feel comfortable with him and his team and know that they always have their patient’s best interest in mind.

When not at the office, Dr. Wilson enjoys spending time with his son, Christopher.
