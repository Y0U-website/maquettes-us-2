Why Choose Implant-Retained Dentures?

Improved Stability - Unlike traditional dentures that can shift or move while you eat or speak, implant-retained dentures are securely anchored to dental implants. This eliminates the discomfort and inconvenience of loose dentures, giving you a more natural and confident experience. Here at Svoma Design in Dentistry we offer a one stop solution at our family run practice in Avon, Indiana for anyone wishing to get Implant-Retained dentures.

Greater Comfort

Traditional dentures can sometimes cause sore spots or irritation. Implant-retained dentures provide a snug fit, reducing the risk of discomfort and making it easier to enjoy your favorite foods and everyday activities.

Better Oral Health for a Better Life Implant-retained dentures are easier to clean and maintain compared to traditional dentures. They don’t trap bacteria and plaque as easily, promoting better oral hygiene. With the added stability and ease of use, patients often find their confidence and quality of life improve dramatically—they can eat, speak, and smile with freedom and ease.

A Long-Term Solution for Your Smile Implant-retained dentures provide a natural-looking and reliable option for tooth replacement. By improving stability, function, and oral health, they can transform the lives of patients dealing with missing teeth.
