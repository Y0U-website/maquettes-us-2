Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Membership

Is the Sayva Membership Plan dental insurance?

No. The Sayva Membership Plan is an in-house dental membership plan and dental savings plan, not dental insurance. It gives members included preventive services, one complimentary emergency exam, and a 10 % discount on additional treatment without deductibles, yearly maximums, claim forms, or waiting periods.

How much does the dental membership plan cost?

The Adult Wellness Plan is $485 per year or $48 per month. The Child Wellness Plan is $375 per year or $34 per month.

When does coverage start?

Membership starts after enrollment and the membership fee, with no waiting periods.

What services are included?

Membership includes 2 annual dental cleanings, 2 dental examinations, 2 oral cancer screenings, all necessary radiographs, one professional fluoride treatment, and one complimentary emergency exam.

Do members get discounts on other dental treatment?

Can children join the membership plan?

Yes. Children can join through the Child Wellness Plan for $375 per year or $34 per month.
