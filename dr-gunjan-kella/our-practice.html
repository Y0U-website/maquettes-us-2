Custom designed for you, we work with you to understand how we can reshape, realign, and whiten your smile.

Covered by most PPO insurance! Check yours

In office whitening: 60-90 minute treatment that delivers 2-3 shades whiter. Great for those short on time and want fast results. Take home: Custom fabricated trays made to achieve superior and long lasting results. Maintain and whiten as often as you like. Great for control freaks: since you control the results and you control your schedule. Also good for coffee, wine, and tea drinkers, aka frequent stainers.

*Recommended: In office whitening with custom trays included. Value package and pricing for those looking for not only immediate results, but also want to optimize and maintain those pearly whites for their lifetime.

We recommend preparing for a whitening treatment with a cleaning within the last 2 weeks. Cleaner teeth, improved results and less sensitivity.

We take care of your teeth, but with your wishes, preferences, and priorities in mind. We only use tooth colored fillings that are safe and long lasting. We research and purchase the best materials and use the latest technologies for a predictable process, leading to better dental outcomes for all.
