1. Zirconia is Metal-Free and Biocompatible

One of the primary reasons dentists choose zirconia implants is that they are metal-free. Unlike titanium implants, zirconia is a ceramic material, making it an excellent option for patients with sensitivities or metal allergies. Dr. Gyu Hwang, who practices holistic dentistry in Annandale, VA, values zirconia for its high level of biocompatibility. This means zirconia integrates well with natural tissue, reducing the risk of inflammation or adverse reactions.

2. Superior Aesthetics

A significant advantage of zirconia dental implants is their natural, tooth-like appearance. Zirconia is white, blending seamlessly with the rest of your teeth, even if your gums recede slightly over time. In contrast, titanium implants can sometimes show through as a grayish hue beneath the gums. For patients who prioritize the aesthetics of their smile, zirconia provides a more natural and visually appealing option.

3. Reduced Risk of Plaque Build-Up

Another reason dentists favor zirconia is that it has a smooth surface that resists plaque build-up more effectively than titanium. Plaque accumulation around implants can increase the risk of peri-implantitis, an infection that affects the gum and bone surrounding the implant. By choosing zirconia implants, dentists like Dr. Gyu Hwang help reduce the likelihood of bacterial growth and improve the overall long-term health of the implant site.

4. High Strength and Durability

Zirconia is known for its impressive strength and durability, making it an excellent choice for patients who need a long-lasting solution. Although some may assume that metal, like titanium, is stronger, zirconia dental implants are incredibly resilient and can withstand significant bite forces. This makes them comparable to titanium in terms of functionality while offering additional benefits like improved aesthetics and biocompatibility.

5. Zirconia is Corrosion-Resistant

Unlike titanium, which can corrode in the presence of certain bodily fluids and environmental factors, zirconia is highly resistant to corrosion. This makes it a stable, long-lasting material for dental implants. Dentists who prioritize holistic approaches, like Dr. Gyu Hwang, often prefer zirconia for patients concerned about the long-term effects of introducing metals into their bodies.

Seeking An Alternative To Titanium Dental Implants?

Zirconia dental implants offer several key advantages over traditional titanium implants, including improved aesthetics, biocompatibility, and plaque build-up and corrosion resistance.
