Invisalign Treatment

What is invisalign?

Invisalign is a modern way to treat misaligned teeth. Instead of attaching metal brackets and wires to your teeth as traditional braces do, Invisalign uses custom-made clear aligner trays. These aligners are worn over your teeth and strategically exert pressure to shift the position of your teeth, just like traditional braces. When used properly, you’ll start to see results in just a few weeks.

Invisalign aligner trays are made of high-quality plastic that is tailored to fit your teeth exactly. Using advanced diagnostic imaging, Dr. Najjar can accurately predict how long your treatment will take. We can even use a 3D computer model to show you how your treatment will progress so you can get a sneak peek at your future smile!
