Common orthodontic issues we treat.

Orthodontic treatment can correct a wide variety of dental problems, including:

  • Overcrowded Teeth When teeth overlap or are too close together, it can make cleaning and flossing difficult, leading to plaque buildup and decay.
  • Gaps Between Teeth Spaces between teeth can cause aesthetic concerns and may also affect how your teeth function.
  • Overbite An overbite occurs when the upper teeth significantly overlap the lower teeth, potentially causing tooth wear or jaw discomfort.
  • Underbite In an underbite, the lower teeth extend beyond the upper teeth, leading to bite and speech problems.
  • Crossbite A crossbite occurs when some upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth rather than outside, which can cause uneven wear and alignment issues.
  • Open Bite An open bite is when the upper and lower teeth do not meet properly when the mouth is closed, often due to habits like thumb-sucking or tongue-thrusting.

Orthodontic treatment options.

At Nuwave Dental & Orthodontics, we offer a range of orthodontic treatment options to fit your lifestyle and preferences. Dr. Najjar will discuss these options with you during your consultation to help you determine the best solution for your smile.

  • Traditional Braces Traditional metal braces are one of the most common and effective methods for straightening teeth. They consist of metal brackets bonded to the teeth and connected by a wire, which is adjusted over time to gently shift your teeth into the proper position. Today’s metal braces are more comfortable and discreet than ever before, making them a popular option for patients of all ages.
  • Invisalign If you’re looking for a more discreet way to straighten your teeth, Invisalign may be the ideal solution. Invisalign uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners that gradually shift your teeth into place. These aligners are virtually invisible and can be removed for eating, brushing, and flossing, offering added convenience and comfort. Invisalign is a popular choice for both teens and adults who want to straighten their teeth without the appearance of traditional braces.

The orthodontic treatment process.

At Nuwave Dental & Orthodontics, we strive to make your orthodontic experience as comfortable and efficient as possible. Here’s what you can expect during your orthodontic treatment journey:

  • Consultation During your initial visit, Dr. Najjar will assess your teeth, bite, and jaw alignment using X-rays and other diagnostic tools. We’ll discuss your goals and recommend the best treatment options for your unique needs.
  • Treatment Plan Once we’ve determined the right treatment, we’ll create a customized plan that outlines the estimated timeline, costs, and any necessary follow-up visits.
  • Fitting the Appliance Whether you choose traditional braces, Invisalign, or another option, Dr. Najjar will ensure a precise and comfortable fit.
  • Adjustment and Monitoring Throughout your treatment, we will schedule regular check-ups to adjust your appliance and monitor your progress. These visits ensure that your teeth are moving as planned.
  • Completion and Retention Once your teeth are properly aligned, we’ll remove your braces or stop your aligner treatment. A retainer will be provided to help maintain your new smile and prevent teeth from shifting back to their original positions.
