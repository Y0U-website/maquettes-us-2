Straight teeth are healthy teeth.

Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of plaque buildup, cavities, and gum disease. This alignment also promotes better jaw function, reducing the likelihood of jaw pain and headaches caused by misalignment issues. Additionally, straight teeth facilitate proper chewing and digestion, leading to improved nutrition absorption. Beyond the health advantages, orthodontic treatment at NuWave Dental enhances self-confidence and mental well-being, promoting a happier and more fulfilling life.

Clear aligners / Invisalign

Clear aligners are an orthodontic treatment that uses removable alignment trays to straighten teeth. Your dentist will fit you with custom-designed alignment trays that slowly shift your teeth into the desired position. Every few weeks, you will receive a new set of trays until your teeth are perfectly aligned. The trays are barely visible in your mouth your friends, family and coworkers may not even notice they are there. The smooth plastic surface will not scratch or damage the inside of your mouth and you can remove the trays for eating, cleaning, and special occasions.

Traditional brackets

Traditional brackets are small brackets that are bonded directly to each tooth and attached with a wire. These traditional brackets and wires are far less noticeable than they used to be! Children and adults of all ages are choosing traditional brackets for their orthodontic needs near 60561.
