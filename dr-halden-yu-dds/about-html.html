About

Dr. Halden Yu grew up in San Francisco and attended Lowell High School and UC Berkeley. He graduated from UC San Francisco School of Dentistry in 1995 and later completed a post-doctoral General Practice Residency program at Los Angeles County-University of Southern California Hospital.

Dr. Yu believes in providing excellent care to his patients and commits more than 100 hours per year in his busy schedule to attend continuing education courses. Some of his advance training include cosmetic dentistry training at UC Los Angeles School of Dentristy, orthodontic training with Dr. Rondeau, and a 2-year occlusion and TMJ treatment program at the premier Foundation for Advanced Continuing Education in Burlingame, CA.

He is a member of the Academy of General Dentist and is working towards attaining fellowship status in the academy, which is awarded to members with an outstanding knowledge of all fields of dentistry.

Dr. Yu currently lives in Burlingame with his wife and two young children. Outside of the office, he enjoys gardening, swimming, and spending time with his family.
