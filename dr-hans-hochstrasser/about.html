About Us

DR. Renee Vogel

Dr. Renee Vogel is a highly skilled and compassionate dentist at Elkhorn Family Dentistry, committed to creating a warm, welcoming environment where patients of all ages feel cared for and confident in their smiles. A proud Marquette University graduate, Dr. Vogel combines her strong academic foundation with a gentle, personalized approach to oral health.

Beyond her practice, Dr. Vogel enjoys a rich and balanced life—she can often be found flowing through yoga sessions, exploring new destinations on her travels, or getting lost in a good book. She cherishes quality time spent with family and friends, believing that these meaningful connections are just as important to wellbeing as a healthy smile.
