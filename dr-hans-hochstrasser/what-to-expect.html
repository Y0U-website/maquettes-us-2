What To Expect

Elkhorn Family Dentistry is here to provide a great experience from the time you give us a call to walking out smiling after your appointment. Comfort, efficiency, and a great smile are our mottos. If you want to learn more about why we’re different from other dental offices simply click the link below.

When You Get Here

Check-In

So you took the leap and scheduled your appointment. When you arrive at your appointment we will gladly welcome you at the front desk. After a quick hello it’s time to fill out your paperwork if you’re a new patient. Please arrive 15 minutes early to fill out your paperwork.

Meeting Your Dentist & Hygienist

Once your paperwork is completed you will be able to meet with your doctor and hygienist to start your appointment. Now sit back, relax, we got the rest.

During Your Appointment

Now the fun starts! Remember comfort, efficiency, and a smile is our motto. Our team will go through your exam, procedure, or consult to get you the results you’re looking for. Throughout the whole appointment we are dedicated to your comfort. It’s our job not only to keep you smiling but also care for you and your health.

After Your Visit

Sadly our brief time together has come to an end. Once the appointment has concluded we will guide you out with care just like you entered. The team will let you know of any necessary follow up appointments to get you scheduled in. We will also provide you with any tips to keep you comfortable, healthy, and smiling between now and the next time you see us.
