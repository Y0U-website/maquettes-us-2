Types of Dental Implants We Offer

At Advanced Smile Center, we provide a range of implant options to suit different needs:

Single-Tooth Implants

Ideal for replacing one missing tooth, a single-tooth implant consists of a titanium post, an abutment, and a custom crown for seamless restoration.

Implant-Supported Bridges

If you have multiple missing teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge can replace them without requiring individual implants for each tooth. This offers a stable, cost-effective solution.

All-on-4® Full Arch Implants

For patients missing all or most of their teeth, All-on-4 implants provide a full-arch restoration using just four strategically placed implants per arch. This method offers a permanent alternative to traditional dentures.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Traditional dentures can slip and cause discomfort. Implant-supported dentures lock into place for a secure, comfortable fit with no adhesives required.

The Dental Implant Process

1. Consultation and Evaluation

Dr. G will conduct a comprehensive dental exam, take 3D digital scans, and discuss your goals to determine if implants are right for you.

2. Implant Placement

During the procedure, the titanium implant is surgically placed into the jawbone. IV sedation is available for patients who prefer a relaxed experience. Learn more about our IV sedation services .

3. Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant integrates with the jawbone, creating a stable foundation for your new tooth.

4. Abutment and Crown Placement

Once healing is complete, a small connector called an abutment is placed on the implant, followed by a custom crown, bridge, or denture for a fully restored smile.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The implant itself is permanent, while the crown or denture may need replacement after 10-15 years.

Is the implant procedure painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and sedation is available for added comfort. Most patients report minimal discomfort, similar to a tooth extraction.

How long does the entire process take?

The implant process typically takes 3-6 months, allowing time for the implant to fuse with the bone before placing the final restoration.

Can I get an implant immediately after a tooth extraction?

In some cases, same-day implants can be placed immediately after extraction. However, this depends on bone health and other factors. Dr. G will determine the best approach for your situation.

What if I don’t have enough bone for an implant?

If you have insufficient bone density, bone grafting may be necessary to build up the jawbone before implant placement.
