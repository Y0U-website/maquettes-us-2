Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacement you can count on.

Missing teeth can affect more than just your appearance—they can impact how you chew, speak, and feel about your smile. At Sherman Oaks Dentistry, we offer Dental Implants as a long-lasting, natural-looking solution to restore function and confidence.

A dental implant replaces the root of a missing tooth with a small titanium post, which supports a beautiful crown, bridge, or denture. Whether you’re missing one tooth or several, implants offer unmatched stability, durability, and comfort.

What It Treats

• Jawbone deterioration (when paired with bone grafting)

• Difficulty chewing due to gaps or missing teeth

Treatment Time

3 to 6 months total , depending on healing and case complexity.

Some patients may qualify for immediate placement with a temporary crown .

Technology Used

• High-strength biocompatible titanium implants

• Precision-crafted crowns and restorations

Treatment Process

Your dental implant journey is a step-by-step process designed for comfort and precision:

Comprehensive Evaluation

We use digital X-rays and 3D imaging to assess bone health and plan precise implant placement.

Surgical Implant Placement

We place a lifelike crown, bridge, or denture on top of the implant to complete your new smile.

Healing & Osseointegration

Over several weeks, the implant naturally fuses with the jawbone to ensure long-term support.

Custom Restoration

You’ll receive personalized care, state-of-the-art technology, and exceptional results every step of the way.
