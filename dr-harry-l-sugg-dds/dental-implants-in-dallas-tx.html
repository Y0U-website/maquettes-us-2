FAQs

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular dental visits, implants can last for many years and often decades.

Can one implant replace one tooth?

Yes. A single tooth implant is commonly used when only one tooth is missing.

What are full mouth dental implants?

Full mouth dental implants are used to support a complete arch of replacement teeth when most or all teeth are missing.

Are dental implants permanent?

Permanent dental implants are designed as a long-term tooth replacement solution and can last many years with proper care.

Do dental implants look natural?

Yes. The final restoration is custom-made to blend with your natural smile.

What are same day dental implants?

In certain cases, same day dental implants allow a temporary restoration to be placed shortly after implant placement. Eligibility varies from patient to patient.

We will help you find a tooth replacement solution that feels comfortable, looks natural, and fits your lifestyle.
