Following these steps will help you achieve the best possible results throughout your Invisalign journey.

  • Remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water.
  • Brush and floss before putting your aligners back in.
  • Clean your aligners daily using lukewarm water and a soft toothbrush or approved cleaning solution.
  • Store your aligners in their protective case when not in use.
  • Attend your scheduled progress appointments to keep treatment moving forward.

FAQs

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on your smile, but many patients complete Invisalign treatment within 12 to 18 months.

Can I eat while wearing Invisalign?

No. You should remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water, then brush your teeth before putting them back in

Is Invisalign better than traditional braces?

For many patients with mild to moderate alignment concerns, Invisalign offers a comfortable and discreet alternative to traditional braces. During your consultation, we will determine the best treatment for your needs.

Where can I find an Invisalign dentist in Dallas, Texas?

Wheatland Dental Care is a trusted Invisalign dentist in Dallas, Texas, providing personalized treatment for teens and adults.

How many hours should I wear my aligners each day?

For the best results, Invisalign aligners should be worn for 20 to 22 hours a day.
