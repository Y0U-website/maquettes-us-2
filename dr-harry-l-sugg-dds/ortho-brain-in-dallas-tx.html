FAQs

How is Orthobrain different from traditional braces?

Orthobrain uses removable invisible aligners instead of metal brackets and wires, making treatment more discreet and convenient for everyday life.

Who is a good candidate for Orthobrain?

Patients with mild to moderate crowding, spacing, or minor bite concerns are often good candidates. A consultation will determine whether Orthobrain is right for you.

How long does Orthobrain treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on your smile and orthodontic needs, but many patients complete treatment within 12 to 18 months.

Can I remove my aligners while eating?

Yes. Orthobrain aligners should be removed before eating or drinking anything other than water and placed back in after brushing your teeth.
