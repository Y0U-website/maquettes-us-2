Taking good care of your braces helps keep your treatment on schedule and your smile healthy.

  • Floss daily using orthodontic flossers or threaders.
  • Avoid hard, sticky, and chewy foods that could damage your braces.
  • Wear any elastics or appliances exactly as instructed.
  • Attend your scheduled adjustment appointments.
  • Continue routine dental checkups and professional cleanings.

FAQs

Who is a good candidate for braces?

Braces are suitable for children, teens, and adults with crooked teeth, crowding, spacing issues, or bite concerns.

What types of braces do you offer?

We provide metal braces and can discuss other orthodontic treatment options during your consultation based on your smile goals.

How long will I need to wear braces?

Treatment time varies depending on your orthodontic needs, but many patients wear braces for 12 to 24 months.

Do braces hurt?

You may experience mild soreness for a few days after your braces are placed or adjusted, but this usually improves quickly.

Are ceramic braces available?

Depending on your orthodontic needs, ceramic braces may be an option. We&#x27;ll discuss the best treatment choice during your consultation.
