About Dr. Harshiv Karia

Prosthodontist. Digital dentistry educator. Thouron Scholar. Trained in London and Philadelphia, now at UConn Health.

The practice

Dr. Karia completed his BDS at Queen Mary University of London, earned his MPros from the Royal College of Surgeons of England, and received his Certificate in Prosthodontics from the University of Pennsylvania as a Thouron Scholar. His research focuses on digital prosthodontics, implant outcomes, and modern approaches to complete mouth rehabilitation.

He is a member of the American College of Prosthodontists, the Academy of Osseointegration, and the International Team for Implantology (ITI).
