The practice

In our practice, we consider every patient a valued member of our extended family. From routine six-month checkups and cleanings to urgent dental treatments, we are here to address all aspects of your dental well-being.

Dr. Sidky approaches dental care with a gentle touch, utilizing minimal invasive dentistry techniques to ensure your experience is as comfortable as possible. With a conservative philosophy, she places emphasis on recommending only necessary dental work, prioritizing your oral health while respecting your individual needs and concerns.

Join our dental family at Sidky Family Dentistry, where exceptional care meets a warm and friendly atmosphere. Experience dentistry that goes beyond the routine – it’s about fostering lasting relationships and ensuring your smile radiates with health and confidence.
