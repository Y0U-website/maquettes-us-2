The All-on-4 Implant Process at Lanier Valley

It is important to understand that installing All-on-4 dental implants near you is a different process compared to applying a single implant.

Your journey will start with a comprehensive assessment. Dr. Lentz will gather information about your history and your dental goals. On surgery day, you’ll be given local anesthesia or dental sedation to help you stay relaxed. Marks will be made in your gum tissues, indicating where the implants will be drilled into your jaw. Questions About All-On-4 Implants

What are All-on-4 dental implants?

All-on-4 dental implants are a dental restoration technique that uses four strategically placed implants to support an entire arch of teeth (either upper or lower). It is an efficient solution for individuals with multiple missing teeth or those needing full arch reconstruction.

How do All-on-4 implants differ from traditional dental implants?

All-on-4 implants use only four implants per arch to support a complete set of teeth, while traditional implants may require more implants. The strategic placement of these implants provides stability and support for a complete set of prosthetic teeth.

Who is a good candidate for All-on-4 dental implants?

All-on-4 implants are suitable for individuals with significant tooth loss or those facing the prospect of complete edentulism (loss of all teeth in one arch). Qualified candidates should have sufficient bone density to support the implants.

How do I care for All-on-4 dental implants?

Oral hygiene is essential to the long-term durability of All-on-4 implants. Brushing, flossing, and seeing the dentist regularly are all important. Your dental team will provide specific care instructions based on your individual needs.

Can All-on-4 implants be used for both upper and lower arches?

Yes, All-on-4 implants can be used for both upper and lower arches. The versatile technique can provide a stable and functional solution for full-arch tooth replacement in either jaw.

What is the cost of All-on-4 dental implants?

The cost of All-on-4 implants can vary based on factors such as location, the complexity of the case, and the materials used. During your consultation, your dental team will provide a personalized treatment plan and discuss associated costs.
