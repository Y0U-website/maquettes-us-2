Questions About Dental Implant

How do dental implants work?

Dental implants function as anchors for replacement teeth. The implant attaches to the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. Once the implant is stable, a dental crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant, providing a secure and natural-looking restoration.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Generally, individuals with good overall health and adequate bone density in their jaws are considered good candidates for dental implants. Your dentist will assess your oral health and medical history to determine if implants are a suitable and appropriate option for you.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Dental implants offer several benefits, including improved aesthetics, enhanced chewing function, prevention of bone loss in the jaw, and a long-lasting solution that can often last a lifetime with proper care.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, so patients do not feel pain during the surgery. After the procedure, some discomfort and swelling are common, but they can be managed with prescribed medications.

Can dental implants replace multiple missing teeth?

Yes, dental implants are versatile and can be used to replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, or even an entire arch of teeth. They can support individual crowns, bridges, or implant-supported dentures, offering flexible solutions to address different dental restoration needs.

Can smokers get dental implants?

While smoking can potentially hinder the success of dental implants by delaying the healing process, it does not automatically disqualify individuals from receiving implants. Smokers are encouraged to quit or reduce smoking to enhance the likelihood of successful implant integration.
