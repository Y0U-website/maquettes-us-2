Questions About Multiple Tooth Implants

What are multiple tooth implants?

Multiple tooth implants, also known as implant-supported bridges, are dental prosthetics designed to replace several missing teeth. They consist of dental implants strategically placed in the jawbone, supporting a bridge that mimics the appearance and function of natural teeth.

How do multiple tooth implants differ from traditional bridges?

Unlike traditional bridges, multiple tooth implants don’t rely on adjacent natural teeth for support. Instead, they are anchored securely to dental implants, preserving the integrity of surrounding teeth and promoting better long-term oral health.

Is the implant placement procedure painful?

The procedure for placing implants is normally carried out with the use of local anesthesia, ensuring minimal discomfort for the patient. Patients may experience some soreness afterward, but it is manageable with prescribed medications and generally subsides within a few days.

Are multiple tooth implants easy to maintain?

Yes, maintaining multiple tooth implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups are essential for optimal oral health. Our team will provide detailed post-implant care instructions to ensure the longevity of your new smile.

Is the cost of multiple tooth implants covered by insurance?

Coverage varies among insurance plans. It’s advisable to check with your insurance provider to determine the extent of coverage for dental implant procedures. Our team can also assist in navigating insurance-related inquiries.
