What are dental implants?

Dental implants are prosthetic structures that act as tooth roots to replace missing teeth. They are mostly made of Titanium and are surgically placed into the jawbone beneath the gums where the tooth is missing. The nature of your implant surgery would depend on the condition of your jawbone and the type of implant being placed. Patients can benefit from implants as they would provide a stable base for your teeth.

Why would you need implants?

Dental implants serve as artificial tooth roots to replace missing teeth thereby improving the quality and health of your smile. As they are surgically implanted into the jawbone, implants won’t slip, cause any bone damage, or make noise. It is also necessary that the patient has adequate bone structure and healthy gums to hold the implant in place. What is the procedure involved in getting dental implants? Getting your dental implants would involve three different phases:

  • One or missing teeth
  • Teeth shifting
  • The dentist would place the implant into the jawbone via surgical methods.
  • A certain healing period would be advised that would allow the bone to thoroughly heal around the implant. This process is otherwise known as osseointegration.
  • After this phase, the dentist would place an abutment post on top of the implant. The restoration in the form of a crown would be attached to this post. This would serve as an artificial tooth. Single tooth replacements would only require a single crown.
