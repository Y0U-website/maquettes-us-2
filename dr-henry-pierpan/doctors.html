Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Henry J. Pierpan

Dr. Henry is a retired U.S. Navy captain and served as a Dental Corps officer at Camp LeJeune with over 30 years of military service. Henry is a charter member and past president of the Hampstead Kiwanis Club . He enjoys his work in the community while serving on the board for NC Parks & Recreation. Dr. Henry enjoys golfing, hunting, fishing and studying the martial art of Hapkido with his two sons. He is also an adult leader for Troop 19 Boy Scouts in Wilmington.

Dr. Monica M. Pierpan

Drs. Henry and Monica are both members of the American Dental Association , Tri-County Dental Society, and the NC Dental Society.

Drs. Henry and Monica live in Scott’s Hill with their two sons and two dogs. They enjoy boating and camping as a family as well as traveling to visit family and friends.

Dr. Andrew Troupes

Dr. Andrew Troupes was raised in Charlotte, NC. He attended the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill where he became a life long Tar Heel fan and earned a bachelor’s degree in Economics in 2012. Dr. Andrew received his D.M.D degree in 2017 from East Carolina University and has since made the Wilmington area his home. He is passionate about being a lifelong student in order to provide the highest quality dentistry in a comfortable environment. In his free time, Dr. Andrew enjoys spoiling his dog, golfing, the beach, and traveling.

Dr. Jordan Holmgren

Dr. Jordan Holmgren was raised in a small town in rural Minnesota. He attended the University of North Dakota, where he earned his Bachelor of Science degree in Zoology. Dr. Holmgren received his DDS degree from the University of Minnesota in 2021 and has since made the Hampstead area his home. He is passionate about providing high-quality, comfortable dental care and building lasting relationships with his patients.

In his free time, Dr. Holmgren enjoys spending time with his wife and four children. Together, they enjoy camping, playing board games, and spending time at the beach.
