Cosmetic Dental Bonding

Sometimes it’s the little details that get in the way of a brilliant smile. Cosmetic bonding can be used to give your teeth a quick touch up, hiding small chips, cracks, or discolored spots. This procedure uses composite resin, a tooth-colored substance that is also used for fillings. Once we’ve finished molding and blending the material, very few people can tell that the tooth ever had any changes made to it in the first place.

Cosmetic Dentistry FAQs

Is Cosmetic Dentistry Expensive?

Many patients assume cosmetic dentistry involves higher upfront costs, especially since these treatments aren’t typically covered by dental insurance. However, this doesn’t necessarily mean you’ll have to pay a hefty out-of-pocket fee to improve your teeth. Firstly, our team offers alternative financing options like CareCredit that can help break up your treatment costs into manageable monthly payments with almost zero interest. You’ll also want to consider the benefits of boosting your self-esteem and quality of life, which can make your cosmetic procedure a worthwhile investment.

Is Cosmetic Dentistry Safe?

Most cosmetic dental services are safe to undergo as long as you’re receiving treatment from a qualified professional. That said, you’ll need to first schedule a consultation with our team so that we can look over your oral health. In some cases, cosmetic dental treatments can end up posing a risk to your smile if there are developing issues that haven’t been treated, such as decay and gingivitis. Before moving forward with your procedure, we’ll address the problem to ensure your treatment has the best chance for success.

How Do I Know If I Need Cosmetic Dentistry?

Do you often hide your teeth when smiling? Do you wish you could change something about your pearly whites? If so, then you might benefit from cosmetic dentistry. Whether you have stained, gapped, or mildly damaged teeth, our team offers procedures that are designed to enhance the look and health of your smile. To determine which option is best for you, we’ll need to check your oral situation, discuss your dental goals, and then walk you through which of our treatments can most benefit you.

How Long Does Cosmetic Dentistry Last?

The lifespan of your cosmetic dental results will depend on the type of service you choose. Some treatments will naturally last much longer than others. For instance, professional teeth whitening can brighten your smile and go as far as several months to a few years, whereas the results from dental bonding can generally last around 10 years before needing touchups. To get a more accurate timeframe estimate, our team can look over your oral health and discuss your treatment options along with their potential lifespan.
