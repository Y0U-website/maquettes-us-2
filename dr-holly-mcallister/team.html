Our Team Dan Merker, Dentist Born and raised in Zanesville, Ohio, Dr. Daniel Merker has dedicated his career to providing compassionate dental care to his community. After graduating Cum Laude from Miami University with a Bachelor of Arts degree in zoology, he pursued his passion for dentistry at The Ohio State University College of Dentistry, where he earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree with honors.

Following his education, Dr. Dan joined his father’s private practice in Zanesville, where he honed his skills and dedicated himself to carrying on family traditions in dental care. He continues to serve alongside his son, Dr. Brian Merker, ensuring that their family’s legacy of exceptional dental care lives on.

Dr. Dan is honored to be part of dental honorary societies such as Omicron Kappa Upsilon, The American College of Dentists, and The Pierre Fauchard Academy. He has held leadership roles in organizations such as the Muskingum Valley Dental Society and the Zanesville Kiwanis.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Dan finds joy in his family, faith, and hobbies. Married since 1986 to his wife Leslie, he cherishes time spent with his children, Brian and Mae, as well as his four grandsons. Dr. Dan’s passions extend beyond the dental office; he enjoys sports, woodworking, stained glass, and spending time outdoors. Additionally, he currently serves as an elder at the Muskingum Valley Vineyard Church.

Driven by his passion to serve and improve lives, Dr. Dan embraces each day with dedication and enthusiasm, embodying the values of hard work, compassion, and family that he learned growing up on a farm in Ohio.

Dr. Brian Merker was born in Zanesville, Ohio in 1989. He graduated from Tri-Valley High School in Dresden, Ohio in 2008. He did his undergraduate training at The Ohio State University. Upon completing his requirements a year early, and he then entered The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery in 2015. He is proud to be the fourth-generation dentist in our Zanesville practice.

Dr. Brian is a member of the Ohio Dental Association, a branch of the American Dental Association. He is also a member of several conservation organizations.

Brian was married in May of 2015 to his beautiful wife Brianna. He has two boys Luke and Levi. He and his wife both enjoy the outdoors. Brian enjoys fishing, hiking, woodworking and nature. The thing that brings him the most joy is his faith in Jesus Christ, to whom he owes everything.
