Meet Dr. Randone

Dr. Randone is dedicated to creating and maintaining healthy smiles for children that will last a lifetime, and believes that early prevention is the key to doing so. Dr. Randone has attended several continuing education courses on orthodontic treatment to diagnose and treat early growth and development needs in children. Furthermore, Dr. Randone places strong emphasis on providing positive and educational dental experiences for her patients and their parents.

Married to husband, Joe, they have a daughter Mila. Dr. Randone’s hobbies include reading, golfing, and spending time with family and friends.

Dr. Randone is available for appointments at the Grand Island and Hastings locations.
