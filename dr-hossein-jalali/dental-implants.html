What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants New Brighton are artificial tooth roots. Titanium implants provide a permanent base for replacement teeth, either fixed or removable. Their natural look blends easily with your natural teeth, and they’re durable and long-lasting.

Process of Dental Implants

Here is how we perform our dental implants New Brighton procedure with precision and care:

  • Our experts assess your dental health and see if you are a suitable candidate. We use scans and X-rays to plan the procedure.
  • We plan a customized treatment customized to your specific needs, outlining each step of the process.
  • Our dentists surgically place the titanium implant into your jawbone. This implant works as a strong foundation for your new tooth.
  • We allow time for osseointegration. It is a process where the jawbone naturally fuses with the implant, ensuring stability.
  • Once the healing period is complete, we attach an abutment to the implant. This will hold the new tooth securely.
  • Finally, we place a custom-made dental crown onto the abutment. It gives you a natural-looking and fully functional tooth.
