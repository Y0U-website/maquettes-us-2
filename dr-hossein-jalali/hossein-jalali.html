Dr. Hossein Jalali, DDS

Dr. Hossein Jalali attended Rochester Mayo High School and The University of Minnesota. In 1988 he graduated, with honors, from The University of Minnesota School of Dentistry. Upon graduation, Dr. Jalali held a faculty position of Associate Professor at The University of Minnesota School of Dentistry.

In 1988 he started Long Lake Family Dentistry. For over 30 years, Dr. Jalali has provided exceptional, comprehensive dental care to families in New Brighton and the surrounding area.

As cosmetic dentistry has evolved, so too has Dr. Jalali’s ability to transform the smiles of his patients and give them new confidence and self-esteem. Dr. Jalali believes strongly in the importance of continuing education with the express purpose of remaining at the forefront of innovation in the fields of comprehensive and cosmetic dentistry.

He is a member of The American Dental Association and is an Invisalign Gold Provider. Dr. Jalali loves soccer and enjoys spending time outdoors.
