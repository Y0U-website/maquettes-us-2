What Is a Single Tooth Implant?

It replaces a missing tooth with a strong, natural-looking solution. It consists of three parts:

  • The Implant: A titanium post placed into your jawbone. It acts like a tooth root.
  • The Abutment: A small connector that attaches to the implant.
  • The Crown: The part that looks like a tooth. It’s custom-made to match your smile.

Who Needs It?

  • People who want a permanent solution.
  • Anyone tired of using removable dentures or partial bridges.
  • Patients looking to avoid damage to their healthy teeth.

Steps of the Single Tooth Implant Process

At Long Lake Family Dentistry, we handle your entire implant process from start to finish. Here’s what you can expect from our single implant New Brighton, Minnesota process service:

  • We start by examining your mouth and taking 3D images to see if you’re a good candidate for an implant.
  • The implant goes into your jawbone during a minor surgery. Our dentists use local anesthesia to ensure you feel comfortable throughout the procedure.
  • Once the implant is in place, your jawbone needs time to heal. This process (osseointegration) usually takes a few months. During this time, the bone grows around the implant, making it strong and secure.
  • After your implant heals, we attach an abutment to the implant. This small piece holds your new crown in place.
  • Finally, we place a custom crown on top of the abutment. We craft the crown carefully to match your tooth color, shape, and size.

AfterCare & Recovery

Most patients recover from implant surgery within 2 to 3 days, while full healing typically takes 3 to 6 months. Here are some tips to make sure your recovery goes smoothly:

  • Rest and take it easy for a few days after surgery.
  • Stick to soups, mashed potatoes, and smoothies. Avoid hard or crunchy foods.
  • Gently brush around the implant area to keep it clean.
  • Listen to your dentist’s advice for aftercare.
  • Avoid smoking because it slows down your healing process.

How Long Do Single Tooth Implants Last?

A single tooth implant New Brighton, MN is a long-term solution. The implant itself can last a lifetime if you take care of it. The crown may need replacing after 10 to 15 years. Good oral hygiene, regular dental checkups, and healthy habits help your implant last longer.
