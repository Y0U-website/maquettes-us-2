Hubert W. Hawkins IV MPH DDS FAAID DABOI/ID (Dr. Hugh)

A resident of Northern New Hampshire, Dr. Hawkins enjoys spending quality time with his family and German Shepherds, officiating for USA Swimming competitions, snow skiing, mountain biking, waterskiing, windsurfing, and an occasional triathlon.

Dave DeSalvo, DDS, FICOI, FAAIP

Dr. Dave earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Stony Brook University. During his four years in dental school, he completed four surgical externship programs, eleven community outreach initiatives, and was a contributing author for an American Student Dental Association biannual newsletter. He also simultaneously completed his Master’s degree in Sustainability from Harvard University, where he conducted independent research on the utilization of Passivhaus design for residential homes in the state of New Hampshire.

Dave is a resident of the Lakes Region of New Hampshire. His passion for the state and its residents is a major driving factor for him to continue learning and consistently improving his work. He is a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists as well as a Fellow of the American Association of Implant Prosthodontics.

During his free time, Dave enjoys rock climbing, exploring New Hampshire, and expanding his knowledge in various fields. He continues to tutor and mentor both pre-professional and dental students. Now a hobbyist, he has retired his career as a competitive runner where he was a 2x USA Track and Field Club National medalist and founder of a successful post-collegiate club team. Our Dental Staff

Frank Valenti, MS, CRNA

Frank Valenti, MS, CRNA is board certified in Nurse Anesthesia and Non Surgical Pain Management and is principal and owner of Plymouth Anesthesia Associates, PLLC (PAA). We are your sedation professionals and look forward to helping make your dental visit a pleasant experience. With over 17 years of experience, PAA works closely with your dentist to ensure a safe, comfortable sedation experience in the office setting. Our goal is to provide a customized, pleasant sedation plan for you and to make your dental visit as stress free as possible.

We look forward to working with you and welcome any questions you may have.
