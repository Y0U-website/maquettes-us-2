The practice

Comprehensive treatment planning allows patients to fully understand their options before moving forward. The goal is simple: help each patient achieve and maintain optimal oral health while preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible.

Modern dentistry means having access to comprehensive care in one convenient location. The practice provides a full range of general, cosmetic, and restorative services to meet the needs of children, adults, and seniors alike.

Whether you need preventive care or advanced restorative treatment, solutions are delivered with precision and a gentle touch.

Thorough evaluations include oral cancer screenings and careful assessment of the gums, teeth, and supporting structures. Education plays a key role, empowering patients to make informed decisions about their dental health.

Preventive care helps avoid serious and costly dental problems while supporting a confident, healthy smile.

Francis Dentistry is led by Dr. Scott Francis, DDS, FAGD, and Dr. Hunter Francis, DDS both Hampton natives with decades of clinical experience and strong ties to the community.

Ongoing continuing education ensures the practice stays current with evolving dental techniques, materials, and technology. Commitment to professional development reflects a dedication to providing high standards of care for every patient.

A highly trained team supports every visit, delivering personalized service from the front desk to the treatment room.
