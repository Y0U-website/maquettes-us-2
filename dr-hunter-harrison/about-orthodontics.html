About Orthodontics

Orthodontic treatment addresses these concerns by correcting bite issues, improving oral health, and enhancing your smile.

  • Tooth Decay and Gum Disease : Crowded or crooked teeth can be harder to clean, increasing the risk of cavities and gum disease.
  • Jaw Pain and Headaches : An improper bite can place extra strain on your jaw muscles, leading to discomfort, headaches, or even disorders of the jaw joint (the temporomandibular joint or TMJ).
  • Aesthetics and Confidence : A straight, healthy smile can boost self-esteem and improve your overall appearance.

How does orthodontic treatment work?

Orthodontic treatment works by applying gentle, constant pressure to the teeth over time, gradually moving them into the desired position.

  • Consultation and Planning : Dr. Harrison will first conduct a thorough examination, which may include X-rays, photographs, and digital impressions of your teeth. Based on this, a personalized treatment plan is created. Sometimes you are not ready for treatment due to uneruption of teeth or preliminary treatment needed before orthodontic treatment begins. Dr. Harrison will help coordinate all these things so that you will have a winning smile when your treatment is complete.
  • Appliances : Depending on your needs, Dr. Harrison may recommend traditional braces, clear aligners, or other specialized appliances. These devices help guide the teeth into proper alignment.
  • Regular Adjustments : Throughout your treatment, you’ll visit your orthodontist every six to eight weeks for regular checkups and adjustments. Each adjustment allows the appliances to continue guiding your teeth toward their ideal position. The initial wires are heat activated and tuned to your body s temperature so they will try to put your teeth into an ideal archform based on the wire s shape, bracket prescription and position. Stainless steel finishing archwires are later adjusted to make small changes to individual teeth and your bite to reach the final positions.
  • Retention : After your teeth have been moved into place, you’ll wear a retainer to ensure the teeth stay in their new positions and don’t shift back. Many times Dr. Harrison will use a fixed wire behind intially rotated or spaced teeth to help maintain their ideal relationships.

How can you make orthodontic treatment successful?

Success in orthodontic treatment requires a combination of regular orthodontic care and good personal habits.

With dedication and proper care, orthodontic treatment can deliver long-lasting, life-changing results, leaving you with a healthy, beautiful smile!

  • Follow Your Orthodontist’s Instructions : Whether it’s wearing aligners for the recommended time each day or showing up for regular adjustments, sticking to Dr. Harrison’s plan is crucial. Non-compliance can increase your treatment time or lessen its success.
  • Maintain Excellent Oral Hygiene : Brushing and flossing regularly is essential, especially with braces or aligners, to prevent plaque buildup and cavities. Decalcification and tooth decay can result from poor oral hygiene, but not from the braces themselves.
  • Avoid Harmful Habits : Chewing on hard objects (like ice or pens) or eating sticky, crunchy foods can damage braces or disrupt the treatment process. Multiple broken appliances will lengthen your treatment time.
  • Wear Retainers as Prescribed : Once your treatment is complete, wearing retainers will ensure your teeth stay in their new position and don’t shift back over time.
