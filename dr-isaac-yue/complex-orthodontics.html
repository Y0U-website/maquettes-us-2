Why does Dr. Yue’s craniofacial background matter for complex cases?

Complex cases require an orthodontist who understands how the face, jaws, and teeth develop together. Dr. Yue has a specific passion for helping craniofacial patients and conducted patented research during his training at Harvard. That depth of experience shapes how carefully he diagnoses and plans demanding cases.

How do I find out if my case is complex?

Book a complimentary consultation. Dr. Yue examines your bite and jaw relationship and tells you honestly what level of treatment your case needs. If a simpler approach will work, he will recommend it. Call (630) 323-7906 to schedule.

Sleep Apnea

Obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) can affect the health and quality of life of individuals suffering from it. Here at Willowbrook Orthodontics, Dr. Yue works closely with patients to identify possible signs of OSA and offers orthodontic solutions that can help open the airway and improve breathing during sleep. Our goal is to support better rest, better health, and a better quality of life.

Vpro5

Shorten orthodontic treatment time with VPro5, whether you choose traditional metal, clear braces, or Invisalign. It uses high-frequency vibrations to stimulate the bone surrounding your teeth and ensure your aligner trays are seated better. These micro-pulses speed up tooth movement comfortably by using the AcceleDent mouthpiece for 20 minutes daily.
