Orthodontic Care for Darien Children, Teens, and Adults

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a child’s first orthodontic check around age seven. For Darien families with kids at Mark DeLay, Lace, or Eisenhower Junior High, that early visit gives us a chance to monitor growth, identify potential crowding or bite issues, and recommend Phase I treatment only when it would clearly benefit your child. Early visits are informational and short.

Teen treatment is the largest part of our practice. Darien teens at Hinsdale South or Eisenhower often begin treatment between sixth and ninth grade, and we coordinate appointment times around school schedules, sports seasons, and exam weeks. Both braces and Invisalign Teen are available, and we openly discuss the trade-offs so each family can choose the option that fits.

Adult orthodontics has grown steadily as more Darien professionals look for discreet treatment options. Clear aligners and clear ceramic braces both let adult patients improve their bite and smile without the look of full metal brackets. Dr. Yue also treats complex adult cases involving jaw position or severe crowding that benefit from coordinated care with an oral surgeon.
