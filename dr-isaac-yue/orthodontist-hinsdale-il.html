Orthodontic Care for Hinsdale Children, Teens, and Adults

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a child’s first orthodontic check around age seven. For Hinsdale families, that often lines up with the second-grade year at Madison, Monroe, or Oak Elementary, when adult teeth begin erupting and bite issues become visible. We use these early visits to monitor growth, identify potential crowding, and decide whether Phase I treatment would simplify a later full course of braces or aligners. Early visits are short and informational. There is no pressure to begin treatment unless it would clearly benefit your child.

Teen treatment is the largest part of our practice, and Hinsdale Central and Hinsdale Middle School families know the rhythm well. We coordinate appointment times around school schedules, sports seasons, and exam weeks, and we offer both braces and Invisalign Teen so each family can choose what fits.

Adult orthodontics has grown steadily as more Hinsdale professionals look for discreet treatment options. Clear aligners and clear ceramic braces both let adult patients improve their bite and smile without the look of full metal brackets. Dr. Yue also treats complex adult cases involving jaw position, severe crowding, or bite issues that benefit from coordinated treatment with an oral surgeon.

Why Hinsdale Patients Choose Willowbrook Orthodontics
