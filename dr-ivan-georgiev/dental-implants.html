Dental Implants

Dental implants replace missing teeth and provide a long-term solution imbedded in your jawbone. They look just like your natural teeth. Additionally, dental implants do not affect neighboring healthy teeth or lead to any bone loss in the jaw. When properly cared for, implants can last a lifetime.

There’s no short cut to get around dental implant surgery. However, Dr. Georgiev has the specialized education and training to deal with the complexities of the bone, skin, muscles and nerves involved and will ensure you get the best possible results.

Dental Implant Surgery

Dental implant surgery may be done to replace a single tooth, several teeth or to support a full set of dentures. During your initial consultations, Dr. Georgiev will discuss the treatment plan and procedure, and will find out what is right for you.
