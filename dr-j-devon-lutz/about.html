JOY CALLAHAN

Hygienist

Joy Callahan provides exceptional oral care to our patients. She has years of hands-on experience and training that has enabled her to help countless patients regain and maintain a beautiful smile. She performs comprehensive dental cleanings and conducts thorough exams on new patients to review their overall oral health. She takes gum measurements, checks for signs of tooth decay, and periodontal disease. Lastly, she records detailed notes of any conditions that are being treated and the specific procedures performed.

Our Facility

Duxbury, situated about 35 miles south of Boston, is a historic residential community on the Atlantic coast in Plymouth County, Massachusetts. The coastal setting significantly contributes to the town’s unique character. Our Class A Medical office building, spanning 14,190 square feet, features handicap accessibility, a welcoming Koi pond garden entry, and a timeless Cape Cod cedar exterior.
