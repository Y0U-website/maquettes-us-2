Meet Dr. Greg Osborne

Dr. Osborne has been providing exceptional orthodontic care in the Birmingham area since 1994 . He is a dedicated member of the American Association of Orthodontists, American Dental Association, Alabama Dental Association, Southern Association of Orthodontists, and the Birmingham District Dental Society .

Dr. Osborne and his wife, Kathryn , have three children: Tucker, Griffin, and Audrey . He is an active member of Church of the Highlands , serving on the Dream Team in Events and as a Small Group Leader and Host . In his free time, he enjoys Bible study, kayaking, reading, fitness, and participating in obstacle course races .
