Meet Dr. Paige Whitt, DMD, MS

A Gadsden, Alabama native, Dr. Paige Whitt attended the University of Alabama , where she graduated summa cum laude with a major in Mathematics and a minor in Blount Liberal Arts . She was inducted into Phi Beta Kappa , the nation ’ s oldest and most prestigious academic honor society.

Dr. Whitt earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Alabama School of Dentistry , where she was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon dental honor society. She then pursued advanced specialty training in orthodontics at Louisiana State University , where she completed her orthodontic residency and earned a Master of Science in Orthodontics .

Dedicated to staying at the forefront of orthodontic advancements, Dr. Whitt is an active member of the American Association of Orthodontists, American Dental Association, and the Southern Association of Orthodontists .

Outside of the office, Dr. Whitt enjoys running, skiing, tennis, yoga, biking, outdoor activities, and spending time with family and friends .
