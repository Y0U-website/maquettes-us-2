The practice

Our philosophy at Dr J. Spinnato Family Dentistry is to address all of your oral health care needs. We accomplish this by taking a patient-centred approach. Each patient is unique with individual needs. Through one-on-one conversations we will work together with you to provide the ideal approach to address your preventative, restorative and periodontal dental care needs.

Brush your teeth twice a day with either a manual or electric toothbrush. The best times to brush are first thing in the morning and before going to bed at night. Be sure to brush for at least two minutes each time.

Our practice provides restorative dental procedures in a warm and caring atmosphere. Treatments such as: tooth extractions, dental implants, gum and bone grafting can improve and restore your oral health. Dr. Spinnato will make your visit a comfortable experience.

Dr J. Spinnato Family Dentistry provides preventative, cosmetic and pediatric dental care in Sarnia. We help our patients achieve optimal oral health through a full array of dental services to patients of all ages.
