Orthodontics

Orthodontics is a specialty branch of dentistry that focuses on diagnosing, treating, and preventing tooth or jaw misalignment. Orthodontic treatments involve using retainers, braces, and other specialized appliances to align the teeth and jaws properly. What We Offer

Space Maintainers

Space maintainers are appliances used in dentistry to keep enough space open for the permanent tooth to grow. These appliances are often used when a baby tooth is lost prematurely, either due to decay, trauma, or injury. They can help maintain the space until the adult tooth can erupt. At Swartout Dental, we offer space maintainers, which are used to preserve the space of a missing tooth until a permanent one grows. These maintainers are available in various sizes and shapes and can be fixed or removable.

What We Offer

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are another popular form of orthodontic treatment that aligns teeth without the use of traditional braces. They are a series of clear, plastic devices that cover your teeth and gradually guide them into their correct position. Clear aligners are removable and nearly invisible, making them a great choice for people who don’t want to wear traditional braces. Dr. Swartout can work with you to ensure you achieve your desired smile.

Benefits of Orthodontic Treatments

They can fix crowded teeth

Most orthodontic appliances can help fix crowded teeth by slowly moving them into their correct position. This helps keep your teeth straight and can create a more aesthetically pleasing smile.

Correct overbites

An overbite occurs when the top front teeth overlap with the lower top teeth. Using clear aligners and other orthodontic devices, the teeth can be shifted into a more suitable position, correcting overbite.

Correct underbite

An underbite occurs when lower top teeth overlap with upper top teeth. This can be corrected by wearing braces or other orthodontic appliances to move the teeth and jaws into the proper position.

Treat impacted teeth

Dr. Swartout may recommend braces or other corrective appliances to reposition the impacted teeth so they can erupt correctly.

Correct gaps between teeth

This can be treated using various orthodontic devices to help shift teeth into a more suitable position.

Fix protruding teeth

Orthodontic treatment for protruding teeth may include retainers, clear aligners, braces, and other appliances.
