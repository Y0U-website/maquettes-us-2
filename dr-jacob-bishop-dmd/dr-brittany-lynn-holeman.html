PERSONAL

Dr. Brittany is a Louisville native with strong ties to the Oldham County community. She believes in preventative and conservative dental treatment while focusing on patient comfort. Its Dr. Brittany’s belief that your dental experience should be relaxing and even enjoyable. Dr. Brittany has been happily married to Craig, a North Oldham Middle School teacher since 2007. Together they have three young children, Aviana, Carson and Avalena. Dr. Brittany loves to travel, attend her children’s school events and cheer on the University of Louisville Cardinals.
