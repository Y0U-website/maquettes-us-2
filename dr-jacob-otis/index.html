We designed and built this office to challenge all those staunchly held and negative assumptions about root canal treatment.

The practice

We believe that root canal therapy does not need to be a painful or difficult experience. Integrity Endodontics is equipped with all the most current technology to deliver the best care through the most peaceful process.

Our goal is to deliver that care in a way that centers on the person, not on the tooth. With decades of experience behind us, we are moving forward in a new direction.

Teeth come with people attached to them. Our goal is to care for the whole person as well as the tooth. 30 years as a practicing endodontist brings the highest level or experience and expertise into caring for our patients in Newport Beach. We look forward to sharing these benefits with you.

We are committed to using the most current tested and safe technologies. Combining these with the best available instruments & materials will allow us to achieve the best clinical outcomes in a comfortable patient centered environment. We will treat our patients in a warm and friendly atmosphere so that our office is comfortable for them and for us as well.

This caring & friendly culture benefits our patients, and it makes a healthy and gratifying place for our staff to grow and develop as well.
