0 Jacqueline A. Fellows, DMD, Ficd Dr. Fellows joined the dental practice of Gerald R. Theberge D.D.S. in 1992 as an associate. They later formed the partnership of Professional Dental Care. After growing up in Connecticut, Dr. Fellows graduated from Boston College in 1985 and The University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine in 1989. She completed her general practice residency in 1990 from Monmouth Medical Center in New Jersey.

As a past school board member, winter sports and booster club volunteer as well as other activities, Dr. Fellows has been involved in the Brattleboro community. Affiliations include Academy of General Dentistry, American Dental Association, Vermont Dental Society, the International College of Dentists, and the New England Dental Society. Pastimes include outdoor activities, walking, downhill skiing, water-skiing, and boating. She and her husband Michael now live in Antrim, NH.
