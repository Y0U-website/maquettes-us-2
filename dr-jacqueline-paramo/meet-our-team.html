We’re proud of our expertise but even prouder that patients and partners say, “You’re just fun to be around.”

  • Genuine friendliness : No stiff scripts. No forced smiles. Just real people who like people.

Community-Minded Dental Care in the Heart of the Rivertowns

Dr. Paramo and her highly qualified staff take pride in offering professional services in a warm, friendly atmosphere. We take great care to make you feel welcome, understand your needs, and provide treatment options tailored to you.

By weaving together dental technology with compassionate care, we provide comprehensive exams and cleanings , as well as children’s dentistry , cosmetic dentistry services , Invisalign treatments , oral surgery , and more.

Our Location

Our new location at Rivertowns Square is conveniently located just off the Saw Mill Parkway, where Dobbs Ferry, Hastings, Ardsley, and Irvington meet. We’re close to neighborhood favourites like Starbucks, Ulta Beauty, and MOM’s Organic Market just look for our sign directly across from New York Sports Club!

Parking is easy, with spaces available out front and indoor parking located at the back of the building.
