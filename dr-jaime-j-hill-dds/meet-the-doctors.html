JEREMY W HILL was born in Worland, and raised in the southwest Wyoming town of Green River. Jeremy attended Northwest College in Powell, WY and the University of Wyoming in Laramie, earning his Bachelor Degree in Molecular Biology in 2000. He then followed his dream to becoming a dentist by entering Creighton Dental School Class of 2006. Upon earning his DDS Degree, he moved to Cody, WY and took on the challenge of starting his own dental practice. His determination and quality of character are the backbone of what makes Hill Family Dentistry the outstanding dental practice it has grown to be.

Just 2 years after opening the doors, he was able to bring in a second dentist, his wife Jaime. They now have 4 children and enjoy spending every available moment with them. The family enjoys fishing, hunting, camping and touring the winding roads in and around Wyoming. Jeremy is a member of the American Dental Association and serves as the Northwest District Director of the Wyoming Dental Association.
