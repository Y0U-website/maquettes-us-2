Dental Tooth Implants

Using a titanium alloy anchor that’s safe for your body, an implant is permanently attached to your jaw. Dental implants are stable, sturdy, and do not have to be removed regularly like dentures or a bridge. Rather than attaching to other teeth, a dental implant is supported by your jaw bone and is as natural of a solution for missing teeth as you can find.

At Valley Village Dental, our team uses state-of-the-art equipment to make the process as comfortable as possible. Initially, we’ll implant the anchor and allow roughly six months for it to heal and become permanently affixed to your jaw. Then, we will attach the custom made implant and after ensuring that everything is secure, you’ll be on your way with a beautifully improved smile!

Whether you’re interested in one or two implants to fill in some gaps or you’re in need of dentures and require implants as anchors, our staff can help you to determine a treatment plan that will best meet your needs.
