Meet The Valley Village Dentists

Experienced and Compassionate Dentists in Valley Village & Los Angeles

Combining years of experience with a family-friendly focus, our dental team provides the highest level of dental care in Valley Village and the greater Los Angeles area. From education and practical tips for home care to state-of-the-art equipment that makes your visit a breeze, we’re committed to your oral health.

You’re in good hands at Valley Village Dental, as Drs. Michelle and James Grosleib and their team are highly qualified and stay up to date on advances within the dental industry.

Dr. Michelle Grosleib

I love to get to know my patients, and chat about their lives. My motherly instincts bring out the most compassionate and caring part of me, which allows me to be patient, understanding and supportive to anxious adults and children. I am proud and honored to have the trust and confidence from my patients. I never take for granted how difficult it is for some patients to allow themselves to relax in the dental chair. I feel empowered and fulfilled when my most anxious patients leave with a smile and give me the thumbs up!

For my formal education, I attended the University of California, San Francisco, which is a top-ranked school. I graduated top in my class.

Taking continuing education is an integral part of broadening my scope of knowledge. Courses I have taken include cosmetic procedures, restorative dentistry, periodontal therapy, implant restorations and ClearCorrect invisible orthodontic braces. My commitment to continuing education encompasses not only lifelong learning for myself, but also my passion for giving instruction to others.

Dentistry includes everything that I love about life: taking care of others, cutting edge medicine, creativity and hands-on treatment, and meeting fascinating people. I thrive to be the best and most approachable dentist I can be. I believe that the treatment planning session is a two-way street. I love to educate and include my patients in the decision making process.

With children, I believe in the long-term, lifetime positive associations with dentistry. I believe that children deserve the respect of being told what to expect in a creative and playful manner. I have seen some children grow from scared and confused three-year-olds to confident older children who had to have extensive dental care, but no longer feel threatened. We listen and learn from each other every day. With patience, positive reinforcement and a little imaginative distraction, the sky is the limit!

I grew up in Valley Village, and currently reside very close to our office, in Burbank. My husband is Dr. James Grosleib, and we were high school sweethearts. We have two beautiful girls (Eliana and Isabel) and one sweet little boy (Ronen). All share a special place in my heart. When I’m not working, I enjoy spending time with my family, leisure walks on the beach, reading, snorkeling and fine dining.

I strive to be a part of the community, and do so by offering underserved individuals the ability to obtain dental care. I also participated in Give Kids a Smile, where children receive free dental screening. I have received an award for the most-attended student at the community dental clinic at UCSF, where I served homeless patients. I spent months at a community dental clinic in Eureka and worked with top surgeons at the San Francisco General Hospital Oral Surgery department where I provided care to the underserved.

Dr. James Grosleib

I consider the dental profession as a true art, allowing me to serve residents of Valley Village with new and innovative treatments that provide them with the highest level of satisfaction. Being a dentist isn’t simply a 9-5 job for me, as I value the connections I make with each and every patient and appreciate the ability to develop oral health plans that meet their specific needs.

Education is a large focus of my practice, and I take continuing education to ensure that I can offer patients the best in dental care. My experience includes cosmetic procedures, endodontic root canals, periodontal management, gum treatments, ClearCorrect invisible orthodontics, and much more. I also actively partner with Give Kids A Smile to offer dental screenings to children and enjoy the ability to get to know my patients and provide them with a better quality of life.

Ultimately, it’s the human connection of dentistry that gives me so much satisfaction. Getting to know my patients like they’re a part of my own family is priceless, and it’s these relationships that I value above and beyond offering comprehensive care. Valley Village truly feels like home as I grew up in Los Angeles and get to work with my high school sweetheart, and now wife, Dr. Michelle Grosleib, every day. Our daughters are my pride and joy, and when I’m not in the office I enjoy reading, going on vacation, playing games on my iPhone, and spending time with my family.
