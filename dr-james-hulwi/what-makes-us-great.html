Our Team

Our team is not only specialized in smiles, but they are specialized in making sure you enjoy your time at Orthodontic Specialists of Southern Minnesota each and every time you walk through the door! After all, we know that our office is only as welcoming as the people who work in it, which is why we take such pride in our friendly and caring staff.

Patient-Centered Care

Every patient is unique, which is why you will always receive customized treatment based on your specific needs. Dr. Hulwi will meet with you to discuss your orthodontic concerns, review your photos and x-rays, and develop a treatment plan specifically for you. We are also experts early orthodontic intervention, which often times can reduce the need for extracting teeth, something we like to avoid doing unless absolutely necessary. Throughout your treatment journey, we will explain each and every step and are happy to answer any questions you may have.

Affordable Payments

We never want finances to stand in the way of orthodontic treatment, which is why we offer flexible payment plans and interest-free financing at no extra cost. We can help determine if you have any orthodontic coverage through your insurance provider and file it on your behalf. To learn more about our financing options, click here .

Innovative Technology

Dr. Hulwi is a committed, life-long student of orthodontics, meaning he continues to learn about innovative and technology-driven advancements in orthodontics. In our clinic, we utilize start-of-the-art equipment, including an iTero Scanner, which takes hundreds of photos per second to replicate a 3D scan of your teeth, pretty cool right? We also offer ceramic braces and clear aligners, in addition to traditional metal braces, as more cosmetically atheistic options. With Dr. Hulwi’s expertise and today’s technology, tooth movement is more efficient and treatment times are faster.
