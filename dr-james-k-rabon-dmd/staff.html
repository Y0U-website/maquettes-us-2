Dr. James Keith Rabon DMD

Dr. James Keith Rabon is a native South Carolinian, being born in Kingstree, South Carolina. He graduated from Camden High School in 1979, and from Clemson University in 1983 where he received a Bachelor of Science Degree in Economic Zoology. Dr. Rabon earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine degree from the Medical University of South Carolina College of Dental Medicine in 1989 and has practiced General Dentistry in Sumter since January 1995. Dr. Rabon is married with three children. He enjoys various outdoor activities, especially hunting and family days at the lake or beach.

Our practice focuses on developing a relationship with each of our patients. We view our patients as friends and as the individuals they are. Upon meeting each patient we formulate a detailed treatment plan for them focusing on long term oral health. We believe in the concept of complete dentistry to maintain the dentition, optimal gum health, and the chewing capabilities everyone needs to be healthy.
