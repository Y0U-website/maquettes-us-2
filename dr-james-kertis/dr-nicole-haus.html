DR. NICOLE HAUS

A Healthy Life Begins With A Smile

Dr. Haus grew up in Vadnais Heights, Minnesota. She completed both her undergraduate education and her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree with high distinction from the University of Minnesota in 2014. After dental school, she completed a residency at the University of Minnesota Fairview Riverside Hospital, where she gained valuable experience treating patients with head and neck cancer, organ transplants, and complex medical conditions.

Dr. Haus is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable, cared for, and confident in their dental health. She strives to create a warm and welcoming environment where patients of all ages know they are receiving dental care from someone who truly cares about them.

She now lives in Hudson with her husband Jon and their two daughters, Tenley and Teagan. Outside of the office, she enjoys staying active outdoors, watching live music, traveling to the Caribbean waters, fishing, and rooting for her favorite Minnesota sports teams.

Total Health

Dr. Haus is always focused on her patients total health and not just their teeth. She believes her patients decisions affect more than their oral health. A healthy lifestyle simply starts with a smile.

Personable Approach

It is a key component of Dr. Haus’s care to keep a personable approach with her patients. It is one way she can keep her patients comfortable and healthy throughout their experience.

Lasting Relationship

Dr. Haus continually focuses on treating her patients as if they were family. She wants to have a lasting relationship with every patient to guarantee the success of their overall health.
