DR. RYAN TIETZ

A Dentist That’s Here For You

Dr. Tietz is originally from New Prague, Minnesota. He attended St. John’s University where he earned his Bachelor of Arts in Natural Science and minored in Theology. As an undergraduate, he was an NCAA All-American and two-time Scholar All-American in wrestling. Upon graduation, he attended the University of Minnesota, School of Dentistry. After graduating from dental school, Dr. Tietz was commissioned as a Captain in the United States Air Force.

When not at the office, Dr. Tietz enjoys spending time with his family, outdoor activities, fishing, hunting, hiking, sports and volunteering.

No Concerns. No Problems.

Dr. Tietz wants his patients to feel at ease during their time at Oakwood Dental. With an easy-going attitude and personalized care he will leave you without concern and wishing you could come back sooner.

Quality Care

Striving to give you the smile you deserve Dr. Tietz takes pride in his top-notch care. He allows his patients to have a variety of options and details exactly what you can achieve with your smile.

Honest Approach

Honesty is paramount at Oakwood Dental. Dr. Tietz is a straight shooter. He will give you a clear cut path for both your health and smile without any biases. You will be in good hands with Dr. Tietz.
