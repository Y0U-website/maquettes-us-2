The Role of Technology in Precision Placement

Accuracy matters enormously in implant dentistry. At Precision Cosmetic & Family Dentistry, we use CBCT scanning and digital intraoral scanning to map the jaw and plan placement before a single incision is made. This level of detail allows Dr. Kortbus to position implants with confidence, reducing guesswork and supporting better long-term outcomes for our patients.

The Dental Implant Process

The implant process unfolds over several appointments, though the timeline varies depending on your starting point and whether any preparatory work is required.

Initial Consultation and Planning

The first step is a comprehensive evaluation. We review your dental and medical history, take detailed 3D scans of your jaw, and discuss your goals. If there are any issues with bone volume or gum health, we address those before placing the implant. Our oral surgery experience allows us to handle preparatory procedures and implant placement under one roof, which simplifies the process for patients considerably.

Implant Placement and Healing

Once we confirm that your jaw is ready, the titanium post is placed during a surgical appointment. A healing period follows while the implant bonds to the bone, typically spanning several months. During this time, we keep a close eye on your progress and can fit you with a temporary restoration if needed.

Restoration and Final Results

After osseointegration is complete, the final crown or prosthetic is secured to the implant. We use our in-house digital dentistry technology, including same-day crown milling, to create restorations with a precise fit and natural appearance. The digital dentistry tools we rely on make this stage both efficient and accurate, so you leave with a result that matches your surrounding teeth seamlessly.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants in New Smyrna Beach

Who is a good candidate for dental implants? +

Most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth are potential candidates for dental implants. Adequate jawbone density and healthy gum tissue are important factors, and we evaluate your oral health with a consultation and imaging before recommending treatment. If additional bone support is needed, bone grafting can be done first.

What does the dental implant process involve? +

The process typically starts with a consultation and 3D imaging, followed by surgical placement of the implant post. After a healing period while the implant integrates with the jawbone, we attach a custom crown or restoration using our in-house digital dentistry technology for a precise fit.

How long does it take to complete dental implant treatment? +

Timelines vary based on your starting point and whether preparatory work like bone grafting is needed. Many cases take several months from placement to final restoration to allow proper healing, and we’ll map out a timeline specific to your case during your consultation.

How much do dental implants cost, and are financing options available? +

Implant cost depends on how many teeth are involved and whether any preparatory treatment is needed. We work with CareCredit and Cherry financing, including a 0 % interest plan for up to 24 months, and also offer an in-house membership plan for patients managing costs without traditional insurance.

How long do dental implants last? +

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term tooth replacement option when properly placed and maintained with routine dental care. We use CBCT scanning and digital planning to support accurate placement, which contributes to a stable, long-lasting result.
