The practice

Dr. James Kortbus leads Precision Cosmetic & Family Dentistry in New Smyrna Beach, where he has built a career around treating patients as individuals rather than appointment slots. Here is a closer look at his training, his professional involvement, and how he approaches the work itself.

Dr. Kortbus was raised in Port Orange and graduated from Stetson University before pursuing his dental degree at New York University College of Dentistry. Choosing to study in New York gave him exposure to a large, diverse patient population early in his training, which shaped how he thinks about treating patients from very different backgrounds and with very different needs.

Dr. Kortbus is a member of the American Dental Association and the Florida Dental Association, and he treats continuing education as an ongoing part of the job rather than a box to check. He has also taken on leadership roles at the local level. Dr. Kortbus previously served as president of the Volusia Flagler County Dental Association and currently serves as a delegate to the Central Florida Dental Association. He views dentistry as a lifelong learning process, and that involvement keeps him connected to peers who are working through the same clinical questions he is.

Dr. Kortbus makes a point of discussing every treatment option with a patient before moving forward, rather than presenting a single recommendation as the only path.
