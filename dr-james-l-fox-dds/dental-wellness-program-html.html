Dental Wellness

Fox Family Dental is proud to offer a discount plan for patients without dental insurance. The plan requires you to keep your routine preventive cleanings and receive discounts as a reward for doing so. The plan has many advantages over insurance. There are no membership fees, no deductibles, no maximum benefit, only rewards for having recommended cleanings and x-rays. Simply get your routine cleaning and check-up and receive a progressive discount.
