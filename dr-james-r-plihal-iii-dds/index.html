The practice

At PS Dental Studio, we believe your smile is part of a larger system; your bite, jaw joints, muscles, airway, bone, and overall health are all connected. When one part is ignored, dentistry breaks down. When everything is considered, dentistry lasts.

Led by Dr. Priya Sridhar, a Fellow of both the Academy of General Dentistry and the International Congress of Oral Implantologists, our practice offers the full spectrum of care under one roof. From complex dental implants and full mouth reconstructions to TMJ therapy and biomimetic restorations, we’re equipped to treat what matters most to you.

That’s why we don’t chase quick fixes. We design long-term solutions.

Our practice offers a high-end, relaxing experience, ensuring that even those with dental anxiety can comfortably receive the care they need.

When multiple teeth are damaged, worn, or missing, a coordinated plan restores full function and protects your long-term oral health. Every case is personalized, not protocol-driven.

Jaw pain. Morning headaches. Clicking or locking. These aren’t just annoyances; they’re signs your bite needs attention. Our targeted TMJ therapy addresses root causes so you can eat, sleep, and move through your day without discomfort.

Damaged or missing teeth compromise both function and structure. Our CEREC® crowns and custom bridges restore strength, comfort, and appearance, often in a single visit.

Dental anxiety shouldn’t stand between you and the care you need. From mild nitrous oxide to deeper options, we make it possible to get the treatment you’ve been putting off, comfortably.

Les soins

Lose a tooth, not your quality of life. Our implant solutions, from single implants to full-arch replacements, restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. Permanent. Natural-looking. Designed to last.
