DR. JAMIE COHN

Jamie completed her undergraduate studies at the University of Albany, with a Bachelor’s Degree in Chemistry and a minor in Biology. She attended the University of Buffalo School of Dental Medicine, where she graduated Magna cum Laude with a minor in Pediatric Dentistry. She then completed a General Practice Residency at Ellis Medicine and gained proficiency in all areas of general dentistry.

Following her residency, Jamie was honored to be accepted to a prestigious Fellowship in Dental Oncology at Memorial Sloan Kettering. She treated a wide variety of cancer patients, helping them through treatment and post treatment care.

She has worked in many capacities including several years as the Director of Dental Medicine at Wyckoff Hospital in Brooklyn, New York. She has seen many dental situations and is dedicated to providing patients with a safe and welcoming experience at the dentist.

Jamie is a member of the American Dental Association, the New York State Dental Association and the Fourth District Dental Society. She also participates in various continuing education courses and attends lectures throughout the year.

Originally from Voorheesville, New York, Jamie loved the small town atmosphere and has captured that feeling again in Glens Falls. Jamie looks forward to raising her children and being part of the local community for years to come.
