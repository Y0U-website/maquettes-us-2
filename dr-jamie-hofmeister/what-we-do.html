What We Do Hofmeister Family…

  • Check-ups | Regular check-ups are important not only for your dental health, but because it is a component in your overall health. Early detection of dental disease before it hurts is the best way to treat something when it is small.
  • Imaging | We use dental xrays as a tool to diagnose cavities especially between the teeth as well as to check bone levels and root end health. Bitewing xrays (between the back teeth) are recommended once a year and full mouth xrays are usually done every three to five years.

Restorations

  • Tooth colored fillings | With dentistry continuing to modernize, options that are not silver or gold in color are available in one visit appointments. Composite "tooth colored" fillings and Glass Ionomer "fluoride releasing" fillings are used in our practice. We strive to approach restorations conservatively electing to maintained and support healthy tooth structure.
  • Crowns | Many patients ask about crowns and veneers when they come to our office. We offer All ceramic/porcelain crowns, porcelain and metal crowns, or full gold crowns. We work with you to choose the best treatment options for each patient based on their concerns and dental needs.
  • Bridges | When you lose a tooth, there are a variety of options for replacement. One option is placing a bridge. We use the teeth on either side of the space to create a “bridge” to fill the space. With bridges, it is important to floss under the bridge to avoid cavities.

Mouthguards

Do you have jaw muscle soreness in the morning? Does your partner mention that you grind your teeth at night? We offer custom night guards for patients suffering from TMJ disorders and nightly grinding to elevate pain and protect tooth structure. We work with a dental lab to adjust and customize nightguards just for your mouth and bite.

Whitening

We fabricate custom whitening trays for our patients interested in improving their smile when over the counter products are not enough! We will take a photo of the initial shade of your teeth and then you can conveniently use our personalized trays and strong whitening solution to improve the shade of your teeth from home. Don’t worry, we will be here to answer questions and check back in after a few weeks of whitening. We also offer whitening refills that you can use with your trays in the future.
