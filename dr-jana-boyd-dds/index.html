The practice

We look forward to helping you achieve and maintain your smile.

Dr. Boyd began her career in dentistry 24 years ago. She grew up in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. While in Baton Rouge, Dr. Boyd attended Louisiana State University and received a Bachelor of Science in Microbiology. Subsequent to graduating from college, Dr. Boyd relocated to New Orleans, Louisiana to attend L.S.U. Dental School. In 2001, Dr. Boyd received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery.

After completing dental school, Dr. Boyd served as a dental officer in the United States Navy. She served two years with the Marines at Camp Pendleton in San Diego, California, and then aboard the USS Theodore Roosevelt in Norfolk, Virginia, also for two years.

Once the four years of service in the military sector were complete, Dr. Boyd began practicing dentistry in the Hampton Roads private sector, as an associate. Dr. Boyd practiced as an associate in three different locations and learned a myriad of techniques.

While practicing dentistry in the private sector, Dr. Boyd decided to call Suffolk her home. While doing so, Jana Boyd Family Dentistry opened its doors in October 2015. In addition to dentistry, Dr. Boyd decided to have Jana Boyd Dentistry help the community by sponsoring the Suffolk Humane Society, (Dr. Boyd has a passion for all animals). Les soins

Over time teeth can become stained by the foods we eat, drinking red wine, tea, coffee and also smoking. Teeth also become discolored with age and by certain medications. Teeth cleaning polishes and removes stains from teeth.

Gum disease (periodontal Disease) is responsible for more than 75 % of adult tooth loss. It is characterized by swollen, inflamed gums surrounding the teeth.

Clear aligners help treat a wide variety of issues. Straighter teeth don’t just look better, they work better too. Poorly-aligned teeth can interfere with bite function, wear out faster, and are more prone to cavities.

We offer custom trays with specially prescribed home teeth whitening kits.
