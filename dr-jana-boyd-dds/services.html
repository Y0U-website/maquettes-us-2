Routine Checkups

Over time teeth can become stained by the foods we eat, drinking red wine, tea, coffee, and also smoking. Teeth also become discolored with age and by certain medications. Teeth cleaning polishes and removes stains from teeth.

Scaling / Root Planing

Gum disease (periodontal Disease) is responsible for more than 75 % of adult tooth loss. It is characterized by swollen, inflamed gums surrounding the teeth.

Extractions

Extraction is a dental procedure in which a tooth is carefully removed from its socket. Our skilled team at Jana Boyd, DDS performs extractions with precision and a focus on patient comfort, providing supportive care throughout the procedure to ensure your well-being.

Restorations

A composite filling is a tooth-colored quartz-like material. After tooth decay is removed and cleaned, this tooth-colored resin, or plastic material, is layered into the tooth.

Crowns and Bridges

A crown (often called a cap) covers the tooth and restores it to its original shape and size. Decay is removed and cleaned from the tooth and a highly accurate impression or mold is made natural looking, undetectable replacement for the missing tooth.

Teeth Whitening

We offer custom trays with specially prescribed home teeth whitening kits.

Invisalign

Clear aligners can treat a wide variety of issues that keep people from achieving their ideal smiles. Straighter teeth don’t look better; they work better too. Poorly-aligned teeth can interfere with bite function, wear out faster, and are more prone to cavities. Ask your doctor how Invisalign can help you.

Single Unit Implants

Single unit implants are an excellent solution for replacing individual missing teeth. These implants provide a permanent, durable option that looks and functions like a natural tooth. By securing the implant directly into the jawbone, they offer long-term stability, preserve bone health, and enhance both the appearance and functionality of your smile.
