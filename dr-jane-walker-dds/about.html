Meet Dr. Jane

Dr. Jane Walker is a graduate of The Ohio State College of Dentistry and has completed a General Practice Residency at the University of Louisville. She has also mentored, trained and educated dental students and residents. Dr. Jane is committed to improving her patients’ dental health.

She is a member of the American Dental Association and dedicated to continuing her education. She recently completed a course at the renowned Pankey Institute where she focused on the complete patient experience including restorations and aesthetics. She helped in the development of a city-wide program for improving dental health for children in under-served communities.

Dr. Jane is native of Cincinnati and enjoys yoga and running.
