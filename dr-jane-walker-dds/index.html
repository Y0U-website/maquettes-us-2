Les soins

Bridges : We can develop and apply bridges to restore a gap caused by missing teeth. They are made to be permanent by cementing or bonding the artificial tooth in the bridge to natural teeth, teeth prepared with crowns, or implants next to the space filled by…

Tooth removal : We can perform tooth extractions or removals to alleviate issues like decay, excessive pain, trauma or a crowded mouth. This in-office procedure can be done as part of a treatment plan to enhance oral health.

Dental crowns : Based on the level of tooth damage we might recommend a crown as a more permanent solution. We can bond the crown to your own tooth or to an implant in order to improve the look, alignment, and strength of your teeth.

Invisalign : As an alternative to traditional braces, we offer a clear-aligner treatment. This treatment utilizes a transparent, plastic and removable orthodontic device that provides an alternative to conventional dental braces used to adjust teeth.
