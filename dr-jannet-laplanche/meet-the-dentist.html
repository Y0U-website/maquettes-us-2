Meet the Dentist Everbrite Family…

0 Dr. Jannet Laplanche, DDS “ We are artists. We must have the artistic sense to envision the aesthetic end-result to help our patients look their best. ”

Dr. Jannet Laplanche, DDS received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2009, from Marquette University School of Dentistry in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. She initially practiced at a dental clinic in an FQHC (Federally Qualified Health Centers) in Chicago. This allowed her to develop a passion for treating those in underserved communities who have limited access to care.

In 2013, Dr. Laplanche decided to start her own practice to better provide family dentistry. At Everbrite, she values every patient relationship, taking the time to fully understand who patients are as individuals and as families.

Unfortunately, the average person neglects their oral health. We are always taught how important it is to brush our teeth to avoid bad breath and cavities. But little focus is placed on protecting teeth to avoid medically related issues or the harmful effects of losing a tooth.

During her own personal experience, Dr. Laplanche learned she was no different. She was totally intimidated by the dentist. She knew she was taking care of her teeth but clearly not as recommended by the dentist. This planted the seed to change the minds of people who dread going to the dentist.

“Now I focus on making patients feel better about their smiles while educating them on how to prevent future problems today.”

Throughout the years, Dr. Laplanche has also learned to provide care beyond the dental chair. As a result, she enjoys educating the public on the importance of oral health and disease prevention, among all ages and cultures.

“We believe in treating the whole patient.”

The Everbrite Approach

At Everbrite Family Dentistry we believe it is important for all our patients to know that they are our number on e priority . We consistently strive to maintain the highest standards in customer service, always going the extra mile to make visits a pleasant and enjoyable experience.

Everbrite Family Dentistry is rooted in continuity of care — understanding that poor oral health and medical conditions are often connected. Dentists have a wonderful opportunity to help patients understand the link between oral health and medically related issues. Our strong belief in continuity of care is a major reason for our commitment to educating patients.
