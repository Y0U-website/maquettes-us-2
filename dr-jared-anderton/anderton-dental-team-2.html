Anderton Dental Team

Our team consists of dedicated individuals who are energetic and friendly. They are experienced, well-trained people with a common goal to communicate well with our patients and provide the best care possible. We take pride in the compliments we have received from patients on the competence and interpersonal skills of our staff.

Jennifer has worked in this office since March 1988. Through the years her role and specialty has changed from a front office assistant to a dental assistant and now her current responsibilities since 1996 are as our very own insurance specialist. If you have a question about insurance, she no doubt can and will be able to answer it. She is truly a must-have for this office, as anyone who has dealt with insurance companies can attest.

She has been married since 1994 and has two wonderful teenage boys.

Rosanne has been with Anderton Dental Group since Oct., 1984, and has over 26 years experience as the financial coordinator. She is a member of the American Association of Dental Office Managers.

She has been happily married since June 1983, and resides in Syracuse. She has a lovely daughter in her early twenties and a Jack Russell Terrier. Rosanne enjoys interior decorating, reading, traveling and spending time with family and friends.

Halee started working as a front office assistant in October 2005 while finishing high school. Since then, she completed the dental assisting program at the Ogden-Weber Applied Technology College and has worked as a chair-side since June 2008.

She and her husband, Blake, were married in May 2009, and they love spending precious time together when not at work. You will also catch Halee busily cooking or scrapbooking with her spare time.

Nicole has worked in our office since February 2004. She graduated from Weber State University as a registered dental hygienist in 2003. She also works as an orthodontic assistant for Dr. Craig Coombs. She loves helping patients maintain a healthy smile for their lifetime. All the patients love her quirky and fun attitude and professional skills.

Nicole is happily married and has two beautiful children who keep her hopping in her spare time. She loves camping and fishing though can be found enjoying just about any outdoor activity.

Manya is our full-time hygienist serving you as a critical member of our team since 2001. She graduated with honors from Weber State in 2000 with her dental hygiene & anesthetic degree.

Patients appreciate Manya’s gentle touch and dedication to excellence often commenting on the high quality of care they receive from her. In her free time, Manya enjoys reading, traveling, being outside, and spending time with her husband and two children.

Salina received her dental assisting certificate from Intermountain dental assisting in 2011 and worked in a different office before joining us in September of 2013. She and her husband Lucas were married in May 2011 and had their first child in February 2015. In her spare time she loves spending time with family, skiing and attacking new fun projects.

Desiree graduated from the Utah College of Dental Hygiene in 2008 and started subbing at Anderton Dental in 2013. While working in other offices across the Wasatch Front, she has temped for Anderton Dental often as the office has grown until she eventually was hired as a regular part-time dental hygienist. She loves working as a dental hygienist and is especially detail oriented, as she is often complimented on being very thorough and gentle by her patients.

Desiree grew up in Northeast Oregon and married a local from North Ogden just after she finished dental hygiene school. She lives in Riverdale with her husband, twin boys, and little girl. She loves cooking, singing, crafting, and is always in the middle of some sort of home renovation project.

Megan grew up in Missoula, Montana, but has lived in South Ogden since 2013. Megan loves every aspect of dentistry and is seeking a Zoology degree at Weber State University in hopes of pursuing dental school once finished.
