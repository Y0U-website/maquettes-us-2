Bite Problems & Airway Habits

Overbite Too much bite overlap, often linked to upper front teeth extending forward.

Underbite A condition that often needs early treatment to help prevent surgical correction.

Crossbite A reversed bite affects the front or back teeth.

Open Bite Little or no overlap, most commonly involving the front teeth.

Crowding Teeth that are misaligned are usually caused by limited space.

Spacing Excess space or noticeable gaps between the teeth.

Tongue Thrust The tongue pushes between the upper and lower teeth during swallowing or resting.

Mouth Breathing Breathing through the mouth is often related to limited skeletal airway space.
