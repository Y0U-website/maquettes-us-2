Bite Problems & Airway Habits

Overbite Excessive bite overlap is often associated with the upper front teeth extending too far forward.

Underbite A bite issue that frequently benefits from early treatment to help reduce the likelihood of surgical correction.

Crossbite A misaligned bite where the upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth, affecting the front or back of the mouth.

Open Bite Minimal or no overlap between the upper and lower teeth, most commonly involving the front teeth.

Crowding Teeth that overlap or are misaligned due to limited space in the jaw.

Spacing Extra space or noticeable gaps between teeth caused by tooth size or jaw differences.

Tongue Thrust A condition where the tongue presses between the upper and lower teeth during swallowing or at rest.

Mouth Breathing Breathing primarily through the mouth is often connected to a limited skeletal airway space.
