Bite Problems & Airway Habits

Overbite Excessive vertical overlap of the teeth, commonly caused by the upper front teeth extending too far forward.

Underbite A bite condition where the lower teeth sit in front of the upper teeth, often requiring early intervention to reduce the need for surgery later.

Crossbite A misaligned bite where one or more upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth, affecting the front or back of the mouth.

Open Bite Minimal or no overlap between the upper and lower teeth, most often involving the front teeth.

Crowding Teeth that overlap or twist due to insufficient space in the jaw.

Spacing Extra space or visible gaps between teeth caused by missing teeth or jaw size differences.

Tongue Thrust A habit where the tongue presses between the upper and lower teeth during swallowing or at rest.

Mouth Breathing Breathing primarily through the mouth is often associated with restricted airway development or skeletal concerns.
