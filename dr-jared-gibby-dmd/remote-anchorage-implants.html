Pterygoid Implants

Pterygoid implants anchor in the pterygoid process and plates of the sphenoid bone, providing posterior support for full-arch restorations.

  • ✓ Eliminates need for sinus grafting
  • ✓ Provides distal support for full arches
  • ✓ Engages dense cortical bone

Frequently Asked Questions

Are remote anchorage implants safe?

Yes, these procedures have been performed successfully for many years with excellent safety profiles. They require special training and expertise, which is why they&#x27;re only offered by specialists like those at Hope Dental Implant Center.

How long is the recovery time?

Most patients experience swelling and mild discomfort for 5-7 days. You can typically return to normal activities within a week, though complete healing and osseointegration takes 3-6 months.

Will I need to be without teeth during treatment?

No. In most cases, we can provide temporary teeth the same day as your implant placement. These provisional restorations allow you to eat, speak, and smile while your permanent teeth are being fabricated.

Are these procedures more expensive than traditional implants?

Remote anchorage implants typically cost more than conventional implants due to their technical complexity and specialized components. However, when compared to the cost of extensive bone grafting plus traditional implants, they can be comparable or even more cost-effective.
