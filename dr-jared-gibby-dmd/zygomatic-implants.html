Frequently Asked Questions

Are zygomatic implants painful?

The procedure is performed under sedation or general anesthesia, so you won&#x27;t feel anything during surgery. Post-operative discomfort is typically manageable with prescribed pain medication and is similar to traditional implant procedures.

How long do zygomatic implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, zygomatic implants can last decades or even a lifetime. The implants are made from high-grade titanium, which has excellent biocompatibility and strength.

Are zygomatic implants covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by insurance provider. Our team will work with you to maximize any available benefits and offer financing options to make treatment affordable.

What is the recovery time?

Most patients experience swelling for 3-5 days after surgery. You can typically return to work and normal activities within a week. Complete healing and osseointegration takes 3-6 months, though you&#x27;ll have functioning teeth throughout this period.
