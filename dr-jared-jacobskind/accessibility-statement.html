0 Accessibility statement Accessibility Commitment At Dr. Jared Jacobskind PLLC DBA Windsor Progressive Dental Care, we are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users. To guide our accessibility work, we’ve partnered with Accessible Web , a team of experts in digital accessibility. Our goal is to comply with the Americans with Disabilities Act Title II and Title III regulations, aiming for conformance with WCAG 2.2 AA standards. We value inclusion and strive to provide a seamless experience so every visitor can access our information and services comfortably.

Known Limitations

Currently, there are no known accessibility limitations on our website.

What We’re Doing

We conduct manual accessibility audits and actively incorporate accessibility considerations into our design processes to improve usability for all users continuously.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or require assistance related to website accessibility, please contact us by phone at (860) 253-2255 or email at frontdesk@windsorprogressivedentalcare.com. We strive to respond to all inquiries within 5 business days.

Our Promise

Our team is dedicated to providing the highest level of care and comfort, and this commitment extends to our digital presence. We aim to ensure that everyone, including those with disabilities, can access our services and information with ease. Thank you for visiting our website.
