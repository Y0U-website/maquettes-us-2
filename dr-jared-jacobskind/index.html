I decided to stop pursuing a career in engineering, and apply to dental school in the weeks after Hurricane Sandy.

Being a dentist is more than toothpaste and porcelain crowns, and that is why I pursued a dual degree as both a Doctor of Dental Surgery, and an MBA in Healthcare Management. I know that to best serve my patients, my office has to be a well run operation, that puts our client’s needs first.

After serving our Veteran community during my General Practice Residency at the Northport Veteran Affairs Medical Center, my wife and I decided to relocate our growing family to central Connecticut. We fell in love with crisp New England fall mornings, vibrant downtowns juxtaposed with quiet farms, and the community we have found in the Greater Hartford area.

I practiced as an associate dentist at two practices before finding Windsor Progressive Dental Care. Upon my first meeting with Dr. Steven Farley, it was clear that this practice fit the ethos that I was looking for. Dr. Farley practiced with a patients first mindset, and I know he considers his patients his extended family.

The team and I look forward to continuing to put your dental needs at the forefront of every decision and interaction at Windsor Progressive Dental Care. The marriage of excellent dental care, modern technology, patient comfort, and classic rock and roll is the cornerstone of Windsor Progressive Dental Care.

Outside of the office, I am lucky enough to be surrounded by wonderful girls. My wife, Marsha, and I have three daughters. Watching our family grow has been the greatest joy in life.
