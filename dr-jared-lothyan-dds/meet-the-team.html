Isha’s Bio

Dr. Isha Suri is a board-certified pediatric dentist who recently relocated to Seattle after practicing in the New York City for the past six years. She earned her Bachelor of Science in Biomedical Engineering with a minor in Psychology from the University of Texas at Austin, and graduated Magna Cum Laude with her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Boston University School of Dental Medicine in 2015.

She completed a General Practice Residency at Stony Brook University Hospital in New York, where she gained extensive experience caring for patients with special needs and dental anxiety. Dr. Suri then pursued advanced specialty training in Pediatric Dentistry at the University of Texas School of Dentistry at Houston, earning both a certificate in Pediatric Dentistry and a Master of Science in Dentistry. Her advanced training encompassed oral, IV, and general anesthesia sedation, as well as the management of medically complex children.

Known for her compassionate and personalized approach, Dr. Suri is dedicated to building meaningful connections with her patients and their families. She is an active member of the College of Diplomates of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.
