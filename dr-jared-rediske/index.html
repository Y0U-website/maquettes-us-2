The practice

He attended University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, graduating with his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2006. After graduation, Dr. Rediske moved to Vermont because Brattleboro, VT won his heart. Initially he worked as an associate in a group practice.

At the end of 2009 he purchased his own practice in Townshend, Vermont. He closed the Townshend office February 2019 and merged the staff and patients to our second location in Brattleboro. Shortly after the merge, we began renovations at a new location at 999 Putney Rd. On July 17th, 2019, we made our final move to our current location at 999 Putney Road, Brattleboro, Vermont to better serve his…

Dr. Rediske served on the board of the AIDS Project of Southern Vermont, and he was president of the board for two years. In college, Dr. Rediske was part of Delta Lambda Phi, a social fraternity of which he was president.

He is one of the founders of the Brattleboro Study Club, as well as a member of the Seattle study club, the American Dental Association, and Vermont State Dental Association. Dr. Rediske is currently a member of the “New Hampshire Club of Excellence.” He’s the attending dentist for the Head Start and Early Head Start program for the Windham County area.

In 2006, Dr. Rediske received the Practice Manager Award from the University of Minnesota. He was named Squadron Airman of the Year in 1998 and Airman Below the Zone in 1999. Dr. Rediske also founded the Well Baby Clinic, serving children between the ages of 1 and 3 years. During the 19 years since the program began, they’ve served over 1000 children.

Dr. Rediske’s mother and two of his sisters live in South Eastern Minnesota, close to where Dr. Rediske grew up. His third sister lives in Wisconsin. He’s been partnered for eighteen years and has a 34-year-old step-daughter. He has four cats, and two saltwater fish aquariums.
