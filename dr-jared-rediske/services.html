Laser Periodontal Therapy

Gum disease is destructive. It causes bleeding, tenderness, bone loss, and ultimately tooth loss. It can cause you to suffer from chronic bad breath (Halitosis). Research has pointed to a possible link between gum disease and stroke, heart disease, diabetes, and more. Also known as periodontal disease, later-stage gum disease should be addressed with laser periodontal therapy. Using leading edge technology, we can treat your gum disease with greater comfort and improved efficiency, lessening the healing time that’s required in the process.

Dental Implants

We both surgically place and restore dental implants at West River Family Dental. This is good news for our patients because it means that you get one team of dentists in one location replacing your missing teeth, streamlining the process and making things as simple for you as possible. Dental implants offer a number of benefits in comparison with other tooth replacement options; however, if for some reason you do not desire dental implants, we also offer dentures, partials, and fixed bridges.
