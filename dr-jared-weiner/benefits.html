Top Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental Implant Benefits

When it comes to your smile, you want your teeth to function properly, feel comfortable, and look spectacular. Dental implants combine these benefits to create the ideal option for tooth replacement, giving you the freedom to smile and improve your oral health. At Innovative Oral Surgery & Dental Implants, our experts offer many tooth replacement options and will customize your treatment to fit your exact needs.

Advantages of Dental Implants Over Other Tooth Replacement Options

Implant-supported tooth restorations have helped millions of people regain their oral health and self-esteem. Dental implants are unique because they provide a solution to tooth loss while restoring full functionality, health, and appearance. When you choose dental implants, you receive numerous benefits that traditional dentures and bridges do not provide.

Boost Your Self-Esteem

When you are missing teeth, you may not feel as confident about your smile as you’d like. You may avoid eating around others or avoid showing your teeth in photos. After dental implant treatment, you will have a beautiful and healthy smile that you will love to show off!

No Removable Parts

While dentures can also restore your smile’s appearance, they must be removed during meals or brushing, which can cause feelings of embarrassment. With implants, your new teeth stay in place at all times, so you can feel confident about your smile without the inconvenience of removable parts.

Protect the Jaw Bone

Missing teeth can lead to jaw bone atrophy because there is not a tooth root present to keep the jaw strong. Unlike dentures, dental implants have a post that replicates a natural tooth root. This post stimulates the jaw, keeping it healthy and preventing bone loss.

Improve Your Overall Comfort

Your dental implant will look, feel, and function just like a natural tooth. You can enjoy your favorite foods with no dietary restrictions and will never have to deal with dental adhesives or oral sores caused by ill-fitting appliances. Implants will also allow you to speak more clearly without bulky dentures.

Long-Lasting Results

Dental implants are not susceptible to cavities and are designed to last for many years. They can be more expensive upfront than dentures or bridges, but they have better value in the long run because they promote better oral health and do not have to be replaced every few years. If you keep your implants clean and cared for, they can last a lifetime.

Protect the Health of Other Teeth

Dental implants are self-supporting, which means they can support themselves without compromising the health of other teeth. Dental bridges, however, require neighboring teeth to be ground down to fit the bridge and rely on your existing teeth for support.
