Dr. Weiner always makes sure his patients feel heard. He understands the nervousness that accompanies surgery and encourages patients to express these feelings so he can help calm their worries. He helps patients feel comfortable by fostering a welcoming atmosphere in the office and by treating every patient as if they are a member of his own family.

Dr. Weiner graduated from the University of Florida with a food science and human nutrition degree in 2006. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry in 2011 and completed his oral and maxillofacial surgery residency at Lincoln Medical and Mental Health Center in 2015.
