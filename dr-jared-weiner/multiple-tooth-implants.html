Multiple Tooth Implants

When restoring your smile, you have many options to consider, such as dental implants, removable dentures, or fixed oral appliances. Multiple dental implants offer numerous advantages over other tooth replacement options. They are the only solution that provides the same aesthetics, functionality, and durability as natural teeth and because they fuse to the jaw, they prevent jaw bone loss. What Are Multiple Tooth Implants?

Your oral surgeon will place multiple tooth implants in your mouth to replace missing or failing teeth. You can receive as many implants as you need, as long as there is sufficient jaw bone to support them. Some patients receive a full mouth of dental implants, while others only require a few. Multiple tooth implants are unique because they have implant posts that function as teeth roots, which keep the jaw bone healthy and strong.

Multiple Dental Implant Procedure

The process of placing multiple implants is similar to placing a single implant. We will take 3D scans of your mouth, discuss your anesthesia options, and design your custom treatment plan. Our surgeons at Innovative Oral Surgery & Dental Implants perform surgery in one of our state-of-the-art surgical suites designed for your privacy and comfort.

After the anesthesia takes effect, your surgeon will make incisions in the gum tissue as needed and insert the implant posts into the jaw. If you need a tooth extracted, we will remove it and immediately place an implant as long as there is sufficient jaw bone.

In many cases, we place single-stage implants where the abutment is already attached to the implant post. This eliminates the need to return to our office for a second procedure to have the abutment placed.

The Value of Choosing Dental Implants

Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime. The post becomes a fixed part of your smile and by keeping your implant clean and cared for, it will serve you for many years. By attending regular dental appointments with your general dentist and practicing good oral hygiene, you can increase the longevity of your implants. This is a huge benefit of dental implants over dentures, which you must replace as often as every few years and have them professionally fitted to avoid slipping or oral sores.
