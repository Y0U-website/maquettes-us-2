Contact Lens Fitting Exams

Contact lenses rest on a thin layer of tear-film directly on the cornea of the eye. It is important that the lenses fit your eyes properly. The better the fit, the more comfortable the contact lenses and the better your overall vision.

During a contact lens exam, the overall health of your eyes is assessed. If they are suitable for contact lenses, detailed measurements are taken to determine the shape and size of your cornea. This information is used to find a suitable pair of contact lenses that will provide comfortable vision correction.

During the initial contact lens fitting, we will provide you with in-depth information about handling, storing, and caring for your contacts. We also will teach you how to properly insert and remove contact lenses in a safe and efficient manner.

There is a type of contact lens for almost every prescription. Compared to eyeglasses, contact lenses often require less maintenance and are comparable in overall price.

While it is true that your eyes must undergo a brief adaptation period, ranging from a couple of days up to two weeks, most people quickly find contact lenses comfortable to wear. The first few times you insert and remove them it can be challenging, but it does not take long for it to become part of your daily routine.
