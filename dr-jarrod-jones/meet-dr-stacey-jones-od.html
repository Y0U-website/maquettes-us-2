Meet Stacey R. Jones, OD

About

Dr. Stacey Jones is a therapeutic optometrist and a native of northwest Kansas. She holds the designation of Diplomate of the American Board of Optometry and is also a dietitian.

She prides herself on providing a high level of care for a myriad of conditions, including glaucoma, cataracts, macular degeneration, emergency eye care, dry eye disease, and vision-related learning disabilities. She enjoys working with patients of all ages, with experience treating those from 6 months to over 100 years old.

Her passion for international optometry began with a service trip to Guatemala in 2017. Since then, she has committed herself to working with professional teams providing eye exams, glasses, and surgery to people in underserved countries who desperately need vision restoration. These experiences abroad continually expand her clinical knowledge as she cares for her patients back home.

Dr. Stacey practiced with Kendall Krug, O.D., in Hays, Kansas, for over fifteen years before fulfilling a long-held dream: opening eyeSMILE Vision & Dental in January 2019 with her husband, Jarrod, a dentist.

Hays is their family home, and they always dreamed of practicing together under the same roof. They enjoy spending time with their two daughters. In her downtime, Dr. Stacey enjoys family vacations, cooking, gardening, and various fitness activities.
