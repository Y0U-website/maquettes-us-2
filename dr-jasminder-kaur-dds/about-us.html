Your Experience With Us

We want your experience to be pleasant and productive — a truly valuable expenditure of your money and time.

  • A Welcoming Office. Our office has been elegantly remodeled with your comfort and personal needs in mind. Open spaces, soothing pastel colors and rich leather chairs all blend together to create a pleasant atmosphere.
  • During Your Visit. We take time getting to know you before we start your treatment, and we take the time needed to give you the highest level of care.
  • Free Patient Education: To ensure your comfort with procedures, we have literature available for you to use. You will have a lot of one-to-one contact during your visit with the dentist and hygienist, so ask as many questions as you like.

Taking It Home With You

In addition to in-office education, we also instruct patients in proper home care. To keep visual track of your progress, we use a unique tool we call our Dental Fitness Report. After each examination, we mark the appropriate level of your dental fitness, as well as give you a detailed explanation of how you can improve your dental report card”. Each visit you can compare your scores and be involved in your dental care.

Sterilization And Safety

Ensuring safety is an important part of our practice. We use the latest in sterilization technology and adhere to OSHA safety procedures. We use disposables where possible, and all reusable instruments are thoroughly sterilized using autoclave techniques.

Fees and Insurance

Insurance policies are designed to assist you in paying for health care. Most cover major medical bills, like those generated in emergencies. Because insurance provides only partial coverage, you pay for the rest of the services you and your family need. We can help you get the most out of your insurance, and we will gladly file it for you. We are also partners with Delta Insurance.

  • New Patients: If you are visiting our office for the first time, we request payment for services the same day, and that you bring in your insurance card and forms.
  • Established Patients: For patients whose services are under $100, we request same-day payment. For more extensive procedures covered by insurance, we ask that you pay your portion the day of the procedure.
