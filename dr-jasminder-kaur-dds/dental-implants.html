FAQ about Dental Implants

How long does dental implants treatment take?

The treatment process for dental implants typically spans several months. It usually involves multiple steps, such as initial consultation, implant placement, and healing time for the jawbone to integrate with the implant. The healing process, called osseointegration, can take 3-6 months, after which a crown or bridge is attached. Total treatment time can vary depending on individual factors, such as the need for preparatory procedures like bone grafting.

How many teeth can I replace?

Dental implants are versatile and can replace a single tooth, several teeth, or even an entire arch. Options include single-tooth implants, implant-supported bridges for multiple teeth, or full-arch solutions like All-on-4, where a complete upper or lower arch is supported by just four implants.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The implant itself is typically made from titanium, a durable material that integrates with the jawbone. The crown or bridge on top may need replacement every 10-15 years due to normal wear. Regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene practices help ensure the longevity of your implants.

What factors affect the cost of dental implants?

Several factors can influence the cost of dental implants, including:

  • Number of implants needed
  • Location of the implant(s) in the mouth
  • Necessity of preparatory procedures (like bone grafting or sinus lifts)
  • Type of implant and materials used
  • Expertise of the dental surgeon and geographical location of the practice

How much does most dental insurance pay for implants?

Dental insurance coverage for implants varies widely. Some plans may cover a portion of the procedure. However, many insurance providers consider implants elective, offering limited or no coverage. How painful is getting a dental implant?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the dental implant procedure. Dentists use local anesthesia to numb the area, and sedation options are also available if needed. Post-surgery, mild soreness and swelling are common, but can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Most people report that the recovery process is easier than expected.

What is the average cost of dental implants in the US?

In the U.S., the average cost of a single dental implant ranges between $3,000 and $5,000. This includes the implant post, abutment, and crown. Complex cases, involving bone grafts or multiple implants, may raise the overall cost.

How many teeth do you get on all-on-4 dental implants?

The All-on-4 technique typically supports a full arch of 10-12 teeth on just four implants. This solution provides a complete, natural-looking set of teeth on one arch (upper or lower), ideal for patients who have lost most or all teeth in that arch.
