Meet Dr. Ko

Dr. Jasmine Ko, DDS

General and Family Dentist

Dr. Jasmine Ko is a family dentist who provides exceptional comprehensive dental care to her patients. Her main focus is in the area of preventive and restorative dentistry. She loves helping her patients maintain healthy smiles with a gentle touch.

In her free time, you will find Dr. Ko volunteering at her children’s school and local club events. She also enjoys traveling to experience different cultures, and baking delicious treats for her family.
