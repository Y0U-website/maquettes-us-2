Brian Quan, DDS

Dr. Brian Quan was born and raised in the San Francisco Bay Area. He received his Bachelor of Science degree in Genetics from The University of California, Davis. He then went to the University of Southern California School of Dentistry to receive his Doctorate of Dental Surgery.

Dr. Quan has been practicing for over 15 years in San Francisco and is passionate about doing conservative and quality dentistry. Dr. Quan continually takes different courses in CEREC restorative procedures, aesthetics, and surgeries to ensure that patients get the quality care that they need.

Dr. Quan continually takes courses at the Kois Dental Center in Seattle to provide the most current evidence based treatment for full mouth reconstruction and prevention. Dr. Quan also recently was in Guatemala to help the local children and adults receive dental care.

Dr. Quan enjoys spending his free time with his wife and two children. Dr. Quan is a sports fan and roots for his local favorites of the Niners, Warriors and the Giants. He also enjoys attending the USC Football games that come up to the Bay Area.
