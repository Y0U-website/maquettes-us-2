The practice

The Nevada Dental Arts team is led by dentists, Jason Ferguson, DDS and Tomás Kutansky DDS. Both dentists hold special credentialing and is one of the only offices in Fallon to offer IV Sedation. This means our patients can receive need oral surgery such as wisdom tooth extractions, implants, and pre-prosthetic treatment.

Dr. Ferguson and Dr. Kutansky take pride in their private practice knowing all patients are given the time they deserve to be educated about their dental health, and providing gentle treatment.

Our caring, supportive team of registered dental hygienists and dental assistants are here to provide you and your family with effective, yet gentle preventative care including cleanings and x-rays using our digital x-rays.
