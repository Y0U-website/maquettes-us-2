Sarah

Sarah is a licensed dental hygienist with the Minnesota Board of Dentistry and a graduate of St. Cloud Technical College, where she earned her Associate of Applied Science (AAS) degree in Dental Hygiene. She brings over 20 years of experience in the dental field, beginning her career as a dental assistant before returning to school to pursue dental hygiene.

Outside of work, Sarah enjoys traveling, spending time with her two children and their dog, reading, and binge-watching her favorite shows.

Theresa

Theresa joined the Friesz Family Dentistry team in the fall of 2013. Theresa is married with two sons. She enjoys watching her sons sporting events, as well as spending time on the lake with friends.

Leah

Melanie

Amy
