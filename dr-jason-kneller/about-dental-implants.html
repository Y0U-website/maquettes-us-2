0 ABOUT DENTAL IMPLANTS If you are tired of living with missing teeth, whether it be one tooth or multiple teeth, it is time to consider dental implants. Dental implants can restore your ability to eat the foods you love, speak clearly, and most importantly, smile confidently.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are the best solution to replacing missing teeth. With dental implants, there is no need to file down perfectly healthy teeth to fit a bridge, or wear an uncomfortable partial denture. Instead, the dental implant is placed in the jaw bone and acts as the root of your new tooth. The implant integrates with the bone in the jaw and a crown is placed on top. Just like regular teeth, the implant replaces the root and keeps the crown of the tooth anchored in the jaw.

Dental implants can be used to replace a single missing tooth, multiple missing teeth, or with TRUE TEETH to support a full fixed bridge of teeth. Using the most recent advances in dental implant technology, our board certified specialists are able to place dental implants precisely and painlessly using the anesthesia of your choice.

If you have been thinking about replacing your missing teeth, dental implants are the simplest and fastest way to restore your smile. The process is easy and your TRUE TEETH provider and care coordinator will explain everything to you, step-by-step along the way so you can be confident you are receiving the best care from start to finish.
