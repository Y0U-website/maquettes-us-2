The trueteeth process

0 THE TRUE TEETH PROCESS SIMPLE 4-STEP PROCESS. If you have been thinking about dental implants or a full-mouth rehabilitation, TRUE TEETH is the simplest and fastest way to restore your smile. Our process is easy and your TRUE TEETH provider and care coordinator will explain everything to you, step-by-step along the way so you can be confident you are receiving the best care from start to finish.

Consultation

To determine if you are a candidate for this same-day dental implant procedure, you will meet with a TRUE TEETH Specialist to evaluate if there is sufficient bone quality to support the procedure.

Design your Smile

Your Specialist will utilize advanced technology, including CT scans, 3D facial scanners and intra-oral cameras to create your TRUE TEETH surgical plan. At this time, you will also schedule your surgical procedure and be provided with pre-op instructions.

Procedure

You will arrive for your appointment at the specialist’s office where you had your consultation, and designed your smile. The doctors and staff will put you at ease and take great care of you during your procedure, and you will not have to come back for multiple surgeries. Your TRUE TEETH provider will review your options for sedation to ensure you are comfortable throughout your treatment.

Walk Out Smiling

After your TRUE TEETH procedure, you will be able to smile with confidence. Working with your restorative doctor and our laboratory, we will provide you with a fixed set of teeth the very same day. Our doctors and staff will guide you with post-op instructions and discuss how to care for your new smile.
