The practice

If you are dissatisfied with the appearance of your teeth, we will help you get your confidence back and get the healthy, beautiful smile you deserve.

Our goal is to help you preserve your natural teeth as long as possible, and ensure your oral health.

Defective teeth can be caused by several factors. We determine the cause and provide safe and effective treatment options.

Our job is to listen to you, respect you, educate you and not judge your decisions. Dr. Moore understands you might be anxious about the process of receiving dental care. He makes it a point to explain your treatment plan carefully and answer all your questions. Modern dentistry has many techniques, local anesthetics and relaxation methods to increase your comfort.
