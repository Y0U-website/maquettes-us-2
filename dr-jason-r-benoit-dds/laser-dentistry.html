What Is Laser Dentistry?

Laser dentistry uses focused light energy to conduct precise dental procedures. This technology gives dentists the ability to treat dental conditions while minimizing discomfort and the need for anesthesia. From addressing cavities to complex surgeries, laser dentistry is proven to be efficient and effective for treating both hard and soft dental tissues.
