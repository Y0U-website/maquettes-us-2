Dr. Jason G. Souyias

Jason G. Souyias was born and raised in Michigan. He cherishes the core values of the Midwest: honesty, integrity, and family. His father, Socrates, is an immigrant from Greece and his mother, Gloria, was born and raised in Detroit. Although Jason is proud of his heritage, he is even more proud to be an American.

Jason has lived in a variety of places across the United States. He lived in Chicago during his college years at Northwestern University and spent three years living in Portland, Oregon but Michigan is home. “I like the idea of living in a place where deals can be done on a hand shake and peoples’ word means something to them,” said Jason when asked why he chose to settle in this area.

Jason’s passion for his profession is reflected in his desire to share it with others. He is a part-time Assistant Clinical Professor, Department of Periodontology and Hygiene, at the University of Detroit Mercy. Dr. Souyias regularly educates dentists and hygienists by giving presentations. Anyone who has seen him speak knows how much he loves what he does on a daily basis. Jason is committed to helping the dentists he works with succeed. He frequently presents to small office groups on business practices, periodontal therapy, and dental implants.

In their free time, Jason and his wife Paula enjoy traveling, playing sports, and cooking. Homemade Greek food is one of their specialties. Dr. Souyias loves to scuba dive whenever the opportunity arises during his travels.
