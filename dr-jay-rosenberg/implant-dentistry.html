The Placement Process

What’s it like to get an implant at Drs. Goodman & Rosenberg General Dentistry?

Having missing teeth can feel stressful, but our goal is to make treatment simple and comfortable. Here’s how the process works:

  • Consultation: We review your oral health, discuss options, and create a plan tailored to you.
  • Placement: A titanium implant is gently positioned into your jawbone.
  • Healing: After a few months, the implant bonds naturally with bone.
  • Restoration: A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached, giving you a strong, natural smile.

How Dental Implants Compare to Other Options

Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, implants:

  • Protect existing teeth by not requiring alteration
  • Stimulate bone to prevent further bone loss
  • Provide a more secure and comfortable fit
  • Lasts decades with proper care

Maintaining Your New Smile

Caring for implants is simple. Brush and floss daily, schedule regular checkups, and consider preventive treatments like teeth whitening to keep your smile looking bright. With proper care, your implants can last a lifetime.

FAQ: Implant Dentistry in Pleasantville, NJ

  • How painful are dental implants? Dental implants are comparable to extractions in that you may feel very mild discomfort, but overall, it’s a pain-free experience. Our restorative dentists ensure your procedure is comfortable from start to finish. In addition to using a local anesthetic to prevent any pain, we plan your procedure using 3D imaging. This allows us to insert the implant into the best location while avoiding nerves. After the procedure, our team explains how to care for your mouth at home to promote successful healing.
  • Is a dental implant worth it? Yes, dental implants are worth it because they’re a long-term investment in your oral and overall health. There’s no other tooth replacement that looks, feels, and functions just like your natural teeth! Please contact us to get a dental implant consultation and estimate in Atlantic County, NJ.
  • How soon after tooth extraction can you have an implant? The entire procedure takes a few months because your mouth must heal while the implant fuses with the jawbone. Once ready, our dentist creates and places your new tooth or teeth on top of the implant. On average, we wait 10-12 weeks for the area to heal.
  • Can I get a dental implant years after extraction? Yes, you can get a dental implant years after extraction. It doesn’t matter if you’ve recently experienced tooth loss or have been living with missing teeth for years. Our 3D imaging allows us to analyze what’s underneath the gumline, including your bone, so that we can plan out your procedure. We also work with denture patients who are ready for an upgrade.
