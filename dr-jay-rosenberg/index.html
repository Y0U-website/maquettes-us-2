The practice

In addition to caring for your teeth, he recently began delivering Botox treatments for both esthetic and therapeutic purposes. Dr. Rosenberg trained at the American Academy of Facial Esthetics to offer safe, proven facial injections and fillers right in our dental office.

Our Pleasantville dentist never stops learning, and through numerous courses at the Scottsdale Center for Dentistry, he became an advanced level user of the CEREC® CAD/CAM system. This allows us to create life-like, durable dental restorations in just one visit to our office.

Outside of work, Dr. Rosenberg gives back through the Donated Dental Services Program and is a past vice president of Congregation Beth El in Margate. He’s passionate about his work, helping others, living a healthy lifestyle, and enjoys running and tennis.

Dr. Goodman is a Pittsburgh, PA native dedicated to giving you personalized dental solutions through modern technology, continuing education, and a caring heart. In addition to serving our Pleasantville, NJ community, Dr. Goodman is a sought-after speaker and educator.

Our skilled dentist is a mentor in the CEREC Doctor Group , an exclusive association of dentists with extensive training in one-visit dentistry. Dr. Goodman trains and gives advanced users the skills to treat even the most complex cases.

He also gives back when he can and has been acknowledged for continued humanitarian service to the disabled, elderly, and medically compromised. Our state and The New Jersey Foundation of Dentistry have recognized Dr. Goodman for his years of service to the Donated Dental Services Program, serving our country’s most vulnerable populations.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Goodman stays active in the community serving on the board of trustees and as a former president of Northfield’s Congregation Beth Israel. In his free time, you can find him on the golf course, in the gym, snow skiing, or traveling with his wife Jodi and their two children.
