Invisalign Invisalign aligners are the clear alternative to metal braces for adults and teens. These plastic trays cover your teeth which over time, gently pulls the teeth into a more desired, acceptable position. Such clear aligners are the virtually invisible way to improve your smile.

LANAP LANAP (Laser Assisted New Attachment Procedure) is an advanced dental treatment for gum disease. Using a laser, it removes infected tissue and promotes natural healing, avoiding traditional cut-and-suture methods. Minimally invasive and with reduced downtime, LANAP offers patients a more comfortable and efficient solution to restore healthy gums and save teeth.
