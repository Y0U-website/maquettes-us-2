Meet Dr. Gelb

Dr. Jeffrey M. Gelb

Dr. Gelb is a fourth-generation dentist and a graduate of the top-ranking University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. After graduating with honors from the University of Pennsylvania’s Doctor of Medical Dentistry (DMD) program, Dr. Gelb completed his orthodontic residency at their world-renowned orthodontic program. There, he studied with leaders in the field of orthodontics including the former President of the American Board of Orthodontics. Dr. Gelb continues to enrich his education through numerous lectures, conferences and study groups.

He is passionate about orthodontics, and you can always catch him reading the latest research and studying his cases from every angle to make the treatment and experience even better for his patients. Dr. Gelb enjoys treating both children and adults. You can follow Gelb Orthodontics on Instagram at @GelbOrthodontics .

Dr. Gelb utilizes the latest technological advances in the industry, such as Invisalign, Invisalign Teen, ceramic braces and self ligating braces and temporary anchorage devices, along with digital imaging and advanced computer graphics, to ensure that you receive the most effective and comfortable care possible.

Dr. Gelb’s hobbies include skiing, rock climbing, tennis, and frisbee. Dr. Gelb and his amazing wife Debbie love spending time with their two children.
