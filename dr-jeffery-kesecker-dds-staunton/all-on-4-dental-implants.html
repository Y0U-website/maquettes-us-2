Replacing a Full Arch With All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 is a full-arch dental implant technique used to replace an entire upper or lower arch of missing or failing teeth. A full arch of custom replacement teeth is securely supported by strategically placed dental implants , providing a stable and long-lasting solution for extensive tooth loss.

You may also hear this treatment called All-on-X , where X refers to the number of implants used to support the restoration. While many patients are candidates for four implants, others may benefit from five, six, or more depending on their anatomy and treatment needs.

With the traditional All-on-4 technique, the two front implants are placed vertically while the two back implants are angled up to 45 degrees. This allows the implants to engage more of the available jawbone, often providing excellent stability while reducing the need for bone grafting.

Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

Full arch dental implants offer several advantages over traditional removable dentures.

Fixed, Stable Teeth

Your replacement teeth are securely attached to dental implants, eliminating the slipping and movement often associated with removable dentures.

Improved Chewing Ability

Because the implants are anchored within the jawbone, patients can enjoy a stronger bite and greater confidence when eating many of their favorite foods.

Natural Appearance

Each restoration is custom designed to complement your facial features and create a smile that looks natural and functions comfortably.

Support for Jawbone Health

Dental implants stimulate the jawbone in much the same way as natural tooth roots. This helps reduce the bone loss that commonly occurs after teeth are lost.

Fewer Daily Maintenance Concerns

Unlike removable dentures, full arch restorations do not require adhesives or nightly removal. They are cleaned similarly to natural teeth using regular brushing and flossing techniques recommended by your dental team.

What to Expect During Treatment

Every patient’s treatment is customized, but the process generally follows these steps:

Consultation and Treatment Planning

Your appointment begins with a comprehensive evaluation, digital imaging, and discussion of your goals and budget.

Preparation

If damaged or failing teeth remain, they are typically removed at the time of surgery. Some patients may require bone grafting before implant placement, although the All-on-4 technique often minimizes this need.

Implant Placement

Local anesthesia is used to numb the treatment area, and sedation options are available for your comfort. Using a customized surgical guide, Dr. Kesecker places the dental implants according to your personalized treatment plan to create a stable foundation for your full-arch restoration.

Temporary Teeth

Many patients receive a temporary fixed prosthesis on the same day as implant placement, allowing them to leave the office with a functional smile while healing begins.

Final Restoration

Once the implants have fully integrated with the jawbone, you will return to your restorative dentist for your custom final restoration. Throughout the process, Dr. Kesecker works closely with your dentist to help ensure a seamless transition from implant placement to your completed smile.

FAQ

Are full-arch dental implants removable?

Most patients choose a fixed full-arch restoration that is securely attached to the implants and cannot be removed by the patient. However, removable full-arch implant options are also available.

Are there any food restrictions with full-arch dental implants?

A soft-food diet is recommended while your implants heal. After your final restoration is placed, most patients are able to enjoy a full, unrestricted diet and eat comfortably without the slipping or instability associated with traditional dentures.

Will my new teeth look natural?

Yes. Your final restoration is custom designed to complement your facial features, bite, and smile. The goal is to create teeth that look natural, feel comfortable, and function like healthy natural teeth.

Am I too old for full-arch dental implants?

Age alone does not determine candidacy. Many adults in their 60s, 70s, and beyond successfully receive full-arch dental implants. Your overall health, bone quality, and ability to heal are much more important factors.

Do full-arch dental implants help prevent bone loss?

Yes. Unlike traditional dentures, which rest on the gums, full-arch dental implants are anchored into the jawbone. This allows the implants to stimulate the bone much like natural tooth roots, helping reduce the bone loss that typically occurs after teeth are lost. Preserving the jawbone also helps maintain facial structure and supports the long-term fit of your restoration.

How long do full-arch dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene, routine dental care, and regular maintenance visits, the dental implants themselves can last for decades. The prosthetic teeth may eventually require maintenance or replacement due to normal wear.

Can I replace all of my upper and lower teeth at the same time?

Many patients are candidates to replace both arches during the same course of treatment. Whether this is recommended depends on your oral health, medical history, and treatment plan.
