A mentor once told Dr. Allen the most important decision he will make in his career is deciding where to raise his family. Knowing that Michigan would always be home, Dr. Allen could not think of a better place than northern Michigan. Dr. Allen and his family moved to Traverse City after graduation and founded Shoreline Endodontics in 2011.

Dr. Allen enjoys sharing his passion for endodontics with his peers and has hosted various lectures at local dental society meetings and study clubs. He is also proud to serve as a guest lecturer for the dental assisting program at Northwestern Michigan College. He is a member of the Resort District Dental Society, American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association, American Association of Endodontists, and Michigan Association of Endodontists.

When he isn’t practicing dentistry, Dr. Allen enjoys spending time with his wife and two children, a good Petoskey stone hunt, boating, water sports, traveling, hockey, and Michigan State football.

Dr. Barrord grew up in Middletown, OH. She received her undergraduate degree from The College of Wooster in 2015 and her Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry degree from Case Western Reserve School of Dental Medicine in 2018. Dr. Barrord then stayed in Cleveland for an additional two years of schooling to complete her specialty training in endodontics. She received her Masters degree and Certificate of Endodontics from Case Western Reserve in 2020.

While in dental school, Dr. Barrord completed research in collaboration with the endodontics department. It was then that she fell in love with the practice and art of root canal therapy. She was drawn not only to the finesse and intricacies of treatment but also to the opportunity to really help people, especially those in pain. "Nothing is more rewarding to me than helping patients save their natural teeth while alleviating pain in the process. It is my utmost goal to offer a comfortable environment while providing gentle care to my patients."

Dr. Barrord is a member of the American Dental Association, American Association of Endodontics, Michigan Dental Association, and Michigan Association of Endodontics. In her free time, Dr. Barrord loves trying out new recipes, oil painting, staying active, and boating.

Dr. Haag attended Michigan State University before entering the University of Michigan, School of Dentistry, and received his D.D.S. degree in 1994. During dental school, Dr. Haag accepted a scholarship and committed to three years of service in the United States Navy. Over the course of five years in the U.S. Navy, Dr. Haag completed an Advanced General Dentistry program; served as the Dental Officer on board the Amphibious Assault Ship, USS New Orleans; and served at the Naval Dental Center San Diego, where he developed an interest in endodontics.

Following the completion of his endodontic Masters program at the University of Michigan, Dr. Haag worked in a group endodontic practice in southeastern Michigan and continued to teach part-time at the University of Michigan, School of Dentistry. Dr. Haag and his wife were raised in Manistee, MI, and decided to return to northern Michigan in 2003. Dr. Haag joined the team at Shoreline Endodontics in 2017.

Dr. Haag is a Diplomate of the American Board of Endodontics, past president of the Resort District Dental Society, and has served on the Michigan Board of Dental Specialty Examiners. Dr. Haag enjoys spending time with his wife and three children. Together, they seek opportunities to enjoy the beauty of this region fishing, skiing, and mountain biking. Dr. Haag also plays the guitar and piano.
