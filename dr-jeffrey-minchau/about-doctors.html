SPENCER R. STILES D.D.S., M.S.

Dr. Stiles is from Kimberly, WV – a small town in the western end of Monongalia County. He received his undergraduate BS degree from WVU in Forensic Identification and chose to pursue dentistry after completing an internship with a forensic dentist. Dr. Stiles graduated from the WVU School of Dentistry in 2008, and went on to obtain his postdoctoral specialty degree in Endodontics from WVU in 2010.

Spencer married his high-school sweetheart, Bethanie, in 2006, and they had a son, Henley, in 2009. In 2013, they had their second son, Challen, and on Spencer’s birthday (April 29th) of 2016 they had a daughter, Briston.

DR. SEAN M. HORAN D.D.S., M.S.

Dr. Horan grew up in Wheeling, WV. He received his undergraduate degree from Susquehanna University in Selinsgrove, PA in 2009 and his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from West Virginia University in 2013. Following dental school, he completed a one year advanced education in general dentistry residency in Cincinnati, OH and worked as a general dentist in Steubenville, OH the following year. Dr. Horan completed his post-doctoral specialty training in endodontics at WVU in 2017.

Sean is a member of the American Dental Association and the American Association of Endodontists. He met his wife, MacKenzie, in dental school, and they have been married since 2015. Sean enjoys hiking, golfing, playing soccer, going to Mountaineer games, and hanging out with his wife and dog, Graham.

Dr. Leslie P. Wolford D.M.D, M.S

Dr. Wolford is originally from Lima, Peru where she grew up and completed her undergraduate and graduate studies at University of San Martin de Porres. She graduated with a Bachelor’s of Dentistry and Dental Surgeon degree in 2012, as well as earning the Salutatorian designation of her class. In 2015, she graduated with her Masters in Endodontics from Norbert Wiener University.

After meeting the love of her life in Richmond, VA, she moved to the U.S and completed her degree in Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry at Boston University in 2021. She has recently graduated from WVU with her Master of Science in Endodontics.

Dr. Wolford enjoys dancing to Latin music and exploring the world with her family. West Virginia is her new home, due to the mountains stealing her heart!

JEFFREY G. MINCHAU D.D.S., M.S.

Dr. Minchau grew up in Johnstown, PA. He received his undergraduate degree from the University of Pittsburgh at Johnstown in 1996 and his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from West Virginia University in 2000. Following graduation, Dr. Minchau practiced general dentistry in Johnstown for two years. He then completed his post-doctoral specialty program in endodontics at WVU in 2004.

Dr. Minchau is a professional member of the Monongalia Valley Dental Society, Allegheny & Garrett County Dental Society, the WV Dental Association (where he has served on the executive council), the American Dental Association, and the American Association of Endodontists.

In his free time, Jeff enjoys staying active with his family and friends. Some of his favorite hobbies include playing golf, stand-up paddle-boarding, and boating on our amazing local lakes.

Jeff married his wife Beth, a family nurse practitioner and Morgantown native, in 2000. They have two sons, Owen and Ryan, and a daughter Sara – the main reason for his grey hair!

Dr. Kent McBride

Dr. McBride was raised in Damascus, MD and Petersburg, WV. He received his undergraduate degree from West Virginia University in 2001, his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from West Virginia University in 2005, and then completed his post-doctoral specialty training in endodontics at WVU in 2007.

Dr. McBride is an advocate for collaborative multi-disciplinary dental care and believes that dental care is best provided when specialists and general dentists are working in harmony.

In his spare time, Kent enjoys skiing, running, fitness, and spontaneous outdoor adventures with his family. Kent and his lovely wife, Sarah, have three boys Cole (born in 2013), Cooper (born in 2015), and Hudson (born in 2019). Kent lived in Montgomery County throughout his childhood and has many family ties in the area.

Dr. Seyedeh Maryam Razmi

Seyedeh Maryam Razmi completed her undergraduate dental education in Iran in 2016 and earned her post-graduate degree in Periodontics, along with a Master’s degree in Oral Sciences, from the University at Buffalo in 2024. She is Board Certified by the American Board of Periodontology and is a dedicated periodontist with a passion for combining clinical excellence with compassionate, patient-centered care.

She is a dedicated periodontist with a passion for combining clinical excellence with compassionate patient care. With a strong focus on periodontal surgeries, soft tissue grafts, and implant therapy, Dr. Razmi is committed to preserving the foundation of oral health through precision and advanced techniques. Her professional interests lie at the intersection of periodontal and systemic health, and she finds great fulfillment in helping patients improve not only their oral well-being but also their confidence and quality of life.

She chose a career in dentistry because it uniquely blends science, art, and human connection. She values the opportunity to make a lasting impact on her patients’ lives through hands-on care, education, and thoughtful communication. Known for her attentive listening skills and patient-centered approach, she ensures every patient feels heard, informed, and empowered throughout their treatment journey.

Outside of the dental office, Dr. Razmi enjoys spending quality time with her family, especially baking and exploring new places with her young daughter. She is fluent in both English and Farsi (Persian), which helps her connect with a diverse patient population.
