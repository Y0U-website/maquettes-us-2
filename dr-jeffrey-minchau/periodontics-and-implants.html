Periodontics and Implants

We are pleased to have Dr. Kerri T. Simpson, B.S.D.H., D.D.S., M.S., and Dr. Seyedeh Maryam Razmi, D.D.S., M.S., offering advanced dental implant care at our practice. Both doctors specialize in precision-guided surgical implant placement using state-of-the-art CBCT (3-dimensional) radiography to ensure the highest level of accuracy, safety, and long-term success.

Dental implants are often the best treatment option for missing or severely damaged teeth. When a tooth is removed, both the visible crown and the root are lost. A dental implant replaces the root and fuses with your natural bone, creating a strong and lasting foundation for a replacement tooth, bridge, or denture. Whether you are missing a single tooth, multiple teeth, or require full-arch restoration, implants provide the closest possible alternative to healthy, natural teeth—restoring not just your smile, but your full confidence to eat, speak, laugh, and enjoy everyday life without worry.

All-On-X Dental Implants

We are also proud to offer the All-on-X dental implant solution—a transformative option for patients in need of full-arch tooth replacement. This advanced procedure uses four to six strategically placed implants to support a fixed, natural-looking prosthetic arch, offering superior stability, function, and aesthetics compared to traditional dentures. Dr. Simpson and Dr. Razmi bring expert training and experience to every All-on-X case, delivering life-changing results through personalized care and the latest surgical technology.

If you’re considering dental implants or would like to explore your options, we invite you to schedule a consultation with Dr. Simpson or Dr. Razmi. Discover how modern dental implants can help you reclaim your smile—and your quality of life.
