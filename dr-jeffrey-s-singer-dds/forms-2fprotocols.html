On-line Registration and Health History Form

The COVID crisis has been a challenge for everyone and we feel it is important to maintain our vigilance for the safety of our patients and our staff. Although we won’t require that masks be worn when in our office, we recommend you stay vigilant and if your patient is feeling ill and/or has a fever, we prefer to reschedule that appointment. This policy is subject to change based on exposure in our community.

Our latest office information and protocols during the COVID -19 crisis.

Everyone entering the office is encouraged to wear a facemask. We ask that patients bring a pair of glasses for eye protection during their appointment. If you are feeling ill, you may be asked to reschedule your appointment. If the patient stayed home from school or work due to illness, we prefer you reschedule your appointment. Parents and siblings are allowed in the office. We recommend a facemask, which is optional. Parents: If you prefer to wait in the car, we will come out to update you.

If you have a specific question regarding COVID or how we protect our patients and staff, please contact us
