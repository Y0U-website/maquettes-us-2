Cost of Dental Implants and What Affects Pricing in Brunswick

  • Does the $5,000 starting cost include everything? The typical starting cost of about $5,000 refers to a general baseline and may not reflect every patient’s total treatment cost. Your final estimate depends on your specific needs, such as imaging, any additional procedures, and the type of final restoration. You will receive a comprehensive treatment plan after your consultation with clear expected costs.
  • Are dental implants covered by insurance? Some insurance companies are now providing partial coverage for implant treatment. We work directly with your insurance provider to confirm benefits and, when appropriate, request coverage allowances comparable to bridges or dentures. Because policies vary, reviewing your benefits is an important part of planning your treatment.
  • How do I get an accurate quote for dental implants? The best way to get an accurate quote is to schedule an implant consultation. This visit includes evaluation and a detailed treatment plan outlining recommended procedures, number of appointments, and associated costs. Our team will walk you through the plan and answer any questions so you can make an informed decision.
  • Can financing help make implant treatment more manageable? Yes, financing and structured payment options may help spread treatment costs over time. Since implant treatment is planned in stages, many patients prefer a payment approach that aligns with the timeline of care. Our team can review available options and help you choose what works best for your budget.
  • What happens if an implant placed elsewhere has complications? Dr. Oshetski sees many patients who experience issues with implants placed by less experienced providers, which can require additional visits, procedures, and expense. A thorough evaluation is needed to understand the situation and recommend next steps. The goal is always to create a plan that supports stability, comfort, and long-term function.
