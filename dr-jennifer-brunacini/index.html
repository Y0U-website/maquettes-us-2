The practice

Whether you are missing one tooth or need a full-arch solution, dental implants offer a long-term way to restore comfort, function, and confidence. Dr. James A. Oshetski provides both implant placement and final restoration under one roof, using advanced diagnostics and patient-focused care to support predictable planning and long-term results.

Dr. James A. Oshetski is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral Implantology and Implant Dentistry (ABOI/ID) and has advanced training in both implant placement and restoration. This means patients can receive implant treatment planning, surgical placement, and final restoration under one roof with continuity of care from start to finish.

Our team supports patients through every step with clear communication, detailed planning, and a focus on long-term function and comfort.

At James A. Oshetski, DDS, Implant and Restorative Dentistry, your care is guided by experience, advanced diagnostic technology, and a commitment to patient comfort. We take the time to understand your goals, explain treatment clearly, and deliver personalized implant and restorative care in a calm, supportive environment.
