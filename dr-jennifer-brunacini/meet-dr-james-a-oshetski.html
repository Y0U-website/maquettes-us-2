National Recognition and Advanced Focus

Dr. Oshetski is nationally recognized for his work in implant dentistry and restorative dentistry. He uses digitally driven technology and modern diagnostic tools to support precise planning and predictable outcomes. His primary areas of focus include implant rehabilitation, restorative dentistry, and sedation dentistry. Dr. Oshetski is also active in continuing education and professional mentorship. He serves as an educator and guest lecturer and has created educational opportunities in implant dentistry through Oshetski Implant Seminars and an AAID study club in Maine.
