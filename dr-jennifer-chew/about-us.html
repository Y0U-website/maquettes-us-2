Your First Visit

Modern Comforts & The Best in Tech

From your first visit to Nest Dental, we know you’ll feel right at home. Our top-of-the-line A-dec dental chairs are some of the most comfortable in the industry—so kick back and put your feet up while we clean your teeth. Our patients also enjoy the use of headphones to tune out and listen to their favorite songs while we work. Our office carries the latest dental tools and technology for your convenience, from CEREC same-day crowns to digital x-rays and mess-free impressions for Invisalign thanks to our iTero scanner.
