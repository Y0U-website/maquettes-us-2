Crowns & Bridges

If you’ve had a tooth extracted, a dental bridge can be used to replace a missing tooth, and it uses the teeth on either side as anchor teeth. A crown is placed in between these to restore your smile. Ceramic crowns can be used to provide strength and fortification to teeth after root canals or serious decay. Dr. Jennifer Ray’s team uses the best dental technology available for creating ceramic crowns and performing tooth bonding treatments. We also provide composite, tooth-colored fillings for a natural, healthy looking smile.

Dentures and Partials

At Jennifer Ray Family Dentistry in Biloxi, Mississippi, our team offers two types of dentures: complete and partial dentures. If you are missing all teeth, a complete denture is the best solution. If you have some teeth, we can form a partial denture that will perfectly fit around the teeth and gums, comfortably. Your self-esteem should not suffer, we can bring smile back to life with complete or partial dentures.

Implant Restoration

Are you ready to restore your smile with a permanent solution? When teeth are missing or unable to retain a dental prosthesis, like a crown or dentures, dental implants are used to restore a smile. We use best products and techniques to make sure your implants feel and look natural.

Root Canal Treatment

At Jennifer Ray Family Dentistry, we use the latest techniques and technology to perform root canals. A root canal is a treatment used to repair and save a tooth that is badly decayed or becomes infected. During a root canal procedure, the nerve and pulp are removed and the inside of the tooth is cleaned and sealed. Without treatment, the tissue surrounding the tooth will become infected and abscesses may form. Schedule your appointment today!
