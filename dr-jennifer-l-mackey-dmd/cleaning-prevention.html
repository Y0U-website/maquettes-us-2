0 Cleaning & Prevention Maintaining a regular schedule for preventative care with your hygienist and Dr. Mackey is key to safeguarding your oral health. Not only does this regimen help prevent tooth decay and periodontal disease, but it also helps you avoid costly procedures and lengthy appointments in the hygiene chair.

In addition to your daily brushing and flossing routine, there are supplementary tools available to make oral care more effective. Depending on your specific needs, special toothpaste, rinses, and flossing aids can enhance your oral hygiene regimen and contribute to a brighter, healthier smile. At Mackey Dental Care, we can assist you in selecting the most suitable tools to complement your daily routine, ultimately making your professional cleaning appointments more efficient and stress-free.

Diet plays a significant role in oral health as well. While acidic foods and drinks can erode enamel, a balanced diet can help strengthen your teeth and gums, promoting overall oral health.
