We our excited to announce that our Kids No Cavity Club has been revamped to celebrate back to school, we want to reward cavity free children in our community.

The practice

This exciting news will be in local community newspapers this month as well as on the Portland Examiner news page.

This unique dental program promotes positive and healthy oral care habits to children who are cavity free. Students of all ages can now join the No Cavity Club and be eligible to win prizes throughout the year!

To participate in this educational program, children must brush and floss their teeth every day, then make an appointment . When the doctor confirms that the children have no cavities they are immediately entered into the program.

Our primary focus is to support healthy oral care habits, and encourage a positive association with visiting the Dentist. We find that the No Cavity Club creates a sense of community which helps to support a healthy lifestyle throughout the rest of the child’s life. —Says Doctor Jennifer McLeod.

Whens students qualify for this program, Dr. McLeod and her compassionate team at Newberg Family Dental will take the child’s photo and place it on the office No Cavity Club tree.

Every three months, a staff member at the Newberg Dental Practice will put all the No Cavity Club members into a drawing for a chance to win fantastic prizes.

Each participant and winner will also be featured on our dental practice’s Facebook page .
