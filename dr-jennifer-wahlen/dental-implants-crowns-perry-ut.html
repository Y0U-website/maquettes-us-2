What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a tiny titanium post that our team surgically implants into your jawbone. The post serves as a replacement root for the tooth you lost. A few months later, an abutment is connected to a custom crown that looks and feels just like a real tooth.

How Does Placing a Crown on a Molar Implant Work?

After the team implants your post, it goes through a process called osseointegration. This means it fuses with the jawbone, becoming part of your body.

Your back molars are your hardest-working teeth. They go through a lot of chewing force. And the porcelain crown that tops off the implant is built to handle all that pressure. You’ll eat and chew your food just like you did before.

Am I a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

If your remaining teeth and gums are in good shape, you may be a good fit. One thing is that you need enough bone in your jaw to support the titanium post. If you’ve experienced some bone loss, we may be able to perform a bone graft so you can get an implant.

Some conditions can slow down the healing process. Heavy smoking or uncontrolled diabetes, for example. Our team will review your medical history and take X-rays to ensure dental implants are a good fit for you.

Does Insurance Cover Dental Implants?

Some dental insurance plans cover part of the cost of dental implants. But your coverage depends on your plan. You’ll need to call your insurance company to see what your benefits are. At Wahlen Dentistry, you’ll also have financing options that can help make implants and other treatments more affordable.

What Should I Expect With the Dental Implant Process?

Here’s a breakdown of what getting dental implants at Wahlen Dentistry entails:

  • X-rays and digital imaging taken to map out your treatment.
  • Titanium post surgically placed into your jawbone.
  • Healing period of a few months while the bone fuses with the implant.
  • Abutment attached to the top of the post.
  • A custom crown is placed.
