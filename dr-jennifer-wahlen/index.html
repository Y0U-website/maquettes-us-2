The practice

At Wahlen Dentistry, our dental team is committed to providing general and cosmetic dentistry services for patients in and around Northern Utah. We offer high-quality dentistry with leading-edge technology, ensuring you have access to both the latest and most advanced solutions for your smile!

Drs. Jennifer Wahlen and John Torghele provide a warm and inviting atmosphere to ensure every visit to our office is a positive experience. We offer comfortable, relaxing care so you never have to feel nervous visiting the dentist’s chair! Our practice allows patients to have everything from a simple cleaning to a complex complete mouth restoration with our experienced and skilled team.

As a new patient, you will first see the dentist for a comprehensive exam. Once your examination is complete your dentist will discuss any findings with you, and if necessary will work out a treatment plan for you. To register as a new patient, simply call us. If possible, please print out and complete our Patient Registration Form prior to arriving. This will save you time on your initial visit.
