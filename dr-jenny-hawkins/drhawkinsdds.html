Dr. Jenny O. Hawkins, DDS

Dr. Hawkins was born and raised in St. Mary’s County and is a graduate of St. Mary’s Ryken High School. She received her bachelor’s degree from Towson University and obtained her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry . Dr. Hawkins has a heart to serve others, believes in treating her patients with compassion and respect, and is dedicated to providing dental care that promotes optimal oral health and a healthy smile. She is a member of the American Dental Association , Maryland State Dental Association and Patuxent Dental Society.

Dr. Jenny Hawkins is married to Michael Hawkins, and they have three children, Reagan, Jace (JJ) and Bryce. They live in the Lexington Park area, where they enjoy spending time outdoors with their family and friends.
