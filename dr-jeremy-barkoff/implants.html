  • 11 I had an extraction with a bone graft, how long do I need to wait to get an implant placed? The bone graft that was placed prevents bone from atrophying after the extraction. We do need to ensure the bone has completely grown into the bone grafting site and is of the quality that will hold an implant. We recommend waiting 4 months for a mandibular (lower jaw) bone graft, 5 months for a maxillary (upper jaw) bone graft, and 6 months if you&#x27;ve had a sinus lift. These timeframes give us the best chance of a successful implant.
  • 12 What is a dental bone graft? A dental bone graft is a procedure that uses bone tissue to help repair and heal around a dental surgery site. It&#x27;s often used to support dental implants, secure loose teeth, or rebuild bone lost due to gum disease or trauma.
  • 13 I am getting a tooth removed. Do you recommend a bone graft? What are the reasons to get a bone graft? We do recommend a bone graft for any teeth that are being removed, especially if you may get an implant in the future. Additionally, we recommend a bone graft for any teeth that cause bone loss or periodontal disease on adjacent teeth. Bone grafts help maintain the structure and density of your jawbone, providing a stable foundation for future dental implants and preventing further complications associated with bone loss.
  • 14 What types of bone grafts are available? There are three main types of bone grafts used in dental procedures: Autograft: Bone taken from another part of your own body, such as your hip, ribs, or wrist.
  • Allograft: Bone taken from a deceased donor or a cadaver, which is cleaned and sterilized.
  • Xenograft: Bone taken from a different species, usually a cow or pig, which is also processed and sterilized.
  • 15 Which type of bone graft is best for me? The best type of bone graft depends on your specific situation and preferences. Autografts are often considered the gold standard because they come from your own body and have a lower risk of rejection. However, allografts and xenografts are also effective and may be preferred in certain cases.

  • 17 How long does it take to recover from a bone graft? Recovery time varies, but generally, it takes about 4-6 months for the bone graft to heal and integrate with your existing bone. During this time, you&#x27;ll need to follow your dentist&#x27;s care instructions to ensure proper healing.
