Jeri Coffey, D.D.S.

Dr. Jeri Coffey received her BA from North Central College in Naperville, Il.

She received her DDS from Loyola University School of Dentistry in Maywood, Il.

Dr. Coffey was an Associate Professor of Operative Dentistry at Loyola Dental School for four years.

She has completed advanced training from Dawson Academy, in St. Petersburg, Florida, in Occlusion Studies. She is also certified in the use of TEKSCAN, a computer generated Occlusion Analysis Program, and has completed training in Neuromuscular Dentistry.

Dr. Coffey has completed advanced training and is a certified BOTOX and DERMAL FILLER injector.

She has also completed advanced training in MYOFUNCTIONAL THERAPY and is a certified MYOBRACE provider. She has completed additional training in INVISALIGN.
