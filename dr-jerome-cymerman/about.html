Dr. O’Dwyer’s Publications & Research

In addition to endodontic private practice, Dr. Cymerman is an associate clinical professor at the Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine. He was the Director of the Division of Endodontics and pre-doctoral endodontics. Dr. Cymerman was also the post-graduate director of the Advanced education program in Endodontics for several years. He has been involved and continues to be involved in Endodontic research and has published numerous peer reviewed research articles in the Journal of Endodontics.
