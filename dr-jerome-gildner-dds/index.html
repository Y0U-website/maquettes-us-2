The practice

Dr. Gildner was born in Mequon, and attended Homestead High School before graduating from the Marquette University School of Dentistry. He went on to become a clinical professor at Marquette, with a focus on orthodontics and pediatric airway development.

Dr. Gildner is affiliated with the International Association of Orthodontics, and he has extensive knowledge on the treatment of sleep apnea, full-mouth rehabilitation, and cosmetic dentistry. Dr. Gildner and his wife, Mona, have been married for 37 years, and they have two grown children. In his recreational time, Dr. Gildner enjoys golf, scuba diving, skiing, and cycling.

Dr. Julia Snell grew up in the small town of Edgerton, Wisconsin before receiving her undergraduate degree in Biology and Chemistry from Edgewood College in Madison, Wisconsin. She then attended Marquette University School of Dentistry to receive her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

Here she focused her studies on Operative Dentistry, Fixed and Removable Prosthodontics, Oral Surgery and Pediatrics. She is a member of the American Dental Association and the Wisconsin Dental Association. Being compassionate and available to her patients is what she strives for.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Snell enjoys spending time with her family, traveling, being outdoors and cheering on Wisconsin sports.
