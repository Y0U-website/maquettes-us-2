INFECTION CONTROL

Infection control in dental offices is a subject of concern to everyone. In our practice, nothing is more important than the health and safety of our patients. We observe controls required by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), and we follow, and in many cases, exceed, American Dental Association and Center for Disease Control guidelines.

Our practice utilizes many infection control techniques.

We want you to know that we are working hard to always do our best for you.

  • We completely sterilize all dental instruments before each use.
  • We heat / steam sterilize all high speed handpieces (drills) in an autoclave before each use.
  • We chemically sterilize all instruments which are sensitive to heat.
  • We use disposable, single use products whenever possible.
  • We disinfect all surfaces, such as chairs, light handles and counters prior to each patient treatment.
