Check My Implant Eligibility What Is a Single Tooth Implant? A single tooth implant is a small titanium post that acts as an artificial root for one missing tooth. We gently place the post into your jawbone, where it naturally bonds with the bone over a few months to create a solid foundation. Once healed, we attach a custom crown (the visible tooth) that matches the shape, size, and color of your natural teeth. The result looks, feels, and functions just like the tooth you lost.

At Harmony Dentistry, we plan every single tooth implant in Westfield & Fortville, IN using advanced 3-D cone beam imaging and digital scanning. This technology lets us map the exact position of your bone and nerves, so placement is precise and personalized to your smile.

Preparing Your Smile

If your jaw needs extra support, we may gently rebuild the area with a bone graft before placement. When your tooth still needs removing, we handle that comfortably with local anesthesia and sedation options if you’d like them. This groundwork creates the strong, stable foundation your new tooth needs to thrive for years to come.

Implant Placement

On placement day, we numb the area completely, so you feel only gentle pressure, never pain. Using your custom digital plan, we carefully position the titanium post into your jawbone with precision. The visit is usually quicker than patients imagine, and sedation keeps you calm and relaxed throughout. You’ll head home the same day with clear guidance.

Healing and Integration

Over the next few months, your implant naturally bonds with the surrounding bone, forming a permanent anchor. We’ll check in during this phase to make sure everything is healing beautifully. Many patients are surprised by how little this stage interrupts their daily routine, and a temporary option can keep your smile looking whole.

Your Final Crown

Once healing is complete, we attach your custom-made crown, color-matched to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. We check the fit and your bite until everything feels just right. You’ll walk out with a complete, confident smile and a tooth that looks and works like it was always there.

Schedule Appointment What to Expect After Your Single Tooth Implant Most patients are surprised by how manageable recovery really is, with far less discomfort than they anticipated. Healing does take a little time, and our team stays close by to support you every step of the way. First 24-48 Hours You may notice mild swelling or tenderness around the area, which is completely normal and easy to manage. Rest, stick to soft foods, and use cold compresses to stay comfortable. Most patients find any discomfort settles quickly with simple over-the-counter pain relief.

First 1-2 Weeks

Swelling continues to fade and you’ll gradually return to your usual routine and foods. The area heals steadily during this time, and most patients feel back to normal within a few days. Gentle care around the site keeps everything progressing smoothly.

Long-Term Healing

Over the next few months, the implant quietly bonds with your bone beneath the surface. You won’t feel this process happening, and you can live your life as usual while it strengthens. This is the phase that gives your new tooth its lasting stability.
