The practice

(I’m the one who will be checking your teeth, holding back your tongue with a mirror and helping you smile your best smile.)

Even before I became a dentist , I always loved being a part of Sarnia’s tight-knit local community. And it’s that small-town sense of togetherness, friendship and willingness to go the extra mile that I want to bring to my practice.

Sure, I want to make sure that you and your teeth are as healthy as they can be. But, to me, you’re more than just a set of teeth.

That’s why we make sure that when you come to our office, you’re not just coming for a check-up or procedure, you’re stopping by to see friends. (Albeit, a group of friends that know a lot about your teeth…)

Whether you need a quick check-up or a complicated procedure, we’re always here to help. Just pencil yourself into a window that suits you and we’ll call you back to arrange an appointment. It’s as easy as that.

If your appointment time becomes inconvenient for you, we are always happy to change it if you provide us with one (1) business days’ notice. This allows us to schedule a patient who may be in urgent need of care.

We strive to accommodate the scheduling needs of our patients, and we will make every effort to keep your schedule on time. Failure to provide us with 24 hours advance notice or failure to show up for a scheduled appointment will result in a cancellation/no show fee.

(There’s nothing worse than waiting to see a dentist when you’ve got a toothache. If you’re in pain, just give us a call and we’ll make sure that we see you as soon as possible.)
