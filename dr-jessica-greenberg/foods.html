Foods to Avoid with Braces & Eating Tips

These foods are gentle on braces, easy to chew, and less likely to cause breakage.

  • Foods to Avoid with Braces & Eating Tips | NA Orthodontics top of page yes foods Soft fruits and vegetables: bananas, blueberries, steamed carrots, cooked broccoli
  • Dairy products: yogurt, cheese, milkshakes, smoothies
  • Soft grains: pasta, rice, pancakes, tortillas
  • Tender proteins: eggs, fish, shredded chicken, meatballs
  • Snacks and treats: soft cookies, applesauce, pudding, ice cream without nuts

NO foods

These items can bend wires, pop off brackets, or get stuck and make cleaning difficult.

  • Hard foods: nuts, hard candies, raw carrots, popcorn kernels
  • Sticky foods: caramel, taffy, chewing gum
  • Crunchy foods: chips, pretzels, ice
  • Foods you bite into with force: whole apples, corn on the cob, crusty bread rolls

Tips for Success

With the right food choices, you’ll protect your braces and keep your treatment on track while still enjoying a wide variety of meals and snacks.

  • Cut crunchy fruits and veggies (like apples or carrots) into small, bite-sized pieces.
  • Choose softer alternatives when possible.
  • Brush and floss carefully after meals to keep teeth and braces clean.
