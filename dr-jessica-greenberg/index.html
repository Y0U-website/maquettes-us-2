The practice

At NA Orthodontics, our team of expert orthodontists combine advanced training, research, teaching and years of clinical experience to deliver exceptional care. From braces to Invisalign , they bring a patient-first approach, modern technology, and a passion for creating confident, healthy smiles.

At NA Ortho, our advanced Self-Ligating braces make treatment cleaner, more comfortable and often faster. Choose clear or metal with optional colors and enjoy a healthier, straighter smile with fewer office visits

Early orthodontic treatment helps guide jaw growth, create space for permanent teeth and correct developing bite issues before they become more serious. At NA Orthodontics, we carefully monitor children’s smiles to recommend the right time for treatment, making care easier and more effective.

At NA Orthodontics, we use advanced 3D technology including digital scanning, 3D X-rays and precision wire bending - all to create customized, comfortable treatments that ensure accuracy, efficiency, and beautiful results for every patient and smile.

At NA Ortho, we are participating providers for most insurance plans. We also help you handle your insurance claims.

NA Ortho offers multiple in-house, interest- free payment plans and we accept flex spending accounts.
