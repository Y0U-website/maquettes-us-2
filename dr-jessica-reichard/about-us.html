What We Offer

The caring team at Westlake Dental offers a wide range of services for families, including comprehensive check-ups, fillings, crowns, bridges, veneers, inlays, onlays, simple and surgical extractions of unsalvageable teeth, Zoom teeth whitening, invisible braces, dentures and partial dentures, bite balancing, and treatment of TMJ.

To protect teeth in special situations, Drs. Constantinou and Reichard address clenching, grinding, and snoring, as well as sports injuries, with custom-fit mouth guards.

How We Keep Our Promise

Dr. Constantinou’s first task upon heading up the practice in 2013 was to gather the entire staff as a group and craft a mission statement to define their culture, which he describes as “very non-corporate.”

The group approach is to treat patients like family, providing the best-case synergy of technology, relationships, and compassionate care.
