Dr. Jessica Reichard

Dr. Jessica Reichard was born and raised in the Cleveland suburb of Brecksville, Ohio. Dr. Jessica earned her undergraduate and Doctor of Dental Medicine degrees from Case Western Reserve University. There, she graduated summa cum laude and at the top of her dental school class. Upon graduation, she was inducted into the OKU National Dental Honor Society. Dr. Reichard also completed advanced training in a general practice residency at the Louis Stokes Cleveland VA Medical Center.

Dr. Divya Bekal

Dr. Divya Bekal is from Sylvania, Ohio. She received her undergraduate degree from The Ohio State University, master’s degree in medical physiology from Case Western Reserve University, and dental degree from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. One year prior to attending dental school, Dr. Bekal worked for AmeriCorps where she taught children in underserved areas of Ohio about health and nutrition. After graduating dental school, she completed a general practice residency at the Summa Health System in Akron, Ohio.
