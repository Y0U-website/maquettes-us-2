Teeth that are out of place affect the way a person chews and talks and how their smile looks. In some cases, they can affect the way the jaws line up and can cause pain and discomfort.

Although it is usually your family dentist or orthodontist determine if orthodontic treatment is necessary for you after comprehensive examinations, the following questions may help in prompting you to seek orthodontic advice.

Do you see any signs of crooked teeth, gaps between the teeth or overlapping teeth?

Does the center of your upper front teeth line up with the center of your lower front teeth?

Is there any space between the biting surface of the front and /or side teeth when the back teeth bite together?

Do you find the upper teeth not come down slightly in front of the lower teeth?
