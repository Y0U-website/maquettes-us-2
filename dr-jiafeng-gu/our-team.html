MEET DR GU

Dr. Jiafeng Gu earned his medical degree from Fudan University in China in 2004. After earning his Ph.D. degree in molecular biology from University of Southern California, he went on to attend Columbia University for his DDS degree. He then finished orthodontic specialty training from The Ohio State University with a Master of Science degree.

Dr. Gu is a Board Certified Orthodontist by the American Board of Orthodontics. Of all the orthodontists, only about 1/3 are board certified. He is a preferred provider with Invisalign. He is also a member of American Association of Orthodontists and American Dental Association.
