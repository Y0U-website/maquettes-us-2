Dr. Jim Knight

Dr. Knight is thankful to have the career he always wanted and to serve the community where he grew up. When he isn’t practicing dentistry, Dr. Knight enjoys spending time with his adult children and grandchildren, playing tennis and being involved at CrossWay Evangelical Free Church.
