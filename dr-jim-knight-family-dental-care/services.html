The dental practice of Dr. Jim Knight has been family-owned and operated for three generations. Since taking over his father’s practice in 1984, Dr. Knight has provided family-friendly dental services to patients in every stage of life. Our knowledgeable team and dentists offer patients from across the Fort Dodge area with the care they need to maintain healthy teeth for a lifetime.

  • Preventative Dentistry Preventive dentistry is focused on preventing dental problems — cavities, gum disease, etc. — from developing. Our doctors and staff focus on helping each patient improve their immediate and long-term oral health through a range of treatments including: • Checkups & Cleanings • Oral Cancer Screenings • TMJ Treatment/Appliances • Gum Disease Treatments • Bruxism Appliances • Sleep Apnea • Snoring Appliances
  • Restorative Dentistry Our restorative services focus on repairing teeth damaged due to cavities, teeth, decay, or trauma. Restoring strength and structure to your teeth helps your smile stay healthy and beautiful for a lifetime. We offer restorative services including: • Crowns • Fillings • Root Canals • Extractions • Orthodontics
  • Cosmetic Dentistry Cosmetic dentistry services help restore brightness and luminescence to your smile. To ensure you receive modern treatments and thorough results, we utilize state-of-the-art technology and equipment. Our cosmetic treatments include: • Teeth Whitening • Bonding and Contouring • Cosmetic Veneers • Invisalign
  • Prosthetic Dentistry The goal of prosthetic dentistry treatments is to restore function, stability, and aesthetics to your smile after experiencing tooth loss. Our dentists and team provide personalized prosthetic solutions for each patient. These services include: • Bridges • Partial • Dentures • Implants
  • Emergency Services If you or a loved one are experiencing a dental emergency, please call our office at 515-573-8251 to receive emergency care. We will make every effort, especially if you are in pain, to see you as soon as possible. Contact us if you have experienced: • Losing a tooth due to trauma • Chipped or broken tooth • Tooth pain
