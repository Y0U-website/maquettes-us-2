How Does the SureSmile® Process Work?

  • Initial Consultation : We evaluate your teeth and bite to determine if SureSmile® is right for you.
  • Custom Treatment Plan : Using advanced 3D imaging technology, we create a personalized plan for your smile goals.
  • Aligner Fabrication : Your aligners are custom-made for a perfect fit.
  • Wearing the Aligners : Wear your aligners for 20-22 hours a day, changing to a new set approximately every two weeks.
  • Follow-Up Visits : We monitor your progress and make adjustments as needed.

Who Can Benefit from SureSmile®?

SureSmile® aligners are ideal for patients with:

  • Crooked or misaligned teeth.
  • Gaps or spacing issues.
  • Mild to moderate bite problems, such as overbite or underbite.

How Long Does Treatment Take?

The length of treatment varies depending on your specific needs, but most patients achieve their desired results within 6 to 18 months.
