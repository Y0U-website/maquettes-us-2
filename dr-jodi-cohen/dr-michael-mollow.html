Meet Dr. Swati Khambe

Dr. Swati Khambe joined North Branford Dental Group in 2013. She is a 2008 graduate of The Ohio State University with a BS in Microbiology and a minor in Business. She graduated in 2012 from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. She additionally completed a one year General Practice Residency at The Yale New-Haven Hospital.

Dr. Khambe lives in Branford and enjoys playing tennis, running, cooking for friends and spending time with her family.

Meet Dr. Jacob ltskovich​

Dr. Jacob ltskovich grew up in West Bloomfield, Michigan. He graduated from Michigan State University with a BS in Nutritional Sciences, an emphasis on Human Nutrition. He earned his Master of Science in Biomedical Sciences from Barry University in Hollywood, FL. In 2020, he graduated with his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston. To further his education, he completed a one year Post Graduate Residency in Advanced Education in General Dentist (AEGD) at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine.

When Dr. Itskovich’s isn’t busy caring for his patients, you can usually find him hanging out with family and friends, exercising at the gym, running outside, playing basketball, or watching football/basketball games.

Meet Dr. Joseph Manzella​

Dr. Manzella now lives in Ivoryton with his husband. When he’s not at work, you can find him enjoying the outdoors, training at a CrossFit gym, exploring the Connecticut shoreline, visiting family on Long Island, or engaged in a DIY project for his new home.

Meet Dr. Young Seok Jeong ​​

Dr. Jeong grew up in Bergen County, NJ. He graduated from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute in 2017 with a Bachelor’s of Science degree in Industrial and Systems engineering. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the Touro College of Dental Medicine in 2024. He then completed an Advanced Education Program in General Practice Residency (GPR) from Yale New Haven Hospital.

When Dr. Jeong is not at work, you can find him hiking, biking, camping and cooking.
