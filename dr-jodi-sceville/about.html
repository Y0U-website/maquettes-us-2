About Sceville Dentistry

Dr. Jodi Sceville, DDS is a dentist who partners with physicians and other health care providers to help patients manage sleep apnea and TMJ disorders. With more than 20 years of clinical experience, she’s compelled to creating individualized solutions for her patients. Dr. Sceville became passionate about education in sleep apnea and TMJ pain after helping her own family members with these health issues.

No matter what she’s doing her goal is simple-Listen to patients and find treatment options that improve their quality of life.

Dr. Sceville is active in the local community and when she isn’t working you’ll find her on the water enjoying houseboating, fishing and waterskiing with her family and golden retriever.
