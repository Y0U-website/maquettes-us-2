Conventional Dentures

Conventional dentures are good if you already have missing teeth. They are a full set of dentures placed in your mouth after all your existing teeth have been removed. It takes about 6 to 8 weeks to replace your teeth with a new set of conventional dentures.

Immediate Dentures

Immediate dentures are fitted and delivered on the same day your tooth extractions are completed. You will leave our office with a new set of teeth. Once they start to heal, you will come back for a relining to ensure they don’t get loose.

Removable Dentures

Removable dentures are an affordable way to bring you back your smile. They are fabricated in our lab using acrylic material.

Partial Dentures

Partial dentures are another option when not all your teeth need to be removed. This is like a bridge, but it is not a permanent fixture in your mouth.
