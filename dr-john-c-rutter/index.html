The practice

Thank you for your interest in Port Jefferson Dental Group. Our commitment is to provide you with the highest standard of personalized dental care in a gentle, efficient, and professional manner. We trust you will find the atmosphere calming, the staff friendly, and our dental services exceptional.

Good dentistry is more than excellent clinical skills and state-of-the-art equipment. Establishing lasting relationships with our patients, based upon mutual trust and open communication, is an important factor. We invite your questions and value any suggestions you may have. Working together, you can have the smile you’ve always wanted.

One of the founding partners along with Dr. Rosner and Dr. Marson, Dr. John C. Rutter was always guided by a firm belief in continually striving to improve patient care. By providing the highest quality of dentistry mixed with exceeding patients expectations, Dr. Rutter exemplified the definition of caring. Now retired, he spends his time with his lovely family and developing the perfect golf…

As providers and staff, we continue to follow in his footsteps. Never compromising and always delivering the highest standard of care to our patients.
