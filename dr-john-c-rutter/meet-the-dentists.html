0 Meet The Dentists Dr. Sophia C. Manolis, D.D.S. Dr. Sophia C. Manolis received her dental degree from New York University College of Dentistry in 2003. In addition, she spent one year as a resident at St. Charles Hospital serving the dental needs of physically and mentally challenged patients. Since 2004, she has served the community by volunteering at St. Charles Dental Clinic as an attending instructor.

With over 20 years experience, Dr. Manolis continues to deliver exceptional care and make each patient interaction both pleasant and comfortable.

When she is not caring for patients, Dr. Manolis spends her time with her beautiful family and enjoying all the wonderful outdoor activities the north shore of Long Island has to offer.

You can usually find her on a lacrosse or soccer field cheering on her girls!!
