Academics/Research/Honors

Dr. Sterrett has been an avid “ teacher ”, with over 30 years of teaching excellence and dedication whether university-based or lecturing locally or nationally.

“I love the practice of dentistry, but even more, I love the chance to share my experience with others”

As well Dr. Sterrett has had the good fortune to engage in groundbreaking dental research . Because of his many years of outstanding contribution to the profession of dentistry, Dr. Sterrett has had the honor of being inducted into 3 of dentistry’s most prestigious honor societies . In addition, The Tennessee Dental Association has honored Dr. Sterrett for his leadership and clinical excellence by awarding him the Fellowship Award for Distinguished Service .

Personal Interests

Dr. Sterrett, active in athletics his whole life, was a diver for the Varsity Swim Team at the Virginia Military Institute and played 4 yrs of Division I Soccer at both VMI and William & Mary. Later in life, while in his advanced specialty program, and at the age of 30, Dr. Sterrett was a member of the Dalhousie University Cross Country Team.

“My lovely wife and I have 2 very active children, 2 cats, and 1 dog! Outside a busy family life, I have had a keen interest in competitive running, swimming, bicycling & triathlons. And on the more sedate side, I love photography, fishing, golf, and bicycling touring.”
