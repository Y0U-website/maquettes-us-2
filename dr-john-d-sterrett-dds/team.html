Meet the Team

Patient Coordinator: Cody

Hi! My name is Cody and you could say I was raised in a dental family, my mom and aunt both being dental assistants.

I earned my RDA certification from Walter State Community College and I have been with Dr. Sterrett for 10+ years now, first as his surgical assistant and more recently as Patient Coordinator. I couldn’t be happier with my work environment, team members, and Boss!

I married my high school sweetheart and together we have 2 young children. We keep busy with coaching baseball, family camping, and our backyard mini-farm, with goats, ducks, turkey, chickens, and a donkey.

Surgical Assistant: Alyssa

Hi! My name is Alyssa and I am Dr. Sterrett’s surgical assistant. I was born and raised in Maynardville, TN, attending Union County High School.

Looking at, career options a friend suggested dentistry, a suggestion that changed my life! I completed my RDA program, with National Honor Society recognition, at the Tennessee College of Applied Technology. I was fortunate to be offered a position with Dr. Sterrett. It has been a wonderful move. I love the work, my team members, and more importantly the patient interaction.

In my free time, I like photography, gardening, hiking, and spending quality time with loved ones.

Dental Hygienist: Kerry

My name is Kerry and I have been in the field of dental hygiene for over 30 yrs now. I grew up and trained in New Jersey but later in life we moved to Tennessee and we love it here.

I have worked in many kinds of practices but working in a periodontal practice is the “Best”. There are so many things to see and learn. It has been fun meeting so many wonderful patients here in Dr. Sterrett’s practice.

In my free time, I like to spend time with my daughter and our loving pets.
