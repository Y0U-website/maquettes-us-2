2. Veneers

Veneers are ultra-thin shells placed over the front of the teeth to dramatically improve appearance. They can correct:

  • Tooth discoloration
  • Chips and cracks
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Uneven or crooked teeth
  • Worn-down enamel

3. Dental Implants

Dental implants are considered the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. They look, feel, and function like natural teeth and can last a lifetime with proper care.

How Implants Work

Dental implants can replace one tooth, multiple teeth, or a full arch. They restore chewing function, preserve bone structure, and enhance appearance.

  • A titanium post is placed in the jawbone
  • The post integrates with the bone over several months
  • A natural-looking dental crown is attached to complete the restoration

4. Cosmetic Tooth-Colored Fillings

Gone are the days of silver fillings. We use tooth-colored composite materials that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. These fillings are durable, safe, and aesthetically pleasing, perfect for restoring decayed or damaged teeth without compromising your smile.

FAQs

What is a cosmetic dentist?

A cosmetic dentist focuses on improving the appearance of your smile through treatments like whitening, veneers, implants, and bonding. At Park Falls Dental, we combine cosmetic and functional care to ensure your smile looks great and stays healthy.

How much do cosmetic dental treatments cost?

Costs range depending on the type of treatment. Whitening is typically the most affordable, while veneers and implants are higher investments due to materials and complexity. A consultation is the best way to get an accurate estimate.

How long do cosmetic procedures take?

Simple treatments like whitening or bonding take one visit. Veneers take a few weeks, and implants take several months. Larger smile makeovers vary based on your customized plan.

Are cosmetic dental procedures painful?

Most procedures are minimally invasive and performed with local anesthesia. Patients typically experience little to no discomfort.

How long do results last?

Veneers can last 10–15 years, while with proper care, implants can last many years. Whitening results last months to years, depending on habits, and bonding typically lasts several years. Remember that good oral hygiene and regular dental visits help extend results.

Will insurance cover cosmetic dentistry?

Insurance usually covers treatments with restorative benefits, but may not cover purely cosmetic procedures. Our team helps you maximize any eligible coverage.

What are the benefits of cosmetic dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry improves confidence, restores function, enhances appearance, and helps maintain long-term oral health. Many patients feel more comfortable smiling, speaking, and eating after treatment.
