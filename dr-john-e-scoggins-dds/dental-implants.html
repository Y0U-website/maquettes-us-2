Benefits of Dental Implants

Choosing dental implants in Ukiah, CA offers advantages that go far beyond aesthetics:

1. Natural Look and Feel

Implants mimic the appearance and function of natural teeth. Most patients forget they even have an implant.

2. Long-Term Durability

With proper care, implants can last more than 20 years, many last a lifetime.

3. Improved Chewing and Speaking

Unlike dentures that may slip or shift, implants remain securely in place, allowing you to eat and speak confidently.

4. Jawbone Preservation

Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss that occurs after missing teeth.

5. Supports Facial Structure

Because they prevent bone shrinkage, implants help maintain a youthful facial shape.

6. No Impact on Adjacent Teeth

Traditional bridges require grinding down adjacent healthy teeth. Implants eliminate that need.

7. Easy to Maintain

Care for implants just like natural teeth, brushing, flossing, and regular dental visits.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults in good oral and general health can qualify for dental implants. Ideal candidates:

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Have sufficient jawbone density
  • Do not smoke (or are willing to quit)
  • Are free from gum disease
  • Want a durable and fixed tooth replacement option

FAQs About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to last 20 years or longer . With excellent oral hygiene and routine checkups, many patients enjoy their implants for a lifetime.

What are the benefits of dental implants compared to dentures?

Implants provide superior stability, prevent bone loss, improve chewing ability, and look more natural. Dentures may slip, require adhesives, and do not preserve jawbone structure.

How much do dental implants cost in Ukiah?

Costs vary based on the number of implants, type of restoration, bone grafting needs, and materials. During your consultation, we provide a transparent, personalized breakdown of treatment costs.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The full process typically takes three to six months , depending on healing time and whether preparatory treatments are needed.

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort. Local anesthesia and sedation options help make the procedure smooth and comfortable. Post-treatment soreness usually lasts a few days.

Am I too old for dental implants?

There is no upper age limit for implants. As long as you’re healthy enough for routine dental procedures and have sufficient bone, you may qualify.

Do dental implants require special care?

No. Care for them just like natural teeth, such as brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings.
