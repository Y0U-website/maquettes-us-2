Fitting and Adjustments

Once your dentures are ready, we carefully check the fit and make any necessary adjustments. Our goal is to ensure comfort, stability, and proper bite alignment. Follow-up visits may be scheduled to fine-tune the fit as your mouth adjusts.

Adjusting to New Dentures

It is normal to experience a brief adjustment period when first wearing dentures. You may notice mild soreness, increased saliva production, or slight changes in speech. These issues typically resolve as your mouth becomes accustomed to the dentures.

Our team at Park Falls Dental provides guidance and support during this transition. We are always available to address concerns and make adjustments as needed to improve comfort and function.

Dentures and Long-Term Oral Health

Replacing missing teeth is not just about appearance. Dentures play a critical role in maintaining oral health by supporting proper bite alignment and preventing excessive wear on remaining teeth. They also help preserve facial structure and reduce jaw strain.

Regular dental visits allow us to monitor gum health, check denture fit, and address any changes in your mouth over time. Maintaining these appointments ensures your dentures continue to function effectively and comfortably.

Caring for Your Dentures in Ukiah, CA

Proper care is essential for keeping your dentures clean, comfortable, and long-lasting.

Good oral hygiene helps maintain healthy gums and extends the lifespan of your dentures.

  • Remove and rinse dentures after eating
  • Brush dentures daily using a soft brush and non-abrasive cleanser
  • Soak dentures overnight in a denture-safe solution
  • Clean your gums, tongue, and palate daily
  • Avoid using hot water, which can warp dentures
  • Schedule regular dental checkups for professional evaluation

FAQs About Dentures in Ukiah

How long do dentures last?

With proper care, dentures typically last between 5 and 10 years. Over time, natural changes in the mouth may require adjustments or replacement to maintain comfort and fit.

Are dentures uncomfortable?

Modern dentures are designed with comfort in mind. While there may be a short adjustment period, most patients find their dentures become comfortable with time and proper fitting.

Can I eat normally with dentures?

Yes. Dentures restore chewing ability, though it may take some practice. Starting with softer foods and gradually reintroducing harder textures can help ease the transition.

Do dentures look natural?

Today’s dentures are crafted to closely resemble natural teeth. At Park Falls Dental, we carefully select tooth shape and color to create a realistic, attractive smile.

How should I clean my dentures?

Dentures should be brushed daily with a soft brush and non-abrasive cleanser. They should also be soaked overnight and rinsed thoroughly before wearing.

Will dentures affect my speech?

Some patients notice minor speech changes initially. Practicing speaking and reading aloud can help your tongue and mouth adjust more quickly.

Are implant-supported dentures better than traditional dentures?

Implant-supported dentures offer increased stability and comfort for many patients. However, the best option depends on your oral health, bone structure, and personal preferences.
