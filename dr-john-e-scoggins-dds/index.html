Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of biocompatible materials such as titanium or zirconia. Once placed in the jawbone, they fuse with the bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a strong, stable foundation for replacement teeth.

Compared to removable dentures or traditional bridges, implants offer unmatched durability, stability, and natural appearance.

Implants provide superior stability, prevent bone loss, improve chewing ability, and look more natural. Dentures may slip, require adhesives, and do not preserve jawbone structure.
