How 3i Dental Implants Are Placed

The 3i implant procedure typically involves two short visits. The first visit usually lasts about an hour, and the second around 30 minutes. Procedures are performed under local anesthetic unless other sedation is requested.

Stage 1: Implant Placement

Healing is typically sufficient for the second stage to occur 10–12 weeks after the first visit.

  • An incision is made in the gum tissue to expose the jawbone.
  • A tiny hole is drilled into the bone to position the implant.
  • The implant is screwed or tapped into place.
  • A temporary stop-cap is placed, and the surgical site is sutured.
  • Approximately 10 days later, sutures are removed and healing is assessed.

Stage 2: Abutment Placement and Prosthesis

Once completed, 3i dental implants offer a secure, durable, and natural-looking solution for missing teeth.

If you have any questions about 3i dental implants, please consult your dentist.

  • Local anesthetic is administered, and a small incision exposes the implant root.
  • The temporary stop-cap is removed, and the abutment is attached to the implant.
  • Bite impressions are taken to create the final prosthesis (usually a crown).
  • The prosthesis is secured with a tiny screw, and adjustments are made for comfort and stability.
