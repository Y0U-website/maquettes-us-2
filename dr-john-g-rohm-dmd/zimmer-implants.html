What to Expect When Getting Zimmer Biomet Dental Implants

Before implant placement, your dentist develops a personalized treatment plan based on X-rays, bite impressions, and examinations. The first stage of the procedure begins under local anesthesia, where an incision is made to expose the bone and a small hole is drilled. The implant is then tapped or screwed into place. In some cases, implants can be placed directly through soft tissue without an incision.

For two-step treatments, the second stage occurs after the implant has integrated with the jawbone. During this visit, local anesthesia is applied, the implant post is accessed, and the abutment is attached. Some Zimmer Biomet implants come with a pre-attached abutment, eliminating this step for certain patients.

The final dental prosthesis is fabricated shortly after post-healing bite impressions are taken, ensuring a custom fit. The primary goal is a stable, long-lasting, and comfortable restoration that enhances function and appearance.
