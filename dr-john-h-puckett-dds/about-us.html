About Ridge Kids Dental

Dr. Puckett feels that it is his job to physically care for his young patients while practicing in such a way that will motivate them and their parents to improve and maintain good overall health. He especially enjoys helping his younger patients conquer their fear of going to the dentist.

The Ridge Kids Dental team takes great pride in making sure patients have a great experience from beginning to end. Dr. Puckett wants them to feel valued and to know they are receiving the best care possible!

When away from the office, Dr. Puckett enjoys traveling, hunting, fishing, and spending time with his wife, Elizabeth, and their three children Jack, Anna-Tipton, and Liza.

Dr. Puckett’s memberships and affiliations include:

  • Academy of Gp Orthodontics
  • Surgical Staff, Outpatient Surgery Center of Jonesboro
  • Recent certifications and training through continuing education include:

  • Pediatric mini-residency (University of Florida)
  • Two-year Comprehensive Orthodontic training (Academy of Gp Orthodontics)
