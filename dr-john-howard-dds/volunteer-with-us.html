Volunteer

Volunteers are crucial in enabling our clients and residents to participate in diverse programs and events.

North Central Health Care offers care for mental health, addiction, developmental disabilities, aquatic therapy, and adult protective services. Our main campus is located in Wausau with offices in Merrill and Antigo. Many of our volunteer opportunities center around activities at Mount View Care Center, a skilled nursing facility in Wausau.

How can you help out?

Learn more about upcoming volunteering opportunities HERE !

  • Heartfelt Gift Shop at Mount View Care Center in Wausau
  • On-site Activities with residents at Mount View
  • Community Outings with residents
  • And many other opportunities to serve
