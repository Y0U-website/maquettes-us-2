Meet the Team

Dr. John Choi

Dr. Choi grew up in NYC but now makes his home in the Boston area with his wife Sandy, son Zachary and their Havapoo Maki.

Dr. Choi is known for his gentle touch and attention to detail. His motto of “do it right and do it once” makes for beautiful restorations which last a long time. Dr. Choi is eager to meet you to maintain your oral and overall health.

Dr. Choi started at Choice Dental in 2008, taking over the practice of Dr. Robert Paquette, who was here for 41 years prior. It was the culmination of his vision of being a place that cares for the staff and patients like he would care for his family and friends. Over a decade later, it still continues to be a place that gives him purpose and joy, as he serves that local community and stays mindful to create a fun and fulfilling place for the staff to spend their days.

Outside of work, he enjoys spending time with his family and friends and serving in his local church, especially kids and youth programs. Dr. Choi has made it a part of his family rhythm to do service trips with his son Zachary. Since the age of 10, they have gone on many service trips to build homes in Baja, Mexico, do dental missions in San Luis Colorado, MX, and many local projects in the greater Boston area through Boston Faith and Justice.
