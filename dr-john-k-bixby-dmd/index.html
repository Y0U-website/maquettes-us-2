More than 50 million Americans suffer from some kind of sleep disorder such as sleep apnea, narcolepsy or severe insomnia.

Affecting millions of people in the U.S., sleep apnea is one of the most common sleeping disorders. It’s a life-threatening disorder that causes someone to stop breathing repeatedly when sleeping: from as short as ten seconds to over a minute.

Narcolepsy is a chronic sleep disorder characterized by excessive daytime sleepiness and sudden attacks of sleep. The extreme tiredness that a narcoleptic experience causes them to suddenly falling asleep mid-day despite the fact they may be getting sufficient sleep at night.

Everyone seems to know about insomnia – but what exactly is it? Basically, it’s being unable to continuously fall or stay asleep regularly. It’s not a disease or diagnosis in and of itself; instead, it’s a symptom that may be rooted in a medical or emotional problem.
