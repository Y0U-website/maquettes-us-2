The practice

Dr. Michele Holzinger is a graduate of Mercy High School in Middletown, CT. She went on to attend the University of Notre Dame in South Bend, IN, where she majored in pre-med and history. Go Irish! After graduation, she volunteered for a year through Amate House, an Americorps program in Chicago. She served as a kindergarten teacher and director of the Junior Scholars Program in the South Side.

Dr. Michele moved back East to attend the Boston University Henry Goldman School of Dental Medicine , where she graduated Summa Cum Laude. She was also awarded the “Periodontics” Award. She went on to complete her residency training in Periodontics at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Michele recently got married and enjoys time with her husband, Paul, and their dog, Sam. In her free time, she loves swimming, running, Crossfit, and anything outdoors. She is also an avid reader and loves to crochet.

Traditional dentures rely on an acrylic base that sits directly on the gums, held in place by a paste or adhesive. Using implants as supports for dentures allows for a smaller and more comfortable base and less shifting of the prosthesis during use.

Implants can support both full and partial dentures as well as both fixed and removable dentures. If a removable denture is selected, the denture will “snap” on to the implant rather than requiring the use of denture paste or adhesives.

This procedure is a unique implant solution that allows patients with a completely edentulous (toothless) upper or lower arch to replace all of the teeth in that arch using only four implants as anchors.

Guided Implant Placement With this technique, specialized software is used to create custom surgical guides. These guides direct the exact location, angle, and depth of each implant placement. The use of the guide increases the accuracy, efficiency, safety, and predictability of the procedure.
