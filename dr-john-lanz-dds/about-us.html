Our Philosophy of Care

Our dedicated team at Gaston Perio strives to provide the highest level of comprehensive periodontal and implant dentistry in a friendly, caring and professional manner.

We believe that the most satisfying and predictable dentistry is delivered with a team approach utilizing restorative dentists, orthodontists and other dental specialists as needed. This means that our patient is receiving the best care available today, free of constraints and limitations imposed by insurance companies and other external considerations.

We welcome you as a new patient to our practice and promise to make your dental condition, thoughts, and outlook towards treatment a positive and rewarding experience. We will collect necessary diagnostic data, consult with other health care providers as needed, and discuss our findings and recommend treatment options with you completely. If you find that you are not yet ready to pursue the ideal treatment that you desire, we can discuss alternative options and ways to stage your treatment over time.

Our team will be available to answer your questions and guide you through the diagnostic and treatment phases. They are exceptionally good at what they do and take great pride in providing excellent, comfortable care. Several of our team members have helped us serve Gaston County for more than 40 years. We have developed many wonderful relationships with our patients and hope to do so with you.

Please contact our office to schedule a consultation or comprehensive examination to discuss your dental concerns.

Request a Consultation Menu Test Dr. Brenda Lopez, DDS, MS Periodontist Dr. Brenda Lopez is a periodontist who is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through personalized, compassionate care. Born in Mexico and raised in Asheboro, North Carolina since the age of 10, Dr. Lopez is fluent in both English and Spanish and enjoys connecting with patients from diverse backgrounds.

Dr. Lopez completed her dental education and specialty training at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree, Master’s in Oral Biology, and Specialty Certificate in Periodontics.

Community service has always been an important part of Dr. Lopez’s career. She has volunteered at a free dental clinic, mentored dental students in caring for underserved patients, and participated in dental mission trips to Honduras, Mexico, Moldova, and Brazil.

Dr. Lopez’s clinical interests include digital dentistry with a focus on guided implant surgery, esthetic soft tissue management, guided bone regeneration, and the treatment of TMJ-related concerns using Botox/Xeomin therapy. She is committed to staying current with the latest advancements in periodontics and implant dentistry in order to provide high-quality, predictable care for her patients.

Dr. Lopez is a member of the American Academy of Periodontology, American Dental Association, Southern Academy of Periodontology, North Carolina Society of Periodontists, and North Carolina Dental Society.

Outside of the office, Dr. Lopez loves spending quality time with her family. Some of her favorite memories are made during quiet moments at home, hiking trips through the North Carolina mountains, and motorhome adventures to explore U.S. National Parks with her husband, Ali, their daughter, Gabby, and their goldendoodle, Frida.
