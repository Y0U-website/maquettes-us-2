Dental Implants FAQs

How do I know if I’m a candidate for dental implants?

How long do dental implants last?

With proper maintenance and regular care, dental implants may last for many years.

What are implant-supported dentures?

Implant-supported dentures use dental implants to improve stability and support for removable or fixed restorations.

Why see a periodontist for dental implants?

Periodontists have advanced training in gum and bone support that is important for implant stability.

Can dental implants replace multiple missing teeth?

Yes. Dental implants may be used to replace a single tooth, several missing teeth, or to support full-mouth reconstruction, depending on your needs.
