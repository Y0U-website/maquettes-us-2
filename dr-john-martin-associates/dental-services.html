Why Routine Dental Cleanings are Important

Regular dental cleanings are essential to maintaining good oral health. Even with proper brushing and flossing, plaque and tartar can build up in hard-to-reach areas of the mouth. Over time, this can lead to tooth decay, gum disease, and other oral health problems. During a cleaning, our experienced dental hygienists use specialized tools to remove plaque and tartar from your teeth, leaving them feeling clean and refreshed.
