Our Services

Dr. John and his skilled staff treat each and every patient as individuals. They take the time to listen and understand a patient’s concerns. Dr. John assesses a patient’s dental needs and devises an appropriate treatment plan.

Cosmetic Dentistry

A winning smile can help you in business, romance and simply give a person the confidence to get out there! People who have confident mouths are considered more attractive by others. It’s no wonder that cosmetic dentistry is now one among many popular procedures requested at dentist offices contact us today for whitening and other cosmetic options.

Comprehensive Family and General Dentistry

Maintaining proper dental health is vital not just because it impacts the quality of life but also plays into how we feel about ourselves when eating certain foods or smiling properly during social occasions don’t wait until there are problems before getting treated, schedule a check-up today!

Dental Implants

Dental implants have been a breakthrough for patients who want to achieve that perfect smile. Removable dentures can slip and make noise, while fixed bridges are anchored into other teeth or jawbone with the help of porcelain crowns-but both removable dental appliance systems have their drawbacks if you don’t take good care of them!

Partial and Complete Dentures

We know all about dentures. Whether you need new ones, or your old ones cleaned and restored for optimal function by a professional, we can help! Contact us today to discuss the best solution for your situation.

Oral Surgery and Extractions

Tooth removal is often an unpleasant experience, but it’s made more bearable with modern techniques that help to keep your mouth safe while the dentist performs this task.

Cleanings and Periodontal (Gums) Maintenance

Regular cleanings ensure your smile always looks its best! The health of your gums is also important to maintain for optimal dental health. Regular cleanings and maintenance can prevent more serious problems from occuring before they begin.

Single Visit Crowns and Bridgework

Dental crowns can reinforce and restore the appearance of teeth effected by cavities or damage to the tooth structure, making them look and feel like new. Our state of the art technology allows us to mill and fit the crown in a single visit! Bridgework can replace missing teeth by attaching an artificial tooth to the surrounding natural teeth.
