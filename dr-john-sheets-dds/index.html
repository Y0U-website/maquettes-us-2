The practice

Dr. Sheets was born and raised in Jackson. The education he received graduating from Vandercook Lake High School in 1981 readied him for the University of Michigan. He knew early in his high school career that he wanted to become a dentist. After working in the research lab, he entered dental school a year early and graduated from the University of Michigan Dental School in 1988.

Dr. Sheets’ motivation is to treat every patient as though they are a family member. He strives to give special attention to each patient. The most satisfying aspect of dentistry is knowing that the team can change their life. Dr. Sheets feels blessed to be a dentist, but the most important people in his life are his family. He has been married to his wife, Kim, since 1985 and she is the most special and caring person. They have four great kids, Nicole, Katie, Kristen, and JJ, and have been blessed with one grandson, Ty, and a granddaughter, Taelynn.

Dr. Sheets is also dedicated to volunteerism by donating time to numerous community events. He was a long-time coach for Western softball, little league, youth football and youth basketball programs. He has also been honored to serve on the Western District School board since 2007 and has served as president since 2009. Dr. Sheets strives to lead by example and being fair to everyone.
