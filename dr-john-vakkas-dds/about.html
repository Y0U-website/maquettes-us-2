Board-Certified Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon

Meet Dr. John Vakkas

Dr. John Vakkas is a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon dedicated to delivering exceptional surgical care with a compassionate, patient-first approach. As both a licensed dentist and physician, he brings a unique depth of expertise to every procedure, combining advanced surgical training with more than 15 years of clinical experience.

Passionate about the full scope of oral and maxillofacial surgery, Dr. Vakkas has a special interest in dental implant reconstruction, dentoalveolar surgery, and oral pathology. Throughout his career, he has remained committed to providing personalized care while helping patients restore their oral health, function, and confidence.

Dr. Vakkas is board-certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. He is an active member of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, the Pennsylvania Society of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, the American Dental Association, the American Medical Association, and the Spear Dental Study Club. He is also a member of Omicron Kappa Upsilon, the National Dental Honor Society, and previously served as President of the Lancaster Dental Society, where he continues to support the advancement of organized dentistry within the local community.

Outside the office, Dr. Vakkas enjoys leading an active lifestyle. An avid tennis player and member of the Lititz recCenter, he values staying active and spending quality time with his family. He resides in Lititz, Pennsylvania, with his wife, their two sons, and two cats. His dedication to his family, community, and patients continues to shape the thoughtful, compassionate care he provides every day.

Board-Certified Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon

Meet Dr. James P. Murphy

Dr. James Murphy comes from a proud lineage of oral surgeons, inspired by his father and grandfather to pursue a career bridging medicine and dentistry. With a passion for the diverse scope of oral and maxillofacial surgery and the unique responsibility of administering anesthesia, Dr. Murphy has dedicated his life to serving patients with exceptional care and precision.

A proud military veteran, Dr. Murphy served in the U.S. Army and Air Force, where he provided care to service members and trained residents at renowned institutions like Walter Reed Naval Military Medical Center. He continues to serve in the Pennsylvania Air National Guard, embodying his lifelong commitment to service and teamwork. Known for his humor and sensitivity, he has a special understanding of families with special needs, inspired by his middle son’s autism.

Murphy graduated Summa Cum Laude from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in 2009 after earning his undergraduate degree from Boston College. He completed advanced training through the Air Force’s Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) program in Alaska and his oral and maxillofacial surgery residency at David Grant USAF Medical Center in California.

Dr. Murphy is board-certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (ABOMS) and a Fellow of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (AAOMS). He is also a member of the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology (ADSA) and the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society. He maintains certifications in Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS) and Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS).

Outside of work, Dr. Murphy enjoys playing tennis, running with his wife, and spending time with his three sons. A decorated veteran and Bronze Star recipient, he is one of six siblings and a proud dog dad to a Bernese mountain dog. His dedication to family, patients, and community defines his compassionate approach to care.

Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon

Meet Dr. Eben Larrabee

Dr. Eben Larrabee brings a wealth of knowledge and a passion for excellence to the Pennwood Oral Surgery. Born and raised in Maine, Dr. Larrabee pursued his education with dedication, earning his DMD from Tufts University and his MD from Drexel University College of Medicine. His extensive training in oral and maxillofacial surgery includes rotations in anesthesia, plastic surgery, ENT, and trauma surgery, as well as specialty training at the University of Pennsylvania, where he served as chief resident and earned the prestigious Program Director’s Pinnacle of Proficiency Award.

Dr. Larrabee graduated Cum Laude from the University of New England with a BS in Human Biology. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University in 2016 and completed his Doctor of Medicine degree at Drexel University in 2020. His oral and maxillofacial surgery training at the University of Pennsylvania included a focus on advanced procedures like dental implants, wisdom tooth extractions, bone grafting, and corrective jaw surgery utilizing virtual surgical planning and patient-specific implants.

Dr. Larrabee is certified in Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS), Advanced Trauma Life Support (ATLS), and Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS). His clinical interests include office-based anesthesia, oral pathology, and innovative techniques for dental implants and jaw surgery.

Outside the office, Dr. Larrabee enjoys spending time with his wife, daughter, and their Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, Berkeley. Together, they love traveling, dining out, and creating lasting memories with family and friends. His compassionate care and dedication to advanced surgical techniques make him a trusted partner in achieving optimal oral health.
