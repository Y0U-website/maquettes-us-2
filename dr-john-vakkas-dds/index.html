Wisdom teeth, or third molars, are the final teeth to come in. For most people, however, they do more harm than good and need to be removed to maintain optimal oral health.

While we will always try to save a natural tooth before resorting to an extraction, in some cases, removing a problematic tooth is the best way to keep your oral and overall health in top shape.
