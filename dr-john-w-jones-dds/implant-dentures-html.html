What Are Implant Dentures?

Traditional dentures work by using the shape and suction of the mouth to remain in place. Alternatively, implant dentures are supported by multiple dental implants strategically placed throughout the jawbone. Dental implants usually made from titanium, which fuses to the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This gives dental implants their stability, comfort, and longevity. There are two different types of implant dentures. Here’s how each of them works.

Fixed/Permanent Implant Dentures

A fixed implant denture is made to stay in place at all times. It can only be removed by a dental professional. In most cases, four to six strategically placed dental implants are used to hold the permanent denture. This is a preferred option for many because they act more like real teeth.

Removable Implant Dentures

Removable implant dentures, sometimes called snap-on dentures, are also held in place using dental implants. The difference is that they use clips or ball sockets to secure them. This way, the wearer can remove their dentures at home as needed for cleaning and sleeping.

The Implant Denture Process

During your Consultation, we will examine your smile to determine if you are a good candidate for implant dentures. At this point, we will put together a treatment plan that meets your needs. For some patients, preparatory procedures are needed before getting dental implants to improve the probability of their success.

Once your gums and jaws are in good condition, the dental implants are surgically inserted into the jawbone. Then, they fuse together in a process called osteointegration. This process can take anywhere from three to six months. Next, metal connector pieces, called abutments, are placed on the ends of the dental implants. Your final denture is designed and crafted according to your needs and secured in place by your dentists.

Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

The majority of healthy adults can get implant dentures. During your consultation, we will go over your needs and options in detail. To be a good candidate, you must meet the following criteria.

  • Be healthy enough to undergo minor surgery
  • Be free of existing oral health issues
  • Be able to maintain excellent oral hygiene
  • Have sufficient jawbone density

Implant Denture FAQs

Are implant dentures removable?

There are two different types of implant dentures: fixed and removable. Many patients prefer fixed dentures because they can go back to how life was before they lost their teeth and no longer need to worry about removing dentures to soak and clean them. In fact, fixed implant dentures don’t require any special care besides excellent oral hygiene.

Your dentist can discuss the pros and cons of each type of implant denture at your consultation. Please be aware that fixed implant dentures may require greater jawbone density than removable.

Are Implant Dentures Better Than Traditional Dentures?

There are many advantages to implant dentures over traditional dentures. Implant dentures offer greater stability and support than traditional dentures, which allows for better biteforce and jawbone preservation. They also help by allowing for clearer speech and are less likely to slip or shift while eating or speaking, improving your confidence.

How Do I Care for Implant Dentures?

It’s easy to care for implant dentures! The most important step is to make sure you are practicing excellent oral hygiene, including brushing twice a day and cleaning around your implant attachments. It is also imperative to continue attending your regular, six-month preventive exam and cleaning appointments with your dentist to ensure your mouth and dentures are in excellent condition.

How many teeth can implant dentures replace?

Implant dentures can replace an entire arch of missing teeth. In fact, they are an ideal tooth replacement option for anyone who is tired of dealing with traditional dentures and would like a beautiful, fully functional smile!
