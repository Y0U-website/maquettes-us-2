The practice

Dr. John W. Johnson, DDS has spent decades studying biological dentistry, airway health, ceramic implant systems, and whole-body approaches to oral care.

His philosophy is rooted in the belief that dentistry should consider more than isolated symptoms or individual teeth. Through years of advanced training and clinical experience, Dr. Johnson has developed a patient-centered approach focused on long-term wellness, biocompatibility, and individualized care.

Patients seeking biological dentistry, safe mercury removal, zirconia ceramic implants, and airway-focused care often travel from throughout Ohio and the surrounding Midwest to work with Dr. Johnson and the Midwest BioHealth team.
