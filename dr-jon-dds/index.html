The practice

Whether you’re addressing damaged or missing teeth or looking to improve the health and appearance of your smile, every treatment plan is tailored to your individual needs. We take time to explain your options and help you feel confident in every step of your care.

Our goal is to create a dental experience that feels welcoming, supportive, and focused on long-term oral health for every patient we serve.

At Dr. Jon, DDS, we offer a full range of modern dental services to support both the health and appearance of your smile. Whether you’re looking to enhance your smile with porcelain veneers , replace missing teeth with dental implants , restore strength with same-day crowns , or simply maintain your smile with routine cleanings and exams, our care is tailored to your needs.

At Dr. Jon, DDS, our experienced dental team is committed to providing attentive, high-quality treatment for every patient. With strong training and a dedication to continuing education, we stay current with modern techniques and advancements in dentistry so patients receive thoughtful, up-to-date care at every visit.
