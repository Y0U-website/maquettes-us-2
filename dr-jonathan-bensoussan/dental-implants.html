Precision Implant Surgery

  • For efficient and predictable surgery, Dr. Bensoussan plans implant cases ahead of time using state-of-the-art 3D digital technology. He plans the surgery with the exact end in mind of how the implant and final tooth or teeth should be positioned. During surgery, Dr. Bensoussan receives real-time feedback using the X-Guide—a 3D navigation system that enables precise placement of the implant’s position, angle, and depth. This eliminates any guesswork and makes the outcome predictable.

Faster Healing and Implant Stability with State-of-the-art Nobel Biocare Dental Implants ™

This includes Dr. Bensoussan’s choice of dental implants. He uses implants from one of the top implant providers, Nobel Biocare, that have a special surface on the implant that promotes integration with the jawbone, speeding up healing and increasing bone stability. This surface is also very gentle to the bone and oral cavity and lessens risk of peri-implantitis (implant gum infection).

A. The collar of the implant has a smooth, non-corrosive surface, designed to be easy on your gum tissue and reduce plaque accumulation. B. The body of the implant has a textured, porous surface that makes it easier for the bone to grip and it attracts healing fluids to speed up recovery.

Same-Day Implants

In most cases Dr. Bensoussan can extract your tooth, place a dental implant and attach a temporary tooth in the same visit.

Dr. Bensoussan can extract any teeth needing replacing and place a dental implant in the same visit. If bone is deficient and needs augmenting, he can usually place the implant and a bone graft at the same time. Patients with little-to-no available bone might have to wait for bone to grow back before placing the implant. At the end of your visit, Dr. Bensoussan attaches a temporary tooth or provides you with a snap-on tooth or teeth for the visible area of your smile, so no gaps are showing.
