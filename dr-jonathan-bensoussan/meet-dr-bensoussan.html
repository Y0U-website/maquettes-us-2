Why Dr. Bensoussan became a dentist

Dr. Jonathan Bensoussan was born and raised in Paris and moved to the United States as a teenager. In his developing years, he had a traumatic dental experience that affected his smile and self-confidence. This early challenge sparked a deep interest in dentistry at a young age, inspiring him to explore how advanced dental care could transform lives. His journey ultimately led him to pursue the passion he has today for helping patients restore their smiles, regain confidence, and experience the life-changing benefits of modern dentistry delivered with compassion and precision.

“I became a dentist because I had dental phobias and low self-esteem when smiling because I was missing my two front teeth for a long time. So I decided to become a dentist to help others improve their confidence, self-esteem and image.
