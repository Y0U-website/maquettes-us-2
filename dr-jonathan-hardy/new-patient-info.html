What to expect.

Call us to schedule.

Give us a call at (606) 679-1204 . Our front desk team will find a time that works for you and answer any questions before you arrive.

The hygienist you meet on your first visit has likely been doing this work for a decade or two. She’ll make you feel welcome, listen to your concerns, and take great care of you. Most first visits start with a cleaning, giving you time to get comfortable while we get to know you and your teeth.

Next up, we’ll do a complimentary dental health scan—a detailed visual of your teeth completed right in the room. You’ll see exactly what Dr. Hardy sees, and he’ll use the 3D image to make sure you understand what your options are moving forward.

What will it cost? Will it hurt? How long will it take? We will walk you through everything with complete transparency so you can make a confident decision—no uncertainty, no pressure.

From a simple filling to a full implant, chances are we can handle anything you need right in our office so you don’t have to bounce around to others. We’re delighted to help!

Financial Information

Every patient’s situation is different, so we’ve made it a priority to offer flexible payment options that work for real life. We accept cash, check, and all major credit cards—and between our membership program, insurance, and financing options, we’ll find an option that works best for you.

Insurance Coverage

We accept most major PPO dental insurance plans. Want to check if we take yours? Give us a call at (606) 679-1204 , and we’ll explore together.

Financing

If you would like to extend payments over a longer period of time, we offer CareCredit and Cherry Financial.

We understand that unexpected dental bills can strain finances. If this happens to you, don’t hesitate to call us at (606) 679-1204 . If you keep the line of communication open with us, we can work out an acceptable arrangement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will it hurt?

Most patients are surprised by how comfortable their experience is. Dr. Hardy and his team take the time to make sure you’re at ease before anything begins. If you’re nervous, just tell us—we hear that more than you’d think, and we know how to help.

How soon can I be seen?

We do our best to see new patients as quickly as possible. For urgent situations, we can often get you in within a day. Give us a call at (606) 679-120 4, and we’ll find the earliest time that works for you.

Do you see kids?

Yes! Patients of all ages are welcome at Somerset Family Dentistry.

Do I need a referral to make an appointment?

Not at all. Just give us a call at (606) 679-1204 and we’ll get you scheduled.

What if I m embarrassed about the state of my teeth?

You’re not the first person to feel that way, and you won’t be the last. What we can tell you is that every single person on our team is here to help—not to make you feel worse about anything. Ever.

Why should I trust Somerset with my care?

Dr. Hardy completed two years of advanced training beyond dental school and served six years in the military dental field. His team brings decades of combined experience—including a hygienist who has been with the practice for almost three decades.

What if I have a dental emergency after hours?

If you’re an established patient experiencing an urgent dental matter after hours, call us at (606) 679-1204 and leave a message. Dr. Hardy will get back to you as quickly as possible.

Can you really handle everything in one place?

Yes, most things, and it’s something we’ve worked hard to provide. Dr. Hardy’s advanced training and his team’s decades of combined experience mean patients get specialist-level care without ever leaving this office.
