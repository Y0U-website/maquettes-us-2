How Do All-on-4 Dental Implants Work?

Dental implants are a permanent tooth replacement option that uses your natural jawbone as an anchor point. Dr. Ross will use his diagnostic tools and equipment to find the strongest parts of your jawbone before using the Yomi implant robot to assist him in placing four titanium implants with maximum accuracy.

Once the implants are placed, a custom-fitted denture is placed over the top and secured. There’s no waiting between getting your dental implants and your denture. This means as soon as you’re done with the appointment, you’ll go home with your new, gorgeous grin. Over the next few months, these implants will fuse with your jawbone and make your denture strong and stable.

Am I a Candidate for All-on-4 Dental Implants?

If you’re wondering if All-on-4 dental implants are possible for you, step in to see Dr. Ross for a consultation. During this appointment, he’ll assess your gums and jawbone health. By the time your meeting is over, you’ll have a clear path forward for your treatment.

Most patients who have total tooth loss are eligible for the All-on-4 treatment, even if they have some bone loss due to aging or missing teeth. This is because the rear implants in the All-on-4 system are angled, reducing the bone density required to support them. Even if issues barring your treatment are present, Dr. Ross can work with you to address them before continuing with your dental implants.

Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • All-Natural Look: All-on-4 dentures look just like regular teeth, allowing you to smile with confidence.
  • Restored Function: Your new All-on-4 dentures work like normal teeth to restore your ability to chew your favorite foods.
  • Prevents Jawbone Loss: All-on-4 dental implants stimulate your jawbone as you chew, just like natural tooth roots. This keeps your face looking young by stopping jawbone resorption.
  • A Permanent Solution: All-on-4 dentures are anchored in place. Since you don’t need to remove them, they can be cleaned with a soft toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste.
  • Maximum Comfort: Once your All-on-4 dentures have healed, you’ll forget they’re there.
  • Saves Time: Since All-on-4 dentures are placed at the same time as your dental implants, so you’ll be able to enjoy your new teeth as soon as the procedure is over.
  • Avoid Added Surgery: Most patients who opt for All-on-4 dentures don’t need bone grafts to support the treatment. This saves you time and money!

All-on-4 vs. Traditional Dental Implants

You may be wondering what the difference is between traditional implant dentures and the All-on-4 dental implant treatment. While they may seem similar, All-on-4 dentures offer several advantages.

For one, they require fewer implants than traditional implant dentures. This means that you’ll need a less invasive and simpler surgery, making the treatment more economical overall.

Additionally, All-on-4 implant dentures don’t typically require bone grafting and are placed on the same day as your implant surgery. Not having to undergo added procedures and receiving your denture on the same day as your implant placement means you can enjoy your new smile as soon as you leave the chair!

All-on-4 FAQs

Do All-on-4 Dental Implants Look Natural?

You will find that All-on-4 dental implants strongly resemble natural teeth in both appearance and function.

We carefully place dental implants so they are well-concealed beneath the gumline. We also pay great attention to detail when designing the dentures that get attached to the implants. Although your provisional (temporary) denture may not be ideal, it should still look good. Your permanent denture should be a beautiful complement for your natural facial features; no one should be able to tell that it is not your real teeth!

Does the All-on-4 Treatment Hurt?

During the All-on-4 surgery, you should experience little to no discomfort thanks to the use of local anesthesia and sedation. After the anesthesia and sedation wear off, you can expect to feel some soreness and swelling; these symptoms are normal and should fade as your body recovers. Most patients find that the pain diminishes significantly within a few days.

You can also take simple measures to reduce your discomfort. For example, we may recommend that you take appropriate doses of pain medication. It is also important that you get adequate rest and avoid eating any foods that might irritate your surgical sites.

How Long Does All-on-4 Treatment Take?

The duration of the All-on-4 treatment varies depending on individual circumstances, but you can expect the entire process to be completed relatively quickly. The initial surgery, where the implants are placed, typically takes a few hours. Following the surgery, you will receive temporary teeth to wear while your implants heal and integrate with your jawbone. After a healing period, your permanent teeth will be placed.

During your consultation, our team can provide an estimate for your treatment timeline. From start to finish, the entire process may take a few months or longer.

What Should I Eat After All-on-4 Surgery?

After your All-on-4 surgery, you should follow a soft diet to allow your implants and the surrounding tissue to heal properly. You should avoid hard, crunchy, or sticky foods that could interfere with the healing process. Opt for nutritious, easy-to-chew foods such as soups, smoothies, mashed potatoes, and yogurt. As your implants heal, you can gradually reintroduce more solid foods into your diet.

How Much Do All-on-4 Dental Implants Cost?

The cost of All-on-4 dental implants can vary based on several factors, including the complexity of your case, the materials used, and whether or not you require any preparatory procedures. You should consult with our team to obtain a personalized quote that reflects your individual needs and circumstances.

While this treatment involves an investment, many patients find that the benefits of improved oral health, functionality, and aesthetics make it worthwhile. Our team welcomes insurance, and we can also discuss financing options to help manage the cost of your All-on-4 dental implants.
