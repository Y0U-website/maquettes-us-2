The practice

When it comes to oral hygiene, it’s important to start at a very young age to provide a strong foundation for healthy adult teeth. Our pediatric dentistry team will work closely with you and your child to develop oral hygiene habits that help fight against gum disease and tooth decay.

Even though “baby teeth” are temporary, they play an important role in speech, permanent teeth development, nutrition and self image. The attitudes your child develops toward dental care during his or her early years can also have a major impact on the way he/she looks at oral health later in life.

For these reasons, it is very important to start your child off on the right foot early on. Our specialized pediatric dental services help foster a combination of preventive visits to the dentist and positive oral health habits at home.
