Restorative Dental Procedures

No need to have anxiety over restoring your smile. With our gentle touch, advanced technology, and dedication to your comfort, we’ll make rebuilding your smile a stress-free experience. Don’t wait, schedule a visit today!

Dental Bridge Dental bridges replace missing teeth. Specifically, they can replace one tooth or a row of missing teeth. It is a fixed (non-removable) appliance that allows patients to restore their smile and confidence.

If you have missing teeth that’s preventing your perfect smile, click learn more to see how easy it is to apply a bridge and give you the confidence to smile!

Dental Crown A Dental Crown restores cracked or decayed teeth. A crown (or cap) is a covering that encases the entire tooth surface restoring it to its original shape and size. You can even get a new crown in one appointment with same-day dental crowns. LEARN MORE Denture Complete Complete Dentures are a removable prosthesis of white plastic teeth in a pink gum-colored plastic base that rests on the remaining gum ridge once all of the teeth in the arch have been removed.

Dentures-Partial Partial Dentures are often used when only some of the teeth are missing. They allow missing teeth in the same arch (either the upper or the lower) to be replaced with one appliance.

Implants A Dental Implant is a metal device designed to replace missing teeth. The device is usually made out of titanium and is surgically placed into the jawbone where the tooth is missing. Unlike a dental bridge, an implant is permanent.

Fillings Fillings are used to restore areas of your tooth affected by decay. Dentists use both amalgam (silver) and composite (tooth-colored) materials to "fill in" the surface of the tooth after all decay has been removed.
