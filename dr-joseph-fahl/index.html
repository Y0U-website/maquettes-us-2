Dr. Jonathan Aiuppa pursued his Bachelors in Biomedical Sciences at the University of Wisconsin-River Falls. He then completed his Doctor of Dental Medicine at Midwestern University in Chicago.

Dr. Aiuppa takes great pride in providing the highest level of care for his patients. Dr. Aiuppa provides an expanded scope of general dental care including dental implants, restorative dentistry, and endodontics. He is a member of the Wisconsin Dental Association and Chicago Dental Society.

In his free time, Dr. Aiuppa enjoys spending time with family and is an avid outdoorsman.

Dr. Fahl joined Bayer and Fahl Dentistry in 1991. He received his Bachelor of Science degree in Biomedical Engineering from The University of Iowa in 1986. He continued at The University of Iowa for dental school and received his D.D.S. in 1990.
