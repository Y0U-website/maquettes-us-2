The office of Joseph W Langford DDS, in Fitchburg, Massachusetts , offers safe and proven procedures in general dentistry for patients of all ages. We provide dental care in a thorough and soothing manner.

Dental Care

Make an appointment with Joseph W Langford DDS today to receive excellent dental care. Services include:

Crowns: When a tooth is damaged beyond repair, or when the teeth have been filled multiple times, crowns are the restoration of choice. Crowns are available in porcelain or precious metal.

Bridges: Bridges are created to replace teeth. They are bonded to the natural teeth and look, feel, and work like natural teeth.

Cosmetic Dentistry: This branch of dentistry includes veneers, cosmetic/composite fillings, full and partial dentures, partial denture repair, and denture realignments.

Inlays & Onlays: These tooth-colored restorations are excellent for damaged or broken parts of the tooth.

Snap-on Smile: Less expensive than veneers, Snap-On Smile is a patented technology that is excellent for stained teeth, crooked teeth, and gaps. There are no drillings or shots involved.

Sportsguards & Nightguards: These devices protect the mouth during sports or during the night.
