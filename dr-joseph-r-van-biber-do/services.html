Well Visits

Having a long-term relationship with your family gives our doctors and nurse practitioners the opportunity to monitor your child’s progress and development over time. Walk-in Urgent Care

We understand that urgent matters don’t always occur during normal business hours. For that reason, our pediatric urgent care clinic is available 7 days a week.

Immunizations

Vaccinating children and young adults is the single most important health-promoting intervention we perform as health care providers.

Allergy and Immunotherapy

Have allergies? Cockerell & McIntosh Pediatrics has designed a cost-effective and comprehensive program to diagnose and treat patients with allergies.

Attention Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)

If your child is struggling in school, seems overly hyper, or is easily distracted or forgetful, we want to help.

Mental Health

Country Crossroads Counseling offers a variety of counseling and therapy services to our patients and families. The practice now has an office at our Blue Springs location.
