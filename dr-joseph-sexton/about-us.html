About Us

Joseph P. Sexton, DDS , is located at 3448 Navarre Avenue, Oregon, Ohio. We proudly serve the dental needs of Oregon and the greater Toledo, Ohio area.

Our practice was started in 1978 by Dr. Leland MacDonald. After working with a couple different dentists, Dr. Joseph Joseph P. Sexton joined Dr. MacDonald in 2001. Drs. MacDonald and Joseph P. Sexton worked as partners together for five years until Dr. MacDonald retired from the practice in 2006.
