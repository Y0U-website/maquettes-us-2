Les soins

A dental bridge will bridge the gap created by one or more missing teeth, by joining an artificial tooth permanently to your adjacent teeth.

Crowns provide additional support for teeth, can mask an implant, or mend broken teeth. We use bridges to fill the gaps of missing teeth. A bridge makes chewing easier and prevents surrounding teeth from shifting out of place.
