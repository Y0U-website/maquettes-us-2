Brushing and flossing daily at home combined with regular exams and cleanings helps to prevent dental disease. Preventative dentistry saves you time and money and is our priority.

Our dentists here at Joseph P. Sexton, DDS, provide traditional general dentistry treatments to protect and restore your teeth. Services include teeth cleaning and protective sealants. We provide the most state-of-the-art general dentistry treatments to protect and restore your teeth.

Our dentists restore chipped and/or discolored teeth to give you a remarkable smile. Many options are available, including white fillings, crowns, bonding, veneers, Lumineers and teeth whitening.

We can keep your smile looking great by replacing missing teeth with crowns and bridges. A crown is a tooth-shaped dental cap that fits over a weakened or decayed tooth to prevent further damage and restore its natural appearance.

A dental bridge will bridge the gap created by one or more missing teeth, by joining an artificial tooth permanently to your adjacent teeth.

Our dentists use veneers to treat discolored teeth, worn or chipped front teeth, crooked teeth, and gaps and spaces between your teeth. Get the smile of your dreams!.

We offer tooth whitening services to brighten your teeth and remove stains and discoloration. In addition, we make custom-made trays for tooth whitening. We offer take-home pre-filled whitening trays as well as Crest professional strength white strips.

W e can make mouth guards and snore guards, which are protective devices molded to fit your teeth, to prevent damage associated with teeth grinding, teeth clenching, bruxism and injury during sports.

Twice a year, our skilled hygienists will thoroughly clean your teeth, including every crevice that you might have missed with a toothbrush. Our dentists also check the health of your gums, and respond to any personal dental concerns or questions you may have.

We perform non-surgical gum therapy to remove plaque and calculus from your teeth to catch periodontal disease early. Replacing worn fillings or crowns that can accumulate plaque, scaling and root planing procedure (removing plaque from slightly below the gum-line), and antibiotics may be used to restore the health of your gums.

Non-Surgical Periodontal Treatment treatment guidelines stress that periodontal health should be achieved in the least invasive and most cost-effective manner. This is often accomplished through non-surgical periodontal treatment.

Non-surgical periodontal treatment does have its limitations. When it does not achieve periodontal health, surgery may be indicated to restore periodontal health. Click image for additional information.
