0 Our Team Our uniquely qualified and talented staff has the education, dedication, and experience to help you with all of your dental needs. Joseph M. Skladany, DDS

Dr. Skladany has many hobbies, including traveling, cooking, golf, landscaping, and interior construction and design!

Jeanne

Jeanne joined Dr. Skladany’s team in April 2000. She has worked in the dental field since 1980. She first worked with a young dentist from Brooklyn, NY, at his practice in Clearwater, FL. Jeanne studied dental assisting with expanded duties at the University of Florida; she also attended St. Petersburg Junior College for certification in radiography. Jeanne also worked in forensic dentistry for Pinellas County, FL. She then moved to Moore County, and worked as a dental assistant for Dr. Walter Morris, Jr. for 14 years prior to joining Dr. Skladany’s office.

Jeanne loves gardening, and being a grandparent to Teresa and Sullivan!

Jeneé

Jeneé is a native Minnesotan, and has been a dental hygienist since 2007. She graduated from Rochester Community & Technical College, in Rochester, MN where she worked in hygiene until her move to North Carolina in January of 2013. Jeneé has been with Dr. Skladany’s team since 2016. She enjoys meeting new faces, and educating patients about their oral health and how it can impact your whole-body health. Jeneé has a very calming demeanor, which really helps ease patients’ anxieties!

Outside of the office, she enjoys traveling, the outdoors, and just spending time with her husband and four young children.

Stephanie

Stephanie graduated from Virginia Commonwealth University in 1997 with a Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene, and has enjoyed her career in dentistry for over 20 years! She loves helping patients maintain their oral health as part of their whole-body health, and helping those with anxiety to feel comfortable in the dental chair.

Moore county has been her home since 2011, and has lived in Whispering Pines since her husband retired from the Army. She is the proud mom of 2 adult sons that attended NC State. In her spare time she loves to go to the mountains, practice photography, and to do jigsaw puzzles.

Lisa

Lisa joined Dr. Skladany’s practice as Office Manager in August 2001. She previously worked as a Registered Nurse at First Health, Pinehurst Medical Clinic, and Kaiser Permanente Urgent Care in her “active nursing days.” With her prior work experience at Carolina Power & Light customer accounting & servicing, she discovered the perfect work blend at Dr. Skladany’s - assisting with patient treatment planning, office accounting, and keeping the overall practice running smoothly.

Lisa has two adult children and two granddaughters. She enjoys traveling, especially to the NC coast and mountains; she feels fortunate to have lived in Moore County for most of her life.

Cathi

Her hobbies include painting watercolors and acrylics, gardening, pickleball, golf and reading. Cathi loves living in North Carolina by the lakes and being so close to the ocean.

Gloria

Denise
