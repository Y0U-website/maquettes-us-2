Dr. Austin Brasher

Dr. Brasher is a native Oklahoman, born and raised in Edmond, OK. He attended Brigham Young University where he walked onto the football team as a kicker before attending The University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry. After graduating from dental school, Dr. Brasher chose to pursue an advanced surgical training residency and was accepted by his first choice, The Foundry Dental Implant Center, outside Birmingham, Alabama.

Dr. Brasher stays at the forefront of technology and procedural techniques to provide his patients with the best possible care. He is involved in a number of communities in which he shares his knowledge and experience with colleagues around the world. Dr. Brasher has also been involved with teaching dental implantology through the Alabama Implant Education program and is a member of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry and is on track to be a diplomate of the American Board of Oral Implantology; the highest award that can be achieved in implant dentistry.

Beyond dentistry, Dr. Brasher loves attending sporting events, playing golf, and spending time with friends. He loves most spending time with his wife Baily and their wonderful kids. Dr. Brasher is also an active member of his church and is fluent in Spanish.

Dr. Conner Brasher

Dr. Conner Brasher is a friendly and dedicated general and cosmetic dentist. He pursued his undergraduate degree at Brigham Young University and graduated with a degree in exercise science. He earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Oklahoma.

Dr. Conner believes in the importance of giving patients a comfortable experience as well as providing them with exceptional dental care. He makes every effort to stay on top of current developments in dentistry and takes advantage of ongoing education in both general and cosmetic dentistry. Dr. Conner’s scope of dental expertise encompasses procedures such as teeth whitening, tooth extractions, root canals, restorative crown and bridge treatments, removable dentures, orthodontics, and dental implants.

In his free time, Dr. Conner enjoys spending time with his family, restoring cars, racing cars, and playing pickleball and golf. We are excited to have Dr. Conner join our team! He can’t wait to meet you!
