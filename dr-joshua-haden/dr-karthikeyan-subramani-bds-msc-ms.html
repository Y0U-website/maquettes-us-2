Meet Dr. Karthikeyan Subramani

Dr. Karthikeyan Subramani, BDS, MSc, MS

Dr. Karthikeyan Subramani is a dedicated orthodontist committed to helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through personalized care and advanced treatment techniques. He believes every patient deserves a beautiful smile and takes pride in building lasting relationships while delivering exceptional orthodontic care.

Dr. Subramani is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) and the Pacific Coast Society of Orthodontists (PCSO). Since 2015, he has been helping patients of all ages achieve healthier, more confident smiles through customized orthodontic treatment. Outside the office, Dr. Subramani enjoys fitness, hiking, movies, and road trips with his family. He and his wife are proud parents of two children and enjoy making memories together through travel and outdoor adventures.
