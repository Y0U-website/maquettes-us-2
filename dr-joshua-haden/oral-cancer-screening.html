1. How often should I get an oral cancer screening?

We strongly advise everyone to undergo annual oral cancer examinations as part of their routine dental check-ups. These exams are crucial for the early detection and prevention of oral cancer. However, individuals categorized as high-risk, such as tobacco users or those with a family history of cancer, should consider more frequent screenings to monitor their oral health closely. Prioritizing these exams can significantly improve health outcomes.

2. What are the common symptoms of oral cancer?

Be vigilant for signs of mouth cancer, including persistent mouth sores, red or white patches, difficulty swallowing, chronic hoarseness, or unusual lumps. Should you observe any of these symptoms, it’s crucial to take immediate action. Schedule a mouth cancer screening without delay. Early detection is key to effective treatment, so don’t hesitate to seek professional help if you experience these warning signs.
