About Us

Implants are state-of-the art, single tooth solutions for single tooth problems.

Bridges can also replace missing teeth while restoring beauty.

Partials can replace many missing teeth for patients on a tighter budget.

We use digital x-rays to gain a thorough understanding of your particular condition, to detect cavities, and to screen for bacterial abscesses.

Routine cleanings help to maintain optimal health of your teeth and gums by removing small amounts of calculus buildup that is colonized by inflammation-causing bacteria.

Porcelain veneers, crowns , and bridges restore normal tooth function and appear as good or better than your natural teeth.

Tooth Bleaching is safe, does not damage the teeth, and can take years off your appearance.

One half of adults over the age of 30 have moderate or severe periodontal disease, and over 70 % of adults over 65 have periodontal disease, which affects the gums and supporting bone for the teeth and can lead to early tooth loss.

Some people feel anxious about coming to the dentist. Dr. Murphy uses several techniques to help keep you comfortable.

Anti-Anxiety medications and Nitrous Oxide . Some people call it laughing gas. It helps calm your nerves and wears off in about 5 minutes, so it’s safe to drive after your visit.

New Patient FAQ

We will want to get to know you and will want to understand your past experiences, current conditions, and your goals.

We will likely need some xrays to help us accurately diagnose your problem.

If you have a dental insurance plan, you should bring your card with you to your first appointment.

We accept all major dental insurance providers and will file your insurance claim for you for any dental insurance benefit provider.

Before treatment is performed, we will discuss treatment and financial options. We want you to fully understand your dental treatment, what to anticipate in fees, and allow you time to make the necessary financial arrangements.

Payment is due at the time services are rendered. We accept cash, checks, Visa, MasterCard, Discover, and American Express.

Emergency patients, new to our practice, should expect to make a payment at the time of service.

Your insurance coverage depends on what you or your employer wishes to purchase. Benefits vary greatly based on the wide variety of policies sold to employers and patients, and it is impossible for any dental provider to know the insurance benefits of every patient. We will be happy to file your claim for you as a courtesy if you present your dental insurance wallet card and all required employer information. We will attempt to estimate what your plan may cover, but it is your responsibility to know your insurance policy rules and benefits.

Any deductible or estimated co-payment amount will be due at the time of treatment.

If payment for services already rendered has not been paid in full within 45 days, either by you or your insurance company, the remaining balance for your treatment is considered due and must be paid by you.

Appointments are time that is reserved exclusively for you. We reserve the right to charge and collect $30 for any broken appointments. Broken appointments are considered those that are missed (no-show) and cancelled with less than 24 hour advance notice. Patients who miss three (3) consecutive appointments will be asked to transfer their records to another doctor.

Child Advocacy: Separated or divorced parents of minors, who are responsible for one half of the cost of a child’s/children’s dental care: The parent who brings the child in to the dental appointment is responsible for paying the copayment or full fee.
