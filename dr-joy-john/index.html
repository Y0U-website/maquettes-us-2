Welcome to Duxbury Children’s Dentistry, your trusted dentist for infants, children, and teen dental needs across the entire South Shore. As specialists in pediatric dentistry, we expertly address all children’s dental requirements. From our kid-friendly waiting room to our warm, experienced staff, we ensure your child feels at ease from the moment they enter our practice.

Conveniently located off exit 20 of Route 3 in Duxbury, we proudly serve all towns of the South Shore, including Plymouth, Pembroke, Kingston, and Marshfield, as your go-to pediatric dentist.

Our office takes pride in our common sense guidelines that have effectively prevented any known incidences of in-office Covid transmission during the two years of declared emergency. We have rescheduled patients who were sick or symptomatic of contagious illness, which has proven to be very effective in keeping our staff and other patients well.

For the well-being and safety of our patients and staff, if your child is not feeling well, please call to change your appointment.

Additionally, if you or anyone in your household is sick or has had Covid in the last 7 days, please call our office to reschedule.
