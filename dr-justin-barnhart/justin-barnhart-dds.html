Meet Hutchinson Dentist, Justin L. Barnhart, DDS

Originally from St. Charles, MO, Dr. Justin Barnhart graduated from Francis Howell North High School. Growing up, Justin always knew he wanted to do something in the healthcare profession so he could help others. After high school, he earned his B.A. in Biology at Southwest Baptist University, where he and Melissa met, and he found his passion for dentistry. He then attended the UMKC School of Dentistry where he obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

As a member of both the Kansas Dental Association and the American Dental Association, Dr. Justin genuinely enjoys sharing his knowledge of dentistry with patients to help them become more informed about their oral health. He is married to Dr. Melissa Barnhart, and the newest love of his life is his daughter, Brooklyn. During his time outside of the office, Dr. J loves spending time with his family and serving in his local church. His other interests outside of dentistry include fishing, playing ice hockey, and golfing.
