Meet Dr. Tyler Bendixsen

Meet Twin Falls’s Dentist

Dr. Tyler Bendixsen

Dr. Bendixsen believes that great dentistry starts with genuine care and trust. With a calm, friendly approach, Dr. Bendixsen is dedicated to helping patients feel comfortable, heard, and confident in their dental care—whether they’re visiting for a routine checkup or a more involved procedure.

Dr. Bendixsen earned his Bachelor of Science at Brigham Young University and a Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Oregon Health and Science University and continues to pursue advanced training to stay current with the latest dental techniques and technology. This commitment to ongoing education allows Dr. Bendixsen to provide modern, thoughtful care tailored to each patient’s unique needs and goals.

Patients often appreciate Dr. Bendixsen’s gentle touch and willingness to take the time to explain treatment options clearly and honestly. Offering a full range of services—from preventive and restorative care to cosmetic dentistry—Dr. Bendixsen focuses on building long-term relationships and helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles at every stage of life.

Outside the office, Dr. Bendixsen enjoys golfing, playing the piano, traveling, and spending time with his wife and 4 wonderful children. Proud to serve the Twin Falls area, Dr. Bendixsen looks forward to welcoming you and your family and making every dental visit a positive experience.
