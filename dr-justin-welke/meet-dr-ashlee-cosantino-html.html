Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Whenever she isn’t at the office and treating growing smiles, Dr. Cosantino spends her time with her amazing husband and their two boys. Other than hanging out with her family and loved ones, she also enjoys hobbies like golf, fitness classes, baking/cooking, traveling, painting, and throwing parties. One interesting fact about her: she is the residency program director at Ann & Robert H. Lurie Children’s Hospital of Chicago!
