Dental Implants

Dental Implants in Pittsburgh, PA & Surrounding Areas

Pittsburgh Dental Spa offers patients in Pittsburgh, PA a better way to replace missing teeth. Thanks to new technology, we are able to provide the most innovative treatments.

A patient needs basic things in order to be a good candidate for dental implants: decent oral health, stable overall health, a nonsmoker and a thick enough jawbone to support implants. Once all of these conditions have been met, our team can offer you one of the following restorative options:

A dental implant body and an all-porcelain crown can be placed between two healthy teeth without altering the other teeth.

Implant crowns like those mentioned above can be positioned throughout the mouth. If a patient has a few missing teeth in a row, just two implants are needed to support an implant fixed bridge.

If you’re missing most of your teeth or are just sick and tired of dealing with an unstable denture, dental implants may be the solution your smile needs. Using just 4-6 implants strategically located along the jawbone we can provide either a Snap-In Implant Denture or a more Specialized Hybrid Implant Denture.

The Doctors at the Pittsburgh Dental Spa work with patients on a case by case treatment plan and sometimes an oral surgeon is needed to help with advanced implant cases.
