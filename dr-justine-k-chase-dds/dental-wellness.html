When a cavity is left untreated, it can lead to an abscessed (infected) tooth. This refers to a pocket of pus that forms in the pulp of the tooth, which can cause pain and swelling, tooth sensitivity, and/or a chip or crack in the tooth. An abscessed tooth needs immediate attention to prevent the infection from spreading to other parts of the body.

Patients who need dentures but prefer a prosthesis that is not moveable may prefer an implant-supported full-arch prosthesis.

Implant-supported dentures are anchored to your jaw with titanium posts and remain permanently in your mouth. This eliminates the need for adhesives and prevents embarrassing slippage while eating or talking. They also help wearers to speak more clearly by remaining set in place.
