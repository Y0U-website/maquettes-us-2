The practice

With a warm and approachable style, Dr. Grabowski strives to create a comfortable environment where patients feel heard and understood. He believes in open communication and compassionate care, ensuring that each patient feels confident in their treatment plan. His clinical interests include implant dentistry, cosmetic treatments, digital dentistry, and incorporating cutting-edge advancements…

Outside the office, Dr. Grabowski stays busy with his three children, who keep him on his toes with their many extracurricular activities. He met his wife in dental school, and she recently opened a pediatric dental practice in Hartland.

Dr. Grabowski is excited to join Fenton Family Dentistry, where he can continue providing the highest level of care while also spending more quality time with his family. He looks forward to meeting new patients and helping them achieve healthy, beautiful smiles!
