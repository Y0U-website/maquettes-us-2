Facility

Fenton Family Dentistry renovated 17197 Silver Parkway in Fenton, MI from a defunct piece of property to a state-of-the-art, fully functional ten operatory dental office with cutting edge technology. Dr. Sarah Paxton was involved heavily in the design, aesthetics, flow and overall functionality of the building.

The patient experience is extremely important to us at Fenton Family Dentistry. We want each and every unique individual to know that they are not simply a patient but are, truly, a family member to us. Keeping this in mind, the facility is fully accessible for individuals with or without disabilities.

Each operatory is loaded with the latest dental technology. The dental chairs are Pelton & Crane 1800 series (four chairs have optional massage technology built-in, for patient enjoyment). The chair boasts a sleek modern appearance and a commitment to patient relaxation. This chair is engineered for operator efficiency and has a thin chair back to ensure each patient is comfortable.

We use Gendex GXS-700 which takes the high quality x-ray image from an x-ray and digitally inserts it into your patient chart. This means, the Doctors as well as our patients are better able to have a clear understanding of the dental issues which are in need of repair. Utilizing the latest sensor technology, the GXS-700 delivers real-time images of truly amazing clarity and detail - greatly supporting diagnosis, treatment and overall patient care.
