EMERGENCY EXAMS

This is a problem focused examination. Our main concern is getting you out of pain as quickly as possible. This will just be an evaluation and we will most likely have to set up a second appointment if the problem cannot be fixed quickly. Initial treatment may include a prescription for antibiotics. We make every effort to see emergency patients as soon as possible, but be prepared for a short wait as we fit you into our schedule.
