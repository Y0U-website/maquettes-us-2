How Kids Benefit from Invisalign®

Comfortable & Nearly Invisible Invisalign aligners are smooth and gentle on the cheeks and gums, offering greater comfort than traditional braces. Since they’re custom-made, BPA-free clear aligners, they’re also very discreet perfect for children who prefer a more natural look while straightening their teeth.

Removable for Eating & Brushing Because Invisalign aligners can be taken out, kids can enjoy all their favorite foods without worrying about breaking brackets or getting food stuck. Removable aligners also make brushing and flossing easier, promoting better oral hygiene. Plus, children can continue playing sports or musical instruments without interference.

Personalized Treatment Plans At Pinole Valley Orthodontics, every Invisalign treatment plan is custom-designed based on your child’s age, dental development, lifestyle, and goals. Our team carefully tracks their progress throughout treatment, keeping you informed every step of the way so you know exactly how long it will take to achieve a beautifully aligned smile.
