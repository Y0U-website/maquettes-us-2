About Us

At Tender Dental Care, our patients’ comfort is our priority. We strive to get you the quality dental care you deserve by professionals who truly care about you.

Dr. Kamilia Said

Dr. Kamilia is a graduate of University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine. She completed her residency at University of California San Francisco. She was practicing in NJ and CT prior to her practice in Ossining. "I really enjoy helping patients overcome their fear of visiting a dentist so they don’t neglect their oral health just because of dental anxiety" says Dr. Kamilia. She sees adult and pediatric patients offering a wide range of services with focus on preventative and restorative dentistry.
