Proud To Be Your Family’s Dentist Office in Saratoga Springs, NY

Saratoga Family Dentistry offers dental treatments for the whole family. Located in Saratoga Springs, NY, Saratoga Family Dentistry is the area’s choice for regular cleanups, cosmetic dentistry , dental implants , teeth whitening , and more. If you want brighter, healthier teeth, or have a painful or embarrassing issue that needs to be fixed, Saratoga Family Dentistry is here to help.

For over 20 years, Saratoga Family Dentistry has been a trusted choice for family dental care and a host of cosmetic dentistry procedures. Our team can help give you the smile you’ve always wanted. We can fix missing teeth with dental implants and dentures, brighten your teeth with teeth whitening, add crowns and bridges to protect teeth, treat gum disease and TMJ, and offer emergency dental services when you need immediate attention.

A healthy mouth is the gateway to a healthy body.

A Friendly Face to Trust

At Saratoga Family Dentistry, our team members are friendly and highly knowledgeable. This allows us to provide the best care possible in a comfortable atmosphere. We are a family dental facility, so that means we treat men and women of all ages. Children can come see us for routine checkups without worry. We put you at ease with comfortable dental care and even offer sedation dental treatments.

Whether you need to schedule an appointment for a dental exam or have an emergency that needs to be treated right away, Saratoga Family Dentistry is here to help. Trust the experienced, qualified staff at Saratoga Family Dentistry. We are available Monday through Thursday from 7:00 a.m. to 4 p.m and Friday 7:00 a.m. to 1 p.m. for all the dental work you need. Call us today to make an appointment.
