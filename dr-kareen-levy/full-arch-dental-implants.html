Full-Arch Dental Implants In…

If you have missing teeth, implants can restore strength and stability. Even patients with bone loss may qualify with bone grafting or additional procedures. During your consultation, our dentist evaluates your jawbone, bone density, and oral health. We also review existing teeth and neighboring teeth to protect your long-term results.

Our goal is to replace what you lost while supporting your mouth, jaw, and smile. If full-arch implants are not ideal, we review dental implant services that fit your needs.

  • Loose or painful dentures
  • Gum disease or bone loss
  • Trouble chewing or speaking
  • One missing tooth progressing to multiple tooth loss
  • Trauma affecting your teeth or jaw

The Process Of Getting Full-Arch Dental Implants

A clear, guided dental implant journey.

Step One: Personalized Consultation Your consultation includes imaging, exams, and planning. Our dentists review your dental implant process, bone health, and treatment goals. We explain cost, financing options, and timelines clearly.

Step Two: Implant Placement Using guided implant surgery, we surgically place implants into the jawbone. In some cases, bone grafting supports long-term stability. Many patients qualify for same-day placement to reduce visits.

FAQs About Full-Arch Dental Implants

Answers to common patient questions.

  • How are full-arch implants different from dentures? Dentures rest on the gums and can move. Implant-supported dentures attach to implants placed in the jawbone, offering better stability and comfort without adhesives.
  • Will I need bone grafting? Some patients need bone grafting if bone loss has occurred. This supports proper implant placement and long-term success.
  • Is the procedure painful? Most patients describe minimal discomfort. Surgery is planned for utmost comfort, and we support you through every step.
  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care and routine visits, implants can last decades. They support oral health and protect the surrounding bone.
  • Can implants replace one tooth or multiple teeth? Yes. Single tooth implants, multiple implants, or full-arch solutions are available. We tailor treatment to your needs.
