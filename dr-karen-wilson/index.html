The practice

Our mission at Cedar Valley Family Dentistry is to treat our patients as family. Our goal is to help our patients achieve a healthy, beautiful smile that will last a lifetime. We strive to provide top quality care that is delivered in a caring, professional manner, that consistently exceeds our patients’ expectations.

Visit Dr. Wilson for a range of dental services including general and cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, teeth whitening, and emergency care. Enjoy complimentary amenities such as TV, music and coffee/tea.

Dr. Kären Wilson, a dentist, is from Dubuque, Iowa. She has two sons and is married to Mark Wilson. She earned her D.D.S. from the University of Iowa in 1999. Dr. Wilson is involved in professional and local communities, and enjoys spending time with family and friends, exercising, cooking, reading, shopping, traveling, and volunteering.
