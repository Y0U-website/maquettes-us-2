The practice

Ready to meet Dr. Thompson? Your complimentary consultation is exactly that, complimentary. No commitment, no pressure, just clarity on what’s possible.

Dr. Thompson earned her bachelor’s degree from the University of California, Los Angeles and her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Howard University , where she also completed her orthodontic residency. Howard’s College of Dentistry is one of the country’s longest-running institutions for training oral health practitioners, a program with deep roots in evidence-based clinical practice, which is…

She has been practicing orthodontics since 2001 , which puts her clinical experience comfortably past the 25-year mark. South Pasadena is where she chose to put down roots, and where SmileHaus has stayed ever since.

The SmileHaus approach is straightforward and intentionally a little old-fashioned: every patient is seen by Dr. Thompson personally at every visit. There’s no rotation of associate doctors, no handoff to a hygienist for the meaningful conversations. If your treatment plan changes, Dr. Thompson is the person explaining why.

She’s also conservative about when to recommend treatment, particularly for children. If a child is too young to benefit from early intervention, Dr. Thompson will say so and ask you to come back in a year. That’s the right answer more often than parents expect, and it’s the answer you’ll get here regardless of what a different office may have told you.

For adults, the conversation is different but the principles are the same. Many adult patients arrive a little embarrassed about the timing, feeling like braces are something they should have handled at 14. Dr. Thompson doesn’t make that weird. A meaningful percentage of the practice’s adult work is fixing imperfect childhood orthodontics or prepping teeth for a major milestone, and both are…

Honestly, the first visit is mostly a conversation. Dr. Thompson will take a look, listen to what’s brought you in, and walk you through the options that actually apply to your case. If braces or aligners aren’t the right call right now, she’ll tell you. If they are, she’ll explain the trade-offs in plain language and answer the questions you didn’t know to ask. That hesitation a lot of adults…
