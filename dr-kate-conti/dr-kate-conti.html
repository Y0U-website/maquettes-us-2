Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

Dr. Conti gives a lot of credit to her younger sister for where she is today. Even though Dr. Conti was a biology/pre-health sciences major in undergrad, her sister had the idea that they should both become dentists, so they could own a dental office and work together. This led to Dr. Conti shadowing a few dentists and working in a variety of practices throughout college and her summer breaks, and she fell in love with the profession. While she and her sister are both dentists today, they don’t work together but are very happy in their current positions.
