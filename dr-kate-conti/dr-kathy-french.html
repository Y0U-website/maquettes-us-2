Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

Dr. French’s interest in dentistry sparked when she was 22 while attending college at the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor. During this time, she began to see the perfect blend of medicine, architecture, and engineering that goes into the profession, and it seemed like the perfect fit for her.
