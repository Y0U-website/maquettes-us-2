0 Meet Dr. Grosso Dr. Katherine Calvert Grosso is a Board Certified Periodontist with training in the complete spectrum of periodontics and implant surgery. She is proactive in the treatment of disease while offering a conservative and gentle approach to treatment.

Dr. Grosso graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Biology from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. She then continued her education at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine in New York City, receiving her Doctorate of Dental Surgery.

In her spare time, she enjoys hiking, being outdoors and spending time with her husband and two sons.
