More About Dental Implant Restoration at Signature Smiles of Cullman

Dental implants serve as durable and stable replacements for missing teeth, whether it’s one tooth, multiple teeth, or full dental arches. The implant consists of a titanium screw surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as an artificial tooth root to support your dental restoration. A healing period allows the bone to bond with the biocompatible post, ensuring stability and longevity.

Once healed, our dentists will attach the dental implant restoration, such as a dental crown, bridge, or denture, customized to fit your smile comfortably and aesthetically. Each restoration mimics natural teeth in appearance, function, and feel, offering restored oral health, function, and a beautiful smile.

Choosing a dental implant can significantly enhance your smile’s health and appearance, as it’s designed to last a lifetime with proper care.
