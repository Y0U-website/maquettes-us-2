The practice

Our team at Elizabethtown Family Dentistry is pleased to offer a full range of dental services for the entire family!

Our office features innovative technology that makes it possible for us to provide unsurpassed oral care.

Comprehensive care, honesty, and compassion are the centers of our practice. We strive to make sure that you have a positive experience by communicating clearly what you can expect during your dental treatment.

Tooth-colored fillings can retain decayed teeth in a way that is both effective and appealing.

Custom-fitted tooth prosthetics that are used to replace or restore damaged or missing teeth.

Orthodontic appliance system used to inconspicuously treat crooked and crowded teeth.

Botox blocks signals between the muscle and the brain, ultimately preventing muscle contraction.

Teeth whitening consists of a thorough cleaning and polishing, with all debris carefully removed.
