Kathryn Davies, DDS, joined The Hartford Dental Group in 2003 and is a partner in the practice. She received her undergraduate degree from UW Lacrosse and graduated Summa Cum Laude from Marquette University School of Dentistry in 2001. Dr. Davies is a member of the American Dental Association, Wisconsin Dental Association, Washington-Ozaukee Dental Association and Omicron Kappa Upsilon of the National Dental Honor Society. Each year Dr. Davies and her assistants enjoy visiting local area 1st and 2nd graders during National Dental Health month to educate them on the benefits of healthy habits.

Dr. Davies is a life-long resident of the Hartford area. She resides in Hubertus with her husband Marcus and children Allie and Charlie. She enjoys attending her children’s many sporting activities and cheering on the Brewers. She also loves entertaining, a good book, knitting, and Disney anything!
