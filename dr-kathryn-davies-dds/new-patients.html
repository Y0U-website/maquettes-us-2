New Patients

From your first hello to your personalized treatment plan, our team will guide you through each step with clear communication, comfortable care, and helpful financial information.

Arrival and check-in

When you arrive, our receptionist will greet you and ask you to complete some paperwork. Please plan to arrive 10 to 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time.

What happens during your first visit

After your paperwork is complete, a dental assistant will bring you to the treatment room, get you settled, and ask a few preliminary questions. The doctor will then come in, introduce themselves, and review your medical history while listening to your dental concerns. A comprehensive exam follows, which includes a cancer screening, TMJ and periodontal evaluation, and a full review of your x-rays. At the end of your visit, you will meet with our financial manager to go over your treatment plan, insurance details, and payment options.

Flexible payment options

We believe quality dental care should be accessible. We accept most major dental insurance plans, offer a 5 % discount for uninsured patients, and work with CareCredit and Cherry to help make your treatment affordable.
