The practice

Our office is open Monday Thursday from 8am 5pm . For your convenience, 7:00am cleanings are available.

We are conveniently located in the Back Cove neighborhood of Portland, Maine at 173 Ocean Avenue, between Austin and George Streets. Off-street parking is available on the side of the building.

If you’re traveling on I-295 North: Take exit 6B for ME-100 N/US-302/Forest Avenue and continue on this road for a little over one mile. Turn right onto Ocean Avenue for about .4 miles and our office will be on your right.

If you’re traveling on I-295 South: Take exit 9 for Baxter Blvd towards Maine 26/Washington Avenue. Continue on Baxter Blvd for .4 miles and turn right into Payson Park (Catafalque Dr). Turn left onto Ocean Avenue for .7 miles and our office will be on your left.

Coming from Westbrook: Travel on ME-25 E/Westbrook Arterial and turn right onto Brighton Avenue. After one mile, take a slight left onto Woodford Street and after .9 miles, a left onto Forest Avenue. Turn right onto Ocean Avenue and after .4 miles, our office will be on your right.
