As we welcome in the new year, we are extremely excited to announce the addition of acclaimed adult cosmetic dentist Dr. Katie Staub to the EJL Team!

The practice

Dr. Katie Staub is a magna cum laude graduate of the University of Maryland Dental School, graduating second in her class. She was selected as a Diamond Scholar during her senior year, allowing her to enter an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency a year early. She then completed an additional year of residency after graduation, where she was elected Chief Resident.

Dr. Staub enjoys giving back to the community and has participated in Special Olympics and Maryland Mission of Mercy events. She has also participated in the Maryland Donated Dental program. In her spare time, she enjoys cooking, eating at great restaurants, traveling, reading, listening to music, and attending concerts.

Dr. Staub grew up in York, Pennsylvania and attended The Pennsylvania State University, graduating with a Bachelor of Science degree in mathematics. Prior to attending dental school, she worked as an actuarial analyst for 10 years. She previously worked in private practice alongside our very own Dr. Matelis (both pictured above) for 7 years and we’re thrilled to have her join the team!

With a focus on precision, long-term results and a patient-centered approach, we help children, teens and adults achieve their best smile with clarity and confidence.

These days, orthodontic care is commonly being given to patients of all ages, from childhood through adulthood! Although orthodontic treatment can start at different stages of development, there is no best time for every person to have treatment.

We always look forward to welcoming new patients to the orthodontic side of our practice! The first visit will be a simple and pleasant appointment. The purpose of the initial exam is to determine if orthodontic treatment is warranted, and if so, when is the proper time to begin treatment.
