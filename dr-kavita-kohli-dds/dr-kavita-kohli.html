About Dr. Kohli

Dr. Kohli was in full-time academics from 1996 to 2007 at West Virginia University and Columbia University-New York Presbyterian Hospital. She was Program Director of the Residency Program at New York Presbyterian Hospital from 2002 to 2007.

She has published several articles in dental journals such as Pediatric Dentistr y, Journal of Dentistry for the Child , Journal of Dental Research , and New York State Dental Journal . She serves on the Editorial Board for the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry’s journal, Pediatric Dentistry .

She has lectured at dental conferences and meetings including the annual meetings of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the International Association of Dental Research and at state meetings in West Virginia, New York County Dental Society and area New York City hospitals.

Dr. Kohli was a preceptor for the Oral Health Initiative for Pediatricians, Residents and Nurses as on-site trainer at facilities in El Paso, TX, Baton Rouge, LA, and for the Seminole Tribe, Hollywood, FL.

She was an invited lecturer for the Pediatric Preventive Oral Health Project at the West Virginia University Institute for Health Policy Research, and provided on-site training to staff at several sites.

She is active within her profession as an examiner for the Oral Exam for the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, and serves on several committees for the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

Dr. Kohli also volunteers to teach at the Westchester Medical Center in Valhalla.

A resident of Dobbs Ferry since 2002, her interests include traveling, gardening, cooking, music, cultural shows. She likes exploring and enjoying the offerings of the local neighborhoods of Dobbs Ferry and Westchester County.
