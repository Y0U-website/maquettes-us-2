  • Veneers In Coeur d’ Alene Dental veneers are thin, custom-made shells that are bonded to the front surfaces of teeth to improve their appearance. They can be used to treat a variety of cosmetic concerns, such as chipped, broken, discolored, or crooked teeth. Veneers are most commonly made from porcelain or resin composite materials. What are the benefits of dental veneers? Dental veneers offer a number of benefits, including: They rejuvenate the appearance of your teeth, making them look whiter, straighter, and more symmetrical.
  • They are predictable and durable, lasting for many years with proper care.
  • They are natural and comfortable, you are able to eat and drink normally after they are placed.

How long does the procedure take?

The procedure for placing dental veneers typically takes two to three appointments, depending on your case. The first appointment is for the dentist to plan and prepare your teeth and take dental impressions. No more gooey impressions, we use digital technology with intraoral scanners to make all impressions. The second appointment is for the dentist to fit the veneers and bond them to your teeth. The third appointment is for a follow-up checkup.

What is the maintenance for dental veneers?

Dental veneers require a normal brushing and flossing routine, just like your natural teeth. You should also see the team at the CDA Dental Center for regular checkups and cleanings. Preventative care ensures your veneers will give your smile a long lasting improvement.

If you are considering dental veneers, be sure to talk to your dentist at CDA Dental Center about the benefits, risks, and costs involved. We can help you decide if veneers are the right choice for you.
