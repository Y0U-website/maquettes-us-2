Schwartz Family Dentistry

Care starts with how we treat people.

For more than 40 years, our practice has grown through trust—between patients and clinicians, between colleagues, and across generations of Capital Region families.

A place where people support one another.

Our exceptionally low staff turnover reflects something patients feel when they walk through the door: this is a team that genuinely enjoys working together. Many colleagues build long careers here, sharing knowledge, stepping in for one another and welcoming new people with generosity.

We believe a family-style team is not only about how colleagues treat each other. It is also how we care for patients—with familiarity, respect, patience and the confidence that someone will be there when help is needed.

We know one another

Continuity matters. Familiar faces build trust and help patients feel comfortable returning year after year.

We share the work

Clinical and administrative teams communicate closely, support one another and keep the patient experience connected.

We keep learning

New team members, new technology and continuing education help us expand care without losing what makes the practice special.

We care like family

That means listening carefully, explaining clearly and treating every patient with the same consideration we would want for our own families.

More support for the care our patients expect.

As demand grows, we are adding talented clinicians, dental assistants, hygiene and front-office support. Growth gives our team more capacity to serve patients well while protecting the personal attention and high standards that have defined the practice since 1983.
