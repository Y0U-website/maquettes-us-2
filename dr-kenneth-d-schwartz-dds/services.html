Preventive care

Comfortable, consistent care that helps protect your teeth, gums and overall health.

Cleanings and oral exams

Routine preventive visits tailored to your needs, with clear guidance for home care.

Sealants and fluoride

Added protection for teeth that may be more vulnerable to decay.

Digital imaging

Digital X-rays and CAT scans when needed for efficient, detailed diagnosis and planning.

Periodontal care

Gum-health evaluation, scaling and root planing to help manage periodontal disease.

Oral cancer screening

A careful screening of the mouth and surrounding tissues as part of comprehensive care.

Restorative care

Thoughtful solutions that rebuild comfort, function and a natural-looking smile.

Composite restorations

Tooth-colored fillings designed to blend naturally with your smile.

Porcelain crowns and veneers

Custom restorations used to strengthen, protect or reshape a tooth.

Bridges

Fixed restorations that replace missing teeth and help restore a balanced bite.

Partial and full dentures

Personalized removable options for replacing several or all missing teeth.

Implant-supported restorations

Crowns, bridges and dentures designed to work with dental implants.

Root canal therapy

Treatment intended to preserve a tooth and relieve pain or infection.

Cosmetic dentistry

Conservative, personalized treatment designed around how you want your smile to look and feel.

Professional whitening

A brighter smile with a treatment approach selected for your needs.

Cosmetic bonding

Composite material used to repair chips, close small spaces or refine tooth shape.

Porcelain veneers

Thin, custom porcelain restorations that can change shape, shade and proportion.

Cosmetic crowns

Natural-looking coverage for teeth that need both structural and aesthetic improvement.

Clarity clear aligners

Customized, nearly invisible aligner treatment from our new partner, Solventum, designed around your smile goals and clinical needs.

Advanced services

Expanded treatment options, delivered in a familiar office by a team you know.

Dental implant placement

Surgical placement and restorative planning for long-term tooth replacement.

Extractions and oral surgery

Careful removal of teeth, including wisdom teeth, when preservation is not possible.

Bone augmentation

Bone grafting and socket preservation to support healing and future treatment.

Sleep apnea and snore guards

Custom oral appliances for eligible patients seeking a more restful night.

Soft-tissue laser care

Precise laser treatment for selected gum and other soft-tissue procedures.

Comfort and sedation options

Nitrous oxide and oral conscious sedation for eligible patients who need added support.

Athletic mouthguards

Custom protection made for a more secure and comfortable fit.

Pre-medication guidance

Coordination and instructions for patients whose health history may require medication before care.
