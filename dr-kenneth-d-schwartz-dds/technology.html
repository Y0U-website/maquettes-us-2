Coming soon · Advanced surgical navigation

X-Guide dynamic 3D navigation

X-Guide NXT is a state-of-the-art, computer-guided navigation system for dental implant placement and selected root-canal procedures.

Robotic-style guidance. Dentist-controlled care.

Real-time direction for precision and control

X-Guide combines cone-beam 3D imaging with live tracking of the patient and dental handpiece. During implant surgery, the screen continuously shows the planned position, angle and depth as the dentist works—similar to GPS guidance for a dental handpiece.

For appropriate root-canal cases, X-Guide can also support navigated access to difficult, calcified or hidden canal anatomy. The system does not operate autonomously: the dentist plans and performs every step while X-Guide provides live visual guidance. Individual treatment and outcomes vary.

Better information at every step.

Our current imaging and chairside tools help the team diagnose, explain and plan care while keeping visits as comfortable and efficient as possible.

Planmeca CBCT imaging

Three-dimensional imaging gives our clinicians a detailed view of teeth, bone and surrounding anatomy for diagnosis and treatment planning—from focused areas to broader views of the jaw.

Digital intraoral scanners

Digital impressions can replace traditional impression material in many cases, helping us plan restorations and share accurate information with our dental laboratory.

Intraoral cameras

Small chairside cameras help you see what we see. Clear images make it easier to understand a diagnosis, compare changes and participate in treatment decisions.

Connected digital planning

Imaging and digital scans can work together to support restorative, implant, endodontic and surgical planning with more visual information available to the care team.
