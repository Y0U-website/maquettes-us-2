Scaling and Root Planing

The goals are to remove bacteria, plaque, and tartar, and to halt the progress of the disease. While most treatments can be completed comfortably without anesthetic, our dentist is happy to offer anesthetic and other comfort options to make certain that you feel at ease during your treatment. The treatment may be split up into several appointments, in which we treat one section of the mouth at each appointment.

Scaling and root planing may be combined with other types of periodontal therapy, such as antibiotic treatment. For most patients, this treatment is enough to prevent periodontal disease from progressing further. For more information about the benefits of scaling and root planing, and to learn more about the other types of periodontal treatment we offer, please do not hesitate to contact our office .

  • Root Planing: This step involves cleaning the roots of the teeth, smoothing the root to remove bacterial toxins.
