About Our Staff

We strive to for our services to be available in a gentle, caring and comfortable atmosphere, where you are treated as one of our family. And as one, for whom we have accepted the responsibility of your best dental health.

Our office staff is courteous, professional and efficient. They will help you competently and with your best interests in view. If you have any concerns or questions, please ask for their help.

Appointments are made for maximum treatment on a timely and efficient schedule. We appreciate your efforts to be courteous of that fact. Time is set aside daily for emergency treatment and care. If you are unable to keep your scheduled appointment, please give notice 24 hours in advance to enable another patient to use that time.
