Dr. Kent Zocher

Pediatric dentistry has been a perfect fit for Dr. Kent. The energy, sense of humor and frankness of children makes going to work exciting, fun and fulfilling! Additionally, helping those with special needs has been an honor and a privilege and gives Dr. Kent a great deal of satisfaction. Developing a trusting relationship in a safe environment with the greatest level of care possible, is always his ultimate goal. Dr. Kent is a member of the American Dental Association, Wisconsin Dental Association, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and the Wisconsin Society of Pediatric Dentists.

When not in the office, Dr. Kent is usually in the woods or on the water. Most recently, he traveled into the Yucatan jungle with his recurve bow and cedar arrows to complete the World Slam of turkey hunting. He also regularly performs professionally as a drummer in several bands locally and abroad. He lives in Campbellsport with his lovely wife, Stacy and daughter, Tessa and often visits his son, Tanner, who is completing his degree at UW-Madison.
