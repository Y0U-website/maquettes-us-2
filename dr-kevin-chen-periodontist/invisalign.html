What Is Invisalign?

Invisalign is a revolutionary orthodontic treatment that uses clear, removable aligners to straighten teeth over time. Unlike traditional braces, which rely on metal brackets and wires, Invisalign aligners are custom-made to fit snugly over your teeth. Each set of aligners is designed to make slight adjustments, gradually moving your teeth into their desired positions.

The process begins with a consultation and digital scan of your teeth. Advanced imaging software creates a 3D model of your mouth, allowing Dr. Hiruma and her team to map out a personalized treatment plan. This technology not only predicts the alignment process but also gives you a preview of your expected results. Once the plan is set, your custom aligners are fabricated and delivered.

Patients typically wear each aligner set for about two weeks before switching to the next one in the series. Depending on the complexity of your case, Invisalign treatment can take anywhere from several months to over a year. Regular check-ins help ensure your progress stays on track.

What Are the Benefits of Invisalign Over Traditional Braces?

One of the standout benefits of Invisalign aligners is their nearly invisible appearance. Unlike braces, which are highly noticeable, Invisalign aligners are transparent, making them an ideal choice for adults and teens who want to maintain a natural look during treatment. This discretion allows you to smile confidently throughout the process.

Invisalign aligners are also removable, which makes them highly convenient. You can take them out while eating, brushing, or flossing, ensuring your oral hygiene routine remains unaffected. This feature also allows you to enjoy all your favorite foods without the dietary restrictions often associated with braces.

Comfort is another key advantage. Invisalign aligners are made from smooth plastic, eliminating the risk of cuts or irritation caused by metal wires and brackets. While some mild pressure is normal when switching to a new set of aligners, most patients find this sensation far more tolerable than the discomfort of tightening braces.

Who Can Benefit From Invisalign?

Invisalign is a versatile treatment that is suitable for addressing a variety of dental concerns. It can correct issues such as overbites, underbites, crossbites, and open bites, as well as overcrowded or widely spaced teeth. It’s an excellent option for patients with mild to moderate alignment issues, and advancements in technology now make it possible to address more complex cases.

Teens and adults alike can benefit from Invisalign. For teenagers, Invisalign offers the ability to straighten teeth without the embarrassment of braces during school years. For adults, it provides a discreet solution that fits seamlessly into work and social settings.

However, Invisalign isn’t for everyone. Severe alignment problems or jaw issues may require traditional orthodontics for more effective results. Additionally, Invisalign success depends on the patient’s commitment to wearing the aligners for the recommended 20–22 hours per day. Skipping aligner wear or not following the treatment plan can delay progress.

The Invisalign Treatment Process

Initial Consultation & Exam

Prior to starting your treatment, you will have an opportunity to discuss your desired smile outcomes with your Albertson dentist . They will carefully examine your bite and smile to determine if Invisalign is the appropriate solution for you. This is also a great time to ask any questions you may have regarding your smile, Invisalign, or other available treatment options.

Aligner Fitting & Pick Up

Once you have been given the green light to proceed with Invisalign, your dentist will take scans or impressions of your teeth to ensure your aligners fit perfectly. Once your aligners are ready, you will visit our office to pick them up and begin your treatment. In some cases, your dentist may need to attach small fixtures to your teeth to aid in keeping the aligners securely in place.

CHECK-INS

While every treatment timeline is unique, you’ll most likely stop by our office every 4 weeks to pick up your next set of aligners. These check-ups are crucial as they ensure that your treatment is progressing as planned and that your Invisalign trays are fitting correctly.
