Non-Surgical Periodontal Treatments

Scaling and Root Planing (Deep Cleaning)

This thorough cleaning procedure serves as the foundation of periodontal treatment in Albertson, NY. During this process:

Clinical studies show that scaling and root planing can reduce pocket depth by an average of 0.5-1mm and stabilize attachment levels in patients with moderate periodontitis.

  • We apply a gentle local anesthetic to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure
  • Our periodontist carefully removes all plaque and tartar from above and below the gumline (scaling)
  • The tooth roots are smoothed (planing) to eliminate bacterial toxins and create a clean surface for gum reattachment
  • Depending on your condition, this may be completed in one visit or divided into multiple appointments for different quadrants of your mouth

Antibiotic Therapy

To enhance the effectiveness of deep cleaning procedures, we often incorporate targeted antibiotic treatments. These may include local delivery antibiotics placed directly into periodontal pockets, oral antibiotics prescribed for specific bacterial infections, antimicrobial mouth rinses for ongoing home care, and subgingival irrigation with antimicrobial solutions during office visits.

Advanced Periodontal Disease Treatments

For more progressive cases of periodontal disease in Albertson, NY, our practice offers several advanced treatment options:

LANAP® Laser Therapy

This revolutionary FDA-approved treatment represents the cutting edge of periodontal care. LANAP® laser therapy uses precise laser energy to target diseased tissue while preserving healthy gum tissue, creating a sterile environment for healing with minimal bleeding and swelling. The procedure promotes regeneration of bone and gum attachment while requiring significantly less recovery time than traditional surgery. Patients experience a comfortable treatment with no cutting or sutures needed

Pinhole Surgical Technique™ for Gum Recession

When periodontal disease has caused gum recession, this minimally invasive alternative to traditional gum grafting provides exceptional results. The technique uses specialized instruments to make a small pinhole in the gum tissue, through which our periodontist gently repositions the gum tissue over exposed tooth roots. The procedure requires no donor tissue, cutting, or sutures, resulting in minimal discomfort and quick recovery while providing immediate cosmetic improvement.

Guided Biofilm Therapy

Our Albertson, NY periodontal practice utilizes this innovative Swiss technology for both prevention and treatment. The therapy combines air, water, and fine erythritol powder for gentle yet effective biofilm removal, using specialized lighting to identify areas needing attention. It provides superior cleaning around implants, orthodontic appliances, and sensitive areas where traditional methods might cause discomfort. This comfortable approach optimizes the effectiveness of subsequent periodontal treatments.

The Deep Cleaning Treatment

For those currently navigating gum disease, Enso Dental Arts is proud to offer a deep cleaning service that includes:

Local Anesthetic

First, your dentist in Albertson, NY will apply a local anesthetic to keep you comfortable during your treatment. Deep cleanings may cause more sensitivity for patients than regular dental cleanings, but you’ll be comfortable throughout the procedure thanks to our gentle numbing techniques.

Scaling & Root Planing

Next, your doctor will use special tools to “scale” tartar and plaque from between your teeth and below your gum line. The goal is to remove the harmful bacteria that causes gum disease. Then, once your teeth and gums have been cleaned, your dentist will “plane,” or smooth, the roots of your teeth. This process eliminates the pockets and pits that form below the roots of your teeth which harbor harmful bacteria.

Antibiotic Treatment

A healing antibiotic treatment will complete your deep cleaning. Depending on your individual needs, you may receive topical antibiotics that are applied to gums during treatment, an antibiotic rinse that you can use at home, or a prescription antibiotic that will be taken orally.
