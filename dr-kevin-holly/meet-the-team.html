Amber

Amber graduated in 1995 from Century College as a licensed dental assistant. She did her internship with Vibrant Dental and enjoyed it so much that she never left! In 2004, she transitioned to a front office role and eventually to office manager.​ Amber is so grateful to be a part of such an amazing team at Vibrant Dental and looking forward to many more memorable years and continued relationships with our patients.​When not working Amber enjoys her time on the beach in Florida or at the cabin with her husband Bob, three children-Andrew, Ally, Samantha and their many pets.
