Pediatric Airway Program & Growth…

Comprehensive Approach to Oral Health: Integrating airway considerations into pediatric dentistry provides a more comprehensive approach to oral health, recognizing the interconnectedness of oral health with overall well-being.

  • Improved sleep quality
  • Prevention of orthodontic problems down the road
  • Enhanced Cognitive Development: Quality sleep is crucial for cognitive development in children. By optimizing the airway and addressing potential sleep-related issues, airway-focused dentistry may positively influence cognitive function, concentration, and overall academic performance.
  • Reduced Risk of Behavioral Issues: Children with untreated sleep-disordered breathing may be more prone to behavioral issues such as hyperactivity and attention deficits. Improving sleep quality through airway-focused interventions can contribute to better behavior and emotional well-being.
  • Prevention of Enlarged Tonsils and Adenoids: Addressing airway concerns early on may contribute to the prevention of enlarged tonsils and adenoids, reducing the likelihood of surgical interventions such as tonsillectomy and adenoidectomy.
  • Establishment of Healthy Habits: Airway-focused dentistry encourages the development of healthy oral habits, including proper tongue posture and nasal breathing. These habits can positively influence oral and overall health throughout a child’s life.
  • Facilitation of Facial Harmony: By considering the relationship between facial growth and the airway, airway-focused dentistry aims to promote facial harmony and aesthetics, contributing to a balanced and aesthetically pleasing appearance.
