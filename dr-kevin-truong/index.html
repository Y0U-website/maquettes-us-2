The practice

Dr. Truong has been in private practice in Virginia since 2003. He graduated from Virginia Commonwealth University (VCU) and then continued on to complete his dental education at the VCU School of Dentistry.

During his free time Dr. Truong enjoys the outdoors, traveling, and spending time with his wife and 3 young children.

At Riverton Dental Care we are committed to ensuring that your smile remains healthy and beautiful for years to come. We pledge to always strive for the highest standards of excellence.

By providing comprehensive treatment plans, as well as cosmetic dentistry options, we ensure you receive the optimal dental care.

Please do not hesitate to inform us if we can make your visit a more enjoyable and comfortable one. Thank you for your trust in us!

In addition to diagnosing and treating conditions associated with the teeth and gums, we strive to enhance the overall dental health of our patients by providing preventative care and maintenance. In conjunction with dental exams and cleanings during regular visits, deep cleanings are provided as a proactive treatment option for patients with chronic gum disease.

We offer composite fillings that match the shade and color of natural teeth. These fillings function, feel and are aesthetically more suitable than traditional metal fillings. Additionally, unlike the metal (amalgam) fillings of the past, these fillings do not pose the potential side effects associated with the mercury present in metal fillings.

Root canal treatments are provided to treat and save damaged, infected teeth by removing the infection within. The restored teeth have the potential to remain healthy for a long period of time after successful treatment.
