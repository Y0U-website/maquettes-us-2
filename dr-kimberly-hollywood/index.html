The practice

Dr. Hollywood knew from an early age that dentistry was for her; in fact, if you find her sixth-grade yearbook, you will see that even then she wanted to be a dentist! Dr. Hollywood started working for a dentist during high school and then continued to work for a dentist during her college summers. Seeing these dentists work and have an amazing impact on the lives of their patients was her…

Dr. Hollywood attended Lehigh University where she received a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. She continued on to dental school where she earned her Doctorate of Dental Medicine at the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey. Outside of the office, Dr. Hollywood enjoys being a mom to sons, Grant and Griffin, and daughter, Tessa, and Aunt Kiki to her adorable nieces and nephews. She also enjoys shopping with her mom and sisters, trying new restaurants with her husband Dan, and taking barre classes to stay fit in a world where new restaurants are always popping up!

We are a pediatric dental practice dedicated to providing high-quality dental care to the children of Nutley, Montclair, Bloomfield and surrounding areas.

We offer a full range of pediatric dental services in a fun, kid-friendly atmosphere. From preventive cleanings to pediatric restorative care, we have all the tools to keep your child’s smile healthy and beautiful.

At Nutley Pediatric Dentistry, we believe that early impressions of dental care can impact a patient for a lifetime. Our team is devoted to establishing a positive relationship between your child and their dental health. We love watching your child’s smile grow from infancy to their teenage years.
