Meet Dr. Hollywood

REQUEST APPOINTMENT A white background with a few lines on it (973) 667-5507 Get in touch Meet Dr. Kimberly Hollywood Dr. Kimberly Hollywood brings a ray of sunshine and a dynamic personality that enlightens our team and brightens each child’s experience. Her goal is to create a happy and warm environment so that children enjoy going to the dentist. She is passionate about establishing a relationship with her patients from an early age to educate them on the importance of oral health.

Dr. Hollywood Is a board-certified pediatric dentist. This is someone whose credentials have been verified, completed voluntary examinations, and demonstrates specialized knowledge and skill. She is dedicated to providing exceptional patient care through a voluntary commitment to lifelong learning and expansion of her knowledge in dentistry. She attends numerous continuing education courses throughout the year so she can give her patients the most up-to-date options for dental treatment and use the most advanced materials and equipment.

Dr. Hollywood knew from an early age that dentistry was for her; in fact, if you find her sixth-grade yearbook, you will see that even then she wanted to be a dentist! Dr. Hollywood started working for a dentist during high school and then continued to work for a dentist during her college summers. Seeing these dentists work and have an amazing impact on the lives of their patients was her inspiration to join the dental profession.

Dr. Hollywood attended Lehigh University where she received a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. She continued on to dental school where she earned her Doctorate of Dental Medicine at the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey. While in dental school, she was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society, a membership that is exclusive for graduating students who have demonstrated exceptional scholarship and character. Following dental school, Dr. Hollywood completed specialty training in pediatric dentistry at the Rutgers School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Hollywood is a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the New Jersey Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. She is involved in many community service projects, notably volunteering her time with the Indian Health Service in Albuquerque, New Mexico, to help children and adults who would otherwise have no access to dental care.

Outside of the office, Dr. Hollywood enjoys being a mom to sons, Grant and Griffin, and daughter, Tessa, and Aunt Kiki to her adorable nieces and nephews. She also enjoys shopping with her mom and sisters, trying new restaurants with her husband Dan, and taking barre classes to stay fit in a world where new restaurants are always popping up!
