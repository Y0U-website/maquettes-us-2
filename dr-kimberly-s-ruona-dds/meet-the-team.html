Dr. Melany Bloomquist

While her father was graduating from the University of Washington School of Dentistry, Dr. Melany Bloomquist was born in Seattle, Washington. She was raised in the Port Orchard area. Dr. Bloomquist attended Orchard Heights and Manchester Elementary schools, Marcus Whitman and John Sedgwick Junior Highs, and graduated from South Kitsap High School in 1986. Throughout the late 1980’s, Dr. Bloomquist attended the Washington State University and Arizona State University.

Dr. Bloomquist loves traveling, home decorating, skiing, clam digging, and rooting for Seattle Mariners-(something she got used to while watching her brother play there). She especially loves spending time with her family. Dr. Bloomquist lives in Port Orchard with her husband Steve, their twins, Phillip and Phoebe, and son Curt.

Dr. JUSTIN ROBISON

Dr. Justin Robison graduated from Brigham Young University with a Bachelor of Science degree. While working towards his doctorate in dentistry, he was able to serve the people in rural Brazil on a humanitarian mission providing basic dental services. In 2006, he graduated from Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine receiving his DMD degree.

After dental school Dr. Robison began working for a community health clinic that provided dental care to the underserved populations of southwestern Idaho. While in Idaho he also began working in private practice where he was able to expand the services he provided, including cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Robison was born and raised in southern Idaho and moved to Port Orchard to be closer to extended family. He enjoys living in western Washington where he volunteers in church and community with his wife and four children. Washington also provides an environment where Dr. Robison is able to enjoy outdoor activities which include camping, hiking, skiing, and cycling.
