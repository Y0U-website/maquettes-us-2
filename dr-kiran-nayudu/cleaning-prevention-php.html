Cleaning & Prevention

Preventive dental care is a key to keeping your teeth healthy life long. But it goes beyond that, as it is said that the oral cavity is the mirror of your whole body or general health. So let us know What is preventive dentistry, what are the benefits, and what kind of dental plan covers you for preventive care.

Preventive dentistry is a branch of dentistry that helps maintain good oral health. Basically, it involves both the patient and doctor, in maintaining good oral hygiene with regular dental check-ups and good habits like brushing and flossing. Good oral hygiene habits like brushing, flossing, and regular dental visits should be inculcated early in childhood.

Dental cleaning and prevention can help to keep your mouth clean and protect you from any disease. Wisdom Teeth

Wisdom teeth are the set of last teeth that occur during the teenage years. The wisdom tooth is important in the mouth to chew hard substances like meat, nuts, etc. In case any disinfection is caused due to the wisdom tooth, there is a chance of causing the effect of another tooth.

Sometimes wisdom teeth are misaligned and positioned horizontally in the gum line, this type of poor alignment causes damage to the teeth, nerves, and the gum line which results in pain, swelling, etc. Such cases involve the removal of Wisdom teeth. Apart from this misalignment, they are important for everyone.

If people have any pain in the gums, consult a dentist and he will take an X-ray of your complete teeth. there you can find your wisdom teeth. The dentists will suggest treatment before the problem occurs. If this process is neglected it can be more complicated in the future for the extraction. This may affect your overall health.

A dental surgeon takes up this procedure. A wisdom tooth which is completely broken out through the gum can be extracted as easily as any other tooth. A wisdom tooth that is below the gums and immersed in the jawbone will require a slit into the gums and then removal of the portion of bone that lies over the tooth.

Before the teeth removal, the dentist will give anesthesia to your gum to numb the pain. Then the teeth are removed. After the wisdom teeth removal, recovery of your gum depends on the care and food that you are taking after the removal.
