The practice

From our convenient digital check-in to the calming presence of our beloved therapy dogs, Charlie and Meg, we want you to feel at ease. We welcome everyone, from families with young children to retirees, into a comfortable space where high-quality, patient-centered care is our promise to you.

For your convenience, we offer same day crowns created right here in our office. Using CEREC Primescan and a PrimeMill milling machine, we can design, create, and place your permanent crown in a single visit. This service is ideal for those with busy lifestyles who need to schedule care quickly.

Comprehensive Care for Your Family We provide a complete range of services to meet your family’s needs, from general and pediatric dentistry to advanced cosmetic and restorative solutions. Our high-tech treatments, including Botox and gum contouring, are designed for your optimal health and a beautiful smile.

We offer Botox for cosmetic enhancements and therapeutic relief from TMD and migraines.

We restore your smile with dental implants, providing a stable and permanent solution for your missing teeth.

We provide gentle, friendly dental care for patients of all ages, including infants.

Straighten your teeth easily with clear aligners, aided by vPro technology for faster results.

We can reshape your gumline for a more balanced smile using our laser.
