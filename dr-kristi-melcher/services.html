Teeth Whitening

Are you unhappy with the color of your teeth? Perhaps you have staining from smoking, coffee, wine, or medications. Having your smile whitened in the office will yield excellent results in a short period of time, depending on the staining. Ask your hygienist at your routine preventive visit about our Philips Zoom in-office whitening for whiter teeth in less than an hour and take-home whitening for initial whitening or upkeep.

Bridges

Fixed and removable bridges are an option for missing teeth if you do not wish to have the implant procedure. Removable bridges are attached to adjacent teeth with wires and must be taken out for cleaning. Fixed bridges are permanently affixed to the adjacent teeth, which must be prepared with a crown to provide strength and prevent damage from the bridge.

Invisalign® Braces

For adults and teens who want to straighten their teeth without traditional braces, we offer Invisalign® . Invisalign® aligners are customized to your teeth. Each aligner is worn for a specified amount of time before moving on to the next one. After the series is finished, your smile will be straight and aesthetically pleasing. Talk to Dr. Frill about an examination for Invisalign® today.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does family dentistry mean?

Family dentistry involves comprehensive dental care tailored to patients of all ages, from children to seniors. It focuses on preventive care, diagnosis, and treatment of common dental issues to maintain overall oral health for the entire family.

What does cosmetic dentistry mean?

Cosmetic dentistry refers to dental treatments designed to improve the appearance of teeth, gums, and smile. These procedures enhance aesthetics through teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, and more without compromising oral health.

What is family and cosmetic dentistry?

Family and cosmetic dentistry combines general dental care for patients of all ages with specialized treatments aimed at enhancing the smile’s appearance. This integrated approach ensures comprehensive oral health and cosmetic improvements under one roof.

What services are included in family dentistry?

Family dentistry services include regular check-ups, cleanings, cavity fillings, fluoride treatments, sealants, root canals, extractions, and preventive education. They focus on maintaining oral health and addressing dental problems early.

What treatments are considered cosmetic dentistry?

Common cosmetic dentistry treatments include teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, dental bonding, crowns, bridges, and orthodontic procedures like Invisalign. These treatments enhance the smile’s appearance while often improving function.

Who can be treated in a family dentistry practice?

A family dentistry practice treats patients of all ages—from toddlers to grandparents—providing personalized care that meets the specific dental needs at every stage of life.

What does it mean when a dentist offers both services?

When a dentist offers both family and cosmetic dentistry, it means patients can receive comprehensive care covering routine dental health needs and aesthetic treatments, ensuring convenience and consistent care quality.

How is cosmetic dentistry different from family dentistry?

Family dentistry primarily focuses on oral health maintenance and treatment of dental conditions, while cosmetic dentistry emphasizes improving the visual appeal of the teeth and smile.

Can cosmetic dentistry improve function as well as appearance?

Yes. Many cosmetic dentistry treatments not only improve aesthetics but also restore function by repairing damaged teeth, correcting bite issues, and providing durable restorations.

What are the benefits of getting both types of care in one office?

Receiving family and cosmetic dentistry services in one office ensures continuity of care, convenience, and personalized treatment plans that address both oral health and aesthetic goals efficiently.
