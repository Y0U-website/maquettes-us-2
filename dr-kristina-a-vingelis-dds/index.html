Vingelis & Lee Family Dentistry works with many dental and medical insurance plans and will help patients file claims and complete the necessary paperwork to ensure they receive the maximum reimbursement from their insurance provider. The team is happy to answer questions about coverage and assist with insurance submissions.

For patients paying out-of-pocket, the office offers several convenient payment options, including cash, checks, and major credit cards. They also provide a 5 % pre-payment discount for services over $300 and offer CareCredit financing, which may allow patients to receive up to 12 months of interest-free financing with approval.
