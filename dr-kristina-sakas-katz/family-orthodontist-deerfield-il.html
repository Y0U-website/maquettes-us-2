Orthodontic Solutions for Every Member of Your Family

Our team works with young children, teenagers, and adults to create personalized plans addressing crowding, bite issues, spacing, and alignment concerns. Treatment options include:

▸ Traditional metal braces, a reliable and effective option for a wide range of cases.

▸ Clear ceramic braces, a more discreet alternative for complex alignment issues.

▸ Invisalign and Invisalign Teen, removable clear aligners offering comfort and flexibility.

▸ INBRACE lingual braces, placed behind the teeth for a nearly invisible result.

▸ Retainers, used after active treatment to maintain long-term results.

We also use iTero Digital Scanning technology for a more comfortable, accurate experience from the very first visit.

What Can You Expect at Your First Appointment?

Your first visit is welcoming and informative. Here is what a typical new patient appointment looks like:

1. Our front desk team gathers basic information to get you set up in our system.

2. We take digital scans, photos, and any necessary X-rays for a thorough diagnosis.

3. One of our doctors completes a comprehensive exam.

4. We discuss your orthodontic findings, recommended treatment type, and realistic timelines.

5. We review insurance coverage, financial options, and family care plans so treatment fits your budget.
