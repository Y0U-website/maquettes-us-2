Bonding

If a tooth is chipped or cracked, this procedure taps into tooth-colored composite material to correct its shape or color. She also does bonding to fill the gaps between teeth and protect a portion of the root that may be exposed due to receding gums.

Bonding process:

First, Dr. Kristina makes the surface of the tooth rough.

Then she applies a conditioning liquid that allows the bonding liquid to adhere to the tooth.

She applies a putty-like resin to the tooth and molds it to the desired shape of a perfect tooth.

Lastly, the surface is hardened by a blue light.

Veneers

To improve the esthetics and function of your smile, Dr. Kristina can apply custom-made shells over existing teeth to conceal cracks, chips, stains, cosmetic imperfections or irregularities.

Veneer process:

After the initial consultation, where she makes sure you’re a viable candidate for veneers, she will prep the tooth by removing a small amount of enamel.

She’ll take an impression of your teeth with 3D imaging.

The veneers are sent to a lab, where they are created.

Dr. Kristina will bond the veneers over the teeth with a permanent seal.

Digital Smile Design

Thanks to proprietary software, Dr. Kristina can load an impression of the patient’s smile and then alter it to illustrate what the smile will look like after cosmetic treatments to make teeth more uniform and pleasant looking. She says this is one of her favorite parts of the job, helping patients smile confidently again.
