Routine Cleaning Check-up and Preventative Dentistry

Every six months, during a preventive visit, you’ll receive a professional cleaning and polishing, images will be taken of your teeth and mouth with a 3D panoramic scanner and intraoral camera, and Dr. Kristina will also perform a thorough cancer screening. The hygienist will go over the best brushing and flossing practices.

How to schedule?

You can book appointments here or just give us a call. If your call is not answered by a person, feel free to leave a message and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Early detection is important in mouth and throat cancer, which has been on the rise over the years. Dr. Kristina will thoroughly inspect your mouth to detect signs of cancer or pre-cancerous conditions and lesions. Risk factors for oral cancer include tobacco use, heavy alcohol consumption, and sun exposure.

Digital X-Rays

A picture is worth a thousand words. The more precise the imaging, the better the outcomes. Dr. Kristina has invested in top-of-the-line a 3D panoramic scanner and intraoral camera to allow us to take accurate images of your teeth, gums, and bite.

Emergency Check-Up

We make every possible effort to see people the same day they have a critical issue such as an infection or cracked or broken tooth. Just give us a call.

What qualifies as an emergency?

If your tooth is cracked, broken, or knocked out, or if you have sudden pain, bleeding, or swelling, you should see Dr. Kristina right away.

Cavity Control & Fillings

Dr. Kristina takes the time to find out about each patient’s brushing and flossing habits, providing tips for improving them and touching on things to avoid, like smoking, candy, and sodas. But even with good brushing habits, decay can creep in. If decay is detected, they will do a filling, with a tooth-colored composite, that saves the integrity of the tooth.

Dental Bridge

For both esthetics and function, missing teeth should be replaced. A dental bridge consists of crowns that fit over existing teeth on either side of the missing tooth or teeth. Depending on a patient’s needs, she can perform a traditional bridge that attaches to existing teeth with metal wings or create an implant-supported bridge that’s attached through implants.

Crowns

Dr. Kristina sees her practice as a holistic blend of science, art and precision. For the most precise outcomes, she’s invested in a state-of-the-art CEREC crown mill and oven, which taps into 3D digital technology and CAD. She now has control of every part of the process nothing is ever sent out to a lab. Within a single visit, a new crown is designed, milled from ceramic and finished in her offices. No more goopy impressions, temporary crowns, or having to wait weeks to have a permanent one placed at a second appointment.

Custom Night Guards & Sports Guards

Bruxism, or grinding your teeth in your sleep, can take a toll on damaging teeth. Dr. Kristina recommends if you suffer from this condition to wear a nightly guard. Playing sports can also open you up to injury. There’s a big difference between boil-and-bite mouthguards you’d buy at a sporting goods store and ones that are custom-fit. She will take an impression of your teeth, and then send that to a lab for fabrication.

Botox for Clenching

Another treatment for bruxism, or clenching, is Botox. Dr. Kristina injects into the masseter muscle of the jaw. This reduces nerve signals to lessen the ability to clench.
