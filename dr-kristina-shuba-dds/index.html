OASIS WELLNESS DENTISTRY

Why Oasis Wellness Dentistry: A dedication and passion for connecting with patients for a heightened level of care is what inspired Dr. Kristina Shuba-Amos to become a dentist.

The practice

She understands that most people don’t look forward to dental visits. That’s why she created a relaxing and welcoming environment.

Tapping into natural and organic essential oils and treatments, Dr. Kristina treats each patient as a whole person, not just the sum of teeth and gums. She gets to know her patients and they eventually become like family. Being a mentor to encourage patients to achieve their best health is important to her.

Dr. Kristina believes in investing in the highest level of technology, including a Diode Laser for work with soft tissues, and a 3D panoramic scanner and intraoral camera to provide the most precise images for the best outcomes.

Advanced technology informs every step of preventive care at Oasis Wellness Dentistry. Taking state-of-the-art images, screening for oral cancer, and assessing teeth, gums, and bite are hallmarks of Dr. Kristina’s preventive care. She takes the time to focus on education about proper habits and hygiene.

From tooth extractions to the placement of dental implants, creating crowns, dentures, and bridges, Dr. Kristina can fix most problems with dentition.

Dr. Kristina likes to take the worry out of dentistry, and that includes budgeting, too. For Insurance currently, the practice is credentialed with Dental Advocacy Group. In the near future, also look for them to work with Delta Dental, and Blue Cross Blue Shield, as well as offer a financing program.

That Includes everything from clinical exams and cleanings, digital X-rays, intraorals imaging, and emergency exams if needed.

Dr. Kristina’s goal is to provide holistic and comprehensive dental to all patients, whether or not they have insurance. She founded a Membership Program with low monthly payments.
