Welcome to Kurt D’Amico, DDS. We treat patients of all ages and welcome people from every locale and all walks of life. We take your smile health very seriously, but we’re still a fun and friendly group of dental professionals with a helpful support team to round us out.

In our dental office, you’ll find a funny but professional dentist eager to make you smile and the latest in dental technologies to make your dental experience that much easier.

We are conveniently located in the quaint town of Syracuse, NY on Syracuse Rd. and near the intersection at Woodchuck Hill Road. Nearby parks and recreation include Clark’s Reservation and Drumlins. At our dental office, you’ll find ample parking, too!

Our patients include staff and students within the local school district and Syracuse University—along with teachers, doctors, retirees, lawyers, retirees, homemakers, families, and more!

Our dental team also enjoys participating in the local community, which includes prior or current involvement with the fire department, Boy Scouts of America, and our local library.

We are currently accepting new patients, and we proudly serve the areas of Syracuse, Manlius, DeWitt, Fayetteville, and all surrounding and outlying locations.

If you’re looking for professional dentistry in a safe and clean environment—with a fun and friendly team to serve your dental needs—we would love to put an end to that search!
