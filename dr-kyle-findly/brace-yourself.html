Dental Insurance

We are happy to accept numerous insurance companies including Aetna, Cigna, Humana, MetLife, Principal, and more. We also accept all major credit cards, cash, and checks. Contact our Marshfield office for specific costs, post-insurance, and information on what’s covered under your insurance plan.

External Payment Options

LuxeSmile Dentistry accepts CareCredit®, Lending Club, and Proceed Finance as alternative payment options, so you can get the care you need and create a payment plan that best fits your budget. Each application process is efficient and simple.
