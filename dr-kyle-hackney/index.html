The practice

Dr. Kyle joined Hackney Family Dentistry in 2008. Dr. Kyle sees most of our young patients and takes pride in making dental appointments easy and fun for the many children that we see in our practice. Dr. Kyle is a member of multiple local study clubs and served in 2015 as President of the Thurston-Mason Counties Dental Society. As President he helped form a policy which ensures charitable money…

Dr. Kyle is passionate about being involved in the local Shelton community. He has been an active member in the Shelton Rotary Club since 2009, has served as Club President (2018), and is currently on the Club’s board of directors. Dr. Kyle has also been an active advocate for the Shelton YMCA program and has worked hard to bring money into our community for this important project.

In 2009 Dr. Kyle married his longtime girlfriend Kelly. They met while studying at Willamette and continued to date as she completed her doctorate degree in Pharmacy at the University of Washington. Kelly is currently a Clinical Pharmacist in Oncology at Tacoma General Hospital. Kelly and Kyle now have two energetic boys Cason (6) and Grady (3) and a new little dog, Reggie (1).
