What to Expect

At your first visit expect to spend about an hour with us so that we can get to know you, and so that we can make you a plan that best fits your dental needs. We will take the time to address your dental concerns and explain treatment options, both for emergent dental needs and comprehensive dental care plans. Our thorough approach involves a comprehensive exam, digital x-rays, and an oral cancer screening.

Please bring any dental insurance information to your appointment so that we can best serve you. We will research your plan and ensure that you receive the full benefits your policy offers.

If you need to reschedule an appointment we request 48 hours notice so that we can best serve all our patients.

Technology

Our modern dental office is equipped with top of the line dental instruments and modern technology in order to give you the most modern dental treatment. Our digital x ray system will not only make your visit quicker and easier it will expose you to less radiation and allow us to get the most accurate diagnostic images.

Patient Care

Our staff takes pride in patient comfort. We want to understand your dental needs, explain options to give you choices, and find a plan that works best for you. We have neck pillows and blankets to keep you comfortable during your visit. If you think you’ll need help relaxing during your appointment, we offer several different levels of sedation. In addition to complimentary nitrous oxide, we offer deeper levels of sedation that can make dentistry tolerable for even our most fearful patients. Together, we can find the best level of comfort for you.

If fear or anxiety has kept you from the dentist, please call us today. Come see how easy modern dentistry can be in a friendly, relaxed office with staff and doctors that will care for you with compassion and kindness.
