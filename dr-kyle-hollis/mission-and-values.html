Mission & Values

Our mission is to create a virtuous cycle that improves the lives of our patients, partners and team.

Our mission and values are the F.I.R.E that fuels SGA Dental Partners.

Our goal? To continuously improve the lives of our patients, partners, and team. We believe in the power of a virtuous cycle, where each part of the cycle builds upon the previous component and improves the outcome of the next.

At SGA, we exist to serve our patients and we never take our patients for granted. SGA teams are held to the highest standards to ensure the safety and satisfaction of our patients. Our clinical leadership structure ensures that our patient experience remains top priority.

SGA is the DSO that you would design. Designed by dentists for dentists, we understand what your practice needs to achieve sustainable growth. SGA carefully developed a team of practice support experts that work behind the scenes to enable growth and improve patient satisfaction. We believe in investing in our teams and collaborating with our partners to provide exceptional dental care while growing the SGA community throughout the Southeast.

We work relentlessly to achieve financial freedom for our partners through our unique equity and value creation model. But we don’t stop there! We are determined to improve the lives of our patients, partners, and team, and better the communities we serve. What sets us apart from other DSOs? We maintain a hometown approach to dental care, giving back to our local communities and amplifying the unique local brand and goodwill of each office.

SGA equips our teams with modern technology and continuously educates our team on techniques to ensure we are providing the best possible outcomes. We believe in being patient focused, seeking to improve the patient experience in all aspects, including timeliness and convenience.
