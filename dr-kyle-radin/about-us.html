Meet Dr. Kyle Radin

With a multitude of experience in a variety of disciplines, Dr. Kyle Radin has dedicated his dental practice to providing comprehensive, compassionate, and personalized dental services to each and every patient. Trips to the dentist can be frightening Dr. Kyle knows this and has taken steps to ensure that your appointment is both comfortable and even enjoyable.

Dr. Kyle grew up in Hinckley, Ohio, and graduated from Saint Ignatius High School in 2009. After venturing to Florida to complete his undergraduate studies at the University of Miami and his dental training at Nova Southeastern University, Dr. Kyle was determined to return home and make a difference in the Cleveland community

Doing the Right Thing For You.

Since 2018, Dr. Kyle has been treating patients across northeast Ohio while persistently enhancing his skills and expanding the procedures that he offers for his patients.

Over the past several years, Dr. Kyle has completed courses with Thommen Medical, the Cleveland Implant Institute, and the internationally-known Dental Training Center in Miami, FL, which has allowed him to place several hundred dental implants throughout his career.

Meet Dr. Rachel Catalano

Dr. Rachel Catalano is a passionate general dentist who takes pride in caring for the community she’s always called home the suburbs of Cleveland, Ohio. After earning her undergraduate degree in Biology from Kent State University, she went on to achieve her Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD) at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts.

Known for her warm approach and genuine care, Dr. Catalano is dedicated to building lasting relationships with her patients and helping them achieve not just healthy smiles, but overall wellness. She places a strong emphasis on patient education, empowering individuals with the knowledge and confidence to make informed decisions about their oral health. Always eager to grow her skills, she actively pursues continuing education and advanced training to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Catalano can often be found working on hands-on DIY projects or giving her time to local animal shelters a reflection of her creativity, compassion, and community spirit.
