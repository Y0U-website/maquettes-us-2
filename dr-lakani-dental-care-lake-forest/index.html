The practice

Every detail of our office is designed to create a welcoming experience — where you feel relaxed, informed, and confident about your care.

Our Lake Forest office features digital X-rays, 3D cone-beam CBCT imaging, and CEREC same-day crowns — so Dr. Lakani can plan your treatment with exceptional precision and complete many restorations in a single visit, reducing the time you spend in the chair.

Whether you require single tooth implants, full-mouth reconstruction, porcelain veneers, or routine preventive care, Dr. Lakani provides tailored treatment plans designed to preserve long-term oral health and facial harmony. Sedation options, including nitrous oxide and oral conscious sedation, mean even the most anxious patients feel completely at ease.

Appointments are never overbooked: Dr. Lakani takes the time to walk you through every option in plain language, preview your expected result using digital smile simulation, and answer every question before treatment begins. Our Orange County patients consistently describe it as the first dental experience that actually felt calm and transparent.
