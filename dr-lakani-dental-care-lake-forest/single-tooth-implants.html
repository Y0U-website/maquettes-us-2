What happens to your jaw when a tooth goes unreplaced

  • Up to 25 % of bone volume at the extraction site can be lost within the first year
  • Adjacent teeth drift/tilt; opposing tooth may super-erupt
  • Preserves alveolar bone, prevents tipping, restores occlusal function, maintains facial profile

The single implant process

CBCT Consultation & Digital Planning

3D scan reviewed directly; Simplant/Nobelvision planning and surgical guide design.

Implant Placement

Minimally invasive placement, usually under an hour under local anesthesia; IV sedation available.

Osseointegration (6–12 weeks)

Implant integrates with bone; periodic check-ins.

iTero Scanning & Final Crown

Digital impression; zirconia or PFM crown fabricated and seated.

Are you a candidate?

Cost depends on the implant system used, whether a bone graft is needed first, and the final crown material chosen. Dental insurance often contributes toward diagnostic imaging and a portion of the crown, though coverage for the implant fixture itself varies by plan — we verify your benefits and provide an itemized estimate at your consultation.
