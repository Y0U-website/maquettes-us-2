In House Dental Plan

Affordable Dental Care Without the Insurance Hassles

Grandville Dental’s In-House Plan: The care you know and trust. At a price you’ll love.

Tired of Dental Insurance Limitations?

If You’re Uninsured:

You’re not alone. Millions of Americans go without dental insurance, often delaying essential care due to cost concerns. At Grandville Dental, we believe everyone deserves quality oral health care, regardless of insurance status.

If Your Insurance Falls Short:

High deductibles, annual maximums, waiting periods, and missing tooth clauses can make dental insurance more frustrating than helpful. Our In-House Plan eliminates these barriers entirely.

Our Solution: Grandville Dental’s In-House Plan

At Grandville Dental Health Center, our priority has always been to make high-quality dental care as affordable as possible. Our In-House Dental Plan directly addresses financial barriers, ensuring that more patients can access the care they need without the burden of high costs.

✓ No Annual Deductible

Start saving from day one. No waiting to meet a deductible before your benefits kick in.

✓ No Annual Maximums

Get the care you need all year long without hitting a coverage ceiling.

✓ No Prior Authorizations

No delays or denials. If Dr. Bishop or Dr. Vander Baan recommends it, you can get it.

✓ No Waiting Periods

Need treatment now? Get started immediately with no waiting periods for coverage.

✓ No Missing Tooth Clause

We’ll help restore your smile regardless of when you lost a tooth.

✓ No Claim Forms or Insurance Cards

Simple, straightforward care without the paperwork hassles.
