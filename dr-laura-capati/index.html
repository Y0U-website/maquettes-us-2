The practice

At Pristine Periodontics & Implants, you re never just a patient; you re part of our extended family. We celebrate your successes, support you through challenges, and remain committed to helping you achieve and maintain optimal oral health for life. Learn more about our specialist and team members here.

Dr. Laura Capati grew up in Omaha, Nebraska, and earned her bachelor s degree at Washington University in St. Louis. She went on to graduate from Southern Illinois University with a Doctorate in Dental Medicine, then completed her three-year periodontic residency at St. Louis University.

Dr. Capati is a Diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology, a distinguished credential that signifies she has achieved significant accomplishments beyond the mandatory educational requirements of the specialty. This board certification demonstrates her commitment to the highest standards of periodontal care and expertise.

Beyond her professional achievements, Dr. Capati is deeply committed to her family and community. She and her husband, Bill, are the proud parents of four young children: Charlotte, Cecilia, Caroline, and William Jr. The Capati family resides in St. Louis, where Dr. Capati enjoys balancing her passion for periodontics with quality time spent with her loved ones.

Samantha serves as the office manager at Pristine Periodontics and Implants, where she has been an integral part of Dr. Capati s team since 2019. With experience in dentistry dating back to 2016, Samantha brings expertise and dedication to ensuring every patient receives exceptional care in a smoothly operating practice.

Samantha received her education and training at STLCC. She was drawn to the dental field because it uniquely blends healthcare with organization, communication, and patient interaction. Working in a structured environment where she can help coordinate care, support patients, and keep the office running smoothly is what she finds most fulfilling about her career.

"Working in dentistry requires close teamwork between hygienists, dentists, assistants, and front-desk staff," Samantha shares. "I love the synergy when we re all aligned on patient care goals. Each day brings a mix of tasks, and collaborating with people who care about quality makes it a really energizing environment."

When she s not at work, Samantha enjoys traveling, kayaking, and lounging on the beach. She values spending quality time with friends and family, embracing opportunities to relax and explore new places.
