Who Makes A Good Candidate?

Most adults who are healthy enough for a routine dental procedure can consider implants. We assess bone volume, gum health, and medical history. People who have worn removable dentures often report a major quality-of-life improvement after switching to implant-supported teeth.

We don’t pressure you. We explain options and recommend the path that fits your goals.

  • A missing single tooth or several missing teeth
  • Difficulty wearing dentures
  • Healthy gum tissue or treatable gum disease
  • Desire for a long-term, fixed solution

Recovery And Caring For Implants

After implant placement, you’ll rest and follow simple home care. You may feel mild soreness; over-the-counter pain relievers and cold packs help. Keep the surgical site clean and follow the short list of activity restrictions we provide.

We’ll schedule follow-ups to check healing. Once your restoration is in place, treat the implant like a natural tooth — regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups protect your investment.

  • Gentle brushing and a soft toothbrush around the site
  • Short, warm salt rinses after 24 hours
  • Avoid chewing hard foods on the new implant during healing

Benefits Compared To Other Options

Many patients prefer implants because they preserve nearby teeth and jawbone. Implants stabilize chewing, improve speech, and feel more natural than removable options. They also make daily life easier; you don’t need adhesives or removal routines.

At High Mesa Dental Arts, we use digital impressions and CEREC to craft lifelike crowns. You’ll leave with a plan tailored to your mouth, not a template.

  • Restores chewing and speech function
  • Protects jawbone from shrinking
  • Feels and looks like a natural tooth
