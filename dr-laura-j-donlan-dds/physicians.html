Laura J. Donlan, DDS

Wow time flies!! This is my 25th year in dentistry! What a privilege and blessing it is to help provide the Peoria area children with dental care each and every day.

Those that know me, know that my proudest accomplishments are my two daughters, Kate and Allie. The girls attended Peoria Notre Dame High School and are now attending Bradley University and University of Florida. Kate is a cheerleader at Bradley while Allie is active in her sorority. They both would like to go to dental school and join me here in practice!! You can find them at the office on their summer breaks helping out and assisting me.

I have served on the board of the Peoria District Dental Society, was named one of Peoria s "40 Leaders Under 40" and have been recognized as one of WEEK s 25 Women in Leadership. I am a sustaining member of the Peoria Area Junior League and as an office we enjoy volunteering and being involved in community efforts! I graduated from Bradley University in 1990 and went on to receive my Doctorate degree in General Dentistry from the University of Illinois College of Dentistry in 1994.

I enjoy girl s nights with my friends and working out to keep up with the kids!

I love children s dentistry and the opportunity it has given me to become a part of your family s lives over the years. Watching "our" children grow and prosper has been one of the greatest joys of my life! I want to say "thank you" for the continued confidence you have in me and my staff. I look forward to many more years of great dental health!

Kate (Donlan) Shadid, DMD

Dr. Kate is a graduate of Peoria Notre Dame High School and earned her Bachelor of Science in Biology from Bradley University. She went on to earn her Doctorate degree (DMD) from the University of Illinois Chicago College of Dentistry. Dr. Kate is a member of the American Dental Association, the Illinois State Dental Society and the Peoria District Dental Society.

As a Peoria native, Dr. Kate is excited to be practicing in her hometown; providing expert level care to this wonderful community she s grown up with. Proudly following in her family s footsteps as a third-generation dentist, Dr. Kate looks forward to continuing a family legacy of dedicated care alongside her mom, Dr. Laurie, and sister, Dr. Allie, here at Kids First Dental Group.

In her spare time, Dr. Kate enjoys reading, spending time with her husband, Luke, and new baby girl, Eve. The family likes going on walks with their dog, Jerry.

Allie Donlan, DMD

Dr. Allie is a graduate of Peoria Notre Dame High School and earned her Bachelor of Science in Applied Physiology and Kinesiology at the University of Florida. She went on to earn her Doctorate degree (DMD) from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine, where she received the Dean s recognition award for being class valedictorian. Dr. Allie is a member of the American Dental Association, the Illinois State Dental Society and the Peoria District Dental Society.

Growing up in Peoria, Dr. Allie is thrilled to begin her career here; providing the highest quality dental care to this community that feels like family. Joining her mom, Dr. Laurie, and sister, Dr. Kate, here in practice at Kids First Dental Group enables the tradition of excellent dental care to continue. Dr. Allie is no stranger to our practice, as she s worked with us during past summer vacations. You may have already seen or met her!

In her spare time, Dr. Allie likes swimming, reading and spending time with family and friends. She recently moved into a new home right here in the Peoria area, keeping her connection to this community close.
