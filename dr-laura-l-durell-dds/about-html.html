Durell & Durell Family Dentistry is a family practice with an upbeat, friendly environment. Our dental team is knowledgeable and highly trained, and our dentists and staff are gentle and can put any patient at ease.

Meet the Doctors

Dr. David Durell

Born and raised in Plainfield, Indiana, Dr. David Durell has been deeply rooted in the community throughout his life. He graduated from Plainfield High School in 1983 before pursuing his passion for science at Purdue University, where he earned a degree in Biology in 1987. His commitment to dentistry led him to the Indiana University School of Dentistry, from which he graduated in 1991 and earned his Doctorate in Dental Surgery.

Dr. Durell began his professional career in 1991, joining the practice of Dr Leon Turner. A year later, Dr. Laura Hicks Joined the practice, and in 1993, following Dr. Turner’s retirement, Dr. David and Dr. Laura got married and Durell & Durell Family Dentistry was born. The practice has been a cornerstone of the community, providing quality dental care in the beautiful building they constructed in 1998.

Outside of his professional life, Dr. David is a passionate aviator. With a pilot’s license in hand, he has flown planes and helicopters, and he is currently enjoying the thrill of paramotoring. When he’s not in the sky, Dr. David can often be found rollerblading or klteboarding, hobbies that reflect his love for adventure and the outdoors.

Dr. David and his wife, are proud parents of three children: Julia, Paul, and Claire. For Dr. David, family values and strong relationships are at the heart of everything he does, both in his personal life and in his professional life. The warmth and care that define his family life are the same principles that guide his approach to treating patients.

Dr. Laura Durell

Dr Laura Durell was the first female dentist in Plainfield, she brings a pioneering spirit and exceptional expertise to the practice. She Is originally from Marlette, Michigan and moved to Indiana to attend college at Indiana University for her undergraduate and graduate studies and received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 1992.

On a personal note, the Doctors have raised three wonderful children, Julia, Paul and Claire along with dogs and cats to round out the family. She enioys biking, hiking, walking, bird watching, travel and is an avid sports enthusiast especially baseball!

Both Dr David and Dr Laura are passionate about providing outstanding care emphasizing a patient-centered approach that prioritizes kindness, friendliness and personalized treatment plans. Together they strive to deliver the highest quality dental care at reasonable prices, ensuring a comfortable and welcoming environment for all their patients.
