Life Beyond Dentistry

Outside the office, Dr. Harkin enjoys a full and active life with her husband, Sean, and their children, Denis and Lianne. Together, they enjoy family life in Lancaster and staying connected to the community they call home.

A lifelong learner by nature, Dr. Harkin is passionate about continually expanding her knowledge both within dentistry and beyond. Whether she is pursuing advanced continuing education, exploring a new book, or developing a new skill, she believes there is always something valuable to learn.

To stay active, she enjoys playing tennis, hiking and skiing, and her appreciation for the arts continues through her talents as both a flutist and pianist. When she finds a quiet moment, you’ll often find her with a good book in hand, enjoying the opportunity to learn, reflect, and recharge.
