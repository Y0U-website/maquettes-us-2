Our Dental Services

At Laura S. Harkin, DMD, we believe exceptional dental care starts with understanding each patient’s unique needs and goals. Whether you’re visiting for a routine cleaning, exploring cosmetic improvements, or seeking advanced restorative treatment, our team is committed to providing personalized care in a comfortable, welcoming environment.

We combine modern technology, clinical expertise, and a patient-first approach to help individuals and families maintain healthy, confident smiles for life. Explore our services below to learn how we can help you achieve your oral health goals.

Restorative Dentistry

Gold restorations remain one of the most durable, biocompatible, and long-lasting options available in modern dentistry. As one of Dr. Harkin’s areas of special interest, she offers expertly crafted gold restorations for patients seeking exceptional strength, longevity, and precision fit. Gold restorations are particularly well-suited for certain back teeth, where durability and function are critical, and they continue to be regarded by many dentists as the gold standard for long-term performance.

In addition, Dr. Harkin offers advanced dental implant reconstruction and restoration services, helping patients repair, replace, or restore implant-supported crowns, bridges, and other implant restorations.

Preventative Dentistry

The best dental treatment is prevention. Regular cleanings, exams, screenings, and patient education help us identify potential concerns early and keep your smile healthy for years to come.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance the appearance of your smile with personalized cosmetic treatments. We offer solutions designed to improve the color, shape, and overall appearance of your teeth while maintaining a natural look.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Straighten your teeth discreetly with Invisalign clear aligners. This comfortable, virtually invisible orthodontic option helps patients achieve a beautifully aligned smile without traditional braces.

TMJ Splint Therapy

Custom oral splints can help relieve jaw pain, teeth grinding, clenching, and TMJ-related discomfort. Splint therapy is designed to improve function, protect your teeth, and promote long-term comfort.

We Specialize in

Full Mouth Reconstruction

When extensive dental treatment is needed, Dr. Harkin develops a personalized treatment plan to restore function, comfort, and aesthetics. Full mouth reconstruction can address worn teeth, bite issues, missing teeth, failing restorations, and extensive dental damage.

Family & General Dentistry

Comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages. From routine exams and cleanings to fillings and everyday dental needs, we’re here to help your entire family maintain healthy smiles.

iTero Digital Scanning

We use the iTero digital scanner in various aspects of dental care. This technology allows us to create highly accurate digital impressions without messy traditional molds and improves comfort, precision, and treatment planning.
