Dr. Laura VanDyk

Dr. Laura VanDyk is a Sumner native, originally raised on Lake Tapps. Dr. VanDyk received her Bachelor of Science in Biology with a Minor in Chemistry from the University of Washington in 2004. She then attended the University of Washington School of Dentistry, where she received a Doctorate in Dental Surgery in 2008. Dr. VanDyk began her dental career in Sumner as an associate to Dr. Rolf Edlund and is proud to carry on in his footsteps.

Dr. VanDyk and her husband, Corty, also a Sumner High School graduate, feel blessed to be able to call Sumner their home again. The VanDyk family has expanded over the past years with the addition of two wonderful children, Corty Isaac and Reace Angle, as well as Lucy and Roxy, their Yellow Labs. The family enjoys many activities including snow skiing, camping, boating, hiking and golfing.

Dr. VanDyk places a high value on providing excellent, quality dentistry to all of her patients. She is constantly expanding her dental knowledge and skills through dental continuing education opportunities. Dr. VanDyk is excited about her career in the dental profession and the opportunity to live and work in the Sumner and Lake Tapps area.

Understanding

We feel that real understanding starts with careful listening. We take the time to listen to you and understand your needs, wants and fears. We then educate you in a manner that empowers you to make confident decisions regarding your oral health. This level of mutual understanding helps you to feel confident, relaxed and in control.

Gentle Dental Treatments

Relationships

Dr. VanDyk grew up in Sumner and loves being part of this community. We are interested in building generations of relationships with you and your family. This is why we take the time to get to know you and work every day to create an atmosphere that feels like home.

High Quality Dental Care

We take pride in the quality of our work and you will see and feel the difference it makes. Dr. VanDyk is always improving her education and stays up to date with the latest techniques and treatments.

Convenience

Because of her education and excellent clinical skills, Dr. VanDyk is able to perform a wide range of dental services. These include: fillings, crowns, bridges, implants, cosmetic dentistry, bleaching, root canals, extractions, periodontal care and children’s dentistry. This saves you time as you can get all your dental needs fulfilled in one place. Additionally, we offer CareCredit to ensure you can afford the dentistry you and your family need.

If you value these qualities in a dentist, call us today! We’re passionate about your smile and passionate about helping you achieve optimal health!
