36 years of exceptional specialty care.

For over 36 years, Galitsis & Bovino Dental Specialists has been proudly serving the Westwood, NJ community with exceptional specialty dental care — built on clinical excellence, compassion, and a commitment to smiles that last a lifetime.

Specialists you can trust.

Dr. Paul Galitsis

Dr. Paul Galitsis brings over 36 years of experience to every patient he treats. As a prosthodontist, he specializes in the restoration and replacement of teeth — helping patients regain confidence in their smiles through crowns, bridges, implant restorations, and full smile reconstructions.

D.D.S. — Fairleigh Dickinson University Postgraduate Prosthodontics — Columbia University Implant Crowns & Restorations Porcelain Crowns & Bridges Full Mouth Reconstruction Veneers & Smile Makeovers Full & Partial Dentures Orthodontist

Dr. Laurie Bovino

Dr. Laurie Bovino is a dedicated orthodontic specialist with over 36 years of experience creating beautiful, healthy smiles for patients of all ages. She is passionate about helping patients achieve straighter, healthier smiles through personalized orthodontic treatment — from traditional braces to clear aligners.
