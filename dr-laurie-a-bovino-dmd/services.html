In-Office Whitening

Experience a visibly brighter smile in a single visit. Our in-office treatment takes approximately one hour, using a professional-grade whitening gel to lift years of staining quickly and comfortably. You&#x27;ll leave our office with a noticeably luminous smile — the same day.

Custom Take-Home Kits

For those who prefer the flexibility of whitening on their own schedule, we offer custom-fitted whitening trays paired with professional-grade gel. These deliver superior results compared to anything available over the counter, with trays designed specifically for your bite.

How to Maintain Your Bright Smile

After your whitening treatment, our team will guide you on how to protect and prolong your results.

  • Limit coffee, red wine, tea, and dark berries — especially in the first 48 hours
  • Avoid tobacco, which dulls whitening results over time
  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush and floss each evening
  • Stay hydrated and maintain a balanced diet to support gum health

How Often Should You Whiten?

In-office whitening results typically last up to a year with proper care. Most patients maintain their brightness with one to two professional visits per year.

Custom take-home kits can be used every four to six months as a convenient way to touch up between visits. Regular professional cleanings every six months are also key — they remove surface buildup that dulls your smile and allow our team to monitor your results over time.
