Meet the Staff

Janis has been practicing dental hygiene for over 44 years. She earned her Associate of Science degree from Wharton County Junior College in 1982. Janis has been the recipient of the Fayette County Reader’s Choice Award for Best Dental Hygienist for 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, and 2023. Janis and her husband Chris of 46 years have lived in La Grange since 1980. Outside the office, Janis enjoys spending time with her family and spoiling her 7 grandchildren.

“In my practice as a dental hygienist, I affirm my personal and professional commitment to improve the oral health of the public.”

“I enjoy working with this amazing team to help better each and every patient’s oral health and make a difference.”

Jan earned her Associates of Applied Science Degree in 2016 from Blinn’s Dental Hygiene Program in Bryan, Texas. She has been practicing Dental Hygiene ever since. She spent two years of her hygiene career working in a periodontal practice where her main focus was treating and maintaining periodontal/gum disease. Her recent years have been spent practicing in General Dentistry, and she joined Dr. Hatfield’s team in 2022.

Prior to working as a Dental Hygienist, Jan spent 15 years working as a Dental Assistant before deciding to return to school to further her education and become a Dental Hygienist

“Dentistry is my life’s passion. I truly enjoy working with patients to help them obtain optimal dental health!"

She does a fantastic job of making every patient feel comfortable in the dental chair. She currently resides in La Grange, TX and loves to be with her family. She is a mom to 3 beautiful children all of whom are married and a Nana to 6 grandchildren.

“Goal in life is to be as good as you can at what you do best. Providing quality care to all of patients is what we do best. Love Life.”

Outside the office Carolyn Loves to: Travel

“Making patients happy with their time spent in our office is important. I am happy to be apart of a team that can provide to our patients every day.“

Outside the office Donna Loves to: Enjoy baseball and family time.
