Dr. Schmitt has practiced dentistry since July 2017. He worked as an associate dentist for six years in Bemidji, MN in both private practice and public health settings before deciding to settle in Grand Rapids with a practice to call his own. He acquired Dr. Lee Jess’s practice in September 2023 and founded Pokegama Dental.

Your smile needs regular care. Visit Dr. Schmitt for all your general and family dental needs.

Our team is committed to providing you and your family with the quality dental care you need to maintain a healthy smile. We offer a wide range of services to meet your needs so that you can enjoy all the benefits of good oral health.

General and family dentistry encompasses a wide range of treatments, including preventive care as well as more complex restorative procedures. Whether you are bringing your child to our office for a routine dental checkup or are seeking treatment for a dental problem you are experiencing, we are here to help you.

We recommend that you visit our office at least twice each year. These regular visits for preventive care allow us to provide you with treatments that will help you avoid developing cavities or other problems and monitor your oral health so that any problems that do occur can be treated quickly.
