Financial Options

We are currently an in-network provider with Delta Dental Premier.

We are happy to work with most other dental insurance plans as an out-of-network provider. As a courtesy to our patients, our office will submit insurance claims on your behalf for out-of-network plans.

At this time, we are not accepting new patients with plans under the Medical Assistance umbrella (including IMCare) as primary or secondary insurance.

If you have questions about your specific coverage or benefits, our team will be glad to assist you.

We accept the following forms of payment: cash, check, credit cards, HSA/FSA, CareCredit.
