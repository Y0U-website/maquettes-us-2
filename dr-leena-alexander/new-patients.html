New Patients : Starshine Dentistry

Your first visit to Starshine Dentistry establishes a vital foundation for our relationship with you and being well-prepared ensures we have all the information we need to provide the best possible care for you. During the initial consultation, we will obtain all necessary background information such as your medical history, work with you so that we are educated on any symptoms or anxiety you are experiencing and give you time to get to know Dr. Leena Alexander.

Dr. Alexander is hands-on in everything with our practice, whether it be scheduling appointments, returning phone calls, and making post-op calls to ensure everything is going smoothly and that you aren’t experiencing any complications.

We are eternally grateful that you have become a part of our family practice. If you haven’t had an opportunity yet, please print and complete the New Patient Forms above to expedite your first visit.

  • Providing a warm, caring, professional and stress-free environment
  • Never overselling our services. We make suggestions based on what YOU need
  • Streamlining services to limit the need for additional appointments, referrals to other clinics
  • Flexible appointment times
  • Maintaining your oral hygiene in a way that keeps your teeth and mouth healthy for a lifetime

Come see us and let us take care of your smile!
