We will spend time with you, listen to your needs, and only recommend services that are in your best interest.

E veryone needs a good dentist, but going can feel like pulling teeth when you’re continually being referred out to other facilities for treatment. Trust us; it happens more often than you think, and having to schedule so many appointments can be taxing on a family in today’s busy world of work, school, church, activities, and other commitments. We love treating families in the Corinth, Lake Cities, Denton, Bartonville, Highland Village, Argyle, and Flower Mound areas.

Wouldn’t it be nice to work with a dentist office that offers comprehensive care and is also highly skilled?

Dr. Leena Alexander D.D.S. averages more than 120 credit hours per year in continuing education so that she can be hands-on in a litany of traditional and advanced dental services. She has also built ongoing partnerships with leading oral surgeons, TMJ specialists, periodontists, and endodontists – all of whom regularly practice at Starshine to make the most of your family’s trip to the dentist. While some cases may necessitate the need to refer out services, the focus at Starshine will always be on streamlining the process.
