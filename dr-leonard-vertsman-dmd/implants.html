For patients seeking a permanent, slip-resistant solution.

What to expect

  • Tooth Extraction If the damaged tooth is still present, it will be gently removed. You’ll be fully numbed—no pain, just light pressure. Healing starts right away.
  • Implant Placement A small titanium implant is placed precisely into the jawbone. This acts as a new root. You’ll be numb throughout, and healing takes about 3–4 months while the bone fuses to the implant. We’ll check in monthly with a quick X-ray.
  • Healing Cap Placement Once the implant is stable, we’ll place a small healing cap to shape the gum and prep for your new tooth. This step may happen during implant placement or at a brief follow-up visit.
  • Crown Placement You’ll come in for two final visits: first for impressions, then to place your custom-made crown. It’s color-matched and shaped to look and feel like a natural tooth—so you can smile, chew, and speak with ease.

You’ve got questions, we’ve got answers

  • Are dental implants painful Most patients say the procedure is easier than expected. You’ll be numbed during surgery, and any post-procedure soreness is usually mild and manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.
  • How long do dental implants last? With good hygiene and regular dental checkups, implants can last 20 years or more—many even last a lifetime.
  • Do implant dentures feel different than regular ones? Yes—in a good way. They’re more secure, comfortable, and natural-feeling, especially when eating or speaking.
  • how many implants do I need for a full denture? It depends on your jaw and needs, but most full-arch dentures are supported by 4 to 6 implants.
  • Is bone grafting safe, and where does the bone come from? Yes, it’s a safe and common procedure. Grafts can come from your own body, a donor source, or a synthetic material—all biocompatible and carefully selected.
  • How long is the full treatment process- from bone grafting to final implant If bone grafting is needed, healing can take 3–6 months. After that, the implant is placed, followed by several more months of healing before the final restoration. We’ll guide you through each phase with care.
  • Will I be left without teeth during my implant process? No—temporary teeth or dentures are typically provided so you never go without a smile while your implant heals.
