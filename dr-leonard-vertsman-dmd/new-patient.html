You’ve got questions, we’ve got answers

  • Do I need to bring anything for my visit? Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card (if you have one), and a list of any medications you take. If you’ve had recent dental X-rays, bring those along too.
  • How early should I arrive for my appointment We recommend arriving 15-30 minutes early to allow time for check-in, completing any necessary forms (if you haven’t done so already), and settling in.
  • What can I expect for my first appointment/ Our friendly front desk team will welcome you, complete any needed forms, and have a thorough exam with one of our dentists. We’ll review your dental and medical history, take any necessary X-rays, and discuss your goals before creating a personalized treatment plan.
  • Will you review my dental medical history? Yes, understanding your full health picture helps us provide the safest, most effective care for you.
  • How do you handle treatment planning for new patients? After your exam, we’ll go over any findings with you, explain your options, and work with you to choose the plan that best fits your needs, goals, and budget.
  • How long will my first visit take? Most first appointments take about 45–60 minutes, depending on whether you’re getting a cleaning the same day.
  • What if I don’t have insurance? We’ll review all your options, including payment plans, so you can still get the care you need without surprises.
  • How often should I schedule my check-ups after my first visit? Most patients benefit from a check-up and cleaning every six months, but we’ll recommend a schedule that’s right for you.
  • What if I have dental anxiety? Let us know! We have experience working with anxious patients and can take extra steps to make your visit more comfortable.
  • Do you offer same-day treatment for urgent needs? If we discover an urgent issue during your exam, we’ll do our best to treat it the same day whenever possible.
