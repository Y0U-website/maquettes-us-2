What to expect

  • Warm Welcome From the moment you arrive, you’ll be greeted by our friendly front desk team and offered help with anything from forms to insurance questions.
  • Oral Health Review Your hygienist will review your medical history, ask about any changes or concerns, and perform a quick check of your teeth and gums.
  • Plaque & Tartar Removal Using gentle tools or an ultrasonic scaler, we’ll remove buildup from your teeth—especially in hard-to-reach spots.
  • Polishing & Flossing You’ll come in for two final visits: first for impressions, then to place your custom-made crown. It’s color-matched and shaped to look and feel like a natural tooth—so you can smile, chew, and speak with ease.
  • Personalized Tips & Follow-Up Get tailored advice on brushing, flossing, or gum care, plus schedule your next visit. We’ll make sure you leave confident and cavity-free.

You’ve got questions, we’ve got answers

  • How often should I get a dental cleaning? We recommend a professional cleaning every 6 months to remove plaque and tartar buildup—even if you brush and floss regularly.
  • Why are digital x-ray’s important? Are they safe? Digital X-rays let us spot issues early with minimal radiation exposure (up to 90 % less than traditional film). They’re safe, fast, and give clearer results.
  • What happens during a dental exam? Your dentist will check your teeth, gums, bite, and jaw for any signs of decay, gum disease, or other concerns. It’s also a chance to ask questions and plan next steps.
  • Do I still need checkup’s if i’m not having nay pain? Yes—many dental issues, like cavities or gum disease, develop silently. Regular exams help us catch small problems before they turn serious.
  • What is gum health check, and why does it mattter? We’ll measure the space between your teeth and gums to look for signs of gum disease. Healthy gums are essential for long-term oral and overall health.
  • What is an oral cancer screening? It’s a quick, non-invasive exam during your checkup where we examine your mouth, tongue, and throat for signs of abnormal tissue. Early detection saves lives.
