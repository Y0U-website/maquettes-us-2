Advanced Periodontal Care for Healthier Smiles

Maintaining healthy gums is crucial for your overall dental health, and at Wilson Family Dental, we provide comprehensive periodontal and gum care services to patients throughout Roseburg, OR. Gum disease affects millions of Americans, often progressing silently until symptoms become severe. Our team combines practical experience with modern technology to detect early signs of periodontal issues before they worsen.

Using our digital imaging systems, including digital Nomad X-rays and advanced CBCT scans, we can clearly see what’s happening beneath the gumline. This allows us to create personalized treatment plans that address your specific needs. We recognize that gum treatments may cause anxiety for some patients, which is why we’ve created a welcoming environment where friendly faces greet you and compassionate care guides your visit.

With over 20 years of periodontal care experience, Dr. Wilson and our team have successfully treated many cases of gum disease, from mild gingivitis to advanced periodontitis. Our practice has served the Roseburg community since 2015, building a reputation for reliable periodontal care that treats active gum disease and prevents future problems. We make treatments accessible by accepting most insurance plans and offering financing options through CareCredit for those needing payment flexibility.

For patients without insurance, we provide a cash or check payment discount, helping you maintain good gum health without financial worry. By using the latest technologies, you benefit from more comfortable treatments and better results. We address the symptoms and underlying causes of gum disease, helping you develop better oral hygiene practices that protect your gums long-term.

Your comfort and health remain our priorities, and we always provide careful attention to your individual needs. If you’re experiencing bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, or simply need a checkup to ensure your gums are healthy, please contact us today.
