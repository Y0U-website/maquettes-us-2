About the Doctors

Linda N. Chen, D.D.S.

Dr. Linda N. Chen earned her D.D.S. degree in 2000 at University of the Pacific, School of Dentistry in San Francisco. She did her undergraduate work at University of California at Davis, receiving a Bachelor of Science degree in Genetics/Molecular Cell Biology.

Dr. Chen is very passionate about dentistry and the care she provides to her patients of all ages. She strives to take the utmost care of all her patients, ensuring that each patient’s needs and concerns are addressed and met. She takes time to listen and develop personalized care so that each patient leaves with a positive experience.

Dr. Chen specializes in providing gentle dental care, especially for patients who are not used to going to the dentist on a regular basis.

We know that some patients do not exactly enjoy going to the dentist. So here at Dublin Family Dentistry, we make every effort to make sure our patients are comfortable and receiving only the best care. You no longer have to be scared of going to the dentist. We want to help you keep that great smile of yours for a long time to come!

Originally from New York City, Dr. Chen has called the Bay Area home for more than 40 years. When Dr. Chen is not in the office, she and her husband, Johnny, adore and enjoy spending time with their daughter and son.

Dr. Chen is grateful to all the patients she has had the privileged opportunity to meet and provide care for throughout her career. She looks forward to an occasion to meet with you, to go over your dental needs, and to develop a lasting relationship with you. Welcome!

Pamela Wong, D.D.S.

Dr. Pamela Wong was born in Hong Kong and raised in the Bay Area. She attended University of California, Berkeley for her undergraduate studies with a double degree in Neurobiology and Economics. She then attended University of the Pacific, School of Dentistry in San Francisco where she received her D.D.S. degree in 2000.

Being skilled in all aspects of general and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Wong truly enjoys treating patients of all ages. She believes in patient-oriented approaches by listening to and understanding the patients’ needs and concerns. She frequently attends continuing education courses to keep up-to-date with current dental techniques and advancing her knowledge in different facets of dentistry.

Dr. Wong is an active member of the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association, and the Southern Alameda County Dental Society. She was an appointed clinical instructor by University of California, San Francisco School of Dentistry, A.T. Still University and Lutheran Medical Center Department of Dental Medicine. She enjoys her role being a mentor to fellow dental students and residents.

Dr. Wong loves great outdoors and traveling around the globe. Since her graduation from dental school she has been to eighteen different countries. Her favorite experiences include skydiving in Australia as well as white-water rafting under thunderstorm in Costa Rica. She and her husband Eric have travelled to Brazil and Argentina earlier this year. She hopes to fulfill her passions with dentistry and travelling by volunteering through Global Humanitarian Expedition which provides dental care to underpriviledged childrens oversea.

Dr. Wong and Eric currently reside in San Ramon with their daughters Allison, and Alexis. She is very appreciative of all the wonderful patients and families she has been caring for the past 12 years at Dublin Family Dentistry. She looks foward to providing care to and building a long-lasting relationship with all of you.

Leyla Tabesh, D.M.D.

Dr. Leyla Tabesh grew up in Plymouth, Minnesota and graduated from Wayzata High School. After graduating with honors from St. Olaf College, she attended Tufts University School of Dentistry in Boston. She earned her DMD, Summa Cum Laude, in 2008. Before relocating to the Bay Area, Dr. Tabesh practiced dentistry in a family dental office in Plymouth that shared the same philosophy as Dublin Family Dentistry.

Dr. Tabesh enjoys all aspects of general dentistry and is a strong advocate of continuing education to ensure optimal patient care. She believes in high quality dentistry and always spends the time to communicate with her patients while delivering dentistry at its highest level. Dr. Tabesh looks forward to meeting all the patients at Dublin Family Dentistry and making them feel a part of the family.

Leyla lives in Fremont with her husband and two children. In her spare time, you will find her spending time with her two sons. She enjoys outdoor activities such as hiking and camping trips. She also spends a lot of time in the kitchen, as she loves to cook cuisines from all around the globe.

Dr. Tabesh is a member of the American Dental Association and the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Honor Society.
