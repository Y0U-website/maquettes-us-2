Greenville Rancheria provides many services to the communities of Greenville and Red Bluff. All members of the community, Indian and non-Indian, are welcome.

The Greenville Rancheria is currently located in the Indian Valley at the 3,500 foot level in the Sierra Nevada Mountains of Northern California. The Rancheria lies about three miles east of Greenville, California, which has a population of 2000.
