Dr. Ringler is a board certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon. He is a graduate of Columbia University, New York, NY.

During his residency, Dr. Ringler has completed a Medical Doctor degree at Columbia University School of Medicine. He was trained in general surgery and general anesthesia at New York Presbyterian Medical Center, as well as extensive training in dentoalveolar surgery, dental implants, orthognathic surgery, maxillofacial trauma surgery, facial cosmetic surgery, and benign head and neck pathology.

Dr. Ringler has graduated with high honors, at the top of his class, from Boston University School of Dental Medicine, Boston MA. His previous dental education was completed as well, with high honors, in Tel Aviv University, Israel. He was bestowed numerous awards and induction into prestigious societies such as the Omicron Kappa Upsilon national dental honor society.

Dr. Ringler is an Assistant Clinical Professor at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine where he participate in training the next generation of oral and maxillofacial surgeons. He maintains hospital admitting privileges at Columbia – Presbyterian Medical Center, NY. He is also an attending physician at Holy Name Medical Center in Teaneck, NJ.

As a skilled surgeon, he volunteers annually in medical missions to treat children and adults with cleft lip and palate to regions where access to quality health care is limited.
