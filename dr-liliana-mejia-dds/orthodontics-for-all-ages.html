The American Association of Orthodontics recommends an initial checkup by age 7. At this age, permanent teeth are beginning to erupt. Some children have specific orthodontic issues that can be more easily corrected with early intervention. Evaluations are always complimentary for patients of any age.

Traditional Metal Braces can include colorful bands, if desired, to give a fun way to express yourself during alignment.

Ceramic Braces may be chosen as a more discreet orthodontic option.

Clear Including Invisalign is a popular choice in orthodontic treatment. Aligners are made with medical-grade plastic and fit snugly over the teeth.

Retainers are an important part of orthodontic treatment. To maintain your beautiful smile retainers must be worn and maintained.

Our office uses the latest technology that includes digital scanning that mostly eliminates the use of messy impression material making for a more pleasant appointment experience.
