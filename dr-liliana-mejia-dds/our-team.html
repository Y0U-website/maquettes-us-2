0 Our Team Our team consists of highly motivated and well-trained professionals with over 100 years combined experience in the dental and orthodontic field. Our team is well trained with expanded duties and certifications in CPR and OSHA.

“We are dedicated to innovation, integrity and excellence in everything we do. Our mission is to nurture the spirit of every individual we treat and to provide a warm, friendly and fun environment. We strive to work as a team to create beautiful smiles and lasting relationships”

Lacy - Financial Coordinator

Lacy graduated from the Orthodontic Assistant Training School in Atlanta in 1999. She began working with Dr. Mejia as an orthodontic assistant at that time. She joined the team full-time in 2004. In 2006 she assumed the role of financial coordinator, handling both insurance and in-house financing. She brings many years of financial experience to our office.

Lacy enjoys raising her two daughters and son. In her free time, she enjoys traveling, photography, camping, and Disney!

Georgia - Marketing Coordinator

Georgia joined Dr. Mejia’s team in 2006 as a certified orthodontic assistant. She currently manages the internal and external marketing for our team. Georgia loves meeting new people and generating new patients for the office while maintaining current relationships with our dental family.

Georgia enjoys spending time with her husband and their two boys. In her spare time, she enjoys watching her boys play sports and is actively involved in her church.

Jessica - Orthodontic Assistant
