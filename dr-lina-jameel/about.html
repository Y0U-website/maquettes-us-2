Dentist Herndon

Lina Jameel, D.D.S.

Dr. Jameel believes that every patient deserves to be treated like family. Driven by a deep passion for dentistry and an unwavering commitment to integrity, she delivers high-quality dental care with both precision and heart. Simply stated, she loves what she does — and it shows.

Her journey to dentistry is one of remarkable dedication. Dr. Jameel began her dental education in 2004 at the School of Dentistry at the University of Baghdad. After relocating from Iraq to Jordan as a refugee, she earned her Bachelor of Dental Surgery from Jordan University of Science and Technology. Upon moving to the United States, she continued her pursuit of excellence — working as a dental assistant while furthering her education — and ultimately earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from Virginia Commonwealth University, graduating in the top three of her class.

A firm believer in lifelong learning and the power of advanced technology, Dr. Jameel has completed extensive continuing education through prestigious institutions including The Dawson Academy, The Implant Pathway, and the Reengage Clear Aligner Continuum. She is a proud member of the American Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, the Virginia Dental Society, and the American Academy of Clear Aligners.

Outside of the office, Dr. Jameel enjoys exercising, cooking, and exploring Virginia’s wine country. She resides in Great Falls with her husband, her son Ario and their soft-coated Wheaten Terrier, Bruno.

Monique Guraya, D.D.S.

Dr. Monique Guraya, DDS, is a highly skilled and compassionate dentist with a passion for delivering high-quality, detail-oriented dental care. A graduate of Virginia Commonwealth University’s Doctor of Dental Surgery program (2021) and a Bachelor of Science in Biology from the College of William & Mary (2017), Dr. Guraya brings a strong academic foundation and diverse clinical experience to her practice.

Dr. Guraya completed a two-year General Practice Residency at the West Los Angeles VA Hospital, where she served as Chief Resident and gained extensive experience treating medically and dentally complex patients. Her training encompassed comprehensive treatment planning, esthetic dentistry, oral surgery, endodontics, implant placement, bone grafting, and full-arch restorations, including All-on-4 cases.

Her clinical expertise spans fixed and removable prosthodontics, esthetic dentistry, implant placement, guided bone regeneration, surgical extractions, and medical management.

Dr. Guraya is committed to providing every patient with a gentle, compassionate, and comprehensive dental experience.

Ashling Wilson RDH

Ashling has served the local community for over two decades in a variety of dental healthcare roles, most of which she has spent as a Registered Dental Hygienist. She graduated from Germanna Community College with an Associates of Science in Dental Hygiene in 2010 and completed her Bachelors of Science Degree in Dental Hygiene through Old Dominion University with a minor in Human Service and Counseling in May 2023.

Ashling’s goal as a Dental Hygienist is to interact with patients on a one-on-one basis and to help develop oral health care plans suited to the individual’s needs. It’s important to relate oral health to total health and well-being. She uses a customized preventative approach catered to each patient while building a friendly rapport in a professional and unhurried manner. Ashling believes that dental health is a journey, not a quick fix.

Ashling’s father was an active-duty military service member and although she has traveled all over the continental United States, She has lived in Virginia most of her life. She is a lifetime Washington Capitals Hockey fan and looks forward to the CAPS winning their second Stanley Cup. She enjoys riding her Yamaha MT-07 motorcycle on weekends while touring and exploring the rural parts of Virginia. She is very active and enjoys attending the variety of seasonal festivals, concerts and exhibits that are showcased here in the Washington Metro Area.
