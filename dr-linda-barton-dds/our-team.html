LINDA BARTON, DDS

Dr. Linda Barton, DDS, has been in private practice with her husband, Dr. Bill Lee, DDS, for over thirty years. A graduate with honors from UT Knoxville, Dr. Barton completed her professional training and received her DDS from the UT Center for Health Sciences in Memphis in 1981.

Dr. Barton has been married to Dr. Lee for over 30 years and they have three children, Jeffrey, Angela, & Jennifer as well as two granddaughters, Clara and Ellie.

Dr. Barton enjoys her family and the best dog ever.

Like Dr. Lee, she enjoys having so many great patients. They also enjoy going to and taking continuing education through multiple sources such as the Panky Institute, the Dawson Institute, the Spear Institute and especially Rick Barnes and Progressive Orthodontic training.

Dr. Barton and Dr. Lee have actively incorporated new technology such as Carbon Dioxide & Biolase Lasers, digital scanning instead of impressions, the wand surge computer aided anesthesia, Tech scan bite analysis, digital shade guide, ORAL ID cancer detection using fluorescence technology, Clear Aligner, conventional Orthodontics, digital radiography and a voice activated probe for gum disease.
