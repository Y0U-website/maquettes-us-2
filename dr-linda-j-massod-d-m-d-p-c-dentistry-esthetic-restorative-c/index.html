Dr. Linda J. Massod, D.M.D. earned her B.S. from the University of Massachusetts before graduating from the Boston University School of Dental Medicine. She was elected to the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Honorary Dental Society. The doctor is a member of the American Dental Association, the Massachusetts Dental Society and is the Treasurer of the North Shore District Dental Society.

She is actively involved with the CRA educational and research organization, which is devoted to extensive, independent evaluation of dental products that serve to help dental professionals provide the best care for patients.

She has been an integral member of the North Shore dental community since 1991.
