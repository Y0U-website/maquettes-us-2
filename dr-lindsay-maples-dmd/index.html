The practice

Bachelor of Science, Exercise Physiology at University of Florida (Cum Laude)

Doctor of Dental Medicine at University of Florida College of Dentistry (Cum Laude)

Today, Dr. Maples is proud to be a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry .

Dr. Maples’ path into pediatric dentistry was shaped by compassion, service, and a desire to help children feel cared for. Her father’s example of kindness and generosity had a lasting influence on her. A mission trip to Honduras also deepened her belief in the power of dental care to change a child’s life.

That early calling still guides how she practices today. She loves helping children build confidence, healthy habits, and positive feelings about the dentist from a young age.

Dr. Maples is proud of her Cajun-Florida roots , her family’s deep legacy in dentistry, and the chance to raise her children right here in the community she loves. She and her husband, Dr. Andrew Maples, are both dentists Ponte Vedra Premier Dental and Maples Dental and together they share a strong commitment to caring for families both in and out of the office.

Outside of work, you’ll usually find her with Andrew and their three kids, soaking up family time, cheering on the Florida Gators and Jacksonville Jaguars, and staying involved in local schools, events, and charitable programs that support children and the wider Ponte Vedra community.

Dr. Maples believes parents deserve to understand not only what is recommended for their child, but why. Her care is prevention-first, practical, and focused on healthy growth over time. She works hard to make visits feel calm and predictable. That is especially important for first visits, anxious children, and kids who need a slower, more supportive start.
