Why families choose Invisalign at our office:

When should my child first see an orthodontist? At Ponte Vedra Pediatric Dentistry & Orthodontics , we recommend a first orthodontic evaluation around age 7 , which aligns with guidelines from the American Association of Orthodontists.

At this age, our orthodontists can evaluate jaw growth, bite alignment, spacing, airway concerns, and early crowding—even if no braces are needed yet. Many children in the Nocatee and Ponte Vedra area benefit from simply being monitored until the timing is right.
