Clear (Ceramic) Braces

For those who want a less noticeable option, ceramic braces blend with the natural color of teeth. They work just as well as metal braces but offer a more discreet appearance, making them a popular choice for older teens and adults. While they are slightly more fragile than metal braces, they provide an excellent balance between aesthetics and functionality.

  • Less visible than traditional metal braces for a more natural look
  • Effective in straightening teeth while maintaining a subtle appearance
  • A great alternative for those who want discreet treatment but prefer braces over aligners
  • Suitable for both teens and adults looking for a low-profile option

Self-Ligating Braces

These braces use a special bracket design that eliminates the need for elastic bands. The built-in mechanism secures the archwire, allowing for smoother adjustments and easier cleaning. Some patients find them more comfortable due to reduced friction. Fewer required adjustments may also mean slightly shorter appointment times.

  • Free from elastic bands, reducing friction for potentially more comfortable wear
  • Easier to clean since there are no elastics to trap food and plaque
  • May reduce treatment time with more efficient tooth movement
  • Shorter adjustment appointments compared to traditional braces

Braces Traverse City for All Ages

Orthodontic care isn’t just for kids and teens. Early intervention can help guide proper jaw development, while teens and adults can achieve long-term dental health and improved confidence through braces at any stage of life.

Braces for Kids

Early orthodontic evaluations can prevent more serious issues down the road. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic check-up by age seven. At this stage, we can detect potential concerns such as overcrowding, bite misalignment, or jaw growth issues and determine if early treatment is beneficial.

Braces for Teens

The teenage years are the most common time for orthodontic treatment. As permanent teeth settle in, braces help correct spacing, bite alignment, and overall dental function. With modern options like clear and self-ligating braces, teens have more choices than ever to fit their personal preferences.

Braces for Adults

More adults than ever are choosing to improve their smiles with braces. Whether correcting long-standing alignment issues or addressing new concerns, adult braces provide long-term benefits for dental health and function. With more discreet options available, orthodontic treatment can fit into any lifestyle.

What to Expect – Our Braces Traverse City Treatment Process

01 Initial Consultation – A full orthodontic assessment, including digital scans or X-rays if needed. Discussion of concerns, goals, treatment options, and estimated cost.

02 Custom Treatment Plan – A structured approach outlining the type of braces, adjustment schedule, and overall treatment timeline. Patients receive guidance on oral hygiene and brace care.

04 Regular Adjustments – Routine visits allow for modifications, wire tightening, and progress monitoring. Adjustments ensure steady movement and keep treatment on track.

05 Post-Treatment Retention – Retainers prevent teeth from shifting after braces are removed. Options include clear removable retainers, traditional Hawley retainers, or bonded retainers for extra stability.
