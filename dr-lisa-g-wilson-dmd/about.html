Meet Dr. Wilson

Dr. Wilson established her private practice in the Indian Springs area of north Shelby County in September of 1990. She recently built a new facility where she continues to provide preventive, restorative and cosmetic services that now include invisible braces and single visit crowns. She is conveniently located in the Oak Mountain School district and has provided custom mouthguards to both the high school and middle school athletes. She sponsors many activities associated with area schools and colleges.

Our Philosophy

Comfort We pride ourselves in the courteous and caring attention we deliver to each person who walks through our doors. Our goal is to win your trust and cultivate a long term relationship with you.

Quality Our team of highly skilled clinicians work with state of the art dentistry, to provide our patients with safe, comprehensive care insuring that a thorough diagnosis of treatment is fully explained so you can make good dental choices for you and your family.

Education We believe that dentistry is ever changing and education is important, so we strive to stay abreast of the latest technologies by attending continuing education seminars throughout the country.

We are all committed to helping you and your entire family achieve the smiles you deserve healthy, beautiful ones!
