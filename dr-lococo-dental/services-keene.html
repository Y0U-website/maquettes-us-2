Services

Cleaning and Prevention

Prevention is our main priority! Our friendly and welcoming hygiene team take pride in providing each patient with an exceptional cleaning , treatment and care.

Crowns and Bridges

When teeth are missing or heavily restored the best option is often a crown or bridge . Dr. Lococo has extensive experience in this area and will treat your case with meticulous detail to deliver the best results possible.

Dentures

We are able to offer you a variety of denture options. Paramount to denture success is restoring your entire dentition to the optimal state to receive a denture, which we are able and happy to do.

Extractions

We perform extractions on a daily basis however if more complicated situations arise we will gladly refer you to one of our excellent oral surgeons in the area.

Fillings (Restorative)

It’s what we do, all day, every day yet we take great care in every case. Fillings are used to restore the shape and function of a tooth after a cavity is removed or the tooth is broken.

Implants

A dental implant mimics the form and function of a natural tooth, which consists of a root and a crown. Implants are used to replace missing teeth in appropriate cases.

Cosmetics & Whitening

A variety of cosmetic techniques are available to give you the perfect smile including bonding, veneers and crowns. Professional teeth whitening is faster, more effective, and longer lasting that treatments you find over the counter.
