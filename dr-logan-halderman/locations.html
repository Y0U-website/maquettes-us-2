Hours & Locations

If you’re having a medical emergency, please call 911. If you are a patient and your problem is urgent and cannot wait until regular office hours, please call Wayne Healthcare at (937) 548-1141, and leave a message with the operator. A provider will return your call. If your telephone call is deemed to NOT be a medical emergency, you will be subject to charges for a telephone consultation. If you are calling to refill a prescription, please contact your pharmacy for them to send a refill request to your provider.

If you’re having a mental health emergency you may call the crisis hotline for Darke, Miami, and Shelby County at 1 (800) 351-7347. If you need to speak with the provider on call for Family Health, please call Wayne Healthcare at (937) 548-1141, and leave a message with the operator. A provider will return your call. If you are calling to refill a prescription, please contact your pharmacy, and they will send a refill request to your provider.
