Dr. Derek Hoffman, Orthodontist

Dr. Derek Hoffman .

An orthodontic specialist who has been treating Clay County families from this Fleming Island office since 2015.

Dr. Derek Hoffman is a board-eligible Orthodontic Specialist practicing in Fleming Island, Florida. He completed his Doctorate of Dental Medicine at the Medical University of South Carolina and his Master of Science in Orthodontics at the University of Florida.

Dr. Hoffman is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, the Florida Association of Orthodontists, and the Florida Dental Association.

Dr. Hoffman grew up in Ocala and lives in Fleming Island now. He and his wife Susan were high-school sweethearts, have been married more than fifteen years, and have three children. Time with them comes first. What is left over goes to running, and he has finished a marathon, plus golf, surfing and pickup soccer.
