Dr. Samantha Montoya, Orthodontist

Dr. Samantha Montoya .

A board-certified orthodontist, and the doctor most of our aligner cases go to. She plans every case herself rather than handing you a template.

A former collegiate swimmer from North Carolina, Dr. Montoya traded lanes for braces. She earned her DDS at UNC Chapel Hill before completing her orthodontics residency at the University of Florida and becoming certified by the American Board of Orthodontics.

She loves combining the precision of orthodontics with an easy-going, upbeat patient experience, and places a special focus on mastering clear aligner treatment for patients of all ages to deliver comfortable, esthetic results.

When she’s not crafting smiles, you’ll find her on a pickleball court, training for triathlons, sipping a great latte, or making footprints in the sand with her sons Jaden and Noah.
