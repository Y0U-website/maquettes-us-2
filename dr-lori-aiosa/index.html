The practice

“We don’t want to be your orthodontist for now . We want to be the practice your kids remember when their kids need braces.”

Every face here is a real patient of ours, and every one of these smiles was finished in our Fleming Island office on Business Center Drive. Some of them are grown up now and sending us their own children.

Both of our doctors completed an accredited orthodontic residency, which is the training that separates an orthodontist from a general dentist who also straightens teeth. Dr. Montoya is board-certified and Dr. Hoffman is board-eligible. They share charts and second opinions, so whichever one you see, you are getting both of them.
