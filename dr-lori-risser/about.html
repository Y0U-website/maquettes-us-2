She also enjoys cooking and spending her nights at the gym.

Danielle - Expanded Function Dental Assistant & Business Office

Danielle is one of our expanded dental assistants, as well as a business assistant. Her duties include placing restorations, and when needed she will assist the business staff with daily duties. Since 2000, Danielle has enjoyed working with patients and creating beautiful smiles. She enjoys working with the doctors because they are compassionate and believe in the staff when it comes to helping patients. Danielle is a mom of 4 kids’ s, when she is not at sports functions; she enjoys spending time with family and friends.

Jody - Dental Hygienist

Our dental hygienist Jody provides dental care through preventive cleanings, treatment of periodontal disease, radiographs/diagnostics, and patient education. Jody enjoys helping her patients achieve improved health. She enjoys working with the team at Erskine Family Dentistry because they are compassionate and strive for the best in patient care. Jody is married and has two children. She embraces their farm life, the outdoors, and spending time with family and friends.

Lauren - Expanded Function Dental Assistant

Lauren is an expanded function dental assistant who has a passion for patient care and is detail oriented. As a great addition to our team, Lauren strives to make every patient comfortable with a calm and compassionate approach. Outside of the office, Lauren enjoys spending time outdoors, being on the water and taking care of her fur babies.

Stacy - Dental Hygienist

Stacy is one of our dental hygienists who treats periodontal disease, performs periodontal therapy, and maintains healthy gums and teeth. Her favorite part about her job is helping people become confident with their smile and helping them to become healthy overall. She learns a lot from the doctors at our office, and respects that they have good communication skills with their patients. Outside of work, Stacy is married with two daughters and a stepson. Her hobbies include gardening, the outdoors, and being near the water.

Stacey - Dental Hygienist

Our dental hygienist Stacey gives oral hygiene instructions to our patients, cleans teeth, takes X-rays, places fluoride, and does periodontal therapy. She also orders our office supplies. For Stacey, the best thing about her job includes working with such a great team, getting to know our patients and their families on a personal level, and educating them on their overall health. She also appreciates that our office is dedicated to staying on top of the latest technology. In her spare time, Stacey enjoys being with her friends and family, traveling, cooking, and shopping.
