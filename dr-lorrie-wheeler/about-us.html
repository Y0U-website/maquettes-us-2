Meet the Team at Oak Hill Dental Group

Ready to Meet Our Team?

At Oak Hill Dental Group, our greatest strength is our people. We are a longstanding team of local professionals who genuinely care about the community we serve. Many of our patients have been with us for years, and we value the relationships we’ve built just as much as the care we provide.

Our doctors bring skill, experience, and a shared philosophy of calm, compassionate dentistry. Dr. Johann Ramkissoon is the owner of Oak Hill Dental Group and leads our dynamic team of doctors and staff.

We look forward to caring for you at Oak Hill Dental Group.

Dr. Johann Ramkissoon

Dr. Johann Ramkissoon has lived all over south and central Florida. After earning a Bachelors in Biomedical Sci-ences from the University of South Florida, Dr. Ramkissoon went on to graduate from the University of Florida College of Dentistry. He is an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry, American Dental Association and the Florida Dental Association. He is committed to continuing his education in dentistry and providing the best treatment for his patients.

Dr. Jonathan Weiss

Dr. Jonathan Weiss is one of the owners of Oak Hill Dental Group and has been providing high-quality dental care for over 10 years. A graduate of the University of Florida College of Dentistry, Dr. Weiss is committed to delivering modern, patient-centered care with a focus on comfort, trust, and long-term results.

He takes pride in building strong relationships with his patients and believes that great dentistry starts with listening and understanding each individual’s needs. Whether providing routine care or more advanced treatment. Dr. Weiss strives to make every visit a positive and stress-free experience.

Outside the office, Dr. Weiss enjoys spending time with his family, being involved in his church, and fishing whenever he gets the chance.

Lindsey

Lindsey is a dedicated dental hygienist at Oak Hill Dental who is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy smiles and feel confident in the dental chair. She graduated from Santa Fe College in 2011 and has been practicing dental hygiene ever since, bringing more than a decade of experience to her role. Lindsey joined the Oak Hill Dental team in September 2025, where she quickly became known for her positive energy and patient-focused approach.

In her role, Lindsey works closely with patients to help treat and prevent periodontal disease, maintain healthy smiles, and educate individuals on proper dental hygiene habits. She believes that education and prevention are key to long-term oral health and enjoys empowering patients with the knowledge they need to care for their smiles between visits.

Lindsey’s passion for dentistry began long before her career started. After experiencing difficult dental visits as a child, she became determined to help create a better experience for others. While still in high school, she dual enrolled in college courses and completed her Associate of Science degree along with the prerequisites needed for dental hygiene school by the summer after graduation. This early dedication set the stage for a career centered on compassion and patient comfort.

One of Lindsey’s favorite parts of her job is helping patients overcome dental anxiety and rediscover a sense of ease when visiting the dentist. She strives to make every appointment as pleasant and comfortable as possible, believing that a positive dental experience can make a lasting difference in someone’s overall health and confidence. She also loves working alongside the Oak Hill Dental team, where the supportive environment and positive energy make every day rewarding.

Outside the office, Lindsey enjoys spending quality time with her husband and children. She loves baking with her kids, traveling, tackling home improvement projects, gardening, and enjoying time outdoors at the beach or in the mountains.

Lindsey runs a small cinnamon roll business called Lulabell’s, which allows her to connect with members of the community while sharing something she loves to create.

Rachel

Rachel is a dedicated dental hygienist at Oak Hill Dental Group with over 12 years of experience in dental hygiene. She received her education and training at South Florida State College and has been a valued member of the Oak Hill team since 2021.

In her role, Rachel is responsible for providing thorough dental cleanings, removing plaque, tartar, and stains, taking diagnostic x-rays, assessing patients for periodontal disease, and educating patients on proper oral hygiene techniques. She is passionate about keeping patients comfortable while delivering high-quality care that supports long-term oral health.

Rachel’s favorite part of her work is helping patients feel at ease and ensuring they receive detailed, effective cleanings for optimal outcomes. She was inspired to join the dental field as a teenager after her dentist invited her to assist with sterilization an experience that sparked her interest in hygiene and patient care.

Outside of the office, Rachel enjoys spending time with her family, especially her three young daughters. Together, they love being outdoors, caring for their animals, and traveling whenever possible.

Penny

Penny is a dedicated and compassionate dental assistant at Oak Hill Dental Group, bringing decades of experience and a personal touch to every patient interaction. In her role, Penny assists the doctor during dental procedures, prepares and sterilizes treatment rooms, takes x-rays, supports treatment planning, and consults with patients about their care. She also monitors patient comfort throughout appointments and ensures instruments and supplies are properly sterilized and stocked.

Penny completed her training through the University of Florida Expanded Functions in Dental Assisting and Radiology and began her career in dentistry in 1995. She joined Oak Hill Dental Group in July 2025, adding a wealth of knowledge and skill to the team. Penny’s journey into dentistry began unexpectedly when she accepted a role in an orthodontic office, where she quickly discovered her passion for patient care and hands-on clinical work.

What Penny enjoys most is building genuine relationships with her patients. She values getting to know them personally and takes pride in educating patients and helping them transform their dental habits. Penny also appreciates that every patient is different, which keeps her work both engaging and rewarding.

Outside the practice, Penny stays active by teaching fitness classes and participating in Bible study. One of her most unique experiences involves traveling regularly with her daughter, who shows cattle something Penny proudly supports by attending shows several times a month.

With her warmth, experience, and dedication, Penny is a valued member of the Oak Hill Dental Group team and a familiar, friendly face patients trust.

Edye

Edye is a dedicated dental assistant at Oak Hill Dental Group, where she has been a valued member of the team since 2006. With a career in dentistry that began in 1993 in Phillipsburg, New Jersey, Edye brings decades of experience and compassionate care to every patient she serves.

She received her specialized diploma in dental assisting from Northampton Community College in Pennsylvania in 1992 and earned her Certified Dental Assistant (CDA) credential in 1994. Throughout her career, Edye has remained committed to excellence in patient care and clinical support.

In her role, Edye performs four-handed dentistry, takes dental x-rays, sterilizes instruments and handpieces, orders supplies, assists with treatment planning, and helps patients understand their recommended treatment. She is passionate about building relationships with patients and strives to make each visit as comfortable and positive as possible.

Edye was inspired to enter the dental field after having an amazing dentist during her childhood who sparked her interest in dentistry. That early inspiration continues to influence the compassionate care she provides today.

Outside of the office, Edye enjoys cooking and always has a home improvement project underway. She and her husband also love bike riding and hiking together.
