Welcome to Oak Hill Dental Group! Our team is here to change the way you think about dental care. We believe dentistry should feel unhurried, thoughtful, and truly personal. As a privately owned dental office in Lake City, FL , we are proud to care for our neighbors in a setting that s calm, welcoming, and built on trust.

From your very first visit, you’ll notice that we take a different approach. We slow down, sit with you, and take the time to understand your concerns, goals, and comfort level. There is never pressure and never a rush. We prioritize having honest conversations and care that moves forward at a pace that feels right for you. Our patients are our top priority, and we treat everyone the way we would…

Oak Hill Dental Group offers comprehensive dental services for patients of all ages, with a strong focus on preserving natural teeth whenever possible. Many of our patients appreciate our patient-centered, detail-oriented philosophy and the time we spend carefully planning treatment, especially when care is more complex.

We believe great dentistry is about partnership. We explain your options clearly, answer questions honestly, and support you in making informed decisions about your oral health.

Our Lake City dental office is designed to feel peaceful and reassuring from the moment you arrive. Appointments are intentionally unrushed so we can spend as much time as needed to ensure you feel comfortable. After treatment, our team follows up to monitor your healing and ensure you’re doing well.

To help you relax, we offer thoughtful comforts like complimentary water and cozy blankets, as well as a children’s room with books and toys to make visits easier for families.
