The practice

At Kinston Dental Associates, we’re proud to care for smiles across Kinston, Lenoir County and nearby communities. Whether you’re coming in for preventive care or need more advanced cosmetic treatment, our expert team provides comfortable, personalized dentistry for patients of all ages.

As a family dental practice, we understand that every smile has its own needs at every stage of life. That’s why we take the time to understand your goals and build a treatment plan that fits both your oral health and your lifestyle.
