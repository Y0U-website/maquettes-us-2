Payment & Insurance Information

Q: What payment methods do you accept?

A: Payment is expected at the time services are rendered. For your convenience, we accept cash, check, MasterCard, Visa, and Discover.

Q: What insurance providers do you accept?

A: We are in-network with Delta. We accept and file all insurances except Medicaid. Accepted insurance includes: Sunlife, Blue Cross, Aetna, Metlife, Ameritas, Principal, Guardian, Humana, MedCost, Cigna.

Q: Do you offer financing?

A: If you need an easy payment plan, visit CareCredit for low-interest financing, or call our office for information.
