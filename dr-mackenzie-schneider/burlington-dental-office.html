Meet Your Dentists

Skilled and Compassionate Professionals Committed to Your Optimal Dental Health

Dr. Bilal Khan

Background

Dr. Khan graduated from Lake Forest College with a bachelor’s degree in Neuroscience and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Midwestern University Illinois. He is a compassionate general dentist who is dedicated to helping patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident in their dental care. Dr. Khan enjoys building lasting relationships with his patients and believes that taking the time to listen and understand each person’s goals is the foundation of exceptional care.

Dental Style

Dr. Khan centers treating every patient like family. He believes in educating patients about their oral health, creating individualized treatment plans, and helping them make informed decisions without pressure. Whether a patient is visiting for routine preventive care, restorative treatment, cosmetic dentistry, or tooth replacement options, his goal is to make every experience as comfortable and positive as possible.

In addition to general dentistry, Dr. Khan has pursued advanced training in dental implants as a part of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry. He enjoys staying current with the latest techniques and technology to provide modern, evidence-based care that helps patients achieve healthy, functional, and confident smiles.

Fun Facts

Outside of the office, Dr. Khan loves watching sports, traveling, exploring new restaurants and coffee shops, staying active, and following advancements in dentistry. Above all, he values meaningful connections with people and feels grateful for the opportunity to serve and care for his community.
