WE ARE SPECIALISTS IN SAVING TEETH Endodontics is the area of dentistry that focuses on the root tissues and dental pulp. Endodontic procedures can often save a badly decayed or damaged tooth that would otherwise need to be extracted.

After numbing the tooth and surrounding tissues with a local anesthetic, we drill through the crown of the tooth to the root chamber. We use specialized tools to carefully clean and reshape the root canal(s), place a post if needed, and fill and seal the canal(s) with biocompatible gutta percha. Although root canals performed by endodontists have a success rate of 95 %, they can occasionally fail. If this happens, we can retreat the tooth, using a microscope to search for any additional canals or unusual anatomy that may have led to the failure. We will again carefully clean and seal the affected canal(s), place a temporary crown, and ask you to return for your final crown.

After the tooth and surrounding tissues are fully numb, we cut a small incision in the gum tissue to expose the tooth root. Using magnification and precision instruments, we remove a few millimeters of inflamed tooth root. We then place a filling and close the gums with sutures. Over the next few months, the bone heals around the treated root.

A cracked tooth can cause intense pain, especially during chewing. A crack provides an easy entry point for bacteria, which can cause infections in the tooth pulp and spread to the surrounding gum and bone tissue. Therefore, it is important to diagnose and treat the cracked tooth as soon as possible.

Les soins

If a root canal retreatment fails, you may need apical surgery, known as an apicoectomy. This allows us to replace the root tip with a filling.
