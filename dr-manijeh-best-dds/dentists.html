Dr. Manijeh Best

Dr. Manijeh Best received her doctorate degree in 1994. She completed her advanced education in a General Dentistry Residency program at the University of Nebraska Medical Center. The following year, she practiced dentistry along with teaching at UNMC. Dr. Best was in private practice in Kansas City, Missouri for seven years before moving to Maine and joining our practice in 2003.
