How to Wear your Invisalign Trays

  • Each set of trays should be worn for a full two weeks before switching to your next set. Moving into a new set too fast/early can compromise the remodeling bone resulting in bone loss and tissue recession.
  • Switch to your new set at bedtime. This will allow for easier first-time removal of the new set when it’s time to eat or brush your teeth.

How to Clean your Invisalign Trays

  • Clean your retainers with a toothbrush and antibacterial soap. (Use of denture cleaners or toothpaste will dull and scratch the surface).

Invisalign Appointments

  • Typically happen every 6 weeks and are between 20-30 minutes long.

  • What if my teeth aren’t moving as expected? As with any type of orthodontics there are times when adjustments in treatment need to be made. There are no increases in cost to you when this happens, it is just part of the process.
  • What if I lose one of my trays? Lost trays can be replaced. Additional fees may apply. Using your tray case every time you take out your trays will help. Putting your trays in a napkin inevitably ends up in a lost tray. To maintain optimal results and protect your investment wear trays as directed
