Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Matt and his wife, Sara (who is a dentist in South Windsor), have two daughters named Emilia and Molly. When he isn’t busy at work, being with his family consumes most of his free time. His many hobbies include playing golf, growing vegetables in his garden, trying new recipes in the kitchen, and cheering on the Yankees. He’s also very passionate about SCUBA diving and even has a saltwater reef tank at his house with fish and coral! As if that wasn’t enough, his latest goal is to become fluent in Italian.
