Michelle, Expanded Duties Dental Assistant

Michelle resides in Walhala, South Carolina, and she holds an Expanded Duties Dental Assisting degree from the University of Florida at Gainsville. In addition, she obtained a Radiology degree from the University of Tampa. Michelle has been a dental assistant for over twenty years and finds assisting to be very rewarding and enjoyable. Outside of work, Michelle appreciates spending time with her husband, children, grandchildren, and her two puppies. Her favorite leisure activities include camping and motorcycle riding with her husband.
