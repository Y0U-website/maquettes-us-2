Procedures that receive a 15 % courtesy include but not limited to:

  • Crowns/ Bridges

  • Sealants
  • Dentures and Partials
  • Whitening Trays

Exclusions and Limitations:

Our Concierge Membership Plan is not an insurance plan, it has been designed to provide a way for our patients to obtain the dental care they need at the most affordable rate. The membership plan:

  • Is standalone coverage not to be combined with insurance benefits.
  • If you choose to use Care Credit to pay for treatment, the office will pay the 10 % interest for 12 months AND extend to you an additional 5 % courtesy off your treatment for a total of 15 %.
  • Cannot be used in a workman’s compensation treatment plan.
  • Cannot be combined with any other courtesies.
  • Is for use for treatments that lie within our realm of expertise.
  • Cannot be used for treatment with another dentist or specialist to whom we refer.
  • Cannot be used for dental products.
  • Benefits may only be utilized by the member and are not transferable.

Program Guidelines:

There are no refunds for paid memberships, even if the plan benefits are not utilized during the participation period. The plan will be active for 12 consecutive months from the time the member joins.
