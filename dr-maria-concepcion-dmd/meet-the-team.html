Meet the Team

Maria Concepcion, DMD

Dr. Maria Concepcion graduated Cum Laude from Temple University School of Dentistry in May, 1996 and completed her Endodontics specialty at Temple in May, 1999. While doing her Endodontic training at Temple, Dr. Concepcion also worked in the evenings and on weekends to further master her profession.

Post graduation, Dr. Concepcion practiced in Wayne and Phoenixville for 7 years. In 2006 she opened her own practice in Paoli, across from Paoli Memorial Hospital. She is available to patients five days a week plus additional times, as needed, for emergency endodontal treatments.

Dr. Concepcion is a member in good standing of the American Association of Endodontists (ADA) and the Pennsylvania Dental Association (PDA) . She lives in Malvern and in her free time enjoys traveling with her two sons Michael and Will.
