Suffolk Pediatric Dentistry and Orthodontics provides a full range of dental services, including:

Preventive and Diagnostic Services​ – Part of our job as pediatric dentists is to lay the foundation for a lifetime of good oral health. This includes dental cleanings and exams every 6 months, along with hygiene instructions to give children the tools they need to take good care of their teeth.

Restorative Services​ – Cavities happen. When they do, we’re here to help. We offer amalgam and composite fillings, pulp treatment, and dental crowns to restore health to decayed or damaged teeth. We also offer extractions when the best course of treatment is to remove a tooth.

Cosmetic Services​ – When a child chips a tooth, we can use cosmetic bonding to restore its appearance. For teens who have recently had braces removed or have staining for any other reason, we offer whitening treatments to give them confidence in their smiles.

Interceptive Orthodontics​ – The goal of interceptive orthodontics is to minimize or eliminate the need for orthodontic treatment once a child’s adult teeth have come in. These treatments include habit appliances to stop thumbsucking and re-train improper tongue position, space maintainers, and molar rotation.
