Brooklyn

Take Belt Parkway to Van Wyck Expressway North to L.I.E. (i495) Go West to Junction Blvd. Make a LEFT at Junction Blvd. to 62nd Drive. Make a RIGHT at 62nd Drive to Queens Blvd. Make a LEFT at Queens Blvd. onto the service road to 66th Ave. Parking at the back of the building.

Manhattan

Take Queens Borough Bridge Lower Level to Queens Blvd. East to 66th Ave. Alternate Route: Take Queens Midtown Tunnel onto L.I. Expressway (i495) East to Queens Blvd. Make a RIGHT on Queens Blvd to 66th Ave.

Long Island

Grand Central Parkway West Take EXIT 11E-W toward 69 RD / Jewel Ave. Take the 69 RD WEST / HARRY VAN ARSDALE JR Ave. ramp Turn RIGHT onto Queens Blvd Turn LEFT onto 65th Ave.

By Train

Take G, R or M trains to 67th Avenue station Proceed to exit South Side Queens Blvd. Walk to corner of 66th Ave & Queens Blvd.

By Bus

Take Q60 to 66th Ave., and Queens Blvd. Bus stop is in front of the building.
