The practice

We don’t rush, and we don’t double-book. Great dentistry and great relationships require time and effort. Our pace allows us to educate, build relationships, and ensure you leave feeling valued and heard.

Privately owned by a couple that has served the community for over 30 years, we know how to treat people right and not like numbers.

We know how to be efficient without being pushy-a rare find in the city! You’ll feel like you’re our only patient.

We’ve partnered with a certified periodontist so you can get a wide array of dental services under the same roof.

Between our accepted dental insurance and financing options, affordable dentistry has never been easier.

A graduate of the prestigious NYU College of Dentistry and member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, Dr. Velazquez loves to show his patients what he’s seeing using high-definition images while explaining their care options, adding transparency and certainty to the dental process.

Dr. Montes also attended NYU, and her decades of experience pair well with her countless hours of advanced training focused on pediatrics, particularly working with children with special needs. She and the rest of our team help patients of all ages achieve smiles they can rely on.
