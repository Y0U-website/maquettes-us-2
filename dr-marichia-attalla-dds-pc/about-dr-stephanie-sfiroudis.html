The Newest Periodontal Technology

Dr. Sfiroudis utilizes the latest in procedures, instruments and technologies, and strives for minimal invasiveness for maximum comfort, so patients don’t fear going to the periodontist.

Digital radiographs and radiology provide Dr. Sfiroudis and her staff with an instant picture of your oral health for quicker treatment, while also minimizing your exposure to radiation for enhanced safety and comfort. The digital panoramic radiography scanning system rotates around your head in a complete circle for an exact picture of your smile, so the doctor can assemble an effective treatment plan for your health.

For patients facing major procedures, Dr. Sfiroudis utilizes the precise technology of the SlimPlant software system to create a comprehensive plan. This innovative software digitally scans a 3D image of your smile to a computer to help the doctor develop the best plan for your implant procedure for peak effectiveness.

Sedation for Patients with Severe Anxiety

Dr. Sfiroudis offers sedation dentistry for patients who suffer from severe anxiety about visiting the dentist. Nitrous oxide is a proven relaxing agent and provides a safe and comfortable way to relax during your treatment. With nitrous oxide, patients remain aware and are able to talk with Dr. Sfiroudis during the procedure. Nitrous oxide is fast acting and wears off quickly. IV sedation is also available.

Early Detection of Periodontal Disease

Many adult Americans suffer from some form of periodontal disease but aren’t even aware of it. The early detection of gingivitis is the best defense against developing periodontal disease. By making the choice to see Dr. Sfiroudis, you have taken the first step towards protecting yourself against periodontal disease.

She will conduct a thorough evaluation of your teeth and gums and provide you with a diagnosis. She will then go over your dental history and daily habits to comprise a dental plan based on your individual needs. Whatever your dental goals, Dr. Sfiroudis will work closely with your general dentist to ensure you have access to complete oral care.
