At PDA Dental Group, we believe that everyone deserves access to high-quality dental care. Our mission is to give patients the confidence and ability to take control of their dental health and, in turn, their overall wellness. To support that commitment, we are proud to offer affordable in-house PDA Membership Plans, powered by Dental HQ, designed to provide year-round care at a reduced cost.

Our membership plan is structured to cover your preventive and routine care while also providing discounts on additional services. You simply pay an annual or monthly fee – think of it as a subscription plan for your dental health, designed to keep your smile healthy while providing predictable, manageable dental costs.

Elect from a variety of plans tailored to your needs, including options for children, teens, adults and periodontal maintenance care. Choose a discounted one-time annual rate or a convenient monthly plan. You can sign up online using our secure online portal, powered by DentalHQ, which accepts all major credit/debit cards and most FSA cards.

By joining our in-house membership program, you’ll have access to affordable, high-quality care delivered by our trusted team that puts your health and comfort first. If you have any questions about our PDA Membership Plans, please don’t hesitate to call our Billing Office at 781-944-7799. We’re happy to assist you. A dental membership plan offers those patients paying out of pocket a discount and more affordable option for their dental care. Instead of dealing with the complexities of insurance, you pay an annual or monthly fee to PDA Dental Group and enjoy discounted services. It’s an alternative for those without insurance and maybe even a helpful supplement.

Choose a discounted one-time annual rate or a convenient monthly plan. You can sign up online using our secure online portal, powered by DentalHQ, which accepts all major credit/debit cards and most FSA cards.
