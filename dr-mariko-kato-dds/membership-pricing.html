Join our PDA Membership Plans Discover the benefits

At PDA Dental Group, we believe that everyone deserves access to high-quality dental care. Our mission is to give patients the confidence and ability to take control of their dental health and, in turn, their overall wellness. To support that commitment, we are proud to offer affordable in-house PDA Membership Plans, powered by Dental HQ, designed to provide year-round care at a reduced cost.

Why Choose Our Membership Plan?

  • No Insurance, No Problem: No need to worry about maximums, deductibles, claim forms, pre-authorization forms or time constraints.
  • Cost Savings: Enjoy significant discounts on preventive and routine dental services without the limitations of traditional insurance.
  • Accessibility: Perfect for patients without dental insurance, retirees who have lost employer-sponsored coverage and families seeking an affordable alternative to traditional insurance.
  • Peace of Mind: Consistent, affordable care helps prevent costly dental problems before they arise.
  • Simplicity: No deductibles, waiting periods, or claim forms—just straightforward savings and care.
  • Added Bonus : A PDA Membership Plan is an eligible expense for most Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA). FSAs are special savings account that allows you to use pre-tax funds to cover qualified medical and dental expenses, helping you save money while managing your healthcare costs. By using an FSA card to pay for a membership plan, patients enjoy an added advantage of pre-tax savings.

How It Works

Our membership plan is structured to cover your preventive and routine care while also providing discounts on additional services. You simply pay an annual or monthly fee – think of it as a subscription plan for your dental health, designed to keep your smile healthy while providing predictable, manageable dental costs.

Elect from a variety of plans tailored to your needs, including options for children, teens, adults and periodontal maintenance care. Choose a discounted one-time annual rate or a convenient monthly plan. You can sign up online using our secure online portal, powered by DentalHQ, which accepts all major credit/debit cards and most FSA cards.
