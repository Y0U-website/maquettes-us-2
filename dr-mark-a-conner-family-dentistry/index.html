The practice

During your first visit to our office, you will meet Dr. Conner and your dental hygienist to discuss your goals. You will receive a thorough exam and cleaning, along with x-rays. Dr. Conner will then create a treatment plan specifically tailored to your needs.

We accept most dental insurance programs including Delta Premier, Medigold, Superior, Careington, Careington 500, Guardian, Cigna, DHA, Metlife, Anthem 300, Premera, Coresource, Meritain, and EBMC.

Our goal is not to let the expense prevent you from obtaining the quality care and service you deserve. We strive to maximize our patients’ insurance benefits, and work closely with those who do not have dental insurance. We also gladly accept Cash, Checks, Visa, MasterCard, and Discover.
