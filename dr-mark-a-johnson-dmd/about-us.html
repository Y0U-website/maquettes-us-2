Our Team

The Team

From dental hygienists and dental assistants to our lab technicians and office staff, we show that the best customer experience comes from a genuine care and concern. Our goal is to ensure every appointment is comfortable, relieves any discomfort or gives you the smile you’ve always wanted!

Over the past 17 years, Dr. Johnson has continued his educational training from the Misch, Dawson, and Kois Institute. During his time away from patient care, Dr. Johnson enjoys spending time with his family and numerous hobbies including kiteboarding, sailing, boating, and playing piano.

Dr. Sarah Smith, DMD Dr. Sarah Smith is a graduate of Florida State University. She then interned in our office for a year before completing her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at the University of Florida. She was raised in Tarpon Springs and is passionate about serving her community and helping patients improve their oral health and achieve their ideal smiles.

Our Services

Preventative Care & Cosmetic Dentistry

Preventative care is the key to a healthy smile. Regular checkups allow us to treat issues before they become a problem. But whether it’s elective or for treatment, we’ll make sure your smile is both healthy and beautiful!

Emergency Care & Oral Surgery

All teeth are different, and accidents happen. Whether your teeth are crooked, chipped, damaged or if you’ve had mouth trauma, we are here to relieve your pain and restore your healthy smile.
