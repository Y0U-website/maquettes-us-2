What’s It Like to Get Dental Implants?

When you come to us at Hyannis Dental Partners, the first thing we will do if you are looking to get dental implants is to assess your oral health and make sure you are a good candidate for the procedure. In most cases, the patient is eligible for dental implants so long as you are in good health and have good bone density in the jaw. Even if you have oral health issues or bone density issues, there are still options. The dentist can fix your problems, and a bone graft can correct bone density issues.

Once you’re ready for your implants, the first thing that happens is a titanium post is surgically implanted for each tooth you’re getting put in. This is the anchor point for the prosthetic tooth. The abutment and then the prosthesis is installed, and once the surgery site heals and the implant grows to become part of the jaw, your new implant or implants will feel just like natural teeth, only these teeth are tougher and stronger.
