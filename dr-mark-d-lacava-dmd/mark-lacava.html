Mark LaCava

Dr Mark LaCava is originally from Wilmington, MA. He attended St John’s Preparatory school in Danvers, MA followed by Boston College in Chestnut Hill, MA. Dr LaCava graduated cum laude from Boston College in 1994. He went on to attended Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, MA. While at Tufts, Dr LaCava was awarded the Merit Scholarship for academic excellence. He also received a Prosthodontic Award for excellence in prosthetic dentistry. Dr LaCava graduated from Tufts in 1999.

Dr LaCava began his private practice in Chatham, MA. In 2002, Dr LaCava relocated his practice and joined Hyannis Dental Partners. He is an active member of the American Dental Society , Massachusetts Dental Society , and Cape Cod District Dental Society.

In 2011, Dr LaCava was proud to graduate from the London Institute for Implant Dentistry. Dr LaCava is a general dentist with special interest in endodontics (root canals), prosthetic dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, and implant dentistry both implant placement and restoration.

Outside of the office, Dr LaCava is an avid kitesurfer and windsurfer. He has a daughter, Addison, with his wife, Dr Dawn Ackermann, and is a devoted father and husband.
