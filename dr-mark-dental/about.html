Meet your doctor

Get to know Dr. Mark Breinholt

Dr. Mark Breinholt brings a thoughtful, patient-centered approach to dentistry rooted in quality care, clear communication, and genuine connection. In just three years of practice, he has built a reputation for combining excellent clinical work with a calm, approachable bedside manner that helps patients feel comfortable and cared for throughout their visit.

Dr. Mark earned his Bachelor of Science in Microbiology from Brigham Young University before completing his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Utah School of Dentistry. Dedicated to continually improving his craft, he completed more than 100 hours of continuing education within his first year after dental school, what he jokingly calls his “à la carte residency.”

His passion for dentistry began through job shadowing and a fascination with the human body, but what continues to inspire him most is the ability to make a meaningful impact on people’s lives. Whether helping someone rebuild a broken-down smile or simply restoring comfort and confidence, Dr. Mark loves seeing patients leave able to smile, eat, and feel like themselves again.

He especially enjoys restorative dentistry, including crowns, fillings, and dental implants, and is passionate about delivering work that looks natural, feels comfortable, and lasts. Just as important to him is the experience patients have along the way, which is why he prioritizes respectful communication, honesty, and helping patients feel informed without ever feeling rushed.

Outside the office, Dr. Mark enjoys spending time with his wife and two children, taking his kids to the park, hiking, mountain biking, rock climbing, water skiing, listening to audiobooks, playing strategy games, and watching a good movie. Now settled in Bellingham, he and his family enjoy making the most of everything the Pacific Northwest has to offer. That balance between adventure, family, and personal connection is reflected in the warm, approachable atmosphere he strives to create for every patient.

What new patients can expect

Your first visit

From the moment you walk through our doors, our goal is to make your first visit feel easy, comfortable, and informative. We’ll take time to understand your needs, answer your questions, and clearly explain any findings so you feel confident moving forward. Depending on your visit, your appointment may include an exam, X-rays, and a cleaning, along with personalized recommendations tailored to your smile.

Patient Experience At Dr. Mark Dental, we believe great dentistry is about more than quality treatment — it’s about how you feel throughout the experience. We take the time to listen, explain your options clearly, and make sure you feel comfortable and confident every step of the way in a private, welcoming environment designed around personal attention and genuine care.

What to Bring Please bring the following items to help make your first visit go as smoothly as possible:

Your insurance information, if applicable

Any completed patient forms, if provided in advance

A list of current medications and health information you would like the doctor to know

A focus on what matters most

Private, cozy office setting

Our office has a warm, private feel designed to make visits feel more comfortable and less intimidating. Tucked into a quiet corner suite, the space feels welcoming and personal — creating an experience that’s calm, approachable, and centered around genuine one-on-one care.

Beautiful communal building space

The building’s shared space features beautiful live trees and greenery that help create a peaceful, inviting first impression. Thoughtful details throughout the environment add to the relaxed, comfortable feel of each visit.

Reading materials in the office

We keep a selection of reading materials in the office so patients have something simple and enjoyable to browse while they wait for their appointment.
