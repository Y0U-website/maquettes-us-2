Meet the Team

As a scheduling coordinator, I ensure that our patient’s appointments run smoothly. I always try to accommodate our patients’ busy lives with the best time and date available within our schedule. My husband Art and I enjoy lake life and have two adult daughters, Kristen and Lindsay. We also have two granddaughters that we love to spoil, but I am looking forward to the day when there are more! In my spare time, I enjoy relaxing on the lake and tending to my garden. Trouble with your tomatoes? Talk to me!

As one of Dr. Nadeau’s assistants, I help with all aspects of patient care from taking impressions, intra-oral scans and X-rays to changing wires and teaching proper oral hygiene. I enjoy the variety that each day brings and the interesting patients that share their beautiful smiles with us. My husband Paul and I have two daughters, Olivia and Ivy. In my free time, I enjoy travel, hiking, gardening, cooking, and vacations to visit family in England!

I previously worked as an assistant in a pediatric practice for many years, and I am thrilled to be a part of the Nadeau Ortho family and to be able to use my orthodontic training to assist you with your smile! I am a new dog mom and love snuggling with her, but her big horse brother is annoyed he has to share his mom. Teaching therapeutic horseback riding lessons in my leisure time is something I really like doing. I also like to craft, read a good book on my front porch while drinking a cup of coffee and spend time with friends and family.

As a clinical assistant, I help Dr. Nadeau with every aspect of the patient’s treatment from taking X-rays to bringing them back to the office chair. I love my job and the reward of seeing each patient transform into a healthier, happier, and more confident individual. My husband Tom and I have two kids, Jack and Sara. I am an avid nature lover, and when I’m not heading to a sporting event with the kids, I enjoy hiking the mountain trails all over Maine and New Hampshire with my dog Wilson.
