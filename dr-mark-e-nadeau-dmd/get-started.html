First Complimentary Visit

During your first appointment, you will meet our experts, learn about orthodontics, have a comprehensive clinical exam, and choose which treatment choice is best for you. We will also review your dental history, take some x-rays, create a custom treatment plan, and go over the financial aspect of treatment.

Financing & Insurance

Orthodontic treatment costs vary according to your requirements and our treatment strategy. Our team will explain in detail the treatment costs and payment options so you can make the best decision for you and your smile. We believe that all patients deserve the finest orthodontic treatment to achieve a healthy and radiant smile, so we do our best to make treatments as affordable as possible. Our office works with all major credit cards and insurance companies, and we also offer flexible payment plans so that you and your family can receive the dental care you deserve.

We offer an online platform that enables you to get account details anytime, make payments, obtain receipts, and more. If you do not have a patient login yet, let us know at your next visit, and we will happily assist you in creating one.

Patient Forms
