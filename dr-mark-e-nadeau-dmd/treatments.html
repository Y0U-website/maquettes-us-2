Dentofacial Orthopedics

The fact our doctor does "orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics" might have caught your eye. While most patients know what orthodontics is about, many aren’t familiar with the term "dentofacial orthopedics". This type of treatment is crucial for the correct development of your children’s smile, so it is vital to understand the importance of orthopedics on time.

Unlike orthodontics, which regulates tooth movement, dentofacial orthopedics focuses on the growth and development of the face throughout infancy. Dentofacial orthopedics uses devices that guide the development of facial structures, so if the orthodontist detects any abnormality from an early age, he can intercept this problem with an orthopedic appliance. This early treatment prevents more severe skeletal problems that are complex to solve when the patient is a teenager or an adult.

Orthopedic devices usually are combined with traditional braces. This enables a controlled development of facial structures and the eruption of permanent teeth. Dr. Nadeau has vast experience in both orthodontic and dentofacial orthopedic treatment, so do not hesitate to bring your child for a complimentary consultation!

Surgical Orthodontics

Surgical orthodontics, also known as orthognathic surgery, is a treatment that combines traditional orthodontics with major oral surgery. It is used to treat, jaw bone anomalies, asymmetries, and severe malocclusion. This treatment is carried out by a team of professionals, which includes an orthodontist and an orthognathic surgeon. If you require surgical intervention, Nadeau Orthodontics will coordinate with an orthognathic surgeon, and Dr. Nadeau will work hand in hand with him to ensure optimum results.

This type of treatment can only be performed in adults. They are usually indicated after the age of 18, when most women and men stop growing their jaws. It starts with traditional orthodontic treatment (braces), and when the orthodontist agrees with the surgeon that it is time for intervention, the patient gets ready for surgery. When the patient recovers from surgery, the orthodontic treatment continues and braces move teeth into the final place to finalize the treatment.

For people who cannot get a beautiful and healthy smile with traditional treatment, orthognathic surgery is the only way to achieve oral health and aesthetic goals. Orthognathic surgery can help you improve your self-esteem and leave you smiling with confidence for years to come.
