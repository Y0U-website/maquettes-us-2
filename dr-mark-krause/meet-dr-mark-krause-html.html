Meet Dr Mark Krause | Dr. Mark Krause | Krause Smiles

During Dr. Mark’s undergrad at Salisbury University he trained under the tutelage of his father, becoming a graduate of SU in 1999. He encouraged his passion for dentistry and treating children of all ages. He then completed a two year General Practice Residency at the University of Rochester/Strong Memorial Hospital. During his second year, Dr. Mark focused his training treating both children and patients with special needs in a clinical, as well as a hospital setting. Ultimately, he received his dental degree from Howard University College of Dentistry.

Dr. Mark is a past president of the Eastern Shore Dental Society. He is a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Southeastern Society of Pediatric Dentistry, American Dental Association, Maryland State Dental Association, The Salisbury Dental Study Club and the Gnathos Orthodontic Study Club. He continues to educate himself and keeps up to date with evidence-based dentistry on treatment of children by attending extensive courses throughout the year.

Dr. Mark and his wife, Kara, grew up locally and currently reside in Salisbury with their dog Wellington. Upon their return to the area in 2008, Dr. Mark initially practiced with Dr. Jerry. From 2011 until his return to the practice Dr. Mark served the community as a staff dentist for Three Lower Counties (TLC) in Princess Anne, Maryland. He was proud to be the dental director from February of 2013 to October of 2015. Dr. Mark and his wife enjoy spending time with family and friends. They have an interest in music, biking, the beach and travel.
