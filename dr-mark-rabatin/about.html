Dr. Mark Rabatin

Dr. Rabatin is originally from the Uniontown, PA area and returned home in 1989 to begin practicing dentistry. He took over the practice established by Dr. Linsey Howard, later moving into his current state-of-the-art location in 2007. With decades of hands-on experience, Dr. Rabatin offers a full range of dental services — from preventive care to cosmetic and restorative treatments.

He believes in blending the fine art and science of dentistry, helping patients feel confident in their smiles while prioritizing overall oral health. Patients appreciate his approachable style, commitment to continuing education, and dedication to creating a comfortable experience at every appointment.
