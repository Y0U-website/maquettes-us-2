Mark Rabatin Completes Surgical Fellowship at Resnik Implant Institute

Uniontown, PA – Mark Rabatin, DMD, of 160 Wayland Smith Dr., Suite 202, Uniontown, PA 15401, has successfully completed a prestigious Surgical Fellowship at the Resnik Implant Institute . This advanced training marks a significant achievement in his career and further elevates the level of care available to his patients.

  • Sep 22 — 2025

Advanced Training in Dental Implants

The Resnik Implant Institute is internationally recognized for its comprehensive education in implant dentistry, led by Dr. Randolph Resnik, one of the foremost authorities in the field. The Surgical Fellowship is designed to provide intensive, hands-on training in all aspects of implant placement, bone grafting, sinus augmentation, and other surgical techniques critical to modern restorative dentistry.

By completing this fellowship, Dr. Rabatin has expanded his expertise in complex implant procedures, allowing him to provide patients with cutting-edge treatment options that improve function, aesthetics, and long-term oral health.

Commitment to Excellence in Patient Care

Earning this fellowship reflects Dr. Rabatin’s ongoing commitment to professional development and his dedication to offering the highest quality dental care in Uniontown and the surrounding region. With advanced training from the Resnik Implant Institute, he is uniquely positioned to provide patients with innovative solutions for tooth replacement and oral rehabilitation.

Dr. Rabatin’s accomplishment underscores not only his clinical skill but also his dedication to keeping pace with the latest advancements in dentistry. Patients can feel confident knowing their dental health is in the hands of a practitioner committed to excellence, education, and compassionate care.
