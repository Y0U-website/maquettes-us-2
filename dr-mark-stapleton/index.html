Because dental emergencies can vary so greatly, pricing can too. For example, something as minor as dental sensitivity may be able to be easily addressed at home with special toothpaste, while a severe toothache may require a root canal and dental crown. These two situations would drastically differ in pricing.

If you’re concerned about treatment costs, let our team know when you arrive. We’ll be sure to keep you updated about your coverage, do complimentary benefits checks so you’re aware of what you’ll be expected to pay, and do everything we can to keep you in the loop.
