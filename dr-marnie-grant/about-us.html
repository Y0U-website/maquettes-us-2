Meet Our Team

Welcome to our office! Our dentist and team are here for you when visiting our office. We pride ourselves on being patient focused, caring, experienced, professional and knowledgeable. Our goal is to help you achieve optimal oral and overall health. You are in good hands while visiting Dr. Grant! If you have any questions about dental care in Chelsea, Michigan, please give us a call today at 734-475-1866.

Dr. Marnie Grant is a dedicated and experienced dentist who has been serving the Chelsea, Michigan, community and surrounding areas for over 25 years. A 1996 graduate of the University of Michigan, she is passionate about providing high-quality dental care in a welcoming and supportive environment.

Committed to continuous learning, Dr. Grant stays at the forefront of modern dentistry by actively participating in advanced training, professional seminars, study clubs, and dental organizations. Her expertise includes implant restorations, TMJ evaluation, bite splint therapy, and prosthodontic restorations, ensuring comprehensive care for her patients. She is a proud member of the American Dental Association® and the Michigan Dental Association.

Dr. Grant believes in a personalized approach to dentistry, working closely with each patient to create individualized treatment plans that promote healthy, beautiful smiles. She prioritizes education, quality treatments, and mutual respect, fostering lasting relationships with her patients built on trust and compassionate care.
