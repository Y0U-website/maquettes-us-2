Cheney

Marshall Gibbs, DDS

Dr. Marshall Gibbs is the Founder and CEO of Mint Condition Dental, which opened in January 2014 in Cheney, WA. Mint Condition Dental has grown to 4 locations: Cheney, Colfax, Pullman, and Liberty Lake.

Marshall is from the small town of Culdesac, Idaho, where he grew up on a dryland farm and cattle ranch. He appreciates the Inland Northwest for its four seasons, easy-going lifestyle, and small communities. Upon graduating from the University of Idaho (2007), Marshall attended the University of Iowa College of Dentistry and graduated in 2011. He enjoys all aspects of dentistry, including pediatrics, orthodontics, jaw pain (Botox), facial esthetics, sedation, oral surgery, endodontics (root canals), and implant dentistry.

Marshall’s passion is to provide communities with the best dental experience by providing exceptional customer service, comprehensive care, and one-visit dental appointments. He believes the foundation for providing this experience is developing an aligned company culture with consistent training and development, and utilizing the most advanced technology.

Outside the office, Marshall enjoys spending time with his wife and their two young boys. As a fifth-generation farmer, he enjoys remaining active on the family wheat farm. Whether it’s skiing in the winter or working the land in the summer, he values time outdoors and making memories with his family.

John Wesley, DDS

Dr. John Wesley grew up in a small town in Alberta, Canada. There, he attended Canadian University College and earned his bachelor’s degree in Biology and Chemistry.

He then went to Loma Linda University School of Dentistry in California and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery in 2008. Dr. Wesley added to his dental education with a dental residency here in Washington alongside his wife and 3 children.

Dr. Wesley started dentistry in public health so he could provide excellent care to the underserved in the community. He volunteered for veterans and hygiene students at EWU, was the Dental Director for the non-profit clinic run by the Spokane District Dental Society Foundation and was part of SDDSF and WSDA non-profit foundations that assist people at the local and state level. In his free time, he enjoys playing games with his children and stay-cations with family.

Pullman

Dakota Baugus, DDS

Dr. Dakota Baugus is the Pullman practice owner. He grew up in small-town Pottsboro, Texas. After graduating from Pottsboro High School, he attended Austin College, where he received his BA degree. He married his high school sweetheart, and they both moved to Dallas, Texas, where he attended Texas A&M University College of Dentistry and received his DDS degree.

Dr. Baugus and his wife needed to leave the hustle and bustle of big-city living behind and wanted to get out of the triple-digit heat in Texas. They both fell in love with the nature and beauty that Washington State had to offer during their travels, thus leading to a 4-day drive to Pullman, Washington.

Dakota enjoys meeting new people and creating personal connections, which was one of the many aspects of dentistry that he fell in love with. He is dedicated to helping each patient achieve better health and improved self-confidence. Patients are very comfortable and relaxed in his care, knowing they will receive a personal and fulfilling experience each visit.

When Dakota is not in the office, he likes to spend his time hiking, snowboarding, and fishing with his wife. He is also an avid hockey fan and is looking forward to the expansion team coming in 2021, but will always be a Dallas Stars fan. He and his wife enjoy the outdoor lifestyle and are looking forward to their new journey in Washington State.

Colton Erho, DDS

Born and raised in East Wenatchee, Washington, Dr. Colton Erho is proud to care for patients in the community he now calls home. He earned his dental degree from the University of Washington and is passionate about creating comfortable, positive experiences for patients of all ages.

For Dr. Erho, dentistry is about much more than fixing teeth—it is about helping people feel like themselves again. Whether he is relieving a patient’s pain, helping a child feel safe and confident at the dentist, or restoring a smile that changes the way someone feels, those moments are what make his work truly meaningful.

Outside the office, Dr. Erho enjoys spending time with his wife, daughter, and dog. He also loves getting outdoors and going fishing.
