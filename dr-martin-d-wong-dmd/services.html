Cosmetic Dentistry

Wouldn’t it be nice if your smile looked as beautiful as that of your favorite celebrity? Wong Family Dentistry will make it happen. If you are even slightly disappointed in the look of your smile, reach out to us today at (510) 222-4178 to schedule a consultation. Our team whitens teeth, straightens out crooked teeth and conceals compromised teeth with lovely veneers. In short, we will transform your smile from a weakness into a strength.

Our teeth whitening service is particularly popular. We whiten teeth upwards of several shades, eliminating those unsightly stains and yellowing. In fact, it merely takes a single appointment to whiten teeth by several shades. Our porcelain veneers are also quite popular as they conceal tooth imperfections such as stains, damage, misalignment and gaps. Porcelain veneers are thin yet durable shells that look just like regular teeth.

Our cosmetic dentistry options also include dental bonding. Bonding corrects tooth flaws without busting your budget. Whether you have misshapen teeth, dislike the color of your teeth or have gaps in between your teeth, our dental bonding will beautify your smile. We use composite bonding material that matches the natural tooth hue so it blends in perfectly with the rest of the teeth.

General Dentistry

General dentistry at Wong Family Dentistry is centered on preventing oral health problems with proactive exams, cleanings, treatments and procedures. We recommend everyone schedule an appointment with our practice at least once every six months for general dentistry services. Meet with our dentist and we will develop a dental hygiene plan specific to your unique needs. We perform in-depth dental examinations including the use of x-rays to identify cavities between the teeth.
