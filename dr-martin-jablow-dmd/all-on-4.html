Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 implants provide several advantages over traditional dentures and other tooth replacement options.

Full Smile Restoration

Restore a complete, natural-looking smile with one comprehensive treatment.

Improved Stability & Comfort

Unlike removable dentures, All-on-4 implants stay securely in place, allowing you to eat and speak with confidence.

Faster Treatment Timeline

In many cases, temporary teeth can be placed the same day as your implant procedure.

Bone Preservation

Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss and maintaining facial structure.

Long-Lasting Results

With proper care, All-on-4 implants can last for many years, making them a reliable long-term investment.

The All-on-4 Procedure

Consultation and Planning

Your journey begins with a comprehensive exam and consultation. Advanced imaging such as 3D CT Scanning is used to plan precise implant placement.

Implant Placement

Four dental implants are strategically placed in the jawbone to maximize support and stability.

Temporary Teeth Placement

In many cases, a temporary set of teeth is placed on the same day, allowing you to leave with a functional smile.

Final Restoration

After healing, your final custom-designed teeth are securely attached, providing a permanent and natural-looking result.
