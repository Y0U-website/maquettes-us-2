Types of Dental Implant Treatments

Single Tooth Implants

A single dental implant replaces one missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth. The implant is topped with a custom dental crown designed to match your natural teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridges

For patients missing several teeth in a row, implant-supported bridges can restore function and appearance without the need for traditional bridges.

Learn more about our Dental Bridges treatment options.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Patients missing most or all of their teeth may benefit from implant-supported dentures, which offer greater stability than traditional dentures.

Our practice also provides Full & Partial Dentures for patients exploring multiple tooth replacement options.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

The All-on-4 implant system uses four implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth. This advanced treatment offers a stable, permanent alternative to traditional dentures.

The Dental Implant Procedure

The dental implant process typically involves several stages to ensure long-term success.

Initial Consultation

Your dentist will perform a comprehensive exam and review your oral health. Advanced imaging such as 3D CT Scanning may be used to evaluate bone density and plan precise implant placement.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone where it will function as a replacement tooth root.

Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant integrates with the surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration.

Restoration Placement

Once healing is complete, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant to restore your smile.

Are dental implants painful?
