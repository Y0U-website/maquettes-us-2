Meet the Doctors Dr. Martin Jablow Dr. Rob Antmann Dr. Jonathan Tiger Dr. Rinil R. Patel Dr. Jennifer Kantor Dr. Jablow is America’s Dental Technology Coach.

He graduated from Rutgers University and University of Medicine and Dentistry of N.J. For over 20 years, Dr. Jablow has participated with the Middlesex County Peer Review Committee, which mediates disputes between patients and dentists over the quality of their dental care.

Dr. Rob Antmann attended New York College of Dentistry where he received his D.D.S. (Doctor of Dental Surgery) degree in 1982. He spent the next four years as a commissioned officer in the United States Public Health Service during which he was awarded an Outstanding Service Medal. Dr. Antmann also lectured during these years on both practice management and forensic dentistry.

Dr. Jonathan Tiger received his Dental Degree from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey in 2002. He continued his education doing a General Practice Residency at Coler-Goldwater Memorial Hospital in New York and spent three years studying periodontology at Nova Southeastern University in Florida.

Dr. Jennifer Kantor graduated from Rutgers University with a Bachelors of Science in finance. She completed a post-baccalaureate program at William Paterson University. She attended Rutgers School of Dental Medicine, graduating with a DMD. She completed a General Practice Residency at Jersey City Medical Center, where she was chief dental resident.

Dr. Kantor is a member of the American Dental Association, New Jersey Dental Association, and the Middlesex County Dental Association.
