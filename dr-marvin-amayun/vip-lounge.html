VIP Lounge

At Smile Creations Dental, we are committed to excellence by providing personalized service. In our office, you should not feel like you’re at the dentist. We try our best to minimize the clinical atmosphere of the dental office to make it a relaxing and enjoyable experience for patients. We want you to feel at home.

For those dental guests who need more of that extra comfort and privacy, we have a dedicated lounge with concierge service and amenities for you to relax while you wait.

Guests have computer and internet access, large flat screen with Web TV, private phone, and music source. We also offer computer tablets for reading or internet use. All offered beverage and snacks are complimentary for patients. Some patients may need fluids and source of nutrition and energy before or after their procedure, especially for surgery and long procedures. The lounge also serves as a presentation room for complex treatment plans and procedures through the audio-visual projection
