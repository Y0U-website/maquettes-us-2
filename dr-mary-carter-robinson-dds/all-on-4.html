How long does it take to complete the All-on-4 implant procedure?

One of the remarkable advantages of All-on-4 dental implants is the speed at which the procedure can be completed. Are All-on-4 dental implants a permanent solution for missing teeth?

All-on-4 dental implants offer a permanent and long-lasting solution for missing teeth. The titanium implants fuse with your natural bone, creating a stable and durable foundation for your new teeth. With proper care and maintenance, All-on-4 implants can give you a beautiful smile for many years.
