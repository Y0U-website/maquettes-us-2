Are Dental Implants Permanent?

One of the most significant advantages of dental implants is their permanence. Unlike some other tooth replacement options like dentures, dental implants are designed to last a lifetime with proper care. They consist of three main components: the implant, the abutment, and the crown. The implant is a titanium post that fuses with your jawbone, creating a stable foundation for your replacement tooth. This integration with the bone ensures longevity, making dental implants a lasting investment in your oral health.

Who is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a versatile solution for individuals with missing teeth, but not everyone is an ideal candidate. Our skilled oral surgeons will assess your oral health, bone density, and overall medical history to determine your suitability for dental implants. Generally, candidates should have good oral hygiene, sufficient bone structure, and overall health. Even if you&#x27;ve been told you&#x27;re not a candidate in the past, advances in implant dentistry may now make this option available to you.

How Long Does the Dental Implant Procedure Take?

The duration of the dental implant procedure can vary based on individual factors, such as the number of implants needed, the condition of your jawbone, and your overall health. On average, the process can take several months, involving implant placement, osseointegration (the fusion of the implant with your bone), and the attachment of the final crown. However, the result is well worth the time investment, as you&#x27;ll enjoy a strong, natural-looking smile that functions just like your original teeth.

Do Dental Implants Look and Feel Like Natural Teeth?

Dental implants are designed to mimic the look and feel of natural teeth closely. The custom-made crowns are carefully crafted to match the color, shape, and size of your existing teeth, ensuring a seamless and aesthetic blend with your smile. Moreover, because dental implants are anchored in your jawbone, they provide the same biting and chewing capabilities as your natural teeth, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods without worry.

What is the Cost of Dental Implant Surgery?

The cost of dental implant surgery can vary depending on several factors, including the number of implants required, any necessary preliminary procedures (like bone grafting), and your choice of materials for the crown. During your consultation at Maryland Oral Surgery Associates, we&#x27;ll provide you with a personalized treatment plan and a clear breakdown of the costs involved.
