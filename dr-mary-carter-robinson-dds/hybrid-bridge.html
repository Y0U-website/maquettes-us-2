Maintaining your fixed hybrid bridges is essential to ensure their longevity and your oral health. Here are some practices to follow:

  • Oral Hygiene: Brush and floss regularly to prevent plaque buildup around the implants and bridge. Consider using antimicrobial mouthwash to maintain oral hygiene.
  • Regular Dental Visits: Schedule routine check-ups with our experts at Maryland Oral Surgery Associates. Our team will monitor the condition of your fixed hybrid bridges and address any issues promptly.

Who is a Suitable Candidate for Fixed Hybrid Bridges?

Fixed hybrid bridges are an excellent option for individuals who:

  • Have several missing teeth
  • Are looking for a permanent solution to tooth loss
  • Have sufficient jawbone density to support dental implants
  • Seek a stable and comfortable alternative to traditional dentures

Do Fixed Hybrid Bridges Require Any Special Care or Maintenance?

Yes, fixed hybrid bridges do require specific care to ensure their longevity. Proper oral hygiene practices, as mentioned earlier, are crucial. Additionally, avoid biting on hard objects or using your teeth as tools to prevent damage to the prosthetic.

How Does the Cost of Fixed Hybrid Bridges Compare?
