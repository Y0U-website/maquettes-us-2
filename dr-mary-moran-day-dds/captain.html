Dr. Day and her team participate in Give Kids A Smile each February. GKAS is a charity that provides less privileged children in our area with the necessary dental care.

Dr. Day and her husband, Travis are both long-time residents of Morehead City. Travis is a Professor of Strategy & Entrepreneurship at the Kenan-Flagler Business School , University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. They have two little girls: Dylan and Anderson. Their family enjoys boating, so you may see them on the water most any weekend of the summer.
