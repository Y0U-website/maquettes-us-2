The implant must be allowed about 3-6 months to heal, and during this time the jaw bone will form around the implant in a process called osseointegration. During this healing time you can have temporary crowns installed so that you can eat and speak normally and maintain a proper esthetic appearance for your smile.

After the implant has healed it is time for Dr. Day to restore the area with a new, permanent crown. A mold will be taken of your implant and surrounding teeth and sent to a lab for fabrication. It usually takes about 2 weeks for the lab to custom make your crown. The crown will be cemented to the implant and you can start using it immediately! Periodontal (gum) disease is an infection of the tissues that hold your teeth in place and affects approximately 50 % of the adult population . It’s typically caused by poor brushing and flossing habits that allow plaque—a sticky film of bacteria—to build up on the teeth and harden. A periodontal examination is used to assess the health of your gums and teeth. It can help Dr. Day diagnose the gum diseases gingivitis and periodontitis . It also can reveal receding gums, exposed roots, tooth grinding ( bruxism ) and other problems.

A Per iodontal Probe is an instrument used to measure the depth of the sulcus or free space around the tooth. The sulcus is like a collar around the tooth and in a normal healthy mouth the depth of the sulcus is usually 1 to 3 millimeters deep.
