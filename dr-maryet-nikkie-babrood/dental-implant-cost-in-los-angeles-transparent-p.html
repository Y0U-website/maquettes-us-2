Dental implant pricing in Los Angeles varies significantly — from $2,500 for a budget implant from a high-volume chain to over $6,000 for a premium single-tooth restoration at a boutique practice. Understanding what drives cost helps you compare apples to apples and choose the right provider.

The total cost of a dental implant typically includes three components: the implant post itself (the titanium or zirconia screw placed in the bone), the abutment (the connector between post and crown), and the implant crown (the visible tooth-colored restoration). Each component may be billed separately.

Most dental insurance plans do not cover dental implants, classifying them as “cosmetic.” However, if tooth loss resulted from an accident or medically necessary extraction, your medical insurance may provide partial coverage. Some PPO dental plans offer partial coverage for the implant crown. Frequently Asked Questions

How much do dental implants cost in Los Angeles?

A single dental implant in Los Angeles typically costs between $3,000 and $5,500 for titanium, or $3,500 to $6,500 for zirconia (ceramic). The total includes the implant post, abutment, and crown. Additional costs may apply if bone grafting or sinus lifting is needed. Rite Dentist provides a complete itemized quote at your consultation — no hidden fees.

Are dental implants worth the cost?

Dental implants are the only tooth replacement option that preserves jawbone density, prevents adjacent tooth shifting, and functions identically to a natural tooth. Bridges and dentures need replacement every 7 to 15 years; implants last 25+ years with proper care. When compared over a 20-year period, implants are often the most cost-effective option — particularly when factoring in the bone loss and structural changes that occur with other solutions.

Does dental insurance cover implants?

Most dental insurance plans do not cover implant posts, classifying them as cosmetic. Some plans partially cover the crown component. If tooth loss resulted from accident or medically necessary extraction, medical insurance may provide coverage. Rite Dentist provides thorough insurance documentation to maximize your reimbursement and offers transparent payment arrangements.

What is a bone graft and when is it needed?

A bone graft adds bone volume to areas of the jaw where it has been lost due to tooth extraction, gum disease, or trauma. Sufficient bone volume is required for an implant post to integrate properly. If a bone graft is needed, it is typically performed at the time of extraction or as a separate procedure, adding 3 to 6 months to the overall timeline and $400 to $3,000 to the cost depending on volume required.

How long does the implant process take?

The typical single-tooth implant process takes 3 to 6 months from placement to final crown. After the implant post is placed, a healing period of 3 to 4 months allows osseointegration (bone fusion). The abutment and crown are placed at a second appointment. Same-day or immediate loading options exist in specific clinical situations — ask Dr. Babrood whether you qualify.
