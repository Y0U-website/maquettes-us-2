She received her dental degree from the USC Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry, one of the most respected dental schools in the western United States, training that established her foundation in evidence-based, patient-centered care.

What distinguishes Dr. Babrood is not just where she trained, but what she chose to do with that training. Most dental practices focus on procedures. Dr. Babrood built a practice around relationships, transparency, and whole-body health.

Credentials and Professional Memberships

DDS — Doctor of Dental Surgery Doctoral degree in dentistry from the USC Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry. USC Dental is consistently ranked among the top dental schools in California and the western U.S., with a curriculum emphasizing both clinical excellence and research-informed care.

FICD — Fellow, International College of Dentistry Fellowship in the International College of Dentistry is one of the most prestigious honors in the dental profession. Founded in 1920, the ICD is the world’s oldest and largest international dental honor society, with more than 12,000 fellows in over 140 countries. Fellowship is by invitation only, awarded to dentists who demonstrate outstanding professional achievement, meritorious service to dentistry, and dedication to advancing the field. Dr. Babrood’s FICD credential places her among dentistry’s most recognized practitioners worldwide.

IAOMT SMART Certified Certification from the International Academy of Oral Medicine and Toxicology for the Safe Mercury Amalgam Removal Technique. SMART certification requires completing IAOMT coursework on mercury toxicology and safety protocols, passing written assessments, and maintaining specific equipment standards. It is the highest available credential for mercury-safe amalgam removal — verifying that a dentist follows a proven protocol to protect patients, staff, and the environment during removal.

Board Member, San Fernando Valley Dental Society Service on the San Fernando Valley Dental Society board reflects Dr. Babrood’s commitment to the professional community she practices within. Board membership involves advocacy for patient protections, professional standards, and continuing education for dentists throughout the Valley.

Dr. Babrood’s Philosophy

At Rite Dentist, Dr. Babrood practices what she calls whole-body dentistry — the belief that oral health cannot be separated from systemic health. That means using only biocompatible materials (no mercury, no BPA, no fluoride in hygiene protocols), applying IAOMT SMART protocol for every amalgam removal, evaluating airway and sleep health alongside dental health, and taking the time to actually explain what she finds and why it matters.
