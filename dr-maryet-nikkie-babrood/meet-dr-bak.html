Meet Dr. Neema Bak, Periodontist in Valley Village

Dr. Bak is a specialist in Periodontology and Implant Dentistry — the visiting implant authority at Rite Dentist for advanced bone, gum, and implant work.

He earned his DDS and PhD from Florida State University, where his research focused on Bone Biology — the foundation of every successful implant. He completed subspecialty training at the University of Southern California (USC), and currently serves as Director of the Biomedical Implants and Tissue Engineering (BITE) program at USC’s School of Dentistry.

At Rite Dentist, Dr. Bak places dental implants using 3D CT-guided planning and biocompatible zirconia and titanium options. He is widely recognized as a leading dental implantologist in Valley Village and across Los Angeles.

Research, Teaching, and Languages
