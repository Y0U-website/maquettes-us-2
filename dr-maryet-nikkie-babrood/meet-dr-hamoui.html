Meet Dr. Naji Hamoui, General Dentist in Valley Village

Dr. Hamoui is Rite Dentist’s lead general and cosmetic dentist — the doctor behind cleanings, crowns, veneers, whitening, extractions, and most of what you’ll need day to day.

A Burbank native, Dr. Hamoui completed his undergraduate studies at UCLA and earned his DDS from Western University of Health Sciences before returning home to serve the community where he grew up. He has delivered holistic dental care to Valley Village, Burbank, and North Hollywood ever since. His practice is built on an integrative philosophy: every treatment decision accounts for the patient’s full-body health, not just the tooth in front of him.

Dr. Hamoui is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA) and the California Dental Association (CDA). He stays current on advances in biocompatible materials, minimally invasive techniques, and the growing body of research connecting oral health to systemic health.

At Rite Dentist, that means no fluoride treatments, no mercury fillings, no unnecessary procedures — and a genuine conversation about what’s best for you, not what’s fastest to bill.

A Holistic Philosophy, In Practice

Dr. Hamoui doesn’t treat teeth in isolation. He asks about sleep, diet, medications, stress — because the mouth is not separate from the rest of the body. Gum disease is linked to cardiovascular risk. Bite problems can drive jaw pain and headaches. Mercury fillings carry systemic concerns that most conventional practices ignore.

At Rite Dentist, every material used in your treatment is biocompatible. No fluoride, no amalgam, no unnecessary imaging. When a restoration is needed, Dr. Hamoui selects composite, ceramic, or zirconia — materials chosen for how your body responds to them, not just how they look on an X-ray.
