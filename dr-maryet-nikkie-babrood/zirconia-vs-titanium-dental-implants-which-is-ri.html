Not all implants are created equal. Dr. Babrood, DDS, FICD — a Fellow of the International College of Dentistry — offers both zirconia and titanium implants and helps patients choose the right option for their body, health history, and goals. Call 818-766-7776 .

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth — but the debate between zirconia (ceramic) and titanium implants is one of the most important conversations in modern dentistry. Both integrate with the jawbone and can last decades. The differences matter most to patients with metal sensitivities, autoimmune concerns, or a preference for completely biocompatible, metal-free care.

At Rite Dentist, we don’t push one option. We evaluate your bone density, health history, bite mechanics, and personal values before recommending an implant material. Dr. Babrood trained at USC Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry and holds IAOMT certification — the credential that specifically requires training in biocompatible material selection.

Zirconia vs Titanium: A Direct Comparison

Three reasons patients choose zirconia .

No metal in the body

Zirconia contains zero metal — no corrosion, no metal ions leaching into tissue, no galvanic reaction with other dental metals.

Aesthetics at the gum line

Zirconia’s white color means no gray shadow through thin gum tissue — critical for front teeth with delicate gingival margins.

IAOMT-aligned care

IAOMT protocols recommend biocompatible materials. Dr. Babrood’s IAOMT certification and training at USC ensure material selection is guided by science, not trend.
