Meet Our Doctors

Our team is eminently qualified and caring. Using the latest dental technology, we treat our patients with compassion and respect.

Dr. Mehran Massoumi

Dr. Mehran Massoumi attended Syracuse University for his undergraduate education. After completion of his dental degree from New York University College of Dentistry, he served a two-year residency at Beth Israel Medical Center in New York.

He continued his formal education by completing a fellowship at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center. Dr. Massoumi holds the prestigious Mastership in the Academy of General Dentistry and is a member of the American Dental Association, Connecticut Dental Association, and the Middlesex Dental Association.

Dr. Kirsten Guerette

Dr. Kirsten Guerette, a Northern Connecticut native, is dedicated to providing exceptional dental care with a personalized approach. She earned her Bachelor of Science in Biomedical Sciences from Quinnipiac University before obtaining her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. To further refine her skills, she completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at UConn.

Passionate about continuing education, Dr. Guerette stays at the forefront of dental advancements to offer a comprehensive range of treatments, from preventive care to advanced procedures tailored to each patient’s unique needs.

Outside the office, she enjoys camping, skiing, and crafting. Dr. Guerette looks forward to building lasting relationships with her patients and helping them achieve healthy, confident smiles.
