The practice

Imagine Orthodontics has been serving the Omaha and Papillion communities for ten plus years. We have created beautiful smiles all over town. Our highly trained team is committed to providing the best care in an energetic and fun atmosphere.

We offer a large variety of treatments . On your first visit we will acknowledge your treatment needs. Dr Becker will perform a comprehensive clinical examination including x-rays and then explain your treatment plan moving forward.

Our staff will discuss with you the cost of your treatment and each available payment plan option, so that you can make the best choice for you. We will work with you to create a payment plan that fits your budget, and you will know what to expect before treatment.
