The key to dental health is regular hygiene checks and cleaning. Our well qualified hygienist s take the time to make sure your entire mouth is in tip top shape.

Veneers are thin, custom-made shells crafted of tooth-colored materials designed to cover the front side of teeth. They are an option for correcting stained, chipped, decayed or crooked teeth.

A dental crown is a tooth-shaped "cap" that is placed over a tooth -- to cover the tooth to restore its shape and size, strength, and improve its appearance. The crowns, when cemented into place, fully encase the entire visible portion of a tooth that lies at and above the gum line.

A dental implant is an artificial tooth root that is placed into your jaw to hold a replacement tooth, bridge, or dentures. Dental implants may be an option for people who have lost a tooth or teeth due to periodontal disease, an injury, or some other reason.

Invisalign is a clear-aligner treatment. It is an orthodontic device that is in a transparent, plastic form of dental braces used to adjust teeth.
