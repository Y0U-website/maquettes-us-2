The Dental Implant Process

Understanding the process can make treatment feel less overwhelming. Here is what you can expect when having dental implant surgery at Green Lake Oral & Facial Surgery and JAWNT Oral Surgery.

Consultation and Imaging

Your first visit is dedicated to evaluation and education. A 3D CBCT scan shows us the details of your bone, nerves, and surrounding teeth. Together, we will discuss whether implants are right for you and review your options, including sedation, material choices, and whether bone grafting is needed.

Day of Surgery

When you arrive, our team makes sure you are comfortable and cared for before treatment begins. After your chosen sedation takes effect, your surgeon will precisely place the implants using X-guide technology.

Recovery and Follow-Up

Exparel and PRF help reduce discomfort and speed recovery. Most patients return to daily activities within just a few days. You will leave with clear instructions and receive a same-day follow-up call from our team. We remain available to answer questions throughout your healing process.

FAQs

Why should I choose dental implants?

Dental implants are the most natural-feeling and reliable solution for tooth loss. They preserve bone, protect surrounding teeth, and provide stable replacement teeth that look and function like your own.

What is the success rate of implants?

With today’s techniques and technology, dental implants have a 95 % success rate. When properly placed and cared for, they can last for decades or even a lifetime.

How much do dental implants cost?

The cost varies depending on how many implants you need and whether additional procedures like bone grafting or extractions are required. At your consultation, we will provide a detailed treatment plan with a clear estimate and review financing options.

How many teeth can I replace?

Dental implants can replace a single tooth, multiple teeth with a bridge, or a full arch with solutions like All-on-4 or implant-supported dentures.

Can my general dentist place dental implants?

Some general dentists place implants, but oral surgeons are the most qualified specialists for this procedure. Our board-certified oral surgeons have advanced surgical training, hospital experience, and access to a full range of sedation options for both routine and complex cases.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most patients are candidates for dental implants. Even if other doctors have told you that you do not qualify for implants, Dr. Epstein’s advanced surgical training allows him to perform procedures that fall outside the scope of other specialists. We can determine your candidacy after a comprehensive evaluation at our office.

What if I don’t have enough bone to support implants?

Bone loss is a natural consequence of losing teeth. We specialize in advanced bone grafting techniques to restore the proper foundation for dental implants. In cases where bone is insufficient in the upper jaw, Dr. Epstein can place zygomatic implants, which secure to the zygoma (cheek) bones instead.

Does implant surgery hurt?

With our sophisticated technology, surgical skill, and custom sedation options, the chances of a painful surgery at our office are virtually zero. We are careful to ensure your treatment is comfortable from beginning to end.

Where will my implant surgery take place?

All implant procedures are performed at our offices in Seattle or Shoreline. Both locations are equipped with advanced technology and designed for your safety and comfort.

Can I have a tooth removed and an implant placed the same day?

Yes, in many cases. Our surgeons are highly experienced in immediate implants, which means we can extract a tooth, place the implant, and even provide a temporary crown in one visit.

What if I experience complications?

Complications with implants are rare, but if they do occur, we are here to help. Our surgeons have the expertise to manage issues quickly and safely to protect your health and your investment.

What if I currently have an implant that’s failing?

We can evaluate your existing implant, determine the cause of the problem, and provide options to restore stability and function.

Who restores my dental implants?

While we do not provide the final restoration, we will partner with your dentist to ensure a seamless treatment process from consultation to final restoration.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Dental implants are cared for much like natural teeth. While they will not stain or decay, they are susceptible to bacteria that accumulates without proper brushing and flossing. Be sure to maintain healthy oral hygiene habits and visit your general dentist regularly to keep both your natural teeth and your implants safe.
