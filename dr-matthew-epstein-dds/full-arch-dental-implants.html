The Full Arch Dental Implant Process

Knowing what will happen step-by-step can make the process feel less overwhelming. Here is how a typical full arch dental implant journey unfolds at our practice.

Consultation and Imaging

Your initial consultation focuses on understanding your needs and giving you clear information. We will take a 3D CBCT scan to plan implant placement with precision and review sedation options so you feel comfortable with the process.

Day of Surgery

On the day of your procedure, implants are placed using X-guide technology. Our in-house prosthodontist and lab team design and print your temporary teeth while you rest. You leave the office with a secure, functional smile the very same day.

Recovery and Follow-Up

Most patients return to normal activities within a few days. You will receive a same-day follow-up call and continued care throughout your healing process. Once your implants have fully integrated with the bone, your dentist will provide the final, custom restoration.

FAQs

Am I a candidate for full arch dental implants?

Most patients missing multiple teeth are candidates. A 3D scan will show if you have enough bone support, and bone grafting may be recommended if needed.

How long does the procedure take?

In most cases, the entire process, from placing implants to receiving temporary teeth, can be completed in a single day.

Is dental implant surgery painful?

Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the process is. With sedation and modern anesthetics, you will not feel pain during the procedure. After surgery, long-lasting medications like Exparel and the use of PRF help reduce discomfort and support healing, so recovery is typically smoother than expected.

What is the cost of full arch dental implants?

The cost varies based on the number of implants, whether bone grafting is needed, and the type of prosthesis. We provide a clear estimate at your consultation and offer financing options.

Will insurance cover full arch dental implants?

Most dental insurance plans cover part of the procedure. Every insurance company is different so it’s important to check with them to see what they cover. Our team will help maximize your benefits and review financing options if needed. We have partnered with 3 financing companies to help make this treatment a reality.

Where can I get full arch dental implants near me?
