The practice

Connection, Clarity, and Trust Philosophy & Approach to Care Known for his steady presence in the operating room, Dr. Epstein combines surgical expertise with the latest technology to deliver a patient experience that feels calm, informed, and safe. His approach is guided by the belief that surgery is not just about technical mastery, it is about connection, clarity, and trust.

A graduate of Bellevue High School, Dr. Epstein earned his B.S. in Biology and D.D.S. from the University of Washington before completing advanced training in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at St. Joseph’s Regional Medical Center. His residency experience provided extensive exposure to both oral and facial plastic surgery, allowing him to develop a broad surgical skillset rooted in precision and…

Dr. Epstein’s commitment to community health extends beyond his practice. He has volunteered at the 45th Street Clinic, served as clinic coordinator, and traveled to Guatemala with The Free to Smile Foundation to provide cleft lip and palate care for children in need.

Now in his twelfth year of practice, he runs multiple study clubs collaborating and educating local dental providers in the Greater Seattle area.

Dr. Epstein is a lifelong Washington resident and has deep roots in the Pacific Northwest. Outside of the office, he enjoys spending time outdoors, whether that means hiking local trails, camping, or biking around Seattle. He and his wife, Rachel, enjoy exploring the region with their daughters, Eva and Lily, and staying connected to the community they call home.
