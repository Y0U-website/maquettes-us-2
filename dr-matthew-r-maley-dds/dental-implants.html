Dental Implants

  • Who is a good candidate to get dental implants? Ideal candidates are individuals with one or more missing teeth and sufficient bone structure. They’re especially great for people seeking a long-term alternative to dentures or bridges.
  • What’s the procedure for dental implants? After a thorough evaluation, the implant is placed and allowed to integrate with your jawbone a process called osseointegration. Once healed, a custom-made restoration is attached for a secure, comfortable fit.
  • How long do they last? With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them one of the best investments in your oral health. Let the team at Maley Family Dental help you rediscover your smile with our personalized, high-quality dental implant services.
