Meet Dr. Matthew Yarborough

Dr. Matthew Yarborough, new owner of Magnolia Family Dentistry

The practice

B orn in Lodi, California, Dr. Matthew Yarborough moved to the San Francisco Bay Area growing up in Ross Valley, attending Bacich Elementary School, Kent Middle School, and Redwood High School. He studied at Pepperdine University and had the opportunity to spend his sophomore year abroad in Heidelberg, Germany. After receiving his Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from Pepperdine, he…

Following his graduation from dental school, Matt began his career in oral health in South Texas. He provided dental care for underprivileged patients in McAllen, Brownsville, Mission, and Rio Grande City. After working in south Texas for a few years, he returned home and held dental positions in the San Francisco Bay Area before acquiring Magnolia Family Dentistry.

Matt is passionate about patient education, an honest approach to dental care and a comfortable experience for all his patients. In his time off, he enjoys taking advantage of the natural beauty and culinary diversity of the Bay Area, and feels very fortunate to be able to do what he loves in the neighborhood he grew up in!

Our practice is open to new patients of all ages! Our convenient hours are 8-5 Mon - Thurs, 8-2 every other Fri, Closed Sat

Les soins
