About San Diego Dental Lounge

San Diego Dental Lounge in El Cajon, California, is dedicated to providing top-quality preventive, cosmetic, and restorative dental care for patients of all ages. At the heart of our practice is Dr. Mays Alshawi, whose passion for dentistry and patient-centered care inspired the creation of a warm, modern, and comfort-focused dental home for families in our community.

A proud graduate of the University of Southern California (USC), Dr. Alshawi combines advanced clinical training with a gentle, compassionate approach. She believes in empowering patients through education, prioritizing oral health, and creating personalized treatment plans that support lifelong wellness.

Outside the office, Dr. Alshawi enjoys cooking, biking through San Diego’s scenic areas, and spending time solving puzzles and exploring optical illusions—interests that reflect her attention to detail and love for problem-solving.
