Our Team

Choosing a dentist is a big decision. You want an expert, someone who makes you feel comfortable during your cleaning or treatment, while at the same time using the latest technology.

Fortunately, River Rock Dental has the experts, technology, and care you’ve been searching for to make dental visits exceptional! We have private rooms, each with massage chairs and personal TVs. Free Wi-Fi is available along with nitrous oxide for those that may be anxious or need some help relaxing. We use the latest in dental technology including digital x-rays, intraoral camera, gentle anesthesia techniques, and the Solea Laser System, which allows us to perform many fillings without shots.

Our kind, knowledgeable team will make you feel comfortable from the minute you pick up the phone or walk in the door. Our dental hygienists have experience and use the latest techniques to promote your oral health. We listen to your concerns and your goals, use all the tools at our disposal, and work with you to create a plan to help you maintain a healthy mouth and a beautiful smile, both now and for the long term.
