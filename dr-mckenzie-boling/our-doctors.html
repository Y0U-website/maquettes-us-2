Our Doctors

Arielle Hall, DDS

Dr. Arielle Hall, DDS is originally from Cookeville, TN where she grew up in a family of 6. There, she attended Tennessee Tech University for her undergraduate degree in Biochemistry. While in college, Dr. Hall met her husband, Ryan, while he was pursuing a pre-medicine major. After graduation, they moved to Memphis, TN where Dr. Hall started dental school at University of Tennessee Health Science Center. After finishing dental school, Dr. Hall and her husband relocated to the Chattanooga area for Ryan to pursue residency at UT Family Medicine Erlanger.

Dr. Hall and Ryan have a 4 year old Pomeranian named Nala who is as sweet as she is playful. Be sure to ask Dr. Hall to ask to see a picture of her when you come into the office!

In her spare time, Dr. Hall enjoys sewing, playing with her pup, and spending time with her husband and family.

McKenzie Boling, DDS

Dr. McKenzie Boling grew up in Rhea County ultimately graduating from Rhea County High School in 2004 where he played soccer and football. As a huge Vol fan, he attended the University of Tennessee at Knoxville, so he could cheer on the Big Orange in person. He is pumped about the return of Tennessee athletics whether it’s baseball, basketball, or football.

Julie Briggs, DDS

Dr. Julie Briggs was born and raised in Columbia, Tennessee and has called the Chattanooga area home since 2004, when she married her husband, Scott. She earned her undergraduate degrees in Zoology and Nursing from Auburn University before beginning her career as a registered nurse in both Nashville and Chattanooga.

Driven by a passion for patient care, Dr. Briggs went on to attend the University of Tennessee Health Science Center, where she earned her dental degree. She has been practicing dentistry in Chattanooga since 2010 and is an active member of the American Dental Association, Tennessee Dental Association, Chattanooga Area Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

Outside of the office, Dr. Briggs enjoys playing tennis, traveling, reading, and spending time with her family—especially when it involves being out on the lake.
