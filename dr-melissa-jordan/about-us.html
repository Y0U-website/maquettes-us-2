Cyber City’s Best Award 2023 & 2024

Meet Dr. Melissa Jordan

Dr. Jordan has been practicing as a family dentist in Columbia County since 2011. She graduated with honors from the Medical College of Georgia School of Dentistry and is a member of the Georgia Dental Association and American Dental Association.

She has received several awards during her career including the American Academy of Esthetic Dentistry Award in 2011, the American Association of Oral Medicine Award in 2011, the American Academy of Implant Dentistry Award in 2011, and a Georgia Dental Association scholarship. She was also inducted into the Alpha Upsilon Phi Leadership honor society in 2011.

Dr. Jordan grew up in Columbia County, Georgia. She is married to Daniel Jordan and is “Mommy” to four precious children! Dr. Jordan enjoys traveling, eating good food, spending time with her family, and reading books. The Jordan family also enjoys cheering on the Georgia Southern Eagles. She and Daniel both graduated from Georgia Southern University where Daniel was the punter for the football team for four years.

Dr. Jordan is an active member of Redeemer Church in Evans, Georgia. She has been on two dental mission trips: to Trinidad in 2008 and to the Amazon in 2011

Meet Dr. Tayler Brinson

Dr. Brinson grew up on her family’s farm in Soperton, GA and graduated from Robert Toombs Christian Academy in Vidalia, GA in 2011.

She continued her studies at the University of Georgia and graduated Summa Cum Laude in 2015 with a BS degree in Biological Science.

Despite being a Georgia girl and a loyal Bulldog fan, she went on to complete her dental education at the University of Florida, where she earned her DMD degree in 2019 and was honored with the UF Class of 2019 Award for Clinical Excellence and the American Academy of Oral and Maxillofacial Pathology Award.

Dr. Brinson completed a General Practice Residency at the Dental College of Georgia at Augusta University, where she became trained in IV sedation, implant dentistry, and complex restorative work. She is a member of the ADA and GDA, and is a fellow in the International Congress of Implant Dentistry (ICOI).

In her free time Dr. Brinson enjoys exercise, travel, trying new restaurants, spending time outdoors, and hanging out with her furry poodle friends, Parti and Disco.

A bit of exciting news we are thrilled to report that Dr. Brinson was engaged in April 2024! She has hit the road running and is happily planning her October 2025 wedding.

CLINICAL TEAM

SHANNON C. MACGREGOR, DENTAL HYGIENIST

Shannon has been practicing dental hygiene for over 20 years. She received her training in her home state of Michigan and began her career in Atlanta before moving to Augusta. Prior to joining Jordan Family Dentistry, she had been with the same practice for two decades, so it’s no surprise that she prides herself on the long-term relationships she creates with her patients. She is passionate about how proper dental care can lead to better health and improved quality of life, so she stays current with the latest in dental care in order to provide her patients with the best care possible.

Shannon lives in Augusta with her husband, Scott, and their two children. Her interests include horses, family, friends, and gardening.

AMIE THOMAS, DENTAL HYGIENIST

Amie has been married to her husband Baylan for 19 years and they have two sons. She is an active member of Trinity Baptist Church. She enjoys spending time with family and watching her boys play baseball and basketball.

KRISTY LEWIS, DENTAL HYGIENIST
