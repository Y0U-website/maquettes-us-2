Our Team

Meet the team that’s committed to keeping you smiling!

Dr. Davis has been practicing dentistry at Jessie Albert since 2003. She received her undergraduate degree from Bates College and her Doctor of Dental Medicine from The University of Pennsylvania. In addition to her kind and caring manner, she has additional training in the use of nitrous oxide (laughing gas), a wonderful tool for children and adults wishing to make their visit more comfortable.

Dr. Round bring his extensive knowledge, having received his dental degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, MA and his post-doctoral certificate in Orthodontics from the University of Rochester. Dr. Round is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, and board eligible for the American Board of Orthodontics. He also served four years in the United States Marine Corps. When he’s not improving the smiles of children and adults alike, you can find Dr. Round woodworking, sailing, and enjoying all the great outdoor activities Maine has to offer.

Karen Bazemore, IPDH, RDH, BSDH Director of Oral Health Services

Karen served as a hygienist at Jessie Albert for 15 years before becoming office manager, now in her current role as the Center’s Director of Oral Health Services she works closely with all team members to ensure that every patient’s experience is the best it can possibly be.

The Jessie Albert Dental & Orthodontic clinical staff is backed by a team of dedicated Dental Hygienists, Orthodontic Assistants, Clinical Assistants and our front desk staff.
