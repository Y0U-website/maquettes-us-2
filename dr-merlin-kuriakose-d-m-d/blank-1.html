About Us

At Middlesex Family Dental, we’re here to remind you that one of your best assets is your pearly whites. Established in 2022, our practice focuses on providing the most important aspects of cosmetic and aesthetic dentistry to the Middlesex, Bridgewater, Warren area and beyond. We make it our mission to master the breakthrough technological developments within the dental industry in order to give you the best care available. From simple cleanings to complex restorations like crowns, bridges and dentures, we’re here to help you get your confident smile back.

Our Team

The team at Middlesex Family Dental is made up of experienced professionals who have a true passion for the dental field. From standard cleanings and preventative treatments to Invisalign and cosmetic procedures, our doctors are trained to do it all. Scroll below to learn more about our team.

DR MERLIN KURIAKOSE

The first step toward a healthy smile starts with Dr Merlin Kuriakose or Dr MK as she is fondly called.

Dr MK is a graduate of Rutgers University and has been practicing dentistry for over 15 years. She received her D.M.D from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine, where she was awarded the Dean’s List Academic Award for being in the top 5 % of her class. Dr. MK is skilled in diagnosing and treating complex, multidisciplinary cases and is comfortable treating both medically compromised and emergency patients following her residency at University Hospital (RSDM). She is also a Invisalign certified practitioner.

Dr. MK is dedicated to providing compassionate care using the latest technology and techniques. She values her patients’ comfort and strives to make every visit a pleasant experience.

In her free time, Dr. MK enjoys baking, gardening, and spending time with her family.
