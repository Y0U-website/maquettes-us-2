At Middlesex Family Dental, we’re all about top-notch dental care in a warm and friendly atmosphere. Our personalized treatment plans are tailored just for you. Thanks for considering us we can’t wait to take care of you! 🦷

Dental CleaninG Regular dental cleanings are an essential part of maintaining good oral health. Our team will work with you to develop a personalized cleaning plan that meets your individual needs.

Root Canal A root canal is a dental procedure that is used to treat an infected or damaged tooth. During the procedure, the infected or damaged pulp is removed, and the tooth is cleaned and sealed to prevent further damage.

Bridges A bridge is a dental restoration that replaces one or more missing teeth. It is anchored to the remaining teeth on either side of the gap and can help restore your smile and improve your ability to chew and speak.

CROWN A dental crown is a tooth-shaped cap that restores a decayed, broken, weak, or worn-down tooth. Crowns are used to cover dental implants and root canal-treated teeth

DENTURES Dentures are removable oral appliances that replace missing teeth. There are different types, including full dentures (replacing an entire arch of teeth) and partial dentures (replacing several missing teeth and attaching to natural teeth for stability).Implant-retained dentures offer more stability by attaching to dental implants

Veneers Dental veneers are crafted from tooth-colored materials and are designed to cover the front surface of teeth, altering their appearance. They are effectively concealing cosmetic imperfections such as cracks, chips, stains, and gaps. They are a popular cosmetic dentistry treatment, enhancing smiles and providing a natural-looking aesthetic

invisalign Invisalign is an orthodontic treatment that uses clear, removable aligners to gradually straighten teeth, correct gaps, crowding, and mild bite issues. Unlike traditional metal braces, Invisalign aligners are virtually invisible and can be removed for eating, drinking, and brushing

Emergencies Dental emergencies can happen at any time, which is why we offer emergency dental services. If you experience a dental emergency, don t hesitate to call our office. We will do everything we can to see you as soon as possible.
