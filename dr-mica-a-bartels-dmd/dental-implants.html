Dental Implants in Near Stow

Our team coordinates the entire dental implant process for you

Getting dental implants or implant-supported dentures is a multi-step process. After an initial evaluation by one of our doctors, you will be scheduled with an oral surgeon to have the implants placed. You’ll also need to visit our office for your restorative work. The MFFD team as years of experience planning and coordinating every step in the process for you to ensure it goes a smoothly as possible.

We work with the most esteemed oral surgeons in the Stow and Akron Areas

In order to have the best results possible, our doctors only work with local oral surgeons with the highest standards and practices in place. Moreover, Drs. Evan and Bartels have years of experience working with these surgeons. Thus, the level of communication and collaboration between doctors and surgeons is excellent.

Our implant restorative work is second to none

During the dental implant process, we’ll fit you with a temporary prosthesis that looks natural. Once the implants are ready, our doctors will transform your implants into natural-looking teeth in the comfort of our familiar, friendly office. We use only the highest grade of prosthetic teeth, and our doctors don’t stop until you’re completely satisfied your new smile looks and feels. Thus, you’ll enjoy those dental implants before and after pictures even more!
