Meet Dr. Mica Bartels Dentist at MFFD

Dr. Mica Bartels is originally from Carrollton, Ohio. She received her B.S. from Asbury College near Lexington, Kentucky and graduated in 1999 from the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry. She shares Dr. Evan’s promise to patients of practicing Dentistry by the Golden Rule.

Dedicated to bringing her patients the most up-to-date dental care possible, Dr. Bartels continually furthers her education. Indeed, she had the fortune of attending a series of lectures in 2017 on the latest techniques in cosmetic and esthetic dentistry. This series was taught by Dr. Ross W. Nash, D.D.S., a leading international expert in the field of esthetic dentistry. Dr. Bartels loves the fusion of art and science that cosmetic dentistry entails.

A member of the community she serves, Dr. Bartels resides in Munroe Falls, Ohio with her husband Brett, a pastor at Heart for the City Church in Akron, Ohio. Together they have three children. An animal lover, Dr. Bartels also shares her home with three dogs, two birds, and many fish.

In her free time, Dr. Bartels loves spending time with her family and friends, reading, exercising, and cooking. She also enjoys volunteering her time at her church where she helps coordinate a food bank and participates in the music ministry.
