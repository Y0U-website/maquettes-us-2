Meet Dr. Evan

Dr. Michael Evan (pronounced ee-Vahn) has called Stow, Ohio home since 1990 when he joined the practice. He took over ownership of the practice in 2001 from Dr. Young. Dr. Young established a solid reputation for treating patients with compassion and respect, and Dr. Evan maintains that focus today. Indeed, Dr. Evan’s guiding principle of Dentistry by the Golden Rule shapes and influences all aspects of the practice.

Dr. Michael Evan grew up in Warren, PA and received his B.S. from Westminster College in Pennsylvania. He received his D.M.D. from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine.

On a more personal note, Dr. Evan enjoys spending time with his wife, Jacquelyn, and their two daughters. An avid bicyclist, he can be seen pedaling to work in all the glorious Ohio weather! A lover of the great outdoors, Dr. Evan also enjoys golfing, hiking, skiing, hunting, fly fishing, and never-ending yard work.

With his warm, easy way, Dr. Evan is welcoming new patients. Call our office today at (330) 688-4942 for an appointment. He also welcomes dental emergencies near Stow, Cuyahoga Falls, Kent, and Tallmadge , Ohio.
