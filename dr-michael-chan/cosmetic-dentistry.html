Cosmetic Dentistry Options

Whiten your teeth on your own time in the privacy of your home with Dr. Chan Dentistry . Simply pop in the custom trays we make for you and watch your teeth get whiter over the next couple of days. Our custom fit trays provide a perfectly even coating of whitening gel and minimize gum irritation, ensuring a comfortable and effective whitening experience.

Whether you have an important event coming up requiring you to show off your pearly whites or you want instant results, you can also opt for getting your teeth whitened at Dr. Chan Dentistry. A concentrated whitening gel and special blue light allows us to speed up the process to an hour or two delivering immediate results. Additional treatments or sessions may be indicated if teeth are severely stained or you want them even whiter.

When there are imperfections in your teeth that do not require extensive treatment, dental bonding may be the perfect solution. Bonding can help to improve your smile by simply covering up recession of the gums, stains on the teeth, or minor chips and wear. Bonding is a less costly option compared to veneers, but is an optimal solution to enhance your smile.
