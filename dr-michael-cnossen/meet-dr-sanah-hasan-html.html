Get to Know Dr. Sanah Hasan

Dr. Sanah Hasan grew up in Dallas, Texas and graduated from Bates College in 2019 with a Bachelor of Science degree, where she studied psychology and Spanish. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine in 2024, graduating magna cum laude . During her time in dental school, Dr. Hasan enjoyed working with her peers as a teaching assistant in courses such as physiology and occlusion.

After dental school, Dr. Hasan completed a one-year Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at the Providence VA Medical Center. There, she expanded her surgical skills, cared for medically complex patients, and developed comprehensive treatment plans tailored to each patient’s unique needs.

She values building meaningful relationships with her patients and believes that thoughtful, compassionate care is the foundation of great dentistry. She is an active member of the Massachusetts Dental Society and the American Dental Association.

Outside of the office, Dr. Hasan loves biking along the Charles River Esplanade, spending time with her husband and her dog, cooking new recipes, traveling, reading, and trying out new restaurants around Boston (her favorite is Sarma!).
