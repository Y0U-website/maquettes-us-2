Meet Dr. Michael Ontiveros

Originally from Phoenix, Arizona, Dr. Michael Ontiveros has dedicated his career to helping his patients look, feel and smile their very best for nearly 30 years. From an early age, Dr. Ontiveros knew he wanted to become a dentist. After graduating with a degree in chemistry from Arizona State University, he moved to Colorado and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Colorado Health Science Center. His passion lies in cosmetic and surgical dentistry and he stays up to date with the latest techniques in the industry.

Dr. Ontiveros prides himself in providing his patients with the highest quality dental care while always keeping their best interest at heart. He loves working with families and creating meaningful relationships; he truly takes time to educate and inform his patients so they can make educated decisions. He treats a wide range of dental needs and is also focused on making patients feel comfortable while undergoing dental treatment.

When not in the office, Dr. Ontiveros loves to fire up his grill and spend time with his family. He also enjoys golfing and taking long walks in the beautiful Colorado outdoors.
