Your Experience at Rocky Mountain Dental Group

When you arrive at Rocky Mountain Dental Group, our highly trained and compassionate team will be ready to serve you. Whether you are confident or apprehensive about visiting the dentist, we are committed to making your experience with us as comfortable as possible by providing a warm and welcoming atmosphere throughout the duration of your visit.

As part of our dental family, we are focused on keeping you informed every step of the way and addressing all questions and concerns in a timely manner. Dr. Ontiveros conducts thorough examinations and openly discusses all of his findings with his patients. He is committed to providing you with options tailored to you and your needs, keeping your best interest in mind at all times.

If there is anything that we can do to make your visit more enjoyable, please let us know - we are here to serve you.

Welcome to Rocky Mountain Dental Group. We are proud to have you join our dental family.
