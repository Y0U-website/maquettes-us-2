The practice

Let Valley Dental give you that picture perfect smile you’ve always dreamed of! We offer a variety of services such as veneers and composite bonding to enhance your smiles shape and natural aesthetics.

Teeth whitening is a great way to upgrade your from ‘Like It’ to ‘Love It!’ Valley Dental has different whitening options to allow even the most sensitive of teeth to whiten for that ‘Picture Perfect’ smile!

Does your smile have a few holes in it? Dental Implants are the perfect replacement for missing or damaged teeth. Implants provide the look, feel, and functionality of a natural tooth. Here at Valley Dental, we handle every step of the process from initial placement to the final restoration.

In certain cases, a damaged tooth cannot be saved and needs to be extracted. Valley Dental offers nitrous (laughing gas) and oral sedation to help make the oral surgery process more comfortable.

Whether you need adjustments made to an existing partial/denture or you would like a brand new appliance fabricated, Valley Dental can help!
