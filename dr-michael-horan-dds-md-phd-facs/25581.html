What Are Mini Implants?

Mini implants are a popular tooth replacement option, offering an alternative to full-sized implants. They have the same structure as regular implants but use a titanium post with a ball on the end to hold your new tooth. Mini implants are usually less than 3mm in diameter.

Practical Applications in Dentistry

Mini dental implants can be used in a variety of dental applications:

  • Stabilizing Dentures : Lower dentures tend to be less stable than upper ones.
  • Single Tooth Replacement : Especially in locations where space is limited.
  • Dental Bridge Support : Acting as robust anchors for multiple missing teeth.

Introducing the All-on-4 Treatment Concept

The All-on-4 treatment concept revolutionizes the approach to fully edentulous (toothless) and soon-to-be edentulous jaws. The concept is grounded in providing a full-arch prosthesis on the day of surgery, maximizing treatment efficiency, and minimizing recovery times. In contrast to individual tooth implants, All-on-4 facilitates full-arch restoration with only four implants, thus:

  • Rapid Improvement of Quality of Life : Immediate results in function, aesthetics, sense, speech, and self-esteem.
