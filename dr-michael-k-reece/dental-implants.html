Exceptional, Transformative & Caring Get Pricing And Options For Treatment Find Out Now Our Dental Implant Process From start to finish, our team personalizes dental implant treatment to meet your specific oral health needs and smile goals. The first step is to complete a consultation with our implant dentists in Bryan–College Station, TX.

During this initial visit, we’ll discuss your dental and medical history, take digital x-rays and a 3D image with our CBCT scanner, to create a customized treatment plan. If we determine you need bone grafting or tooth extraction , this is done first.

On the day of surgery, we use fully guided techniques to ensure an efficient procedure with highly exacting and predictable results. In addition to numbing your gums with a local anesthetic, we offer several types of sedation to alleviate anxiety and make the procedure more comfortable.

With our full arch protocols, you’ll receive a temporary prosthesis to wear as your mouth heals over the next 3–4 months. This will enable you to eat comfortably and enjoy restored smile aesthetics until your permanent, custom-made zirconia restoration is ready.

Although you may experience some discomfort after the anesthesia wears off, the majority of our patients manage this well with over-the-counter pain relief.
