OUR DENTAL PRACTICE

High-Tech Office The latest advances in dentistry

Lakewood Ranch Dental offers advanced contemporary dentistry.

Dr. Kanter and his team use the latest technologies, including digital radiography. Digital radiology provides more clarity while exposing you to far less radiation than conventional x-rays, making them safer and more efficient. Intraoral digital imaging helps you see and better understand your condition. With digital image modification, we are able to modify a photograph to preview your new smile before any dental work begins.

Our goal is to ensure the highest of quality care for all our patients.

Payment Options Making quality dental care affordable

Dr. Kanter’s business staff is highly trained to answer questions about dental care, offer payment options, and to schedule appointments convenient for our patients.

We accept most major credit cards and we can help arrange financing of your dental health investment with our attractive payment plans. We accept a variety of dental insurance plans and will fill out and file your forms as a courtesy to you.

If you have questions regarding finances and billing, please ask. Together we’ll make achieving a more attractive and healthy smile surprisingly affordable.
