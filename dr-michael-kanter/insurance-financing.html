INSURANCE & FINANCING

Insurance: We have worked with many patients’ insurance plans as well as many insurance carriers over the years. We are please to lend our experience to help every patient maximize his or her insurance benefits by carefully planning, pre-estimating and assisting them in the filing of their claim.

Financials Concerns: Dental treatment is an excellent investment in your medical and psychological well-being. Financial considerations should not be an obstacle to obtaining this important health service. Being sensitive to the fact that people have different needs in fulfilling their financial obligations, we provide a variety of payment options. Our promise to every patient is that we will sit with them and discuss finances prior to beginning any treatment. We believe an individual’s finances are very personal and private issue. Therefore, we choose to give it the privacy it deserves.
