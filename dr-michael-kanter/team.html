If you’re looking for a change in your life, you’ve come to the right office! Dr. Kanter and his team of dental professionals are here to help you capitalize on your greatest asset, your smile.

DENTISTS

Dr. Michael S. Kanter

In 1991, Dr. Kanter founded and established a second practice, Braden River Dental, in Bradenton, FL. After several years and the growth of the Lakewood Ranch Community, Dr. Kanter expanded his range of services in the area by developing his new office in Lakewood Ranch. This office is a state of the art facility with all the latest equipment and technology that modern dentistry has to offer.

Dr. Kanter has extensive training in bone grafting , dental implants and cosmetic restoration. He is a graduate of the prestigious Misch International Implant Institute and has earned his Diplomate Status with the International Congress of Oral Implantologists. He is also a member of the American Dental Association, the West Coast Dental Association, the Manatee County Dental Society and the Sarasota County Dental Society.

When he isn’t working he enjoys spending time with his family (he has 5 children), boating, flying, riding his Harley, attending Gator football and life!

Dr. Peter N. Masterson

Dr. Masterson and his wife live in the Lakewood Ranch Community. Their 5 children are graduates of Lakewood Ranch High School. They maintain active roles in their church and their community and are involved in the promotion of the local arts. They are supporters of the Sarasota Opera and Sarasota Ballet, where Dr. Masterson is the dentist for the Ballet’s Doctors Circle. Dr. Masterson enjoys travel, running, biking and the Florida weather!

Dr. Violet Laurey

Dr.Violet Laurey was born in Rochester, NY and grew up in Sarasota, FL. She went to the University of Florida and earned a Bachelor’s Degree in Health Sciences. Dr.Laurey went on to complete her dental education at the University of Florida and received her D.M.D. degree in 2017.

Following graduation, Dr.Laurey moved back to her hometown of Sarasota, FL. Dr. Violet Laurey loves the sunshine state and being close to her family. In her spare time she enjoys cooking, yoga, and home interior design.

In practice, she holds herself to the highest standards. Patient comfort is top priority for Dr.Laurey, and she wants every patient to walk away satisfied.
