Our Team

Our doctors are highly skilled dentists dedicated to providing gentle and effective care to patients of all dental backgrounds. Our doctors believe that excellent oral health begins with education and prevention and have made it our mission to enlighten patients to ways they can take greater control over their own dental health. Our doctors are committed to continuing education and remaining aware of the latest advancements in oral health and dentistry.

Dr. Michael Higginson

Born in Idaho and raised, for a time, in Eagle, Dr. Higginson received his bachelors degree from Brigham Young University and earned his dental degree at Creighton University in Omaha, Nebraska. In 2001, Dr. Higginson was the founding dentist of Lighthouse Dental. Through his membership in the American Dental Association and the Idaho State Dental Association, as well as annual continuing education, Dr. Higginson keeps up to date on the latest techniques in cosmetic and restorative dentistry.

And, as anyone can tell you, you’re more than just a patient to Dr. Higginson, you’re also a friend.

Dr. Higginson’s greatest joy comes from spending time with his family. He and his wife Marci and their five children love to go on family trips and in 2008 returned from a dental service mission to Tonga.

Dr. Brian Jensen

Born and raised in Boise, Dr. Jensen received his bachelors of science degree at Brigham Young University. Before attending dental school at the University of Kentucky, he received his MBA from Boise State University. Dr. Jensen is a member of the American Dental Association and the Idaho State Dental Association and continues his education in the latest techniques and technology in order provide his patients with the best treatment and care possible. The most important part of being a dentist is earning his patient’s trust. Dr. Jensen joined the Lighthouse Dental team in 2005.

Most important to Dr. Jensen is his wife Tammy and their five children. He is actively involved in his church and enjoys the outdoors. A favorite passion is visiting our national parks.

Dr. Marvin Oto

It was while visiting Idaho during a family reunion that he and his wife April decided to move here with their family, which now includes four children. Dr. Oto enjoys spending time with family and his hobbies include mountain biking, snowboarding, and wakeboarding.
