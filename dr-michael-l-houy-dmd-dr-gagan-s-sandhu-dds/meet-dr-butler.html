Meet Dr. Taylor H. Butler, DMD, RDH, MPH

Dr. Taylor H. Butler is a dedicated dental professional committed to providing compassionate, patient-centered care. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine from Midwestern University and her Master of Public Health from The George Washington University, where she strengthened her foundation in both clinical excellence and community health. Dr. Butler also holds an Associate’s degree in Dental Hygiene and a Bachelor of Science in Psychology from the University of Pittsburgh, bringing a unique blend of clinical and behavioral insight to her practice.

With over a decade of experience in the field, Dr. Butler’s journey in dentistry began at just 15 years old. She has worked in nearly every role within a dental office including secretary, dental assistant, and registered dental hygienist prior to becoming a dentist. These diverse experiences allow her to understand and value each aspect of the patient experience and team dynamic.

Dr. Butler grew up in the South Hills of Pittsburgh in Baldwin and is a proud Pittsburgher both a fierce Steelers fan and an enthusiastic Penguins supporter.

She is an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD) and the American Dental Association (ADA), continually engaging in professional development to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry.

Outside of the office, she enjoys reading, exploring new restaurants, cooking and baking, and spending time with arts and crafts projects.
