Proper Care for Your Dentures

Caring for your dentures properly is crucial to maintain both their function and appearance. Here’s how to keep your dentures in top condition:

For more guidance on denture care or any other dental needs, give us a call today. We’re here to help you maintain your best smile.

  • Cleaning Routine: Clean your denture over a sink full of water to prevent damage if accidentally dropped.
  • Soaking: Soak your denture in a commercial soaking solution to keep it hygienic.
  • Rest Period: Remove your denture for sleeping or for at least 6 to 8 hours a day to allow your gums to rest.
