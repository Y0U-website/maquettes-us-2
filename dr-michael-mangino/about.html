ABOUT MANGINO DENTAL

Providing quality oral health care and comfort to our patients is our top priority. Regardless of the treatment needed, new patients are often surprised at the individual attention, courteous treatment and personal convenience that come with visiting Mangino Dental. The entire staff is committed to business practices designed for the comfort and convenience of our patients and their families.

To relieve extreme anxiety and apprehension, we offer all of our patient’s water, blankets and headphones to help relieve any discomfort. While dental health is a vital component to overall health and wellbeing – it is often neglected due to fear or nervousness. To further alleviate anxiety, we specialize in conscious sedation techniques to provide an amazing level of comfort. For sedation, we offer nitrous oxide or oral conscious sedation .
