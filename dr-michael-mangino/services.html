SEDATION

DON’T LET ANXIETY MAKE YOU RUN FROM GETTING THE TREATMENT YOU NEED.

Does the thought of going to the dentist give you anxiety? At Mangino Dental we understand your fear. We also know that it can be overcome with sedation dentistry . Sedation dentistry is ideal for patients that suffer from any of the following causes of dental anxiety:

• Bad Experience – A negative prior dental experience can cause anxiety.

• Sensitivity – Sensitive soft tissues and nerves that do not respond well to typical sedation techniques.

• Sights and Sounds – Enhanced sensitivity to sights and sounds at the dental office.

• Gag Reflex – A strong gag reflex can make it difficult to receive the oral treatments you require.

There is no need to run from the dentist! New techniques and equipment enable Dr. Mangino to provide you with healthy dental treatment, leaving you feeling good about your dental experience. We have sedation options for every patient that will guarantee you are relaxed and anxiety free!

Like many of our patients, if you have an extreme fear of dental treatment, Mangino Dental offers a number of options to assist patients who express this concern.

• Nitrous Oxide – This gas is delivered through a special noninvasive mask that goes over your nose. You breathe in the nitrous oxide and feel a sense of calmness. After the treatment, the gas wears off almost immediately. Most patients report feeling refreshed, and many do not remember much about the procedure.

• Oral Conscious Sedation – Oral sedation relaxes a patient in a manner where he or she is consciously responsive but completely relaxed. Many of our patients rest relaxed through their dental appointment and rave about the comfort of their visit once the treatment is completed. Oral conscious sedation is an amazingly predictable and extremely safe way to provide dental care to anxious patients.

BIOLOGICAL DENTISTRY AND WHOLE BODY WELLNESS

YOUR MOUTH IS A GATEWAY TO THE ENTIRE BODY

At Mangino Dental, we recognize the impact of using toxic materials and offer more health conscious approaches to practicing dentistry, taking into consideration the cause-and-effect relationship between your oral health and the health of the rest of your body. When your mouth is healthy, you are healthy and vice versa!

Mercury Removal –Our mercury amalgam removal process is designed to remove toxic dental material including mercury amalgam and /or other fillings, crowns or bridges that appear to be biologically incompatible with your body while keeping you comfortable and safe from the toxic by-products generated during the removal process.

Protection Protocol – You Are Well Protected. A complete protection protocol is utilized to prevent you from ingesting or inhaling any of the mercury or mercury vapors while it is being removed.

Should I have my mercury fillings removed?

If a patient truly wants to achieve health and well-being, they need to consider removal of their mercury amalgam dental fillings. It is of great importance to note, however, that the patient must be protected during the removal process to avoid greater contamination by exposure to the mercury debris.
