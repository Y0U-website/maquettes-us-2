Thorough Exam and Digital Diagnostics

We start with a comprehensive dental exam to assess your overall oral health. This includes:

If you’re here for a specific service such as dental implants, orthodontics, or cosmetic dentistry, we’ll tailor the visit to your needs and walk you through next steps.

  • Full-mouth digital X-rays
  • A detailed examination of your teeth, gums, and bite
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Evaluation for signs of decay, gum disease, or other concerns

Let’s Talk About Your Smile Goals

After your exam, your dentist will sit down with you to review the findings and discuss your goals. Together, we’ll build a treatment plan that aligns with your lifestyle, timeline, and budget. Whether you’re here for routine care or a complete smile makeover, we’ll explain your options clearly so you can make informed decisions.

Helpful Information to Bring

To help make your first visit as smooth as possible, please bring:

  • A valid photo ID
  • Your insurance card (if applicable)
  • A list of medications or medical conditions
  • Any previous dental records or X-rays, if available
