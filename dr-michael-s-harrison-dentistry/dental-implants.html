Tooth Replacement Options in Hot Springs, AR

As a trusted dental implant dentist in Hot Springs, Dr. Harrison helps patients choose the best tooth replacement solution for their needs. While bridges and dentures have their place, implants are often preferred because they don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support. Instead, the implant and abutment serve as their own strong foundation, preserving the natural structure of surrounding teeth.

Why Implants Are a Better Choice Than Bridges or Dentures

  • No impact on surrounding teeth: Implants don’t require altering healthy teeth for support.
  • Superior function: Implants provide a stronger bite foundation than most dentures.
  • Cosmetic and functional benefits: They improve both appearance and performance, offering stability dentures often lack.
