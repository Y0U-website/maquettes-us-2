Meet the Staff of Harrison Dentistry of Hot Springs

Dr. Michael Harrison

Dr. Michael Harrison is a longtime resident of Hot Springs, Arkansas. He is married to Mary C. Harrison and has three children who were all raised in Hot Springs. Dr. Stephen Harrison has an endodontic specialty(root canals) in Hot Springs. Kevin is in the corporate world and daughter Kristen is in restaurant service.

Dr. Harrison graduated in Hendrix College then went on to graduate in the University of Tennessee Dental School.

Dr. Harrison has done higher education in a number of fields including TMJ treatment, crown and bridge, rehabilitation, implants, and other dental care. He is a graduate of the Dawson Center for Advanced Dental Study.

He is currently in good standing with Garland County Dental Society, Arkansas Central District Dental Society, Arkansas State Dental Association and American Dental Association. He is also a member of the Academy of General Dentistry. He is recognized in the Pierre Fauchard Academy as a fellow in the International Honor Organization. He is past president of the Arkansas Central District. He served in the Charitable Christian Clinic treating patients in need.

His goal is to treat his patients with the utmost in state-of-the-art care with quality and compassion.

Kendra

Kendra is a registered dental assistant and joined the office in 2013. Kendra has worked hand-in-hand with Dr. Harrison to bring each patient the highest quality of care possible.

Kendra will put your mind at ease in the clinic with her positive and upbeat personality. She is a native of Hot Springs, Arkansas , and looks forward to seeing friends in the community.

Karli

Karli obtained her first bachelors degree at Tarleton State University in Kinesiology before attending the University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences, where she earned her second bachelor’s degree in Dental Hygiene.

What she loves most about her work is the opportunity it provides to build relationships with her patients.

Cassie

Cassie received her Registered Dental Assistant (RDA) certification through Accelerated Academy in Hot Springs, AR, and is excited to continue building a long-term career in dentistry. She is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable, supported, and confident during every visit, whether they are here for a routine cleaning or more advanced care.
