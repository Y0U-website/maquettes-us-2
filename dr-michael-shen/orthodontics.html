Orthodontics

Orthodontics is a branch of dentistry that specializes in treating patients with improper positioning of teeth, bite, various aspects of facial growth, and shape and development of the jaw. Orthodontics can close gaps between teeth, make sure the tips of teeth are aligned, improve speech or eating, prevent long-term excessive wear or trauma of teeth due to misalignment, correct crowding of teeth, and so much more.

If you wish to improve the appearance of your smile, or facial shape, or to correct malocclusion or function, contact our offices to schedule an appointment to consult with Dr. Shen on what treatment options may be available for you. Traditional braces as well as Invisalign are available in our offices.
