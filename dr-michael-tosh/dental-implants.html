Our Case-By-Case Approach

Our case-by-case treatment plan means that we look at the whole picture. Lost teeth can be frustrating and damaging to your smile and your self-esteem. But implants may not be for everyone, and there may be better and less invasive options. That is why we discuss the good, the bad, and the ugly of implants, and what may happen if you choose other, less permanent treatments.

We will be open and frank with treatment options and their benefits and drawbacks. Our approach means that you are empowered to make decisions for you and your family’s oral health. You will not feel pressured to commit to a treatment you are concerned about or one that is out of your budget.

Implants with Tosh Dental

When a patient does choose to get dental implants, we are excited to help them get started. Implants meet the needs of our patients in many ways:

With our implants, we try to provide the most benefit with the least amount of risks and drawbacks. We also utilize pain management techniques for our implant surgeries. We want you to be excited about your new smile!

  • Durability: Dental implants last a long time and rarely need to be replaced.
  • Comfort: An implant attaches to the jaw, like your real teeth, so they feel completely natural! Patients with ill-fitting or painful dentures often find relief with implants to secure dentures in place.
  • Function: Chewing feels more like natural teeth than other replacement options.
  • Natural Look: Dental implants are the most similar replacement option to natural teeth.
  • Less Involvement of Adjacent Teeth: Other replacement options usually require modifying the surrounding teeth. Implants only involve the open space.

What Do Implants Treat?

With dental implants, the Tosh Dental Group can resolve your missing teeth problem. Whether you are missing a single tooth or multiple teeth, we can restore your smile with dental implants. When you replace your missing teeth with implants, you kill two birds with one stone—or teeth in this case. Your cosmetic concerns about missing a tooth will be fixed. No longer will you have a gap in your smile.

Implants also treat several oral health concerns. If you lose a tooth, it can lead to jawbone deterioration. Placing an implant can strengthen your jawbone again. Implants also treat several other dental concerns:

Implants are often a good solution if you are experiencing several oral health concerns due to your missing teeth. Contact us today if you have a missing tooth you need to treat.

Implants are not a good treatment option if your jawbone has deteriorated too much for us to place an implant, or if you cannot handle surgery and recovery at this time. If those things apply to you, we will work out other options with you!

  • Infections of the gum area
  • Nearby teeth deterioration
  • Teeth shifting
  • Deterioration of your jawbone
  • Improper wear on your teeth
  • Eating and chewing concerns

Implant Costs
