The practice

Our sophisticated dental office offers digital radiography, sterilization, nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and many other up-to-date features to offer you comfort and convenience while we provide superior service and quality dental services.

We are located in Parma, Ohio (a suburb of Cleveland, Ohio) where we service many existing patients and welcome their referrals and new patients.

The practice of dentistry has evolved dramatically in the last two decades, and we are keeping pace with the latest innovations. Our staff are experienced, friendly, pleasant and very often funny. Whoever said going to the dentist wasn’t fun hasn’t been to our office!

Our mission is to serve you best with One Stop Dental Care in a friendly and caring environment, so that you can achieve and maintain a beautiful smile with straight healthy teeth, gums and jaw joints.
