Dental Implant Process

It begins with a small, sturdy, titanium cylinder that is placed into the bone. Once the bone in your jaw has healed and grows around the implant it is ready to be restored. The next step is to place an Abutment, or implant post , this is the part that goes from the bone through the gums to allow a Crown, Bridge, or Denture to be put in place. Because implants are very durable and can last a lifetime, they require the same maintenance as natural teeth brushing, flossing, and regular dental check-ups .

More reasons you may want to consider dental implants:

Dental implants offer numerous advantages for those concerned with replacing missing teeth. They are a permanent solution that improve oral health and aesthetics by restoring the functionality of your teeth. Unlike removable dentures, dental implants are secured in the jawbone , providing a strong foundation for replacement teeth.

Dental implants are custom-made to fit your mouth and smile perfectly, ensuring a comfortable and natural look and feel. They can fill the gap left by tooth loss and prevent bone loss that can occur when teeth are removed. In addition, dental implants can assist in maintaining the structure of your face , preventing the sunken appearance that can result from missing teeth.
