Dr. Waldron and the staff at Waldron Smile Center provide quality dental care to patients in the Middletown area , focusing on a holistic approach to oral health. By treating and educating patients, our team helps patients to stay healthy in between visits. Our staff understands the importance of listening to patients, helping them to feel comfortable and relaxed.

Cosmetic dentistry can help you achieve a brighter, healthier smile by adding crowns, veneers, or bonding to address imperfections. Our dentists are committed to providing excellent dental care that enhances your life and gives you the smile you deserve.

Our team provides dental care for children, fostering a positive experience that builds the foundation for lifelong oral health. While we are not Pediatric Specialists, we regularly treat children and are fully equipped to handle most of their dental needs with the same gentle and caring approach. Our goal is to make dental visits enjoyable, reduce fear, and help families maintain healthy smiles.
