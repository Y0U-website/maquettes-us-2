Dr. Weitzman stays on the leading edge of dentistry by using state of the art technology, including the most advanced materials and equipment, as well as the newest techniques available in the dental industry. He continually devotes time to studying leading edge developments to ensure that patients are treated with the latest technology for dental care.

Dr. Weitzman is a Baltimore native and has spent his entire career treating its residents with an exceptional level of dental care.

He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry in 1977. Prior to dental school, Dr. Weitzman attended The University of Maryland, College Park, where he graduated with a B.A. in 1973.

Dr. Weitzman is an avid sports fan, regularly attending Baltimore Ravens and University of Maryland sporting events with his family. He also enjoys listening to classic rock, keeping up with his favorite television shows and spending quality time with family and friends.
