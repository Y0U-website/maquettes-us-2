Dr. Michael Zhou

Dr. Michael Zhou grew up in Maryland and earned his undergraduate degree in history from Johns Hopkins University. He then completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree at the University of Maryland Dental School, followed by a general dentistry residency (AEGD) at the Martinsburg Veterans Affairs Medical Center in West Virginia. This advanced training provided him with comprehensive experience in treating complex dental cases and working with patients from diverse backgrounds.

A Foundation in Research and Lifelong Learning

Dr. Zhou’s dedication to lifelong learning is evident in his extensive research and professional experience. Before pursuing dentistry, he contributed to several high-level research projects, including studies on molecular genetics at Harvard Medical School and the National Institutes of Health. This strong scientific foundation has shaped his methodical and innovative approach to dental care.

Personalized Care and Smile Transformations

At Peartree Dental Care, Dr. Zhou stays at the forefront of dental advancements, integrating leading-edge technology to deliver the most comfortable, precise, and efficient care. He also takes pride in offering personalized smile-transforming treatments such as dental implants and Spark clear aligners, ensuring his patients achieve functional and aesthetic goals.

Beyond his practice, Dr. Zhou has completed hundreds of hours of continuing education in all areas of dentistry, reflecting his commitment to providing his patients with the best care possible. He is a member of the American Dental Association and the Maryland State Dental Association.

When he’s not helping patients achieve healthier, more confident smiles, Dr. Zhou enjoys spending time with his family and friends, fishing, golfing, and cheering on the Baltimore Ravens.
