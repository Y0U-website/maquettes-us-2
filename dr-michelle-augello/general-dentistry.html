Dentures

All of our doctors are skilled at designing partial, complete and implant-supported dentures, which are made by local labs right here in Western New York. As we get your mouth ready for comfortably fitting, fully functional dentures, we’ll make sure you you’ll never have to go without any teeth during the healing phase of your treatment.

Extractions

When a tooth becomes too far gone to be properly fixed, our team is ready to help guide you through the extraction process. We’ll talk through how the tooth will be removed, what to expect, and come up with plan together to decide if, when, and how the tooth will be replaced.

Fillings

We always plan white fillings for our patients for two reasons – it’s a restoration that more closely matches the color of the tooth, and you can eat and drink right after your appointment without waiting. While silver fillings are also available, we would love to have a candid discussion about their pros and cons before treatment.
