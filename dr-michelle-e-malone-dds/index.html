Home Our Team Services Contact Us FAQ New Patient Paperwork Bill-Pay General & Family Dentistry Malone Dental is dedicated to providing quality treatments for your entire family. We happily welcome children to our office, and we provide complete dental care throughout your entire life to help keep your mouth healthy and your smile beautiful.

We offer a wide range of services including preventative procedures such as cleanings or sealants to restorative procedures such as fillings, crowns, bridges, and dentures.

Les soins

A : The ADA recommends a child’s first visit to the dentist be no later than their first birthday. This visit will establish a dental home for your child. Early examination and prevention care will protect your child’s smile now and in the future.
