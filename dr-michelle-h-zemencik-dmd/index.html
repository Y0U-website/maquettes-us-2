We are a General Dentistry office located in the business district of Bridgeville, PA. Established in 1985, the practice has been treating patients of the Bridgeville area for over 30 years.

An appointment in our office is reserved for you and you only. We do not double (or triple) book patients. Occasionally an emergency will arise and we do our best to treat them without interfering with our scheduled appointments.

Our patients choose us for our quality of care, not because their insurance forces them to choose from a list of dentists.

We are not a participating office in any dental insurance plan. Our philosophy is that any dental decisions and treatment are between the patient and the dentist. Services rendered here are based on what is best for your dental health, not because your insurance will pay for it.

We make it our responsibility to provide high quality, affordable dental care for you not the insurance company.

Regular check-ups and routine dental care make up the heart of our practice. Maintenance of your teeth and gums is just as important after your work is completed. At regular intervals, we are attempting to prevent small issues from becoming major issues.
