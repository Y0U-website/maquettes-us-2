Indications for Invisalign

Getting Invisalign clear aligners is about much more than just making your smile look better. Orthodontic abnormalities can seriously harm your teeth, oral health, and overall health. With Invisalign treatments, your smile will not only be more attractive but also healthier and stronger!

Invisalign can be used to treat many of the most common orthodontic problems, including:

Crowded Teeth

Your other teeth may shift and become crowded when teeth start to develop and there isn’t much open space. This makes it difficult to maintain a healthy and clean mouth. Thankfully, the Invisalign system can move your teeth using transparent aligners in a way that puts them in the right alignment and gives your smile the room it requires.

Spaced Teeth

Too-wide gaps between your teeth might affect more than just how you look and how confident you feel. These bare, challenging-to-brush areas are also a great haven for germs and food particles, where they can build up and significantly raise your risk of problems like cavities and gum disease. Invisalign has the ability to close these gaps, depending on the amount of space involved.

Bite Alignment

Do you have an overbite, underbite, or crossbite? A misaligned bite can result in a wide range of significant problems, including increased dental wear and tear, nighttime jaw clenching and grinding, and even TMJ dysfunction. Thankfully, Invisalign can fix a lot of mild to moderate biting problems!

Invisalign First

At Rosetown Dental, we offer Invisalign First phase 1 orthodontic treatment. The procedure Invisalign First was created especially for tiny smiles. A child’s smile develops along with them as they mature. Children between the ages of 6 and 10 may be at the ideal age to begin orthodontic treatment, even if they still have baby teeth. This is known as Phase 1 treatment.

At this point, we can notice problems that may require early intervention to solve a growing condition. The Invisalign First clear aligner treatment is intended to meet the special demands of developing children while providing predictable outcomes and a positive experience.

What is Invisalign First?

Invisalign First aligners are much like standard Invisalign aligners for adults, except they are designed to accommodate the development of permanent teeth as they erupt.

Invisalign First can be used to help correct issues like arch development and expansion, tooth spacing, crowding, and protrusions, and to change the overall appearance of the smile.

Invisalign First guides children’s teeth into the correct positions as they grow, which may lessen or eliminate the need for more extensive or invasive orthodontic treatment when they are older.

Invisalign First aligners are a great alternative to traditional phase 1 orthodontics with metal appliances and brackets and wires because it is:

  • Designed to treat a broad range of teeth straightening issues in growing children, from simple to complex, including crowding, spacing, and narrow dental arches.
  • Removable for easy hygiene, making it easier for growing children to brush and floss.
  • Designed for comfort – which means no discomfort from rubbing brackets or poking wires.

The Benefits of Invisalign

Invisalign is quickly becoming one of the most popular orthodontic treatments available, and it’s easy to see why! This unique service offers important advantages over traditional metal braces, like:

Clear aligners: The clear plastic that makes up your aligners is hardly detectable by others, allowing you to smile with confidence while wearing them.

No diet changes: You can eat whatever you like during your Invisalign treatment.

Metal-free: Clear plastic aligners are also the ideal choice for patients with metal allergies or sensitivities.

Faster results: You can expect to achieve a straighter smile in just 6-18 months.

Easy to clean: All you need is a toothbrush, clear soap, and lukewarm water to keep your aligners clean. No complicated floss threaders or extra tools!

No irritation: Unlike traditional braces that rub against your lips and cheeks, Invisalign is smooth, so there is no irritation to your soft tissues, offering a much more comfortable straightening experience.

Fewer dental visits: With Invisalign, your scheduled appointments will not only be fewer than those needed with metal braces but quicker too!

How Invisalign Works

First, we will take a digital scan of your teeth. This information will be used to create a customized treatment plan to then create an entire series of custom aligners that gradually corrects your orthodontic issues. In many cases, we will also put small, virtually invisible pieces of white filling material on your teeth. These help the aligners connect snugly to your teeth and encourage better, faster results. These “attachments” do not harm your teeth and will be smoothed away at the end of your treatment, so you would never know they were even there in the first place.
