About Dr. Rasmussen

DR. RASMUSSEN

Dentist, Central Point, OR

Dr. Mikyl Rasmussen was born and raised in Southern Oregon. He graduated from Oregon Health Science University School of Dentistry.

He is a member of the American Dental Association, Oregon Dental Association and Southern Oregon Dental Society.

After graduation he returned to Southern Oregon to establish his career and raise his family. He and his wife, Megan are the proud parents of their son Quinn and their newest addition to their family, their daughter Charlie.

Dr. Rasmussen enjoys family time, outdoor activities and visiting national parks.
