Milos R. Janicek, DMD

Dr. Milos R. Janicek is dedicated to providing the highest standard of care in a relaxed and caring environment.

In addition to private practice, Dr. Janicek enjoys teaching and sharing his passion for endodontics. He returns to the Tufts University Department of Endodontics to lecture on endodontic microsurgery technique, among other topics. His professional affiliations include the American Association of Endodontics, the American Dental Association, and the Connecticut Dental Association. Dr. Janicek also served for five years on the board of the New London County Dental Society and was also president of that organization.

Dr. Janicek lives in Stonington, Connecticut with his wife, Erin, and their three children. In his spare time... well, there isn’t much spare time with three kids... but he enjoys cycling, playing tennis, golf, and skiing anyway.
