Teeth Whitening

ZOOM! Teeth Whitening

The ZOOM! in-office teeth whitening system makes your teeth dramatically whiter in about an hour. The procedure is simple and begins with a short preparation to cover your lips and gums, leaving only your teeth exposed.

Porcelain Veneers

Veneers are a widely used cosmetic dental procedure in which our dentist places a covering over the outside (visible area) of the tooth. Porcelain Veneers are usually only applied to teeth that will be visible when a patient is talking or smiling.

Cosmetic Bonding

Cosmetic bonding involves adhering composite resin material matched to the color of the tooth to the front of the tooth. Bonding may be done to repair damage caused by decay, to alter a tooth’s alignment, to close gaps between teeth or for cosmetic purposes.
