Comprehensive Pediatric Dental Services

At Pediatric Dental Specialist of Dallas, we offer a full range of pediatric dental services designed to promote healthy smiles and lasting oral health:

  • White Fillings: Tooth-colored fillings for a natural and seamless restoration.
  • Teeth Cleanings: Gentle cleanings to remove plaque and maintain oral hygiene.
  • Crowns: Durable and protective crowns to restore decayed or damaged teeth.
  • Digital X-Rays: Advanced imaging technology for safe and accurate diagnostics.
  • Simple Extractions: Careful removal of teeth when necessary.
  • Dental Emergencies: Prompt and effective care for urgent dental needs.
  • New Patient Exams: Thorough evaluations to assess your child’s dental health.
  • Fluoride Treatments: Strengthening enamel and preventing cavities.
  • Recall Exams: Regular check-ups to monitor your child’s oral health.
  • First-Time Visits for Infants and Toddlers: Gentle care and guidance for your baby’s first dental experience.
  • Sedation Dentistry: Stress-free and comfortable treatment options for children with dental anxiety.
  • Dentistry for Special Needs Kids: Personalized care in a welcoming environment.
