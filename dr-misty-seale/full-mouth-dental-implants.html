What Are All-on-X Implants?

All-on-X dental implants are a modern approach to replacing a full arch of teeth using an “X” number of strategically placed implants, usually anywhere from four to eight implants, depending on the location. These implants support a secure, non-removable set of replacement teeth that look, feel, and function just like your natural smile. Through a process called osseointegration, the implants fuse with your jawbone to create a stable foundation for long-term success.

Compared to traditional implants—which often require one post per tooth—All-on-X is faster, more cost-effective, and ideal for patients with reduced bone volume.

How Does the All-on-X Procedure Work?

At iHeart Dental, our process is designed for both precision and comfort. Here’s what to expect:

What Is the Difference Between All-on-4 and All-on-X?

While All-on-4 uses exactly four implants, All-on-X allows us to place four, six, eight, or any number of implants based on your unique anatomy, jaw strength, and bone health. Implants have a 95 % success rate, and with a 5 % failure rate, the All-on-X prosthesis is over-engineered and designed for success that lasts a lifetime.

At iHeart Dental, we customize your treatment using 3D imaging to determine the number of implants needed, ensuring your full dental arch reconstruction is built to last. Whether you need four implants or eight implants, the entire process and procedure are still the same. Therefore, you get better support and long-term success without greatly affecting the overall costs.

Are All-on-X Dental Implants Permanent?

Yes—All-on-X implants are a permanent, fixed solution for tooth loss. With proper care and maintenance, they can last a lifetime. Unlike removable dentures, these implants won’t shift or slip and require no adhesive.
