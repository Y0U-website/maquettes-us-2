About Esral Dental

Dr. Esral believes that his patients should have sufficient information to make informed decisions about their oral health. He strives to educate his patients and enjoys taking the time to answer questions in order to create the ideal doctor-patient relationship. You’ll find important information about treatment options on this website. Some of these options include: tooth-colored fillings, ZOOM teeth whitening , dental implants, dentures, and treatment for sleep apnea .
