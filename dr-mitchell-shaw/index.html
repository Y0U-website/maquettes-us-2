Mitchell B. Shaw D.D.S., P.L.L.C. of Restorative and Implant Dentistry of Long Island (RIDLI), grew up in North Bellmore and attended W. C. Mepham High School, where he was active in student government, civic organizations and interscholastic sports. Dr. Shaw attended Tufts University in Medford, Massachusetts, where he was a four-year letterman on the varsity baseball team, served as an elected member of the Student Senate and continued his participation in various civic activities. He graduated with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from Tufts University.

Upon graduation from Tufts University, Dr. Mitchell Shaw matriculated at the State University of New York at Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine. Dr. Shaw graduated with numerous scholastic Honors and was chosen by his classmates as Class Salutatorian, while maintaining his interest in student government and politics, as well as athletics.

Following his graduation from S.U.N.Y. Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Shaw completed a General Practice Residency at Long Island Jewish-Hillside Medical Center in New Hyde Park, N. Y., where he served in the dental facilities at L.I.J., Hillside medical center, Queens Hospital and principally at Parker Geriatric Center.
