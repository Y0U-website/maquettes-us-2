Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults who are missing one or more teeth are potential candidates for implants, but a thorough evaluation is always the first step. The ideal candidate has sufficient jawbone density to support the implant and is free from uncontrolled systemic conditions that could interfere with healing.

For patients who have experienced bone loss due to long-term tooth absence or prior extractions, implants may still be possible with preparatory procedures. Our team may recommend bone grafting to rebuild the foundation needed for a stable implant. We also use advanced platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) technology to accelerate healing and support tissue regeneration, a cutting-edge approach Dr. El-Ounsi incorporates to optimize recovery and long-term implant health.

Comfort and Sedation During Implant Surgery

Concern about pain is one of the most common reasons patients delay implant treatment and one of the most manageable. Dental implant surgery is performed under local anesthesia, and we offer a full range of sedation options, including IV sedation, to ensure you are comfortable and relaxed throughout the procedure.

For patients with dental anxiety or those undergoing more involved cases, our general anesthesia services allow you to sleep through the procedure entirely. Dr. El-Ounsi holds ACLS and PALS certifications and has extensive training in sedation from his residency at Bellevue Hospital, where IV sedation and implant surgery were central to his clinical focus. Our entire team is trained to manage your care at every level of sedation safely and effectively.
