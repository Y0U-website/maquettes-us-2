Meet the Doctor

A practicing general dentist for more than 10 years, Dr. Ghani is passionate about providing advanced quality dental care to his patients.

His interest in dentistry arose while studying Biology at Philadelphia University, where he graduated Cum Laude. He went on to earn his DMD (Doctor of Dental Medicine) from Temple University, School of Dentistry, in Philadelphia where again he earned his degree with honors and special distinction in Restorative Dentistry and Endodontics.

After working for sometime, Dr. Ghani decided to start his private practice in the heart of Philadelphia. For better access to quality care for his patients, he planned and furnished a brand new dental clinic in Bala Cynwyd, where he practices today. Since 2009, his office philosophy has been to provide personalized and advanced quality care to patients, in a comfortable and friendly environment.

Committed to providing a beautiful and healthy smile for his patients, Dr. Ghani keeps himself current on advances in dental medicine and routinely attends courses and seminars in cosmetics and endodontics. Email us: dr@advance-dentalcare.com
