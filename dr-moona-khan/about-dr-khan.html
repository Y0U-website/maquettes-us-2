Meet Our Doctors: A Team Focused on Comfort, Understanding, and Long-Term Care

At South Sound Dental Care, we believe dental visits should feel calm and predictable. Our approach is simple. We take the time to understand your needs, explain each step before treatment, and move at a pace that helps you stay comfortable. Whether you are returning to care after years away or maintaining a routine schedule, our goal is to make every visit feel manageable.

Our doctors share a commitment to patient-first dentistry. They offer thoughtful communication, steady pacing, and sedation options when needed. This combination helps many patients feel at ease and supports better long-term oral health. Dr. Moona Khan, D.D.S. How She Practices Dr. Khan’s approach centers on clarity, trust, and patient comfort. Many people delay care due to fear or past experiences, so she creates an environment where questions are encouraged and decisions are shared. The foundation of her care is her calm, steady communication and attention to detail.

Training and Focus

Dr. Khan is a graduate of the Kois Institute, where she trained in complex restorative and aesthetic dentistry. This advanced background guides her work with smile design, long-term oral health planning, and comprehensive treatment. She routinely works with patients who have not been to the dentist in many years and helps them rebuild confidence through practical steps and clear explanations.
