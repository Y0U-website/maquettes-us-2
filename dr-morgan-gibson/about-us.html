Meet the Team at Gibson Family Dentistry

Welcome to our office! Our entire team looks forward to meeting you and helping your family have the healthy and attractive smiles that you deserve.

At Gibson Family Dentistry, we understand the true meaning of high-quality and compassionate dental care. Every patient who walks through our doors is treated like we would want our own families to be treated: with the utmost respect and kindness.

We are always honest with our patients about their oral health, and we value our patients as individuals; you are never just a number to us. Because of our dedication to providing excellent family dentistry, we are the trusted dental home of many families in Calhoun County.

We look forward to welcoming you to our Anniston dental office!

Morgan Gibson, DMD

I remember the exact day that I discovered I wanted to be a dentist. I was sitting in the dental chair in 8th grade, looking around the room and noted the materials, the attention to detail, and the care that the dentist and the team took with my treatment. I remember thinking, "I really like it here. I think I could do this!" From that moment forward, I dedicated all of my efforts to going to dental school so that I could combine my love of science and art into a career that would allow me to make a difference.

I attended the University of Alabama, where I completed my bachelor s and master s degrees and went on to get my Doctorate of Dental Medicine degree at the University of Alabama Birmingham. I completed a residency in Advanced General Dentistry (AEGD) at UAB Hospital, which allowed me to gain experience in complete smile rehabilitation, dental implants, and how to treat patients with complex medical histories. I love what I do, and I love making people smile!

I am married to Kevin (Dr. David Gibson s son), and we have two wonderful little girls, Amelia and Sutton. If I have any spare time let s be real, there are not a lot of that these days! I like to paint, cook, and play pretend that I am a Disney Princess with my girls.

I look forward to seeing you and your family in my office!

Kylan Oden, DMD

She earned her bachelor’s degree from the University of Alabama, and then her Doctorate of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Alabama at Birmingham. Dr. Oden resides in Birmingham with her husband Sam and their dogs Penelope and Polly.
