Dental Implants

In addition to over 17 years of practicing general dentistry, Dr. Chauhan is committed to continued education on the latest dental implant techniques while maintaining the most up-to-date implant technology including a CT imaging machine and intraoral scanner. His devotion to individualized treatment and care for his patients result in lasting, successful results. A dental implant procedure typically includes the following steps and is treated in stages:

  • Home / Treatment / Dental Implants A dental implant is an artificial tooth root that is placed into the jaw bone to replace a missing tooth. Dental implants are the best option to replace a missing tooth as a result of loss due to: Extensive decay
  • Periodontal (gum) disease
  • Trauma
  • Congenitally missing teeth
  • To replace a single missing tooth
  • To support a bridge that replaces a missing tooth or teeth
  • To anchor a denture to achieve a better fit
  • A titanium implant or screw is placed in the jaw bone. (A bone graft may or may not be indicated at this stage to establish ideal retention of the implant)
  • The site is then covered back over with gum tissue for approximately 3 weeks to allow for proper healing
  • The implant site is uncovered
  • A dental impression (mold) is taken so it may be sent to a dental lab where the abutment (connector) and crown will be custom fabricated
  • The site is recovered with gum tissue
  • The final crown is fitted onto the implant using the custom abutment (connector) and is permanently cemented after patient and doctor approval.
