The practice

After careful consideration and reflection, we have made the very difficult decision to close the dental clinic and retire. We understand that this decision may come as a surprise, so we want to take a moment to explain the reasons behind our decision.

Over the years, we have had the privilege to serve this wonderful community and build strong relationships with all of you. It has been a true honor to be a part of your lives, and we are grateful for the trust you have placed in us to care for your dental health.

While we have enjoyed every moment of our time serving you, we have faced several unforeseen challenges in recent times. These challenges have made it increasingly difficult to maintain the quality of care and service we provide to our patients. As such, we have made the difficult decision to close.

Should you have any questions or require assistance in selecting a new dental provider, please do not hesitate to reach out to our office. Our staff will be more than happy to assist you. However, initially, we do recommend you contact your dental insurance for assistance in selecting a new dental provider.

Thank you for your understanding, support, and loyalty throughout the years. We will always cherish the memories we have created together and wish you all the best in your future dental care.
