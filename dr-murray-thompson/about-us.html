About Pierre Dental Clinic

Located right in the center of Pierre and Fort Pierre , our practice has the pleasure of treating patients from all areas of central South Dakota. Drs. Thompson and Umiker are committed to the most up-to-date dental procedures, yet have not forgotten that patient comfort is our primary responsibility. We offer a wide range of general dentistry services and are conveniently open early in the morning. Our staff is not only friendly, but trained to the highest standards available in South Dakota.

We look forward to meeting you and providing you and your family top notch dental treatment with a caring personal touch.

Doctors

Dr. Murray Thompson

COL (Dr.) Murray Thompson grew up in Pierre, South Dakota. He is a 2001 graduate of Creighton University with a BS in Physics and a 2005 graduate of the Creighton University Medical Center School of Dentistry. He currently practices general dentistry and is the owner of the Pierre Dental Clinic.

When not at work, you will find him spending time with his wife Jen, their 2 children, 4 dogs, and 4 goats. He is also very active in flying small general aviation aircraft as an Instrument Rated Commercial Pilot and Flight Instructor, helping manage a family cattle ranching corporation, and playing guitar, the piano, and/or singing in his church’s worship band on Sundays.
