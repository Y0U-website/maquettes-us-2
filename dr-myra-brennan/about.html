Renowned Greater-Boston-based cosmetic dentist, Dr. Myra Brennan is known best for her exceptional finesse for creating facial balance and harmony when designing teeth. She specializes in creating natural and beautiful smiles for her patients with a high-touch, meticulous approach.

Her keen eye for aesthetics combined with her unparalleled work ethic and training gives her patients a true partner when it comes to transforming their smiles.
