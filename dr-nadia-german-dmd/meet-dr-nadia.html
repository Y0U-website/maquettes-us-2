Meet the Doctor

0 Meet Dr. Nadia German Dr. Nadia German is excited to bring more than 30 years of dental experience to Wheeling, Illinois with the opening of New Generation Dental Care . She is proud to serve the community with a compassionate and modern approach to dentistry.

She received her professional dental degree in Kharkiv, Ukraine , before moving to the Chicago area in 1998. She continued her advance training at Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine , developing an expertise in clinical excellence and patient-centered care.

Prior to NGDC , Dr. German was the lead dentist and owner of a bustling dental practice in the northern suburbs of Chicago . For 19 years , she earned the trust and reverence of generations of patients. Today, she carries on her lifelong mission of providing high-quality dental care as a general dentist with advanced training in cosmetic dentistry and orthodontics .

Dr. Nadia’s Professional Focus

Dr. Nadia’s philosophy centers on the intersection of preventative and restorative dentistry to create a path towards maintaining a healthy smile beyond each visit. By putting patient comfort and education at the forefront of your care, she believes that dental care can be a natural part of anyone’s health routine.

Her speciality in cosmetic dentistry combines advanced dental techniques with an eye for artistry to create a natural, beautiful smile that you can’t wait to show off.

Beyond Denistry

Outside the office, Dr. German is an artist at heart . She is passionate about painting, working primarily with oil paint and liquid art techniques . This is where creativity and precision come together—much like in dentistry.

She loves to travel the world with her husband, Dmitriy , hiking in the mountains and biking , always excited to embrace new experiences and cultures.

Dr. German’s daughter, Dasha , lives in Los Angeles , pursuing their dream as an artist and filmmaker, while her son, Jacob , is following in her footsteps. He is currently completing pre-dental prerequisites at the University of Iowa , preparing for dental school, with a dream of one day practicing dentistry alongside his mother.

Dr. German believes dentistry is both a science and an art—and she looks forward to caring for your smile with skill, experience, and heart.
