Venice Dental Group

Venice Dental Group was found by Dr. Metias who has over 25 years of experience dental surgery. Our office has serve our community and neighboring communities for the past since 2002. The Dental Exam is done using Digital X-Rays and Digital Intra Oral Camera.

Dr, Metias received her dental license on 1975 from Cairo University - School of Dentistry. since then Dr. Metias has practiced dentistry in Cairo, Egypt, and London, United Kingdom. After practicing dentistry for several years, Dr. Metias moved to the United States, i n 1997 and got her dental license from the University of Pacific - school of dentistry , San Francisco.

Dr Mary Makar, is a graduate of Miss International University Cairo Egypt 2006, graduate of New York University 2010. I’m very passionate in getting to know my patients. Dr Mary has been finding joy in treating patients and listening to their need to offer the best treatment.
