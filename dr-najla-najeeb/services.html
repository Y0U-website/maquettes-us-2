Services

We Cover All Aspects Of Dentistry

From providing you with a new set of dentures to doing full mouth smile makeovers, we do it all!

Cosmetic Dentistry

Our Levittown dental team provides gentle, modern care to keep your smile bright, and confident.

Endodontics

Each tooth holds delicate nerves inside its canals, and infection often requires a root canal to relieve pain.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative and cosmetic dentistry repair and enhance teeth using veneers, crowns, fillings, implants, and more.

Invisalign

Invisalign offers Long Island patients a clear, comfortable, and discreet alternative to traditional metal braces.

Pediatric Dentistry

We provide children of all ages with gentle, fun, and preventive dental care they can enjoy.

General Dentistry

Comprehensive dental care to keep your teeth and gums healthy for life.

Tooth Extractions

Safe and gentle tooth extractions to relieve pain and protect your oral health.

TMJ Disorder Dentist

Expert TMJ care to relieve jaw pain and restore comfortable movement.

Emergency Care

Being in pain is the worst! We provide 24-48 hour emergency care for all our patients.

Holistic Dentistry

We provide conventional and complementary dental care, focuses on whole body health.

Periodontal Maintenance

Periodontal maintenance is a dental procedure that is required for the people who have their tooth extracted

Dental Bone Grafting

Dental bone grafting is a process that is required sometimes when tooth is damaged

Wisdom Teeth

Wisdom teeth are the final set of molars that usually emerge during the age of 17 to 25

Dental Sealants

Sealants are protective coatings applied to keep your teeth away from all kinds of damage

Cosmetic Bonding

Bonding is the process of applying a composite resin resembling normal teeth colouration.

Dental Deep Cleanings

Deep cleaning is a method used by dentists and remove plaque as well as tartar from the oral cavity.
