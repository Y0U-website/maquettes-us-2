Meet Our Team

Dr. Najla Najeeb DDS

Following graduation, Dr. Najeeb completed her residency at the New York Hospital Trauma Center in Queens, gaining invaluable experience in high-complexity emergency care. She then spent several years refining her skills within private and corporate dental environments. Driven by a desire to escape the fast-paced, high-volume corporate model, she decided to branch out and establish her own practice—one centered entirely around personalized, patient-first care.

Zahra Haider

My name is Zahra Haider, and I am a dedicated Dental Assistant here at Posh Dental. I thoroughly enjoy collaborating closely with Dr. Najeeb to help our patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles. In my role, I am responsible for taking precise dental X-rays and supporting the clinical team in preparing tailored treatment plans for each individual. Patient comfort is my top priority; I always do my absolute best to provide a gentle touch, ensuring that every patient feels safe, comfortable, and genuinely cared for throughout their visit.
