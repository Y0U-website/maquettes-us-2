Common Questions About Emergency Dentistry

  • What counts as a dental emergency? A dental emergency typically includes situations causing severe pain, such as a tooth infection, a knocked-out or broken tooth, or significant swelling.
  • Can you help with a broken tooth or filling? Yes, we offer repair for broken teeth, fillings, and other restorations to restore your smile and function quickly.
  • How soon can I be seen for emergency care? We strive to see emergency patients as soon as possible, often the same day.
  • Do you accept walk-ins? We do our best to accommodate walk-ins; however, calling ahead can help us prepare for your arrival.
  • Are emergency dental visits covered by insurance? Many insurance plans cover emergency visits. Our team will work with you and your provider to help determine coverage options.
