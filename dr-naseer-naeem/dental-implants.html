What patients ask us most.

Do dental implants hurt?

Placement is done with local anesthetic, and most patients compare the recovery to a routine extraction — mild soreness for a day or two, managed with over-the-counter pain relief. We’ll review comfort options with you beforehand.

How long does the whole process take?

Most implants take three to six months from placement to final crown, because we allow time for the bone to fuse to the implant. You’ll have a temporary tooth in visible areas the entire time.

How long do implants last?

With good home care and regular checkups, the implant itself can last decades — often a lifetime. The crown on top may eventually need replacing, just like a natural tooth’s restoration.

Are implants better than a bridge or denture?

Implants are the only option that replaces the tooth root, which helps preserve the jawbone and doesn’t rely on neighboring teeth. We’ll walk through the trade-offs honestly so you can choose what fits your mouth and budget.

Can you place and restore the implant in one office?

Yes. We handle both the surgical placement and the final crown, bridge, or denture in-house — so your care, records, and team stay in one place from start to finish.
