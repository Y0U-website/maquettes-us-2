Les soins

We attach a custom crown, bridge, or denture — color-matched to your smile — and check the fit and bite.

Placement is done with local anesthetic, and most patients compare the recovery to a routine extraction — mild soreness for a day or two, managed with over-the-counter pain relief. We’ll review comfort options with you beforehand.

A dental implant is a small titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth. Once the bone bonds to it, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached on top — restoring how your smile looks, feels, and functions, while helping preserve the jawbone…
