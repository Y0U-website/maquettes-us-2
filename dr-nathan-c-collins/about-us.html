Technology for Modern Care

All Digital X-Rays

With digital X-rays, we can spend less time waiting for images to be developed and more time actually treating you. Even better, the images are higher quality, and the process is more comfortable for you.

NOMAD Handheld X-Rays

Instead of waiting for an X-ray machine to be ready or moving you between rooms, we can use this handheld tool to take multiple, accurate images quickly.

Isolite™ Comfort System

Say goodbye to uncomfortable dental visits and a post-appointment sore jaw. This advanced system makes it easy to keep your mouth open while we work and protects your cheeks and tongue, too.

Intraoral Camera

We use our intraoral camera to show you detailed images of your own mouth, giving you a dentist’s view of what’s going on. This helps the patient to better understand their mouth, allowing them to make more confident decisions.

Sedation Options

Dental fear and anxiety are no match for us! We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) at no charge, and we have oral conscious sedation available to help you completely relax.
