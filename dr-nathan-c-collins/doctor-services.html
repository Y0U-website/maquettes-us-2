Our Dental Services

We aim to promote, restore, and maintain your oral health. This includes regular dental exams, cleanings, and x-rays. Prevention helps avoid serious and costly dental problems and is the key to having a healthy, confident, beautiful smile. It is our goal to assist you in maintaining optimal oral health through comprehensive and preventive patient care.

1. Evaluation

2. Treatment

After the oral evaluation the dentist may choose to create a treatment plan to address the areas of concern. In many cases a filling, root canal, crown, or bridge are used to restore your smile to its original luster. Usually impressions are taken on the first visit to prepare the laboratory.

3. Happy Smile!

On your final visit, your final restoration will be placed or your custom prosthetic will be delivered if needed. We will make adjustments to any appliances you may have been prescribed. You and your doctor will go over any questions or concerns you may have going forward.
