Dr. Nathaniel Fogel

Dr. Nathaniel Fogel was born and raised in Queens, NY, and is one of four children. Blessed with an aptitude for hands-on work and science and an outgoing personality, his interest in dentistry was sparked by family members who worked in the field. Dentistry perfectly combined his passion for science with his enjoyment of creating things with his hands while allowing him to develop meaningful relationships with patients.

Dr. Fogel attended dental school at the University of Maryland , graduated in 2012, and completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency in 2013. He is a member of the Maryland State Dental Association , Howard County Dental Association , American Dental Association , and Maimonides Dental Study Club.

After working in various family and cosmetic dentistry offices in the Baltimore vicinity, Dr. Fogel purchased his own practice in 2016. He enthusiastically cares for patients of all ages with his trademark passion, precision, and pleasant demeanor. What he loves most about dentistry is helping his patients and developing lasting relationships with them as they work together toward optimal oral health.

Dr. Fogel lives with his wife, Ayelet, and their four children in Baltimore. When he s not in the office, he pursues several other pastimes. He s an accomplished cook and baker who creates artisanal bread with a true doctor s appreciation for the scientific aspect of cooking. He also enjoys reading, playing with his kids, and rooting for the Islanders and Jets.

Dr. Roberta Dosh

Dr. Roberta Dosh joined Pineview Dental in January 2025, bringing her over three decades of dental expertise. She graduated from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry in 1993, completed a residency at the Veterans Administration Hospital in 1994, and has accumulated hundreds of hours of continuing education throughout her career.

After establishing her solo practice in Burtonsville, Maryland, in 1994, Dr. Dosh dedicated herself to providing exceptional care until selling it in 2021. Though now enjoying a part-time schedule, her commitment to dentistry remains steadfast. What draws Dr. Dosh to the profession is the immediate impact she can make patients often arrive with a problem and leave with a solution. For Dr. Dosh, dentistry perfectly combines technical skills with the deeply human elements of compassionate listening and care.

What she values most about her years in practice are the meaningful relationships cultivated with patients, built on trust and friendship. This patient-centered approach has been the cornerstone of her professional philosophy throughout her career.

Outside the dental office, Dr. Dosh enjoys tending to her flower garden and delighting in the colorful blooms outside her kitchen windows. While she collects many recipes, she admits to cooking only a select few. A unique fact about Dr. Dosh is that she simultaneously took ballroom dancing and golf lessons while working with a personal trainer and lifting weights. A fascinating historical connection? Dr. Dosh had the unusual opportunity to hold one of George Washington s dentures, gaining firsthand understanding of the dental challenges faced by the first president.
