Frequently Asked Questions

  • Am I a candidate for dental implants? Most adults with good general health are candidates for at least one implant option. The 3D CBCT scan at your consultation is what tells us for certain. It shows us the underlying bone and anatomy in three dimensions, and lets us build a plan tailored to you.
  • How long does the process take? It depends on the treatment. Some single-tooth implant cases are completed in a few visits over a few months. Full-arch cases often involve a same-day set of temporary teeth, followed by final restorations once healing is complete. Your consultation includes a personalized timeline.
  • Are implants painful? Implant placement is done under local anesthesia. Most patients report they were more comfortable than they expected. We also offer sedation options for patients who want additional comfort.
  • What if I need financing? We work with several third-party financing partners and offer flexible monthly payment plans. The September pricing on this page reflects payment without financing; financing fees, if applicable, are separate. We’ll go over all your options at your consultation.
  • What happens if I don’t book by September 30th? Our standard fees return on October 1st: $30,000/arch for fixed full-arch and $20,000/arch for snap-on dentures. The consultation and 3D scan are also complimentary only through September 30th as part of Dental Implant Month.
