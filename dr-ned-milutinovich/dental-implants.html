Dental Implants

Each type of dental implant has its unique characteristics, advantages, and considerations. Your dentist or oral surgeon will evaluate your oral health and discuss the most suitable implant option based on your individual needs and treatment objectives.

  • What types of dental implants exist? Endosteal implants, the most common type, are placed directly into the jawbone for strong support.
  • Subperiosteal implants sit on the bone’s surface, making them ideal for patients with lower bone density.
  • All-on-4 implants use four strategically placed posts to support a full arch of teeth.
  • Mini implants are smaller in diameter than standard implants and are often used in situations with limited bone volume or space. These implants are commonly used to stabilize removable dentures, providing increased retention and stability for denture wearers.
  • Zygomatic implants are longer implants that anchor into the zygomatic (cheek) bone rather than the maxillary (upper jaw) bone. They are used in patients with severe upper-jaw bone loss who are not candidates for traditional implant placement due to insufficient bone volume. Zygomatic implants offer a viable solution for restoring dental function and aesthetics in these challenging cases.
  • What is the aftercare process for dental implants? After your dental implant procedure, proper aftercare helps ensure a smooth, successful recovery. We’ll guide you on managing discomfort, keeping the area clean, and choosing soft foods while your implant heals. Gentle brushing, prescribed rinses, and avoiding smoking are key. Attend follow-up visits to monitor progress and answer questions. With a little care and our support, you’ll be smiling confidently again in no time. We’re here every step.
