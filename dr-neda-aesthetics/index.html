The practice

Cleanings, periodontal care, and oral cancer screenings keep small issues from turning into bigger ones. Regular visits give you a clear picture of your oral health and a comfortable place to keep it on track.

From whitening to veneers, cosmetic dentistry at Neda Aesthetics is built on careful planning and an eye for natural-looking detail. Dr. Neda&#x27;s continuing focus on full-mouth veneer cases means the work is as considered as it is beautiful.

Crowns, bridges, dental implants, and oral surgery handled in one office, with CEREC milling and CBCT imaging on site. Whether you need a single tooth restored or a more complex plan, the technology and the expertise stay under one roof.

Neda Aesthetics is a small practice on purpose. That means more time with your dentist, more attention to the details of your treatment, and access to the kind of digital technology you&#x27;d expect from a much larger office. It&#x27;s dentistry that feels personal and current at the same time.

iTero, Primescan, CEREC milling, and CBCT imaging let us scan, plan, and treat with precision. No goopy impressions, faster turnarounds, and treatment plans you can actually see.

Dental implant placements and restorations, bone grafts, sinus lifts, and impacted wisdom teeth are all handled at our office. Fewer referrals out means a more connected experience from start to finish.

Dr. Neda&#x27;s whole approach is built around helping patients feel at ease, including patients who fall asleep mid-procedure (it happens often). Monitors, music, and a calm pace are part of every visit.

New patient forms are fully digital, so you can fill everything out before you arrive. Your first visit starts with care, not a clipboard.
