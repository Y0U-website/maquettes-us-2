Does dental implant surgery hurt?

We understand that surgically placing a dental implant into the jawbone sounds like an intense experience. However, patients at our Gaithersburg dental office report that the process was much easier than a dental extraction.

Placing a dental implant doesn’t cause severe discomfort because there are few nerves in the bone that would cause pain. It also helps to have experienced dentists like Dr. Daftary and Dr. Zaner perform the procedure. They plan the exact placement of your implant before surgery, so they know what to expect before they begin.

We also offer nitrous oxide that will help take the edge off any anxiety you experience. With comforting sedation and a team that puts your needs first, you can expect a comfortable, stress-free experience.

How long does it take for a dental implant to heal?

Our mouths heal quite quickly, but our team will review ways to promote faster healing. They’ll talk to you about post-procedure care and how to handle any discomfort. Most people quickly find the relief they need with over-the-counter anti-inflammatory medication like ibuprofen.

The surgical site should heal in a couple of weeks, and then we need to allow three to six months for the implant to integrate fully with the bone in your jaw. Patience is a virtue, and this is a critical step in the implant process. Allowing the implant to heal completely before adding a restoration like a crown or bridge ensures that your implant will withstand the force of chewing.

What are implant-supported dentures?

Full and partial dentures have helped millions of people enjoy a whole and healthy smile. But since dentures sit on top of the bone, they come with inherent problems. They tend to be unstable, which can cause irritation and discomfort. Dentures can also slip and shift unpredictably making for awkward and embarrassing moments when interacting with others.

But dentures supported with dental implants are as close in form, function, and feel as possible to natural teeth. If you’re getting a full denture, we can place several implants along your jaw to hold it securely in place. Partial dentures are designed to fit in and around remaining teeth and can also be secured in place with dental implants.

Are dental implants permanent?

Since dental implants in Gaithersburg, MD, replace the root of a missing tooth, they are an ideal long-term solution. With proper care that includes preventive dental exams and cleanings and excellent oral hygiene at home, your dental implant should last a lifetime.

The biggest threat to the longevity of your implant is untreated gum disease that attacks the bone in your jaw. Without sufficient bone to support it, the dental implant loses its stability and can ultimately fail.

When you get a dental implant, you must make a lifelong commitment to excellent oral health. You must be sure to brush twice a day and floss once, visit Lakeforest Dental Associates for routine visits for dental exams and cleanings, and eat a healthy diet.

Who should get dental implants?

If you want to see if dental implants are the right solution for you, the first step is to schedule an appointment with Dr. Daftary or Dr. Zaner. They will take digital x-rays to determine if there is sufficient dense bone in the area where we plan to place the implant. If they detect bone loss, they may recommend an additional procedure such as bone grafting or a sinus lift to support your implant adequately.

Our Gaithersburg dentists will also make sure that periodontal disease does is not present. An untreated gum infection can reduce the success of your dental implant.

Finally, a medical history review will determine if you are healthy enough for surgery and will reveal any medication, such as bisphosphonates, which can interfere with the healing of the jawbone

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Unfortunately, there isn’t a straightforward yes-or-no answer to this question. Some insurance companies cover the cost of an implant, but most don’t. However, they may cover a portion of the cost of tooth extraction (if necessary) and your restoration whether it’s a crown, bridge, or denture.

The first step is to contact your insurance provider and ask what portion of the dental implant cost they will cover. If you need assistance, please call our Gaithersburg dental office, and a member of our team will be happy to help you.
