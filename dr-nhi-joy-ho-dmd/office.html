Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Nhi T Ho Your Smyrna Dentist

Dr. Joy earned her Bachelor’s degree in Chemistry from Georgia State University. After her undergraduate studies, she worked as a Forensic Scientist for two years before attending the Medical College of Georgia School of Dentistry, now known as Augusta University. Following obtaining her Doctorate of Dental Medicine, she furthered her dental training and knowledge by completing an additional one-year, hospital-based advanced postgraduate General Practice Residency at the Medical Center Knoxville, TN.

Dr. Joy’s personal life mostly consists of spending time with her family. She enjoys exercise, cooking and a short morning walk around the neighborhood parks. Dr. Joy and her team strive to provide our patients quality care in a comfortable and relaxing environment.
