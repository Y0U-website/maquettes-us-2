About Our Dental Office Newport, RI

Dedicated to Providing High-Quality, Trustworthy Dental Care

At Newport Dental Associates, our team of skilled professionals is dedicated to providing each patient that visits our dental office with an exceptional experience and amazing results. Whether you’re visiting us for a checkup and cleaning or to restore missing teeth, Dr. Baldelli has a wealth of knowledge and skills that will ensure that you receive exceptional care at each of your visits. Committed to Your Safety

Our top priority is your safety and wellbeing, which is why we have thorough sanitary precautions that we follow every day. Due to the emergence of COVID-19, we have also implemented over a dozen additional protocols that meet regulations from leading infection prevention organizations, including OSHA, the CDC, and WHO.

Many Services Under 1 Roof

When you visit Newport Dental Associates, you’ll be able to get most or all of your dental needs taken care of under one roof. That way, you won’t need to worry about travelling to multiple dentists across town to receive the treatments you require, and you’ll be able to stick with the same, trusted team of professionals. Comfortable Dental Office

When you visit Newport Dental Associates, you’ll enjoy the charming traditional, shingle shuttered, Newport building. It’s homey and warm feel always helps our patients feel at-ease when they enter through our doors. Even though it has a historical and welcoming atmosphere, you can count on receiving high-quality and advanced dental care from both Dr. Baldelli, because he stays up-to-date with the latest technology and techniques in the field.

A Team of Patient Advocates

At Newport Dental Associates, we work with our patients to determine treatment plans and will always keep you in-the-loop about your oral health. Transparency and good communication are two of the ways that we’re able to provide exceptional care that makes us special. Whether you have questions about your insurance coverage or want to learn more about a treatment, our team is always there to help put any of your concerns to rest.
