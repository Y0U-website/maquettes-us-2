Get to Know Dr. Nicholas Baldelli

As our dedicated dentist, Dr. Baldelli is proud to provide a vital healthcare service to his local community while at the same time getting to form lasting relationships with people from all walks of life. He’s been working in the world of dentistry for over seven years now with hopefully many more to come! If you would like to learn more about him, just keep reading below.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

For Dr. Baldelli, dentistry combines two of his biggest passions: healthcare and being able to work directly with his hands. This career is a perfect fit for him, and his passion for the work only grows with time.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Baldelli is close with his parents and three brothers. He also has a wonderful fiancée named Sarah. As for personal hobbies, he enjoys spending his summers golfing, being an active member of the Audrain Museum in Newport, and traveling with Sarah, especially to places like Nantucket and Martha’s Vineyard to spend quality time with loved ones. They regularly follow two of his brothers in their baseball careers as well; Rocco is the manager of the Minnesota Twins, and Dante plays baseball for the Boston College Eagles.

Dr. Baldelli is also a big car enthusiast! You can often find him going to various car shows and even partaking in car rallies for special causes.
