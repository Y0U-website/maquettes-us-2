Nicholas Keeling, DDS

Dr. Keeling is a friendly and dedicated general dentist based in the Oklahoma City metro area.

Dr. Keeling obtained his undergraduate degree from Oklahoma State University, where he graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Chemical Engineering. He then went on to earn his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry, where he graduated with special distinction in the top ten percent of his class. After dental school, Dr. Keeling completed a general practice residency at the Oklahoma City Veteran’s Affair’s Hospital, where he learned advanced procedures and had the opportunity to serve our country’s veterans.

Through working with patients and other healthcare professionals in a hospital setting, Dr. Keeling has developed a deep appreciation for how oral health impacts our entire bodies. This experience guides his treatment recommendations to not only improve patients’ oral health, but also to help them live a healthier life. Dr. Keeling is committed to staying current on the latest science and trends in dentistry so that he can provide the best treatment for his patients, and he is currently a member of the American Dental Association, Oklahoma Dental Association, and OKU Dental Honor Society.

Dr. Keeling and his team are committed to caring for patients the same way they would care for members of their own families. This commitment means taking the time to explain to patients what they are doing and making sure that patients are happy with the care Dr. Keeling and his team provide.

When Dr. Keeling is outside of the office, he loves to spend time with his wife Courtney and their two children Nora and Charlie. They also have a rescue dog Sophie who helps keep things interesting! Dr. Keeling also enjoys cooking, traveling and spending time with friends.

Leyann Adi, DDS

Originally from Texas, Dr. Adi was born in Dallas and raised in Houston. She attended the University of Texas at Austin, where she earned a Bachelor of Science and Arts in Nutrition. Her educational background gave her an appreciation for the connection between oral health and overall wellness.

Before dental school, Dr. Adi worked as a dental assistant, an experience that further solidified her passion for dentistry and patient care. She went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry, graduating with special distinction in the top ten percent of her class. She was also inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon, the national dental honor society recognizing academic excellence and leadership in dentistry, and is an active member of the American Dental Association and the Oklahoma Dental Association.

Dr. Adi enjoys all aspects of general dentistry and is committed to continuing education and lifelong learning to provide modern, evidence-based treatment for her patients. She especially values building long-term relationships and helping patients feel more confident about their oral health.

Outside of the office, Dr. Adi enjoys cooking, traveling, attending concerts and music festivals, trying new restaurants, and spending time with family and friends.
