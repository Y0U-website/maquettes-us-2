Why might I need the Perio (Gum Health) Plan?

Periodontal disease is the primary cause of tooth and dental implant loss.

Our genetics can predispose us for periodontal disease, just like heart disease or diabetes. Like any chronic disease, we cannot simply cure periodontal disease, but we can treat it and help you maintain good oral health to reduce your risk for progression of the disease that could lead to tooth or implant loss.

This is our individualized recommended plan for you to stabilize and maintain your periodontal health and prevent tooth and/or implant loss.
