What to Expect From a Cleaning & Hygiene Visit

A Warm Welcome and Exam

Your visit starts with a friendly greeting from our team in Oconomowoc, WI. We’ll review your dental history, listen to any concerns, and make sure you’re comfortable. Dr. Huggett will also perform a gentle oral exam, checking for areas that need extra care or monitoring.

Professional Cleaning and Polishing

Our hygienists carefully remove plaque and tartar buildup using modern tools designed for your comfort. After a thorough cleaning, we polish your teeth to remove stains and leave your smile feeling smooth and looking radiant.

Personalized Education and Plan

Once your cleaning is complete, Dr. Huggett will provide tailored recommendations to keep your smile in great shape between visits. Whether it’s brushing tips, product suggestions, or a treatment plan, we’ll ensure you leave feeling prepared and confident about your oral health.

The Benefits of a Cleaning & Hygiene Visit

Healthier Gums and Teeth

Regular cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup that even the most diligent brushing can’t tackle. By preventing gum disease, a cleaning & hygiene visit keeps your smile healthy and reduces risks to your overall health.

A Brighter Smile

Professional cleanings do more than protect your oral health. They polish away surface stains, leaving your teeth sparkling. You’ll walk out of our office feeling refreshed and ready to show off your brighter, more confident smile.

Early Detection of Potential Issues

During your visit, Dr. Huggett thoroughly examines your teeth and gums to catch cavities, gum disease, or other concerns early. Early detection means easier, less invasive treatments, and better outcomes for your smile.
