ABOUT US

Whether you’re exploring full or partial dentures, considering implant-supported options, or need extractions before treatment, our team in Minneapolis is here to guide you every step of the way.

Practice

Dentures ASAP is a locally owned business, founded in 2007 by Dr David Domaas and is now owned by Dr Noah Berge. We offer a wide range of dental services including preventive, restorative and cosmetic dentistry. Our long-standing commitment to quality dental care has made us a trusted choice for families in the area.

Provider

Dr Noah Berge is a Minnesota native. He completed his undergrad education at Arizona State University with a degree in Biochemistry and graduated from University of Minnesota School of Dentistry in 2011.

He has been practicing for the last 15 years at Community Dental Care, the largest nonprofit Medicaid dental provider in the state. Dr Berge’s focus is oral surgery with extensive experience and expertise in extractions, implants, and dentures.

Patients

We welcome new patients to our practice and look forward to providing you with the best possible care. Our team will work with you to ensure that your first visit is comfortable and stress-free.

Every patient is unique, and at Dentures ASAP, we tailor our services to meet your individual needs. Our personalized approach ensures that you receive the care that is best suited for you.
