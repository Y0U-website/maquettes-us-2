What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that’s placed in the bone socket of the missing tooth. During the healing process, the jawbone will grow around it, which will properly secure it. Once healed, the dental implant will provide a strong foundation to support a crown.

Who is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

While dental implants can replace missing or broken teeth, candidates must have a strong and healthy saw bone in order to support the tooth replacement. They can be a more permanent solution for those with dentures, partial dentures, and dental bridges.

What Are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

While there’s a range of dentistry solutions to choose from, dental implants are one of the most natural-looking options. From shape to color, our team can customize the dental implant to blend in seamlessly with the surrounding teeth.

While the process may seem a bit overwhelming, the results are worth it. The titanium post that’s used creates a bio-compatible dental implant, meaning it’s not only nontoxic but it’s also less likely to be rejected by the body.

After just one year after losing a tooth, the jawbone in that area loses about 25 % of its volume. While other solutions such as dentures may improve functionality, they can also accelerate bone loss. Dental implants are one of the most effective ways to prevent any further bone loss.
