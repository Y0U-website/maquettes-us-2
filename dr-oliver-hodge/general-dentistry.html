Fillings

Fillings are offered at New Beginnings Dental Center as a way to treat your teeth and restore them following tooth decay and cavities. Fillings are also effective at preventing tooth decay and cavities in the future. Your new filling slides in an open space that could be filled with bacteria otherwise. It’s typically your back teeth that will need fillings. Fillings are regarded as an affordable way to take care of teeth that have been experiencing problems. It also increases your comfort level with chewing on hard foods and candies.
