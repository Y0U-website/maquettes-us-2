How We Give Back

Magic of Science

We support Magic of Science, a nonprofit that strives to inspire in-need children with a love for science through fun science experiments.

Toy Drives for Local Hospitals

We regularly hold toy drives for local hospitals in our area so that children suffering from health challenges can experience more fun and relaxation.

Mouth Guards for High School Sports Teams

We’re not just here to help fix dental issues! We’re also here to prevent them, and that mission extends to protecting the smiles of our local sports teams. To do this, we donate mouthguards to the high school sports teams in Waxhaw.

Our Community Outreach Partnerships

Our philosophy of care goes beyond the office walls. We believe that it’s our responsibility to care for our patients, both in and out of the operating chair, and one of the best ways to do this is through our community outreach programs. Whether we’re supporting Smiles for Survivors, toy drives for local hospitals, Magic of Science, or donating mouth guards to our local sports teams, we’re passionate about helping our community smile.
