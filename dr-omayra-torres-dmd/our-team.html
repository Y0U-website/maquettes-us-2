Our Dentist

Dr. Torres

Dr. Omayra Torres has operated private practices since 2002. She has a B.A. in General Sciences (Magna Cum Laude) and earned a Doctorate Degree in Dental Medicine (DMD) in 2001 from the University of Puerto Rico. She then completed a fellowship in General Practice Residency in 2002. She is a member of both the American Dental Association and the Florida Dental Association.

In 2011, Dr. Torres opened I Beliv Dental Studio with the focus of providing the best and most honest dental care in Central Florida. She values building trusting relationships with her patients. Dr. Torres uses her fluency in English and Spanish to ensure there are no barriers to communication between her and her patients.
