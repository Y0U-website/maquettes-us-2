The practice

He continued his specialty training in pediatric dentistry for an additional two years at New York University and the world-renowned Bellevue Hospital where he was trained in growth and development, orthodontics, hospital dentistry, sedation and general anesthesia. Dr. Paddy also trained at the RFK Center for the treatment of children with developmental disabilities and the Hassenfeld Center of…

After moving to Atlanta, Georgia, he became the lead pediatric dentist and partner of a multi-specialty, multi-location group practice. While he loved his time in Atlanta, after four years, Dr. Paddy Smithwick decided it was time to move to his dream city of Denver, Colorado, where he could fulfill his love of the outdoors and extreme skiing – don’t worry, he wears a mouthguard.

Dr. Paddy has been in private practice in Colorado since 2012. He is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), the Colorado Dental Association (CDA), the Metro Denver Dental Society (MDDS), the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD) and the Colorado Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (CAPD).

Dr. Paddy has helped the underserved in Denver, as well as in New York City, Baltimore and Atlanta. On the global front, he led outreach programs in Central America (Honduras) and is looking at opportunities to start a pediatric dental office in Kenya, Africa. Dr. Paddy donates his time to both Give Kids a Smile and KIND (Kids in Need of Dentistry).

When not engaged in tooth-related pursuits, Dr. Paddy is vigorously involved in the Denver and Colorado lifestyle. As a three-sport athlete, Dr. Paddy grew up playing soccer, ice hockey and lacrosse, but skiing is his passion. In the summer, Dr. Paddy loves to take advantage of Colorado’s sunshine by camping, fly-fishing, white-water rafting and golfing.

Dr. Paddy enjoys making memories and feels blessed to love his job. It is his honor and privilege to be part of the life of each one of his patients. Dr. Paddy looks forward to getting to know you and your children.
