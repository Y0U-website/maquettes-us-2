Dr. Pamela O. Edwards was born to be a dentist and loves her work. She has practiced dentistry for over 35 years. Her father was a dentist, practicing for 60 years, her uncle was a dentist, her brother is a dentist who has been practicing for 50 years, and her daughter is a dentist.

Dentistry truly runs in her family. After graduating from the University of North Carolina with a Bachelor of Science degree, she moved to Birmingham AL and graduated from the University of Alabama School of Dentistry in 1988.

Spending her time with family and friends is what she enjoys most of all. But don’t get it wrong, she also enjoys all spectator sports, loves to swim, exercise, travel, garden, and playing her piano.
