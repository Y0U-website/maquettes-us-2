The practice

Dr. Singh is a world-recognized dental and oral surgeon, published author, academic researcher, inventor, and lecturer. He has spent twenty-five years researching and developing technologies in oral surgery, implant dentistry, TMJ and orofacial pain, and dental sleep medicine.

As founder, CEO, and chief scientific officer of APP-NEA, a healthcare biotechnology firm, he applies deep learning and augmented intelligence to develop predictive algorithms for the screening, diagnosis, and treatment of obstructive sleep apnea.

He has been awarded several patents and invented the minimally invasive guided implant and harvesting system — used worldwide for dental implant surgery, autogenous tissue harvesting, and regenerative medicine.

The standing reference on dental and oral implantology and surgery for postgraduate students and clinicians.
