Translating research into clinical practice.

A significant focus of my work has been transforming published evidence into practical systems that clinicians can use every day. This philosophy ultimately led to the development of Appnea™, an AI-enabled clinical platform designed to standardize and scale evidence-based diagnosis and management of dental sleep medicine and temporomandibular disorders.

Rather than functioning as another electronic health record, Appnea serves as a clinical intelligence platform that integrates diagnostic reasoning, documentation, treatment planning, patient education, outcomes measurement, and reimbursement workflows into a single ecosystem.

The goal is simple: improve patient outcomes while reducing clinician burden.

Building the future of dental sleep medicine.

As healthcare shifts toward integrated, value-based care, dentistry will play an increasingly important role in identifying systemic disease. My work focuses on helping organizations build comprehensive dental sleep medicine and orofacial pain programs.

Bridging clinicians, engineers, and enterprise.

Healthcare is entering an era where artificial intelligence will not replace clinicians — but will fundamentally change how clinicians deliver care.

The opportunity is not merely to digitize healthcare, but to redesign it around intelligence, interoperability, and measurable patient outcomes.

My mission is to help build that future through rigorous science, thoughtful innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, and technologies that empower providers while improving the lives of patients.
