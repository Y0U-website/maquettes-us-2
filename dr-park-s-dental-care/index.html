The practice

When you’re in pain, waiting isn’t an option. We provide prompt exams, digital X-rays, and same-day treatment options to relieve urgent dental issues quickly. Call us anytime or walk in—we’ll guide you on the fastest next step.

We reserve time for urgent cases so you can often receive evaluation and treatment on the same day. Same-day availability depends on the condition and clinical needs, but we’ll always aim for the quickest relief possible.

Dental emergencies don’t follow business hours. We offer care during evenings and weekends to help you get relief when most offices are closed. Call 24/7 to confirm the quickest option for your situation.

We’re open seven days a week to make it easier to get help without delaying care. Check our current hours on the website or Google Maps, or call and we’ll confirm availability.

No appointment? No problem. Walk-ins are welcome for urgent dental needs. For the fastest service, call ahead so we can prepare and reduce your wait time.
