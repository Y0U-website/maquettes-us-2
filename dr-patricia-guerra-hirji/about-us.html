Dr. Guerra-Hirji obtained her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Baylor College of Dentistry in Dallas. Her excellence in academics and clinical skills was recognized with The General Dentistry Program Award and placed her on the Dean’s list of such a prestigious Dental School. She is also a Cum Laude Science Graduate of U.T. Dallas in Richardson.

Dr. Guerra- Hirji is an outstanding professional and caring dentist with both a gentle touch, and the knowledge of the newest dental technologies.

When she is not healing or creating beautiful smiles, she is spending time with family, friends, and her golden retriever.

Dr. Guerra-Hirji’s philosophy is centered on prevention, treating her patients as if they were part of her own family.
