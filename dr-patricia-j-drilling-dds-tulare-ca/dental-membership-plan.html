Membership Plan Details

Dr. Drilling’s Dental Membership Plan is not insurance, but a direct savings plan to help patients access the care they need. It is standalone coverage, cannot be combined with insurance, and applies only to treatments provided in-office.

Plan Requirements

  • No refunds if benefits are not used within the plan year.
  • The plan is subject to annual changes.
  • Payment is due at enrollment to receive the discounted rate.

Terms & Limitations

  • Rates may change annually.
  • The plan begins on the payment date and ends one year later.
  • The plan is non-refundable and not transferable.
  • The plan cannot be billed to insurance or paid with CareCredit or Cherry.
  • A 3 % processing fee applies to credit card payments.
  • Products are excluded.
  • Unused services do not roll over.
  • The plan is only valid at the office of Patricia J. Drilling, DDS.
