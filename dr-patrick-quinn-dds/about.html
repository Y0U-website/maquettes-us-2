About Patrick Quinn, DDS

0 Meet Patrick Quinn, DDS Dr. Patrick Quinn is a Helena, Montana, native and has been practicing in Helena for over 29 years, seeing multiple generations of families. Following his graduation from Carroll College with a degree in biology, Dr. Quinn attended the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry. He received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree in August 1994, and returned to Helena and took over the long-time dental practice of his father, Dr. James Quinn.

In his spare time, Dr. Quinn enjoys spending time in the outdoors, taking in all that Montana and the Helena area has to offer. Dr. Quinn and his wife, Kim, are the proud parents of three sons and enjoy following and being active in their activities.

Dr. Quinn has been generous in supporting his local community. He has volunteered his time and skills in organizations and events such as Special Olympics Special Smiles, Give Kids a Smile, Donated Dental Services, and school screening programs. He has been a long-time member of the American Dental Association, Montana Dental Association and the local 5th District Dental Society. He recently completed a humanitarian trip with a dental charity called Smiles for Life in the Dominican Republic.
