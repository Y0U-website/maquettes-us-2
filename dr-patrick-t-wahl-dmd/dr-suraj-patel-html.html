Meet Wilmington Dentist

Suraj Patel, DDS

Dr. Suraj Patel was born and raised in Delaware. He attended the University of Delaware majoring in Biological Sciences and went on to attend the highly prestigious New York College of Dentistry.

He has completed a year of general practice residency at Penn State Health before returning home to the state of Delaware. He looks forward to creating long lasting relationships with patients.

He prides himself on attention to detail and is known for his uplifting sense of humor.

He enjoys all aspects of general dentistry including crowns, root canals, extractions, dentures, and implants.

Outside of work, Dr. Patel enjoys traveling, sports, and watching movies.

Education: B.A. in Biological Sciences, University of Delaware; D.D.S, New York University College of Dentistry; General Practice Residency, St. Joseph’s Medical Center Penn State Health Professional Affiliations: American Dental Association, Delaware State Dental Society
