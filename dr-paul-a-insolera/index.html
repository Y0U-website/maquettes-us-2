The practice

TMJ disorder often hides behind symptoms that don’t immediately feel like a jaw problem. Many patients visiting our TMJ clinic first come in for headaches, ear pain, or sleep issues — and only later learn the jaw joint is the root cause.

If these TMJ symptoms sound familiar, your jaw joints and bite alignment may be causing stress and inflammation throughout the head and neck.

The good news: TMJ pain is highly treatable without surgery when the underlying jaw position and joint function are corrected — not just masked. When TMJ disorder is untreated, the pain often spreads — from the jaw to the temples, ears, neck, and even into posture and sleep patterns. Many people spend years “managing” the discomfort with pain relievers, mouthguards, or chiropractic visits without lasting relief .
