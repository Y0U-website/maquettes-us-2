Meet Our Doctors:

Paul F. DiMattia, D.D.S.

Paul F. DiMattia, DDS was born in Boston, son of a Boston principal and a homemaker who instilled in him a love of education, family and community service.

After graduating from Boston Latin he received his bachelor and masters degrees at University of Mass, Boston. He went on to get his Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree from Temple Dental School in Philadelphia in 1974, before serving as a Captain in The Air Force.

After returning home, he practiced for 10 years in his East Boston practice. In 1987 he purchased his Lexington practice from retiring dentist, Dr. Robert Crooke on Muzzey Street. A few years later, he outgrew the Muzzey Street office and moved to the current location on Bedford Street.

Dr. DiMattia and his wife, Dianne, reside in Lexington, where they raised their two children and are active in the community.

Lisa E. Elder, D.D.S.

Dr. Lisa Elder graduated from the University of Maryland Dental School in 1993 where she completed a Prosthodontics Clerkship and was a member of Gamma Pi Delta Honorary Society. She has a Bachelor of Science in Biology from Muhlenberg College.

Dr. Elder enjoys practicing Cosmetic Dentistry and has attended LVI for Advanced Prosthodontics training. She is also trained in BOTOX and Dermal Fillers and Laser-Assisted Dentistry. She is a member of the American Dental Association and the Massachusetts Dental Society.

She lives in Lincoln, MA with her husband of 25 years, where they have raised 4 children. She enjoys cooking for her family and friends, skiing in New Hampshire and traveling.

Jack Giglio, D.M.D.

Dr. Giglio earned his DMD from Tufts Dental School in 1988 and resides in Bedford, MA.

He maintains extensive training in all areas of general dentistry including:

cosmetics, endodontics, periodontics and TMJ disorders.
