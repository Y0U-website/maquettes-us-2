ABOUT US

The Jacobs Smile Center of Escanaba, Michigan has been at the forefront of family and cosmetic dentistry for over 30 years. We know that a healthy mouth is necessary for having good whole-body health. Dr. Paul Jacobs and the team use best practices in cosmetic dentistry smile enhancement, the latest medical research, and state-of-the-art technology to offer patients in the Upper Peninsula the highest quality of modern dental care.

We are a team of dental professionals with a passion for enhancing our patients’ health and image creating “healthy beautiful smiles.” We’re dedicated to making your visits to our office comfortable and educational. We pride ourselves in placing the individual needs of our patients first, and we strive to provide state-of-the-art services to keep your mouth in the most healthy, attractive condition possible. Feel free to ask questions about treatment alternatives or new procedures in cosmetic dentistry.

Our team is committed to you and your dental health. We draw from years of experience in dentistry, and from a focus on continuing education and sound business practices. Our goal is not only to provide the best care options available today but to make these options affordable. Our business team will work with you to keep the financial aspects of your care comfortable as well.
