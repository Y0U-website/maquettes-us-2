Paul K. Chu, DDS

Dr. Chu received his Bachelors of Science in Environmental Toxicology from the University of California at Davis. He received his Doctor of Dental Science Degree from the University of the Pacific’s Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco, California. During dental school, he served as Class President

Narmatha Sinnarajah, DDS

Dr. Sinnarajah is a native of Toronto, Canada. She received her Bachelor of Science from the University of Toronto, where she graduated top of her class. She received her dental degree from Columbia University’s College of Dental Medicine. Dr. Sinnarajah’s peers in dental school recognized her exemplary leadership and elected her Class President
