Services

Dental Home

We recommend children visit a dentist no later than their first birthday. We also endorse the Age One dental visit so we can establish a Dental Home for your child.

Restorative Dentistry

We offer prevention & treatment of diseases of teeth and gums in order to restore to their best health. This includes the repair/replacement of damaged or defective teeth.

Behavior Management

Behavior management techniques, including sedation, are used to provide your child with dental care in a safe and calm manner.

After Treatment Care

Caring for your child after the use of localized anesthesia is important to allow for proper healing.

Preventive Dentistry

The goal is to help prevent the onset of oral disease through procedures and protective devices to ensure healthy gum and tooth development in your child.

Cosmetic Dentistry

We can enhance the aesthetic quality of your child’s teeth with in-office procedures that also lead to a healthier smile as they grow and develop.

Special Needs Care

At Rye Pediatric Dentistry, our doctors are trained to manage and treat children with special needs in care and treatment.
