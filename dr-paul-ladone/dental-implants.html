Dental Implants Overview

What are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

Dental implants are one of the most advanced solutions that can replace missing teeth.

  • They are surgically placed in the jawbone and fuse with it, providing stable support for artificial replacement teeth. This is a significant advantage compared to regular bridgework and dentures , as they won’t slip when speaking or eating, nor will they make noise or cause any damage to your jawbone.
  • Since titanium does not decay as natural tooth material does, dental implants have shown to be a durable and reliable alternative to other tooth replacement options.

What are Dental Implants Made of?

Dental implants in Naperville include two separate elements: an implant body and a dental crown which is fitted to the implant post.

How is Dental Implant Surgery Performed?

Dental implant surgery is a multi-step process, depending on the type of implant and the condition of a patient’s jawbone. Implants are preferred for their ability to provide solid support for new teeth, but this feature necessitates sufficient time for healing.

After an implant is placed it must be given proper time to heal and integrate with the bone, adding stability and durability to the implanted tooth structure. This healing period can range from a few months to over a year depending on the individual person, ensuring they have strong and reliable new teeth.
