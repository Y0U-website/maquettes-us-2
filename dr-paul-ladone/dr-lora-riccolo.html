Dr. Lora Riccolo

Dr. Lora Riccolo has been practicing at Ladone Family Dental since 2019.

Education and Background Originally from Niles, IL. Dr. Lora attended the University of Iowa and received her Bachelor’s Degree in Nutrition and Dietetics in 1987. She continued her education at Loyola University School of Dentistry and was awarded her Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 1992. Dr. Lora practiced in Carol Stream with one of her classmates for ten years.

Now, joining Ladone Family Dental, Dr. Lora focuses on using Botox as therapy for temporomandibular joint disorders, migraine pain, and facial aesthetics.

Advanced Treatments and Care: Dr. Lora Riccolo As a lifelong learner, she strives to use the latest research to give the best quality care to her patients. Dr. Lora is a member of the American Dental Association, the Illinois State Dental Society, the American Academy of Facial Esthetics and the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine. When not working, Dr. Lora enjoys traveling, reading, walking her dog and spending time with her family at their farm.
