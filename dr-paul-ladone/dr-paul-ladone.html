Dr. Paul Ladone

Dr. Paul Ladone is an accomplished dentist who has outstanding credentials. Beyond his skills and talent as a practitioner, he brings compassion and empathy to every treatment.

A Family-Based Dental Practice

Dr. Paul is a life-long resident of the western suburbs of Chicago. Growing up in Glen Ellyn, he attended St. Francis High School in Wheaton, and then on to his undergraduate studies and to play golf at the University of Iowa, Iowa City. He followed in his father’s footsteps and attended Loyola University of Chicago, School of Dentistry. In 1992, Dr. Ladone obtained his Doctoral of Dental Surgery, Magna Cum Laude and ranked second in his dental class. During his education, he completed an honors program in Endodontics and became an endodontic faculty member upon graduation.

Upon completion of his dental training, Dr. Paul joined his father, Dr. Joseph Ladone, in a family practice established by his father in Westchester in 1966.

In 1999, Dr. Paul moved the practice to its current location in Lisle, IL to be closer to his family and friends.

Dr. Paul continues to expand his knowledge through numerous continuing education sessions and is dedicated to remaining up to date on the latest techniques, dental materials and technologies to provide the highest level of quality care to his patients. He remains steadfast in patient-centric care and will not compromise in any facet of treatment.

Education and Background: The Journey to Excellence Dr. Paul Ladone completed his undergraduate requirements at the University of Iowa.
