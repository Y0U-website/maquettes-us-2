Am I a Candidate for Implants?

Let’s Find Out Together

Ideal candidates for dental implants are in good general health and have enough jawbone to support the implant. Even if you’ve experienced bone loss, we may be able to help with grafting or alternative techniques. We’ll evaluate your health history, take detailed images, and design a plan that fits your goals and lifestyle.

The CDC reports that nearly 1 in 5 adults over 65 have lost all their teeth, underscoring the importance of permanent replacement solutions like implants.
