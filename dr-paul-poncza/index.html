The practice

He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry and holds a Bachelor of Science from the University of Michigan . With more than two decades in dentistry, he has developed an extensive focus in dental sleep medicine.

His dental sleep medicine work includes helping patients manage snoring and diagnosed obstructive sleep apnea using customized oral appliance therapy, often in coordination with medical providers. He also lectures on the subject for dental and medical professionals.

Dr. Poncza sees patients at Smile On Dental Salon’s Deerfield office and Lakeview office . His approach emphasizes attentive communication, individualized treatment, and helping patients feel comfortable throughout their care.

Without proper brushing, flossing, and routine dental care, this can lead to permanent tooth damage, pain, and even infection.
