What to Expect at Smile On

Step-by-Step Care for Your Best Results

Your Invisalign journey begins with a consultation at our Lakeview or Deerfield office. We’ll evaluate your teeth, take digital impressions, and create a personalized treatment plan using 3D imaging software.

You’ll receive a series of aligners and instructions for wear. Most patients complete treatment in 6–18 months, depending on their case. We’ll monitor your progress and make adjustments as needed along the way.
