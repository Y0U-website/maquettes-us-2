Cedar Rapids area families trust locally owned Dental Health Partners to provide reassuring service and excellent care. We welcome new patients and strive to keep your smile bright!

Our Cedar Rapids office is conveniently located near I-380 and is appointed with modern technology and amenities. We welcome patients from nearby neighborhoods as well as other Eastern Iowa towns.

While you’re here, help yourself to our wifi and coffee, tea, or water. During your appointment, watch cable or listen to streaming music.

For families, our enclosed playroom offers a quiet space with toys and videos to keep children at ease. Parents can even watch children in the playroom during appointments.
