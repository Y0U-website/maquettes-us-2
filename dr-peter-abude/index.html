The practice

Our goal is for you to leave our office with a memorable and enjoyable dental experience, which is why our welcoming and compassionate staff will do everything they can to make you feel right at home.

My joy comes from seeing the transformation in a patient’s smile after you have met and exceeded their expectations.

A firm believer in the power of a smile, Dr. Abude loves to smile and loves to make people smile. Dr. Abude listens to and encourages his patients to be active participants in both their oral and general health care. He believes in teaming up with his patients to align their oral care needs with their treatment goals.

Dr. Abude received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery Degree from the prestigious New York University College of Dentistry. He is a member of the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry. Dr. Abude also has a Master of Business Administration from the Johns Hopkins University Carey Business School.

Away from the office, Dr. Abude likes traveling, hiking, cycling, and is an avid auto enthusiast.

Located in Highland, CA, New Smiles Dental Studio truly believes everyone deserves a smile that they can feel good about. We perform a wide range of dental services at our office so you can feel confident knowing you are in good hands, and will not require multiple appointments from different dental specialists.
