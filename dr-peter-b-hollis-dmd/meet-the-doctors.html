Dr. Stephen E. Recupero

His Education

The fourth out of eight children of Dr. Thomas and Claudia Recupero, Dr. Steve received his BS in Biology from Boston College in 1991. He graduated Cum Laude, won the Prosthodontics award, and earned his DDS degree from the Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry in 1995. In 1997, Dr. Steve complete a 2 year residency in prosthetic dentistry at VCU.

His Career

Dr. Steve completed his two-year Prosthodontics residency at the Medical College of Virginia while working at Dr. Oley, Shaia & Associates. He and his wife joined Park Dental Group in 1997 after finishing his residency. Using his unique skill set, Dr. Steve, in the past has worked as both a part-time lab/clinical instructor at the VCU School of Dentistry as well as Harvard University School of Dentistry.

His Affiliations

His Married Life

Dr. Bain and Dr. Steve married in 1995. They have three children:

  • Carolyn, a graduate of Villanova University and a fourth year dental student at VCU School of Dentistry
  • Stephen Jr., a graduate of Johns Hopkins University
  • Christopher, a third year at the University of Virginia studying aerospace engineering

Life Outside the Clinic

In college, Dr. Steve played as a walk-on kicker on the football team. That is why he is a huge supporter of Boston college sports and enjoys all college and pro sports in the area. When he’s not in the clinic, he loves running, trying to improve his golf game, going to the gym after work with Dr. Bain and spending time with his family.

Dr. Thomas A. Recupero Retired

His Education

Dr. Tom graduated from Thayer Academy and received a BS degree from Boston College. He earned his DDS degree from Georgetown School of Dentistry in 1962.

His Career

After finishing his studies, Dr. Tom founded Park Dental Group. He also worked as a part-time clinical instructor at the Harvard University School of Dentistry as he started our dental practice. A significant member of the Stoughton community, Dr. Tom served on the Stoughton School Board Committee for 12 years and as a selectman for 4 years.

His Affiliations

  • American Dental Association
  • Massachusetts Dental Society
  • Lifetime Member of the South Shore District Dental Society.
  • Immaculate Conception Church in Stoughton, Massachusetts
  • Lions Club

His Married Life

Dr. Tom is married to Claudia Recupero, with whom he has 8 children and 12 grandchildren. One of his children is a fellow Park Dental doctor, Steve. Dr. Tom is a patriarch figure to his big family and a very good role model for his colleagues.

Life Outside the Clinic

When he’s not working on people’s smiles, Dr. Tom enjoys yoga, reading, walking Castle Island, and spending time with his large family.
