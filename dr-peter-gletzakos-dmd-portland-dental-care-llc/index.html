The practice

The American Dental Association (ADA) recommends two routine follow up prophylaxis visits per year. Necessary x-rays, intra-oral and extra-oral examinations are done to rule out any pathology.

Both amalgam (silver) and composite (tooth colored) restorations are done depending on the needs and wants of our patients.

Implants, a leading replacement for missing teeth are utilized to save adjacent teeth and stabilize occlusion.

Partial and complete dentures are made to replace missing teeth.

All cosmetic procedures are performed to provide the best possible smiles to our patients.

Different types of crown restorations (porcelain to gold, zirconia, all porcelain, all gold) are done to restore broken teeth to normal form and function.
