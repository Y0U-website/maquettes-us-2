What You Can Do:

Our goal is to ensure the health and safety of our patients, community and staff with as little disruption as possible. If you have any concerns regarding making an appointment, or about an upcoming appointment, we encourage you to contact us.

  • Wash your hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds
  • Wear a mask to prevent the spread of germs
  • If you are feeling ill prior to your appointment, please call the office to reschedule
  • We know there is a strong connection between oral health and overall health, so it’s especially important you do all you can to stay healthy by brushing and flossing. Being active in maintaining your dental health can supplement your immune system.
