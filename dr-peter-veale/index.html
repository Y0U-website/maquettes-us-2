Get directions Welcome to Veale dentistry Dental Clinic Our Services Patient Resources Meet Our Team We offer a range of dental services including teeth cleaning, fillings, extractions, and Implants.

We also offer cosmetic dentistry services such as teeth whitening and veneers.

Our team of highly skilled and experienced dentists and dental hygienists are dedicated to providing you with the best possible care. We are committed to helping you achieve and maintain optimal dental health.

We understand that visiting the dentist can be stressful, which is why we provide a range of resources to help you prepare for your appointment and feel more comfortable during your visit.

We accept most major insurance plans and offer a range of payment options to make dental care more affordable and accessible including CareCredit. Please note we are third party to your insurance company so it is your responsibility to know your plan and coverages accordingly.
