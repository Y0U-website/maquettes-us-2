Dental Implants FAQs

Dental implants are a tooth replacement option for missing teeth that use titanium screws to anchor the artificial teeth to the jawbone.

People choose dental implants in San Rafael because they look and function like real teeth. They are permanent restorations and improve the look of smiles.

Our implant dentist in San Rafael starts the process with an initial consultation. The dental implant procedure involves recording mouth impressions or 3D scans to get models for constructing the implants. Our dentist places short-term implants and, later on, permanent ones.

You can get dental implants if you can undergo standard surgical procedures. However, our dentist will first evaluate you for the dental implant procedure.

A dental implant procedure may take between three to eight months for people who do not require initial dental work, such as gum grafting.

Dental implants are wholly cemented in place. Therefore, food and other debris cannot get stuck underneath the implants. If you find that food is getting stuck in your implants, it could mean that they were not installed correctly.

Dental implant surgery is not painful since a patient is under anesthesia. A dentist near you that you visit for implant placement will administer a numbing agent that helps promote comfort.
