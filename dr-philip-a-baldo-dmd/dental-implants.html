Advantages of Dental Implants Look, feel, and function like natural teeth
  • Preserve jawbone and facial structure
  • Don’t affect adjacent healthy teeth
  • Provide superior chewing ability
  • Easy to care for with normal brushing and flossing
  • Can last a lifetime with proper maintenance
