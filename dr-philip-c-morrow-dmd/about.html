About Us

Our Practice

Carolina Smiles was founded in 1981 by Dr. Morrow , and we have been serving the Spartanburg area ever since. Dr. Davis joined our team in 2007, Dr. John Coleman joined in 2023, and Dr. Irene Sakellis joined our team in 2024. In the past 44 years, we have constantly been growing and learning the best ways to help our patients. As we learn the latest technological advancements , we are committed to explaining why we provide certain treatment so patients can make informed decisions about their dental work.

We value patient relationships, and we make it a priority to provide the compassionate care you deserve. Our team works together to create a relaxing, calming environment where patients feel at ease.
