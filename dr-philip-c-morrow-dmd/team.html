Meet Our Team

Carolina Smiles is a multi-doctor practice with a compassionate, knowledgeable team. We strive to provide a relaxing, calming environment where we can help you with all your dental needs. We treat our patients like family, and we work hard to maintain our patient relationships. Our team combines the latest innovative technology with a family atmosphere in order to bring you a healthy smile that lasts a lifetime!

Our entire team at Carolina Smiles is excited to meet you, and we cannot wait to become your Spartanburg dental family!
