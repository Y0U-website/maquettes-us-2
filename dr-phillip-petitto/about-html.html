Meet the Doctor and Staff

Meet Dr. Petitto

Dr. Phillip A. Petitto is a native of Louisiana. He attended Louisiana State University where he received a Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences in 2007 and received his dental degree from LSU School of Dentistry in 2012. He went on to complete an AEGD (Advanced Education in General Dentistry) residency at the University of Alabama Birmingham School of Dentistry in conjunction with the Veterans Affairs Hospital in Birmingham. He is certified in adult conscious sedation, Advanced Cardiac Life Support, and CPR.

Dianna • Office Manager

Dianna was born and Raised in LaPlace and has been a resident of Ponchatoula for over 20 years. She attended Eastern College of Health Vocations receiving certifications in CPR, Radiology, and Dental Assisting. She is also certified by the Louisiana State Board of Dentistry in expanded duty Dental Assisting.

Diana is a devoted wife and mother. She has four wonderful children and two fur babies. She loves spending time with her family. She also enjoys reading, going to church and the outdoors.

Terri • Dental Hygienist

Terri is a registered Dental Hygienist. She graduated from Loyola University in 1982, where she earned her Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene. She is certified in local anesthesia, and nitrous oxide administration. She has been working in this office for over forty years. Terri has been a lifelong resident of Albany, Louisiana. She enjoys spending time with her granddaughters. Terri loves her patients and considers them as her extended family.
