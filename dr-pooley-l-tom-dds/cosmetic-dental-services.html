Why Choose Us

Porcelain Veneers Porcelain veneers are among our most effective cosmetic solutions at River Valley Dental of Mankato. These thin, custom-made porcelain shells are carefully crafted to cover the front of teeth, instantly fixing discoloration, chips, gaps, or minor alignment issues. Our dental team designs each veneer to match your facial features, creating a natural look that beautifies your smile.

Smile Makeovers A smile makeover at River Valley Dental of Mankato combines several cosmetic treatments to fully enhance your smile. This custom approach may include veneers, whitening, bonding, and other services tailored to your needs. During your visit, our dentists will check your oral health, discuss your goals, and create a treatment plan that delivers balanced results while respecting your facial features and personal style.

Teeth Whitening Professional teeth whitening at River Valley Dental of Mankato delivers remarkable results that work better than store-bought products. We offer both in-office and take-home options using professional whitening agents that remove stains while protecting your enamel and reducing sensitivity. Our team carefully monitors the process to help you achieve your desired brightness while keeping your teeth and gums healthy.
