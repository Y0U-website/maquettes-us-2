Dr. Pravin Aturaliya

Doctor

Dr. Pravin Aturaliya (pronounced PraVeen A trail ya) has been practicing dentistry for 20 years and welcomes you to his Duluth practice. Raised in Duluth and a 1984 graduate of Marshall/Cathedral High School, he has a passion for living and raising his family in Duluth. Dr. Aturaliya’s expertise and experience include:

Education : DDS-1993 University of Minnesota Dental School MIS-1988 University of Minnesota (Engineering) BS-1988 University of Minnesota (Biology with Chemistry minor)

Experience : 1995 to present – Duluth Family Dental, Pravin Aturaliya DDS PA, Duluth, Minn. 2009 to present – Minnesota Maxillofacial and Oral Consultants, Plymouth, Minn. 1993-1995 – Lakeview Dental, Wayzata, Minn. Post Graduate Experience : Oral Surgery Summer Program – University of Minnesota Implant Surgery and Restoration Peter Dawson Complete Dentistry Management of the TMJ (Temporomandibular Joint Disorder) IV Sedation Course University of Minnesota Multiple Courses in Botox Therapy, Bone Grafting, Implant, Crown and Bridge

Certificates/Licenses : DDS – State of Minnesota IV Sedation Certificate – State of Minnesota ACLS, BLS
