What Does a Dental Implant Look Like?

Our high grade dental implants are made up of 3 parts.

*Some materials may differ depending on your health condition and circumstances

  • A surgically-implanted titanium post that acts in place of your natural tooth root
  • An abutment that connects the new tooth (aka dental crown) to the titanium root
  • And your new custom-designed tooth (or set of teeth) that matches your smile

Dental implants in Shoreline, WA can replace a single missing tooth or several missing teeth with unique benefits.

Unlike dentures, dental implants can last a lifetime with proper hygiene, are stain-resistant, and stronger than natural teeth.

Your dental implants become part of your natural smile since they fuse with the jaw bone. Nobody will notice you have a dental implant!

Compared to dentures, dental implants provide the greatest advantages when it comes to eating, speaking, and having a holistically healthy smile.
