Meet the Team Cherry Valley…

Priscilla Tall, DDS, MA

Dentist

Dr. Tall understands that most people experience some level of anxiety when visiting the dentist. She knows how this can prevent us from getting the proper dental care we need, so she dedicated her Master’s research on how to naturally reduce anxiety in dental patients. Cherry Valley Family Dental has turned the dental experience from a fearful one to a relaxing experience for thousands of people, and we can’t wait to do the same for you.

Dr. Tall graduated with a Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Loma Linda University after earning her bachelor’s degree in psychology and master’s degree in social ecology at UC Irvine where her research focused on reducing anxiety in dental patients.

When not at the office, Dr. Tall enjoys traveling with her family and enjoying the great outdoors with her husband and kids. Dr. Tall strives to keep each patient comfortable by using a gentle touch and enjoys educating her patients on oral health. She can’t wait to get to know you at your next visit!

Lynda vega, DDS

Dentist

Dr. Vega attended Andrews University in Michigan and received her bachelor’s degree in Fine Arts. She has always had a passion for art and wanted to use it to help others, which is why she went on to pursue a career in dentistry at Loma Linda University. She learned that dentistry merged her creative interests and goal to serve others in a unique way by allowing her to help patients improve their oral health and attain aesthetically pleasing results so patients can look and feel their best.

When not at the office, she enjoys spending time with her husband—going on hikes, relaxing at the beach, drawing, and volunteering at free clinics local and abroad. She’s excited to be working with such a great team and looks forward to meeting all our patients. Providing quality, gentle care while making sure you are kept comfortable are her top priorities.

Elisa shin, DDS

Dentist

Dr. Shin has a heart for delivering excellent dental care in a compassionate way. As a mother of three young children, she understands how important it is to find a caring, honest healthcare provider, and she seeks to build trust with her patients by delivering the kind of exceptional care that she would want for her own family.

A graduate of Loma Linda University School of Dentistry, Dr. Shin graduated with distinction in the top five of her class. She has taken advanced courses in CEREC same-day crowns, Invisalign, and endodontics so that she can produce superior outcomes while keeping patients comfortable and relaxed.

Outside of the office, Dr. Shin’s passions revolve around her three children whom she is homeschooling. She loves to travel with her family and to invest time in experiences, such as medical mission trips that will broaden her children’s minds and build their characters.
