Why Early Dental Visits Matter?

We recommend that children visit the dentist by their first birthday or when their first tooth comes in. These early visits help us check for any problems and give parents helpful tips on brushing, nutrition, and teething. Starting dental care early helps children build healthy habits and lowers the chance of dental problems in the future.
