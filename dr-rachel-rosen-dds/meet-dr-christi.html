Meet Dr. Christi Walsh

Dr. Christi Walsh, DDS

Pediatric Dentist and Breathe Ambassador

Dr. Christi is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. Dr. Christi strives to make every child’s visit an enjoyable experience to help them and their parents understand the essentials for building good oral health. She establishes patient relationships on trust and open communication combined with the best-individualized care. Her main goal as a pediatric dentist is to have a shared vision with caregivers regarding treatment options for their child while maintaining a positive atmosphere the child enjoys!

A native of Ohio, she completed her undergraduate degree at Bowling Green State University. She completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery at Ohio State University. Upon graduation, Dr. Christi moved to NYC for a general practice residency and successfully practiced as a general dentist for four years. At that time, she decided to pursue her true passion for children’s dentistry. Dr. Christi completed her Pediatric Dental Residency at St. Barnabas Hospital in the Bronx, NY. While there, she served as Chief Resident.

While out of the office, you can find Dr. Christi spending time with her husband, little one, and two dogs! She also loves the outdoors, reading, and traveling.
