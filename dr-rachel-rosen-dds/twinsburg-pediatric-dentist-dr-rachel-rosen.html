A Mom First A Pediatric Dentist Always

As the mother of two young boys, Nash and Colby, I’ve experienced firsthand how tongue, lip, and cheek ties can affect infant feeding. Both of my boys struggled with ties as infants, and I found myself on both sides of the treatment chair.

That dual perspective gave me so many insights beyond my clinical training. I understand the panic parents feel when something feels off with their baby’s feeding. I understand the relief when a functional evaluation finally gives answers. And I understand how much it takes to get the best outcome, from the initial evaluation through stretches at home and beyond.

Now that my boys are old enough to receive routine dental cleanings and exams, I once again find myself on both sides of the chair. I feel my own nerves in my stomach when my kid won’t sit still or has a meltdown. In those moments I have to remind myself of what I tell every parent: this is normal, and we’ll work through it.

That’s the thing about being a mom and a pediatric dentist at the same time. I can’t unsee the parent side. I know when textbooks say a child should give up the pacifier, but I also know that some days the pacifier is the only thing giving your household some much-needed sleep. That’s why I won’t push a by-the-book plan that doesn’t fit your child on your hardest day. I’m here to work with you, not lecture you. Together we’ll help your child enjoy dental visits and build a lifetime of healthy breathing and smiles.

Dr. Rachel Would Love to Meet Your Family

Every family who walks through our door gets our same commitment to seeing and welcoming the whole child. Bring your questions, your concerns, your anxious or confident child, and Dr. Rachel and her team will handle the rest.

They help you connect the dots between how your child breathes, sleeps, grows, and smiles. They will meet your child where they are during dental cleanings and exams, empowering them to be active participants in their own health care while sparking a few giggles along the way.

Dr. Rachel Rosen and team are accepting new pediatric dental patients for regularly scheduled cleanings and exams, second opinions, and functional tongue and lip tie evaluations and releases. Dr. Rachel would love to be a trusted partner in your child’s health.
