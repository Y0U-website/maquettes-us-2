Meet Our Staff!

Dr. Phillips became the proud owner of Today’s Dental Care in January of 2021. She grew up on a farm in southern Illinois about 40 minutes southeast of Collinsville. After she attended Southern Illinois University Edwardsville, she received her dental training from Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine in Alton and graduated in 2009. She began her career working in public health and then in a private practice near Bethalto. Outside the office, Dr. Phillips spends her time with her husband, daughter, and son. She enjoys cooking, gardening, and traveling.

Kelly J started her dental career in high school. She was a part of Collinsville s Cooperative Education program and once she graduated, it became her full-time career. She has continued to be Today s Dental Care s well respected dental assistant since 1997. Kelly, a resident of Caseyville, IL, lives happily with her husband, Ron. They love to take themselves on trips and really any opportunity they have to travel. But most importantly, they enjoy spending time with their children and grandson as much as they can.

Laura is a graduate from Missouri State University with a Bachelor of Science in Criminology and Ozarks Technical Community College with an Associate of Applied Science in Dental Hygiene. She is certified in local anesthesia and uses other pain reducing options to help keep you comfortable during cleanings. Laura enjoys taking continuing education classes so she can keep her patients updated with the most current dental products and recommendations. In her free time she enjoys spending time with her husband and two daughters, cheering for the Razorbacks (Woo Pig Sooie!) and playing pickleball.

Jessica has been a resident of Collinsville since the day she was born. She s a proud graduate of Collinsville High School. Jessica is currently a student at South Western Illinois College and plans to further her education in the medical field. She is a mom of three boys, and an aspiring dental hygienist. She is a huge animal lover who formerly worked at a veterinary hospital. She enjoys movies, music, organizing, and decorating. Most of all, she loves to get outdoors and make memories with her children at zoos, parks, pools, and farms.
