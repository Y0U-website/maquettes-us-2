Our Services

Preventative: Check-ups & Cleaning According to the American Dental Association, patients of all ages should have at least 2 check-ups and cleanings each year in order to keep their teeth and gums healthy. Yearly x-rays and complete periodontal exams are also recommended. Our hygienists are specialist in this area and will always recommend the best suggestions for your oral health.

Restorative: Fillings, Crowns & Bridges A cavity is a hole in your tooth caused by decay. If left untreated, cavities can lead to toothaches, infections, and even tooth loss. A filling involves removing all decay from the tooth and restoring it with a strong, color-matched material so you can still properly chew and smile. For larger issues, such as broken/missing teeth, we can create dental crowns and bridges to mimic your natural teeth and restore confidence in your beautiful smile!

Clear Retainer Ortho dontics Dr. Phillips is trained in using Smilers, which is a clear aligner orthodontic treatment that will help align your teeth and bite which is beneficial to your overall oral health. Instead of going through the process of braces, we can create a transparent way to make your smile even more perfect! Ask us for more information!

Gum Disease Therapy: Scaling & Root Planing If your gums are sore or bleed when you brush, this can be the early signs of gum disease. Scaling and Root Planing, also called a deep cleaning , is recommended to stop the progression of periodontal disease to help prevent tooth loss. Our highly trained hygienists use specialized ultrasonic instruments to clean deep pockets to remove bacteria and tarter. This will allow the tissues and bone to heal.

Dentures & Partials Dentures and partials can help patients with missing teeth restore their smile. A full denture replaces all teeth on the top or bottom arch. A partial denture replaces missing teeth when some natural teeth can be retained. While eating and speaking with dentures and partials can be a big adjustment, over time and with modifications they can be comfortable. Dentures and partials can help improve patients’ confidence, appearance, speech, and function.
