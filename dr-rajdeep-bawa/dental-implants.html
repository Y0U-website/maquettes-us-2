Dental Implants In Manassas VA

Dental implants are designed to match the color and shine of your natural teeth, enhancing your smile’s aesthetics.

Missing teeth make speaking and eating feel awkward and uncomfortable. Implants are stable and secure, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods and speak clearly.

A missing tooth can cause your face to lose support, creating a sunken look. Dental implants preserve proper bone levels in the jaw, giving your face a more youthful appearance.

With excellent care and maintenance, your dental implants can last a lifetime! Treat them like your natural teeth.

  • Abutment: An abutment is the next piece of your implant that connects your new tooth to the post.
  • Crown: Your dental crown is what is placed on the abutment. It is custom crafted to blend perfectly with your natural smile.
