About Rana Muneeruddin DDS

0 About the Doctor Dr. Rana Muneeruddin was raised on Long Island where she currently resides. She is a proud alumna of Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine and has been happily serving the Long Island community for decades.

Dr. Muneeruddin’s warm and compassionate approach extends to patients of all ages. Whether it’s a child’s first dental visit or providing senior care, she understands the unique needs of each individual. Dr. Muneeruddin is committed to staying on top of the latest advancements in dental care to ensure her patients receive a top-notch experience. Her gentle demeanor and ability to create a comfortable environment make every visit to her practice a positive experience.
