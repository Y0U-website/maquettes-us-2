Our Story and Philosophy

Nestled in the heart of Reading, PA, Reinecker Dental Group stands as a beacon of top-tier dental care and personalized patient experience. Our practice, established over four decades ago, has grown on the foundations of superior dental services, cutting-edge technology, and a heartfelt mission to serve our community. Our philosophy is simple: deliver comprehensive dental care that caters to the unique needs of every patient.

Our Mission

Our mission extends beyond providing excellent dental care. We are dedicated to making dental health achievable for all, including those without insurance, through our comprehensive care plans and community-focused approach.

Our Legacy

Reinecker Dental Group, with a legacy spanning over four decades, has been a cornerstone of dental excellence in Reading, PA. Our journey is marked by a deep commitment to community health and continuous advancement in dental care.

Our Services

We offer a wide range of dental services including general dentistry, cosmetic enhancements, and specialized treatments for sleep apnea and dental anxiety, featuring options like general anesthesia for our most anxious patients.

Our Approach

Patient care is our top priority. We strive to create a comfortable and reassuring environment where each patient receives personalized care tailored to their specific needs.

Our Commitment to the Community

Beyond dental procedures, Reinecker Dental Group is deeply ingrained in the fabric of Reading, PA. We believe in empowering our patients with knowledge about oral health and actively contribute to community wellness through various outreach programs and initiatives.
