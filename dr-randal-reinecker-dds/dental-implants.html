The Dental Implant Process

Step 1: Consultation and Planning

Your journey begins with a personalized consultation to assess your oral health and determine if dental implants are the best option. We’ll discuss your goals, take x-rays, and design a custom treatment plan.

Step 2: Implant Placement

Using advanced technology, our dentists precisely place the implant post into the jawbone. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia for maximum comfort.

Step 3: Healing and Restoration

Once the implant integrates with the jawbone (a process called osseointegration), we attach a custom crown, bridge, or denture, completing your new smile.

For more details about implant restoration, explore our Restorative Dentistry services.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are an excellent option for patients who:

  • Have one or more missing teeth.
  • Have sufficient bone density in the jaw (or can undergo bone grafting).
  • Are in good overall health and committed to maintaining oral hygiene.
