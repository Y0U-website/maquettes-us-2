What Is All-on-6?

All-on-6 is a full-arch implant technique that uses six titanium posts placed directly in the jawbone to support a complete fixed prosthesis. The six-post design distributes chewing force more evenly across the arch than four-implant configurations do, reducing stress on each post and creating a broader, more stable foundation for the restoration. For patients with sufficient bone density, this added support translates to a prosthesis with excellent long-term structural integrity.

Research published through PubMed Central examining All-on-Six prosthetic rehabilitation describes the approach as a favorable treatment option for edentulous patients, with notably reduced cantilever stress compared to four-implant designs and meaningful gains in dental function, comfort, and aesthetics. Because the final prosthesis is fixed permanently to the implants, it remains in place at all times, functioning exactly as natural teeth would.

How All-on-6 Compares to Other Full-Arch Options

Knowing where All-on-6 fits relative to other full-arch solutions makes it easier to understand whether it is the right match for your specific case.

All-on-6 vs. All-on-4

All-on-4 uses four implants, two of which are angled toward the back of the jaw to take advantage of available bone. It is an effective, well-documented technique that works especially well for patients with bone loss, as the angled placement often eliminates the need for grafting before treatment can proceed. All-on-6 builds on the same principle by adding two more implants, spreading the load more evenly, and providing greater structural depth for patients whose bone density supports it.

The better choice between the two is never predetermined. It depends on your bone volume, anatomy, and prosthetic requirements, all of which our doctors carefully examine before recommending a course of action.

All-on-6 and the All-on-X Framework

All-on-X describes the broader approach of customizing implant count to each patient’s anatomy rather than defaulting to a fixed number. All-on-6 is one of the most commonly recommended configurations in this framework when the bone structure allows it. Dr. Binns and Dr. Aquino apply this anatomy-first philosophy to every full-arch case, using digital imaging and thorough pre-surgical planning to determine the right number and placement of implants before any procedure begins.

Who Is a Candidate for All-on-6?

Candidacy depends on a set of clinical factors our doctors evaluate in detail during your consultation.

Ideal Candidates

All-on-6 is built for patients who have lost all or most of their teeth in one or both arches and have adequate jawbone to support six posts. Patients who have retained more bone volume, whether naturally or through prior grafting, tend to be well-suited for this configuration. Good general health, controlled chronic conditions, and reliable oral hygiene habits all contribute to strong long-term outcomes.

Patients who have been living with conventional or implant-supported dentures and want to move to a fully fixed restoration often find All-on-6 to be a compelling path forward. A consultation will clarify whether this configuration fits your anatomy or whether a different approach would serve you better.

What the Process Looks Like

Treatment starts with a comprehensive evaluation that includes imaging and detailed planning to map every implant’s position before surgery begins. The implants are placed in a single procedure, and a provisional prosthesis is typically delivered the same day, so you leave with functional teeth.

After the healing period, during which the implants integrate with the surrounding bone, the final prosthesis is secured in place. For patients whose cases involve additional complexity, we offer full-mouth dental implants as part of a comprehensive range of full-arch restoration options.
