Benefits of Dental Implants

Many prosthodontists already consider dental implants the gold standard for replacing missing teeth, and for good reason. They restore full chewing capacity, preserve your natural facial contours by preventing bone loss, and never slip or require messy adhesives. According to the American Academy of Implant Dentistry , this solution has a documented long-term success rate exceeding 95 %, and with proper care, they can last 30 years or more.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Caring for them properly is key to making your investment last 30 years or longer. Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush, floss around the posts using unwaxed tape or implant-specific floss, and visit our Naples office every six months for professional cleanings. Avoid chewing on hard objects like ice or pen caps, and if you grind your teeth at night, wear a protective nightguard.
