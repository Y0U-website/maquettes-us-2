THE KENYA INITIATIVE FOR NEW DENTISTRY IS A MISSION TO BRING PREVENTIVE DENTISTRY TO THE REMOTE VILLAGES OF KENYA AND TANZANIA.

Dr. Randy Shoup is the director of K.I.N.D. He has pioneered preventive and biomimetic dental therapies. He lectures and teaches across the U.S., in Europe, Kenya and Asia.

Using his advanced techniques, Dr. Shoup can render a tooth 90 % decay resistant with no shot, no numbing, and no drilling. He has been perfecting these techniques over his 40 plus years career. Sharing this technology and these techniques with people who suffer greatly on a daily basis with extensive dental disease is the primary mission of K.I.N.D.
