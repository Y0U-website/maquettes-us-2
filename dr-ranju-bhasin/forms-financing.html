New Patient Forms

New to our office? Filling out these forms will help our team get to know you and the details of your oral health so we can focus on what matters most during your first visit — your smile! Download the forms, fill them out, and bring them with you to your first appointment. We’re excited to meet you and see you smile!

Accepted Insurances

To make care as convenient and affordable as possible, we are in-network with a wide range of PPO insurance providers. The list includes Aetna, Ameritas, Anthem, BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, Delta Dental, Metlife, and more. Don’t see your provider on the list? We accept most PPO insurances — give us a call to see if your plan is covered!

Third-Party Financing

For patients who would like to finance their procedure, we work with CareCredit, one of the largest and most reputable healthcare financing companies in the U.S. We do our very best to help patients bring the cost of care into their budget!
