Our Mission

Passionate about your oral health. Considerate of your lifestyle. Happy to serve you.

We’re dedicated to promoting wellbeing while serving up smiles in the community. At Schererville Family Dentistry, we believe in providing exceptional care in the most efficient manner possible.

The health of our patients is our top priority, but we know that balancing all of your obligations can be tricky at best. For this reason, we offer flexible office hours from 7 am to 8 pm, in order to accommodate hectic schedules and ensure oral care is accessible to even the busiest of patients.

With over 20 years of experience, our trustworthy doctors and professional staff are meticulously skilled in all forms of dentistry, unphased by “complex” problems, and proactive about long-term wellness. Our passion for creating a friendly atmosphere ensures that patients of all ages will feel at ease when reclining in one of our chairs.
