About

At the office of Dr. Raul G. Molina, Jr., DDS, PA, patients always come first. Our warm and welcoming office, with the combined expertise of Raul G. Molina Jr., DDS, and his son Raul D. Molina, DMD, offers residents of Miami, Florida, the highest quality implant and cosmetic dentistry services possible.

Our entire team at Dr. Raul G. Molina, Jr., DDS, PA, is committed to spending quality time with each patient, ensuring they don’t feel rushed during their visit and receive the best possible care. At our state-of-the-art practice, we offer the latest in dental technology and equipment, from digital X-rays and CBCT to intraoral cameras and 3D scanners, to ensure precision and create predictable outcomes. We also offer cutting-edge solutions such as laser dentistry and air abrasion, so you don’t have to deal with the needles, drilling, or numbness typical of a dental visit.

As an additional convenience to our valued patients, we also offer a wide menu of snacks and amenities to help all you feel relaxed and at ease during the entire visit.

We at Dr. Raul G. Molina, Jr., DDS, PA, believe that our job starts by educating our patients on the overall health benefits of caring for your oral health. We provide a wide variety of cosmetic dentistry services to help patients feel their best about their smiles including veneers, dental implants, smile makeovers, and teeth whitening. Our team also offers Botox®, fillers and PDO threads for patients seeking facial rejuvenation solutions, without the need for costly and invasive surgery.

The entire team at Dr. Raul G. Molina, Jr., DDS, PA, is proud to be your partner on your oral health journey. To make an appointment, call the office or book online today!

Dr. Raul G. Molina, DDS, PA

Complete Health Dental Care Practice University of Florida College of Dentistry Faculty Member Southeast Florida Academy of General Dentistry Board of Directors

Raul Molina Jr., DDS, PA, provides implant and cosmetic dentistry services to patients at his practice, Dr. Raul G. Molina Jr., DDS, PA, in Miami, Florida.

Dr. Molina believes that a patient’s healthy bright smile should also be framed by beautiful lips and a wrinkle-free face. This philosophy of facial esthetics has led him to receive extensive training and experience in the use of Botox®, Juvederm®, Voluma®, PDO threads, and many other procedures to enhance a patient’s facial appearance, lips, and smile. He is currently an active member of the American Academy of Facial Esthetics.

He currently teaches postgraduate students at the University of Florida’s College of Dentistry AEGD Residency Program. He also continues to lecture and speak at dental conferences about various topics including implants, occlusion, orthodontics, and The Concept of Complete Dentistry. Dr. Molina serves as a forensic odontologist with the Miami-Dade County Medical Examiners Department (Miami CSI).

Dr. Molina and Karen, his wife, who is a practicing attorney, have been blessed by God with seven beautiful children, and recently, with a granddaughter named Sarai! Together they attend Cornerstone Christian Church International and are active in several community programs and humanitarian organizations. Dr. Molina has been awarded the prestigious Humanitarian of The Year Award by the Florida Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Cabanillas

Dr. Cabanillas is a graduate of New York University with a degree of Doctor in Dental Surgery. He completed a General Practice Residency at Mount Sinai Medical Center , in Miami Beach, FL where he received extensive training in Oral Surgery, Periodontics and Restorative Dentistry. Afterwards, he completed a combined Prosthodontics Program at Harvard University/VA Hospital in Boston, MA and upon completion, received the first honorary mention in the 22 years of existence of this combined program.

Dr. Cabanillas has been working in private practice since 1997. He served as a Clinical Assistant Professor at the University of Florida from 1998-2005.From 2015-2017, he was involved in treating complex multidisciplinary cases in medically compromised patients at the VA Hospital in Palm Beach, FL.

His patients love his attention to detail, soft hands, sense of humor and passion for his profession, as well as, his ability to complete full mouth reconstruction in only two appointments; most cosmetic cases in just one appointment.

Dr. Cabanillas is an active member of the American College of Prosthodontics and the Academy of Osseointegration.

Dr. Cabanillas loves spending time with his wife and two children. He enjoys traveling, playing golf, scuba diving, cooking, shooting skeet and sporting clays.
