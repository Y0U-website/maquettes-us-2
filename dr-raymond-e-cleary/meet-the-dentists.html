Raymond Cleary, DDS

Dr. Raymond Cleary is a native of Cincinnati, Ohio, and earned his dental degree from The Ohio State University. After serving as Director of Operative Dentistry at Shaw Air Force Base, he established his first practice in Surfside Beach in 1985 — laying the foundation for what is now Tidelands Dental.

In addition to dentistry, Dr. Cleary has a long-standing history of civic involvement. He served as President of multiple organizations, including the Grand Strand Soccer League, the YMCA, Rotary Club, Sertoma, and more. He also held leadership roles on dental boards and helped establish two community banks.

In 2004, Dr. Cleary was elected to the South Carolina Senate, where he served for 12 years and was instrumental in passing critical healthcare legislation. Despite his public service, he remained devoted to his dental patients.

In 2018, he returned to his original practice location on Glenns Bay Road and reestablished the office as Tidelands Dental — continuing his legacy of care with the same integrity and purpose that Surfside Beach families have trusted for decades.

Enea Pavliqoti, DDS

Dr. Enea Pavliqoti grew up in Lakewood, Ohio, and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry after completing his undergraduate studies at John Carroll University.

With over five years of experience, Dr. Pavliqoti is passionate about providing high-quality, patient-centered care. He has advanced training in implant dentistry and surgical procedures, including implant placement, bone grafting, and sinus augmentation.

After marriage, Dr. Pavliqoti relocated SC to be closer to family, Dr. Pavliqoti has focused on creating a welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident in their dental care.

Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with family, traveling, watching football, and continuing his professional education.

Jared Little, DDS

Owner and Founder of Raceway Orthodontics & Mint Hill Orthodontics

Dr. Jared grew up in Michigan where he attended college and dental school at the University of Michigan (Go Blue!). He escaped the Michigan winters and moved to San Francisco, California for his orthodontic specialty training, focusing on making beautiful smiles and perfect bites with braces and clear aligners for patients of all ages – Winning him a national Invisalign case competition the year he finished residency!

After completing his orthodontic residency, Dr. Jared ventured to Charlotte, North Carolina, where he fell in love with the beautiful weather, fun car culture, and his wife, Caroline. Since 2018, Dr. Jared leverages meticulous attention-to-detail, the latest technology, and an artistic eye to create smiles across the Carolinas.

When not straightening things out at the office, Dr. Jared can be found golfing, cheering on the Panthers, working on cars and bikes, or at any motorsports event he can find.
