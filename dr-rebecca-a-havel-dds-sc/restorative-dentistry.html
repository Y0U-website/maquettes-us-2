Restorative Dentistry

Restoring Your Smile

Restorative Dentistry is the process of restoring and reinstating the function of your teeth and bite back to a healthy state. This is done by repairing damaged, diseased or missing teeth and their supporting structures. Below are several restorative options Dr. Havel provides.

Implant Restoration

Single tooth restorations with root form dental implants.

Implant Supported Dentures

Long lasting dentures that are designed to feel and look natural.

Crowns & Bridges

Strengthen and protect damaged teeth or bridge gaps.

Conventional Dentures

Removable dentures used to replace missing teeth.
