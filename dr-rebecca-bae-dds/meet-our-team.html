Meet Our Amazing Team

At the core of our dental practice is a friendly team of professionals that are dedicated to your utmost comfort and optimal oral health. We view each patient as a unique individual and tailor each treatment to your specific needs. As part of our commitment to providing close, personalized attention to every patient, we encourage patient communication. Please know that we are here to provide outstanding patient care and that we value the trust you have placed in us.

We treat each and every patient like our family in the most conservative way. We actually have fun practicing dentistry and meeting new people!

Dr. Bae

Dr. Bae graduated in 2008 from NYU College of Dentistry where she was enrolled in the accelerated 7 year BADDS program. She went on to further her education with a one year general practice residency at Hackensack University Medical Center in Hackensack, NJ. Upon completion of her residency program, she was asked to return as an attending faculty at the hospital. She was an attending medical staff at Hackensack Meridian Health where she taught and oversaw dental residents for over 15 years.

Patricia

“Pat” as she is affectionately known among all patients, has been with the practice for more than 35 years with Drs. Wakai, Stamler and now with Dr. Bae. She graduated with Associates in Applied Science Degree in Dental Hygiene from New York Technical College in Brooklyn, NY.

Monica

Monica joined our team after being our lovely patient for over 5 years. We adore her warm, caring personality, and humor that we had to have her as a part of our team!

Before joining us, she had a 51-year work career that began with a 25-year stint with Federated Stores as an Assistant Accounts Payable Manager. She then spent the next 26 years working in two Boards of Education—6 years at Pequannock Township and 20 years at West Milford.

She currently lives in Wayne after raising two beautiful daughters in West Milford for over 30 years.
