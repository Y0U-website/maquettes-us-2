Dental Implants in Schaumburg

Our Approach to Dental Implants

Dental implants are a modern and innovative solution that replaces missing teeth with natural-looking artificial ones. They&#x27;re designed to blend seamlessly with your own teeth, giving you a strong and stable bite. With dental implants, you can enjoy all the benefits of having a full set of healthy teeth without feeling self-conscious about your appearance.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Improved Look & Feel

Dental implants are custom-made to match the color and shape of your natural teeth, resulting in a beautiful, natural-looking smile. You&#x27;ll feel more confident and comfortable in social situations and daily activities.

Preserves Jaw Function

Missing teeth can cause your jawbone to deteriorate over time, leading to facial sagging and difficulty chewing. Dental implants preserve your jaw function by stimulating your bone, preventing further damage, and maintaining your facial structure.

Long-Lasting Solution

Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime with proper care. Their durability and strength make them a smart investment for a healthy, functional, and stunning smile.

The Dental Implant Process

Consultation

During your initial visit with Dr. Montgomery or Dr. Eaton, they will assess your dental health, discuss your goals, and determine if you&#x27;re a good candidate for dental implants. Then they’ll establish a personalized plan to restore your smile.

Placement, Abutment, & Healing

Once your treatment plan is established, your dentist will carefully place the implant and abutment (a connector piece) into your jawbone. You&#x27;ll then be given time to heal, during which the implant fuses with your bone, creating a strong and stable foundation for your new tooth. The healing process typically takes a few months.

Restoration

Your custom-made dental crown will be securely attached to the abutment, completing the process and giving you a fully-functioning, natural-looking tooth. You’ll go home with a renewed smile and boosted confidence.

Frequently Asked Questions about Dental Implants

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

The best way to determine your candidacy for dental implants is through a comprehensive evaluation by Dr. Montgomery or Dr. Eaton. They will consider factors such as the health of your gums and jawbone, the condition of your remaining teeth, and your overall oral health. Generally, you must have sufficient bone density and healthy gums to be a candidate. However, some patients may undergo additional treatment to ensure their qualifications. We recommend giving our office a call to schedule your consultation and see if you qualify for dental implants.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants have the potential to last a lifetime with proper care and maintenance. However, it&#x27;s important to note that the longevity of dental implants can vary depending on various factors, including oral hygiene practices, overall health, and lifestyle habits. Regular dental check-ups, good oral hygiene, and avoiding habits such as smoking can significantly contribute to the long-term success and durability of dental implants.
