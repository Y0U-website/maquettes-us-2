Technology for Your Comfort

3D CT Scanner

Experience enhanced comfort and outcomes with our 3D CT scanner. This cutting-edge technology creates detailed, non-invasive, three-dimensional images of your mouth, aiding in your diagnoses and treatment planning. Benefit from improved precision, efficiency, and comfort as our scanner provides high-resolution images of your teeth, gums, and bite.

iTero Scanner

Elevate your dental experience with our iTero Scanner. This state-of-the-art device revolutionizes the way we capture digital models so you can say goodbye to messy traditional molds. The iTero Scanner offers a faster, more precise, and more comfortable scanning process, providing detailed 3D images of your teeth and gums.

3D Printer

From dental models and surgical guides to crowns and aligners, our 3D printer allows for a more precise, efficient fabrication of dental devices. Embracing digital dentistry lets us provide you with enhanced comfort, accuracy, and personalized care. With optimized treatment outcomes, we ensure your restored smile is truly tailored to you.
