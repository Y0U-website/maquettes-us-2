0 Same Day Crowns and Bridges At Capital City Dental we are proud to use CAD/CAM (computer aided design/ computer aided manufacturing) technology to help us create beautiful, accurate, and custom teeth for you in one day.

First the dentist will prepare the tooth for a crown. Then we will use our CEREC scanner to capture a 3D digital image of your teeth. After the image has been captured the doctor will use computer software to design a tooth that is specifically made for you! Finally, after you and the doctor review and approve the design it will be sent to an in office mill to and ready to use in about 30-45…
