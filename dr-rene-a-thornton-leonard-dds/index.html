Help your child s smile shine with early orthodontic treatment from Princeton Dental.

Early orthodontic treatment focuses on guiding the growth of the jaw and teeth to correct any imbalances between the upper and lower jaws. This treatment is most effective during childhood when bones are still flexible. It helps create space for permanent teeth, improves facial balance, and can even reduce the need for tooth extractions or surgery in the future.
