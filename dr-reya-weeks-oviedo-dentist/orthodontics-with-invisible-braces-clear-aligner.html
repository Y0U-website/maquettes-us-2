Benefits of Clear Aligners

Choosing invisible braces in Oviedo, FL offers several advantages over traditional orthodontic treatments:

Clear aligners are designed to be worn for 20-22 hours per day, and each set of aligners is worn for about 1-2 weeks before moving on to the next set in the series. Over time, your teeth will gently shift into their ideal position, giving you a straighter, more aligned smile.

  • Discreet Appearance : Clear aligners are virtually invisible, making them an ideal option for those who want a subtle approach to orthodontic treatment.
  • Comfortable Fit : Unlike metal braces, clear aligners are smooth and custom-fit, so there are no sharp wires or brackets to cause discomfort.
  • Removable : You can easily remove your aligners when eating, drinking, brushing, or flossing, allowing you to maintain better oral hygiene and enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions.
  • Fewer Office Visits : With clear aligners, you typically require fewer office visits compared to traditional braces, making treatment more convenient for busy schedules.

When you choose clear aligners in Oviedo with Dr. Reya Weeks, you’ll receive a personalized treatment plan that ensures a comfortable and efficient process from start to finish. Here’s what you can expect:

Orthodontics with Clear Aligners FAQs

How long does treatment with clear aligners take? The length of treatment varies depending on the complexity of your case, but most patients complete their treatment within 12-18 months.

Are clear aligners uncomfortable? Clear aligners are generally more comfortable than traditional braces. However, you may experience mild discomfort when you switch to a new set of aligners, as this is when your teeth are shifting the most.

Can I eat and drink with my aligners? You should remove your aligners when eating or drinking anything other than water. This helps prevent damage to the aligners and keeps them clean.

How do I clean my aligners? We recommend brushing your aligners with a soft toothbrush and rinsing them with lukewarm water daily. Avoid using hot water, as it can warp the aligners.
