What to Expect During the Dental Implant Process

Receiving a dental implant is completed in several stages to promote long-term success.

Comprehensive Evaluation

Your treatment begins with a detailed examination to determine whether dental implants are appropriate for your oral health needs.

Implant Placement

The implant is placed into the jawbone using local anesthesia to help keep you comfortable throughout the procedure. Over the following months, the implant gradually bonds with the surrounding bone, creating a stable foundation for your new tooth.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, an abutment is attached to the implant, followed by a custom-made dental crown, bridge, or denture that is carefully matched to the appearance of your natural teeth.

Dr. Antolin will explain each stage of treatment, expected healing times, and any follow-up appointments needed throughout the process.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term tooth replacement option. With proper oral hygiene, regular dental checkups, and healthy lifestyle habits, many implants can remain successful for many years. Individual results vary depending on overall health and home care.

Is getting a dental implant painful?

Most patients report that dental implant placement is more comfortable than expected. Local anesthesia is used during the procedure to help minimize discomfort, and mild soreness afterward can usually be managed with recommended medications and home care.

How long does the dental implant process take?

Treatment time depends on your individual needs. Some patients require additional procedures, such as bone grafting, before implant placement. Because the implant must heal and integrate with the jawbone before the final restoration is attached, the complete process may take several months.

Will my dental insurance cover dental implants?

Coverage varies depending on your dental insurance plan. Our team can help review your benefits, explain your treatment options, and discuss available financing when applicable.
