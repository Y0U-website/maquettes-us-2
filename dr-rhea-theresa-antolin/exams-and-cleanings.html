Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have a dental exam and cleaning?

For many people, dental exams and professional cleanings are recommended about every six months. However, the ideal schedule can vary depending on oral health, gum health, history of cavities, and other individual risk factors. A dentist can recommend the appropriate frequency based on each patient’s needs.

What happens during a dental exam and cleaning?

During a dental exam, a dentist checks the teeth, gums, bite, and overall oral health for signs of cavities, gum disease, damaged teeth, and other concerns. A professional cleaning removes plaque and tartar buildup from the teeth and along the gumline. Digital X-rays may also be recommended when needed to detect dental problems that cannot be seen during a visual examination.

Can regular dental exams and cleanings prevent cavities and gum disease?

Yes. Regular dental exams and professional cleanings can help reduce plaque and tartar buildup, which can contribute to tooth decay and gum disease. Routine visits also give dental professionals an opportunity to identify potential problems early. Along with regular dental care, brushing twice a day, flossing daily, and maintaining good oral hygiene at home can help support a healthy smile.
