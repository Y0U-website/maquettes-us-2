The practice

We will treat each child with respect in a relaxed and fun atmosphere. Our commitment is to allow children to establish good healthy dental habits that will last a lifetime.

(1) to provide an environment where children of all ages can feel comfortable and safe. Pleasant visits to the dental office promote the establishment of trust and confidence in a child that will last a lifetime.

(2) to focus on teaching patients and their parents how to establish a lifetime of good oral hygiene and dental health.

(3) to have your child leave our practice with a healthy smile and a positive attitude toward dental care.

The entire Henderson Pediatric Dental Team look forward to meeting you and your child!
