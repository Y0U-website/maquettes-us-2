Richard Dillon, DDS

Dr. Dillon strives to create a comfortable environment for his patients. He enjoys being a family dentist and developing personal relationships with his patients. His patients know he will treat them with gentleness, compassion, and respect and that he will act in their best interest in the services that he provides. Dr. Dillon enjoys attending continuing education courses regularly, including at the well-respected Frank Spear Institute. He also volunteers with MOM, Missions of Mercy.

Dr. Dillon is married with two wonderful children. When not in the office, he enjoys spending time with his family and watching college and professional sports, as well as fishing and cooking.
