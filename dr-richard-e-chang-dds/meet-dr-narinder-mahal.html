Introducing Dr. Narinder Mahal: Your Trusted Partner in Dentistry

Dr. Narinder Mahal is committed to helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through exceptional dental care delivered with compassion and care. With advanced training, years of clinical experience, and a genuine passion for dentistry, he provides personalized treatment that puts every patient’s comfort and well-being first.

A Strong Foundation in Dentistry

Born and raised in India, Dr. Mahal’s journey into dentistry reflects his dedication to lifelong learning and excellence. He began his academic career in the United States at the University of Michigan-Flint , where he pursued a Master’s program in Healthcare Administration. During this time, he gained invaluable hands-on experience by working as a dental assistant before becoming a licensed dental hygienist. He also served as a teaching assistant and tutor for pre-medical students, developing a strong appreciation for patient education and mentorship.

Dr. Mahal earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree with honors from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine in New York in 2012. While at Columbia, he received the Award for Excellence in Operative Dentistry, in recognition of his outstanding clinical skill and attention to detail.

Compassionate Care You Can Trust

After practicing in Miami, Gainesville, and Boston, Dr. Mahal made the Sacramento area his home in 2015. Since then, he has earned the trust of individuals and families throughout the community by providing gentle, comprehensive dental care in a relaxed, welcoming environment. From routine check-ups to advanced care, he approaches every procedure with the same commitment to excellence and patient comfort.

Outside the Office

When not in the office, Dr. Mahal enjoys spending time with his wife and two sons. An avid golfer and licensed private pilot, he embraces new challenges and continuous learning, qualities that keep him at the forefront of modern dentistry. Dr. Mahal looks forward to welcoming patients of all ages and helping them enjoy healthy, beautiful smiles for years to come.
