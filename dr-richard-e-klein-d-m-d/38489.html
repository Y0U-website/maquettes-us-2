Frequently Asked Questions

How do dental implants work?

  • Assessment Dr. Klein evaluates your oral health and jawbone density to ensure you re a good implant candidate.
  • Implant placement A small titanium post is carefully placed into your jawbone during a simple surgery with a specialist. This is usually done under local anesthesia for your comfort.
  • Osseointegration Over several months, the implant fuses with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a strong, stable foundation for your new tooth.
  • Abutment attachment Once healing is complete, we attach a small connector piece called an abutment to the implant.
  • Crown placement Finally, a custom-made crown is secured to the abutment. This crown is designed to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size.
