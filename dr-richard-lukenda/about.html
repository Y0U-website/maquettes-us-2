Dr. Richard Lukenda, DMD

Rutgers 2000. UMDNJ 2004. Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists since 2014 — and the third dentist in his family.

He was handing over instruments before he could drive.

Dr. Lukenda’s father and uncle were both dentists, and he spent his teenage years assisting in their offices — so he had watched a few thousand appointments before he ever sat one of his own exams. Undergraduate degree at Rutgers in 2000, doctorate at UMDNJ in 2004, then hundreds of hours of further training pointed at a single thing: implants. The International Congress of Oral Implantologists made him a Fellow in 2014.

What that adds up to in practice is the cases other offices send away — dentitions that are failing all at once, full-arch reconstruction, and jaws that have been told there isn’t enough bone.

When he’s not creating confident smiles, Dr. Lukenda enjoys making the most of life outside the office. A talented guitar player, he loves unwinding with music and spending time with his two French Bulldogs, Lulu and Bluey, who outrank everyone. An avid traveler and frequent scuba diver, Dr. Lukenda loves exploring new places, experiencing different cultures, and making memories along the way.
