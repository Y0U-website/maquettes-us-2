The number that stops people is not knowing the number.

You get the plan on paper with the fee on it at the consultation — before any decision, and before anything is booked.

Written plan, written price

After the scan you get it on paper: every stage, in order, with a number next to it. If the plan has to change mid-treatment you see the new number before we start — not on the invoice afterwards.

Insurance, filed for you

Most PPO plans pay toward something here — extractions, the surgical stage, the prosthetics. We check your benefits, file the claims, and on bigger cases send a pre-determination so the coverage is confirmed in writing before you commit.

Monthly financing

Almost nobody pays for a full arch in one go, and nobody should have to postpone it for that reason. We work with both major dental lenders and can run an approval while you’re still in the chair.

Will my plan cover any of this?

The short version: usually some of it, and we confirm the figure in writing before you commit.
