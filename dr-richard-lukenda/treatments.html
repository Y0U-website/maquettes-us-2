One office for the cleaning, the surgery and the crown.

Most implant patients get passed around: a general dentist for the exam, a surgeon across town, someone else for the crown. Here it all happens in one chair, with one dentist accountable for the result.

01 General dentistry The everyday half of dentistry, in the same chair as the surgery: cleanings and annual check-ups, zirconia crowns, root canals, partials and full dentures. No second dentist across town for the routine work, and the same records either way.

02 Dental implants & All-on-4 A titanium root that fuses to your jaw, then a crown you can bite an apple with. One tooth, several, or an entire arch — planned, placed and finished by the same dentist, in the same building.

03 Cosmetic dentistry & veneers Your teeth edited, not swapped for somebody else’s from a single chipped edge to wafer-thin porcelain across the front eight. Shape, length and shade get matched to your face and your age, and you sign off on the design on screen before a drill is switched on.

04 Invisalign Clear trays you change every couple of weeks. Nothing cemented to your teeth, nothing struck off the menu, and nobody on a video call has ever noticed one.

05 Oral surgery Wisdom teeth, fractured roots, the tooth that has been watched for six years. Removed under local anesthetic in the same room your implant will go into, so the site is prepared properly the first time.

06 Emergency dentistry Swelling, a crown that came off in a sandwich, pain that had you awake at four in the morning. Call before 6pm on a weekday and we’ll find you a slot out of pain today, plan tomorrow.

Also handled here: fillings · bridges · night guards for grinders · children’s dentistry.
