Teeth Whitening

Whitening involves making teeth whiter and, therefore, more attractive. The tray method involves having impressions taken from the laboratory, where custom vinyl trays are created. These are filled with Carbamide peroxide gel and are worn for 3 hours or overnight. The tray method is recommended for badly stained teeth and may take a few months to see significant color improvement.

Veneers

Veneers are part of a dental procedure in which a covering is placed over a tooth’s outside or visible area. Veneers are typically done to the visible part of the teeth when smiling or talking. The direct technique of veneers involves composite resin being placed on the outside teeth using bonding. The indirect procedure is more involved, requiring fabrication from another dental laboratory. By getting molds of your teeth, we can create the perfect ceramic or porcelain veneers to bond to your teeth.
