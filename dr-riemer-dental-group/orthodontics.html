Orthodontics

Orthodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry focused on diagnosing, preventing, and treating dental and facial irregularities primarily related to misaligned teeth and jaws. Through various orthodontic treatments, such as braces, aligners, and other appliances, orthodontists in Lemoore, CA , aim to improve oral health, function, and aesthetics by straightening teeth, correcting bite issues, and aligning the jaws.

Beyond enhancing the smile’s appearance, orthodontic treatment is crucial in promoting overall oral health, as straighter teeth are easier to clean and less prone to dental problems like decay and gum disease. Additionally, orthodontic interventions can alleviate speech, chewing, and jaw pain issues, ultimately improving an individual’s quality of life and self-confidence. Therefore, orthodontics is indispensable in achieving a harmonious and attractive smile and ensuring optimal oral function and well-being.

Common Orthodontic Issues

Crooked Teeth

Crooked or misaligned teeth are among the most prevalent orthodontic issues individuals encounter. This condition could have resulted from genetic factors, childhood habits like thumb sucking or tongue thrusting, or insufficient jaw space.

Crooked teeth affect the smile’s appearance and can make oral hygiene more challenging, leading to an increased risk of tooth decay and gum disease. Orthodontic treatments in Lemoore, CA , such as braces or clear aligners, are commonly used to straighten crooked teeth and improve overall dental alignment.

Overbite

An overbite occurs when the upper front teeth excessively overlap the lower front teeth. This malocclusion can be caused by genetics, thumb sucking, or jaw misalignment. In addition to affecting the smile’s aesthetics, an overbite can lead to difficulty chewing, speech problems, and jaw pain. Orthodontic treatments like braces or aligners and orthognathic surgery in severe cases can help correct an overbite and achieve proper bite alignment.

Underbite

Conversely, an underbite occurs when the lower front teeth protrude further forward than the upper front teeth when the jaw is closed. This condition may result from genetics, jaw misalignment, or thumb-sucking habits. Like an overbite, an underbite can impact the ability to chew and speak properly and may lead to facial asymmetry and temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders. Orthodontic treatment and orthognathic surgery in severe cases can effectively correct an underbite and restore proper bite function.

Crossbite

A crossbite occurs when some upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth when the jaw is closed. This condition can be caused by genetics, abnormal tooth eruption, or habits like thumb sucking. If left untreated, crossbites can lead to tooth wear, gum recession, and jaw misalignment. Orthodontic interventions such as braces, expanders, or aligners can help correct a crossbite by gradually moving the affected teeth into their proper positions.

Open Bite

An open bite is characterized by a gap between the upper and lower front teeth when the jaw is closed. This malocclusion may be caused by genetics, tongue thrusting, or prolonged pacifier use. Individuals with an open bite may experience difficulty biting into food, speech impediments, and an increased risk of temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders. Orthodontic treatments like braces, aligners, or habit-breaking appliances can be utilized to close the open bite and achieve proper dental alignment.

Orthodontic Treatment Options

Traditional Braces

Traditional metal braces consist of brackets bonded to the teeth and connected by wires and elastic ties. They apply gentle pressure to move the teeth into proper alignment gradually.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces function similarly to traditional braces but use tooth-colored or clear brackets, making them less noticeable and more aesthetically pleasing. Contact us today!

Lingual Braces

Lingual braces are placed on the back surfaces of the teeth by our orthodontist at Dr. Riemer Dental Group , making them virtually invisible from the front. They are a discreet treatment option for individuals concerned about the appearance of braces.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are removable plastic trays that gradually move the teeth into alignment. They are virtually invisible, comfortable to wear, and allow easy eating and oral hygiene removal.

Invisalign

Invisalign® aligners are a popular orthodontic treatment option designed to straighten teeth discreetly and effectively. Unlike traditional metal braces, Invisalign aligners are made from clear, medical-grade plastic material that is virtually invisible when worn. Each set of aligners is custom made to fit snugly over the patient’s teeth, gradually shifting them into the desired position over time. The treatment process typically involves wearing a series of aligners, each set slightly adjusted to continue the teeth-straightening progress.

One of the key advantages of Invisalign is its removability, allowing patients to easily take out the aligners before eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing. With Invisalign, patients can achieve a straighter smile without the discomfort or appearance of traditional braces, making it a popular choice for both adults and teenagers seeking orthodontic treatment.

Retainers

Retainers are custom-made appliances worn after orthodontic treatment to maintain the results and prevent teeth from shifting back into their original positions.
