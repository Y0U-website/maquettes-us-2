Dr. Robert Bukowski was born and raised in Superior, Wisconsin. He received his undergraduate degree from the University of Minnesota-Duluth . He then went on to the University of Minnesota , where he obtained his Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree. He has been practicing in Superior for over 39 years.

Dr. Heidi Roberts is a graduate of the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine . Following dental school, she completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at Bay Clinic in Hilo, Hawai’i. Dr. Roberts has practiced dentistry in Hawai’i, Wisconsin, and rural Alaska, treating a broad spectrum of oral health conditions in diverse and often underserved communities.

When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Roberts enjoys spending time with her husband, whom she met and married in Maui in 2018, and raising their two young sons. Together, they embrace the outdoor lifestyle and close-knit community values that Northern Wisconsin has to offer.
