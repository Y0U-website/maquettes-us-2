Our Periodontal Services

At Gentle Dental, our periodontal services encompass a range of treatments tailored to halt the progression of gum disease and restore your oral health:

  • Professional Cleanings : Regular professional cleanings are vital in removing plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing at home can’t manage.
  • Scaling and Root Planing : This deep-cleaning, non-surgical procedure involves removing tartar from above and below the gum line and smoothing out rough spots on the tooth root.
  • Periodontal Maintenance : Following treatment, we provide ongoing maintenance cleanings to prevent the recurrence of periodontal disease.

Personalized Treatment Plans

Every mouth is unique, which is why we create personalized treatment plans for each of our patients. During your initial visit, our skilled periodontists will conduct a comprehensive examination, including digital X-rays, to assess the health of your gums and bone structure. We’ll discuss your treatment options and work with you to develop a plan that best suits your needs and lifestyle.

Post-Treatment Care

Recovering from periodontal treatment requires proper care and attention. We provide you with detailed aftercare instructions and recommend lifestyle changes that can aid in the healing process and prevent future occurrences. These include proper brushing and flossing techniques, the use of antimicrobial mouthwashes, and quitting smoking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Periodontal gum disease is an infection of the tissues that hold your teeth in place, typically caused by poor brushing and flossing habits that allow plaque to build up on the teeth and harden. If left untreated, it can lead to sore, bleeding gums, painful chewing problems, and even tooth loss.

Common signs of periodontal disease include red, swollen, or tender gums, bleeding while brushing or flossing, persistent bad breath, loose teeth, and receding gums. If you experience any of these symptoms, it’s important to schedule an appointment with a dentist for evaluation.

Treatment options range from non-surgical therapies that control bacterial growth to surgical procedures to restore supportive tissues. A professional cleaning known as scaling and root planing is often the first step, followed by medications, surgery, or both if necessary.

Yes, periodontal disease can largely be prevented through regular dental checkups, proper brushing and flossing techniques, and avoiding tobacco use. Maintaining a healthy diet and managing conditions like diabetes also play a role in prevention.

Research suggests that there is a link between periodontal disease and other health issues such as cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and Alzheimer’s disease. Therefore, maintaining good oral hygiene is important not just for your oral health but also for your overall well-being.
