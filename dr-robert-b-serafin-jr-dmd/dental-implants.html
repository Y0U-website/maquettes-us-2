Facts About Missing Teeth in Adults

  • More than 20 % of adults 65 years of age or older have lost all their teeth because of tooth decay or gum disease. (Source: Dentatus)
  • Approximately 180 million people are missing at least one tooth. (Source: University of Pittsburgh)
  • 90 % of patients missing all of their teeth have dentures. (Source: GotoaPro.com)
  • Approximately 10 % of the population between 50 and 64 years are edentulous. (Source: National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research)
