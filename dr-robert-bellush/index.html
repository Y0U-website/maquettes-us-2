Les soins

Losing teeth can be a frightening experience. We are here to help. We listen to your concerns and help you choose the treatment best for you.

Missing tooth? Implants replace the root. Once healed, they are restored with a porcelain crown making them functional again. If many teeth are missing, implant-retained bridges, dentures, and partials are used to securely restore your smile.

We offer custom whitening trays to be used at home. We also offer in-office whitening so that you can achieve that brighter smile quickly!
