Meet Dr. Bogan

With over 28 years of experience, we are proud to provide high-quality dental care to patients in Babylon and the surrounding areas. As a second-generation dentist, our lead practitioner, Dr. Robert Bogan, is passionate about delivering exceptional dental services.

After graduating from the New York University College of Dentistry in 1994, Dr. Bogan established a successful practice in New York City before relocating to Babylon in late 2021.

With a focus on general dentistry, we offer a comprehensive range of services to patients of all ages. From x-rays and cleanings to whitening, bonding, veneers, implants, and Invisalign, we have you covered.

At our Babylon office, we prioritize patient care and comfort. Dr. Bogan stays up-to-date with the latest developments in dentistry and utilizes advanced dental technology to ensure accurate diagnoses and effective treatment. During consultations, we take the time to discuss treatment options, ensuring you have a clear understanding of your oral health and can make informed decisions.

As a proud member of the American Dental Association and the Bronx County Dental Society, we are committed to providing personalized and comprehensive dental care to you and your family. Whether you need routine check-ups or more extensive treatments, we are here to serve your dental needs.

Visit us at our Babylon office and experience the difference our expertise and compassionate care can make.
