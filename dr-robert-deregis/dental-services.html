Full Dental Exams

We believe in providing comprehensive dental care that goes beyond just cleanings and restorative services.

That’s why our full dental exams are designed to give you a thorough assessment of the health of your teeth and gums, along with a personalized plan for your oral care.

Our skilled team of dental professionals will visually inspect your mouth to check for signs of dental issues such as cavities, gum disease, and oral lesions. We also utilize X-rays to help identify hidden cavities, impacted teeth, or bone loss that may not be visible during a visual examination. Additionally, our periodontal examination focuses on the health of your gums and supporting structures of the teeth.

Nightguards

Nightguards act as a barrier between your upper and lower teeth, preventing the harmful effects of teeth grinding, also known as bruxism. This common habit can cause excessive wear and tear on your teeth, leading to fractures, tooth sensitivity, and even jaw pain.

With our custom fitted nightguards, you can rest easy knowing that your teeth are shielded from the damaging effects of grinding and clenching.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Oral cancer screenings are an essential part of maintaining your oral health. While regular dental exams focus on issues such as cavities and gum disease, oral cancer screenings play a crucial role in early detection and prevention of potentially life-threatening conditions.

These screenings can help catch oral cancer in its early stages when it is most treatable and increase the chances of successful outcomes. With our commitment to providing comprehensive dental care, including oral cancer screenings, we prioritize your overall well-being as well as your smile.

Veneers

Dental veneers are a wonderful solution to achieve a flawless smile. Crafted from thin, custom-made porcelain shells, veneers are designed to cover the front surface of your teeth, correcting imperfections and enhancing aesthetics.

Our expert team takes pride in offering meticulous assessments and personalized veneer treatment plans for you.
