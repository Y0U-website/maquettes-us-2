Alexandria

Alexandria is a Registered Dental Hygienist from Lunenburg, Massachusetts.

She received her Bachelor of Science degree from Forsyth School of Dental Hygiene and graduated with honors from the accelerated program, and is certified in radiology and local anesthesia.

Her clinical experiences include providing dental care at offices in both Lexington and Concord, Codman Community Health Center in Dorchester, and Harvard School of Dental Medicine in Boston.

Alex is enthusiastic about dentistry and believes in providing the highest quality dental care to her patients.
