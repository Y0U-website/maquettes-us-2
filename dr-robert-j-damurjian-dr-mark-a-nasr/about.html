About Us

Patient Info Contact At Damurjian & Nasr Dentistry, our approach is simple: we treat every patient as we would our own family. Located in the heart of West Long Branch and serving nearby areas including Jersey City, Summit/Short Hills, Princeton, and Hoboken, we’ve proudly upheld a reputation of integrity, skill, and patient-centered care for over 60 years. What began as a family-owned dental office has evolved into a state-of-the-art, full-service practice offering general, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry tailored to meet the needs of every generation.

This tradition of excellence continues today under the guidance of Dr. Robert J. Damurjian & Dr. Mark A. Nasr. Together, they deliver compassionate, high-level care rooted in trust and experience.

What We Believe In

Our practice was built on the foundation of personalized service and community connection. We don’t believe in a one-size-fits-all model — every patient receives a care plan customized to their health goals, lifestyle, and preferences.

We’ve served families across generations, establishing deep-rooted trust with our patients.

We work with multiple insurances to help patients maximize out-of-network benefits.

No high-volume treatment plans. Every visit is designed for comfort, clarity, and results.

From digital imaging to minimally invasive treatments, our tools support precise, comfortable care.

Patients can access everything from routine cleanings and tooth extractions to smile makeovers — all in one place.

Our Mission

We’re here to deliver dentistry with intention. Our mission is to restore, protect, and enhance oral health while honoring the unique needs of each patient. Whether you’re dealing with a dental emergency, exploring cosmetic enhancements, or seeking a long-term dental home, our team is committed to providing honest guidance and outstanding clinical outcomes.

From pediatric dentistry to full mouth reconstruction, every procedure is approached with a high level of precision and empathy.

Meet the Doctors

The heartbeat of our practice lies in the knowledge and passion of our dentists. Dr. Robert J. Damurjian & Dr. Mark A. Nasr bring decades of combined experience and advanced training in areas such as dental implants, oral surgery, sedation dentistry, and cosmetic dentistry.

Their shared commitment to lifelong learning and meaningful patient relationships shapes the culture of the practice and ensures that each treatment meets the highest standards of care.
