Our Implant Process

Our practice follows a meticulous and personalized process to ensure your dental implant treatment is as smooth and effective as possible:

We also offer oral conscious sedation and nitrous oxide to make your visits more relaxed and anxiety-free.

  • Comprehensive Consultation – We evaluate your oral and medical health, take 3D images, and determine your candidacy.
  • Treatment Planning – Every case is customized based on bone density, number of missing teeth, and desired aesthetics.
  • Implant Placement – The titanium implant is placed precisely into your jawbone with minimal discomfort.
  • Healing & Osseointegration – Healing takes several months, during which the implant fuses to your bone.
  • Final Restoration – A custom dental crown, bridge, or denture is attached, completing your restored smile.

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have a success rate of over 95 %, especially when placed by an experienced provider and maintained through proper hygiene and regular dental visits.

How long does the implant process take from start to finish?

The complete process can take 3–6 months, depending on healing time and whether additional procedures like bone grafting are required.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Treat your implants like natural teeth—brush twice daily, floss, and schedule regular cleanings and checkups. Implants are durable, but hygiene is key to their longevity.

Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by plan. While some insurance may contribute, many patients at our fee-for-service practice use out-of-network benefits. We’ll assist you with all paperwork and claims.

How much do dental implants cost?

Dental implant cost varies based on how many teeth are being replaced, whether bone grafting is needed, and the type of restoration used, so there is no single price that applies to every patient. As a fee-for-service practice, we provide a clear, itemized estimate after your consultation and help you understand your out-of-network insurance benefits and financing options.

What&#x27;s the difference between a dental implant and a bridge?

A dental implant replaces the tooth root itself with a titanium post anchored in the jawbone, while a bridge replaces only the visible tooth by anchoring a false tooth to the healthy teeth on either side of the gap. Implants preserve jawbone density and do not require altering neighboring teeth, which is why they are often considered the more conservative long-term option.

Can I get dental implants if I have bone loss or smoke?

Bone loss does not automatically rule out dental implants, in many cases a bone grafting procedure can rebuild enough volume to support one. Smoking can slow healing and affect long-term success, so we will review your specific health history and habits during your consultation to determine the safest path forward.
