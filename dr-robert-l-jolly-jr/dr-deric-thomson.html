Dr. Deric Thomson

Dr. Deric Thomson has a deep commitment to helping patients and seeing the positive outcomes that change their lives. Originally from Kennesaw, Georgia, he earned his undergraduate degree in Organizational Sociology from the University of Utah before completing his dental education and earning his DMD at Augusta University . He began practicing in 2019 and is highly trained in a wide range of oral, maxillofacial, and facial cosmetic surgery procedures. He loves the opportunity to connect with individuals and deliver exceptional patient experiences.

Dr. Thomson offers comprehensive services at our clinic, including implants, bone grafts, wisdom teeth removal, and both IV and nitrous oxide sedation.

When he isn’t in the clinic, Dr. Thomson enjoys traveling, spending time with his family, and participating in outdoor activities including golf, fishing, boating, and gardening. His compassion and dedication to improving patients’ lives will leave you feeling confident that you will receive excellent care during your visits.
