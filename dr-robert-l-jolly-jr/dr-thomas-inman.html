Dr. Thomas Inman

Dr. Thomas Inman is an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Specialist in Bryant, AR and a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery . He earned his undergraduate degree in biology from the University of Arkansas at Fayetteville before completing his dental training at the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry. He is also a proud member of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons .

Dr. Inman is passionate about helping patients find solutions to their oral health concerns. He enjoys the daily interaction with patients and takes pride in providing care that improves both comfort and quality of life. His expertise ranges from routine procedures such as pain- and anxiety-free tooth extractions and dental implant placement to more complex treatments, including reconstructive jaw surgery to address challenges with chewing efficiency or speech.

Outside of his practice, Dr. Inman enjoys spending time with his wife and son. He is an avid outdoorsman and can often be found hunting, fishing, golfing, or hiking.
