The practice

I attended the University of Nebraska for my undergraduate degree and continued on to the University of Nebraska Dental School where I received my Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 1973.

I enjoy the continuing education aspect of dentistry and I feel I never can learn enough. I have recently been involved with the Scottsdale Dental Center under Dr. Frank Spears, learning about full treatment planning especially in the areas of aesthetics. Everything I have learned has helped me in helping my patients reach their goals for full mouth care.

I am married to my wife, Denise, a retired speech therapist, and we have seven grown children, Ben, Natalie, Alicia, Amber, Tony, Ryan, and Samantha. I enjoy golfing, socializing with friends, and family outings. We have 15 grandchildren with #16 on the way. Grand kids are the greatest !

I have been practicing dentistry since 2003. My dental career began in Chicago, where I practiced for two years. I moved back home to Siouxland to join my dad’s team in 2005. I am very lucky to be part of such a great team! Dentistry is a great profession and helping patients maintain a healthy smile is fun. I am surrounded by great people everyday.
