Meet Dr. Robert Marx

Dr. Marx joined our practice in July and is excited to bring in the new year with us at TriState Dental. He got his undergraduate degree at St. Mary’s University of Minnesota where he was also a member of the university’s hockey team. Dr. Marx then went on and earned his doctorate of dental surgery from Ceighton University School of Dentistry. He is a Sioux city native and is excited to live and work in the Siouxland area. Dr. Marx has enjoyed meeting many of our current patients and is accepting new patients.
