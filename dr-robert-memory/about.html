ABOUT US

Meet the Doctors

Born and raised in Massachusetts, Dr. Memory always wanted to become a doctor, as long as he could remember, to use his love of science to help people.

Dr. Memory is trained in the full scope of oral and maxillofacial surgery including extractions, pathology, facial trauma, anesthesia/sedation and implant and bone-grafting procedures. He was part of a team that performed the first ever CT-computer guided implant placement at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Memory is board certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and the National Dental Board of Anesthesiology. He is a Fellow of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons and a member of the American Dental Association.

Dr. Memory enjoys spending time with his two daughters as well as travel, golf and skiing.

Dr. Fahey’s approach to oral surgery includes a focus on comfort, attention to detail, safety, and communication. He believes in using modern technology and continuing education to enhance patient care and improve treatment.

Dr. Fahey is trained in the full scope of oral and maxillofacial surgery and has a special interest in extractions, pediatric oral surgery, anesthesia/sedation, implant and bone grafting procedures, and pathology

He is board certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. He is a Fellow of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons and a member of the American Dental Association.

Dr. Fahey holds a Bachelor of Science degree in biology from Emory University and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree with honors from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. He completed his oral and maxillofacial surgery residency training at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. While at Tufts, Dr. Fahey was honored to receive the Sterns Scholarship for Excellence in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery.

Dr. Fahey maintains hospital privileges at both Newton-Wellesley Hospital and Franciscan Children’s Hospital.

Dr. Fahey lives in Boston with his wife and their rescue dog, Nessie. In his free time, he enjoys traveling, exploring Boston, taking improv classes, and watching his favorite sports teams, especially the UNC Tar Heels and Arsenal.
