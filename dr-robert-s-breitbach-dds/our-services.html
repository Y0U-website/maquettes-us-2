Our Services

Crowns can restore and protect damaged teeth. We offer same day, all ceramic crowns fabricated in office with the CEREC CAD/CAM technology. Saving you time with the convenience of a single appointment.

Dental cleanings include removing plaque and tartar from your teeth, polishing, taking x-rays and examining your mouth and gums. Dental hygiene is crucial for maintaining oral health and over all well-being.

Dental Emergencies include toothaches, chipped teeth, lost or broken restorations or trauma to the mouth. We are prepared to help you navigate your treatment.

If you have been diagnosed with periodontal disease, the good news is that it can often be treated. Scaling and root planing is a special cleaning to remove plaque and tartar deposits on the tooth and root surfaces. This procedure helps gum tissue to heal and periodontal pockets to decrease.

Fillings help repair damaged or decayed teeth. Composite filling material is a great option for restoring the function and look of one s teeth. ​ ​

Dental X-rays are essential for proper oral health and maintenance. X-rays are taken every year to help detect oral health issues including cavities, infections and bone loss.

Dental Implants replace a single tooth or a full arch. We work with amazing Oral Surgeons to place the titanium implants. Once integrated in your mouth, implant supported crowns or dentures are custom made to complete your smile.

Proper dental care for children is important to their overall health. We strive to provide a good experience in the dental chair. Getting kids familiar with regular checkups including cleanings, x-rays and fluoride treatments. Developing lifelong habits of good oral hygiene.

Dental extraction is a procedure resulting in the removal of teeth. There are many reasons that can lead to a tooth extraction including, decay, infection, gum disease, trauma, crowding and not being able to maintain wisdom teeth.

Your teeth have pits and grooves that can trap plaque, bacteria and food. Sealant is a material that flows into those pits and grooves. It acts as a barrier by sealing out plaque, bacteria and food. A kid friendly practice to provide the best comfort.

Dentures are removable appliances that replace missing teeth. There are many types of dentures including full, partial and implant supported. The benefits of dentures are improving chewing function, aesthetics and speech function.

Nitrous Oxide sedation is offered in office for patient comfort. It safely

manages pain, anxiety and eliminates or minimizes the gag reflex.

Bridges replace missing teeth by attaching to the natural teeth adjacent to the space. The bridge is cemented "fixed" in place.

We offer take-home and in-office whitening services. ​Brighten your smile with custom trays to take home or give your confidence a boost in the dental chair with our in-office whitening performed by our dental professionals.

Root canal therapy can save infected, damaged or diseased teeth. An exam and x-rays can determine whether an in-office root canal treatment is required or referral to an Endodontist.

We offer custom athletic mouth guards, and bruxing night guards. Mouth guards can help protect your teeth and jaw.

Custom made porcelain veneers cover the front surfaces of one s teeth for appearance. Veneers can cover chips, gaps and stains. Improves aesthetics and gives a brighter smile.
