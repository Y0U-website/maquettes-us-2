The practice

Our surgeons invest in equipment, knowledge, and staff that keep our practice on the cutting edge of oral surgery technology and techniques. We are pleased to serve our community by offering a full suite of oral surgery procedures.

With three offices across southern Maine, we look forward to serving your oral surgery needs, and thank you for choosing us to care for you.

Our team of oral surgeons focuses on creating a friendly, welcoming atmosphere for our valued patients. Our dedicated surgeons will explain every aspect of your surgery and ensure that your experience is comfortable and enjoyable!

Our service is at the core of everything we do. Our team works together to provide a warm, comfortable, and relaxing environment.

We are among the first oral surgery practices in Maine to become a North American X-Guide Center of Excellence . To achieve this distinction, our surgeons and team obtained a high degree of knowledge and experience in employing X-Guide Dynamic Navigation technology to provide the best results to patients.
