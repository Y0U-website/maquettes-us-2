Dr. Robert Sachs

Dr. Robert Sachs is a University of Michigan Graduate, a Member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Michigan Dental Association (MDA), Invisalign and Clear Correct Certified.

In his 30+ year tenure at Sachs Family Dentistry, Dr. Robert Sachs focused on cosmetic dentistry, crown and bridge, and endodontics.

Dr. Robert Sachs has pivoted to dental technology, as he is now the Chief Clinical Officer of Freedom Technologies Group L.L.C. They’re working to develop the world’s first wireless, direct-conversion intraoral X-ray sensor.
