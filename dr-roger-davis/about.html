Though of Cuban descent, Dr. del Rio attended the University of Florida for her undergraduate degree. Now before you say “Go Gators”, you should know she received her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree with high distinction from the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry. Following additional training, she moved to the Treasure Coast and we are so glad she’s our neighbor! When she’s not working on improving smiles, she’s spending time with family and spoiling her fur baby Enzo. You can also find her staying healthy in the gym and exploring outdoors.
