Accessibility

At RA Dental Care PC, we believe that everyone deserves access to quality care—both in our office and on our website. We’re dedicated to providing an online experience that’s accessible, inclusive, and easy to use for all visitors, regardless of ability.

We are actively working toward compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and following recognized standards such as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). Our goal is to ensure that every patient and visitor can navigate our website comfortably and with confidence.

If you experience any accessibility issues or have suggestions for improvement, we’d love to hear from you. Please contact us:

We appreciate your feedback and are continuously improving to make our website welcoming and accessible for everyone.
