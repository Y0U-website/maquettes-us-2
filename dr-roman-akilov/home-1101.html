Meet the Dentist

Roman Akilov, DDS

Dr. Roman Akilov is one of New York’s finest implant dentists, known for his gentle and patient approach. Serving patients in New York City, Dr. Akilov attended New York University for his medical education. He is multilingual, speaking English, Russian, and Spanish, ensuring he can communicate effectively with a diverse range of patients. Currently, Dr. Akilov is advancing his education in dental implants to stay at the forefront of dental technology.

Dr. Akilov performs a wide array of procedures, extending beyond the dental chair to include gum surgery and wisdom tooth removal. He is meticulous in performing root canals and excels in general dental procedures such as mouth guard fittings, dental cleanings, teeth whitening, and handling dental emergencies. His expertise in dental implantation covers Lumineers, implants, veneers, bridges, dentures, and crowns. Most of his consultations and procedures last between 45 minutes to an hour, as he is committed to providing individualized, compassionate care to each patient.
