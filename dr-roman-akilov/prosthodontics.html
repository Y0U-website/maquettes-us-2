Implant Dentistry Services Offered at RA Dental Care, Ridgewood, NY

RA Dental Care provides comprehensive prosthodontics services to restore the function and aesthetics of your smile. Located at 70-02 Forest Ave, Ridgewood, NY 11385, our practice offers a variety of treatments designed to replace missing teeth and enhance oral health.

How do implants and dentures work?

Implants and dentures are essential components of prosthodontics, designed to replace missing teeth and restore your smile. Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. Dentures are removable appliances that replace missing teeth and surrounding tissues.

What kinds of issues can prosthodontics address?

Prosthodontics can help address a variety of dental issues, including:

These treatments not only restore the function and aesthetics of your smile but also boost your self-confidence and improve your overall quality of life.

  • Replacing missing teeth
  • Restoring the function of your bite
  • Improving the appearance of your smile
  • Enhancing oral health and hygiene
  • Supporting facial structures

What is involved in getting dental implants?

Getting dental implants involves several steps. First, a comprehensive dental exam is performed, including X-rays and impressions of your teeth and jaw. Next, the implant is surgically placed into the jawbone. After a healing period, during which the implant fuses with the bone, an abutment is placed on the implant. Finally, a custom-made crown is attached to the abutment, completing the restoration.
