All-on-4 Implants Hewlett, NY

When considering a tooth restoration option, our experienced doctor will evaluate your oral health and guide you in making the best decision for your needs.

  • Permanently Attached to the Jawbone: The implants are securely placed in the jawbone, mimicking the function and appearance of your natural teeth.
  • Faster Treatment: Compared to traditional restoration methods, All-on-4 implants offer a quicker, more efficient solution for full-mouth restoration.
  • Jawbone Health: The titanium posts used in All-on-4 implants stimulate the jawbone, promoting strength and preventing bone loss.
  • Enhanced Aesthetics and Confidence: The full-mouth restoration provides a natural, beautiful look, boosting your confidence and improving your overall appearance.
