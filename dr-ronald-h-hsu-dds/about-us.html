Meet the Dentists

Dr. Ronald Hsu, DDS, M.S. Board Certified Pediatric Dentist

Outside of work, my wife Misty and I are proud parents of five children, and we stay involved with the Hockinson and Vancouver School Districts.

Dr. Lilia Yu, DDS Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Lilia is a board-trained pediatric dentist who believes that early dental visits should be positive, gentle, and even fun. Her goal is to help children feel safe and confident at the dentist, turning first appointments into happy experiences and building lifelong healthy habits.

Originally trained as a dentist in Taiwan, Dr. Lilia brings a global perspective and a calm, patient-centered approach to every visit. As she continued her journey to the United States, she completed her pediatric dentistry residency at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, where she received advanced training in caring for children of all ages and needs.

In addition to clinical practice, Dr. Lilia has a strong passion for education. She has served as faculty and course director at the University of Detroit Mercy, teaching and mentoring future dentists. This academic background allows her to provide care that is both compassionate and firmly grounded in the latest evidence-based techniques.

Outside the office, Dr. Lilia loves music, video games, and all things Disney — interests that help her connect easily with kids and make dental visits feel a little more magical.

Dr. Elysia Sybert, DDS Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist

I was born and raised in Sonoma County, California, and earned both my bachelor’s and dental degrees at UCLA. After completing a hospital dentistry residency at UCSF and pediatric training at USC, I’ve had the pleasure of working in California, South Carolina, and now the beautiful Pacific Northwest. One of my favorite parts of pediatric dentistry is providing preventive care that has a lifelong impact. I’m board certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and a proud member of several esteemed dental associations.

When I’m not at work, I enjoy cooking with my husband, spending time with our rescue pup, and exploring Portland’s incredible food scene.

Questions About Our Team and Practice Are all of your dentists pediatric specialists? Yes. Dr. Ronald Hsu, Dr. Elysia Sybert, and Dr. Lilia Yu are board-certified pediatric dentists who have completed advanced training focused on caring for infants, children, teens, and patients with unique dental needs.

How do your dentists help children feel comfortable?

Our team takes a gentle, child-centered approach, using positive communication, patience, and kid-friendly techniques to help children build confidence during every visit.

Does your team treat children with dental anxiety?

Yes. We understand that every child is different. We offer gentle dentistry techniques, advanced technology, and sedation options when appropriate to create a calm and comfortable experience.

Why should my child see a pediatric dentist?

Pediatric dentists receive specialized training in children’s oral health, growth and development, preventive care, and behavior guidance, allowing them to provide age-appropriate care for every stage of childhood.

What languages are spoken at Storybook Dental?

Our team welcomes families from diverse backgrounds. Dr. Ronald Hsu is fluent in Mandarin and knowledgeable in Korean, helping us better serve our community.
