About Us

Our Story

If it’s been a while since you’ve smiled, then we can help. Stillwater Family Dental in Stillwater, MN, is a judgment-free practice with the goal of getting your oral health back on track.

With many comprehensive solutions under one roof, including general, cosmetic, restorative, implant, and sedation dentistry services, we’re your partner for a beautiful and healthy smile. And the best part is, we make it easy!

From the modern technology to the in-office comforts, our friendly team and expert dentists help ensure a positive dentist experience for the whole family each and every time. We work with kids, teens, adults, and seniors and look forward to bringing out your best smile.
