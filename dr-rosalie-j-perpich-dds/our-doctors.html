Dr. Tricia Carlson

Putting you at ease while listening to your concerns.

I like to find common interests with people and find that listening to patient concerns and past experiences helps provide a safe place. Making people smile goes a long way toward building trust, and we smile a lot here!

Dr. Carlson traveled with her parents on short mission trips to Africa, where she shadowed the dentist and discovered her calling. Today, she loves working with children and anxious patients to create positive dental experiences from a young age. Dr. Carlson goes above and beyond to put patients at ease, listen to their concerns, and develop treatment into the best care options.

Before finding the right team and joining Stillwater Family Dental, she worked as a dentist in different northern metro area practices. Dr. Carlson enjoys furthering her education with Spear Education study club and Kois Center for Dental Excellence. In her free time, she enjoys spending time with family, photographing sporting events, and serving at church.

American Dental Association, Minnesota Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, St. Paul District Dental Society, Spear Education / Study club, and Kois Dental Education Center.

  • University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, Doctor of Dental Surgery
