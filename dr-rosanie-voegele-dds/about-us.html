Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Dalton Family and Cosmetic Dentistry different from other dental offices?

Our combination of advanced technology, personalized care plans, and genuine compassion sets us apart. Dr. Jacob Lee and our team take time to understand your unique needs, explain every treatment option clearly, and create a warm, anxiety-free environment. We’re not just your dalton dentist—we’re your partners in lifelong oral health.

Do you accept new patients and dental insurance?

Absolutely! We welcome new patients and work with most major dental insurance plans. Our team verifies your benefits before treatment and explains any out-of-pocket costs upfront. We also offer flexible payment plans and financing options to make quality dental care accessible for every family in Dalton.

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your first visit includes a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays if needed, and a consultation with Dr. Lee. If a cleaning is right for you, we’ll schedule it separately based on your exam findings. We’ll review your dental history, discuss any concerns, and create a personalized treatment plan. Most new patient visits take 60-90 minutes. We’ll make you feel comfortable and answer all your questions.

Do you offer emergency dental appointments?

Yes! We reserve same-day slots for dental emergencies like toothaches, broken teeth, or knocked-out teeth. Call (706) 226-2228 immediately—we’ll fit you in as soon as possible. For after-hours emergencies, our answering service will connect you with Dr. Lee. Your comfort and oral health can’t wait.

What services do you provide?

We offer comprehensive family and cosmetic dentistry: preventive care (cleanings, exams, sealants), restorative treatments (fillings, crowns, bridges, dental implants), cosmetic services (teeth whitening, veneers, bonding), emergency dentistry, and children’s dentistry. Whatever your smile needs, our dalton dentist team delivers it with precision and care.

How do you help patients with dental anxiety?

We specialize in gentle, anxiety-free dentistry. Options include warm blankets and our proven Tell-Show-Do technique. Dr. Lee and our team patiently explain each step, go at your pace, and create a calming atmosphere. Many anxious patients become our most relaxed regulars!
