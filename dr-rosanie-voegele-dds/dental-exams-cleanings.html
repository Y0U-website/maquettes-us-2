Your Professional Cleaning Experience

Our cleanings aren’t just about making your teeth look good—they’re about keeping your entire mouth healthy.

Step 1: Plaque & Tartar Removal Ultrasonic scalers and precision hand instruments gently remove buildup, especially below the gumline. This is what you simply can’t achieve at home.

Step 2: Polishing We polish to remove surface stains from coffee, tea, and wine. Smooth enamel = harder for plaque to stick.

Step 3: Deep Flossing Professional flossing removes remaining debris and checks for problem areas between teeth.

Step 4: Fluoride Treatment Strengthens enamel and prevents cavities. (Recommended for kids, optional for adults based on risk.)

Step 5: Personalized Instruction We’ll show you the best brushing and flossing techniques for YOUR mouth. No generic advice—just practical tips that work.

Total time: 30–60 minutes Result: Teeth that feel smooth, breath that’s fresh, confidence that’s real

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get dental exams and cleanings?

Every 6 months for most patients with healthy teeth and gums. If you have gum disease, diabetes, smoke, are pregnant, build tartar quickly, or have high cavity risk, we may recommend every 3–4 months. Children need cleanings every 6 months (sometimes 3–4 months if high risk). Regular visits remove tartar you can’t at home, catch cavities early, screen for oral cancer, monitor gum health, and prevent 90 % of serious dental problems.
