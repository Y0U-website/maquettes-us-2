Why Choose Dental Implants?

Unlike dentures or bridges , dental implants:

  • Preserve jawbone by stimulating bone growth (prevent facial collapse)
  • Don’t slip or move like removable dentures
  • Restore 90 %+ of chewing function
  • Look and feel like natural teeth
  • Last 20+ years or lifetime with proper care
  • Don’t damage adjacent teeth (standalone restoration)

Our Implant Services

We offer comprehensive implant solutions including:

  • Single tooth implants — replace one missing tooth without touching its neighbors
  • Multiple tooth implants — implant-supported bridges for several missing teeth
  • Full arch replacement (All-on-4, All-on-6) — a complete, fixed new smile on 4–6 implants
  • Implant-supported dentures — snap-in stability for loose dentures
  • Bone grafting and sinus lifts — rebuild support when bone has receded

What the Process Looks Like

  • Consultation & 3D imaging. We examine your mouth, take 3D scans, and map out a treatment plan with clear pricing — no surprises.
  • Preparation (if needed). Extractions, bone grafting, or gum therapy come first when they’re needed for a stable foundation.
  • Implant placement. Placing an implant takes about 60–90 minutes under local anesthesia. Most patients are back to normal activities within a day or two.
  • Healing (3–6 months). The implant fuses with your jawbone — the key to its strength. You’ll have a temporary tooth in the meantime.
  • Abutment and crown. We attach the connector and your custom, color-matched crown. Then you eat, speak, and smile like you never lost the tooth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are dental implants and how do they work?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed in jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. After healing (3–6 months), implant fuses with bone (osseointegration). Then abutment (connector) and crown (visible tooth) are attached. Result: looks, feels, functions like natural tooth! Implants preserve jawbone (prevent facial collapse), don’t slip/move, restore 90 %+ chewing function. Success rate: 95–98 % at 10 years. Ideal for: single missing tooth, multiple missing teeth, full arch replacement. Call (706) 226-2228 for consultation!

Cost depends on: need for bone grafting ($300–1,200), tooth location, material choice, complexity. Dental insurance may cover 50 % of crown/abutment. Does dental implant surgery hurt?

During surgery: NO—you’ll be completely numb with local anesthesia. Afterward: mild-moderate soreness for 3–5 days, manageable with OTC pain relievers (ibuprofen) or prescription if needed. Most patients report LESS discomfort than tooth extraction! Ice packs, soft foods, rest help significantly. Modern techniques make implant surgery far more comfortable than old horror stories. Your comfort is our priority!

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants last 20+ years or lifetime with proper care! Success rate: 95–98 % at 10 years, 90–95 % at 20 years. Implant post (titanium) can last lifetime. Crown may need replacement after 10–15 years (normal wear). Longevity depends on: oral hygiene (brush/floss daily), regular dental visits (every 6 months), not smoking (smokers have higher failure rate), overall health (diabetes control), avoiding hard foods/ice. Implants are most durable tooth replacement option available!

Who is NOT a candidate for dental implants?

Not ideal for: uncontrolled diabetes (healing issues), heavy smokers (2–3x failure rate), severe gum disease (must treat first), insufficient bone (may need grafting), radiation therapy to jaw (healing complications), certain medications (bisphosphonates), pregnancy (elective procedures postponed), children/teens (jaw still growing). Many ’non-candidates’ can become candidates with treatment (bone grafting, gum therapy, smoking cessation, diabetes control). We evaluate each case individually. Call (706) 226-2228 for evaluation!

What is the dental implant process?

Process: (1) Consultation (exam, 3D imaging, treatment plan). (2) Tooth extraction (if needed) + bone graft (if needed). (3) Implant placement (60–90 min per implant). (4) Healing (3–6 months for osseointegration). (5) Abutment placement (minor procedure). (6) Crown placement (final tooth). Total time: 4–9 months. We provide temporary teeth during healing. Most patients continue normal activities within 1–2 days of surgery. We guide you through every step. Call (706) 226-2228 to start!

What are alternatives to dental implants?

Alternatives: (1) Dental bridge—anchors to adjacent teeth, $2,000–5,000, lasts 10–15 years. (2) Partial denture—removable, affordable, $500–2,000. (3) Full denture (multiple teeth), $1,000–5,000. (4) Do nothing—NOT recommended (teeth shift, bone loss, bite problems, TMJ issues). Implants are gold standard because they: preserve bone, don’t damage adjacent teeth, last longest, feel most natural, restore most function. Bridges/dentures are less expensive upfront but may cost more long-term due to replacements. We’ll discuss all options!
