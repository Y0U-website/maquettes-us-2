Frequently Asked Questions

What is general dentistry and what services are included?

General dentistry focuses on prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of common dental issues. Services include: dental exams and checkups, professional cleanings, fillings for cavities, root canal therapy, tooth extractions, gum disease treatment, oral cancer screenings, fluoride treatments, dental sealants. Unlike cosmetic dentistry (which enhances appearance), general dentistry maintains oral health and function. Both are important for a complete, healthy smile. How often should I visit the dentist for general care?

Most patients: every 6 months for exam and cleaning. Some need more frequent visits: gum disease history, diabetes, smokers, pregnant women, weak immune system, cavity-prone individuals. Children: first visit by age 1, then every 6 months. During visits, we check for cavities, gum disease, oral cancer, bite issues, and clean teeth thoroughly. Regular visits prevent major problems and save money long-term. We’ll recommend schedule based on your oral health. Call (706) 226-2228 to book!
