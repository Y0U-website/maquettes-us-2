Implants

Implants are synthetic structures strategically placed where the tooth root typically resides. Anchored securely to the jawbone or a metal framework, implants provide a robust foundation for artificial teeth or permanent bridges, offering a reliable solution for tooth replacement. In specific cases, they are also utilized for attaching dentures, ensuring a snug and comfortable fit for users.

At R&F Dental Clinic, we specialize in Dental Implant Services, providing personalized and expert care at every step.
