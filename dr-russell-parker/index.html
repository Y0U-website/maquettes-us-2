The practice

Pascagoula native Dr. Jessica Bosarge Taylor is a 2007 graduate of Pascagoula High School and a summa cum laude graduate of the University of Mississippi with a Bachelors of Art degree in Biology and minor in Chemistry.

She earned her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the University of Mississippi Medical Center School of Dentistry in Jackson in 2014 before returning home to Pascagoula to practice dentistry in the same office where she’s been a lifelong patient.

In her spare time, Dr. Taylor enjoys time outdoors boating, biking, and playing tennis, being with her family and friends, and staying involved in the community.

She is married to John Paul Taylor, also a lifelong resident of Pascagoula, where they reside with their three children, Camilla, Mary Margaret, and John Paul.

0 Dental Implants Rediscover the comfort and confidence to eat, speak, and smile with natural-looking dental implants.

Dental implants are titanium posts which are placed into the jaw bone in areas of missing teeth. These posts, or implants, act as new tooth roots which will provide a strong foundation for artificial teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Our office hours are Monday through Friday from 8 am until 5 pm

Les soins

Dental implants can be used to restore various combinations of missing teeth. They can be used to replace a single missing tooth, to replace several adjacent missing teeth, or they can even be used to replace all of your teeth with implant supported bridges…
