Heart of Texas Endodontics - Waco

Welcome to Heart of Texas Endodontics, proudly serving the Waco community with expert endodontic care. Our experienced team of specialists is committed to providing compassionate, high-quality treatment in a state-of-the-art environment. Whether you’re seeking relief from dental pain or require complex root canal treatments, we’re here to help you preserve your natural teeth and restore your oral health.

Heart of Texas Endodontics proudly provides endodontic services to patients from Bruceville-Eddy , Lacy Lakeview , Hewitt , Robinson , McGregor , Woodway , and Bellmead , TX.

Our Endodontic Services Include:

In addition to these procedures, we offer comprehensive services including cracked tooth treatment and endodontic therapy. Our practice caters to patients’ unique needs, ensuring a relaxing and modern environment where you’ll feel supported every step of the way. We look forward to helping you achieve optimal oral health.

  • Root Canal Therapy to save your natural teeth with advanced care.
  • GentleWave® System for minimally invasive, effective treatment that thoroughly disinfects and cleans the inside of the tooth.
  • Endodontic Retreatment to address previously treated teeth.
  • Dental Trauma Treatment to handle injuries from dental trauma and emergencies with care, protecting damaged teeth from further harm.
  • Sedation Dentistry for a comfortable, dental anxiety-free experience.

About Waco, TX

Nestled along the Brazos River, Waco, TX, is a dynamic city known for its rich history and vibrant culture. The city blends small-town charm with big-city amenities, making it a delightful place to live, work, and visit.

  • Magnolia Market at the Silos : A must-visit destination featuring boutique shopping, delicious eats, and a picturesque market area.
  • Dr Pepper Museum : Explore the history of America’s iconic soda brand at this fun and interactive museum.
  • Waco Mammoth National Monument : Witness the fascinating remains of Columbian mammoths in this National Park Service site.
