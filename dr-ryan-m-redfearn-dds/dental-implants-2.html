The Unmatched Benefits of Dental Implants

Choosing dental implants for your tooth replacement offers significant advantages for your oral health and overall well-being.

  • Built to Last: With proper care, dental implants are a permanent solution that can last a lifetime.
  • Preserves Jawbone Health: Implants stimulate the jawbone just like a natural tooth root, preventing bone loss that occurs when teeth are missing.
  • Looks and Feels Natural: Your implant restoration is custom-designed to match your natural teeth, providing a result that is both beautiful and functional.
  • Restores Full Chewing Power: Unlike other options, implants allow you to eat all your favorite foods with confidence and without restrictions.

FAQs About Restorative Dentistry

Most people with one or more missing teeth and good general oral health are candidates for dental implants. A consultation, including an evaluation of your jawbone density, is necessary to determine if implants are the right choice for you.

The procedure is performed with local anesthesia to ensure you are comfortable and feel no pain. Most patients report that the process is much more comfortable than they expected and compare the post-operative feeling to that of a simple extraction.

The entire process can take several months, as it requires time for the implant to fully integrate with the jawbone—a process called osseointegration. The exact timeline varies depending on the individual patient’s healing process and treatment plan.

You care for your dental implants the same way you care for your natural teeth: with regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups. Excellent oral hygiene is key to ensuring the long-term success of your implant.
