Restorative Dentistry

Damage, decay, or tooth loss can affect not only your ability to chew and speak comfortably but also your confidence. At Fit To Smile Dental, our restorative dentistry services are designed to repair and replace teeth, bringing back the full function and natural beauty of your smile. We use high-quality materials and advanced techniques to deliver results that look great and are built to last.

  • Same-Day Dental Crowns: A custom-fitted cap placed over a damaged or weakened tooth to restore its shape, strength, and appearance. We offer same-day crowns to repair your smile in a single visit.
  • Dental Bridges: An effective solution for filling the gap left by one or more missing teeth, using adjacent teeth as anchors to support a replacement tooth.
  • Dentures & Partials: Whether you are missing some or all of your teeth, we create custom-designed dentures that provide a comfortable fit and restore your ability to eat and smile with confidence.
  • Dental Implants: The gold standard for tooth replacement, implants provide a permanent, stable foundation for a new tooth that looks and feels completely natural.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance the beauty of your smile with our cosmetic dentistry options, designed to help you achieve the confident look you’ve always wanted.

  • Teeth Whitening: Professional in-office or take-home whitening treatments to brighten your smile safely and effectively.
  • Veneers: Thin, custom-made porcelain shells placed over your teeth to correct chips, stains, or gaps.
  • Bonding: A quick and painless procedure that uses a tooth-colored composite resin to repair minor imperfections.

Invisalign®

Straighten your teeth discreetly and comfortably with Invisalign clear aligners. As a Preferred Provider, we have the experience to help you achieve a perfectly aligned smile without traditional braces.

  • Nearly Invisible Aligners: A clear alternative to metal brackets and wires.
  • Removable for Easy Care: Take your aligners out to eat, brush, and floss as you normally would.
  • Predictable Results: Advanced 3D imaging allows you to see your new smile before you even start.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth, offering a permanent and natural-looking solution.

  • Single Tooth Implants: A durable, three-part restoration that replaces a missing tooth from root to crown.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges: A stable solution for replacing several missing teeth in a row, supported by implants instead of natural teeth.

Gum Disease Therapy

We provide effective treatments to manage and reverse the effects of gum disease, protecting your gums and the foundation of your smile.

  • Scaling & Root Planing: A deep cleaning procedure to remove plaque and tartar buildup from below the gumline.
  • Perio-Protect® Trays: Custom trays that deliver medication deep below the gumline to fight infection.
