Specialized Pediatric Dental Care Call us: 918.921.8822 Our mission is to provide your child with comprehensive dental services to our patients, including, Pediatric Dentistry, Orthodontics, Laser Dentistry, Frenectomy, and Infant Feeding Care.

Every child deserves specialized pediatric dental care. At On The Cusp Pediatric Dentistry, your doctors are Board-Certified Pediatric Dentists and have special training to meet the needs of each child and family. Together, we focus on the prevention of oral disease and gentle treatment options, and provide helpful tips and guidance as your child grows and develops.

Pediatric Dentistry

Your pediatric dentists have a passion for the oral health of children from infancy to teen years. All of our dentists have completed four years of dental school, plus two additional years of residency training in dentistry for infants, children, teens, and children with special needs. You’re getting Board Certified Pediatric Specialists at OTC!

Orthodontics

We practice interceptive and comprehensive orthodontics, which means diagnosing and treating malocclusions (the abnormal alignment of the upper and lower teeth) as soon as they are detected.

Sedation

On the Cusp is home to a board-certified Anesthesiologists. Don’t settle for non-anesthesiologist providers for your child. Your child will receive care from Doctors who are trained professionals. Your child will also go through recovery with a special pediatric trained RN. We offer in-office treatment for patients, and our dentists have hospital privileges, as well. Dr. Roberts is the Department Chief of Oral Maxillofacial and Dental Surgery at St. Francis Hospital.

Frenectomy

Your Doctor uses a laser for a safe and quick procedure that allows for greater tongue and lip mobility. In some cases, frenectomies can aid in the prevention of other health problems like dental decay or spacing, speech difficulties, and digestive issues. These can be helpful in assisting proper breastfeeding and bottle feeding for your infant.

Laser Dentistry Laser dentistry for kids can be used for both tooth and soft tissue related procedures. Local anesthesia and sutures are not required, so healing time is faster!
