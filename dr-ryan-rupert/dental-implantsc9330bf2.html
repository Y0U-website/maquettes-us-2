Missing teeth are a bigger problem than you’d think. Your oral hygiene is greatly affected by even one absent tooth. Luckily, with modern dentistry there’s really no reason to allow gaps in your teeth to remain. If you’re hoping to permanently restore your smile, Dr. Ryan Rupert and Robinson Township Smiles suggest dental implants in Coraopolis, PA.

While dentures and bridges need replacements, dental implants are a more permanent solution to missing teeth. The implants match the shape of the lost tooth, so your smile will be perfectly restored. But why do you need to replace a missing tooth?

A gap between teeth creates many problems for your jaw. Without a replacement, oral hygiene will decline. Your teeth start slanting towards the open space, exposing areas that enamel does not protect. Food can also become lodged in this gap.

Your bite changes with a missing tooth, too. The way your lower and upper arcs of teeth fit together known as dental occlusion will deviate, leading to overcrowding or teeth grinding. Dental implants can stop these problems before they arise. If left untreated, your missing teeth could end up costing you more than the dental implant.
