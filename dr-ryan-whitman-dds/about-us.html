Samantha

Samantha became a registered dental hygienist in 2016 and joined the team at Durango Family Dentistry in 2022. She received her Bachelor of Science degree in Dental Hygiene from Old Dominion University and her Bachelor of Science degree in Health Service Administration from the University of Southern Indiana.

One of Samantha’s greatest joys is staying current on dental research and then giving her patients the tools and skills they need to improve their overall health by improving their oral health. She loves building long-term relationships with her patients and feels honored to work with the caring and compassionate dental team at Durango Family Dentistry.

When she isn’t working with patients, Samantha enjoys breeding chickens, gardening, and managing her family’s homestead.
