Meet Columbia MD Dentist Sahil Goyal DMD

Dr. Sahil Goyal is the Founder and Clinical Director of Veritas Dental Institute, where he is dedicated to transforming lives through exceptional dentistry. His philosophy extends far beyond treating teeth—he is passionate about restoring health, function, confidence, and quality of life through comprehensive, technology-driven dental care.

After earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine (D.M.D.) degree from Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Goyal has devoted his career to mastering advanced implant, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry. Through thousands of hours of continuing education and advanced clinical training, he has developed expertise in full-mouth rehabilitation, dental implant surgery, bone grafting, cosmetic smile makeovers, sedation dentistry, laser dentistry, endodontics, and complex restorative procedures.

Over the course of his career, Dr. Goyal has placed more than 2,000 dental implants and has helped transform the lives of hundreds of patients through more than 500 smile transformations. He has a particular passion for full-arch implant rehabilitation, providing life-changing solutions for patients seeking to replace an entire arch—or a full mouth—of failing or missing teeth. Combining advanced digital technology with meticulous treatment planning, he strives to deliver results that are not only beautiful, but functional, comfortable, and built to last.

His philosophy is simple: to combine exceptional clinical care, advanced technology, and genuine compassion to create an elevated dental experience where every patient feels valued, heard, and confident in their smile. Whether restoring a single tooth or rebuilding an entire smile, Dr. Goyal believes every treatment plan should be personalized to achieve optimal health, long-term function, and natural aesthetics.

As the visionary behind Veritas Dental Institute, Dr. Goyal is committed to building a destination for comprehensive dentistry where patients can receive world-class care under one roof. By bringing together advanced technology, multidisciplinary expertise, and a patient-first philosophy, he is creating an environment that redefines the dental experience—one focused on trust, innovation, comfort, and exceptional clinical outcomes.

Dr. Goyal believes that exceptional dentistry begins with listening. He takes the time to understand each patient’s concerns, explain treatment options thoroughly, and develop customized solutions that align with their goals and lifestyle. His mission is not only to restore smiles but also to build lasting relationships founded on honesty, integrity, and excellence.

Outside the office, Dr. Goyal enjoys spending time with his wife, Dr. Anshu Goyal, and their two sons. Together, they are committed to serving their community while building a practice where patients feel welcomed, respected, and confident they are receiving the highest standard of dental care
