Your First Visit

During your first visit, you can expect a warm and welcoming environment. Our friendly team will greet you and guide you through the process, ensuring your comfort every step of the way. Here’s what you can expect:

X-Rays & Diagnosis Our office has digital X-ray equipment and state-of-the-art 3D imaging to aid us in our diagnosis. This ensures adequate treatment while keeping radiation exposure as low as possible.

Treatment Depending on your unique needs, we provide same-day treatment if needed. Your comfort is important to us so we will keep guide you through your treatment and keep you at ease during your procedure.

Aftercare After your treatment, we are available to answer any questions or concerns you may have and offer 24/7 emergency contact for any after-hour care.
