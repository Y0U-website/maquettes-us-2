Laminates Veneers

Laminate veneers are a very thin shell that fits to the front of your tooth. They are sometimes referred to as “instant braces” as they are used to change the shape or color of your smile or fill gaps in between teeth. Another benefit of Veneers is that they tend to discolor less than natural teeth to retain the brilliant white smile of your dreams.

Tooth Whitening

If you have stained or yellowed teeth and are looking to enhance your smile, tooth whitening may be for you. Tooth discoloration is a result of many different factors, including genetics, antibiotics, and eating certain foods. In office whitening gives you a brighter white while also protecting your enamel and gums. The strength and protection of in office whitening is extremely effective and can last years with proper maintenance, even managing tooth sensitivity. Call us today to find out more about teeth whitening.

Botox

We are proud to offer a comprehensive solution to help you achieve all your facial esthetic goals in one place. Fine lines and wrinkles in the forehead, “11” area, and around the eyes can be softened and prevented with botox therapy. Botox is injected into the muscles which are usually the underlying cause of wrinkles. This allows the muscles to relax around the injection site, making the skin smooth again and reducing the amount of wrinkles for up to 6 months.
