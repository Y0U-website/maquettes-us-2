Digital X-Rays

Digital X-rays are the new and improved way to replace traditional X-rays in dentistry. They have many benefits including less radiation, a quicker image so you don’t have to hold still as long, and a higher quality image to get all the detail needed to create the best treatment plan for you. Don’t let traditional X-rays slow you down, we use digital X-rays because they’re safer for you and the environment.
