Specialty Dental Services

We offer a range of specialty dental services that cater to the diverse needs of our patients.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are metal posts or frames that are used to attach a crown, denture, or bridge securely in the mouth. Implants anchor dentures and bridges to prevent them from shifting in your mouth, and can also be used when placing a crown for a missing tooth. All of these provide options to restore your perfect smile.

We also offer mini dental implants, a wonderful way to replace missing teeth or secure loose dentures with minimal invasion. Most procedures allow you to eat on the same day with little to no recovery time. Mini dental implants are smaller, longer implant that allows for treatment with minimal bone present. These implants are often less expensive than standard implants and have less recovery as well.

Schedule a consultation to find out more about dental implants.

Botox

Chronic clenching and grinding can be difficult to treat, and may cause everything from muscle soreness to fractured teeth. One exciting way to treat this condition is with botox therapy. With a few injections, the muscles that control clenching and grinding can be relaxed for a several month period, oftentimes leading to relief from symptoms.

Digital Impressions — Itero Element Scanner

The Itero Element Scanner is a powerful tool that enables dentists to capture detailed and accurate impressions of patient’s teeth and gums. This state-of-the-art scanner uses advanced technology to create a three-dimensional digital model of the patient’s mouth, eliminating the need for messy and uncomfortable traditional impressions. The scanner’s accuracy ensures that restorations such as crowns, bridges, and orthodontic appliances fit precisely, leading to better outcomes.
