Q: DOES STROUDSBURG FAMILY DENTAL PLACE DENTAL IMPLANTS?

Dr. Iglio restores implants previously placed by specialists. We do not perform the initial placement of the implant.

Q: WHAT ARE DENTAL IMPLANTS?

Dental implants are the standard of care for tooth replacement. They integrate with the bone to provide support for crowns and dentures.

Q: WHAT WILL HAPPEN WITHOUT TREATMENT?

Your jawbone begins to shrink from lack of stimulation. Losing your teeth will affect your smile and change the shape of your face, causing you to look prematurely aged.

Q: WHO IS A CANDIDATE?

Dental implants can be placed in most adults. Please ask Dr. Iglio to explain the benefits and risks to see if dental implants are right for you.

Q: WHEN WILL I RECEIVE MY NEW TEETH?

Treatment time varies by patient. Typically, you will have some type of temporary teeth until your implants integrate with the bone. Dr. Iglio will provide a time estimate based on your treatment needs.

Q: HOW DO I CARE FOR MY IMPLANTS?

Home care for your implants consists of brushing and flossing. Regular dental visits are required for long-term health and success.

Q: HOW LONG CAN IMPLANTS LAST?

Implants can last many years if cared for properly. Many implants have been in place for more than 40 years.

Q: HOW DOES THE LONG-TERM VALUE OF IMPLANTS COMPARE TO OTHER TREATMENT OPTIONS?

Dental implants are low maintenance and provide stability and confidence in your smile. When a tooth is missing, your body resorbs the bone from the space and uses the minerals from it elsewhere in your body. If your teeth are missing, jawbone shrinkage results in facial collapse. Dental implants prevent jawbone shrinkage and facial collapse. The long-term value of dental implants far exceeds conventional treatment options.

Q: ARE DENTAL IMPLANTS COVERED BY INSURANCE?

Insurance policies vary but most cover a portion of the restorative procedure. Financing options dental implants are often available with competitive interest rates, and payments can be tailored to your budget.
