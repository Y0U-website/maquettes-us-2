DOCTOR BIO

Dr. Sarah Lennan Masterson

Education

Professional Membership/Involvement

Dr. Lennan Masterson currently serves on the Board of Directors for the Michigan Association of Endodontists. Dr. Lennan Masterson is also an active member of the Spear Dental Study Club. She has previously served on the Board of Directors for the Kent County Dental Society, the Editorial Advisory Board for the Michigan Dental Association Journal, the Executive Board for the West Michigan District Dental Society, the Michigan Dental Association House of Delegates, and on the Board of Directors for Opera Grand Rapids.

  • American Association of Endodontists
  • Michigan Association of Endodontists (Treasurer 2019-2022)
  • American Dental Association
  • Michigan Dental Association
  • West Michigan District Dental Society (President 2021-2022)
  • Kent County Dental Society (President 2019-2020)
  • Fellowship in the American College of Dentists

Additional

In her spare time, Dr. Lennan Masterson enjoys spending time with her husband and their son Chase and their daughter Landon. Her hobbies include cooking, gardening, and playing the piano.
