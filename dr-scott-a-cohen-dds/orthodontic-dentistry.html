Orthodontic Dentistry

Some people have naturally straight teeth that fit together. But if you have problems with your bite or the spacing of your teeth, you may need orthodontic care. Orthodontia is the branch of dentistry that deals with abnormalities of the teeth and jaw. We offer Invisalign orthodontic treatment.

Here at Cohen Dental we specialize and take pride in sharing the Invisalign experience. Giving you the smile of your dreams! First we will start out with a consultation. Dr Cohen will examine your teeth and show you what Invisalign can do for you. We will map out a custom treatment plan, you’ll even be able to preview your smile. Invisalign clear aligners are designed to transform your smile up to 2x faster than traditional orthodontic care. Each aligner throughout your treatment gradually and predictably moves your teeth as you continue to live your life the way you want.

Your life is moving. Your smile should keep up too!
