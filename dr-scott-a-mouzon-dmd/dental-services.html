RESTORATIVE DENTISTRY

If there are problems identified during a professional exam that need restorative treatment, your dentist will review all of the possible courses of action. Dr. Mouzon will work to ensure that the treatment will be the best option for your individual circumstances so as to ensure that it is the most effective as well as most comfortable.

Dental Fillings: Fillings are used to restore the structural integrity of your teeth, and there are four main options of fillings to choose from; gold, amalgam, composite resin, and porcelain. Each type has its own advantages and disadvantages so your dentist will work with you to identify which is best for you.

Dental Implant Restorations: Dental implants are a permanent solution to replacing missing teeth. They are implanted into the bone socket of the missing tooth and can last for many years when properly taken care of.

Crown (Caps): Sometimes referred to as a cap, a Crown is a small covering for a damaged tooth to ensure that it is not further damaged. They can also be used for cosmetic reasons such as covering misshaped or discolored teeth.

Dentures & Partial Dentures: Dentures are used to replace missing teeth or teeth that otherwise cannot be restored and must be removed. Partial dentures are used when not all of the teeth are missing. They ensure that the remaining teeth do not shift.

Fixed Bridges: Fixed Bridges are another option for replacing missing teeth. They are affixed to anchoring teeth which surround the missing space and are not removable.

Root Canal Therapy: Root canal therapy is used to restore a tooth suffering from decay or infection of its nerve to its original working function. It is achieved by removing any decay as well as the living tissue inside the tooth, nerves, and bacteria and filling the remaining cavity with special dental materials. This procedure is not performed in house, but we can refer you to a specialist, an Endodontist.

CHILDREN

It is important to start a healthy relationship between your child and his/her dentist at a young age. Children’s teeth pose special problems in terms of dental hygiene. It is more difficult to ensure that children’s teeth are properly cleaned. Children are also known to enjoy sugary foods which lead to tooth decay. Although their baby teeth are going to be replaced by permanent teeth once they are older, it is important to maintain healthy baby teeth as they are a foundation for children’s health and development.

At Mouzon Family Dentistry, we work to ensure not only that your children’s teeth are taken care of, but also that your child is comfortable. It is important that your child is comfortable during their routine exams and cleanings, so that they remain a routine for them throughout their life, guaranteeing their dental health is well taken care of.

TEENS

Adolescence is a time of much change in a person’s life, and that doesn’t stop at dental care either. It is when children begin to truly take care of their dental health on their own something they may not be the most enthusiastic about. It is also when they are most likely to begin their journey with braces. These two factors attribute to adolescence having the highest rate of cavity formation for people. It is imperative that teenagers receive the best oral care possible during these years to ensure there is no lasting damage to their oral health.

Increased hormones during teenage years also put young adults at a higher risk for gum disease. Their fluctuation in hormone levels change the bacterial makeup and blood circulation throughout their gums, which when combined with inadequate oral hygiene can lead to gingivitis and ultimately periodontal disease.

Adolescence is also the most common time for people to start noticing movement in their wisdom teeth, which can create issues within their mouth if there is not enough room to support these extra teeth. Therefore, it is important that wisdom teeth be monitored closely through the use of x-rays to make sure removal of these teeth is done before they are able to create any issues.
