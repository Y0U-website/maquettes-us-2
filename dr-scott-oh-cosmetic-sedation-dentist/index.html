The practice

0 Meet Dr. Scott Oh Dr. Scott Oh is a cosmetic and restorative dentist in Mountain View, California, known for blending artistry and modern dental science to create healthy, radiant smiles that inspire confidence and promote lifelong oral health. His practice philosophy combines aesthetic design, digital precision, and personalized care to deliver natural, high-end results for every patient.

Through comprehensive treatment planning and the use of the finest materials and advanced technology, Dr. Oh collaborates with a team of Master Ceramists to craft custom smiles that achieve facial harmony, balance, and lasting beauty. His focus on detail, function, and aesthetics ensures each patient receives care that reflects the highest standards of modern cosmetic dentistry.

Known for his welcoming demeanor and compassionate approach, Dr. Oh fosters genuine connections and creates a comfortable, anxiety-free experience. He recognizes that every patient is unique and takes pride in offering tailored, concierge-level care that builds trust and transforms smiles.

A native of Orange County, California, Dr. Oh earned his B.S. in Human Biology from UCLA, where he conducted research in the Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Department and volunteered with Operation Smile, helping deliver care to underserved communities. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry in San Francisco.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Oh enjoys skiing, mountain biking, surfing, and camping. Passionate about culture and cuisine, he finds inspiration through travel and new experiences that reflect his commitment to artistry, balance, and lifelong learning.
