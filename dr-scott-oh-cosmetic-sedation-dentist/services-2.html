Dental Implants

Dental implants are permanent tooth replacements that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. Made from biocompatible titanium with a custom porcelain crown, they restore smile aesthetics, bite strength, and gingival health.

With digital planning and precision placement, Dr. Scott Oh creates natural, long-lasting results for patients seeking a high-quality, confident smile.

IV sedation

Sedation dentistry helps patients relax and feel comfortable during dental treatment. Using safe, moderate conscious sedation, Dr. Scott Oh provides a calm, anxiety-free experience for those with dental fear or receiving complex dental care.

This approach ensures patients can undergo advanced cosmetic and implant procedures with effortless comfort and confidence, reflecting the same precision and luxury that define Dr. Oh’s smile transformations

Same Day Crowns

Dr. Scott Oh offers same-day CEREC crowns designed with digital precision and crafted from high-strength ceramic for natural, lasting results.

Using advanced CAD/CAM technology, your crown is scanned, designed, and milled in a single visit—eliminating the need for temporary restorations or multiple appointments. Each restoration is custom-fit for comfort, function, and aesthetics.
