The practice

Dr. Scott takes pride in providing highly personalized service, assessing each child’s situation individually and discussing every treatment option with parents himself, including general anesthesia. No question is too small.

He went on to complete his pediatric dental residency at Children’s Medical Center of Connecticut and the University of Connecticut satellite dental clinics, where he treated patients across the full range of pediatric dental needs and complexities.

Dr. Scott works with a highly trained, experienced, and caring team at Gold River Pediatric Dentistry. As soon as you walk through our doors, our friendly team will welcome you, assist you, and help put you and your child’s minds at ease. We focus on creating a warm, comfortable, and relaxing atmosphere for every visit.

Pediatric dentistry is a recognized specialty that requires two to three years of additional training after dental school. Board certification is an additional voluntary credential. Dr. Scott has dedicated his career exclusively to children’s oral health, including infants, toddlers, school-age kids, teens, and patients with medical complexities or special needs.
