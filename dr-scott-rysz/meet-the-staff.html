Meet The STAFF

Elizabeth graduated from Malcolm X College as a dental hygienist in 2020 and was previously a dental assistant since 2016. Elizabeth takes pride in patient education and offering the same level of care and comfort that she looks for as a patient herself. When away from the office, she enjoys spending time with her family and being outdoors.

Mariana assists Dr. Rysz and the hygienists by providing a more efficient and comfortable dental experience. She prepares the treatment rooms, sterilizes equipment and instruments, passes instruments and materials to the dentist, and assists in the taking of dental radiographs always in compliance with the Dental Practice Act. Mariana is fluent in Spanish.

Erika has been a patient coordinator in the dental field since 2017. She genuinely enjoys meeting new patients and helping them plan for all their dental needs. Her forte is navigating dental insurance companies and making it a smooth process for our patients. When away from the office, she enjoys spending time with her family and friends and loves traveling.
