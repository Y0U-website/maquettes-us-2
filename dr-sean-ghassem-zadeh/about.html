Our Community Outreach Partnerships

Our philosophy of care goes beyond the office walls. We believe it’s our responsibility to care for our young patients both in and out of the operating chair, and one of the best ways to do this is through our community outreach programs. We regularly support a variety of local organizations, including:

Our rockstar Community Education Team also frequently visits local daycares and preschools to teach Southcoast tots how to care for their teeth in a playful, engaging way. Our interactive program introduces dental hygiene basics and helps kids feel comfortable with what to expect during a visit to the dentist.
