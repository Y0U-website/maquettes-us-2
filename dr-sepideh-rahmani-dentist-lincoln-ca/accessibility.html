Accessibility Statement

Our ongoing effort to improve the online experience for all visitors

Overview

Lincoln Smiles Dental values its patients and website visitors, including those with disabilities, and aims to make its website (the "Site") a more welcoming place to explore information about our practice and services. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we work to improve the Site from time to time as resources allow and as technology evolves.

Approach

Ongoing Efforts

Third-Party Content

The Site may incorporate content, tools, or services provided by third parties, including without limitation mapping services, appointment request forms, payment or financing portals, video players, social media widgets, and review platforms. The accessibility of such third-party content is determined by the respective providers and is not within our direct control. We encourage visitors who encounter difficulty with any third-party element to contact us using the information below so that we can try to help obtain the information or service by alternative means.

Feedback and Alternative Access

We welcome feedback from visitors regarding the Site. If you experience difficulty accessing any content, need information in an alternative format, or wish to share suggestions for improvement, please reach out using the contact information below. We will make reasonable efforts to review feedback and, where practical, to help provide the requested information through alternative means such as by phone, email, or in person at our office.
