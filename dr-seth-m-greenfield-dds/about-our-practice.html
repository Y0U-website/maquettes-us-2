We welcome new patients to make their first appointment with our wonderful hygiene department. If you have a specific concern, prospective patients can easily schedule a consultation/limited examination with one of the doctors first.

At this first visit a comprehensive medical history will be taken in order to maximize our understanding of your overall health and how it relates to your oral health. Because we understand dental treatment is a personal process we welcome our patients to discuss any concerns or reservations about dental care.
