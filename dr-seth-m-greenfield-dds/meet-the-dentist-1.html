Dr. Seth M. Greenfield FAGD

Dr. Greenfield earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in 1999. He completed a one-year general practice residency at The Veterans Administration Hospital in Cleveland, Ohio. During his training there, he focused on comprehensive care, treating patients who were medically compromised.

Dr. Greenfield received his Fellowship in The Academy of General Dentistry in 2006. The Fellowship "symbolizes a dentist’s commitment to the professional responsibility incurred upon entering the dental profession, that is, to maintain knowledge and skills at the highest possible level, and to continue the education process uninterrupted." (agd.org)

As well as being a certified Invisalign provider, Dr. Greenfield’s continual educational experiences has sent him to Seattle to partake in a one-week didactic and clinical program at the Kois Center. The Kois Center’s mission statement: "Enabling motivated dentists to achieve extraordinary results, expanding knowledge and application skills in restorative dentistry."
