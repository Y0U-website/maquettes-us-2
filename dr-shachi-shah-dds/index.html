The practice

Dr. Shah continues to grow academically, providing the best in the field of dentistry. She continues to participate in numerous continuing dental education courses to bring you the most up to date in treatment planning and delivery of specialized care. Dr. Shah is an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Dental Association and Florida Dental Association.

Dr. Shah has studied dentistry at the Government Dental College and Hospital, Ahmedabad, India which is in one of the Asia s largest medical campuses. After relocating to the USA, she joined the Advanced Standing program at University of Texas School of Dentistry at Houston. She lives in Lake Mary with her husband and daughter. She enjoys cooking, travelling and dancing in her free time.

No matter where you are on your journey towards dental health and a beautiful smile, Dr. Shah will enthusiastically guide you through the process because in modern dentistry it is never too late to improve your smile!
