Dental Implants in New City, NY

Our team at R ockland Dental Specialists provides a full range of dental services designed to restore both function and confidence in your smile. Dental implants in New City are the most successful and predictable way to replace missing teeth in both the upper and lower jaw. The implant itself is a small titanium fixture with tiny threads that your surgeon places into the jawbone as an anchor. Simply put, dental implants are root replacements.

The Dental Implant Procedure: What to Expect

Before beginning any implant procedure, our periodontists conduct a thorough diagnosis and review of your medical history to confirm you are a healthy candidate for dental implants. Because successful implant outcomes depend heavily on the health of the surrounding bone and gum tissue, this evaluation goes beyond a standard dental checkup. The procedure typically involves several stages.

Initial Consultation and Planning

During your first visit, our dental implant team will evaluate factors that may affect your treatment, including bone density, overall health, and any previous injury to the mouth area. We use advanced 3D imaging and cone beam CT scans to plan placement with precision, allowing us to reduce treatment time and optimize outcomes before the surgical appointment.

Implant Placement

The surgical procedure can often be completed in a single appointment, with sedation options available for comfort. Dr. David Peto and our periodontists precisely place the titanium implant post into the jawbone, where it will serve as the new artificial root. In select cases, immediate implant replacement at the time of extraction may be appropriate, reducing overall treatment time.

Healing and Osseointegration

After placement, the implant undergoes osseointegration — the biological process in which the jawbone grows around and bonds to the titanium surface. This typically takes several months and is monitored through follow-up appointments and X-rays. Most patients experience minimal discomfort during this phase, and any sutures dissolve or are removed within 7 to 10 days.

Final Restoration

Once osseointegration is confirmed, an abutment is attached to the implant and your final crown is fabricated and placed, completing the restoration. The crown is matched in shape, size, and color to blend naturally with your remaining teeth.

How Do Dental Implants Replace Missing Teeth?

Dental implants replace missing teeth by anchoring a small titanium post directly into the jawbone, which then supports a prosthetic crown, bridge, or denture. Because the implant fuses with the bone through osseointegration, it functions as an artificial tooth root — providing the stability and bone stimulation that no other tooth replacement option can replicate. When a tooth is removed without replacement, the surrounding jawbone begins to resorb, which can affect the structure of the jaw and the health of neighboring teeth over time.

Implants are also highly versatile. Their most common use is replacing a single missing tooth, but they can support fixed bridges that replace multiple adjacent teeth, anchor implant-supported dentures for patients missing several teeth, or serve as the foundation for full mouth dental implants .

Why Choose Dental Implants Over Other Tooth Replacements?

Dental implants outperform traditional tooth replacements across every meaningful measure — long-term durability, natural appearance, ease of cleaning, restored bite force, and overall quality of life. For patients in Rockland County weighing their options, the differences are clinically significant.

High Long-Term Survival Rate

According to a 20-year meta-analysis published in Clinical Oral Investigations , dental implants demonstrate long-term survival rates that place them among the most reliable tooth replacement options available. Unlike bridges and partials, whose success depends on the health of the supporting teeth, an implant stands independently. A cavity on a tooth beneath a bridge can compromise the entire restoration. Dental implants cannot develop cavities.

Implants can be affected by in the form of peri-implantitis — a bacterial infection of the tissue surrounding the implant — but complications are uncommon when patients maintain proper oral hygiene. They can also develop periodontal disease , but complications are uncommon when patients maintain proper oral hygiene and attend regular periodontal maintenance visits. Because our team consists of periodontists, we are specifically trained to monitor and treat peri-implantitis, protecting your implant investment for the long term. s failure.

Most Natural Appearance

Implant crowns emerge from the gum tissue just as natural teeth do, with no connection to adjacent teeth. Our periodontists use the implant to precisely shape the gum contour, and the final crown is matched in shape, size, and color so it blends seamlessly into your smile. Unlike bridges, which span neighboring teeth, or dentures that sit above the gumline, implant crowns look as natural as possible without altering the structure of surrounding teeth in any way.

Easiest to Clean

Because an implant crown is freestanding and not attached to neighboring teeth, cleaning it is as straightforward as cleaning a natural tooth. Patients can brush and floss normally without special tools or threading devices. Many patients report that their implant feels indistinguishable from their natural teeth once the restoration is complete.

Restored Chewing Force

Missing teeth reduce your ability to chew certain foods effectively, which can affect nutrition and quality of life over time. Because dental implants are anchored directly into the jawbone, the biting force they recreate is comparable to that of a natural tooth. Patients can return to a full diet without the restrictions that come with removable partials or bridges that place stress on surrounding teeth.

Protects Remaining Teeth and Bone

Bridges and partials place elevated chewing forces on the teeth that support them, increasing the risk of cracked teeth, gum recession , and bone loss.

Because bone loss accelerates when a tooth root is absent, early implant placement — or LARiP ridge preservation at the time of extraction — is the most effective way to protect the jawbone and keep future restorative options open.

Candidacy for Dental Implants in New City

Most adults who are missing one or more teeth are candidates for dental implants, though several factors influence whether placement can proceed directly or requires preparatory treatment first. Good candidates generally have adequate jawbone volume to support the implant, healthy gum tissue, and no untreated periodontal disease. Patients with controlled systemic conditions such as diabetes can often receive implants successfully with appropriate management.

Implant Financing and Insurance in Rockland County

Dental insurance coverage for implants varies widely by plan. Many traditional dental plans offer limited or no coverage for the implant fixture itself, though they may cover associated procedures such as extractions, bone grafting, or the final crown. Our team provides complimentary insurance verifications and will give you a clear picture of your estimated out-of-pocket costs during your consultation.
