SPECIALTIES & CREDENTIALS Board Certified Periodontist and Implantologist

Before beginning any implant procedure, our periodontists conduct a thorough diagnosis and review of your medical history to confirm you are a healthy candidate for dental implants. Because successful implant outcomes depend heavily on the health of the surrounding bone and gum tissue, this evaluation goes beyond a standard dental checkup. The procedure typically involves several stages.

During your first visit, our dental implant team will evaluate factors that may affect your treatment, including bone density, overall health, and any previous injury to the mouth area. We use advanced 3D imaging and cone beam CT scans to plan placement with precision, allowing us to reduce treatment time and optimize outcomes before the surgical appointment.

The surgical procedure can often be completed in a single appointment, with sedation options available for comfort. Dr. David Peto and our periodontists precisely place the titanium implant post into the jawbone, where it will serve as the new artificial root. In select cases, immediate implant replacement at the time of extraction may be appropriate, reducing overall treatment time.

After placement, the implant undergoes osseointegration — the biological process in which the jawbone grows around and bonds to the titanium surface. This typically takes several months and is monitored through follow-up appointments and X-rays. Most patients experience minimal discomfort during this phase, and any sutures dissolve or are removed within 7 to 10 days.
