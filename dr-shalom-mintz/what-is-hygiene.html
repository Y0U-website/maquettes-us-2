Endodontics (Root Canal Therapy)

Treatment for infected or damaged tooth pulp that saves natural teeth from extraction. Root canal procedures remove infection, relieve pain, and preserve tooth structure using advanced techniques and microscopic precision. Additional Specialized Services

ORAL SURGERY

Sleep Apnea Treatment

Oral appliance therapy for obstructive sleep apnea as an alternative to CPAP machines. Custom-fitted devices position your jaw to keep airways open during sleep, reducing snoring and improving sleep quality.

Myofunctional Therapy

Exercises and techniques to improve oral and facial muscle function, addressing issues like mouth breathing, tongue thrust, and sleep-disordered breathing . Myofunctional therapy promotes proper tongue posture and breathing patterns.
