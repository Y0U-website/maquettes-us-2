Shane Klingonsmith, DMD

Dr. Shane Klingonsmith attended the University of Pennsylvania’s School of Dental Medicine . As a student, he was honored to be selected as one of a handful of his class to practice dentistry in the Penn Dental Faculty Practice, alongside UPENN’s prestigious faculty of specialists.

Again, after graduation, Dr. Shane was honored to stay at UPENN for a residency in Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD), which ultimately led to a job offer as a faculty dentist. Dr. Klingonsmith turned down this offer of full time employment to enter private practice.

Since 2003, Dr. Klingonsmith has continued his passion for clinical expertise, completing advanced coursework in cosmetic dentistry, oral surgery, pedodontics, periodontics, sedation, and public health. His commitment to lifelong education means he is constantly evaluating emerging dental methods and technologies in order to provide his patients the highest level of comfort and care.

He is also profoundly loves his wife and 6 beautiful children, who all love going to the dentist.
