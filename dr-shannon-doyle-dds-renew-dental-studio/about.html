ABOUT US

Our Philosophy

At Renew Dental Studio, Dr. Doyle’s belief is that modern dentistry should feel safe , personalized , and relaxing . From your first interaction to treatment, she focuses on building trust, using the latest gentle technologies, and ensuring every patient feels informed and cared for.

MEET DR. SHANNON DOYLE

OWNER & DENTIST

Background

Dr. Shannon Doyle earned her DDS from Louisiana State University School of Dentistry in 2009 and has been dedicated to patient-centered care for over 16 years with a focus on cosmetics and comfort . As a member of the American Dental Association, American Academy of Clear Aligners, Louisiana Dental Association, and a Spear Study Club, Dr. Doyle continually hones her skills through advanced education in areas like Fastmill.io same-day crowns, Invisalign, and the iTero intraoral scanner.

A Little More About Her

As a mother of three, Dr. Doyle brings a gentle, nurturing approach, especially when caring for young patients. She serves the broader North Shore community (Covington, Mandeville, Madisonville, Abita, and beyond). Outside the office, she volunteers with Special Olympics Louisiana, where she helps organize sporting events for special needs children. She also enjoys playing tennis for Pelican Athletic Club, living an active lifestyle, and spending time with her family and friends.

MEET DANIELLE LOMBARDO

DENTAL HYGIENIST

Background

Danielle Lombardo is a proud graduate of the LSU School of Dentistry , Class of 2015, where she earned her Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene. With over 10 years of experience including 3 years in Lake Charles and 7 on the Northshore Danielle is known for her gentle approach, strong clinical skills, and commitment to patient comfort. She is licensed to administer nitrous oxide and local anesthesia, and has a special passion for caring for patients of all ages, including those with dental anxiety or special needs.

A Little More About Her

A Northshore native and Mandeville High alum, Danielle has been married for 9 years and is a proud mom to three children two sons and a daughter. Outside of work, she stays busy cheering them on at football, baseball, and dance, volunteering at their school, and spending time in the kitchen. Danielle is passionate about creating a warm and welcoming environment for patients and looks forward to helping every smile shine a little brighter.

MEET MIKAILA FALGOUT

OFFICE MANAGER

Background

Mikaila Daigle Falgout began her career in dentistry in 2017 and quickly fell in love with helping patients feel confident in their smiles. After completing dental assisting school, she went on to earn her EDDA (Expanded Duty Dental Assistant) certification from LSU , expanding her skills and clinical knowledge. With over 7 years of experience in the field, Mikaila brings both technical expertise and a warm, welcoming presence to her role as Office Manager. Her top priority is helping patients achieve their oral health goals while ensuring every visit feels smooth, comfortable, and positive.

A Little More About Her

Mikaila is newly married and the proud mom of two boys, who keep her busy on the sidelines of football and baseball fields. She’s passionate about health and fitness, and loves supporting others on their wellness journeys. Outside the office, you’ll likely find her at the gym, meal prepping, or enjoying quality time with her family.

MEET JESSICA VERRETTE

DENTAL ASSISTANT

Background

Jessica Verrette earned her degree in Dental Assisting and X-ray Certification from Bryman College in 2002. With over 23 years of experience in the dental field, she brings a wealth of knowledge and warmth to the Renew Dental Studio team. Jessica is CPR certified , bilingual , and known for her caring nature and lighthearted sense of humor, which help put patients at ease.

A Little More About Her

Born and raised on the Westbank, Jessica now lives in Covington with her husband and their beloved Chihuahua. She’s passionate about animals, especially dogs, and finds joy in cooking, traveling, and organizing. Weekends often include football games, backyard relaxation surrounded by wildlife, and spending quality time with family. For Jessica, dentistry isn’t just a job; it’s a way to make people smile and create a positive, comforting experience for every patient she meets.
