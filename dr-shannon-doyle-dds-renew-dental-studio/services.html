RESTORATIVE DENTISTRY

Restorative dentistry helps repair or replace damaged and missing teeth so you can eat, speak, and smile with confidence. We use advanced materials and techniques to ensure your results are both functional and natural-looking.

  • Fillings Tooth-colored material used to treat cavities and restore strength.
  • Crowns and Bridges Custom restorations that protect weakened teeth or replace missing ones.
  • Root Canals A gentle procedure to remove infection and save your natural tooth.
  • Tooth Extractions Safe, comfortable removal of teeth when necessary.
  • Implant Restorations Restore your smile with crowns placed on top of dental implants.
  • Bone Grafting Strengthens the jawbone in preparation for implants or after tooth loss.

COSMETIC DENTISTRY

Cosmetic dentistry enhances the look of your smile , whether you re seeking subtle refinements or a full transformation. We tailor every treatment to your goals, helping you achieve results that look and feel like the best version of you. ​

  • Invisalign Clear aligners that straighten teeth discreetly and comfortably.
  • Teeth Whitening Brighten your smile with safe, effective professional whitening options.
  • Veneers Thin porcelain shells that cover imperfections like chips, stains, or gaps.
  • Full Mouth Reconstruction (Smile Makeovers!) A custom combination of treatments to restore your bite, function and smile.
  • Cosmetic Bonding A quick, affordable way to fix small chips, gaps, or discoloration using tooth-colored resin.
