TECHNOLOGY

INTRAORAL SCANNER

High-Precision Scanning, Early Detection Our intraoral scanner captures a precise 3D model of your teeth and bite. This allows us to track tooth movement, erosion, and gum recession over time, helping us detect changes early and treat conditions before they progress.

DIGITAL X-RAYS

Clear Imaging with Minimal Radiation Our digital X-rays provide detailed images instantly while using up to 90 % less radiation than traditional X-rays. This helps us detect cavities, bone loss, and other issues early so we can treat them before they become bigger problems.

3D IMAGING / CBCT SCANNER

A Complete Picture of Your Oral Health For more complex procedures like implants or root canals, we use 3D cone beam imaging (CBCT) to capture incredibly detailed views of your teeth, nerves, bone, and sinuses. This helps us plan your care with precision and gives you better results.

CAD/CAM DENTISTRY

Same-Day Crowns with Advanced Design & Precision Using CAD/CAM (Computer-Aided Design and Manufacturing) technology, we can design and fabricate dental restorations like crowns right here in our office. This means fewer appointments, no temporary crowns, and a beautifully precise fit often in just one visit.

DIGITAL IMPRESSIONS

Fast, Accurate Scans for a Better Fit (No Goopy Trays!) Using digital scanning technology, we can create highly accurate models of your teeth without uncomfortable impression material. Whether you re getting crowns, bridges, or Invisalign, this means a better fit and faster turnaround.

DIGITAL FORMS & RECORDS

A Streamlined Experience From Start to Finish

We use secure digital systems to manage patient records and forms. This makes check-ins, scheduling, and insurance processing more efficient and eco-friendly.
