Dr. Nhung Quan Board Certified Orthodontist

Dr. Nhung was born and raised in Rockland County, NY. She knew she wanted to become an orthodontist while working for an orthodontist during her senior year of high school. She received her bachelor’s degree in Economics from NYU and completed her dental and orthodontic education at NYU. Dr. Nhung has earned the additional distinction of being board certified by the American Board of Orthodontics.

Dr. Nhung believes in patient-centered care. Developing trust and rapport is essential to delivering the best outcomes for her patients. She is gentle, kind and never hesitates to answer any questions. She understands that communication is vital for great results.

She currently lives on the Upper East Side with her partner George and her puppy Luna. In her free time, Dr. Nhung enjoys spending time at the beach, hiking, fishing on Long Island, and going to Central Park with her puppy Luna.

She is an active member of the American Board of Orthodontists, American Association of Orthodontists, Northeastern Society of Orthodontists, and New York State Dental Association.

Dr. Shruti Patel Pediatric Dentist

As a child, Dr. Shruti Patel could spend hours with a box of crayons and paper. After shadowing her uncle, she realized that dentistry was the perfect way to combine her passions for art, science, and helping others. Today, she has traded crayons for a mirror and explorer, using her creativity and compassion to help children build healthy smiles.

She is dedicated to making every visit a positive and comfortable experience, helping children develop healthy habits that last a lifetime. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Shruti enjoys baking, painting, and spending time with her family and friends.

Dr. Moones Rajabi Board Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Moones Rajabi is a specialist in Pediatric Dentistry. She completed her degree as a Doctor of Dental Medicine through Temple Dental School in Philadelphia, PA.

Following dental school, she completed a General Practice Residency at Bronxcare Hospital. Soon after that, she completed one year of fellowship in Pediatric Dentistry at Montefiore Hospital in the Bronx and discovered her love for treating children. She decided to pursue a specialty in Pediatric Dentistry at the Saint Joseph’s University Medical Center in NJ.

Dr. Moones strongly believes in providing the best care possible in a warm, caring, and child friendly environment. Making a child feel at ease and comfortable is Dr. Moones’s top priority. She strives to provide dental education for both the child and their families on preventative dentistry and oral hygiene.

Dr. Moones resides in Bergen county with her family. She loves discovering new restaurants, going to classical concerts, and traveling with her family! She is a fan of Italian and Persian food and enjoys cooking them.
