We’re Part of the Community

It’s easy to see that we love Papillion and being your neighbor! Not only do we want to help you smile with confidence, but we recognize the importance of being involved in the community we call home!

During Children’s Dental Health Month, Dr. Powell spends time in area schools teaching kids the importance of taking care of their teeth. Being trained as both a Pediatric Dentist and Orthodontist, he loves getting children excited about dental health!

We are partnering with the Papio Pool and Storm Chasers Select Baseball. The entire Papillion Orthodontics family will also be participating in the annual Papillion Days Festival Market in the Park and Parade where we look forward to meeting you!

Check our Facebook page and website for updates on community involvement!
