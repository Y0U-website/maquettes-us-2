How Invisalign Works

Versatile in its ability to address mild to moderate cases of overcrowding, gapped teeth, and misaligned bites, Invisalign uses clear, plastic aligners that are custom-made for your smile as it progresses through treatment.

It is necessary to wear your trays 20-22 hours a day so that your teeth will continue to receive gentle pressure, keeping them moving in the right direction. After two weeks, you will be required to switch to a new set in the series, as your smile will be at the next stage of treatment.

Every six weeks or so, you will undergo a follow-up appointment with Dr. Alex or another member of our team to ensure that your teeth are moving at an appropriate pace.

Who Can Invisalign Help?

Good news: Invisalign isn’t just for patients with mild crowding and spacing issues. In fact, clear aligners have proven to be effective at correcting several common orthodontic problems over the last two decades, including overbites. So, if you want to straighten your teeth and align your bite without the metal, don’t hesitate to schedule a consultation at our Wayne dental office!

Crowded Teeth

Do your teeth crowd together to the point that they overlap? Unfortunately, the unique crevices between your teeth can easily harbor food particles, plaque, and bacteria, making it harder to maintain a healthy smile. Fortunately, traditional braces are no longer the only option. If we determine that you’re a good candidate for Invisalign during your visit, then we can align your teeth without any metal.

Gaps Between Teeth

As the years go on, are you less and less fond of the gap between your two front teeth? If so, don’t hesitate to get in touch with our team at Main Line Dental Group! Once we know more about your unique dental history, needs, and goals, we can determine if Invisalign is right for you. If the answer is yes, then we can gradually guide your teeth closer together with clear, barely noticeable aligners.

Overbite

When you bite down, do your upper teeth extend beyond your lower ones significantly? This bite problem, known as an overbite, can make it difficult to chew properly and enunciate clearly. For those reasons, we recommend exploring your treatment options, like Invisalign. If we determine that you’re a good candidate, then we can use a custom series of aligners to straighten your teeth and align your bite.

Underbite

Underbites are often quite noticeable. However, that isn’t the only reason we recommend scheduling an Invisalign consultation with us. Even relatively mild cases can lead to serious issues, including chronic jaw pain. So, it’s best to have it treated sooner rather than later. If we decide together that Invisalign is the ideal solution, then your orthodontic treatment will be virtually undetectable.

Crossbite

When you bite down, do only a few of your upper teeth sit behind your lower ones? In that case, you likely have a crossbite, not an underbite. Even so, it’s best to get the orthodontic treatment that you need before you experience premature wear, chronic jaw pain, and the like. Luckily, many patients with mild to moderate crossbites are candidates for Invisalign!

Open Bite

If you suspect that you have an open bite (when the upper and lower teeth don’t meet evenly when biting down), then schedule an appointment at our office! Once we’ve completed a comprehensive oral exam, we can determine if that’s the case and, if it is, explore your treatment options . Many patients end up choosing Invisalign because the aligners are clear, comfortable, and convenient.

Benefits of Invisalign

Invisalign is one of the most popular orthodontic treatment options, and for good reason! It is designed to fit into even the busiest modern lifestyles. During your consultation, we can answer your questions about the specific benefits that you can expect to enjoy during your treatment period. In the meantime, here is a general overview of some of the advantages provided by this remarkable orthodontic method:

Comfortable Treatment

Whereas traditional braces feature metal brackets and wires that can irritate soft oral tissues, Invisalign is different. Its aligners are made of smooth plastic that rests comfortably against your gums. Plus, because you have to change your aligners more frequently than you would get braces adjusted, you can expect less soreness when your teeth are moving.

Discreet Aligners

You deserve to smile with confidence throughout your orthodontic treatment! Invisalign aligners are clear and do not draw a lot of attention to themselves. They are unlikely to be the first thing that others notice about your appearance, so you can interact in social situations while maintaining a professional, adult image.

Easier Oral Hygiene

No one wants to navigate around brackets and wires while they are brushing and flossing their teeth. Thankfully, Invisalign makes it easier to maintain your oral hygiene. All you have to do is remove your aligners, brush and floss as normal, clean your aligners, and then resume your treatment. It is that simple!

Quick & Easy Follow-Ups

Invisalign check-in appointments usually happen every 6 8 weeks or so. They tend to be quite brief, often lasting just 15 30 minutes. During these visits, we simply check on your progress, answer any questions you may have, and provide you with the next set of aligners in your series. If we ever determine that you require refinements (adjustments to your treatment plan), you may need to attend a longer appointment.

Freedom from Dietary Restrictions

Traditional braces come with a list of foods that are off-limits. With Invisalign, though, you have to remove your aligners at meals. That means no foods are restricted! Simply eat what you want (even if it is a food that is not recommended for regular braces), take care of your oral hygiene, and then start wearing your aligners again.

Treatment That Fits Your Lifestyle

Invisalign is designed to fit in with a busy and fast-paced lifestyle. The removable nature of the aligners means that you can take a short break from your treatment for special occasions, such as photoshoots. Plus, it has a shorter average treatment timeline than regular braces, so you can expect to start seeing results in a relatively short period of time.

Living with Invisalign Aligners

Getting orthodontic treatment doesn’t need to mean a mouthful of metal brackets and wires. With Invisalign, teens and adults can work toward a straighter smile without taking away from their appearance in the meantime. Here is what you can expect life to look like when you have Invisalign clear aligners.

Wearing Your Trays

Having removable aligners is beneficial when it comes to eating and cleaning your teeth. However, it also means that you need to be highly disciplined about putting them back into your mouth in a timely manner. This way, your treatment will stay on track. You should try to keep your aligners in for at least 20 to 22 hours each day so that your teeth continue to shift as anticipated. You should only remove them to eat, drink, and clean your teeth and trays.

Cleaning Your Aligners

When you get Invisalign, you don’t need to worry about getting any special product to keep your trays in good condition. All you need is a soft-bristled toothbrush and clean water. Just use the toothbrush to gently brush each surface. If your aligners seem stained or you just want some extra freshness, use your Invisalign cleaning crystals.

Eating & Drinking

Your trays need to be removed when you eat or drink anything that isn’t water. When your trays are removed, you don’t need to stick to any food rules or restrictions. That being said, getting cavities during your Invisalign treatment can slow down your progress, so be mindful of a number of sugary foods and beverages you are consuming and drink plenty of water to stay hydrated.

Losing or Damaging a Tray

When you aren’t wearing your aligners, you should keep them in their case or a safe place to minimize your probability of losing them. If one does become lost, let us know right away so we can tell you what to do next. The same goes for if one of them becomes damaged. You shouldn’t try to repair it on your own. Instead, give our office a call so we can help you out.

Routine Check-Ins

In addition to getting new aligners during your checkups, we will also examine your smile to ensure that everything looks good. Teeth don’t always move exactly how we expect him to, so adjustments might need to be made. This is also an excellent time to ask any questions you have about your treatment.

Factors That Affect the Cost of Invisalign

During your consultation, we’ll perform a close oral exam. This effort lets us confirm (among other things) the factors that affect your treatment cost, such as:

  • Complexity of Care Invisalign gets pricier as the required tooth movements become more complex.
  • Number of Arches Invisalign doesn’t need to treat both of your smile’s arches. In some cases, it can just treat one for a lower price.
  • Treatment Timeline As your time with Invisalign increases, so will your treatment’s price.
  • Patient Compliance If you neglect Invisalign’s rules and best practices, you’ll face delayed treatment results. These will force you to pay a higher price than you otherwise would.

Invisalign vs. Mail-In Aligners: Which Costs More?

The price of Invisalign ranges from $3,000 to 8,000, so treatment costs more upfront than mail-in aligners. Even so, it’s the better financial choice.

Today’s mail-in aligners are a DIY effort, lacking input from professional dentists. That means using them can lead to harsh oral issues poorly-made impressions, bad aligner habits, and so on. Such problems tend to demand professional help, which defeats the whole point of mail-in aligners.

On the other hand, a pro dentist oversees Invisalign from start to finish. Our team assesses the entire process to ensure your treatment avoids obstacles and stays on track. Thanks to this oversight, Invisalign will give you high-grade impressions, successful care everything that justifies the aligners’ price.

Does Dental Insurance Cover Invisalign?

Plans don’t often apply to orthodontics, so dental insurance rarely covers Invisalign. Even those policies that do encompass alignment work can exclude aligners.

Of course, exceptions exist to every rule. There’s always a chance your dental plan covers some of Invisalign’s cost, reducing your financial burden. You’d thus do well to check your policy for potential benefits; our team can even help you do so.

Invisalign FAQs

Does Invisalign Hurt?

To be honest, all orthodontic treatments come with some soreness from time to time. The good news is that there aren’t any metal components with Invisalign. Instead, the aligners are crafted from smooth plastic and are custom-made to fit the contours of your teeth so they don’t dig into the soft tissue in your mouth. Plus, if any discomfort does arise from the movement of your teeth, you can use a cold compress, sip on cool water, and take OTC pain medication as directed to alleviate it.

Can You Get Invisalign After Braces?

It’s possible, yes! So, if you’ve experienced orthodontic relapse in the years since your time with traditional braces, don’t hesitate to get in touch with our team at Main Line Dental Group. That way, we can assess the severity of your misalignment, examine the condition of your teeth and gums, and determine if you’re a good candidate for Invisalign in Wayne.

Are There Any Dietary Restrictions with Invisalign?

There aren’t any dietary restrictions with Invisalign. However, you do need to remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water. Otherwise, they will stain, warp, or crack, negatively impacting their appearance and function.

What Should I Do If My Invisalign Tray Broke?

If one of your Invisalign trays broke, do NOT try to repair it with superglue or any other household adhesives. Instead, call our office so we can learn more about what happened, including how severe the damage is. If there is a small hairline crack on the surface, then we may ask you to continue to wear your aligners (using the utmost care and caution, of course). Conversely, if your trays have broken into multiple pieces, then we will either ask you to progress to the next set in the series or revert to the previous one while we have a replacement made.

What Happens After Invisalign?

Once you’ve made it to the end of your series of aligners, we will make sure you’re happy with the results. Then, we will take impressions of your teeth so a custom retainer can be made. This usually takes a few weeks, so you’ll need to continue wearing your current trays in the meantime.

Once your retainer arrives at our office, we will schedule your final visit. This is when we will make sure that it fits properly, show you how to take care of it, and discuss how often you need to wear it. It’s very important that you wear yours as directed. Otherwise, your risk of experiencing orthodontic relapse increases considerably.
