Dr. Shoba Viswanathan – Practice Philosophy

Welcome to Spring Grove Dental! I often get asked, “Why would anyone want to be a dentist?” Going down the memory lane, as a kid I remember spending a lot of time in the dental chair – that experience naturally made me curious about the profession. As I grew older, I was not only attracted to the autonomy and independence of the profession but also was attracted to the challenge of taking something that is perceived to be so unpleasant , making it into a positive and rewarding experience for the patients.

And if you have gotten this far – Thank you for reading this and I look forward to seeing you at your next dental visit!!
