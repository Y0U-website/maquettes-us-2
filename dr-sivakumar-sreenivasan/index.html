The practice

With his warm smile and compassionate nature, our oral surgeon Dr. Sivakumar Sreenivasan instantly puts patients at ease. Dr. Sreeni, as he’s known to his colleagues and patients, began his career as an oral and maxillofacial surgeon in India. He also completed dental school and residency a second time in order to practice in the United States.

Dr. Sivakumar Sreenivasan provides a full range of oral and maxillofacial surgery services, with a focus on dental implants. In a compassionate setting, we provide personalized attention and tailored services that meet each patient’s needs in order to ensure an incredible experience for everyone.

We seek to provide personalized attention to each of our patients in order to create an incredible experience.

Rockville oral surgeon Dr. Sreeni follows the philosophy of his spiritual teacher, Sri Sathya Sai Baba, and finds many opportunities to put this philosophy into practice.

Twice a month Dr. Sreeni volunteers his time at a local Montgomery County dental clinic to provide desperately needed dental care for those who cannot afford it.

Each year Dr. Sreeni travels to India to provide oral surgery service at a hospital which offers free medical services to anyone who comes in.

Dr. Sreeni periodically travels to help following natural disasters such as those in Haiti, Tacloban in the Philippines, and has also been to British Guyana.
