Our Dental Services

Partials and complete dentures are used in certain situations to replace missing teeth. Partial dentures are used by connecting to remaining teeth in the patient’s mouth and are removable, which means they come in and out of the mouth each day. Complete dentures are used when a patient is missing all of their teeth, and they are removable as well.

Clear aligners are a comfortable and discreet way to gradually straighten your teeth without traditional braces. Your treatment is customized to your unique smile using advanced digital technology, with removable aligners that make eating, brushing, and flossing easy.

At this time, if you need an implant to replace a missing tooth, Dr. Pirtle will send you to one of our amazing oral surgeons in the area to get it placed. Dr. Pirtle will then restore the implant once the healing process is complete with an additional part of the implant, an abutment, and a crown.

Periodontal disease is disease of the gum and bone around the teeth. Signs of periodontal disease include: red, bleeding gums, recession of the gums, bone loss around the existing teeth, mobile teeth and tartar below the gumline. Our periodontal therapy focuses on stabilizing your gum health to provide you with the healthiest teeth possible.

Night guards can be used in situations when we see areas on your teeth that are consistent with clenching or grinding your teeth. The guard can help protect your teeth from additional damage as well as lessen the stress on your jaw muscles.

  • Crowns - a restoration that covers all surfaces of the tooth. These are made to match up to the color of your natural tooth structure. Crowns are necessary if a tooth is broken, a root canal is done and the tooth needs to be covered.
  • Bridges - are similar to crowns. This procedure involves two crowns on either side of an area you are missing a tooth with a tooth to cover that space in the middle. They are permanently cemented in, like crowns.
