Dr. Haesung Chung, DDS

In 2007 he joined Trappe Family Dental as a full time dentist where he continues to serve the community. He has consistently stayed up to date on the most current dental advancements through active participation in continuing education courses. He is a graduate of Rosenthal Institute where he completed a renowned Aesthetic Advantage program. In addition, he holds a certificate in dental implant surgical placement.

Dr. Chung believes in a comprehensive approach to dental care as it relates not only to people’s systemic health but also to their aesthetic appearance. He truly cares for his patients and takes his profession as a God-given calling while striving each day to deliver the best care possible in every circumstance.

Outside of dentistry, he loves spending time with his wife and three little children, Alyssa, Ava and Andrew. He likes to play tennis, enjoys singing, and is actively involved in his church. Dr. Chung also volunteers his time to serve the people of Sierra Leone and the Ivory Coast, two of the most impoverished areas in the world by treating them of their dental infections. He hopes to continue to serve those people in need.

Dr. Soo S. Lee, DMD

Dr. Soo S. Lee graduated from the University of Michigan with a Bachelor of Science in Biology followed by earning her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Temple University School of Dentistry. Upon graduation, she practiced in Philadelphia and New York City and finally has found her "home" at Trappe Family Dental. Dr. Lee is committed to advanced continuing education so that she can offer the latest techniques and technology to her patients. She is a graduate of Rosenthal Institute where she completed a renowned Aesthetic Advantage program.

She is an avid sports fan, especially enjoys watching her Philadelphia sports teams and the Michigan Wolverines and also hopes to have time to play a round of golf one day! But for now, she is quite busy as a mother of three young ones, Alyssa, Ava and Andrew - she considers them pure joy and a source of constant amazement and laughter.
