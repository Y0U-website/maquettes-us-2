The practice

Most Cape Cod patients come to my practice because their family dentist recommended it, or because they don’t like the way their teeth look. But did you know that orthodontics also improves your overall health and well-being? It does!

My team and I are proud to provide gentle care, open communication, and empathy. I can’t wait to meet you at our Falmouth or Hyannis office. Let’s celebrate your smile!

Our offices are newly designed with calm, soothing decor, bright windows, and a cozy waiting room. Live plants, inspirational quotes, flowers, and prints make our office feel like you’re at a spa. Dental offices are one of the safest indoor environments. We ensure that every visit to our office is as safe as possible. We meet or exceed all CDC, OSHA, and ADA guidelines for orthodontic practices, including adding HEPA filters and an upgraded HVAC system.
