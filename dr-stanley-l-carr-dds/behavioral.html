Behavioral Health

Your mental health matters. At Heart City Health, we aim to serve all your primary healthcare needs so you can enjoy the highest quality of life possible. This includes an understanding that your mental wellness can have a direct impact on your physical wellness, and vice versa.

Counseling Services

We want you to be happy and healthy in body and mind. Behavioral health counseling is a great way to ease mental health struggles with or without additional medication. Whether you have insurance or not, you are welcome here, and our range of counseling services to help you thrive.

Anxiety and Stress

Panic Attacks Insomnia Stress Management Mental Health

Even though stress and anxiety are different, they often become intertwined. Stress is a natural part of life, but too much stress can become detrimental to your health, especially when combined with anxiety. Counseling will give you practical techniques for dealing with anxiety, as well as pharmaceutical remedies, when needed. Reestablish control over your life, your stress, and your overall well-being. We’re also able to help you navigate the aftermath of trauma with PTSD counseling.

Depression

Suicide Prevention Crisis Intervention Seasonal Depression Postpartum Depression

From seasonal depression to suicide prevention, Heart City Health recognizes the significance of the struggles that depressed patients face. Depression stems from things like chemical imbalances, genetics or traumatic life events. Our licensed therapists are equipped to help you cope with your depression with natural or pharmaceutical methods. Happiness and contentment is possible, and we’ll help you take steps to get there.

Additional Counseling Services

Individual Counseling Group Therapy Court-Mandated Therapy

In addition to individual counseling sessions, we also offer court-mandated therapy, and a group therapy session on Wednesday afternoons form 4-6 PM for adults with depression. Call 574.968.2461 to make an appointment.

Prescription Management Services

Alongside counseling, our behavioral health team includes psychiatric mental health nurse practitioners who prescribe medications to meet your mental health care needs. With both the behavioral health center and pharmacy located at our Simpson office , you’re often able to get your prescriptions prescribed and fulfilled the same day.

Now Offering Telehealth Services

Due to the pandemic, we’re finding new ways to connect with our patients. The telehealth program allows you to keep your behavioral health appointments without leaving the house. Call 574.968.2461 to make an appointment and learn more about our telehealth services.
