The practice

Our Navigators are certified by the state of Indiana to provide free education and assistance for insurance enrollment. Whether you’re a Heart City patient or not, we want you to be an active partner in your health, and that begins with health coverage.

We have a full retail pharmacy on-site at our Simpson location. As a patient, you can get the most prescriptions at a discounted price. We can also assist you in registering for Medicine Assistance Programs (MAP). If you qualify, the MAP can help you receive prescriptions for a fraction of the original cost.

Contribute to the health of our community—that’s our mission. Our outreach professionals meet you where you are, at schools, stores, other organizations, to talk about Heart City Health and insurance enrollment.

Patient Care Coordinators are trained professionals who provide education, training and assistance managing chronic health conditions such as diabetes, obesity or hypertension. A valued part of your patient care team, these coordinators supplement the care provided by your physician and help ensure a healthier community.

Our financial services staff helps you determine the best method of payment for the services received, including a sliding fee scale that can help you save up to 90 % on the cost of services based on your household income and family size.
