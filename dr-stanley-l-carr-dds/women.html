Women’s Health Statistics in Indiana are Grim We’re Here to Change That

“Indiana has one of the highest maternal and infant mortality rates in the country. This is unacceptable and we think we should do better, starting with the women in our own community,” said Esleen Fultz, CEO at Heart City Health.

Between 2011-2015, Indiana had the seventh-highest infant mortality rate and the third-highest maternal mortality rate in the country.

The mission of Heart City Health is to provide medical care for the whole person, regardless of their personal or financial situation.

“We accept most forms of insurance as well as offer a sliding fee scale for those without insurance. At Heart City Health, we feel that all women deserve access to high-quality healthcare regardless of age, race or financial situation and that’s why we will not deny care to anyone,” Fultz said.
