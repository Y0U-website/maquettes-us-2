About

For Dr. Stephanie, the idea to open a dental practice began while she was still in dental school. At the time, students had to create a business plan for an assignment. Somewhere along the way, the idea of starting a practice became more than just a graded exercise. While in her pediatric residency, Dr. Stephanie decided to purchase the property that would eventually become West Haven Pediatric Dentistry.

Did you know the name West Haven has sentimental value? In 1992, Dr. Stephanie’s mother and grandmother started a daycare in Evans, Georgia and they named it West Haven Preschool and Learning Center. She basically grew up at the daycare and even had her first job there at the age of sixteen. When it came time for Dr. Stephanie to name her practice- it was a no brainer!

Here at West Haven Pediatric Dentistry, we are proud to specialize in pediatric dentistry. We truly believe in starting children off right when it comes to their dental health. It is our goal to ensure that each visit goes well so that they think of the dentist’s office as a good place. Our entire staff is wonderful with children, allowing them to relax while we care for their teeth.

Our dentists and staff have received extra training for primary teeth. Our goal is to protect your children’s teeth so they can eat and speak correctly. We will do whatever we can to help your children have the bright smiles they deserve.

We are also here for you, the parents. We know how important (yet difficult) it is to take care of your children’s teeth at home. During your appointment, we will go over the care that you need to be providing at home and look for problems as well. If needed, we will work with your child’s pediatrician.

If you have any questions or would like your children to be seen, don’t hesitate to contact us today.
