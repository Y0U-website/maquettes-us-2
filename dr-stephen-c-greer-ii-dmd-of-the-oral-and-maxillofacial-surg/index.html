Located in Madison, MS, we are an oral and maxillofacial surgery practice that performs intra and extra oral surgery, the surgical removal of teeth, dental implants and facial trauma repair. Dr. Greer is dedicated to providing the local populations with reputable service, help, and reassuring care.

Dr. Greer received his doctor of dental medicine degree from the University of Mississippi’s School of Dentistry, and then an internship in oral and maxillofacial surgery at the University of Texas Medical Branch at Galveston. Dr. Greer did his residency at the Medical College of Georgia and is currently a member of the American Dental Association, Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons…

The Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery Center of Mississippi is dedicated to providing patients with the highest level of surgical care in a safe and affordable environment.

With more than 13 years in the field, you’ll get the treatment you need.

I am trained and certified, and attend workshops on a regular basis.
