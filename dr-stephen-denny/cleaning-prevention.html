Cleaning & Prevention

Preventive dentistry is based on developing good oral hygiene habits so you can reduce the risk of problems like cavities, gum disease, and periodontitis. Routine dental exams and cleanings, along with regular brushing and flossing, are all part of preventative dental care.

The Importance of At Home Care

A key element of preventative dentistry is to brush and floss your teeth twice a day. At Indian Ripple Dental, we’ll help you decide if a specific type of toothpaste, toothbrush, or toothpick will best support your needs.

Your diet also plays a vital role in oral health. For example, acidic foods and drinks can corrode teeth while foods and drinks high in calcium can fortify tooth enamel. Talk to Dr. Denny or your hygienist about how your diet can either positively or negatively affect your dental health.

What are the Benefits of Professional Exams and Cleanings?

By taking a pre-emptive approach to dental health, we can help you prevent minor issues from becoming major procedures. And because many dental plans cover cleanings, you’ll save money on dental expenses in the long run by taking advantage of your policy.
