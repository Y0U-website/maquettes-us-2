Cosmetic Dentistry Services

Feel Confident About Your Smile!

Let’s face it. Everyone wants a great a smile but the reality is many people feel embarrassed or uncomfortable with their current situation. That’s where cosmetic dentistry comes in.

Cosmetic dentistry refers to a combination of services that include custom-made restorative work. Dr. Steve will hold a consultation meeting to find a specific treatment plan to meet your needs. Treatment can consist of (or combination of): crowns, veneers, fillings, whitening, orthodontics, replacing missing teeth, and more. If you are unhappy with your smile, we can help!

Clear Braces

Clear Braces

Whitening

Whitening

Teeth whitening consists of coming to the office to have custom-made retainers or trays made for the teeth. These trays hold in whitening gel for a treatment you can easily do in the comfort of your home.

Crowns and Veneers

Crowns and Veneers

Commonly referred to as a crown or a cap on the tooth, this is a protective covering that preserves damaged teeth. This is a long-term solution that improves function, durability, and appearance.

Implants

Implants

Implants are a great option to replace missing teeth and secure loose dentures or partials. Dr. Steve has had training on placing implants and restoring implants. At the moment we do not have the capabilities to place implants, but can restore all implants. Dr. Steve can refer you to an oral surgeon or periodontist to be evaluated for the placement of dental implants.

Bonding

Bonding

Tooth colored fillings that can change the shape and color of teeth. Bonding can be done to help close small spaces between teeth or help improve the color of teeth if they are unable to improve with whitening.
