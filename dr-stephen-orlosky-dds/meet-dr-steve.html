Meet Stephen Orlosky, D.D.S.

Dr. Steve is a native to the Youngstown area, growing up in Girard and graduating from John F. Kennedy High school. He attended Walsh University for his undergraduate degrees in Chemistry and Biology which led him to receiving his doctorate degree from The Ohio State University’s College of Dentistry. After coming home, Dr. Steve met his wife and got married in 2017. Dr. Steve and his wife Chelsea have two children, Eugene and Annalise who often accompany their parents to work.

Dr. Steve has been working in the dental field for over 10 years and has traveled all over the country to receive specialized continuing education on cosmetic dentistry, sleep dentistry, orthodontics, and implant dentistry. Dr. Steve enjoys the variety of treatment that general dentistry offers and would love to talk to you about how he can best serve you.

In his spare time, Dr. Steve loves nothing more than spending time with his family and friends. Other hobbies include traveling, watching his favorite Ohio sport teams, indulging in craft beer, and being a “handy man” around the house.
