Dental Implants

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer several significant benefits, making them an attractive option for many of our patients:

  • Natural Appearance : Implants are designed to look and feel like your natural teeth, providing a seamless appearance and restoring your confident smile.
  • Improved Functionality : Implants allow for better chewing and speech capabilities compared to removable alternatives by securely anchoring prosthetic teeth.
  • Long-Lasting Solution : With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them a cost-effective long-term solution.
  • Bone Preservation : Dental implants help stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss that typically occurs with missing teeth.

Our Dental Implant Process

At Marin Modern Dentistry, we focus on providing a personalized and comfortable experience for dental implant procedures. Here’s what you can expect:

Initial Consultation

The first step is an in-depth consultation with our experienced team of dental professionals. During this visit, we will assess your oral health, discuss your goals, and determine if dental implants are the right choice for you. We will also use advanced imaging techniques to evaluate the condition of your jawbone and plan the precise placement of your implants.

Implant Placement

Once the treatment plan is finalized, you will undergo a surgical procedure to place the implants into the jawbone. We can perform this procedure under local anesthesia. We ensure minimal discomfort and a swift recovery.

Osseointegration

After implant placement, a process known as osseointegration occurs. Over several months, the implant will naturally fuse with the jawbone, forming a solid anchor for the replacement teeth. You may receive a temporary restoration to maintain your smile during this healing period.

Restoration

The final step involves attaching a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture to the implant. Our restorations are crafted from high-quality materials to match your natural teeth’ shape, size, and color, providing a flawless finish.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy individuals who maintain strong oral hygiene practices and have sufficient jawbone density are candidates for dental implants. However, certain conditions, such as uncontrolled diabetes, gum disease, or insufficient bone mass, may require preliminary treatment or an alternative solution.

Maintaining Your Dental Implants

Caring for dental implants is similar to maintaining your natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and dentist visits are essential to ensure the longevity of your implants and overall oral health. Our team will provide guidelines and recommendations to keep your implants in top condition.
