About Our Pediatric Dental Team…

Our mission is to create a welcoming and comfortable environment that encourages patients of all ages to feel at ease, inspiring them to embrace regular dental care and prioritize their oral health. Using the latest technology and techniques, we’re committed to bringing beautiful new smiles to children, teens, and adults, always putting your family’s needs first.

husband & Wife Duo

Dr. Steven Kim & Dr. Christina Seong

Dr. Steven Kim, a board-certified pediatric dentist, and Dr. Christina Seong, a board-certified orthodontist, share not only a love for each other but also a passion for delivering exceptional dental care to children and families. Together, they bring a comprehensive approach to pediatric and orthodontic care, ensuring every patient receives the highest level of personalized treatment.

Dr. Kim was born in Korea and raised in Minnesota, where he completed his undergraduate studies at the University of Minnesota. He earned his dental degree from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine and further honed his skills through a general dentistry residency at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine. His commitment to children&#x27;s oral health led him to pursue a pediatric dental residency at St. Barnabas Hospital, where he served as chief resident.

Dr. Seong earned her degree in Biochemistry from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill before receiving her DMD from the University of Pittsburgh. She completed a three-year orthodontic residency at the University of Connecticut, specializing in both traditional braces and Invisalign treatments. Her focus is on creating beautiful, healthy smiles for patients of all ages through customized care.

As proud parents of two wonderful children, Dr. Kim and Dr. Seong understand the importance of compassionate, family-centered care. They enjoy spending time together on family walks, exploring new parks, and dining at local spots. Both are excited to serve the Long Island community and look forward to building lasting relationships with the families they care for.
