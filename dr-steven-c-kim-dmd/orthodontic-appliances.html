Orthodontic Facemasks

Orthodontic facemasks are used to correct underdeveloped upper jaws, especially in growing children. This appliance gently pulls the upper jaw forward using elastic bands connected to a custom-fitted face mask. It&#x27;s typically worn for 12–14 hours a day over several months, depending on the severity of the misalignment. Early treatment can guide jaw growth and reduce the need for surgery later on.

Palate Expanders

Palate expanders are devices used to widen the upper jaw, often to create space for crowded teeth or improve the fit between the upper and lower jaws. The appliance is anchored to the upper molars and gradually widened over time using a special key. Most patients wear a palate expander for 4–6 months to achieve and stabilize the desired expansion. In many cases, this can help avoid tooth extractions or more complex treatment down the road.

The Benefits Of Orthodontic Appliances

Improved Oral Health Orthodontic appliances help align teeth properly, making it easier to clean them effectively. This reduces the risk of cavities and gum disease, promoting better oral hygiene and overall health.

Enhanced Facial Aesthetics By correcting jaw alignment and teeth positioning, these appliances contribute to a more balanced facial profile. This improvement can significantly boost self-esteem and confidence in social and professional settings.

Prevention of Future Dental Issues Early intervention with orthodontic appliances can prevent more severe dental problems later in life. Addressing issues like overcrowding and jaw misalignment early can save time and money on future dental procedures.
