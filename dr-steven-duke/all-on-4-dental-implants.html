Benefits of All-On-4 Procedure

  • Immediate increase in quality of life by bringing comfort to eating, appearance, and speech.
  • With only four implants required and provisional teeth available on the same day, this therapy is unquestionably faster and less expensive than other options.
  • The expenditures may appear costly at first look, but the hefty maintenance costs of other restorations end up making them more expensive in the long run.
