The practice

We are always accepting new patients. Please call to schedule an appointment and fill out the new patient paperwork. Our goal at Wheatland Family Dental is to help you reach and maintain maximum oral health.

Some patients desire sedation to reduce excitement or nervousness prior to and during dental treatment. Our doctors and staff are uniquely qualified and highly experienced in the sedation environment.

Our Doctors and staff at Wheatland Dental are highly trained and our patients are always our top priority. We have over a decade of experience and have served thousands of patients.

To make an appointment or for general questions please call or use the contact form.

If you are a new patient please completely fill out our new patient forms and bring them to your first appointment.

I hold dental licenses and general anesthesia permits in Washington and Oregon. I’m certified in BLS, ACLS, and PALS. Our office has a sedation suite with a level one medical gas system, complete with backup generator. This high level of redundancy enables us to provide our treatments in the safest of office based environments.

When I’m not in the clinic or attending continuing education, I enjoy camping and fishing the Northwest, aviation, and constructing radio controlled models with my son Vincent.

Graduate of Walla Walla University and Loma Linda University School of Dentistry.
