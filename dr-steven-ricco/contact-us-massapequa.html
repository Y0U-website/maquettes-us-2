About Massapequa, NY

Massapequa is a charming waterfront community in Nassau County, offering residents a perfect blend of suburban comfort and coastal living. This vibrant town boasts scenic waterways, historic districts, and abundant recreational opportunities that make it a sought-after destination for families and professionals alike.

  • John J. Burns Park - A premier waterfront park featuring multiple sports fields, picnic areas, and a scenic marina, providing year-round recreational activities for residents.
  • Massapequa Preserve - A stunning 423-acre nature sanctuary offering hiking trails, fishing spots, and diverse wildlife viewing opportunities through pristine wetlands and forests.
  • Sunrise Mall - A major shopping destination serving as a commercial hub for the community, offering retail, dining, and entertainment options.
