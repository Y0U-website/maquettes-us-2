What is a Dental Implant?

Dental implants are small anchors made of a biocompatible metal called titanium that are placed in the jawbone. Precise placement using advanced dental sciences ensures optimal results. Dental implants are the replacement of tooth roots in the mouth. They provide a foundation as strong as natural teeth. Once placed, the anchors begin to fuse with the bone over the course of a few months. After the fusing process, known as osseointegration, abutment posts are inserted into the anchors to allow for the permanent attachment of the replacement teeth.

What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a natural-appearing tooth replacement. They are the ideal solution for missing teeth and tooth loss. There are many alternatives on the market, but none quite as effective as these. Alternatives include dentures and bridges, but implants offer superior results for tooth loss. Whether you are missing one tooth or all your teeth, dental implants can provide you with the functionality and appearance of a real natural tooth.

A titanium post becomes the artificial root implanted into the jaw and is allowed to fuse to the bone. This could take a few weeks or a couple of months. The recovery process varies by patient. Once the dentist believes the root is secure and ready to proceed, an abutment is added to the post. This will hold the artificial tooth - a dental crown - in place. This restores your smile beautifully.

Dental implants are meant to be a lifelong solution for tooth replacement. The implant is there to stay, though the dental crown will last about 10-15 years (maybe longer if you take care of it) before it will need to be replaced.

Perhaps one of the best things about dental implants is that no one will ever know you have them - unless you tell them!

Benefits of Dental Implants

Those who have already invested in dental implants are quick to tell you that they come with many benefits. They are the gold standard when it comes to tooth replacement. Let’s take a look at just a few of the benefits you can expect.

There are so many positive experiences that patients have had with dental implants that the list is endless.

  • Dental implants enhance the appearance
  • Dental implants function just like a real tooth
  • Dental implants last for a lifetime
  • Dental implants cannot fall victim to decay
  • Dental implants keep surrounding teeth stable
  • Dental implants help reduce bone loss
  • Dental implants restore bite force
  • Dental implants are easy to care for
  • Dental implants won’t impact speech
