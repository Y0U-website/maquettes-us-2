Dr. James Brady

I grew up in the southwest suburbs of Chicago. I attended the University of Illinois for my Bachelor’s degree and then went to the University of Michigan, where I earned my Doctorate of Dental Surgery. Immediately following dental school, I completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency program with the South Texas Veterans Health Care System in San Antonio, Texas. My training during residency allowed me to expand my skill set in all areas of general dentistry while simultaneously giving back to our nation’s veterans.

I’ve been fortunate to participate in meaningful service experiences that have allowed me to give back to both local and global communities. Through the Victors for Veterans program, I provided comprehensive dental care to Michigan veterans with the greatest need. Internationally, I’ve taken part in multiple outreach trips to Cape Town, South Africa, and Meru, Kenya, where I delivered oral health care and education to elementary school children. These experiences have affirmed my commitment to compassionate, accessible care and demonstrated the positive impact of oral health.
