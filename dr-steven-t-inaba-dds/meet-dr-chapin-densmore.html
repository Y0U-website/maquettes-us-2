Dr. Chapin Densmore

My passion for dentistry is rooted in empowering people to improve their health and confidence through their smiles. Dentistry provides me the opportunity to connect with and contribute to the lives of individuals and the community, as a whole. To ensure patients are receiving the most up-to-date treatment and highest standard of care, I continually invest significant time into continuing education and training.

Born and raised in the South Puget Sound region, I attended the University of Washington for undergrad before earning my dental degree from the University of Southern California School of Dentistry. I then completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency in Seattle, allowing me to expand and refine my clinical skills.

I participate in multiple study clubs and serves as an affiliate instructor at the Lake Washington Institute of Technology’s dental hygiene program. During my dental education and training, I participated in a variety of outreach programs treating the underserved populations of Los Angeles and Seattle. Since returning to the Seattle area, I have volunteered at the annual Seattle/King County Dental Clinic and worked with Medical Teams International.
