Dr. Josh Walker

Dr. Josh Walker, MAGD

It is exciting to wake up each morning and come to Meridian Dental Clinic I love what I do! Empowering people to take control of their oral health is rewarding. I want to build lasting relationships with people as their oral health advocate. You and I will function as a team to help you achieve your oral health goals. This team-based care is facilitated by our fantastic team members at MDC that are devoted to encouraging and coaching you along the way.

We value your health! I want you to reap the benefits of life-long care.

In addition to completing my residency, I have committed to continually furthering my dental education and knowledge. After completing an additional 1,100 hours of training, I was awarded Mastership in the Academy of General Dentistry in June of 2021. Less than 2 % of all General Dentists in the U.S. and Canada have earned the award and recognized as Masters by the AGD.

Meet Dr. Josh Walker, MAGD

Outside the Office..

I was born and raised in the Puget Sound area and love practicing dentistry in the community I call home. Away from the office I enjoy maintaining an active lifestyle snow skiing, surfing, golfing, running, hiking, and even managing to still play some basketball! I have a wonderful and supportive wife, and two young children. They are such a joy, and keep me quite busy when I’m not in the office!

Feeling a strong sense of pride and commitment to my community, I am passionate about giving back. I teach in the pre-doctoral clinic at the University of Washington School of Dentistry and have volunteered with the Seattle King County Dental Society on the Budget and Finance Committee.
