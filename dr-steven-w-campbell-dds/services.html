General Dentistry We provide state of the art general dentistry services to keep your smile looking its best in Kenosha, WI, Pleasant Prairie, WI, and the nearby areas.

Cosmetic Dentistry If you want to get a crooked tooth fixed or improve the overall appearance of your teeth, rely on our experienced team for cosmetic dental procedures.

Dental Implants Pike Creek Dentistryspecializes in dental implants to cover up lost or decayed teeth which are damaged due to disease or oral injury.

Dental Crowns Crowns are synthetic caps to be placed on top of a tooth. They are made of a material like porcelain and are used to restore a tooth’s function and appearance after a restorative procedure such as a root canal.

Teeth Whitening Teeth whitening is a terrific approach to cure yellowed stained teeth.

Invisalign Invisalign is a technologically advanced way of correcting teeth alignment through custom clear aligners.

Root Canals Root canal treatment is designed to eliminate bacteria from the infected root canal, prevent reinfection of the tooth and save the natural tooth.

Porcelain Veneers A veneer is a thin piece of porcelain used to re-create the natural look of teeth, while also providing strength and resilience comparable to natural tooth enamel.
