MISSION STATEMENT

At Stoddard Dental Group, we are dedicated to caring for our patients with gentle care, highly trained expertise, and steadfast trust. We promise to combine our advanced dental technology with active listening and informative consultation. In our friendly and relaxing environment, you are treated and cared for like family. We exude enthusiasm for all your dental needs, while having a genuinely fun time doing it. At Stoddard Dental Group, your smile is our favorite compliment.

Dr. Tommy Stoddard is a native of Greenwood who graduated from Greenwood High School in 1976. He attended Clemson University and graduated cum laude in 1980 with a Bachelor’s of Science Degree in Microbiology. He received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree (D.M.D.) from the Medical University of South Carolina in 1984 and began his practice of General Dentistry in Greenwood.

Dr. Stoddard has had extensive post-doctoral training in all areas of dentistry. Some of his particular interests are cosmetic smile design and restorative dentistry using life-like ceramic materials that closely resemble natural teeth.

Dr. Stoddard completed training through the American Academy of Facial Esthetics (AAFE) for non-surgical and non-invasive facial aesthetic injectable techniques, as well as TMJ and facial pain therapy.

Dr. Stoddard is also the Dental Provider for Connie Maxwell Children’s Home, Wesley Commons Healthcare Center and National Healthcare Center.

He is an active sports enthusiast who would rather participate than watch. He plays tennis and golf regularly and participates in triathlons and distance running events when time permits. Dr. Stoddard and his wife, Tara, have two children, Katie and Thomas, and five precious grandchildren.

MEET DR. WALTER STEVENS

Dr. Stevens is a native of Greenwood who graduated from Greenwood High School and then earned his undergraduate degree in Biology from Wofford College before receiving his dental degree at the College of Dental Medicine at the Medical University of South Carolina in Charleston.

Following graduation, Dr. Stevens pursued advance training through a one-year General Practice residency at the University of Alabama at Birmingham. During this time, he gained extensive experience in a hospital setting, treating medically complex patients and performing surgical procedures, including extractions and dental implants.

Dr. Stevens has also completed advance training and is certified in Invisalign.

Dr. Stevens completed training through the American Academy of Facial Esthetics (AAFE) for non-surgical and non-invasive facial aesthetic injectable techniques, as well as TMJ and facial pain therapy.

His professional interests include dental implants and restorative dentistry.

MEET DR. JOHN TURNER

MEET DR. COURTNEY WOOLLEN

Dr. Woollen is from Lexington, South Carolina, where she completed her undergraduate studies at the University of South Carolina. She earned her dental degree at the College of Dental Medicine at MUSC in Charleston and then completed a one year General Practice residency at Prisma Health in Columbia. She has clinical experience in both private practice as well as in academic dentistry, and served as an assistant professor at West Virginia University School of Dentistry.

Dr. Woollen loves the Gamecocks and enjoys spending time with her husband, son, family and friends!
