Get to Know

Dr. Chiman Lad

Dr. Chiman Lad established Lad Family Dentistry in 1977, creating a practice that has now served the San Gabriel Valley community for almost 50 years. His vision was to create a dental office where patients would be treated like family—receiving personalized attention, quality care, and honest recommendations.

With more than four decades of experience, Dr. Lad brings exceptional clinical expertise and a wealth of practical knowledge to each patient interaction. While he now practices part-time, his influence remains strong throughout the practice in the values, approach, and patient-centered philosophy that guides our team. His partnership with his son, Dr. Sunjay, represents the perfect blend of established experience and contemporary techniques, offering patients the benefits of traditional values combined with modern dental advances.

Dr. Lad takes particular pride in the multi-generational relationships he has built with patients over the decades. Many families who first visited him in the late 1970s continue to receive care at our practice, with their children, grandchildren, and even great-grandchildren now becoming part of our dental family.

Dr. Lad is an active member of several professional organizations, including the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association, and the San Gabriel Valley Dental Society. In recognition of his commitment to leadership and professionalism in dentistry, Dr. Lad has been inducted as a Fellow of the Pierre Fauchard Academy.

When he’s not creating smiles, you can find Dr. Lad growing vegetables in his garden, building things with his grandkids, and skiing and hiking with his family.

Dr. Lad provides care in both English and Gujarati.

Your First Visit

Welcome In

Modern Comfort and Advanced Care

At Lad Family Dentistry, we&#x27;ve thoughtfully combined patient comforts with modern dental technology to create an experience that&#x27;s both pleasant and effective. Our office environment is designed to help you feel at ease while ensuring you receive the highest standard of care.

Intraoral Cameras

We utilize intraoral cameras to give you a clear view of your teeth and gums during your appointment. These small, handheld devices allow us to show you exactly what we&#x27;re seeing, helping you better understand your oral health and treatment recommendations through visual education.

Intraoral Scanner

We use a state-of-the-art intraoral scanner to capture precise digital impressions of your teeth—no messy trays required. This advanced technology is fast, comfortable, and highly accurate. It allows us to plan treatments more efficiently and deliver better-fitting restorations with exceptional results.

Digital X-rays

Our practice uses digital X-ray technology, which provides immediate, high-resolution images while significantly reducing radiation exposure compared to traditional film X-rays. This advanced imaging helps us detect issues earlier and plan treatments with greater precision.

Cozy Amenities

Your comfort matters to us. We offer amenities including a beverage station to help you feel refreshed and noise-cancelling headphones to help you relax during treatment. These thoughtful touches are part of our commitment to creating a dental experience that feels more like visiting friends than a typical medical appointment.
