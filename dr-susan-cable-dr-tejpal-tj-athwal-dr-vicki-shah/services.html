Our Services

We offer a wide variety of dental services from preventative dentistry to restorative dentistry. We can provide you with quality dental care to have you smiling with confidence.

PREVENTATIVE DENTISTRY

Cleanings : removal of tartar and plaque build up from above and below the gum line. Routine cleanings (every 6 months), in addition to proper home care, help prevent periodontal disease and gingivitis.

Digital Radiographs : digital sensors are used to capture images of the teeth, instead of traditional film.

Sealants : thin, resin coating that can be placed on the biting surfaces of back teeth to help prevent cavities.

Fluoride Treatment : a supplement, usually a liquid or paste, that is applied topically to teeth to help prevent tooth decay.

RESTORATIVE DENTISTRY

Crowns : tooth shaped covering that is placed over a tooth to reinforce and strengthen it. They are also commonly used to enhance the appearance of teeth. May also be referred to as a "cap."

Composite Fillings (tooth colored fillings) : restoring a tooth&#x27;s function and shape by removing decay and filling in the tooth with a tooth-colored mixture of plastic and glass.

Bridges : a fixed dental restoration that fills in gaps, created by missing teeth, with fake teeth, often flanked by crowns.

Whitening : lightens teeth and helps remove stain and discoloration.*

Porcelain Veneers : thin, custom made shells of tooth colored material designed to cover the front surface of a tooth, changing the color, shape, size or length of a tooth.

Bonding : tooth colored material placed on a tooth to improve a tooth&#x27;s appearance.

Partial Dentures : a removable appliance used to replace missing teeth while natural teeth still exist in the mouth.

Full Dentures : a removable appliance used to replace all teeth in the mouth when natural teeth are missing.
