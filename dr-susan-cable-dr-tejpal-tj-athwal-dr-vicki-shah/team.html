Meet the Team

Jean, Dental Hygienist

Jean has been a dental hygienist for many years, and has over 20 years of experience in the dental field. What she enjoys most about her job is making beautiful smiles. Outside of work, she enjoys the Green Bay Packers and bowling.

Emily, Dental Hygienist

Emily has been a Dental Hygienist for many years. She enjoys meeting new people and helping them achieve and maintain a healthy smile. In her free time, she enjoys spending time with family and friends, cooking, traveling, gardening, reading, and baseball.

Stephanie, Clinical Assistant

Stephanie has been a Clinical Assistant for many years. She enjoys putting people&#x27;s dental fears to rest. Outside of work, she enjoys spending time with family and friends, running, camping, and other outdoor activities.

Holly, Clinical Assistant

Holly has worked for Dr. Cable for many years. She enjoys everything about her job, and the dentistry field, and spending time with family and friends.

Susan Erickson, Business Administrator

Susan Erickson has been in the dentistry for 37 years. She specializes in insurance and helping patients use it to their best advantage. Outside of work she enjoys traveling, scrapbooking and spending time with her husband, grandchildren and dog.

Dee Dee Nehls, Clinical Assistant

Lori McDonald, Business Administrator

Lori McDonald is new to the Dental field, but brings many years of experience in customer service with her. She enjoys reading, baking and spending time with her family, friends and church.
