Accessibility Statement for St. Johns Pediatric Dentistry

  • Use sufficient color contrast so text is readable for people with low vision or color blindness.
  • Provide text alternatives for meaningful images so they can be understood with assistive technology.
  • Structure our pages with clear headings and labels so they can be navigated with a keyboard and screen readers.
  • Treat accessibility as an ongoing effort and consider it when we add or update content.

Ongoing effort

Accessibility is not a one-time project. We continue to monitor and improve our website over time, and we welcome feedback that helps us do better.

Need help, or found a problem?

If you have difficulty using any part of our website, or you would like to request information in an alternative format, please contact us — we are happy to help and will do our best to provide the information you need through another method.

Alternative access

If any content on our website is not accessible to you, you can always reach our team directly by phone during office hours to schedule an appointment, ask about insurance, or get answers to your questions.
