Early Signs When to Consider Orthodontic Care Orthodontic concerns often develop gradually and aren’t always easy to spot. Early evaluation helps identify alignment and bite issues, allowing us to guide proper growth and prevent more complex problems later.

Crowded Teeth Teeth that overlap or lack space can affect oral health, make cleaning difficult, and lead to alignment issues over time.

Spacing Issues Gaps between teeth may impact alignment and bite function, and can affect how teeth come together as your child grows.

Bite Problems Overbites, underbites, and crossbites can affect chewing, speech, and jaw development if not addressed early.

Chewing Issues Difficulty biting or chewing comfortably may indicate alignment or bite concerns that benefit from early orthodontic evaluation.

Tooth Timing Early or delayed loss of baby teeth can affect how permanent teeth come in and impact overall alignment.
