Kids Dental Care Services

Kids Dental Care Services in St. Johns Complete Pediatric Dental Care for Every Stage From preventive visits to restorative treatments and advanced care, we provide everything your child needs for a healthy, confident smile—all in one comfortable, child-friendly environment here in St. Johns.

Teaching Healthy Habits That Last a Lifetime

We believe great dental care starts with education. Our team takes time to show kids how to brush, explain what’s happening, and build confidence through hands-on learning. By making oral health simple and engaging, we help children develop habits that support strong, healthy smiles for life.

Preventive Care Protecting Healthy Smiles Early Preventive care helps stop problems before they start. We focus on early detection, education, and guidance—including infant care and habits—to keep your child’s teeth healthy and strong.

Gentle Cleanings Gentle cleanings remove plaque and buildup while supporting healthy gums and helping children build positive, lifelong dental habits.

Comprehensive Exams Routine exams evaluate teeth, gums, and oral development, helping detect issues early and guide proper growth as your child matures.

Digital X-Rays Low-radiation digital radiographs detect cavities and monitor growth, allowing early, precise treatment while keeping your child safe and comfortable.

Sealants & Protection Dental sealants and preventive treatments protect vulnerable teeth from decay by sealing deep grooves where bacteria commonly collect.

Fluoride & Remineralization Fluoride treatments strengthen enamel and support remineralization, helping reverse early decay and protect teeth between visits.

Custom Mouthguards Custom-made athletic mouthguards protect teeth during sports and physical activity, reducing the risk of injury while providing a secure, comfortable fit.

Advanced Care Specialized Support When Your Child Needs It While many visits are routine, some situations require more focused care. From urgent dental needs to orthodontics and sedation, we provide specialized services designed to keep your child comfortable, safe, and supported every step of the way.

Orthodontics

Early orthodontic care helps correct alignment and bite issues before they become more complex. We offer evaluations and treatment options to support proper development long-term.

Special Needs

We provide sensory-friendly, individualized dental care for children with special needs. Our team works closely with families to create a calm, comfortable experience tailored to each child.

Emergency

Tooth pain and dental injuries can happen when you least expect them. Our team provides prompt, gentle care to relieve discomfort quickly and protect your child’s smile when it matters most.

Sedation

For children who feel anxious or need longer procedures, we offer safe sedation options including nitrous oxide and IV sedation to ensure a relaxed, positive experience.

Restorative Care Repairing and Restoring Growing Smiles When dental issues arise, we provide gentle, effective treatments to restore your child’s teeth, relieve discomfort, and protect long-term oral health while maintaining a natural, confident smile.

Tooth-Colored Fillings Natural-looking fillings repair cavities while blending seamlessly with your child’s teeth for a strong, healthy, and confident smile.

Dental Crowns Durable crowns restore damaged teeth, protecting structure and function while supporting long-term oral health.

Pulp Therapy Pulp therapy treats infection or damage inside the tooth, relieving pain and preserving the tooth whenever possible.

Space Maintainers Custom space maintainers help guide proper alignment by holding space for permanent teeth after early tooth loss.

Extractions Gentle extractions remove severely damaged or problematic teeth to protect overall oral health and prevent complications.

Cosmetic Bonding Tooth-colored bonding repairs chips and minor imperfections, restoring appearance and confidence in your child’s smile.
