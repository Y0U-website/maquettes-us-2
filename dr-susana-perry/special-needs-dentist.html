Board Certified Pediatric Dentist Child-Friendly, Stress-Free Environment Serving St. Johns, Nocatee & Ponte Vedra Understanding Your Child A Better Experience for Children with Special Health Care Needs We understand that dental visits can feel overwhelming for children with autism, sensory sensitivities, or developmental differences. That’s why we take time to listen, adjust, and create an experience built around trust, patience, and comfort.

We also understand medically complex children require knowledge and thorough understanding of their needs. We take the time to go over medical histories with parents and coordinate with specialists as necessary.

Our Services Complete Dental Care for Every Child We provide a full range of pediatric dental services, carefully adapted to meet each child’s comfort level and unique needs.

Comprehensive Exams Routine exams evaluate teeth, gums, and development, helping detect issues early while guiding proper growth in a comfortable setting.

Digital X-Rays Low-radiation digital x-rays detect cavities and monitor growth, allowing early treatment while keeping your child safe and comfortable.

Preventive Protection Sealants and fluoride treatments strengthen enamel, prevent decay, and help protect your child’s teeth between visits.

Restorative Treatments Fillings, crowns, and pulp therapy restore damaged teeth, relieve discomfort, and protect long-term oral health.

Gentle Procedures Extractions and space maintainers are performed carefully and calmly to support development and overall dental health.

Orthodontics

Sensory-Friendly, Personalized Care

Our team provides sensory-friendly dental visits designed to reduce stress and build confidence. We adjust pacing, communication, and the environment to fit your child’s needs—creating a calm, predictable experience that helps them feel safe and supported.

Social Story Help Your Child Feel Ready Before Their Visit We’ve created a simple social story to help your child understand what to expect at the dentist. Reading it together before their visit can reduce anxiety, build familiarity, and help them feel more comfortable and confident.
