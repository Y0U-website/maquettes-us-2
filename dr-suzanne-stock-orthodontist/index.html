The practice

Dr. Suzanne Stock has worked with countless patients across Iowa City, Fairfield, Williamsburg, and Washington, to create straight and healthy smiles for the entire family. Dr. Suzanne Stock provides comprehensive orthodontic services, braces, & 3M™ Clarity™ Aligners to patients in Iowa City, Washington, Williamsburg, Fairfield, and the surrounding areas.

We specialize in Orthodontics. Dr. Stock strives to provide excellent orthodontic treatment to every patient.

No matter which office you visit, you will receive the same excellent care and have access to the same wide variety of treatments.

Virtually invisible and comfortably fitting, there are several advantages of Clarity™ clear brackets.

Using small, translucent brackets, Clarity™ Ceramic braces are designed to blend in with your teeth and are easy to care for!

The best part about Clear Aligners is that the only thing people will notice is your beautiful smile!

A modern alternative to wearing traditional braces, Clear Aligners offers our patients the same effective orthodontic treatment.
