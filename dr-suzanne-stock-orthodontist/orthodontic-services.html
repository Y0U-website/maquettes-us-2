Types of Orthodontic Treatment

Are you looking for an orthodontist in Iowa City, Fairfield, Washington, or Williamsburg, IA? Call 319-338-8658 to schedule an appointment at one of our four locations in Eastern Iowa.

  • Metal Braces Made from stainless steel, traditional metal braces and wires are still the most popular types of braces as they produce high quality results. We combine the use of advanced technology with metal brackets to decrease treatment time and improve efficiency.
  • CLARITY™ Clear Braces Virtually invisible, 3M™ CLARITY™ clear braces are an excellent alternative to traditional braces. Clear braces combine the efficacy of traditional metal braces and the discretion of invisible aligners to deliver an exceptional treatment experience. Learn More
  • Clear Aligners For patients seeking an alternative to wearing traditional braces, Clear Aligners can offer effective orthodontic treatment that utilizes clear aligners and specialized features to straighten your smile more comfortably and conveniently than other orthodontic treatments. Learn More
  • Orthodontic Appliances Designed to address or prevent a variety of orthodontic concerns, orthodontic appliances can be an effective part of your orthodontic plan and can be used during any phase of treatment. Learn More
  • Orthognathic Surgery Orthognathic Surgery is used to correct misalignments or other abnormalities in the jaws and teeth. Using advanced technology to construct an overview of your facial structure, we will create a treatment plan that offers improved function and appearance. Learn More
  • Retention Once your braces are removed, it is time to celebrate and enjoy your smile - But the most important step is still to come! Wearing your retainer helps maintain your new smile and also allows for small adjustments. Learn More
