Because whitening is supervised by a dental professional, Dr. Patel can evaluate your teeth and gums before treatment and help determine which option is most appropriate for you. Dental Veneers Dental veneers are thin, custom-made shells bonded to the front surfaces of the teeth. They may be used to improve the appearance of teeth that are chipped, worn, uneven, discolored, or separated by small gaps.

Each veneer is designed to blend naturally with the surrounding teeth. Dr. Patel will evaluate your smile, discuss your goals, and explain whether veneers or another treatment would be the most appropriate option. Personalized Smile Makeovers A smile makeover combines one or more cosmetic or restorative treatments to address several concerns within a personalized plan.

Depending on your needs, a smile makeover may include:

  • Red wine
  • Certain foods
  • Tobacco use
  • Natural aging
  • Shape of a tooth
  • Apparent length of a tooth
  • Color of the smile
  • Symmetry between teeth
  • Appearance of small chips or cracks
  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Dental veneers
  • Tooth-colored fillings
  • Dental crowns
  • Replacement of missing teeth
