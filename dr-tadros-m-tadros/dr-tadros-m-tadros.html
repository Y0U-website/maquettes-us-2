Dr. Tadros M. Tadros

Dr. Tadros earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Washington School of Dentistry in Seattle, WA, where he received the Dental Practice Administration Award. He then pursued his Endodontic Certificate of Advanced Graduate Studies at Boston University School of Dental Medicine, receiving the Certificate of Recognition for Clinical Achievements. Dr. Tadros is also an associate professor at Tufts School of Dental Medicine.

Recently, Dr. Tadros was promoted to Major in the US Army Reserve Dental Corps and serves at the 804th Medical Brigade at Fort Devens, MA. Additionally, he was honored as a Fellow of the American College of Dentists (ACD), a prestigious recognition awarded to those who demonstrate exceptional leadership, contributions to dentistry, and commitment to professional ethics.

In 2019, Dr. Tadros became a Diplomate of the American Board of Endodontics , which made him the first and only Board Certified Endodontist in Hudson and Nashua. He serves as an Associate Clinical Professor at the Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. He is also on staff at Franciscan Children’s Hospital in Boston, where he provides specialized root canal treatments for special needs children.

Dr. Tadros is actively involved in professional organizations, including the American Association of Endodontists (AAE), American Dental Association (ADA), New Hampshire Dental Society (NHDS), and the Greater Nashua Dental Society . Fluent in English, French, and Arabic, Dr. Tadros, originally from Egypt, currently resides in Northern Massachusetts.

In his free time, he enjoys tennis, hiking, canoeing, and cooking, and can often be found exploring New England’s great outdoors with his wife, Grace and their dog Nova.
