The practice

Southside Dental Clinic provides comprehensive dental care by offering services such as preventive care (cleanings), restorations (fillings), crowns & bridges, extractions, implant restorations, implant dentures, partial dentures, and full dentures. Our in-office laboratory allows us to provide Same-day Custom Fit Dentures. We can also repair and reline your denture in one day!

Our goal at Southside Dental Clinic is to help each patient achieve the smile they desire by promoting good oral health practices and by providing quality dental care.
