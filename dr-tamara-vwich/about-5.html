Meet The Doctors

Dr. Tamara Vwich (Owner)

Dr. Vwich has earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Southern California (USC) in 2003. After graduating, she attended advanced training in the different fields of Dentistry including Oral Surgery, Prosthodontics, Pediatrics, Orthodontics and Periodontics. Dr. Vwich has special interest and passion for Cosmetic Dentistry in which she got further education and training at the UCLA Esthetic Continuum Program.

Dr. Vwich continues to stay current with the newest technologies and developments in the field of Dentistry through attending continuing education programs. Some of the numerous courses she finished include: The UCLA Esthetic Continuum, The Essentials of Occlusion, Implants in the Esthetic Zone, Advanced Endodontics, Whitening Procedures, and Radiation Safety. She also attended many national and international dental conferences.

Dr. Vwich is a member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, San Fernando Dental Society, and USC Endodontic study Club. She was a selected member of the dental curriculum review at USC.

Dr. Vwich is dedicated to her patients’ dental health and education, and believes in providing the optimum dental care in the most comfortable and caring environment. She has been a Palmdale/Lancaster dentist for over 10 years.

Dr. Vwich is married and has one kid, enjoys playing the piano, reading, travelling, and swimming and resides in the AV area.
