Our Dental Practice

Sunnyside Dental is a trusted name in family dentistry in Queens. Dr. Tatyana Yapport leads our experienced team with a commitment to providing exceptional dental care in a comfortable environment.

Our mission is simple: we believe everyone deserves a healthy, beautiful smile in a stress-free environment. That’s why we’ve designed our practice with your comfort in mind, from our soothing office atmosphere to our gentle treatment approach.

We take pride in offering our community a comprehensive range of dental services, from preventive care to more complex treatments, all performed with the highest standards of excellence and patient care.

Meet Dr. Tatyana Yapport

Dr. Tatyana Yapport has been providing exceptional dental care in the Sunnyside community for over 10 years. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree and has dedicated her career to helping patients achieve optimal oral health and beautiful smiles.

Dr. Yapport is committed to staying at the forefront of dental techniques and technology through continuing education. Her warm, compassionate approach has earned her the trust and loyalty of patients of all ages.

Outside the office, Dr. Yapport enjoys spending time with her family and contributing to community dental health initiatives.

Our Modern Dental Office

At Sunnyside Dental, we’ve created a warm, inviting environment equipped with state-of-the-art technology for all your dental needs. Our office features advanced diagnostic and treatment equipment that enables us to provide efficient, precise, and comfortable care.

Our friendly, professional staff are dedicated to making your visit as pleasant as possible. From the moment you walk in, you’ll experience the perfect combination of modern dentistry and personalized attention that makes Sunnyside Dental special.

Dr. Yapport’s gentle approach and exceptional chairside manner ensure that even patients with dental anxiety feel comfortable and at ease throughout their treatment.
