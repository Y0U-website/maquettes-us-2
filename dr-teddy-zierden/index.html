The practice

With a passion for helping people and a dedication to excellence, Dr. Zierden provides individualized care built on trust, honesty, and a gentle touch.

Our friendly, experienced dental team shares Dr. Zierden’s philosophy of compassion and professionalism. From the front desk to the operatory, we aim to create a calm and reassuring experience for every visit.

We are proud to serve Sidney, OH and surrounding areas with consistent care and strong community values. Many of our patients have trusted us for years — and we’re honored to support generations of healthy smiles.

At the office of Dr. Teddy Zierden, we believe dental care should be gentle, accessible, and tailored to your individual needs. Located in the heart of Sidney, OH, we specialize in general and restorative dentistry for patients of all ages.
