Mouthguards to Prevent Snoring

With a thorough examination, and usually some X-rays, we can determine if a mouthguard will help you to stop snoring. The shape of your airway and septum can affect the way air moves through your airway as you sleep.

Snoring is a result of air flow causing excessive vibrations in the soft tissues of the upper airway. Mouthguards work to prevent snoring by transitioning your lower jaw slightly down and forward, which allows air to move through your airway more easily. Since you snore while you are asleep, you may not be aware that it is happening. Below are some good indicators that you snore at night and might benefit from a mouthguard:

  • You wake up with a sore throat
  • You don’t feel rested when you wake up and are tired throughout the day
  • You don’t sleep soundly and are restless throughout the night.
