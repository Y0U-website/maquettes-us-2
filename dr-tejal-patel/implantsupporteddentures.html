Implant Supported Dentures

What are Implant-Supported Dentures?

An implant-supported denture is a type of overdenture that are attached and supported by dental implant. A traditional dentures rests on you gums rather than the dental implants. Implant-supported dentures are used when a person doesn’t have any teeth.

Do you need an Implant-Supported Denture?

Why Implant-Supported Denture is better than Dentures or Bridges?

the most important advantages of dental implant-supported denture are:

1. Preserve jaw bone recession. 2. Improve comfort & minimize your speech impediments. 3. You can have a firm bite. 4. preserve your facial contour & beauty.

Types of Implant-Supported Dentures & Bridges

Implant-supported dentures or bridge are categorized into two main types: 1. Fixed Implant Supported Bridge or Dentures 2. Removable Implant-Supported Bridge or Dentures

Denture Stabilization Implant

Consist of minimum 2 implants per arch. Provides needed support for correct speaking, chewing, and smiling.

Implant-Supported Overdenture Removable

It is a removable prosthesis that attaches in some manner to dental implants. Consists of 4 or more dental implants.
