BPA-, Aluminum-, and Metal-Free Materials

Many conventional dental restorative materials may contain substances such as BPA, aluminum, or other metals. Dr. Isbell is committed to using biocompatible, BPA-free, aluminum-free, and metal-free restorative materials whenever clinically appropriate. By selecting materials designed with both safety and compatibility in mind, the practice aims to provide durable restorations while supporting each patient’s overall health and well-being.

Our practice does not use or recommend fluoride. Instead, we focus on a biologic approach to oral health by recommending oral probiotics and other products that support the mouth’s natural balance. We believe in promoting healthy teeth and gums through preventive care, nutrition, and biocompatible treatment options tailored to each patient’s needs.

With a simple blood draw from your practitioner, you now have the ability to check your dental material compatibility against thousands of name-brand products. If there is a reactive chemical in the product, it is marked as non-suitable. This lab provides a report of results by product name and by category.
