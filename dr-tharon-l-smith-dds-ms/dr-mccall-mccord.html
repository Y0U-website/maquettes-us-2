Dr. McCall McCord

McCall McCord, DDS, MS

Dr. McCall McCord is a proud Utah County native. She graduated from Utah Valley University where she was awarded the Presidential Scholarship and honored as valedictorian of her college. She then attended dental school at the University of Utah, finishing as one of the top students in her class. During her dental training, Dr. McCord completed an orthodontic externship working with a craniofacial team at Primary Children’s Hospital.

Following dental school, Dr. McCord moved to St. Louis, Missouri, to complete an advanced orthodontic residency program and master’s degree at Saint Louis University. During her residency, she treated a wide variety of patient cases and gained clinical expertise in using digital technology to perfect patient smiles.Dr. McCord loves getting to know her patients and customizing her treatment to their individual needs. She is especially passionate about creating excellent outcomes centered on beautiful aesthetics crafted for each patient’s unique smile.

When not in the office, Dr. McCord enjoys spending time with her husband, Taylor, and her daughter, Ivy. She also loves hiking, running, cycling, skiing, and many other outdoor activities.
