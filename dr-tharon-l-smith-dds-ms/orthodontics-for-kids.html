When Should My Child See an Orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists® recommends that children have their first orthodontic screening by age 7. At this age, many children have a mix of baby teeth and permanent teeth, which allows our orthodontists to evaluate how the teeth, jaws and bite are developing.

An early orthodontic visit does not always mean your child needs braces right away. In many cases, we simply monitor their growth until the right time for treatment. However, early orthodontic care can help identify issues before they become more complex.

Parents in Pleasant Grove, Spanish Fork, Saratoga Springs and Vineyard often schedule an early orthodontic evaluation if they notice crowding, spacing, bite issues, mouth breathing, thumb sucking or teeth that do not seem to be coming in correctly.

What Is Early Orthodontic Treatment?

Early orthodontic treatment, also called interceptive orthodontics or preventive orthodontics , is designed to guide your child’s teeth, jaws and bite while they are still growing. This type of care can help correct developing orthodontic problems early and may make future treatment shorter, simpler or more effective.

Interceptive orthodontic treatment may be recommended if your child has:

Our goal is to help your child develop proper oral function, a balanced bite and a confident smile.

  • Crowded teeth
  • Spacing between teeth
  • Protruding front teeth
  • Crossbite, overbite or underbite
  • Jaw growth concerns
  • Teeth that are not erupting properly
  • Thumb sucking, finger sucking or tongue thrusting habits
  • Airway or breathing concerns
  • Speech concerns related to oral development
  • Difficulty biting or chewing comfortably
