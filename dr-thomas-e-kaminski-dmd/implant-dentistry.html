Implant Dentistry

GORGEOUS DENTAL IMPLANT RESTORATIONS

For most patients, dental implants are the preferred method of replacing missing teeth. Unlike dentures, dental implants are permanent replacements that most closely approximate the look, feel and function of your natural teeth.

The typical dental implant consists of three parts: a titanium "root" that’s implanted into the jawbone, an abutment that connects the titanium root to a dental crown and the crown or restoration itself. This last part is the part you and others see when you smile. That’s why Dr. Thomas E. Kaminiski creates precision-crafted dental restorations that look and feel like your real teeth.

ALL ON 4

This is a newer type of dental implant that actually replaces all your upper or lower teeth using just four individual implants for each arch. Instead of having 14 individual dental implants to replace all of your bottom teeth, All on 4 replaces them all using just four titanium implants that anchor a full denture.

Dr. Kaminski is an implant restoration dentist who can help you determine which type of implant solution is best for your individual needs in Green Bay.
