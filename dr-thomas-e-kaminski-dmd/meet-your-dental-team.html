Meet Your Dental Team

THOMAS E. KAMINSKI, DMD

Dr. Tom is the youngest in a family of four. He grew up in a dental family, with his father being an orthodontist and his brother becoming a general dentist several years before him. He has always had an interest in the field.

Support Staff

Stacey Clark, Office Manager

“Patients who walk through our door come from all walks of life, but when they leave they have one thing in common and that is the smile, that’s my favorite part is seeing those smiles, and being a part of the amazing dental team that made that smile possible.”

Stacey has over 20 years of experience in customer service and in the insurance industry. Stacey has worked within dental offices as well as in a large dental insurance company. With her experience in both the Dental office and the Insurance Company she delights in helping our patients understand their insurance plans and coverage.

Stacey has one grown son, Tyler, and a beautiful daughter-in-law, Tara. Stacey is anxiously awaiting her time to be a Grandma (Gigi) but for now she is proud of her fur grandpuppies, Bud and Liberty.She also has her own fur baby, Oliver. Stacey enjoys crafting with her best friend and spending time with her family including her 9 nieces and 4 nephews and 4 great nieces and 4 great nephews.
