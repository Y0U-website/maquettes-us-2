Dr. Jeff Graceffo, DMD

Jeff Graceffo is an Auburn native, who graduated from Auburn High School in 2005. He went on to earn a BS in psychology at St. Lawrence University from 2005-2009. After a year of working in the dental field Jeff pursued his degree at the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, graduating in 2014, then completed a yearlong General Practice Residency at Tufts Dental School. Jeff is a member of the 7th District Dental committee. Jeff is also certified in CEREC®, CBCT, and Dental Implants.
