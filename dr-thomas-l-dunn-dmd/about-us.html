About Us

At his practice in Adamsville, Dr. Dunn combines decades of expertise with a deep-rooted commitment to his patients. His services range from essential preventive care and restorative dentistry to advanced facial esthetics, ensuring a comprehensive and stress-free experience for patients of all ages.

Outside of the office, Dr. Dunn and his wife, Leigh, are active members of Hunter Street Baptist Church. When they aren’t serving in their church community, they enjoy Alabama football and basketball, traveling, and finding time to read and relax at the beach.

MEET DR. Jack

Dr. Jack Cutrer

DENTIST

Dr. Jack Cutrer was born and raised in Ridgeland, MS. He graduated from Mississippi State in 2018 with a degree in Accounting. He received a Masters in Biomedical Sciences from the University of Mississippi Medical Center (UMMC) in 2019, followed by his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from UMMC in 2023. After moving to Birmingham in July 2023, he spent 9 months practicing part time at two different offices in Jasper and Trussville.

Dr. Jack is married to Mrs. Jordan Cutrer, who is a Registered Nurse at Alabama Oncology. When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Jack enjoys playing basketball or pickleball, spending time with his wife and dog (Nellie), and hanging out with friends, and watching sports. Dr. Jack and Jordan are active members in their church, Faith Presbyterian church where they both serve in the youth ministry.
