The practice

The Dr. Nykamp’s Dental Office has barrier-free access and is conveniently located at 2540 Woodmeadow St. SE, Grand Rapids. which is a few blocks North on Breton Road from the intersection Breton Road and 28th Street SE.

If you have not previously been to Dr. Nykamp’s Dental Office on Woodmeadow St. SE, please take a look at the map as there are several dentist offices nearby on Breton Road and several other dentists located close by on Woodmeadow Street.

You can click here for a map . Some information about using the city bus line can be found here: How to Get to Dr. Nykamp’s Office .

A 1979 graduate of Calvin College and 1983 graduate of the University of Michigan School of Dentistry , Dr. Thomas Nykamp established his Grand Rapids Dentist practice in 1984.

Dr. Nykamp is a member of the West Michigan District Dental Society and the Michigan Dental Association .

Our Southeast Grand Rapids Dentist office participates in many insurance plans, in fact there are simply too many to list. However, if you don’t see yours listed, please call the office or use our online contact form to make an inquiry regarding your specific dental plan.

My Dental Plan covers only a portion of the cost of this major dental expense — What can I do?

Major dental procedures can be expensive and can be unexpected. Often even the best Dental Plans only cover a limited amount of the cost and the rest is up to you.
