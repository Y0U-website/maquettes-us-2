The practice

Our practice has been serving patients of all ages across Rochester, NY, for over 50 years. We offer a wide range of general and cosmetic services to provide a comprehensive approach to dental care. Our goal is to help our patients prevent dental issues from arising, and to promptly, gently, and successfully treat those that do. From hygiene visits to teeth whitening and Invisalign® to more…

We are proud to offer the Sonicare toothbrush, a professional model that patients can purchase at a discounted rate and with rebates.
