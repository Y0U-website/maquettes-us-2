Dental Implants

Implants in Gaithersburg, MD

Tooth loss can be tragic, impacting your speech, ability to chew, and self-esteem. Whether the loss is due to accident or disease, many patients feel that they do not have good options when it comes to tooth replacement. This feeling can lead patients to simply leave their missing teeth unreplaced.

The Benefits

Dental implants may be the perfect solution for you if you are missing one or more teeth. Missing teeth can make it difficult to chew, speak clearly, and feel confident in your smile because of gaps. Implants will help fill those spaces and maintain jaw bone levels and strength. Unlike dentures, implants are fixed and long-term, giving you confidence that they won’t shift or fall out while you’re eating or talking.
