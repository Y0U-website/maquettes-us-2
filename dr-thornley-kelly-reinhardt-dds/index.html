Dr. Sarah Thornley, a proud Spring Lake, Michigan native, was born and raised with a strong appreciation for dentistry, having grown up in a dental household where her father, Dr. Dennis Thornley, has long served the community as a dedicated dentist. Much the same as Dr. Kelly, Sarah spent time in the office, observing and learning, and later worked as a dental assistant during summers…

Dr. Sarah pursued her undergraduate studies at the University of Michigan, earning a bachelor’s degree in psychology, which strengthened her understanding of patient care communication. She went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from A. T. Still in Missouri, where her commitment to serve others and promoting oral health within her community continued to grow.

Dr. Sarah is a member of the American Dental Association, the Michigan Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry. She is dedicated to lifelong learning and staying current with advancements in dental science and techniques.

Dr. Reinhardt attended the University of Detroit Mercy; where she received her bachelor’s degree in Chemistry and went on to achieve her Doctorate of Dental Surgery. Dr. Dennis Thornley was born in Japan while his father was stationed there with the U.S. Army. His family returned to Spring Lake when he was young, and he graduated from Spring Lake High School. He did his undergraduate studies at Muskegon Community College and Grand Valley State University and received his DDS from the University of Detroit Dental School. He joined our practice in 1994.

Dr. Dennis and his wife, Pam, have four children. He is a member of the American Dental Association, the Michigan Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry.
