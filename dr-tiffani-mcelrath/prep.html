We have locations in Inkster , Taylor , Lincoln Park , and Dearborn . We are community health centers and accept most health insurance coverage, including Medicaid. We also have a sliding fee scale based on income if you don’t have insurance so the cost can be very reasonable.

Call 313-561-5100 for an appointment to see one of our health providers, who will connect you to a CHW.

We have family practice and internal medicine nurse practitioners and doctors you can see for this and all your primary care needs.
