Western Wayne Family Health Centers are your one-stop health and wellness care providers!

Our skilled providers offer medical and dental care for both adults and kids, women’s health care, and behavioral health services.

We’re not only here for you when you get sick we’re here to help you stay healthy, too! Community health centers like ours are unique in that we offer community health workers who will partner with you to create a wellness program that meets your goals. We also hold regular classes to help those living with chronic conditions such as diabetes or hypertension, as well as general fitness and cooking classes.

We accept most insurance plans, including all state Medicaid/Healthy Michigan plans. We also offer a sliding fee scale for anyone who is uninsured.

Make an Appointment

Open Monday through Friday, with appointment times available throughout the day.

If you are a new patient, please call us at 313-561-5100 to make an appointment; returning patients can make an appointment using the patient portal .

You can often get an appointment on the same day, or even walk in on a first-come, first-served basis. If you do plan to walk in, it’s best to show up early and prepare to wait until a provider is available. Dental emergency walk-in patients are seen Monday through Friday, from 8:30 am to 3 pm, to the best of our ability.

If you are having a medical emergency, please call 911 or go to the closest emergency room for care.

For patients with after-hours urgent care situations, you can call our main number and you’ll be connected with our answering service. They’ll contact the on-call provider for you, who will call you back as soon as possible.

Appointment Arrival Times

New Patients

Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled time to complete the registration process. Bring your driver’s license/government ID (or other photo ID) and insurance cards. If you don’t have insurance, bring proof of household income: tax forms, paycheck stubs, bank statements, or MDHHS eligibility forms.

Returning Patients

Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your appointment time to sign in and do any updates to your information that may be needed. Bring your driver’s license/government ID and insurance cards.

Coordinated Health & Wellness Care

Our Community Health Workers (CHWs) are at the heart of how we operate as a community health center. They’re a bit like your coach and are here to support you in achieving your wellness goals!

CHWs can provide help and guidance on a variety of topics, including:

We also offer nutrition, cooking, wellness, chronic disease management, fitness, and exercise classes that are open to the community.

  • Ideas, tips, referrals, and recommendations for community-based health services
  • Improving your nutrition or learning how to eat better
  • Stopping tobacco use smoking, chewing, or vaping
  • Tips on medication and chronic condition management
  • Connecting you to resources you need to live better

Adult Medical Care

All your health and wellness care in one place!

We offer primary medical care for adults, including the following:

  • Annual check-ups/wellness exams and physicals
  • Management of chronic health conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, and asthma
  • Preventive health screenings and testing
  • Coordinated care and wellness coaching
  • Substance use disorder treatment

Dental Care

Affordable dental care for children and adults.

A healthy mouth is essential for a healthy body! Our dental care team offers affordable, general family dentistry for all ages, including:

We accept most dental insurance plans, including all state Medicaid/Healthy Michigan plans. We also offer a sliding fee scale for anyone who is uninsured.

  • Regular cleanings, exams, and X-rays
  • Cavity filling
  • Extractions
  • Dentures and partials
  • Root canals
  • Treatment of infections
  • Emergency dental care

Pediatric Medical Care

We love helping kids establish lifelong healthy habits! Our pediatric medical services include:

  • Well-child visits / annual physicals and exams
  • Sick child visits
  • Immunizations
  • School, camp, and sport-related physicals
  • Growth and development assessments
  • Care for chronic conditions such as asthma or allergies

Women’s Health Care

Women have unique health and wellness needs depending on their stage of life, and we’re here for you every step of the way!

You can trust our doctors and nurse practitioners to provide exceptional care, including:

  • Annual wellness exams
  • Evaluations and support for menstrual issues
  • Cervical and Breast Cancer screenings
  • Birth control, including tubal ligation
  • Testing and treatment of infections, including sexually-transmitted
  • Prenatal and delivery care
  • New baby wellness care and assistance
  • Menopause care

Behavioral Health

We practice “integrated care” — which means that your behavioral health treatment starts with a primary care visit.

Our community health centers are designed to treat the whole person! We offer an integrated, holistic approach to behavioral health because we know that your physical and mental health are often interconnected.

You’ll begin with a primary care visit so that your doctor or nurse practitioner can refer you to the behavioral health services that best meet your needs.

Each of our locations offers psychiatric care coordinated by social workers (MSWs), counselors (LPCs) or psychologists (MA, MS, or Ph.D.), including:

  • Counseling and therapy
  • Psychiatry and psychiatric medication, offered via on-site telepsychiatry
  • Evaluation and treatment for depression, anxiety, grief & loss, and trauma
  • Medication-assisted treatment (MAT) for substance use disorder, including opiate use

Partners in Community Health

Your health and wellness is about more than just going to the doctor or the dentist. Our integrated approach to care means that we understand that many of the challenges we face in life can have a negative impact on health.

WWFHC partners with community organizations that can provide assistance and guidance on a variety of issues, including:

  • Utility assistance
  • Debt and financial management
  • Job placement
  • Early childhood education
  • Transitioning from nursing or care facilities to home
  • Mental health and counseling services
