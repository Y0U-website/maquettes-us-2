0 Tiffany Wade DDS Dr. Tiffany Wade! Dr. Wade was born and raised in Blythe, California. After graduating from Palo Verde Valley High School she earned her Bachelors of Science degree in Dental Hygiene from the University of Hawaii at Manoa.

Dr. Wade worked as a registered dental hygienist for 4 years in Los Angeles and the Coachella Valley until she was accepted into dental school. Dr. Wade attended dental school at the University of the Pacific in San Francisco, California. She graduated with her doctorate with high honors and was inducted into three honors societies. She began working under the guidance of Dr. David Choi and Dr. Richard Brown before purchasing her current dental practice in Blythe, California.

Dr. Wade is married with two children. She is thorough, thoughtful, and loves the opportunity to serve the community that has given so much to her.

Dr. Wade’s Office

Since 2018 Dr. Wade has continuously made improvements to the office inside and out. She has invested in a complete interior remodel with upgraded dental equipment and even a new parking lot!
