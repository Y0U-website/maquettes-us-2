What Are The Types of Dental Implants?

Single Tooth Implants: This solution involves replacing one tooth with a dental implant topped with a crown. The implant is surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as an artificial tooth root. Once healed, a crown is attached via an abutment, offering a seamless and natural-looking result that blends perfectly with your existing teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridges: Perfect for individuals with multiple missing teeth, implant-supported bridges use dental implants as anchors, offering significant strength and stability. The implants are strategically placed in the jawbone, and the bridge is securely attached, filling in the gaps and restoring function and aesthetics. This treatment addresses tooth loss effectively.

Full Mouth Dental Implants - All-on-4 Implants: Designed for those who are missing all of their teeth, the All-on-4 system provides a final set of teeth supported by only four implants. These implants are strategically placed to maximize stability and support, offering a secure and functional prosthesis that allows for normal speech and eating, improving quality of life. This entire arch restoration gives you new teeth that function like natural ones.

Are You a Good Candidate for Implants?

Ideal candidates for dental implants are those with healthy gums and enough bone to support the implant. Additionally, maintaining good oral hygiene and visiting the dentist regularly is essential. Patients who need extra support may also benefit from bone grafting at our clinic to rebuild the jaw before placement. If you’re considering implants, our team can help assess your suitability during an initial consultation and build a care plan rooted in compassionate, personalized care.
