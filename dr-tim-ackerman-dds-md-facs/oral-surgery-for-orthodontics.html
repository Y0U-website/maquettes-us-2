Oral Surgery for Orthodontics

  • Improved Oral Health: By correcting jaw or teeth irregularities, oral surgery reduces the risk of dental complications, contributing to overall oral health. Patients dealing with jaw joint pain, bad bites, or obstructive sleep apnea may find meaningful relief through surgical orthodontics.
  • Functional and Aesthetic Benefits: The combination of surgery and orthodontics enhances your smile and improves functions like biting, chewing, and speech. Surgical treatment can also correct an unbalanced facial appearance, boosting self-esteem and delivering a healthy new smile.

Surgical Procedures Offered

  • Tooth Exposure: This procedure involves revealing impacted or unerupted teeth to facilitate their inclusion in orthodontic appliances, ensuring proper alignment.
  • Extractions: Necessary for removing problematic teeth that obstruct orthodontic treatments, extractions are performed with precision to aid in successful alignment outcomes.
  • Temporary Anchorage Devices (TADs) : Small titanium devices placed in the mouth to provide stable support for moving teeth into their desired positions.
  • Orthognathic Surgery: Corrects jaw discrepancies that cannot be addressed with braces alone, improving both functional and aesthetic outcomes. As a form of corrective jaw surgery, this major treatment repositions the upper and lower jaws under general anesthesia. The actual surgery typically takes several hours, after which orthodontic braces are used to fine-tune your bite and complete your new smile.
  • Impacted Canines: Specialized procedures to guide unerupted canine teeth into place, essential for a balanced and aligned smile.
