Meet Our Team

We’re more than a dental office we’re a team that genuinely cares about you. The people you’ll meet here have been building real relationships with patients and families for a very long time. That’s not an accident. It’s just how we do things.

Dr. Caleb Fuller, DDS

Four hygienists. Decades of experience.

Our hygiene team is the heart of this practice. Between them they’ve cleaned teeth in this office longer than we can count and patients who’ve been coming for decades will tell you they’re a big part of why they keep coming back.

Cheryl

Cheryl has been a familiar face around here for years she worked with us as a temp hygienist before joining our team permanently this year. She’s here three days a week, and honestly, we wonder how we managed without her.

Tammy

Tammy has been part of our team for nearly as long as we can remember starting as a part-timer and going full time a few years ago. She’s married with two kids and one very important canine son, Thor, a beautiful Labrador Retriever. Her patients love her warm, caring approach and keep coming back to prove it.

Lynn

Lynn has been with us since the very beginning one of the originals, and still going strong. Her patients adore her gentle touch and follow her loyally, year after year. Outside the office, Lynn is a proud mom and grandmother with a creative spirit and a serious talent for making candy. She is, quite simply, beloved by all.

Michelle

Michelle joined our team this year and has already made herself right at home and so have our patients with her. A Baker College graduate with her hygiene license since 2013, she brings experience, enthusiasm, and a smile that’s hard to miss. Outside the office, Michelle is a busy mom of two (ages 17 and 11) who stays active with workouts, walking, rollerblading, and baking. We’re so glad she’s here!

Hands-on care, every step of the way.

Liz

Liz has been with us for three years and has quickly become an invaluable part of our team. As our lead assistant, she keeps things running smoothly chairside every day. She’s currently studying dental hygiene at Wayne County Community College and we’re hoping she’ll make our office her lifelong home. Something tells us she just might.

Diana

Diana has been part of our team for two years and works most closely with Dr. Fuller. She’s a familiar, friendly face chairside and brings a calm, caring presence to every appointment. When she’s not here, you’ll find her soaking up time with her family.

Amber

Amber is our newest team member and a recent graduate and she has already made a wonderful impression. Her enthusiasm for dentistry, warm personality, and genuine passion for patient care make her a natural fit for our team. You’ll know her by her smile!
