The practice

Our Invisalign and orthodontic treatments go beyond straightening teeth. Each case is carefully designed and planned with attention to bite alignment, jaw position, facial balance, and airway health to deliver both aesthetic and functional improvements.

Dr. Gross offers mercury-free dentistry and safely removes amalgam fillings using protective protocols, replacing them with durable, tooth-colored materials. His holistic approach supports whole-body health while staying grounded in modern science.

Dr. Gross provides FDA-approved oral appliances for sleep apnea, gently repositioning the jaw to support airway health. Using advanced neuromuscular training, he designs comfortable, functional appliances to improve breathing and sleep.
