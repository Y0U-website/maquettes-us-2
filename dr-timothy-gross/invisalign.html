The Benefits of Invisalign® in Hilton Head, SC

One of the biggest advantages of Invisalign® is convenience. Because the aligners are removable, they offer flexibility that traditional braces cannot. Key benefits include:

Discreet Appearance The clear trays are nearly invisible when worn.

Comfort No brackets or wires to irritate the cheeks or lips.

Removability Aligners can be removed for meals and special occasions.

No Dietary Restrictions Eat the foods you enjoy without concern.

Improved Hygiene Brushing and flossing remain simple and effective.

Fewer Emergencies No broken brackets or poking wires.

For professionals, parents, and teens who want orthodontic correction without the look of traditional braces, Invisalign® provides an appealing option. Our team at Timothy Gross, DMD, can help you start the process to a straighter smile when you call us for a consultation today!

What Can Invisalign ® Correct?

At our Hilton Head Island practice, Invisalign® is not simply a cosmetic service. It is part of a comprehensive orthodontic philosophy that prioritizes both aesthetics and long-term function. Invisalign® is capable of addressing many mild to moderate orthodontic concerns. In experienced hands, it can treat more complex cases as well.

  • Crowded Teeth: Overlapping or rotated teeth.
  • Spacing Issues: Gaps between teeth.
  • Overbite and Underbite: Vertical and horizontal discrepancies.
  • Crossbite: Improper alignment of upper and lower teeth.
  • Relapse: Teeth that have shifted after previous orthodontic treatment.

Invisalign ® and Function

While Invisalign® is often chosen for its cosmetic appeal, proper bite alignment is equally important. Misalignment can contribute to uneven wear, muscle strain, and jaw discomfort.

Dr. Gross evaluates every Invisalign® case with attention to:

  • Bite Balance: Ensuring even contact between upper and lower teeth.
  • Jaw Comfort: Reducing strain on the temporomandibular joints.
  • Long Term Stability: Creating alignment that lasts.
  • Proper Alignment: Misaligned teeth can cause uneven pressure.

Are You a Candidate?

Successful Invisalign® treatment requires commitment. Aligners must be worn for at least 22 hours per day and only removed for eating, drinking anything other than water, brushing, and flossing. Ideal Invisalign® candidates are:

Dr. Gross will carefully assess your bite and discuss all options before recommending a treatment plan.

  • Motivated adults or teens who are willing to follow instructions carefully
  • Patients with mild to moderate issues seeking discreet correction
  • Individuals preparing for cosmetic treatment, such as veneers or reconstruction

Invisalign ® vs Braces

Although Invisalign® is highly effective, it is not the best option for every patient. Severe or complex bite discrepancies may be better addressed with traditional bracket and wire orthodontics. During your consultation, Dr. Gross will evaluate if Invisalign is right for you. If you are unable to wear your aligners for 22 hours a day, whether you will forget or find them uncomfortable, bracketed braces may be better. If Invisalign® is not ideal for your situation, alternatives such as ceramic braces or traditional metal braces may be recommended.
