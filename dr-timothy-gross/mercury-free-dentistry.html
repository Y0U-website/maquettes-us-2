Safe Mercury Removal

Removing older amalgam fillings requires considerably more planning than placing a new filling. Our goal is to minimize unnecessary exposure while protecting both our patients and our clinical team throughout the procedure. When patients choose to replace mercury-containing restorations, we follow a carefully designed protocol intended to reduce mercury vapor and particulate exposure during treatment. Our mercury removal protocol includes:

These techniques significantly reduce airborne particles and help create a cleaner, safer treatment environment. Every procedure at Timothy Gross, DMD is performed thoughtfully and with careful attention to both patient comfort and clinical precision.

  • Non-latex rubber dam isolation to protect surrounding oral tissues
  • High-volume evacuation throughout the procedure
  • Continuous sterile water irrigation
  • Sectioning amalgam fillings into larger pieces rather than excessive grinding
  • Thorough cleaning of the prepared tooth before placing the new restoration
  • Careful handling and disposal of removed amalgam materials

Environmental Responsibility at Our Dental Office

Mercury-free dentistry is not only about caring for our patients. It is also about protecting our community and the environment. Dental amalgam must be handled according to strict environmental regulations because mercury should never enter standard wastewater systems.

To support responsible environmental practices, our office utilizes a dedicated mercury filtration system that captures and separates mercury particles before wastewater leaves our practice. This system helps reduce environmental contamination, protect local waterways, meet environmental regulations, and support responsible dental practices.

We believe exceptional dentistry includes caring for both our patients and the community we serve throughout Hilton Head Island and the surrounding Lowcountry.

Frequently Asked Questions About Mercury-Free Dentistry

Should I replace all of my silver fillings?

Not necessarily. While many patients choose to replace older amalgam fillings, replacement is not always required.

During your examination, Dr. Gross carefully evaluates each restoration for signs of leakage, cracks, wear, or decay. If a filling is functioning well and the surrounding tooth is healthy, it may not need to be replaced. Our recommendations are always based on what is best for your long-term oral health.

Is removing mercury fillings safe?

Yes. When performed using the proper techniques, amalgam removal can be completed safely and comfortably. Our office follows a carefully designed mercury removal protocol that includes rubber dam isolation, high-volume suction, continuous water irrigation, sectioning the filling into larger pieces, and proper environmental filtration to minimize exposure during the procedure.

What materials do you use instead of silver fillings?

We restore teeth using modern tooth-colored composite fillings and high-quality ceramic restorations. These materials are selected for their strength, durability, aesthetics, and ability to bond directly to the natural tooth, helping preserve healthy tooth structure whenever possible.

Are tooth-colored fillings as durable as silver fillings?

Today’s composite materials have improved significantly over the years. When properly placed and maintained, composite restorations provide excellent longevity while offering the added benefits of bonding to the tooth and creating a more natural appearance.

Larger restorations may be better suited for ceramic inlays, onlays, or crowns, depending on the amount of remaining tooth structure.
