The Best Solution for Missing Teeth

When a single tooth or several teeth are lost, finding a replacement that truly restores your smile and quality of life is no small matter. Dental implants stand out as the top solution for missing teeth for several reasons:

Permanent & Stable

Dental implants use medical-grade titanium posts surgically placed in your jawbone. Unlike removable dentures, these posts fuse with the bone through osseointegration. The result is a stable, permanent solution that becomes a part of your mouth.

Natural Look & Feel

Implants are custom-designed to seamlessly match your natural teeth’ shape, color, and size. The result is a restoration so lifelike that others won’t be able to tell the difference.

Prevents Bone Loss

When teeth go missing, the jawbone no longer receives the needed stimulation, leading to bone loss. Dental implants act like natural roots, preserving the bone structure and supporting long-term oral health.

Restores Full Function

Chewing, speaking, and smiling should feel effortless. Dental implants restore your full dental function so you can enjoy your favorite foods and talk confidently.

Step-by-Step Dental Implant Placement in Sanger

Many patients wonder, What can I expect from dental implant placement? Here’s a step-by-step overview of the process at Prewitt Dental:

1. Consultation & 3D Imaging

Your dental implant journey begins with a thorough consultation and digital imaging. At our Sanger location, Dr. Garcia uses modern CBCT (cone-beam computed tomography) and intraoral scanners to assess bone density, map your oral structures, and plan your implant surgery with precision.

2. Implant Surgery

On the day of surgery, you’ll receive local anesthesia (with sedation options for enhanced comfort) before the titanium post is gently placed in the jawbone. Our advanced technology and minimally invasive techniques minimize discomfort and speed up recovery.

3. Healing Period

After placement, the healing period allows the titanium post to fuse securely to your jawbone. This process, called osseointegration, can take several weeks or a few months. You’ll return for regular checkups during this phase to ensure proper healing.

4. Abutment & Crown Placement

Once healing is complete, an abutment (connector piece) is attached to the implant, and your custom dental crown, bridge, or denture is secured in place. Our restorations blend perfectly with your existing teeth, delivering a comfortable, natural bite.

Implant-Supported Bridges

If you’re missing several teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge uses two or more implants to anchor a bridge securely without affecting neighboring natural teeth.

All-on-4® Implants

For patients needing full-arch restoration, the All-on-4® system uses just four strategically placed implants to support an entire set of upper or lower teeth. This option is efficient, stable, and often eliminates the need for bone grafting.

Dental Implants vs. Alternatives

It’s important to understand how dental implants compare to other tooth replacement options:

You invest in your smile and long-term oral health by choosing dental implants.

  • Dentures: While removable and generally more affordable upfront, they can slip or shift and feel less stable during eating and speaking. They also do not prevent bone loss.
  • Bridges: Dental bridges are fixed in place but usually require grinding down neighboring healthy teeth to anchor the bridge. Bridges typically last 5–15 years before needing replacement.
  • Implants: Dental implants do not rely on neighboring teeth, stimulate the jawbone to prevent bone loss, and can last 20 years or more with proper care, making them the longest-lasting restorative solution.

Most healthy adults with good oral health and adequate jawbones are candidates. Certain chronic conditions or insufficient bone may require additional procedures, such as bone grafting.
