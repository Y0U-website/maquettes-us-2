What Are Dental Implants? A dental implant is a small titanium post surgically positioned in your jawbone to replace the missing root portion of a tooth. After healing, it’s topped with a customized dental crown to replace the visible part of the tooth. Implants fuse directly with bone through osseointegration, providing unmatched strength and stability.

Implants can also anchor bridges, partial dentures, or full-arch replacements for multiple missing teeth. Their unparalleled integration with your jaw and gum tissue gives dental implants a fit, feel, and function that is virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth.

What are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

Dental implants are considered today’s gold standard for replacing missing teeth because they offer advantages that other options can’t match:

  • Natural Appearance: Customized implant crowns and prosthetics are crafted to blend in seamlessly with your smile.
  • Long-Lasting Solution: Dental implants are a permanent, lifelong restoration.
  • Preserve Face Shape: Implants stimulate healthy bone growth and prevent bone deterioration that leads to premature aging.
  • Unrivaled Strength: With implants replacing the root structure, you’ll enjoy excellent chewing power up to 90 % as strong as natural teeth!
  • Protects Other Teeth: Unlike conventional bridges, implants don’t require grinding down healthy teeth for anchors.
  • Improved Speech and Digestion: Implants help you eat, speak, and laugh confidently without slippage like dentures.

Why Replace Your Missing Teeth?

Missing teeth can negatively impact oral health and overall quality of life. Gaps allow remaining teeth to shift, increase the risk of decay and gum disease, impact diet and nutrition, alter facial appearance, and even cause bone loss over time. Dental implants provide predictable, permanent replacements that help prevent these issues.

What To Expect When Getting Dental Implants

The implant process at Prewitt Dental takes several months from start to finish, but each step is straightforward:

Consultation/Treatment Plan: We evaluate your case and create a customized plan using our in-house CT scanner for precise implant positioning.

Tooth Extractions (if needed): Depending on the treatment, we will need to extract any remaining damaged teeth.

Bone Grafting (if needed): Some patients require bone grafting to thicken areas of low bone density.

Implant Surgery: In an in-office surgical procedure, we precisely place the titanium posts into the jaw.

Osseointegration Period: You can wear temporary partial dentures while the implants fuse with bone over 3-6 months.

Final Impressions: Once healed, we take impressions to craft your customized implant crowns, bridges, or dentures.

Final Placement: Your new teeth are securely attached to complete the procedure.

How To Care For Dental Implants

With the right care and maintenance, your implant restorations can last 25+ years. Tips for long-term success include:

Compared to bridges or dentures that need regular replacement, dental implants are a wise investment in oral health and quality of life.

  • Brush and floss daily
  • Use interdental brushes around implants
  • Visit Prewitt Dental every six months for cleanings/checkups
  • Don’t chew on hard foods or non-food objects
  • Quit smoking and practice good oral hygiene
  • Let us know if parts become loose or damaged
