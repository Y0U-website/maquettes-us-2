Meet the Team

Timothy D. Sines, DDS

Dr. Timothy Sines was born and raised in Garrett County, Maryland. After graduating from Northern Garrett High School, Dr. Sines continued his education at Frostburg State University where he graduated Phi Eta Sigma with a degree in Biology. With degree in hand Dr. Sines moved to Baltimore, Maryland where he focused on his dental education. While studying at the University of Maryland Dental School, Dr. Sines earned a membership into the prestigious Gamma Pi Delta Prosthodontic Society as well as the Ferdinand 1.S. Odontological Society.

Dr. Sines graduated with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2004. He practices all facets of dentistry. This includes family, oral surgery, fixed and removable prosthodontics, fillings, root canal therapy and cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Sines had extensive training in oral surgery while at the University of Maryland Dental School and has attended numerous continuing education courses throughout his career to maintain a knowledge of the leading trends of dental therapy.

He lives with daughter, Myla and family, Dr. Sines enjoys all outdoor activities, including skiing and golf. He looks forward to meeting with you and ensuring you the best possible treatment.

Marissa Fletcher, DDS

Dr. Marissa Fletcher is a West Virginia native who was born and raised in Nicholas County. She attended West Virginia University, where she earned her bachelor’s degree in Biochemistry and master’s degree in Health Sciences. She continued her education at WVU, earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2022, followed by a General Practice Residency in 2023.

As a general dentist, Dr. Fletcher is passionate about making dental care a comfortable and positive experience. She takes a thoughtful, patient-centered approach to dentistry and enjoys educating her patients so they feel confident and informed about their oral health. She is committed to providing quality care while helping each patient feel at ease in the dental chair.

Outside of the office, Dr. Fletcher enjoys cooking, golfing, following Formula 1 racing, and spending time with her family. She also loves spending time with her dog, Nala, who is a big part of her life.

Dr. Fletcher feels fortunate to have a career that allows her to make a difference in the lives of her patients and looks forward to welcoming you to the practice.
