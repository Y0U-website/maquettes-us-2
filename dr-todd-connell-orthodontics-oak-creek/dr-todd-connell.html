ABOUT TODD

Dr. Connell is committed to lifelong learning and he has served as an adjunct associate professor at Marquette University’s highly regarded Dental School for more than 25 years.

His independent research applied laser interferometry to study the effects of maxillary expansion. Dr Connell’s recommendations are based on an understanding of the peer reviewed scientific literature and a realistic approach toward novel advances in technology. His concerns regarding overtreatment have been published in the Journal of the American Dental Association(JADA) and the American Journal of Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopedics (AJODO). His favorite things to talk about include missing laterals, aviation, and his grandchildren.

Dr Connell received a BS from UW Madison majoring in Medical Microbiology and Immunology and then a DDS and MS from MUSOD earning a specialty certificate in Orthodontics. Dr Connell is a diplomat of the American Board of Orthodontics and a member of OKU, ADA, WDA, AAO. He also serves on the Board of the Wisconsin Society of Orthodontists.
