The practice

Dr. Todd places about 400 dental implants a year and has placed over 5,500 implants in his career. We can provide you a complete digital workflow. Our practice has an intra-oral scanner that allows for digital impressions for printed models and combined with a CBCT can employ digital planning and guided surgery protocols.

After completion of healing, we provide an intra oral scan and the digital impression can be sent to your dentist’s lab of choice for fabrication of the crown or restoration. In most cases, this means that you will not need to see your dentist until the crown needs to be seated.

Dr. Todd has considerable experience in hard and soft tissue grafting and is one of a few surgeons in the area utilizing the AlloGraft Ring technique for deficient ridges. This means fewer procedures for patients and less time for healing.

We are experienced and comfortable with fixed hybrid restorations and are actively looking for patients who seek this tremendous treatment. We have partnered with ROE dental lab and can offer the Chrome Guided Smile which significantly shortens chair time for fixed hybrid restorations.

Dr. Todd has great expertise in anesthesia and has served on several committees for AAOMS related to anesthesia and patient safety. In addition to IV sedation, our office was designed to offer general anesthesia for appropriate patients and has a CRNA scheduled for certain days for patients who desire this modality.

Dr. Todd removes about 950 impacted wisdom teeth a year. We offer IV sedation and general anesthesia for treatment and utilize Exparel for post-operative pain relief. The Exparel provides about 72 hours of localized pain relief after the procedure.
