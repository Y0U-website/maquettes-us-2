The practice

For almost 80 years, our office, called Mountain Dental PC since 1996, has been a trusted name for cutting edge family dentistry in Lander, WY. Our practice is locally owned and operated by Dr. Todd Sutton. Currently operating out of two locations in Lander and Dubois, WY, Dr. Sutton and Dr. Derrick Hinkle, along with their dedicated team, provide a full range of dental services to meet all of…

We truly enjoy providing dentistry to our friends and neighbors in this area. Because our Doctors and Staff continually educate themselves on the best methods and materials available, you and your family can expect high-quality treatment from our practice for everything from routine cleanings, root canals, crowns and bridges, dentures, and fillings to advanced procedures such as implant placement…

It is important to us that all who visit our practice feel welcomed and relaxed. Our fun staff are well-trained to employ excellent conscious sedation techniques for those who desire it.

Our team focuses on helping our patients restore what they have lost, heal broken and diseased teeth and gums, and calming the worried and concerned through sedation dentistry techniques and prevent future problems through continued care and information.

This commitment makes Mountain Dental a fun and enjoyable place to work for our staff and wonderful place to come for our patients.

Mountain Dental was originally founded in Lander, WY in the 1940s. Dr. Whiting bought the practice in 1964, and in 1973, he moved it to its current location. Dr. Todd Sutton came into the practice with Dr. Whiting in 1994. They worked together for six years until Dr. Whiting retired in 2001. In 2012, we expanded our practice to a second location in Dubois, WY.
