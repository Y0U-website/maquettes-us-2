YOUR FIRST VISIT - LOVE DENTAL ARTS

As you prepare for your first visit at Love Dental Arts, please take a moment to complete our online patient forms which will allow us to begin your dental treatment. We will ask you to fill out several forms that will get you acquainted with our office.

Dr. Ray take prides in making your dental experience as pleasant and comfortable as possible. We will thoroughly present treatment options, fully explain our procedures and provide customized instructions for you to maintain your oral health. It is our goal to have you feel secure in the treatment of your dental needs.

We use the latest techniques to assure you are comfortable during each procedure. These options will be discussed with you during the treatment planning consultation.

Our friendly and courteous staff will offer you a tour of our office and answer any questions you may have.
