We Provide a Variety of Dental Services to Meet Your Needs

Our dental cleanings are designed to keep your teeth and gums in excellent condition. Whether you need a routine cleaning or a more intensive deep cleaning, our skilled hygienists will ensure your oral health is at its best. We also polish your teeth, giving your smile that extra sparkle.

We provide both white and silver fillings to restore the function and appearance of your teeth. Our dentists place fillings with precision and care, ensuring you are satisfied with the results before you leave our office.

For teeth that require extra protection, our experienced dentists use high-quality gold and metal crowns. These crowns are designed to strengthen and safeguard your teeth, providing long-lasting durability.

Achieve a straighter smile with Invisalign clear braces. Our dental office offers this innovative treatment, which involves taking x-rays and impressions of your teeth to create custom-fit trays. Typically, it takes about 30 days to receive your first tray. Invisalign trays are removable, making it easier to eat and maintain oral hygiene, and they help reduce the risk of cavities compared to other treatments.

If you have a painful or problematic tooth, our extraction services can provide relief. We perform surgical extractions for wisdom teeth and other troublesome teeth. Before the procedure, we take x-rays to ensure an accurate diagnosis. The extraction process is quick and efficient, allowing you to return to your daily activities in no time.
