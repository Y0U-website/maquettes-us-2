Meet Dr. Torin Marracino

Get to Know Your Texarkana Dentist

As a dentist and owner of Pleasant Grove Family Dentistry, Dr. Torin Marracino couldn’t be happier to be working alongside such a passionate, hardworking team and serving the best patients in the world! Feel free to keep reading to learn about his educational background and life outside the practice.

Why Did You Become a Dentist?

Though Dr. Marracino enjoys educating his patients on the value of good oral health, he finds the most rewarding aspect of dentistry to be the positive change he sees in his patients after brightening their smiles. With over 16 years of dental experience under his belt, he looks forward to many more years of service and creating the most comfortable and welcoming office atmosphere possible.

What is Your Educational Background?

Originally from Lewisville, Arkansas, Dr. Torin Marracino earned undergraduate degrees in both chemistry and biology in 2001 from Ouachita Baptist University. He then earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree from the University of Tennessee in 2005. After graduating, Dr. Marracino joined Dr. Robert E. Whitehead’s private practice, taking over for Dr. Whitehead in 2009. He’s also achieve Fellowship status with the International Dental Implant Association and heavily trained on Dental Sleep Medicine over the years.

What Do You Enjoy Outside the Practice?

Dr. Marracino is currently married to his wonderful wife of 3 years, Michaelyn. Together, they raise a 14-month-old daughter named Brynleigh and care for a pup named Bailey. In his off time, he enjoys being with family and unwinding with hunting, sports, riding SxS, traveling, and his favorite hobby, old muscle cars. One interesting fact about him is he’s left-handed!
