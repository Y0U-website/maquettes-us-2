Dental Implants to Replace Missing Teeth

Dental implants are the closest tooth replacement option to a natural tooth. A dental implant acts similarly to a natural tooth root, providing stimulation to the jaw bone to encourage natural bone growth. It’s also an incredibly secure option for patients.

Since the durable titanium implant is placed within the jaw and fuses with the jaw bone, a process known as osseointegration , patients can enjoy a lifetime of use from their dental implant, and they never have to worry about it shifting or coming loose while they’re talking or laughing.

It also provides better bite force than most other tooth replacement options, meaning that once you have fully healed after a dental implant procedure, you can enjoy eating the same foods with a dental implant as you would a normal tooth.

Caring for Dental Implants in Logansport

Dental implants are also incredibly easy to maintain, which is another great benefit for patients. Your regular oral hygiene routine for your natural teeth should keep your dental implant in excellent shape. Of course, your oral hygiene should include thorough brushing twice daily and flossing at least once every day.

You should also visit your dentist when recommended for dental cleanings and exams. At Parker Family Dentistry, we ask our patients to visit us every six months for a cleaning and check-up.

CBCT Cone Beam Technology

Traditional x-rays show teeth in two dimensions. CBCT imaging provides a precise, three-dimensional view of everything, including teeth, bone, nerves, sinuses, and airways. In seconds, this low-radiation scan captures detail that flat x-rays simply can t reveal.

Our dentists use CBCT imaging to plan dental implant placement, diagnose TMJ disorders, evaluate bone loss, and map complex root canal anatomy before treatment begins. The result is treatment planned with pinpoint accuracy, meaning fewer surprises, better outcomes, and a treatment plan you can feel confident in.
