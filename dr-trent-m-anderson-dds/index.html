The practice

Dr. Trent Anderson is the owner and lead dentist at Hastings Dental. With over 25 years of experience, Dr. Anderson has dedicated his career to providing exceptional dental care. He holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in Biology from Saint John’s University in Collegeville, MN, and earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Minnesota in Minneapolis, MN.

Dr. Anderson has achieved prestigious recognitions, including a Fellowship (FAGD) and Mastership (MAGD) in the Academy of General Dentistry—a testament to his commitment to advanced education and excellence in dentistry.

In addition to general dentistry, he specializes in placing and restoring dental implants, as well as enhancing patients’ smiles and overall quality of life. At Hastings Dental, he blends expertise, experience, and a compassionate approach to provide every patient with personalized, exceptional dental care.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Anderson enjoys traveling and cherishing moments with his wife and four daughters.
