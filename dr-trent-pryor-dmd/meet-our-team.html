Dr. Josh Jenkins

READ BIO

There is nothing Dr. Josh loves more than being with his wife Kathryn and their four children. He continues to participate in dental mission trips to the Dominican Republic and looks forward to expanding these trips to other countries as well. Dr. Josh loves spending time in the mountains with family and friends as well as running, biking, fishing, and exploring all that Idaho has to offer.

Dr. Jenkins belongs to the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the Idaho State Board of Dentistry. Dr. Trent Pryor Dentist READ BIO Dr. Trent Pryor was raised in Cedar City, Utah, and graduated from Cedar High School. He obtained his pre-dental requirements at Southern Utah University. Dr. Pryor received his Doctorate in Dental Medicine in 2007 Nova Southeastern University in Ft. Lauderdale Florida. He then completed his 2-year Pediatric Specialty, Dental Residency at Nova Southeastern University in 2009.

Dr. Pryor grew up coaching youth sports programs and knew he wanted to be a positive influence in the life of a child for the rest of his life. When not in the office, Dr. Pryor enjoys spending time with his wife Holly and their 3 children, competing in triathlons, playing basketball, golf, and basically any other sporting activities.

Dr. Pryor belongs to the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the Idaho State Board of Dentistry and is Board Certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Berg’s favorite thing to do is to spend time with his wife, Katelyn, and their two children exploring the great outdoors of Idaho. Dr. Sam Upton Dentist READ BIO Hailing all the way from Michigan, Dr. Upton is happy to call the Magic Valley Home. He and his wife moved to Twin Falls in 2021 and he has been happily practicing dentistry with Smiles 4 Kids ever since.

Dr. Upton completed both his bachelor’s degree and doctorate in dental surgery at the University of Michigan. After graduating in 2015, he completed an AEGD residency program with NYU Langone, where he found he enjoyed treating and taking care of children. He loves any opportunity to practice speaking Spanish and has volunteered with Global Dental Relief to provide free dental treatment to children in Guatemala.

When Dr. Upton is not in the office he loves spending time with his wife Cassie, their son Clark, and their rescue dogs. He is an avid fly fisherman, and on the weekends he can usually be found knee deep in a river or making too much noise archery hunting in the woods. He also enjoys watching rugby, and having played with the University of Michigan in college he is always cheering on the Wolverines.

He is excited for the opportunity to meet you and your family and to give your child a caring, fun, and easy dental experience while trying to provide the highest quality treatment as possible.
