Oral sleep apnea devices are custom-made appliances that help keep your airway open during sleep by gently repositioning the jaw. They are a comfortable, non-CPAP option for reducing snoring and improving breathing in patients with mild to moderate sleep apnea.

Sleep Apnea Devices are custom-fitted to help keep the airway open while you sleep . They work by gently moving the lower jaw to prevent airway collapse. This is a common cause of obstructive sleep apnea and snoring. These devices are made custom to your mouth for comfort and effectiveness.

Each device is designed slightly different, but they all work relatively the same: keeping your airway open while you sleep.
