Meet Dr. VALERIE OKEHIE

Diplomate, American Board of Endodontics

Dr. Okehie is a Board Certified Endodontist, a distinction that is awarded to less than twenty percent of current endodontic specialists in the United States.

Quote from Dr. Okehie: “Undertaking the rigorous steps necessary to achieve board certification status is a demonstration of my commitment to the practice of endodontics at an elite level. Furthermore, it is also a testament of my dedication to the process of continued professional growth required in order to maintain that elite level of education and clinical expertise.”

Dr. Okehie has received numerous awards for her research and presentations in various aspects of endodontics. She has been published in the Journal of Endodontics and was recognized at the American Association of Endodontics Annual conference for her oral presentation on FoxP3 DNA methylation in periapical lesions. She has also been recognized for her research on Revascularization of immature teeth.

She is currently a member of the American Association of Endodontists, American Dental Association, Maryland State Dental Association, and National Dental Association.

Dr. Okehie is happily married to her husband and is a proud mother of two children. She is an avid basketball fan. In addition to following basketball, Dr. Okehie enjoys exercising and traveling in her spare time.
