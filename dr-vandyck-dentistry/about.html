About Dr. VanDyck Dentistry

At Larry VanDyck DDS, Holly Street Dentistry, we focus on providing a exceptional care to our patients. Our team has been serving the community with compassionate dental care since 2006, in a warm, inviting atmosphere.

Our staff understands that you may not have been to the dentist for several years and may feel traumatized or fearful due to past experiences. We wil do our best to make you feel comfortable and put you at ease with our positive atmosphere and upbeat environment. Everyone at our practice is here to help, and get you on the road to a healthier smile!

About Dr. Larry VanDyck & Gina VanDyck

Dr Larry Vandyck knowing what hard work is, started from humble beginnings, his father and multi-generations before him, were wheat farmers in Canada, where they managed and farmed on land that had been in their family for nearly 100 years. After marrying his sweet wife Gina, who was born and raised in West Texas, whom he met while going to college for agriculture studies, they married and relocated back to his home town in Canada where he was to begin his expected career path in farming, to eventually take over the family business so his Father could retire.

Dr Vandyck thrived in his farming career, enjoying working with the land, and caring for their cattle. After a few years of farming, He and Gina had started a family and now had very young children to think about.

Winters were long and harsh, which left Gina missing her Texas home and a warmer climate. One day, out of the blue, Larry expressed his desire to move their young family back to the States, where she would be closer to her family, with much milder weather. Gina, who was happy with this news…Now had a bigger question. What do you want to do? Larry said, “I think I want to be a Dentist”.

He told this to his Dad, who was relieved to know, as his Dad was in no way ready to retire from his lifelong passion of farming, and was supportive of his Son’s decision.

They moved back to the States, where Larry worked long hours and studied hard in Dental school, graduating at the top of his class. He found his own passion in his new career of Dentistry, and truly wanted to help people.

After graduating, they settled in Colcord, Oklahoma, just outside of Siloam Springs Arkansas where they started his practice.

He and Gina thoroughly enjoyed meeting new patients, getting to know them, and their families, relating to each of them with their story of small town life.

Larry and Gina quickly became involved with their new residence, finding a church home, raising their children, caring for their neighbors and being involved with their community.

After a few years, Dr. Larry VanDyck, outgrew the office in Colcord, Oklahoma, and moved his practice just a few miles, to their current location, becoming LARRY VANDYCK, DDS, HOLLY STREET DENTISTRY in Siloam Springs, Arkansas.

For Dr Vandyck being a Dentist is a life choice, one he is very passionate about and truly enjoys! We look forward to working with you!
