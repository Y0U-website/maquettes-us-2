The Process of Implant Placement at Familia Dental

The journey to getting implants starts with a thorough evaluation to assess your bone density and identify the best placement location. During the surgical stage, our dentists will insert the titanium post into your jawbone while you are under local anesthesia. Following a healing period of several months, where the implant fuses with your bone, we will attach an abutment and a custom crown. Most patients report minimal discomfort and can typically return to their normal activities the next day.

Common Questions About Dental Implants

What is the cost of dental implants?

The price of implants can differ depending on how many teeth need replacement and if additional treatments, such as bone grafting, are required. We accept major credit cards to facilitate affordable treatment.

Are there affordable dental implants available near me?

We provide competitive pricing for dental implants at Familia Dental and offer financing options to accommodate various budgets.

What is the longevity of dental implants?

With consistent oral hygiene and regular dental appointments, implants can last 25 years or more. The crown may require replacement after 10 to 15 years based on usage and upkeep.

Am I eligible for dental implants?

Most adults with sufficient bone density and healthy gums can qualify for implants. We conduct individual assessments to determine the most suitable treatment plan for each patient.
