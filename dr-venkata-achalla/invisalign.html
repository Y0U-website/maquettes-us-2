Procedure and Timeline for Treatment

The journey begins with digital impressions and 3D imaging to formulate an accurate treatment plan. Dr. Rohit Vadlamani and Dr. Alina Aryal create a series of personalized aligners that progressively shift teeth into their optimal positions. Each set is typically worn for about two weeks before transitioning to the next. Most patients will finish their Invisalign treatment within a span of 12 to 18 months, depending on the complexity of their orthodontic needs.

Common Questions Regarding Invisalign Aligners

How many hours must I wear my aligners daily?

For the best results, aligners should be worn between 20 to 22 hours a day. They should only be removed for eating, drinking anything other than water, and during your oral hygiene routine.

Can you handle complicated orthodontic cases?

Clear aligners are capable of addressing a wide range of orthodontic concerns, such as crowding, spacing issues, overbites, and crossbites. Our dentists will assess each individual case to determine the best course of action.

What should I do if I misplace an aligner tray?

If you lose an aligner, please reach out to our office without delay. We will advise you on whether to proceed to the next tray or if a replacement is necessary.

Where can I find affordable Invisalign treatment with payment options?

We accept major credit cards to ensure treatment is accessible to everyone. We will review all available payment options during your consultation.
