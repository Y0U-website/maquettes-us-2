Our Retainer Options

We provide both removable and fixed retainer types to accommodate the unique needs of our patients. Removable retainers are convenient for meals and cleaning, while fixed retainers are bonded to the back of your teeth for continuous support. Our dentists will assess each situation to suggest the most fitting retention strategy based on the intricacy of your initial treatment and your lifestyle. We also offer thorough guidelines for the proper care and upkeep of your retainers to ensure their durability.

Common Inquiries Regarding Retainers

What is the duration of retainer wear required?

Initially, many patients wear their retainers full-time for several months, before transitioning to wearing them only at night. The specific duration will depend on your individual circumstances and healing process.

What should I do if my retainer is broken or lost?

Please reach out to our office immediately for a replacement. Delaying the acquisition of a new retainer may result in teeth shifting, which could necessitate further orthodontic adjustments.

Is it okay to eat while using my removable retainer?

Always remove your retainer before consuming food or beverages, except for water. Food residues can harm the retainer and lead to hygiene complications.

What is the proper way to clean my retainer?

Daily cleaning of removable retainers should be done with a soft toothbrush and mild soap. Avoid hot water, as it may distort the retainer&#x27;s plastic material.
