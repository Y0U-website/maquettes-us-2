Les soins

Keep the healthy part of your tooth, and the porcelain inlay or onlay keeps your dazzling smile intact.

All-on-four uses four titanium dental implants as a foundation while an entire arch of teeth is then bonded to those implants. This allows for a full mouth of teeth without needing an implant for each missing tooth without the need of dentures .
