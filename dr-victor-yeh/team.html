Menu Meet Our Staff Victor Yeh, DMD Dentist Dr. Victor Yeh, DMD was born in New York City and raised in Taiwan and Georgia. He earned his undergraduate degree from the Georgia Institute of Technology before completing his dental education at Tufts University School of Dentistry, where he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in 2014. During dental school, Dr. Yeh volunteered with the LOTUS Community Service Group, providing free dental care to seniors throughout the Boston area.

Dr. Yeh is committed to delivering high-quality preventive, esthetic, and restorative dental care to patients of all ages. He places a strong emphasis on patient comfort and has pursued advanced techniques to help reduce pain and anxiety during dental treatment. Outside the office, Dr. Yeh is an enthusiastic Boston Celtics fan and a devoted animal lover.
