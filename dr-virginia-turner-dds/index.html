Dr. Layal Rabadi is originally from Westchester County, and was born and raised in Yonkers, NY. Dr. Rabadi is the first in her family to obtain a professional degree along with her sister. Both attended school together and completed the same residency program together.

Dr. Rabadi graduated Magna Cum Laude with Honors from Mercy College with a Bachelor of Science majoring in Health Sciences. She went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree at Dalhousie University Faculty of Dentistry in Nova Scotia, Canada where she was a JD McLean Scholarship Recipient. She then returned to her home state of NY and completed her General Practice Residency through…

Dr. Rabadi strives to provide quality care and education to her patients in a comfortable and friendly manner, in the hopes of reducing the intimidation commonly associated with dentistry and optimizing patients oral health. Dr. Rabadi is always eager to learn and continues to take courses to master new skills and advance her knowledge in dentistry.

Dr. Rabadi is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), New York State Dental Association (NYSDA), Ninth District Dental Association, Westchester South Dental Society and Scarsdale Dental Society.

When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Rabadi enjoys cooking, traveling and spending quality time with her family and friends.
