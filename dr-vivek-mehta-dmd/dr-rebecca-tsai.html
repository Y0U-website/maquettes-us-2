Meet Dr. Rebecca Tsai

Meet Dr. Rebecca Tsai

Dr. Rebecca Tsai

Originally from Orange County in Southern California, Dr. Tsai earned her Bachelor of Science in Integrative Biology from the University of California, Berkeley, in 2015. Following her undergraduate studies, she continued her educational pursuit at Harvard School of Dental Medicine, where she earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree in 2020.

Dr. Tsai’s passion for endodontics led her to further specialize in the field. She completed her residency at the Harvard School of Dental Medicine’s Advanced Graduate Program in Endodontics in 2023, earning a Certificate in Endodontics and a Master of Medical Sciences (MMSc) degree in oral biology.

As a dedicated professional, Dr. Tsai is an active member of several dental associations, including the American Dental Association, the American Association of Endodontists, and the Massachusetts Dental Society. Her commitment to excellence is further underscored by her recognition as the recipient of the Leon Dogon Research Award for her contributions to research.

In addition to her professional accomplishments, Dr. Tsai has a passion for international travel, where she immerses herself in different cultures and experiences. When she’s not in the office, she enjoys outdoor activities such as hiking and rock climbing. Indoors, she nurtures her love for gardening.
