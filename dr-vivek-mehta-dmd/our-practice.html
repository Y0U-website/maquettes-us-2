Our Practice

Aptly named, Welcome Smiles has been welcoming our Auburn and Worcester County dental patients for more than thirty years. We’ve earned a reputation as a caring, professional office that is focused more on our patient’s well-being and comfort than anything else. In short, we have earned the trust of our patients. You can expect to be treated like family, whether it’s your first visit or your fiftieth.

Our personal demeanor and genuine warmth may seem old-fashioned, though our methodology and technology are anything but. Modern dental technology combined with superior skills helps us provide our patients genuine comfort and superior long lasting results.

We invest in the latest advances and equipment, which are designed for better overall dentistry, as well as superior patient comfort. You can feel confident that we are highly skilled in our craft, and that your smile is receiving the best care possible.

We also handle all manner of dental services. For example, we perform root canals and TMJ treatments in-office. This is important to our patients, as we do not require our patients to seek other dental offices for these special treatments and will present every option available to you. We have everything to gain by being 100 % honest, 100 % of the time. It’s just how we do things.

Our Philosophy

We practice dentistry with a mindful consideration of who you are and where you hope to be. Your needs, desires, and lifestyle all play an integral part of the process.
