Your fun, caring family.

Our dentists and team at Payson Premier Dental believe in fun, caring, and family. Our patient-focused practice works hard to provide you with dental care that you will actually enjoy receiving. We care about you, your happiness, and your well-being from the first moment that you contact us, and we treat you like we would members of our own family. Our dentists and team strive to provide you with gentle, personalized service and a wide array of treatments, including Invisalign®, sedation dentistry, and dental implants, to meet your oral health needs.

Your First Visit

Your first appointment at Payson Premier Dental is designed to help you and Dr. Kristin Wade get to know each other. During this appointment, we will take the time to understand your needs, concerns, and goals. We examine your mouth and take any necessary pictures to better understand your oral health.

We encourage you to contact our office today to learn more about your initial oral examination and schedule an appointment with our friendly team!

Digital X-Rays

Digital X-rays allow our dentists to more closely examine your mouth and determine if there are any problems in your teeth or supporting structure.

Intraoral Camera

Our dentists use intraoral cameras to take a closer look at the areas in your mouth that are more difficult to see.

iTero Scanner

iTero® digital impressions give you a more precise treatment while eliminating the mess that comes with traditional impressions!

Denture Exams & Maintenance

Your dentures will need regular exams and maintenance to ensure that they continue to fit and function properly, and to ensure that they provide the best results for your smile.

Immediate Dentures

For more information about immediate dentures in Payson, Arizona, please contact our office today.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Call Payson Premier Dental today to learn more about renewing your smile with implant-supported dentures.

Dental Bridges

If you are missing a single tooth or multiple teeth in a row, we may recommend a dental bridge to restore your smile.

Dental Crowns

Payson Premier Dental provides dental crowns to restore severely decayed or broken teeth back to their former strength, function, and appearance.

Dental Fillings

If one of your teeth is decayed or damaged, a dental filling may be a great choice for restoring your oral health and function.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a great way to achieve the appearance of natural, healthy teeth while living with the confidence to eat, smile, laugh, and enjoy everyday activities without worry.

Non-Surgical Root Canal Treatment

A root canal treatment can often be completed with minimal discomfort thanks to advanced techniques, tools and anesthesia.

Teeth Whitening

We offer professional in-office and take-home teeth whitening in Payson, Arizona.

Tooth Extraction

Give us a call today to learn more about tooth extractions and make your appointment with our experienced dentists at Payson Premier Dental.

Invisalign®

With Invisalign®, you can straighten your smile while maintaining the natural beauty of your teeth!

Laser Gum Therapy

Laser gum treatment is a type of treatment for periodontal disease that is completed using a dental laser.

Teeth Cleaning

One of the first treatments our dentist may recommend for periodontal disease is prophylaxis, or a deep dental cleaning.

Scaling & Root Planing

If you have developed gum disease, we can perform a scaling and root planing treatment to remove harmful substances from your mouth.

Nitrous Oxide

This mild sedative is a great option for those who experience fear or anxiety, or who simply need a little help to comfortably receive treatment.
