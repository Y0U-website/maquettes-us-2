About : Your Family s Smile Team

Smiles That Stick. People Who Care.

How It All Started (And Why It Still Matters)

Dr. Nicole Wax started this practice in 2014 with a simple mission: to make a positive impact in the lives of her patients and community. What began with a handful of patients and a whole lot of heart has grown into a trusted name for families across Linden, Flushing, and Highland.

But while we’ve expanded, our mission hasn’t changed. Every smile we help create is a reminder of why we do this—to build confidence, connection, and a whole lot of joy.

We’re not in this just to straighten teeth. We’re here to lift people up.

Big Hearts. Big Work Ethic.

We’re a team of smile-obsessed, service-driven professionals who believe in showing up big—for our patients and for each other. Whether we’re adjusting an appliance or making you laugh at the front desk, we bring energy, kindness, and accountability to everything we do.

It’s simple: we do the hard work because we care deeply. We don’t believe in “just okay”—we believe in going the extra mile, every time.

Core Values We Live By

Growth & Excellence We never settle. Every day is a chance to learn, improve, and serve better—both as individuals and as a team.

Ownership & Accountability We take responsibility for what we do, how we do it, and the impact it has. No excuses—just action.

Kindness First Every interaction matters. We lead with warmth, empathy, and respect—for each other and for our patients.

Community Impact We’re not just an orthodontic office. We’re part of something bigger. Giving back and lifting up our community is baked into everything we do.

Meet Dr. Nicole Wax

Dr. Wax is a mom, a business owner, a big believer in doing what’s right—and yes, an orthodontist. But she’s more than a doctor in a white coat. She’s someone who worked her way up, remembers what it’s like to hustle, and built this practice to reflect the values she lives by: kindness, integrity, and showing up for people.

When she’s not transforming smiles, you’ll find her at the coffee shop she founded (Evers & Ollie), cheering on her kids, or mentoring other women in business. She’s proud to lead a team that cares just as much about who you are as they do about your bite.

The People Behind the Smiles

What Makes Us...Us

Every orthodontist can straighten teeth. We partner with families.
