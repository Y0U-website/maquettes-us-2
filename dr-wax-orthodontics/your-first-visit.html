Your First Orthodontic Visit: What Actually Happens

  • You’ll meet our friendly team and get a tour of the office.
  • We’ll take a few photos and X-rays to understand your smile.
  • Our team of doctors will sit down with you (and your parent, if applicable) to walk through your options and answer every question.
  • You’ll leave with clarity—whether you’re ready to start treatment today or simply want a clear plan for the future.
