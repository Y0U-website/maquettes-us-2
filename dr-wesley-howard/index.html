Some patients need specialized periodontal cleaning beyond regular cleaning. Called a deep scaling and cleaning, or scaling and root planing, this treatment can help to stop and reverse gum disease. Gum disease is a major problem in the United States, and the consequences include tooth loss, cardiovascular issues, and much more.

In a deep scaling and cleaning, the team at South Vue Dentistry cleans beneath your gumline and along the roots of your teeth. There is no need to worry you’ll be kept comfortable the entire time. While you may never end up needing a deep scaling and cleaning, this treatment can be invaluable for resolving gum disease and its symptoms, including bleeding, swollen gums, and bad breath.
