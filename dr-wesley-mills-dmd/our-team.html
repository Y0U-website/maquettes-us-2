0 Meet Our Team Dr. Wesley Mills, DMD Dr. Wesley W Mills grew up in Kennebunk, Maine. He attended undergrad in Denver, Colorado and returned to Maine to get his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at UNE. Wes has spent time learning dentistry and patient care in a number of settings including Caring Hands of Maine, a non-profit dental office in Ellsworth, ME. Dr. Mills focuses on holistic oral health for the whole family.
