What Makes Us Different

We offer modern dental methods and the latest technologies to ensure our patients the highest level of care, both today and tomorrow.

At Moss Family Dentistry, we are committed to providing exceptional dental experiences for every member of your family. We achieve this goal through a combination of convenience and technology .

Convenience

Treatment for the Whole Family

At Moss Family Dentistry, we offer services for both adults and children . Your family will love their visits to our office. We are sensitive to the needs and problems of patients of all ages and provide the unique care they require.

Same-Day Emergency Care

If you face a dental emergency, we are here to relieve your pain and address the problem as soon as possible.

Relax for Your Treatments

We have nitrous oxide or "laughing gas" available to make every treatment easy and more comfortable for our patients. While the medication will relax you, it is mild enough that you can still drive yourself home after your treatment. Additionally, local anesthesia is applied to further diminish any pain.

Convenient Location

The Moss Family Dentistry office is located at 1567 N. Eastman Rd., Suite 16 Kingsport, TN 37664.

Online New Patient Forms

Our new patient forms are available online . This allows you to take your time filling them out at home with all the necessary information.

A Complete Range of Financial Options

There are various payment options for our services, with or without insurance. If you do have insurance, we file your claim to ensure you receive all your benefits. For your payment, we accept major credit cards and have a monthly, interest-free payment plan available.

Technology

Digital X-Rays Mean Less Radiation

We use only digital X-rays that are available immediately for viewing on our computer. There is no waiting time for images to develop, no polluting chemicals used in the process, and 75 % less radiation than conventional X-rays.

Our Intra-Oral Camera Allows You to See Up-Close Views of Your Smile

Dr. Moss wants you to participate in your dental decisions, and modern technology makes this possible. With our miniature intra-oral video camera, you get a clear, close-up view of the interior of your mouth. We can show you where the problem areas are and what teeth are just fine. This helps you to make informed decisions on necessary treatment.

State-of-the-Art Sterilization

We never take any risk when it comes to guarding your health. We use an autoclave to sterilize all reusable tools. This special unit is monitored by an outside lab to ensure all federal guidelines are met in cleanliness. Any single-use tools are disposed of after treatment.

Our goal is to provide you with comfortable, convenient dental visits.
