Dental Implants in Camden, SC

Each implant solution is designed with aesthetics, bite strength, and long-term health in mind. We also collaborate with trusted oral surgeons and periodontists as needed to ensure every step of the procedure is handled with precision and care.

  • Multiple Teeth Implants: Two or more implants can support bridges or partial restorations, offering greater durability than traditional bridges or dentures .
  • Full Arch Replacements: A full arch of missing teeth can be replaced using a fixed implant-supported bridge, eliminating the need for removable dentures.
  • All-on-4® Implants: This innovative method uses four strategically placed implants to support an entire arch of prosthetic teeth, ideal for patients seeking a quicker full-mouth solution with fewer implants.
  • Implant-Retained Dentures: For improved stability and comfort, implant-retained dentures snap securely into place using 2–4 implants, reducing slippage and improving function.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer several advantages over traditional restorations:

Implants are often recommended after tooth loss, extraction, or failure of other restorations. We’ll assess your oral health and bone density to determine if you’re a good candidate.

  • Mimic natural tooth appearance and function
  • Prevent bone loss and preserve facial structure
  • No impact on surrounding healthy teeth
  • Long-lasting and highly durable with proper care
  • Improved chewing ability and speech
  • Greater confidence and stability compared to removable options

Supporting Services

Dental Implants FAQs

  • How long do dental implants last?: With proper oral hygiene and maintenance, implants can last 15 years or more—often a lifetime.
  • Is dental implant surgery painful?: Most patients report minimal discomfort. Sedation and local anesthesia are used to ensure a relaxed, pain-free experience.
  • How long does the process take?: From implant placement to final restoration, the process can take several months. Temporary options are available during healing.
  • Can anyone get dental implants?: Most healthy adults with sufficient bone density are candidates. Dr. Owen will evaluate your case during your consultation.
  • Are implants better than dentures?: Implants offer more stability, prevent bone loss, and feel more natural compared to traditional dentures.
