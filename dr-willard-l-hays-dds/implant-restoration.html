Why Should I Get Implant Restoration?

If you&#x27;re struggling with tooth loss, implant restoration can be life-changing. Unlike dentures or bridges, dental implants are designed to last a lifetime, providing a sturdy and natural-looking replacement that mimics your real teeth. They not only restore the aesthetics of your smile but also protect your oral health by preserving jawbone and preventing facial sagging. Implant restoration can help you eat, speak, and smile again with confidence, improving your overall quality of life.

The Benefits Of Implant Restoration

Improved Oral Health Implant restoration doesn&#x27;t just improve your smile; it enhances your overall oral health. Dental implants prevent bone loss that occurs when you lose natural teeth. By stimulating the jawbone, they promote bone growth, maintain facial structure, and prevent further tooth loss.

Comfort and Convenience Say goodbye to the discomfort and inconvenience of removable dentures. Implants are permanent, stable, and feel like natural teeth. You can eat your favorite foods without worry, speak without impediment, and maintain your oral hygiene routine as usual.

Long-lasting Solution Unlike other tooth replacement options, dental implants are designed to last. With proper care and regular dental check-ups, your implants can last a lifetime. This makes them a cost-effective solution in the long run.

The Implant Restoration Treatment Process

Initial Consultation

Your journey towards a restored smile begins with an initial consultation. Dr. Saslaw will assess your oral health, discuss your needs and expectations, and determine if you&#x27;re a suitable candidate for implant restoration. This involves a thorough examination and possibly, some diagnostic imaging.

Implant Placement

The next step is the placement of the dental implant, which acts as a substitute for the root of the missing tooth. This is a minor surgical procedure where the implant is placed into the jawbone. After placement, a period of healing is necessary for the implant to integrate with the bone.

Restoration Placement

Once the implant has fully integrated with the bone, the final step is the placement of the restoration (crown). The crown is custom-made to match your natural teeth in color and shape, providing a seamless and aesthetically pleasing result. After this step, you&#x27;ll be ready to show off your restored smile.
