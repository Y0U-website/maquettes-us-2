Your First Visit

What to Expect

At Riverglen Dental, we value your time and trust. During your first visit, expect a comprehensive oral examination, a personal consultation to understand your dental goals, and a curated treatment plan tailored just for you. We strive to make your initial experience with us as comfortable and informative as possible.

What to Bring

Please bring your identification, insurance details, and any previous dental records if available. Fill out your new patient forms before your appointment to ensure a seamless and efficient experience.

APPOINTMENT GUIDELINES

To ensure a smooth and productive appointment:

These guidelines help us provide the highest quality care for all our patients. We look forward to welcoming you to Riverglen Dental!

  • Complete all paperwork before your scheduled appointment time. This allows us to dedicate your full visit time to your comprehensive appointment.
  • Notify our front desk staff of any changes to your contact information, insurance details, or health information at check-in.
  • All copays, coinsurance, deductibles, and outstanding balances are due upon arrival. Patient payments are due at the time of service.
  • Please arrive on time. If you are 15 minutes past your scheduled appointment time, you may need to reschedule.
