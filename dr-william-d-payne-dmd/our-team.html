Rebecca Flaiz, D.M.D.

Rebecca Flaiz is a general dentist currently working as the lead dentist at One Community Health in Hood River. She grew up in eastern Oregon and was excited to move back to her home state when she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from Loma Linda University in 2011. She enjoys both the technical and human elements of dentistry and excels at creative problem solving. In her free time, she enjoys traveling, scuba diving, mushroom hunting, hiking, backpacking, snowboarding, camping, pottery and currently is very into tie dye. Rebecca is also Spanish/English bilingual.
