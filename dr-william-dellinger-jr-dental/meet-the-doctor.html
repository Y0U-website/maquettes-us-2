Meet the Doctor

Dr. William Dellinger is an Atlanta native and third generation dentist. He received his undergraduate degree in Vertebrate Zoology from the University of Georgia, where he was a member of Phi Gamma Delta Fraternity. He received his Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree from Emory University School of Dentistry in 1983, where he received the Ronald Goldstein Award for Esthetic Dentistry. He is a member of the American Dental Association, The Northern District Dental Society, and The Hinman Dental Society. He has been in private practice in McDonough since 1990.

Dr. Dellinger is married to the former Alicia Sullivan of McDonough and they have three children: Chelsey, Will and Cole. He is an avid outdoorsman and enjoys deer and duck hunting, skiing, cycling and Georgia Bulldog football. Dr. Dellinger has enjoyed coaching his children in various sports and working with young athletes. He is an active member of Eagles Landing First Baptist Church in McDonough.
