Molly Thurmond, DMD

About Dr. Thurmond

Dr. Molly Thurmond is a highly skilled dentist who loves working with families & is passionate about helping her patients achieve beautiful, healthy smiles.

As a native Kentuckian, she is proud to be part of her community. She attended Centre College for her undergrad before going on to University of Kentucky and also completing her advanced education in General Dentistry at University Hospital in Cincinnati. She has been practicing dentistry since 2006.

Dr. Thurmond loves to dance, is a huge Elvis fan, and has two cats, Shadow and Smudge! She loves enjoying outdoor activities with her husband, Robbie, and daughters, Maddie and Annabeth. Family is very important to her and she considers her patients an extension of her family. There’s no better feeling than improving a patient’s quality of life by helping them love their smile. Dr. Thurmond would love for you to join her family and trust her with your smile!

Dr. William E. Hampton is a compassionate and experienced dentist who always makes his best effort to develop a close relationship with his patients so that he can better serve their needs .

When he isn’t in the office, you can find him attending a game at his Alma Mater, enjoying a round of golf at his favorite course, or out on the lake with his wife, Misheala , and their three children, Seth, Sloane, and Catie Grace.
