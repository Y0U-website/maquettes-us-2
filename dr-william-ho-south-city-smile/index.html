Covers most general dental procedures including fillings, crowns, periodontal treatment, root canal treatment, and dentures. We take children as well as adults!

Committed to the safety of our patients we decided to invest in a low-radiation, high quality X-Ray machine. With our brand new Planmeca X-Ray machine, only two pictures are enough to reconstruct a full mouth image. Moreover, these shots are taken from outside the mouth, so the amount of radiation received is a marginal fraction as compared to traditional film-based X-rays.
