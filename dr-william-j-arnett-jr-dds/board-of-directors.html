Board of Directors

Support and advocate for PCHS providing accessible and sensible health care services for the community I live in and care for.

I love being involved in our community and it’s important to me that our community has access to affordable and quality health care. It’s an honor to serve on a board that not only offers affordable healthcare to everyone, but is also very involved in our wonderful community.

It is a privilege to be on the board of an organization such as PCHS that provides outstanding services in the community in which it serves. I have been able to see the thought and care that goes into decisions in order to truly provide excellent health care for all.

I want to be on the PCHS Board to strengthen Behavioral Health Services and Community Engagement and improve healthcare and outcomes for consumers receiving services.

I’d like to give back to the community with my professional expertise.

I believe being healthy is a fundamental, universal human right. I would like to work for better health care for those who cannot speak for themselves.

Serving on the PCHS Board is an honor for me and also a natural outgrowth from two core values that I hold. First: health care is a fundamental human right. Second: a healthy community actively promotes health and healing for the whole person as an essential for true wellness. It is my privilege to be a Board member of a community organization that seeks to regard and relate to each person in this way.

I feel it is the obligation of the healthy to help those less fortunate. When times are tough for people it is the Christian thing to help. Being on the PCHS board is my way of giving back to those in need.

I value service to our country and local community. I retired from the USAF after 24 years and was the pediatrician for Kenaitze Indian Tribe Dena’ina Wellness Center for 9 years. Serving on the PCHS board affords me the opportunity to continue to support and improve my community.

As a local retired paramedic/firefighter and now a PCHS Board Member, I’m excited to be in a position that enables me to continue to provide a much needed service to the community I care so much for.

I have lived on the peninsula since October 2000 I have raised my nine children in this wonderful place. Our family has several successful small businesses in town. I have watched Soldotna grow and become one of the greatest places to live. PCHS has been a part of that and has improved our quality of life and I believe in what goes on at PCHS and I’m excited to be a part of it all.

I love living on the Kenai Peninsula and being part of our fun, exciting community! Serving on the PCHS board is one way I can use my experience in healthcare to help make our community healthier and safer.
