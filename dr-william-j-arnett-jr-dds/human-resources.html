Human Resources | Kenai and Soldotna, AK

Patty Eissler, PHR

Chief HR Officer

Patty started working with CPCS in October 2005, before becoming Director of Human Resources when it merged and became PCHS in October 2008. She has worked in the HR field for over 20 years and has obtained her Professional in Human Resources as well as her HR certificate through SHRM.

She enjoys hiking, fishing, reading and wildlife viewing. She is married with one adult son who lives in Anchorage. She has lived here on the Kenai Peninsula for 10 years, having moved up from CA, and loves the small town feel here on the Peninsula.
