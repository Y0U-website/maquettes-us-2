Benjamin Wright - Chief Executive Officer

Experience: Benjamin Ben’ Wright has worked as an administrator for organizations dedicated to serving those in need for more than 30 years. That experience has included serving the mentally ill, medically fragile, and overseeing a variety of licensed facilities. Ensuring the health and wellness of those served, while supporting the viability of the organization providing services, has been the cornerstone of his career.

Ben historically has encouraged organizational success development and expansion. Each of the organizations Ben has been a part of has grown in both the programs offered, as well as the number of individuals served; which was accomplished while increasing the reinvestment margin of the organization. Ben has also provided consulting for nonprofit boards.

Education: Ben has a Masters degree in Organizational Leadership from St. Ambrose University, of Davenport, IA.

Profile: Ben and his family are happy to be part of the Soldotna/Kenai community. His children are outdoor enthusiasts, and Alaska will provide many opportunities for adventure!

Cristina Lee - Chief Operating Officer

She continues to be inspired and motivated by PCHS patients, clients, and staff to bring her best efforts to PCHS every day.

Zachary Huot, PT, DPT - Director of PT & OT

Undergraduate: University of North Dakota

Graduate School:University of North Dakota
