The Millington Family Dentistry Team

When you visit our Millington, TN, office, you’ll immediately feel welcomed. We want to make you feel at home when you’re here. Our highly qualified, caring team will work with you toward the common goal of maximizing the health of your teeth and gums.

Dr. Taylor Bolland, DMD

A proud Jackson, Mississippi native and Ole Miss alumnus, Dr. Bolland brings both hometown roots and world-class training to every patient who walks through the door. After earning his dental degree from the University of Mississippi Medical Center, he went on to complete an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency through NYU Langone Hospitals in Nashville.

That combination of advanced training and genuine Southern hospitality means you’ll always feel comfortable, confident, and cared for. Whether you’re coming in for a routine visit or a complex dental procedure, Dr. Bolland has the expertise to deliver exceptional results — from crowns and bridges to dental implants, restorative dentistry, whitening, and more.

When he’s not caring for patients, Dr. Bolland enjoys spending time with his wife Stephanie and son Henry, cheering on both the Ole Miss Rebels and Liverpool FC.
