Restorative

Damaged or decayed teeth can affect more than just the appearance of your smile. Left untreated, cavities, cracks, and worn teeth can lead to increased sensitivity, difficulty chewing, and a greater risk of more extensive dental problems over time.

Dr. Bolland provides restorative dental care designed to repair damaged teeth and restore both function and appearance. Tooth-colored fillings are used to treat cavities while blending naturally with your smile, and custom dental crowns strengthen and protect teeth that have been weakened by decay, injury, or large fillings. With personalized treatment and quality materials, our goal is to help you maintain a healthy, comfortable smile for years to come.
