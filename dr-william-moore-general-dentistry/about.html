ABOUT

Will Moore is a general dentist practicing in Roanoke, Virginia. A Roanoke native and a proud graduate of Patrick Henry High School, Dr. Moore is thrilled to be back in the valley after studying at the University of Virginia and receiving his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry.

During his time in dental school, Dr. Moore participated in numerous Mission of Mercy (“MOM”) Projects, including those in Roanoke, which provided free service to low-income and underserved populations throughout the state. He continues to volunteer his time at the MOM Projects and the Bradley Free Clinic.

Dr. Moore feels Roanoke is an unparalleled venue for the outdoors. You can find him outside of work biking or running the Greenways and the Blue Ridge Parkway.

Dr. Wiliam Moore is dedicated to the practice of dentistry and is grateful to make a difference in the lives and health of so many wonderful people. He strives to treat everyone he meets like family and hopes you will find his practice welcome and personable
