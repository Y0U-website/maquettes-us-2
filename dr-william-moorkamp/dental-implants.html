Transform Your Smile with Dental Implants

Elegant and natural-looking, dental implants and implant restorations offer a permanent solution for missing teeth that restores both function and aesthetics. In addition to enhancing your smile, dental implants help maintain your jawbone health, prevent bone loss, and reduce the risk of cavities and gum disease. If you’re searching for an experienced provider who offers dental implants in Rolla, Missouri, Moorkamp & Arthur Family Dentistry would love to help you on your journey toward improved oral health!

Dr. Stephen Moorkamp, DDS, and Dr. Charlie Arthur, DDS are Rolla’s leading Dental Implant experts, providing comprehensive services for Dental Implant Surgery, Single Implant Restoration, Implant-Supported Dental Bridges, Implant Supported Dentures, and beyond. We serve patients at all stages of the implant process from individuals who have already undergone surgical treatment to those who are just getting started.

Dental Implants have a powerful ability to turn back the clock and reverse signs of aging and damage that have occurred to teeth over the years. Yet they are much more than just a cosmetic treatment alone. They’re an important investment in your health. That’s why it’s important to have the right dental team on your side who you can trust to provide the very best possible results.

Below, we’ve discussed our background and experience in Implant Dentistry and provided information on the different options and benefits available today.

Restore Beauty & Functionality with Dental Implants

Dental implants are small titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as artificial roots for replacement teeth. They provide a sturdy foundation for tooth restorations that look and function like natural teeth. As a health-centered dentistry focused on individualized care, we’ve chosen to offer dental implants because they:

  • Preserve jawbone health
  • Enhance functionality and comfort
  • Offer longevity and durability
  • Can be customized to each patient’s needs

Services for Dental Implants & Implant Restorations

At Moorkamp & Arthur Family Dentistry, we offer a variety of dental implant services to restore your smile’s beauty and functionality including:

Single Implant Restorations

Single implant restorations are perfect for replacing individual missing teeth. First, a titanium post is placed in your jawbone. Then, a custom-made crown is attached to provide a natural look and feel.

Implant-Supported Dental Bridges

Ideal for replacing multiple missing teeth, these bridges are anchored by dental implants instead of adjacent teeth, ensuring stability and preserving your natural teeth.

Implant-Supported Dentures

A superior alternative to traditional dentures, implant-supported dentures are anchored in place by titanium implants, preventing them from slipping or shifting and enhancing your ability to chew and speak properly.

Dental Crowns

A custom dental crown can be created to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, which can be attached securely to your implant for lasting results.
