Dr. William Moorkamp

I became a dentist because I learned the rewards of patient care while helping my father develop x-rays at his dental office beginning at 12 years old. I was graduated from the University of Missouri Columbia with a degree in Psychology and a DDS degree from University of Missouri Kansas City, and established a solo private practice that continues today in Rolla.

Our family enjoys many outdoor activities at the lake and river. I continue to be on the Centre Advisory and continue to be a member and past President of the Noon Kiwanis club since 1985.

My hobbies include: being a new Grandparent! outdoor activities, triathlon/multi-sport, hunting, fishing, canoeing/kayaking, boating, water/snow skiing, working for our son’s cattle operation on the family (“hobby”) farm, and handyman tasks. In addition, we enjoy traveling to see family outside of Rolla. Volunteering in the community is a priority for me as my life-long desire is to leave the world and our Rolla community better than I found it.

Moorkamp & Arthur Family Dentistry has the experience you expect and the credentials you can trust.
