About Noll Family Dentistry

A Trusted Practice in Carlisle, PA

At Noll Family Dentistry, we’re proud to offer extraordinary dental care not only to Carlisle but also to residents of Boiling Springs and Newville, PA.

Our Comprehensive Services

From routine cleanings and cosmetic whitening to advanced treatments like implants, TMJ therapy, and smile restoration, we’re dedicated to addressing all your dental needs under one roof.

Dental Education & Resources

We believe in empowering our patients with knowledge. That’s why we offer an ever-growing library of resources from practical tips like mouthguard safety for kids to answers for common questions answered by our practice administrator.

Your Experience Matters

Our goal is to create a welcoming atmosphere where every patient feels informed, confident, and cared for. From your first appointment to ongoing preventive care, we’re here for your whole-family dental journey and we’d love to help you share your story with us.

Our Mission

At Noll Family Dentistry, our mission is to enhance the lives of our patients and team through comprehensive, collaborative dental care.

We’re committed to delivering high-quality, affordable treatment in a warm, welcoming environment where every patient, young or old, feels confident and cared for. Through trust, compassion, and clear communication, we build lasting relationships while continuously advancing our skills and services to offer the best care possible.

Our Team

At Noll Family Dentistry, our team is made up of experienced dentists, skilled hygienists, dedicated restorative professionals, and a friendly administrative staff all working together to provide personalized, high-quality care.

From the moment you arrive, you’ll experience a welcoming environment, clear communication, and a focus on comfort and confidence. Whether it’s preventive care, advanced treatments, or everyday support, our team is committed to making every visit as smooth, professional, and reassuring as possible.

Learn More

In the Community Noll Family Dentistry is deeply committed to supporting and uplifting the Carlisle community. Each year, the practice selects a local organization to support both financially and through donations—helping causes like the Domestic Violence Services of Cumberland and Perry Counties during the holiday season.

Beyond charitable giving, the team embraces local traditions and spreads joy throughout the neighborhood. A highlight of the year is their participation in the town’s Halloween parade, where they bring out the “Molar Machine” float to hand out candy, engage with families, and celebrate in a fun, engaging way.

These efforts reflect Noll Family Dentistry’s core belief: that a caring practice goes beyond the exam chair, building stronger community connections with every gesture.
