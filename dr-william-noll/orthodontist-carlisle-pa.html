How Does The Orthodontic Process Work?

Schedule a complimentary orthodontic consultation with our team to assess your dental needs and determine if Braces or Invisalign is right for you.

If Braces or Invisalign is a fit for you, a customized treatment plan will be created. Using advanced 3D imaging technology, we will map out the precise movements of your teeth throughout the treatment process.

Whether your orthodontic treatment is Braces or Invisalign, a custom treatment plan will be created specifically for your teeth. Each treatment plan will be slightly different but with the main goal of gradually shifting your teeth into their desired positions.

Once you’ve completed your entire orthodontic treatment, your teeth should be in their final positions. Your orthodontist may recommend the use of retainers to help maintain your new smile, but each circumstance is different, so we’ll guide you on continued care as you progress along!
