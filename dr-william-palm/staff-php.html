Meet Our Team

Dr. Savannah Banas Dr. Savannah Banas is a native of Albuquerque, New Mexico. She studied dentistry at the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine in Denver, Colorado and graduated with her D.D.S. degree in 2014.

Dr. Banas has fallen in love with Michigan since moving her in 2014 and is proud to have made her home in the Detroit area. When not practicing dentistry, she enjoys spending time with here husband and children, attending sporting events, going to concerts, swimming, and visiting her family in New Mexico.

Dr. Daisy Barajas Originally a “California girl”, born in the heart of the Silicon Valley, Dr. Daisy started her academic career at California State University East Bay, majoring in Biological Sciences and minoring in chemistry. Dr. Daisy started her move eastward when obtaining her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Roseman University of Health Sciences in 2022. After 4 years in the beautiful state of Utah, she began practicing in Michigan as a National Health Scholar Corp scholar. She completed her service in 2024 and officially made Michigan home.

We’re thrilled to have Dr. Daisy join our family practice where she continues providing compassionate, high quality dental care to our valued patients.

Outside of the office, Dr. Daisy enjoys relaxing by the water, camping, and hitting the slopes on a snowboard in the winter. She also loves hosting Sunday watch parties to cheer on the Lions, alongside her fiance Ian (who is also a dentist!) and their two dogs, Bruno the Berner and Nala.

Dr. Daisy is an active member of the American Dental Association and the Michigan Dental Association and remains committed to lifelong learning through extensive continuing education courses.

Laurie, Dental Assistant Laurie joined the Palm Family Dental team in 2008 as Dr. Palm’s assistant. Laurie earned her Associate in Science degree from Central Michigan University in 1997 and also attained her certificate as a Certified Nurse Assistant and Phlebotomist from The American Medical School of Flint, Michigan in 2010. In her spare time, Laurie enjoys hiking, traveling and spending time outdoors. Laurie enjoys working with the friendly team at Palm Family Dental.

Brittany, Front Desk Brittany started working at a health insurance company out of high school. She enjoyed working there along with making great friends in the process. While working in reception, she found great opportunities at Palm Family Dental.

Brittany enjoys spending time with her friends and family. She has excellent organizational skills at work and home. Brittany also likes going to concerts, watching The Walking Dead, going out with friends, visiting her niece and going up north with her parents.

She is excited to be a part of a strong team at Palm Family Dental and looks forward to a future with them!

Whitney, Dental Hygienist Whitney is a graduate of University of Detroit Mercy with her Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene. She is certified in local anesthesia, nitrous oxide administration, and CPR/BLS. As part of her practice she performs oral cancer screenings and periodontal exams necessary to diagnosis a patient’s needs.

Whitney also received her Bachelor of Science degree in Psychology from the University of California, Davis. She is a member of the Sigma Phi Alpha Dental Hygiene Honor Society and Alpha Sigma Nu Jesuit Honor Society.

She loves her patients. Her passion in dental hygiene is to address and provide advanced dental hygiene care based on the needs of her patients and current dental research in order to support the quest to maintain, or regain, oral and overall health.

Originally from California, she has lived on the east coast as well as Chicago for 10 years before settling in Michigan.
