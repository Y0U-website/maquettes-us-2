Dr. Wisdom Teeth would like to welcome you to our site! We are a modern dental practice focused entirely on teeth extractions, with a primary focus on wisdom teeth. Believe it or not, your wisdom teeth play a major role in your overall oral health.

From the moment you walk through our doors, we want you to feel the difference in our practice. Your comfort is our highest priority and it shows.

If you have a problematic wisdom tooth, it is recommended that you have them removed. Call us for wisdom teeth removal.

You may need a tooth extraction if you have impacted wisdom teeth, tooth decay, dental injury, periodontal disease, or tooth overcrowding.
