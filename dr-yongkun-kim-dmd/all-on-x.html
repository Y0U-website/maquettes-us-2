All-on-X Implant Treatment: What to Expect

Comprehensive Evaluation

Every All-on-X treatment begins with a comprehensive evaluation. Your dentist will assess your teeth, gums, bone structure, and overall oral health using advanced diagnostic imaging and digital treatment planning.

Treatment

Depending on your individual needs, treatment may involve removing failing teeth, placing dental implants, and attaching a temporary fixed prosthesis.

Healing

The implants then require time to integrate with the surrounding bone. Once healing and implant integration are complete, your temporary restoration can be replaced with a custom-designed permanent prosthesis.

Treatment Timeline

Your treatment timeline will depend on your oral health, bone quality, implant stability, and individual treatment plan.

All-on-X Implant Aftercare

Proper aftercare is essential for successful healing and long-term implant health. Your dental team will provide personalized instructions based on your procedure.

Immediate Post-Surgical Care

  • Manage bleeding: Bite gently but firmly on gauze as directed by your dental team. Some mild oozing is normal after surgery.
  • Reduce swelling: Use cold packs on the outside of your face as directed, especially during the first 24 to 48 hours.
  • Follow rinsing instructions: Avoid vigorous rinsing or spitting immediately after surgery. Begin gentle rinsing when recommended by your dentist.
  • Follow a soft diet: Start with liquids and soft foods as directed. Avoid hard, crunchy, or sticky foods during the initial healing period.
  • Avoid smoking: Smoking can interfere with healing and increase the risk of implant complications.

Protecting Your Implants During Healing

Dental implants typically require several months to fully integrate with the surrounding bone. During this healing period, follow your dentist’s dietary recommendations and avoid putting excessive pressure on the implants or temporary restoration. Protecting the implants during osseointegration is important for successful healing.

Long-Term Care for All-on-X Implants

Even though your implant-supported teeth cannot be removed at home, daily cleaning is essential. Plaque and bacteria can accumulate around the implants and beneath the prosthesis, potentially contributing to inflammation and periimplantitis disease.

With proper care and regular professional maintenance, All-on-X dental implants can provide a stable and long-lasting solution for patients with extensive tooth loss or failing teeth.

  • Use a waterflosser: Gently clean beneath the prosthesis to remove food particles and plaque, following your dentist’s recommendations.
  • Clean underneath the prosthesis: Use implant-specific floss, floss threaders, or inter-dental brushes as recommended by your dental team.
  • Brush twice daily: Use a soft-bristled toothbrush or electric toothbrush with a non-abrasive toothpaste or cleaning product recommended for your restoration.
  • Maintain regular dental visits: Professional cleanings and routine implant evaluations are essential for monitoring your gums, implants, and prosthesis.
  • Follow your personalized maintenance plan: Your dentist may recommend specific cleaning tools and techniques based on your restoration and individual needs.
