Dental Implants

  • Resolve Bite & Joint Problems Prevent teeth shifting and maintain proper alignment and jaw function.
  • Restore Your Smile Implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth.
  • Improve Chewing & Speech Restore the ability to chew properly and speak clearly.
  • Enhance Facial Structure Help prevent bone loss and maintain natural facial contours.
  • Secure Dentures & Bridges Provide greater stability and comfort for dentures and bridges.

What Does Getting Dental Implants Involve?

  • Consultation & Planning X-rays and impressions are taken to determine bone structure, gum tissue, and spacing available for implants.
  • Implant Placement Surgery The implant is surgically placed into the jawbone and allowed to heal.
  • Healing & Osseointegration The implant gradually fuses with the jawbone to create a strong foundation.
  • Final Restoration Custom artificial teeth are created and attached to the implant.

Caring for Your New Implant

You will receive care instructions when treatment is completed. Good oral hygiene, healthy eating habits, and regular dental visits help extend the life of your implant.

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime and provide a reliable solution for tooth replacement.

Aftercare Tips

  • Brush & Floss Daily – Clean your implant like natural teeth.
  • Regular Checkups – Visit your dentist every six months.
  • Healthy Habits – Avoid chewing hard objects and maintain a balanced diet.
