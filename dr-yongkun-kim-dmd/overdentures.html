overdentures

  • Bone Preservation Implants and natural roots stimulate the jawbone, preventing the sunken facial appearance and bone loss associated with conventional dentures.
  • Improved Diet Patients can bite and chew a wider variety of foods because the prosthesis is firmly anchored.
  • Cost-Effective They offer many of the functional benefits of fixed, full-mouth implants at a fraction of the cost.

What To Expect

Implant Placement

2 to 4 titanium implants are surgically placed into the jawbone.

Healing Phase

A temporary denture is typically worn while the implants fuse to the jawbone (osseointegration), which can take a few months.

Fitting

Once healed, customized abutments are attached to the implants and your final, custom overdenture is fabricated to snap onto them.

After Care and Maintenance

  • Daily Removal Unlike fixed bridges, overdentures must be taken out daily for cleaning and to give the underlying gum tissues a break.
  • Wear and Tear The small rubber or plastic attachment rings inside the denture (like Locator attachments) will wear out over time and require periodic replacement to maintain a tight grip.
  • Routine Dental Care You need to see your dentist once every 6 months to get your implants and dentures professionally cleaned.
