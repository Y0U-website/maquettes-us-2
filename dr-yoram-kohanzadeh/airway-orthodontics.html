Southern California’s First Certified Airway Orthodontist What is airway orthodontics? Traditional orthodontics straightens teeth. Airway orthodontics goes deeper — treating the root cause of crooked teeth, sleep problems, and breathing issues. Not just the symptoms.

Why airway orthodontics changes everything

Most orthodontic problems aren’t caused by genetics. They’re caused by how the jaw and airway develop during childhood — influenced by breathing patterns, tongue position, and muscle habits.

When a child breathes through their mouth instead of their nose, the tongue drops to the floor of the mouth. This restricts jaw growth, narrows the airway, crowds the teeth, and sets off a cascade of health issues that can last a lifetime.

Airway orthodontics addresses these root causes — not just the crooked teeth that result from them. The goal isn’t just a straighter smile. It’s better breathing, better sleep, better focus, and better health.

It’s not genetics. It’s how we breathe.

Research now shows that the majority of orthodontic problems are caused by soft tissue dysfunction — not heredity. These habits start early and compound over time.
