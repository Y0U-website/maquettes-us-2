Our Mission The mission of Monongalia County Health Department is to achieve a healthy community by protecting and promoting public health through education, training, advocacy and the provision of health services.

For Your Convenience

MCHD operates under the laws set by the West Virginia Legislature, specifically those found in West Virginia Code Chapter 16, Article 2.

Monongalia County Health Department is mandated to conduct a Community Health Needs Assessment (CHNA) , which will be updated every three to five years.

The Community Health Improvement Plan (CHIP) , completed in 2026, is Monongalia County Health Department’s plan to make health more accessible and obtainable for the public, based on the CHNA survey.

About the Board

The Monongalia County Board of Health is composed of five members, each serving a five-year term. Board members are appointed by the Monongalia County Commission. No more than three of the members can belong to the same political party and no more than two can reside in the same magisterial district.

Meetings

Meetings are held the last Thursday in January, March, May, July, September and November and begin at 9 a.m. in the MCHD Training Center, with dates subject to change. Follow our social media and website alerts for updates.
