Dr. He has excellent clinical skills, which make you and your family more comfortable and at ease than you ever thought possible. Dr. He has over twenty years dental experience. She received her Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD) from University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. She obtained her first dental degree from China Medical University, where she practiced general dentistry for 14 years. She also earned her PhD of dental science in China before she received her postdoctoral research fellow training at University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. He is an active member of American Dental Association (ADA), Pennsylvania Dental Association, and Philadelphia Dental Society. She also has been taking continuing education courses to update her skills and broaden her knowledge of dentistry. Her goal is to provide utmost comfort, highest quality of care and most current treatment options for her patients.
