The practice

Welcome to Stubbs Family Dentistry! For over 20 years, our team of skilled and experienced dental professionals have been providing quality personalized care for families in the West Allis community. We are excited to serve you and your family for your dental health needs!

Dr. Yvonne Stubbs and our team of hygienists, assistants, and front office staff work together to provide high-quality dental care for patients of all ages. We believe in a judgment-free zone, helping you and your family improve your oral health and answering any questions you may have about your care. Our team works with you to ensure your care is convenient and fits with your schedule.

To repair teeth and treat issues like cavities, we can offer custom and lifelike crowns and fillings.

Following her active-duty career in the Air Force, Dr. Stubbs has worked 16 years in private dental practices in New Berlin and Wauwatosa. In 2020, she became the owner of Stubbs Family Dentistry, LLC, and is proud to serve the community of West Allis, WI, and nearby southeastern Wisconsin communities.

Dr. Stubbs loves making connections and friendships with her patients, listening to people’s stories and hearing about their joys and sorrows. She feels it is a privilege to be part of people’s lives in such an intimate way!
