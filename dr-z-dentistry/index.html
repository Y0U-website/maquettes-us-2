The practice

At Doctor Z Dentistry, we provide cosmetic and family dentistry to patients throughout the Monmouth and Ocean County, NJ area. Whether you’re visiting our office for a regular cleaning, or for more intensive dental work, you will be welcomed by our friendly, compassionate staff. If you have an urgent dental situation, we see same-day emergencies .
