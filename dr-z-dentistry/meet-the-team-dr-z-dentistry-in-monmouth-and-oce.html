Meet the Dr. Z Dentistry Team

Our friendly staff is here for you no matter what type of dental procedure you come in to our office for! Here’s a little bit about each and every one of us!

Dr. Robert Zona, DDS

Dr. Robert J. Zona received his Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from the State University of New York at Binghamton. He then attended the State University of New York at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine, where he received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree. He completed his training in a GPR program at Staten Island University Hospital, near where he grew up. His residency program focused on advanced training in pediatric dentistry, oral surgery, and management of dental emergencies.

While residing in Colorado, Dr. Zona established Broadway South Dental Group, with four other partners. He is a member of the ADA, CDA, NJDA, and AGD.

Kelly - Dental Assistnat

With a passion for dental health and patient well-being, both Kelly and Michelle bring excellent communication skills, a strong attention to detail, and a caring approach to every interaction. Whether you’re here for a routine checkup or a more involved procedure, our dental assistants are here to support you every step of the way.
