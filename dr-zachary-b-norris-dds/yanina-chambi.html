Meet The Doctor

Caring, Friendly & Dedicated

Doctor Chambi

Yanina Chambi D.D.S.

With the belief in the importance of giving back to her community, Dr. Chambi has dedicated part of her time working with low-income clinics and traveling abroad to provide free dental care to the less fortunate. She is proficient in all aspects of dentistry, and devotes much of her time to continuing education courses ensuring she is able to provide the best and most innovative dental care to her patients. She is bilingual, speaking fluent Spanish.

In her free time, Dr. Chambi enjoys traveling, staying active, and spending time with family and friends. With her love for hiking and trail running, she has fallen in love with Whidbey Island’s breathtaking scenery and countless trails. She is so excited for this new adventure at Island Family Dentistry, and looks forward to becoming partners in your dental care.
