MI Paste ONE Kids brings the clinical benefits of the MI Paste line to little ones. Its formula combines Recaldent™ (CPP-ACP) and fluoride to strengthen young enamel, help prevent cavities, and reduce sensitivity from an early age.

FluoriMax 5000 is a professional-strength toothpaste with a high fluoride concentration (1.1 % sodium fluoride), recommended for patients at high risk of cavities and for those with orthodontic appliances, dry mouth, or tooth sensitivity.

Its patented formula has a more fluid texture that improves fluoride uptake into enamel, reinforcing tooth structure and helping prevent new cavities. Available in Vanilla Mint and Spearmint.
