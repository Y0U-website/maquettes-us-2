Propel

Drabik Orthodontics offers Propel, an orthodontic appliance, to reduce the duration of orthodontic treatment by up to 50 %. Patients can achieve their desired smile faster and more comfortably with this revolutionary technology.

About Propel

How Propel Works

Propel speeds up the bone remodeling process in the jaw, thus leading to faster tooth movement. Using a patented SoftPulse technology, Propel sends micro-pulses into the bone that supports your teeth. These precisely calibrated vibrations increase cellular activity in the bones, accelerating the natural process of bone remodeling. Dr. Drabik provides patients with a Propel mouthpiece that easily fits over Invisalign aligners or braces, making it convenient to incorporate into daily routines. This hands-free treatment allows patients to engage in other activities while Propel works its magic.

Benefits of Propel

Propel significantly decreases overall treatment time, offering a faster path to a straighter smile. It can be used in conjunction with traditional braces or Invisalign, enhancing treatment flexibility. The non-invasive and gentle nature of Propel ensures a comfortable experience, contributing to more accurate and satisfying results.
