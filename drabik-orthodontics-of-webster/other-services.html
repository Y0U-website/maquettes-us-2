Dentofacial Orthopedics

Drabik Orthodontics offers dentofacial orthopedic services to create strong foundations for beautiful smiles. While orthodontics primarily focuses on straightening teeth, dentofacial orthopedics ensures that the underlying bone structures of the jaws and face are in the correct positions for optimal function. Dr. Drabik specializes in correcting overbites, underbites, and crossbites in children before adulthood when invasive jaw surgery may be the next step. Bringing children in for an evaluation between the ages of 7 and 9 is ideal for catching these issues.

Orthodontic Emergencies

Ideally, orthodontic treatment progresses smoothly with routine adjustments every six to twelve weeks leading to a hassle-free completion. However, in reality, life’s unpredictability can lead to orthodontic emergencies. Rest assured, if a pressing issue arises during treatment, our team is fully prepared to offer the necessary assistance to address any concerns promptly and effectively.

Helpful Info About Orthodontic Emergencies

Common Orthodontic Emergencies

Orthodontic emergencies don’t happen often, but they can still occur. If you or your child is in pain or worried, it’s best to get help from a qualified professional rather than trying to fix it at home. Dr. Drabik is the best person to handle issues with your braces or aligners and can get them back on track quickly.

  • Toothaches
  • Loose Bracket
  • Broken Wire
  • Poking Wire
  • Mouth Sores
  • Broken Ligature
  • Soft Tissue Cuts
  • Something Stuck Between Teeth
  • Broken or Lost Retainer
  • Loose Teeth

Tips For Avoiding an Orthodontic Emergency

  • Commit to a Solid Oral Hygiene Regimen: To maintain healthy teeth and gums and prevent dental issues, brush twice a day, floss daily, and use an antimicrobial mouthwash regularly.
  • Abide by the No-No List: Avoid hard, crunchy, or sticky foods, and opt for softer, nutrient-dense options to protect braces or aligners. Choose water as your primary beverage to promote oral health.
  • Prioritize Your Follow-Up Visits: Regular check-in visits with Dr. Drabik are crucial to monitor your progress and ensure the health of your orthodontic appliances, potentially preventing last-minute emergencies.
  • Limit Your Intake of Sugary Foods: Minimize consumption of sugary foods and drinks to reduce the risk of tooth decay and prevent delays in your orthodontic treatment plan.
