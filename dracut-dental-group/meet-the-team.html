DR. VINCENT OH

DMD, FICOI

Dr. Vincent Oh graduated from the University of Wisconsin in Madison with a bachelor’s degree in biochemistry and biology. He earned his Doctorate in Dental Medicine (D.M.D) from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine. After receiving his doctoral degree, he finished his post-doctoral training at Birmingham VA Medical Center and University of Alabama in Birmingham in Advanced Education in General Dentistry for one year. He is a member of the American Dental Association and Massachusetts Dental Society.

Dr. Oh was born and raised in South Korea until the end of high school, which strengthened his cultural background. He believes in making his patients’ dental experiences as comfortable as possible and customizing his care according to each patients’ needs while emphasizing on patient education. He is committed to providing quality dental care to his patients and enjoys providing a wide range of dental care. In his leisure time, Dr. Oh loves travelling.
