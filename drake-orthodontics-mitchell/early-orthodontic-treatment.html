Navigating Your Child’s Early Orthodontic Journey

The process for early orthodontic treatment begins with a comprehensive evaluation which includes X-rays and possibly impressions for study models. Depending on the individual needs of your child, treatment options might include fixed appliances, partial braces, or full braces. The goal is always to address immediate issues while planning for future dental development.

Setting Expectations: What Parents Should Know

Embarking on early orthodontic treatment requires commitment from both parents and children. It’s essential to maintain regular appointments at Drake Orthodontics for adjustments and monitoring progress. Educating your child about the importance of oral hygiene during treatment will also contribute significantly to achieving successful outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should my child first visit an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an initial visit at age 7.

What signs might indicate my child needs early orthodontic treatment?

Crowded or misplaced teeth, difficulties with chewing or biting, and mouth breathing are common indicators.

How long does early orthodontic treatment last?

Treatment duration varies but generally ranges from 12 to 24 months, depending on the complexity of the case.
