A warm and welcoming office where you can receive state-of-the-art dental care! About Us

Dr. Alex Drake opened Drake & Seymour Dentistry in July 2012. He had worked for local dentists, but he wanted to open his own business. He had a vision of what the perfect dental office would be, and he intended to fulfill that vision. His goal was to offer Oxford, NC and the surrounding areas the absolute best in dentistry , while providing a hometown feel and personal touch. Through his practice, he wanted to build strong relationships with people in the community seeking healthy, beautiful smiles.

Our team is caring, knowledgeable, thoughtful and kind, and we are all local to the community. We truly care about offering our patients the state-of-the-art general, cosmetic, and restorative dental treatments they deserve!

Will Seymour

DDS

Dr. Seymour is a North Carolina native, born and raised in Greensboro, NC. He attended North Carolina State University where he majored in Construction Engineering and Management with a minor in Biological Sciences. He continued his education at East Carolina School of Dental Medicine where he earned his dental degree.

Giving back to the community is always something that Dr Seymour has enjoyed. He has volunteered at North Carolina Missions of Mercy Dental Clinics across the state for over 8 years. Prior to dental school, Dr. Seymour assisted in running the free dental clinics through Missions of Mercy and has attended over 35 clinics. He has also had the opportunity of traveling abroad with the Rotary Club to provide dental care to the underserved population in Nicaragua, Costa Rica and Honduras.

When Dr. Seymour is not in the office you can find him working outside on his mini farm with wife Ashley and dog Levi. Some of his other hobbies are rebuilding engines and restoring classic cars. He is proud to provide patients with the latest improvements in dental care by staying ahead of the curve on dental technology and materials. Dr. Seymour is thrilled to be able to offer comprehensive dental care to the people of Oxford and all surrounding communities.
