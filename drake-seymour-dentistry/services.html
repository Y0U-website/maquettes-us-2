General Dentistry

General dentistry is the treatment area that involves preventing dental issues from developing. Preventative care can help you avoid many dental and oral problems, such as cavities, jaw bone damage, and gum disease. At routine appointments, we have the opportunity to educate our patients on proper oral care and detect any developing problems.

Restorative Dentistry

Whether your smile has been damaged by trauma, decay or gum disease, with our restorative treatments, we aim to restore the strength, structure, and appearance of your teeth. These services help prevent further damage from occurring and allow your beautiful smile to last for a lifetime!

Dental Implants

If you have missing teeth, we provide dental implants to help restore the strength, function and appearance of your smile. An implant is a titanium post that is surgically placed into the gums. After a few months, this post will meld with the jawbone, creating a sturdy foundation for the crown restoration. Dr. Drake will work with you to determine if you have sufficient jaw bone to support dental implants.

Implants can be used to replace one tooth or multiple teeth, and can even be used to secure dentures and bridges. They improve your chewing ability and speech, and help prevent the negative side effects that can result from maintaining a gap in the smile, such as jaw bone damage and gum disease. If you are looking for a tooth replacement that will last a lifetime, a dental implant may be the right choice for you!
