Accessibility

Our Commitment

Dreamhouse Dental is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to people with disabilities. We strive to provide a positive experience for all users and are continually improving the user experience for everyone while applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance Status

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities and are the internationally recognized benchmark for web accessibility.

While we strive to adhere to the accepted guidelines and standards for accessibility and usability, it is not always possible to do so in all areas of the website. We are continually seeking solutions that will bring all areas of our website up to the same level of accessibility.

Measures Taken

Dreamhouse Dental takes the following measures to ensure accessibility of our website:

Include accessibility as part of our development and design process

Provide text alternatives for non-text content

Ensure sufficient color contrast throughout the site

Make all functionality available from a keyboard

Provide clear and consistent navigation mechanisms

Ensure forms include descriptive labels and error messages

Use semantic HTML to convey structure and meaning

Support assistive technologies including screen readers

Provide captions and alternatives for multimedia content where applicable

Assistive Technologies Supported

Our website is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies:

Screen readers (including JAWS, NVDA, and VoiceOver)

Known Limitations

Despite our best efforts to ensure accessibility of our website, there may be some limitations. We are aware of potential accessibility gaps and are actively working to address them. If you encounter any barriers, please let us know so we can work to resolve the issue.

Third-Party Content

Our website may include content provided by third parties, such as embedded maps, social media feeds, or payment processors. While we strive to ensure these components are accessible, we may not have full control over their accessibility features.

In-Office Accessibility

Our commitment to accessibility extends beyond the digital experience. Dreamhouse Dental locations are designed to accommodate patients with disabilities, including:

Wheelchair-accessible entrances and treatment rooms

Bilingual staff (English and Spanish) at all locations

Patient communication accommodations upon request

Flexible scheduling for patients with special needs
