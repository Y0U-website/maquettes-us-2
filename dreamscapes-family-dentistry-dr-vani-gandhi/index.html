The practice

Dreamscapes Family Dentistry was built from the ground up by Dr. Benay Dabney in 1987. Known for her hard work, integrity, and patient-first approach, Dr. Dabney laid the foundation for the trusted dental care we continue to offer today.

In 2024, Dr. Vani Gandhi took over the practice. A graduate of NYU and an experienced dentist since 1995, Dr. Gandhi brings both technical skill and a deep belief in personalized care. She is passionate about building long-term relationships with patients through mutual trust and transparency.

Our modern Oak Park office is designed for your comfort, featuring ergonomic treatment rooms and a calm, welcoming atmosphere. Every detail from the layout to the lighting was chosen to help patients feel relaxed from check-in to checkout.

At Dreamscapes Family Dentistry, we treat patients of all ages with personalized dental care. Whether you’re visiting for a routine cleaning or a full-mouth restoration, Dr. Vani Gandhi and our experienced team are here to help.
