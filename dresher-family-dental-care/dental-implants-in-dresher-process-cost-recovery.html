Dental Implant Process (Step-by-Step)

The entire process can take several months, but the result is worth the wait: a strong, permanent tooth replacement that looks completely natural.

  • Consultation & Imaging: We begin with a full exam and digital scans to plan the implant precisely.
  • Implant Placement: We proudly offer IV sedation and nitrous oxide to soothe fears and help patients feel comfortable. The titanium post is then placed into your jawbone.
  • Healing & Osseointegration: Over 3–6 months, your jawbone fuses with the implant.
  • Abutment & Crown: Once healed, we attach the abutment and top it with a custom crown.

How to Care for Dental Implants

Dental implants in Dresher are low-maintenance but still need daily care. Here’s how to keep them healthy:

  • Brush twice a day with a soft toothbrush.
  • Floss around the implant daily.
  • Visit us in Dresher for regular checkups.
  • Avoid chewing on hard objects (ice, pens, etc.).

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With consistent oral health care, dental implants can last several years or more. The crown may need replacing after 10 years, but the titanium post can endure for a long time.
