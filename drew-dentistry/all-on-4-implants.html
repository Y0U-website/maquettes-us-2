Improved Aesthetics With All-on-4 Implants, you can achieve a natural-looking smile that enhances your facial features. The prosthetic teeth are custom-made to match your existing teeth in color, shape, and size, ensuring a seamless blend. This aesthetic improvement not only boosts your self-esteem but also contributes to a more youthful appearance by supporting the facial structure and preventing the sunken look associated with tooth loss.

Long-Term Oral Health All-on-4 Implants play a crucial role in maintaining your oral health. By stimulating the jawbone, they prevent bone deterioration that often follows tooth loss. This stimulation helps preserve the bone structure, reducing the risk of further tooth loss and maintaining the integrity of your facial features. Additionally, All-on-4 Implants are easy to care for, requiring only regular brushing and flossing to keep your mouth healthy.

The All-on-4 Implants Treatment Process

Consultation and Planning

The journey to a new smile begins with a comprehensive consultation at Drew Dentistry. Dr. Shane Drew will conduct a thorough examination of your oral health, using advanced imaging technology to assess the condition of your jawbone and gums. This step is crucial in determining your eligibility for All-on-4 Implants and allows for the creation of a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs and goals.

Implant Placement

Once the treatment plan is finalized, the next step involves the precise placement of the titanium implants. Dr. Drew will strategically position four implants into the jawbone, using minimally invasive techniques to ensure a comfortable experience. These implants serve as the foundation for your new teeth, and in many cases, a temporary set of prosthetic teeth can be attached on the same day, allowing you to leave the office with a complete smile.

Final Restoration

After a healing period that allows the implants to integrate with the jawbone, you&#x27;ll return to Drew Dentistry for the final restoration. During this visit, Dr. Drew will replace the temporary prosthetic teeth with a permanent, custom-made set that matches your natural teeth in appearance and function. This final step completes the process, providing you with a durable, beautiful smile that you can enjoy for years to come.
