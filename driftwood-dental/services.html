Endodontics

This procedure cleans infections out of the chamber and roots of a tooth. It alleviates pain and clears out infection areas that our body can’t clear on its own.

Laser Therapy

Laser Therapy dives deep into modern dental treatments, leveraging the power of precise laser technology. From addressing subgingival bacteria to alleviating TMJ disorder discomfort and treating cold sores and canker sores, it’s a method that minimizes discomfort and maximizes outcomes. Our certified professionals harness this technology to ensure swift healing and stellar results.

  • Temporomandibular Joint Pain Relief
  • Cold Sore & Canker Sore Treatment

Dentures

With dentures we can replace missing teeth whether you need a few teeth replaced or a whole arch, dentures can give you back the function and esthetics similar to what you had before. We also offer flippers for time in between treatments for temporary purposes.

Emergency Appointments

Perio Protect

Perio Protect provides a non-invasive, adjunct periodontal treatment for at-home use. The Perio Tray therapy kills oral pathogens in 10-15 minutes. Favorable side effects include fresh breath and whiter teeth.
