Hair Restoration

Procell Microchanneling for the scalp uses tiny needles to create micro-injuries, stimulating collagen, elastin, and growth factors to rejuvenate hair follicles, reduce shedding, and promote thicker, fuller hair growth for both men and women experiencing thinning hair. It’s a non-surgical, minimally invasive procedure with little downtime, combining the body’s healing response with powerful stem cell growth factors for noticeable regrowth, often requiring multiple sessions for best results.

  • Creates micro-channels: A stamp-like device creates microscopic punctures in the scalp, triggering the body’s natural healing process.
  • Delivers growth factors: A serum with stem cell-derived growth factors (cytokines, peptides) is applied, penetrating deeply through the channels to nourish follicles.
  • Stimulates growth: This boosts collagen, elastin, and signals dormant follicles to enter the active growth (anagen) phase, leading to thicker hair and reduced shedding.
  • Results: Noticeable results like less shedding and new "baby hairs" in 8-12 weeks, with thicker strands and improved density in 3-6 months.
  • Downtime: Minimal, often just 2-24 hours.
  • Discomfort: Minimal, due to the fine needles.
  • Sessions: Typically a series of 3-6 treatments, 4 weeks apart, with maintenance sessions recommended.
  • Before: Clean, dry scalp; avoid sun exposure and hair products.
  • After: Avoid washing hair for 12-24 hours; use gentle, sulfate-free products; protect from sun; follow serum application instructions
