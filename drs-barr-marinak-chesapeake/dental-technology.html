Piezosurgery

Refined for oral treatment and implantology, Piezosurgery is an innovative means of cutting bone tissue utilizing ultrasonic vibrations. A process invented by Tomaso Vercellotti. By adjusting the frequencies on this device, a surgeon can cut hard tissues while not disturbing soft tissues such as nerves, sinus membranes, and gum tissues. This reliable system enhances the results, Piezosurgery can precisely cut bone during augmentation procedures.
