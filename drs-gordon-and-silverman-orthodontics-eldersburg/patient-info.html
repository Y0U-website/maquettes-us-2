Virtual Appointments

Convenient Orthodontic Care

Virtual appointments provide a convenient and efficient way to receive orthodontic care from the comfort of your home. Through secure video conferencing, Dr. Silverman can assess your progress, answer any questions, and adjust your treatment plan as needed. This approach saves you time and ensures that you receive continuous, high-quality care without the need for frequent office visits.
