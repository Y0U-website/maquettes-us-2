Treatments All Ages Children Teen Adult Though we can enhance a smile at any age, there is an optimal time period to begin treatment. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that the initial orthodontic evaluation should occur at the first sign of orthodontic problems or no later than age 7. Beginning treatment at this time ensures the greatest result and the least amount of time and expense.

Orthodontic treatment for teens is designed to correct dental issues while accommodating their active lifestyles. With options like traditional braces, clear braces, and aligners, teens can achieve a healthier smile with minimal disruption. Our team works closely with each teen to ensure their treatment is effective, comfortable, and fits seamlessly into their daily routine.

Orthodontic treatment isn’t just for kids anymore. Tooth alignment can be changed at any age, if your gums and bone structure are healthy. We offer a variety of treatments that are designed for different age groups – including adults. Orthodontic treatment at later stages in life can dramatically improve your appearance and self-esteem.

Braces

Traditional braces, made from stainless steel, are the most common type of braces used to straighten teeth and correct bite issues. They consist of metal brackets and archwires that work together to move your teeth into the desired position gradually. These braces are highly effective and allow for customization with colored elastics, making them a popular choice for patients of all ages.

Ceramic braces offer a more discreet option. Made from clear or tooth-colored materials, these braces blend in with your natural teeth, making them less noticeable. They work like metal braces, using brackets and archwires to move your teeth into the desired position gradually. While they are less visible, they require the same level of care and maintenance to ensure effective results.

Self-ligating braces offer a modern alternative to traditional braces. They use a bracket instead of elastic bands to hold the archwire in place. This innovative design reduces friction and allows teeth to move more freely, often resulting in shorter treatment times and fewer appointments. These braces are also known for being more comfortable, as they apply gentler, consistent pressure on the teeth.

Mini metal braces are a smaller and more discreet version of traditional metal braces. Despite their reduced size, they are just as effective at aligning teeth and correcting bite issues. Their compact design makes them more comfortable and less noticeable, making them a popular choice for teens and adults seeking orthodontic treatment.

Combination treatment in orthodontics involves using more than one type of appliance to achieve optimal results. This approach can include a mix of traditional braces, clear aligners, and other orthodontic devices tailored to address specific dental issues. By combining different treatments, we can effectively and efficiently correct complex alignment problems, ensuring a straighter and healthier smile.

Invisalign®

Transform Your Smile Discreetly

Invisalign offers a discreet and comfortable way to straighten your teeth using clear, removable aligners. This innovative treatment is tailored to your specific needs and gradually shifts your teeth into the desired position. Under the expert guidance of Dr. Silverman, Invisalign treatment becomes even more effective with precise adjustments and optimal results.

Retainers

Essential for Post-Orthodontic Care

Retainers play a crucial role in maintaining the corrected position of teeth after orthodontic treatment. Whether removable or fixed, these devices help prevent regression when worn as directed by Dr. Silverman. Regular follow-ups ensure that your retainer functions correctly and adjustments can be made for optimal results.

Technology

iTero

The iTero scanner revolutionizes the way we take dental impressions, eliminating the need for uncomfortable, goopy molds. This advanced technology captures highly accurate 3D images of your teeth and gums, ensuring precise treatment planning and improved patient experience. This innovation leads to better fitting orthodontic appliances, enhancing the overall effectiveness of your treatment.

Airway Focused Treatment

Airway orthodontic treatment improves both your smile and your ability to breathe comfortably. By addressing issues, such as jaw alignment, Dr. Silverman creates space that supports a healthier airway. Personalized care and ongoing monitoring ensure long-term improvements in breathing, sleep, and overall quality of life.
