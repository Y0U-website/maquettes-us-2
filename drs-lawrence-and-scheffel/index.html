The practice

Every patient’s situation is unique. Health, age, finances, and dexterity are examples of things that vary patient to patient. We enjoy getting to know our patients and then providing care that specifically meets their needs.

Patient comfort is extremely important to us and we do all we can to make patients’ visits as pleasant as possible.

Our team members have been with the practice for an average of 10 years each. This helps with continuity of care and continued strong relationships with our patients.

From many hours of continuing education, to paying attention to details, to taking the time to do things well, we do all we can to provide quality care for our patients.

We take our time to thoroughly explain options with words and pictures that the patient can understand.

We are a group of professionals who are dedicated to serving you with the most pleasant dental experience. Our mission and commitment is to provide you with the best possible dental care that we are capable of so that you may establish and maintain optimal dental health throughout your lifetime.
