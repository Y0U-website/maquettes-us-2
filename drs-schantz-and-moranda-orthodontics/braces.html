FAQs About Damon Braces

  • How long does treatment with Damon Braces typically take?: Treatment times vary, but Damon Braces often reduce total treatment by several months compared to traditional braces.
  • Are Damon Braces more comfortable than regular braces?: Yes. The self-ligating system applies lighter forces and eliminates the pressure from elastic ties, reducing soreness.
  • Do Damon Braces cost more?: While initial costs can be similar or slightly higher, fewer visits and shorter treatment times may offset expenses.
  • Can I get Damon Braces if I already had braces before?: Absolutely. They&#x27;re often used for relapse cases or to correct bite issues that weren’t fully addressed previously.
  • What happens after Damon Braces come off?: You’ll be fitted with a retainer to keep your smile aligned long-term.
