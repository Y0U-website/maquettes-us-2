FAQs About Invisalign

  • How long does Invisalign treatment take?: Most treatments last 12–18 months, though some minor corrections may be completed in as little as 6 months.
  • Is Invisalign more expensive than braces?: Invisalign is competitively priced with traditional braces. We offer flexible payment plans and can discuss insurance options during your consultation.
  • Can I eat with Invisalign in?: No — aligners should be removed during meals. But this also means no food restrictions like with braces.
  • What if I lose an aligner tray?: Contact our office right away. We can often reorder or adjust your treatment plan to stay on track.
  • Is Invisalign painful?: While some pressure is normal when switching trays, Invisalign is generally more comfortable than braces due to the lack of wires and brackets.
