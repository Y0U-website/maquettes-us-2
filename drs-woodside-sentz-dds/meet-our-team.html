Meet Your Team of Registered Dental Hygienists

Born and raised in Warrenton, VA, Aimee began her dental career in 2007 with Dr. Jennifer Woodside Pediatrics, where she discovered her passion for patient care. After graduating from dental hygiene school, she joined Woodside and Sentz Dental in 2012 and has been dedicated to providing exceptional service ever since.

In 2022, Aimee and her family moved to Florida, where she continued to practice dental hygiene part-time. Three years later, missing her loved ones and her hometown, she and her husband returned to Virginia. Knowing exactly where she wanted to be, Aimee rejoined the Woodside and Sentz family in June 2025.

With over 13 years of experience, Aimee is known for her gentle touch, patient-centered care, and ability to make every visit comfortable and positive. She takes pride in easing dental anxiety, promoting preventive care, and celebrating her patients’ oral health milestones. Outside of work, Aimee enjoys spending time with her husband, their three boys, and their yellow Lab—whether walking, baking, or simply enjoying time together.

Her husband’s career brought their family to Virginia in 1999, and they’re proud to call Fauquier County home. Dorie is deeply committed to building genuine relationships with her patients. She takes time to understand their individual needs and concerns, creating a comfortable experience for every visit. Known for her gentle touch and personalized care, she takes pride in helping patients maintain healthy, confident smiles.

Outside the office, Dorie enjoys spending time with her husband—her high school sweetheart—their two grown sons, and their families. She loves exploring the outdoors, tending to her hobby farm, and connecting with friends and loved ones. With Dorie as part of our team, patients can always expect care that’s both compassionate and skilled.

Jodi Mathews RDH Jodi was raised in Wausau, Wisconsin—so naturally, she loves cheese!
