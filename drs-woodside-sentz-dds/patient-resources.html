Our Philosophy on Dental Insurance We believe every patient deserves the freedom to choose a dentist based on trust, quality, and personal connection—not an insurance company’s list.

Our practice accepts all dental insurance plans and is happy to file your claims. However, we are not contracted as an in-network provider. Many patients assume this means they can’t be seen here, but that’s not true—you have the right to choose your provider. That means our fees may be higher than what insurance companies consider “usual and customary,” but they are fair—and more importantly, they are grounded in your best interest.

By staying out of networks, we offer individualized treatment where quality and integrity come first.

Answers to Your Top Dental Questions

  • How does a dental savings plan work? Once enrolled in our savings plan, you’ll pay a reduced rate for dental treatments. There are no deductibles, waiting periods, or claim forms just straightforward discounts on your needed services.
  • What should I expect during my first visit? Your first visit will include a thorough consultation where we discuss your concerns, review your medical history, and create a customized treatment plan. You’ll also receive information on the procedure and any necessary pre-surgery instructions.
  • Will I be asleep during my procedure? Not necessarily. With nitrous oxide, you’ll remain awake and aware but feel deeply relaxed. Oral sedation may make you drowsy, but you’ll still be responsive. If you require deeper sedation, we can discuss options tailored to your needs.
  • Will my dental insurance cover my treatment? We accept insurance plans and will help you navigate the process by assisting with paperwork and checking your coverage. Since we are primarily a fee-for-service provider, coverage may vary depending on your plan, but we’ll work with you to maximize any benefits available.
  • What if I can’t afford to pay for treatment all at once? We offer flexible payment plans to help make dental care more accessible. Our team will work with you to create a plan that fits your budget, and we’ll be upfront about any costs from the start no surprises. We believe finances shouldn’t be a barrier to getting the care you need!
