About us

Our Mission

To enhance the clinical experience for dentists and patients by optimizing and innovating dental handpiece.

Our Vision

To be the world s most respected dental handpiece manufacturer.

Core Values

Optimizing & Innovating

R&D Journey

With more than a decade of experience in developing dental handpieces, our engineering team has established a comprehensive product line of dental handpieces, securing numerous patents and utility models in a relatively short period.

DTL-dent is dedicated to listening to the needs of every dentist and are committed to continuously enhancing the clinical experi- ence for both dental professionals and their patients.

Manufacturing facilities

Equipped with advanced CNC precision automatic lathes, Machining centers, Precision grinding machines.

​Our commitment to precision and quality ensures that every part and product meets standards that maximize reliability and performance.

Quality Assurance

DTL maintains rigorous standards of quality assurance, our comprehensive quality control process includes pre-production inspections, in-process quality checks and final product performance evaluations to ensure compliance from raw materials to components to completed products.

Furthermore, DTL s dedication to continuous improvement in production management ensures repeatability and reproducibility, guaranteeing consistency, reliability, and superior quality that meets a higher standards of the industry.
