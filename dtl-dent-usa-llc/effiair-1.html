Rapid Stop Mechanism

EffiAir is equipped with Rapid Stop Mechnism, which operates by incorporating a special flexible seal component on the bearing. This seal opens under air pressure when the air supply is on and automati- cally returns to its closed position when the air supply is off.

This design enables the handpiece to stop quickly after operation, and has been proven to maintain this performance after 250 reprocessing cycles.

OptiAir achieve bur stop in less than 1.66 seconds

In conventional handpieces, after the dentist turns off the tool (stops the air supply), the bur continues to spin for up to five seconds due to inertia. During this time, the dentist must carefully hold the tool in place inside the patient s mouth to prevent injury to the gums or inner lips.

100 % Zero-Suction

DTL s signature aerodynamic zero-suction design prevents blood, saliva, or other bio material drawing back into the handpiece.

Effectively prevents cross-contamination and extends the service life of the cartridge.

Optimized Ceramic Ball Bearing Utilizes optimized ceramic balls bearings, ensuring smoother and quieter operation.

Providing reliable and consistent torque and overall improved efficiency for dental procedures.

Long-Lasting Clamping DTL’s 3 Points Spring Chucking mechanism that dynamically enhance the retentional force in proportion to the rotational speed, can reach up to 40N .

Ensuring enhanced stability and precision at higher speeds.

Dynamic balanced This rigorous balancing procedure improves the performance and longevity of the handpiece, reduces vibration and noise, and leads to a smoother operating experience.

Precise Concentricity Utilizes chucking shafts with improved concentricity to ensure that the runout does not exceed 4 m , thus further enhancing the stability and precision of the cartridge s operation.
