When Is General Anesthesia Recommended?

While many children can comfortably complete treatment with traditional care or sedation options, some situations may require general anesthesia. This approach allows dental treatment to be completed while your child is fully asleep and unaware of the procedure.

General anesthesia may be recommended for young children, patients with special healthcare needs, severe dental anxiety, or children who require extensive dental treatment.

What Can Parents Expect?

Before treatment, our team will review your child’s medical history and explain every step of the process. We work closely with qualified anesthesia professionals to help ensure your child’s safety and comfort throughout the procedure.

One advantage of general anesthesia is that multiple dental procedures can often be completed during a single appointment, reducing the need for repeated visits.
