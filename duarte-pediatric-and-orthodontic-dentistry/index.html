The practice

Same convenient location with a new focus on children’s dentistry and orthodontic care.

From routine cleanings and dental exams to orthodontic care and sedation dentistry, we offer a full range of services designed to support your child’s oral health at every stage of development. We are committed to creating a welcoming environment where families receive compassionate care and children build healthy habits that last a lifetime.

Every child’s smile has different needs. Our pediatric dental and orthodontic services are designed to support healthy growth, prevent dental problems early, and make each visit as comfortable as possible for children and parents.

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends scheduling your child’s first dental visit by age one or within six months of the first tooth appearing. Our goal is to make this first experience positive, comfortable, and educational for both children and parents.

Families in Duarte trust our team for gentle, compassionate dental care designed to help children feel comfortable, confident, and cared for at every visit.

Our friendly team is here to answer your questions and help your child enjoy a healthy, confident smile.

Orthodontic treatment can help straighten teeth, improve bite alignment, and support a healthy, confident smile as your child grows.

Crooked teeth, crowding, spacing issues, and bite problems can affect both the appearance and function of your child’s smile. Orthodontic treatment helps guide teeth into healthier positions while supporting proper jaw development.
