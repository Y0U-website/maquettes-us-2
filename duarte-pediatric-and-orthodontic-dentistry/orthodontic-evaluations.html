Orthodontic Evaluations for Children in Duarte, CA

Why Orthodontic Evaluations Matter

As children grow, their teeth and jaws continue to develop. Orthodontic evaluations allow our team to assess tooth alignment, jaw growth, spacing, and bite function to determine whether future orthodontic treatment may be beneficial.

Early evaluations help us identify concerns at the right time and create a plan that supports healthy smile development.

When Should My Child Have an Orthodontic Evaluation?

Not every child will need orthodontic treatment immediately, but regular evaluations allow us to monitor changes as your child grows. By identifying potential issues early, we can recommend the most appropriate treatment options when needed.

Every child’s smile develops differently. During an orthodontic evaluation, we take a close look at how the teeth and jaws are growing to identify potential concerns before they become more complicated. Early evaluations allow us to monitor development and recommend treatment at the most appropriate time if needed.

Benefits of Orthodontic Evaluations

Identifies alignment concerns early

Monitors jaw and facial development

Evaluates bite function and spacing

Helps determine the ideal timing for treatment

Supports long-term oral health

Provides peace of mind for parents
