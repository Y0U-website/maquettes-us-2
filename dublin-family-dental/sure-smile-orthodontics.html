Our SureSmile Clear Aligner Process

  • Consultation and Evaluation: During your initial consultation, Dr. Cynthia Ignacio will assess your teeth and bite, discuss your smile goals, and determine if SureSmile Clear Aligners are the right treatment option.
  • Treatment Planning: Using advanced 3D imaging technology, we will digitally represent your teeth and develop a personalized treatment plan. You will have the opportunity to preview the expected outcome of your treatment before it even begins.
  • Aligner Fabrication: Once we finalize your treatment plan, your custom aligners will be fabricated using precise measurements and advanced technology. You will receive a series of aligners designed to move your teeth into their desired positions gradually.
  • Wearing the Aligners: You will wear each set of aligners for about two weeks, following the recommended wearing schedule provided by our dental team. We will schedule regular check-up appointments to monitor your progress and ensure your treatment is on track.
  • Retention Phase: After completing your SureSmile Clear Aligner treatment, we will provide you with retainers to help maintain your newly aligned smile. Wearing retainers as prescribed will ensure that your teeth remain in their corrected positions.
