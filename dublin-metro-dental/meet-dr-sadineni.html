Meet Dr. Sadineni

Aparna Sadineni, DDS, FAACD, MAGD, FICOI – A Commitment to Dental Excellence

Dr. Sadineni’s commitment to oral health extends to her family, as both of her daughters are pursuing Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery.

In her free time, Dr. Sadineni likes to watch movies with her husband, attend social gatherings, travel with her firends & family. Her passion towards Running is what keeps her active & fit — she is a Marathon runner & just completed her Seventh Half marathon!

  • Dr. Meghana is pursuing Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery at NYU Health Hospitals, New York. She graduated with a degree in dentistry from the Case Western Reserve University School of Dental Medicine.
  • Dr. Srighana is pursuing Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery at the UPenn School of Dental Medicine, Pennsylvania. She graduated with a degree in dentistry from the UPenn School of Dental Medicine.
