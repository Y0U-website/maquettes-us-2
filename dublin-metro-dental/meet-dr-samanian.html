Meet Dr. Samanian

Yasaman Samanian, DDS – A Commitment to Dental Excellence

Dr. Samanian is deeply committed to ensuring your comfort and well-being throughout every visit. She offers personalized, compassionate care, focusing on honest and clear communication to ensure you fully understand your treatment plan. She aims to help you leave each appointment with a healthy, confident smile and a positive experience.

A graduate of The Ohio State University, Dr. Samanian earned her dental degree Magna Cum Laude. She has a passion for continuous learning and stays at the forefront of dental advancements. She is dedicated to incorporating the latest research and technologies into her practice to provide the best care possible.

Dr. Samanian excelled in providing a wide range of dental services, from routine exams and cleanings to more complex dental procedures such as surgical tooth extractions, root canals, implants, and Invisalign clear braces. She has developed and implemented comprehensive treatment plans for diverse dental issues, collaborating with specialists to ensure optimal patient outcomes. Her clinical experience also includes proficiency in prosthodontics, restorative dentistry, and advanced procedures like CAD/CAM dentistry.

Outside of the dental office, Dr. Samanian enjoys traveling to new destinations, immersing herself in different cultures, and savoring local cuisines
