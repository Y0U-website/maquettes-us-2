Meet the Team at Dubos & Stewart General Dentistry

Our dental practice has been a part of the Circleville, OH community since 1977. Over the years, we’ve learned that helping you achieve your personal oral health goals is about more than your teeth and gums.

When they take the time to understand all your family’s unique needs including your general health, lifestyle, career, family life, and budget Drs. Dubos & Stewart can provide personalized dentistry that meets all your unique needs.

We’re pleased to offer a full range of general dentistry and cosmetic dentistry procedures, from routine cleanings to dentures, dental fillings, crowns and bridges, and implants. Together, our knowledgeable dental team ensures that you receive the personalized care you deserve in a comfortable and welcoming country setting.

We are grateful for our long-term patients who have been part of our dental family for decades. Our friendly dental care team also enjoys introducing new patients to our practice, and you can be confident that we will welcome you warmly and treat you like a valued and important member of our family.

Please call us at (740) 474-1900 to schedule an appointment for your next dental exam and cleaning.

Lisa D. Dubos, DDS

Dr. Lisa D. Dubos joined Dr. Ely in 2000. Dr. Dubos believes that the key to overall health is to maintain good dental health.

I love greeting my patient with a warm caring smile and helping them give one back.

With a primary goal of changing patients’ perception of dentistry, Dr. Dubos strives to put her patients at ease and to help them understand that the dental experience no longer has to be a fearful one.

Dr. Dubos grew up in East Palestine, Ohio, and she and her husband, John, are kept busy caring for their dogs, Leo and Archie. John is also a dentist and practices in Grove City.

Community Service

Dr. Dubos serves as a worship leader for the Grove City United Methodist Church, and is president of the Pinnacle Ladies Association at Pinnacle Golf Club. Previously, Dr. Dubos also served as a Circle of Caring board member, program coordinator for the Angel Tree Ministry, chair of Pickaway Health Services, and has provided free dental care through the Mission of Mercy Project in Parkersburg, West Virginia,

Education and Professional Associations

Dr. Dubos graduated from East Palestine High School and went on to attend both Youngstown State University and The Ohio State University. In 1995, Dr. Dubos graduated from Youngstown State in 1995 and earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree (DDS) from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in 2000.

Dr. Dubos is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Ohio Dental Association, the Columbus Dental Society, the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Association of Women Dentists, and the American Society of Forensic Odontology. She is also a member of the Sfumato Dental Study Club.

Interests and Hobbies

Dr. Dubos enjoys traveling, reading, and entertaining.

Brian M. Stewart, DDS

Dr. Stewart joined Drs. Ely and Dubos in May 2015. He believes that the key to overall health is to maintain good dental health.

It is my goal to have every patient that walks into our office walk out with better oral health. I believe the best thing I can do is to educate our patients about prevention and proper home care.

With a goal of educating and caring for his patients in such a way that each one feels special, Dr. Stewart is excited to reacquaint with friends and family as he welcomes new and existing patients.

Dr. Stewart was born and raised in Circleville, Ohio. He and his wife Whitney, also a Circleville native, reside in southern Pickaway County with their daughter Madison and son Blake.

Community Service

Dr. Stewart regularly volunteers at the Hope Dental Clinic in Chillicothe, and has served in Circleville Sunrise Rotary. He also volunteers with the Pickaway County Department of Health, administering hundreds of COVID-19 vaccines to Pickaway county residents during the pandemic.

Education and Professional Associations

A graduate of Circleville High School, Dr. Stewart began his college career at Miami University in Oxford. He then completed his undergraduate degree at Ohio State, graduating with magna cum laude honors with a Bachelor of Science degree in biology. Subsequently, Dr. Stewart was accepted into the Ohio State University College of Dentistry, where he received his doctorate in 2015. He completed his internship at the Chillicothe VA Dental Clinic in 2014 and 2015.

Dr. Stewart is an active member of the American Dental Association, Ohio Dental Association, and the Rehwinkel Dental Society.

Interests and Hobbies

Dr. Stewart enjoys working in his yard, going to the movies, and spending time with his family. Weekends may also find Dr. Stewart watching his favorite sports teams, including the Reds, Bengals, and Buckeyes.
