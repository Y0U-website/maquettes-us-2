Signs You Might Benefit from Dental Implants

Knowing when dental implants could help you is important for your oral health. Dubuque Dental Associates, PC can help you spot these signs:

If you notice any of these issues, you might want to think about dental implants. Our team at Dubuque Dental Associates, PC is here to look at your situation and give you professional advice.

  • Problems with chewing or speaking
  • Missing one or more teeth
  • Ill-fitting dentures
  • Sunken facial features
  • Loss of jawbone
  • Teeth moving out of place
