Snoring is not innocent noise

A common disorder in which you have one or more pauses in breathing or shallow breaths while you sleep.

The practice

We use a small device to conduct an in-home sleep test. We have a great local sleep clinic to refer patients to when and over night or CPAP study is needed.

Dr. Duke offers a variety of Sleep Apnea devices. All are designed to keep the lower jaw from falling backwards and blocking the airway.

The TAP and Herbst are small and comfortable sleep apnea therapy appliances. They are wonderful alternative to the CPAP machine for mild to moderate apnea, and they work well in conjunction with the CPAP machine for those with severe apnea. They work by holding the lower jaw forward to keep the airway open (see the diagram below)
