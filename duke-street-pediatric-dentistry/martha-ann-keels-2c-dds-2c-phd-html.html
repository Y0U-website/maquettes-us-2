Martha Ann Keels, DDS, PhD

Dr. Keels loves living in the triangle area, especially with the transformation of Durham into an awesome town for yummy food, Durham Bulls, DPAC, Museum of Life and Science, Durham Farmers Market and Duke Chamber Music. Duke Chapel holds a special place in her heart as that is where she and Dr. Clements were married. They were the 999th wedding in Duke’s Chapel’s history.

In her free time, she enjoys being with her family. Her husband, Dr. Dennis Clements, is a pediatrician and a professor in global health at Duke. Dr. Keels and Dr. Clements love helping provide medical and dental care for the children in Roatan, Honduras. They have also help the Roatan Marine Park preserve the coral reef and marine life off the coast of Roatan for future generations to enjoy.
