The practice

We believe a healthy smile and a good quality of life go hand-in-hand. We chose dentistry because we wanted to help others improve their oral health, and we continually strive to deliver the highest quality dental care.

Dr. Andy K. Dulaney is a native resident who has devoted his life and career to serving the Greater Jackson area. After graduating from Forest Hill High School, Dr. Andy studied chemistry at Mississippi College, where he earned his undergraduate degree in 1982. Afterward, he earned his D.M.D from the University of Mississippi School of Dentistry in 1986.

Dr. Andy operated his own dental practice in Jackson, MS, for over 20 years. In 2006, he moved his practice to the current location in Byram. His son, Dr. Drew Dulaney, joined the practice in 2014, and they formed the partnership of Dulaney Dental, PLLC.

In 2010, Dr. Drew earned his undergraduate degree in biochemistry from Freed Hardeman University. He entered the University of Mississippi School of Dentistry where he earned his D.M.D. in 2014. His teachers and mentors voted him “Most Likely to Succeed in General Dentistry”. After graduation, he joined his father in practicing dentistry and they formed the partnership of Dulaney Dental, PLLC.

Dr. Drew and his wife, Morgan, have two young children, Aniston (5) and Cal (3). Their family worships at Siwell Road Church of Christ. Dr. Drew’s faith in God shapes his life in every aspect, and he strives to carry out his faith with every patient interaction.
