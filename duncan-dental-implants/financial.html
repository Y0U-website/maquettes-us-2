Financial FAQs

  • How much will my dental visit cost? The cost of dental care depends on your treatment, insurance coverage and specific plan details. Our goal is to offer affordable options for every patient, ensuring you receive the care you need without financial stress.
  • Do you offer third-party payment options? Absolutely! We accept CareCredit ® and Sunbit , which provide flexible financing options. The application process is fast and easy, so you can get the treatment you need without delay.
  • Can I use third-party payment with my insurance? Yes! You can use third-party payments to cover your balance in full, partially or for any remaining costs not covered by insurance. It’s a flexible and convenient way to manage your dental expenses.
