Education & Expertise

Dr. Heather Hradek is proud to hold two of dentistry’s most prestigious honors. She is a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry (FAGD) —a distinction earned by fewer than 6 % of general dentists nationwide through 500+ hours of advanced continuing education and a rigorous exam. She is also a Fellow of the American College of Dentists (FACD) , an invitation-only recognition awarded to just 3.5 % of dentists for excellence, leadership, and the highest standards of ethics.

Together, these honors reflect Dr. Hradek’s unwavering commitment to continual learning, clinical excellence, and serving her patients and profession with integrity.

Focused on Family, Community & Care

Being a mom gives Dr. Heather a real understanding of how stressful dental visits can be for both kids and parents. She welcomes children as young as one and works closely with pediatricians in the Valparaiso area to support each family’s dental needs with care and patience.

She’s also proud to give back to her community through her partnership with Girls on the Run of Northwest Indiana , donating a portion of every new patient visit to help empower young girls through fitness, connection, and confidence.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Heather loves spending time with her family, staying active outdoors, and enjoying the beauty of Northwest Indiana. Her warmth and authenticity shine through in everything she does, and it’s what makes Dunes Dental Studio feel like more than just a dental office.
