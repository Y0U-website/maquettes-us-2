About Dr. Aaron Dalman

Meet Dr. Aaron Dalman

Your friendly, Family dentist in Muskegon, Michigan, here to make great dental care comfortable, personal, and stress-free.

A passion for healthy, confident smiles

Dr. Aaron Dalman is passionate about providing modern, high-quality dental care while building lasting relationships with the patients and families he serves.

As the owner of Duneside Dental, Dr. Dalman’s vision is to provide high-quality, technology-driven, honest, and ethical family dentistry in a welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable, informed, and valued. He believes every patient deserves personalized care and treatment recommendations based on what is truly best for their health and goals. By combining advanced technology with a patient-centered approach, he strives to help every individual achieve a healthy, confident smile.

Dr. Dalman studied Biopsychology, Cognition, and Neuroscience at the University of Michigan, where he developed a strong appreciation for the science behind health, behavior, and patient care. He went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry.

Dedicated to lifelong learning, Dr. Dalman regularly pursues advanced continuing education to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry. Since entering practice, Dr. Dalman has continued to expand his clinical skills through advanced training in dental implants, Invisalign , and modern restorative dentistry, allowing him to provide a wide range of treatment options under one roof. He continually invests in advanced education, technology, and techniques to provide patients with the highest standard of care.

What Dr. Dalman loves most about dentistry is the unique combination of science, artistry, and service. He enjoys getting to know his patients, working with his hands to restore smiles, and using both technical skill and creativity to improve confidence and quality of life.

Originally from Spring Lake, Michigan, Dr. Dalman is thrilled to be practicing back in West Michigan. After spending the past seven years commuting to the Grand Rapids area for work, returning to serve the lakeshore community has been especially meaningful. He is proud to care for generations of local families and contribute to the health and well-being of the community that helped shape him.

Outside of the office, Dr. Dalman enjoys spending time with his wife, Rebecca, their daughter, Isla, and their dog, Leonard. Whether he’s fishing on Lake Michigan, boating with family, golfing, hiking, hunting, or simply enjoying the outdoors, he appreciates everything that makes West Michigan a special place to live and raise a family.

Dr. Dalman looks forward to welcoming you to Duneside Dental and helping you achieve a healthy, confident smile for years to come.
