OUR Dental Technology

Navigator Soft Tissue Laser

The Navigator is used by doctors in clinical dentistry for soft tissue modification for preventative care, periodontal work, seating crowns, and implementing bridges. The laser allows for effective treatment in various procedures such as gingival troughing, hemostasis, and frenectomy. The Navigator soft tissue laser also allows Dr. John and Dr. Mara to quickly reduce the pain and increase the speed of healing for mouth ulcers. Traditionally, a patient could apply over the counter medicine to the ulcer to ease discomfort while it heals on its own.
