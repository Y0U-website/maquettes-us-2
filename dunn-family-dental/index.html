The practice

Dr. Dunn offers a variety of restorative dental treatments that can help you get your smile back and better than ever.

This involves tooth-colored fillings, porcelain veneers, professional teeth whitening, and much more.

If your gums feel tender, look red or swollen, or bleed everytime you floss - you could be at risk for gum disease.

We understand that many children feel scared or uneasy about dental visits, and we always treat our younger patients with extra sensitivity and care. Our friendly and gentle manner will help your child feel at ease with us, and having a positive dental experience during childhood can encourage a lifetime of good oral habits. We even have a treasure chest full of prizes for your child to choose…

We go above and beyond the American Dental Association (ADA) and OSHA recommended standards for cleanliness and safety. Before your visit, we thoroughly sanitize each room and disinfect all of our equipment in a designated sterilization area. We worry about the cleanliness of our office so you’ll never have to!

We empower you to make wise, well-informed decisions for your oral health through individual consultation and education. We also make sure to stay up-to-date on your medical history, current medications, and any other factors that might affect your dental health and treatment.
