The practice

We can treat your entire family from the youngest to oldest members and we have the necessary skills to provide a good dental experience for everyone. We want even our youngest patients to discover that a trip to the dentist can be a positive experience.

In dentistry, prevention is key. Maintaining healthy gums and teeth is a crucial piece of the puzzle for overall wellness. To that end, regular visits to the dentist for examinations and cleanings are essential.

Because of the importance of early detection, we offer oral cancer screenings as a part of each preventive care appointment.

We are also happy to answer any questions you might have about your oral health, whether it is about recommended treatment or making sure you are practicing good home care.

You can trust us to be honest about treatment options, knowing we will always recommend the most conservative route first. We will never suggest a treatment that is not in your best interest, nor will we proceed until you are completely comfortable.

At Dunwell Dentistry, we offer the latest in technology to assist in accurate diagnoses of dental issues and to provide our patients with better understanding of their condition. We want our dental care to exceed your expectations every time you walk through our doors. Each of our team members works hard to make sure you experience the best care possible.

All the technology we select and use in our practice is purposeful and chosen to provide you with the easiest and most comfortable care possible. For example, we utilize digital impressions, digital records, digital sensors, digital x-rays, intra-oral cameras, and rotary endodontic instruments.

Our team works hard to create a friendly and welcoming atmosphere regardless of when your last dental appointment may have been. If you are anxious about visiting the dentist, we will do what we can to make you comfortable. We would be honored to be the first choice for all of your dental needs. Give us a call today to make an appointment!
