Explore Different Types of Dental Implants Offered

  • We offer various types of affordable dental implants to meet individual needs, including: Endosseous Implants : The most common type, placed directly into the jawbone.
  • Subperiosteal Implants : Positioned under the gum but above the jawbone, suitable for patients with low bone height.
  • Transosteal Implants : Less common, these pass through the jawbone to anchor above the gums.
  • Mini Dental Implants : Smaller in diameter, they are an option for patients needing smaller fixtures.

Understand the Benefits of Choosing Dental Implants

Choosing affordable dental implants comes with significant advantages:

  • Bone Preservation : Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing tissue loss.
  • Improved Aesthetics : They look and feel like natural teeth, boosting your confidence.
  • Enhanced Oral Hygiene : Easier to maintain compared to traditional dentures.
  • Decreased Sensitivity : Adjacent teeth are protected from decay and damage.

Key Surgical Procedures for Dental Implant Placement

The process of getting dental implants at our Dunwoody practice typically involves:

  • One-stage Surgery : An efficient approach where the implant is placed, and the crown is attached in a single visit.
  • Two-stage Surgery : The implant is placed first, allowing time for osseointegration before attaching the crown.
  • Immediate Loading : Offers quick restoration by attaching a temporary crown shortly after implant placement.

Factors Affecting Successful Dental Implant Outcomes

Several factors can influence the success of dental implants. These include:

  • Primary Stability : The implant’s initial stability is vital for long-term success.
  • Patient Health : Overall health and specific medical conditions may impact healing.
  • Oral Hygiene : Maintaining a proper oral care routine is crucial for avoiding complications and enhancing implant longevity.
  • Experience of the Provider : A skilled dental team helps support successful implant placement and long-term outcomes.
