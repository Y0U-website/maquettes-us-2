Meet Dr. Ravi Patel, Trusted Dunwoody Dentist

Dr. Ravi Patel is originally from Georgia. He completed his undergraduate studies at Indiana University before earning his Masters at Purdue University. Afterwards, Dr. Patel received his doctorate from Nova Southeastern University’s College of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Patel takes time every year to attend continuing education courses all around the country. He offers a wide variety of general dentistry procedures to his patients including tooth-colored fillings, crowns, dental implants , Clear aligner therapy (clear braces), extractions, root canals, teeth whitening, and night guards. Dr. Patel is a self-admitted techie and has incorporated the best in cutting-edge technology into his practice. He finds that it not only yields better clinical results, but it also provides the patient with the benefit of increased comfort and safety.

Dr. Patel is committed to supporting the Inspire Smiles Project, which donates food, water accessibility, medicine, and malaria nets to people and families in need. Dr. Patel realized that the true joy in dentistry is being able to give back and help as many people as possible. Dr. Patel has pledged to give a portion of revenue from dental procedures to help those in need, at no additional cost to his patients.
