ClearCorrect

Your lifestyle is important to you and so is your smile. So why should one cost you the other? With ClearCorrect, it doesn’t have to. Dr. Hedlund will evaluate your teeth to determine if ClearCorrect is the right treatment option for you and start you on your way to the smile you’ve always wanted.

ClearCorrect is the clear and simple way to straighten your teeth. No wires. No brackets. No reason not to smile. just a series of clear, custom, removable aligners that gradually straighten your teeth as you wear them, each aligner moving your teeth just a little bit each time.

How Does It Work?

ClearCorrect manufactures your aligners according to the records and precise instructions we send them. You will then wear your aligners under our supervision until you get your ideal smile. At that point, we would get you into a set of retainer trays to ensure you keep that smile!

The Choice Is Clear.

Is It Right For You?

ClearCorrect has the advantages of braces without the limitations of other aligner systems. It’s clearer and easier to keep clean, with better control for your dentist and better results for you.
