Get To Know Dr. Luke Robert

Dr. Luke Robert is a board-certified pediatric dentist with a passion for creating positive dental experiences for children. Born into an Air Force family, Dr. Luke&#x27;s journey in dentistry began with an inspiring childhood experience at his own dentist&#x27;s office. This early fascination led him to pursue a career in pediatric dentistry.

Dr. Luke completed his undergraduate studies at the University of Georgia, graduating Magna Cum Laude with a BSA in Biological Science. He then attended the Dental College of Georgia at Augusta University, where he obtained his DMD. Dr. Luke remained in Augusta for 2 more years while completing his pediatric dental residency. Following residency, Dr. Luke completed the voluntary process to become board-certified, demonstrating his commitment to providing the highest standard of care for his young patients.

During dental school Dr. Luke met his wonderful wife, Dr. Mary Cameron Robert, who is a practicing general dentist in Roswell. From Augusta they moved to Richmond, VA, where Dr. Luke gained valuable clinical experience in a long-standing and well-loved pediatric dental practice. This experience solidified his desire to create a practice that prioritizes personalized care and building strong relationships with patients and their families.

When not caring for his patients, Dr. Luke enjoys playing music (guitar, banjo, and mandolin), reading, road cycling, gaming, and walking his dog, Guinness.

Dr. Luke is a Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist and a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry (ABPD), an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD), and the Southeastern Society of Pediatric Dentistry (SSPD), the Georgia Dental Association (GDA), and the Georgia Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (GAPD).

Your Child&#x27;s First Visit

A Positive Dental Experience

At Dunwoody Pediatric Dentistry, we&#x27;ve designed the first visit to be a positive, comfortable experience for both you and your child. For children under three, we offer a comprehensive exam that includes a thorough check-up, gentle cleaning, and parent education. We take the time to get to know your child, answer your questions, and provide guidance on at-home care. Our goal is to create a foundation of trust and comfort that will last throughout your child&#x27;s dental journey.

What to Bring

For your first visit, please bring your child&#x27;s medical history, insurance information, and any questions you may have. We also recommend bringing a favorite toy or comfort item to help your child feel at ease.
