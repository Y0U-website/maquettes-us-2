What Is Considered a Dental Emergency?

Dental emergencies come in many forms. Some are obvious, while others may start small and become serious if ignored. You should contact our emergency dentist in Warrenville if you experience:

Even if you’re unsure, it’s always better to call. Our team at DuPage Dental Smiles can help determine the urgency of your situation and schedule care accordingly.

  • Severe or persistent tooth pain
  • A broken, cracked, or chipped tooth
  • A knocked-out or loose tooth
  • Swelling in the gums, jaw, or face
  • Signs of infection, such as pus or fever
  • Lost fillings, crowns, or bridges causing pain
  • Dental trauma from accidents or sports injuries
