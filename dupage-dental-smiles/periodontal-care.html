What Is Periodontal Disease?

Periodontal disease, commonly called gum disease, is a bacterial infection that affects the gums and supporting structures around your teeth. It typically begins as gingivitis, causing redness, swelling, and bleeding during brushing or flossing. Without treatment, the infection can spread below the gumline, damaging the bone that holds your teeth in place.

Common signs of periodontal disease include:

Early diagnosis is one of the best ways to protect your smile. Routine dental visits allow us to detect problems before significant damage occurs.

  • Red, swollen, or tender gums
  • Bleeding while brushing or flossing
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Gum recession
  • Loose or shifting teeth
  • Pain while chewing
  • Pus around the gums

Comprehensive Periodontal Disease Treatment

Every patient has different needs, which is why we customize every treatment plan. Our goal is not only treating periodontal disease, but also helping prevent it from returning.

Depending on the severity of your condition, treatment may include:

Professional Deep Cleaning

For mild to moderate periodontal disease, scaling and root planing may be recommended. This deep cleaning procedure removes plaque, tartar, and bacteria from below the gumline while smoothing the tooth roots to encourage healthy gum reattachment.

Ongoing Periodontal Maintenance

After active treatment, regular periodontal maintenance visits help control bacteria and monitor your gum health. These cleanings are typically more frequent than standard dental cleanings and play an important role in preventing future flare-ups.

Advanced Laser Therapy

At DuPage Dental Smiles, we utilize modern technology whenever it can improve patient outcomes. Laser therapy for periodontal disease allows us to precisely target infected tissue while preserving healthy gums.

If you are looking for a less invasive approach to periodontal disease laser treatment, laser dentistry may be an excellent option.

Gum Recession Treatment

Gum recession is one of the most common effects of advanced periodontal disease. As gum tissue pulls away from the teeth, more of the tooth root becomes exposed. This can lead to sensitivity, increased risk of decay, and changes in the appearance of your smile.

Our gum recession treatment begins by identifying and addressing the underlying cause. In many cases, treating gum disease can stop recession from progressing.

Depending on your needs, treatment may include:

Because every case is unique, we create individualized treatment plans designed to preserve as much healthy gum tissue as possible.

  • Deep periodontal cleaning
  • Improved home care recommendations
  • Bite adjustments if excessive forces are contributing
  • Periodontal maintenance
  • Referral for gum recession surgery when significant tissue restoration is needed

Laser Treatment for Gum Recession

When appropriate, laser treatment for gum recession can be used to remove diseased tissue and encourage healthier healing. While lasers cannot regenerate lost gum tissue by themselves, they are valuable tools for eliminating infection and preparing the gums for healing or additional periodontal procedures.

Using advanced laser technology allows us to perform many treatments with improved precision and patient comfort.

Preventing Future Gum Disease

After your periodontal treatment is complete, maintaining healthy gums becomes a partnership between you and our dental team.

Regular dental visits allow us to monitor your progress and catch any changes before they become serious.

  • Brushing twice each day with a soft-bristled toothbrush
  • Flossing daily
  • Keeping up with periodontal maintenance appointments
  • Avoiding tobacco products
  • Managing health conditions such as diabetes
  • Eating a balanced diet that supports gum health
