The practice

Dr. Ahmed Shareef and our experienced dental team are committed to providing exceptional, personalized dental care in a comfortable, stress-free, and friendly atmosphere. As skilled dental professionals with a personal commitment to our community, our team works diligently to ensure that every patient receives the dental care they deserve.

We go above and beyond standards for cleanliness and safety, as recommended by the American Dental Association (ADA) and OSHA.

We pride ourselves in offering our patients an exceptional environment where they can feel comfortable knowing their oral health is in good hands. We develop an individualized plan for each patient. From regular cleanings and exams to advanced restorative treatments and cosmetic dentistry, our family dentistry practice is committed to you.

Visiting your dentist at least twice a year is critical to your dental and overall health. Good dental hygiene is incredibly important in preventing conditions such as tooth decay, gum disease, and other problems. We offer regular and periodontal cleanings, imaging, exams, and oral cancer screenings for both adults and children. Other preventative care services include athletic mouthguards…

Cosmetic dentistry can enhance your smile and improve function. If you are interested in our office helping transform your smile, feel free to ask us for more information regarding veneers, dental bonding, whitening services, clear aligners, and more.

Let our team help manage your dental anxiety through nitrous oxide sedation. Nitrous Oxide, also known as laughing gas, provides a general sense of well-being and ease for the duration of your procedure. The effects wear off within minutes of stopping nasal inhalation, so you can quickly return to your day after your procedure.

Dr. Shareef is an exceptional dentist who is highly regarded by patients and dental professionals alike for his compassionate approach to dentistry and his excellent clinical outcomes. Graduating with a D.M.D. from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine in Illinois, Dr. Shareef is passionate about helping people improve their oral health as well as their overall well-being.
