Our Family

we DO smiles

We recognize that all of our patients are unique and deserve to receive orthodontic care that reflects their individual needs. Our father-daughter doctor team epitomizes the local-family-feel and strives to make each patient feel like a part of our family. Our entire team is committed to working with you and your family to create a comfortable, stress-free, and rewarding orthodontic experience every time you visit our practice. We are pleased to welcome you and your family to the “DupTroop”.

MEET DR. JOHN J. DUPLESSIS JR.

Dr. John J. DuPlessis, Jr. has been practicing orthodontics since 1987. He was sure he wanted to be an orthodontist since seventh grade when he had braces himself. The thing he enjoys most about orthodontics is showing a patient their before and after photos, knowing he has made a positive change in their lives. Dr. D. enjoys working with children and adults, but has a special bond with younger patients. He is known as a magician and practical joker at times!

Dr. D attended the University of Kentucky for his undergraduate and dental school education where he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in 1985. He completed his Orthodontic Residency at Saint Louis University in 1987. He regularly attends continuing education courses, usually doubling the required number of hours.

Professional Affiliations: American Association of Orthodontists Kentucky Association of Orthodontists Orthodontic Education and Research Foundation, Board Member

Dr. D is very proud of the team at DuPlessis Orthodontics. They have over 120 hours of combined experience. Their experience speaks volumes and allows patients to be exceptionally cared for.

Dr. D has been married to his wife Julia since 1982. They have three girls; Elizabeth, who is now Dr. D’s partner in the practice, Sarah, a physician’s assistant in Elizabethtown, and Emily, an accountant in Chicago. When Dr. D is not working, you can find him walking and taking care of the trail around Freeman Lake, playing Words with Friends, and traveling. He is active at his parish, Saint James Catholic Church, and Rotary Club of Elizabethtown. He looks forward to helping in the revitalization of downtown Elizabethtown in the next few years.

ANDREA

Andrea began her career with DuPlessis Orthodontics in 1995 as an orthodontic assistant. She took a brief leave of absence to add to her family and to further her college education. After graduation, she returned to serve as our practice manager. Andrea works with our patients and their families to ensure a positive experience during treatment. She enjoys meeting new patients and welcoming them into our practice.

Andrea lives in Elizabethtown with her family of all boys and her Australian shepherd, Emmett. An avid reader, Andrea also enjoys gardening and spending time with her horses.

DENISE

Denise started in the orthodontic field in 1981 after graduating from an accredited dental assistant program in Louisville. She joined DuPlessis Orthodontics in February of 1995. She really enjoys making various appliances in the lab. Denise loves the positive effect orthodontic treatment has on our patients! Everyone loves a beautiful smile! She especially enjoys when the parent is so proud of the way their child’s treatment turned out that they decide to go into orthodontic treatment themselves!

Denise lives in Elizabethtown and loves spending most of her free time outdoors. She looks forward to going to the lake, skiing, camping, hiking, flying airplanes, traveling out west with her husband Tom on their motorcycles, and playing with their Standard Poodle, Romeo. CHRISTY
