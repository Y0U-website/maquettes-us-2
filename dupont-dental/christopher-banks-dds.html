Meet Washington, DC, Dentist Christopher Banks, DDS

Christopher Banks, D.D.S.

Washington, DC, dentist Dr. Banks was born in Virginia and raised in West Virginia. He attended West Virginia University, obtaining his Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS) and completing his undergraduate studies in three years.

As a student at the West Virginia University School of Dentistry, Dr. Banks was actively involved in many philanthropic organizations. Following dental school, Dr. Banks worked alongside his father, Dr. Kenneth Banks, at his practice, Smile Designs of the Shenandoah Valley.

As a member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, American Dental Association, International Academy of Facial Aesthetics, and the Maryland Dental Association, Dr. Banks is committed to expanding his knowledge of dental practice.

He keeps up with the new strides that technology and research to help patients at Dupont Dental improve their oral health. In 2013, Dr. Banks was selected as Chair of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry’s Board of Trustees. For more information about Dupont Dental, please visit About Our Office .
