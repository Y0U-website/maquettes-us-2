Our Story

Growing from a small lab to a one of the leading dental laboratories in Illinois, Durable Dental Lab specializes in All-on-X Implant Supported Hybrid Prosthesis among other advanced dental solutions. Our highly professional staff has decades of combined experience in the industry and is committed to earning customers for life. We partner with dental specialists and general dentists across the entire U.S., providing seamless preplanning, surgical, and prosthetic support to ensure superior patient outcomes and streamlined procedures.

Our CEO - Leo Varshavsky, CDT

Leo Varshavsky, our esteemed CEO and President, brings over three decades of specialized dental technology experience. A graduate from the College of Dental Technology in Odessa, Ukraine, Leo’s expertise spans across ceramic restorations to cutting-edge Implant Supported Hybrid Prosthesis. Outside the lab, Leo is a family man and sports enthusiast, drawing on his passion for craftsmanship to create smiles that transform lives.

Certified Dental Lab

As a testament to our commitment to excellence, Durable Dental Lab proudly holds certifications from the National Association of Dental Laboratories, along with other certifications. This recognition underscores our adherence to rigorous quality standards, ensuring we deliver nothing but the best.
