Why should I choose a board-certified pediatric dentist?

At Durango Kids Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Angela Pinkerton and Dr. Edie Weststeyn are the only two board-certified pediatric dentists in Durango! This certification means they have undergone additional training and rigorous exams to provide the highest level of care for kids. From complex dental issues to providing a gentle and positive experience, you can trust that your child is in amazing hands.

More Events and Community Outreach

Through our fun and educational events, we’re dedicated to giving back and promoting healthy habits throughout Durango and the Four Corners.

We celebrate our young patients who maintain cavity-free checkups through great brushing and flossing habits. Being part of this special club is a badge of honor that shows your child&#x27;s dedication to their dental health!

Every February, our team visits local preschools to teach kids the importance of oral health! Our team (including a friendly tooth fairy!) brings dental education to life with interactive lessons and take-home dental care kits to kick-start great habits.

We&#x27;re proud to partner with LPFCC during annual health fairs, offering free dental screenings for Spanish-speaking families in our community. Each family leaves with healthier smiles and oral hygiene kits to maintain their dental health at home.

As a clinical director, Dr. Edie leads our team in providing dental screenings, custom mouthguards, and oral health education to our amazing athletes.

As board members of the Southwest Colorado Oral Health Coalition, we focus on fostering collaboration among providers and developing new strategies to improve oral health for underserved populations.

Our practice proudly sponsors local Girls on The Run teams, supporting youth fitness and empowerment while promoting healthy smiles in our community.
