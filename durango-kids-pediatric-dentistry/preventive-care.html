What is preventive care?

Benefits of preventive care

Catch issues early

With regular check-ups, Dr. Edie and Dr. Pinkerton can spot potential problems early. This often prevents the need for more extensive treatments down the road.

Learn great habits Our kid-friendly approach helps children develop good oral hygiene habits and a positive attitude towards dental care.

Healthy smiles for life Regular preventive care helps build a strong foundation for lifelong oral health. This ensures your child&#x27;s smile stays bright and healthy as they grow.

Our preventive treatments Dental Cleanings Our gentle, thorough cleanings remove plaque and tartar, leaving your child&#x27;s teeth sparkling clean. We make it fun and educational when teaching proper brushing techniques along the way!

Silver Diamine Fluoride

Dental Sealants

Think of sealants as protective coats for your child&#x27;s teeth. We apply these thin sealants to shield the chewing surfaces of back teeth from cavity-causing bacteria.

Pediatric Dental Fluoride

Our kid-friendly fluoride treatments help strengthen tooth enamel, making it more resistant to decay.

Pediatric X-rays

These diagnostic images can help Dr. Edie and Dr. Pinkerton detect early signs of decay so they may determine a prevention plan and stop cavities in their tracks. They also emit very low radiation.

Povidone Iodine Treatment

This special antimicrobial treatment helps reduce harmful bacteria in the mouth, providing an extra layer of protection against cavities.

Hydroxyapatite Products

As an alternative to fluoride, we use these innovative products to help remineralize and strengthen tooth enamel naturally. This gives your child&#x27;s teeth an extra boost of protection.

Space Maintenance

We offer space maintenance to help ensure your child’s permanent teeth have room to grow properly after the premature loss of a baby tooth. This simple treatment prevents future alignment issues and keeps their smile on track.

Nutritional Counseling

Dr. Edie and Dr. Pinkerton offer friendly advice on tooth-healthy foods and drinks. This can help your family make choices that support strong, healthy smiles.

Customized Oral Hygiene Care

We work with you and your child to develop personalized oral care routines that make brushing and flossing fun and effective.

Want to learn more?

During this first visit, we&#x27;ll gently examine your child&#x27;s mouth, discuss diet and oral hygiene practices, and give you tips on preventing common issues like tooth decay. It establishes your child in a dental home, so if there is ever an emergency at home or away, we can help with urgent care and advice. It&#x27;s also a great opportunity for your little one to get comfortable with the dental office environment.

Many insurance plans cover preventive care. This typically includes regular exams, cleanings, fluoride treatments, and sometimes even sealants. However, coverage can vary depending on your specific plan. We encourage you to use your insurance company&#x27;s patient portal and provider search tool to verify that we are in-network and to understand your covered services. While we&#x27;re happy to provide our best estimate of your coverage upon request, checking directly with your insurance company will give you the most accurate information.

We typically recommend check-ups and cleanings every six months. These regular visits allow us to monitor your child&#x27;s oral health, catch any potential issues early, and provide preventive treatments like fluoride applications. However, Dr. Edie and Dr. Pinkerton may suggest more frequent visits based on your child&#x27;s individual needs. For example, if your child is at higher risk for cavities, has orthodontic appliances, or has certain medical conditions, we might recommend visits every 3-4 months.

During each visit, we&#x27;ll assess your child&#x27;s oral health, clean their teeth, and provide personalized advice on home care. Remember, these regular visits aren&#x27;t just about cleaning — they&#x27;re an opportunity for us to partner with you in maintaining your child&#x27;s beautiful smile!

Fluoride is a safe and proven way to prevent cavities, which is why it&#x27;s recommended by the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. We understand you may have questions about fluoride treatment, and we encourage you to discuss any concerns with Dr. Edie or Dr. Pinkerton during your visit. Our goal is to have an open conversation about fluoride&#x27;s benefits, safety, and alternatives so we can find the right approach for your family.
