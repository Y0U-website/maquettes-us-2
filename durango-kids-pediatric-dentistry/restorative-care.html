What is restorative care?

Has your child complained of tooth pain? Have you noticed suspicious areas in their mouth that you want more information about? We are here to help! Restorative care repairs damaged teeth and restores comfort and function to your childs teeth. At Durango Kids Pediatric Dentistry, we use modern techniques with a little TLC to restore your childs oral health so they can go back to being a kid again without dental pain or problems.

Benefits of restorative care

Pain-free smiles

No child deserves to struggle with tooth pain. Our treatments can help them smile, laugh, and enjoy their favorite foods without discomfort.

Prevents future problems By addressing issues now, we can often stop them from becoming bigger headaches down the road.

Boosts confidence A healthy mouth can make a world of difference in how your child feels about themselves. Let&#x27;s bring back that big, bright smile!

Our restorative treatments Stainless Steel Crowns Stainless steel crowns, made from surgical-grade material, are the most successful treatment option for restoring baby molars. These crowns completely seal the tooth, restore natural function, and are highly biocompatible with the body.

White Zirconia Crowns

Zirconia crowns are perfect for repairing visible front teeth that are severely decayed, discolored, or misshapen. They&#x27;re durable enough to withstand chewing forces while looking just like natural teeth.

Resin and Glass Ionomer Crowns

For small cavities in permanent teeth and some baby molars, we offer resin and glass ionomer fillings as effective treatment options. Dr. Edie or Dr. Pinkerton will help you choose the best type of filling for your child&#x27;s specific needs.

Frenectomies

This quick procedure addresses issues with the small fold of tissue under the tongue or upper lip. It can help with everything from easier feeding for babies to clearer speech for older kids.

Pulpotomies

When a tooth has a deep cavity, this treatment can be a real lifesaver. It&#x27;s like giving the tooth a mini root canal, helping to keep it healthy and in place.

Extractions

Sometimes, removing a tooth is the best way to keep your child&#x27;s smile healthy. This might be necessary for badly damaged teeth, to make room for adult teeth, or to prepare for orthodontic treatment.

Special Needs Dentistry

Every child deserves great dental care. We have special techniques and a gentle, compassionate team to ensure all kids get the care they need in a way that works for them.

Emergency Care

Accidents happen, and sometimes they happen to teeth. Whether it&#x27;s a weekend or a holiday, we&#x27;re here when you need us most.

Hospital Dentistry

For more complex procedures or kids who need extra care, we can take our board-certified expertise to the hospital. Our dentists are dedicated to providing the safest, most comfortable experience possible.

In-Office General Anesthesia

For our more anxious young patients or those requiring extensive procedures, we partner with Dr. Taylor Clark, our dental anesthesiologist, to provide safe and comfortable general anesthesia right in our familiar office setting.

Nitrous Sedation

Also known as "laughing gas," this helps nervous kiddos relax during their visit. It&#x27;s a safe way to make dental work a breeze.

Sometimes, the need for restorative care isn&#x27;t obvious. That&#x27;s why regular check-ups are so important. During these visits, Dr. Edie and Dr. Pinkerton can spot early signs of decay or damage that might not be causing noticeable symptoms yet. If you&#x27;re ever unsure, it&#x27;s always better to give us a call. We can often provide guidance over the phone or schedule a quick examination to put your mind at ease.

It really depends on what we&#x27;re tackling. Some quick fixes might take less time than a typical playdate, while others might need a couple of visits. Our team will walk you through the whole process so you know exactly what to expect.
