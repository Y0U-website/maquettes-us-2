What is special needs care?

As a parent of a child with special needs, you know that everyday activities can sometimes present unique challenges, and dental visits are no exception. Special needs care at our practice means we adapt everything — from our dental techniques to the office environment — to suit your child&#x27;s individual needs. Whether your kiddo has sensory sensitivities, physical limitations, or developmental differences, we&#x27;re here to ensure they receive great dental care in a way that works for them.

Benefits of special needs care

Personalized approach

No two kids are alike! We get to know your child&#x27;s needs, personality, and preferences to make their visit as easy and productive as possible.

Improves oral health Regular dental care tailored to your child&#x27;s needs prevents oral issues from creeping in and helps them stay healthy.

Builds confidence Establishing a routine and habit of visiting a familiar office can help reduce anxiety around each dental visit, which in turn boosts confidence.

Here&#x27;s what to expect at the appointment Step 1 Getting to know your child We know that every child is unique, especially yours. That&#x27;s why Dr. Edie and Dr. Pinkerton start by taking the time to learn about your child&#x27;s specific needs, preferences, and concerns. We&#x27;ll use kid-friendly tools like educational models and puppets to explain procedures in a way that makes sense to your child.

Step 2

Creating a comfortable space

Your child&#x27;s comfort is our priority. We&#x27;ll work with you to adjust our office environment to suit your child&#x27;s sensory needs. Need dimmer lights? A quieter space? Or maybe a favorite comfort item from home? Just let us know, and we&#x27;ll do our best to accommodate.

Step 3

Flexible, patient-centered treatment

We know that traditional dental visits might not work for every child. That&#x27;s why we&#x27;re committed to meeting your child where they&#x27;re at. This might mean taking breaks during treatment, using step-by-step explanations, or even scheduling shorter, more frequent visits. We&#x27;ll find an approach that works best for your child and adjust as needed.
