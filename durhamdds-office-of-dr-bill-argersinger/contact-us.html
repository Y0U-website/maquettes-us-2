Frequently Asked Questions About Our Services

You can call us at (919) 286-0779 or fill out the contact form on our website. We try to return new-patient inquiries within one business day, and we keep room in our schedule for same-day emergencies.

Every patient’s first visit is a little different. Some patients have been receiving regular dental care elsewhere and are simply transferring to our office. In those cases, the first visit typically includes a brief exam, a cleaning, and possibly updated X-rays.

For patients who haven’t been seen regularly or are experiencing a specific dental issue — such as a toothache, cavity, or broken tooth — the appointment focuses on diagnosing the problem and developing a plan to address it. Whenever possible, we’ll also provide treatment during that same visit to help resolve the issue promptly.

Every patient is different, and so are their dental health needs. Some benefit from a checkup and cleaning once a year, while others may need visits every 3–6 months. For most patients, scheduling a routine exam and cleaning every six months provides the best long-term benefit.
