Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are widely considered the gold standard for tooth replacement because they offer numerous benefits:

With proper care, dental implants can last decades or even a lifetime .

  • Natural look and feel
  • Permanent, long-lasting solution
  • Restore chewing ability
  • Prevent bone loss in the jaw​
  • Protect surrounding teeth​
  • No slipping or movement like dentures​

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Many adults with missing teeth are good candidates for implants. You may qualify if you:

The best way to determine if implants are right for you is with a personalized consultation at DurhamDDS .

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Have healthy gums
  • Have adequate jawbone density
  • Do not smoke or are willing to quit during healing
  • Want a long-term solution for missing teeth

Types of Dental Implant Solutions

Single Tooth Implant Replaces one missing tooth with a natural-looking crown.

Implant-Supported Bridge Replaces multiple missing teeth without relying on adjacent teeth.

Implant-Supported Dentures A stable alternative to traditional dentures that snap securely into place.

Our team will recommend the best option based on your oral health and smile goals.

The Dental Implant Process

1. Consultation and Evaluation

We examine your teeth, gums, and jawbone using advanced imaging to plan implant placement.

2. Implant Placement

The titanium implant is placed into the jawbone during a precise surgical procedure.

3. Healing and Integration

Over the next few months, the implant fuses with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration.

4. Final Restoration

A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to complete your smile.
