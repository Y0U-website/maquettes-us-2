Your Durham Dentist Dr. Bill Argersinger

The short story

A long winding road Dr. Bill grew up in Ann Arbor, Michigan. He attended the University of Michigan College of Engineering where he was also a three-year starter and captain for the Men’s Lacrosse Team. Prior to becoming a dentist, Dr. Bill was a global business professional. As he was considering changing careers, his company asked him to move to Singapore. But instead, Dr. Bill decided to pursue dentistry, and the rest is history.

Dr. Bill graduated

Dr. Bill graduated with honors from the University of North Carolina School of Dentistry. His personality and temperament combine a people-person style, the precision and attention to detail of an engineer, and the eyes and hands of an artist. Dr. Bill is a straight shooter who will communicate clearly and directly with you about your options, their pros and cons, and give you the information you need to make the right decision about your oral health.

Practice History at DurhamDDS

Our heritage of dental service in Durham, NC – since 1954.

Our dental practice has held a special place in Durham since 1954 when it was founded by Dr. Leroy Heath, one of UNC Dental School’s inaugural graduating class. In 1982, Dr. Don Wheless took over the practice. Dr. Wheless was valedictorian at Emory Dental School and a UNC Dental School Professor of Prosthodontics. When he retired in 2012, he chose Dr. Bill Argersinger to succeed him and continue the practice. In 2013, another veteran Durham dentist Dr. Jim Thompson retired and entrusted his patients to Dr. Bill. Dr. Jim and Betty are still regulars around the practice.
