Our Services

General Dentistry

Our goal is to help you live a happier, more fulfilled life by maintaining the health of your mouth and smile. We will provide minor treatments that are preventative rather than intensive so we can ensure optimum oral hygiene for all parts related to body wellness

We want everyone’s teeth working together as one strong entity in order produce an amazing person who has never had any problems with their dental care!

Restorative Dentistry

Dutchtown Dental Center understands that each patient is unique and has their own set of needs. Whether you’ve had repairs or need a complete replacement, we recommend continual check-ups to assess your gums and bone density so our team can help keep things in check around the implant area as well!

Cosmetic Dentistry

At our practice, we know how important a beautiful smile is to everyone. Whether wanting straight teeth or better looking chompers like before adulthood got its hooks on them (and let’s be honest--it happens!), it becomes one of the most sought after cosmetic features around! So if you need some minor adjustments made or major treatment plans implemented; rest assured knowing that "your" bright white pearly whites will soon appear again.

Sedation Dentistry

Many people are anxious about visiting the dentist, but at Dutchtown Dental Center we offer sedation dentistry to help you relax and let us work while still being conscious during your procedure. It’s a great alternative for those who have trouble with fear or anxiety around undergoing routine checkups like teeth cleanings!

Pediatric Dentistry

We know that children’s teeth and gums continue to develop through their early years. That’s why we’re here for you, providing a wide range of dental care services designed specifically with your child in mind!

We’ve got everything from checkups (and cleanings) before it starts shaping up as well as when signs indicate something may need attention - all under the supervision/oversight by one our highly trained staff members who have extensive experience working alongside kids just like yours.

Orthodontic Dentistry

Dutchtown Dental Center’s team of dentists is excited to craft your perfect smile. We want you not only satisfied but proud, too! After an examination and X-rays at our clinic in Geismer, La , we will be happy discuss all orthodontic treatment options with you. Before you know it your mouth will be on the right track for beautiful teeth like yours. No matter what type or style works best for YOU--we have got IT covered!!
