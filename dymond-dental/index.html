Dr. Elizabeth Dymond Dr. Dymond is a family dentist in Wyomissing, PA who prioritizes long-term outcomes by building personal relationships and delivering collaborative, patient-focused care.

Liz started as a dental assistant and quickly found a passion for dentistry’s blend of art and science. She completed her Doctorate in Dental Medicine at Temple University’s Kornberg School of Dentistry, graduating Summa Cum Laude in 2016, and has been providing comprehensive dental care to patients ever since.

Away from the office, she enjoys baking, ceramics, gardening, and time with her two children, husband, and three dogs.

Dr. Dymond is a Pennsylvania Board Certified Dentist and active member of the American Dental Association and Pennsylvania Dental Association committed to providing high-quality, preventative dental care.
