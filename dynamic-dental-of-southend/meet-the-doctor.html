About Dr. Mann

Dr. Amy Mann was born and raised here in Charlotte NC. She earned her bachelors degree in Nutrition Science at Indiana University. She then went on to earn a MA in Medical science at Boston University and her Doctorate of Dental Medicine, graduating cum laude in 2012.

She completed her Residency at Lutheran Medical Center. Dr. Mann worked at Boston, MA as a General Dentist for 4 years, before moving back to the Charlotte area. Dr. Mann is passionate about providing great care to her patients. She takes pride in her abilities to soothe and put at ease even the most anxious of patients

Dr. Mann has taken numerous continuing education courses to stay up-to-date with the latest technology and advancements in dentistry. Dr. Mann is a member of the American Dental association and the North Carolina Dental association.

In her spare time Dr. Mann, her husband and their two little boys love the outdoors (especially the beach), traveling, and trying new restaurants.

About Dr. Gbarbea

Hi, I’m Dr. Eshere Gbarbea. I received my Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine at Boston University in 2019, after completing my Master of Science in Oral Health Science at the Boston University School of Medicine in 2015.

I believe dentistry is about more than healthy teeth—it’s about caring for people. My focus is on patient-centered care, where I take the time to listen, build trust, and create lasting relationships with each person I treat. My goal is to make every visit a positive experience, helping patients feel comfortable and confident while supporting their long-term oral health.

Outside of the clinic, I enjoy connecting with my community and staying up to date on the latest advances in dentistry so I can always provide the best care possible.
