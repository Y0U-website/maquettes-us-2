Preventative Dentistry

If you’re looking for a way to avoid cavities, gum disease, and enamel wear, then preventative dentistry is the key. Prevention is always better than cure, and that’s especially true when it comes to your oral health. With just a few simple steps like brushing and flossing twice a day and coming in for regular checkups, cleanings, and exams, you can keep your smile healthy and shining for years to come.

Dental Cleanings & Exams

Your smile is one of the first things people notice about you, so it’s important to keep your teeth and gums healthy. A big part of maintaining good oral health is having routine dental cleanings and exams.

During a dental cleaning, our trained team will remove any plaque and tartar from your teeth. We will also polish your teeth and, if needed, provide a fluoride treatment to help keep them strong.

A dental exam is a chance for us to check for any problems with your teeth or gums. We will also take X-rays to check for any issues below the surface. If we find any problems, we will develop a treatment plan to get your mouth back on track.
