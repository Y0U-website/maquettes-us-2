Meet Our Team

Dr. Williams is a dedicated and friendly general dentist committed to providing high-quality, personalized dental care to patients of all ages. He completed his undergraduate studies at Michigan State University before earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Midwestern University in Arizona.

With a strong foundation in both the science and art of dentistry, Dr. Williams focuses on creating comfortable, positive dental experiences while helping patients maintain long-term oral health. He believes in building lasting relationships with patients based on trust, communication, and patient education. By staying current with the latest techniques and technology, he provides care that is both effective and tailored to each patient’s unique needs helping them achieve beautiful, lasting results.

Outside of the office, Dr. Williams enjoys spending quality time with his fiancé and their animals. He loves being outdoors and especially enjoys relaxing at the beach. New to the area, he’s excited to explore everything North Carolina has to offer from its beautiful coastline and hiking trails to local restaurants and cultural events. He also enjoys traveling, staying active, and catching up on his favorite sports teams.

Dr. Williams is excited to be part of Dynamic Dental and looks forward to building strong relationships with his patients and the local community.
