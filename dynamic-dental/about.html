A modern practice, built around how you actually feel about the dentist.

Dynamic Dental (formerly Palmquist Dental) is a family-run practice in Watertown, South Dakota — offering the full spectrum of general dentistry plus advanced sedation and oral surgery in one bright, calm clinic.

Why we exist

Too many people avoid the dentist because of fear, cost, or bad past experiences. We set out to build a practice where none of those need to be the reason.

We combined the routine care of a great family dentist with the advanced skills — implants, oral surgery, IV sedation — that usually require a trip to a bigger city. Then we invested in the technology and the team to do it all under one roof.

The result is a practice that can handle a checkup, a full-mouth reconstruction, or an emergency — with the same level of care.

Comfort first From the waiting room to our sedation options, every choice lowers the stress of a dental visit.

Safety, always We separate anesthesia and dentistry with a dedicated in-house CRNA — no shortcuts, ever.
