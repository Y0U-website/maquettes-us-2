Dr. Rachel Wiarda, DDS

Dr. Rachel Wiarda grew up in Winona, a small town on the Mississippi River in southeastern Minnesota. From a young age she was captivated by the art and science of dentistry, which led her to pursue a dental career.

She began her undergraduate courses at California Lutheran University and completed her degrees at Winona State University, graduating with honors — a Bachelor of Science in Cellular and Molecular Biology with a minor in Biochemistry, and a Bachelor of Arts in Spanish. She then attended Creighton University School of Dentistry, graduating with her Doctor of Dental Surgery.

Her career began in Centuria, Wisconsin as a general dentist at a small private practice. She moved to Castlewood, South Dakota in 2023 and joined Dr. Palmquist at Dynamic Dental. She takes pride in building trusting relationships with patients and offering personalized, compassionate care.

Outside the office Dr. Rachel enjoys traveling — she spent six months living in Spain and has traveled much of Europe, along with parts of Africa and Central America. She also loves being active and spending time with friends and family.

Dr. Allison VanLaecken-Heggelund, DDS

Dr. Allison VanLaecken-Heggelund grew up in Watertown, SD surrounded by several dental professionals who sparked her interest in the dental field.

She attended the University of South Dakota and graduated summa cum laude with a Bachelor of Science degree and minors in Interdisciplinary Sciences and Chemistry. She then attended Creighton University and graduated as a Doctor of Dental Surgery in 2023.

She looks forward to giving back to the community that raised her. What she enjoys most about dentistry is that every day is an opportunity to serve patients — helping them out of pain or restoring their mouth to great oral health.

When she&#x27;s not working, Dr. VanLaecken-Heggelund enjoys spending time with her husband Marcus, daughter Rosie, and son RJ. She enjoys singing at church, playing golf, and spending time with friends and family.
