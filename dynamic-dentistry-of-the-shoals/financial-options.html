Financial Options

We believe everyone should have access to quality dental care. That’s why here at Dynamic Dentistry of The Shoals we offer convenient, affordable payment options to meet your unique financial needs.

Dynamic Savings Plan

Single

  • NO Annual Maximums
  • NO Waiting Periods
  • NO Deductible

Spouse

  • NO Annual Maximums
  • NO Waiting Periods
  • NO Deductible

Dependent

  • NO Annual Maximums
  • NO Waiting Periods
  • NO Deductible

What’s Included?

  • Comprehensive, Periodic & Emergency Exam ( 2 per year )
  • Cleanings (2 annually), Prophy, or Periodontal Maintenance
  • All needed X-rays including Panoramic, Bitewings, & Periapicals (PA)

What’s Not Included?

  • Oral Appliance Therapies
  • Dentures & Partials
  • Clear Aligners
  • Sedation Fees

Additional Savings

You will also receive a 20 % discount on the following services:

  • Veneers
  • Whitening Procedure & Products
  • Root Canals
  • 3D Scans
  • Abutments
  • Crowns & Build-Ups
  • Posts
  • Periodontal Treatments
  • Nightguards (In-Office)
  • Implants
  • Fillings
