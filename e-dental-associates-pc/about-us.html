0 About Dr. Jason Yang Dr. Jason Jung Hoon Yang holds a Doctorate in Dental Surgery from the New York University College of Dentistry.

Dr. Yang completed a General Residency Training in Dental Medicine at Wyckoff Heights Medical Center in New York (An Academic Affiliate of Weill Medical College of Cornell University).

Dr. Yang has worked for a private dental practice in the New York City for over 12 years, and he has taken numerous continuing education courses in cosmetic and restorative dentistry, including the GIIA Implant course. Also, he is an active member of AAFE (American Academy of Facial Esthetics).

He lives in Bergen county with his wife and two daughters.
