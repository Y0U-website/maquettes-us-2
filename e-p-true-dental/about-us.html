Dr. Lisa Holst

Dr. Lisa Holst always wanted to do something in healthcare because she enjoys working with her hands. She got into dental school after three years of undergrad studies, a year ahead of most students. Both she and Dr. Pete Olberding were class presidents of their dental class, and classmates who began dating at dental school.

After graduating in 1989, Dr. Holst completed a general practice residency in 1990. Dr. Holst practiced dentistry in her hometown of Knoxville for 30 years. She joined her husband at E.P. True Dental in August 2024. Their daughter, Erica, has also joined the team.

Retirement is definitely not on the horizon for Dr. Holst. After 35 years in dentistry, she is still very passionate about dental care and she thoroughly enjoys her profession. In fact, she has three part-time jobs: working for a dental education testing agency, the practice, and insurance consulting. Dr. Holst also loves continuing education and is always updating her skills. Most recently, she proudly received the 2025 Iowa Dental Association President’s award.

Dr. Holst and Dr. Olberding were married in 1996 and are parents of twin daughters, Allison, an accountant, and Erica, a dentist who joined the practice in 2025. When she’s not busy in the dental office, Dr. Holst enjoys golf, traveling, watching the Hawkeyes, and going to football games. Dr. Holst lives close to the office, and she enjoys walking Bogey back and forth to work.

Dr. Erica Olberding

Dr. Erica Olberding grew up in the West Des Moines community and attended the University of North Carolina Asheville as well as Drake University. After graduating with her degree in biology, Dr. Erica followed in her parents’ footsteps (Dr. Pete and Dr. Lisa) and went on to the University of Iowa’s College of Dentistry. She graduated with her Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 2024, but she wanted to expand her dental knowledge further. So she attended a one-year residency program in general dentistry at the Medical University of South Carolina.

Her time in the South reiterated what she already knew: she loves her Iowa community and hometown. She’s glad to be back home and ready to help the community one smile at a time. She’s known around the office for putting people at ease and making them laugh. When she’s not in the office, she’s either taking Bogey for a walk, playing golf, watching reality TV, attending trivia night with her friends, or watching sports. She will always be an Iowa Hawkeyes fan.

“I have grown up around dentistry my whole life with two great role models — Dr. Pete and Dr. Lisa. That experience showed me dentistry is the perfect blend of getting to interact with patients and seeing the immediate impact of improving someone’s quality of life by relieving pain, restoring function, and enhancing smiles. That is exactly what I want to do.” —Dr. Erica Olberding

Bogey the Dental Dog

Bogey has been with his dental family since 2019. He loves greeting patients (and occasionally getting treats from patients, too). He is off on Mondays, but he spends the rest of the week helping patients to feel calm and comfortable. If you don’t see Bogey, be sure to ask for him and he’ll be sure to come around to say hello during your visit.

Bogey is especially proud of his education and training in turning around, giving a double-paw shake and a high five. When he’s not at the office, he is enthusiastic about his hobbies, such as digging in the snow, taking his humans for walks on the Jordan Creek Nature Trail, and catching and hoarding all the tennis balls.
