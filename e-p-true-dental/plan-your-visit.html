Plan Your Visit

  • Full-Mouth Digital X-Rays. These radiographic images use reduced radiation and provide clear, instant images. They offer a comprehensive view of your teeth and jaw so we can assess your current oral health. These X-rays will also establish a baseline for your future care.
  • Panoramic Imaging. This involves a wide-field X-ray that gives a high-quality view of your entire mouth. This technology requires only a small stick-like sensor to be held between your teeth, making the process both quick and comfortable.
  • 3D Wellness Scans. Using PrimeScan 2 and iTero scanners, we capture advanced 3D images of your teeth and gums. These scans help track changes over time, such as wear, movement, and any problems with your emerging teeth. Along with all the other scans, 3D wellness scanning helps our team to proactively monitor your oral health.
  • Periodontal Readings. At your first appointment, we will complete a detailed assessment of your gum and bone health to identify any signs of periodontal disease.
  • Intraoral Camera Imaging. Our team uses an intraoral camera to show you exactly what we see during your evaluation. This is useful so that patients can better understand their current oral health.

How long is your first appointment?

Plan to spend about 60 to 90 minutes with us during your first visit. This ensures we have plenty of time to complete your evaluation, answer your questions, and create a plan tailored to your needs.

A Whole-Body Approach to Your Care

With Communication and Personalized Planning

At E.P. True Dental, we care about more than just your teeth. As part of our approach, we take routine blood pressure readings during your visit to help us provide you with optimal care. We will also thoroughly review the information you give us about your health history to evaluate how your systemic health, medications, and health habits can affect your oral health.

After your evaluation, we’ll take some time to discuss:

We believe that informed patients are empowered patients. So, our team is here to provide you with all the information you need to make informed decisions about your treatment.

  • Our findings from your scans and evaluations
  • Your overall health and oral health goals
  • Personalized recommendations to help you achieve those goals
  • A comprehensive list of recommended treatments, if needed.

Honest Pricing

and the E.P. True Smile Plan

We understand that affordability can be a barrier to patients accessing the dental care they need. Our practice is upfront and honest about pricing, with all treatment investments discussed during a financial consultation at your first visit. Our team will also provide you with information about the payment options available to you.

For patients without insurance, we’re proud to offer the E.P. True Smile Plan. This is a membership-based savings plan program that makes dental care more affordable for those without dental insurance. With access to preventive treatments and savings on additional treatments, this plan is designed to ensure you can achieve and maintain a healthy smile.
