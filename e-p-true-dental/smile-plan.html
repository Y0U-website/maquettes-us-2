Smile Plan

By simplifying dental care, the E.P. True Smile Plan empowers you to focus on your health and well-being rather than worrying about unpredictable dental care costs.

  • No waiting periods. You can access your benefits immediately after signing up.
  • No complicated paperwork. Say goodbye to claim forms, pre-authorizations, and insurance restrictions.
  • Reduced investment amount. Save 20 % on preventive, restorative, cosmetic, and additional dental procedures performed in our office.
  • Comprehensive preventive care. So you can stay on top of your oral health with routine cleanings, evaluations, and screenings with a reduced investment.

How to Sign Up and What to Expect

Getting started with an E.P. True Smile Plan is easy!

If you have any questions, please don’t hesitate to contact our friendly team, who will help you navigate the sign-up process.

Please note: The E.P. True Smile Plan is a membership-based savings program, not a dental insurance plan. It cannot be combined with insurance benefits but is designed to provide an affordable alternative for patients seeking quality dental care without the restrictions of traditional insurance.

  • No need to choose a plan: Whether you’re an established patient or a new patient, $99 unlocks your reduced investment on all dental services for a year.
  • Sign up: This can be done in our office or over the phone.
  • Start using your benefits immediately: Once you’re a member, you’ll have complete access to the plan’s benefits!
