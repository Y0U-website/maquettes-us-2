Dental Implants 101

The Implant

Titanium hardware that is surgically implanted in the jaw, where it becomes permanently fused.

The Abutment

A connector attached to the top of the implant

The Restoration

A crown, bridge, or denture attached to the abutment, resulting in a complete replacement from the root to the chewing surface.

High-Tech Treatment

For High-End Results

Dr. Holwager uses advanced technology to ensure dental implants and other treatments are performed with exceptional safety and precision. These are just two of the sophisticated tools we will use to make your dental implants treatment a success:

3D X-Rays

Cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) is a tool that gives Dr. Holwager a three-dimensional image of your jaw, allowing her to assess the strength of your jawbone and identify the best position for your dental implants. This information will reveal if you need any preliminary bone grafting, and play an integral role in planning an implant procedure that can provide decades of benefits.

Digital Impressions

Did you ever get a crown or other restoration that required taking an impression using putty? We avoid the mess of this outdated method by using digital scanners that take incredibly precise digital images of your smile. This doesn’t just create a more pleasant treatment experience—it also ensures that your replacement teeth will fit comfortably in your smile and contribute to a properly balanced bite.

Dental Implants FAQ

How long do dental implants last?

Again, a complete tooth replacement using this treatment involves an implant (which replaces the tooth root), the restoration (crown, bridge, or denture), and the abutment (which connects the implant to the restoration). While restorations tend to last 10–15 years, implants and abutments can last 40 years or longer with proper care. Dr. H can provide ongoing care to help you implants last as long as possible.

Do dental implants hurt?

When you choose Dr. H, you can expect a comfortable surgery thanks to local anesthesia and optional sedation. You may experience some mild discomfort during the initial healing phase after receiving your implants, but this can be easily controlled using over-the-counter pain relievers.

How long does treatment take?

From your initial consultation until you receive your permanent restoration, your treatment could take three to six months. That’s because it will take time for the implants to fuse with the jaw, and this process must be complete before we can move ahead with permanent restorations. Don’t worry—until you get your permanent crown, bridge, or denture, we will provide you with temporary restorations so you won’t leave our office without teeth.

How much do dental implants cost?

Because this treatment is highly customized to the individual, a consultation with Dr. H is the very best way to gain an understanding of what you might expect to pay for dental implants. The most important cost factors are the number of implants you require, the amount of preparatory work needed, and the type of restoration that will be used to replace your lost teeth.
