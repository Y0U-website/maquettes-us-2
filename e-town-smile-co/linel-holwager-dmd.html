A Kentucky Native

Making E-Town Smile

Dr. Linel Holwager (known by her patients as Dr. H) was born and raised down the road in Leitchfield. She loves Western Kentucky and loves giving Elizabethtown something to smile about every day. Under her care, you can expect a personalized experience focused on your satisfaction and the optimization of your smile.

After earning her doctorate from the University of Louisville School of Dentistry and completing a fellowship in developmental dentistry at the Underwood and Lee clinic, she spent five years working with special needs adults at the clinic. She has led a private practice in Leitchfield for more than 10 years, and now proudly shares her services and talents as head of E-T own Smile Co.

"When I see patients feeling good about themselves and their oral health after I provide comprehensive care, that leaves me with a feeling of success. I want to set you on a course for lifelong oral health.”

Lifelong Learner

In addition to leading E-Town Smile Co ., Dr. Holwager mentors colleagues in dental implants and oral surgery at the Rasner Institute as a clinical instructor four times every year. She also teaches general dentists IV sedation techniques with DOCS Education.

Dr. Holwager is a Fellow of the Resnik Implant Institute, where she studied implant dentistry. She has undergone more than 1,000 hours of continuing education and spends about 150 hours each year studying innovations in the field to ensure that her patients are always getting the best treatment.

“I love to learn. There is no end to the number of courses that I take.”

Come Meet Dr. H

If you’re looking for a dentist in Elizabethtown who can meet all your needs, from simple checkups to complex restorative procedures, Dr. H is a prime choice for your care.
