I don’t trust dentists

The mission of Eaddy Cosmetic and Implant Dentistry, PLLC is to offer excellent and complete oral care to our patients and to become the clear choice for exceptional care in the Austin area. We are built around the four essential principles – trust, comfort, affordability, and quality. Our highly expert dentists and our dedicated dental staff are committed to helping you and your entire family achieve optimal oral health and well-being.

If you don’t trust dentists or are afraid of dental visits, we will give you reasons why you should entrust us.

Honest Approach

We are ethical and honest in our approach - we never pressurize any of our patients into services you don’t need.

Relationship-Focused

We are passionate about building relationships and treating you like family to make your dental visits happy and relaxing.

High-Quality Dentistry

We do everything possible and beyond to offer you high-quality dentistry.

Dental Insurance Friendly

Our dental office is insurance friendly - we accept all major insurances and help you make the claim request process easy and hassle-free.

Dental Emergencies Welcome

In case of dental emergencies, we provide same-day dentistry to give you fast and effective relief from pain.

Flexible Financing

We accept low or no-interest payment plans if you are not covered by dental insurance through CareCredit.
