Dr. Daniel Eaddy

Dr. Daniel Eaddy is a native of the Austin area and a 2006 graduate of Lake Travis High School. He played on the Cavalier football team and was named Elite Academic All-State. As a student, he received the Outstanding Senior in Science Award, the Cavalier Award, and the Pride and Spirit Award.

He attended the University of Texas at Austin, where he was accepted into the Dean’s Scholars Science Honors Program and Business Honors Program, graduating with honors in 2011 and receiving degrees in Honors Chemistry, Honors Business, and Finance. He was a competitive rower with Texas Crew, a Texas Cowboys service organization member, and named to the Phi Beta Kappa honor society.

Dr. Eaddy received his Doctor in Dental Surgery degree in 2015 from The University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio School of Dentistry and was one of twelve members of his class to be inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon national dental honor society.

He is passionate about developing trusting and authentic relationships with his patients to truly understand their concerns and desires and provide the most minimally-invasive and evidence-based treatment options available to improve their oral and overall health. He always invests in new technology to improve patient experience and outcomes and is a fervent lifelong learner through continuing education.

Dr. Eaddy lives in Lakeway with his wife, Ashlyn, his identical twin daughters, Ellie and June, and his son, David. Outside of dentistry, he enjoys spending time with his family, cooking, boating, reading, live music, and Longhorn sports.
