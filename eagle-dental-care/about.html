Meet Dr. Thomason & Our Team

Kindness, knowledge, and skill — from a team that treats you like family.

Dr. Chris Thomason, DMD

Dr. Thomason earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine at the University of Florida in 2008, after completing his undergraduate studies in biology at BYU-Idaho with multiple academic honors and scholarships. Following graduation, he provided humanitarian dental services in the Dominican Republic, and practiced in the Boise and Meridian area for twelve years before making Eagle Dental Care his home.

His philosophy is simple: keep learning, stay conservative with treatment, and care for every patient like family. He’s committed to continuing education so his patients always receive the best care possible.

Dr. Thomason and his wife Meredith are both Rexburg, Idaho natives, and they have three children. Outside the office you’ll find him camping, motorbiking, fly fishing, or practicing archery.
