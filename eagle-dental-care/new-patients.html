Your First Visit What to Expect Your first appointment is all about getting to know you — your health history, your goals, and anything that’s been bothering you. A few tips to make it easy: Arrive about 10 minutes early so we can finish any paperwork together.
  • Bring your insurance information and any other important documents.
  • Plan on 1 to 1.5 hours for a cleaning; other visits vary by treatment.
  • Bringing kids? We have a play area — we just ask that children not being treated are supervised.
  • We ask that you pay your portion at the time of service — and we’ll always tell you the cost before treatment begins.

Save Time — Fill Out Your Forms at Home

Download and complete your new patient forms before your first appointment — it helps us process your information quickly and accurately.

Exam + X-Rays + Cleaning

Start with a complete introductory visit: a comprehensive exam, X-rays, and a teeth cleaning (prophy). Free consultations are also available for second opinions or exploring treatment options.

Cleaning excludes scaling and root planing for patients with periodontal disease. Cannot be combined with other offers.

Wondering About Insurance?

We accept most major plans, offer CareCredit financing, and have an in-house dental plan for families without insurance.

Ready for Your First Visit?

Call us and we’ll find a time that works — we’ll even verify your insurance before you come in.
