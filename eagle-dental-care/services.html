Our Services

Dental Services in Eagle, Idaho

From routine cleanings to complete smile transformations — comprehensive care for every member of your family, all under one roof.

Hygiene & Preventative Care

Dr. Thomason recommends a daily oral hygiene routine and a checkup every six months. Your hygiene visit includes diagnostic X-rays, an oral cancer screening, gum disease evaluation, tooth decay examination, and a thorough cleaning and polishing.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth that are discolored, chipped, misshapen, or missing can all be treated. Options include bleaching, bonding, crowns, veneers, and reshaping and contouring. We want you to love your smile!

Fillings

A filling restores a tooth damaged by decay back to its normal function and shape. We remove the decayed material and fill the cavity with a natural, tooth-colored material that prevents further decay.

Crowns

A crown is a protective covering made of porcelain that looks like a tooth. Crowns protect weakened teeth, restore broken ones, anchor bridges, and cover teeth that are discolored or badly shaped.

Root Canals & Endodontics

Root canal treatment is needed when the pulp — the soft tissue inside your tooth — becomes inflamed or diseased from a deep cavity, repeated procedures, or an injury. Dr. Thomason’s gentle technique surprises most patients with how comfortable it is.

Dental Implants

The gold standard for replacing missing teeth. An implant anchors a natural-looking replacement tooth permanently in place — comfortable, secure, and designed to last a lifetime.

Veneers

Thin, custom porcelain shells bonded to the front of your teeth — a beautiful, long-lasting fix for chips, gaps, stains, and uneven shapes.

Periodontal Treatments

Gum disease is inflammation and infection of the gums and supporting bone. Left untreated it can lead to tooth loss — we catch it early with periodontal screenings and treat it with deep cleanings tailored to your needs.

Tooth Extractions & Oral Surgery

When a tooth can’t be saved, we make extractions as gentle and stress-free as possible — and walk you through every replacement option before you leave the chair.

Tooth Decay & Cavity Treatment

Decay caught early is decay treated easily. Regular exams let us find and treat cavities at an early stage — saving you time, money, and unnecessary discomfort.

X-Rays & CT Scans

Many problems can’t be seen in a visual exam. Modern digital imaging reveals issues beneath the surface early — when they’re simplest and least expensive to treat.

Emergency Dental Care

Tooth pain, a broken tooth, or a lost filling? Call us first. We do our best to see emergency patients the same day.

Not Sure What You Need?

Free consultations are available — whether you’d like a second opinion or just want to explore what’s possible for your smile.
