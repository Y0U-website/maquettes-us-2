About Our Edmond, Oklahoma Dentistry

Eagle Dental believes every patient deserves high-quality, personalized dental care.

Our team warmly welcomes you and your family to our Edmond office to experience dental care that puts your needs first. Our team values your comfort, leading you every step of the way with state-of-the-art technology so you can step out the door with a healthy, confident smile.

We provide comprehensive dentistry for you and your family, from routine cleanings to restorative dentistry and much more. We strive to serve our community and those nearby in metro Oklahoma City, Deer Creek, Oakdale, Guthrie, and Piedmont with the respect and expert care they deserve.

Every patient deserves the best. We’ll work with you to build relationships and develop treatment plans for a lifetime of better health. We provide flexible payment plans to meet your budget and provide a compassionate environment that puts your comfort first. We look forward to meeting you and your loved ones at Eagle Dental.

Comfort + Amenities at Our Edmond Dental Office

We want to change the way you feel about visiting the dentist. We understand you may feel anxious. May the moment we have the chance to greet you, your comfort is a top priority. You can expect the greatest care and attention for what fits your needs.

When it’s time for your appointment, we aim to provide a comfortable environment so you can focus on your dental health without worry. During your treatment, enjoy a show from the TVs in our operatories or relax to some music. We also provide warm blankets and neck pillows to cozy up while we care for you. For those who are extra dental phobic, we provide sedation dentistry options. Don’t let nerves stop you from the care you deserve. We’re here for your peace of mind.

For a welcoming visit that puts you first, contact us to schedule an appointment at Eagle Dental today.
