Financing & Insurance

We want to take the anxiety out of your dental visit, including your financial worries. We strive to get to know you and your needs, working together to find a payment plan that works best. Transparency matters. That’s why we’ll make sure you know exactly what you’re paying for and why. Expect a detailed treatment plan that outlines the cost of services, plus options for financing. With or without insurance, we offer flexibility when it comes to payments so you can be at ease.

Financing & Payment Plans

Regardless of your financial situation, we believe dental care should be accessible to everybody and we’ll work with you to find the right plan for you. We’ll learn about your unique needs and challenges so we can discuss different plans that fit within your budget. Whether it’s your routine checkup or an emergency visit that you weren’t expecting, we can find a plan to get you where you need to be. Affordable care is just as important as quality treatment.

Insurance Accepted

Insurance is one of the best ways to keep your dental expenses down. We accept the majority of dental insurance providers and will work with you to get the most of your yearly benefits. We understand navigating insurance plans can be overwhelming, so we’re happy to candidly discuss your options with you. We’ll provide a treatment plan that best utilizes your insurance plan for the lowest costs out-of-pocket.

Fee for Service

For further flexibility, we provide a fee-for-service payment plan to cover your treatment. This option is perfect for those without insurance or looking to reserve their benefits for a future visit. Before your appointment begins, we’ll discuss what to expect so you know exactly what you’re paying for and how much it will cost. Your confidence and trust is important to us, and we take time to make sure your treatment plan is clear and justified.

Membership Plan

To cut down on dental expenses, we offer membership plans to our patients. A yearly fee of $399 grants a patient two cleanings or periodontal maintenance treatments, two checkups including necessary radiographs, and any other services that might be needed or desired are discounted 20 % from their normal cost.

Membership plans are great choices for families. We offer discounted rates for children and others in your family, so you make sure you’re well taken care of at an affordable cost.
