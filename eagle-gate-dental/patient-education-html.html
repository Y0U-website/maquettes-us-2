Bad Breath

Are you suffering from bad breath? Your bad breath could be stemming from a range of reasons. Bad breath can be caused from dental and non-dental factors. At Eagle Gate Dental, we will help determine the cause of your bad breath and come up with a viable treatment plan. Learn about the different causes of bad breath and your treatment options!

How to Brush Your Teeth

Brushing your teeth might seem straightforward. After all, it’s something you’ve probably done your entire life. You’d be surprised to know that a lot of people use improper techniques when brushing their teeth. Check out our best practices for how to brush your teeth, so you can make sure you’re effectively brushing.

Learn More About How to Brush Your Teeth

Dental Health and Your Diet

Your diet directly impacts your dental health. Sugar is a leading cause of tooth decay - especially when it’s consumed in high doses throughout the day. There are actually techniques for eating sugar and snacking without it impacting your dental health as much. Learn about the best ways to snack, and how getting your diet under control can improve your dental health.

Learn More About Dental Health and Your Diet

Electric vs. Manual Toothbrush

When it comes to making choices about your dental health, you’ve probably wondered about the effectiveness of an electric toothbrush versus a manual toothbrush. While not all electric toothbrushes are created equally, on average, electric brushes are deemed more effective at removing plaque. Learn why you should think about switching to an electric toothbrush.

Learn More About Electric vs. Manual Toothbrush

Flossing

Do you have a hard time flossing your teeth? Flossing is one of the primary ways to ward off gum disease, and the only way to clean the surfaces of the teeth that you can’t reach brushing. Like brushing, flossing correctly can take some practice. Checkout our tutorial on how to floss your teeth correctly with dental floss.

Flouride

Fluoride can help defend against tooth decay and prevent cavities. One of the greatest ways to ensure that your children have strong teeth is by making sure they consume fluoride in early childhood. After teeth have erupted, you can still defend against cavities and strengthen your enamel with fluoride pastes and rinses. Learn more about integrating fluoride into your family’s dental health routines.
