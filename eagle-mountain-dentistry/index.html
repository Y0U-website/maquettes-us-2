The practice

Dr. Renas completes the majority of procedures in-house, including root canals, extractions, and dental implants, instead of referring every complex case to a specialist. That means fewer offices to coordinate with, and a dentist who understands your mouth over time.

It also shows up in details patients notice but rarely mention: heated neck towels and TVs in the treatment rooms, a team that remembers your kids’ names, and Hygiene Days at local schools where our team teaches proper brushing and flossing to help prevent dental anxiety before it starts.

Dr. Renas’s passion for dentistry started after he witnessed his brother have a life-changing dental experience. Seeing the remarkable change it made in his brother’s life, Dr. Renas decided he wanted to create the same experience for his patients. His mission is to provide high quality dentistry while being able to connect with each patient.

Dr. Renas has trained with some of the most advanced technology and constantly pursues continuing education credits to ensure his patients receive the best quality care that dentistry has to offer.
