The practice

We love welcoming new patients to our growing dental family here in Azle, TX. As a true family-centered practice, we offer comprehensive, quality dental care to patients of all ages in Azle, Springtown, Lake Worth, Lakeside, Weatherford, Boyd, Paradise, and many surrounding areas.

In 2022, Dr. Darin Bateman purchased our practice from Dr. Reeves who was retiring after 20 years in dentistry. Dr. Bateman plans to carry on the same tradition of excellence. Today, we have many patients who have been coming to us for over twenty years, including some second and third generation families.

Our dental team loves what they do, and it shows in every interaction they have with our valued patients. You can expect to be treated with care and respect for your needs, and enjoy a few laughs along the way. Maintaining a healthy smile is crucial, but it doesn’t have to be a chore!

At Eagle Mountain Family Dental, our practice philosophy is simple. We want to help our valued patients reach optimal oral health in a manner that fits their needs and expectations. If you need a family dentist in Azle, TX, we’re here to serve you.

One thing that sets us apart from other dental practices is our individualized approach to patient care. When addressing problems in dentistry, there’s often more than one approach to help you achieve a healthy smile. Dr. Bateman believes that you have the right to choose the treatment that fits your needs and allows you to enjoy a healthy smile.

Our team understands that many of our patients deal with dental anxiety. We ve designed our office to be warm and welcoming, and we offer amenities like neck pillows and blankets for your comfort in the dental chair.

Dr. Bateman is also certified to provide nitrous oxide, oral conscious sedation as well as Intravenous (IV) conscious sedation that gives you that little bit of extra help you need to relax or if you have anxiety when it comes to the dentist.

We meet your needs by keeping an open dialogue and encouraging you to ask questions whenever something’s not clear. In our experience, patients who are involved and informed are more likely to enjoy a high level of oral health. Our team looks forward to working with you to achieve this goal!
