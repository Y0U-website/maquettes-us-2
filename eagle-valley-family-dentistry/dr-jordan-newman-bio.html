Dr. Jordan Newman

Continuing a long family heritage of military service, Dr. Jordan Newman is a member of the Army National Guard, recently being appointed as a Captain. Military service creates a certain pride and experience that Dr. Newman brings to his patients. He is also community-minded, volunteering his time, efforts, and tidings to the causes he holds dear. Achieving the honor of Eagle Scout, Dr. Newman is involved in contributing to and helping other Eagle Scout projects.

When not in the office, Dr. Newman is an avid outdoorsman. Hiking is his preferred activity, he also enjoys golfing, soccer, woodworking, and shooting skeet. You can also find Dr. Newman at the gym, as keeping in shape is a must for his military service.

Being involved in Eagle Valley Family Dentistry has allowed Dr. Jordan Newman to expand his dental horizon and pursue the aspects of dentistry that he enjoys most. With an easy disposition, down to Earth s common-sense approach to patient care, Dr. Newman is on the right path to a great career in dentistry.
