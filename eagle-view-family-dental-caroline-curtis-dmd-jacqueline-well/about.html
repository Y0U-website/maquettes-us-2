We have three primary goals that we believe will keep us on track.

1) Invest in our family practice. We will continually invest in our patients, employees and our community. We pledge to take care of our employees and our expectation is that they will, in turn, take care of you. We will keep our equipment updated so that our providers will be able to focus on your oral health. Improvements in technology not only help our providers deliver better quality of care, it also helps us to communicate and make your experience the best it can be.

2) Provide you with care that is focused on your needs. We will not try to sell (or pressure you to buy) products or services which you don’t need. The philosophy of care for all of our providers starts with your overall HEALTH and well being. When you get a treatment plan from us it is based on issues indexed on prevention and removal of active disease. With most plans, our Doctors will discuss alternatives and options that are available given your situation.
