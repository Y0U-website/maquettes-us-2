What is Preventative Dentistry?

Preventative dentistry is at the heart of a lifelong journey towards optimal oral health, especially for children. By focusing on early detection, treatment, and education, we not only safeguard young teeth and gums but also lay down strong foundations for healthy oral habits. The easiest and best way to treat oral health health issues is to prevent them from happening in the first place. We encourage you to schedule your child&#x27;s first visit when their first tooth comes in or by their first birthday at the latest.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride Treatments Fluoride treatments are offered as an effective component of preventative care. By strengthening the tooth enamel and reducing the bacteria that cause cavities, fluoride can help protect teeth from decay. Fluoride treatments are always optional and are recommended on a personalized basis.

Dental Sealants

Dental Sealants Dental sealants provide an effective barrier against cavities. By applying a protective coating to the chewing surfaces of the back teeth, we can significantly reduce the risk of tooth decay.

Teeth Cleanings

Teeth Cleanings Professional teeth cleanings are essential for maintaining good oral hygiene. These routine cleanings help to remove plaque and tartar buildup, preventing tooth decay and gum disease.

Digital X-rays Our digital x-ray technology offers a safe, efficient way for Dr. Dana to provide the most comprehensive examination and recommendations for your child. Exams & Diagnostics Regular exams and diagnostic tests are the backbone of preventative dentistry. These checks allow us to closely monitor your child’s dental growth and development, identifying any potential issues early on and formulating effective treatment plans.

FAQs

How often should my child visit the dentist for preventive care?

The American Association of Pediatric Dentistry recommends that children visit the dentist for preventative care every six months. Regular dental check-ups allow for ongoing monitoring of oral health and early detection of potential issues.

Why is preventive dentistry important for children?

Preventative dentistry is crucial for children as it sets the foundation for healthy dental habits. Early dental care helps in identifying and treating issues before they become complex, ensuring healthier teeth and gums as they grow. For example, early detection of dental decay can allow for more conservative dental treatment, and can make the difference in whether a child can receive a small filling rather than a crown.
