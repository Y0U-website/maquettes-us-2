Our team is comprised of volunteers, passionate about supporting dental health education around the world.

Our core purpose is to teach the world the importance of dental hygiene and how it is foundational to healthy living.

One of the most important things I emphasize to patients, is the correlation between their oral health and their body`s general health. Studies and practical experience has demonstrated such a close relationship between poor oral hygiene and several serious medical conditions. It has been my experience as a practicing dentist for nearly 20 years, that my patients with good oral health are generally more healthy overall. Gum disease such as Gingivitis and Periodontal disease along with tooth decay can cause or accentuate other health issues. I see this each day I go to the office.
