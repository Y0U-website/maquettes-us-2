Bone grafting is a procedure performed to increase the amount of bone in a part of the jaw where excessive bone has been lost or additional support is needed. This is required for patients who are going to receive implants in place of missing teeth or with the occurrence of bone loss due to the effects of gum disease.

A tooth that is broken or cracked below the gums is in danger of not surviving. However, in many cases these types of fracturs can be treated. Crown lengthening allows the gum line to be lowered to gain access to the damaged portion of the tooth, allowing for a restoration which can save the tooth structure.

A gingival biopsy tests concerning or abnormal gum tissue to help diagnose a variety of oral lesions. The information from the biopsy can be used along with the findings of imaging tests to help your doctor diagnose oral cancer as early as possible. Earlier diagnosis means less scarring from the removal of tumors and a survival rate.
