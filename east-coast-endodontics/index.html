The practice

Our doctors are proud to call this place home. The East Coast Endodontics team is dedicated to helping others and enriching our local communities.

We specialize exclusively in root canals and endodontic microsurgery. Our team of highly trained endodontists — Dr. Jesse Harris , Dr. Richard Sedwick , and Dr. Adam Sarnowski — provides compassionate, cutting-edge care designed to relieve pain, preserve teeth, and restore smiles.

With three conveniently located practices in Mechanicsville , Short Pump , and Midlothian , we make expert endodontic care accessible throughout the greater Richmond area.

We use GentleWave®, 3D CBCT imaging, and surgical microscopes to ensure precise, minimally invasive treatment.

Our sedation options and gentle techniques help you feel relaxed and comfortable every step of the way.

Known for our precision and compassion, we’re the go-to referral choice for dentists throughout the Richmond region.

We never charge for post-treatment follow-up visits. Healing matters to us.

East Coast Endodontics is a practice limited to endodontics located in Richmond, VA. This means that root canals is all we do, all day, every day…your root canal is our primary focus. Dr. Harris specializes in all aspects of endodontics from conventional root canal therapy, root canal retreatments , cracked teeth , traumatic injuries, and apical surgery .
