What to Expect During the Root Canal Procedure

We know the unknown can feel intimidating, so here’s a step-by-step look at what happens during a root canal at East Coast Endodontics:

Most procedures take 60–90 minutes and are often completed in one visit.

  • Diagnosis & Imaging We take digital X-rays and CBCT scans to see the full 3D structure of your tooth. This allows us to detect infection, hidden canals, or cracks that standard X-rays might miss.
  • Comfort & Anesthesia Your comfort is our priority. We fully numb the tooth and surrounding area with local anesthetic. For anxious patients, we also offer sedation options to ensure you feel calm and relaxed.
  • Access & Pulp Removal A tiny opening is created in the tooth to access the pulp chamber. Using high-powered surgical microscopes, our endodontists carefully remove the inflamed or infected pulp tissue.
  • GentleWave® Ultracleaning Unlike traditional root canals, we use GentleWave® technology . This system uses sound waves and fluids to clean even the microscopic spaces in your canals, eliminating bacteria more thoroughly while preserving healthy tooth structure.
  • Shaping, Sealing & Filling Once cleaned, the canals are shaped and filled with a biocompatible material (gutta-percha) to seal the tooth against reinfection. A temporary filling is placed to protect your tooth until your dentist provides the final restoration.
  • Restoration After healing, you’ll return to your general dentist for a crown or permanent filling. This step is important because it restores the tooth’s strength and function long-term.

How long does a root canal take?

Most procedures take about 60–90 minutes , and many can be completed in a single visit. More complex cases may require two visits.

Will I need a crown after treatment?

Yes, in most cases. A crown protects your tooth from fracture and restores full strength and function after a root canal.

How successful are root canals?

Root canals have a 95–97 % success rate when combined with proper restoration. With modern tools like CBCT imaging and GentleWave® technology , your chances of long-term success are even higher.
