Endodontic retreatment is a procedure performed by endodontists to address issues that arise following a previous root canal treatment. It involves removing the filling material from the tooth’s canal system, cleaning the canals, and refilling them with new material. It is a safe and effective solution that can be completed in one or two appointments.

Apicoectomy is a minor surgical procedure performed by endodontists to address issues with the tip of a tooth’s root. Apicoectomy treatment can help alleviate pain, prevent the spread of infection, and preserve the natural tooth. It is a safe and effective solution that can often be completed in one appointment.

During an Apicoectomy, we access the top of the root through an incision in the gums to expose the bone and inflamed tissue. We remove the end of the root and any infected material, and then place a root-end filling to protect it. The gums are stitched and the bone heals on its own over a few months.
