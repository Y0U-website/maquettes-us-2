Safe And Pain-Free Root Canal Treatment Procedure In Glenview

Our root canal treatment includes:

Removing Infected Pulp:

The first step in our root canal process is the careful removal of the infected pulp. This pulp, located inside the tooth’s root, can become inflamed due to decay, repeated procedures on the tooth, or a crack or chip in the tooth. By removing this infected or inflamed canal root pulp, we eliminate the source of pain and infection, setting the stage for the healing process. This step is critical not only to alleviate discomfort but also to prevent the spread of infection to surrounding teeth and gum tissues.

Cleaning the Canal:

After the infected pulp is removed, the next step is to thoroughly clean the canal system within the tooth. This involves meticulously shaping the canals with fine dental instruments and flushing out the debris and bacteria with disinfecting solutions. Our expert endodontists ensure that every microbe is eradicated, leaving the canals clean and free from infection. This thorough endodontic treatment and cleaning process is vital to prepare the tooth for the final filling and sealing, preventing future infections and promoting long-term dental health.

Filling and Sealing:

Once the canals are clean, the next step is to fill them with a biocompatible material, usually a rubber-like material called gutta-percha. This material is placed into the cleaned canals to seal them off from any further bacterial invasion. After the canal expands, the tooth is further sealed with a dental crown to restore its full functionality and appearance. This not only protects the tooth root from further damage but also restores its natural function and aesthetic look, allowing you to chew and bite without discomfort.

After the treatment, our patients often report immediate relief and a quick return to their daily routines. Don’t let anxiety keep you from the care you need. Our team is here to guide you through every step and ensure your comfort and satisfaction.

Recognizing the early signs of needing a root canal can save you from prolonged discomfort and complex tooth issues down the line. We emphasize patient education to help you identify when to seek treatment. Common symptoms indicating a root canal may be necessary include:

  • Persistent pain that affects your daily life
  • Increased sensitivity to hot and cold foods
  • Tooth discoloration or deep decay
  • Gum tenderness or swelling near the affected tooth
