About Us

Dr. DeSantis has been providing high quality, safe, modern, and compassionate dental services in the Capital District for the better part of a decade. He has experience with family and general dentistry services as well as more complicated treatment and the comprehensive rehabilitation of broken smiles.

He is a certified provider of Invisalign clear aligner orthodontics to get you the straight teeth you always wanted. Most adults are great candidates. Combining braces free orthodontics and restorative dental treatment means that you can get your smile to 100 %, all planned out from the beginning and without having to go to multiple offices.

Our new office opened in East Greenbush in 2015 and we have a state of the art facility. Conveniently located just 0.5 miles from I90, our office is easy to get to. We are using 100 % digital record keeping and low exposure digital xrays. Many of our xrays including routine checkup xrays can usually be taken from outside of your mouth without having uncomfortable plastic parts to bite on. Combine all of this with a skilled and compassionate Doctor and staff and you’ve got a winning combination.

Clean and modern, our office is your new safe haven where you can get your smile back or keep the great smile you’ve got!
