About East Hill

East Hill Medical Center has been caring for Auburn and Cayuga County for more than 50 years. Each year, we provide primary care, dental, and behavioral health services to over 10,000 children and adults. Our vision is to strengthen community wellness so that every individual can achieve a healthy, fulfilling life.
