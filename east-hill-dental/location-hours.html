TESTIMONIALS

13 N Fulton St. Auburn, NY

East Hill Family & Pediatric Medicine, is located at 13 N. Fulton Street. Parking is free in the parking lot located behind the building.

This location is referred to as East Hill s Fulton Street campus, which was formerly Duckett Family Medicine and Summit Pediatrics before coming together in the same office.

144 Genesee St. Auburn, NY

East Hill Medical Center s main office is at 144 Genesee Street in downtown Auburn. Administration, Adult Medicine, Finance, and Dental offices are in the Metcalf Plaza building on Genesee Street.

Metered, back-in parking is available in the front of the building. The City of Auburn also offers parking in the garage across the street at $1 per hour, enforced Monday through Friday from 9 AM to 5 PM.

Our offices are open both before 9 AM and after 5 PM. If you re concerned about parking fees, we’re happy to help you schedule an appointment outside of those hours.
