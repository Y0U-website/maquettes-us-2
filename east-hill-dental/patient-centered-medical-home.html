Patient-Centered Medical Home

  • Listen to your questions and concerns. Explain disease, treatment, and results in an easy-to-understand way.
  • Create individualized care plans to address your health care goals.
  • Coordinate your overall care, sending you to trusted specialists if needed.
  • Provide you with same-day sick appointments whenever possible.
  • Provide instruction on how to access the care you need when the office is not open.
  • Provide clear instructions about your treatment goals and future plans for every visit.
  • Encourage you to take an active part in your own healthcare.

YOUR Responsibilities in a PCMH are

  • Understand that you are a full partner in your own health.
  • Ask questions and be active in your care.
  • Provide your health history and other important information, including any changes in your health.
  • Call our office first, rather than urgent care, with your health concerns unless it is an emergency.
  • Inform us whenever you utilize other health system such as the emergency room or specialist.
  • Have a clear understanding about your treatment goals and future health goals.
