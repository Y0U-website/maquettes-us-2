Accessibility

  • Descriptive link text that makes sense out of context
  • Alt text for images
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds
  • Resizable text that can be enlarged without loss of functionality
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and forms
  • Clear headings to organize content
  • Simple, readable language

Office Accessibility

  • Ground-level entrance with no steps
  • Wide doorways for wheelchair access
  • Accessible restroom facilities
  • Comfortable waiting area with accessible seating
  • Staff trained to assist patients with special needs

Assistive Technologies

Our website is designed to be compatible with assistive technologies including:

  • Screen readers
  • Screen magnification software
  • Speech recognition software
  • Keyboard-only navigation

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some content may not yet be fully accessible. We are actively working to identify and fix these issues. Known limitations include:

  • Some older PDF documents may not be fully accessible
  • Third-party content and tools may have their own accessibility limitations
