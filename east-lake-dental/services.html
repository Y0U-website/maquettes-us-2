Common Questions

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?

We recommend professional cleanings every six months. Regular cleanings prevent plaque buildup and catch problems early.

Do fillings hurt?

No. We use local anesthesia to ensure you’re completely comfortable. Modern composite fillings typically require less drilling.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last 25 years or longer. They’re the most durable option available.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes. Professional whitening supervised by a dentist is safe and more effective than over-the-counter products.

How much does Invisalign cost?

Costs vary based on treatment needs. Most cases range from $3,000 to $6,000. Many insurance plans cover a portion.

Are you a mercury-free practice?

Yes. We use tooth-colored composite resins instead of metal amalgam fillings.

What’s the difference between a crown and filling?

Fillings repair small areas of decay. Crowns cover and protect the entire tooth when damage is too extensive for a filling.

Do you see patients from outside South Lyon?

Absolutely. We serve Northville, Novi, Milford, Whitmore Lake, Wixom, and throughout the surrounding area.
