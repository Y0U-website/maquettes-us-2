Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants can help prevent further jawbone loss. Your jawbone depends on the roots of your teeth to receive the stimulation it needs to keep creating bone cells.

When you lose a tooth, the root is no longer there to provide that stimulation, leading to bone loss in your jaw. Because dental implants function like a tooth’s root, they can help you maintain jawbone density.

Dental implants also provide more stability than dentures if you need to replace many of your teeth. Dentures can slip despite the use of adhesives, making it impossible to enjoy some of your favorite foods and in some instances, even affecting your speech.

With dental implants, you can eat foods like steak and corn on the cob confidently and speak with ease. You can stabilize dentures with implants as well. With a few implants in place, you can enjoy full or partial dentures without the worry of dealing with adhesives.

And unlike getting dental bridges or crowns , dental implants do not require the removal of any enamel from neighboring teeth. Dental implants offer a natural look and a perfect fit while also letting you use the full force of your jaw to chew.

The Day of Your Procedure

On the day of your appointment, you will receive anesthesia to numb your gums and jaw and any other requested sedation. Once you are comfortable, Dr. Zirakian will make the necessary incisions. For standard implants, you can expect more incisions than for mini dental implants .

If you are getting standard implants, he will drill into your jawbone and place the implant, screwing it so that it doesn’t move. Mini dental implants do not need to be completely embedded into the jawbone, so the procedure is quicker.

Dr. Zirakian will then stitch the incisions and offer tips on what to expect from the recovery process. Dr. Zirakian will make the impressions needed so the lab can create the false teeth.

If you received sedation during the procedure, you will need to have someone drive you home. Once Dr. Zirakian receives the false teeth from the lab, he will schedule another appointment to fit them on the abutments of standard implants or directly on the mini dental implants.

Aftercare and Maintenance of Dental Implants

As you recover from the procedure, you will want to avoid chewing hard or sticky foods. Make sure to give the implants time to settle.

You want to brush your teeth and floss as you normally would, but be gentle around the incisions. You also want to avoid strenuous exercise or heavy lifting for at least 72 hours and remember to chew on the other side of your mouth.

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for your natural teeth. You want to maintain good oral hygiene and limit foods and drinks that can stain the implants. If you grind your teeth at night, ask Dr. Zirakian about getting a mouthguard because your jaw can put a lot of pressure on your false and natural teeth, potentially cracking them.

You also need to schedule regular appointments with Dr. Zirakian, so he can monitor your dental implants and overall oral health.
