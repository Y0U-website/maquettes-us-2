The practice

From the moment you step into our East Milton office, we’ll help you feel comfortable and at ease.

Dr. Sweeney–Regan is a graduate of Dalhousie University Faculty of Dentistry with extensive training in Advanced Restorative Techniques. Dr. Regan also received a Masters in Biology from UMass Boston summa cum laude and a degree in Biology from Colby College.

Dr. Regan loves dental CE courses, reading her geeky dental journals and all things dentistry! She strives to stay abreast of the most current dental research, techniques and materials.

Prior to pursuing a career in dentistry, Dr. Regan worked in a biochemistry/genetics research laboratory at the Marine Biological Laboratories in Woods Hole, MA. She also enjoyed teaching high school Biology and Chemistry at Blue Hills Regional High School.

Dr. Regan grew up in Milton and still proudly lives in the town! She was in the first French Immersion class and a graduate of Milton High School where she was an active athlete playing field hockey, ice hockey and softball.
