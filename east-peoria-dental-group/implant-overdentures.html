How the Overdenture Process Works

  • Consultation and Planning We begin with a comprehensive exam and digital scans to determine candidacy. Dr. Josh Hudson or Dr. John Zimdars will explain all options, including how overdentures compare to traditional dental implants .

Implant Overdenture FAQs

How many implants are needed for overdentures?

Typically, 2 to 4 implants are placed per arch. The exact number depends on your bone health and desired level of retention.

Are implant overdentures removable?

Yes. Unlike fixed implant bridges, overdentures are designed to be removed by the patient for cleaning, which many find more convenient.

How long does it take to get overdentures?

The timeline varies, but most patients complete the process in 4 to 6 months, including healing time after implant placement.

Will I be without teeth during healing?

No. Temporary dentures are provided so you can maintain appearance and function while your implants integrate with the bone.

Are overdentures covered by dental insurance?

Some insurance plans provide partial coverage. Our team can review your benefits and help you explore financing options if needed.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Self-esteem: You’ll gain the confidence to smile in pictures again with dental implants, as people likely won’t even be able to tell you have them.
