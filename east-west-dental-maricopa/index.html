Dr. Ted H Tang is a native of Los Angeles, CA. He attended the University of California at Berkeley where he graduated with honors in Molecular Cell Biology. He later went to the University of California at Los Angeles School of Dentistry where he received his D.D.S. degree.

Dr. Tang is a member of the American Dental Association and the Arizona Dental Association. He spends many hours each year pursuing continuing education to stay abreast of the latest dental techniques and thinking to ensure that the highest quality of care is delivered to his patients at all times.

Dr. Tang moved to Chandler, Arizona with his wife, Jennifer and their beloved Rottweiler, Tutu in 2000. Since then, they have added a boy and a girl to their family. Dr. Tang enjoys playing tennis, basketball, traveling, and most of all; he enjoys spending time with his family.
