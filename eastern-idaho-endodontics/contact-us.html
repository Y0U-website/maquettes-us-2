Scheduling a root canal or endodontic treatment

Eastern Idaho Endodontics is open Monday – Friday from 8:00 a.m. until 5:00 p.m. We will schedule your appointment as promptly as possible. If you have pain or an emergency situation, every attempt will be made to see you as soon as possible.

We try our best to stay on schedule to minimize your waiting. Due to the fact that the practice provides surgical services, various circumstances may lengthen the time allocated for a procedure. Emergency cases can also arise and cause delays. We appreciate your understanding and patience.

If you have any questions or need to schedule an appointment please call Idaho Falls Office Phone Number 208-524-3770 for our Idaho Falls, Jackson Hole, Pinedale and Sun Valley offices, 208-637-1300 for our Pocatello and Burley offices.
