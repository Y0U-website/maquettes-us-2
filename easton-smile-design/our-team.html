Dr. Mrunal Dave

Dr. Mrunal Dave was born and raised in India, where he earned his Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) in 2018. He moved to the United States in 2019 and completed a Master of Public Health (MPH) from Rutgers University in 2021. He went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the prestigious New York University College of Dentistry in 2024.

Since joining our practice, Dr. Dave has been delivering comprehensive dental care with a focus on precision, efficiency, and patient-centered outcomes. His clinical expertise spans preventive, restorative, surgical, and esthetic dentistry. He remains committed to continuing education and staying current with the latest advancements in dental technology and techniques.

Dr. Dave practices alongside his wife, Dr. Shivani Naik, who is also an associate dentist at our dental practice. Together, they are proud to serve the community with shared values of excellence, integrity, and compassion.

In the office, Dr. Dave collaborates closely with the clinical team to ensure a seamless and comfortable experience for patients of all ages. Outside of dentistry, he enjoys traveling, playing and watching cricket, and spending quality time with family and friends.

Dr.Shivani Naik

Dr. Shivani Naik began her dental journey in India, graduating from dental school in 2018. With a strong vision to serve communities on a broader scale, she pursued and earned a Master of Public Health (MPH) from West Chester University in Pennsylvania.

Her passion for excellence in dentistry led her to graduate from the renowned New York University College of Dentistry, where she gained advanced clinical training and a patient-focused approach to care.

As a general dentist, Dr. Naik provides comprehensive care that includes restorative treatments, oral surgery, and esthetic dental procedures. She firmly believes that oral health is closely connected to overall health and is dedicated to helping her patients achieve long-term wellness and confident smiles.

Dr. Naik works alongside her husband, Dr. Mrunal Dave, sharing a united mission of restoring smiles and improving lives. Together, they bring a compassionate and collaborative spirit to every aspect of patient care.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Naik enjoys traveling, hiking, and spending quality time with family and friends. Her well-rounded approach to life mirrors the balance and care she brings to her profession.
