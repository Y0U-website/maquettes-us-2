Restoring Smiles at Eastridge Family Dental

At Eastridge Family Dental , nothing brings us more joy than seeing our patients’ healthy, confident smiles.

Our practice is dedicated to providing top-quality family dental care to patients of all ages in Lee’s Summit and nearby areas. We offer a comprehensive range of services, including sedation dentistry, routine cleanings, adult dentistry, and Invisalign ® , making it easy to get the care you need under one roof. Whether you’re seeking preventative, cosmetic, restorative, or emergency services, our team is here to support your unique dental goals.
