No Insurance? No Problem.

Our team is committed to making dental care accessible for all, even if you don’t have insurance. We offer affordable in-house plans designed to help you save on essential services, so you can keep your smile healthy without breaking the bank.

Financial FAQs

  • Are there any in-house financing programs? Yes, our in-house Dental Savings Plan is perfect for patients without insurance. With a monthly or yearly fee, you gain access to a variety of dental services, including checkups, cleanings, and crowns—all without a deductible! Discover Dental Savings Plan
  • Do you offer third-party payment options? Absolutely! We accept Proceed Finance , CareCredit ® , and Sunbit , which offer flexible financing solutions and an easy application process to fit your budget. External Payment Plans
  • Can I use CareCredit ® with my insurance? CareCredit ® may be applied to your balance however you would like. You may choose to use your CareCredit ® card to pay your balance in full, in conjunction with your insurance coverage, or in small portions. Visit CareCredit
