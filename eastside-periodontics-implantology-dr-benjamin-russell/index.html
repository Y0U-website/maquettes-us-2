The practice

With three convenient locations in and around Portland, complete oral care is right around the corner.

If you have been experiencing symptoms of periodontal disease, such as red, swollen or tender gums or other oral discomfort it may be a good idea to schedule an appointment with a periodontist. You may also be experiencing bleeding while brushing your teeth, persistent bad breath, or a change in the way your teeth fit together when you bite. These are all symptoms of periodontal disease.

You may be wondering, “What exactly is periodontal disease?” or “Why is it a good idea to see a periodontist for treatment options?” These are common questions, and their answers are important! We are committed to providing the highest quality dental care in a comforting environment and take pride in offering you a pleasant office experience where our qualified staff is friendly and knowledgeable.

Often, we specialize in treating more problematic periodontal cases, such as those with severe gum disease or a complex medical history. We offer a large range of treatments, like scaling and root planing (cleaning the infected surface of the root) and root surface debridement (removing damaged tissue). During your first visit, we’ll review your complete medical and dental histories.

It is important for us to know of any medications prescribed, or if you are being treated for any condition which could affect periodontal care like heart disease, diabetes, or pregnancy.

We’ll then examine your gums, looking for any gum line recession, assess how your teeth fit together when biting and inspects for loose teeth. We also inspect, measure and probe between the teeth and gums to determine the depth of those spaces known as periodontal pockets to assess the health of the gums. X-rays may also be taken to observe the health of the bone below the gum line.
