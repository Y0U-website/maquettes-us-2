Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Amy Kim

Dr. Amy Kim is a board-certified pediatric dentist with a lifelong love for art, science, and helping others—three passions that come together in the way she cares for kids. After earning her degree in Neuroscience from Smith College, she completed her Doctor of Dental Medicine at Tufts University, where she fell in love with pediatric dentistry. Her commitment to serving communities led her to complete an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) through NYU Langone, where she worked on a mobile dental van delivering care to schools throughout the Navajo Nation in Winslow, Arizona.

Inspired by this experience, Dr. Amy returned to Tufts for her pediatric specialty training and served as chief resident during her final year. She’s spent the past decade helping children feel safe, comfortable, and confident in the dental chair. Known for her calm presence and gentle approach, Dr. Amy believes in meeting each child where they are, educating families along the way, and building trust that lasts a lifetime.

When she’s not caring for little smiles, Dr. Amy enjoys spending time with her family, playing golf, and staying active with pilates. She’s also a creative at heart and loves bringing small touches of joy—like balloon animals and popsicles—into her daily patient care.

board certified Endodontist

Dr. James Lin

Dr. James Lin is an experienced board certified endodontist who brings advanced specialty care to kids in a setting they already know and trust. After earning his dental degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, he pursued additional specialty training in endodontics at Tufts. Throughout his career, he developed a particular interest in helping pediatric patients who require complex treatment—especially children experiencing dental trauma or needing root canal therapy on permanent teeth.

At Eastvale Kids Dental Studio, Dr. James works in tandem with Dr. Amy to provide continuity of care and in-house treatment that’s both expert and reassuring. With the ability to perform treatments using nitrous oxide and in coordination with monthly IV/general anesthesia services, he helps make difficult dental situations more manageable for families and less intimidating for kids.

Outside of the operatory, Dr. James enjoys golf, staying active, and spending time with Dr. Amy and their growing family. His thoughtful, calm demeanor and clinical precision make him a trusted partner in delivering the highest quality care for young patients who need it most. He looks forward to meeting your kiddo soon!

Preparing for takeoff

Your child’s first visit

Step 1

Step into our space-inspired studio

From the moment you arrive, your child will be welcomed into a calming, clean environment with twinkling galaxy ceilings, friendly faces, and playful touches that set the tone for a great visit.

Step 2

Easy check-in and big smiles

Our front desk team will guide you through a simple check-in process, while your child enjoys the prize wall, character décor, and a relaxing waiting space.

Step 3

A gentle, educational exam

Dr. Amy will take time to get to know your child and walk them through their visit step by step—focusing on prevention, comfort, and education.

Step 4

Personalized care and clear guidance

We’ll go over everything with you, share tips and recommendations, and help build a care plan that works for your child’s health, personality, and smile goals.
