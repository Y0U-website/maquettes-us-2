Our preventive treatments

Dental exams & cleanings

Dental exams & cleanings

We recommend regular visits every six months to monitor your child’s oral health, gently clean their teeth, and reinforce healthy habits. Our cleanings are tailored to each child’s age and comfort level, and our exams include low-radiation digital X-rays when needed.

Silver diamine fluoride

Silver diamine fluoride

For early cavities or high-risk areas, silver diamine fluoride (SDF) offers a non-invasive way to stop decay in its tracks—without drilling or discomfort. It’s a great alternative for young children or those with special healthcare needs.

Dental sealants

Dental sealants

Sealants are thin coatings that protect the pits and grooves of your child’s molars from bacteria and food particles. Placed in minutes and lasting for years, sealants are a simple and effective way to prevent cavities.

Oral hygiene education

Oral hygiene education

We believe every visit is a chance to teach. Dr. Amy and the team show kids how to brush and floss correctly, while also giving parents tips for keeping those skills going strong at home.

Space maintenance & growth monitoring

Space maintenance & growth monitoring

We monitor your child’s growth and development from an early age to spot potential issues and intervene early. We also place space maintainers when needed to preserve room for adult teeth after premature tooth loss.

Special healthcare needs

Special healthcare needs

We take a proactive, personalized approach to preventive care for children with special healthcare needs. From sensory-friendly appointments to extra time for cleanings and exams, we meet each child where they are—helping build healthy habits and positive dental experiences from the start.

FAQs When should my child start seeing a dentist? The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends scheduling your child’s first visit by their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth erupting. These early visits help your child get used to the dental environment and give us a chance to catch any developmental concerns early.

How often should my child get cleanings and checkups?

Most children benefit from visiting the dentist every six months for a cleaning and exam. However, if your child is more prone to cavities or has special oral health needs, we may recommend more frequent visits. Routine checkups are essential to preventing issues and keeping care comfortable and manageable.

Is fluoride safe for kids?

Yes—when applied professionally in appropriate doses, fluoride is safe and effective at preventing tooth decay. At Eastvale Kids Dental Studio, we use fluoride varnish that quickly sets on the teeth and provides ongoing protection. We’re also happy to discuss any concerns you may have and recommend fluoride use at home based on your child’s age and risk level.
