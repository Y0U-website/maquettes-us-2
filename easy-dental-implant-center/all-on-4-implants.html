The All-on-4 Process: What to Expect

Getting All-on-4 or All-on-6 implants is a multi-step process that’s customized for each patient. Here’s what you can expect:

Step 1: Consultation and Planning

Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation where we assess your oral health, jawbone density, and overall suitability for the procedure. We use advanced imaging, such as CBCT scans and 3D imaging, to map out the precise placement of each implant. This ensures a highly accurate and personalized treatment plan.

A Personal Tip: Patients who understand the procedure feel more confident going into surgery, so don’t hesitate to ask questions during this step!

Step 2: Implant Placement

Once we’ve determined you’re a good candidate for All-on-4, we’ll schedule your implant surgery. During the procedure, we make small incisions in the gums to access the jawbone, placing four (or six) titanium implants in strategic locations. The back implants are angled for optimal bone support, while the front implants are placed vertically.

Note: Many patients worry about the pain of surgery, but modern anesthesia and techniques make the experience comfortable. Patients often report minimal soreness, easily managed with over-the-counter medication.

Step 3: Attaching the Temporary Bridge

One of the best things about All-on-4 is that you can often receive a temporary bridge on the same day as your surgery. This means you’ll walk out with a full set of teeth while your implants heal.

Tip: The temporary bridge lets you start adjusting to your new smile right away, while the permanent bridge is crafted for a perfect fit.

Step 4: Healing and Final Restoration

Over the next few months, a process called osseointegration takes place, where the implants fuse with the jawbone. This fusion is essential for creating a stable, lasting foundation. Once osseointegration is complete, we’ll replace the temporary bridge with a custom-made permanent bridge, designed from durable materials like porcelain or zirconia.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How long does the All-on-4 procedure take?

A: The surgery typically takes a few hours, and in many cases, a temporary bridge can be placed the same day. Healing takes 3 - 6 months, after which the final bridge is attached.

Q: Will All-on-4 feel like natural teeth?

A: Yes! All-on-4 restorations are designed to look, feel, and function like your natural teeth, allowing you to chew, speak, and smile with ease.

Q: What is the difference between All-on-4 and All-on-6?

A: All-on-6 uses two additional implants, providing extra support and stability, especially useful for patients with softer jawbone density.

Q: Is All-on-4 right for me if I have bone loss?

A: In many cases, All-on-4 does not require bone grafts because of the angled placement of the implants. We’ll assess your jawbone during your consultation to confirm.
