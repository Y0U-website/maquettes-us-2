Frequently Asked Questions Q: I’ve heard of bone grafting. Do I need it before getting implants?

If your jawbone isn’t strong enough, a bone graft can strengthen it, making implants possible even after bone loss from missing teeth.

Q: I want implants but was told I might need a sinus lift. What is that?

A sinus lift adds bone to your upper jaw to create a foundation for implants, especially if bone loss has occurred in the upper molar area.

Q: Do I need a tooth extraction before getting implants?

Sometimes, yes. If a tooth is damaged or impacted, extraction prepares the site, ensuring a secure base for the implant.

Q: What’s the All-on-4 approach, and is it right for me?

All-on-4 uses 4-6 implants per arch to support a full set of teeth, offering a fixed, cost-effective solution for those missing most or all teeth in an arch.
