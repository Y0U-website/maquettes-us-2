SERVICES OFFERED

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are fully customized, wafer-thin sheaths of tooth-colored dental porcelain. This lifelike material even possesses the glass-like properties that make tooth enamel slightly reflective and translucent; this means that when your veneers are bonded to your teeth, they’ll blend seamlessly into your smile in a natural way that is entirely undetectable. They effectively promote a beautiful, even smile free of common cosmetic flaws, including chips, cracks, gaps, moderate misalignment, and discoloration.

Dental Implant Restorations

Teeth Whitening

A brighter smile starts here. Dr. Sox offers professional teeth whitening treatments that safely lift stains and discoloration — giving you results that over-the-counter products simply can’t match. Whether you’re preparing for a special occasion or just ready to love your smile again, in-office whitening delivers dramatic results in a single visit.

Smile Makeovers

A smile makeover brings together the best of cosmetic dentistry in one personalized plan. Dr. Sox combines treatments like veneers, whitening, bonding, and Invisalign to address everything from color and shape to alignment — transforming your smile in a way that looks completely natural and feels uniquely yours.
