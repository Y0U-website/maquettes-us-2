Dr. Scott Wagner, DMD

Associate Dentist at Eccella Smiles & Aesthetics

Dr. Scott Wagner brings high-level expertise and a down-to-earth, fun-loving personality to the Eccella Smiles & Aesthetics team. Known for his precision, approachable nature, and sense of humor, he has a special way of making patients feel relaxed and confident in the care they receive.

A proud graduate of the University of Florida College of Dentistry and a lifelong Florida Gators fan, Dr. Wagner channels his school spirit and drive for excellence into everything he does. As a Fellow of the prestigious Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies (LVI), he is highly trained in advanced cosmetic and neuromuscular dentistry . His focus on comprehensive, minimally invasive care helps patients achieve lasting health and beautiful results.

Outside the operatory, Dr. Wagner is an accomplished guitarist and singer who’s never shy about sharing a tune with his patients. He also considers himself a true “waterman”—passionate about surfing, wakeboarding, snowboarding, water skiing, scuba diving, windsurfing, and fishing. He lives by the adage “work hard, play hard,” and believes his zeal for life allows him to be a better, more inspired dentist for his patients.

At Eccella, Dr. Wagner isn’t just a skilled clinician—he’s a partner in your dental journey, committed to making sure you feel comfortable, cared for, and excited about your smile. Florida Dental License Number DN14961
