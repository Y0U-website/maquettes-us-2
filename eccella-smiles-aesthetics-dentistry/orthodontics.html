How to Care for Your Teeth During Orthodontic Treatment

You must adjust your oral hygiene routine during orthodontic treatment, whether you get aligner trays, traditional braces, or something in between. Depending on the care you receive, the following tips and tricks may help you throughout this process:

If you need additional assistance while you’re receiving orthodontic care, don’t hesitate to contact our team at Eccella Smiles. We can provide additional guidance and helpful information. You’re also welcome to discuss specific questions and concerns with your dental provider.

  • Brush and floss your teeth daily.
  • Consider getting an alternative floss, such as an air or water-flossing tool.
  • Speak with your dental provider for recommendations for toothpaste, mouthwash, or other tools.
  • Go to your Jacksonville Beach dentist and orthodontist as recommended by your providers.
  • Specific orthodontic tools may have diet restrictions, so listen to dentist directions related to crunchy, hard, or sticky foods.
  • Removable appliances need to be cleaned regularly. For example, Invisalign trays should soak in an appropriate cleansing solution as directed by your dentist.
  • Brush your teeth after every meal, snack, or drink other than water.
  • When removing removable braces or aligner trays, take care to put them into their appropriate travel cases. Putting your appliance into a napkin could result in it accidentally getting thrown away.

Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontic Treatment

Can adults get orthodontic treatment? Absolutely! There’s no age limit for orthodontic treatment, and more adults than ever are seeking treatment to improve their smiles. Clear aligner therapy like Invisalign is particularly popular among adult patients because it’s discreet and fits well with professional lifestyles.

Adult orthodontic treatment follows the same biological principles as treatment for younger patients, though treatment times may be slightly longer as adult bones are no longer growing.

What is the difference between traditional braces and Invisalign? Traditional braces use metal brackets and wires to gradually move your teeth into place, while Invisalign consists of clear, removable aligners that offer a more discreet and comfortable alternative. The best option depends on your specific dental needs and lifestyle preferences.
