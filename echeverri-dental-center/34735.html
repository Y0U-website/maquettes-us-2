Dental Implants A Solution for Missing Teeth

Dental implants consist of an artificial tooth root and a porcelain crown that mimics the appearance of natural teeth. The artificial tooth root is made of a titanium post and will give your jaw added support, just like a real tooth root.

An abutment connector links the titanium alloy post and the porcelain crown that we customize for your smile. A trusted dental lab fabricates your crown using the highest quality materials and customizes it to blend in seamlessly with your smile. The crown fills the gap in your smile and looks like a natural tooth.

How many appointments do I need for a dental implant?

Getting a dental implant requires multiple appointments that occur over several months. These include appointments for planning, placing your implant, placing the abutment, and placing the implant crown on your tooth. There may be an additional appointment if you are considering bone grafting .

Dental Implant Planning

The first step is a thorough examination with Dr. Juan Echeverri, who will place your implant. At this appointment, he evaluates your oral health and reviews your dental and medical history. He also takes x-rays and 3-D cone beam scans to determine if there’s enough bone mass to place the implant and if any other steps are needed, such as tooth extraction or bone grafting.

What is bone grafting in dentistry?

Bone grafting is a process that supplements the bone that supports your teeth and increases their density. One reason our dentists at Echeverri Dental Center would recommend bone grafting is if you want a dental implant but don’t have the jawbone mass to support it.

How We Determine Jawbone Density

We measure bone levels using advanced imaging such as digital x-rays, panoramic x-rays, and 3-D cone beam CT scans. Insufficient bone prevents certain procedures from being successful, but also puts remaining teeth at risk.

If bone levels continue to decline, commonly due to tooth loss or advanced periodontal disease, surrounding teeth become unstable and loosen. Bone grafting at our Houston, TX, dental office helps prevent mobile teeth and allows patients to get implants to replace missing teeth.

How Bone Grafting Works

We obtain bone for grafting in several ways, including using your bone, bone from a donor, or a bone substitute. Bone from these resources is a safe solution, similar to receiving a blood transfusion.

After our dentist expertly places the grafting material, it supplements the bone to increase its mass. It can take 3 6 months for the graft to heal, after which we can plan successful implant placement.

Dental Implants Technology

3-D cone beam imaging provides more comprehensive x-rays in a fraction of the time traditional x-rays take. It is innovative because it can capture x-rays from multiple angles of your face. These angles and scans combine to create a 3-D x-ray of your entire face.

Our 3-D imaging allows us to give precise diagnoses, administer more accurate treatment, and reduce your appointment time. It is easy to use, too! All you have to do is stand underneath the cone beam. Then the camera, located on the arm of the cone beam, will rotate 360 degrees around your head, capturing 3-D images of your face. Then, you’re all set! This process typically takes less than a minute!

A cephalometric or ceph x-ray is a unique diagnostic tool your dentist uses to capture an image of the side of your face. We take ceph x-rays outside the mouth (extraoral), making them more comfortable than a routine digital x-ray.

Since ceph x-rays show your dentist your entire oral structure in one image, they are commonly used when planning implant placement and orthodontic treatment . Ceph x-rays also show tooth fractures, jawbone injuries, and bite problems.
