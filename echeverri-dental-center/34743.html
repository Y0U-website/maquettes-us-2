Do you offer clear aligners?

We offer comprehensive orthodontics at Echeverri Dental Center for all ages, including clear aligner therapy similar to Invisalign. Clear aligners are an excellent alternative for an older teen or adult who wants straighter teeth without metal braces!

Treatment involves wearing a series of clear aligners customized just for your smile. Your dentist maps out your treatment using specialized software to plan the type and extent of movement needed to align your teeth.

Wearing Your Clear Aligners

When your custom aligners are ready, we provide instructions that tell you to wear each set for 22 hours a day for about two weeks. The number of aligners you receive corresponds to your dentist’s plan, and when you’ve worn them all, you’re finished with treatment. The final step is to wear a custom retainer as instructed to prevent your teeth from relapsing to their crooked positions.

The Benefits of Clear Aligner Therapy

Adults and teens appreciate the benefits clear aligners offer that bracket-and-wire braces don’t, including:

  • Discreet You can keep your treatment a secret because most people won’t notice you’re wearing aligners.
  • Comfortable No metal brackets and wires on your teeth means less irritation and soreness.
  • Removable You can easily remove your aligner trays and continue to eat the foods you want and brush and floss easily.

Can a six-year-old get orthodontic treatment?

Children commonly get full braces between ages 9 and 14, but that doesn’t mean a six-year-old can’t benefit from a visit to the orthodontist. Age seven is the recommended age for an orthodontic evaluation at our Houston dental office, but you are welcome to bring your younger child in anytime if you have concerns.

Interceptive or Phase 1 Orthodontics

The treatment a child would receive at age six is called interceptive orthodontics or Phase 1 orthodontics. This early intervention allows our pediatric dentist to address issues they can’t correct after your child’s face and jaw stop growing.

Some reasons we would recommend early orthodontic treatment include the following:

  • Create space in the jaw for adult teeth to erupt properly
  • Address a malocclusion or bite problem
  • Hold space for teeth that haven’t erupted
  • Minimize trauma to protruding front teeth
  • Reduce the need to remove teeth
  • Speed up braces’ treatment times
