Benefits of Dental Implants for Cottage Lake Residents

Choosing dental implants from Eckland Family Dentistry offers numerous advantages:

Improved Appearance and Confidence

Missing teeth can make you self-conscious about smiling or speaking in public. Dental implants provide a natural-looking replacement that blends seamlessly with your remaining teeth, restoring your confidence in social and professional situations.

Enhanced Comfort and Functionality

Unlike removable dentures, implants become a permanent part of your mouth. This means no slipping, clicking, or uncomfortable adhesives. You’ll be able to eat your favorite foods, speak clearly, and laugh without worry.

Preservation of Jawbone and Facial Structure

When teeth are missing, the jawbone beneath begins to deteriorate over time. This can lead to a sunken facial appearance and make you look older than your age. Dental implants stimulate bone growth, helping maintain your natural face shape and preventing bone loss.

Long-Term Oral Health

Dental implants don’t require reducing other teeth, as a tooth-supported bridge does. Because nearby teeth aren’t altered to support the implant, your natural teeth are left intact, improving long-term oral health. Individual implants also allow easier access between teeth, improving oral hygiene.

Durability and Convenience

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. They eliminate the inconvenience of removing dentures and the need for messy adhesives. Simply care for your implants as you would your natural teeth with regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups.

The Dental Implant Process at Eckland Family Dentistry

We’ve streamlined the dental implant process to make it as comfortable and efficient as possible for our Cottage Lake patients:

Throughout this process, our team will ensure your comfort and address any concerns you may have.

  • Comprehensive Evaluation : We’ll examine your teeth, gums, and jawbone using advanced imaging technology to create a personalized treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement : The titanium implant is surgically placed into your jawbone in a precise location to support your new tooth.
  • Healing Period : Over the next 3-6 months, the implant will fuse with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration.
  • Abutment Placement : Once healing is complete, we’ll attach an abutment to the implant, which will hold your new tooth.
  • Crown Placement : Finally, we’ll attach a custom-crafted crown that matches your natural teeth in color, shape, and size.
