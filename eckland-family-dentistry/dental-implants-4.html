Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants in Kenmore

How Long Do Dental Implants Last? +

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Research shows survival rates near 98 percent, making implants one of the most predictable tooth replacement options available.

Am I a Candidate If I Have Bone Loss? +

Bone loss does not automatically rule out implants. A bone graft can often build the necessary foundation before or during implant placement, and we discuss this option during your evaluation if it applies to your case.

Are Implants Better Than Dentures? +

It depends on your bone density, budget, and preferences. Implants are fixed in place and preserve jawbone density more effectively, while dentures are removable and do not require surgery. We review both options honestly during your consultation.
