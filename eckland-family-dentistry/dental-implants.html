Dental Implants in Woodinville, WA

If you’re unsure whether you’re a good candidate for dental implants, we can help determine the best option for your needs.

  • Healthy gums : Because implants fuse directly with the jawbone, healthy gums are essential for proper healing. Those with gum disease may need treatment before undergoing implant placement.
  • Sufficient jawbone density : A strong jawbone provides the necessary support for the implant. If bone loss has occurred, a bone graft may be needed to strengthen the area before placement.
  • Good overall health : Certain conditions like uncontrolled diabetes, autoimmune disorders, or heavy smoking can impact the healing process.
  • Commitment to oral hygiene : Proper brushing, flossing, and regular dental visits are crucial for long-term implant success.
  • Not a heavy smoker : Smoking can slow down healing and increase the risk of implant failure. While smokers may still be candidates, quitting or reducing smoking can improve outcomes.
  • Looking for a permanent solution : Unlike dentures or bridges, implants are designed to last for decades. If you’re seeking a long-term, hassle-free solution to missing teeth, implants may be the right choice.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

What are dental implants, and how do they work?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. Once healed, they support crowns, bridges, or dentures, offering a long-term solution that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, implants can last 20 years or more—many last a lifetime. The crown or restoration on top may need replacement every 10–15 years, depending on wear and hygiene habits.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

You may be a candidate if you have healthy gums, adequate jawbone density, and no uncontrolled medical conditions. Eckland Family Dentistry will assess your oral health and bone structure through X-rays or 3D scans before recommending treatment.

What is the recovery time for dental implant surgery?

Initial healing takes 1–2 weeks, but full osseointegration (when the implant fuses with bone) takes 3–6 months. After this phase, the final crown or prosthetic is attached for a permanent restoration.

Are dental implants better than bridges or dentures?

Implants offer superior stability, prevent jawbone loss, and preserve adjacent teeth—unlike bridges, which may require shaving down healthy teeth. They also outperform removable dentures in comfort, function, and longevity.
