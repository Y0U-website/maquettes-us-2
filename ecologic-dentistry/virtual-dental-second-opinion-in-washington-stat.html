Do I need to be in Washington during my appointment?

Yes. You must be physically inside Washington while the appointment happens. You do not need to live here. This comes from dental licensure rules rather than practice preference. Dentists must be licensed where the patient receives care, according to the ADA policy on teledentistry . A patient visiting family in Spokane qualifies.
