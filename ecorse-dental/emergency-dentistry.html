Simple steps that help before you arrive

  • 1 Rinse gently with warm salt water to keep the area clean.
  • 2 Use a cold compress on the outside of the cheek for swelling.
  • 3 Take over-the-counter pain relief as directed on the label.
  • 4 Avoid very hot, cold, hard or sugary foods on that side.
  • 5 Never place aspirin directly against the gum — it can burn the tissue.

Life-threatening symptoms

If you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding, or facial swelling spreading toward the eye or neck, go to the nearest emergency room or call 911 immediately. Otherwise, call our office and we&#x27;ll help you right away.
