All About Dental Implants

If you have a broken or missing tooth, or if you are missing multiple teeth, in most cases we can add new teeth by placing dental implants and crowns. For more details and to find out what treatment option is best for you, please contact us for a dental implant consultation.

Same Day Dental Implants Bone Graft Extraction Implant Dentist Westchester Scarsdale NY

Dental implants are the most advanced treatment in dentistry to replace the roots of broken or missing teeth, and they provide long-term predictable results.

If broken teeth cannot be saved, and the only option is to remove teeth from your mouth, dental implants can add new teeth to your mouth by inserting titanium screws into your jaw bone and placing crowns on top of the screws.

Several dental implant treatment options are available at Edgecare Dental.

For a missing tooth space, we can place a dental implant, and about two to four months later we can put a crown on top of the implant. If you have a broken tooth that requires an extraction, with complicated infection or bone defects, we will recommend waiting an additional two to six months for your bones to heal before placing a dental implant.

Immediate implants are placed on the same day extractions of teeth are done.

This treatment option is recommended in many cases to get better looking gum results, and this will save two to four months of additional time required for bone healing after the extraction of teeth. Immediate implants can be done as long as you do not have significant infection or bone defects under your gums. We can place crowns usually three to four months after an immediate implant placement.

(Extractions, Implants with temporary teeth in one visit)

In some "limited" circumstances, we can place a dental implant and add a temporary tooth on the same day. This treatment option is for someone who needs a tooth as soon as possible, usually to replace a broken or missing front tooth. However, applying a chewing force directly by placing a tooth on the same day of implant surgery can potentially stress implants and bones, which can compromise the healing. Thus, same day tooth replacement can be done, but it is dependent on multiple conditions that need to be determined at the time of consult and surgery.

Affordable Dental Implants vs. Premium Dental Implants

We believe more people should have the benefits of dental implants; sometimes, high treatment costs can delay treatment, but missing teeth can only do harm to your mouth.

At Edgecare Dental, we have two implant pricing options for affordable and popular US-made dental implants and slightly more expensive premium-brand dental implants. We offer the same quality surgery for both, and all dental implants we use have conical connection designs which provides better long-term treatment results.
