Dental Implants in Montclair, NJ

Single Tooth Implant A single tooth implant is cost-effective to replace a missing tooth. It is a superior alternative to a tooth supported bridge that requires cutting the adjacent teeth. A dental implant, by contrast, replaces an entire tooth from the root up. Because it is permanent, you care for it like your natural teeth.

Multiple Teeth Implant You can get replacement teeth for multiple lost teeth with several single implants or opt for implant dentures, which replace several teeth in one complete prosthetic that attaches to one or more implants.

All Teeth on Implants (All-On-X Implants) A full arch bridge can be designed and attached to between four and six implants spaced around the jawbone for anyone with a complete row of upper or lower teeth. The full arch bridge can be permanently attached requiring minimal alteration over the life of the bridge.

Full Mouth Implants Full mouth reconstruction is the most extensive form of dental implants, involving replacing all the teeth, both top and bottom. This treatment is usually life-changing for individuals with serious facial trauma or multiple damaged teeth.

Dental Implant Surgery in Montclair The dental implant process is simple. After consultation and diagnosis with Dr. Davidson, we prioritize your goals with a treatment plan that works for you. We then place the implants, allow time for the bone to fuse, and fit your permanent replacement teeth.

Cost Of Dental Implants in Montclair The cost of dental implants varies depending on how many teeth need replacing, their location, and whether bone grafting is needed. Dental implants preserve bone structure and let you chew more comfortably, making them well worth the cost.
