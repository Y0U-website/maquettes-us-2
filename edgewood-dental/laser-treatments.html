Laser Treatments

Edgewood Dental now offers several new, non-invasive laser treatments. NightLase™, SmoothLase™ and LipLase™.

Schedule your laser APPOINTMENT at edgewood dental

Nightlase™ laser snoring treatment

Nightlase™ treatment is a fast, non-invasive and friendly way of improving your quality of sleep by increasing your air intake and reducing snoring. The procedure is comfortable and you can resume your daily routine immediately afterwards.

The Nightlase™ treatments work by applying a laser to the soft tissues of the throat, tightening and heating them so that they remain open while you sleep and allow you to breathe easily. Your laser treatment will require 3-5 monthly sessions for you to receive the full benefits. Yearly maintenance sessions are recommended for lasting results.

Smoothlase™ Anti-aging facial rejuvenation

• Reduce the Appearance of Fine Lines and Wrinkles

Smoothlase™ therapy can treat aged skin with mild to moderate wrinkles, smoothing out the texture of your skin for a more youthful and energized appearance. Smoothlase™ tightens the skin in and around your mouth by applying a laser to heat and tighten the soft tissues of the cheeks and lips (treated from within your mouth). Your laser treatment will include 3 sessions, over a period of 2-3 months. Yearly maintenance sessions are recommended for lasting results.

liplase™ laser all natural lip-plumping

LipLase™ is a procedure designed to enhance the appearance of your smile by giving your lips a fuller look. LipLase™ laser treatment is capable of encouraging the collagen growth in your lips resulting in a fuller and smoother appearance. The procedure is non-invasive (treated from within your mouth) and involves a quick treatment process that usually only takes 20 minutes. Once your treatment is completed, we recommend that you return for at least 2-3 more sessions to achieve a longer-lasting result.
