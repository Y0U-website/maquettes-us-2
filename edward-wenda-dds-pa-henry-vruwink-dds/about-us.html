About Our Family Dental Practice

The dental office of Dr. Wenda DDS PA and Henry Vruwink DDS was started in 1972 by Dr. Vruwink and has served the community for 46 years. Dr. Wenda worked as Dr. Vruwink’s associate from 2001 to 2004 and has returned to Asheboro to partner with Dr. Vruwink. Over the years the office has continuously been updated with the latest equipment and technology. Drs. Vruwink and Wenda have over 60 combined years of experience ready to serve you with care and compassion.
