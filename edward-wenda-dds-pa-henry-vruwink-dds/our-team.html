Our Doctors and Dental Staff

Dr. Edward Wenda

Dr. Wenda was born and raised in Raleigh, NC. He attended UNC Chapel Hill for his undergraduate studies, graduating in 1996. He then studied dentistry at UNC School of Dentistry at Chapel Hill, earning his DDS degree in 2000.

Following dental school, Dr. Wenda came to work with Dr. Vruwink from 2001 to 2004 before returning to open his own practice in Raleigh. Dr. Wenda sold his dental practice in Raleigh after 10 years and opened a craft brewery in Raleigh. With the birth of twins, Dr. Wenda decided to leave the craft beer business and return to dentistry, for which he has a renewed passion.

Dr. Wenda practiced and lived in Atlanta for 2 years before returning to Asheboro to partner with Dr. Vruwink.

Dr. Wenda prides himself in taking the best possible care of his patients. He enjoys learning about new techniques and technologies in dentistry. He is a member of the ADA, North Carolina Dental Society and Randolph County Dental Society.

Dr. Wenda and his wife, Susan, have 4 children: Hannah, Tyler, and twins Harper and Jeffrey. In his spare time, Dr. Wenda enjoys spending time with his family, boating, and playing and watching sports.

Susan Wenda

Susan Wenda was born and raised in the Asheboro area. She attended UNC Greensboro and after working in her family business she entered the medical field, working for Randolph Medical Associates. She then went to work with Dr. Vruwink and her husband, Dr. Wenda at Dr. Vruwink’s practice in 1999.

She and Dr. Wenda opened a dental practice in Raleigh followed by a craft brewery ten years later. After living and working in Atlanta for the last 2 years, she is excited to return to her home town of Asheboro with her husband, who is partnering with Dr. Vruwink.

Susan prides herself on communicating with patients regarding their treatment and helping patients overcome financial hurdles that may keep them from getting the treatment they need.

Susan and Dr. Wenda have four children: Hannah 21, Tyler 6, and twins Harper and Jeffrey 3. She enjoys spending time with her family, boating and reading.
