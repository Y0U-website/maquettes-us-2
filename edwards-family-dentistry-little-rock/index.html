The practice

Edwards Family Dentistry proudly provides a comprehensive range of dental services designed to meet the diverse needs of patients of all ages. Our commitment to exceptional oral health care ensures that we cater to everything from routine check-ups and cleanings to more specialized treatments, allowing every member of your family to achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile.

General Dentistry Our team is committed to using the latest technology and techniques to provide high-quality general dentistry services, from routine cleanings to more complex procedures. Our goal is to not only enhance your oral health but also foster a trusting and friendly environment.

Dental Services for Children We enjoy caring for patients of all ages, including children. We encourage you to bring your child in every six months for a regular dental exam and cleaning. Sleep Dentistry IV Sedation At Edwards Family Dentistry, we understand that visiting the dentist can be a source of anxiety for many individuals. That’s why we offer Sleep Dentistry, also known as IV Sedation, to ensure a comfortable and stress-free experience for our patients.

Both of our doctors are proud members of the American Dental Association and the Arkansas State Dental Association, showcasing their commitment to upholding the highest standards in dentistry. With a focus on patient-centered care, our team is dedicated to ensuring that each visit is comfortable and tailored to meet your individual needs.
