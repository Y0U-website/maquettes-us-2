Dental Hygiene

At the heart of proper oral health is preventive care. Our dentists and hygienists promote oral well-being through proactive, preventive dentistry and patient education. Our goal is to motivate and assist you in increasing the longevity of your smile.

Our doctors and their hygiene team provide examinations, using digital radiographs and proper clinical testing. We evaluate for dental conditions such as gingivitis, periodontitis, bruxism, and TMJ disorders. These conditions have direct influence on diabetes, heart disease, chronic headaches, and stroke. We also provide oral cancer screenings for all of our patients.

Your dentist and hygienist will not only evaluate and treat your dental hygiene needs, but they will teach you the best ways to manage your oral care at home. Proper dental care is a team effort between you and your dental providers. Marion Dental Group is here for you.
