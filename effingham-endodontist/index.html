Root Canal Retreatment Occasionally, a tooth that has undergone root canal treatment may not heal as expected or may develop new problems months or even years later. When this happens, root canal retreatment can help save the tooth.

At Effingham Endodontist, our specialists follow the standards of the American Association of Endodontists (AAE) to deliver exceptional care. Retreatment involves carefully reopening the tooth, removing the previous filling materials, and thoroughly cleaning the root canals to eliminate infection or address complications.
