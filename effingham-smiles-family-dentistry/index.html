The practice

Your smile isn’t just a smile- it’s the first thing people notice about you. A healthy, beautiful smile benefits you physically and mentally. It is key to both looking and feeling great. That is why our Dentists and the entire Effingham Smiles Family Dentistry team are dedicated to seeing that you achieve the smile you desire. Your overall oral health is our focus, and when you become a patient…

Whether you need a routine cleaning, crown, or filling, our staff will meet your general dentistry needs with quality, comfort-centric service.

Effingham Smiles provides comprehensive dental surgery treatments to restore missing or damaged teeth all in one convenient location.

Dental implants are the superior choice for restoring the beauty and functionality of your smile.

Ceramic crowns are special high-quality crowns used for cosmetic enhancement.

Invisalign ® treatment is the clear alternative to metal braces for kids, teens, and adults.

Dentures, either removable or fixed, are a tooth replacement option for patients missing one or more teeth.

Our doctors and staff are dedicated to providing you with a pleasant visit and results that you’re proud to show off. Our administrative staff is ready to help you with questions about scheduling, financial policy and insurance, to make that part of the process as simple as possible.
