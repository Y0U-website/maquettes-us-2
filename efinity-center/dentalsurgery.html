Dental Surgery

A dental surgery consultation is your first visit to talk about a possible surgical procedure. We’ll review your health history, examine your mouth, and take X-rays or 3D scans (Additional Fee for CBCT) if needed. The dentist will explain what treatment is recommended, review anesthesia options, and answer your questions. By the end of the visit, you’ll understand your options and have a clear plan moving forward.

Add to cart Biopsy Consultation $100.00 A biopsy consultation is a visit to explain why a biopsy is needed. We’ll review your health history, medications, and allergies, and explain how the procedure will be done. You’ll learn what to expect, any risks involved, and how to prepare before your appointment. We’ll also answer your questions so you feel informed and comfortable moving forward.

Add to cart Full Mouth Rehab Consultation (All-On-X or Overdenture) $100.00 This visit is to see if you’re a candidate for replacing a full set of teeth using just 4–6 dental implants (per arch). We’ll examine your mouth and take 3D scans (CBCT fee not included with consultation) to plan where the implants should go. The dentist will explain the process, including temporary teeth and a permanent fixed smile. By the end of the visit, you’ll understand your options and have a clear plan to restore your smile and confidence.

Add to cart Tongue or Lip Tie Release Consultation (Frenectomy) $100.00 This visit checks for a tongue or lip tie that may affect feeding, speech, or comfort. The dentist will examine the area and talk with you about any symptoms or concerns. If a release is needed, we’ll explain the simple procedure and what to expect. You’ll also receive aftercare instructions and exercises to help with healing and proper movement.

Add to cart Sedation Consultation $100.00 A sedation consultation is a visit where we talk about ways to help you relax during dental treatment. We’ll review your health history and discuss different sedation options, such as laughing gas, oral medication, or IV sedation. The dentist will explain what each option feels like and help choose the safest and most comfortable choice for you.
