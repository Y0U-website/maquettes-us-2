EMFACE

EMFACE® Consultation – All Natural Facial Re-contouring

Our EMFACE® consultation is designed to help you achieve a more lifted, refreshed look—without needles or downtime. During your visit, we’ll assess your facial structure and goals to create a personalized treatment plan using EMFACE® technology, which works by gently stimulating key facial muscles while also improving skin quality.

EMFACE® can help recontour and lift areas such as the forehead, eyes, cheeks, and submental (under-chin) area by strengthening and toning the underlying muscles that naturally support your face.

This consultation is relaxed, informative, and tailored to you—so you can feel confident, informed, and excited about your treatment options and expected results

For optimal results, EXION® should be used in conjunction with EMFACE®, allowing us to address both skin quality and underlying facial support for a more balanced, refreshed appearance. Our consultation is patient-focused, informative, and tailored just for you—so you know exactly what to expect every step of the way.

Smile Esthetics Services

  • Gummy Smile Treatment Gummy smile surgery is a minimally invasive procedure designed to rebalance the proportions between your teeth and gums. Through crown lengthening or gingivectomy, excess gum tissue is carefully reshaped to reveal more of the natural tooth structure. The result is a more harmonious, confident smile with improved symmetry and esthetic balance.
  • Veneers and Crowns Dental veneers are ultra-thin, custom-crafted porcelain shells bonded to the front of the teeth to enhance shape, color, and alignment. They are ideal for correcting chips, discoloration, gaps, and mild misalignment. Veneers create a natural, radiant smile while maintaining a conservative approach to tooth structure.
  • Teeth Whitening Professional tooth whitening safely and effectively brightens your smile by lifting deep-set stains and discoloration. Our customized approach delivers noticeably whiter teeth while protecting enamel and minimizing sensitivity. The result is a refreshed, youthful smile with long-lasting brilliance.
  • Smile Esthetics Consultation The Smithe Esthetics Consultation is a personalized assessment focused on your unique facial features, smile goals, and esthetic concerns. We take time to understand how your smile fits within your overall facial appearance. This consultation allows us to create a tailored treatment plan aligned with natural beauty and facial harmony.
  • Comprehensive Smile Design and Assessment This in-depth smile design consultation evaluates your teeth, gums, lips, and facial proportions as a complete esthetic system. Using advanced diagnostic tools and esthetic principles, we analyze balance, symmetry, and harmony within the face. The goal is to design a smile that enhances not only your teeth, but your overall facial appearance.
  • Clear Aligner Consultation Our clear aligner consultation assesses tooth alignment, bite function, and esthetic goals to determine suitability for orthodontic treatment. We collaborate closely with our sister office, where the aligner treatment is expertly delivered. This seamless partnership ensures coordinated care and optimal esthetic results.
  • Full-mouth Rehabilitation Full mouth restoration is a comprehensive approach to rebuilding function, comfort, and esthetics for patients with complex dental needs. This treatment addresses worn, damaged, or missing teeth while restoring proper bite and facial support. Care is provided through our sister office, ensuring advanced rehabilitation with a focus on long-term health and esthetic excellence.

Whether you have a question about your skin, or you just want an introduction to how our technology can rejuvenate your skin and help you look younger, feel free to reach out—we’re here to help.

  • What is esthetics? It’s the practice of improving the esthetics of your smile and your skins health and appearance (as well as the underlying muscle) through treatments like facials, facial contouring, peels, waxing, focusing on rejuvenation and maintenance.
  • How often should I get treatments? Depending on the treatment type, maintenance appointments can range from a few weeks to over 6 months
  • What to bring/tell your esthetician? Photos of current products, any allergies, medications (like Accutane/Retin-A which are contraindications), and your skincare goals.
  • What’s my skin type (oily/dry/combo)? A professional analysis reveals true needs; you might be dehydrated (lacks water) not dry (lacks oil), requiring different care like exfoliation for dry vs. hydration for dehydrated skin.
