Myofunctional Therapy Myofunctional therapy uses simple exercises to strengthen the muscles of the mouth and face. These exercises help improve breathing, swallowing, chewing, and speaking. Therapy focuses on proper tongue position, lip closure, and nasal breathing. It’s a natural, non-invasive approach that can help with issues like mouth breathing, jaw discomfort, sleep problems, and speech concerns.
  • Temporomandibular Joint Therapy (TMJ) TMJ assessment and treatment focuses on finding the cause of jaw pain, headaches, or clicking. We’ll talk about your symptoms and examine how your jaw moves, how your teeth fit together, and if your jaw muscles are sore. X-rays or other imaging may be taken if needed. Based on what we find, we’ll explain the cause of your discomfort and recommend treatment options to help relieve pain and improve jaw function. We will offer a comprehensive treatment plan to address your TMJ concerns and will explore the utilization of EMFACE for TMJ treatment.
  • Airway/Sleep Therapy This visit focuses on identifying issues that affect your breathing and sleep. We’ll talk about your symptoms, examine your mouth, jaw, and airway, and may take 3D images if needed. Based on what we find, we’ll explain possible causes of snoring or sleep problems and recommend treatment options to help you breathe better and sleep more comfortably.
  • Solea Laser Snoring Treatment Reduce snoring without surgery or downtime. Solea® Laser Snoring Treatment uses advanced CO₂ laser technology to gently tighten and strengthen the soft tissues of the palate, helping keep the airway more open during sleep. The procedure is quick, comfortable, and non-invasive, allowing most patients to return to normal activities immediately. Enjoy quieter nights, improved sleep quality, and greater comfort for you and your partner.

Myofunctional therapy is a guided program that focuses on retraining the muscles of the tongue, lips, face, and jaw to improve proper function and posture. Through personalized exercises and habit correction, it helps address issues such as mouth breathing, improper tongue position, swallowing patterns, and muscle imbalances. This therapy supports healthy breathing, facial development, orthodontic stability, and overall oral wellness.
