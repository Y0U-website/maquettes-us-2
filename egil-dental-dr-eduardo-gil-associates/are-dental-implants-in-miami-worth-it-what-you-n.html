What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are permanent, artificial teeth that mimic the function and appearance of natural teeth. They consist of three parts:

Dental implants help preserve your jawbone, improve bite function, and restore your smile. They’re a long-lasting solution for individuals who have lost teeth due to injury, decay, or gum disease.

  • The implant body (usually titanium or zirconia), which fuses with your jawbone
  • The abutment, which connects the implant to the crown
  • The crown, the visible part of your new tooth

How Much Do Dental Implants Cost in Miami?

The typical cost of a single dental implant in Miami ranges from $3,000 to $7,000. This includes the surgical placement of the implant, the abutment, and the crown. Several factors influence this price:

If you’re looking for affordable dental implants in Miami, EGil Dental offers flexible financing options and clear, upfront pricing.

  • Geographic Location : Urban centers like Miami tend to have higher fees
  • Dentist’s Experience : Specialists or surgeons may charge more
  • Number of Implants : Multiple implants or full-mouth restorations cost significantly more
  • Material : Titanium is standard, while zirconia (a newer ceramic material) may add several hundred dollars
  • Additional Procedures : Bone grafting ($600+), sinus lifts ($1,500–$2,500), or extractions can increase your cost
