Dental Implants at EGil Dental: The Gold Standard for Replacing Missing Teeth

Missing a tooth isn’t just about appearance; it’s also about your oral health and function. At EGil Dental, we pride ourselves on providing top-notch dental implants, ensuring you regain not only your radiant smile but also your chewing efficiency and confidence. Delve into the world of dental implants with us and learn why it’s the preferred choice for tooth replacement.

Why Dental Implants?

Dental implants have surpassed other tooth replacement methods because of their longevity, functionality, and appearance. They closely replicate the natural tooth root, providing a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture.

Benefits of Dental Implants:

  • Natural Look & Feel: Dental implants are designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, ensuring that no one can tell the difference.
  • Preservation of Jaw Bone: They prevent bone loss that typically occurs beneath missing teeth.
  • Improved Functionality: Enjoy your favorite foods without worry.
  • No Impact on Adjacent Teeth: Unlike bridges, there’s no need to grind down neighboring teeth.

The Dental Implant Process

The procedure to place dental implants is a collaboration between you, the dentist, and the dental lab. At EGil Dental, we make sure the process is smooth and comfortable from the initial consultation to the final placement.

Steps in the Dental Implant Journey:

  • Consultation: A detailed assessment to ensure you’re a suitable candidate for implants.
  • Planning: Advanced imaging to ascertain the best implant placement spots.
  • Implant Placement: A minor surgery to position the implant in the jawbone.
  • Osseointegration: A healing period where the implant fuses with the bone.
  • Final Restoration: Fitting of the crown, bridge, or denture to the implant.

Post-Procedure Care & Maintenance

Dental implants have a high success rate, but their longevity depends on proper aftercare.

  • Regular Oral Hygiene: Treat your implant as you would a natural tooth.
  • Regular Dental Checkups: Allows timely identification of potential issues.
  • Avoid Excessive Pressure: Avoid biting down on hard objects.

Are You A Candidate for Dental Implants?

FAQs about Dental Implants

  • Is the procedure painful? With modern techniques and anesthesia, discomfort is minimal.
  • How long do implants last? With proper care, they can last a lifetime.
  • Can implants get cavities? No, but the surrounding tissue can still be affected by gum disease.
