Meet Dr. Ehsan Rezvan

Dr. Rezvan is married to his lovely wife, Elizabeth, and together they have three beautiful children.

On a personal note, I have been privileged and honored to be serving the communities of Gilroy, Morgan Hill and Monterey county for the past 14+ years. My staff and I strive for the best possible results under the most comfortable settings and only use the most extensively researched materials for all procedures performed at our office.

  • Santa Clara Society Advanced Dental Study Club: Multi-disciplinary approach to treatment planning. It meets 6 times a year and moderated by Dr. Curtis Jansen and Dr. Rezvan.
  • Dental Hygiene Study Club: Designed for dentists and dental hygienists. It meets in May and November of each year and the lectures are given by Dr. Rezvan.
