In-House Dental Implant Lab in Sewell, NJ

Custom zirconia teeth designed, crafted, and refined under one roof.

The practice

At Sewell Dental Arts, our in-house lab is part of your treatment, not a separate step. Dr. Pastiu personally guides the design and creation of implant restorations so your new teeth fit comfortably, function properly, and look natural.

That is why Dr. Pastiu plans with the final result in mind from the beginning. The shape, size, shade, bite, and position of your new teeth all matter. By combining restorative dentistry with an in-house lab process, Sewell Dental Arts can create restorations that are customized to the patient, not pulled from a standard template.

Your smile should not look manufactured. It should look like it belongs to you.

Our lab process combines advanced technology with hands-on artistry. Each restoration, from implant crown restorations to larger implant bridges, is carefully planned, designed, shaped, and refined to support comfort, strength, and appearance.

We begin by evaluating your mouth, bite, bone structure, and restorative needs. Digital tools help us plan the restoration with accuracy and visualize how your new teeth should fit and function.

Dr. Pastiu personally reviews the design of your teeth, including shape, proportion, smile line, and bite alignment. This allows the final restoration to be created with both function and appearance in mind.

Our in-house lab gives us the ability to create, test, and refine restorations with greater control. When adjustments are needed, they can often be handled directly in our office instead of being sent back and forth to an outside lab.

For permanent implant restorations, Sewell Dental Arts uses zirconia because of its strength, durability, biocompatibility, and natural appearance. Each restoration is shaped and refined to create a result that looks clean, balanced, and lifelike.

Les soins

Dental implant treatment is not just about placing implants. It is about creating teeth that feel stable, look natural, and support your quality of life for years to come.
