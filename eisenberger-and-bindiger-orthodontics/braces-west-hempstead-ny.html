Braces for Every Age: Kids, Teens Adults

Orthodontic issues can affect people of all ages. Our braces treatments in West Hempstead are suitable for children, teenagers, and adults alike.

Braces for Kids

Early orthodontic intervention can make a significant difference in long-term oral health. We typically begin evaluations around age 7, allowing us to identify and correct problems like crowding, crossbites, and jaw development issues early on.

Benefits of early braces treatment include:

  • Correcting harmful oral habits
  • Guiding jaw and facial growth
  • Creating space for incoming teeth
  • Reducing the need for future extractions

Braces for Teens

The teenage years are a common time for orthodontic care, as most permanent teeth have erupted. Our braces for teens help address misalignment, overbites, underbites, and spacing issues with solutions that fit into busy lifestyles.

Why teens (and parents) love our braces:

  • Modern options like ceramic and self-ligating braces
  • Customized treatment plans for sports, school, and social activities
  • Flexible scheduling and follow-ups

Braces for Adults

Orthodontic treatment isn’t just for kids and teens. More adults than ever are choosing braces to improve their smiles and correct long-standing alignment issues. Whether for cosmetic reasons or to support better oral health, we offer braces solutions that fit the adult lifestyle.

Regardless of your age, a straighter smile is always within reach at Eisenberger & Bindiger Orthodontics.

  • Discreet ceramic or lingual options
