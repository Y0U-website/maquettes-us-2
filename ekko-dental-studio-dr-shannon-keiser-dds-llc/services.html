Dental Implant Restoration

The dentists at Ekko Dentist Studio are experienced in restorations with dental implants. The feeling of missing a tooth can be embarrassing or you may be struggling with challenges when eating or speaking. A dental implant is an amazing solution using modern technology to create a synthetic tooth that looks, feels, and works just like the real thing!

Improving innovations in dentistry allow us to provide dental implants to most patients, but not every patient is a candidate for dental implant restoration. Schedule an appointment for a comprehensive exam to learn more about your options and how easy it is to revitalize your smile with a dental implant!

Same-Day Dental Crowns

When your tooth is damaged beyond general decay, you may need a dental crown. These custom-fit caps are placed over your damaged tooth to provide protection and restore your bite as if your tooth were in perfect condition.

Using our CAD/CAM technology, we can provide same-day crowns for most patients, limiting the number of necessary visits for your dental crown.

Veneers

Dreaming of that picture-perfect smile? Porcelain veneers are a popular option to enhance your teeth to achieve a flawless look. Almost any cosmetic issue can be treated with veneers including small gaps, chipped teeth, discoloration, crooked positioning and general wear.

Veneers look just like your real teeth and are color-matched to blend in! The process includes an examination, preparation, taking impressions, and then creating the veneers in our dental lab. While you wait for your fully-custom veneers, we provide you with temporary veneers too!

Dentures

Teeth are vital to your everyday life. Everything from eating to speaking to even maintaining healthy bone structure in your mouth relies on teeth! When your natural teeth have been lost due to wear, decay or any other reason, dentures are a complete solution to restore your mouth’s full function.

Whether you need partial dentures or a full-mouth set, the dentists at Ekko Dental Studio can guide you through the multi-step process. We’ll discuss your dental health, your needs, and the steps required to design you a custom-fit set of dentures that will feel as natural as possible!

Root Canals

When the root of your tooth infected with bacteria, it can often be saved through root canal treatment. Despite the myths, root canals are fairly common and far less painful than you might have been lead to believe! Improving dental technology has come a long way to minimize discomfort and enhance the success of root canal treatment!

Root canal treatment involves removing the infected pulp, cleaning the area, and then filling the tooth.

For some patients, root canals can be done in a single visit. However, depending on the severity of the infection, you may need two or more visits to ensure your tooth’s health is fully restored!
