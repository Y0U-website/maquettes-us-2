Frequently Asked Questions

What is All-on-X?

All-on-X is a dental implant technique that supports a full arch of replacement teeth using four to six implants. It provides a permanent, fixed alternative to removable dentures. The &#x27;X&#x27; refers to the number of implants used — commonly 4 or 6 — depending on your specific needs.

How much do All-on-X implants cost in El Cajon?

All-on-X costs vary based on the number of implants, materials used, and complexity of your case. We offer free consultations to provide personalized estimates and discuss financing options including CareCredit and flexible payment plans.

Am I a candidate for All-on-X?

Most patients who are missing all or most teeth in an arch are candidates for All-on-X. Even patients who have been told they don&#x27;t have enough bone for traditional implants may be candidates, as the angled implant technique often eliminates the need for bone grafting. A comprehensive evaluation will determine your eligibility.

How long does the All-on-X procedure take?

The implant placement surgery typically takes a few hours per arch. Many patients receive temporary teeth the same day. The permanent prosthesis is placed after 3–6 months once the implants have fully integrated with the jawbone.
