How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment length varies based on the complexity of your case. Most patients complete orthodontic treatment in 12–24 months. Minor corrections may take less time. We will give you a personalized timeline during your consultation.

Do you offer orthodontics for kids in El Cajon?

Yes, we provide orthodontic treatment for children, teens, and adults. Early orthodontic evaluations are recommended around age 7. Starting treatment at the right time can simplify and shorten treatment in the long run.
