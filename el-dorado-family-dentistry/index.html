The practice

Our team loves getting to know our patients by name and make you feel like a friend, not just another mouth. We pride ourselves in providing our patients with not only top-quality dentistry but with an overall comforting dental experience.

From the moment you walk in the door, you’ll be welcomed by a caring staff in a relaxed, stress-free environment where the whole family can feel at ease.

Our team consists of dedicated individuals who are kind, friendly, and caring. Our staff is here to ensure a pleasant experience during your visit to our office.

“My philosophy is to provide treatment to the whole person with a special focus on how your teeth and gums relate to your total body health. I want you to be confident that your smile achieves your goals for comfort, function, and appearance.

The entire team at El Dorado Family Dentistry approaches oral and dental health as an integral part of your general health. Our mission is to provide the highest level of comprehensive, evidence-based, oral health care based exclusively on current scientific research.

We accept most PPO plans. Please contact our office to verify acceptance of your plan. We accept check, cash, and credit cards (Visa and MasterCard).
