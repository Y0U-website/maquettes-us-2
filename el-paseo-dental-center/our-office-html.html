As a dentist in Rancho Santa Margarita, Dr. Andrew Lee is experienced in all phases of dentistry with an emphasis on saving natural teeth.

Dr. Lee will kindly go over each treatment option that fits your needs and will explain in detail the benefits of each option to create that perfect smile while keeping your teeth and gums healthy.

Dr. Lee will help you choose the best course of treatment based on your goal and budget whether it is a simple chipped tooth or a full mouth smile makeover.
