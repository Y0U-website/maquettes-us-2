Services

dental crowns & bridge

A dental crown is a dental restoration that is placed over a severely damaged tooth when a filling is not sufficient to restore the proper form and function of the tooth. A custom-fitted crown is a great way to improve the quality of your overall dental health and dental hygiene as well as preserve your tooth’s durability for many years.

invisible braces & TMJ

Invisible clear aligner such as ClearCorrect is a great way to straighten your teeth without wires and brackets of traditional braces. Unlike traditional braces, clear aligners are nearly invisible and can be easily removed. It is easier for you to brush and floss your teeth helping you keep your gum healthier.

A correctly designed and custom fitted acrylic mouthguard minimizes the abrasive action of tooth surfaces during sleep, which prevents damage to teeth and to the temporomandibular joint (TMJ).

cosmetic treatments

Cosmetic treatments include teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, bondings, and tooth colored fillings. The best type of cosmetic treatment for you depends on the results you want to achieve and the budget. Dr. Lee will take the time to discuss with you about pros and cons of each treatment option so that you can choose the option that will best suit your needs.

dental implants

Dental Implants offer an ideal combination of an exceptionally natural look, durability and function as well as provide a healthier, and more advanced alternative to conventional bridges and dentures. Dental implants also provide excellent support and stability for dentures and partial dentures.

root canal therapy

Root canal therapy is a treatment used to save and repair a badly decayed or damaged tooth. Root canal therapy is a safe procedure meant to ensure that your natural tooth is not extracted while eliminating painful infections.

periodontal treatment

Gum disease treatment options depend on the severity of the individual case. Both surgical and nonsurgical treatment options are available, including deep scaling and pocket elimination surgery.
