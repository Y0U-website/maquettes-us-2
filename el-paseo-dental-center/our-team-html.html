about us

Our office is a small private practice with only one dentist, Dr. Andrew Lee, who treats each patient personally in a gentle and friendly way.

Dr. Andrew Lee works with only one patient at a time. When you schedule an appointment in our office, the time reserved is yours and yours alone.

You will love our warm and welcoming atmosphere where you can relax and receive the care you need.

our team

ANDREW LEE, D.D.S.

Dentist

Dr. Andrew Lee grew up in San Jose, Northern California and moved to Southern California for his college education at UCLA in 1993.

Dr. Lee received his B.S. degree in Chemical Engineering from UCLA School of Engineering in 1997 and received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from UCLA School of Dentistry in 2001.

He has been practicing Cosmetic & Implant Dentistry in Beverly Hills, CA since 2001 and established his practice in Rancho Santa Margarita, CA in 2005.

Dr. Lee is married to his college sweetheart and they have an amazing son. Dr. Lee enjoys spending time with his family and working in the garden. His recent backyard project was building a treehouse for his son.
