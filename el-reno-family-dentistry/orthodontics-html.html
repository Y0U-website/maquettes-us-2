How Does Invisalign Work?

Invisalign uses clear plastic aligners that are designed using SmartTrack technology. Known for their comfortability and predictable results, these trays are customized to fit correctly and follow a patient’s progress as they move through treatment.

Individuals are to wear them 20-22 hours each day so that gentle, continuous pressure is applied to shift teeth in the right direction. After 1-2 weeks, patients swap out the current set they are wearing for the next set in the series, as this will ensure that teeth keep moving as predicted.

The average timeframe for an individual’s treatment is usually between 12-18 months; however, this will vary based on the severity of a person’s case.
