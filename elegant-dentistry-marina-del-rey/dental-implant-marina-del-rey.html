A long-term solution for missing teeth

Unlike a bridge, an implant does not rely on neighboring teeth for support, and it helps preserve the jawbone that can shrink after a tooth is lost.

What Dental Implants Offer

Looks and Feels Natural A custom crown is shaded and shaped to match your surrounding teeth.

Preserves Bone Implants stimulate the jawbone the way a natural tooth root does, helping prevent bone loss.

Protects Neighboring Teeth Unlike a bridge, an implant does not require reshaping healthy adjacent teeth.

Built to Last With proper care, implants can last decades, often a lifetime.

Restores Full Function Bite and chew normally, without the limitations of dentures.

Implant Residency Training Our team includes implant residency training from UCLA, treated in-house from start to finish.

What to Expect at Your First Visit

1. Consultation and Planning

We evaluate your jawbone and gum health with imaging to confirm you are a good candidate.

2. Implant Placement

The titanium post is placed in the jawbone under local anesthesia, then given time to heal and fuse with the bone.

3. Crown Placement

Once healed, a custom crown is attached to complete your new tooth.

Dental Implant Questions

Am I a candidate for a dental implant? Most healthy adults with adequate jawbone are candidates. We confirm this with an exam and imaging before recommending an implant.

Are dental implants painful? The placement procedure is done under local anesthesia, and most patients report the recovery is more comfortable than they expected.

How long do dental implants last? With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, implants commonly last decades, often for the rest of your life.
