Danielle

Danielle has been a certified dental assistant since 2011, she has been part of our team since 2021 and has been a great addition. Danielle enjoys helping patients feel at ease when they come into our office, her extensive knowledge and calming personality help to comfort patients before, during and after their dental procedures. Danielle is also very proficient in our ITero scanner and in our invisible braces treatment, Invisalign.

Danielle lives in Wakefield with her husband and her dog Bentley. She enjoys spending time with her family and doing outdoor activities.
