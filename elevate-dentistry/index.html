Dental implants are replacement tooth roots and are a procedural option to replace missing teeth. Implants provide a strong foundation for fixed (permanent) or removable replacement teeth that are made to match your natural teeth. They can also be used for support of bridgework when adjacent teeth are not available to provide the appropriate support.
