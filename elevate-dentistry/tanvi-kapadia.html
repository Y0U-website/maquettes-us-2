Tanvi Kapadia, DDS

Dr. Tanvi Kapadia earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from New York University College of Dentistry in 2014. Her passion for both science and the arts led her to pursue a career in dentistry. While she is skilled in all aspects of general dentistry, she has a particular interest in Esthetic Dentistry. During her time at NYU, she completed an honors program in Esthetic Dentistry and took part in several community outreach initiatives throughout the New York area.

Before entering private practice, Dr. Kapadia gained experience in community health, offering dental care to a diverse range of patients, including children and those with high dental anxiety. She is dedicated to delivering high quality, patient-centered care and values building relationships with her patients.

Dr. Kapadia is an active member of the American Dental Association, Massachusetts Dental Association, Worcester District Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry. She keeps up with the latest advancements in dentistry by participating in various continuing education courses, including those offered by Spear Education and The Kois Center in Seattle.

Since 2017, Dr. Kapadia has made Massachusetts her home, where she lives with her husband and their two-year-old son. Outside of her professional life, she enjoys spending time in nature, traveling, and engaging in the arts.
