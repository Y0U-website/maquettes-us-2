Dental Implant Costs in Westfield, IN

Most patients ask about pricing pretty early, and that makes sense. Dental implants aren’t a small decision, and costs can vary quite a bit depending on what’s needed.

For a single implant, most cases in Westfield fall somewhere between $3,000 and $5,000. That usually includes the implant post, abutment, and crown, but not always, we go over that in detail during your visit.

Full-mouth cases are more complex. The final cost depends on things like bone support, number of implants, and whether extra procedures are needed first.
