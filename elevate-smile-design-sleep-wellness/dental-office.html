Our Secret to Pain Management

Our state-of-the-art technology plays a major part in us being able to provide our patients with dental care that is both efficient and comfortable. It’s also our secret to ensuring you experience dentistry comfortably when it comes to receiving injections. We use a high-tech device called the DentalVibe that blocks you from feeling pain sensation by emitting soothing vibrations to your gums during an injection. Since these vibration signals travel to the brain faster than pain signals do, most patients are unaware that they’ve just received a shot.

To enhance your comfort even further, we use a high-strength topical numbing gel and warm up the anesthetic solution to body temperature before using the DentalVibe. If a fear of pain or injections has stopped you from getting the care you need, we invite you to visit Elevate Smile Design to experience the difference of modern, stress-free dentistry.

COMMUNITY OUTREACH

At Elevate Smile Design, we believe in doing more than just taking care of smiles— we take care of our community as well. In addition to linking up with local organizations, schools and businesses, our team regularly participates in local outreach events and programs.

Currently, Dr. Perlman and Dr. Mai are working with local schools to provide free dental screenings to children who may otherwise never visit a dental office. They collaborate annually with Expanding Your Horizons Network, which teaches and encourages young women to pursue careers in science, technology, engineering, and math (S.T.E.M.). Dr. Mai also works with Union Gospel Mission Dental Clinic and Medical Teams International Mobile Dental Clinic to provide free dental care to the homeless and uninsured members of our community.
