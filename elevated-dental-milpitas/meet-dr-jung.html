Meet Milpitas Dentist Dr. Timothy Jung

Tim Jung, DDS, FACD

General Dentist in Milpitas

Dr. Tim Jung was born in Seoul, Korea, and raised in Southern California. He earned his Bachelor of Arts in Molecular and Cell Biology from the University of California, Berkeley, before continuing his dental education at the prestigious University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) School of Dentistry, where he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) degree.

Dr. Jung is a dedicated and compassionate general dentist who is passionate about providing comprehensive and personalized care. He believes in putting patients first, listening to their concerns, and tailoring treatment to meet their individual needs. His commitment to improving oral health helps his patients achieve beautiful and healthy smiles.

Dr. Jung is honored to be a Fellow of the American College of Dentists (FACD), an esteemed organization that recognizes outstanding leadership, service, and contributions to the dental profession. This prestigious fellowship is awarded only to dentists who have demonstrated excellence and a dedication to advancing both the practice of dentistry and the well-being of society.

In his spare time, Dr. Jung enjoys exploring the vibrant local scene, particularly seeking out new places to enjoy delicious food and drinks. Ask him for recommendations on where to grab a great brunch or find the best dessert in town!
