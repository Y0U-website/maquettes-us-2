Get To Know

Dr. Andrew Peterson, DMD, MS

Andrew Peterson, DMD, MS, is a periodontal surgeon. He received his Bachelor of Arts in Biology from Carroll College in Helena, Montana, in 2009. Subsequently, he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery and Master of Science from Oregon Health & Science University in Portland in 2013 and 2017, respectively.

He practiced in the greater Portland area until 2019, at which time he accepted an adjunct teaching position at Loma Linda University in the School of Graduate Periodontics.

Get To Know

Dr. Jaycee Lim, DMD

Dr. Jaycee Lim is a Southern California native; born and raised in the South Bay, attended UCLA for his undergraduate studies, and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine Degree from Western University of Health Sciences in Pomona, California. Dr. Lim holds a strong compassion for dentistry, always learning and engaged in the art of dentistry.

Being able to assess all types of patients and treatment, Dr. Lim’s skillset includes cosmetic dentistry, surgical dentistry, Invisalign dentistry, and implant dentistry.

A member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, and Orange County Dental Society ; Dr. Lim is involved and up to date in the world of dentistry.

Outside of the office Dr. Lim spends time with his lovely fiancé and dog, traveling and trying out the all the food spots. Furthermore Dr. Lim enjoys drinking wine, watching movies, and playing golf whenever he can.
