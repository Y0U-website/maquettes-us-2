  • Placement Dr. Amini uses a surgical guide to place a small metal post (the implant) in place of your missing tooth root. Over time, this implant fuses with your jawbone to create a strong and solid foundation for your restoration.
  • Restoration We then attach an abutment (connector) onto the implant along with your tooth replacement. This replacement depends on your needs and may be a crown, bridge, or denture. Enjoy a full smile that looks and feels all-natural.
  • Aftercare You’ll receive at-home care instructions once we complete your treatment. Good oral hygiene and eating habits, alongside regular dental visits, will aid in the life of your new implant and overall dental health.
