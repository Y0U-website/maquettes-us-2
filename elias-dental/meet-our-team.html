Dr. Richard Elias III, D.D.S.

Dr. Richard Elias III, D.D.S. has been practicing dentistry since 1991. After receiving his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Northwestern University, he joined his grandfather in an established practice in East Grand Rapids. Utilizing the most technologically advanced equipment and techniques, Dr. Elias performs all aspects of modern dentistry.

From cosmetic veneering and bonding to implant tooth replacement, every facet of a healthy and beautiful smile is addressed. Dr. Elias attends over 30 hours of continuing education every year to stay abreast of the newest and most advanced techniques, materials and practice management tools.

Dr. Elias has been married to his wife Amy since 1989, and they have three boys: Jake, Luke and Peter. The Elias family attends Ada Bible Church at the Cascade Campus and are quite involved with the church family. Dr. Elias is also highly involved with the autism community, serving as President of Autism Support of Kent County (ASK). Last year ASK raised over $40,000.00 to support children and families affected by Autism.

When not performing dentistry, Dr. Elias enjoys spending time with his wife, Amy and family, golf, gardening, and playing the mandolin. Dr. Elias is an active member of the following: American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association, West Michigan Dental District, Kent County Dental District, Chicago Dental Society, Christian Medical and Dental Association, and The Seattle Study Club.

Kelley Office Manager/ Dental Hygienist

My name is Kelley, and I am 36 years old. I have been practicing as a dental hygienist for 14 years. Of those 14 years I have been employed with Elias Dental for 13 years. I work as a full time hygienist and love to see my dental family throughout the year. When I am not practicing hygiene I am maintaining and coordinating our hygiene department and dental treatment plans/consultation. I received my associates degree from Kalamazoo Valley Community College in 2006 with honors in dental hygiene.

My husband and I enjoy spending our time with our daughter, Sydney. She is our world and amazes us each day with her smile and personality. I am a small town country girl who enjoys the outdoors and spending time with my family. My country roots have blessed me with a great attitude and personality. Building patient rapport within the practice is always at the top of my to do list. I encourage you to join the practice of Elias Dental so that you too can become part of a great dental family!
