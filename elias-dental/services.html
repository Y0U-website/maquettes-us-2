Teeth Cleaning

Brushing your teeth twice a day and flossing once a day are important to maintain good oral health; however, sometimes you need professional help to remove plaque, tartar, and get those harder to-reach-places. Read more about Teeth Cleaning

Fillings

If you get a cavity, it’s important to have it filled to prevent further damage and complications. Our East Grand Rapids dentist and his experienced team are well equipped to restore your natural teeth with composite tooth-colored fillings so no one will know you had a cavity in the first place. Read more about Fillings

Dentures

Whatever the cause may be for missing teeth, dentures are a great option to restore the look and function of your mouth especially if you are missing multiple teeth. Dentures not only help you eat but they help you speak and enunciate better. Read more about Dentures

Dental Implants

If you’ve recently lost a tooth, a dental implant may be a great solution for you. Dental implants are a titanium root topped with a crown giving the appearance that you never missed a tooth in the first place. Read more about Dental Implants

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry is one of the specialities of Elias Dental. Featuring a number of procedures including, veneers , teeth whitening (in-office & take-home trays), and minor tooth movement. We’re eager to help you have a smile you can be proud of. Read more about Cosmetic Dentistry

SureSmile® Aligners

One of the main benefits of SureSmile aligners is that you can straighten your teeth without the inconvenience of having metal glued to your enamel. In fact, most people won’t even realize that you’re wearing aligners unless they’re standing close. This is perfect for teens, college students, and people who work public-facing jobs. Read more about SureSmile® Aligners
