The practice

At Manhasset Endodontics, we use state-of-the-art technology to provide excellent patient care, in a peaceful and calm environment.

Dr. Shamoon received her Doctorate in Dental Medicine from the Harvard School of Dental Medicine in 2013. She went on to complete her Endodontics residency program at the Columbia University College of Dental Medicine in 2015. She is committed to providing excellent care while prioritizing patient comfort and satisfaction.
