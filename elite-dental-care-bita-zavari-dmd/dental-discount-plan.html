FAQs

Who is eligible to join the Elite Dental Discount Plan?

Individuals and families in Beaverton and Portland who do not have traditional dental insurance. It’s also an excellent option for those whose current insurance plans have very limited coverage, high deductibles, or extensive waiting periods.

Do I have to wait till I can use my benefits?

No! One of the biggest advantages of our Elite Dental Discount Plan is that there are no waiting periods. As soon as you enroll and your membership is active, you can immediately begin enjoying your discounts on all covered services.

How does this plan differ from traditional dental insurance?

Our plan is simpler and more direct. Unlike traditional insurance, there are no deductibles, no annual maximums, no complicated claims to file, and no reimbursement paperwork. You pay an annual membership fee, and then receive direct discounts on services at our office.

Can my entire family join the Elite Dental Discount Plan?

Yes, absolutely! Our plan offers convenient family membership options to ensure that everyone in your household can access exceptional dental care at discounted rates.

What if I need a complex procedure, like a dental implant or veneers?

Our Elite Dental Discount Plan provides valuable discounts on a wide array of advanced and cosmetic procedures, including dental implants, veneers, crowns, and more.
