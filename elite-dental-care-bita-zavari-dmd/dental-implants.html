The Dental Implant Procedure & Steps There are multiple steps and visits to the dentist involved in a dental implant procedure.

First, if a damaged tooth is present, it is removed. Second, grafting, also known as jawbone preparation, is performed. The following step is the placement of the dental implant. After this is completed, significant time is given for the bone to heal and fuse to the implant. In some instances, this can take 4 to 6 months.

One of the main purposes of our teeth is to break down food so that we can digest it properly. However, when teeth are missing, it’s almost impossible to chew comfortably, break down foods, and speak clearly. Replacing any missing or damaged teeth with dental implants can vastly improve eating and speech.

A lack of teeth can create a distorted facial frame. It also leads to shifting teeth. Crowded teeth are more susceptible to plaque buildup and gum disease as well. Dental implants prevent teeth from shifting, offer a permanent replacement for teeth, and help individuals achieve a younger look.
