Leena Bahu, DDS, FAGD, MAGD

Dr. Leena Bahu has been a part of the dental community for nearly 25 years. After graduating from the University of Detroit Mercy with a Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree (D.D.S.), Dr. Bahu has continued her education each day by attending seminars, continuing education courses and traveling all over the United States to offer patients the most up to date techniques in dentistry.

As the president of her woman’s study club, Dr. Bahu and many speakers in the field of dentistry meet to discuss cases and technology updates to bring the most up to date techniques and equipment to our community. As a past member of the Spear study club, a highly prestigious training program in dentistry, Dr. Bahu continues to expand her knowledge to educate her patients and staff to provide the best care for you and your family.

Dr. Leena Bahu will ensure the overall health for each one of our patients including those who suffer from obstructive sleep apnea. Dr. Bahu works with many sleep physicians while also being on staff at St. Joseph’s Mercy Hospital in Pontiac, Dr. Bahu offers an alternative to our C-PAP intolerant patients.

After receiving the prestigious American General Dentistry Fellowship Award, Dr. Bahu continues to advance her education and has achieved her Master’s in July 2024 through the American Academy of General Dentistry to offer our community the ELITE care they deserve!
