Clear Retainer

Clear Retainers are essential for preserving orthodontic results by preventing teeth from shifting after treatment. Fabricated from thin yet durable thermoplastic material, they offer a discreet and comfortable solution for long-term retention.

Clear orthodontic retainers and bonded lingual retainers provide an efficient, enduring method for maintaining the alignment of anterior teeth following treatment. The transparent plastic design of these retainers renders them less conspicuous compared to conventional wire retainers, and they can be customized to incorporate denture teeth if required.

These retainers are ideal for patients who have completed orthodontic treatment and seek a virtually invisible option to maintain their perfect smile. Unlike traditional wire retainers, clear retainers are stain-resistant and easy to wear, ensuring high patient compliance.

How It Works

  • A digital or physical impression is taken post-orthodontic treatment.
  • The clear retainer is custom-fabricated using durable, BPA-free material.
  • Patients wear the retainer overnight or as prescribed to maintain alignment.

Quick Questions

1. How long does a clear retainer last?

With proper care, our clear retainers can last up to 2 years, depending on patient wear and maintenance.

2. Can you create retainers for patients with missing teeth?

Yes, we can incorporate pontics to replace missing teeth in clear retainers for esthetic and functional purposes.

3. Do you offer digital scans for clear retainers?

Yes, we accept digital impressions for precise fabrication and improved fit.

4. What material do you use for clear retainers?

Our traditional material for clear retainers is .040 thickness. However, we offer a variety of materials based on specific needs: Bleach Trays Night Guards: Made with dual-soft material for enhanced comfort and durability/preference. Clear Retainers: Available in .020, .040, and .080 thickness.

5. How soon can I get a replacement retainer for my patient?

We provide quick turnaround times, with replacement retainers available within 1-2 business days.
