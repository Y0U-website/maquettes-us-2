ABOUT US

Based in Cromwell, CT our doctors are committed to your individual attention and strive to provide personalized dental healthcare tailored for your unique needs and goals. We strive to develop lifelong relationships with our patients by combining the latest technology with a professional and compassionate staff.

Mission It is our mission to educate and provide our patients with the best care and to provide you with all of the important information you need to make healthy decisions.

Our offices always welcome new patients of every age. Feel free to contact us with any questions. We look forward to making each visit to our office a positive experience.

History

Elite Dental was created in 2016 by Dr. James Nguyen, a licensed dentist. He established his first dental practice, Dental & Vision Associates located in Wethersfield in 2003. Elite Dental is an extention to his commitment to make dental care services accessible and convenient for patients.
