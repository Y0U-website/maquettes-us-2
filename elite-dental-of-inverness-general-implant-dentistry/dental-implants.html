Dental Implants: What Are They?

Dental implants are remarkable restorative solutions designed to replace missing teeth while providing a strong and natural-looking foundation. Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, dental implants are surgically placed into the jawbone, effectively mimicking the structure of natural teeth. These implants consist of three essential components:

The Dental Implant : This titanium screw-like post is inserted into the jawbone, acting as the root of the new tooth.

Implant Abutment : The abutment connects the implant post to the prosthetic tooth on top of it.

Fixed Restoration : A crown, bridge, or All-on-4 prosthesis that is custom-designed to complete the visible portion of your beautiful smile.

Dental implants in Inverness have many benefits. It gives better stability and improves oral function. The implants last a long time and prevent bone loss. It also helps keep your facial structure looking natural and youthful.
