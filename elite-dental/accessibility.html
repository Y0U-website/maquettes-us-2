Accessibility Statement

If you contact us about an accessibility problem, please let us know the page and what you were trying to do, so we can help you and address it as quickly as possible.

  • Email us: office@littlerockelitedental.com
  • Visit us: 802 Kirk Road, Little Rock, AR 72223

Help with your visit, too

Accessibility doesn’t stop at our website. If you have specific needs for your visit to our office, please tell us when you schedule — we’ll do everything we can to make your time with us comfortable and accessible. Our office is single-story, with step-free access and parking right out front.

We see accessibility as part of treating people the way we’d want to be treated. Thank you for helping us do it better.
