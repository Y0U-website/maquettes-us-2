Coordinated care without guesswork

Not every part of implant treatment is necessarily completed by the same provider. After evaluating your case, we explain which steps can be handled at Elite Dental and when a specialist should be involved. When referral is appropriate, we help coordinate the sequence so you understand who is doing what and when.

An implant is also not the only way to replace a missing tooth. A fixed bridge or removable option may fit some patients better. The goal of the consultation is to compare realistic choices and help you make an informed decision without pressure.
