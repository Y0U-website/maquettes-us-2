What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone to act as a replacement for the root of a missing tooth. Once the implant integrates with the bone, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is placed on top to restore your smile.

  • Surgical Placement: The implant is placed in your jawbone under local anesthesia.

  • Restoration: Once the implant is secure, a custom crown or restoration is placed to complete your smile.
