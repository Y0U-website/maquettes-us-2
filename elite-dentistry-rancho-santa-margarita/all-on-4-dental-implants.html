For most patients, All-on-4 implants feel dramatically more natural than dentures ever did. Because the prosthesis is anchored firmly to implants rather than resting on the gums, there is no slipping, no rocking, and no need to cover the roof of your mouth, so eating and speaking feel far more like your natural teeth. There is a brief adjustment period as you get used to the new bite, but most people adapt quickly, and Dr. Lari can fine-tune the fit if anything feels off.
