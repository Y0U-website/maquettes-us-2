Common Types of Dental Implant Treatment

From a single missing tooth to a full arch, there is an implant solution to fit your needs.

Single-Tooth Replacement

Perfect for replacing one missing tooth. A single titanium post is placed in the bone and topped with a custom-made crown that blends seamlessly with your natural teeth, preserving the integrity of adjacent teeth and preventing bone loss.

Multi-Tooth Implant Bridges

Instead of wearing a partial denture or traditional tooth-supported bridge, a fixed bridge can be installed on top of a pair of dental implants. Implant bridges are ideal for 3-4 missing teeth.

All-on-4 Implants

An innovative solution for replacing an entire arch of teeth. Four strategically placed implants support a full arch of prosthetic teeth, offering a permanent, non-removable alternative to dentures.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Sometimes called "snap-on" or "clip-in" dentures, these overdentures are removable but use 2-4 implants for added reinforcement throughout the day. No messy pastes or adhesives are needed.
