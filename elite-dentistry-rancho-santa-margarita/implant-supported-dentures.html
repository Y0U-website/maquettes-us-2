Signs You May Need Implant-Supported Dentures

Loose Dentures Your current dentures slip, click, or feel uncomfortable throughout the day.

Missing Most Teeth You are missing most or all teeth in an arch and want more stability.

Adequate Bone You have enough jawbone for implants, or are open to bone grafting.

Secure Solution You want a natural-feeling option that stays firmly in place.

Glowing Reviews From Our Patients

Most cases use between two and six implants per arch, depending on your bone and denture type. Dr. Lari will recommend the ideal number for lasting stability.

Some snap on and off for easy cleaning, while others are fixed and removed only by your dentist. We will help you choose the option that fits your lifestyle.

The implants can last a lifetime with good care, while the denture portion typically lasts ten to fifteen years. Regular checkups help maximize their lifespan.

The procedure uses local anesthesia, often with sedation, so you should not feel pain during placement. Any soreness afterward is usually mild and short-lived.

Clean the denture daily and brush and floss carefully around the implant sites. Regular checkups let us monitor your implant health for the long term.

The cost depends on several factors, including how many implants you need, whether additional procedures like bone grafting are required, and the type of denture restoration you choose. While implant-supported dentures are a meaningful investment, they often prove more economical over time than traditional dentures that require frequent adjustments, relining, or replacement.
