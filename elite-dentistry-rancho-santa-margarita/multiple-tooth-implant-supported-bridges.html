Implant-Supported Bridges

  • 02 Implant Placement Two or more implants are surgically placed at strategic points to support the bridge.
  • 03 Healing Period The implants fuse with the jawbone over several months through osseointegration.
  • 04 Abutment Placement Once healed, connector abutments are attached to the implants.
  • 05 Bridge Placement A custom bridge is secured onto the abutments for a stable, natural result.

Signs You May Need Implant-Supported Bridges

Several Missing You have multiple missing teeth in a row you would like to replace.

Good Oral Health Your gums are healthy and ready to support implant treatment.

Adequate Bone You have enough jawbone for implant placement, or are open to grafting.

Stable Solution You want the most secure, natural-feeling option available.
