Signs You May Need Single-Tooth Implant

One Missing Tooth You are missing a single tooth from decay, injury, or an extraction.

Healthy Gums Your gums are healthy and free of active periodontal disease.

Enough Bone You have sufficient jawbone to support the implant, or are open to grafting.

Fixed Solution You want a permanent option rather than a removable partial denture.
