Meet Our Expert Endodontic Team

Dr. Vanessa Cabrera-Saez, DMD

Dr. Vanessa Cabrera-Saez was Born and raised in Peru. She received her Doctorate in Dental Surgery at the Catholic University in Arequipa Peru.

She then moved to Philadelphia where she earned a Doctorate in Dental Medicine from the University of Pennsylvania. During Dental school she participated in dental research and received many awards including The Samuel and Louis Rossman Endodontic Scholarship by the University of Pennsylvania Department of Endodontics.

Dr. Cabrera then completed her endodontic residency training at the University of Pennsylvania. During her time there, she provided hands-on training in microsurgical techniques to visiting endodontists and residents from around the world. Her education and clinical experiences have led her to develop a unique treatment philosophy that is not only of technical excellence but one that patients can find comforting.

Dr. Cabrera combines technological innovation with genuine compassion to provide her patients with outstanding quality care. She thoroughly explains the best treatment options for each individual and focuses on minimizing dental anxiety and discomfort throughout the entire treatment.

Dr. Cabrera is an active member of the American Association of Endodontists, the New Jersey Dental Association, the American Dental Association, and the Hispanic Dental Association.

In her free time, she enjoys running, traveling, and exploring the newest restaurants in Philadelphia.

Dr. Cabrera is fluent in English, Spanish, and Portuguese.

Dr. Aysel Iranparvar, DMD

Dr. Aysel Iranparvar was born and raised in Tehran, Iran. She earned her Doctorate in Dental Surgery (DDS) from Tehran Azad University, School of Dentistry. After graduation, she practiced as a general dentist.

She then moved to the United States, and she earned a Doctorate in Dental Medicine (DMD) from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston. At Tufts University, Dr. Aysel was awarded the Merit Scholarship for her exceptional academic performance. She graduated with the highest honor, Summa Cum Laude, as the top student in her class and was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society.

Following dental school, Dr. Aysel pursued specialty training in Endodontics at Temple University. There, she completed a rigorous post-graduate program, earning her certificate in Endodontics and a master’s degree in Oral Health Sciences. She is a member of the American Association of Endodontists and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Aysel currently resides in Philadelphia with her husband. In her free time, she loves to explore new restaurants and spend time with friends and family.
