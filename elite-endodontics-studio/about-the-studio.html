About The Studio

A different kind of endodontic studio in Garden City

Elite Endodontics Studio was created to make one of dentistry’s most anxiety-provoking experiences feel calm, modern, and welcoming—more like a studio than a traditional clinic. We combine advanced endodontics with a hospitality-driven atmosphere so you can relax, feel informed, and leave with real relief.

Care that feels calm, clear, and compassionate

We believe endodontic care should feel straightforward. We listen first, explain options in plain language, and guide you step-by-step—especially when you’re in pain or unsure what comes next. From diagnosis to aftercare, our goal is to reduce anxiety and make every visit feel thoughtful, not rushed.

Meet Dr. Shi

Dr. Shi is a dedicated endodontist committed to transforming one of dentistry’s most anxiety-provoking experiences into one defined by comfort, clarity, and compassion. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the Stony Brook University School of Dental Medicine, followed by advanced specialty training in Endodontics at NYU College of Dentistry. This dual foundation in rigorous clinical education and evidence-based practice shapes every aspect of her patient care.

Dr. Shi understands that most patients visit an endodontist at a stressful moment—often in pain, worried, or unsure about what comes next. Her goal is simple yet profound: make every patient feel as comfortable as possible during a time when they may be experiencing an uncomfortable toothache or dental emergency.

Outside the practice, Dr. Shi enjoys spending time with her husband and their two children, traveling the world together, and discovering new and diverse cuisines wherever they go. These experiences enrich her perspective and reinforce her belief in leading with empathy, curiosity, and kindness—both at home and in the treatment room.

This practice also represents something uniquely meaningful: the union of Dr. Shi and Dr. Elsharouny—not only in marriage, but in a shared philosophy of care. Together, they built a space where advanced endodontics and heartfelt patient-centered values are seamlessly intertwined.

The space—and people—behind your care

Our studio is designed with soft tones, natural textures, and open spaces to feel more like a modern lounge than a medical office. Behind the scenes, our team focuses on the details that matter: keeping visits organized, communicating clearly, and checking in often so you always know what’s next. From the front desk to the treatment chair, we want every interaction to feel calm, respectful, and genuinely caring.
