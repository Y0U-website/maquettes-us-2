Endodontic Treatment | Endodontic Surgery

Sometimes, even after root canal therapy, additional treatment may be needed to preserve the health and function of your tooth. This is where endodontic surgery comes in, offering solutions for complex dental issues that can’t be resolved with nonsurgical methods alone. With the right expertise, this procedure can help save your natural tooth and prevent future dental complications.

  • Addresses hidden or persistent infections – It can treat complex canals, small fractures, or infections that regular root canal therapy could not fully resolve.
  • Promotes long-lasting dental health – By removing infected tissue and sealing the root, it helps prevent further complications or the need for more invasive treatments later.
  • Provides relief from ongoing discomfort – If you’ve experienced lingering pain after a root canal, endodontic surgery may finally bring lasting comfort and peace of mind.
  • Supports overall oral health – Keeping your natural teeth intact contributes to better chewing function, jaw stability, and alignment, helping avoid issues linked to tooth loss.
