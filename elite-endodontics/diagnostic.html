ENDODONTIC DIAGNOSTIC SERVICES

When tooth pain strikes, the most important first step is an accurate diagnosis. At Elite Endodontics, Dr. Cruz takes a thorough, methodical approach to identifying the source of your discomfort so that every treatment decision is grounded in a complete picture of your oral health.

Your diagnostic visit begins with a detailed review of your medical and dental history. Dr. Cruz takes the time to understand your overall health, any previous dental treatments, current medications, and the specific symptoms that brought you in. This step is essential because your medical background can influence both diagnosis and treatment planning. By gathering this information upfront, Dr. Cruz ensures nothing is overlooked before moving forward.

Once your history is reviewed, Dr. Cruz performs a comprehensive hands-on examination to locate the source of your pain or discomfort. Tooth pain can sometimes be difficult to pinpoint, discomfort in one area may actually originate from a completely different tooth. Dr. Cruz carefully evaluates each tooth and the surrounding tissues to narrow down the precise cause, rather than relying on assumptions.

Advanced Imaging for an Accurate Diagnosis

To see what’s happening beneath the surface, Dr. Cruz uses advanced imaging technology. This allows her to view the internal structures of your teeth and surrounding bone in detail that a visual exam alone cannot provide. Advanced imaging helps reveal hidden infections, fractures, or damage to the tooth’s inner pulp that may not be visible to the eye. The result is a more confident, accurate diagnosis that leads to more effective treatment.

After your examination and imaging are complete, Dr. Cruz develops a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific diagnosis. Her approach is always focused on preserving your natural teeth whenever possible and relieving your pain as quickly as she can. She will walk you through her findings, explain your options clearly, and answer every question you have so you can make informed decisions about your care.

We understand that visiting a specialist can feel stressful, especially when you’re already in pain. That’s why our entire team is committed to creating a welcoming, supportive atmosphere from the moment you walk through the door. Your comfort and well-being are always our top priority, whether that means taking extra time to explain a diagnosis or simply making sure you feel at ease in the chair.

Frequently Asked Questions About Endodontic Diagnostics

What happens during an endodontic diagnostic appointment?

Dr. Cruz starts by reviewing your medical and dental history, then performs a thorough clinical examination to locate the source of your discomfort. Advanced imaging is used to see beneath the surface and confirm the diagnosis. From there, she creates a personalized treatment plan focused on preserving your natural tooth.

Why was I referred to an endodontist for diagnosis instead of staying with my general dentist? Endodontists like Dr. Cruz specialize in diagnosing tooth pain and problems within the tooth’s interior. Some cases require the specialized training and advanced imaging that an endodontic practice provides in order to reach an accurate diagnosis.

How long does a diagnostic appointment take?

Most diagnostic visits are completed in a single appointment. Dr. Cruz takes the time needed to review your history, perform a thorough exam, capture any necessary imaging, and discuss her findings and recommendations with you.

The examination itself is gentle and designed with your comfort in mind. Dr. Cruz and our team prioritize making the experience as stress-free as possible.
