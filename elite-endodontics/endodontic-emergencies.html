Frequently Asked Questions About Endodontic Emergencies

Can Elite Endodontics see me the same day I call?

We will do our best to see you the same day. If a same-day appointment isn’t available, we’ll schedule you for urgent care the next business day so you’re seen as quickly as possible.

Sudden or severe tooth pain, swelling, cracked teeth, and traumatic dental injuries are all considered endodontic emergencies. If you’re unsure whether your situation qualifies, call our office and our team will help you determine the next step.

Will I receive treatment during my emergency visit, or just a consultation?

Dr. Cruz will perform a comprehensive consultation using advanced diagnostic imaging and a clinical exam. If endodontic treatment is recommended and you choose to proceed, she can often begin treatment during that same visit.

What happens after my emergency treatment?

Dr. Cruz will recommend seeing your general dentist within 30 days for a permanent restoration if needed. Some cases may also require follow-up appointments with Dr. Cruz at intervals recommended by the American Association of Endodontists.

How do I schedule an emergency appointment?

Call us directly at (215) 997-9888 . Let our team know you’re experiencing an emergency and we’ll work to get you in as soon as possible.
