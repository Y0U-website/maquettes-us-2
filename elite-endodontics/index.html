The practice

Our team specializes in gentle, expert retreatments, using advanced technology to address previous root canal challenges and restore your tooth’s health with care and precision.

We offer comprehensive diagnostics, from reviewing your medical and dental history to using advanced imaging, so we can pinpoint the issue and design a personalized, tooth-preserving treatment that keeps you comfortable.

We are ready to handle endodontic emergencies with same-day care, providing swift, compassionate relief using advanced techniques to ease your pain and protect your smile.

Now based in Pennsylvania, Dr. Cruz is the owner of Elite Endodontics and remains deeply engaged in professional development. She is an active member of the American Association of Endodontists, the American Dental Association, and the Pennsylvania Dental Association, and is currently pursuing board certification to become a Diplomate of the American Board of Endodontics.

Dr. Cruz is dedicated to providing the highest standard of care by incorporating the latest endodontic technology and research into her practice. Known for her gentle, calming chairside manner, she is committed to making each patient’s experience as comfortable and stress-free as possible. She looks forward to welcoming you to the practice.
