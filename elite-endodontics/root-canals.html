What Is a Root Canal?

A root canal is a treatment that removes damaged or infected tissue from inside your tooth. At Elite Endodontics, Dr. Cruz carefully cleans and disinfects the interior of the tooth, then seals the space with biocompatible materials to prevent future infection. The goal is always to preserve your natural tooth using an attentive approach and advanced techniques.

What to Expect During Root Canal Treatment

Consultation

Dr. Cruz begins with a thorough evaluation using advanced diagnostic imaging and clinical findings. She presents your treatment options and prognoses so you can make a confident, informed decision. If root canal treatment is the right choice, local anesthesia is administered for your complete comfort.

Disinfection & Filling

Once you’re fully numbed and at ease, Dr. Cruz gently removes the infection from within your tooth. She creates a small opening to thoroughly disinfect the interior before sealing the canals with biocompatible materials. A temporary filling is placed on the chewing surface to protect your tooth until a permanent restoration can be placed by your general dentist.

Post-Treatment Care

After your root canal therapy is complete, Dr. Cruz recommends that you see your general dentist within 30 days for a permanent restoration. Our team always follows up with you the next business day to check in on your recovery. Our priority is making sure you’re on the path to a fully healed, comfortable tooth.
