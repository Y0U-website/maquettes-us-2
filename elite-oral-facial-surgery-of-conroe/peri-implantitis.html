Peri-implantitis Treatment

Even an implant that has successfully integrated into the jawbone can become infected over time. That’s why monitoring the health of your implant is crucial.

  • Pain or discomfort near the implant
  • Bone loss that may loosen the implant, potentially causing it to fail

Treatment for Peri-Implantitis

At Elite Oral & Facial Surgery of Katy + Periodontology , we use advanced, minimally invasive techniques to effectively treat peri-implantitis in its early stages. Our approach focuses on:

  • Removing Diseased Tissue – We eliminate infected and damaged tissue surrounding the implant.
  • Eradicating Bacteria – Using specialized equipment, we thoroughly clean the affected area to reduce the risk of reinfection.
  • Bone Regeneration – If bone loss has occurred, we can regenerate the lost bone to support the implant and improve long-term outcomes.

When to Seek Treatment

If you notice symptoms such as bleeding, swelling, or irritation around your implant, it’s important to act quickly. Left untreated, peri-implantitis can lead to the spread of infection and increase the risk of losing your implant.

Don’t wait to address the signs of peri-implantitis. Early intervention can save your implant and safeguard your oral health. Contact us today to schedule your consultation and take the first step toward preserving your smile!
