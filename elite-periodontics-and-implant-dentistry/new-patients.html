New Patients

  • Try to arrive early to complete medical and patient information forms
  • At your first appointment we will listen closely to your concerns and conduct a thorough examination. We will discuss our findings with you and your restorative dentist.
  • Bring any x-rays which you may have
  • Bring both your dental and medical insurance forms
  • If your normally take an antibiotic prior to your dental cleanings please do so prior to your examination and consultation.

Office Policies

Appointments

In respect of your time, all of our patients will be seen on an appointment basis. We will make every effort to be on time for you. Because we are a surgical practice it is not always possible to stay exactly on time. Still, we pride ourselves on staying on schedule and will make every effort to keep your waiting time to a minimum. Most scheduling delays arise because of patients arriving late. Please remember to allow for unpredictable traffic and give yourself a few minutes to spare.

Payments

Payments are due at the time of service. For your convenience, we are happy to accept Visa, MasterCard and American Express and will file all insurance on your behalf. Alternative payment methods are available upon inquiry.

Insurance

There is a great deal of confusion about dental insurance. Please realize that we do not work for any insurance carriers. We work for you. Our treatment recommendations are based solely on what we feel will provide the best periodontal outcome for you regardless of your coverage.
