Cosmetic Services

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are thin shells that, when placed over your natural teeth, can correct a variety of cosmetic issues. They are customized and made to look identical to your natural tooth enamel using high-quality porcelain. When applied with expertise, they are a permanent cosmetic solution resulting in a complete smile transformation.

Implants (Single and Multiple)

Dental implants restore function and appearance to the mouth, not just in the teeth but in the surrounding bone and tissue. Inserted into the jaw bone, implants are very durable and will last many years. With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.

All-On-X Technology (Fixed Prothesis)

All-on-X implants , also known as All-on-Four, are a dental implant technique where the upper or lower set of teeth are replaced with just four implants . These four implants act as a support mechanism for the placement of a new set of fixed teeth. Because the All-on-X method uses fewer implants, the surgery is less invasive than other techniques and may not require a prior bone grafting procedure.

Full-Mouth Restorations

Full-mouth restorations involve everything from veneers and lip repositioning to implants and crowns and can produce life-changing results by completely improving both the look and function of a smile.

Bonding/Tooth-Colored Restorations

Dental bonding is a fast, affordable cosmetic treatment that uses tooth-colored resin to correct minor imperfections like chips, gaps, or uneven edges. The material is expertly sculpted and polished to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. While not permanent like veneers, bonding can significantly enhance your smile in just one visit.

Crowns

Dental crowns are tooth-shaped caps that are custom-made to fit over a weakened tooth to restore the smile and protect it from further damage.

Bridges

A dental bridge is used to fill in the area left by a missing tooth. This prosthetic is placed between two porcelain crowns that are attached to your own existing teeth. Elite Smiles offers fixed bridges, a permanent solution to replace one or more missing teeth as well as cantilever bridges, to correct teeth in your smile line. A bridge will look and feel like your own teeth and can reduce your risk of gum disease, correct bite issues, and even improve your speech.

Gummy Smile Treatment

Gummy smile treatment, through either lip repositioning or laser gingivectomy (or a combination of both), is a minimally invasive procedure with maximum results. It both enhances the aesthetics of your smile by reducing the appearance of gums when smiling and improves oral health by protecting your gums from over-exposure. It is a permanent solution, versus other popular temporary treatments such as Botox and fillers.
