What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a permanent solution for missing teeth where both the tooth and root are replaced with an artificial, durable implant restoring both the function and aesthetics of your smile. Dr. Meden is highly trained in implantology and utilizes the highest quality of implants to ensure your smile stands the test of time.

Benefits Of Dental Implants

Unlike bridges or other solutions for missing teeth, like dentures, implants offer reliable, permanent, and tooth-saving benefits. Dental implants prevent bone loss and gum recession caused by missing teeth and look, feel, and function like your own teeth making them a popular choice for those who want aesthetic and functional results.

Caring For Dental Implants

Proper care is imperative for the longevity and health of your new dental implant. In addition to routine check-ups, you will need to implement some best practices, such as daily brushing and flossing, to ensure your implant stays free of harmful bacteria and plaque that can result in implant loss. Just like implants look and feel like real teeth, they should be looked after as such.
