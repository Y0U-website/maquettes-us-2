Elegant, Precise & Elite

Elite Smiles by Walter Meden DDS is a high-end cosmetic dentistry practice located in Draper, Utah specializing in veneers, implants, and full mouth restorations.

The practice

Using custom porcelain veneers, Dr. Meden takes a careful approach to color, texture, and appearance to achieve a natural-looking and functional smile that radiates for a lifetime.

Dr. Meden’s extensive training in implantology and use of premium implants makes him a leader in both single and All-on-X implants that transform smiles and replace missing teeth with an aesthetic, functional, and permanent solution.

Dr. Meden is an expert and perfectionist in restoring broken smiles through a combination of procedures, ranging from veneers and lip repositioning to full-arch implants, that produce life-changing results.

About Us Elite Smiles is where passion and excellence meet. We combine our passion for creating beauty with utilizing state-of-the-art technology and the latest techniques to provide our patients with newfound confidence, flawless function, and a lifetime of smiles.

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on enhancing the appearance of a patient’s smile through various treatments and procedures such as veneers, dental implants, gummy smile treatment, and full mouth implants. Dr. Meden specializes in cosmetic dentistry, offering personalized treatments to help patients achieve their ideal smile with precision and care.

Dr. Meden provides comprehensive general dentistry services, offering patients expert care in everything from routine check-ups to more advanced dental procedures, ensuring long-term oral health and well-being. Services include preventive and routine care like cleanings, exams, and fillings, as well as more specialized treatments such as endodontics and periodontics.

Dr. Meden provides oral surgery procedures with a focus on careful planning, modern techniques, and patient comfort. Surgical services include extractions, bone grafting to support bone volume, membrane and tissue grafting to promote healthy gum and bone regeneration, and the use of biologic therapies such as PRP/PRF (platelet-rich plasma/fibrin) to support healing.

Whether it’s a single restoration or a more comprehensive full mouth rehabilitation, I create for my patients the “smile lift”—natural looking teeth that are meant to complement the natural facial symmetry. Great aesthetic enhancement has to be performed with great planning and precise execution.
