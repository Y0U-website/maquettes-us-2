Michael Tillery, DDS FAGD

An Indianapolis native bringing smiles to the Wabash Valley.

Dr. Michael Tillery hails from Indianapolis, where he previously owned and operated Tillery Family Dental for 45 years. He now calls Elite Smiles Dental his dental home and is excited to treat patients in Wabash Valley!

After graduating from high school, Dr. Tillery knew he wanted to channel his passion for helping others into a career. This led him down the path towards dentistry, where he’s able to build lasting relationships and create beautiful smiles for his patients.

He attended Wabash College This link opens a new tab to the Wabash College website , where he received his Bachelor of Science in Biology. Afterward, he graduated from the IU School of Dentistry This link opens a new tab to the IU School of Dentistry website with his doctorate in 1979. Dr. Tillery is also a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry This link opens a new tab to the AGD website .

In his free time, you can find Dr. Tillery enjoying time with his kids and grandchildren, going on dates with his wife, playing golf, teaching grief share ministry, and going on mission trips with his church.

William Lewis, DDS

Originally from Salt Lake City, Utah, Dr. Lewis chose dentistry because it perfectly combines his love for working with his hands and helping people feel confident about their smiles. Dr. Lewis earned his dental degree from Indiana University and has been practicing dentistry for the past few years.

Dr. Lewis has special interests in implants, dentures, and cosmetic dentistry, and he especially enjoys treatments that help patients achieve healthier lives through improved oral health. His approach to patient care is conservative and centered around each patient’s long-term goals and vision.

Outside of the office, Dr. Lewis enjoys spending time outdoors with his family. He and his wife homeschool their children. He also enjoys cooking and playing fetch with their dog, Daisy.
