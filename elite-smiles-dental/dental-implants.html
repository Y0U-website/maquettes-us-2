Your Dental Implants Journey

A permanent tooth replacement is simpler than you think.

Elite Smiles Dental in Terre Haute, IN offers dental implants, which are one of the best ways to make your smile whole again. They work like tooth roots and are placed into the jawbone, where they fuse with the jawbone to support the tooth replacement.

Unlike dentures, dental implants are fixed and won’t move, fall out, or affect speech and chewing.

Get the top tooth replacement in Terre Haute, IN with dental implants.

  • Step 1 During the consultation, we’ll review your teeth, gums, X-ray, and medical history to ensure you’re a candidate for dental implants. Patients must have enough jawbone and must also have healthy gums to proceed with the dental implant procedure.
  • Step 2 The procedure includes placement of the implant, a small titanium post, into the missing tooth bone socket. The jawbone fuses with the post so that it’s fixed. Next, an abutment, which is a small connector, is attached to the post where the new tooth (AKA the crown) goes.
  • Step 3 Impressions are taken of your teeth so that a tooth can be created to match your existing smile. The new tooth is custom-made to look and feel like your own natural tooth and is known as the crown. The crown is attached to the abutment.
  • What is the average cost of a dental implant? The average cost of a dental implant varies from patient to patient. Each dental implant is unique, just like the patient, and prices vary. Please contact us for an estimate.
  • What is the downside of dental implants? While there are slight risks involved with all surgeries like dental implants, our experienced dentist will ensure a pain-free and stress-free procedure. Dental implants have a very high success rate. Risks involved with dental implants include infection, nerve damage, damage to surrounding teeth, and sinus problems. This is why it’s important to visit an experienced and professional dentist, for example, at Elite Smiles Dental.
  • How painful is getting a dental implant? The dental implant procedure is not painful, and you’ll feel little discomfort during healing. While you may feel slight pressure during the procedure, you’ll be comfortable because we use numbing. Local anesthesia numbs the nerves surrounding the site of the implant.
  • Can dental implants be done in one day? Same-day dental implants can be done in one day. The number of visits needed for your dental implant procedure depends on the severity and number of implants required.
