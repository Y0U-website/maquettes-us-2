Adult Dentistry

We generally start seeing children at age 11-12 when their 2nd molars erupt. We are happy to see your children prior to this age for their preventive care but have found that pediatric dental practices tailored specifically towards young children’s mouth size and special needs are best if your child needs any restorative or specific dental treatment.
