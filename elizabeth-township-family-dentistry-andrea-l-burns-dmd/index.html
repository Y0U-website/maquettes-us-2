The practice

Led by Dr. Andrea Burns, Dr. Jessica Zugai, and Dr. Jonathan Caso, we are a close-knit and genuine team. We have created a family-friendly dental office where patients of all ages can receive customized dental care in a warm and relaxing environment.

Our team sees every patient as part of our extended dental family, and we can t wait to welcome you.

For the smallest smiles in your family, we offer gentle children s dentistry starting at age one. We ll provide dental exams and cleanings, preventive treatments like fluoride and sealants, and any other service they need to maintain a healthy smile.

We re also pleased to offer The HealthyStart System, an innovative approach to straightening teeth without braces.

Our goal is to inspire all of our patients to achieve excellent oral health. We offer dental exams and teeth cleanings to maintain healthy teeth and gums, along with tooth fillings and dental crowns if something goes wrong.

If you need to replace missing teeth, we offer bridges, full and partial dentures, and dental implants. One of our dentists would be happy to meet with you and determine which of these options best fits your needs.

We are also pleased to offer advanced dentistry, TMJ pain relief treatment, and innovative VIVOS sleep apnea treatment.

Everyone in your family has dental needs that change throughout their lives. With our three dentists collective and diverse experience, we can meet those changes and provide age-appropriate dental care at each stage of life.
