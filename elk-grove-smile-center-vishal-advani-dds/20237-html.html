What are dentures?

Many people may have heard of dentures or know someone who wears them, but not everyone fully understands what dentures are or how they work. Dentures are a dental apparatus designed to take the place of missing teeth in the mouth. Dentures can be removed, and there are many different types of dentures. Sometimes, dentures are designed to replace an entire row or entire mouthful of teeth. Other times, dentures are only meant to replace a portion of your natural teeth.

Below, we discuss some of the most popular types of dentures in more detail to help give you a better idea of what may be right for you:

Full Dentures

Dentures can either come in full or partial sets. As their name implies, full dentures function as a full tooth replacement option and will replace all of your teeth. These types of dentures are the most conventional option and what you probably picture when you hear the word denture. Our dental office can create full dentures for your upper jaw, lower jaw, or both. Conventional dentures rest on top of the gums and can also be removed as needed for sleeping, cleaning, etc.

Partial Dentures

Partial dentures are best used when you still have one, several, or many of your natural teeth remaining in your mouth. Partial dentures are a good treatment option because they help keep your remaining natural teeth from shifting or from moving around. Just like with conventional dentures, partial dentures are also removable.

Snap-On Dentures (Implant-supported Dentures)

Snap-On dentures, which are also commonly called snap-in dentures are dentures that are stronger than regular dentures and utilize 4-implants, which help stabilize the denture. Instead of a denture being loose and needed denture adhesive or glue to help hold in place, implants help provide retention for the denture and keep them in place.

Snap-On dentures are such a common treatment option with our Elk Grove Village dentist because they provide increased stability and help keep dentures firmly in place. When your dentures literally snap onto your teeth and attach to your mouth, you never have to worry that they will shift around or embarrassingly move out of place.

How do I care for my dentures?

It is important to properly care for and clean your dentures if you want them to last.

Caring for your dentures is relatively straightforward, but it does take practice to learn how. Although dentures cannot decay or rot like natural teeth, they can still become dirty and discolored if they are not well-cared for. Follow these simple tips to keep your dentures in prime condition:

Even if your natural teeth are gone, you can still care for your gums and mouth by gently brushing with toothpaste and rinsing with a mild mouthwash each day. Your dentures will be of the most service to you if you keep your gum line and the surrounding areas clean and free of debris or buildup of any kind.

  • Remove your dentures when sleeping: It is good practice to sleep without your dentures in so that your gums, tissues, and bones can recover and relax. Additionally, removing your dentures and soaking them in a cleaning solution overnight helps your dentures remain moist and hold their shape
  • Rinse your dentures after eating: Your dentures will naturally become dirty after you eat. To help keep them looking and feeling like new, simply remove them after mealtime and rinse them with cold or lukewarm water
  • Gently scrub and clean your dentures: You will also need to clean your dentures with a soft-bristled toothbrush and a denture-specific cleaner at least once per day. For many of our Elk Grove Village dental patients, it is easiest to do this just before bedtime. Be sure to avoid whitening toothpaste and strong, abrasive cleaning products
