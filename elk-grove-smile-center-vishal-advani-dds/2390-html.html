Dentists Recommend Dental Implants Over Other Tooth Replacement Options

When it comes to replacing missing teeth, dental implants are not the only option, but they are widely considered the best option. Dental implants are the tooth replacement option that most closely resembles the natural teeth.

Dental implants are long-lasting, and for most people, they can last a lifetime with proper care. Once your dental implant heals in place, it will function the same way your natural tooth root would have. Your Elk Grove Village, IL dentist can attach a dental crown, dental bridge, or denture to your implant, ensuring they remain fixed in place.

Dental implants have another benefit that some may overlook, but which is an important one: They protect the jawbone from deterioration over time. When tooth roots are missing, the body is no longer signaled to send nutrients to the area, and the jawbone begins to shrink over time. People with dental implants do not experience this facial collapse that gives the face a sunken-in look, making people look older than they are.

The Importance of Replacing Missing Teeth

Often, when patients want to replace missing teeth, it’s because they are concerned about their appearance and the unfortunate stigma that has (unfairly) been placed upon them. While appearances and aesthetics are very important, especially since low self-esteem and low self-confidence can eventually lead to depression, this is not the only reason why missing teeth should be replaced.

Missing teeth make it difficult to enjoy a lot of what life has to offer and can cause additional health problems over time. If you have tooth loss, you may find yourself avoiding healthy foods because they are not easy to chew. This can lead to problems with malnutrition and vitamin deficiencies. As you avoid using your teeth, the bone and tissue of your jaws become weaker and may begin to degenerate. Over time, this will change the sound of your speech and even the shape of your face, making you appear older than you really are.

Gum disease is another genuine concern in people who are missing teeth in Elk Grove Village, IL. In some cases, people may lose their teeth because of advanced gum disease, or gum disease may develop in people who have lost their teeth due to trauma or decay. In either case, gum disease and other oral diseases have been linked to serious health conditions, including heart disease and diabetes.

Bone Grafting and Sinus Lifts to Increase Bone Available for Dental Implants

Because dental implants are designed to replace entire teeth all the way from the roots up to the crown of the tooth it is crucial that the jawbone is healthy and able to support the dental implant. If the jawbone is not strong enough, the dental implant may fail.

Sometimes, a dental patient will not naturally have thick enough jawbone to receive a dental implant. Having a jawbone that is too thin or too soft does not necessarily disqualify a patient from receiving dental implants. In many cases, we can perform a bone graft to help give your jawbone the extra support it needs.

A bone graft procedure is when a doctor such as our Elk Grove Village dentist, Dr. Advani, uses natural or synthetic grafting material to transplant tissue onto your existing jawbone. This natural grafting material can be made from a bone within your own body or an outside cadaver or donor bone. Bone grafting allows us to transfer bone or material with bone-like properties onto your jawbone so that new bone can eventually grow around the bone graft.

Sinus Lifts

Bone grafting is made possible through sinus lifts. Our Elk Grove Village dental office offers two different types of sinus lifts to our patients: crestal and lateral. If you need a sinus lift for your dental implants in Elk Grove Village, IL, we encourage you to meet with Dr. Advani. Whether you require a crestal sinus lift or a lateral sinus lift will depend on how much bone you currently have in your jawbone and how many dental implants you need to have placed.

So why are sinus lifts necessary? Your sinuses are located on both sides of your nose. During a sinus lift, your sinuses are moved upward to make room for the bone graft that will eventually be placed between your sinuses and your jawbone. Sinus lifts are often necessary when patients do not have enough bone for dental implants and need a bone graft to move forward.

If you need a sinus lift in Elk Grove Village, IL, Dr. Advani will first need to take comprehensive x-rays and scans of your mouth so he can better understand the layout of your mouth and your sinuses. Sinus lifts are surgeries where precision is very important.

During the sinus lift procedure, the membrane lining your sinus is moved upward, or lifted, away from your jaw. Bone is then added above your jaw using the bone-graft materials previously discussed. Once the sinus lift is complete, your jaw will need time to heal and for the jawbone to fuse with the bone graft. This process usually takes around six months. Once the area has healed, we can generally place your dental implant(s) right away.

The Advantages of Dental Implants

Dental implants restore your teeth to full functionality and help avoid the common problems associated with missing teeth. Unlike dentures or partials, which may slip at the wrong moment and limit the foods you can enjoy, dental implants allow you to resume your life freely and confidently, enjoying the foods you love and smiling and laughing whenever you feel like it.

Providing Oral Care for Dental Implants

Because dental implants integrate into your jawbone, they become part of your body. They don t even need special or specific care; just brush your teeth and use floss regularly and see your dentist every six months for examinations and cleanings. Dental implants never decay like natural teeth, but no restoration or tooth replacement protect you from gum disease, so it’s crucial to get regular checkups.
