Why should crooked teeth be corrected?

Are crooked teeth really that big of a concern? Many patients wonder if misaligned teeth are really worth fixing. The answer is a resounding, "yes!" Aesthetics aside, even mildly misaligned teeth can set the stage for future dental problems, including cavities, gum disease, and worn down or fractured teeth.

When your teeth are crooked or overlap, it isn’t easy to brush them properly, and you may completely miss several surfaces of your teeth. While we can try to clean these areas at your regular teeth cleaning appointments, your toothbrush is really your first-line preventive therapy for avoiding cavities. If bacteria and acids are allowed to remain on your teeth for any length of time, plaque forms, and creates the perfect environment for cavities and gum disease.

If crowded teeth are your problem, you have probably already noticed that flossing your teeth correctly can be challenging. When plaque and tartar are allowed to develop beneath the gum line, gum disease is likely to result.

People with an overbite, underbite, or crossbite are susceptible to headaches and jaw pain. This is because their teeth are handling pressures in a way they were never designed to. Over time, this can cause enamel to erode and eventually lead to cracked or broken teeth.

So, orthodontics is designed to give you a beautiful smile that functions correctly. Braces and clear aligners are an investment in your oral health future and crucial to maintaining a healthy smile for life.

How can traditional braces help me?

Braces or "traditional orthodontic treatment" is a great option to correct crooked teeth, spaced teeth, and teeth that are simply not in the correct position. The best age to correct the position of teeth with braces is ages 13 to 19. People can have braces at any age, even as adults.

Many children and teenagers need braces to correct the position of their teeth to avoid bigger problems in the future. Braces work very well to move teeth to the correct position because they put 24/7 pressure on them to move them correctly. All teenagers should have a consultation regarding braces once all of their adult teeth have erupted.

How will Invisalign improve my smile?

Invisalign, sometimes referred to as invisible braces, uses aligners worn throughout the day and night. The design of the aligners gently guides your teeth into their correct places. Instead of returning to our office to have wires adjusted, you switch to the next aligner in the series when it’s time.

You remove Invisalign when you eat and clean them, which gives them an advantage over braces. There are no limitations to the foods you can eat as long as you brush your teeth before replacing your aligners or your appliance.

After treatment, you ll have a straight smile that you can be proud of!

What are the biggest differences between braces and Invisalign?

Braces rely on wires and brackets to bring your teeth in line across the treatment timeframe. You visit our dental office periodically for wire adjustments and so your dentist can track your progress.

Invisalign is a virtually invisible clear aligner. You wear your aligner continuously unless you are eating or brushing your teeth, changing it out every few weeks to bring your teeth in line.

The treatment timeframe for braces and Invisalign are similar. Depending on your orthodontic issues, your treatment can take anywhere from six months to two years. Your dentist gives you a timeline during your consultation. And your dentist may recommend traditional braces for more severe cases.

What happens after orthodontics?

Your dentist designs a custom retainer to keep your teeth in their new straight positions after your orthodontic treatment. This aligner is made of smooth and comfortable plastic and is a fantastic way to protect your investment by preventing orthodontic relapse.
