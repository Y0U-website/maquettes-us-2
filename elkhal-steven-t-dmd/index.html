The practice

All general dentists receive endodontics training in dental school. However, for more challenging cases, a general dentist may refer their patient to endodontic specialists.

Have you been told you that you need root canal treatment? If so, you’re not alone. Millions of teeth are treated and saved each year with root canal, or endodontic treatment.

In rare cases, root canal therapy fails to work as expected. An additional procedure may be able to support healing and save your tooth. If you have pain or discomfort in a previously treated tooth.

A pulpotomy is also referred to as a “baby root canal” because it is usually done on baby teeth. It is a very common procedure in children and has a reasonable good prognosis of success.

Apexification is a treatment used to seal an open apex. The apex is the bottom part of a tooth’s root.

Even with proper brushing and flossing habits, and regular dental checkups, you can have problems with your teeth and gums.

An apicoectomy is done only after a tooth has had at least one root canal procedure and retreatment has not been successful or is not possible.
