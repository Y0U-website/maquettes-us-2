FAQs about all-on-4 dental implants in Elkins Park, PA

All on 4 dental implants in Elkins Park, PA, are a full-arch tooth replacement approach that uses four implants to support a fixed set of prosthetic teeth. The implants integrate with the jawbone to provide stable support, allowing patients to restore chewing function and comfort without relying on removable dentures.

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution when properly cared for. With consistent oral hygiene, regular dental visits, and healthy lifestyle habits, implants can function for 20 years or more.
