Ella DerMinassian, DDS, provides highly personalized and attentive dental care for men, women, and children of all ages at Fallsgrove Park Dental in Rockville, Maryland. Dr. DerMinassian is known for her compassionate and gentle dental care, and she specializes in helping patients with high dental anxiety to feel comfortable and confident while under her care.

Dr. DerMinassian, often known as Dr. D, is a native of Montgomery County in Maryland and graduated with a Bachelor of Arts in history from the University of Maryland-College Park. She earned her dental degree from Howard University College of Dentistry in Washington, DC, where she also completed a one-year residency in advanced education in general dentistry.

Dr. DerMinassian also performs minor oral surgery when needed, such as bone grafts, extractions, and gingivectomies. She uses the most advanced dental tools, technology, and techniques, including the innovative iTero® scanners.
