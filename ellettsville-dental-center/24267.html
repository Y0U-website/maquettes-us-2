Straighten Your Smile with Clear Aligners

For adults who want to straighten their teeth but prefer a solution that’s more discreet than traditional metal braces, there’s Invisalign. With Invisalign’s clear, custom-made mouthpiece-like plastic aligners, you’ll be able to gradually straighten your smile while maintaining your normal social and professional commitments, with no one being the wiser!

With Invisalign, every two weeks you will receive a new set of clear aligners. Each set of aligners, which are to be worn 20-22 hours per day, differs slightly from the set before and serves to continue gradually moving your teeth into alignment.

In addition to being practically invisible, Invisalign clear braces are removable, This means that, unlike metal braces, with Invisalign, there are no restrictions regarding what you can eat. Additionally, not only are Invisalign aligners extremely easy to clean, their removal allows you to brush and floss as you normally would, reducing your risk of dental issues both during and after the treatment.

When Should Someone Start Orthodontic Treatment?

The ideal time to start orthodontic treatment varies depending on individual circumstances.

Typically, orthodontic treatment begins between the ages of 9 and 14. During this time, most permanent teeth have erupted, allowing an orthodontist to properly evaluate alignment and identify any potential issues. However, as an individual is still developing during these ages, the jaw and facial structures are still highly receptive to orthodontic interventions.

It s worth noting, however, that orthodontic treatment can be initiated at any age, provided an individual’s teeth, gums, and jawbone are healthy. Of course, one should consult with an orthodontist to determine their suitability for treatment, as well as to get an assessment of their specific needs and a personalized plan for achieving a healthy, confident smile.
