Who We Are

Our enthusiastic team is the heart of our practice.

The employment of the highest caliber people allows us to execute our mission. All team members demonstrate a strong commitment to growth. Their positive mental attitude is always evident in their willingness to excel to new levels and work together as a team.

We are dedicated and compassionate people who are excited about our chosen profession. We view the attainment of excellence as a journey requiring constant growth and openness to change.

What We Believe

Our commitment is to provide the highest quality of dental care through excellence in clinical care and personal relationships. Through this our patients receive improved smiles while being guided toward overall health. It is our goal to keep your teeth healthy for a lifetime.

For your comfort, we offer state-of-the-art technology and use the highest quality dental materials. At your first visit, you will meet with Dr. Ellis and we will discuss any concerns you have about your teeth or smile. Then, we will thoroughly examine your teeth and gums, take any necessary x-rays and perform several exams, including an oral cancer screening.

Ellis Dental Philanthropy

Dr. Ellis routinely volunteers in community activities aimed at improving dental health care. Each year she participates in the annual Give Kids a Smile program - a not-for-profit program which continues working to eliminate the silent epidemic of dental disease for the millions of children who cannot sleep, eat properly, pay attention in school, or simply smile.

In 2009 and 2012, Dr. Ellis traveled to Huttersfield, Jamaica on a volunteer dental mission trip. She participated in the 1000 Smiles Project, a humanitarian dental mission that brings oral health treatment to those in need across Jamaica. Dental services are free of charge to children and adults including cleanings, fillings, sealants and extractions.

The experience was extremely rewarding and inspiring. Patients lined up and waited in the Jamaican heat, sometimes for several hours, to have their teeth cleaned and treated.

Dr. Ellis hopes to soon return for her third mission!
