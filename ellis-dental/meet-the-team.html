Dr. Kelly O’Shaughnessy

Doctor of Dental Surgery

Dr. Kelly O’Shaughnessy was born and raised in St. Louis, MO. She grew up in South County, graduated from Oakville High School, and attended Southern Illinois University in Edwardsville for her undergraduate studies. Dr. O’Shaughnessy received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Missouri Kansas City School of Dentistry and advanced her studies through an Advanced Education of General Dentistry program through NYU Langone in Juneau, AK.

Dr. O’Shaughnessy is a member of the American Dental Association, Missouri Dental Association, Greater St. Louis Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentists. Her philosophy centers around the compassionate, comprehensive, and uncompromising care for every patient. Dr. O’Shaughnessy loves spending time with her family, rock climbing, hiking, and camping.

Bachelor of Science Southern Illinois University, Edwardsville

Advanced Education in General Dentistry Program New York University (NYU), Juneau, AK, Langone Dental Medicine

Dr. Kathryn Brennan

Doctor of Dental Surgery

Dr. Kathryn Brennan grew up in Saint Louis, Missouri, and received her undergraduate degree in Biological Sciences from DePaul University. She went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Marquette University School of Dentistry, where she graduated with honors.

Dr. Brennan enjoys helping patients overcome their dental-related fear and anxiety. She believes in creating an environment that fosters effective communication and mutual respect, helping her patients feel valued and comfortable. Dr. Brennan understands the important role dental health plays in a patient’s overall health and well-being, and is passionate about delivering comprehensive and individualized care.

Dr. Brennan is a member of the American Dental Association, the Missouri Dental Association, the Greater St. Louis Dental Society, and the Missouri Valley Study Club. She volunteers annually with Give Kids A Smile and Mission of Mercy to provide dental care to children and adults in need. In her free time, Dr. Brennan enjoys watching Cardinals baseball with her husband, completing home improvement projects, and spending time with her rescue dog.

Doctor of Dental Surgery Marquette University

Bachelor of Science, Biological Sciences DePaul University

Dr. Madison Berg

Doctor of Dental Medicine

Dr. Madison Berg was born and raised in St. Louis, Missouri. She attended the University of Florida for both her undergraduate and dental training, earning her Bachelor of Science in Nutritional Sciences before going on to receive her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Florida College of Dentistry.

Dr. Berg enjoys all aspects of general dentistry and is especially passionate about creating a comfortable, positive experience for her patients. She values building strong relationships and helping patients feel at ease while achieving healthy, confident smiles.

Dr. Berg is committed to continuing her education and staying current with the latest advancements in dentistry. She is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry, Greater Saint Louis Dental Society, Missouri Dental Association, American Dental Association and American Association of Women Dentists.

In her free time, Dr. Berg enjoys traveling, trying new restaurants, running, playing pickleball, and spending time with family and friends.

Doctor of Dental Medicine University of Florida

Bachelor of Science University of Florida

Sandy

Registered Dental Hygienist

Sandy is a native of St. Louis, MO and graduated from St. Louis Community College at Forest Park in 1995. She is licensed in infiltration anesthesia, nitrous oxide administration and certified in laser and oral conscious sedation dentistry. Sandy is excited and honored to be a part of the Ellis Dental Team. Being able to work together with her patients to achieve their goals for overall health and a healthy smile is her favorite part of her job. She’s at her happiest when helping others whether it be in the office or in her day to day endeavors.

Sandy met her husband in 1996 when he sat in her chair. They are blessed with one daughter who is in the high school AFJROTC program and is excited to see where her dreams will soar. She enjoys spending time with her family and pets, cycling, DIY projects, movies, gardening, the great outdoors, music and going to concerts.
