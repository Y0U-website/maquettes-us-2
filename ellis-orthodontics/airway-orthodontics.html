Signs in Adults

  • Loud, chronic snoring
  • Feeling tired even after a full night’s sleep
  • Waking up with headaches
  • Observed pauses in breathing during sleep (apnea)
  • Difficulty losing weight
  • High blood pressure or other cardiovascular issues

Our Approach to Airway Orthodontics

  • Encourage proper jaw growth and development.
  • Widen the dental arches to create more space for the tongue.
  • Improve nasal breathing and discourage mouth breathing.
  • Position the jaw to keep the airway open during sleep.
