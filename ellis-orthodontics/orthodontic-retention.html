Orthodontic Retainers

  • Essix (Clear) Retainer: This popular option looks like an Invisalign® aligner. It’s a clear, thin tray that fits snugly over your teeth, making it virtually invisible.

Fixed (Permanent) Retainers

A fixed retainer consists of a very thin, custom-fit wire that is bonded to the back of your front teeth (usually the lower ones).

  • Benefits: It’s always working, so you don’t have to remember to wear it. It’s also completely hidden from view.
  • Special Care: You’ll need to use a floss threader or a water flosser to clean around it, but it quickly becomes a normal part of your routine.
