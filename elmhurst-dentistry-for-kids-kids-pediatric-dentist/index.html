The practice

Whether we are your child’s primary dental home or working with your general dentist on a referral basis, we’re here to deliver the best care. Our focus on preventive care helps children develop healthy smiles that last a lifetime. Using state-of-the-art technology, we aim to make each visit a positive experience and ensure your child’s smile is as healthy as it is beautiful.

We look forward to meeting you and caring for your child’s dental needs!

Pediatric dentists have had 2-3 years of additional, specialized training to care for young children and adolescents. From the first tooth to adolescence, we help your child develop a healthy smile and manage situations that are unique only to children.

Our office is designed to ease nervous children (and their parents!) with a kid-friendly atmosphere. We take extra time to understand the personality of each child and our team works hard to accommodate every personality.
