About Us

Dr. Gotsch founded Elmhurst Smiles with a patient-centered approach, recognizing that he’s not just treating teeth but caring for individuals. Understanding the crucial connection between oral health and overall well-being, our team prioritizes comprehensive care that considers your entire health profile. We believe that oral health, the enjoyment of meals, and even your smile contribute to your emotional wellness.

After ensuring the health of your teeth and gums, we take the time to understand your unique priorities and concerns. Whether it’s achieving a brighter smile or addressing orthodontic needs later in life, we’re here to provide personalized solutions. With a full range of services and collaborative partnerships with specialists, we’re dedicated to addressing even the most complex dental issues.

If you’re seeking answers and compassionate dental care, please reach out to Elmhurst Smiles at (630) 834-8055 or text us at (708) 704-6746 . We eagerly anticipate meeting you and supporting your oral health journey.
