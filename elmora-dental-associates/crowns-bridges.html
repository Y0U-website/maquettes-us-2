Adjacent teeth shift, biting becomes uneven, and what starts as one problem can become several.

Dental crowns solve this by covering the damaged tooth completely — protecting it from further wear while restoring its shape and strength. The crown is custom-made to match the color and contour of your surrounding teeth, so it blends in naturally.

Bridges take care of the gap when a tooth is lost altogether. Two crowns anchor to the teeth on either side of the space, with an artificial tooth in between filling the gap. The result is a stable, natural-looking restoration that lets you chew and speak normally.

Common Questions About Crowns & Bridges How do I know if I need a crown or a bridge? A crown is used to cover and protect a single damaged or weakened tooth, while a bridge fills the gap left by one or more missing teeth by anchoring to the teeth on either side. At Elmora Dental, we&#x27;ll evaluate your specific situation and explain clearly which option makes the most sense — and why — before we do anything.

Will a crown or bridge look like my natural teeth?

Yes. Our crowns and bridges are custom-made to match the color, shape, and size of your surrounding teeth. Most people won&#x27;t be able to tell the difference between a well-fitted crown and a natural tooth. Looking natural is something we take seriously at Elmora Dental.

How many appointments will I need to get a crown?

Getting a crown typically requires two visits. At the first appointment, we prepare the tooth and place a temporary crown. At the second visit, we fit and cement your permanent custom crown. We&#x27;ll keep you informed at each step so you always know what&#x27;s coming.

Does insurance cover crowns and bridges?

Most dental insurance plans provide partial coverage for crowns and bridges when they&#x27;re medically necessary, though the amount varies by plan. Our team will check your benefits and let you know your estimated out-of-pocket cost before treatment begins. We want affordable dental treatment to be a realistic option for your family.

How long will a crown or bridge last?

With proper care, dental crowns and bridges typically last 10 to 15 years or longer. Regular brushing, flossing — especially under a bridge — and routine checkups at our Elizabeth, NJ office will help extend their lifespan. If anything starts to shift or loosen, come in and we&#x27;ll address it early.
