Common Questions About Orthodontic Treatment What&#x27;s the difference between braces and Invisalign — which one is better? Neither is universally better — it depends on your specific teeth and your lifestyle. Traditional braces are fixed and well-suited for more complex alignment cases. Invisalign aligners are removable and nearly invisible, which many teens and adults prefer. At Elmora Dental, we offer both options and will help you choose based on what actually fits your needs.

At what age should my child start orthodontic treatment?

Orthodontic evaluations are typically recommended around age seven, when the first permanent molars come in. This doesn&#x27;t mean treatment starts at seven — it just allows us to spot developing issues early and plan accordingly. Many patients begin active treatment between ages 11 and 14, though orthodontics can be effective at any age.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on how much correction is needed. Many cases range from 12 to 24 months, though some straightforward alignments can be completed faster. We&#x27;ll give you a realistic estimate at your consultation so you can plan ahead — especially if there&#x27;s a graduation, wedding, or other milestone on the horizon.

Does insurance cover braces or Invisalign?

Many insurance plans include an orthodontic benefit that can apply to both braces and Invisalign. The covered amount varies by plan, so our team will review your benefits before treatment starts. We also work with patients on payment plans to help make orthodontic care an affordable part of your family&#x27;s dental treatment.
