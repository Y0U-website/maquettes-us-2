Dental Implants

Missing teeth can impact your ability to eat, speak clearly, and damage self-esteem and confidence. Fortunately, modern dentistry offers more options than ever for replacing missing teeth, so there is no reason to keep suffering. At Elmwood Family Dental, we offer implant dentistry services to our patients to restore the function and appearance of their teeth.

Dental implants are artificial roots for replacement teeth. The implant is made of titanium and surgically placed in the jaw, where it fuses and holds the artificial replacement tooth in place for a permanent solution tor missing teeth. Single and multiple tooth implants are available to provide the best treatment for each patient.

We offer implant dentistry services to meet the unique needs of each patient, whether an individual is missing one tooth, many teeth, or all the teeth. Dr. Singer provides dental implant services for single tooth implants and multiple tooth replacement, as well as full replacement. We will help you find the best solution to improve your dental health.

Don’t let that missing tooth, or teeth stand in the way of your life or lower you self-confidence. Options are available for our patients at Elmwood Family Dental.
