Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Resolution Dental Plan the same as dental insurance?

No. The Resolution Dental Plan is a discount membership plan, not insurance. Members pay a low annual fee for access to free preventive care and discounted rates on other services at participating Elstar Dental & Braces offices, and pay providers directly for services rendered.

Can I add my whole family to one plan?

Yes. The Family Plan covers your entire household for $99 a year, just $10 more than the individual rate.

Are there any waiting periods or exclusions?

No. Coverage is active as soon as you enroll, with no waiting periods and no exclusions on covered services.
