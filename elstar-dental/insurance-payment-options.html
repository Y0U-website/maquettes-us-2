Frequently Asked Questions

Does Elstar Dental & Braces accept Medicaid?

Yes. We accept Medicaid at all 2 of our Texas locations and file your claims on your behalf, so there’s nothing extra for you to manage.

Yes. If we’re not in-network with your specific plan, we’ll still file your claims for you so you get the most out of your out-of-network benefits.
