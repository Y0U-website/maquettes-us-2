Frequently Asked Questions

How many Elstar Dental & Braces locations are there in Texas?

Elstar Dental & Braces has two locations across Texas in Eagle Pass and McAllen.

Do all Elstar Dental & Braces locations accept Medicaid?

Yes. All two Texas locations accept Medicaid and CHIP , and we file your claims on your behalf.

Will I need a crown after a root canal?

Yes. Same-day and emergency appointments are available at Elstar Dental & Braces locations across Texas. Contact your nearest office directly to get scheduled.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. All Elstar Dental & Braces locations are welcoming new patients, with same-week availability for routine exams and cleanings .

What services are available at Elstar Dental & Braces locations?

Services vary by location and include general dentistry , children’s dentistry , orthodontics and braces , oral surgery , and endodontics . Find your nearest location to see the full list of services available there.

Do you offer payment plans for patients without insurance?

Yes. The Resolution Dental Discount Plan and AcceptCare financing are available at all Elstar Dental & Braces locations to make care affordable for every family.
