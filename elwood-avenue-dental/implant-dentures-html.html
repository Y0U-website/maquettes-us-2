What Are Implant Dentures?

Implant dentures are lifelike dental restorations designed to mimic the appearance and structure of natural teeth held in place with small titanium rods surgically inserted into the jawbone. This allows them to remain securely in position and provide superior chewing power, which helps patients remain comfortable and enjoy diverse and nutritious diets. After evaluating your oral health during your consultation at our Jenks office, our dentist may recommend:

Fixed/Permanent Implant Dentures

Fixed implant dentures are designed to remain attached to the dental implants unless removed by a qualified oral health professional like one of the team members at Elwood Avenue Dental. These devices are typically held in place with four to six strategically placed dental implants, and they can function exactly like real teeth.

Removable Implant Dentures

Removable implant dentures are designed to be d5etached from their implants by the patient for hygiene purposes. These restorations usually require only three to five dental implants, which makes them great for many patients with low jawbone density.

The Implant Denture Process

The team at Elwood Avenue Dental is proud to perform the entire dental implant placement process at our office, which can help patients enjoy a comfortable and stress-free treatment experience. The four steps to receiving dental implants are:

  • Consultation: Our dentist will evaluate the patient’s oral health and determine the best way to replace their lost teeth. Some patients may need restorative treatments to address oral infections before they can safely receive implants.
  • Surgery: Our dentist will surgically insert a dental implant into your jawbone. This procedure is always done after administering an appropriate anesthetic to ensure the patient’s comfort. The implants will need several months to fuse with the jawbone after the treatment.
  • Abutments: After the surgical sites have healed, the patient will come back to our office to have abutment pieces placed on their implants through another minor surgical procedure. We’ll also take bite impressions during this appointment.
  • Dentures: About two weeks after receiving abutments, the patient will return to our office to have their denture attached. After any final adjustments, they’ll be ready to show off their new smile.

Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

Most adults who are missing most or all of their teeth can be excellent candidates for implant dentures as long as they are in excellent oral health. While untreated oral infections will disqualify you from the treatment, we can address these problems at our office, which may make you an excellent candidate later. Conditions like pregnancy, diabetes, cancer, osteoporosis, and hemophilia can complicate the process, but they do not necessarily disqualify you from implant dentures.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Traditional dentures have allowed many people to enjoy beautiful smiles after tooth loss, but these devices can be uncomfortable since they tend to slide about on the gum tissue and usually must be replaced every seven years or so. Dentures supported by dental implants, on the other hand, are held securely in place, which allows them to:

  • Prevent irritation of the gum tissue.
  • Exert superior chewing power.
  • Remain stable so they do not fall out during speech or chewing.
  • Last much longer than traditional dentures, which can save a patient large sums of money over time.
  • Restore the stimulation the jawbone needs to stay healthy, which can prevent or possibly reverse the process of jawbone atrophy.
