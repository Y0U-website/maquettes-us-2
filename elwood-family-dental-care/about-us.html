Meet the Team at Elwood Family Dental Care

At Elwood Family Dental Care, our goal is to give you and your family a dental experience like no other. We offer comprehensive dental care to patients of all ages, so whether it’s your child’s first visit, you want to explore enhancing your smile with cosmetic dentistry, or your parents are ready to learn about their tooth replacement options, we’ve got you covered.

From routine exams and checkups to emergency dental care, we are equipped to care for all your dental needs. Our focus is on building relationships; we do this by spending time getting to know you and listening to your unique needs before exploring the best ways to help you achieve a healthy, beautiful smile that lasts a lifetime.

We invite you to visit and discover what sets us apart!

Dr. Paige Elwood, DDS

Dr. Paige Elwood is proud to serve the Yankton community as the owner and dentist at Elwood Family Dental Care. A native of Yankton, Dr. Elwood brings a personal touch to her practice, caring for generations of families she has known for years. She finds immense joy in working with patients of all ages and loves the variety that dentistry brings to her daily life.

Dr. Elwood earned her undergraduate degree from the University of South Dakota before completing her dental education at the University of Nebraska Medical Center College of Dentistry. She began her professional journey in 2009, practicing at Neighborhood Dental in Yankton for nine years. In 2017, she fulfilled a longtime dream by acquiring Dr. Grossenburg’s practice and rebranding it as Elwood Family Dental Care.

For Dr. Elwood, dentistry is more than just a profession; it’s a chance to give back to the community that shaped her. Inspired by a pivotal conversation with her orthodontist just before high school graduation, she found her calling in dental care and has never looked back.

At the heart of her practice is a dynamic and compassionate team. We not only take care of our patients, but we take care of each other, says Dr. Elwood. While our main focus is patient care, we make sure to have fun and enjoy each other’s company while doing it.

Outside the office, Dr. Elwood is an avid supporter of her children’s sports and a proud fan of the Yankton High School Bucks and Gazelles. She s passionate about her community and schools, and when she’s not cheering from the sidelines, you might find her at a country music concert; she’s been to at least 26! A lover of true crime documentaries, Dr. Elwood also enjoys binge-watching Netflix in her downtime.

With deep local roots and a genuine passion for patient care, Dr. Elwood is dedicated to helping her patients smile with confidence while ensuring that her team and practice are filled with positivity and laughter every step of the way.
