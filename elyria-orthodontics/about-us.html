Free Consultations

The teen years are the most common time for orthodontic treatment because most permanent teeth will likely be in place. Dr. Marshall will be able to easily recognize issues with spacing, crowding, bite, and alignment in this age range, and your teen’s bone and jaw structure are formed enough to allow orthodontic treatment to begin.

Untreated dental problems can have a lasting impact on your teen’s oral and overall health. If your teen has any dental issues, we recommend a scheduling a complimentary consultation with Dr. Marshall to see how orthodontics could benefit them.
