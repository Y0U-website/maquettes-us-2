Orthodontics For All Ages

Orthodontics For Kids

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends kids see an orthodontist for their first orthodontic evaluation by the age of seven. By this time, most kids will have a mix of baby teeth and permanent teeth, making it easier for orthodontists to diagnose and correct tooth and jaw problems before they have the chance to become more severe.

Orthodontics For Adults

Orthodontic treatment isn’t just for children and teens. One in every five orthodontic patients is over the age of 21. Many adults are seeking out orthodontic treatment later in life because they understand the importance of maintaining their oral health and they want to gain confidence by attaining their dream smile.

Treatment Options

Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces are the most recognized form of orthodontic treatment. Despite their long-standing popularity, recent technological advancements have made metal braces smaller and thinner, enhancing comfort and oral hygiene. This makes them a reliable and effective option for patients seeking to correct their dental alignment.

Clear Braces

For patients desiring a more discreet orthodontic solution, we offer clear (ceramic) braces. These braces provide the same effectiveness as traditional metal braces but are significantly less noticeable. Although clear braces are slightly more expensive than metal braces, their subtle appearance makes them a preferred choice for teens and adults who want to avoid the metallic look.

INVISALIGN

Invisalign offers a modern approach to teeth alignment using a series of clear, removable aligners custom-made to fit your teeth. These aligners gradually shift your teeth into the desired position, providing a discreet and comfortable alternative to traditional braces. Invisalign is an excellent choice for those seeking a nearly invisible method to achieve a straighter smile with the convenience of removable aligners.

Surgical Orthodontics

In certain cases, orthodontic treatment alone cannot fully correct an orthodontic problem. Surgical orthodontics, or corrective jaw surgery, is necessary for adults needing surgery in combination with orthodontic treatment to achieve a functional and healthy bite. This surgery addresses skeletal issues affecting the ability to bite, chew, and speak. If surgical orthodontics is appropriate for your treatment plan, Dr. Marshall will collaborate with a local oral surgeon to ensure your jaws and teeth are positioned optimally for aesthetics and function.

BOTOX

Botox is a versatile treatment known for smoothing out wrinkles and providing additional benefits such as managing chronic migraines and bruxism (teeth grinding or clenching). It can also be used to improve gummy smiles, offering both cosmetic and therapeutic advantages.

Can Botox help with TMJ disorder?

Yes, Botox is an effective treatment for TMJ (temporomandibular joint) disorder. A 2012 study showed that Botox significantly decreased pain and improved jaw mobility for up to three months after treatment. Another study reported symptom improvement in 90 % of participants, and ongoing research continues to affirm its benefits.

Botox works by relaxing the overactive muscles around the jaw, helping to alleviate TMJ symptoms like pain and stiffness. While Botox is often used as a complementary therapy alongside night guards or physical therapy, it can sometimes be sufficient as a standalone solution.

Here’s what you might notice after a Botox treatment for TMJ:

Reduced or eliminated headaches caused by tension or clenching.

Lower stress levels due to muscle relaxation.

Improved sleep quality as your jaw muscles are less likely to clench during the night.

Protection against bruxism, reducing wear and tear on your teeth.

Less sensitivity in your teeth, as clenching and grinding diminish.

Botox is a versatile and impactful solution to help TMJ sufferers feel relief and improve their quality of life.

Can my braces be installed the same day as a new patient exam?

In certain cases, yes! If your orthodontic evaluation determines that you’re a good candidate and all necessary preparations are complete, braces can be installed on the same day as your new patient exam. This allows you to start your journey to a straighter smile immediately.

Can I get Botox or teeth whitening while my child is getting their braces put in?

Absolutely! We understand that parents are often multitasking. You can take advantage of the time while your child is receiving their orthodontic treatment to enhance your own smile or get Botox treatments. It’s a convenient way to care for both your child’s and your personal dental or cosmetic needs in one visit.
