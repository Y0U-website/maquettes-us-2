Cosmetic Contouring

Dental cosmetic contouring, also known as tooth reshaping, is a procedure that involves the removal of small amounts of tooth enamel to alter the shape, length, or surface of teeth. This minimally invasive treatment is often used to correct minor imperfections such as uneven teeth, slight overlaps, or small chips. The process involves a thorough examination, sometimes including X-rays, to ensure the teeth are healthy enough for contouring. Using a laser or drill, the dentist carefully sculpts the tooth’s enamel, then smooths and polishes the teeth for a natural look.

Botox/Xeomin

Can be used to reduce wrinkles as well as a treatment for temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders, which involve dysfunction or pain in the jaw joint and the muscles that control jaw movement. Botox for TMJ is usually considered when other treatments, like physical therapy, oral appliances, or medications, have not provided sufficient relief. A consultation with a healthcare provider or a specialist, such as a dentist or oral surgeon, is necessary to determine if Botox is appropriate for a specific case.

SureSmile Aligners

SureSmile ® Clear Aligners are an orthodontic treatment that works to gradually straighten your teeth with custom-fit, removable clear aligners.

Your doctor will digitally scan your teeth, then partner with the SureSmile ® Digital Lab to create an individualized treatment plan that enables your aligners to move your teeth from your first aligner to your last.
