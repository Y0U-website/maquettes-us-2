Clear Retainers from EM…

Remember, it is crucial to wear your clear retainers as instructed by your dental professional. Consistency in wearing them will help maintain the alignment of your teeth and protect the investment you made in achieving your desired smile.

Incorporating clear retainers into your post-orthodontic routine can provide long-term benefits, giving you the confidence of knowing that your smile remains in its optimal alignment. Contact us and explore if clear retainers are the right choice for you and start preserving the stunning smile you&#x27;ve worked so hard to achieve.

  • Impression or Digital Scanning : To create your custom clear retainers, your dental professional will take an impression of your teeth or use advanced digital scanning technology to create a precise 3D model. This step ensures that your retainers fit snugly and comfortably.
  • Retainer Fabrication : Using the impression or digital scan, a dental laboratory will fabricate your clear retainers. Highly skilled technicians will craft them to fit your teeth perfectly, providing the necessary support and stability.
  • Fitting and Instructions : Once your retainers are ready, you will return to your orthodontist or dentist for the fitting. They will ensure that your clear retainers fit properly and provide you with detailed instructions on how to wear and care for them. They will also discuss the recommended duration for wearing the retainers each day.
  • Periodic Check-ups : Your orthodontist or dentist will schedule periodic check-ups to monitor the progress of your teeth and the effectiveness of the clear retainers. During these visits, any necessary adjustments or modifications to the retainers can be made to ensure optimal results.
