Types of Specialized Orthodontic Appliances We Provide

Our practice is skilled in providing a range of specialized appliances, including:

  • Palatal Expanders: Designed to widen the upper jaw, allowing teeth to fit better together.
  • Space Maintainers: Used to hold space for permanent teeth after premature loss of baby teeth.
  • Herbst Appliances: Encourages the proper growth of the jaw in patients with overbites.
