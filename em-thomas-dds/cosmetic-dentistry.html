Find the Right Kind of Cosmetic Services for You

Your overall wellness is central to our mission here at Radiance Family & Cosmetic Dentistry. Our cosmetic dentistry services include a variety of treatments to provide a balanced appearance.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are custom-made shells that cover the front surface of your teeth. If you are looking to correct imperfections such as chips or tooth gaps–porcelain veneers may be for you.

Our office utilizes e.max press veneers to maximize your cosmetic potential. The e.max material adds extra durability and requires less tooth preparation than crowns. On top of this perk, our custom smile designs provide a comfortable fit.

Porcelain veneers can last up to a decade with proper care and maintenance. These veneers are able to fit permanently to your tooth thanks to the preparation process. Before the veneers are placed, we will remove tooth enamel to make the product’s fit feel natural.

We are happy to talk through the process if you have any questions!

Dental Crowns

Tooth-colored crowns are placed over a damaged tooth to restore the shape, size, and strength of damaged or missing teeth while blending seamlessly with your natural smile.

We offer e.max, Zirconia, and traditional gold crowns to provide options that fit your personal preferences. E.max and Zircona crowns provide a more translucent, tooth-colored appearance. For those who would like to stick with the basics, conventional gold crowns provide a durable base to fit your day-to-day dental needs.

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Straighten your teeth discreetly with Invisalign, a series of clear removable aligners that provide a straighter smile in about 18 months. Our dental team loves how Invisalign makes orthodontic care seamless and provides our patients the opportunity to express themselves freely.

Invisalign makes orthodontic care flexible and cosmetic, which comes from how the aligners are made. These aligners are built similarly to a retainer, which allows you to remove them to eat and drink. The material we use for Invisalign aligners is also completely transparent, allowing you to perform needed treatment without noticeable and flashy appliances.

Invisalign clear aligners are created by first making digital impressions of your smile. We will then keep in touch with you and monitor your smile. Throughout treatment, we send you progressively tight aligners that will adjust your teeth to an ideal position. All the patient needs to do is wear the aligners around 23 hours a day and keep in touch with the dentist with any concerns.

Dental Bonding

There’s no need to look further to find a quick and cost-effective solution to repair minor tooth chips and cracks. Our bonding is tooth-colored and durable, providing cosmetic and practical results for your look.

Our custom smile designs also allow our staff to precisely place bonding to fit your smile, providing much-needed functionality right when you need it most. The material used in dental bonding, composite resin, is easily moldable and can be adjusted at any time.

Even the slightest dental deterioration can greatly impact your personal well-being. Dental bonding can provide peace of mind and a layer of protection.

Sedation Dentistry For Cosmetics Near Billings, MT

Dental anxiety or fear of pain in general can hold many patients back from cosmetic dentistry. Modern dental practices combat this fear with the help of sedation dentistry! Sedation dentistry is used to help ease any dental fear and anxiety, even for short cosmetic procedures.

Our office primarily uses nitrous oxide, more popularly known as “laughing gas.” This procedure uses a gas released through a mask to help the patient feel a little bit sleepy as the procedure takes place. This treatment eliminates potential pain from the procedure and drifts your attention away from the dental work, allowing Dr. Rath or our hygienists to perform the needed care as efficiently as possible.

If you have any questions about sedation dentistry, feel free to give our office a call! Our head dentist Dr. Rath also comes with KOIS training, which specializes in providing a more elevated experience at the dentist’s office. We are happy to help patients from any background achieve a smile they will never forget, no matter how they feel about dental work.

Tips For Long-Term Results

Radiance Family & Cosmetic Dentistry aspires to create smiles that last. Though the cosmetic updates from our office are powerful, they are not invincible to the demands of everyday life. The longevity of your results depends on the specific procedure and your oral care routine.

Though each cosmetic tool maintains differently, here are some fundamental tips to help maintain your treatment for as long as possible:

Dr. Rath and our team will be sure to provide ample instructions specific to your personalized treatment during the first appointment.

  • Manage Your Diet: Hard, acidic, or tough foods eaten outside of moderation strain natural and treatment-given smiles. Paying attention to what you eat and brushing right after meals can help lessen the impact of unhealthy foods.
  • Protect Your Smile: Be sure to avoid habits that can cause damage to your teeth, such as tooth grinding or smoking. If you or your child plays high-contact sports, mouthguards can also help to reduce potential damage to your grin.
  • Schedule Regular Visits: No matter the discipline, professional coaching is a great way to make sure that you are on the right track. Biannual dental visits can help us detect potential tooth damage and fortify your teeth against dental infections.
