The Health Benefits of General Dentistry

General dentistry encompasses a variety of essential dental services aimed at maintaining and improving your oral health. From routine cleanings and exams to fillings and crowns, our goal is to provide thorough care that keeps your smile healthy and beautiful. Regular visits to Radiance Family & Cosmetic Dentistry for general dentistry services offer numerous benefits:

Our highly trained, all-female team of dental hygienists is ready to put you at ease as you receive your cleanings and exams.

  • Early Detection: Routine exams allow us to detect and address dental issues early, preventing dental damage, toothaches, and disarray to your smile.
  • Comprehensive Care: Radiance Family & Cosmetic Dentistry provides options that help you decide which form of care is ideal for your mouth and overall well-being.
  • Promoting Health: General cleanings help prevent cavities, staining, and other dental issues. Your teeth need professional cleaning to keep your mouth healthy.

Rejuvenate Smiles With General Dentistry

A healthy smile is about more than just fixing cosmetic imperfections. Our oral care approach seeks to analyze any potential concerns and prevent negative consequences to your smile before it’s too late.

That is why Radiance Family & Cosmetic Dentistry offers widespread general dentistry services designed to spot and address the needs of patients of all ages and circumstances.

Routine Cleanings and Exams

Our routine cleanings and exams help keep your teeth in good condition and looking beautiful. Dental hygienists remove plaque and calculus in your smile that you cannot clean with regular brushing and flossing, and exams catch dental issues before they become serious.

Dental Images and Oral Cancer Screenings

Our amazing dental technology and imaging allow Dr. Rath and our staff to analyze your smile using 90 percent less radiation than traditional X-ray techniques. Our cutting-edge techniques are even more precise and environmentally friendly than stereotypical dental options. These detection tools allow our patients to feel confident and spot advanced issues like never before.

At every comprehensive and periodic exam, Dr. Rath and our hygienists will perform oral cancer screenings to monitor and evaluate signs of oral cancer.

Periodontal Therapy

Gums maintain your smile in very important ways, more so than many people think. Gum disease or dental decay may require the need for additional dental work to reverse the damage to these areas. Radiance Family & Cosmetic Dentistry offers periodontal disease treatment to keep your smile appearing and feeling its best.

Remove Mercury Fillings

Our services include the safe removal of amalgam (mercury) fillings and the use of metal-free, tooth-colored restorations to promote a healthier, more confident smile. Dr. Rath offers holistic dentistry services that emphasize the integral connection between oral health and overall well-being. This whole-body approach focuses on treatments that enhance both dental and systemic health, addressing root causes of dental issues and avoiding potentially harmful materials like mercury fillings. Children’s Dental Care in Yellowstone County

Young minds of Billings deserve quality dentistry that cares about them, especially as they’re establishing a lifetime of essential dental habits. We genuinely want to build connections with your family and create a positive experience for your child.

A good experience at the dentist can determine if your child wants to return to the dentist for years to come.

Infant Exams

A common principle for dental care is to start treatment right before they are one year old. Dr. Rath is trained in providing dental care for all stages of life.

Our dental equipment and training allow our team to aid in your child’s development, encouraging them to reach important health milestones and maintain their smile.

Sealants

Children’s and adolescent’s teeth are often fragile as they are starting to grow in place. Dental sealants are an extra barrier that helps reduce the risk of cavities and dental damage.

Dr. Rath may recommend dental sealants if your child is more prone to dental decay due to deep grooves, inadequate home care, or even genetics. Eliminate the added stress and possibility of cavities by securing your child’s teeth.

Fluoride Treatment

Fluoride treatment is a common tool that is critical for pediatric oral care. These treatments fortify your child’s teeth early on against the demands of everyday life.

Dr. Rath will work with you to determine when and how much fluoride your child should be using at this stage in their life. Dental fluoride is easy to apply and offers enduring results.

Scaling and Root Planing

Don’t underestimate the power of deep dental cleaning. Scaling and root planing is a similar term for the same procedure. Our gentle hygienists will go under the gumline to eliminate calculus and bacteria that could be holding you back from pristine periodontal health.

Non-Metal Fillings

Dental decay shouldn’t have to lead to permanent damage that everyone can see when you smile! Radiance Family & Cosmetic Dentistry is happy to join dentists nationwide in providing white, transparent dental fillings that easily blend in with your smile.

Our non-metal fillings are matched in color and shape to your tooth using our expertise. You can feel confident knowing that your dental fillings can withstand the test of time and give you the freedom to express yourself the way you wish.

Teeth Whitening

Hard-to-remove stains can make it difficult to feel confident and express yourself clearly to others. Dr. Rath utilizes the Ultradent whitening products to help patients clear up dark spots or stains. Ultradent’s whitening products provide long-lasting results, a uniform smile, and support for tooth sensitivity. These whitening products also come free from harmful chemicals that can cause damage to your teeth.

Wisdom teeth extractions can be a common procedure for teens, especially wisdom tooth extraction. Dr. Rath and our team are able to address non-impacted wisdom teeth and general extractions to reduce dental pain and discomfort. These procedures are typically completed as more of a last resort for significant dental damage. Our team is focused on providing results by working with your natural teeth as much as possible.

Tooth and Gum Pain

Tooth and gum pain often stems from tooth infections, cavities, or injuries. Don’t let tooth pain keep you from digesting food, expressing yourself properly, or living your best life.

Bad Breath

Even the slightest of dental symptoms can throw you off guard. Chronic bad breath can be a symptom of gum disease or tooth decay, especially if common dental habits don’t seem to help.

Radiance Family & Cosmetic Dentistry offers thorough cleaning solutions that provide immediate results. Our scaling and root planing techniques will refine your smile to eliminate traces of bacteria that often hide in the crevices of your gums or teeth.

Damaging Dental Habits

Many patients deal with dental habits that disrupt their well-being. Bruxism—chronic teeth clenching—is one habit that can cause permanent damage in your smile, teeth, and jaw if not promptly handled.

We’re happy to provide dental appliances such as nightguards and athletic mouth guards to help fight against these habits. Our team can coach you or your child on ways to overcome a tooth grinding tendency and improve the feel of your smile.
