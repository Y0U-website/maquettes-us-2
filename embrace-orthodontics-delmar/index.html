We specialize in orthodontics. Braces are used to assist in aligning and straightening teeth to improve not only appearance but also a person’s bite and overall oral health. Having braces isn’t just about improving your physical smile; it may also have a psychosocial impact that helps improve a patient’s self-confidence.

Orthodontic treatment can correct malocclusions such as overbites, underbites, cross bites, deep bites, crooked teeth, and other imperfections in teeth and jaws. In addition to using orthodontic appliances to offer a straighter smile, orthodontic care can improve airway health and prevent sleep apnea.

Dental technology has improved over the years to include new options to help correct the above issues. A number of techniques and products are available that were not available just a few short years ago. The patient experience has improved, and today’s options are less conspicuous than the full braces that were common in the past.
