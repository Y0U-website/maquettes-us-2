Stop Hiding Your Smile, Start…

  • Placing the Implant: A small titanium post is placed into your jawbone, where it will serve as the root of your new tooth. The procedure is done under local anesthesia; which you’re likely to find much more comfortable than you might anticipate.
  • Healing: Over the next few months, your jawbone fuses with the titanium post. This is what makes implants so durable. You’re building the permanent foundation here.
  • Placing the Crown: Once healing is complete, your custom crown is attached, matched to your natural teeth so closely that it simply becomes part of your smile.
