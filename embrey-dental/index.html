The practice

If you are tired of temporary fixes, you are not alone. We look at the full picture of your oral health to understand what is really causing the issue. Your care is designed to support long-term stability, so you can move forward with fewer setbacks and more peace of mind.

Dr. Lim’s journey into dentistry was inspired by mentors who showed him that great care is about more than just technical skill. It’s about trust! Today, alongside his wife, Dr. Lim has built Embrey Dental to be the place where this belief comes to life.

Our patients come to us for more than routine care they come to regain confidence, restore function, and rediscover their smiles. Hear from real patients who have experienced the Embrey Dental difference.

From restoring missing teeth to enhancing natural beauty, our treatment plans are tailored to each patient’s needs. Explore real before-and-after results that highlight our commitment to precision, health, and aesthetics.

Whether you want a brighter smile, restored function, or a full cosmetic makeover, our team is here to guide you every step of the way.
