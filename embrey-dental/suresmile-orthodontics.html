The Health Benefits of Straight Smiles

A confident new smile is a wonderful goal, but the benefits of properly aligned teeth go much deeper.

  • Properly aligned teeth are easier to brush and floss effectively, reducing the risk of plaque buildup, tooth decay, and gum disease.
  • Correcting your bite can help alleviate issues like jaw pain, headaches, and uneven wear on your teeth.
  • A well-aligned smile is a cornerstone of sustainable, long-term oral health.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long will my SureSmile treatment take? Your treatment time will vary based on your smile’s condition. While many of the cases we see are completed in 12-18 months, Dr. Lim will be able to give you a clearer timeline during your consultation.
  • Will it feel uncomfortable to wear clear aligners? You’ll likely experience some mild pressure or tenderness for the first few days of wearing a new set of aligners. This is normal, since your teeth are gently moving! The feeling itself usually fades quickly and is less uncomfortable than traditional braces.
  • Is SureSmile different from Invisalign or other brands? SureSmile and Invisalign are both great clear aligner systems, and very similar.
