Wisdom Teeth

Wisdom Tooth Pain

Third molars, often called wisdom teeth, begin to push through the gums between the ages 16-22. These teeth, commonly referred to as wisdom teeth, can be troublesome if overcrowding exists. Ninety percent of most adults will have one of the thirds molars removed in a lifetime; most often due to an infected tooth or jaw swelling.

Why is it necessary to remove wisdom teeth?

As you approach adulthood, your dentist may tell you that your wisdom teeth need to be removed. This happens because there simply is not enough space in your upper or lower jaw for these additional teeth. The third molars often cause overcrowding and can cause gum swelling or jaw pain. If you begin to experience these symptoms, it may be time to have your wisdom teeth removed.
