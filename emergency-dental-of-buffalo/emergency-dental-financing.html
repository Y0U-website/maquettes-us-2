What payment options do you accept for emergency dental services?

We accept various payment methods, including credit/debit cards, and insurance plans. We also offer flexible financing options.

Can I use my dental insurance for emergency dental care?

Yes, you may! Our staff always checks to make sure your services can be covered by your insurance provider. In the instance that insurance may not cover your visit, we accept out of pocket pay in full payments or enroll in our payment plans.

Do you offer payment plans for emergency dental treatments?

Yes, we do offer payment plans. Click either one of our providers to learn more and enroll to get your services covered.

Is emergency dental care more expensive than regular dental services?

In most cases, emergency dental care is more expensive than normal dental care. The reason is we are able to get patients in the same day their painful toothache starts instead of waiting days or even weeks for your regular dentist to find an open appointment. We also utilize the latest dental equipment to get patients’ dental accidents not only fixed, but better than it was before.

Can I apply for financing if I don’t have dental insurance?

Absolutely! Our financing options are available to all patients, whether or not you have dental insurance. We’re committed to making quality care accessible to everyone.

Will I receive a detailed invoice for my emergency dental treatment?

Yes, you’ll receive a detailed invoice that outlines the services provided and their associated costs. We believe in transparency in billing.
