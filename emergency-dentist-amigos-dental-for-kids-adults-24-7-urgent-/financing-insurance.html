See If You Qualify — Apply in 60 Seconds

  • No Hard Credit Check - Applying won’t affect your credit score. Cherry uses a soft check to determine eligibility.
  • Approval in 60 Seconds - The application takes less than a minute to complete, right from your phone or in our office.
  • Up to $50,000 in Coverage - From a single filling to a full treatment plan, Cherry can cover a wide range of amounts.

We’ll Always Be Honest About What Things Cost

Before we begin any treatment, we’ll give you a clear picture of what it costs, what your insurance covers (if applicable), and your out-of-pocket responsibility. We’ll also let you know if Cherry financing is available for your specific treatment.

Our goal is to remove every barrier between you and a healthy smile, including financial ones. If you have questions or concerns about affording your care, just ask. Our team is here to help you find a path forward.
