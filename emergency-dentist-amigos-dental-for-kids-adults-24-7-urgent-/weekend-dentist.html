Dental Emergency on the Weekend? Don’t Wait Until Monday.

Weekend dental emergencies are one of the most common reasons patients end up in a hospital emergency room, not because the ER is the right place for dental care, but because they have nowhere else to go.

Hospital ERs are not equipped to treat dental problems. They can manage pain temporarily and prescribe antibiotics, but they cannot treat the underlying dental issue. You’ll pay significantly more for care that doesn’t solve the problem and still need to see a dentist afterward.

Amigos Dental for Kids & Adults handles true dental emergencies on weekends, the same day you walk in.

  • Severe toothache Intense, persistent tooth pain is often a sign of infection or abscess. We assess and treat the source of the pain, not just mask it.
  • Knocked-out tooth Time is critical. Get to us within 30–60 minutes for the best chance of saving the tooth. Call our emergency line on the way.
  • Dental abscess or facial swelling Swelling in the jaw or face accompanied by fever is a serious infection that needs immediate attention — not a wait-until-Monday situation.
  • Broken or cracked tooth A broken tooth causing pain or sensitivity needs to be assessed and stabilized to prevent further damage and infection.
  • Lost filling or crown Leaving a tooth exposed after a filling or crown falls out accelerates damage. We can re-cement or replace same-day in most cases.
  • Object stuck between teeth If you can’t remove it safely with floss, come in — don’t use sharp tools that can damage your gums.

Dental Emergency After 7 PM? Our Emergency Line Is Always On.
