Dental pain has a way of taking over your entire day. Whether it’s a persistent throb that won’t quit or a sharp sting that hits out of nowhere, we know how debilitating it can be and how badly you just want it gone.

Our Salt Lake City emergency dentists are experienced in quickly identifying the source of your discomfort and providing effective, lasting relief. From infected teeth and inflamed gums to cracked enamel and exposed nerves, we treat the problem at its root so you can get back to feeling like yourself as soon as possible.

Severe Toothache Relief Intense pain means something is wrong. We use advanced diagnostics to find the source of your pain (cavities, infection, or abscess) and provide immediate pain management and treatment to get you relief today.

Broken or Chipped Tooth Repair A cracked or broken tooth exposes nerves and can lead to severe infection. Whether you need quick dental bonding, a same-day crown, or a veneer, we will restore your tooth’s strength and appearance immediately.

Emergency Root Canals Don’t lose your tooth to infection. If you have severe sensitivity or swelling, an emergency root canal will remove the infected tissue, stop the pain instantly, and save your natural tooth.

Les soins

Lost Crown & Filling Repair A missing crown or filling leaves your tooth vulnerable to bacteria and extreme sensitivity. We offer same-day repairs and replacements using high-quality materials to protect your tooth and restore your smile.

Common causes of a severe toothache can include dental cavities, gum disease, tooth abscesses, cracked teeth, or impacted wisdom teeth. Our team will diagnose the specific issue during your visit.
