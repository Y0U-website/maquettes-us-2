New Patients

You’re in great hands!

We make sure all our new patients feel welcome, safe, and taken care of.

During your first visit, one of our Doctors will review your dental and medical history. For diagnosis, he will examine your teeth and take radiographs and photos of your dentition. From there, the doctor will explain any issues and help you choose options for treatment. We will typically proceed with a cleaning unless the level of cleaning required necessitates additional time and a separate cleaning visit.

For a shorter time in the waiting room, you may book an appointment with the Booking Link Here .

Quick & Reliable

We are available 7 days a week and get most people seen within 15 minutes.
