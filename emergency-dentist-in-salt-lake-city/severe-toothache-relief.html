Treatment Options for Toothache Relief. Depending on the underlying cause of your severe toothache, several treatment options may be recommended:

Once a treatment plan is determined, we will explain the procedure in detail, ensuring you feel informed and confident. If root canal therapy is required, rest assured we use the latest technology and methods to perform the procedure efficiently. The process typically involves:

  • How long will the treatment take?
  • Are there any risks involved in the procedure?
  • Root Canal Therapy: If your toothache is caused by pulp infection, we may recommend a root canal. This procedure involves removing the infected pulp tissue, cleaning the root canal, and sealing it to prevent future infections.
  • Extraction: In cases where the tooth is severely decayed or damaged, extraction may be the only option. We ensure the procedure is as comfortable as possible, using anesthesia and sedation, if necessary.
  • Fillings or Crowns: For toothaches resulting from cavities, we may suggest fillings or crowns to restore the tooth and relieve pain.
  • Medication: In some cases, over-the-counter or prescription pain medications may be recommended to ease discomfort while we treat the underlying issue.
  • Anesthesia: We start by administering local anesthesia to numb the area, ensuring you feel minimal discomfort during the procedure.
  • Accessing the Tooth: We create a small opening in the affected tooth to reach the pulp chamber.
  • Removing Infected Material: The infected pulp and debris are carefully removed using specialized instruments.
  • Cleaning and Disinfecting: The empty root canals are thoroughly cleaned and disinfected to eliminate any remaining bacteria.
  • Sealing the Canals: Once cleaned, we fill the canals with biocompatible material and seal the tooth to restore its structure.
  • Follow-up Care: We provide you with aftercare instructions to ensure a smooth recovery and schedule any follow-up appointments if necessary.

What should I do if I have a severe toothache?

If you’re experiencing a severe toothache, it’s crucial to contact our emergency dental office in Salt Lake City immediately. We can assess the cause of your pain and provide the necessary treatment to relieve it.

How quickly can I get an appointment for toothache relief?

We offer same-day appointments for toothaches and can often see you within hours of your call. Your comfort and care are our top priorities.

What are the common causes of a severe toothache?

Common causes of a severe toothache can include dental cavities, gum disease, tooth abscesses, cracked teeth, or impacted wisdom teeth. Our team will diagnose the specific issue during your visit.

Will I require a root canal for my toothache?

A root canal may be necessary if the tooth has severe decay or infection affecting the pulp. We’ll determine the best course of treatment based on your specific condition.

How can I relieve tooth pain while waiting for treatment?

Over-the-counter pain relievers like ibuprofen or acetaminophen may help reduce pain. Avoid applying heat to the area; instead, a cold compress may provide temporary relief.

Do you offer emergency dental care after hours?

Yes, we provide emergency dental services 24/7. If you’re dealing with a severe toothache outside of regular hours, don’t hesitate to call us for assistance.

What should I avoid if I have a toothache?

Try to avoid very hot or cold foods and drinks, as they may worsen the pain. Chewing on that side of your mouth should also be avoided until you receive treatment.
