Dr. Brent Sedon is the dentist behind Emergency Dentist LLC in Medina, Ohio, and has been treating patients across Northeast Ohio since 2010.

Education & Training

Born in Canton, Ohio, Dr. Sedon earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Kent State University in 2006. He was then accepted to The Ohio State University College of Dentistry, where he graduated with his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree in 2010. He continues to advance his skills through ongoing continuing-education courses.

Caring for the Medina County Community

Dr. Sedon routinely provides free dental care to Medina County residents who could not otherwise afford treatment, and he has been recognized in the local Medina newspaper for going above and beyond including treating homebound patients in their own homes when they were unable to travel to his office.

Outside the Office

When he isn’t seeing patients, Dr. Sedon enjoys spending time with his lovely wife, Crystal, and their four children and you can routinely find him working on his hobby farm.
