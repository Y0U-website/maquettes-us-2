What are dental implants?

A dental implant (sometimes called tooth implants) is a small titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, providing a foundation for a replacement tooth or teeth. Placing an implant is a common surgical procedure performed by your highly trained clinician at Austin Emergency Dental. How long does the implant process take?

The implant process can vary depending on your personal needs, but it typically takes a few months to complete, given the bone can take up to 6 months to heal. This includes your initial consultation, implant placement surgery and then the placement of the final restoration.

What are the 2 types of dental implants available at Dallas Emergency Dental?

There are two main types of dental implants offered at Dallas Emergency Dental:

Our implant team at Dallas Emergency Dental is committed to recommending the best implant solution tailored to your specific oral health needs. Whether you require endosteal or zygomatic implants, we ensure that your custom treatment plan meets the highest standards of care.

  • Endosteal implants: These are the most common type of dental implants, placed directly into the jawbone. They provide a strong foundation for artificial teeth, similar to natural tooth roots.
  • Zygomatic Implants: Used when there is severe bone loss in the upper jaw and traditional implants are not viable. There are anchored in the zygomatic (cheek) bone rather than the maxillary bone, providing a stable base for dental restorations.
