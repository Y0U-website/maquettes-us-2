We believe that our dentists are among the best that the Northwest has to offer. We proudly provide a wide range of general dentistry services including dental implants, crowns, sedation dentistry, composite fillings, inlays/onlays, fixed bridges, and mercury-free tooth-colored fillings . We also offer emergency dental treatment that is the best in the business.

At the Emergency Tooth Doctor, we strive to make your dental visit as comfortable as possible. With a range of services from restorative dentistry to cosmetic dentistry , we have the ability to solve any dental issue that you may bring to us, in a courteous professional manner.
