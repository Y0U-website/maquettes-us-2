The Top-Rated Dental Care in Johnson County, Kansas

With nearly four decades in dental practice, I take pride in being a second-generation dentist. To carry on the legacy, my son became the third generation to join us just two years ago. Our clinic specializes in a broad spectrum of general and emergency dental services, always prioritizing the well-being of our patients. I’ve had the honor of being an active member of the American Dental Association and the Kansas Dental Association for over 30 years. My dedication to dentistry and our community is also reflected in my 35 years of practice in Prairie Village, Kansas.

Outside our local dental clinic, I cherish moments spent with my family. Traveling and discovering new destinations are among my favorite ways to spend leisure time.

At our practice, we provide extensive dental procedures, striving to deliver the highest standard of care to our patients. We look forward to serving your dental needs and ensuring your smile is at its healthiest best.

Dr. Justin Jirovec, a third-generation dentist, carries forward a rich legacy of dental expertise, offering a compassionate touch to modern dentistry. For Dr. Jirovec, dentistry is more than a profession; it’s a family tradition. His grandfather opened, what is now Emergency Tooth Pain Center, in 1958. Dr. Justin Jirovec proudly works side by side with his father, ensuring a seamless blend of experience and innovation.

Dr. Jirovec’s educational journey began at the University of Missouri - Columbia, where he earned a degree in Health Sciences in 2018. This foundational knowledge sparked his passion for healthcare and prepared him for the next chapter of his career. In 2022, he graduated from the University of Missouri - Kansas City with a Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) degree, solidifying his commitment to top-tier dental care.

Dr. Jirovec’s commitment to your dental health goes beyond the basics. He is a dedicated lifelong learner, constantly seeking to expand his knowledge in dentistry and stay up-to-date with the latest treatments and technologies. Your oral health is in capable hands with a dentist who prioritizes your well-being.

Family is at the heart of Dr. Jirovec’s life. He enjoys spending quality time with his wonderful family and his lovable dog, Milo. These cherished moments keep him grounded and inspire his passion for bringing smiles to his patients’ faces.
