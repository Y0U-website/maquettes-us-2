Financial Options

With our patients’ unique situations and needs in mind, we are pleased to offer several financial options from which you can choose to pay for your dental care. If you have any questions about the payments we accept, please contact our dentist and team at 228-896-4084 and speak to a caring and helpful team member.

The office of Emery L. Edwards, DDS is a preferred provider for Diversified Dental Network, Dental Benefits Providers, and United Concordia®. We are also a premier provider for all Delta Dental® companies and a member of DHA (Dental Health Alliance®). Our diversified partners include Ameritas® , Lincoln Financial®, Principal Financial® and United Healthcare®.

If you do not have dental insurance, you can still receive the personalized treatments you deserve. We accept payments made with the medical financing provided by LendingClub and CareCredit®. We also take payments made with all credit cards, personal checks, and cash.

If you plan to pay for your treatment in cash, we invite you to call our office and learn more about our cash discount. We look forward to always providing you with accommodating dental care in Gulfport, Mississippi.
