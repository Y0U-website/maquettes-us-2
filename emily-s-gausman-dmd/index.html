Emily S. Gausman, DMD was established in 2004 when Dr. Gausman purchased the practice from an established local dentist, Dr. Francis Palmer who had been practicing for almost 45 years prior to his retirement. Over the years, we have continued to educate ourselves and stay updated on the latest technologies and techniques, like digital imaging, to provide the utmost oral healthcare.

Our office is accredited by the Better Business Bureau, and we have received recognition from the Spear Study Club for excellent care. For our patients, we make dental care accessible by participating in CareCredits’ 6-month, no-interest financing. We also provide for electronic claims submissions.
