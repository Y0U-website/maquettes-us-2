Emily Stout DDS is devoted to restoring and enhancing the natural beauty of your smile using state-of-the-art techniques — for beautiful, lasting results.

Dr. Emily M. Stout

Dr. Stout was born and raised in South Bend, IN, and graduated from John Adams High School before earning a Bachelor of Science in Biology (with a minor in Psychology) from Indiana University. She went straight on to the Indiana University School of Dentistry, where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

Today she runs a private practice in Elkhart, where she provides comprehensive general dentistry for the entire family. She keeps up with advances in dentistry through continuing education and practices with digital technology. Dr. Stout is a member of the American Dental Association, the Indiana Dental Association, and the North Central Dental Society, and a proud member of the Concord Rotary Club.

Outside of work, Dr. Stout treasures time with her family — husband Eric, daughter Madeline, and son Charlie. They’re proud to be part of the Elkhart community.

Personalized, comprehensive care Attention to detail and comprehensive treatment planning — using restorative and cosmetic dentistry to maintain the dental health you want.

Prevention & knowledge first We provide dental health care, not disease care — exams, cleanings, sealants, and fluoride that stop problems before they start.

Your safety, always Strict sterilization and infection-control processes following American Dental Association, OSHA, and CDC standards.

Expertise & education A team of highly trained clinicians committed to continual learning — lectures, conventions, and the latest techniques and technology.

Comfortable, unhurried visits We understand dental nerves. Our whole team is dedicated to making every visit as relaxing and pleasant as possible.

For the whole family Comprehensive general dentistry for every age — and a familiar, friendly team that remembers your name.
