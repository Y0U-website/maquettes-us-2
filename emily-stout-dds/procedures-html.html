Procedures & Services

From a complete exam and cleaning to a full-mouth restoration — and everything in between — we promise exceptional care as we enhance the natural beauty of your smile.

On the pages below you’ll find just some of the many procedures and services we regularly provide our patients — with a gentle touch and stunning results. Your smile is our first priority, and we’ll give you something to smile about.

Cleaning & Prevention Exams, cleanings, x-rays, sealants, and fluoride that keep small problems small.

Cosmetic Dentistry Whitening, veneers, bonding, and single-visit crowns designed around your smile.

Periodontal Care Gentle diagnosis and treatment of gum disease to protect your smile’s foundation.

Restorations Fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, implants, and root canal therapy.

Invisalign Nearly invisible, removable aligners that straighten teeth without metal.

SureSmile Precision clear-aligner therapy for faster, more predictable results.

General Dentistry Complete, comfortable dental care for every member of the family.

Pediatric Dentistry Gentle, fun, and friendly care that helps children love the dentist.

Emergency Dentistry Prompt relief for tooth pain, broken teeth, and urgent dental needs.
