Welcome to Emmaus Dental Care

Dr. Parikh understands the delicate balance between the art and science of dentistry, and his goal is for you to achieve positive results and a smile you re proud to show others.

The practice

With the goal of providing comfort-focused care to you and your loved ones, Dr. Parikh and his team hope you’ll make Emmaus Dental Care your dental home.

Our professional team understands how valuable your time is; that’s why we strive to make your visits as efficient as possible. We’ll always do our best to see you in a timely manner, but you’ll never feel rushed. We’ll listen to your concerns before explaining your treatment options, then work with you to make informed decisions about your oral health.

We love treating children and showing them how to care for their smiles, and we understand the importance of building lasting relationships from a young age. When you visit us with your little ones, they’ll see that you value good oral health, making them more likely to carry those habits into adulthood.

Preventive dental care is the cornerstone of maintaining healthy teeth and gums, and we’ll help you schedule your routine exams and cleanings at times that work for your busy lifestyle.

And if the unexpected happens and you need emergency dental care, we’re just a phone call away.

We use the latest technology to streamline your treatment and keep you comfortable while you’re with us.

Whether it’s our intraoral camera that lets you see what’s going on inside your mouth and explain treatment options, our panoramic x-rays that give us detailed two-dimensional images of your entire mouth so we can diagnose a wide range of concerns, or our Medit i700 scanner that replaces traditional dental impressions and can be used for crowns, bridges, and dentures, your comfort and care are…

All of our technologies make your dental appointment easier and more enjoyable while allowing us to educate you about your dental hygiene and oral health.
