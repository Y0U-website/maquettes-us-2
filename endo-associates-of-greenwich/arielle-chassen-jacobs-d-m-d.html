Arielle Chassen Jacobs D.M.D.

Arielle Chassen Jacobs D.M.D.

Early Education

Arielle Chassen Jacobs D.M.D. attended college at Emory University in Atlanta, Georgia, and then traveled to Philadelphia to begin her dental studies at the University of Pennsylvania .Â While here she was awarded the American Association of Endodontic Award of Excellence . She then attended Tufts University Dental School in Boston, Massachusetts where she completed specialty training in endodontics.

Early Career

After her formal education, Dr. Jacobs then returned home to New York where she was born and raised, to practice endodontics in Manhattan. She passed all three portions of the board exam and as a result, is now a Diplomate of the American Board of Endodontics. Dr. Jacobs practices at both the Scarsdale, NY location and Greenwich, CT location.

Associations

In addition to her extensive education, Arielle Chassen Jacobs, D.M.D. participates with local and national general dental and endodontic associations to stay knowledgeable about best care practices and apply the most current technology. Those associations include the American Dental Association , the New York State Dental Association , and the American Association of Women Dentists .

Homelife

Currently, Dr. Jacobs lives in Ardsley N.Y. with her husband Dan and her daughters Zoe and Alana. She enjoys the theater, trying new restaurants, and above all spending time with her family.
