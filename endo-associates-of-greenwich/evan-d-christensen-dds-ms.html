Evan D Christensen, DDS MS

Evan D Christensen, DDS MS

Evan D Christensen, DDS MS currently resides in Greenwich, Connecticut with his wife, Tiffany and their children, Lila and Todd. Originally from Salt Lake City, Utah, Dr. Christensen came east to attend dental school at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine in New York City. Dr. Christensen was extremely active in student government. While attending he received multiple honors and awards, including induction into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon (OKU) national dental honor society.

After graduating, Dr. Christensen was accepted into the Endodontic residency program at Columbia University. There he received training in all aspects of modern endodontics, including surgical and non-surgical treatment, retreatments , trauma management . He also received training on intense multidisciplinary approach to total patient care. All procedures are performed using a surgical operating microscope as well as the most advanced endodontic technology.

In his spare time, Dr. Christensen enjoys spending time with his family and friends. He is an avid outdoorsmen and enjoys fly fishing, camping and visiting national parks. Dr. Christensen also enjoys automobile racing and restoring classic and collector automobiles.

Evan D Christensen, DDS MS professional associations include the American Association of Endodontics (AAE) , Connecticut Association of Endodontics (CAE) , American Dental Association (ADA) , Connecticut State Dental Association (CSDA) , and Bridgeport Dental Society .
