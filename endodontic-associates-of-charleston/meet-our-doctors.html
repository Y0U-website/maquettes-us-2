Meet Dr. Lee

As a board-eligible endodontist, Dr. Lee is actively pursuing his certification, having already completed one of the three components required to become board certified and recognized as a diplomate by the American Board of Endodontics. He is dedicated to lifelong learning and continually strives to provide the most specialized and valuable care to his patients.

Dr. Lee is also deeply committed to community service. Throughout his career, he has participated in numerous outreach programs, providing dental care to underserved communities both locally and internationally. Dr. Lee’s dedication to community service reflects his broader commitment to improving the lives of others through his professional skills and compassionate care.

Dr. Lee and his wife Molly are glad to be back in Charleston and just welcomed their first daughter, Bunny.
