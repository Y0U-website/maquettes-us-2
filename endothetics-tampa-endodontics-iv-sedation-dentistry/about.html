Healthy gums and supporting bone are essential to maintaining your natural teeth and achieving successful dental implant results.

Comfortable Dental Care With Physician-Led Anesthesia

We understand that dental treatment can be difficult for patients who experience dental anxiety, fear of the dentist, special healthcare needs, or significant treatment needs.

That’s why Endothetics offers access to physician-led anesthesia care under the supervision of Dr. Harry Acevedo, M.D., Physician Anesthesiologist.

Dr. Acevedo has advanced fellowship training in Adult Cardiothoracic Anesthesiology from the Cleveland Clinic and extensive experience in hospital-based anesthesia and the care of medically complex patients.

For appropriately selected patients including children and patients with significant dental anxiety or special needs anesthesia options can help make necessary dental treatment more comfortable and manageable.

During anesthesia, Dr. Acevedo is responsible for the patient’s anesthesia care and physiologic monitoring while our dental team focuses on providing the planned treatment.
