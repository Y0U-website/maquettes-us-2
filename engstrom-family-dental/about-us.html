WELCOME TO ENGSTROM FAMILY DENTAL

We welcome you to come visit our office. Visit our friendly staff and you will be surprised by our unique tranquil wooded setting right here in East Lansing. Each room overlooks the woods and we frequently witness an array of wildlife such as squirrels, chipmunks, songbirds, woodpeckers, and possibly even deer, opossums, and raccoons.

We thank you for your interest in our services and the trust you have placed in us. Please contact us if you have any questions.
