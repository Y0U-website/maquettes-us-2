Meet Dr. Allen Enhanced…

0 MEET DOCTOR BRIANO ALLEN Briano Allen PharmD, DDS, MS was born in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. He relocated to Tallahassee, Florida where he earned his Doctor of Pharmacy degree with honors from Florida A&M University. He was accepted into Howard University College of Dentistry and graduated with high honors. Dr. Allen received numerous awards for academic excellence during his tenure at Howard University. In his senior year, he was inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society.

He completed his residency training at the University of Florida and received his certificates in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics as well as Endodontics. He also earned his Master’s of Science degree from the University of Florida for his research in dentofacial pain. In addition to academic achievements, Dr. Allen worked his way through school as a clinical pharmacist for Tallahassee Memorial Hospital, Georgetown University Hospital, and University of Florida Health at Shands.

Dr. Allen is a member of the American Association of Endodontists, the Florida Association of Endodontics, the American Dental Association, the Florida Dental Association, and the Leon County Dental Association.

In his spare time, he enjoys traveling and spending time with his daughters.
