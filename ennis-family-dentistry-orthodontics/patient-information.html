Here at Ennis Family Dentistry & Orthodontics in Ennis, Tx , whether it’s your first or 50th visit, we are focused on you – your comfort, your happiness, and your health.

After we finish these important steps, the doctor will meet with you to discuss your X-rays, help you create a personalized treatment plan and answer any of your questions.

  • Review your dental and medical history forms
  • Take X-rays of your teeth
  • Perform a comprehensive exam
  • Check your teeth and gums for any signs of decay and gingivitis or periodontitis
  • Professionally clean and polish your teeth (Unless our dentist recommends a second appointment).
