Meet The Team

Hillsborough’s Dynamic Duo

Dr. Caroline Latta

When not at the office, Dr. Latta can be found exploring everything the Triangle (and the world) has to offer. She adores her two mischievous cats, Maverick and Ragnar, who were definitely dogs in their past lives. Dr. Latta also has a knack for puzzles, baking, and hand-lettering.

Dr. Latta has many fond memories of her hometown from Hog Day to adventures at Camp Chestnut Ridge, eating at the Colonial Inn to ice skating (somewhat poorly) at the Sportsplex. She has enjoyed seeing the influx of new local businesses and restaurants to Hillsborough and is excited to become an integral part of the local community! These days you’ll likely find her walking along the Riverwalk, downing some food at Hillsborough BBQ, or catching a beautiful sunset at Maple View (okay, she’s there for the ice cream too…).

Dr. Sharon Lau

Dr. Lau grew up in the not-so-little-anymore town of Florence, SC before attending UNC for her undergraduate studies in Biology. She loved the campus so much that she signed up for four more years at the dental school – go heels! After graduating with her DDS degree in 2017, she moved to Hillsborough with her husband, Isaac, to escape the hustle and bustle of Chapel Hill and to return to small-town living. Though she enjoyed working as an associate around the Triangle, she was ready to plant her permanent roots in the Hillsborough area and make a lasting positive impact on her community!

Aside from her passion for creating smiles, Dr. Lau is a bit of a crazy horse gal. Though Scarlett, her redhead mare is no longer competing, Dr. Lau is an active member of the horse community and still enjoys eventing with her horsey friends. If she’s not at the office creating beautiful smiles, she’s probably out riding her horse or taking care of the farm. She and her husband currently live in Cedar Grove with the rest of their four-legged children: an adventure-loving border collie (Ruby) and their three silly, quirky kitties (Abigail, Cleo, & Birdie).

The Laus are healthily obsessed with all things local and love exploring the area! You can catch them getting drinks at Botanist & Barrel, smashing some wings at the Nickel, or grabbing coffee at Cup A Joe.

Dr. Cassie White

Dr. White is originally from Kannapolis, NC. Coming from a long line of Tar Heels, it was only fitting that she completed her undergraduate studies and dental school at UNC Chapel Hill. She’s a huge Carolina fan! Following dental school, Dr. White continued her dental education through an Advanced Education in General Dentistry program through the University of Florida in beautiful St. Petersburg, FL.

While Dr. White thoroughly enjoyed the sun, beach, and warm temperatures of Florida, she always knew NC was home. She now resides in Apex, NC with her fiancé, Will, and pups Wilson and Penny.

Dr. White is excited to have joined Eno River Dental as an associate and has enjoyed getting to know all our incredible patients! When she’s not doing dentistry, you can find her traveling, trying new restaurants around the Triangle, and petting ALLL the dogs…cats too – she loves animals! Dr. White is looking forward to exploring all that Hillsborough has to offer and would love to hear your best local recommendations!

Kaycie

Introducing Kaycie, who grew up in nearby Alamance County. She graduated from the dental assisting program at Alamance Community College in 2021 and started her career in Oral Surgery. After three years in that field, she decided to return to her true passion: General Dentistry.

Kaycie enjoys working closely with patients to help them achieve the smiles of their dreams, and her knack for organization helps keep the office running smoothly. Sarah

Meet Sarah, originally from sunny Florida, who graduated with her certificate in Expanded Functions Dental Assisting in 2018. Sarah is excited to help you achieve your best smile!
