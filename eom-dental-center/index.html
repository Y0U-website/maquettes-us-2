We collect information when you use our website or schedule with our office. This page explains what that means in plain terms.

If you browse the site, fill out a form, or become a patient, this applies to you.

Some details you type in yourself. Other information is collected automatically in the background.

If you request an appointment or send us a message, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, and date of birth. That allows us to call you back, confirm visits, and create or update your record.

If you become a patient, we gather health history, medications, prior treatment details, and current concerns.

This isn’t just paperwork. For example, certain heart conditions or medications change how we plan procedures. Some visits take longer because of medical factors. We rely on accurate information to avoid unnecessary risk.

Like most modern websites, ours logs general browsing information. Pages viewed. Time spent. Basic device data.
