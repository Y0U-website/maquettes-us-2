Who Has Access

Your information stays within the practice.

Team members access it only when necessary to do their job. Front desk handles scheduling. Clinical staff review health history. Billing reviews insurance details. That’s the extent of it.

If we use secure third-party systems for records or messaging, those providers are required to follow privacy regulations.

Text and Email Messages

If you opt in, you may receive reminders by text or email.

You can opt out at any time. Reply STOP. Click unsubscribe. Or call us and say you don’t want reminders anymore. We’ll update it.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information.

That includes controlled access to systems, secure software platforms, and internal processes about who can view what.

No system online is invulnerable. Anyone claiming that would be overstating things. What we do is reduce risk and handle information responsibly.

Cookies

Our site may use cookies to keep pages functioning properly and to understand general usage patterns.

You can disable cookies in your browser. Some features may not behave the same if you do.

If you want to know how your information is stored, who can access it, or how to request changes, contact the office directly. We will explain the process clearly.

Links to Other Sites

If you click a link that takes you to another website, their privacy rules apply there, not ours. We encourage you to review those separately.
