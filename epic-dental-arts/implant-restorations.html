  • 0 IMPLANT RESTORATIONS Dental implant restoration is the process of placing a crown, bridge, or denture onto a titanium implant that has been surgically inserted into your jawbone. Once the implant fuses with the bone—a process called osseointegration—it provides a strong, stable foundation for your custom restoration. The result is a natural-looking, functional replacement that blends seamlessly with your smile. Depending on your needs, implant restorations can take several forms: Single-tooth restoration: A custom crown is placed on one implant to replace a single missing tooth.
  • Implant-supported bridge: Replaces several teeth in a row, supported by two or more implants.
  • Implant-supported dentures: A stable, secure alternative to traditional dentures, anchored by multiple implants.
  • Natural look and feel: Crafted to mimic the shape, size, and color of your teeth.
  • Improved function: Eat, speak, and smile with confidence—no slipping or discomfort.
  • Long-term durability: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.
  • Preserved jawbone health: Implants help prevent bone loss that often follows missing teeth.
