Advanced imaging techniques like Cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) enable us to capture detailed three-dimensional images. These images are invaluable for diagnosis, surgical planning, treatment, and the accurate placement of dental implants .

At Epident, we utilize these advanced diagnostic tools to provide you with the highest standard of care, ensuring that your Dental Exams are thorough and informative.

  • Bitewing x-ray : Captures detailed views of the crowns of premolars or molars, typically used to check for dental decay.
  • Full Mouth Series : A comprehensive set of bitewing and periapical views of all teeth.
  • Panoramic Film (panorex) : Offers a 2D panoramic view of all teeth, the left and right sides of the jaw, erupted teeth, developing teeth, impacted teeth, and allows an assessment of the health and integrity of the jawbone.
  • Cephalometric Film : Provides a profile view of the face and jaws, often used by orthodontists to assess skeletal facial and jaw growth.

How often do I need to get a dental checkup?

Routine dental checkups and professional cleanings help ensure your smile remains in tip-top condition and that any developing problems receive the prompt care they require. While it’s a good idea to schedule a checkup and cleaning twice a year, longer or shorter intervals between routine visits depend on your specific needs.

We understand that life gets complicated and busy, and for various reasons, you haven’t been able to get to the dentist for care. Don’t worry; it’s always a good time to get back in touch with the health of your smile.

Should pregnant women get a dental checkup?

For pregnant women, practicing good oral care and seeing the dentist to maintain a healthy smile is considered an important component of prenatal care. Researchers have found that pregnancy not only increases the risk of oral health problems, but these problems may also lead to pregnancy complications. At the office of Epident, we provide the skilled and compassionate care required to help expectant mothers keep their teeth and gums in tip-top condition. While certain procedures are not recommended during pregnancy, we provide the necessary care and take all the necessary precautions required.

How can I improve the appearance of my smile?

Thanks to advances in modern dentistry, teeth that have been affected by a range of imperfections or that have sustained damage from trauma or dental decay can be restored to produce a naturally beautiful looking and functional smile.

Contact our office to learn more about the many ways we can help you create the smile you’ve always wanted.

How should I prepare for my dental appointment?

If you’re visiting Epident for the first time, it is helpful that you contact your former dental office and have your records sent over to us. Make note of any problems you may have so that you can discuss them with your dentist.

To provide efficient service, we ask you to please bring your insurance card, an ID, plus any referral slips or diagnostic records given to you by another office. If you are taking medications, please note or make a list of what they are as you will need to provide that information.
