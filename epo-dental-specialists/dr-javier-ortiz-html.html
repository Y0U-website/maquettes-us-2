Meet Your Richardson Dentist and Endodontic Expert

Dr. Javier Ortiz

Dr. Ortiz pursued his D.M.D. degree with honors at the University of Puerto Rico where he was born and raised. During his dental training, he participated in different research projects at the dental school in conjunction with Boston University in 2003. He was a member of several dental school associations as well as the president of the student council for the UPR School of Dental Medicine prior to obtaining his dental degree.

Dr. Ortiz is an active member of the American Academy of Endodontists

Dr. Ortiz is looking forward to meeting you and is committed to providing patients with the highest quality of endodontic care possible.
