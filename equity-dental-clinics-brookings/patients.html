Periodontal disease, also known as gum disease is caused by the buildup of plaque and bacteria, leading to inflammation and potential damage to the gums and bone structure. Without proper treatment, periodontal disease can progress and result in tooth loss. Regular dental check-ups, proper oral hygiene practices, and professional cleanings are crucial in preventing and managing periodontal disease, ensuring the long-term health of your teeth and gums.

Innovative Dentistry The world of dentistry is on the verge of changing forever. According to a 2016 study by the CDC, 13 % of children 5-19 and 26 % of adults 20-64 have untreated dental cavities. Many of these cavities if left untreated will require a filling, root canal, crown, or extraction.

Dentists receive most of their dental school training on these major restorations, which consume more time in the dental office and are more expensive. Equity Dental’s approach is to stop the oral infection and prevent reoccurring infection by providing topical treatments that make your teeth stronger and bactericidal. Thereby reducing the need for future restorative dental procedures.

What To Expect at Your First Visit During your first visit you will receive a thorough examination by a dentist, x-rays, a treatment recommendation, and possibly a cleaning. In addition, you will have the time to share your dental concerns with the dentist and the other health care providers. During your first visit, you may also receive topical treatments including fluoride varnish, silver diamine fluoride, and/or betadine varnish. If you require further treatment, you will be scheduled for a 2nd visit.

Silver Diamine Fluoride Silver Diamine Fluoride, commonly known as SDF, is a compound that contains silver, which kills germs that causes tooth decay and can prevents future infections. The silver in SDF leaches into the tooth and can remain for 3 – 12 months providing lasting protection against germs. For more information on SDF visit the American Dental Association at ADA Silver Diamine Fluoride.
