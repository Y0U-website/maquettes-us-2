What are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported or Snap-On dentures are complete dentures that are held in place with the aid of dental implants . They are still removable, but are more stable than traditional dentures.

Candidates for Implant-Supported Dentures

Patients who are missing all of their teeth or will require the removal of all their teeth in one or both arches due to injuries, cavities, or disease may be good candidates for implant retained dentures Glenview. This is also a viable option for patients who want to replace their present denture with a more stable option.

Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-Supported Dentures Glenview offer more stability and are not likely to become loose like traditional dentures.

Other benefits include:

Improved chewing ability-- patients are able to eat harder foods that they would otherwise not be able to with traditional dentures.

  • The fit is more comfortable, thus causing less friction on the gums.
  • More natural-looking than conventional dentures.
  • Prevents bone atrophy because of stimulation of the jawbone.

Caring for Implant-Supported Dentures

Just like other prostheses, the lifespan depends on usage. Factors that impact the lifespan of implant-supported dentures include the following: Daily Cleaning Implant dentures should be cleaned twice a day just like natural teeth. The part of the implants above the gums should also be cleaned regularly. When brushing, pay attention to the gums and areas surrounding the implants. This is to avoid bacteria buildup as it causes disease or gum irritation that is detrimental to the implants and one’s oral health.

A regular visit to your d entist Glenview is advisable to catch any problems early. The dentist may see issues that cannot be seen when wearing the dentures. Routine visits keep dentures and implants healthy and clean to enhance longevity.

It is crucial to eat healthy foods that contain the right nutrients. You should consume fresh foods that are a good source of vitamins. Bones require adequate intake of Vitamin D to maintain their strength. Minimize chewing hard, sugary, sticky, and acidic foods with implants and dentures. These foods are unhealthy for oral health and can also accelerate wear and tear of the surface of the dentures.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What are Implant Supported Dentures? Implant-supported dentures are dental prostheses that are supported by dental implants, which are surgically placed into the jawbone to provide a sturdy foundation for the dentures.
  • What are the Risks of Implant Supported Dentures? The risks of implant-supported dentures are similar to the risks of any other dental surgery. These risks include infection, bleeding, and nerve damage. However, these risks are rare.
  • Does this Procedure Hurt? No, when you visit our office in Glenview, IL. We will provide you with any necessary sedation. At Glenlake Dental Care Office, we understand that dental procedures can be intimidating, and we will take steps to ensure that you remain comfortable and relaxed during the appointment.
  • How to Clean Implant Supported Dentures? Cleaning implant-supported dentures is similar to cleaning natural teeth. It is important to brush the dentures at least twice a day with a soft-bristled brush and non-abrasive toothpaste. It is also important to floss around the implants daily to remove any food particles or debris that may have accumulated.
  • How Much do Implant Supported Dentures Cost? The cost of implant-supported dentures varies depending on the number of implants needed, the type of dentures used, and the dentist’s fees. However, implant-supported dentures are typically more expensive than traditional dentures.
  • How Long Does it take to Get Implant Supported Dentures? The time it takes to get implant-supported dentures varies depending on the number of implants needed and the healing time. However, most people can expect to have their implant-supported dentures in place within a few months.
  • Are Implant-Supported Dentures Right for Me? If you are missing all of your teeth, implant-supported dentures may be a good option for you. Implant-supported dentures can provide a more stable and secure fit than traditional dentures, which can improve your speech and eating. They can also last longer than traditional dentures.
  • What are the Benefits of Implant Retained Dentures? The benefits of implant retained dentures include improved stability and retention, increased comfort and function, better oral health, and improved self-esteem.
