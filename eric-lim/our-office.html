Our Glenview Dental Office

Our office is conveniently located on 946 Harlem Ave. and is accessible from all the major roads. We utilize state of the art equipment to ensure the highest quality in care. We have a modern sterilization system that surpasses OSHA requirements.

Our friendly and skilled Glenview Dentist is dedicated to patient comfort and high quality care. With years of experience combined with the latest in technology, our treatment options are among the best in the area.

We provide gentle, comfortable, and family oriented care to the patients of this community. We utilize top-of-the-line sterilization techniques to ensure outstanding results. In addition to general dentistry and cosmetic dentistry , we work with specialists in Periodontics ( gum diseases ), Implant dentistry and Endodontics ( root canal treatments ) and other areas to treat the diverse amount of cases we receive. Our team emphasizes comprehensive and superb quality in preventative dentistry.

We look forward to welcoming you to our dental family.
