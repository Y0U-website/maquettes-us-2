The practice

The most rewarding part of dentistry for Eric is getting to know each of his patients.

Dr. Strand is married to Jessica Strand. They have two sons, Eli and Isaac.

They enjoy most outdoor activities, especially fishing, canoeing, and camping. Dr. Strand is also a hobbyist beekeeper, enjoys multiple types of photography, and actively participates in the operation of vintage farm equipment at the WMSTR in Rollag, MN.

Dr. Joe Greenagel was born and raised in the Fergus Falls area. After graduating from Fergus Falls high school in 1988, he went on to complete his degree at the University of MN, School of Dentistry.

From 1995 to 1998, Dr. Greenagel served as a Lieutenant in the US Navy. Upon completion of his duty, he went into private practice. He has been at his present location for over 20 years (since 2000).

Dr. Greenagel and wife, Becky, have three children. They enjoy youth sports, golfing, and enjoying time on the lake.

Thank you for your interest in Prairie’s Edge Dental. We look forward to the opportunity to get to know you and your entire family. Our dental practice serves patients from all communities in the Fergus Falls area.
