Veenstra Family Dental began with a simple but powerful vision—to provide dental care that makes people feel safe, heard, and cared for. Dr. Doug Veenstra established the practice with those values at heart, and today, his son, Dr. Eric Veenstra, proudly continues that legacy.

Though Dr. Doug retired from treating patients in 2024, he remains an essential part of our practice’s success and spirit. Together with Dr. Eric, Dr. Noah Ruitenberg, and Dr. Rachel Hudspeth, our dental team is committed to combining modern techniques with a genuine, family-first approach to care.

We’re here for your routine visits, life-changing smile transformations, and every little moment in between.

Dr. Eric Veenstra, DMD Co-Owner and Dentist

Dr. Noah Ruitenberg is proud to serve the community he calls home, having grown up in Ramsey, New Jersey. He earned his dental degree from Rutgers and completed his residency at St. Joseph’s Regional Medical Center before joining Veenstra Family Dental, where he provides compassionate, modern care to patients of all ages.

Outside the office, Dr. Noah loves spending time with his wife, Kelly, their son, Eli, and their dog, Oakley. He can often be found on the golf course or grabbing a cup of coffee at a coffee shop.

He strives to create an experience where every patient feels cared for, relaxed, and confident in their smile every step of the way.

Dr. Rachel Hudspeth is a local Jersey girl who was raised in North Haledon. She attended the Academy of the Holy Angels in Demarest before heading to Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland, Ohio, where she earned her bachelor’s degree in biology with minors in chemistry and cognitive science. She then moved to Boston to pursue her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at Tufts University, where she also served as a teaching assistant during her final year.

After dental school, Dr. Rachel returned to New Jersey to complete a two-year General Practice Residency at the New Jersey Veterans Affairs Health Care System in East Orange. During her second year, she served as Chief Resident, mentoring new dental graduates and helping them manage veterans with complex medical conditions.

Dr. Rachel especially enjoys working with children and has more than two years of experience practicing in a pediatric dental office. She is passionate about creating a positive, comfortable experience for patients of all ages.

Dr. Rachel is excited to care for patients in the community she calls home. Outside of the office, she enjoys spending time with her niece, walking her dogs, traveling, and getting lost in a good book.

Dr. Doug Veenstra, DMD Co-Owner and Retired Dentist

Dr. Doug Veenstra founded Veenstra Family Dental in 1981 after completing his dental degree and residency in New Jersey. With a deep-rooted commitment to caring for others, he has combined decades of clinical excellence with meaningful relationships built on trust and warmth. Now retired from active patient care, Dr. Doug continues to support the practice as a mentor and behind-the-scenes partner. He cherishes time with his growing family and has long been involved in church and medical missions across Central America.
