3D CBCT Imaging (CT Scans)

Think of this as the MRI of dentistry. Cone Beam technology provides a complete 3D view of your teeth, jawbone, nerves, and sinuses in a single scan.

  • Enables safer, more precise implant placement
  • Helps diagnose hidden infections, root fractures, or TMJ issues
  • Reduces the need for exploratory procedures
  • More information = better, more confident care

In-House 3D Printing

We now print night guards, retainers, and temporary restorations right here in our office with cutting-edge 3D printers.

  • Quicker turnaround—no need to wait weeks for a lab
  • Custom-fit appliances made just for you
  • Allows us to make same-day adjustments when needed
  • Streamlined experience from start to finish

Nexus Diode Laser Dentistry

This soft tissue laser allows us to perform many gum-related procedures with greater comfort and precision—often without the need for sutures or extended healing times.

  • Minimally invasive and virtually pain-free
  • Shorter recovery times with less post-op sensitivity
  • Ideal for gum contouring, cold sore treatment, and periodontal care
  • Reduces the need for anesthesia in many cases

Intraoral Cameras & Digital X-Rays

Our intraoral cameras let you see exactly what we see, so you can better understand your dental needs. Digital X-rays offer a safer, faster, and more detailed view of your oral health.

  • Crystal-clear images help catch problems early
  • Digital files can be shared with specialists instantly
  • Up to 90 % less radiation than traditional film X-rays
  • Builds trust by helping you understand your diagnosis
