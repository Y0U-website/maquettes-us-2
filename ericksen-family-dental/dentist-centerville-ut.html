Family Dentist in Centerville, UT

0 Meet Dr. Ericksen Dr. Ericksen is proud to serve patients throughout South Davis County from his office in Centerville, providing personalized, high-quality dental care for patients of all ages.

Having grown up in the area, he is passionate about caring for the community he calls home. Dentistry runs in his family, with his grandfather practicing locally and his father working as a respected dental lab technician in the community.

He earned his dental degree from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine Illinois in 2015 and is committed to continuing education and staying current with the latest advancements in dentistry.

Dr. Ericksen offers a full range of services, from preventive and restorative care to cosmetic treatments, always focusing on patient comfort and long-term oral health.

He is an active member of the American Dental Association, Utah Dental Association, South Davis Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

Outside the office, Dr. Ericksen enjoys spending time with his wife, Taylor, and their children. He also enjoys biking and skiing in the Utah mountains.
