Our Doctors

Dr. Erik Wiseman

Dr. Wiseman graduated from UNMC dental college in Lincoln, Nebraska. He has been practicing dentistry in Brookings since 2007. He loves working in a family-oriented office and taking the time to get to know his patients.

Outside of the office, Dr. Wiseman and his wife are busy raising six children. He is an avid outdoorsman and enjoys taking care of his small acreage.

Dr. Erik Wiseman

Dr. Brock Tidstrom

Dr. Tidstrom was born and raised in Minnesota. He attended Gustavus Adolphus for undergraduate schooling in St. Peter, MN. He graduated from the University of Minnesota Dental School in 2013. Dr. Tidstrom spent two years working in Fairbanks, AK. He enjoys spending time with his wife, Catie, and their four daughters. Dr. Tidstrom enjoys hunting, fishing, riding his road bike, and downhill skiing. He looks forward to providing the Brookings, SD, area with compassionate dental care. He has a special interest in pediatric, cosmetic, and implant dentistry.
