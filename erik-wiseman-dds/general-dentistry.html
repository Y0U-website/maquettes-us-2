Root Canals

Serious cavities and injuries can cause infections in the inside layer of the tooth, known as the pulp. These infections can’t heal on their own, and they usually require root canals. During a root canal, your dentist will clean the infected tooth and disinfect the surrounding area. Your dentist will then seal off the area to prevent further infection, and in some cases, they may restore the tooth with a bond or dental crown .

Periodontal Treatment

It’s essential to receive regular dental cleanings to check for gingivitis, a mild form of gum disease. Gum disease, in the early stages, doesn’t always exhibit symptoms. If left untreated, it can progress into periodontitis. Periodontitis can quickly cause receding gum lines and bone loss. At Prairie Skies Family Dentistry, we prevent and treat early stages of periodontitis.
