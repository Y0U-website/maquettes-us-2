What Are Dental Implants & Crowns?

Dental implants are artificial, root replacements that are surgically placed under the gum line. These implants naturally fuse to the jawbone and serve as a stable, artificial root of a tooth. At Prairie Skies Family Dentistry, we use cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) to place implants more precisely. This type of technology produces 3D images and helps us better understand your teeth, jaw, and facial shape.

When your implants heal, we can cover them with dental crowns or bridges to fully restore missing teeth. Crowns cover individual teeth, while bridges cover areas where there are multiple missing adjacent teeth. In some cases, it’s possible to place dentures over implants for a more natural and secure fit compared to removable dentures.
