Meet the Staff

Clinical Staff

Kate

Say Hello to Kate – Our Smile-Saving Superstar! With over 16 years of experience and a degree from Marshall University (Go Herd!), Kate knows her way around a toothbrush—and a whole lot more. She’s our friendly, calming presence at Erinwood Dental, known for making cleanings feel like a breeze and helping patients feel totally at ease. Kate’s all about keeping smiles healthy and happy with a focus on prevention, education, and a gentle touch. When she’s not brightening smiles, she’s hanging out with her husband and chasing around her two adorable dogs.

Amber

Amber joined the Erinwood Dental team in January 2024, with over 25 years of experience as a Clinical chair side assistant. She earned her dental assisting radiology license in 1998 through the Ohio Dental Board. Amber finds satisfaction in helping patients realize their dream of a beautiful, healthy new smile and a renewed sense of confidence. She and her husband have three beautiful children who are their greatest blessing. They enjoy family time, camping, and traveling.

Lindsey

Lindsey is our knowledgeable insurance coordinator —she makes sense of your benefits and helps you get the most out of them! She’s also a trained dental assistant, so she knows her stuff inside and out. When she’s not at the office, you’ll find her hanging out with her adorable pup, Brownie, and her boyfriend.

Treatment Coordinators

Lori

Lori joined Erinwood Dental in March of 2024 with nearly 30 years of experience in the dental field. She received her radiology license in 1995 through the Ohio Dental Board. Lori loves helping patients navigate through their dental benefits. She finds great satisfaction in giving the patients the knowledge that they need to help utilize their dental health benefits each year. Lori has three children and two bonus children with nine wonderful grandbabies! She is very family-oriented and loves spending time with her family and friends.
