The practice

At Turnersville Family Dentistry, we understand that visiting the dentist can be stressful — especially for children and those with dental anxiety. That’s why we go the extra mile to ensure every visit is as relaxing and positive as possible.

Our caring team, combined with the personal attention of Dr. Ali Eslami, creates a warm, reassuring environment where patients of all ages feel at ease.

From your first exam to your final follow-up, we’re here to support you every step of the way.

Dr. Eslami, earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the prestigious New York University College of Dentistry in 1996.

Following graduation, he completed a comprehensive one-year General Practice Residency at UMDNJ (University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey) from 1996 to 1997.

Since then, Dr. Eslami has been dedicated to providing exceptional dental care in private practice, bringing over 30 years of experience and a commitment to excellence in patient care.
