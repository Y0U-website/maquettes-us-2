Adult Orthodontics

‍ More adults than ever are choosing orthodontic treatment to improve their smiles, oral health, and confidence. Whether you&#x27;ve always wanted straighter teeth, experienced shifting after previous treatment, or are looking to improve your bite, orthodontic care can provide lasting benefits at any age.

At Essex Orthodontics, we understand that adult patients have unique goals and busy schedules. Dr. Elizabeth Clanaman creates customized treatment plans that focus not only on straight teeth, but also on facial balance, bite function, and long-term stability.

Treatment options may include clear aligners, clear ceramic braces, or traditional braces depending on your specific needs. Using advanced digital scanning technology, we eliminate messy impressions and make the treatment process more comfortable and efficient.

Orthodontic treatment can help address: • Crowded teeth • Spacing and gaps • Overbite and deep bite issues • Underbites and crossbites • Relapse after previous orthodontic treatment • Jaw alignment concerns

The result is more than just a beautiful smile. Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, can improve bite function, and contribute to long-term oral health. Many patients also notice increased confidence and improved facial harmony throughout treatment.

Adult orthodontics is ideal for patients who want to improve the appearance, health, and function of their smile. Whether you never had orthodontic treatment as a child, experienced shifting after previous treatment, or are concerned about crowding, spacing, bite issues, or overall smile aesthetics, orthodontic treatment can help. Many adults seek treatment to enhance their confidence, improve oral hygiene, prepare for restorative dental work, or address bite problems that may contribute to uneven tooth wear and discomfort.

Treatment begins with a comprehensive consultation, including digital photographs, x-rays, and a comfortable 3D scan—no messy impressions required. Dr. Elizabeth Clanaman will evaluate your teeth, bite, facial proportions, and goals before creating a customized treatment plan. Depending on your needs, treatment may involve clear aligners, clear ceramic braces, or traditional braces. Throughout treatment, you&#x27;ll have regular visits with Dr. Clanaman to monitor progress and make adjustments, ensuring your smile develops as planned.

‍ The benefits of adult orthodontics extend far beyond straighter teeth. Treatment can improve smile aesthetics, bite function, facial balance, and long-term oral health. Properly aligned teeth are often easier to clean and maintain, while a well-balanced bite can help reduce excessive wear on the teeth. Most importantly, many patients experience a significant boost in confidence as they achieve a smile that looks and feels its best.
