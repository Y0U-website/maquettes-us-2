Surgical Orthodontics

Orthodontic Treatment Combined with Corrective Jaw Surgery

Surgical orthodontics is a specialized treatment that combines orthodontic care with corrective jaw surgery to address significant discrepancies in the position of the upper and lower jaws. While braces and clear aligners can move teeth, some bite and facial balance concerns are caused by the underlying jaw structure and require surgical correction to achieve the best possible result.

At Essex Orthodontics, Dr. Elizabeth Clanaman works closely with experienced oral and maxillofacial surgeons to create a customized treatment plan tailored to each patient&#x27;s needs. Surgical orthodontics can improve bite function, facial harmony, chewing efficiency, speech, and long-term stability while creating a balanced, healthy smile. By addressing both the teeth and the jaws, this comprehensive approach can achieve results that orthodontic treatment alone cannot provide.

‍ Surgical orthodontics is designed for adults with significant jaw discrepancies that cannot be corrected with orthodontic treatment alone. Patients may have concerns such as a severe underbite, overbite, open bite, facial asymmetry, difficulty chewing, speech concerns, or jaw-related discomfort. When the upper and lower jaws are not properly aligned, a combination of orthodontics and corrective jaw surgery may be recommended to achieve the healthiest and most stable result.

‍ Treatment begins with a comprehensive evaluation of your teeth, bite, facial proportions, and jaw relationship. Dr. Elizabeth Clanaman works closely with an oral and maxillofacial surgeon to develop a coordinated treatment plan. Orthodontic treatment is typically completed before surgery to properly position the teeth within each jaw. After the surgical procedure, orthodontic treatment continues to refine the bite and finalize the smile. Throughout the process, our team guides patients every step of the way to ensure a smooth and predictable treatment experience.

Results of Surgical Orthodontic Treatment

‍ Surgical orthodontics can create life-changing improvements in both function and appearance. Treatment can enhance facial balance, improve bite function, make chewing and speaking easier, and create a more harmonious relationship between the teeth, jaws, and facial features. Patients often experience improved confidence, better oral health, and long-term stability that would not be possible with orthodontic treatment alone.
