Dental Veneers

Dental veneers improve your smile cosmetically. Your teeth can be corrected in color, shape, and size. Estey Dental in Brattleboro VT uses the latest technology for the most comfortable and successful procedure. Your smile is our motivation to deliver exceptional results.

An initial exam is needed to begin the process. The examination includes teeth evaluation, x-rays, mouth and teeth impressions. To assure your veneers will fit comfortably, your dentist will remove &frac12; millimeter of enamel. New impressions are made and sent to a laboratory to create the veneers.

Once your veneers arrive, additional trimming may be needed to assure they fit correctly. Once the veneer is ready for application, a special cement is used for bonding. A light beam then cures and hardens the cement. You may be asked to follow up a few days later to evaluate how your mouth is responding to the veneers.

Dental veneers can be treated as natural teeth. They can last 7 to 15 years, depending on regular brushing, flossing and dental cleanings. Avoid foods and drinks that stain. Also, protect your veneers by avoiding hard food and candy.
