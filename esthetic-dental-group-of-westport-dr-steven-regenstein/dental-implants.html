Request an Appointment What Is a Dental Implant? A dental implant is a restorative dental solution that closely mimics a natural tooth. Essentially, it comprises a titanium root that is screwed into the jaw and a ceramic implant that looks like a natural tooth is attached to this root. This structure enables easy integration with the body and promotes swift recovery after the implant.

FAQs

Dental implants are small titanium posts that replace missing tooth roots. After the post is placed in the jaw, it fuses with the bone and supports a custom crown that looks and functions like a natural tooth. Patients choose dental implants in Westport because they are stable, comfortable, and designed to last with proper care.

Most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth are candidates, especially those with adequate jawbone density and good oral hygiene. If you’ve been told you’re not a candidate, our Westport implant dentist can evaluate options such as grafting or All-on-4 to help you qualify.

After an initial consultation and 3D imaging, the implant is placed and allowed to heal while it integrates with the jawbone. A temporary tooth may be used during healing. Once integration is complete, we attach an abutment and place the final crown for a natural look and feel.

Care for implants the same way you would natural teeth: brush twice daily, floss around the implant, and maintain regular professional cleanings. Good home care and routine checkups help keep the gums healthy and support long-term implant success.

Implants help preserve jawbone, prevent neighboring teeth from shifting, and provide the biting strength needed for everyday eating and speaking. Unlike removable options, implant-supported teeth are fixed in place and feel remarkably natural.
