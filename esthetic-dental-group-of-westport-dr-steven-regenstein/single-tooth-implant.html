Request an Appointment What Is a Single Tooth Implant? A single-tooth implant is a tooth loss treatment involving only one implant to support a crown . This treatment is particularly suitable when you have lost just one tooth from decay, bone loss, or gum disease. Single-tooth implants blend seamlessly with your natural teeth and provide an ideal alternative to bridges or removable dentures.

The main objective of a single-tooth implant is to recreate the strength, functionality, and aesthetics of a healthy, natural tooth. This restoration is completely independent of the nearby teeth and allows for easy cleaning and maintenance.

Why Is a Dental Implant Suitable for Single Tooth Replacement?

Choosing a dental implant for single tooth replacement carries significant benefits. Not only does it restore the missing tooth gap, but it also contributes to your overall oral health in several ways:

  • Boosts your oral health by promoting bone growth.
  • It does not require any change to the surrounding teeth.
  • Enhances your natural smile with a tooth that matches your existing teeth.
  • Adds strength to your bite without causing strain on other teeth.
