Crossbite Crossbite occurs when your teeth are naturally misaligned. The main sign of crossbite is when your upper teeth fit behind your lower teeth when the mouth is closed. Our Washington Heights orthodontists can help!

Are Orthodontic Procedures Safe For Children?

Orthodontics is completely safe for children! The AAO (American Academy of Orthodontics) recommends that children visit an orthodontist no later than the age of 7, so that the dentist can evaluate the child’s airway, bite, and oral habits, along with any possible warning signs or concerns.
