Family Owned & Operated

For over 30 years, Estrada Dentistry has proudly served families across west Pasco County, Florida. What started as a commitment to treating every patient like family has grown into a practice that handles nearly all of your dental care under one roof, from routine cleanings and checkups to dental implants, cosmetic work, orthodontics, and sedation dentistry. Keeping treatment in-house means you see the same familiar faces at every visit and one dentist plans your care and sees it through from start to finish, which matters most when a bigger plan comes together over time.

What he is known for, though, is the way he treats people. Dr. Estrada explains things in plain language, never rushes a decision, and takes the time to make nervous patients comfortable rather than pushing them through. That patience is a big part of why so many families across Hudson, Port Richey, and the surrounding communities have trusted him with their smiles for years, and why their children and grandchildren end up in the same chair.

Training and Experience

Dr. Estrada founded the practice in 1990 and has treated west Pasco County patients ever since. Over that time he has taken on the kinds of complex cases many offices refer out, and he has kept that work in-house. One dentist plans, performs, and follows up on your care, so nothing gets lost in a handoff to an outside specialist.

He also treats patients in English and Spanish, which lets families talk through their options in the language they are most comfortable using. That combination of long experience, broad skill, and clear communication is the foundation families throughout west Pasco County have leaned on for decades.
