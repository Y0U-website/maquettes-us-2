Are Clear Aligners an Option?

For patients who would rather not wear visible brackets, clear aligners are a popular alternative. They are a series of smooth, removable trays that gradually guide your teeth into place, and because you take them out to eat and brush, they fit easily into everyday life. Aligners work best for mild to moderate spacing and crowding, while more involved cases are often better served by braces. At your consultation Dr. Estrada maps out the movement your teeth need and recommends the option that will get there.

Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontics

What is the difference between FastBraces and traditional braces?

Traditional braces usually move the crown of a tooth first and the root second, in two stages over about two years. FastBraces uses triangular brackets and a special wire to move the crown and root together from the start, which is why many patients finish in months rather than years. Dr. Estrada can tell you which approach fits your case.

Are FastBraces painful?

FastBraces technology is designed to reduce friction and provide a gentler experience than traditional braces. Most patients report less discomfort compared to conventional orthodontic treatment.

Do braces hurt?

Having braces put on does not hurt, though your teeth usually feel tender for a few days afterward and after each adjustment. Soft foods and over-the-counter pain relief handle it for most patients, and the soreness fades as your mouth gets used to the movement.
