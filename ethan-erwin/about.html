Our Approach to Comfortable, Patient-Focused Dentistry

At Smile Hot Springs, we focus on providing gentle, modern dental care in a welcoming, judgment-free environment where patients of all ages feel comfortable and confident.

We understand that visiting the dentist can be stressful, which is why our team takes the time to listen, explain your options clearly, and ensure you feel at ease every step of the way.

From routine cleanings to cosmetic treatments, we combine advanced technology with a personal, patient-first approach to help you achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile.

We look forward to welcoming you and helping you feel right at home.

Meet the Doctors

Dr. Ethan Erwin, DDS

Dr. Ethan Erwin is a talented and compassionate professional who grew up in Hot Springs and graduated from Lakeside High School in 2012.

Dr. Erwin graduated Summa Cum Laude from the University of Central Arkansas in 2016 with a Bachelors of Science in Biology and a double minor in Honors Interdisciplinary Studies and Chemistry. Dr. Erwin attended Dental School at Texas A&M College of Dentistry formerly Baylor. Upon graduation in 2020, Dr. Erwin practiced dentistry in Central Arkansas at Smile Dailey and Arkansas Dental Centers garnering excellent experience in general and cosmetic dentistry.

In July 2021, he joined Dr. Robert J Barnett and formed Smile Hot Springs serving his home city of Hot Springs. He currently resides in the Hot Springs area with his wife, Dr. Cara Erwin, who is a Physical Therapist at New Hope Therapy. When not practicing dentistry, you can find them with their children, Blakely and Everett, enjoying time on the farm, fishing and exploring the beautiful state of Arkansas. You might even get Dr. Erwin to play the fiddle for you!
