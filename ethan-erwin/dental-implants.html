Facebook icon Instagram icon Google icon Dental Implants in Hot Springs, AR What are Dental Implants? Dental implants are a long-term option for replacing missing or failing teeth. An implant is placed into the jawbone where it acts as a stable foundation for a replacement tooth or teeth.

Depending on your needs, dental implants can be used to replace a single tooth, several teeth, or an entire arch of teeth. They can support individual crowns, bridges, snap-in dentures, implant-supported dentures, and full-arch restorations.

At Smile Hot Springs, Dr. Ethan Erwin and Dr. Britten Balisterri evaluate each patient individually to determine which implant treatment may best fit their oral health, goals, lifestyle, and budget.

What Are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

Dental implants can provide a secure and stable foundation for replacing missing teeth. Depending on the type of restoration, potential benefits may include:

• Improved stability when eating and speaking

‍ • Replacement of one tooth, multiple teeth, or a full arch of teeth

‍ • A natural-looking tooth replacement designed to blend with your smile

‍ • Support for fixed or removable implant-supported restorations

‍ • Greater stability for patients who struggle with loose or moving dentures

‍ • Preservation of healthy neighboring tooth structure in situations where an implant can be used instead of a traditional bridge

‍ • A long-term treatment option when properly maintained

Dr. Ethan Erwin and Dr. Britten Balisterri will evaluate your dental health, bone structure, and restorative needs to help determine which implant approach may be appropriate for you.

What Are Implants in a Day?

For qualifying patients, dental implants can sometimes be placed and temporary implant-supported teeth provided on the same day.

The implants still require time to heal and integrate with the jawbone before the final restoration is completed. Not every patient qualifies for same-day treatment.

Dr. Ethan Erwin and Dr. Britten Balisterri will evaluate your teeth, bone structure, oral health, medical history, and treatment goals to determine whether Implants in a Day may be appropriate for you.
