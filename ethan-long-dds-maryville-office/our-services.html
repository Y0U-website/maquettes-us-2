Crowns

If you have a chipped tooth or severe damage to one or more teeth, we can restore your smile with crowns. We use crowns for restoring and protecting your pearly whites so you can stay confident in your smile and confident in your oral health.

Implant Restorations

Dental implants can wear down over time. We’ll add life to your implants and smile. We make this process comfortable and seamless through Sedation Dentistry to eliminate pain and minimize recovery time.

Fillings

Have a cavity? We can help. We perform both long term and temporary fillings. Dental fillings are a way to block bacteria from getting into your teeth and totally restore damage from decay or fracture.

Root Canals

Larger cavities, infection, and other kinds of tooth trauma can call for treatment like Root Canal Therapy. If you have serious tooth pain or if it hurts to eat, a root canal might be right for you.

Removable Prosthetics

You might think of them as dentures, but there’s a lot more to it! We provide both full and partial dentures, allowing you to restore your small in a noninvasive and cost effective way.
