Common Emergency Scenarios and What to Do

Severe Toothache

A severe toothache can signal infection, a cracked tooth, or advanced decay. Rinse your mouth with warm water and avoid placing aspirin directly on the gum tissue. Call (845) 362-1180 for a same-day evaluation.

Broken or Knocked-Out Tooth

For a broken tooth, rinse gently and save any large pieces. For a knocked-out tooth, time is critical. Pick the tooth up by the crown, not the root. Rinse it gently without scrubbing, and try to reinsert it into the socket. If that is not possible, store it in milk or between your cheek and gum and call our office immediately. Reimplantation is most successful within 30 to 60 minutes of the injury.

Lost Crown or Broken Filling

A lost crown leaves the underlying tooth vulnerable to sensitivity and further damage. Dental cement from a pharmacy can offer temporary protection, but you should still seek prompt care to avoid complications.
