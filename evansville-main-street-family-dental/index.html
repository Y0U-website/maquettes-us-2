The practice

Our experienced and dedicated team, led by Dr. Austin Hixenbaugh, proudly serves the Tri State community. We focus on providing tailored dental solutions designed to enhance and maintain your overall oral health and smile and is located in Downtown Evansville.

Our staff treats every patient as a member of our family. Our goal is to keep your teeth and gums healthy for a lifetime while developing long-lasting, trusting relationships.

0 Prior to Evansville Main Street Family Dental.. Digging deep into our history…

In 1930’s, Dr. Wesner formed the private dental practice which was transitioned to Dr. Edward Bromm in 1946 along with his partner. In the 1960’s, Dr. Bromm continued to practice in downtown Evansville on the 8th floor of the Hulman building (which is still present to this day!).

In 1992, Dr. Patrick Tromley bought the practice from Dr. Bromm. Dr. Tromley would then move the practice to where is located now in 1998. Later, the practice was officially named Evansville Main Street Family Dental.

Dr. Austin Hixenbaugh officially came into the practice as a associate with Dr. Patrick Tromley in June 2019. Dr. Tromley officially retired in December of 2024 where the practice was successfully transition to Dr. Hixenbaugh in May 2025.
