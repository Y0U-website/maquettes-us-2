What Is the Dental Implant Process?

The dental implant process is done in two stages. First, the dentist implants a titanium post into the socket where the tooth used to be. This will take 3-8 months to heal as the titanium rod fuses with the bone.

Once the implant heals, it will provide a solid support for a restoration tooth that our dentist will place at a later date.

The dentist can also place several implants along a section of the gums, allowing them to replace entire rows of teeth with dentures.

Once the process is completed, patients can use their dental implants like regular teeth, returning quality to their daily lives and beauty to their smiles.

Am I a Good Candidate for a Dental Implant?

If you are missing one or more teeth in a row and are looking to fill the gap in your smile, dental implants might be the right choice for you. They are permanent mouth fixtures that add function back to the patient s bite.

As long as the patient s gums and jaw are healthy enough to handle the implant process, they should be a good candidate for dental implants.

Patients with conditions like periodontal disease may not be good candidates for dental implants until the issue has been mitigated and controlled.

To know for sure, patients will have to meet with the dentist for a thorough dental examination that may include x-rays.

Dental implants are designed to be a permanent solution for missing teeth, but in some rare cases, they can fall out.
