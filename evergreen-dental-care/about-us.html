Wendy

Wendy is originally from the Boston area and moved to the Capital Region with her husband and 2 children in 2004. Although her background was originally in Accounting and Human Resources, after being a stay at home mom for 10 years and spending a lot of time in her children’s classrooms, she realized she wanted to spend more time helping and educating people. So began her journey into Dental Hygiene. She graduated from Hudson Valley Community College’s Dental Hygiene program in 2013 and has enjoyed helping patients maintain their oral health in private practice for the last 13 years.

In her free time, Wendy enjoys taking walks with her dog, Lucy, and doing anything outdoors. She also enjoys reading and spending time with her husband and children.

Heisha

Heisha began her journey in dentistry after moving to Houston, Texas, where she discovered her passion for the dental field. She attended college in Houston and graduated as a Dental Assistant in 2017. Since then, she has gained experience in general dentistry, pediatric dentistry and oral surgery.

She believes dentistry is both a science and an art, and finds it incredibly rewarding to be part of the process that helps restore smiles and improve patients’ confidence and comfort. Heisha is dedicated to patient care and enjoys continuously learning and growing within the dental field.

In her free time, she enjoys reading, working out, taking walks with her two French bulldogs, Anakin and Akai, exploring new restaurants with her husband and meeting new friends.
