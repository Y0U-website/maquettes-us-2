Meet The Doctor

Dr. Matthew Lam

Dr. Matthew Lam is a resident of Northern Virginia. He went to George Mason University for his Bachelor degree prior to attending the prestigious University of Iowa College of Dentistry and Dental Clinics to earn the Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree. Collectively, he has experience in both the private and public health sectors, including experience with Invisalign, endodontics, children’s dentistry, restorative and surgery. Dr. Lam’s research experience, volunteer efforts and awards are a reflection of his commitment to provide excellent dental care for his patients.
