Difference Between Clear Aligners and Conventional Braces

Comfortable, removable and practically invisible, clear aligners usually take less time in treatment than traditional braces. Since patients can take them out for brushing and flossing, they also provide a path to straighter teeth while maintaining better oral hygiene. Clear aligners also require fewer visits with the dentist during treatment.
