Answers to Common Questions

Dental implants consist of what materials? Dental implants typically consist of biocompatible materials, particularly titanium, which is known for its ability to safely interact with the human body. Similar biocompatible metals are also utilized in other types of bone implants like shoulder, hip, and knee replacements. The externally visible part of the implant is usually crafted from porcelain material to perfectly blend in with your natural teeth, being customized to match your existing dental structure.

Are dental implants considered long-lasting and durable? Dental implants are specifically crafted to seamlessly integrate with the jawbone, resulting in their permanent anchorage. In general terms, their success rate is exceptionally high, reaching close to 100 %. While rare, there may be instances where the implant fails to fuse as intended and necessitates removal. Should this circumstance arise, the procedure can be reapplied after a few months have passed.

Are dental implants costly? Typically, dental insurance does not provide coverage for dental implants. However, there is a possibility that a patient’s medical insurance might offer coverage for this treatment. Our dental office, in collaboration with your insurance provider, can engage in a discussion with you about the various coverage options available. This will be tailored specifically to your unique case and treatment plan.

How should my dental implants be maintained? Maintaining an implant is a breeze; simply treat it with the same care as a genuine tooth! This entails following a routine of regular brushing, meticulous flossing, and scheduling routine dental checkups. Should any concerns arise regarding your implant, do not hesitate to contact us promptly.
