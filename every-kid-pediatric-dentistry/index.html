The practice

We focus on preventative care and aim to educate families on the importance of regular dental care. Our goal is to ensure that children build healthy dental habits to keep their smiles bright and clean for their whole lives!

We proudly serve infants, children, and teens in Eugene, Oregon. If you have questions prior to your visit, reach out to us and also check out these helpful topics throughout our site:

We will walk side by side with you as your child begins to learn about dental care and establishes their own healthy habits. Plus we have lots of fun distractions and ways to make their visit pleasant.

Routine visits are an essential part of maintaining a healthy smile. Let us help you keep a routine to prevent future issues.

Every Kid Pediatric Dentistry offers compassionate specialized care for patients who have physical or mental disabilities of many kinds.

In the case of dental emergencies, give us a visit. Emergencies include, but are not limited to toothaches, knocked out, chipped, or fractured permanent teeth.

Every Kid Pediatric Dentistry is proud to be the favorite kids dentist of many families in Eugene.
