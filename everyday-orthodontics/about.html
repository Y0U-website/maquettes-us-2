The Everyday Ethos

At Everyday Orthodontics, we believe in providing stellar, affordable care that’s tailored to each unique person we have the privilege of helping — whether that’s you, your littlest family member, your grandparent, or someone you love with special needs. We believe in finding the best possible fit for you and your treatment, even if that means going to someone else. We believe in smiling so much your face hurts, and that unicorns are totally real. We believe in building a practice that champions honesty and integrity, and puts patients first, every day.

What to Expect

Enhancing Your Experience

Fully Digital Office

Skip the clipboard and save time. We’ve got your back with paperless patient forms and check-ins for a stress-free visit as soon as you walk in.

Impression-Free Treatment

Wave goodbye to the goop! We use the TRIOS intraoral scanner, a revolutionary imaging tool that creates a full-mouth digital impression in just minutes.

Virtual Appointments

We’re making high-quality care accessible and convenient with virtual visits. Just hop on a video call from the comfort of home and leave the heavy lifting to us.

Biolase Laser

The world’s leading innovator in dental lasers, this powerful tool allows Dr. Liz to deliver precise treatments with faster healing and unrivaled results.

Beverage Station

Our space feels more like your favorite cafe than an orthodontist office. Make yourself at home with a fresh cup of coffee or tea from our complimentary beverage station.

Sensory Toys

From fidget spinners to the softest plush pals, we have an abundance of sensory toys to help patients with special needs or dental anxiety feel at ease.
