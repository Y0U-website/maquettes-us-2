About Evolve Endodontics

Welcome to Evolve Endodontics, a specialized endodontic practice in Westhampton Beach, NY, led by Dr. Yigit Gol, DDS, MBS. As a proud member of the American Association of Endodontists and the American Dental Association , Dr. Gol is committed to providing exceptional root canal care and advanced endodontic treatments to patients throughout Suffolk County.

We look forward to welcoming you — call 631-998-9899 to schedule your visit at our Westhampton Beach , NY office.

History

What inspired you to become an endodontist? I pursued endodontics because I wanted the skills and expertise to relieve pain while giving patients the opportunity to keep their natural teeth. Tooth loss is not always inevitable, and many teeth that appear hopeless can be successfully treated and restored. One of the most rewarding moments in my profession is telling a patient that their tooth can, in fact, be saved. Helping patients avoid unnecessary extractions and preserve their natural dentition is at the heart of everything we do.

What is the most rewarding part of practicing as an endodontist? One of the most rewarding moments in endodontics is seeing significant healing at a follow-up visit. Watching an infection resolve is a powerful reminder of the body’s remarkable ability to heal. It reinforces the effectiveness of modern endodontic therapy and the value of preserving a patient’s natural tooth whenever possible.

Why did you choose Westhampton for your location? The east end of Long Island has long been underserved when it comes to specialty endodontic care. Many patients have had to travel more than an hour to see an endodontist. I established evolve endodontics to help meet this need by bringing advanced specialty care closer to home. Conveniently located just outside the South Fork, our practice is easily accessible to patients from both the North and South Forks, as well as central Long Island.

How do you hope to impact Westhampton? My goal is to become a trusted resource for dentists and patients throughout Long Island. I strive to build lasting relationships with my referring doctors by providing exceptional care, clear communication, and treating every patient as if they were a member of my own family. I want to be the specialist my colleagues can confidently trust with their patients, knowing they will receive compassionate, evidence-based care and be returned to their office with the highest standard of treatment.

Mission

What does the name "evolve endodontics" represent? Endodontics has advanced significantly over the last few decades, and our practice was built with those innovations at its core. We believe patients deserve the benefits of modern dentistry, which is why we invest in state-of-the-art technology, advanced diagnostics, and contemporary treatment techniques. Our goal is to provide the highest quality endodontic care with greater precision, efficiency, and comfort than ever before.

Philosophy

What is your philosophy when treating patients? A proper diagnosis is the foundation of exceptional endodontic care. Before recommending any treatment, I perform a thorough evaluation using specialized endodontic testing and, when appropriate, advanced 3D CBCT imaging. The answer is not always as simple as, "You need a root canal." Tooth pain can have many causes, and every case deserves careful investigation. My goal is to identify the true source of the problem and recommend the treatment that is most appropriate for your tooth, giving it the best opportunity for long-term success.

If someone could describe your practice in only three words, what would you hope they say? Clean. Calm. Comfortable. These are the feelings we want every patient to experience from the moment they walk through our doors. We have thoughtfully designed our office to feel more like a spa than a dental practice, creating a relaxing environment that helps ease anxiety. While no one looks forward to needing a root canal, our goal is to make your visit as stress-free and comfortable as possible.

Technology

Why should a referring dentist choose evolve endodontics? Our referring doctors share our commitment to providing the highest standard of care for their patients. Modern endodontics combines advanced training with technologies such as 3D CBCT imaging and the dental operating microscope to improve diagnostic accuracy, treatment precision, and clinical outcomes. By utilizing these innovations and evidence-based techniques, we strive to give every tooth the greatest opportunity for long-term success.

Patient Experience

How do you help patients overcome anxiety? In addition to the soothing music and relaxing atmosphere throughout the office, we also offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for patients who may benefit from an extra level of relaxation. Our goal is to help every patient feel comfortable, at ease, and confident throughout their visit.

What is one misconception people have about root canals? One of the biggest misconceptions about root canals is that they are painful. With modern techniques and profound local anesthesia, root canal therapy is a comfortable procedure for the vast majority of patients. In fact, once the tooth is completely numb, most patients find the experience to be very similar to having a routine filling.

What do patients most commonly thank you for? We understand that many patients arrive feeling anxious, and we believe they deserve compassion, patience, and understanding. Every patient is different, and we take the time to listen to your concerns, answer your questions, and move at a pace that makes you feel comfortable. Our goal is to create a supportive environment where you feel informed, respected, and at ease throughout your visit.
