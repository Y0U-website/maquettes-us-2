FAQs: Our SmileWise Dental Savings Plans vs. Insurance

With dental insurance, I can pay premiums monthly. Why don’t I just purchase dental insurance instead?

Dental insurance might be the right option for you. Dental insurance policies vary from provider to provider and plan to plan. If you’re comparing insurance policies, we strongly recommend that you review the complete plan details on covered services, limitations, and exclusions. Our dental savings plans are designed for uninsured patients who either don’t qualify for or carry dental insurance and would like to save money on their dental care right away.

What’s the difference between your prices for someone who doesn’t have insurance and for someone who does?

Dentists and insurance companies agree on contracted prices that are generally less and vary for each service; however, that doesn’t tell the complete story. If you’re shopping for dental insurance, then consider the following cost factors:

  • Your monthly premiums
  • Your deductibles for certain services
  • Your percentage share of the contract price for services
  • Your out-of-pocket maximum
  • The program maximum that insurance will pay for certain services

FAQs: How Our SmileWise Dental Savings Plans Work

If I choose one of your savings plans, do I need to pay the annual price upfront?

Yes, the full amount must be paid before you can take advantage of the benefits.

When will my annual savings plan begin?

Unlike dental insurance policies that begin on January 1 and end on December 31, the dental savings plan you choose from us begins when you purchase it, which means it can coincide with your first appointment and you can take advantage of the full one-year term.

Can I roll over the unused benefits of my savings plan?

No, unfortunately you cannot. This would be the same if you had dental insurance. When you’re here for an appointment, we recommend booking your next appointment before you leave.

Which one of your savings plans is right for me?

We let you know after your initial consultation. Please rest assured that we never pressure you to purchase one of our dental savings plans. Simply put, we care about your dental health and want you to have financial options that help achieve your goals with prevention, maintenance, and restoration.

If our dental savings plans aren’t right for you, we also offer financing options that may help relieve the financial burden for you – CareCredit ® and Cherry are alternative sources of interest-free or low APR financing. Visit our Financing & Payment Options page.
