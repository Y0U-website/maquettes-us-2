Everything you need to know about dental care

Do you accept new patients, and how do I book an appointment?

Yes, we warmly welcome new patients of all ages, including children, adults, and seniors. You can book your first appointment by calling our office, using our online booking form, or walking in during office hours.

We offer flexible scheduling, including early morning, evening, and weekend appointments at select locations, to accommodate busy schedules. Same-day appointments are available for dental emergencies.

What dental insurance plans do you accept?

We are in-network providers with most major dental insurance plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, United Concordia, Humana, Guardian, and many others. We also accept MassHealth (Medicaid) for qualifying patients.

Our team will verify your specific insurance coverage before your appointment and explain exactly what is covered, what your copay will be, and whether any deductibles apply—so there are no surprises at checkout.

What if I don’t have dental insurance?

No dental insurance? No problem. We offer several affordable options for uninsured patients:

We believe everyone deserves quality dental care regardless of insurance status, and we’ll work with you to find a payment solution that fits your budget.

  • CareCredit — a healthcare financing option with low and no-interest payment plans for qualified applicants
  • Sunbit — a buy-now, pay-later option with plans up to 72 months, no hard credit check, no late fees, and over 85 % approval rate
  • Cash, check, and major credit cards are all accepted at the time of service
  • Ask about our new patient specials or in-house savings plans for discounted rates on exams, X-rays, and cleanings without insurance

Do you offer emergency dental appointments for new patients?

Yes. We accept dental emergencies for new and existing patients. If you are experiencing severe tooth pain, a broken or knocked-out tooth, a lost filling or crown, swelling, or a dental abscess, call our office immediately.

We prioritize emergency cases and will make every effort to see you the same day. Do not ignore dental emergencies — infections in particular can spread quickly and become serious if left untreated.

What happens during a first dental visit?

Your first dental appointment typically includes:

The entire first visit usually takes 60–90 minutes. You’ll leave with a clear picture of your oral health and any recommended next steps.

  • Medical & dental history review — we review your health background, medications, and any dental concerns
  • Comprehensive oral examination — the dentist checks your teeth, gums, bite, jaw, and soft tissues
  • Dental X-rays — digital X-rays are taken to detect issues not visible to the naked eye
  • Professional cleaning — a hygienist removes plaque and tartar buildup
  • Treatment discussion — the dentist reviews findings and recommends a personalized care plan

Do you treat children as new patients?

Yes, we offer dentistry for kids and welcome patients of all ages, including infants, toddlers, and teenagers. The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends a child’s first dental visit by age 1 or when their first tooth appears.

Do you offer same-day appointments for new patients?

We do our best to accommodate same-day and next-day appointments for new patients, especially for dental emergencies. If you are experiencing a toothache, broken tooth, lost filling, swelling, or any urgent dental concern, please call us directly and we will make every effort to see you as quickly as possible. For routine new patient exams, appointments are typically available within a few business days.

What makes Excel Dental different from other dental offices?

Excel Dental combines advanced technology, a compassionate team, and convenient multi-location access across the Greater Boston and North Shore area — including Lowell , Billerica , Methuen , Haverhill , Everett , Chelsea , Lynn , Revere and Bridge St. We offer same-day crown technology, in-house specialists, flexible scheduling, and a full range of services from pediatric dentistry to dental implants — all under one roof. Our mission is simple: to provide uncompromising excellence in dental care while treating every patient like family.

Can I complete my new patient forms online or before my visit?

Yes. To save time at your first appointment, you can fill your new patient registration forms in advance from our website. Pre-filling your forms means more time in the chair with your doctor and less time in the waiting room.
