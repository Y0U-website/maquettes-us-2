A dental implant is essentially a small, screw-shaped titanium post that acts as a replacement for the root of a missing tooth. It provides a strong and stable foundation for a custom-made crown that looks and feels like a natural tooth.

The implant placement process is typically straightforward and can be done with local anesthesia, making it a simple, routine procedure. Over time, the implant fuses with the surrounding bone, creating a secure base for the final crown.
