Dental Implants

  • How long does it take to recover from implant surgery? Initial recovery from implant surgery usually takes a few days, while full healing and implant integration may take a few months. During this time, our team will guide you through aftercare and monitor your progress to ensure the best outcome.
  • Are dental implants a permanent solution? Yes, with proper care, dental implants are designed to be permanent tooth replacement solutions. Routine checkups and good oral hygiene help ensure their long-term success.
