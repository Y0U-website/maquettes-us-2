Enroll Now Bright Smiles Plan Membership at Excel Dentistry and Braces Everyone deserves access to quality dental care, even without insurance. That’s why we offer the Bright Smiles Plan—a simple in-house savings plan to help you afford the care you need. Signing up is quick and straightforward. For $99 per year, members receive excellent care at an unbeatable value with: No waiting periods
  • No exclusions for pre-existing conditions
  • No annual benefit caps
  • No deductibles
  • Unlimited exams
  • All required X-rays
  • An annual oral cancer screening

How It Works

  • Sign up and pay: Subscribe and pay online or at our office. It’s quick and easy, taking only a few minutes.
  • Visit your dentist for treatment: Schedule appointments with your dentist.
  • Give feedback: Share your experience to help us maintain and improve your quality of care.

Membership FAQs

  • What forms of payment do you accept? We accept cash, checks, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Venmo, PayPal, major credit cards and third-party options, including CareCredit ® and Sunbit.
  • When can I start using my dental membership? You can start using your plan immediately after signing up.
  • How do I schedule my preventative care? Use our online scheduling tool or call our office to schedule your appointment. Schedule Online
  • How can I use my member-exclusive discount for treatment? Just let us know you’re a member when you schedule. We’ll apply your discount and ensure your membership is active.
  • Can I add members to my account? Yes, your entire family can enjoy the benefits of the Bright Smiles Plan.
  • Can I transfer my membership to another dentist? The Bright Smiles Plan is exclusive to Excel Dentistry and Braces and cannot be transferred.
