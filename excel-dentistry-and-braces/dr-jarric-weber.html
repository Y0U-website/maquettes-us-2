Dr. Jarric Weber

Delivering compassionate, expert dental care in Oklahoma City, Dr. Jarric Weberr brings a personal touch and dedicated expertise to every patient’s experience.

The Professional Side

Dr. Jarric Weber, known to his patients as Dr. Jarric, is a dedicated general dentist who brings enthusiasm, compassion, and a patient-first mindset to his care. He earned his bachelor’s degree from Brigham Young University–Idaho and completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry, graduating in May 2025.

Dr. Jarric began his professional career practicing at Comfort Dental, where he developed a strong foundation in comprehensive general dentistry. His passion for the profession was inspired early in life by his grandfather, a dentist he deeply admired, whose love for dentistry motivated Dr. Jarric to follow the same path. His primary goal is to help patients feel confident when they smile, while ensuring they have a positive and comfortable dental experience.

The Personal Side

Outside of the dental office, Dr. Jarric enjoys spending quality time with his kids and staying active through sports. Whether he’s participating or watching a game, he values teamwork, energy, and balance, qualities that also shape the way he connects with his patients and colleagues.
