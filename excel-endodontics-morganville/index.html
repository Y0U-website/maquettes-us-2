Dr. Rachel Halpern graduated summa cum laude from Muhlenberg College and earned her D.D.S. degree from Columbia University, where she received numerous awards. She completed advanced training in endodontics at NYU College of Dentistry, where her research gained national recognition and was published in the prestigious Journal of Endodontics.

A lifelong resident of Monmouth County, Dr. Halpern combines her technical expertise with a compassionate approach to care. Outside the office, she enjoys spending time with her husband and three young children, along with fitness, traveling, and baking.
