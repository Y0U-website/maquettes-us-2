About us

Being a dentist is incredibly rewarding for me—I genuinely enjoy the privilege of creating beautiful smiles and helping my patients achieve optimal dental health and overall wellness. Dentistry is more than just my profession; it’s a commitment to enhancing the lives of my patients through comprehensive care that merges functionality and aesthetics seamlessly.

With seventeen years of extensive experience, I have dedicated myself to mastering all facets of restorative dentistry, prosthetics, and surgical procedures. My journey in dentistry is driven by a deep passion for continuous education, allowing me to stay at the forefront of cutting-edge techniques, particularly in the evolving fields of digital dentistry, dental aesthetics, and implant dentistry. This ongoing commitment ensures that every patient receives the highest standard of care, carefully tailored to their unique needs and aspirations.

At Excellence Dental, we provide a broad spectrum of dental services designed to meet diverse dental requirements. Our comprehensive offerings range from preventive dental care and routine checkups to specialized treatments such as dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, clear aligners, porcelain veneers, dental inlays and onlays, and effective solutions for gum disease treatment. Whether you’re seeking routine dental hygiene care, emergency dental assistance, or transformative aesthetic enhancements, our team is here to deliver personalized, compassionate, and exceptional dental care.

Conveniently located in Needham, Massachusetts, Excellence Dental warmly welcomes patients from surrounding communities including Wellesley, Newton, Dedham, Westwood, and Dover. Our modern dental office is equipped with state-of-the-art technology such as digital x-rays, panorex imaging, rotary endodontics, and soft tissue lasers, ensuring precise diagnostics and comfortable treatments for all our patients.

Understanding the anxieties that can accompany dental visits, we strive to create an environment that feels comfortable, welcoming, and stress-free. Every member of our professional team is committed to making your experience with us positive and relaxed, emphasizing clear communication and compassionate care at every stage of treatment.

In addition to providing dental solutions that promote oral health, we are passionate about helping you achieve a confident, bright smile that you’ll proudly share. Treatments like Invisalign clear aligners, dental veneers, and professional teeth whitening are just a few examples of how we help our patients transform their smiles. We believe strongly in preventive care and education, guiding our patients on the best practices for maintaining optimal oral health at home.

As a dentist, forming long-term relationships with patients is one of the most fulfilling aspects of my career. It’s truly rewarding to watch individuals and families grow and thrive, knowing that their dental health is in trusted hands. Each visit is an opportunity for us to enhance your dental wellness and overall quality of life.

Outside of my professional endeavors, I have a deep passion for travel and culinary exploration. These personal interests provide a refreshing balance to my professional life, continually enriching my interactions with people from diverse backgrounds and cultures. This holistic perspective helps me better understand and connect with my patients, contributing to the compassionate care that defines our practice.

At Excellence Dental, we are dedicated to exceeding your expectations by providing outstanding dental care in a supportive and attentive environment. We look forward to welcoming you to our dental family and partnering with you to achieve the healthy, beautiful smile you deserve.
