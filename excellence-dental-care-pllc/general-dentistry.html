How often should I visit Excellence Dental Care for a checkup?

At Excellence Dental Care, we recommend regular dental visits every six months, following the guidelines of the American Dental Association. These visits help detect early signs of decay, gum disease, and other oral health concerns before they become more serious. Regular visits also enable us to more closely follow your personalized plan and maintain preventive care tailored to your smile.

What fluoride treatments does Excellence Dental Care offer, and how do they help?

Fluoride is one of the most effective preventive measures for maintaining oral health, helping to keep your teeth strong, healthy, and resistant to decay. Depending on your needs, we may use fluoride gels, varnishes, or professional rinses to deliver maximum protection for both children and adults.

Our personalized approach ensures that every patient receives the right type of fluoride treatment for their unique smile.

Can adults benefit from dental sealants at Excellence Dental Care?

Absolutely. While dental sealants are traditionally associated with pediatric care, research shows that adults without decay or fillings on their molars can also benefit significantly. Sealants act as a physical barrier, protecting the deep grooves and fissures of teeth from bacterial accumulation and the development of cavities.

At Excellence Dental Care, we provide customized sealant applications for adults based on individual risk assessments, oral anatomy, and hygiene habits. When combined with regular examinations, professional cleanings, and preventive care, sealants offer a highly effective, minimally invasive strategy to preserve long-term oral health.

When should I consider a deep cleaning at Excellence Dental Care?

A deep cleaning, also known as scaling and root planing, is recommended when clinical signs of periodontal disease are present. Symptoms may include persistent gum bleeding, gum inflammation or swelling, gum recession, pocket formation between the teeth and gums, tooth mobility, or chronic halitosis.

Untreated periodontal disease can lead to bone loss, tooth loss, and increased systemic risks, including cardiovascular complications and diabetes challenges. At Excellence Dental Care, we perform a thorough periodontal assessment and recommend deep cleaning when necessary to remove subgingival plaque and calculus, reduce bacterial load, and halt disease progression.
