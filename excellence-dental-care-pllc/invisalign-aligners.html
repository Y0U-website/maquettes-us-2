How Invisalign® Works

Your journey with Invisalign® begins with a comprehensive consultation at Excellence Dental . During this visit, we assess your dental alignment, discuss your goals, and determine if Invisalign® is the right option for you. Using advanced 3D imaging technology, we create a customized treatment plan tailored specifically to your needs. This plan maps out the exact movements your teeth will make throughout the process.

Invisalign® aligners are made from clear, smooth plastic that gently shifts your teeth into their ideal positions. You’ll switch to a new set of aligners approximately every two weeks, with each set continuing to move your teeth closer to your final smile. The aligners are designed for comfort, making it easier to wear them throughout the day without irritation.

Why Choose Invisalign®?

Invisalign® offers several advantages over traditional braces. First, the aligners are nearly invisible, which means you can straighten your teeth without drawing attention. This makes Invisalign® particularly appealing for those who are concerned about the appearance of metal braces. Additionally, Invisalign® aligners are removable, so you can take them out during meals or while brushing and flossing, allowing you to maintain your normal lifestyle and oral hygiene routine.

Comfort is another key benefit. The aligners are made of smooth plastic, which reduces the irritation commonly associated with metal brackets and wires. Invisalign® also requires fewer office visits, as you simply switch aligners at home and visit us for check-ups periodically.

Experience a Straighter, Healthier Smile with Invisalign®

The Invisalign® process typically takes several months, depending on the complexity of your case. However, the results are worth the wait—a beautifully aligned smile that improves both your appearance and dental health.
