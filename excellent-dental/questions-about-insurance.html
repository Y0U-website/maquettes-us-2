Dental Insurance And You

More than half of the country has some form of dental insurance, and if you count yourself among this group, then you can rest easy because our dental office is happy to take your plan. Our team is very familiar with the ins and outs of several popular policies, and we find that most patients are eager to learn basic tips on how to maximize their coverage. Below, you’ll find some general ways to get the most out of your benefits, but to learn more regarding your specific policy, we invite you to give our team a call.

Understanding How Dental Insurance Works

The most important thing to understand about dental insurance is that the majority of plans are primarily designed to help people prevent dental issues as opposed to treating them. That’s why services like checkups and cleanings tend to receive more coverage than fillings and crowns. You can still use your insurance to pay for dental repair, but if you’re looking to save as much as possible, focusing on prevention is the best way to go. By doing this, a patient will also drastically lower their need for more expensive procedures over time, helping lower their overall care cost even more.

What Your Dental Insurance Plan Covers

Expanding on what we mentioned above, for most policies, preventive treatments like checkups and cleanings are covered 80-100 %, which is usually the highest offered by any plan. Below that, restorative services like fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, extractions, and root canals are covered about 50-70 %. This is why we tell patients they should use their plans mainly for prevention! For cosmetic dentistry, it is typically NOT covered because the procedures are considered elective. This refers to treatments like teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, and cosmetic bonding.
