Stage 2: Alignment & Smile Arc

With the foundational bite and jaw corrections underway, we begin focusing on the alignment of the teeth and the visual shape of the smile. This stage is where crowding, spacing, and rotations are corrected. Whether your teeth have shifted over time or have never been aligned properly, this is the point in treatment where we begin to transform the appearance of your smile.

We pay close attention to how each tooth is positioned not just in relation to its neighbors, but within the bigger picture of your face and smile. One of the key goals here is to create an ideal smile arc , which refers to the natural curve of your upper teeth following the contour of your lower lip when you smile. This arc is one of the hallmarks of a youthful, confident, and attractive smile.

This stage is not just about straightening it’s about designing a smile that fits you. One that looks great in conversation, on camera, and in the mirror.

Stage 5: Gum Shape & Display

The final stage focuses on the frame of the smile: your gum line. For many adults, the gum display can be uneven, excessive (a "gummy smile"), or asymmetrical due to past growth patterns or how the teeth erupted. While the teeth may be perfectly aligned, the gum contour can still affect the overall appearance of the smile.

At Excelsior Orthodontics, we use laser gingivectomy to gently and precisely reshape the gums, creating a cleaner, more symmetrical frame around each tooth. In more complex cases, where the gums and upper lip move excessively when smiling, we may use TAD-supported intrusion mechanics to help reduce vertical tooth and gum display, improving both aesthetics and comfort.

This stage is all about finishing with intention ensuring your smile doesn’t just look straight, but looks refined, balanced, and complete.

Step 4: Celebrate the Smile

Reaching the end of treatment is a major milestone and we believe it deserves recognition. After months of care, precision, and collaboration, we reveal the final result: a healthy, confident smile that reflects the time and intention you’ve invested.

We’ll capture final records, review your transformation, and provide everything you need to maintain your results including custom retainers. For many adults, this moment is about more than just straight teeth it’s about reclaiming confidence, improving function, and finally having a smile that feels like you.

Step 5: Maintain The Smile

A beautifully aligned smile is an investment and we’re here to help you protect it for life. After treatment, you’ll receive custom retainers designed to keep your teeth in their ideal positions. Dr. Minh will guide you on when and how to wear them, based on your unique case and long-term goals.

We also schedule follow-up visits to check in on your smile, monitor retainer fit, and make sure everything remains stable as you age. Whether you’re years out from treatment or just finishing now, you can count on us for ongoing support and guidance.

Because at Excelsior Orthodontics, we’re not just creating smiles we’re committed to helping you keep them.
