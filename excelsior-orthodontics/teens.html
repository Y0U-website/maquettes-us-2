Stage 2: Alignment & Smile Arc

Once the bite and jaw position are corrected, we shift our focus to straightening the teeth and closing any gaps. During this stage, we carefully position each tooth to follow a smooth, natural curve what we call the smile arc. This helps create an attractive, flowing smile that follows the shape of the lips and enhances overall facial aesthetics. It’s not just about getting teeth in a row it’s about how they look when your teen smiles.

Alignment

Smile Arc

Stage 3: Smile Symmetry & Display

In this stage, we focus on how the smile looks when your teen grins naturally from corner to corner. We make sure the smile is wide, balanced, and fills the space between the cheeks, so it doesn’t appear narrow or uneven. Creating symmetry in the smile display enhances overall harmony and ensures the teeth show evenly on both sides. It’s a detail that makes a big difference in turning a good smile into a great one.

Smile Symmetry

Smile Display

Stage 4: Tooth Shape

With the teeth in the right position, we turn our attention to the shape of each tooth. Sometimes small adjustments like smoothing edges, refining contours, or reshaping worn or uneven teeth can make a big visual difference. This stage helps ensure that every tooth looks proportionate and fits naturally within the smile, adding polish and harmony to the final result.

Tooth Shape

Stage 5: Gum Shape & Display

The final stage focuses on how the gums frame the smile. If too much gum shows when your teen smiles, or if the gum line appears uneven, we may recommend gentle procedures to improve the overall appearance. This can include laser gingivectomy to reshape the gum line or using TADs (Temporary Anchorage Devices) to help reduce a gummy smile by adjusting tooth and lip position. These finishing touches help create a clean, balanced look bringing harmony to the entire smile.

Step 4: Celebrate the Smile

After months of teamwork, encouragement, and steady progress, it’s time to celebrate. Your teen’s new smile is the result of intentional care, detailed planning, and a partnership between our team, your child, and your family. We take this moment seriously because a confident, healthy smile is something to be proud of.

We’ll walk you through final photos, provide retainers, and of course, make it a celebration. This is more than just the end of treatment it’s the beginning of a lifelong smile they’ll carry with confidence.

Step 5: Maintain The Smile

A beautiful smile is built to last but it still needs care. After treatment, your teen will receive custom retainers to help preserve the results and prevent teeth from shifting over time. We’ll give you clear instructions on when and how to wear them, and we’ll schedule periodic check-ins to ensure everything stays on track.

Retainers are a simple but essential part of protecting the smile you and your teen worked so hard to create and we’re here to support you every step of the way.
