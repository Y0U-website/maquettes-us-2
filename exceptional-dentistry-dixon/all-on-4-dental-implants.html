The All-on-4 Dental Implant Procedure

Prior to the inception of All-on-4 dental implants, if you choose a dental implant, you underwent an extended period of treatment. Implant placement started with Exceptional Dentistry extracting any damaged teeth, then allowing for proper healing and temporary denture wearing, bone grafting if jawbone density was needed, more healing, the surgical placement of the implants, more healing time and then finally the placement of the false tooth on the implant. Now, with the new, innovative All-on-4 dental implant treatment, the entire procedure can literally be achieved in just one visit.
