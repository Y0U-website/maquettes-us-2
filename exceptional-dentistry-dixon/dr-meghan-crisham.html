Dr. Megan Crisham

Dr. Meghan Crisham, a Dixon, Illinois native and University of Illinois School of Dentistry graduate, brings a rich background in leadership, sports, and academic achievements to her role at Exceptional Dentistry, inspired by the art and science of dentistry observed in her family dentist’s office.

Dr. Crisham is a 2005 graduate of the University of Illinois School of Dentistry. While in dental school, Dr. Meghan Crisham was a four-year class elected representative to the Chicago Dental Society. During her fourth year, she was awarded the Stanley Tylman Award for providing dental care of the highest caliber to her patients.

In 2006, Dr. Crisham was awarded and completed a one-year post-graduate residency program in Boston, Massachusetts with Lutheran Medical Center. Dr. Crisham then relocated to practice dentistry in New York City. Dr. Crisham is married to Dr. Patrick King who is currently a post-graduate Endodontic resident at the University of Illinois College of Dentistry.

Dr. Hey was her family dentist, and it was in his office as a patient that Dr. Crisham was able to experience first-hand how multi-faceted dentistry can be. By observing Dr. Hey complete complex restorative and cosmetic cases, Dr. Crisham noted the artistic aspect of dentistry that must go hand in hand with the science.

Dr. Crisham is pleased to be a part of the Exceptional Dentistry Team and looks forward to helping the practice continue to grow. Dr. Crisham is impressed by the focus of Dr. Hey and his staff to stay current and knowledgeable about the constant and exciting changes and advances in dentistry.
