Dr. Shailee S. Patel

Dr. Shailee S. Patel, dedicated to compassionate dental care, returned to her roots in Illinois to serve the Sterling-Dixon-Rock Falls community after enhancing her skills through a General Practice Residency in Denver, continuing a tradition of quality patient care.

Upon conclusion of the GPR, she returned to Illinois to take up a position in Freeport. She practiced dentistry in Freeport for five years gaining additional experience and skills.

Shailee felt that her true passion for serving those that helped her during her formative years was not being met and so she decided to pursue the challenge of coming back to this great community to serve it and the people that made her who she is today.

Dr. Patel purchased a practice from a retiring dentist in July 2012 in Sterling. Since then Dr. Patel has continued to provide the best care she can in order to preserve the tradition of compassionate dental care for the community of the Sterling-Dixon-Rock Falls region.

Dr. Patel believes in compassionate dental care and feels the best relationship between a doctor and patient evolves when there is open communication. She strives to do her best in explaining procedures to patients and in doing so, allowing her patients to be involved in their dental care.

In her free time, Dr. Patel enjoys classical Kathak dancing, reading, watching movies, and running. She has run four marathons and plans to run a few more while she can.

Dr. Patel enjoys a variety of music, ranging from rock to pop to the country! Her current favorites are Dave Mathews Band, Jason Mraz, Madonna, Luke Bryan, Brad Paisley, Maroon 5, Adele, and Duffy.
