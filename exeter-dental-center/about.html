About Exeter Dental Center

Get to Know the Team Committed to Your Smile

At Exeter Dental Center, we believe dentistry should be as much about people as it is about teeth. Our practice is built on a foundation of trust, honesty, and a genuine commitment to the long-term health and happiness of our patients. We’re not just here to treat your teeth; we’re here to build a lasting relationship with you and your family.

From the moment you walk in, you’ll notice that we do things differently. We take the time to listen, explain your options clearly, and create a personalized care plan that aligns with your goals. Welcome to a dental experience where you are respected, informed, and valued.

Our Patient-First Philosophy

We have one simple philosophy: to provide exceptional dental care in a welcoming environment where every patient feels confident and comfortable. This guiding principle shapes every aspect of our practice.

A Focus on You

Your comfort, goals, and health are our top priorities. We personalize every treatment plan and take the time to ensure you feel confident and in control of your dental health journey.

A Gentle Approach

We understand that dental visits can be stressful for some. Dr. Reed and our team are known for their gentle touch and ability to make even complex procedures feel calm and stress-free.

Prevention First

Our ultimate goal is to help you maintain lifelong oral health and keep your natural teeth for a lifetime. We emphasize preventive care and patient education to empower you to take charge of your smile.

Honesty & Transparency

We believe in clear communication. We will always discuss your treatment options, explain the process, and provide transparent pricing so you can make informed decisions with confidence.

Meet Our Dentist, Dr. Andrew Reed

Dr. Reed is a general dentist who believes dentistry is about more than just teeth—it’s about building lasting relationships. Guided by his values of trust, fairness, honesty, and integrity, he strives to deliver care that blends comfort, function, health, and esthetics, always tailored to each patient’s unique needs. What he finds most rewarding is meeting new people, getting to know their stories, and helping them achieve healthier, more confident smiles.

Committed to staying at the forefront of his field, Dr. Reed invests heavily in continuing education through advanced courses and professional development. This dedication allows him to offer patients the latest techniques and treatment options available in modern dentistry.

Originally from Delaware, Dr. Reed earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Temple University in 2017 before making New Hampshire his home in 2018. Outside the office, he enjoys meeting new people, playing golf, and gardening. At home, he and his wife share their life with five cats—Willow, Vinny, Georgie, Snickers, and Maximus.
