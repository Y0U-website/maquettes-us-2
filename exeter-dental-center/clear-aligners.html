Frequently Asked Questions About Clear Aligners

What Are Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are a modern alternative to traditional braces, using a series of clear, removable trays to gradually straighten your teeth. They offer a discreet and comfortable way to achieve a straighter smile without the visibility of metal brackets and wires.

Am I a Candidate for Clear Aligners?

In most cases, both teens and adults are suitable candidates for clear aligners. However, the best way to determine your candidacy is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Reed. She will evaluate your specific orthodontic needs and recommend the most appropriate treatment option for you.

What Are the Benefits of Clear Aligners Over Traditional Braces?

Clear aligners offer several advantages over traditional braces, including:

  • Improved Aesthetics: Clear aligners are virtually invisible, allowing you to straighten your teeth discreetly.
  • Enhanced Comfort: The smooth plastic aligners are more comfortable to wear than metal braces.
  • Improved Oral Hygiene: Removable aligners make it easier to brush and floss effectively.
  • Minimal Disruption: You can eat, drink, and participate in activities without restrictions.

How Long Does Clear Aligner Treatment Take?

Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of your orthodontic needs. On average, treatment with clear aligners can range from 12 to 18 months.

What Is the Cost of Clear Aligners?

The cost of clear aligners varies depending on several factors, including the complexity of your case and the length of treatment. We will provide you with a detailed treatment plan and cost estimate during your consultation.

How Do I Care for My Clear Aligners?

Caring for your clear aligners is simple:

  • Rinse your aligners with cool water every time you remove them.
  • Clean your aligners with a soft toothbrush and clear aligner cleaning crystals or denture cleaner.
  • Store your aligners in their protective case when not in use.

Can I Eat and Drink With Clear Aligners?

You should remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water. This helps prevent staining and damage to the aligners.

What Are the Latest Technologies Used in Clear Aligner Treatment?

We utilize advanced technologies such as 3D intraoral scanning and digital treatment planning to ensure precise and predictable results with clear aligners.

How Often Do I Need to Wear My Aligners?

For optimal results, you should wear your aligners for at least 20-22 hours per day, removing them only for eating, drinking, and oral hygiene.

How Often Do I Need to Change My Aligners?

Typically, you will change to a new set of aligners every 1-2 weeks, as directed by Dr. Reed.

Do Clear Aligners Hurt?

You may experience some mild discomfort or pressure when you first start wearing aligners or switch to a new set. This is normal and usually subsides within a few days.

How Do I Know if Clear Aligners Are Right for Me?

The best way to find out if clear aligners are the right orthodontic solution for you is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Reed. She will assess your needs and discuss your treatment options.
