Your Dental Implant Questions Answered

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium fixtures that replace tooth roots when a tooth has been lost. They are surgically placed and integrated into your jawbone by a specialist. They provide a strong and stable foundation for the custom replacement teeth we design for you.

Read more: What to Expect From Dental Implant Procedure

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults in good general health are candidates for dental implants. However, certain factors such as osteoporosis & diabetes or use of bisphosphonate medications can affect your ability to successfully heal following implant surgery. It is necessary to have a full medical review prior to determining your candidacy.

What Does the Dental Implant Process Involve?

  • Consultation and Planning: We’ll discuss your needs, assess your oral health, and create a personalized treatment plan with Dr. Reed.
  • Surgical Placement: We seamlessly coordinate the surgical placement of the implant fixture with trusted elite local specialists, such as Windham Cosmetic and Implant Dentistry, ensuring you receive expert surgical care.
  • Healing and Osseointegration: The implant fuses with your jawbone over several months.
  • Custom Restoration: A custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is expertly crafted and attached to your implant right here in our office.

Is the Dental Implant Procedure Painful?

Your comfort is top priority for both our team and our surgical partners. Advanced local anesthesia and sedation options are utilized during the surgical phase to ensure a stress-free experience. Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

There is no limit to how long an implant will last as long as you maintain ideal oral health. We recommend consistent hygiene appointments at our office every 3-6 months to best maintain your restorations, monitor bone levels, and check for inflammation.

What Is the Cost of Dental Implants in Exeter?

The cost varies depending on several factors, including the number of implants needed and the complexity of your final restorations. During your consultation, we’ll provide a detailed treatment plan and cost estimate tailored to your specific needs.
