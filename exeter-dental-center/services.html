Our Dental Services in Exeter, NH

Comprehensive, Compassionate Care for Your Smile

At Exeter Dental Center, we have tailored our services to meet the specific needs of our community. Dr. Andrew Reed and our caring team provide a complete range of dental solutions designed to restore your health, enhance your confidence, and ensure your comfort at every visit. We are not just caring for your teeth; we are caring for you .

A Focus on Your Lifelong Health & Confidence

We are proud to be your partner in lifelong health. We take the time to listen to your goals and concerns, developing a personalized treatment plan that is right for you. Explore our main areas of care below.

Cosmetic Dentistry

A confident smile is a beautiful thing. Whether you’re interested in professional teeth whitening, durable porcelain veneers, or a complete smile makeover, Dr. Reed combines artistry with clinical excellence to help you achieve the bright, beautiful smile you deserve.

Dental Implants

Explore Dental Implants Restorative Dentistry We are dedicated to restoring your smile’s health, function, and comfort. From durable dental crowns and bridges to custom-fitted full and partial dentures, our restorative solutions are designed to help you eat, speak, and smile with confidence. We also provide beautiful, custom restorations for dental implants.

Oral Surgery

Your safety and comfort are our top priorities. Dr. Reed provides gentle, precise oral surgery services, including tooth extractions and other procedures to prepare your mouth for restorations. We also offer sedation dentistry options to ensure a positive, anxiety-free experience.

Facial Esthetics (Botox® & Fillers)

As a perfect complement to your smile, we offer subtle facial esthetic treatments. With his advanced training in facial anatomy, Dr. Reed safely administers Botox® and dermal fillers to smooth fine lines and restore a refreshed, natural appearance.

TMJ & Migraine Treatment

You don’t have to live with chronic jaw pain, clicking, or headaches. We offer advanced diagnostic and non-surgical treatment options, including custom oral appliances and therapeutic Botox®, to find the source of your discomfort and provide lasting relief.
