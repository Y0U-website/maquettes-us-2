How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, dental implants can last for many years—often 15 years or more.

Does Getting a Dental Implant Hurt?

The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so most patients feel little to no pain. Mild discomfort after the surgery can usually be managed with over-the-counter medication.

How Soon Can I Return to Work After a Dental Implant?

Most people return to work the next day. Depending on the complexity of the procedure, some may need extra time off.
