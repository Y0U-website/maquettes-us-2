Dr. Nakul Rathi BDS, MS

Nakul Rathi, after his dental degree from GDC H, Nagpur; pursued his interest in implants at New York University, with a year-long fellowship program. He then went on to do Masters of Science (MS) and Advanced Prosthodontics Clinical Residency Program from The Ohio State University, Columbus (OSU) that is the oldest Prosthodontics program in USA. His two-year experience at the Implant Clinic at OSU gave him a chance to work in a clinic that has treated over 25000 implants.

He has lectured on implant dentistry at multiple dental organizations. He was selected among the ‘New and Emerging Speakers’ by the American Dental Association at ADA Annual Meeting.
