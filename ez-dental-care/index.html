Les soins

Unlike dentures, implants don’t slip or require adhesives—they stay securely in place, just like natural teeth. Because they integrate with your jawbone, they also help prevent bone loss, keeping your facial structure intact.

It depends on your situation. If your bone is healthy and there’s no sign of infection, Dr. Rathi may be able to place your implant the same day he removes the tooth.
