Everything You’ve Wanted to Ask About Us

Are Same-Day Dental Implants Really Possible?

For a lot of patients, yes. If your bone and gums check out at your evaluation, Dr. Rathi may be able to place your implant and a temporary restoration in the same visit.

Do Same-Day Dental Implants Give You Permanent Teeth in One Day?

Not quite. You’ll likely walk out with a temporary restoration on placement day, and Dr. Rathi will fit your final one once your implant has fully healed.

Can I Eat Normally After Getting Same-Day Dental Implants?

You’ll want to stick to softer foods at first while your site heals. Dr. Rathi will walk you through what’s safe to eat based on your specific restoration.

What Happens If I Don t Have Enough Bone for Same-Day Implants?

That doesn’t rule you out. Dr. Rathi can rebuild the area with a bone graft first, or plan your implant in stages instead.
