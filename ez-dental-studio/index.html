Sedation dentistry helps patients overcome anxiety and receive care in a calm, comfortable environment. Using safe medications, it promotes relaxation during procedures with little memory afterward. Ideal for anxious patients or complex treatments, options like oral sedation and nitrous oxide are carefully monitored to ensure a safe, stress-free experience.

Prepless veneers are ultra-thin porcelain shells that enhance smile appearance without drilling or removing tooth structure. They correct discoloration, gaps, and minor misalignment while preserving natural teeth. This comfortable, minimally invasive treatment offers durable, natural-looking results through a customized design and bonding process for an improved smile.

A dental cleaning and checkup involves removing plaque and tartar, polishing teeth, and assessing overall oral health. The dentist examines gums, teeth, and bite, often using X-rays to detect issues early. This routine visit helps prevent cavities, gum disease, and maintains a healthy smile.

A dental implant is a small medical-grade titanium post that is placed into the jawbone where a tooth is missing. The implant acts as an artificial root and integrates with the bone in a process called osseointegration.

At EZ Dental Studio, single dental implants are designed to look, feel, and function just like your natural teeth—allowing you to eat, smile, and speak with confidence.

Les soins

A single dental implant is the most advanced and natural-looking way to replace a missing tooth. Unlike bridges or removable appliances, a dental implant replaces both the root and the crown of the tooth, restoring full strength, function, and appearance.
