Meet Chewie

Chewbacca, AKA Chewie, is our Ferrara Family Dentistry mascot. He is a four year old golden doodle who can often be seen sporting a crooked smile near the front desk. Chewie was appropriately named in tribute to his work in the dental setting and also due to Dr. Ferrara’s love of Star Wars. We knew it was the right name when we discovered his birthday is Star Wars Day (May the Fourth). He is a beloved pet of Dr. Ferrara and his family and a favorite of our patients. Don’t forget to say hi to Chewie when you visit our office. He will wag his tail and will be happy to greet you.
