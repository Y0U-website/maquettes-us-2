All-On-4®Implants

Losing or missing a tooth is a hassle no one wants to deal with. Lost natural teeth can be replaced by dentures, bridges, or dental implants. Dental Implants are usually the best option amongst the three.

If you are missing multiple teeth, All-On-4® implant technique might be the right option for you! It is basically a bridge-like device, mounted on the arch with just 4 implants serving as an anchor. The All-On-4® bridge consists of acrylic fused to titanium, monolithic zirconia, and porcelain stacked to a cobalt-chromium frame.

Are You An All-On-4® Candidate?

All-On-4® Dental Implant FAQs Considering All-On-4® dental implants? Here are answers to frequently asked questions about this full-arch restoration at our Brandon, FL practice.

  • Edentulous: The All-On-4® is a favorite for full mouth rehabilitation of completely edentulous patients (missing all teeth)
  • If your teeth are in a difficult condition to restore due to grinding, decay, and worn-down teeth.
  • Gum disease which led to loss of teeth.
  • If you don’t have enough bone in the back part of your jaw, traditional implants might not be an option. Such cases can be a good candidate for All-On-4® system as it only needs 4 screws, placed on the front region of the arch, where the bone is thicker.

How long does the All-On-4® procedure take?

The initial surgery and placement of your temporary teeth can be completed in one day. You’ll leave our office with a full set of functional teeth. After 6 months of healing, you’ll return for your permanent, stronger prosthesis.

How much do All-On-4® implants cost?

All-On-4® costs vary based on your specific needs, including extractions and the type of final prosthesis. While the upfront investment is significant, All-On-4® is often more affordable than replacing each tooth individually with traditional implants. We offer financing options to make treatment accessible.

Is the All-On-4® procedure painful?

We use anesthesia and offer sedation options to ensure you’re comfortable during surgery. Most patients report less discomfort than expected. Post-operative soreness and swelling are managed with prescribed medications and typically subside within a week.

How long do All-On-4® implants last?

With proper care, All-On-4® implants can last 20 years or longer—many patients keep them for life. The implants themselves are designed to be permanent, while the prosthetic teeth may need replacement after 10 to 15 years due to normal wear.

Can I eat normally with All-On-4® implants?

Yes, once fully healed, you can eat virtually anything you want without restrictions. Unlike dentures, All-On-4® implants are securely anchored, so you can enjoy steak, apples, corn on the cob, and other foods that denture wearers often avoid.

What s the difference between All-On-4® and traditional dentures?

All-On-4® implants are permanently fixed in your mouth—no removal, no adhesives, no slipping. They preserve jawbone, restore full chewing power, and look completely natural. Traditional dentures sit on your gums, can slip, require adhesives, and contribute to bone loss over time.

What if I don t have enough bone for implants?

All-On-4® is specifically designed for patients with bone loss. The technique uses four strategically angled implants placed in the front of the jaw where bone is naturally thicker, often eliminating the need for bone grafting that traditional implants might require.

How do I care for All-On-4® implants?

Care is simple—brush twice daily, use a water flosser or interdental brush to clean under the bridge, and rinse with mouthwash. Regular dental checkups allow us to monitor your implants and professionally clean areas that are harder to reach.

Am I too old for All-On-4® implants?

Age alone doesn’t disqualify you from All-On-4® treatment. Many patients in their 70s and 80s successfully receive implants. Overall health, bone density, and medical history are more important factors. We’ll evaluate your candidacy during your consultation.

What happens during the healing period?

You’ll wear a temporary set of teeth for about 6 months while your implants fuse with your jawbone. During this time, stick to softer foods and follow care instructions carefully. Once healed, we’ll create your permanent prosthesis from stronger, more durable materials.

Procedural Steps

The All-On-4® Implant procedure can be an ideal solution to your problems. Enhance your smile and quality of life and enjoy a youthful smile, while maintaining your chewing habits and speech pattern. Make the first step, schedule your consultation today!

  • A consultation with your dentist which includes a 3-D scan and all necessary x-rays.
  • A treatment plan is drafted based on the number of tooth extractions and position of implants.
  • Implants are surgically placed in your jawbone. Sedation options are available!
  • Within a few hours, teeth will be mounted on the freshly placed implants.
  • After 6 months, another 3-D scan and x-rays will be taken to assess the implants
  • Another set of teeth will be made from stronger material
