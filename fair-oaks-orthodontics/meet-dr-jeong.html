In the Practice

In my line of work, I get to see the results of what I do. I enjoy getting to know our patients personally, and catching up on their lives while I straighten their smiles. I love smiling and laughing, and I love creating perfect smiles for our patients! I appreciate that our patients and families take the time for orthodontics, making appointments and keeping up with their hygiene to help make their treatment more efficient.

Family Life
