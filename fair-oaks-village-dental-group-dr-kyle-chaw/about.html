About Fair Oaks Village Dental

Fair Oaks choice for compassionate care

A community cornerstone in the heart of Fair Oaks Village, where every patient is treated like family.

At Fair Oaks Village Dental, we extend a warm welcome to every member of our Fair Oaks community. Our doors are open to everyone seeking quality dental care delivered with compassion and professionalism. We take pride in serving our diverse community, offering a full range of services tailored to the unique needs of each individual.

Fair Oaks Village Dental is more than a dental office, it’s a community cornerstone. With decades of service to the area, we proudly continue the great dental care provided by Dr. Kolander and his team. Today, under the care of Dr. Kyle Chaw, we combine that small-town, personal attentiveness with modern technology and techniques. Step into a place where your smile contributes to the vibrancy of our community.

The values behind every visit

Community first We treat every patient like a neighbor, because most of them are. We get to know you and your family.

Honest education We explain what we see and review every option, so you make confident, informed decisions.

Gentle, comfortable care Patients who fear the dentist tell us they finally feel at ease here. Comfort is a priority.

Modern dentistry We pair up-to-date technology and techniques with old-fashioned attentiveness.
