Accessibility Statement

Fair Oaks Village Dental wants everyone to feel welcome, both in our office and online. We care about making our website easy to use for as many visitors as possible, and we work in good faith to provide a positive, accessible experience.

Our commitment

We want our website to be clear, readable, and easy to navigate for as many visitors as possible. We follow widely recognized web best practices and treat accessibility as an ongoing effort rather than a one-time task, making improvements over time. If any part of our website is not working well for you, we want to hear about it.

Making our site easier to use

We have built this website with care, using design choices intended to make it comfortable to read and simple to navigate. You will also find an accessibility settings button on every page that lets you adjust how the site displays to suit your own preferences. We continue to refine these efforts over time, and your feedback helps us do that.

Alternative formats

We are happy to provide any information on this website in an alternative format, such as large print, plain text, or simply reading it to you over the phone and answering your questions directly. Just call or email us and tell us what works best for you, and we will make it happen at no charge.

We’re here to help

If you have any difficulty using our website, or if you would like information from this site in another format, please reach out. We will do our best to assist you, answer your questions, and provide the information you need in a way that works for you.
