The practice

Dr. Tim Richter Dr. Tim Richter graduated from Moeller High School in 1978 and went on to graduate from the University of Dayton four years later with a B.S. in Biology. Dr. Richter received his diploma from Ohio State’s College of Dentistry in 1986, and a few years later settled down as a resident of Fairfield, OH.

Dr. Richter is a member of the American Dental Association as well as the International Academy of Mini Dental Implant Dentistry. Dr. Richter has extensive training and experience in the area of mini dental implant dentistry and is an owner of a Mini Dental Implant Center of America (MDICA) location. Dr. Richter is also a proud member of Keely Dental Society.

Dr. Joe Richter is a 2008 graduate of St. Xavier High School, helping his football team win two state championships during his high school career. Dr. Joe also played four years of Division 2 football at Ohio Dominican University where he graduated with a B.S. in Biology. Following in his father’s footsteps, Dr. Joe then graduated from Ohio State’s College of Dentistry in 2017.

For nearly two years, Dr. Joe worked extensively in the Over-The-Rhine district of Cincinnati, serving the less fortunate. He is a part of the American Dental Association as well as a proud member of Keely Dental Society.
