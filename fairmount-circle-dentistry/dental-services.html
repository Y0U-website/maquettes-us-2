New Patient Exam

Our whole team is excited to get to meet you. The point of this exam is to work through different treatment options. This will be completed through a comprehensive evaluation of your current dental state.

Teeth Cleaning

Professionally cleaning your teeth so that your smile can look its best. We will also evaluate for any dental health related problems. Applying varnish to help prevent tooth decay.

Teeth Whitening

This is the process of visually making your teeth whiter, which is different from having your teeth cleaned. We offer in the office and take home whitening systems. Have your teeth professionally done or try doing it yourself.

Reconstructive Dentistry

This is the process of rebuilding or replacing teeth in a patient&#x27;s mouth. It combines restorative dentistry with esthetics. To give the patient a smile they deserve.

Implant Dentistry

A dental implant is a replacement tooth that is held in by an artificial root. The root is placed into your jaw to hold the tooth which requires minor surgery. This is an option for anybody who has lost teeth and wants them back.

Clear Correct

Similar to braces, but no one will notice them. Clear Correct is a clear piece of material designed to help align your teeth similar. You will have to take them out whenever you want to eat.
