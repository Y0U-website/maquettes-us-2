In Office Airway Evaluation

If you&#x27;ve read this you might have or know someone who has known difficulty breathing while sleeping. The process starts by gathering medical information along with sleep health information to determine if you are at risk for a sleep breathing disorder.

Easy-To-Use Home Sleep Testing

Our home sleep test gathers valuable information about length of sleep, snoring, quality of sleep, if you stop breathing while you&#x27;re sleeping. This data is read by a sleep physician to determine if you have obstructive sleep apnea.

Oral Appliance Therapy

Oral appliances used to treat Obstructive Sleep Apnea are also called mandibular advancement devices. They are designed to allow you to breathe more easily by positioning the lower jaw forward opening your airway.
