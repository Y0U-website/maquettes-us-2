Get to Know

Dr. Johnson

With a calming presence and a technically precise approach, Dr. Paul Johnson has a gift for helping patients feel understood and cared for. Since beginning his dental career in 2017, Dr. Johnson has focused on providing comprehensive dental care with a deeply personal touch. He earned his dental degree from LECOM School of Dental Medicine and holds certifications in IV Moderate Sedation, enabling him to offer advanced comfort options for patients with anxiety or complex treatment needs.

Before becoming a dentist, Dr. Johnson earned degrees in both biology and finance, giving him a unique ability to blend scientific insight with a strategic mindset. Patients appreciate not only his gentle hand and clinical expertise, but also his dedication to educating them throughout their care. Whether you&#x27;re coming in for a root canal or a full smile makeover, Dr. Johnson is committed to making sure you understand your options and feel empowered every step of the way.

What truly sets Dr. Johnson apart is his patient-first attitude. He’s passionate about helping those who’ve delayed care due to fear or financial concerns, and he believes strongly in judgement-free, comfortable care that will keep you coming back. His decision to bring full-scope services in-house means fewer referrals, fewer visits, and greater convenience for his patients.

Outside of the office, you’ll likely find Dr. Johnson out on the golf course, hiking Ohio’s scenic trails, or playing guitar at home with his wife Liz, daughter Siena, and doodle dog Max.

Cozy Comforts, Advanced Technology

Our state-of-the-art amenities mean you get high-quality care with maximum comfort and minimum hassle. We utilize the latest tools in digital dentistry to reduce wait times, increase accuracy, and enhance your overall experience—even offering same-day treatment options.

Same-Day Crowns

Skip the multiple visits. Using the Glidewell IO system, we create durable, natural-looking crowns in a single appointment—no goopy impressions or temporary crowns required.

CBCT 3D Imaging

Our high-resolution CBCT scanner gives us a detailed, three-dimensional view of your teeth, nerves, and bone structure. That means better diagnostics, safer surgeries, and more precise implant planning.

Digital Impressions

Say goodbye to messy impression trays. Our digital scanning technology captures precise 3D images of your teeth in minutes, making the process cleaner, faster, and more comfortable.

IV Sedation for Anxiety-Free Care

We offer IV moderate sedation for patients who are especially nervous or undergoing complex procedures. It allows you to feel completely at ease—and often remember little or nothing of the visit—while still being in safe, qualified hands.

nitrous sedation

We offer safe, gentle nitrous oxide (laughing gas) to help patients of all ages relax during treatment. It’s quick to take effect, wears off fast, and makes your visit feel easier from start to finish.

TVs in Every Operatory

Our TVs help kids and adults alike relax and focus on their favorite shows during treatment—because a little entertainment makes a big difference.

Refreshing Warm Towels

After your visit, enjoy a warm towel to freshen up—just one of the small touches we offer to help you leave feeling cared for and comfortable.

Noise-Canceling Headphones

We provide noise-canceling headphones to help block out dental sounds and make your visit feel more like downtime than a dental appointment.

Beverage Station

Help yourself! From coffee to cold water, our self-serve beverage station is here to keep you refreshed while you wait or accompany your child.
