Dr. Latham grew up in Utah and earned his BA from Brigham Young University in 2003. From there he received a DMD from Case Western Reserve University in 2009 and began his career as a dentist. Dr. Latham loves being able to help patients get the smile they’ve always wanted.

One of his favorite aspects of being a dentist is being able to work with patients to transform their smile in ways they couldn’t have imagined and seeing the boost in a patient’s self-esteem after the treatment is complete. Being able to help patients maintain good oral health and succeed in their home care is one of the greatest aspects of being a dentist.
