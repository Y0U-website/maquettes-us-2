The practice

At Fairy Tale Dental, our goal is to provide exceptional dental care to our patients. We believe that a healthy smile is the foundation of a happy life, and we are dedicated to helping our patients achieve and maintain optimal oral health.

Our team of skilled and experienced dental professionals is committed to providing personalized care to each and every one of our patients. From routine cleanings to complex procedures, we are here to help you achieve a healthy, beautiful smile.

We offer a wide range of dental services, but our particular focus is full arch restorations using our patent-pending TruBridge™ technology. Learn more here .

Fairy Tale Dental is the exclusive home of TruBridge™ TruBridge™ is a patent pending full-arch dental restoration. TruBridge™ is superior to removable restorations such as dentures. TruBridge™ is a fixed, zirconia bridge that is permanently placed and provides a feeling of having strong healthy teeth while providing a beautiful smile.
