FAQs: Answers to Your Pediatric Dentistry Questions

Q: At what age should my child visit a pediatric dentist? A: We recommend scheduling your child’s first visit when their first tooth appears or by their first birthday.

Q: What can I expect at my child’s first dental appointment? A: During the initial visit, we’ll focus on making your child comfortable, examining their teeth and gums, and discussing oral hygiene tips for parents.

Q: How often should my child see the dentist? A: Regular check-ups every six months help maintain healthy teeth and prevent issues before they arise.

Q: Do you treat children with special needs? A: Absolutely! Dr. Talbott has extensive experience providing personalized care for children with special needs.
