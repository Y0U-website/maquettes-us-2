0 Dental that stands by you. At Falls Dental, we’re all about listening to patients. Hearing your concerns, answering your questions, and understanding your dental history allows us to recommend the best treatments and ensure your experience is comfortable and easy. Our Dentists

Michael Frankman, DDS

Dr. Michael Frankman is a friendly and knowledgeable dentist who loves answering patients’ questions. A Sioux Falls native, Dr. Frankman received his undergraduate and DDS degrees from the University of Michigan before joining the Falls Dental team in 2009.

Dr. Frankman is an active member and leader of the American Dental Association, South Dakota Dental Association (SDDA), Southeastern District Dental Society, and Academy of General Dentistry. He also currently serves on the SDDA Board of Trustees and is a Past President of the Southeastern District Dental Society. In his spare time, Dr. Frankman volunteers with the Dakota Smiles Mobile Dental Program and the Donated Dental Services Program. He was named the 2015 South Dakota Young Dentist of the Year by the South Dakota Dental Association.

Holly Heien, DDS Dr. Holly Heien is passionate about educating patients, providing transparent care, and fostering healthy habits. She grew up on a farm near Gettysburg, SD, and worked as a hygienist in Sioux Falls for six years before returning to school and earning her DDS from the University of Nebraska Medical Center.
