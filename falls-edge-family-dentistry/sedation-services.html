Sedation dentistry uses safe, proven techniques to help you feel calm and relaxed at your appointment, whether it’s a routine cleaning or complex treatment.

Sedation Options

Nitrous Oxide (“Laughing Gas”)

Nitrous Oxide is our most commonly used sedation option, and helps you feel calm while remaining awake and aware throughout your appointment. A measured amount of Nitrous Oxide is combined with oxygen and administered through a nose mask to help you relax. The effects wear off quickly, so you can return to your day right after your visit.

IV Sedation

With IV sedation, medication is gently delivered intravenously during treatment to help you feel deeply relaxed. This type of sedation is sometimes called “twilight sleep.” You’ll remain conscious and able to respond to your dentist if needed, but many patients leave their appointment with little memory of the procedure.

IV sedation is not the same as general anesthesia as you won’t be completely unconscious. You’ll simply feel calm, at ease, and free of anxiety while we take care of your smile.

Because IV sedation doesn’t block pain, a local anesthetic may still be used depending on your procedure.

Patients receiving IV sedation will need a trusted driver to and from their appointment, and someone to stay with them for several hours afterward.
