Dental Membership Plan in…

For all recommended procedures, there will be a treatment plan provided with all services and fees shown. The discounted rates and total fees will be provided prior to treatment. No payment plans or outside financing are available with the Dental Membership Plan.

Full payment must be made at time of service to receive a 15 % discount.

  • No annual maximums
  • No age limitations
  • No deductibles
  • No pre-determinations
  • No claim forms
  • Two preventative prophylaxis procedures per year (“cleanings”)
  • Two periodic dental examinations per year
  • Two fluoride treatments with no age restrictions per year
  • Any x-rays associated with preventative visits (1 set of bite wings per year, 1 panoramic x-ray every 5 years, any needed Pas)
  • Oral cancer screening, blood pressure reading, oral hygiene instruction, and any other services typically included in a preventative appointment
  • One emergency examination per year with any necessary radiographs (this does not include any treatment needed due to the emergency)
  • 15 % discount on all other dental services, including cosmetic services (note: this excludes orthodontics, as it is not eligible for the plan)
  • Refunds are only available before the date of your first visit with membership in the program. After you have had a visit, payment is non-refundable.
  • There may be a $75 fee associated with a missed appointment or a cancelled appointment with less than 24 hours’ notice.
  • Membership fees and plan discounts are subject to change on an annual basis.
  • There are no benefits that “roll over” between membership years.
  • The Membership Plan is only valid at Falls Family Dental.
  • The membership rate is $400 for an individual. $280 for one child under the age of 12 in the family
  • $250 for any additional children under the age of 12 in the family
  • You have the option to sign up for 2 consecutive years at a cost of $700.
  • You have the option to sign up for 5 consecutive years at a cost of $1500.
  • Orthodontics are not included in the membership plan.
  • Dr. Fabry and Dr. Keller reserve the right to cancel or discontinue this plan for any reason at the end of the membership term.
