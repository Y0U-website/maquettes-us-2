Smiles for VETS

Kokomo, IN Friday Sept. 11, 2026 : Dr. Melissa Jarrell and team will host their 13th annual Dentistry From The Heart (DFTH) event providing a free day of dental care to the first one hundred veterans of Indiana.

At our annual Smiles for Vets event, our mission is to honor and serve the veterans of Central Indiana by providing high-quality, compassionate, and comprehensive dental care-completely free of charge.

Many of our local heroes face significant barriers to accessing essential dental treatment through the VA system, leaving their oral health needs unmet. Our annual event is dedicated to filling that gap, ensuring that those who have bravely served our country receive the dignity, care, and respect they deserve.

By restoring smiles, we restore confidence, comfort, and quality of life for the men and women who have sacrificed so much for us.

Why do I continue to host this event year after year? My team and I LOVE Smiles for VETS day! It is the highlight of our year. It is so rewarding to host this free dental day. We have several dentists, hygienists, and assistants from other Kokomo dental offices helping us each year, so it’s a fun day. At the end of the busy day of charity, it feels great to know you helped patients (that normally cannot afford care) get out of pain and infection.

Dentistry From The Heart is a registered non-profit organization that provides free dental care for those in need. In the past eleven years, Dentistry From The Heart events have contributed more than $15 million in free dentistry and helped more than 50,000 patients across the country.

  • LIMITED DENTAL EXAMS
  • ORAL CANCER SCREENINGS
  • ORAL HYGIENE INSTRUCTIONS
  • PERIODONTAL MAINTENANCE
