Are implants right for you?

Most healthy adults with adequate jawbone are good candidates for implants. Dr. Warya will evaluate your oral health, take imaging, and discuss whether an implant or another option best fits your situation, always recommending what is truly right for you.

Caring for your implants

One of the best things about implants is how easy they are to care for. You brush and floss them just like natural teeth, and keep up with regular dental checkups so we can monitor the implant and surrounding gums. With good oral hygiene and routine visits, dental implants can serve you well for decades, making them an excellent long-term investment in your smile and health.
