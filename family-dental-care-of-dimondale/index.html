The practice

Dr. Grisdela is a Lansing native, having graduated from Okemos HS and receiving his undergraduate degree from MSU. He completed his doctorate at Marquette University School of Dentistry in Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

After graduating dental school, he completed a general practice residency at St. Joseph Mercy Oakland Hospital in Pontiac. As a dental resident in a hospital setting, Dr. Grisdela was able to practice a wide variety of dental procedures and provide treatment to patients with barriers to comprehensive dental care.

He has practiced in the area since 2018. He lives in the Lansing area with his wife Claire and their springer spaniel Benona. In his spare time he enjoys golfing, family and MSU sports.
