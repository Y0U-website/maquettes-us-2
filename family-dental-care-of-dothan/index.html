Dental crowns are versatile restorations designed to strengthen and protect damaged or weakened teeth. Made from durable materials such as porcelain, ceramic, or metal, crowns cap the entire tooth, restoring its shape

Dental bridges are a reliable and effective solution for replacing missing teeth, restoring both your oral function and smile. Consisting of one or more artificial teeth secured by natural teeth or dental implants on either side, bridges help maintain the shape of your face, improve chewing ability, and prevent surrounding teeth from shifting.

Dental veneers are ultra-thin, custom-made shells designed to cover the front surface of your teeth, enhancing their appearance and providing a natural look. Crafted from high-quality porcelain or composite resin, veneers can correct a variety of cosmetic issues, such as discoloration, chips, gaps, and misalignment. Dental partials are removable prosthetic devices designed to replace one or more missing teeth while preserving the surrounding natural teeth. They can be made of various materials, including acrylic and metal, and are custom-fitted for optimal comfort and functionality. Partials enhance both aesthetic appearance and bite efficiency, allowing patients to regain confidence in their smile and…

Dentures are removable dental appliances designed to replace missing teeth and restore the smile’s appearance and functionality of a full arch. Custom-made to fit the individual’s mouth, dentures enhance chewing ability and improve speech while providing essential support to facial structures.

Oral sleep appliances are custom-made devices that help reposition the jaw and maintain an open airway during sleep. These appliances are commonly used to treat snoring and obstructive sleep apnea, promoting better breathing and improved sleep quality. They fit comfortably in the mouth and are typically constructed from flexible, biocompatible materials for a secure yet comfortable fit.
