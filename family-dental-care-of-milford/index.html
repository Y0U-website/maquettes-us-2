The practice

You’ll sink into a heated massage chair, pick something to watch on the TV in your room, and choose from an eye mask, blanket, pillow, or beverage. Take a look at the full comfort menu; everything on it is yours for the asking.

Rigorous training systems keep the care you need under one roof. Each of Henniker and Milford dentists completes more than 50 hours of specialized training each year , and new hires train for one to six months before they ever care for you on their own.

Every year, our doctors and team open the doors for a Free Day of Care for Veterans. To date, we’ve donated over $500,000 in free dental services to the Veterans of our community.

We also sponsor teams and events across both towns and support the local food pantry.

Whether you’re a current patient, a future patient, or just have a quick question, we’d love to hear from you. Pick the office closest to you and give us a call. Our front desk team is the fastest way to get help.

Call the location closest to you and let our front desk know it’s an emergency. If you reach us during business hours, we’ll do our best to see you the same day. After hours, follow the prompts on the voicemail for emergency guidance.
