Porcelain veneers placed on healthy tooth structure by an experienced clinician typically last 10–20 years with proper maintenance. The most common causes of early failure are placing veneers on structurally compromised teeth, unresolved bruxism (grinding), and inadequate marginal sealing. We assess all of these factors before recommending veneers.

Yes Invisalign is clinically effective for mild to moderate crowding, spacing, and certain bite corrections in adults of any age. For more complex bite issues or severe misalignment, fixed orthodontic treatment may produce more predictable results. In-office professional whitening results typically last 1–3 years with proper maintenance, depending on dietary habits and oral hygiene. Coffee, tea, red wine, and tobacco accelerate re-staining.
