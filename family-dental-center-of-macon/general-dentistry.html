Teeth Cleanings for Adults & Children

At Family Dental Center of Macon, we offer teeth cleaning services to adults and children alike. Routine teeth cleanings are the primary factor in maintaining good oral hygiene. Our professional hygienists can professionally clean and expertly remove plaque from teeth. Regular dental exams are another essential factor in maintaining optimal oral health. For children, we recommend sealants and fluoride treatment during their annual teeth cleaning appointment. If our hygienists locate a potential problem, we offer composite fillings, crowns, bridges, tooth extractions, and other treatments.
