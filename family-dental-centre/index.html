Welcoming children, new and emergency patients to our Sarnia dental office!

At Family Dental Centre, we offer comprehensive dental care.

We understand that a beautiful, healthy-looking smile can only come from regular oral care which is why we offer a range of dental services from dental cleanings, and preventative dentistry to tooth replacement.

At Family Dental Centre, we are proud to serve patients in Sarnia and its surrounding area. As your dental team in Sarnia, you and your loved ones can expect a wide array of services and procedures. Our family-oriented office is modern, comfortable, and staffed with warm and friendly dentists and professionals who listen to their patients’ questions and concerns. Patients of all ages are welcome.

At Family Dental Centre, we give our patients plenty of reasons to smile. Let us be your Sarnia dental office of choice.

Our friendly and courteous office staff are always happy to assist new and existing patients throughout their dentists visits. They will help arrange and manage your appointments and follow-up visits and answer any questions and concerns that you may have.
