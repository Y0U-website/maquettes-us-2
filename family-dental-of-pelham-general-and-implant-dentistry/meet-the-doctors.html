Meet Our Dentist in Pelham, NH

Dr. Hitesh Vij completed his bachelor’s and master’s degrees from one of the best dental schools in India. His passion for teaching, learning, and seeing new places led him to different places in India, then to Saudi Arabia, and finally to the United States. He was a faculty member at a dental school before moving to the United States, where he completed his DMD with high honors from the Boston University School of Dental Medicine. He is now licensed to practice in both the USA and Canada.

Dr. Vij is proficient in various aspects of general dentistry, and his compassionate nature makes him a very thoughtful dentist. He strives to achieve the best in everything he does and loves to be current with developments in his field by attending continuing education courses. He is dedicated to enriching the quality of life for all our patients by giving them happy, healthy smiles.

Dr. Vij recently received his fellowship from the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI) and has a membership in the American Dental Education Association (ADEA). He is currently pursuing a fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD) and is also adding new skills, such as clear aligners, to his skill set to serve his patients better. He is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD). He has also contributed to research in the field of dentistry and has articles in peer-reviewed journals to his credit.

Dr. Ruchieka Vij

Dr. Vij believes in providing the best comprehensive care to her patients. She believes in educating her patients about preventive care and explaining in detail their dental needs and any concerns or questions they have regarding their treatment. She does this with only one thought: her patients must feel comfortable and confident when they come for their dental visits.

She is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD) and is pursuing a Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry (FAGD). She attends many continuing education courses to keep herself up to date with the latest developments in the field of dentistry so she can serve her patients in the best possible way and provide them with the best options according to their needs. She completed the Root Camp Course for Root Canal Treatment as another step in this direction.

When she is not working, Dr. Vij enjoys spending time with her family and friends. She loves listening to music and traveling to different places.
