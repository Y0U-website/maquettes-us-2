Oral Cancer Screening

Oral cancer represents 2.9 % of U.S. cancer cases, with 51,000 new diagnoses and 10,000 deaths expected this year. Most commonly found on the tongue, gums, and lips, it is more prevalent in men over 50 who smoke and drink heavily, with recent rises linked to HPV. Regular screenings, including a thorough oral and neck examination, are crucial for early detection and better outcomes.

Periodontitis Treatments

Gum disease, the leading cause of tooth loss in adults, affects one in two Americans over 30 but is largely preventable. Periodontal disease involves inflammation of the tissues supporting your teeth, often caused by plaque and tartar, and may progress without noticeable pain. Regular oral hygiene and checkups are crucial. Symptoms to watch for include inflamed gums, bleeding during brushing, gum recession, sensitivity, bad breath, loose teeth, and changes in bite or denture fit.
