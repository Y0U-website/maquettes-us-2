Traditional vs. Clear Braces

Traditional metal braces use small brackets and wires to gradually move teeth into alignment. They’re highly effective for a wide range of orthodontic issues and remain the most affordable option for many patients.

Clear ceramic braces work the same way but use tooth-colored brackets that blend with your smile, making them a more discreet choice. Both options deliver excellent results - Dr. Cheung will help you decide which is the best fit during your consultation.

What to Expect During Treatment

Orthodontic treatment at our office typically lasts between 6 and 18 months, depending on the complexity of your case. You’ll visit our office periodically for adjustments, and Dr. Cheung will monitor your progress throughout to ensure your teeth are moving as planned.

For more complex orthodontic cases - such as severe crowding, significant bite issues, or cases requiring jaw repositioning - Dr. Cheung may refer you to a trusted local orthodontist to ensure you receive the specialized care you need.
