Why Choose Implants Over Other Options?

Unlike bridges, dental implants don’t require altering neighboring healthy teeth. Unlike dentures, they don’t slip, shift, or require adhesives. Implants are the only tooth replacement option that actually stimulates your jawbone, helping prevent the bone loss and facial changes that can occur after tooth loss.

With a success rate above 95 % and the potential to last a lifetime with proper care, dental implants are a long-term investment in your oral health, your comfort, and your quality of life.

The Implant Process

  • 01 Consultation & Exam We evaluate your oral health and review X-rays or CBCT scans to determine if implants are right for you.
  • 02 Referral for Placement A trusted local oral surgeon or periodontist places the implant with precision and care.
  • 03 Healing & Osseointegration Over 3 to 6 months, the implant integrates with your jawbone to create a strong, stable foundation.
  • 04 Crown Restoration Dr. Cheung places your custom crown, bridge, or denture for a natural look and comfortable fit.
