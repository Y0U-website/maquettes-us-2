Your Partner in Oral Health

Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing or replacing damaged or missing teeth to improve function, comfort, and appearance. Treatments like crowns, fillings, and bridges help protect your oral health and restore your natural smile.

The most common procedures include dental crowns, bridges, dentures, and dental implants. Dr. Cheung will recommend the best option based on your specific needs and oral health goals.

With proper care, crowns and bridges last 10-15 years or longer. Dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular checkups and good oral hygiene help extend the life of any restoration.
