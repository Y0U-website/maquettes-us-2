Meet Our Doctors

David R. Mack, DDS

A native of Aberdeen, SD, Dr. Mack earned his dental degree from UNMC College of Dentistry after completing his bachelor’s degree from the University of South Dakota. He maintains memberships in the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine and holds Invisalign® Preferred Provider status.

Dr. Mack has a passion for learning advanced dental concepts and treatment and takes many postgraduate continuing education courses each year. He has received extensive training in short-term orthodontics, aesthetic dentistry, and sleep disorders, including snoring and sleep apnea. Currently, Dr. Mack is completing the Clinical Mastery Series curriculum which focuses on cosmetic dentistry in conjunction with managing TMJ/TMD disorders. He is now focusing much of his attention on the rapidly growing area of dental implants.

Dr. Mack has been serving Bellevue and the surrounding communities since 2002 at his current location. He, and his wife, Katie, have 3 daughters who keep them busy outside the office with volleyball, dance, gymnastics, and golf. The Mack family are also animal lovers who have many pets. Outside work and the busy world of family activities, Dr. Mack enjoys golf, traveling, and Husker athletics.

Patrick Wachter, DDS

Dr. Patrick Wachter hails from Omaha, where he graduated from Creighton University School of Dentistry in 2013. He completed a one-year general practice residency at the University of Nebraska Medical Center and Veteran’s Hospital and was a private practice dentist for four years before joining our team at Family Dentistry of Bellevue.

Dr. Wachter enjoys all aspects of general dentistry and has a special interest in root canals and oral surgery. He is committed to ongoing continuing education and is also a part-time faculty member at Creighton University School of Dentistry.

In 2017, Dr. Wachter received the prestigious Fellowship Award through the Academy of General Dentistry. He is also a member of the following professional organizations:

Dr. Wachter is married to Chelsea and they have a two-year-old daughter. In his spare time, Dr. Wachter enjoys spending time with family and friends, exercising, and either watching or attending various sporting events.
