Dental Implants

Dr. Mack, your Bellevue dentist, provides long-lasting solutions for missing teeth with dental implants. Dental implants can’t turn back the clock and give your teeth back, but they can come awfully close! Unlike prosthetic tooth replacement options that attach to teeth or rest on top of the gums, implants are surgically placed and eventually form a new tooth root for a permanent solution to tooth loss.

The tooth root stimulates the jawbone

building a healthy, secure facial foundation and a lifetime of strong smiles. This is why many patients searching for a Bellevue dentist choose implants as their preferred tooth replacement option.

Sleep Apnea

Obstructive sleep apnea is a sleep disorder in which breathing is briefly and repeatedly interrupted during sleep. This can cause oxygen deprivation, and combined with disturbed sleep, lead to a number of serious health issues. CPAP machine therapy doesn’t work for all sleep apnea patients but there is another solution.

Oral appliance therapy is a sleep apnea solution

Dr. Mack, your trusted Bellevue dentist, offers a custom-made mouthguard to help keep the airway open.

Sedation Dentistry

About 60 % of people have some degree of dental anxiety, with about 5-10 % actually phobic. Sedation dentistry options are available to help people with different anxiety levels to relax during dental treatment. Nitrous oxide, breathed through a mask, is the mildest, and oral sedation via a pill is an intermediate sedation solution.

Dr. Mack is certified to provide in-office oral sedation in Bellevue, NE.
