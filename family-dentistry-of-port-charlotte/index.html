The practice

The knowledgeable and experienced team at Family Dentistry provides top-quality, fairly priced dentistry right here in Port Charlotte. Whether it’s time for your regular checkup or you require something more, you can count on us. We offer a wide range of services from general, cosmetic and restorative dentistry to dentures, implants and gum disease treatments.

If you are suffering from tooth loss, dental implants may be the ideal solution! This method of permanent tooth replacement provides a natural look, perfect function, and can save the health of your entire oral structure. We are proud to offer All-on-4 implants which can replace an entire arch of teeth with only four surgically placed implants.

Our team is committed to making your visits pleasant and comfortable. During treatment, you’ll enjoy a massage chair and a flat-screen TV. You can also request a blanket and pillow to get extra-cozy. Our family scheduling options make it easy to bring the whole family, and be sure to ask about financing options to help ensure you can get the treatments you need and desire.
