Meet the Team at Family Dentistry of Royal Oak

The people involved in your dental healthcare make a difference, and that’s why we’ve worked hard to assemble a team of compassionate, caring, smart, and talented dental professionals. We know that it’s important for you to trust and be able to relate to the people who are caring for you!

Every member of our team is dedicated to your healthcare and loves what they do. We are committed to caring for you and your family to the best of our ability and constantly strive to provide you with excellent care. We also believe in ongoing education and learning, and we aim to continue to educate ourselves in order to stay on top of the latest developments in our fields.

Jill

Dental Hygienist Jill has been a hygienist for 22 years. She joined the team here at Family Dentistry of Royal Oak five years ago. I am happy to be working with such a wonderful, caring staff. It’s been a great experience to be part of a knowledgable and professional team.

Jill and her husband have been happily married for 30 years. They have two college age sons. Jill likes to spend time with her family and friends. In her down time Jill enjoys to read and go running. She is excited for the years to come of seeing patients and building relationships here at Family Dentistry of Royal Oak.

Lindsay

Dental Hygienist Lindsay graduated from Ferris State University with an Associate of Applied Science in Dental Hygiene in 2016 and a Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene in 2017, finishing in the top 10 % of her class with Summa Cum Laude honors. She is passionate about educating patients on oral health care and its impact on overall health, and she enjoys building strong, trusting relationships with each of her patients.

Lindsay grew up on a dairy farm in Northern Michigan. In her free time, she enjoys traveling, hiking, playing volleyball, attending sporting events and concerts, and spending time with family and friends. Fun fact: She has a golden doodle named Nevie, named after her favorite dental instrument. Go Red Wings and Go Lions!

Shauna

Dental Hygienist I graduated magna cum laude from Oakland Community College in 2021, and one of my proudest moments was receiving the Patient Care Award — because taking care of people is truly what drives me. I’ve been part of the Family Dentistry of Royal Oak team for almost three years now, and I genuinely couldn’t be happier with the group I get to work with every day.

What matters most to me is that every single patient who sits in my chair feels welcome, comfortable, and cared for. I love talking with patients about their oral health and helping them understand what’s going on — because when you feel informed, you feel confident, and that makes all the difference. My goal is always for you to leave not just with a clean smile, but feeling good about the decisions you’re making for your health.

Outside of work, I’m happiest spending time with my husband, my two girls, and our black lab. I grew up training in classical ballet, and these days I stay active through yoga and time spent out in nature. I think finding that balance outside the office is what helps me show up fully present for my patients every day!
