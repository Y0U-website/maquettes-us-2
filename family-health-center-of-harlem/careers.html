About Us

The Institute for Family Health is a mission-driven health organization with a rich history and expert leadership. We’re one of the largest community health center networks in New York State, serving over 100,000 patients and clients annually at locations across New York City and the Mid-Hudson Valley.

As a federally qualified health center (FQHC), our mission is to improve access to high quality, patient-centered primary health care targeted to the needs of medically underserved communities. No one is turned away.
