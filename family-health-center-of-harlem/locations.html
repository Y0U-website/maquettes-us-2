Locations

Our health centers in the Bronx, Brooklyn, Manhattan and the Mid-Hudson Valley offer high-quality services to people of all ages. All of our centers are accepting new patients. No matter who you are, we’re here to help.

We also offer services in New York City at our Care Found Here program and school-based health center locations.
