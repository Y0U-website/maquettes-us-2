Family Healthcare follows a healthcare model that provides evidence-based care, at the right time, in the most cost-efficient way. This model emphasizes preventive care provided by a consistent patient-centered healthcare team, which includes a physician, nurse practitioner or physician assistant supported by a medical assistant, and clerical staff in these service areas.
