Preventative Dentistry

Our goal is to empower patients with knowledge and preventative care that supports a lifetime of healthy smiles.

  • Fluoride Treatments – Strengthen enamel and help prevent cavities, especially in children and adults at higher risk.
  • Dental Sealants – A protective coating applied to molars to guard against decay in hard-to-reach areas.
  • Oral Cancer Screenings – Early detection through visual and physical examinations of your oral tissues.
  • Oral Hygiene Education – Personalized guidance on brushing and flossing techniques to maintain a healthy smile between visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Regular Dental Cleanings and Exams Really That Important?

Yes! Professional cleanings help remove plaque and tartar that can’t be eliminated by brushing and flossing alone. Exams allow your dentist to catch issues early—before they become serious problems.

What Are Fluoride Treatments and How Do They Help?

Fluoride treatments strengthen tooth enamel and help prevent cavities. They’re especially beneficial for children, people with dry mouth, or anyone at higher risk of tooth decay.

How Do Dental Sealants Work?

Sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of molars. They act as a barrier against food particles and bacteria, helping prevent cavities in hard-to-clean areas.

Why Should I Get an Oral Cancer Screening?

Oral cancer screenings help detect early signs of cancer or pre-cancer. Early detection can significantly improve the chances of successful treatment.

Can You Teach Me How to Brush and Floss Properly?

Absolutely! During your visit, we’ll walk you through proper techniques for brushing and flossing to ensure you’re keeping your teeth and gums as healthy as possible at home.
