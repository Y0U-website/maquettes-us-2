The practice

We understand that going to the dentist is not everyone’s favorite thing to do. From complimentary coffee to noise-blocking headphones to warm towels and blankets, we have created an environment that will help you sit back and relax while we take care of you.

Just like shoes, dental treatment is not a one size fits all approach for every patient. Our team takes the time to get to know each patient, and we work with you to create a treatment plan that best fits you.

Robots haven’t replaced us quite yet. Until they do, we have invested in modern technology, such as digital radiographs and radiation-free cavity-detection lasers, to help us provide you with the safest and most conservative treatment.

We understand that you’ve taken time out of your busy schedule to visit us, and we do our very best to keep you on schedule. From school and extracurricular activities to work meetings, we offer early morning and late evening hours for your convenience.
