Meet the Doctors

Meet Dr. Ali Nematollahi and Dr. Katayoun Attari

They have been residents of the Chicagoland area for over 18 years, and have lived in Glenview since 1997. Both doctors have extensive training in all facets of dentistry. Dr. Attari has her main focus on pediatric dentistry, while Dr. Nematollahi has had advanced training in the field of cosmetic dentistry. Their pursuit of excellence drives them to attend and participate in some of the most advanced seminars available to clinicians.

Dr. Ali Nematollahi graduated from Northwestern University Dental School in Chicago, IL in 1991. He is certified in dental implant surgery and also holds a US patent in the field of dental implantology.

Dr. Katayoun Attari graduated from the Marquette University School of Dentistry in 1993. She has received numerous achievement awards. Both doctors are members of the American Dental Association, Illinois Dental Society and Chicago Dental Society.

Dr. Ali Nematollahi and Dr. Katayoun Attari were married in 1997 and are proud of their two sons Kian and Ryan.

Our Philosophy

Dr. Ali and Dr. Attari have incorporated the latest technology and materials into their practice. They also attend numerous continuing education courses so that they can better serve their patients. Furthermore, both doctors are committed to listening to your questions and concerns. They are dedicated to providing you and your family with personalized care
