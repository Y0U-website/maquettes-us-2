Your visit begins with a thorough evaluation of your oral health. This detailed examination includes:

• Cavity Check: Untreated cavities can lead to serious dental issues. Dr. Scander will inspect your teeth for signs of decay to ensure prompt intervention if needed.

• Gum Disease Assessment: Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy mouth. The examination includes checking for signs of gingivitis or periodontal disease, which, if untreated, can lead to tooth loss.

After the examination, the focus shifts to cleaning and enhancing the health and appearance of your teeth. This service includes:
