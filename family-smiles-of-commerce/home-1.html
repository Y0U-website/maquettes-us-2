Restorative Dentistry

0 Restorative dentistry Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing and rebuilding teeth that have been damaged by decay, wear, or injury. Using modern techniques and high-quality materials, we restore the function, strength, and natural appearance of your smile. Treatments may include fillings, crowns, bridges, veneers, and dental implants—each carefully tailored to your individual needs. Our goal is to help you chew comfortably, smile confidently, and maintain long-term oral health.

COMPOSITE FILLING Composite fillings are tooth-colored restorations used to repair cavities and minor damage while blending seamlessly with your natural teeth. Made from a durable resin material, they are carefully shaped and polished to restore both function and appearance. Composite fillings provide a strong, discreet solution that helps protect your tooth and maintain a natural-looking smile.

CROWNS Dental crowns are custom-made caps that cover and protect damaged or weakened teeth. They restore strength, shape, and appearance while blending naturally with your smile. Crowns are often used after large fillings, root canal treatment, or to repair broken teeth, helping you chew comfortably and maintain long-term oral health.

DENTAL BRIDGES Dental bridges are used to replace one or more missing teeth by filling the gap with a natural-looking artificial tooth. The bridge is supported by the surrounding teeth or dental crowns, restoring your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence. Dental bridges also help maintain proper alignment of your teeth and prevent shifting over time.
