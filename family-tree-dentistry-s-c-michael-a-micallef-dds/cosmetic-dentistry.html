Clear Aligners

Dr. Mike will closely monitor your progress to ensure your teeth are moving closer to alignment each visit!

Clear Aligners: Clear aligners are an alternative to traditional braces. Clear aligners work by applying gentle pressure to your teeth, using a series of customized trays. Dr. Mike will closely monitor your progress to ensure your teeth are moving closer to alignment each visit!

Porcelain Veneers and all Porcelain Crowns

Dr. Mike likes to say porcelain veneers can be “instant orthodontics”

Porcelain Veneers and All Porcelain Crowns: Porcelain veneers are minimally invasive highly esthetic tooth restorations used to improve the color, size, shape, and alignment of your natural teeth. Dr. Mike likes to say porcelain veneers can be “instant orthodontics.” The key difference between a porcelain veneer and a porcelain crown, the veneer only covers part of the tooth whereas the crown covers the entire tooth. Dr. Mike will go through the options and help the patient consider careful what the best treatment for their case is.
