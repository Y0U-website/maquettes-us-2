Fillings

The choice of material depends on factors such as the location and size of the cavity, patient preference, and budget.

Our office does not place amalgam (silver fillings). We strictly utilize composite resin, glass ionomer, and ceramics.

After placement, the filling is shaped and polished to ensure a comfortable bite and smooth surface. With proper care, fillings can last many years, though they may eventually need replacement due to wear, leakage, or secondary decay.

Dental fillings are essential for preserving natural teeth, preventing pain and infection, and supporting long-term oral health. Regular dental check-ups help ensure that fillings remain intact and effective.
