About Our Dental Office Fort Valley, GA

Begin Your Journey Towards a Better Smile

Here at Fantastic Smiles, Dr. Marable and the rest of our excellent team always embrace the opportunity to help patients pursue the healthy, eye-catching smiles of their dreams. Our treatment plans incorporate a wide variety of dental services that can be tailored for your unique needs, and we’ll do everything we can to make your visit a stress-free one. Whether you have oral health concerns or are looking to make some cosmetic improvements, you can call our dental office today to schedule your first appointment; we look forward to helping you achieve your smile goals.

All Ages Welcome

Children have very different dental needs compared to adults, such as a still-developing smile with a balance that could be disrupted if a baby tooth is lost too early. And of course, even as an adult your needs can still change over time; for example, as a senior you’ll generally be more likely to need to think about forms of tooth replacement. Dr. Marable has the skills and knowledge necessary to treat a wide variety of patients, so you can bring the whole family to our dental office.

State-of-the-Art Dentistry

If you look at other dental offices in the county or within a 15 to 20-mile radius, you’ll find that none of them have the same advanced technology that you’ll find at Fantastic Smiles. Keeping up with the latest advances in the dental field allows us to diagnose oral health issues with greater accuracy, plan more efficient treatments, and keep your discomfort to a minimum. You and your loved ones can enjoy all the benefits of seeing a skilled dentist who takes advantage of all that modern dentistry has to offer!

Dental Emergencies Welcome

The last thing you want during a dental emergency is to not have a clear idea of who you’re supposed to call. All emergencies particularly time-sensitive ones such as knocked-out teeth are best dealt with right away, and Dr. Marable will strive to be available as soon as possible whenever you need urgent care. Thanks to the various dental services we offer, we’re well-equipped to help you deal with whatever dental crisis you and your loved ones might be suffering from.
