Dr. Firas Marsheh

Dr. Firas Marsheh is a highly accomplished dentist serving the Midtown Manhattan community. He earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from New York University College of Dentistry and holds a specialty degree in Endodontics from Lutheran Medical Center in Brooklyn, NY.

Dr. Marsheh practiced dentistry internationally in underserved areas for two years, focusing specifically on endodontic treatment . During his time at New York University, Dr. Marsheh conducted research on Anxiety toward Endodontic Treatment, which was published in the Journal of Oral Health Research. He is an active member of both the American Dental Association and the New York County Dental Society.

“I aim to reduce the anxiety and stress that accompany an endodontic treatment experience and provide each patient with care, comfort, and knowledge before treatment. Also, I strive to treat and give each patient the optimal results in a short amount of time.” Dr. Marsheh
