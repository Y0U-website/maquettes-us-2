  • Trying to brush my kid’s teeth is like a wrestling match. What can I do to make it easier? Start young and help your little one understand that dental care can’t hurt them. Introduce toothbrushes and floss in a non-threatening manner at a young age, so they know it is not dangerous. Also, remember that young children learn by playing. Let them watch you brush your teeth or even let them brush your teeth (maybe wear safety goggles just in case!). Chat with our team when you visit for some practical tips and advice!
