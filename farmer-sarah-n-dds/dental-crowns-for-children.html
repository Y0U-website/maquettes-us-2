Signs Your Child May Need a Dental Crown

Dental crowns can be beneficial for children in several situations. Recognizing when your child might need a crown can help you make the right choices for their oral health:

If you spot any signs of tooth damage or decay in your child’s mouth, contact our team for a professional evaluation.

  • Severe tooth decay
  • Cracked or broken teeth
  • Following root canal treatment
  • Developmental issues
  • Reinforcement for weak teeth
  • Aesthetic improvements
