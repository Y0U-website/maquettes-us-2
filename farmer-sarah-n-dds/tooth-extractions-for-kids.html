When Might Your Child Need a Tooth Extraction?

We always try to save natural teeth, but sometimes removing a tooth is necessary for your child’s overall oral health. Common reasons include:

If you see signs that your child’s teeth are causing pain or problems, it’s important to get professional advice quickly.

  • Tooth decay too severe for a filling or crown
  • Getting ready for braces
  • Infected or abscessed teeth
  • Tooth damage from an accident
  • Baby teeth blocking adult teeth from coming in
