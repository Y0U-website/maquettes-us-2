Farnham Dentistry

After receiving his PhD in Physical Chemistry, Dr. Sutton Farnham earned his dental degree from the Medical College of Virginia. Dr. Sutton Farnham completed his residency training at the University of Florida/Shands Hospital, where he treated complex dental cases and medically compromised patients. In additional residency training he focused on patients with temporomandibular joint [TMJ] problems.

Dr. Sutton Farnham has held positions at several Universities including the University of Florida. Dr. Sutton Farnham started and directed the postgraduate General Practice Residency at Shands, Jacksonville. He has authored articles published in peer reviewed journals in chemistry as well as dentistry and holds several patents. Dr. Sutton Farnham is past president of the Florida Academy of General Dentistry.

In addition to general and aesthetic dentistry, his private practice includes the diagnosis and treatment of temporomandibular joint [TMJ] problems.

Dr. Jacquelene Farnham is a graduate of the University of Florida College of Dentistry with a degree in Dental Medicine. Dr.

Jacqui Farnham continued her postgraduate training with a General Practice Residency at the University of Florida’s Shands Hospital. As a hospital resident she routinely treated children of all ages, medically compromised and trauma patients in addition to advanced and complex dental cases. Dr. Jacqui Farnham also has her MBA from the University of Florida.

A strong proponent of continuing education, Dr. Jacqui Farnham has held leadership positions in the Academy of General Dentistry and the Alumni Academy of University of Florida.

Her practice of general dentistry for all ages includes cosmetic and full mouth reconstructions.

For children she believes that a dental visit should be fun and her goal for her young patients is to develop without dental disease and to enjoy every visit to the dentist.

On the other side of the age spectrum, Dr Farnham treats those patients who have lost teeth restoring their dental function and appearance and thereby improving their quality of life.

Dr. Ian MacKenzie Farnham, or Dr. MacK as he is known by his patients, was born into a family of dentists. His mom and dad started Farnham Dentistry in 1983 in Mandarin in Jacksonville, FL. Growing up around the practice of dentistry, he witnessed firsthand the satisfaction his parents received from helping people with their dental needs as well as the happiness they were able to provide to their patients. It was no surprise when he decided to follow in their footsteps.

Dr. MacK graduated Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Science degree in integrative biology from the University of Florida. He continued to earn his Doctorate in Dental Surgery from the University of Maryland where he graduated with the Dean’s Award for Outstanding Community Service. Additionally he completed an extra year of training at the Jersey Shore University Medical Center earning a General Practice Residency degree. He is currently a member of the Northeast Florida Dental Society, the Florida Dental Association, and the American Dental Association.

Picking up where his parents left off, Dr. MacK looks forward to continue offering the highest-quality comprehensive dental care within his community. He enjoys using his training to help patients with dental phobias or anxieties experience a relaxing and pleasant visit to the dentist. Dr. MacK focuses his practice on creating an easy, stress-free, and patient-focused experience in an inviting family atmosphere. Ultimately, Dr. MacK’s goal is to treat generations of Jacksonville families, from grandkids to grandparents.

Dr. MacK loves the outdoors. From fishing to surfing and from soccer to mountain biking, he enjoys staying active. One thing is for sure, his black lab, Nugget, is never too far behind. As a certified therapy dog she even comes to the office twice a week!
