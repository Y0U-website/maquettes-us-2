Our commitment

Farzad Dental is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to people with disabilities. We want everyone who visits our site to feel welcome and to find the experience rewarding, regardless of ability or technology.

Standards we work toward

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.

Measures we take

We strive to use semantic HTML, descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images, sufficient color contrast, keyboard-operable navigation, a skip-to-content link, and clear, consistent structure across the site. We review new content and features with accessibility in mind.

Third-party content

Some elements of our site rely on third-party tools (for example, embedded maps and our online booking form). We do not control the accessibility of third-party content, but we choose vendors that support accessibility and provide alternatives, such as calling our offices directly.

Ongoing effort

Accessibility is an ongoing effort and we are continually working to improve. If you encounter any part of our site that is difficult to use, please let us know so we can help and make improvements.

Contact us

If you have difficulty accessing any content on this website, or need assistance with any part of it, please contact us and we will be glad to help.
