Home / Services / Dental Implants Restorative Dentistry Permanent tooth replacement Dental Implants Implants replace missing teeth from the root up — so they look, feel and function like your own. At Farzad Dental we plan every case with 3D imaging for a precise, comfortable result that lasts.

An implant is a small titanium post placed in the jaw that acts like a natural tooth root, topped with a custom crown. Unlike a bridge, it doesn’t rely on grinding down neighboring teeth, and it preserves the bone that keeps your face looking full. Dr. Philip Farzad plans every case with 3D CBCT imaging for precise, predictable placement.

Whether you’re missing one tooth, several, or a full arch, we offer the right implant option — and we can handle everything from planning to the final crown under one roof, at both our Encino and Palmdale offices.

Why choose implants

Protects your bone The titanium post stimulates the jaw so bone stays strong.

Eat with confidence Chew comfortably without the slipping of dentures.

A lasting investment With good care, implants can last decades.

Step by step The implant process

Evaluation & 3D planning We map the site with CBCT imaging for precise placement.

Implant placement The titanium post is gently placed and left to heal.

Healing & integration The post fuses with bone over a few months.

Final restoration A custom crown, bridge or denture completes your smile.
