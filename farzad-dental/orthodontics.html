At our Encino office at 16661 Ventura Blvd, Suite 815 (91436), Dr. Philip Farzad uses orthodontics to correct crowding, gaps and bite alignment by gently guiding teeth into their ideal positions. The aesthetic payoff is real — a straighter, more confident smile — but the health benefits run deeper. When teeth are properly aligned, they’re far easier to brush and floss effectively, which lowers your risk of decay and gum disease over the years.

A misaligned bite does more than look uneven; it can wear teeth down unevenly, strain your jaw and make everyday chewing less comfortable. Orthodontic treatment addresses crooked or crowded teeth, gaps and spacing, and bite problems like overbite, underbite and crossbite. It can also relieve the jaw discomfort that often comes with a bite that doesn’t fit together the way it should, improving both function and comfort.

We tailor treatment to your smile and lifestyle. For many patients, Invisalign clear aligners straighten teeth discreetly without brackets or wires, and Invisalign Teen offers the same nearly invisible approach designed for younger patients and their routines. Retainers help hold teeth in their new positions and protect the results you’ve worked for. Whatever path fits best, we’ll explain the options clearly so you can choose with confidence.
