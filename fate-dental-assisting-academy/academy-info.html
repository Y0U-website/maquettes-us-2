What We Do

Being in the dental industry for more than a decade, we understand what dentists look for when they want to hire a new employee. This is why we have created a practical and hands on training program. We will work hard to prepare you to be the best candidate for a new job, as well as provide you with all of the necessary training and confidence to demonstrate your skills when asked. Our program is engaging and enjoyable, starting each date with a motivation then extensive training. At the end of your journey, we will celebrate your accomplishments with beautiful graduation.

What We Do

We know what Dentists are looking for when hiring. That is why we focus on hands on education. When interviewing, the doctor will sit you down with one of his patients to see your experience. If you are not extensively trained, you will lose that position offered. We have fun while learning, begin each day with a motivational thought to keep you positive about life and hold beautiful graduations for your achievements.

Why We Are Different

Each student’s challenge is unique, and we are here to help them overcome these obstacles while teaching dental assisting itself. Our students become family, and seeing them achieve their goals is an honor for us. If we can make a difference, we believe we have achieved our goals. Teaching Dental Assisting is only one aspect of our approach; our focus on deep personal engagement, shared growth, and mutual support is what truly sets us apart.

Why We Are Different

I was asked what makes my school different from the one day a week schools.

My reply: Because I get involved in the lives of my students, they become my personal family. Having been in the field for over 00 years, teaching Dental Assisting is the easy part. However, if I can take someone who is insecure, struggling with past hurts, addictions or someone who just needs their Spirit lifted, if I can help just one student, then at the end of the day, my heart says I’ve done my job. Teaching Dental Assisting is just a part of the school. It is about taking time to become involved, sharing and growing together is what I feel makes us Elite.
