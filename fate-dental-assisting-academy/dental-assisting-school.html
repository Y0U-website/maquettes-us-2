Why Become a Registered Dental Assistant?

Affordable & Quick Education

A career in Dental Assisting is very rewarding given the wide range of interactions, responsibilities, and experiences you can have. Just as important, it does not require dedicating two-four years of your life and tens of thousands of dollars to just get your foot in the door.

Great Long-Term Career

In addition to the low barriers, a career as an RDA boasts a median salary of $40,080 according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Also, the job outlook from 2019-2029 is projected to grow 7 % annually.
