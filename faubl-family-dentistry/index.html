Dr. John Faubl received his B.A. in psychology from Marquette University in 1992. He entered the University of Illinois Dental School in Chicago in 1992. He received a B.S. in Dentistry in 1994 and his D.D.S. degree in 1996, graduating with honors.

He graduated fourth in his class and received the Harry Saxon Scholarship for Academic Excellence. He was one of two students selected to participate in an oral surgery externship at UIC.

He is a member of numerous professional associations including: American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society, Chicago Dental Society, and McHenry County Dental Society.

Upon graduation, Dr. Faubl associated in Vernon Hills for 1 year. He then began an associateship in Woodstock for over 2 years. In early 2000, he decided he would like to open his own office. In May 2000, Faubl Family Dentistry was opened in Huntley.

Dr Faubl lives in Algonquin with his wife Amy whom he met while at Marquette University. They have an adult son and daughter.
