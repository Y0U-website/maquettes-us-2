1. Provide a Natural Look & Feel

Implants complete your smile naturally, making it seem like you’ve never lost permanent teeth. Implant crowns, bridges, and dentures are custom-made for your mouth for an accurate, comfortable fit. They match the color, size, and shape of your existing teeth to seamlessly blend in. Your smile will have an authentic look and feel that no other dental restoration can provide.

2. Restore Chewing Power

Functionality is just as important as aesthetics in a smile, and implants don’t disappoint! They fuse with your jawbone permanently to support your new smile. Secured by implants, your new teeth will function just like natural teeth for biting and chewing, expanding your food choices. You’ll be able to talk, laugh, smile, sing, and eat normally, without worrying about the state of your teeth.

3. Versatile Options for Replacing Lost Teeth

Implant dentistry offers the option of replacing one tooth with a single tooth dental implant, several missing teeth in a row with a fixed dental bridge, or all your teeth with implant dentures. Our team will customize your restorations accordingly.

4. Support Jaw Health

Tooth loss inevitably leads to bone loss, as your jawbone weakens and deteriorates over time from a lack of stimulation from tooth roots. Implants help reverse this condition to give you a stronger, healthier jaw. They preserve bone density to keep your face from collapsing, which helps you retain a younger-looking appearance.

5. Prevent Dental Drift

Dental drift is another natural product of losing natural teeth. The teeth that remain start to drift out of their normal positions to fill the gaps left by missing teeth. This alters your bite and causes alignment issues. Implants support existing teeth to prevent dental drift. They work with your natural teeth to keep your smile straight, healthy, and even.

6. Support Long-Term Oral Health

Implants contribute to the long-term health of your smile by minimizing complications that can arise from tooth loss, such as jawbone deterioration, shifting teeth, loose teeth, decay, and gum disease.

7. Last for Decades

Titanium implants can last for decades or even a lifetime, with good oral care. Porcelain restorations can last up to 20 years before needing to be replaced. Together, they restore the integrity of your smile for years to come.
