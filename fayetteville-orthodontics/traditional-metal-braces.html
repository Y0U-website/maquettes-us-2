Traditional metal braces have been a reliable and effective way to straighten teeth for over 200 years. They consist of metal brackets that are bonded to each tooth, and a special wire that is tied into the brackets. The wire generates gentle force, which slowly moves the teeth into the desired position as determined by the orthodontist.

With improvements in technology, metal braces are now smaller, lighter, and more efficient. They are also more affordable than other orthodontic treatments, making them a great option for those on a budget.

At Fayetteville Orthodontics, our experts will carefully plan your treatment to ensure that you receive the best possible results. They will consider your individual needs and goals to create a personalized treatment plan that is made specifically to you.
