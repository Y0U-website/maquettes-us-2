Dental Implants in Agawam, MA

  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution for missing teeth. With proper care and maintenance, they can last for several decades or even a lifetime. Regular dental check-ups and good oral hygiene practices are essential to ensure the longevity of dental implants.
  • Are dental implant procedures painful? Dental implant procedures are typically performed under local anesthesia, ensuring that the patient does not feel any pain during the surgery. Post-surgical discomfort can be managed with pain medication, and most patients report that any discomfort or swelling subsides within a few days.
  • How long does the dental implant process take? The dental implant process can vary depending on individual circumstances. After the initial implant placement, it can take several months for the implant to fuse with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. The overall treatment duration can range from a few months to a year or more, depending on factors such as healing time and the need for additional procedures.
  • Are dental implants noticeable? Dental implants are designed to look and feel like natural teeth. The replacement tooth or dental prosthesis that is attached to the implant is customized to match the surrounding natural teeth in color, shape, and size. When properly placed and restored, dental implants are generally indistinguishable from natural teeth.
  • Can smokers get dental implants? While smoking can have a negative impact on the success of dental implants, it does not automatically disqualify someone from being a candidate. However, smoking can increase the risk of complications and hinder the healing process. You may be advised to quit or reduce smoking before and after implant surgery to optimize the chances of success.
