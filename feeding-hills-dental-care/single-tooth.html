frequently asked questions

your Single Tooth Implant questions, answered

  • Is a single tooth implant painful? The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so you shouldn’t feel pain during the surgery. Some discomfort is expected afterward, but it’s usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers and subsides after a few days.
  • How long do single tooth implants last? With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown on top may need to be replaced after 10-15 years due to normal wear, but the implant itself is very durable.
  • Are there any risks associated with single tooth implants? As with any surgery, there are some risks, such as infection, implant failure, or injury to surrounding teeth. However, dental implants have a high success rate, and complications are rare, especially when performed by an experienced dentist.
  • How much does a single tooth implant cost? The cost can vary depending on factors like the complexity of the case, the need for additional procedures, and your location. At Feeding Hills Dental Care, we’ll discuss your treatment plan and financing options to help make the procedure affordable for you.
  • Can a single tooth implant help prevent bone loss? Yes! Dental implants help stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss that typically occurs after losing a tooth. This makes them a great long-term solution for maintaining oral health and facial structure.
  • What is the recovery time after a single tooth implant? Recovery from the initial implant surgery usually takes a few days to a week. However, it will take a few months for the implant to fully integrate with the jawbone before the final crown can be placed.
