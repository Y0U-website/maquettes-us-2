The practice

At our practice, we prioritize the preservation of your natural tooth structure. I take a conservative approach to treatment—because nothing compares to the strength and function of the teeth you were born with. When you visit, my goal is to understand the purpose of your visit, listen to your concerns, and create a treatment plan that aligns with your health needs, lifestyle, and budget.

Dr. Fei Hwang Dentist Originally from Taiwan, I moved to the United States at the age of 12. I completed my undergraduate studies in Biology at the University of Illinois, then earned my dental degree from Northwestern University School of Dentistry in Chicago. I began my dental career in Evanston, Illinois, practicing general and family dentistry.

Alongside private practice, I became a Clinical Instructor at Northwestern University Dental School, where I also pursued postgraduate courses in prosthodontics, advanced treatment planning, and dental implants. In 2002, I relocated to the Bay Area and established my current practice serving San Jose and Milpitas.

Continuing my passion for education, I joined the faculties at both the University of California San Francisco Dental School and the University of the Pacific Dental School. I believe in providing compassionate, honest care and take pride in developing conservative treatment plans focused on achieving optimal results.
