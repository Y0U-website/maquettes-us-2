The Different Types of Dental Implants

Your dentist will recommend one of the following types of dental implants.

Endosteal

Endosteal implants look like a screw and are implanted directly into your jawbone. They can hold one or more artificial teeth. Endosteal implants are the most commonly used. They have the highest success rate (95 %+) because they are placed into and fuse with your jawbone.

Subperiosteal

Subperiosteal implants are used when you don’t have enough jawbone for an endosteal implant. Subperiosteal implants are placed on the top of your jaws under the gums. They use a metal framework that sticks out of your gums to hold your artificial teeth in place.

Zygomatic

Zygomatic implants are rarely used because of the complexity of the procedure. Zygomatic implants are placed in your cheekbone instead of your jawbone.

The Benefits of Getting Dental Implants

There are lots of benefits you can expect if you get dental implants.

  • They look and function just like your natural teeth.
  • They significantly improve your smile for a lifetime.
  • They help preserve your jawbone by preventing jawbone loss that occurs when you have missing teeth that aren’t replaced.
  • They make it easier for you to chew.
  • They provide a stable foundation for your dentures.
  • They can last a lifetime if properly cared for (the implant post, not the artificial tooth).
  • They make it easier for you to speak and enunciate.
