Meet the Doctors

Dr. Mark Ferdos

Dr. Mark Ferdos grew up in Cobb County but has always loved North Georgia and moved to Habersham with his wife in 2016. He attended Kennesaw State University where he graduated Suma Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biotechnology. Following his undergraduate program, he attended the Medical College of Georgia where he completed his degree in Dental Medicine. During his training, he conducted research at Emory University and published research on Pathology and Oncology.

Dr. Mark’s portfolio includes Cosmetic Dentistry, Sedation Dentistry and Oral Surgery. Dr. Ferdos believes himself to be a life-long learner and loves to attend continuing education classes to further advance his skills and stay up to date with the latest trends in dentistry. He has advanced training in cosmetic dentistry, oral surgery, implant dentistry and is Board Certified to provide conscious I.V. Sedation for patients in the dental setting. Dr. Ferdos is an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry, American Dental Association and the Georgia Dental Association.

Dr. Ferdos met his lovely wife Dr. Meg Patterson Ferdos, a native of Stephens County while attending dental school. Dr. Meg and Mark have a sweet daughter, Louvie Ruth who keeps them constantly on the go. He loves to keep humor in the dental office to ease patients through their dental appointments. In his spare time, Dr. Ferdos enjoys the outdoors and loves spending time with his family. You will find him working in the yard or fishing with his dog Maple. Football is a staple in the Ferdos household, whether rooting for the Falcons or the Dawgs.

Dr. Meg Ferdos

Dr. Meg Patterson Ferdos grew up in Toccoa, Georgia, and has been around dentistry her whole life through her dad who is also a dentist. She attended Young Harris College where she graduated Valedictorian with a Bachelor degree in Biology. She was a part of numerous social organizations in college including the Student Government Association and was voted by her peers as Miss YHC in 2010. Dr. Meg went on to complete her dental degree at the Medical College of Georgia where she received the Quintessence International Award for Achievement in Periodontics upon graduation.

In her spare time, Dr. Meg loves to play tennis, travel, read, and be outdoors with her family and friends. Dr. Meg and Dr. Mark have one daughter named Louvie who they love to spend time with, and who has given them so much joy in their lives. They also have a dog that they rescued named Maple, and an orange tabby cat named Sully.
