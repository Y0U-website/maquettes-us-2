Meet Our Staff

Vicki Ward

Vicki brightens our office with her contagious smile and great attitude. You can often hear her joyous laugh throughout the office! She attended Lanier Tech Dental assisting school and has had a rewarding career in dentistry for over 25 years. She has enjoyed working with Ferdos Family Dental and helping patients throughout their dental journey to a brighter smile.

Vicki and her husband, Joel, are from Toccoa. They have one daughter, Kayla, and a son-in-law, Daniel. She loves spending her free time with her two precious grandchildren, Ellarae and Langston, who keep her very busy.

Jennifer Boss

Jennifer was born in Atlanta and her family moved to the NE GA area when she was 7 years old. She graduated from Habersham Central High School in 1994 and went to Gainesville College. After college, Jennifer moved to Detroit where she lived for a year and then to Colorado Springs where she lived for 5 years before coming back home to Georgia.

She has been in the dental field for 33 years, starting out as a dental assistant for 7 years then transitioned into front office/office management for 26 years and has really enjoyed her dental career. She is the mother to a wonderful 20-year-old son Christian who is in college studying Criminal Justice with Forensics at UNG Dahlonega. In her spare time, you can find Jennifer in her garden, cooking, hiking, fishing, camping, and loving on her 3 sweet kitties (Milo Martin, Baxter Bailey, and Ollie Oliver). In the fall, she loves rooting for her GA Dawgs!!

Outside of the office, Wendy enjoys spending her free time with her husband, Michael, and their son, Issac. Family is her greatest joy, and she cherishes making lasting memories together.
