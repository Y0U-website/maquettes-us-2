Meet Our Dentist

Dr. Paul Ferguson

Dr. Ferguson takes an amount of continuing education every year that far exceeds licensing requirements. He is committed to providing his patients with the most advanced dental techniques and the highest quality dentistry.

Dr. Ferguson feels lucky to both work and live in Fair Haven, and he can be spotted on River Road as he walks to and from work each day. He actively serves his community, serving as the long-time Treasurer of the Fair Haven Business Association, and as a member of the Fair Haven Fields Natural Area Advisory Committee, and volunteering at the Fair Haven Firemen’s Fair each summer.

Dr. Ferguson enjoys getting to know his patients and learning more about their interests. His interests include cooking, gardening, the occasional round of golf, cheering on the NY Yankees, and doing the New York Times crossword puzzle religiously. He and his wife, Carolyn, enjoy time with their families and friends, the beach, and the Monmouth County Parks. Dr. Ferguson especially enjoys traveling and hiking in our National Parks. He looks forward to welcoming you and your family to his office.
