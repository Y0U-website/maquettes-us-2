Parking

Parking for our office is available behind our office, on the street in front of our office, or in the municipal lot behind the Fairwinds Deli, across the street and slightly west of our office.

Financial Arrangements

Our fees are competitive with other providers of high-quality dental care in this area. It is our office policy to receive payment for services at the time of service unless other arrangements have been agreed upon. We accept personal checks and VISA, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, and cash, of course. In the event your planned treatment will result in fees exceeding $2,000, we may consider a mutually acceptable schedule of payments to assist you with budgeting.

Dental Insurance

If you have dental insurance, you can expect us to help you get the maximum benefit to which you are entitled. If you have other types of dental insurance, we will accept assignment of benefits for most plans. However, fees for some services may not fall within a particular plan’s definition of “usual and customary,” and you will be responsible for the difference.
