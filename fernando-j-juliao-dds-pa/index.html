Parkville Dental Arts & Associates is a full service dental practice in Parkville, MD. We welcome new patients of all ages and invite you to become a part of our dental family!

The practice

We offer state-of-the-art technology as well as a wide array of dental services.

We can help you establish an oral hygiene routine that will keep your teeth and gums healthy.

Dental implants are a permanent and appealing solution to replace missing or extracted teeth.

We offer a wide variety of cosmetic dental services including veneers and whitening.

Endodontics, including root canals, is a dental specialty that deals with the nerves of the teeth.

We offer restorative dentistry services including bridges, crowns, dentures and bonding.

We welcome pediatric patients at our practice. Sealants and mouth guards are especially important.

Our periodontal services include gum disease treatment, scaling and root planing, surgery and more.

Les soins

Our primary focus is on dental education and prevention which hopefully results in less time in the dental chair and a positive experience for our patients.

We also offer orthodontic work including Invisalign (as a certified provider), clear braces, night guards and TMJ.
