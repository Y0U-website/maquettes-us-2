Adult Orthodontics

Fernwood Family Dental offers basic adult orthodontic services that last, on average, six months. This keeps the cost well below the average orthodontic specialist. As a general dental office, we often do orthodontics in conjunction with other restorative work like crowns or veneers. This maximizes treatment efficiency since you only need to visit one office for your complete smile makeover. On occasion, for cases that are overly complicated or likely to take a long time, we may make a referral to one of the fantastic orthodontists in our area.

  • Orthodontics Adult Orthodontics Teeth Whitening Orthodontic treatment isn’t just for kids and teens adults can take advantage of smile straightening as well! While orthodontics for children can be complicated, as there are many variables to consider (mixed adult and baby teeth, growth of their jaws and growth of their faces, etc.), adult orthodontics can be very simple and take less time since there are often fewer variables. Adults who choose orthodontic treatment can expect: An improved bite
  • Easier oral hygiene
  • Fewer incidence of periodontal disease
  • Improved aesthetics
