Outside the Office

A native of Burien, Dr. Fugere lives in Poulsbo with his wife Ann and daughters Bella and Ali. Ann teaches math at Olympic High School in Silverdale and the girls enjoy gymnastics, piano, and being a part of their local Scouts BSA group as part of the first wave of females in Scouting.

Having had a fantastic childhood here in the PNW, Dr. Fugere spends as much time outdoors as possible, enjoying all the natural diversity and activities that this region offers. He spends most of his free time trying to cultivate that experience with his own children so they develop the same passion he has for the outdoors.
