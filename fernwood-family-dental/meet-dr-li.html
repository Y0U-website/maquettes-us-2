Dr. Li grew up just across the water in Shoreline, WA. After earning her Bachelor’s degree in Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Washington, she traveled across the country to complete her dental training at the University of Pennsylvania, where she received an honors in community health. She then served as a dental officer in the US Navy for four years an unforgettable experience that eventually brought her back to the Pacific Northwest.

When she is not working, you’ll find her on the tennis court in the summer, skiing in the winter, and experimenting in the kitchen year-round, with her forever hungry fluffy sous-chef, Kuma.
