What can I expect after my treatment?

Mini implants minimize discomfort. You may feel some mild soreness at the treatment sites. Often, Ferrari Dental patients feel well enough to resume all of their usual activities within one or two days. It’s essential to take good care of your mouth, as the implants themselves are vulnerable to gum disease, such as inflammation. We can advise on good oral care products and cleaning techniques based on the type of mini-implant teeth you have.
