Frequently Asked Questions

What are your office hours?

Monday through Thursday 8:00 AM–6:00 PM; Friday 8:00 AM–2:00 PM; Saturday 8:00 AM–12:00 PM. Closed Sunday.

Are you open on Saturdays?

Yes. The office is open Saturday 8:00 AM–12:00 PM.

How do I become a new patient?

Download the Adult Welcome Form and HIPAA Privacy Notice from the New Patient Forms section above, then call (516) 223-6896 or use the online booking link to schedule your first visit. Spanish-language forms are also available.

What areas of Long Island do you serve?

Dr. Fidel Abreu, DDS serves patients from Freeport, Nassau County, and the greater Long Island area.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes — se habla español. The office is fully bilingual, and Spanish-language new patient forms are available in the New Patient Forms section above.
