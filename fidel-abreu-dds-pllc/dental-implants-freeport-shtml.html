Single-tooth implants typically range from $3,000–$5,000 all-in (post + abutment + crown). Full-arch restorations like All-on-Four range from $20,000–$35,000 per arch . How long does the implant process take?

Most cases take 3 to 6 months from initial placement to final crown. The timeline allows the implant to integrate with your jawbone (osseointegration) before the final tooth is attached. Cases requiring bone grafts or sinus lifts may take longer.

Is implant placement painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia. Most patients report only mild soreness afterward, easily managed with OTC medication. Dr. Abreu’s 3D imaging plans the precise placement, minimizing tissue trauma.

How long do implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last 25+ years . The titanium post integrates permanently with bone. The crown on top may need replacement after 15–20 years of normal wear.

Do you offer All-on-Four in Long Island?

Yes. Dr. Abreu is a Diplomate of the International Association of Dental Implantology with All-on-Four Overdenture training. The technique restores a full arch using just four strategically placed implants.
