Les soins

Missing or failing teeth can affect how you eat, speak, and smile. Dental implants are small titanium posts placed in the jawbone that act like artificial tooth roots, supporting a crown, bridge, or denture for a strong, natural-looking replacement.

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia. Most patients report only mild soreness afterward, easily managed with OTC medication. Dr. Abreu’s 3D imaging plans the precise placement, minimizing tissue trauma.
