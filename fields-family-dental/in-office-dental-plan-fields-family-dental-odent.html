REDUCED FEES:

All dental services are offered at 15 % less than the normal office fees.

ELIGIBILITY:

Anybody is qualified to apply for our In–Office Dental Plan. Participants are eligible upon payment of the enrollment fee. This plan is administered solely by this dental office.

Membership in the Dental Plan may be terminated if the member misses multiple appointments, or for abuse and failure to pay membership fees or properly billed work.

SERVICES OFFERED:

This in-office Dental plan offers members a comprehensive dental package of dental benefits. The plan covers Diagnostic, Preventive, Restorative and Cosmetic Services.

PLAN LIMITATIONS AND EXCLUSIONS :

This membership program is a discount plan, not a dental insurance plan.

  • This membership plan is only honored a Fields Family Dental and cannot be used at any other dental or medical office.
  • Membership cannot be used: In conjunction with another dental plan or financing program
  • For services or for injuries covered under workers’ compensations
  • For treatment, which in the sole opinion of the treating dentist lies outside the realm of their capability
  • Membership is non-transferable to another person.
  • For referral to specialist
  • For hospitalization or hospital charges of any kind
  • For costs of dental care covered under automobile medical

CANCELLATION:

Membership will automatically renew on the anniversary date and will continue thereafter until cancelled in writing. Cancellation should be sent 30 days prior to the expected day of cancellation.
