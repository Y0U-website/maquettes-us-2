Dental Anxiety Patients

We would like

to welcome you to our dental family. Our practice is devoted to comprehensive and preventive patient care. We take great satisfaction in helping you maintain optimal oral health. No matter how long it has been since your last visit, we are here to take care of any dental needs you may have.

Our patients are our most important asset, and so we strive to develop long-lasting, trusting relationships. We want you to feel comfortable and relaxed when visiting us. All patients are treated as individuals. Our doctors make recommendations based on each unique need.

Dental anxiety is a common fear and you have come to the right place. Often we hear of past experiences, mostly from childhood memories, and can share with you the option of IV Sedation. This procedure will put you to ease about dental treatment. This can also help you accomplish all treatment to be finished in one visit! We offer a complimentary consultation to hear and discuss your concerns. We would be happy to help you over come your fears, while achieving a healthy smile.
