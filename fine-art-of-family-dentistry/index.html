It includes routine services like cleanings, exams, and preventive treatments, as well as restorative and cosmetic procedures to maintain long-term oral health.

Pediatric dentistry is the branch of dentistry that focuses on the oral health of infants, children, and adolescents. It involves specialized care tailored to children’s developing teeth, including preventive treatments, education, and early intervention.

General dentistry focuses on the overall oral health care of patients, offering preventive, diagnostic, and restorative services. It includes routine checkups, cleanings, fillings, and other treatments to maintain healthy teeth and gums.
