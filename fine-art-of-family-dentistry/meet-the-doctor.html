Dr. Sara Gassman

Dr. Sara Gassman joined the practice in July 2023 as a general dentist. She is a Salina native and excited to be returning to her hometown. She attended Kansas State University where she graduated with her Bachelor’s in Life Sciences with a minor in Biology. Following KSU, she received her Doctorate in Dental Surgery from the University of Kansas City- Missouri.

In her free time she enjoys spending time with her husband, Elliot and her dog Jordy. Some of her favorite hobbies are running, hiking, and skiing. Most weekends in fall you will find her in Manhattan, cheering on K-State.
