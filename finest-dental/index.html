A smile makeover is a customized treatment plan that may include whitening, veneers, bonding, and other cosmetic services to enhance the look of your smile. Whether you’re concerned about discoloration, uneven spacing, or worn-down teeth, we tailor your makeover to achieve natural, radiant results that match your goals and personality.

When dental issues affect your entire mouth such as severe wear, multiple missing teeth or advanced decay, full mouth reconstruction offers a complete solution. This comprehensive treatment plan combines restorative and cosmetic procedures to rebuild your oral health, function, and appearance from the ground up.

When plaque and tartar build up beneath the gumline, it can lead to gum disease and eventual tooth loss. Scaling and root planing is a deep-cleaning procedure that targets this hidden buildup, reduces inflammation, and helps your gums heal. It’s a crucial step in protecting your long-term oral health.

Veneers are ultra-thin shells of porcelain or composite resin bonded to the front of your teeth to instantly improve their appearance. Ideal for correcting chips, discoloration, gaps, or uneven shape, veneers offer a fast, long-lasting cosmetic upgrade with a natural, polished finish.
