The practice

Tooth loss affects more than just your smile, it can change your facial structure and make it harder to eat or speak. When jawbone loss is also present, a bone graft may be needed to support dental implants. We offer comprehensive treatments that combine titanium or zirconia implants with bone regeneration to restore your bite, facial harmony, and confidence.

When teeth are damaged, decayed, or missing, crowns and bridges provide a durable, natural-looking solution. A crown strengthens and protects a weakened tooth, while a bridge fills in the gaps by connecting to neighboring teeth. These restorations not only improve how your smile looks but also restore comfort, alignment, and bite function.

When dental issues affect your entire mouth such as severe wear, multiple missing teeth or advanced decay, full mouth reconstruction offers a complete solution. This comprehensive treatment plan combines restorative and cosmetic procedures to rebuild your oral health, function, and appearance from the ground up.

A root canal is used to save a tooth that’s been damaged by deep decay or infection. By removing the inflamed pulp, cleaning the interior, and sealing the tooth, we stop pain and prevent the need for extraction. Our gentle approach ensures you stay comfortable while preserving your natural tooth.

Veneers are ultra-thin shells of porcelain or composite resin bonded to the front of your teeth to instantly improve their appearance. Ideal for correcting chips, discoloration, gaps, or uneven shape, veneers offer a fast, long-lasting cosmetic upgrade with a natural, polished finish.
