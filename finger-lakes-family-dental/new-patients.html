New Patient: What to expect

We believe that a well-informed patient is better able to participate in their care decisions. We prioritize education and open communication to tailor each treatment to your individual needs and preferences.

  • Fill out new patient paperwork, and medical history and send the insurance card before the first visit
  • Comprehensive imaging
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Gum health evaluation
  • Personalized dental cleaning
  • One-on-one consultation with Dr. Maria E. Marzo to discuss your health, goals, and treatment options
  • Customized financial discussion and treatment planning
