What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a long-lasting option for replacing missing teeth. Surgically placed into the jawbone, they offer unmatched stability and restore both chewing function and a confident smile. We provide:

  • Single-tooth implants for individual gaps
  • Implant-supported bridges for multiple missing teeth

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer key advantages over traditional tooth replacements:

  • Natural Look and Feel – Seamlessly match existing teeth in color and shape
  • Strong and Durable – Anchored into the jaw for lasting stability
  • Prevents Bone Loss – Stimulates the jawbone to preserve structure
  • Restores Chewing and Speech – Eat and speak without discomfort
  • Protects Adjacent Teeth – No need to alter nearby teeth
  • Cost-Effective Over Time – Fewer repairs or replacements needed

Single-Tooth Implants

A single-tooth implant is the best solution for replacing a missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth. This option mimics the structure of a natural tooth, providing both aesthetic and functional benefits.

  • Looks and feels natural – Matches the color and shape of your existing teeth.
  • Preserves jawbone health – Prevents bone loss by stimulating the jaw like a real tooth.
  • Independent support – There’s no need to alter adjacent teeth.

Implant Bridges

For patients missing multiple teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge offers a secure, long-lasting alternative to traditional bridges.

  • More secure than traditional bridges – No risk of shifting or slipping.
  • Prevents bone loss – Keeps the jawbone stimulated and healthy.
  • Improves function – Restores strong chewing ability and natural speech.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the steps involved in getting a dental implant?

  • Consultation & Evaluation – A full dental exam, X-rays, and assessment to ensure you’re a good candidate.
  • Implant Placement – A titanium post is surgically placed into your jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root.
  • Healing Period – The implant fuses with your bone (a process called osseointegration) over several months.
  • Final Restoration – An abutment and custom-designed dental crown or bridge are placed to complete your new smile.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Even if you’ve been told in the past that implants weren’t an option, a consultation can help determine what’s possible now with updated techniques and technology.

  • Have healthy gums and no active gum disease
  • Have enough jawbone to support the implant
  • Don’t smoke, or are willing to quit during the healing process
  • Practice good oral hygiene and visit the dentist regularly

Is the Procedure Painful?

The procedure itself is performed under local anesthesia, ensuring a comfortable experience. For those with dental anxiety, sedation options are available. After the procedure, some mild discomfort is expected, but it can typically be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, implants can last a lifetime. Crowns or bridges may need replacement over time, but the implant itself is very durable.
