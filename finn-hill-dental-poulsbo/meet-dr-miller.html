At Finn Hill Dental, you’ll see the same friendly and talented staff that’s been caring for patients for years. Their dedication to superb dentistry and patient care makes them the best in the business.

Dr. Zachary Miller is the proud leader of the Finn Hill Dental team. While he grew up in fabulous Las Vegas, Nevada, his wife, Alexis, is from the area, and the two chose Kitsap specifically for its lovely people and natural beauty.

Most recently from Sandpoint, Idaho, they are grateful to continue to live and work in a smaller town setting while now being close to family.

Dr. Zachary Miller, or Dr. Z as most patients know him, earned his Bachelor of Science in Sports Medicine from Pepperdine University. He graduated from the University of Southern California School of Dentistry with his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2019.

He has taken extensive continuing education courses in atraumatic tooth extraction, trauma and emergency management, dental implants, implant dentures, helping anxious patients, and sleep disordered breathing.

Dr. Z is a member of the American Dental Association and the Washington State Dental Association.

In his free time, Dr. Z enjoys spending time with his wife Alexis and their son, learning Jiu-Jitsu, reviewing dental thought leaders, and perfecting his Scrabble game.

The Front Desk staff are here to assist you with scheduling and billing needs.

The Dental Assistants keep the office moving efficiently through their attention to detail while ensuring your comfort every step of the way.

The Dental Hygienists bring the highest level of service and care as they clean teeth and educate patients on the best methods to use at home.
