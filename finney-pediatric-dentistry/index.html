The practice

Our goal is to provide the most up-to-date treatment techniques and services to ensure that the children, adolescents and special needs patients that visit our office achieve the healthiest, most beautiful smile possible.

About the Practice APPOINTMENTS To make an appointment, please contact our office by phone or email. We try our best to be as accommodating as possible when scheduling appointments. We have found that very young children and those with extensive dental treatment needs tend to have a more positive experience when seen at early morning visits, when they feel more fresh and well-rested.

Finney Pediatric Dentistry proudly accepts most types of dental insurance and is committed to helping you maximize your insurance benefits. Dental insurances do vary greatly in their coverage of dental procedures and may only cover a portion of the costs for services rendered in our office. We are happy to file dental insurance claims on your behalf and help you to understand your dental…

We can only estimate your coverage benefits in good faith but cannot guarantee coverage due to the complexities of insurance contracts. If for some reason the insurance company denies payment for a procedure, the patient will be responsible for the payment in full. Forms of payment accepted include cash, check, credit card or Care Credit.

If you are unable to make an appointment, we ask for 24 hour notice to allow us to offer this appointment time to another patient. However, we understand that unforeseen things happen in life that may require an appointment to be unexpectedly missed. Continued broken confirmed appointments may result in an additional charge.
