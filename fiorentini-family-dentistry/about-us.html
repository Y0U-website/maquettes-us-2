Doctor Bios / Meet The Team

Meet Dr. Mario S. Fiorentini

Dr. Mario S. Fiorentini has been practicing dentistry in Central New Jersey since receiving his D.M.D degree from Farleigh Dickinson Dental School in 1982. He continued his dental education at JFK Medical Center where he completed a general practice residency in 1983. He later served as chief of the dental staff and remains involved with the hospital where he is currently active emeritus.

Dr. Fiorentini has recently been named a Top 10 dentist in New Jersey by the National Academy for Dental Professionals and NJ Top Doc by Healthy Living.

He achieved Mastership status with the Academy of General Dentistry, an award given to general dentists who have expanded their education studying 16 disciplines, such as periodontics, orthodontics, occlusion, and implants. Only about 1,000 general dentists in the entire country have achieved this Mastership status, meaning that Dr. Fiorentini is in the top 1 % of his profession.

Dr. Fiorentini is a member of the American Dental Association, New Jersey Dental Society, Middlesex County Dental Society, Academy of General Dentistry, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, American Academy of Sleep Medicine, American Academy of Implant Dentistry, Central Jersey Dental Arts Study Club, Jersey Shore Medical Center Study Club, and 3rd Millennium Dental Study Club.

Dr. Fiorentini has trained with some of the world’s most prominent dental specialists including completing the core curriculum at the Dawson Academy for Advanced Dental Studies. He is known as a leading cosmetic dentist in New Jersey for patients who desire comprehensive treatment by a caring professional who is committed to excellence.
