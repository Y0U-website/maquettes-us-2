Intraoral Cameras

Our intraoral cameras are a powerful tool that allows us to bring you inside your own mouth, offering a close-up view of your dental health like never before.

These small, high-definition cameras enable our experienced dentists to detect even the tiniest imperfections, making diagnosis and treatment planning more accurate and personalized. You can see exactly what we see, and we can work together to achieve your optimal oral health.

With our commitment to advanced technology, we ensure that your dental experience is not only effective but also educational. Intraoral cameras are just one of the ways we demonstrate our dedication to your well-being and your smile. Discover the difference at Fiorentini Family Dental.

Digital X-Rays

Unlike traditional film-based radiography, digital x-rays reduce radiation exposure by up to 90 %, ensuring your peace of mind while we gain invaluable insights into your oral health.

With digital x-rays, the process is faster and more efficient, producing high-definition images that can be instantly viewed on our computer screens. This means shorter waiting times and a more streamlined approach to your dental care. Moreover, these images can be easily stored and shared with specialists or insurance providers when needed, making your treatment journey smoother.

VELscope - Advanced Oral Cancer Screening

Our experienced team of dentists uses VELscope to provide you with an extra layer of protection during your regular dental check-ups. This technology uses a safe blue light to reveal any hidden issues that may not be visible to the naked eye. By detecting potential problems in their early stages, we can take proactive measures to ensure your continued oral health.

Your well-being is our top priority, and VELscope is just one example of how we stay at the forefront of dental technology to better serve you. Schedule your next appointment with us and experience the benefits of VELscope for yourself.
