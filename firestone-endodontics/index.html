The practice

Endodontic retreatment can be a necessary step for teeth that have not healed properly or have developed new issues after initial root canal treatment. This service aims to ensure long-term success and health of your tooth.

Endodontic microsurgery targets the very tip of the root to treat persistent infections. This precise approach helps preserve your natural teeth, get rid of any infection, and restore your oral health.

We understand dental emergencies can be stressful. We strive to provide same-day treatment to address severe pain and urgent dental issues swiftly and effectively.
