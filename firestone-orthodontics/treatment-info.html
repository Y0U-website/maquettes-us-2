INVISALIGN AND OTHER INVISIBLE OPTIONS

Dr. Jensen has over 20 years experience in treating patients with Invisalign. With Invisalign, you can get the beautiful straight teeth you’ve always wanted – without braces. A complimentary consultation can determine if Invisalign is right for you.

The Invisalign® System straightens your teeth without wires and brackets, using a series of clear, customized, removable aligners. It’s virtually invisible, which means you can straighten your teeth without announcing it to the world. Invisalign moves your teeth in small steps to the desired final position. Each of your aligners are precisely customized to fit your mouth at each stage of the treatment plan. You switch to a new set of aligners every couple of weeks and only remove them to eat, brush and floss. Before you know it you’ll be smiling like you never have before!

At your complimentary consultation , you can find out about Invisalign along with Dr. Jensen’s other virtually invisible options.
