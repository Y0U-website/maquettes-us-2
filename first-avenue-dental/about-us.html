The Office. The Technology. The Difference.

CBCT 3D Imaging

Cone-beam computed tomography delivers detailed three-dimensional views of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures — supporting precise diagnosis and treatment planning for implants, extractions, and complex cases.

Primescan Digital Scanner

Digital impressions without messy trays. Our Primescan captures highly accurate 3D models in minutes for faster, more comfortable crown, aligner, and restorative workflows.

In-House Crown Milling Unit (CAD/CAM)

Many crowns and restorations are designed and milled right here — often in a single visit — for fewer appointments, excellent fit, and natural-looking results.

3D Printer

On-site 3D printing supports surgical guides, models, and custom tools that improve planning accuracy and treatment efficiency from day one.

In-House Dental Lab

Our on-site lab gives us direct control over quality, timing, and customization — whether you need a same-day crown or a carefully matched restoration.

What to Expect at Your First Appointment

Your first visit at First Avenue Dental is deliberately thorough. We take the time to do it right from the start. You’ll begin with updated X-rays, followed by a comprehensive examination with Dr. Shaikh. He’ll review your dental and medical history, assess every tooth, evaluate your gum health, and take a full-mouth set of clinical records and photographs. Most first appointments run 60 to 90 minutes.

What To Bring

  • Insurance card
  • Photo ID
  • List of current medications
  • Recent dental X-rays from previous provider (if available)
