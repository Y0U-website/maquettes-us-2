Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Invisalign treatment usually take?

Invisalign treatment typically takes 12 to 18 months, but your case may be shorter or longer depending on how complex your alignment needs are. Some patients see results in just a few months. At First Class Dental in Jenkintown, we’ll give you a personalized timeline after your initial consultation.

Am I a good candidate for Invisalign treatment?

You may be a great candidate for Invisalign if you have mild to moderate issues like crowded teeth, spacing, or an overbite. It works well for teens and adults who want a discreet alternative to braces. We’ll evaluate your smile at our Jenkintown office to see if Invisalign suits you.

What should I expect at my first Invisalign appointment?

At your first Invisalign appointment, we’ll take digital scans and photos to map your treatment. You’ll see a preview of how your smile will shift over time. Our team at First Class Dental in Jenkintown will explain how Invisalign works and answer all your questions. The visit is comfortable, informative, and designed to help you feel confident moving forward.
