Meet Our Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Dorothy Theogene

Dr. Theogene graduated with honors from the University of Florida in Gainesville, FL as a double major earning a Bachelor of Science degrees in Food Science – Human Nutrition and Zoology. She then attended Howard University College of Dentistry in Washington, DC where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. After graduation, Dr. Theogene continued her training at Howard University College of Dentistry where she earned her Pediatric Dentistry Certificate.

When she’s not in the office treating patients, Dr. Theogene enjoys spending quality time with her family, participating in community outreach projects including international dental mission trips and traveling.Dr. Theogene is looking forward to meeting patients and their families, collaborating to help create a lifetime of healthy smiles
