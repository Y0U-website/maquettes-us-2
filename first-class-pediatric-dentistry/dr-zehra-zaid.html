Our Story

She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry, followed by a Master of Science in Pediatric Dentistry from the University of Michigan. Her research focused on Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF), a gentle, non-invasive treatment that helps stop cavities in baby teeth while keeping children comfortable and confident.

Dr. Zaid is Board Certified and a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. After several years practicing in Colorado, she’s proud to call the Commerce City area home — just minutes from Denver International Airport. Inspired by her husband, a United Airlines captain, Dr. Zaid brings the same first-class standards of care, precision, and calm to her practice that you’d expect in the skies.

As the owner of First Class Pediatric Dentistry, she’s built her dream: a warm, welcoming space where kids enjoy coming to the dentist and families feel supported every step of the way. Her philosophy is simple: every child deserves a smile on the surface that matches the beauty of a smile within.

Outside the office, you’ll find Dr. Zaid with her husband Jeff and their huskies, Kodiak and Miska. She’s a certified yoga instructor, a five-time half-marathon finisher, and an avid traveler, hiker, and thrifter.
