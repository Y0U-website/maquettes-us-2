Dental Implants

  • Improved comfort while eating and speaking.
  • Long-lasting durability with proper care.
  • Preservation of healthy jawbone.
  • No need to alter neighboring teeth.
  • Support for long-term oral health.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Many healthy adults are good candidates for dental implants. The ideal candidate has healthy gums, adequate jawbone to support the implant, and a commitment to maintaining good oral hygiene.

If you’ve experienced bone loss or have other oral health concerns, that doesn’t necessarily mean implants aren’t possible. During your consultation, we’ll perform a comprehensive evaluation and discuss the treatment options that best fit your individual needs.

What to Expect

Every implant treatment plan is personalized, but the process typically begins with a comprehensive consultation and diagnostic imaging. We’ll evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and create a treatment plan that’s tailored to your smile.

After the implant is placed, the jawbone gradually bonds with the titanium post through a natural healing process called osseointegration. Once healing is complete, a custom restoration is attached to the implant, completing your new smile.

Implants vs. Other Tooth Replacement Options

Several treatments can replace missing teeth, and the right option depends on your oral health, goals, and budget.

Dental implants are often recommended because they replace both the visible tooth and its root, helping preserve the jawbone and providing excellent long-term stability.

In some situations, a dental bridge or denture may be a more appropriate solution. During your consultation, we’ll explain the advantages of each option so you can make an informed decision.

Caring for Your Dental Implant

Dental implants should be cared for much like natural teeth. Brushing, flossing, and maintaining regular dental exams and cleanings help protect the health of your implant and surrounding gums.

With proper care and routine dental visits, implants can provide reliable function and beautiful aesthetics for many years.
