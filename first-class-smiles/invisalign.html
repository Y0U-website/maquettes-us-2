Why Choose Invisalign?

Many patients choose Invisalign because it offers an effective way to straighten teeth while fitting comfortably into everyday life.

  • Nearly invisible aligners.
  • Comfortable, smooth plastic construction.
  • Removable for eating, brushing, and flossing.
  • No dietary restrictions.
  • Fewer office visits than many traditional orthodontic treatments.
  • Improved smile appearance and bite alignment.

What to Expect During Treatment

Your Invisalign journey begins with a comprehensive consultation. We’ll examine your teeth, discuss your goals, and determine whether clear aligners are the right solution for your smile.

If Invisalign is recommended, we’ll take precise digital scans of your teeth and develop a personalized treatment plan. You’ll wear each set of aligners for the recommended amount of time before progressing to the next series, gradually moving your teeth into alignment.

We’ll schedule periodic checkups throughout treatment to monitor your progress and make sure everything is moving as planned.

How Long Does Invisalign Take?

Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of your case, but many patients complete Invisalign treatment within 12 to 18 months. Some minor corrections may take less time, while more complex cases may require a longer treatment plan.

Wearing your aligners as directed—typically 20 to 22 hours each day—is one of the most important factors in achieving successful results on schedule.

Is Invisalign Right for You?

Invisalign is an excellent option for many teens and adults who want to improve their smile without the appearance of traditional braces. However, every smile is unique, and some orthodontic concerns may require a different approach.

During your consultation, we’ll explain your options, answer your questions, and recommend the treatment that best supports your oral health and long-term goals.

Caring for Your Smile During Treatment

Because Invisalign aligners are removable, maintaining good oral hygiene is simple. Brush and floss your teeth before reinserting your aligners, and clean the aligners regularly according to our instructions.

Continuing regular dental exams and cleanings throughout treatment also helps keep your teeth and gums healthy as your smile changes.
