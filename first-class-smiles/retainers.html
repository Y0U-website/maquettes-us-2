Orthodontic Retainers

Schedule an appointment promptly if you notice any of these changes. Waiting too long may allow additional tooth movement.

  • It has cracked, warped, or broken.
  • The wire has bent or detached.
  • It causes new discomfort or irritation.
  • Your teeth appear to be shifting.
  • You lost your retainer.

How Should You Care for Your Retainer?

Proper care helps your retainer stay clean, comfortable, and effective. Follow the instructions we provide for your specific appliance.

Patients with fixed retainers should brush carefully around the wire and use the recommended flossing tools. Regular dental exams and cleanings also help us monitor the retainer and surrounding teeth.

  • Rinse the retainer after removing it.
  • Clean it with a soft brush and recommended cleanser.
  • Avoid hot water, which may warp the material.
  • Store it in its protective case when not in use.
  • Keep it away from pets, napkins, and direct heat.

Retainers After Braces or Invisalign

Retention plays an important role after any orthodontic treatment. Whether you completed traditional braces , ceramic braces , or Invisalign® clear aligners , your teeth can still shift without proper support.

We’ll create a retention plan based on your treatment and show you how to protect your new smile for years to come.
