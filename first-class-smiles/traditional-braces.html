Why Choose Traditional Braces?

Braces have helped millions of patients achieve healthier, more functional smiles. Because they remain attached throughout treatment, they work continuously without relying on patient compliance to achieve results.

  • Effective treatment for both simple and complex cases.
  • Continuous tooth movement throughout treatment.
  • Predictable, precise results.
  • Suitable for children, teens, and adults.
  • No need to remember to wear aligners.

What to Expect During Treatment

Your orthodontic journey begins with a comprehensive consultation. We’ll examine your teeth and bite, discuss your goals, and determine whether braces are the right treatment for your smile.

Once your braces are placed, you’ll visit us periodically so we can adjust the archwire, monitor your progress, and guide your teeth into their ideal positions. Regular visits allow us to ensure treatment stays on track and your smile continues progressing as planned.

How Long Do Braces Take?

Treatment time varies based on the complexity of your orthodontic needs. Many patients wear braces for approximately 18 to 24 months, although some cases may require less or more time.

We’ll provide a personalized estimate during your consultation and explain the factors that influence your treatment timeline.

Living with Braces

Adjusting to braces typically takes only a short time. You may experience mild soreness after placement or adjustments, but this usually improves within a few days.

We’ll also explain how to care for your braces, including foods to avoid and techniques for brushing and flossing around brackets and wires. Good oral hygiene throughout treatment helps keep your teeth and gums healthy while your smile improves.

Braces vs. Invisalign

Both braces and Invisalign can create beautiful, healthy smiles, but each offers unique advantages.

Traditional braces often provide the greatest flexibility for treating complex orthodontic concerns because they remain attached throughout treatment. Invisalign offers a removable, nearly invisible option that works well for many patients with mild to moderate alignment issues.

During your consultation, we’ll help you compare both treatments and recommend the option that’s best suited to your smile, lifestyle, and treatment goals.
