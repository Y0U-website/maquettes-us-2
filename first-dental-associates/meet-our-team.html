Our Providers

Nasrin Sadeghi, DMD

Nasrin Sadeghi, DMD, is a general and cosmetic dentist and the founder of First Dental Associates in Reading, Massachusetts. Before starting her practice, Dr. Sadeghi was an associate dentist in the Boston area for over 10 years.

Dr. Sadeghi started her practice in order to provide quality and compassionate dental care to her patients. She has a passion for improving her patients’ smile and confidence through exceptional dental services.

She attended Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine and graduated in 1996. Following dental school, she went on to hone her skills by working with some of the top dental professionals in Massachusetts. Throughout her career, she has always pursued further education and participated in several international conferences for implantology. She also has specialized training in Snap-On Smile®, dental veneers, dental implants, and Invisalign®.

Dr. Sadeghi remains abreast of the latest developments in modern dentistry by regularly attending dental seminars and study clubs. She is currently a member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry. Dr.Sadeghi is the current board member and the past chair of the East Middlesex District of Massachusetts Dental Society.

She strives to stay active in the dental community by supporting local efforts and national charities, such as the Oral Cancer Foundation, Give Back a Smile, and the School on Wheels.

Outside of improving smile care in her community, Dr. Sadeghi enjoys spending time with her daughter and husband, traveling, and reading.

Medha Singh, DMD, BDS, MS

Dr. Singh earned her bachelor’s degree in dental surgery from the Government Dental College and Research Institute in India. She then relocated to Massachusetts, where she attended Tufts University School of Dental Medicine for her master’s degree, doctorate in dental medicine, and a certificate in advanced periodontology. Following her studies, she was honored to be invited to a fellowship in oral implantology at Harvard School of Dental Medicine.

After completing her training, she continued as an assistant professor at Tufts University and a part-time faculty member in periodontology at Harvard University. During her time as a professor, she completed funded research for the National Institutes of Health (NIH). Dr. Singh has lectured and authored several publications on Sjögren’s syndrome and salivary hypofunction.

Dr. Singh is a reviewer of the Journal of Dental Education and the Journal of Periodontology . She is also a member of the American Academy of Oral Medicine, American College of Dentists, International College of Dentists, Pierre Fauchard Academy, Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society, Sigma Xi Scientific Research Honor Society, and the American Academy of Periodontology Foundation.

In addition to her many accomplishments, Dr. Singh is very active in her local community. She dedicates her free time to educating and serving underprivileged communities in the Boston area as well as in India. She is also a compassionate mentor to students seeking a career in dentistry.

She graduated in 1997 and has been working in private practice and in Research for years. In her free time, Julie enjoys hiking, stand-up paddle boarding and yoga. She lives near the beach with her 2 dogs, Teddy and Daisy. She also teaches CPR and is a clerk for state EMT exams on the weekends.
