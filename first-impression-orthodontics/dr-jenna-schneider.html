Care Built on Confidence & Connection

Dr. Schneider believes orthodontic care should feel empowering, approachable, and personal from the very first visit. She takes time to educate patients, invite questions, and involve them in their treatment so the process feels collaborative, not intimidating.

Known for her warm energy and calming presence, Dr. Schneider is especially passionate about helping patients who arrive feeling nervous or unsure regain confidence along the way. She strives to make orthodontics feel fun and encouraging, while always delivering thoughtful, precise care.

For Dr. Schneider, the most meaningful moments happen at the end of treatment, when patients see their new smile for the first time and realize just how confident they’ve become.
