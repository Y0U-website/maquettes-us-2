Continuing Education

Dr. Lu has been a contributing author in multiple medical publications. He’s had the privilege to present orthodontic topics in national and state orthodontic meetings as well as presenting abroad in Italy.

Committed to providing the most up-to-date treatment experience possible, Dr. Lu is highly-trained in the latest orthodontic techniques and treatments for kids, teens and adults. He has extensive experience with complex cases requiring orthognathic jaw surgery as a former member of the VCU Craniofacial/Facial Reconstruction Team.

Today, Dr. Lu serves as the Immediate Past President of the Virginia Association of Orthodontists (VAO.) The VAO is a non-profit organization of over 270 active and retired orthodontic specialists who have been advancing the science of orthodontics since 1961.
