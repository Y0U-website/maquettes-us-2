A dental implant consultation is the first step in exploring a permanent solution for missing teeth. During this appointment, we take time to evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and explain how dental implants can restore both the function and appearance of your smile.

What Is a Dental Implant Consultation?

Dentist Poquoson

A dental implant consultation is a detailed appointment where we gather the information needed to determine if implants are the right treatment for you. We begin by reviewing your medical and dental history, followed by a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, and jawbone. Digital X-rays or 3D imaging may be taken to evaluate bone density and ensure there is enough support for an implant.

This consultation is also your opportunity to ask questions and learn about the process in detail. We explain how implants are placed, what recovery looks like, and the benefits they provide compared to other restorative options. If implants are appropriate, we will create a customized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs. By the end of your consultation, you will have the knowledge and confidence to decide whether dental implants are the right solution for restoring your smile.
