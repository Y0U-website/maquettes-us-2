Single‑Tooth Implant

Dentist Poquoson

A single tooth implant replaces a missing tooth with a titanium post and natural-looking crown. This solution restores chewing ability, protects surrounding teeth, and prevents bone loss. Multiple Teeth Implants

Dentist Poquoson

When several teeth are missing, implants provide a stable, long-term solution. Implant-supported bridges or crowns restore full function and appearance without relying on neighboring teeth. Implant-supported dentures provide superior stability compared to traditional options. Anchored by dental implants, these dentures eliminate slipping, clicking, and discomfort.
