What Are Multiple Teeth Implants?

Dentist Poquoson

Multiple teeth implants are a restorative treatment used to replace two or more missing teeth. Titanium posts are placed into the jawbone, where they integrate naturally and act as artificial roots. These implants then support custom restorations such as bridges or partial dentures, depending on your specific needs.

Unlike traditional options, multiple implants do not rely on adjacent teeth for support, which means nearby natural teeth remain untouched and healthy. They also help preserve the jawbone by providing necessary stimulation that prevents bone loss. Each restoration is designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, providing strength, comfort, and aesthetics. With proper care, multiple implants offer a long-term solution for replacing missing teeth, restoring your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence.
