What Is a Single‑Tooth Implant?

Dentist Poquoson

A single-tooth implant is a dental procedure used to replace one missing tooth with a titanium implant that acts as an artificial root. Once placed in the jawbone, the implant fuses naturally through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation for a custom-made crown.

This crown is carefully crafted to match the color, size, and shape of your surrounding teeth, ensuring a seamless result that looks and feels natural. A single-tooth implant functions like your original tooth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile without concern. Unlike traditional bridges, it does not require altering nearby healthy teeth for support. By preserving bone health and providing long-term durability, a single-tooth implant is one of the most reliable solutions for tooth replacement.
