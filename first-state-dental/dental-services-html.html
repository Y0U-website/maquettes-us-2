Cosmetic Dentistry

A beautiful smile, confidence, and charisma. Cosmetic dentistry can improve your smile, self-esteem, and social opportunities.

We offer a range of options, from re-contouring the teeth and gums to placing veneers on several teeth.

  • Bonding and Veneering - repair of chipped front teeth. The resurfacing of the fronts of stained, damaged, and misaligned teeth.
  • Crowns for Dental Restoration - we offer options on the best types of dental crowns that meet your needs and finances.
  • Dental Whitening - in-office and at-home whitening options.
  • Invisalign - orthodontic correction restores the form and function of a number of dental bite conditions.
  • Porcelain Veneer - a strong, durable veneer that covers the horizontal surface of the tooth to remove visible imperfections. Porcelain veneers can provide a movie star-like smile.
  • Smile Makeover - Your smile is defined by its color, shape, size, and alignment. We can provide you with the smile of your dreams.
  • Tooth-colored Fillings - white fillings, on-lays, and in-lays for natural-looking restorations.

Restoration & Endodontic Dentistry

  • Dental Bridge - a partial denture that replaces one or more missing teeth between the remaining teeth in an arch.
  • Dental Crowns - we offer options on the best types of dental crowns that meet your needs and finances.
  • Dentures - Custom Designed - we can match the exact shape, color, and alignment of your existing teeth or work with you to create the look you want. Additionally, we will work with you to give you the type of denture that is most comfortable and appropriate for you.
  • Dental Implant - a permanent artificial tooth anchor that replaces the root of the lost tooth with a choice of dental crown options.
  • Full Mouth Reconstruction - is comprehensive care dentistry for the patient whose smile is so broken down that the entirety of one or both arches must be fully restored.

Periodontal Dentistry

Healthy gums are essential for a beautiful smile and self-esteem. We offer several treatments and therapies based on the stage of advancement of the periodontal disease.

Periodontal Dentistry is focused on the health of the gums.

  • Periodontics - Dental scaling and root planing - a non-surgical procedure in which the hygienist removes plaque and tartar from below the gum line. Root surfaces are cleaned and smoothed with specially designed instruments. It is important to remove the plaque and tartar from the pockets. Bacterial toxins irritate the gums, and plaque and the rough surfaces of tartar serve as breeding grounds for bacteria.
