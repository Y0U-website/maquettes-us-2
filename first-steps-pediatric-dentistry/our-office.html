Our Office

Our Office We are proud to welcome you and your entire family to First Steps Pediatric Dentistry. Our staff is dedicated to providing you with an experience that exceeds your expectations at every visit.

We prioritize patient education and involvement as core aspects of our treatment plans.

We don’t stop at treating symptoms but focus on finding the underlying causes and restoring health and quality of life for your child. This includes accessible and compassionate care, along with providing parents with a streamlined process from start to finish.

A child’s positive experiences at the dentist can make an impact that last a lifetime.

Our team consists of people who are experts at every aspect of the field of pediatric dentistry. From checking in at the front desk, through your checkup and then scheduling your next visit, we try to make every aspect of your visit, a friendly and high-quality experience.

Our expert pediatric dentists are fully equipped to treat children of any age or with special healthcare and behavioral needs. We want you and your child to have the knowledge and tools necessary to ensure a lifetime of healthy teeth.

We understand finances & insurance can be very complicated and confusing.

Dental insurance can vary greatly from employer to employer. Our team has the knowledge, training & dedication to helping you navigate through the financial and insurance part of your dental experience. When you have questions, we’ll be here to help!

Our office accepts most traditional insurance plans. As a courtesy, we are happy to assist you in fully maximizing the use of your insurance benefits. Payment is due at the time of services rendered. We accept cash and most major credit cards.
