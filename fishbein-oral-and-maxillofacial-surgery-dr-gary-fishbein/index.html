The third molars, which are commonly called wisdom teeth, are typically the last teeth to erupt. Because of this, there is often not enough room for the wisdom teeth to grow in as they should. When there is not sufficient space for the wisdom teeth, they may become impacted (unable to fully break through the gum tissues and jawbone) or grow in at an incorrect angle.
