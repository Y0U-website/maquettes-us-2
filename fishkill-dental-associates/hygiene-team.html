Dental Hygienists

Carlos

When it comes to scheduling, Carlos emphasizes the importance of individualized care, with time allotted to each patient based on their specific needs. He understands that every patient is different, and he tailors his approach to ensure that everyone receives the attention and care they deserve.

An interesting fact about Carlos is that he made history in his academic career by becoming the first male class president at his school, a role that highlights his leadership and dedication. This achievement is a testament to his commitment to excellence, both in his studies and in his professional life.

​Leslie

Leslie is a skilled and dedicated dental hygienist who graduated from Orange County Community College. Leslie’s passion for dentistry is driven by the satisfaction of helping patients see tangible results from their care. She takes pride in guiding her patients through their treatment journeys and witnessing the positive changes in their oral health.

Introduced to new patients simply as Leslie, she brings a friendly and professional demeanor to her role. Leslie values the importance of allocating adequate time for each appointment, ensuring that every patient receives the thorough and attentive care they deserve. She believes that a well-planned schedule is crucial for delivering high-quality care and addressing each patient’s unique needs.

Leslie’s commitment to her patients is evident in her focus on achieving clear, positive outcomes for them. Her approach is centered around understanding patient goals and working collaboratively to achieve the best possible results.

Bruni

Registered Dental Hygienist of Fishkill Dental Associates

Started in dentistry in 1977 as a surgical periodontal assistant. Attended Hostos Community College in 1988 and graduated in 1991, and have been a hygienist ever since. 1992-1994, contributing oral health care column for the Bronx Times. Certified for laser use in 2010.
