The practice

Welcome to Fishkill Dental Associates – where patient comfort meets advanced care. Our team is committed to providing a welcoming environment that puts you at ease from the moment you arrive.

We combine a friendly, compassionate approach with the latest dental technology—including CEREC® same-day crowns, Invisalign®, and dental implants—to ensure treatment that is precise, efficient, and tailored to your needs. At Fishkill Dental Associates, you can expect modern dentistry delivered with a personal touch.

Our talented duo of dentists, Dr. Kay and Dr. Faber, focus on building long-lasting relationships with patients and making every visit feel like catching up with old friends. With a passion for dentistry and a commitment to restoring smiles, they are the heart of our warm dental practice!

Dr. Jeffrey Kay grew up in Bergen County, New Jersey. He received his degree from The University of Maryland School of Dentistry. Following graduation, he completed a one year General Dentistry residency program in advanced education at Newark Beth Israel Medical Center in New Jersey.
