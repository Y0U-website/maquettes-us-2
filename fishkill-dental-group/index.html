A dental crown is one of the most common treatments for teeth with extensive decay or damage. They can also hold a dental bridge in place, cover misshapen or discolored teeth. Here at Fishkill Dental Group, we have decades of experience in placing dental crowns for patients of all ages.

Crowns are made of either porcelain-fused-to-metal, ceramic or gold. It takes a multi-step process to place your crown. First, a dental impression is made. Then a temporary crown is placed to protect the tooth while the impression is created in an off-site laboratory. In some instances, same-day crowns can be created. With proper care, crowns can last up to 15 years.
