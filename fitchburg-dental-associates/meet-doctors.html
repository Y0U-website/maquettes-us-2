Dr. Sunit Jain

General Dentist

Dr. Sunit Jain graduated with a Bachelor of Dental Surgery from LBDS College, India, in 2012, and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry in 2018. He has been practicing dentistry since graduating in India and, over the past 12 years, has performed numerous dental procedures, including root canals, implants, crowns, bridges, extractions, and dentures.

Dr. Jain is passionate about his work and stays up to date with the latest developments in the dental field. He began practicing in Western Massachusetts in 2019 and has been serving the community ever since. Patients value him for his skill, dedication, and the consistent comfort he brings to their treatments.
