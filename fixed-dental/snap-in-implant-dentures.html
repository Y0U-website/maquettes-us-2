What is the snap in denture process?

  • Consultation and 3D Scan – One highly trained doctor will examine your mouth, medical history, and review your 3D x-ray with you. We’ll get to know you and present options custom treatment options with you. Any questions you have will be answered.
  • Procedure – To ensure the best experience and maximum comfort, we offer IV sedation for all dental implant procedures. Any remaining teeth are removed and dental implants are placed in the jawbone. The implants will be allowed to heal to your jawbone under the gum and will not be visible until healing is complete over 6 months (osseointegration). Immediate dentures will be adjusted and placed in your mouth before you leave so you don’t go without teeth.
  • Post op – The next day, your doctor will examine you and answer any questions you may have. Adjustments will be made to your immediate dentures to make them as comfortable as possible.
  • Soft Liners – Many changes to your jawbone and gums will happen over the next few weeks and coming months. Soft liners are placed inside the denture to account for these changes and provide as snug of a fit as possible as these changes happen. Soft liners are changed periodically throughout the healing process. The first soft liner is typically placed 2 weeks after surgery to allow for initial healing to take place and any sutures to come out.
  • Uncovery – After the healing phase is complete, we expose the implants that were healing under your gums. We attach the snap component on top of the implant and allow the gum to heal for 2 weeks to allow for any changes that might occur before attachment.
  • Attachment – We perform a final fitting of your denture and attach the denture to the implants.
