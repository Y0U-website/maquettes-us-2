Immediate Denture Process

For patients who still have teeth, dentures replace all of the teeth in one or both jaws during healing from tooth extractions.

  • It All Starts With A Consultation – One of our highly trained dentists will examine your mouth, review your medical history, and discuss your treatment options with you. We will answer any questions you might have and customize a treatment plan just for you.
  • Impressions – Molds and other records of your mouth will be taken so that we can begin customizing a new smile just for you!
  • In House Dental Laboratory – Your dentist and our Lab Technician will work together to design your new smile so that it not only looks great but functions well. This is completed in our in-office dental lab so that your teeth never have to leave our office. In many dental offices, dentures are made at third party dental labs which can lead to greatly increased treatment times and costs.
  • Day of the Procedure – After any remaining teeth are removed, your immediate dentures will be inserted and adjusted to provide the best fit possible at that time. Your gums will undergo many changes particularly over the next few weeks and we will make adjustments along the way to provide improved comfort.
  • Post op – Your dentist will see you again, typically the day after your procedure to examine your mouth and make any needed adjustments. Any questions you might have will be answered.
  • Fixed Comfort Package – Your gums and jawbone will undergo changes after tooth extractions. Most of these changes happen over the first 6 months but particularly over the first two weeks as the initial gum healing takes place. A series of soft liners will be placed in your dentures as you heal to provide optimum comfort and fit as these changes take place. The first soft liner is placed 2 weeks after tooth extraction to allow any gum swelling to subside and any dissolvable sutures to come out.

Advantages of Dentures
