Benefits of Ramus Frame Implant Systems Tooth Replacement

  • People with bone loss who may not qualify for some traditional implant options
  • Patients who want an alternative to removable dentures that stays more securely in place
  • People who want better comfort while eating, speaking, and smiling

The Advantages You Should Know About

There are some pretty obvious benefits to replacing missing teeth. First, you’re less likely to experience the shifting that tends to happen with your remaining teeth. And of course, once your missing teeth have been replaced, you won’t be able to help but smile.

But those aren’t the only benefits to full-arch tooth replacement with Ramus Frame implants. Here’s what you can expect.

  • Strong support for a full arch of replacement teeth that stay firmly in place during daily activities
  • Less slipping and shifting compared to traditional removable dentures
  • More comfort while chewing foods like meat, vegetables, and crunchy snacks
  • Clearer speech without worrying about dentures moving while talking
  • A natural-looking smile designed to look more like real teeth and gums
  • Better day-to-day comfort since there’s no need for denture adhesive creams or pastes

What the Treatment Process Looks Like

As you can see, the list of benefits is pretty impressive. But what does the process look like? It all starts with a consultation. Dr. Flanagan will examine your mouth, discuss your goals, and take detailed imaging of your teeth and jaw. This helps determine whether the Ramus system is the right fit for your smile and overall oral health.

Next comes treatment planning and implant placement. We will carefully place the implants that support the frame and your future replacement teeth. During the healing phase, the implants bond with the jawbone over time. This is a process called osseointegration.

Once healing is complete, your final restoration is attached. Patients looking for dental implants in Mystic, CT, often love how this type of tooth repair or replacement can completely transform their smile and improve their comfort. The way we see it, it’s a winning solution.

How the Ramus System Compares to Traditional Dentures

We won’t knock dentures, as they provide an excellent tooth-replacement option for many patients. But dentures do come with some potential downsides, the most common of which include slippage and discomfort.

That’s one reason many patients become interested in implant-supported options like the Ramus system. Because the restoration is attached to implants, it stays in place more securely while eating and speaking. Many patients also like not having to remove their teeth at night or deal with denture adhesives throughout the day. For people who want a more stable feel, the Ramus system can be a very appealing alternative.
