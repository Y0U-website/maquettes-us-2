Services

Comprehensive Dental Solutions

We offer comprehensive dental care for the whole family — from checkups to advanced cosmetic and restorative procedures.

Preventive & Wellness Care

Routine cleanings, exams, digital x-rays, oral cancer screenings, wellness scans, fluoride treatments, sealants, night guards, sleep apnea appliances, and custom athletic mouthguards.

Periodontal Health

Deep cleanings (scaling and root planing) and ongoing periodontal maintenance to support gum health.

Restorative Dentistry

Tooth-colored fillings, crowns, bridges, implants, full and partial dentures, and flippers — designed to restore function and confidence.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, and gum contouring to enhance your natural smile.

Orthodontics

Invisalign® clear aligners, retainers, and smile-straightening solutions for all ages.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle care for children, including exams, cleanings, and preventive treatments.

Urgent & Specialized Care

Same-day emergency appointments, extractions, and root canals when you need them most.
