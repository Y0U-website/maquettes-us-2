Our Legacy of Excellence

Dr. Richard Reddy retired from our practice on August 29th, 2019. Dr. Reddy founded Fleetwood Dental 43 years ago with the idea that good dentistry isn’t good enough. He wanted to deliver exceptional dental care to his patients and the Fleetwood community.

After 43 years of dedication to our patients, the practice, and the Fleetwood community, Dr. Reddy has moved on to the next phase of his life. As Dr. Reddy’s favorite quote states, If I have seen further than others, it is by standing upon the shoulders of giants. We are fortunate enough to stand on Dr. Reddy’s shoulders, continuing his legacy of excellent dental care for years to come.

On behalf of the entire Fleetwood Dental Team, we would like to thank Dr. Reddy for sharing our mission in delivering relationship patient-centered dentistry and helping patients to achieve optimal dental health and wellness.
