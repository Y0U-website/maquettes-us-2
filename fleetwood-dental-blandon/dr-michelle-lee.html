About Dr. Lee

Dr. Michelle Lee is very proud to provide all aspects of comprehensive dentistry which includes Restorative, Cosmetic, TMD, and Sleep Apnea treatment to the Fleetwood and Berks county areas. She is passionate about providing excellent patient centered, comprehensive dentistry to her patients, and strives to provide gentle and customized dental care. She is committed to relationship based and patient centered care. Dr. Lee is a graduate of the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine with a one year post graduate General Practice Residency Program at the Abington Memorial Hospital.

Dr. Lee continues to be a life long learner herself and constantly keeps herself abreast of dental advancements by committing to hundreds of hours of advanced dental education. She is an active member of the Pankey Institute , American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (AACD) , and American Equilibration Society (AES) , and other courses for advanced education.

Dr. Lee has advanced knowledge and training in the TMJ, Disorders of the TMJ (TMD), bite disorders, and Sleep Apnea. She continues to expand her knowledge in this field because she feels passionate about Dentistry based on the relationship of the Jaw Joint, Muscles, and Occlusion (teeth). Dr. Lee provides preventative, cosmetic and implant dentistry, TMJ therapy. She approaches dentistry with precision, accuracy, and artistic perspective.

Professionally, Dr. Lee is also a member of the Academy of General Dentistry , American Dental Association , Pennsylvania Dental Association , and serves on a committee of the American Equilibration Society. She also volunteers to treat pediatric patients through her local dental society.

Dr. Lee strives to have mindfulness in her life and loves being a mother and wife to her husband, MinSuk and her two children, Victoria and Nathan. She truly enjoys hiking and reading, and most of all, spending time with her family.

About Dr. Rechlicz

Dr. Rechlicz is a native of North Eastern Pennsylvania, originally from Montrose Pennsylvania. He earned a Bachelor of Science degree from The University of Scranton in Biology before continuing his education and receiving his Doctorate in Dental Medicine from Temple University.

Upon graduation from dental school, Dr. Rechlicz completed a year long general practice residency program at Lehigh Valley Health Network in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania. There he received advanced training in all aspects of dentistry including dental implants, oral surgery, periodontics, cosmetic, prosthodontic, pediatric, endodontic, and emergency dentistry.

During his dental training Dr. Rechlicz had the opportunity to provide dental care for ten days to a parish in Jamaica through the Great Shapes Foundation.

More locally, Dr. Rechlicz has been involved in the Mission of Mercy in Pennsylvania (MOM-N-PA) dental mission, which provided dental care to those in need in Reading in 2019, and Wilkes-Barre in 2020. He is also involved in several dental societies including the American Dental Association , Pennsylvania Dental Association , and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry .

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Rechlicz enjoys spending time with his fiancé, Samantha, and their rescue dog, Reese. He enjoys all things outdoors including running, swimming, hiking, biking, surfing, and skiing. Most recently he has been training to compete in his first triathlon.
