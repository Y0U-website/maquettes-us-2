The practice

The families of Mount Vernon, NY, deserve a dentist who cares about their oral health, general wellness, and overall comfort. Ariel Stein, DMD, has been that kind of dentist throughout his decades-long career. He puts his patients first.

At Fleetwood Dental, we strive to improve the health of the teeth, gums, jaw, and all structures of the mouth. Our team focuses on preventative dentistry, sound solutions, and patient education.

Dr. Stein has been a practicing dentist for more than 28 years. For the last 19 years, he has proudly provided superior dental care to families in Mount Vernon.

The team at Fleetwood Dental uses state-of-the-art equipment and techniques, and ensures every patient feels comfortable and welcome.

Dr. Stein has taken many continuing education classes, ensuring that his patients receive the most comprehensive and advanced care available.

Dr. Ariel Stein graduated from Boston University School of Graduate Dentistry in 1990. After Dental school, he attended a residency in Advanced Education In General Dentistry . Prior to studying at Dental School, he attended Stony Brook University. Dr. Stein has been practicing for over 25 years.

He has been in Fleetwood for 11years. Since he began practicing dentistry, Dr. Stein has kept up to date on many continuing educational classes, ensuring that his patients receive the most comprehensive and advanced dental care.
