Dental Implants Broomfield

  • Most implants will last you for the rest of your life. Good oral hygiene habits and regular dental visits will help.
  • Dentures and partials may slip and slide, causing you embarrassment. With implants, most people will have no idea that you have had dental restoration done.
  • Dentures may slide while you are eating, and certain foods can be troublesome especially those that are sticky. This problem is eliminated with implants.
  • Dental implants only affect the immediate tooth. Bridges require alteration of the surrounding teeth to provide crown-topped anchors to hold the bridge in place.

The Dental Implant Process

We are happy to meet with you for an initial consultation to discuss whether dental implants are right for you. At this appointment, we will perform a thorough dental exam and also take x-rays. We need to get a good idea of your current oral health and the condition of your bone.

The next step will be to visit an oral surgeon, who will fuse the titanium base to your jaw. Once the post is placed, you will need to heal. This process may take six to eight weeks. Once you have healed, you will return to our office. At that time we will take impressions to send to the lab, which will then create your new tooth. When your tooth is ready, we will attach it to the post.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who will be placing my dental implants?

We refer our dental implant patients to an excellent surgeon. The surgeon will place the posts that are fused to your bone and hold the implant. Once the posts are placed and healed, you will return to Fleischmann Family Dentistry where we will attach the rest of the restoration.
