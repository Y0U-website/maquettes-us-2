How Much Do Implants Cost?

One of dental patients’ most significant concerns before getting implants is how much they will cost. While they may love the strength and reliability of implants, they may worry about whether getting implants is something they can reasonably afford. But the answer to how much dental implants cost will vary from patient to patient.

Dental implants can cost $2,000 to $4,000 per tooth. If you’re only missing a few teeth, this may mean you’ll spend a few thousand dollars to restore the appearance of your smile. However, if you are trying to replace an entire mouth full of teeth, this can cost far more. Some insurance plans will help patients cover this treatment.

If you want to gain a better idea of how much it will cost to get implants, it is important that you start by contacting your dental insurance company. They will help you understand whether your current plan covers this treatment. From there, reach out to our dental team to schedule an essential examination.

During this exam, they’ll determine whether you’re a good candidate for implants and what additional care you may need before getting them. While dental implants can be costly, they are the most reliable, natural looking replacement on the market.
