Program Exclusions & Limitations

Because this is a discount program and not a traditional insurance plan, there are a few standard limitations:

  • Enrollment is available after a comprehensive initial exam.
  • This plan cannot be combined with other insurance, discount offers, CareCredit, or Lending Club.
  • It does not cover services from outside specialists or treatments related to workman’s compensation.
  • Costs for medications or specific dental products are not included in the membership fee.
