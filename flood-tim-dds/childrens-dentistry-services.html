Early Interceptive Orthodontics

"Early Interceptive Orthodontics" is a treatment philosophy in which treatment is provided in stages. Staging the treatment steps can address orthodontic problems as they develop.

The American Academy of Orthodontics recommends that children should have their first orthodontic evaluation by age seven.

This treatment philosophy started years ago. It was seen that all permanent teeth in children erupted around ages 12 to 13. Permanent teeth had to be often extracted as a result. Modern orthodontic methods, however, strive to avoid removing permanent teeth when possible.

This is accomplished by starting treatment early and taking advantage of the child’s facial development. Studies indicate that the long-term results of this "phased treatment" are far more stable. We have several amazing orthodontists in our area whom Dr. Flood refers to.

  • What Is Kids Dentistry, and Why Is It Important? Kids dentistry focuses on the unique dental needs of infants, children, and teens. At the Pediatric Dentistry of Onalaska, we specialize in providing preventive care, education, and treatment tailored to your child’s growing smile. Starting early can help avoid long-term dental issues and set healthy habits that last a lifetime.
  • When Should I Schedule My Child’s First Dental Visit? We recommend bringing your child in for their first dental checkup by their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth appearing. At Pediatric Dentistry of Onalaska, our complimentary baby checks help track your child’s dental development early on and support healthy oral habits from the beginning.
  • How Do You Help Kids Learn Proper Oral Hygiene? Teaching good dental habits is a significant part of what we do. During visits, our team demonstrates proper brushing and flossing techniques and discusses how certain foods can affect your child’s teeth. We believe kids’ dentistry should empower young patients with knowledge and confidence to care for their own smiles.
  • What Treatments Do You Offer for Cavity Prevention? We use fluoride and sealants to help protect your child’s teeth from cavities.
