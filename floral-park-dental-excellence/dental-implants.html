Dental Implants in Floral Park, NY

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth, consisting of a small titanium post placed in the jawbone and a custom restoration placed on top. While the implant itself is placed by a specialist, the restoration is what you see and use every day, such as a crown, bridge, or denture. At Floral Park Dental Excellence, we focus on the prosthetic portion of treatment, carefully designing and restoring your implant for optimal fit, function, and appearance. Our goal is to ensure your final result looks natural and feels comfortable.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implant restorations offer several advantages for patients looking to replace missing teeth.

Learn More about the Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Natural Look and Feel. Implant restorations are custom-designed to match your natural teeth, providing a seamless and realistic appearance.
  • Improved Stability. Unlike traditional removable options, implant-supported restorations are securely anchored, allowing for confident eating and speaking.
  • Long-Term Durability. With proper care, implant restorations can provide a reliable and long-lasting solution for missing teeth.
  • Preservation of Oral Health. Implants help maintain jawbone structure and prevent surrounding teeth from shifting, supporting overall oral health.

Types Of Dental Implant Restorations

Implant Supported Dentures

mplant-supported dentures attach securely to dental implants, providing improved stability compared to traditional dentures. They reduce slipping and allow for more confident chewing and speaking.

Why should I consider implant-supported dentures over traditional dentures?

This technique offers many advantages over traditional dentures.

Easy to care for, this solution can simulate the look and feel of natural teeth and stay fixed in place with the implants acting as anchors. Patients will not experience the typical rocking and movement or gum irritation associated with dentures.

Dental implant placement can also help alleviate the sunken look that can occur with bone loss in the jaw by stimulating bone growth in the area around the implant. This process can also help prevent future bone loss.

Implant Supported Bridges

These bridges replace multiple missing teeth by anchoring to dental implants instead of natural teeth. They offer a strong, stable solution that restores both function and appearance.

What are the advantages of an implant supported bridge?

Because of the natural look and feel of the porcelain and the functional stability provided by the implants, implant supported bridges are an effective solution to replace multiple missing teeth.

A traditional bridge uses teeth on either side of the gap where teeth are missing for support. An implant supported bridge uses two or more dental implants rather than the adjacent teeth. When a bridge uses natural teeth for attachment, the natural teeth must be prepped for crowns and are then subject to increased stress which may be damaging – particularly if the existing tooth, roots, or surrounding bone structure are already compromised. A dental implant supported bridge replaced your missing teeth and avoids putting any additional stress on your natural teeth.

Single Tooth Implant

A single implant restoration replaces one missing tooth with a custom crown. It blends seamlessly with surrounding teeth and helps maintain proper alignment and bite.

Full Arch with Only Four Implants

Full arch restorations replace an entire row of teeth using a series of implants. This option offers a stable, long-term alternative to traditional full dentures.

Zygoma Implants

Zygoma implant restorations are used in cases where patients have significant bone loss in the upper jaw. These specialized restorations work with implants anchored in the cheekbone to support a stable and functional smile.

Multiple Implants

For patients missing several teeth, multiple implant restorations provide a secure and durable solution. Each restoration is customized to restore proper spacing, alignment, and function.

Teeth in a Day

Teeth in a Day allows patients to receive a temporary restoration shortly after implant placement. This provides immediate function and aesthetics while the implants heal and integrate.

Are You A Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Good candidates for dental implant restorations are patients who have missing teeth and have already had or are planning to have implants placed by a specialist. Healthy gums and sufficient bone support are important factors, although options may be available for those with bone loss.

During your consultation, we will evaluate your situation and coordinate with your implant provider to ensure the best outcome.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implant Restorations

Do you place dental implants at your office?

No, we focus on the restorative portion of treatment. We work with trusted specialists who place the implants, and we complete the final restoration.

How long does the restoration process take?

The timeline depends on healing after implant placement, but the final restoration is typically completed once the implant has fully integrated with the bone.

Will my implant restoration look natural?

Yes, we design each restoration to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth for a seamless appearance.

Are implant restorations comfortable?

Implant-supported restorations are designed to feel stable and comfortable, functioning much like natural teeth.

How do I care for my implant restoration?

You should maintain good oral hygiene, including brushing, flossing, and regular dental visits to keep your restoration in excellent condition.

Can implants replace multiple missing teeth?

Yes, implant-supported bridges and full arch restorations are effective solutions for replacing several or all missing teeth.

What happens if my restoration needs repair?

If your restoration becomes damaged or worn over time, we can repair or replace it to restore proper function and appearance.
