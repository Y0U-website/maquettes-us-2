We are proud to be a VA Community Care Provider. If you are a 100 % disabled Veteran, Community Care allows you to receive dental care closer to home – without long drives or long wait times at the VA. Simply contact the VA and let them know you’d like to be referred to our office through Community Care. If you have any further questions, please call our office.
