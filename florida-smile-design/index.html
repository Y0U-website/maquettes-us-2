The practice

Being an Independent Private Practice , decisions for your care here are only influenced by You and Your Doctor . This can be in contrast to the many corporate owned clinics that now seem to be everywhere you look. Fortunately, you Still Have a Choice! Dr. Schnur and his team has the freedom, experience and compassion to provide Exceptional Individualized Care .

We see One Patient at a Time . We see our patients On-Time . We take our time to understand our patients’ needs and concerns regarding dental treatment. And our fantastic team very much enjoys providing a Fun, Inviting, No-Pressure Office to receive honest quality care. Our goal is to make it obvious you have found Your Dental Home!

When providing treatment, Comfort is in the Details! From the importance of not being rushed, to the simple pleasure of a pillow or blanket, to the options of nitrous oxide (laughing gas) or sedation, our team loves to help patients feel Comforted and Welcomed .

For different personal reasons, many patients that come to us have had to put off their dental needs for many years. When that is the case, it’s important to know you are in good hands. We Have Seen it All and we have the expertise to handle the most challenging dental needs. No Judgement, No Lectures, Just Help!
