Dr. Mark Gilstrap Broken Arrow, OK

Meet the Doctors Hi there! I’m Dr. Mark My name is Dr. Mark Gilstrap. And I’m thrilled to share a bit about myself with you.

I grew up in the small town of Porterville, CA, where I spent my early summer days working alongside my dad in his cleaning company. Those years taught me the value of hard work and sparked a love for tinkering with equipment and working with my hands– a passion that still drives me today.

My dental journey took me to Roseman University followed by a pediatric residency at the renowned Children’s Hospital of Colorado, where I immersed myself in the world of pediatric dentistry, especially treating children with special needs and complex medical conditions.

Now, with three amazing daughters – Emma, Adeline, and Zoey – family is my anchor and spending time with them is my daily goal. Beyond the dental chair, you’ll often find me exploring new flavors – whether it’s digging into spicy wings, sampling exotic cuisines, or indulging in the seasonal Blue Bell flavor. If you’re brave enough to mention triathlons, you will get an earful of nerding out.

Meet Dr. Whitney Mumma

Hi, I’m Dr. Whitney Mumma, and I’m proud to serve the Tulsa community as a board-certified pediatric dentist. As both a pediatric dentist and a mom, I understand how important it is for parents to feel confident that their child is receiving excellent care in a kind and nurturing environment.

Outside of the office, I’m a mom to two young boys and our golden retriever, Annie. Most weekends, you can find our family at a local coffeeshop and playing at the park. I also love running, traveling, trying out new restaurants, and cheering on the Sooners and OKC Thunder. Thank you for letting me be a part of your child’s journey! It is a privilege to grow with your child and help them develop healthy habits, happy smiles, and positive experiences that last a lifetime.
