Dr. Kruti Sanghavi

Dr. Kruti Sanghavi has been practicing dentistry since 2015. She completed her dental training at Columbia University and then pediatric specialty training at Rutgers University in New Jersey. She is a board-certified pediatric dentist and a mother to a two-year-old daughter, Nyra. Her husband, Nilay, works in Finance for Johnson & Johnson.

Dr. Sanghavi strongly believes that the foundation of pediatric dentistry is built on strong relationships with her patients and their families. The best way to build these relationships is to start early. That is why we, at Floss Boss, strongly support the AAPD recommendation to start seeing a pediatric dentist by the age of 1.

Dr. Kruti’s Professional Training and Affiliations:

  • Doctor of Dental Surgery—Columbia University (New York, NY)
  • Certificate in Pediatric Dentistry—Rutgers University (Newark, NJ)
  • American Board Certified in Pediatric Dentistry
  • Member of ADA and AAPD
