Coming to the dentist can be a bit frightening to some which is why we offer several treatment options to make your kids experience smoother and more enjoyable:

Nitrous (laughing gas) - The use of Nitrous Oxide and oxygen, inhaled through a mask is an effective treatment option that is gentle and helps reduce minimal anxiety. It helps reduce any discomfort that the patient may fear during the dental procedure. We have several scents that our patients can choose from such as bubble gum, cherry, strawberry or mint!

Oral Sedation - This sedation option is the combination of two medications that our pediatric dentist mixes and then provides to the patient. The use of oral sedation is typically done for patients who have a higher anxiety level and need additional helps to have a more pleasant experience.
