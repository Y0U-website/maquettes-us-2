Dr. Michael Luong, DDS

I’m Dr. Michael Luong, but everyone Calls me Dr. Mike. I am a pediatric dentist in Tacoma, WA and have been practicing for roughly 10 years now. I’m originally from Pullman, WA but a true Pacific Northwestern at heart.

I am a board certified pediatric dentist and a diplomate of the American board of pediatric dentistry.

I am happy and honored to serve my community and look forward to seeing each and everyone of my patients everyday!
