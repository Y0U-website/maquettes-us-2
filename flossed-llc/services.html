Dental X-Rays

Dental x-rays allow us to “see” what can’t be seen with the naked eye, helping us to diagnose and treat diseases of the teeth and surrounding bone. Using the latest digital technology, our x-rays reduce the amount of radiation you’ll be exposed to. We’ll recommend x-rays based on your mouth’s needs at your dental cleaning appointments.
