What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is the only tooth replacement option that mimics the entire structure of a natural tooth. While dentures and bridges only replace the "crown" (the visible part), an implant replaces the root, which is vital for maintaining your facial structure.

The Three Components of Your New Tooth:

  • The Post: A biocompatible titanium screw that acts as your new tooth root.
  • The Abutment: A custom connector that secures the new tooth to the post.
  • The Restoration: A hand-crafted porcelain crown, bridge, or denture that looks and functions perfectly.

The Dental Implant Process: A Step-by-Step Guide

Ready to reclaim your confidence? If dental implants are the right fit for your lifestyle, our team is ready to guide you through our efficient four-step restoration plan. Dr. Matthew Le has invested years in advanced clinical training specifically in cosmetic and restorative dentistry, allowing us to manage your entire case in-house.

This centralized care model is designed to save you both time and money by avoiding the hassle of multiple specialists. We believe in total transparency—explore our detailed process below to see how we use digital technology to rebuild your smile.

Step 1: The 3D Digital Consultation

The journey begins with a CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography) scan . This is a 3D X-ray that allows Dr. Le to see your jawbone, nerve pathways, and sinus cavities with sub-millimeter precision. We use this data to virtually plan your surgery before we ever begin the procedure.

Step 2: Site Preparation (If Needed)

If you’ve been missing a tooth for a long time, the bone might be thin. We may perform a bone graft or a sinus lift to "rebuild" the area. This ensures the implant has a rock-solid foundation.

Step 3: Surgical Placement

On the day of surgery, we use a custom-printed surgical guide . This acts as a GPS for Dr. Le, ensuring the implant is placed at the perfect depth and angle. We offer various levels of sedation to ensure that you are completely relaxed. Most patients describe the sensation as "less intense than a filling."

Step 4: Osseointegration (The Healing Phase)

This is where the magic happens. Over the next 3 to 6 months, your jawbone will naturally fuse with the titanium post. This process, called osseointegration , is what makes the implant a permanent part of your body. We will provide you with a temporary tooth during this time so you never have to walk around with a gap.

Step 5: The Final Reveal

Once healing is complete, we attach the custom abutment and your final porcelain crown. We check your bite, polish the restoration, and give you the green light to go enjoy a meal at your favorite Fort Worth restaurant.

Types of Dental Implant Restorations

We don’t believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. Your treatment plan is as unique as your fingerprint.

Single Tooth Replacement

Ideal for replacing one missing tooth without involving the adjacent teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridges

If you are missing multiple teeth in a row, we can place two implants to support a bridge of three or four teeth. This provides significantly more stability than a traditional partial denture.

All-on-4® / All-on-X (Full Arch)

If you are currently wearing full dentures or are facing the loss of all your upper or lower teeth, All-on-4 is a game-changer. We place four to six implants and attach a permanent, non-removable bridge. This is often called "Teeth in a Day" because you can leave our office with a functional set of teeth the same day.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

While the vast majority of the patients we treat at Flossophy Dental Studio are excellent candidates for permanent tooth replacement, your successful outcome depends on a few foundational pillars of health.

  • Systemic Wellness: Because dental implant placement involves a minor surgical procedure, your body needs to be in good overall health. We’ll review your medical history to ensure your natural healing response is ready for the task.
  • A Smoke-Free Healing Path: If you’re looking for a reason to kick the habit, this is it. Tobacco use significantly restricts blood flow to the gums and can slow down the vital process of osseointegration (the bone fusing to the implant).
  • A Strong Foundation: For an implant to be a lifetime solution, there must be sufficient bone volume to support the post. During your initial 3D exam, Dr. Matthew Le utilizes high-resolution CBCT imaging to measure your jawbone density with sub-millimeter precision. If your bone is thin, we can often "rebuild" it with advanced bone grafting techniques.
