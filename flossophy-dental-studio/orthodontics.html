Early Orthodontic Care: Guiding Your Child’s Growth

Why "Cheap" Orthodontics Can Be Costly

Many "at-home" options only focus on the front six teeth. This can often lead to:

  • Bite Misalignment: Moving teeth without checking the "posterior" (back) occlusion can lead to jaw pain and TMJ disorders.
  • Root Damage: Moving teeth too quickly can cause root resorption, potentially leading to tooth loss.
  • Relapse: Without professional-grade retention, your teeth may shift back, wasting your initial investment.
