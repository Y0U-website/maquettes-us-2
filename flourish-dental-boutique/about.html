Dr. Messer

Dr. Messer grew up in Alexandria, Louisiana, and moved to Texas to attend Baylor University, where she earned her undergraduate degree and met her husband, Jared. She later received her doctorate degree from Texas A&M College of Dentistry in Dallas and has proudly called the DFW Metroplex home ever since.

Dr. Messer’s passion for serving others began at a young age through her church and by watching her grandfather dedicate his time to mission work. Inspired by his example, she has participated in Texas Mission of Mercy, traveled to Panama on a dental mission trip, and led dental career day programs at local elementary schools. She believes dentistry is a unique opportunity to combine compassion, service, and healthcare to make a meaningful difference in people’s lives.

As a general dentist, Dr. Messer believes oral health is an important part of overall wellness. She is passionate about taking a comprehensive approach to patient care by understanding each patient’s unique needs, emphasizing prevention, and helping patients achieve a healthy smile that supports their overall health and quality of life. She values building lasting relationships with her patients and feels blessed to partner with them through education, connection, and compassionate care.

Dr. Messer and her husband live in Garland with their two children, Elena and Jonah, and their rescue dog, Snickerdoodle. Outside of the office, she enjoys spending time with her family, being involved at her church, traveling, and trying new cuisines. She considers herself a “foodie” and loves hearing restaurant recommendations from friends, family, and patients.
