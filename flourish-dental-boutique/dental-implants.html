Dental Implants in Richardson, TX

understanding the

types of implants we offer

We offer single-tooth implant placements, as well as restorations for single-tooth implants with dental crowns. In addition, you can get regular dental implant maintenance from Dr. Badheka at Flourish Dental Boutique .

placing your prosthetic

single-Tooth implants

A dental implant is a small rod-shaped piece that is designed to be implanted into your mouth, where it bonds with your jaw tissue in a process called “osseointegration.” After the implant heals, it functions as an artificial root.

While titanium is still the most common implant material, at Flourish we only place zirconia (“ceramic”) implants because of their improved biocompatibility.

Then, a dental implant restoration such as a dental crown can be attached to the root, restoring your smile. ‍ Single-tooth implants are a great alternative to both partial dentures and dental bridges. Among other benefits, single-tooth implants look and feel more natural, last 30+ years with proper care, and prevent jaw bone loss.

replacing your tooth

dental implant restorations

A dental implant restoration is a false tooth that is attached to your implant to restore your smile. At our office, we only offer single-tooth restorations with dental crowns. However, in some cases, dentists may use multiple implants to support an implant-supported bridge to restore multiple teeth, or a set of implant-supported bridges to replace all of the teeth.

keeping implants healthy

dental implant maintenance

As long as you brush twice a day for two minutes and floss once a day, your implants will remain healthy. However, it’s still important to come into our office every six months for a teeth cleaning, oral exam, and dental implant maintenance from Dr. Badheka

am i a good candidate

for dental implants?

As long as your mouth is healthy and your jaw bone is strong enough to support an implant, you are likely a good candidate for a single-tooth implant. To get more information and see if implants are right for you, contact Flourish Dental Boutique for a consultation with Dr. Badheka.

Frequently Asked Questions.

What are dental implants? +

Dental implants are permanent replacements for missing teeth. A biocompatible post supports a natural-looking crown, restoring both function and appearance.

Am I a good candidate for implants? +

Most healthy adults with adequate bone are good candidates. We’ll evaluate your oral health and overall wellness to confirm the best option for you.

Do you offer metal-free implant options? +

In keeping with our biological philosophy, we discuss biocompatible materials and options with you to align treatment with your health goals.
