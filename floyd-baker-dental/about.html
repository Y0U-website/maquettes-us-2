About Floyd Baker Dental

We treat you like family.

Our Practice & Core Values

Floyd Baker Dental works hard to treat all patients as family. We understand that going to the dentist isn’t always fun, but it is necessary for your overall and oral health. We want to maximize that health while also keeping patients at ease and minimizing anxiety. In addition, Floyd Baker Dental also strives to offer great services at reasonable prices. Patients must consider finances when making decisions about their care. We understand this and work hard to offer many payment options and help educate the patient about their needed treatment and costs associated with that treatment.

Meet Dr. Matthew Evers

Floyd Baker Dental is owned and operated by Dr. Evers — and he is no stranger to Gaffney! He attended Limestone College here and received his Bachelor’s Degree in Chemistry. He went on to earn a Master’s Degree in Human Physiology at Ball State University in Muncie, Indiana (where he met his future wife, Neely.) They moved together to Charleston, South Carolina where they married and Dr. Evers attended the Medical University of South Carolina. He obtained his Doctor of Medical Dentistry Degree in May 2015.

After graduation, Dr. Evers purchased a practice in Sidney, Ohio and changed the name to Evers Dental Center. He grew the practice and gained an outstanding reputation in the community for providing excellent dental care in a welcoming environment. He has since sold the practice and the Evers family is back in the South.

Dr. Evers and Neely have two daughters, Libby and Clara. When not in the dental office, he enjoys reading, golfing, and spending time with his wife and their little girls.
