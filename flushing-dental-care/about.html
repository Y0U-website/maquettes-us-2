About Us

While we offer specialized services, our primary focus remains on comprehensive general dentistry, ensuring we meet all your oral health needs under one roof. By going above and beyond, we strive to make your visit a positive experience that contributes to your long-term health and happiness.

Get To Know

David M. Shorez, D.D.S.

Dr. Shorez is a 2012 graduate of the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry. In 2011, while in dental school, he traveled to Honduras with a group of classmates for a dental missionary trip. He found the trip very humbling and rewarding. Dr. Shorez earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology and Medical Technology with a minor in Chemistry from the University of Michigan-Flint.

Dr. Shorez is a member of the American Dental Association and Michigan Dental Association. He is also a member of the Genesee District Dental Society, where he collaborates with dental colleagues and completes Continuing Education courses to stay on the leading edge of dentistry. As a lifelong community member, Dr. Shorez finds it meaningful to serve the patients of Flushing and the surrounding area.

When he is not caring for his patients, Dr. Shorez enjoys running, golfing, cheering for the Detroit Tigers and Lions, spending time with friends and family, and making memories with his two daughters. Thinking about Flushing Dental Care’s patients brings a smile to Dr. Shorez’s face!

Natalya A. Brezden, D.D.S.

Dr. Natalya Brezden grew up in Hamtramck within a close-knit Ukrainian community, where Ukrainian was her first language. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Brezden treasures time spent with her friends and family. She genuinely loves people and is known for always seeing the best in others. While she modestly claims she has no special talents, patients quickly discover her greatest gift—a big heart that is felt the moment you meet her.

Get To Know

Dr. Zena Sakka, D.D.S.

Dr. Sakka is originally from Homs, Syria and received her doctorate of dental surgery from University of Kalamoon School of Dental Medicine in 2012. While Dr. Sakka was working as a general dentist, she developed an interest in surgical dentistry, implant dentistry and periodontal plastic surgery.

Dr. Sakka then pursued specialty training in Periodontics at the University of Buffalo School of Dental Medicine where she received her specialty certification. During her 3-year intensive specialty program, Dr. Sakka obtained advanced training in atraumatic tooth extractions, dental implant surgery, gum grafting, bone grafting and oral medicine. She is a proud member of the American Academy of Periodontology and actively attends annual meetings, seminars and workshops nationally and internationally.

Dr. Sakka lives in Flushing and is eager to meet with you and your families!

Your First Visit Experience

What to Expect

Your initial visit to Flushing Dental Care is designed to be thorough and comfortable. We begin with a warm welcome and a tour of our modern facility. You&#x27;ll then meet with your dentist for a comprehensive oral examination, including digital X-rays and an oral cancer screening. We&#x27;ll discuss your dental history, concerns, and goals, ensuring we understand your unique needs.

Our team will create a personalized treatment plan and answer any questions you may have. We aim to make your first visit informative, stress-free, and the start of a lasting partnership in your oral health journey.

What to Bring

Please bring your ID, insurance information (if applicable), a list of current medications, and any recent dental X-rays. If you have specific concerns or a history of dental anxiety, let us know so we can tailor your experience accordingly.
