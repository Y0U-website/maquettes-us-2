The practice

Born and raised in Ville Platte, Dr. Ian Fontenot has strong roots in the Acadiana area. At a young age, his parents taught him the importance of customer and patient care. With Dr. Fontenot s mother as a registered nurse and his father an established family dentist in Ville Platte, he learned early on to take pride in caring for patients.

After dental school, he accepted and completed a General Dentistry Residency at the University of Florida College of Dentistry in Jacksonville, Florida. At the University of Florida, he learned all the disciplines in-depth, with a special emphasis on full mouth reconstruction and surgery utilizing dental implants, crowns, veneers, and removable prosthetics.

Dr. Fontenot worked at his father s dental practice in Ville Platte and multiple health centers throughout Acadiana for the past few years. From this experience, he increased his scope of practice to pediatrics, allowing him to treat adults and children in his current practice.

Dr. Fontenot has been married to his high-school sweetheart for almost 10 years. They have one beautiful little girl, Lilah Kate Fontenot, and a son, Liam Paul. His wife is a teacher at a local Catholic school, St. Leo-Seton. Dr. Fontenot lives with his family in Carencro, and they love it! He enjoys exercising, movies, music, and hanging out with friends and family.

From a young age, she found joy in helping others feel confident, especially through their smiles.
