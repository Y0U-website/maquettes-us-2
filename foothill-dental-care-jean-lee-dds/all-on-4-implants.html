All on 4 Implants Q & A

What are All-on-4 implants?

All-on-4 implants are full-mouth, same-day dental implants that replace all of your natural permanent teeth. The implants, which are metal posts, bond to your upper and/or lower jawbone to anchor artificial teeth. All-on-4 to replace all of the teeth in your mouth consists of four dental implants on the top and four on the bottom of your jaw. They transform an imperfect smile into something beautiful and more functional.

What are the benefits of All-on-4 implants?

The benefits of choosing full-mouth implants at Foothill Dental Care include:

All-on-4 implants can last for decades, even a lifetime, when cared for properly.

  • Perfect teeth
  • Flattering smile
  • More self-confidence
  • Easier chewing
  • Clearer speech
  • Less adjacent teeth shifting
  • Enhanced facial contours
  • Lower risk of future oral health problems
  • Preservation of jawbone structure
  • Long-lasting results

Am I a candidate for All-on-4 implants?

Dr. Lee examines your teeth and mouth to determine if you’re a candidate for All-on-4 implants, which are an option if you have jawbone loss. She also views the results of your dental X-rays, discusses your oral health history, and reviews your cosmetic or restorative dentistry goals. Dr. Lee personalizes a treatment plan that offers superior results and a flawless, restored smile.

You might first require jawbone grafting or tooth extraction before receiving full-mouth dental implants.

What happens during All-on-4 implant placement?

Dr. Lee numbs your mouth and offers a sedative to help you relax during All-on-4 implant placement. She inserts the implants securely into your jawbone in specific areas (for full-mouth, four on your top jaw and four on the bottom). You might receive temporary artificial teeth during the healing process.

After your dental implants and jawbone have time to integrate and you heal from the placement procedure, you receive your new permanent artificial teeth. Dr. Lee fastens them securely to the dental implants and ensures a comfortable, functional fit. You then enjoy your new vibrant, aesthetically pleasing smile with perfect teeth that remain fixed in place, often for a lifetime.

What happens after receiving All-on-4 implants?

It’s highly important to take good care of your new teeth by maintaining exceptional oral hygiene habits at home. Brush your teeth after meals, floss every day, and don’t chew on ice or other hard objects. Return to the Foothill Dental Care office if your new teeth become damaged, you have questions, or you’re due for a six-month dental cleaning and exam.
