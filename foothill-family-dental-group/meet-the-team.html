Our Dentists

Mariam Arnouk

Dr. Mariam Arnouk graduated from Loma Linda University with a Bachelor’s of Science in Dental Hygiene and then went on to Arizona School of Dentistry & Oral Health. Her passion for serving the community and educating the public about oral health led her to receive her Master’s in Public health while attending dental school.

Outside of practicing dentistry, Dr. Mariam loves going to the beach, hiking and enjoying a good brunch. She loves to spend time with her husband and their three kids.

Dr. Mariam feels so blessed to go to work with her family everyday.

Yara Arnouk

Dr. Yara Arnouk, a graduate of Loma Linda University, School of Dentistry, recognized a passion for implant dentistry early on in her dental educational career. Her implant educational journey started when she was chosen as one of three students in her graduating class to attend the Loma Linda Implant Continuum after graduation. Since then, she has been trained surgically with various specialists and experts nationally and internationally, focusing her training on implant surgeries and prosthodontics.

Dr. Yara earned the fellowship and mastership status with the American Academy of Implant Prosthodontics as well as the International Congress of Oral Implantologists. She is a pre-fellow with the Academy of General Dentistry and is completing her diplomate with the American Board of Oral Implantology/ Implant Dentistry. She is also a current member with the American Academy of Implant Prosthodontics, International Congress of Oral Implantologists, and Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Yara is passionate about patient education as it can be the most powerful tool in the patient’s hands! Her dedication to her own education empowers her to teach others the importance of their oral health, creating a safer and healthier community.

When not in the office you can find her reading, hiking, baking, or spending time at the beach.

Dr. Duong

Dr. Duong finds great joy in watching her patients’ smiles transform, and she is passionate about guiding them through the journey to build both their smiles and their confidence. With over 10 years of experience in dentistry, Dr. Duong has a deep commitment to her field.

Raised in Southern California, she earned her Bachelor of Science in Biology and Psychology from UCLA before pursuing her Doctorate in Dentistry at UCSF. She further specialized by completing an Orthodontic Residency and earning a Master’s in Business Administration at Roseman University of Health Sciences in Henderson, Nevada.

Outside of her professional life, Dr. Duong is a devoted Disney and food enthusiast. She and her husband enjoy discovering new restaurants, local hangouts, and food trucks. She also loves to travel, hike, and spend quality time at Disneyland with her husband and daughter.
