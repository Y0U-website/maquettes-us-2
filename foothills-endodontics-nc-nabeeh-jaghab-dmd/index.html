The practice

Dr. Jaghab pursued his D.M.D. degree as well as specialty training in Endodontics at the University of Pennsylvania in 2018, 2020 respectively. During his Residency he studied under the supervision of world renowned international faculty.

Our office uses advanced technology to aid in accurate diagnosis, gentle and effective treatment, while delivering an extraordinary experience for patients.

We spend extra time diagnosing each patient and explaining their treatment recommendations. We want our patients to understand the "why" and "how" of their procedure, which helps to alleviate any anxiety they may have about the process.
