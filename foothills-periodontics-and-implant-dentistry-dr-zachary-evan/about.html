Bone Grafting and Dental Implants by Zach Evans

Periodontal Disease and Oral Pathology

Dental Implants

Currently, the closest thing we have to replacing natural teeth are dental implants. Dr. Evans is an international leader in the field of guided implant surgery and utilizes state of the art technology to improve outcomes for all of his implant cases.

Gingival Grafting and Esthetics

Long-term health, esthetics, and comfort requires having good quality gingival (gum) tissue around our teeth. Dr. Evans offers minimally invasive options for treatment to help reduce the risks and discomfort following treatment of gingival recession and other gingival disorders.

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

We work with our restorative dentist colleagues to help develop plans and options for full mouth restorations based upon keeping your natural teeth and/or entirely using dental implants.

Bone Grafting

We offer advanced options for growing bone in the jaw to improve the support of natural teeth, and to establish a foundation for dental implant placement.

Extractions

Despite your best efforts taking care of them, sometimes teeth must be extracted due to infections, fractures, decay, mobility, etc. We perform minimally invasive techniques to improve the quality of healing after the procedure, and specialize in the preparation of these sites for future implant placement.
