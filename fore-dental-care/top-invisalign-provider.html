Invisalign Treatment: What To Expect

Step 1: Consultation

Your Invisalign experience begins with an initial consultation. Our dentist will start with a check-up, discuss your goals, and determine if you’re a good candidate.

Step 2: 3D Imaging & Smile Preview

Our dentist will capture a detailed 3D image of your teeth with digital scanners. After treatment, you’ll even see a preview of how your smile will look.

Step 3: Custom Aligner Creation

After considering your digital scan, Invisalign will develop a set of clear aligners for your treatment plan.

Step 4: Wear & Monitor

You must wear your aligners 20–22 hours a day and switch to a new set every 1–2 weeks, based on your condition. Our dentist will schedule regular check-ups to monitor your progress.

Step 5: Retainers Post-Treatment

After the final aligners, you’ll receive retainers to keep your teeth in place and protect your new smile.
