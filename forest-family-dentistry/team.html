Our Dental Hygienists

Our skilled hygienists provide thorough cleanings and preventive care to keep your smile healthy.

Lisa Ash, RDH

Lisa graduated from Champlain College Dental Assisting Program (1984) and University of Vermont Dental Hygiene School (1997). She worked as a Dental Assistant for 13 years before becoming a Dental Hygienist for 15 years. Licensed in dental anesthesia, Lisa has extensive experience in general practice, orthodontics, and spent 10 years working in a specialty practice dealing with temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders.
