Invisalign

Invisalign is a custom fitted aligner that can help with several problems like crowded teeth, overbite, underbite, and gap teeth. It’s a removable, comfortable, and inconspicuous orthodontic solution that has seen massive popularity in recent years, especially among adults and older teens. Invisalign treatment takes a similar approach to a retainer in straightening teeth and correcting misalignments through removable plastic trays that you wear over your teeth.

This transparent treatment option possesses two primary advantages: first, Invisalign is almost invisible while wearing; and second, it allows for easy tooth and gum care since you can take the entire appliance off for cleaning. When it comes time to brush and floss, simply slip your Invisalign aligners off and continue your normal cleaning routine.

Near the end of your braces, getting night-time retainers or mouthguards will keep that smile great after your braces are taken off.

Mouthguards cover your teeth to protect teeth and gums. Custom-fitted mouthguards fit and protect your teeth better than over-the-counter mouthguards. Night guards can ease bruxism (teeth grinding). Kids and adults should wear mouthguards during contact sports or activities like biking.
