The practice

Forest Hill Dental offers a full range of dental services to meet the needs of our Germantown community. Whether you are in need of routine cleanings, restorative procedures, or cosmetic dentistry, our state-of-the-art facility is equipped to handle all your dental health needs.

We offer a variety of treatments to help you achieve the smile of your dreams, including porcelain veneers, teeth whitening, dental bonding, Invisalign aligners, and more.

If you have damaged or missing teeth, porcelain veneers can help restore both function and aesthetics, helping you achieve the smile of your dreams.

Whiter, brighter teeth from whitening treatments can convey good overall health, provide a younger appearance, and enhance your smile.

Forest Hill Dental is equipped with the latest dental technology to ensure you receive the most effective and efficient treatment possible. Our modern yet inviting office is designed for your comfort and convenience.

We believe in personalized dental care tailored to meet the unique needs of each patient. Our team takes the time to listen to your concerns and develop a treatment plan that fits your individual needs and goals.

We strive to make your visit as convenient as possible. Located in the heart of Germantown, Tennessee, Forest Hill Dental is easily accessible to Memphis and surrounding areas and offers ample parking for guests.

Dr Vick holds associations with the following organizations: American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry , Tennessee Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry , American Dental Association , Tennessee Dental Association , and Memphis Dental Society .
