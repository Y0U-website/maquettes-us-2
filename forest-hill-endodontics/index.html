The practice

Root canals or any other endodontic treatments don’t have to be uncomfortable. We take the time to make sure our environment is as comfortable as possible to mitigate any unnecessary stress.

Endodontics doesn’t have to be confusing. We make sure that our patients understand treatment options, procedures, and outline any necessary aftercare steps that should be taken.

Endodontics is a specialty of dentistry that focuses on treating the inside of your teeth, specifically the root canals. If you have a tooth with deep decay or damage, an endodontist can clean out the infection and save the tooth, helping you avoid extraction and keep your smile healthy. Root canal therapy is a procedure where the inside of a tooth is cleaned out and sealed to fix damage or infection and save the tooth. Learn more.

Treatment for abscessed teeth is necessary when teeth have developed a painful infection at their root or in the surrounding gum tissue.

Treatment for cracked teeth is necessary when a tooth has been damaged by a crack. Untreated cracked teeth can become painful, infected, or even lead to total loss of the tooth.

Traumatic dental injury treatment is necessary to fix a tooth that has been damaged from an accident or injury. Learn more.
