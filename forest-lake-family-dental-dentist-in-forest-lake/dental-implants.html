Advantages of Dental Implants

Dental implants provide many advantages over other tooth replacement options. Some of these advantages include the following:

  • Improved functioning – Dental implants act and look like natural teeth due to the nature of the surgery, which involves fusing the implant with the bone. The bone fusion usually takes 3-6 months.
  • Improved appearance –Dental implants, once fused, allow for a false tooth to be placed on the implant, which appears just like a natural tooth in terms of shape, size, and color.
  • Improved speech – Other restorative dental appliances, such as a denture, can affect the way a patient speaks due to their removability. Dental implants allow patients to speak without issues as they are fused with the bone.
  • Improved self-esteem – Dental implants near you are able to give you a natural-looking smile and restore a patient’s self-confidence.
  • Durability – Dental implants are durable over the course of many years. With good oral hygiene practices, many implants are able to last a patient’s lifetime.
  • Improvement in oral health – Dental implants don’t require dentists to reduce the size of other teeth, as is required for a tooth-supported dental bridge. Nearby teeth are not changed, which can enhance long-term oral health. Individual dental implants allow easy access between teeth for flossing.
  • Convenience – Dental implants near you prevent embarrassing possibilities that removable dentures can cause as well as the need for messy adhesive solutions used to keep them in place. They act and function as natural teeth do once the procedure is completed.

What Is Involved in Getting Dental Implants?

The first step is assessing the patient and developing a treatment plan specific to that patient. Once it is established if the patient is a good candidate and the number of implants needed, a second visit is scheduled for the surgical portion of the procedure. The tooth root implant, a small post made from titanium, is surgically placed into the bone socket. As the jawbone is healing, it surrounds the implanted metal post, securing it into the jaw.

A small connector post called an abutment is then attached to the titanium post in order to hold the new replacement tooth. In order to make the new tooth or teeth, an impression of your teeth will be taken, and a model of the patient’s bite is created. This new tooth is created based on this model. A replacement tooth, or dental crown, is then created and attached to the abutment. Your dentist will match the color, shape, and size of your new tooth to the remaining natural teeth to help it blend in. Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I choose dental implants over other tooth replacement options?

Dental implants are the same in appearance and feel as natural teeth. They’re a permanent, long-lasting solution that preserves jawbone health. They’re not dependent on the support of other teeth, as with bridges or dentures.

Are dental implants painful?

Most people experience little discomfort during the procedure, typically less than with tooth extraction. Local anesthesia takes care of your comfort. Some mild pain later may be relieved by taking over-the-counter pain relievers.

How are dental implants done?

The procedure is done by placing a titanium post in the jaw and giving it a healing time. Once healing is done and the post has become part of the jaw, a crown will be placed for a natural appearance. The procedure is done step by step over several months.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants last 20 years or longer with good maintenance. They are highly resistant and do not rot. Good oral hygiene and regular visits to the dentist are necessary for durability.
