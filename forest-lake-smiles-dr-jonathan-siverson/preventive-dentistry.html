Strengthen Your Teeth

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride has the unique ability to help remineralize teeth and reverse the effects of minor decay. The treatment consists of a fluoride liquid that’s painted onto your teeth after they’ve been cleaned. It’s left in place for a few minutes, then it’s washed away. The fluoride helps strengthen your teeth in addition to fighting back against cavities.

Prevent Tooth Decay

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants use a liquid plastic resin, which is applied to your rear teeth after a cleaning. The resin is painted onto your tooth, then hardened with a UV light. This creates a strong, clear barrier that protects your enamel from direct contact with oral bacteria, food particles, and acid. In turn, this prevents cavities from forming. Sealants can protect your teeth for up to 10 years.

Oral Cancer Screenings

At Forest Lake Smiles, Dr. Siverson will use intraoral cameras to look for lesions, bumps, discoloration and other potential signs of oral cancer. While this cancer is relatively rare, regular screenings give you peace of mind, and ensure that you can get further treatment if we notice an issue.
