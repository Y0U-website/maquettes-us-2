Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Cameron Warner

Dr. Cameron Warner will be the first to tell you: Dentistry is a lot more interesting than most would think, mostly because of the people.

His approach is straightforward: Be honest, be thorough, and make sure you leave understanding your own oral health better than before. No unnecessary upsells. And if you have questions, ask. He&#x27;d rather spend extra time making sure things click than send you home guessing.

Outside the office, he&#x27;s skiing, hiking, or dad-ing somewhere in the Pacific Northwest. He built Forestview Dental to serve this community, and he genuinely looks forward to being your dentist.

Know What to Expect

Your First Visit, Designed Around You

We believe great care begins with a great first impression. From the moment you walk through our doors, we’ll do everything we can to make your visit feel easy and stress-free. We’ll take the time to understand your health history, your goals, and what matters most to you—so we can tailor your care accordingly.

The Experience Your first appointment is comprehensive and relaxed. You’ll receive a full exam, necessary x-rays or scans, and have plenty of time to chat with Dr. Warner about your oral health and any concerns you may have. We want every visit to feel collaborative, comfortable, and never rushed.

What to Bring Please bring the following items to your first visit:

Any records or x-rays from a previous dental provider

A completed health history form (if not submitted online)

Comfort Meets Innovation

We’ve thoughtfully designed our space with the comforts and technology you need to feel at ease. From streaming your favorite show during treatment to seeing your teeth in 3D with a digital scan, our tools and amenities are here to enhance your experience—without the clinical feel.

Ceiling-Mounted TVs with Streaming Options

Catch up on Netflix or YouTube while you relax in the chair—we make it easy to tune out and feel at home during your visit.

iTero Digital Scanner

No more goopy impressions. With our iTero scanner, we can capture detailed, 3D images of your teeth in minutes—making it easier to track progress, plan treatment, and help you understand your dental health.

Comfort Menu

From noise-canceling headphones and cozy blankets to personalized touches, our comfort menu is designed to meet you where you are and help you feel relaxed throughout your appointment.
