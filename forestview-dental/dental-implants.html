The Benefits of Dental Implants

Natural Feel and Function Dental implants closely mimic your natural teeth, allowing you to brush, floss, and eat just like you always have.

Aesthetically Pleasing Each implant is custom-designed to match your existing teeth in shape, size, and color. This personalized approach ensures your new tooth blends seamlessly with your smile.

Preserves Oral Health Made from biocompatible titanium, implants actually integrate with your jawbone, stimulating it just like natural tooth roots. This helps prevent bone loss and maintains your facial structure for years to come.

The Implant Treatment Process Placement Your journey begins with the surgical placement of your dental implant, which can be completed right here at Forestview Dental. During this phase, Dr. Warner places a titanium post into your jawbone to serve as the foundation for your new tooth. From there, the implant is given time to heal and integrate with the surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration, which typically takes 3 to 6 months. Once healing is complete, we can design and place your final restoration, allowing the full implant process to be completed within our practice.

Restoration Planning

Once your implant is fully healed, we’ll begin planning your restoration. At your consultation, we’ll evaluate your implant site, take precise digital impressions, and design a custom crown that blends naturally with your smile. Our priority is to create a restoration that looks, feels, and functions just like a real tooth.

Final Restoration Placement

When your crown is ready, we’ll securely attach it to your implant using a connector called an abutment. We’ll check your bite, fit, and aesthetics to ensure it feels comfortable and looks seamless. With the final restoration in place, your implant will be fully functional—restoring both your smile and confidence.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Check out these frequently asked questions, or call us to speak with our team.

What is a single tooth implant?

A single tooth implant is a modern solution for replacing a missing tooth. It consists of a titanium post surgically placed into your jawbone and a custom-made crown attached to it. Unlike removable options like partial dentures, implants are permanently fixed, offering stability and comfort that feels just like your natural teeth.

How are dental implants made?

Dental implants combine precision engineering with customization. The implant post is made from medical-grade titanium and comes in various standard sizes. The crown, however, is custom-made specifically for you. The crown is typically made from porcelain for its natural appearance and strength. Once ready, we carefully attach it to your implant, completing your restoration.

How long will my dental implant last?

With proper placement and care, dental implants can last for decades. The crown attached to the implant may need replacement over time due to normal wear and tear, but the implant itself often lasts a lifetime. Regular check-ups allow us to monitor everything and address any issues early.

Can my dental implant become infected?

While uncommon, dental implants can develop an infection called peri-implantitis. This typically happens if proper oral hygiene isn&#x27;t maintained. To minimize this risk, it&#x27;s important to follow post-operative care instructions and continue regular preventative visits.

Do dental implants stop bone loss?

Yes! When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area starts to deteriorate from lack of stimulation. An implant acts like an artificial tooth root, transmitting chewing forces to the jawbone. This stimulation maintains bone density and strength, much like a natural tooth would.
