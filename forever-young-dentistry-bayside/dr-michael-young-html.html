What Does It Mean to Be a Kois-Trained Dentist?

Dr. Young has completed the entire continuum of expanded education courses at the world-renowned Kois Center and is a mentor there. He has spent years expanding his knowledge and skillsets by taking courses on a variety of dental treatments, including dental implants, bite and jaw health, and other restorative procedures. By partnering with leaders in his field and learning from the best, Dr. Young is able to provide truly five-star results.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Young enjoys spending time with his family, friends, as well as his dogs. He can play a number of instruments, including the guitar, the bass, and the drums; he really enjoys music a lot! His favorite charity is the Leukemia & Lymphoma Society, and he regularly helps them raise money by running in triathlons and marathons. When it comes to sports, he loves cheering for the Michigan State University Spartans on the football field and the basketball court. He also watches Detroit Tigers baseball and Red Wings hockey.
