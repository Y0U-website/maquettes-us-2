Dr. Nathan Woodward

DDS

Dr. Woodward and his family came to Forsyth in January of 2015, after purchasing the practice of Drs. Ed and A Hammons Schanda.

Dr. Woodward is a graduate of the University of Missouri Kansas City School of Dentistry. While in dental school, he served as class president for four years. He also served on numerous committees and served on many boards. He was chosen by his classmates twice as Student of the Year. Dr. Woodward is a member of the Springfield Dental Society, Missouri Dental Association, Academy of General Dentist, American Association of Cosmetic Dentist and the American Academy of Cosmetic Orthodontics.

Prior to attending dental school, Dr. Woodward received his Bachelors degree in Cell and Molecular Biology from Pittsburg State University. Dentistry is a second career for Dr. Woodward. He previously was a licensed funeral director and embalmer for almost 15 years.

Dr. Woodward and his wife, Sarah, have been married for over 16 years. Sarah is a registered nurse at the Taney County Health Department. They have 3 children- Grant, Emma and Brody.

In his spare time, Dr. Woodward enjoys photography, spending time with his family, and smoking meats. He has won several photography contests and had numerous photographs published. You can see his photographs on display at the office.
