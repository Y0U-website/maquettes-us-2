When to Seek Emergency Dental Care

Knowing when to seek immediate care can make a significant difference in your outcome. Delaying treatment may lead to more serious complications.

You should seek emergency dental care if you experience:

For non-urgent concerns, routine care through General & Preventive Dentistry can help prevent emergencies before they occur.

  • Intense or worsening tooth pain
  • Trauma to the teeth or jaw
  • Bleeding that doesn’t stop
  • Signs of infection such as swelling or fever

What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

Contact our office immediately. We’ll provide guidance and schedule you as soon as possible to address the issue.

Can a knocked-out tooth be saved?

In some cases, yes—if treated quickly. Try to keep the tooth moist and contact us right away for immediate care.

Is sedation dentistry safe?

Yes, sedation options are carefully administered and monitored to ensure your safety and comfort during treatment.

Will I be seen the same day for an emergency?

We strive to accommodate same-day emergency visits whenever possible.

What if I’m nervous about emergency treatment?

Sedation dentistry options are available to help you feel relaxed and comfortable throughout your visit.
