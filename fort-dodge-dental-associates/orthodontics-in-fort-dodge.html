FAQs

Question text goes here

How long does Invisalign® treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on your case, but most patients complete Invisalign® within 6 to 18 months.

Is Invisalign® as effective as traditional braces?

For many cases, Invisalign® is just as effective as traditional braces, especially for mild to moderate alignment issues.

How often do I need to wear my aligners?

Aligners should typically be worn 20–22 hours per day for optimal results.

Will Invisalign® affect my speech?

Some patients notice a slight adjustment period, but most adapt quickly and speak normally within a few days.

Do I need to wear a retainer after treatment?

Yes, wearing a retainer helps maintain your results and prevents teeth from shifting over time.
