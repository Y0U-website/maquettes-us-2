The practice

It is very important to us to offer multiple treatment options when possible and to provide a thorough education of those options. Our administrative team works hard to determine insurance coverage and out-of-pocket costs before treatment. Our patients are part of the treatment planning process and will feel empowered to choose the option that best suits their needs. We are committed to providing…

Our team is passionate about continuing education and we make sure that we stay up-to-date on the best practices that evidence-based dentistry has to offer.

We will walk you through all of your treatment options so that you feel confident in deciding which is best for you. When you return to our office for that treatment, we will talk you through every step and make sure that you are comfortable the entire time.

Our office is able to accommodate families so that the entire family can come together and get their teeth cleaned at the same time. Our office cares for children and adults!
