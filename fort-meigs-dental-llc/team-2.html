0 Dentists Dr. Lauren Kollar, DDS Dr. Kollar is originally from Sylvania, Ohio. In 2010, she earned her undergraduate degree at Miami University and in 2014 her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at The Ohio State University. After graduation, Dr. Kollar joined the team at Fort Meigs Dental with Dr. Gannon. Dr. Kollar is thrilled to be home, serving the people in the community she grew up in.

Dr. Kollar took ownership at Fort Meigs Dental in 2019. She is honored to have the opportunity to care for the long-standing patients of this practice and welcomes new patients.

Dr. Kollar and her husband Jeremiah are the proud parents of Cameron and Olivia. Outside of work, the Kollars enjoy spending time with family and friends, traveling, and being outside.

Dr. Andrea Deuschle, DDS

Dr. Andi is proud to call Northwest Ohio home. Originally from Rossford, she attended Adrian College before earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in 2020. After graduation, she returned to the Toledo area to complete a General Practice Residency at The University of Toledo ProMedica Toledo Hospital, where she gained valuable experience caring for patients with a wide variety of dental needs.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Andi enjoys spending as much time outdoors as possible with her partner, Matt, and their spunky dachshund, Ranger. Whether they’re fishing the Great Lakes, hunting, hiking or taking Ranger on long walks, they love finding new ways to enjoy nature together and with family. They also consider themselves foodies - often spending weekends cooking at home attempting new recipes or enjoying new local restaurants!

Dr. Andi is grateful to be part of the dedicated and experienced team at Fort Meigs Dental and enjoys caring for patients with the same kindness, honesty, and dedication she would want for her own family.
