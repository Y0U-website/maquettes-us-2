Forward Oral Surgery

Dr. Levon Nikoyan is originally from Armenia but has spent most of his life in New York. He earned his Bachelor’s degree in Biochemistry from New York University, obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the Columbia University College of Dental Medicine, and completed his residency in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at Woodhull Medical Center in Brooklyn.

He is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and has published a number of articles on the preoperative management of patients prior to anesthesia. Dr. Nikoyan has a special interest in complex mouth reconstruction cases and specialized techniques to maximize comfort during procedures. He actively contributes to humanitarian causes and has participated in numerous medical missions to the Dominican Republic and Jamaica. Additionally, Dr. Nikoyan speaks multiple languages, including Spanish, Russian, and Armenian.

Outside of oral surgery, Dr. Nikoyan spends time with his family on Long Island, where he and his wife are raising their two children.

From your very first phone call to your post-operative recovery, our team works hand-in-hand with Dr. Nikoyan to deliver compassionate, personalized attention in a warm and reassuring environment. Whether addressing your clinical questions, guiding you through procedure prep, or coordinating insurance benefits, our staff is here to support you every step of the way.
