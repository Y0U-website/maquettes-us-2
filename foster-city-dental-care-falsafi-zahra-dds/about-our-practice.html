Our Practice

Foster City Dental Care is a private general dentistry practice that has proudly served the community since 1996.

The highly experienced team of dentists and registered dental assistants is friendly, compassionate, and dedicated to making every visit as comfortable as possible. Exceptional dental care is provided using the latest techniques and advanced technology.

Our advanced technology includes intraoral scanning, low-radiation digital X-rays, intraoral cameras, and other state-of-the-art tools, allowing patients to better understand their oral health by seeing exactly what the doctors see.

For patients who experience dental anxiety, nitrous oxide (laughing gas) is available to help create a more relaxed and comfortable treatment experience.

Patient education is an important part of the care provided. Questions are always encouraged, and every treatment plan is developed collaboratively to meet each patient’s individual needs. The goal is to help every patient achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile for life.

Comprehensive dental care is available for both adults and children, making it convenient for the entire family to receive care in one location.

The knowledgeable administrative team is happy to assist with scheduling appointments, explaining insurance benefits, and handling the necessary paperwork to help maximize dental insurance coverage.

Whether dental visits have been postponed because of a busy schedule or anxiety, it’s never too late to take the first step toward a healthier smile. Contact Foster City Dental Care today; we look forward to welcoming the entire family.

Languages We Speak The multilingual team speaks English, Spanish, Cantonese, Mandarin, Farsi, Hindi, and Marathi.

Interest free payment plans, most major insurance plans accepted.
