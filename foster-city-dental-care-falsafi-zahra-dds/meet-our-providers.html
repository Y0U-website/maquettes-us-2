Meet Our Providers

Dr. Imani is a General Dentist graduated from University of California at San Francisco in 1994. She regularly attends seminars and classes keeping up to date with the latest in dental technology and procedure. She is Invisalign © Certified and a member of American Dental Association, California Dental Association and San Mateo Dental Society. In her spare time she loves being in nature, hiking and traveling with her family. Dr. Imani is also, fluent in Farsi.

Dr. Tang is a general dentist. She obtained her bachelor’s degree in Molecular Environmental Biology from UC Berkeley in 2013. She then graduated from University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in 2016. Dr. Tang is Invisalign certified and a member of American Dental Association, California Dental Association and San Mateo Dental Society. In her free time, she enjoys reading, art projects, and spending time with her family and pets. Dr. Tang is also fluent in Cantonese and Mandarin.

Dr. Desai received her DDS degree from the University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco. She also has a Masters in Public Health from George Washington University, Milken Institute of Public Health. She is an active member of the California Dental Association, American Dental Association, and San Mateo Dental Society. Dr. Desai is committed to providing the highest quality care to her patients and helping them reach their dental goals. In her free time, Dr. Desai likes to cook, travel, and hike. She is fluent in English, Hindi, and Marathi.

Ashton Beltrami, RDH Registered Dental Hygienist

Ashton is a Registered Dental Hygienist. She earned her Associate’s degree in Dental Hygiene from Carrington College. Ashton loves the educational side of her role, empowering patients with personalized tips for at-home care. Known for her gentle touch and friendly demeanor, she excels at making dental visits a positive, stress-free experience. Outside the office, Ashton enjoys reading and spending time with her friends, family, and cat, Ron.

Katelynn T., R.D.H. Registered Dental Hygienist

Katelynn is a Registered Dental Hygienist. She graduated with an Associate Degree in Dental Hygiene from Chabot College in 2006. In her spare time, she loves to spend time with her family and has a passion for photography. Second Language: Cantonese Memberships: American Dental Hygienist Association., California Dental Hygienist Association.
