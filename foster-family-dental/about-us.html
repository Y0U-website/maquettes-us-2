Meet Dr. Foster Foster Family Dental

If you are looking for conservative, comprehensive, and compassionate dental care in the Evergreen and Conifer areas, look no further than Foster Family Dental. Dr. P. Jenny Foster has a commitment to her patients that sets her apart from other dental offices.

Because she maintains a small practice, she gets to know each of her patients on a personal level. Dr. Foster handles everything from dental cleanings to examinations to restorative and cosmetic work. She runs a judgment-free office, and she is experienced in helping patients who struggle with dental anxiety. Dr. Foster understands that visiting the dentist can be stressful for people but it doesn’t have to be!

Dr. Foster has a true passion for dentistry that goes back to her childhood when she helped out in her father’s dental practice. Find out more about her background by reading below. Then give us a call and find out for yourself what sets Foster Family Dental apart from other practices.

P. Jenny Foster, DDS

Dr. P. Jenny Foster has been practicing in Evergreen for over a decade. She also serves patients from the nearby areas of Conifer, Bailey, Pine, Idaho Springs, Kittredge, Indian Hills, Morrison, and Georgetown.

She received her Bachelor of Arts degree in biological sciences from the University of Missouri-Columbia and her Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Missouri-Kansas City. Dr. Foster s husband Cody is a Colorado native and a financial advisor with his own company, Foster Financial. They are the proud parents of two children and are excited to call Evergreen home.
