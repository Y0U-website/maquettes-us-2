Comforts & Amenities

TVs & Monitors branded tooth icon Beverage Stations branded tooth icon Noise-Cancelling Headphones branded tooth icon Neck Pillows & Blankets branded tooth icon Technology Bar branded tooth icon WiFi Advanced Technology For You At Foundations Dental Group, we invest in cutting-edge technology to provide you with the most accurate, efficient, and comfortable dental care possible.

Intraoral Scanners

Our intraoral scanners create precise digital impressions of your teeth, eliminating the need for messy traditional impressions and ensuring better-fitting restorations.

3D Treatment Planning

We use advanced 3D treatment planning software to visualize your treatment outcomes and create personalized plans for predictable, successful results.

3D CBCT

Our 3D cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) scanner provides detailed images of your teeth, jaws, and facial structure for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.

STA (Single-Tooth Anesthesia)

STA allows us to numb individual teeth without numbing your entire mouth, making your dental experience more comfortable and convenient.

Same-Day Crowns

With our in-office milling technology, we can create and place custom dental crowns in a single visit, saving you time and multiple appointments.

Giving Back

Our Community Outreach

At Foundations Dental Group, we believe in supporting the communities we serve. We proudly support local sports teams and schools through financial contributions and by providing dental education to students. Our practice also partners with local organizations to make a difference. We have sponsored events like the Mother Son Dance, which raises funds for cancer research, and Family Fitfest, organized by the Wiregrass Hope Group, a local non-profit.

Most recently, we&#x27;ve partnered with SEACT (Southeast Alabama Community Theatre) as a sponsor to keep the arts alive and well in our community. Our staff and patients participated in a peanut butter drive for another of our favorite non-profits, Love In Action, who takes care of the homeless and those in need.

By giving back, we aim to improve not only the oral health of our patients but also the overall well-being of our community. We will continue to seek opportunities to make a positive impact in the Wiregrass area. You can always check our blog for community involvement photos and updates.
