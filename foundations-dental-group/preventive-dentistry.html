Preventive dentistry focuses on maintaining optimal oral health and preventing dental issues before they develop. Regular preventive care, including cleanings and exams, helps keep your smile healthy and catches potential problems early.

Healthier Smile Preventive care, such as professional cleanings and fluoride treatments, helps keep your teeth and gums healthy, giving you a brighter, more confident smile.

Overall Wellness Maintaining good oral health through preventive dentistry can also benefit your overall health, as dental issues have been linked to systemic conditions like heart disease and diabetes.

Early Detection Regular check-ups allow our dentists to detect and treat oral health issues in their earliest stages, preventing more serious and costly problems down the road.

Preventive Services

Exams & Diagnostics

Our comprehensive oral exams and advanced diagnostic tools help us assess your dental health and develop personalized treatment plans.

Cleaning & Hygiene

Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup, promoting healthier teeth and gums and preventing cavities and gum disease.

Digital X-Rays

Our digital X-rays provide detailed images of your teeth and jaws while minimizing radiation exposure, allowing for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments strengthen tooth enamel, making your teeth more resistant to decay and reducing your risk of cavities.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are a protective coating applied to the chewing surfaces of molars, helping to prevent cavities in these cavity-prone areas.

Emergencies

For existing patients, we offer same-day emergency appointments to address urgent dental issues and alleviate pain, providing prompt care when you need it most.

Periodontal Care

Our periodontal care services, including scaling and root planing, help prevent and treat gum disease, ensuring the health of your gums and supporting structures.

Sedation Options

We offer sedation options, such as nitrous oxide, to help anxious patients relax during their dental treatments, ensuring a comfortable and stress-free experience.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Our dentists perform thorough oral cancer screenings to identify any abnormalities or early signs of oral cancer, allowing for prompt treatment and better outcomes.

Night Guards

Custom-made night guards protect your teeth from the damaging effects of teeth grinding and clenching, alleviating jaw pain and preventing tooth wear.

TMJ/TMD Treatment

We offer treatments for temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders, such as occlusal adjustments and splint therapy, to alleviate jaw pain, headaches, and other related symptoms.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Can preventive dentistry help me avoid more extensive treatments?

Absolutely! By catching and treating dental issues early through preventive care, you can often avoid the need for more invasive and costly treatments later on.

Are digital X-rays safe?

Yes, digital X-rays expose patients to significantly less radiation than traditional film X-rays, making them a safe and effective diagnostic tool.

How often should I visit the dentist for preventive care?

We recommend visiting our office every six months for routine check-ups and cleanings to maintain optimal oral health.
