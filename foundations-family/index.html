The practice

After being stationed in South Carolina and Georgia, Dr. Render moved with his wife and daughter Lilly back to Kentucky to start his practice ownership journey.

Dr. Render specifically loves using digital workflow to plan and restore cases in which all teeth require removal and restoring a patient’s ability to smile and chew once again.

Dr. Render was recently promoted to Lieutenant Colonel in the Air Force Reserves and hopes to retire in the next few years after twenty years of service.

Render loves Georgia Football, Braves baseball, the PGA tour, and of course, spending time with his family.

0 Dr. Laura Parkman Dr. Laura Parkman has been practicing dentistry for over 13 years. She graduated from the University of Louisville in 2012 and began her career in South Carolina while Dr. Render fulfilled his active duty commitment with the Air Force. After practicing in South Carolina and Georgia for three years, the Parkmans welcomed their first child and moved back to Louisville shortly…

Render and Laura became practice owners in 2015, opening Foundations Dentistry in Middletown and shortly thereafter expanding into St. Matthews.

While her training and experience span most facets of general dentistry, Dr. Laura has pursued extensive advanced training in facial esthetics, traveling across the country to learn from master injectors and stay at the forefront of new techniques and products.

The Parkmans now have three children, Lilly, Wells, and Georgia Mae. Outside of the office, Dr. Laura enjoys planning family adventures, watching sports, and playing tennis in her spare time.
