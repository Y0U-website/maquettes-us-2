Meet the doctors

Dr. Melissa Talbott and Dr. Mark Montano are twins and grew up in a small town south east of Pueblo, CO. She had a son at a young age who has autism and is non-verbal. He became her motivation to go to dental school so she could provide quality care for children with special needs. Dr. Melissa graduated from the University of Colorado School of Dentistry in 2006. She now has another son and a daughter who keep her very busy. She enjoys spending her free time keeping her kids active in sports and you can always find her at community events or supporting local small businesses.

Fun fact: Both twin doctors love to sing!

Dr. Montano completed his education at the University of Iowa College of Dentistry where he mastered techniques in operative, aesthetics, prosthodontics, endodontics, periodontics, and oral surgery. His humor, personal skills, and strong leadership skills have led him to many five-star reviews, and returning patients. He is dedicated to remaining a family owned business and performing quality work over quantity. His goal is to provide quality dental care for each patient and to help give them the confidence they need to be productive members of society.
