Frequently Asked Questions About Pediatric Dentistry

When should my child see a dentist for the first time? We recommend bringing your child to a pediatric dentist by age one, or within six months of their first tooth erupting.

How often should my child have dental checkups? Just like adults, children should visit the dentist every six months for exams and cleanings.

Are dental X-rays safe for kids? Yes. Our digital X-rays use minimal radiation and are safe for children when used appropriately.

What if my child has dental anxiety? We provide gentle care, clear communication, and techniques that help children feel calm. We can also discuss options like nitrous oxide for more anxious patients.

Can you treat dental emergencies for kids? Absolutely. If your child suffers a dental injury or pain, we provide urgent dental emergency care for children.
