MEET OUR TEAM

DOCTOR

Insik David Chung | Implant Specialist

Dr.Chung received Bachelor of Science at McGill University in Canada, and then moved on to complete Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at New York College of Dentistry. His philosophy is to give patients happy and healthy smiles. Dr.Chung’s field of work includes root canal therapy, surgical extraction, bone grafting, implant placement, restoring implant crowns and cosmetic dentistry. Dr.Chung worked at a dental trauma center where he gained tremendous knowledge of oral surgery procedures.

Dr.Chung believes in the importance of continuing education and takes numerous CE courses to keep up to date. His great bedside manner will make you at ease in dental chair. Outside the office, Dr.Chung enjoys golfing and fishing.

DOCTOR

Jennifer Mijin Park | Cosmetic Specialist

Dr. Park received Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry at University of California, San Diego. Then she attended New York University College of Dentistry to become a general dentist. She developed her skills in cosmetic when she worked at a dental trauma center with Dr. Chung.

She has treated numerous patients involved in accidents that resulted in trauma to front teeth. She has completed many smile makeovers that made her patients satisfied and gave beautiful smiles. Dr. Park persistently takes cosmetic courses to keep the practice up to date. Dr. Park’s main goal is to deliver comfortable dentistry to patients to meet patients’ satisfaction.
