When a tooth is missing a large portion of its crown resulted from a decay, root canal treatment or a trauma, a crown is fabricated to protect the remaining tooth structure from a fracture which can jeopardize the restorability of the tooth.

Dental implant is basically titanium metal which is placed in your jaw bone. After your bone fuses with implant, a crown like restoration is placed on an implant Which replaces a missing tooth.

Invisalign is the one method of orthodontic treatment which uses a series of clear, removable aligners to move your teeth to more desirable positions.
