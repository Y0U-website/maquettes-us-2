Dr. Mouni Damineni, DDS

Dr. Mouni was born and raised in India. She graduated with Bachelors in Dental Surgery in 2015 from KLE University in India. She moved to the United States in 2016 and completed her Masters of Science in Administration in 2018. In 2021, she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Michigan, where she developed a strong foundation in comprehensive, patient-centered care.

Dr. Mouni is deeply committed to lifelong learning and clinical excellence. She has earned the designation of Associate Fellow with the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, reflecting advanced training and expertise in implant dentistry. She has also been selected to receive Fellowship status with the International Congress of Oral Implantologists, further demonstrating her dedication to evidence based care and continued professional growth.

Dr. Mouni is passionate about using modern dental technology, including digital scanners, to improve diagnostic accuracy, streamline workflow, and enhance the patient experience. She is equally committed to community outreach and regularly participates in preschool oral health education programs to promote early prevention and healthy habits.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Mouni enjoys traveling and exploring different cuisines.

Dr. Richa Singhania

Dr. Richa Singhania is originally from India and brings a global perspective and strong passion for preventative oral health to the Four States Dental Care team. She completed her Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) in India, evaluated as equivalent to a U.S. DDS/DMD degree, and later earned a MSPP (Health Policy) from Georgia Institute of Technology. She then completed her dental residency at University of California, San Francisco.

With over a decade of experience in the dental profession, Dr. Singhania has worked across both clinical and community settings, consistently staying up to date with advancements in the field. Her background spans general dentistry, public heath programs, dental research, and oral health education for children and families.

Dr. Singhania has a special passion for helping patients overcome dental anxiety and believes that empowering individuals with oral health knowledge can positively impact their over well-being. She finds her greatest joy in building trust, forming meaningful patient relationships, and ensuring that every dental visit is a positive and comfortable experience.
