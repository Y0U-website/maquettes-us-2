About Fourth Street Family Dental

Family Dentist in Hermiston, OR

For over 20 years, Fourth Street Family Dental in Hermiston, OR has provided comprehensive dental services. As a family oriented practice, we provide dental healthcare to patients of all ages. We are committed to helping patients in every stage of life achieve and maintain their best dental health. Our dedicated staff and dentists strive to make every visit to our office as comfortable, efficient, and anxiety-free as possible. Several members of our team have been here for over 15 years. We work with each patient to help them understand their dental health and available treatment options .

Meet Greg B. Jones, DMD

Dr. Jones holds a Bachelor of Science degree from Oregon State University and earned his DMD (Doctor of Dental Medicine) from Oregon Health & Science University (OHSU). He started his own practice in 1994 and has been in the same location for more than 20 years.
