Appointments

We see patients by appointment only. We are currently accepting new patients. Please call our practice at 541-567-1693 to schedule an appointment. This also allows us to open up the appointment time to other patients.

Financial & Insurance Information

To reduce the costs to our patients, while maintaining the highest level of professional care, we offer patients a number of ways to pay for treatment. Payment is due at the time of service. We accept Visa, Discover, or MasterCard. Fourth Street Family Dental offers our patients opportunities to finance their treatment through CareCredit (OAC).

Insurance Information

Our office team electronically submits all insurance claims and works with insurance companies to maximize patient benefits and expedite processing.

We provide professional dental care to you, our patient family, not to an insurance company. We will be happy to help you understand your policy. After you receive treatment, we will send you a monthly statement. Most insurance companies respond within 4 to 6 weeks. If your statement does not reflect your insurance payment, please contact our office. After your insurance has paid, any remaining balance is your responsibility.

You are fully responsible for all fees charged by this office regardless of your insurance coverage.

Consultations

If you are looking for a second opinion on dental treatments diagnosed for you, we are happy to help. Making sure our patients receive the course of treatment they need and deserve is important to us. We make every effort to educate our patients and help them understand the treatments we provide.

If you are referred to our practice from another office or are looking for second-opinion, you are required to undergo a complete examination by our dentist so you can be accurately diagnosed. Before your appointment with us, please ensure all of your available chart notes and x-rays are transferred to our office.

Please note, many offices require 7 14 business days to transfer records. This information, along with your examination allow us to give you a comprehensive treatment estimate. It is imperative that we have all of your available dental and insurance information in hand by the time of your appointment to ensure we give you an accurate treatment estimate.
