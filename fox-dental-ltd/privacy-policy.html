3. No Storage of Protected Health Information (PHI)

Our website does not physically store any Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Any information you share with us online is used only to respond to your inquiries or improve your experience and is not considered PHI.

For communication, Verber Dental Group complies with the 10-Digit Long Code (10DLC) requirements to protect and secure text message communication. We follow the rules and guidelines set forth for 10DLC to ensure that our communications are transparent, responsible, and compliant with applicable regulations.

We use technical and organizational measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, please be aware that no website or internet transmission is completely secure.

We do not sell or share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share information with service providers who assist us in operating our website and business, but only to the extent necessary to provide these services.

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding the personal information we collect. You may request to review, update, or delete your personal information or withdraw your consent where applicable.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we do, we will post the updated policy on this page and update the “Last Updated” date at the top of the policy.
