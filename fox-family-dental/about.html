Isabelle Cyr

Isabelle was born in Montreal, Canada and has lived in Maine since 1981. She was raised in Biddeford where she graduated from Biddeford High School. She grew up in a bilingual home, which has allowed her to maintain a strong base in both English and French. Isabelle attended the University of New England for Dental Hygiene. Since graduating in 1999, she has been happily practicing in the South Portland area.

Isabelle continues to reside in Biddeford with her two children, Mitchell and Madelyn, who bring her great joy and keep her on her toes. She loves to travel and enjoys the beach, which makes Aruba a favorite vacation spot for her and her family. Whether it’s swimming in the summer or skiing in the winter, Isabelle loves the outdoors in Maine. She also has a passion for photography and can be found taking photographs of the beautiful Maine scenery, as well as her patients’ teeth.

Isabelle is thrilled to be joining the team at Fox Family Dental, and we are so excited to have her as well.

Alyssa Titus

Alyssa, a Florida native who grew up in southern New Hampshire, moved to Maine in 2018. She was drawn to all the outdoor activities Maine has to offer as well as the food and music scene in downtown Portland. She is excited to be establishing her roots here and knows Maine will be her forever home. Alyssa lives in Gorham with her husband, Andrew, and their dog, Jyn.

Dental hygiene has always been a passion of Alyssa’s. She loved going to the dentist since her first-ever visit and starting in high school would stick around the dental office after her appointments. She has been working as a dental hygienist since graduating from Ohio State University in 2015. Her favorite part of being a hygienist is working with patients to overcome their dental anxieties. Alyssa is thrilled to be a part of the Fox Family team.
