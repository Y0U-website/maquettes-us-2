Dental Implants

The titanium material used in modern implants has been extensively researched and proven to integrate successfully with human bone tissue through a process called osseointegration.

Advanced Technology for Precise Implant Placement

Fox River Periodontics PC utilizes cutting-edge technology to ensure optimal implant placement and long-term success. The practice’s CBCT scanner provides detailed three-dimensional images that reveal the exact dimensions and quality of your jawbone, allowing for precise implant planning.

This advanced imaging technology helps identify the ideal implant position, angle, and depth while avoiding crucial structures like nerves and sinuses. Such detailed planning significantly improves treatment predictability and helps ensure optimal aesthetic and functional outcomes.

The 3D printing capabilities available at the practice enable the creation of custom surgical guides that transfer the digital treatment plan to the actual surgical procedure. These guides help ensure millimeter-precise implant placement according to the predetermined plan.

WaterLase technology offers gentle tissue management during implant surgery, often reducing post-operative discomfort and promoting faster healing compared to traditional surgical techniques.

The implant process includes several key phases:

The entire treatment timeline typically ranges from three to six months, depending on whether additional procedures like bone grafting are needed and your individual healing characteristics.

  • Initial consultation and comprehensive treatment planning
  • Preparatory procedures, such as bone grafting when needed
  • Precise implant placement surgery
  • Healing period for osseointegration to occur
  • Final restoration attachment and adjustment

Osseointegration and Healing Timeline

Successful implant treatment depends on the osseointegration process, during which your jawbone gradually grows around and bonds with the titanium implant surface. This biological process typically takes three to four months but can vary based on bone quality and individual healing factors.

During the integration period, the implant site requires protection from excessive pressure or trauma that could disrupt the bonding process. Patients receive specific guidelines for eating, oral hygiene, and activity restrictions during this crucial healing phase.

Important healing considerations include:

Once osseointegration is complete, the implant can safely support the forces involved in normal chewing and function.

  • Following prescribed pain management protocols
  • Maintaining excellent oral hygiene around the implant site
  • Avoiding smoking or tobacco use that impairs integration
  • Eating soft foods and avoiding excessive chewing pressure
  • Attending regular follow-up appointments to monitor healing

Implant Restoration Options

After successful integration, dental implants can support various types of restorations depending on your specific needs and preferences. Single tooth replacement involves attaching a custom crown that matches your natural teeth in color, shape, and size.

Multiple missing teeth can be replaced with implant-supported bridges that eliminate the need to involve adjacent healthy teeth in the restoration process. For patients missing all their teeth, implants can support full-arch restorations that provide stability and function far superior to traditional dentures.

The restoration process involves taking precise impressions and working with skilled dental technicians to create prosthetic teeth that blend seamlessly with your natural smile.

Long-Term Success and Maintenance

Dental implants in Geneva have demonstrated excellent long-term success rates when patients maintain proper oral hygiene and attend regular dental checkups. The key to long-term implant success lies in preventing bacterial accumulation around the implant site that could lead to peri-implantitis.

Daily oral hygiene routines should include thorough cleaning around implant crowns using techniques similar to those used for natural teeth. Regular professional maintenance appointments allow for early detection and treatment of any developing problems.

Most patients find that caring for their implants becomes second nature once they adapt to the restoration, and many report that their implant teeth feel completely natural during eating and speaking.
