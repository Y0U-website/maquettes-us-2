Meet Our Doctor

Excellence You Can Feel. Results You Can Trust.

In 2024, Dr. Saleh achieved Diplomate status with the American Board of Periodontology and Dental Implant Surgery—a distinction held by fewer than one-third of all practicing periodontists.

Dr. Saleh began his clinical career at a prominent practice in Virginia, where he performed both simple and complex procedures, including full-arch dental implant cases utilizing Straumann implants, as well as a wide range of periodontal treatments.

He later returned to the Midwest and now practices at Fox River Periodontics, where he is dedicated to helping patients feel confident and cared for through expert periodontal and implant treatment. He is an active member of several professional organizations, including:

American Academy of Periodontology (AAP) American Dental Association (ADA) International Team of Implantology (ITI)

Dr. Saleh is also fluent in Spanish, in addition to his native language, Arabic.

In 1997, Dr. Noruzi was granted Diplomate status by the American Board of Periodontology. A Diplomate is a periodontist who has successfully completed a rigorous examination process and is certified by the American Board of Periodontology.

Dr. Noruzi was a member of the American Academy of Periodontology, the Academy of Osseointegration, the Illinois State Dental Society, and the Institute for Dental Implant Awareness, and was Past President of the Fox River Valley Dental Society and the Geneva Chamber of Commerce.

Dr. Noruzi resided in the area with his wife and their dog, Zeus.
