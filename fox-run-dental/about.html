Get to know your neighborhood dentist

Meet Dr. Alissa Edwards

Originally from Green Bay and now raising her family in the Fox Valley, Dr. Alissa Edwards brings 15 years of experience, compassion, and a whole-body wellness perspective to her practice at Fox Run Dental. She graduated from Marquette University in 2010, where she completed both her undergraduate studies and dental program, and remains deeply committed to ongoing learning and professional growth. A Qualified AADSM Dentist and member of the ADA and WDA, Dr. Edwards strives to stay at the forefront of dental care with a strong focus on airway and sleep-disordered breathing solutions.

Dr. Edwards founded Fox Run Dental with a clear vision: to create a practice that feels like home. Her approach combines technical skill with human connection—educating patients on how oral health impacts systemic health and offering modern, evidence-based care that’s always aligned with patients’ goals. Known for her warmth, knowledge, and dedication to whole-patient care, she builds trusted relationships that help patients feel safe, empowered, and understood.

At each visit, Dr. Edwards takes time to listen and collaborate, tailoring plans that reflect individual needs and values. Whether she&#x27;s guiding a family through preventive care or helping patients understand the impacts of sleep apnea and TMJ disorders, she leads with empathy and clarity. Her favorite part of her work? The opportunity to grow continuously while making a meaningful difference in people’s lives.

Outside the office, Dr. Edwards enjoys traveling and, above all, being a mom. She brings that same spirit of care and attentiveness to her patients, treating everyone with the kindness and dedication she gives her own family. For patients seeking a thoughtful, trusted partner in their dental health, Dr. Edwards is proud to be that person.

Your comfort and care come first

What to expect at your first visit

We know that visiting the dentist can sometimes feel intimidating, especially when you&#x27;re new to a practice. That&#x27;s why we focus on creating a calming and personalized experience from start to finish. During your first visit, our team will take the time to get to know you, discuss your medical and dental history, and understand your goals so we can tailor your care accordingly.

Patient experience Expect a warm welcome, a comforting atmosphere, and attentive care that puts your peace of mind first. Dr. Edwards and the team are all about building trust, providing clear explanations, and creating a judgment-free space where you can feel safe. Whether you&#x27;re here for a routine exam or something more urgent, we’ll walk you through every step and make sure you feel supported along the way.

Amenities and technology that keep you comfortable and confident

Our office environment is designed to help you relax while receiving the high-quality care you deserve. With modern technology, paperless forms, and thoughtful comforts available throughout the office, your visits are efficient, smooth, and catered to your needs.

Pillows and blankets

We offer cozy pillows and blankets during your appointments to help create a reassuring, home-like atmosphere. Sit back, relax, and know you’re in good hands.

Beverage and snack station

Enjoy complimentary drinks and snacks while you wait, making your visit as enjoyable as it is informative.

Laser dentistry

Our soft-tissue laser allows us to perform gentle procedures with precision and comfort, supporting treatments like frenectomies and periodontal care with minimal discomfort and downtime.

3D CBCT Imaging

Our advanced CBCT technology captures 3D images of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures, providing a detailed view for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning. This allows us to detect issues early and plan procedures with precision.

Digital X-rays

We use digital X-rays to provide high-quality images with minimal radiation exposure. These images are available instantly, helping us quickly identify dental concerns and make informed decisions about your care.

Intraoral Scanner

Our intraoral scanner captures detailed images of your teeth without the discomfort of traditional impressions. This technology enhances accuracy and comfort, ensuring a better overall experience.

Giving back to the community we call home

At Fox Run Dental, we’re more than a local dental practice, we’re an active part of the Appleton and Fox Valley community. Our team regularly volunteers at local events like the Appleton Butterfly Festival and partners with dental hygiene and assisting programs to support students and future dental professionals. We&#x27;re also a proud sponsor of our local hockey program. These efforts reflect our belief that oral health is deeply connected to overall wellness and a strong, supported community.

We’re also proud to invest in youth education and innovation by sponsoring the VEX Robotics Program at Xavier Middle School. Supporting hands-on STEM learning helps empower the next generation with confidence, creativity, and problem-solving skills—values we care deeply about both inside and outside the practice.

To make dental visits a little sweeter, we’ve partnered with Frio Mexican Treats. Every child who comes in for a hygiene visit receives a “free ice cream scoop” card as a fun reward for taking care of their smile. It’s one of our favorite ways to support a local business while creating positive, memorable dental experiences for kids.

As our practice grows, so does our commitment to giving back. We’re always exploring new ways to support local organizations and deepen our community impact. If you have a cause or idea for how we can contribute meaningfully, we’d love to hear from you, because when our neighbors thrive, we all do.
