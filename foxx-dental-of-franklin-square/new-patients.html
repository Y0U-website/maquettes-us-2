Appointment Policy

When we make you appointment, we are reserving a room for your particular needs. We ask if you must change an appointment, please notify the office during normal business hours at least 72 hours prior to the scheduled time. This courtesy makes it possible to give your reserved room to another patient who may need it. We confirm via email, text, and phone calls. If you are not receiving these confirmations, please let us know.
