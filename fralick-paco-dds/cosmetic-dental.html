COSMETIC DENTISTRY Timber Drive Dental is all about your smile. Coffee, soda, and smoking can really take its toll on your teeth. Utilizing the latest cosmetic dentistry techniques and materials, we can restore or improve your appearance. We offer tooth whitening, fillings that are tooth colored, and with veneers and bonding techniques, we can get your smile as bright as you want it.

There are many ways to approach each case. We believe it is important to give our patients options and information so that you can make informed decisions. After an exam, we will have a consultation and share many considerations with you: tooth size, proportions, shade, shape, and contours as well as translucency, etc. We can discuss options to modify the gingival display (your gums) around the teeth which often affect one’s smile.

But what good is a nice smile if it’s not functional? Many cosmetic problems develop over time because of poor occlusions (bites), bruxism (grinding) or other habits. With our advanced training in occlusion, we can discuss the big picture with you as well, such as how your bite, muscles, and TMJ all work together as a system and determine to best restore or modify your teeth for a long-lasting result.

We are collaborative, when need be, working with other specialists to give you more options and the best chance of success. We often take a team approach with orthodontists, periodontists, and oral surgeons in putting your treatment plans together.

Communication between providers up front is the key, and of course, share that information with you in a way that is understandable, including options, timelines, costs, etc.

Our cosmetic dentistry work can restore your smile and confidence as well as functionality. Be sure to tell them that your smile came from Timber Drive Dental!
