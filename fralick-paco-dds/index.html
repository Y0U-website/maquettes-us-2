The practice

A DENTIST THAT PLACES PATIENT COMFORT FIRST Timber Drive Dental has been in operation for over 25 years. Our brand of dentistry is family-friendly and comfortable. A relaxed atmosphere goes a long way towards a comfortable dental experience, and we strive to give patients of ALL ages the BEST that dentistry has to offer right here in the Northwoods!

We offer flexible payment options and process insurance claims for our patients to help our patients get the dental work they need. It’s all a part of our dedication to service and your best smile!

Dr. Fralick was born and raised in Rhinelander and graduated from Rhinelander High School in 1986. Dr. Fralick attended UW-Eau Claire for undergraduate studies and graduated from Marquette University School of Dentistry in 1993.

Dr. Fralick opened Timber Drive Dental in 1995 and where he has been delivering quality patient care with state-of-the-art technologies to this day.

Additionally, Dr. Fralick also directed the dental department at the Petersen Health Care Nursing Home for three years providing direct care for 500 elderly and handicapped patients.
