The practice

We are an independent practice dedicated to focussing on the individual and finding the best creative solution for your unique dental needs. We provide honest consultations, fair fees, and high quality work in a comfortable and compassionate atmosphere.

Our office has been in operation for over 50 years, and owned and operated by Dr. Lammer since 1993. Many of our patients have been with us for over 50 years (which we commemorate with a complimentary bottle of champagne). We hope you’ll join our community of satisfied patients.
