Privacy Policy

Who else is involved

Running a website means other companies are in the loop. Ours are:

We also disclose information when the law requires it — a court order, a subpoena, or a regulator with the authority to demand it.

  • Leap AI builds and hosts this website.
  • Simple Analytics produces the anonymous visit statistics above.
  • Matterport hosts the embedded 3D tour of our office, so your browser requests those files directly from them.
  • Vimeo hosts embedded video, so your browser requests those files directly from them.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies. There are no advertising pixels on it, and nothing here follows you to other websites.

How long we keep it

We keep the messages you send us for as long as we need them to care for you and to meet our record-keeping obligations, and no longer than the law allows.

Children

We treat a young patient’s information the same way we treat everyone’s. Where a parent or guardian submits it on their behalf, we deal with that adult.

Your choices

Ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, and we will do so unless the law requires us to keep it. Depending on where you live you may have further rights under your state’s privacy law; ask and we will honor them. Either way, the answer starts with contacting us — we do not charge patients for this.

Security

This website is served over an encrypted connection, and form submissions travel encrypted. No website can promise perfect security, and we will not pretend otherwise.

Changes

When our practices change, this page changes with them and the date below moves. This version took effect on August 4, 2026.
