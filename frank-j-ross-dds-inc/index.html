The practice

Dr. Frank J. Ross and his staff are committed to offering you the best available dental treatment utilizing the latest technology. In order to provide you with this, they minimally attend 40 hours of professional continuing education classes throughout the year.

We are very proud of the fact that our practice has grown almost exclusively by word of mouth referrals. We appreciate your continued confidence and trust in our practice and invite you to refer your family and friends. Thank you for visiting our website!

To better serve our community, Dr. Ross accepts a vast variety of insurance plans. We also offer payment plans, especially helpful for those patients without insurance. Our front desk team is trained and prepared to advocate and manage insurance claims and services, as well as answer any questions you may have.

Allow us to aid you in getting the most from your dental insurance benefits. We take pride in being professional, helpful and ethical.
