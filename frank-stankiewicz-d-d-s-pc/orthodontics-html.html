Six Month Smiles

Myobrace

Myobrace is an innovative, preventive orthodontic system that addresses the underlying causes of malocclusion (crooked teeth), keeping kids smiles healthy and functional at every stage of dental development. Typically recommended for young patients, as early as three years of age, this treatment option is a great way to avoid the need for more advanced, painful orthodontic interventions later in life.

Underlying Causes of Orthodontic Concerns

Many people believe that misaligned teeth are hereditary. Actually, the underlying causes of orthodontic issues may have much more to do with healthy habits than winning the genetic lottery. Myobrace works by addressing developing concerns as they occur to allow teeth to develop and erupt in their ideal natural position rather than moving them after they’ve already erupted.
