WHY CHOOSE FRANKFORT SMILES DENTAL

We have the expertise that will make you smile again and again

Invisalign

Dr. Zoladz is a Gold Provider for Invisalign. Invisalign is an alternative to traditional metal braces and involves straightening teeth with clear aligners. Invisalign is suitable for most adult and teenage cases.

Preventive Health

Maintaining the health of your teeth is our goal. We provide periodontal therapy to those with gum disease and believe prevention with regular cleanings is crucial. We also have the latest technology in oral cancer screenings.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Our office performs a wide array of cosmetic services including whitening, veneers, porcelain crowns, and bonding. If you have any questions regarding any of these procedures, please ask Dr. Zoladz.

Crowns

Crowns are used to restore structure or provide strength to teeth in need. Our office offers metal free crowns and uses 3D digital impressions.

Tooth Replacement

Implants, bridges, partial dentures and full dentures are all methods to replace teeth. Dr. Zoladz can help you decide which option is best for you.

All On 4

The doctors at Frankfort Smiles Dental work with an oral surgeon and a lab technician to have all the teeth in one arch replaced with just 4 implants.
