What Are Regular Routine Checkups?

Routine dental checkups, or dental examinations, are regular dental appointments for promoting proper oral health and hygiene. These checkups, usually scheduled six months apart, involve examining your teeth for early signs of dental complications and removing plaque, tartar, and debris from your teeth. These checkups are a form of preventive dentistry and are crucial for strong and healthy teeth.

Benefits of Routine Dental Checkups

Preserve

Routine dental checkups are immensely beneficial, especially if you’re consistent with them. For starters, these checkups help diagnose dental complications early on before they spiral out of control. That way, you can preserve your oral health and sidestep costly treatments for treating the now-worsened complications in the future. Sometimes the checkups can pick up life-threatening dental issues like mouth cancer.

Techniques

Dental examinations also provide an opportunity for thoroughly cleaning your teeth. Dentists use various techniques to clean the plaque, tartar, and debris off your teeth. They’ll use a scaler to scale the tartar off the surface and crevices of your teeth. This is followed by root planing, which involves smoothing the surface of the teeth’s roots to remove tartar and cementum.

Advice

The dentist will also provide professional guidance and advice on maintaining oral health during these checkups. The Franklin dentist will tailor their advice to your specific dental situation, meaning the advice you get applies directly to you. Taking the advice and instruction to heart will guarantee strong and healthy teeth for the long term.

What to Expect During Your Routine Dental Checkup

Dental Cleaning

The first step in the routine dental examination is dental cleaning. Cleaning your teeth and gums give dentists a clean slate to examine your teeth and mouth for signs of dental complications. This cleaning removes plaque and tartar in the teeth, which are responsible for gum disease and tooth decay. The dentist uses scalers and other equipment to ensure spotless teeth.

Dental Examination

Once your teeth are clean, the dentist will now conduct a series of dental exams to determine how healthy your teeth and gums are. First, the dentist will check for tooth decay by looking out for signs of cavities.

Treatment Planning and Oral Hygiene Instructions

The dentist will create a treatment plan (if necessary) based on the dental examination findings. The plan will detail treatment options and their corresponding timeline for your dental issues. It’s your responsibility to keep up with the appointments indicated in the treatment plan.

The dentist will also provide you with instructions for preserving and improving your oral health. These instructions include proper oral hygiene habits, product recommendations, and foods to avoid for healthy teeth.

How Do I Care for My Teeth Between Regular Visits to the Dentist?

Taking care of your teeth in between dental visits is as easy as brushing your teeth for two minutes twice daily and flossing them once every day. Remember also to brush your tongue while brushing your teeth.

You should also rinse your mouth with a fluoride mouthwash to remove debris and food particles from your mouth. Steer clear of sugary and starchy foods that promote the growth of cavities. Also, do the same with pigmented foods, especially red wine, and coffee, which can stain your teeth.
