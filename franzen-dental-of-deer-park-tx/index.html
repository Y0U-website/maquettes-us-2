Implant crowns are prosthetic tooth restorations designed to replace a missing tooth by utilizing a dental implant as its foundation. Unlike traditional dental crowns that are affixed to natural teeth, implant crowns are anchored to the titanium implant posts, providing a more stable and durable solution.

Implant crowns are attached to the implant posts through an abutment, which acts as a connector. The abutment is placed on the implant, and the crown is then secured onto the abutment. Commonly used materials for implant crowns include porcelain, ceramic, and zirconia, as they offer natural aesthetics and excellent durability.

The process begins with a thorough examination, X-rays, and a discussion of the patient’s dental and medical history to determine if they are suitable candidates for implant crowns.

We send you to an oral surgeon where the dental implant is surgically placed by a specialist into the jaw. Healing and osseointegration (fusion of the implant with the bone) may take a few months.
