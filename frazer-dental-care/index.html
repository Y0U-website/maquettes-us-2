The practice

We are conveniently located in Frazer, PA & are here for all your dental needs. As a Chester County dentist, our practice happily serves our friends & neighbors in nearby Malvern. With a focus on family & cosmetic dentistry, we are happy to help you achieve a beautiful smile in our comfortable, safe & friendly environment.

We look forward to providing you & your family with the dental excellence you deserve.

We maintain the highest standards of sterilization & patient safety, including those of the Centers for Disease Control, in order to best safeguard your health & well-being.

As the online home of your Frazer dentist, this website is intended to provide you with basic information about our practice & the dental services we provide to our community & to Malvern & the rest of Chester County. If you would like to find out more about Frazer Dental Care or the care we provide, please call us or set up an appointment today.

Our low-cost in-house dental plan can cover your entire family. For a small annual fee, you’ll receive free regular cleanings & general care. Crowns, fillings & bridges are covered at a lower out-of-pocket cost. Call us today for details!
