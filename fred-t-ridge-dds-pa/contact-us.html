Dental Plans

Pinehurst Dentistry accepts insurance from a variety of providers. Our office staff will be happy to file your claims for your dental treatment. We will accept assignment of benefits from your insurance, however we will collect your percentage at the time services are rendered. Contact our office for more information.

Financial Policy

Payment for any portion not covered by your insurance is due when services are rendered.

  • We accept cash, checks, Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover
  • We provide third party financing through Care Credit
