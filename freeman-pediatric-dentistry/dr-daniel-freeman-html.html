Dr. Daniel Freeman | Freeman Pediatric Dentistry in Memphis TN

Dr. Daniel Freeman and his team are dedicated to providing quality care for all your children. Dr. Freeman has been practicing pediatric dentistry since 2001. After undergraduate training at Christian Brothers University, Dr. Freeman attended dental school at the University of Tennessee, Memphis. He completed one year of advanced post-doctoral training at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. Additionally, Dr. Freeman completed two years of specialty training in Pediatric Dentistry at the University of Tennessee, Memphis. Dr. Freeman is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist.

He is a member of the American Dental Association, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, American Orthodontic Society, Christian Dental Society and the Memphis Dental Society. In addition to pediatric dentistry, Dr. Freeman also performs comprehensive and interceptive orthodontics.

Dr. Freeman is an active member in his church and enjoys running, cooking, and spending time with his family. He and his wife have five children.
