Services

Smiles Made Simple

From first tooth to teen years, we keep care gentle, prevention-first, and easy for parents. First Visit (Age 1+) Short, sweet, kid-friendly. We say hello, do a gentle cleaning and fluoride, check teeth and gums, and take x-rays only if needed. You’ll leave with clear next steps and tips that actually work at home. Prevention

Cleanings, Fluoride, Sealants

The easiest cavity to fix is the one that never happens. Regular checkups, professional cleanings, fluoride to strengthen enamel, and sealants to protect the grooves of back teeth keep smiles healthy with minimal fuss.

Treatment Options

Comfort-First

When cavities happen, we treat them conservatively and comfortably. For anxious kiddos, nitrous oxide (“laughing gas”) can take the edge off—fast on, fast off. If a child needs extensive work or can’t safely complete treatment in-office, we offer hospital dentistry with an anesthesiologist so everything’s done in one visit.

Special Needs Care

Every child deserves kind, effective care. We tailor visits to sensory, developmental, and medical needs with patient pacing, clear previews, and familiar routines. Tell us what helps—headphones, social stories, appointment time of day—and we’ll plan together. Request Appointment Do you offer desensitization visits? Yes—short “get-to-know-you” appointments can help build comfort. Can we complete forms in advance? We’ll text you the forms to complete—or you can fill them out anytime on our Forms & Policies page.

Emergencies

For patients of record, we’re here with guidance after hours. Toothache, swelling, or accidents happen—call the office and follow the voicemail instructions. For a knocked-out adult tooth, handle by the crown, gently rinse, place back in the socket (or in milk), and call right away.
