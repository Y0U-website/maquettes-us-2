Meet The Doctors

Dr. Kendon Knowles

Dr. Kendon Knowles is a Cosmetic Dentist at Fremont Hills Dental Centre in Ozark, Missouri. He grew up near the Rocky Mountains in northern Utah, and attended Utah State University for his undergraduate studies. For Dental School, Dr. Knowles attended Roseman University of Health Sciences where he earned a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree.

Dr. Knowles and his wife, Misty, enjoy staying busy with their growing family. Together, they share three wonderful children: Nash, Nyla, and Kinley. When he’s not being an awesome Dentist, you can find him spending time with his family or coaching his son’s soccer team!

A fun fact about Dr. Knowles is he learned he wanted to be a Dentist in a taxi in West Africa! During his ride, he met another Dentist and the conversation they shared sparked his interest in the dental field. When he returned to the states, Dr. Knowles shadowed his family Dentist, which solidified his love for dentistry!

Dr. Kalob Ihler, DMD

Dr. Kalob Ihler is a dedicated general dentist passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. Originally from Malad, Idaho, he pursued his undergraduate degree in Biomedical Sciences at BYU Idaho before earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from LECOM in Florida.

From an early age, Dr. Ihler was drawn to the medical field, but dentistry stood out as the perfect blend of healthcare, artistry, and personal connection. He takes great pride in building his patients’ confidence and ensuring they feel comfortable and valued in his care. His top priority is to create an environment where every patient feels at ease, knowing they are receiving the highest quality treatment.

Outside of the office, Dr. Ihler enjoys spending time with his wife, Amber, and their soon to be family of six. As a family, they love exploring the outdoors—hiking, camping, and visiting farms. Whether he’s in the office or out on an adventure, Dr. Ihler’s goal is always to bring smiles to those around him.

Dr. Matthew Leavitt, DMD

Dr. Matthew Leavitt is a general dentist committed to providing high-quality, compassionate care to every patient. He earned his Bachelor’s degree in Biology from BYU–Idaho and went on to complete his dental education at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Leavitt’s favorite part of dentistry is helping patients overcome dental concerns and restoring their confidence through healthy smiles. His core philosophy centers on integrity, patient safety, and satisfaction.

Originally from Las Vegas, Dr. Leavitt is deeply committed to giving back to his community and has participated in various mission trips and service projects. He knew from a young age that he wanted a career centered around helping others, and dentistry allows him to do that every day.

Outside of work, Dr. Leavitt enjoys mountain biking and spending time with his wife, Des, whom he married in 2015. Together they have two children, and a lovable lab/shepherd mix named Bumi.
