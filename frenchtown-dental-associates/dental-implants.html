Types of Dental Implants

Single Dental Implant

These implants replace a single missing tooth with a custom crown attached to a titanium implant. They are perfect for restoring both appearance and function.

Implant Supported Bridges

Ideal for replacing multiple missing teeth, this solution uses implants as anchors, eliminating the need to modify adjacent healthy teeth.

Implant Supported Dentures

Strategically placed implants provide a stable and secure fit for full or partial dentures, offering superior comfort and functionality.

Full Arch with Only Four Implants

Restore an entire arch of teeth with just four implants, an efficient and cost-effective option for full-mouth rehabilitation.

Are You a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults with missing teeth and healthy gums are great candidates for dental implants. Adequate jawbone density is important for supporting the implant, but bone grafting procedures are available if additional support is needed. If you’re ready to restore your smile, a consultation with our team will determine if implants are the right choice for you.
