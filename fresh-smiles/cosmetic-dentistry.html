Teeth Whitening

Even with diligent brushing and exceptional oral hygiene, your smile may dull over time. Luckily, you can restore the whiteness to your teeth with our teeth whitening options. We provide both in-office and take-home treatments, so you can improve your smile in whatever setting feels most comfortable. Veneers

Don’t let chips, cracks, misalignment, or gaps slow you down. Veneers offer a permanent solution to beautifying your smile. These thin shells are applied directly to the front of your teeth, and require little or no preparation of the teeth before they are placed. Fresh Smiles offers both composite and porcelain veneers for a natural-looking smile. With these custom crafted veneers, you’ll get the feel and function of natural teeth. And with proper maintenance, your new veneers can last for decades.
