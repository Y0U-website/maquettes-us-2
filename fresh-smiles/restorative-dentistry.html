Restorative Dentistry

A perfect smile is hard to come by, and teeth can be surprisingly fragile at times. If your smile has been damaged or worn down over time, Fresh Smiles offers advanced restorative treatments to give you your best smile yet. Dr. Bower has experience with many unique dental problems and can suggest a treatment that will fit your needs. If you’re suffering from tooth loss, damage or decay, contact Fresh Smiles today for friendly restorative dentistry in Plymouth Meeting.

Dental Crowns in Plymouth Meeting

Crowns are used to restore teeth that are damaged or decayed. At Fresh Smiles, Dr. Bower uses intraoral imaging technology to create a crown that matches the exact shape and size of the tooth. When the crown is ready, Dr. Bower will permanently place it onto the tooth using a strong dental adhesive. Once complete, your tooth will be perfectly restored and you can walk out of the office with a new smile.

Dental Implants in Plymouth Meeting

Dentures, Partials & Implant-Supported Teeth

For patients who have suffered severe tooth loss, you have options to restore your smile. Depending on how many teeth have been lost and the health of your gums or remaining teeth, dentures and partial dentures can be the right solutions for you. For the right candidates, implant-supported dentures can offer the security of a dental implant with the full arch solution you may need. Schedule a consultation with Dr. Bower and let’s create a treatment plan together.
