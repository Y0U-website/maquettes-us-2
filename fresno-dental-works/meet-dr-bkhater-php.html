Meet Bridgit M. Khater, DDS

MEET DR. BRIDGIT KHATER

Bridgit M. Khater, DDS is a proud graduate of the University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry, where she earned her dental degree in 2008. She went on to complete a General Practice Residency at Community Regional Medical Center in Fresno, gaining advanced training in comprehensive and hospital-based dentistry.

For more than 12 years, Dr. Khater served as the Assistant Chief of Dental Services at the Fresno VA Hospital, where she cared for veterans and trained dental residents. Her leadership role allowed her to share her passion for excellence in dentistry while mentoring the next generation of dentists.

In 2022, Dr. Bridgit Khater joined her husband in private practice, where she continues to provide the same compassionate, personalized care that has defined her career. She is known for her gentle approach, attention to detail, and dedication to helping patients feel comfortable and confident about their smiles.

When she’s not caring for patients, Dr. Khater enjoys spending time with her family, traveling, and exploring new experiences that inspire balance and joy in her life.
