Meet Roger A. Khater DDS

MEET DR. ROGER KHATER

Roger A. Khater, DDS has been part of the practice since 2009. Dr. Khater completed his undergraduate studies at UC Davis where he earned his bachelors degree in Biology. He then went on to University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco and graduated in 2008 with his DDS degree.

After completing his residency at Community Regional Medical Center where he treated many complex cases and performed full mouth rehabilitations, he joined the practice. Dr. Khater takes a special interest in the realm of cosmetic dentistry, improving patient’s smiles and function. His experience with CAD/CAM technology has enabled him to fabricate precision crowns for patients on the same day without the need for impressions or temporaries. His skill set allows him to provide the most current and up-to-date treatment that focuses on addressing his patients concerns through comprehensive care.

"We love what we do, and I believe it shows. Technology and innovation has transformed the dental experience."

"I think our office is different. Our surroundings are modern and simple. It’s our privilege to serve our patients in a relaxed, professional environment."
