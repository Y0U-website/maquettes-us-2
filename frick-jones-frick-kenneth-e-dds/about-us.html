Dr. Maggie Shaw

Dr. Maggie Shaw was born and raised in Bluefield, Virginia. She received her bachelor’s degree in health and exercise science with a minor in biology and chemistry from Wake Forest University. She attended Virginia Commonwealth University for dental school where she developed an interest in Periodontics after shadowing the residents. She loved the surgical side of saving teeth and enjoyed the lifelong patient relationships formed throughout the process.

Upon graduation from dental school, Dr. Shaw enrolled at the Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC) in Charleston, SC for her periodontal residency. She had the privilege of serving as Chief Resident in a program that is renowned for its focus on clinical exposure and case difficulty. Through her extensive training, Dr. Shaw is now confident and proficient with immediate implants, implants in the esthetic zone, ridge and sinus augmentations, soft tissue grafting, traditional and laser periodontal surgery (LANAP and LAPIP), and IV sedation.

MUSC is where Dr. Shaw had the pleasure of meeting Dr. Chris Conzett. As co-residents, they worked closely together treating patients with the same level of empathy, professionalism, and patient-centered care. They developed a strong working relationship and the reason they are so excited about the opportunity to work together again.

Dr. Shaw is a diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology and an active member of the Spartanburg Dental Association, South Carolina Dental Association, American Academy of Periodontology and Southern Periodontal Association. In her free time, she enjoys playing golf, soccer, hiking, and biking with her husband and children.
