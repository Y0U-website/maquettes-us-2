Financing Information

At Piedmont Periodontics & Implant Dentistry, we make it easy to invest in your dental health.

We’re a non-contracted (out-of-network) practice, which means we’re not limited by insurance networks and can focus entirely on your care.

Payment is due at the time of your appointment, and we gladly accept cash, checks, and all major credit cards.

We also offer flexible financing options to make your care more accessible.
