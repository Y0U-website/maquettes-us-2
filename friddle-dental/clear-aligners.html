Clear Aligners FAQ

  • Are Dandy Aligners the same as Invisalign? Dandy Aligners and Invisalign are both clear aligner systems designed to straighten teeth using removable trays. At Friddle Dental, we use Dandy Aligners because they offer great results, high-quality trays, a clear appearance, and increased comfort at a more affordable price point than Invisalign for many patients.
  • How do clear aligners work? Clear aligners work by using a series of custom trays that gently move your teeth over time. Each set of aligners makes small adjustments to your tooth position. As you progress through the trays, your teeth gradually shift closer to their planned alignment.
  • Are clear aligners comfortable? Most patients find clear aligners comfortable because they do not use metal brackets or wires. You may feel mild pressure when starting a new set of trays, which is normal and usually means the aligners are working. Dandy Aligners are designed to fit closely and comfortably over your teeth.
  • Why should I choose dentist-supervised aligners instead of online aligners? Dentist-supervised clear aligners allow your treatment to be monitored by a dental professional who can evaluate your teeth, gums, bite, and progress. With online aligners, you may not receive the same level of in-person care. At Friddle Dental, Dr. Friddle can make sure your treatment is progressing safely and efficiently.
  • How much do clear aligners cost? The cost of clear aligners depends on your specific case and treatment needs. Friddle Dental offers financing options, and patients can start today for $499 with low monthly payments available.
