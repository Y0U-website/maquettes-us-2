Meet Dr. Cody Friddle YOUR Alexandria Dentist

About me

Dentistry has always been a central part of my life. I grew up in Arkansas, where my dad was a dentist. After graduating with a BS in Biology from Baylor University in Waco, and finishing dental school at Baylor College of Dentistry in Dallas, I returned to Arkansas and worked with him for over 12 years. It’s fair to say that dentistry is in my blood.

After starting practice, I acquired additional training in cosmetic, orthodontic, TMJ and implant dentistry. I began an extensive course of continuing education and after countless hours of study and effort, I received my post-doctoral education by becoming a Fellow of the prestigious Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies. This education has given me the skills, knowledge and experience to provide the highest quality of care for my patients.

I’m now a dentist in Alexandria, VA, where I recently opened up my own practice. Drawing on my extensive experience in the field, I wanted to create a practice that offered exceptionally high-quality dental work in a truly stress-free environment. Friddle Dental was launched as a result. I’ve made it my business to offer the latest and most effective treatments to my patients.

To this end, I’m continually exceeding my requirements for Continuing Education in Dentistry to ensure that my knowledge and skills are always up-to-date.

Solving the Age-Old Dentistry Problem

The problem with running a dental practice is that by and large, clients don’t actually want to be there. A visit to the dentist probably means you’re in pain and you’re worried that a dentist is going to inflict even more suffering. There’s a reason why Hollywood depicts us as the bad guys.

The thing is, it doesn’t have to be that way.

With the proper technology (that I have) and the right expertise (which I also have), dental work should be as stress free as possible and an experience that results in beautiful smiles and a healthier life. It can and should be a hugely positive experience.

Beyond Dentistry

Outside of my practice, I’m a family man. If you think root canals are tough, try raising 4 teenagers. Yep, I said 4. I love taking walks with my wife Kathryn along the Potomac, playing golf, coaching my kids’ sports teams, being active in my church and kids schools, traveling, and enjoying great restaurants in Old Town. And with that, you know almost as much about me as I do.

To find out more about my practice and how I can make your visit to the dentist a pleasant one, please don’t hesitate to contact me.
