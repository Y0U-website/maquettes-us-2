The practice

Dentistry has always been in my blood. After earning my BS in Biology from Baylor University and my dental degree from Baylor College of Dentistry in Dallas, I practiced alongside my dad in Arkansas for over 12 years before opening Friddle Dental in Alexandria, VA.

With advanced training in cosmetic, orthodontic, TMJ, and implant dentistry and a Fellowship at the Las Vegas Institute I built this practice to redefine what "going to the dentist" feels like: stress-free, honest, and genuinely comfortable.

Step into a calming, thoughtfully designed office where every detail is intentional from soft lighting and warm seating to the gentle attention of our team. We’ve built a space that helps you exhale before your visit even begins.
