For more than a decade, Dr. Dovid Friedman has enjoyed helping people feel better about themselves by creating beautiful smiles which have improved function and comfort. He is dedicated to his profession and finds it incredibly rewarding to make such a positive impact on his patients’ lives during this critical phase of growth and development.

Dr. Friedman attended the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine for his Doctorate of Dental Medicine. After that, he completed a year of advanced education in general dentistry and two years of orthodontic residency to become a specialist in the field of orthodontics.

Since graduating, Dr. Friedman has taken many hours of continuing education, exceeding the required amount for state licensure. The courses and seminars he attends keep him abreast of current changes in technology and enable him to provide the most advanced treatment options for his patients.
