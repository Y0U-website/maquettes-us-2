Cosmetic Dentistry

Our team appreciates that when you show the world your best possible smile, your confidence soars. Through Digital Smile Design they create an accurate before-and-after visual of your smile with the recommended treatment plan.

They take pride in tapping into their artistic side to envision the procedures it will take to beautify your smile. By partnering with each patient, the team creates a treatment plan that’s customized to them.

Bonding

Bonding uses tooth-colored composite material to repair chips, small gaps, or discoloration. It may also protect root surfaces exposed by gum recession. The result is a natural-looking, beautifully restored tooth.

Botox

Where art meets science. Our dentist has an extensive background in injectables. We evaluate the structure of the face and neck to create a smooth appearance that looks natural like you only younger-looking.

Digital Smile Design

We love helping patients visualize their future smile. With this technology, we take images & digitally model a realistic preview of your results. We use Digital Smile Design alongside bonding, veneers, implants, and Invisalign.

Candidpro

Clear aligners

We straighten teeth using clear aligners that are comfortable, convenient, and nearly invisible. Patients wear them 20 22 hours per day, with most treatments lasting 10 12 months. Our dentist monitors progress every step of the way.

veneers

Veneers create symmetry and enhance the appearance of teeth by correcting chips, gaps, discoloration, & shape concerns. Our dentist removes an amount of enamel to ensure a precise fit & uses high-quality porcelain for long-lasting, natural-looking results.

teeth Whitening

Professional whitening delivers the best results with the least sensitivity. We partner with Zoom, the industry leader, to brighten teeth several shades using medical-strength gel and custom-fit trays.

enameloplasty

Also called tooth contouring, enameloplasty smooths uneven edges, chips, or pointy teeth. Using fine polishing tools, our dentist reshapes the enamel for a more symmetrical, aesthetically pleasing smile.
