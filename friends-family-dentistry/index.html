The practice

Named for that happy circumstance when friends feel like family, the staff at Friends & Family Dentistry firmly believes that your oral health is inseparable from your overall well-being and systemic health.

Our practice is built on the strong, long-term relationships among a dentist, hygienist, assistant, and office manager professionals who have worked together in various offices over the years.

Our experience has helped us value the qualities and systems that make a dental practice run smoothly, without any chaos.

Whether it’s a routine checkup or a complete smile transformation, our focus is on lasting wellness. We approach each patient holistically, caring for individuals of all ages from children to seniors.

We take pride in offering the latest advances and high-tech equipment in our five state-of-the-art operatories.

Our care is built on a foundation of well-being, balance and integrity.

Our team values work-life balance, supporting one another both professionally and personally to maintain a positive, sustainable environment.

As a community-focused, relationship-oriented practice, we prioritize genuine connections with our patients, our team, and our neighborhood.
