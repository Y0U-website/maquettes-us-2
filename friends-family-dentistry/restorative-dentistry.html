Restorative Dentistry

We specialize in the procedures that bring your mouth and teeth back into alignment and functioning at peak efficiency, including tooth extractions, root canal therapy, gum contouring with a soft tissue laser & dentures.

Staying ahead of advances in technology, we’re continually investing in new equipment and treatments that can best restore your mouth to its healthiest state.

Dentures

Denture technology has come a long way. Modern dentures look natural, fit comfortably, and restore confidence. We offer both traditional acrylic dentures and Valplast flexible partials.

Valplast is thinner & more comfortable, while traditional dentures are easier to repair. We help patients choose the option that best meets their needs and teach them how to care for their new smile.

tooth Extractions

We evaluate when a tooth needs to be removed whether it’s severely decayed, damaged, or interfering with proper tooth development.

Our dentist’s goal is always to remove teeth with as little discomfort as possible, often using nitrous oxide to help patients feel at ease.

gum Contouring

An uneven or gummy smile can be corrected and reshaped through gum contouring. We’ve invested in a soft tissue laser for the utmost precision. It results in a more symmetrical smile, while providing for faster healing, less bleeding and discomfort than traditional methods. You can expect minimal downtime. Our dentist will use a local anesthetic to numb the gum area.

Root Canal Therapy

When decay or infection reaches the root of a tooth, prompt treatment is essential. Using specialized instruments, our dentist removes infected tissue and cleans the canal to relieve pain and prevent further issues.

Many premolars and some molars can be treated in-house; more complex cases may be referred to an endodontist.

Dental Implants

For esthetics & functionality, dental implants are one of the longest lasting and most stable methods of replacing a missing tooth. When you lose a tooth, the remaining teeth shift and tilt toward the space, which undermines the foundation of your bite. You also begin to lose bone. We recommend replacing any lost teeth.

Since the root of a dental implant is made from a biocompatible titanium, it naturally integrates with your bone. We collaborate with some of the best oral surgeons in the area to place the implant in the correct position. Our dentist can create the dental implant crown to perfectly match your other teeth.
