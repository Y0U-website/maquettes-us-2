Dr. Oldroyd grew up in Flagstaff, Arizona, and moved to Polson in 2026 with his wife, Natalie, after graduating from Midwestern University. Together, they have two children and are excited to be part of the Northwest Montana community.

When he’s not caring for patients, Dr. Oldroyd enjoys fishing, backpacking, and spending time with family and friends playing board games. He and his family are looking forward to getting to know the wonderful people of our area and putting down roots in their new home.

Dr. Oldroyd is passionate about dentistry, family, and the outdoors, and he is committed to providing high-quality, compassionate care to every patient he serves.
