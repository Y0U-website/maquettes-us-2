The practice

Patients at Frontier Family Dental can receive answers to all of their questions in addition to comprehensive services that help them maintain their health. Dr. Joung, a dentist in Graham WA, encourages patients to visit every six months for exams and cleanings so he can diagnose complications like cavities and gum disease in the early stages when treatment is most simple.

Using high-end technologies like digital X-rays and cone beam computed tomography (CT) scans, Frontier Family Dental is able to detect dental abnormalities and plan strategic treatment. The office is also equipped with an intraoral camera to capture detailed images of a patient’s teeth and gums.

Patients can trust Frontier Family Dental for common dental treatments like root canals , fillings , and extractions . They can also rely on the practice in the event of a dental emergency . Sedation is available for patients with stress or anxiety about the treatment they need.
