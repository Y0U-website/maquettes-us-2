The practice

We promise to serve you with kindness. Your health and comfort is our top priority. We are conveniently located off of Hwy. 50 in the Carson Tahoe Medical Center building.

We will evaluate your overall health and oral hygiene, your risk of tooth decay, root decay, and gum or bone disease. Check your bite and jaw for problems along with your need for tooth restoration or replacement.

The dentist will apply either a protective gel to your gums or a rubber shield to protect your gums.

Your dentist can provide you with a custom-made tray for at-home whitening.

Depending on which type you choose, dentures can either be implanted, rest on your gums, or attached by a clasp to an existing tooth. Veneers will attach to your existing teeth.

We are here to make sure you are back on your feet and smiling.
