ABOUT US

Our Office

Centrally located southwest of Downtown Reading just a few blocks off of Lancaster Ave, our office is convenient to all of Berks County and Lancaster County.

Caring Environment Professional Staff Cutting-Edge Facility Individualized Personal Treatment Our Team DR BRIAN FRUTCHEY Endodontist

Brian C Frutchey, DMD, was born and raised in Riverview Park. He attended Muhlenberg School District and graduated high school in 1991. Brian received his BS in Biochemistry/Cell Biology from Bucknell University in 1995. He received his DMD degree from the University of Pennsylvania in 2000 as the valedictorian of his class. Brian received his Endodontic certificate from Tufts University in 2002. From 2002-2004, Brian worked in a private practice setting in Boston and was a clinical instructor at Tufts University Endodontics department.

In 2004, Brian moved to Boulder, Colorado, and opened multiple Endodontic practices to serve the region. He spent 15 years in Colorado, where he met his wife, Alessia, and started a family.

The Frutchey’s are so very happy to be back in Pennsylvania. His son Carson (11) and daughter Charlie (8) are very fortunate to live close to their Gram, Pap, and Auntie! They all enjoy the kindness of the people here, Avalon, Fightin’ Phils, Penn State and Philadelphia Eagles Football.

Brian coaches Carson’s LaSalle boys’ basketball team. In his spare time, Brian enjoys playing golf, tennis, and Friday morning bowling league. Brian has been practicing Endodontics for over 20 years and is an active member of the American Association of Endodontists.
