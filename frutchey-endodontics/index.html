Brian C Frutchey, DMD, was born and raised in Riverview Park. He attended Muhlenberg School District and graduated high school in 1991. Brian received his BS in Biochemistry/Cell Biology from Bucknell University in 1995. He received his DMD degree from the University of Pennsylvania in 2000 as the valedictorian of his class. Brian received his Endodontic certificate from Tufts University in 2002.…

In 2004, Brian moved to Boulder, Colorado, and opened multiple Endodontic practices to serve the region. He spent 15 years in Colorado, where he met his wife, Alessia, and started a family.
