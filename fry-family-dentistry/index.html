The practice

New patient comprehensive exams become available periodically. Please call for availability.

We offer a variety of restorative materials to suit your functional and esthetic needs to correct teeth needing crowns or replace missing teeth.

We offer a variety of restorative options to suit your financial and cosmetic needs when treating damaged or decayed teeth.

Our office offers root canal therapy for routine endodontic therapy. If you require advance root canal therapy a referral to an endodontic specialist will be utilized.

We accommodate emergencies to evaluate pain and discomfort and determine the best plan for you.

Our office makes night guards for grinding, clenching and other TMD (Temporomandibular dysfunction) concerns.
