  • Before and After Braces Orthodontic treatment has helped countless individuals achieve the smile of their dreams. Braces, in particular, play a significant role in transforming smiles by correcting a variety of dental issues. Let’s explore the remarkable differences that occur before and after braces or Invisalign ® treatment. Common Dental Issues Before Braces Before getting braces, patients often experience various dental issues, including: Overcrowding: Teeth are too close together, causing crookedness and misalignment.
  • Spacing issues: Large gaps or spaces between teeth, affecting aesthetics and functionality.
  • Overbite: The upper front teeth overlap the lower front teeth excessively, leading to potential jaw or teeth damage.
  • Underbite: The lower front teeth protrude past the upper front teeth, causing uneven wear and bite problems.
  • Crossbite: One or more upper teeth bite inside the lower teeth, leading to misalignment and bite issues.
  • Open bite: The upper and lower front teeth do not touch when the mouth is closed, impacting chewing and speech

Upon completion of the braces treatment, patients experience significant improvements in their dental health and aesthetics:

  • Overcrowding: Teeth are too close together, causing crookedness and misalignment.
  • Spacing issues: Large gaps or spaces between teeth, affecting aesthetics and functionality.
  • Overbite: The upper front teeth overlap the lower front teeth excessively, leading to potential jaw or teeth damage.

  • Crossbite: One or more upper teeth bite inside the lower teeth, leading to misalignment and bite issues.
  • Open bite: The upper and lower front teeth do not touch when the mouth is closed, impacting chewing and speech
  • Proper alignment: Teeth are straightened and positioned correctly in the dental arch, resulting in a visually pleasing smile.
  • Improved bite: Overbites, underbites, crossbites, and open bites are corrected, leading to a more comfortable and functional bite.
  • Reduced risk of dental issues: Properly aligned teeth minimize the risk of tooth decay, gum disease, and uneven wear.
  • Boosted self-confidence: An attractive smile enhances self-esteem and can have a positive impact on personal and professional interactions.
