Azura

Dental Lab Specialist & Designer

Azura joined FS Lab in 2023, bringing with her 3 years of valuable experience as a dental assistant. Committed to making the best restorations, she trains annually in various aspects of lab work. Azura loves making a positive change in people’s lives through confidence in their teeth. In her free time, she loves being outdoors, gardening, and relaxing with her cats.

Amanda Dental Lab Technician & Ceramist Amanda has been with us since 2020 as a ceramist, with 20 prior years of dental assistant experience. She is always keeping up with the latest technology and techniques in order to make more realistic prothesis. Her favorite cases are All-On-Four’s with custom gingiva so she can show her creative side. In her spare time, Amanda enjoys spending time with family and floating the river.

Hewitt Chief Case Designer Hewitt joined our team in 2022. He has been all over the country training in digital dentistry, specifically in All-On-X, same day dentistry, and more. His favorite cases to design are All-On-X and he loves to see a before and after transformation that he has been a part of. In his free time, he enjoys working out and traveling.
