Whether you need to replace a single tooth or a full arch, our team has the experience, the technology, and the track record to help you eat, smile, and live with confidence agian.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Permanent Solution | Dental implants fuse with your jawbone and can last 20-30 years or longer with proper care, unlike bridges or partials that need replacement every 5-10 years.
  • Preserve Healthy Teeth | Unlike traditional bridges, implants do not require grinding down adjacent healthy teeth for support, protecting your natural tooth structure.
  • Prevent Bone Loss | Implants stimulate your jawbone just like natural tooth roots, preventing the bone deterioration and facial collapse that occurs with missing teeth.
  • Natural Look and Feel | Custom-designed crowns match your existing teeth perfectly in color, shape, and size, making your implant virtually undetectable.
  • Restore Full Chewing Power | Eat all your favorite foods without worry. Implants provide up to 70 % more biting force than bridges or partial dentures.
  • No Slipping or Discomfort | Permanently anchored in your jaw, implants eliminate the embarrassment and pain of loose partials or bridges that shift when you eat or speak.
  • Simple Maintenance | Care for your implants just like natural teeth with regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups at our Wilsonville office. No special cleaning solutions required.

Is dental implant surgery painful?

You won’t feel anything during the procedure. We use IV sedation and local anesthetics for complete comfort. Afterward, most patients manage any soreness with over-the-counter medication and say the recovery was much easier than expected.

How long does the implant process take from start to finish?

You can receive implants and temporary teeth in a single visit. The full process from placement to your permanent restoration takes three to six months while the implants integrate with your jawbone. You’ll have fully functional teeth the entire time.

Are dental implants better than dentures?

For most patients, yes. Implants are fixed in place, restore up to 90 % of your natural bite force, preserve jawbone health, and can last a lifetime. Dentures sit on top of the gums, require adhesive, and need replacement every 5 to 7 years as bone changes.

How much time off work will I need?

Most full arch patients take two to three days to rest. Single implant patients often return to work the next day. You’ll want to stick to soft foods for the first couple of weeks, but you’ll have functional teeth from day one.
