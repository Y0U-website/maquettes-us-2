The practice

At Full Smile Dentistry, we see infants as young as 6 months old to monitor tooth development and catch issues early. Many offices avoid treating tiny patients, but nurturing those first baby teeth sets your child up for a lifetime of outstanding oral health. We provide complete dental care for patients of all ages.

Backed by a spirit of empathy and connection, we take time explaining diagnoses and treatments, so you can make informed decisions about your oral health. You will always have a voice when it comes to your care. Whether you need a routine cleaning or complex procedure like dentures, we offer kind, personalized care.

Full Smile Dentistry was founded by Dr. Amy Full, a Marshall native herself. After shadowing her childhood dentist, she knew dentistry was her calling. (So much so, that she would always ask her mom when her next dental appointment was!) She feels privileged to now care for the close-knit community that raised her.

We look forward to bringing more smiles to Marshall with thoughtful, compassionate care. Contact us today to schedule your first appointment!
