Dr. Bret Fullenkamp was born and raised in the Dayton area. After completing his undergraduate degree in cellular and molecular biology with honors at Ohio University, he went on to complete his dental education at Case Western Reserve University. After graduating dental school, he returned home and has been practicing dentistry in the area since.

He has received advanced training in cosmetic dentistry, dental implants and TMJ disease, and is a member of several dental organizations, including the ADA, the Ohio Dental Association, the Dayton Dental Society and Spear Education.
