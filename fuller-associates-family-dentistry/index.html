The practice

Is going to the dentist frightening and uncomfortable for you? If so, you might consider a new dentist. Fuller Associates Family Dentistry offers a relaxed family friendly atmosphere, anxiety and pain management, and the ability to give you the smile you deserve.

Whether your needs are minor or extensive, we offer a range of services from whitening to porcelain crowns and bridges. We conduct an initial consultation to better serve you and learn about the smile of your dreams.

From cleanings to digital x-rays, our entire staff is dedicated to keeping your smile healthy and happy!

Dental implants are a permanent solution to replacing missing teeth. Dental implants are placed by a local oral surgeon and our doctors restore the implants to full functionality.

Fuller Associates Family Dentistry opened in 1963 by the late Dr. Richard Fuller. At the time, the Des Moines office was split into two businesses, our dental practice and the osteopathic office of physician, Dr. Jack Wakefield.

After Dr. Wakefield retired, Dr. Richard Fuller purchased the entire building and remodeled the previous duplex into our existing practice. In 1990, Dr. Steven C. Fuller, Dr. Fuller s son, joined the practice and opened the original Altoona location during the fall of 2003.

In 2022, Dr. Kelsey Edwards joined our team, our first female dentist! We also closed our original Altoona location in 2022 and moved down the street to our brand new Altoona location that opened August 2022 with expanded hours and services.
