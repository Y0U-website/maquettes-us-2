The practice

At Fulton Dental Center, Dr. Josh Prather and Dr. Teddy Rogers prioritize patient care above all else. Our mission is to provide outstanding dental services in a warm and inviting atmosphere. We recognize the vital role oral health plays in your overall well-being. From the moment you step through our doors, you will sense our team’s enthusiasm and dedication to dentistry. Routine care, checkups, and cleanings are the critical foundation for excellent oral health. We have a fantastic team of dental hygienists ready to make you smile.
