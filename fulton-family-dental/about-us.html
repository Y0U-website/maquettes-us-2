Meet Our Team

Behind every great smile we help to create is a team that works hard, listens closely, and genuinely cares about our patients.

Jessica

Treatment Coordinator

Jessica has been working in the dental field for over five years and truly enjoys her work. She takes great pride in providing quality care and creating a positive experience for every patient. Jessica navigates all things insurance at the practice, and she is known for her professionalism, dealing directly with patients every day. A reliable team member, Jessica is easy to work with and stays very organized. People know they can count on her to get things done and keep a positive vibe while doing it.

Outside of work, Jessica loves spending time with her husband, Noah, and their pets, which include a dog and three cats. Together, they love relaxing at home and enjoying quiet weekends together. She is a big fan of music, spending time with family and friends, and shopping for home decor and things for her fur babies. While she says it’s not necessarily a hidden talent, Jessica also loves singing.

Mandy

Registered Dental Hygienist

Mandy graduated from the University of Arkansas, Fort Smith, with her dental hygiene degree. She always strives to provide exceptional care to her patients. She also loves creating friendships along the way.

Mandy has two daughters, and she says her life revolves around her girls. She loves watching them grow and transform into young ladies. Mandy says they have given her gray hair, but they keep her young at heart.

Megan

dental hygienist

Megan started her career in dental hygiene later in life, looking for purpose. Little did she know, she’d find her passion. Her first college experience was at the University of Arkansas as an art major. Obviously, that didn’t work out, but she found a parallel universe in dentistry when she went back to school years later for her bachelor’s degree in dental hygiene and a minor in psychology. Megan loves providing comprehensive care, helping her patients understand the connection between oral health and their overall well-being. It brings her joy.

In her free time, Megan enjoys kayaking, outdoor activities, and art.

Dianira

Dental Hygienist

Dianira has qualifications in dental hygiene sciences and has completed additional training in CPR, nitrous, and local anesthesia administration. She grew up in northeast Arkansas and moved to the northwest in 2021.

Dianira is married and enjoys float trips, hanging out at the pool, and binge-watching a good show. In her spare time, she also likes working out and playing pickleball.

Kristin

registered dental assistant

Kristin completed her training in Fayetteville, Arkansas. She’s originally from Bentonville and has been in the northwest area her whole life. Kristin has practiced dentistry for over 20 years. She loves taking great care of patients and helping them feel comfortable. Kristin has always been passionate about dentistry and creating positive dental experiences. She also enjoys keeping up-to-date on the latest dental technologies.

Kristin is married with four kids, and they have one new grandbaby. In her free time, she loves reading, planting, lake days, and spending time with her family.

Beatriz

Registered Dental Assistant

Born and raised in Springdale, Arkansas, Beatriz brings over four years of experience as a dental assistant to her role at Fulton Family Dental. She joined the practice in 2025 after completing both her Dental Assisting Technical Certificate and Certificate of Proficiency in Dental Assisting through the NorthWest Arkansas Community College Dental Assisting Program.

Beatriz is known for her caring nature and calm presence, and she is great at helping patients feel at ease during every visit. Outside the office, she enjoys spending time with her daughter, going on walks, getting lost in a good book, and getting creative with crafts.

Rachel

Office Manager

Rachel has been keeping the front office running at Fulton Family Dental since 2025. Originally from Louisiana, she’s called Northwest Arkansas home since 2014. With a degree from the University of Louisiana at Lafayette and a flair for organization (her color-coded Google Sheets speak for her), Rachel brings both creativity and structure to the practice.

Outside the office, Rachel is happily married to Jared and is the proud mom of a son and two spirited pups. She also carries the memory of her daughters, Shiloh and Emmie, close to her heart. Whether she’s hiking, baking, painting with her toddler, or diving into a fantasy novel, Rachel lives life with energy and heart.

She’s also got a few surprises up her sleeve. Be sure to ask her about her welding and woodworking skills! But most of all, Rachel is passionate about creating a welcoming, efficient environment that makes both patients and team members feel right at home.

Fatima

She is a proud mom of two girls and moved to Arkansas in 2024 to be closer to family. In her free time, she enjoys exploring new places, trying different cuisines, sightseeing, shopping, and dancing.
