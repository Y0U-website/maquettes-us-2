Periodontal Charting

We use gentle probing to measure gum health and identify any signs of inflammation or early gum disease.

Review Our Findings and Discuss Your Goals

After we complete your assessment, we’ll talk through your results and what they mean. We’ll also ask about your overall health, lifestyle, and what you’d like to achieve with your dental care.

Personalized Treatment Planning

If further treatment is needed, we’ll create a plan that fits your needs and explain all your options. At Fulton Family Dental, there’s no pressure. You’re in control of your own treatment choices.

Financial Consultation

We’ll go over costs, timelines, and payment options so you can make informed decisions about your care.

MEET OUR DENTISTS

Dr. Megan Fulton, DDS

Dr. Megan Fulton is proudly from Arkansas, where her dream of becoming a dentist first began. She studied biology in Fayetteville, fell in love with the northwest Arkansas community, and eventually returned after completing dental school in Kansas City. With additional training in Invisalign, Dr. Fulton can assist you with a range of general, cosmetic, and restorative services.

Dr. Dwight Duckworth, DDS, MAGD

Dr. Dwight Duckworth was inspired to pursue dentistry by his uncle’s example of helping others every day. After earning his DDS from Louisiana State University, he served in the U.S. Army, including time in Germany. Today, he enjoys creating healthier smiles and offering a variety of services to the Springdale community.
