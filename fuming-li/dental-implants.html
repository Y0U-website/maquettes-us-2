Dental Implant Surgery

Sinus Lift

The primary benefit of a sinus lift is that it makes it possible to place dental implants in patients who would otherwise not be candidates due to insufficient bone height in the upper jaw. This allows for the restoration of missing teeth with implants, which are widely considered the gold standard in tooth replacement. Learn More

Bone Grafting

A solid bone foundation is crucial for the osseointegration process, where the implant fuses with the bone. Bone grafting improves this process, thereby increasing the success rate of dental implants. A strong bond between the implant and jawbone ensures the long-term stability and functionality of the implant. Learn More

Implant-Supported Crown

An implant-supported dental crown offers great stability as you eat, talk, smile, and laugh. It’s also the perfect option for patients who are missing a single tooth and want to leave the adjacent teeth intact. Our dental implant dentist goes to great lengths to ensure your new implant-supported crown looks and feels natural. Learn More

Implant-Supported Bridge

An implant-supported dental bridge provides exceptional stability during everyday activities such as eating, speaking, smiling, and laughing. It is an ideal solution for patients missing more than one tooth, seeking to preserve the health of neighboring teeth. Our dentist is dedicated to ensuring your new implant-supported bridge is both aesthetically pleasing and feels natural. Learn More

Implant-Supported Denture

Solid Rock Dental is your top destination for implant-supported dentures in Voorhees and Millville, NJ. Our dental implant dentist would be more than happy to evaluate your smile to determine if you’re a candidate for implant-retained dentures. What’s more, Dr. Li is able to handle the entire dental implant process under one roof. Learn More

All-on-X

All-on-X dental implants offer a transformative solution for individuals facing significant tooth loss, providing a sturdy, long-lasting, and aesthetically pleasing replacement for an entire arch of teeth. This innovative approach relies on placing four or more dental implants into the jawbone, which then serve as a foundation for a full set of prosthetic teeth. Learn More

Full Mouth Reconstruction

Full mouth reconstruction offers a comprehensive solution for patients with extensive dental issues, significantly enhancing oral health, function, and aesthetics. This tailored approach addresses multiple dental problems such as missing teeth, severe wear, and bite misalignments in a coordinated manner, restoring the mouth to optimal health. Learn More
