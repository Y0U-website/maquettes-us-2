Implant-Supported Denture

It’s time to renew your smile.

Our dental implant dentist in Voorhees and Millville, NJ , is accepting new patients. Book an appointment to learn more about implant-supported dentures .

Benefits

An implant-supported denture replaces an entire arch of teeth. This restores your ability to eat, smile, talk, and laugh with confidence.

Candidates

Implant-supported dentures are ideal for patients who have experienced significant tooth loss and need to replace an entire upper or lower arch.

Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

Ready to explore the benefits of implant-supported dentures ? Book a consultation with Dr. Li to determine if you’re a candidate for any of our dental implant procedures . One of the main benefits of implant-supported dentures is that you’re able to restore the full look and function of an entire arch of teeth.

Unlike dentures, dental implants have a solid and permanent foundation in the jaw. This helps stop bone loss in its tracks so you can maintain good jaw health. Dental implants are also the gold standard when it comes to replacing teeth. In fact, they can last a lifetime as long as you take good care of your smile.
