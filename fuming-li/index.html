The practice

As a general dentist, Dr. Fuming Li treats patients in all areas of dentistry. He also has specialty training in endodontics , dental implants , and full mouth construction . Dr. Li has been placing dental implants for over a decade now, has extensive experience in implantology, and has successfully treated thousands of patients in the South Jersey area.

Coupled with his dental implantology training and an interest in aesthetic dentistry, Dr. Li’s qualifications deem him one of the most versatile dentists in Voorhees and Millville, NJ . Dr. Li prides himself on giving focused attention to his patients’ needs, empowering patients with a thorough understanding of treatment options and extending compassionate care.

Dr. Fuming Li is a board-certified diplomate and active member of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists. As a certified Lumineers®/veneers provider, Dr. Li is able to treat patients in South Jersey with the thinnest and strongest porcelain veneers on the market. He proudly and diligently takes continuing education programs to maintain his leadership role in the dental field.

Dr. Li became a fellow of the Misch Implant Institute. He has become certified to perform dental implant surgery and offer Invisalign® treatment . His extensive background in all fields of dentistry, as well as his commitment to his trade, makes him your best choice for dental care in South Jersey.

Dr. Li is a marathon runner. Dr. Li recently completed a marathon in Philadelphia and New York City. Passionate about helping his community, Dr. Li and his staff make sure to engage in community outreach by donating to foster care programs and assisting local middle schools by educating their students about hygiene and dentistry.
