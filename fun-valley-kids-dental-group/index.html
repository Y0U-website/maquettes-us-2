The practice

At Fun Valley Kids Dental, we provide gentle and stress-free pediatric dentistry in Sun Valley, CA for infants, children, and teens. Our office is designed to make every visit fun, comfortable, and positive.

We proudly serve families in Sun Valley, North Hollywood, Burbank, Glendale, and the San Fernando Valley.

At Fun Valley Kids Dental, we specialize in providing gentle and effective pediatric dental care. Our team is dedicated to ensuring that every child enjoys a positive dental experience.

Preventive dental care is essential for maintaining your child s oral health. We offer thorough dental cleanings and examinations to prevent dental issues and promote healthy smiles.

Regular exams are very important in finding cavities and other issues early on, before they have a chance to be problematic. It is recommended to have a dental exam every 6 months.

Braces are the classic way to straighten teeth! They gently guide growing smiles into the right place, fixing crowding, gaps, and bites so kids can shine with confidence.

Our fluoride treatments help strengthen your child s teeth and prevent tooth decay. We recommend this treatment as part of our comprehensive approach to pediatric dental care.

We prioritize creating a positive and comfortable environment for children s dental hygiene. Our team is experienced in making dental visits enjoyable for kids of all ages.

Les soins

We offer preventive care, cleanings, exams, fluoride treatments, crowns, kids braces, and Invisalign for children. Our goal is to help every child build healthy dental habits and a confident smile.
