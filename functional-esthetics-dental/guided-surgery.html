GUIDED SURGERY PRODUCTS

We wanted to make guided surgery more affordable for our Doctors. Simply send your DICOM data, Impression, or Intraoral scan. Once the case has been approved, we will provide you with a digitally designed printed guide. All cases are planned together online looking at the cases from the crown down and all guides are fabricated with FDA approved biocompatible material (Med610).

D3 makes sameday provisionals to help control the sulcus and prepare the tissue for you future restoration. This one piece PMMA makes loading easy. Simply time the implant placement to the markings on the guide.

Still want to place temporaries to help control the tissue and the interdental papillae. After a few months of integration and you want to get those perfect circular fences post out of the tissue? Let D3 and FEDL make you temporaries that will set your finals up for great success aesthetically. Send us your impressions or IOS and lets us get started for you. Just like this case for 7-9 that you see here

Want us to take a quick look to see what size implant you can use. Need to know if a sinus lift or grafting may be needed. Send us your CBCT and we will send you screen shots and PDF information of the sites you requested.

Not every case can be loaded the same day with a temporary. You can have a custom healing abutment made (PMMA or Titanium). We will design the CHA to help contour the tissue, just like our temporaries.

We can add a flipper or essex type retainer to float above the CHA while the implant osseointegration takes place.

Do you have an intra oral scanner? In this ever changing industry let’s use the tools you have to save you chair side time and WOW your patients. You can scan your patient a few months after placement. Simply use the correct scan body for the restorative platform and use your IOS. The next time the patient is in the chair you can deliver the abutment and crown, saving you time and money.

Since 2014 we have been providing high quality guides, using the newest products, authentic parts, and cutting edge technology.
