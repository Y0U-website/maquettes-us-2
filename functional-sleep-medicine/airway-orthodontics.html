Airway Orthodontics

Technically, arch expansion to open a person’s airway (as with the Vivos therapy we’ve talked about) involves expanding and growing bone to restore their mouth and upper airway to their genetically and biologically preferred size. While this is often the best way to address the root cause of sleep apnea and snoring, there are times when this can be accomplished by focusing more on moving the teeth. The “professional” term for moving teeth is of course orthodontics; and when orthodontics is done to in a way that preserves or restores the airway, we call it airway orthodontics .

Nearly all orthodontic treatment is done to straighten crooked teeth, which happen to be a cardinal sign that the mouth did not develop large enough. Unfortunately though, most classic orthodontic treatment focuses primarily just on the person’s cosmetic goals, ignoring the fact that by neglecting to correct the root cause of the crowding, they have just set the patient up for possibly developing long-term TMJ and airway problems and all the systemic health issues they can initiate. This is why, in our opinion, all orthodontics should be airway orthodontics .

At Functional Sleep Medicine, our favorite modalities for airway orthodontics are Spark ™ aligners and FastBraces ® . We use these for any patient, but especially children, with sleep apnea or snoring problems that only require mild to moderate arch expansion, as well as for patients who’s primary concern is cosmetic but who also want to avoid any future health issues that a crowded airway may lead to. We also often use them for Phase II finishing of a typical arch expansion case.
