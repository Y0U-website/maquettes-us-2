Why CPAP Isn’t for Everyone

Bulky Setup

Discomfort & Dryness

Masks can cause pressure sores, dry mouth, or skin irritation, making it harder to stay consistent.

Loud Noise

The humming noise from the CPAP machine can disrupt your and your partner’s sleep, leading to sleep divorce. This is where you sleep in a different bed than your partner.

Complicated Maintenance

Daily cleaning, changing filters, and tubing care are all part of the complicated CPAP maintenance you must follow properly.

Low Compliance Rates

Even though CPAP works well for some, many patients simply can’t cope with it long-term. The discomfort, inconvenience, and nightly effort lead to low usage even when the need for treatment is real.
