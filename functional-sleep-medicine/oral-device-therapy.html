Our Oral Appliance Therapy Process

Comprehensive Airway Evaluation

We start with a full assessment of your airway health—including your tongue posture, bite, jaw position, and muscle function—to understand the underlying causes of your sleep disruption.

At-Home Sleep Testing

We’ll guide you through a simple at-home sleep test if you haven’t been diagnosed yet, using devices like WatchPAT or CheckMe O2 Max. These give accurate insights without the need for an overnight lab stay.

Custom Appliance Design and Fitting

We take precise digital scans to get an oral appliance once your diagnosis has been confirmed and we understand your airway. The goal is comfort, effectiveness, and minimal disruption to your daily life.

Fit Check and Instructions

We’ll bring you in for a fitting and adjustment session once the appliance has been made. You’ll learn how to wear and care for the device, and we’ll ensure it feels right.

Follow-ups and Progress Tracking

We monitor your sleep quality, make any needed tweaks, and ensure your therapy delivers consistent results through regular follow-ups and check-ins. Your best sleep starts with an oral appliance.
