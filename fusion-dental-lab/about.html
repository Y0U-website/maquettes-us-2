Gary Wrightson Co-Owner Gary was 10 years old when he was introduced to dental technology: That was the day he received his first motorcycle and promptly knocked out his two front teeth. Gary became a dental technician in 1988, receiving his Dental Technology certification at Los Angeles City College. With 30-plus years of experience in dental technology, Gary has the knowledge to make even the most challenging cases a success, and is a resource for ceramics and custom shades. Gary continues to further his education by attending seminars and courses within the dental field.

Steve Munro Co-Owner Steve uses his nearly 50 years of experience and technical ingenuity in every aspect of his craft. A native of Napa, California, Steve received his dental technology training in the United States Air Force before opening his first lab in 1978. Steve is a leader in dental implant case planning, and has a passion for the technological side of dental restorations. Steve continues to educate himself in dental technology advancements through seminars and courses.
