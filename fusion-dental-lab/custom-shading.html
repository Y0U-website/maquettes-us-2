Custom Shading

Natural-looking restorations require natural-looking color.

We take several factors into consideration when getting the right shade, from base material to position and visibility of the tooth. Then from 30 base shades we can create thousands of shading options and combinations to achieve the desired result.

We are here to add piece of mind in getting the shade just right. We invite you to refer your clients to our office for a custom shade appointment to compliment their crown, bridge or implant. With a short visit to the lab we’ll discuss options with your patient and customize their restoration for the perfect result.

Leave the color matching to our team and you can focus on what you do best.
