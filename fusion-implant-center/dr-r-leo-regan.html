Meet Dr R. Leo Regan

Dr R. Leo Regan

Dr. R. Leo Regan was born and raised in Tallulah, Louisiana. He graduated from both Louisiana State University undergraduate and LSU School of Dentistry. Dr. Regan finished in the top of his dental school class and received numerous honors, including the Oral Surgery and Oral Pathology awards.

After dental school, Dr. Regan completed a residency program in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at the University of Cincinnati. He is board certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. He has maintained a private practice in Baton Rouge since 1997. Dr. Regan serves as the team oral and maxillofacial surgeon for LSU athletics.

Dr. Regan is an active staff member at Our Lady of the Lake Hospital, having served as chief of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and Dentistry. He is also past president of the Louisiana Society of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons. He has devoted his professional career to all aspects of specialty practice with special interest in orthognathic surgery (jaw surgery) and dental implants.

Away from the office, Dr. Regan enjoys spending time at Lake Bruin, playing golf, and is an avid duck hunter.
