Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic Dentistry for Goodyear, Avondale and Litchfield, AZ

We re all about beautiful smiles at G dental studio.

There is no substitute for the confidence you will feel when your smile is exactly what you desire. Dr. Gonzales is an expert at designing and creating the smile of your dreams. It all begins with listening to your needs, your wants, and your desires. Then Dr. Gonzales creates a complete smile makeover plan that includes any number of the cosmetic dentistry services available at G dental studio. We call ourselves the "smile studio" because we create true works of art.

  • Porcelain veneers create natural-looking brilliance and give teeth the perfect shape. Porcelain veneers can give you the smile you have always wanted.
  • Cosmetic bonding repairs minor imperfections – a confidence boost in a single visit!
  • Ceramic crowns help when you need strength and beauty – the most dramatic of results in just two visits.
  • Tooth colored fillings blend beautifully with your teeth so no one will know you have fillings
  • Invisalign allow you to move teeth into perfection while maintaining a natural smile.
  • Teeth Whitening is the most cost effective way to regain a youthful smile.

One-Day Smile Makeovers

You may be considering a smile makeover, but if time is an issue, you might be hesitating to make that appointment. What if we could give you a cosmetic dentistry smile makeover in just one visit to our Goodyear, AZ dental office? We offer one-day smile makeovers with dental veneers we can create for you in the office while you wait!

Spa Teeth Whitening

What cosmetic dentistry practice would be complete without teeth whitening? We offer several options for teeth whitening, including spa whitening. It’s professional-grade tray whitening in our spa-like atmosphere, giving you a chance to get away from it all and enjoy a relaxing afternoon.

Our cosmetic dentistry services range from the simple touch ups to the complete smile makeovers. With cosmetic bonding, we offer a quick fix for small chips and cracks. Our restorative dentistry includes tooth-colored fillings and porcelain crowns for that natural look. And with Invisalign, we can straighten your smile easily and invisibly.

If you’ve been thinking about getting a smile makeover, contact G dental studio today to schedule a consultation withDr. Jose Gonzales. We’ll help you figure out what your goals are and come up with a smile design that best suits you. Patients from Goodyear, Litchfield Park, and Avondale have found their most beautiful smile at G dental studio. You can, too.
