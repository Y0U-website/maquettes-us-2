Meet The Team

Kayla H.

Kayla is our Front Office Coordinator. She was born and raised in Loveland, Colorado. She moved to Arizona in 2016 and enjoys trips to Show Low, AZ because it reminds her of home. She graduated from Carrington College with a certificate in Dental Assisting in 2020 and was the lead assistant at G Dental Studio for many years. She enjoys visiting with patients and helping others to achieve a happy, healthy smile that they can be proud of.

Marcella

Marcella (Marcy) is our Office Manager. She was born and raised in San Jose,California. She moved to Arizona in 2021 with her husband Carlos, 3 sons, daughter in law and her 2 granddaughters. On her days at home she loves spending time with her family & her 2 dogs, BBQ’ing, cooking, crafting and reading. Marcy has over 25 years of customer service experience, "The best part of my job is helping patients feel at ease with every visit. I love seeing our patients leaving with a smile on their face knowing that their visit from the Front office to the Back office was enjoyable".

Joanna

Joanna is our Dental Assistant. She is from Phoenix, Arizona but was raised in Sinaloa, Mexico. She went to Carrington College and earned her dental assisting certificate. She loves dancing, and spending time with her cousins, family, and friends. She is passionate about her job as a dental assistant because it leads her to challenges daily. She loves to learn new skills and loves to make patients feel better about their smiles.

Brenda

Brenda is now our Public Relations Specialist. She "excites the crowds" with informative emails and advertisements. When specials come around be sure to check them out! Our quarterly emails come to all of our patients; however, you can click on the newsletters and become informed too! Brenda is Dr. Gonzales’ wife and they have been married over 20 years. They have four beautiful children, Joey, Sierra, Jacob and Sophia. They were actually high school sweethearts and dated since they were 16 years old. Now, Brenda considers herself a native to this beautiful Valley of the Sun.

Brenda was an intricate part of developing G dental studio from its conception. Brenda enjoys talking and visiting with each patient as she checks them in. She likes to listen to our patients as they share their life experiences, vacations and families stories with her.

Kelsie

Keslie is our Dental Hygienest. Kelsie along with her husband are from Ohio. She is an Alumi of Ohio State. In her free time, she enjoys hiking and spending time with her dog Mac. She also looks forward to a good craft cocktail and new restaurant.

Isabel

Isabel is our Dental Hygienest. She has lived most of her life in the state of Arizona. She graduated from Phoenix College in 2005 as a Dental Hygienist. Since then she has been helping patients with their oral health by providing thorough dental cleanings.

Karyme

Karyme is our Dental Assistant. She graduated from Carrington College. She enjoys trying new foods, going to the movies, and spending time with family and friends. She loves getting to know patients and making them feel welcome during their visits.

Rachel

Rachel is our (Newest) Dental Assitant. She is a native born Arizonian. She graduated from Carrington College with honors and received her certification as a D.A. She has a wonderful husband, daughter and dog. Rachel enjoys hiking, seeing new places and absolutely LOVES Disney.
