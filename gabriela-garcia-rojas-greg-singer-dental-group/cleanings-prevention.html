An innovative approach to dental cleanings and examinations

Dr. Garcia-Rojas and Dr. Singer have equipped our dental office with the latest in preventive dental technology. This allows us to provide patients with all the benefits modern dentistry has to offer and to administer comprehensive care.

Our office provides and performs the following innovative and preventive treatments and techniques:

- Airflow technology is a modern dental cleaning approach that uses a combination of warm water, air, and fine glycine or erythritol powder to gently remove plaque (biofilm) and stains from your teeth and gums. The warm water helps reduce sensitivity, allowing for a thorough, comfortable cleaning. The Airflow is exceptional at cleaning around braces, Invisalign buttons, crowns, implants, and bridges.

- Panoramic Digital X-Rays that capture precise images of the mouth, teeth, gums, jaws, and surrounding bone.

- Diagnodent D evices that scan teeth with harmless laser light for early cavity detection.

- CariVu or Caries Detection Device that can be used for portable dental damage or cavity detection and uses bright light technology.

- Dental Diode Laser that can be used to provide treatment for ulcers, canker sores, and cold sores; uncover erupted teeth; promote periodontal health; and assist with gum contouring.

The value of preventative oral health and partnering with patients on dental hygiene best practices

A strong oral hygiene routine will allow you to better maintain healthy teeth and gums and will encourage a fully-functional and beautiful smile.

Daily brushing and flossing, along with biannual visits, help prevent the development of oral health issues, such as gum disease, that can lead to serious overall health complications.
