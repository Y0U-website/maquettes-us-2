The importance of replacing missing teeth

Prolonged tooth loss leads to a weakened jawbone and eventual bone loss due to a lack of stimulation from healthy teeth roots. Dental implants consist of titanium materials that are accepted by the body; therefore, the posts naturally fuse with the living tissue and bone in a process called osseointegration. This process protects jawbone health and helps to preserve oral health, natural aesthetics, and overall wellness.

From improving digestion to halting the progression of jawbone deterioration and preserving the position of surrounding teeth, the benefits of dental implants go far beyond simply helping you achieve a beautiful smile.
