What Are the Side Effects of Cosmetic Dentistry?

Our Half Moon Bay cosmetic dentist will complete these procedures with the highest level of care. Most patients will not experience any side effects aside from some inflammation in the area.

Others may deal with a few mild side effects after a cosmetic dental procedure, such as:

Bruising and pain in the case of a dental surgery

These are temporary side effects and should subside within two days. Minor discomfort can be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.

How Much Do Cosmetic Dental Procedures Cost?

Your budget likely plays a significant role in deciding your dental treatment. However, cosmetic dentistry at Half Moon Bay Family Dentistry is customized to each patient. No two patients have the same treatment plan, making the price vary wildly.

The best way to learn about the cost of any cosmetic dental procedures you need is to schedule an appointment. Dr. Riley will listen to you and create a treatment plan tailored to your needs.

How Long Do Cosmetic Procedures Last?

All cosmetic procedures will last longer with proper home care and routine checkups. You can expect porcelain veneers to last for 10 to 20 years and dental implants to last for up to 15 years.

Other cosmetic procedures, like teeth whitening, require routine maintenance to lock in lasting results. Whether you schedule annual in-office whitening treatment or use a take-home kit, your results could last a lifetime.
