Can I Visit the ER for a Dental Emergency?

The best action after a dental emergency is to visit an emergency dentist. However, there may be times when this is not feasible. You can go to the emergency room, where you will likely be given antibiotics and pain medication and referred to a dentist.

A doctor in the emergency room cannot pull a tooth. It is not legal for anyone other than a licensed dentist to pull teeth, complete root canals, or provide most other forms of dental care.

It’s best to seek out a dentist who handles emergencies, like Half Moon Bay Family Dentistry, as soon as possible.

Preventing a Dental Emergency

Many dental emergencies occur because of an underlying condition that has yet to be treated. Our caring staff will never lecture or judge you. Instead, we will help you become more active in your oral health to prevent another emergency. Routine visits to your Half Moon Bay dentist will reduce your chances of needing emergency dental care.

Using a mouthguard during sports activities is essential to protect your teeth and mouth from potential injury. A mouthguard can help absorb impact and prevent teeth from chipping, breaking, or getting knocked out. It can also help prevent a concussion or brain trauma. It’s important to wear a custom-fitted mouthguard from Half Moon Bay Family Dentistry and have it replaced if it becomes damaged or worn out.
