Advanced Dental Care Established in Valley Cottage

Welcome to the Practice of Dr. Robert J. Gabriel Jr., DMD

At our Valley Cottage dental practice, we combine advanced technology with personalized care to deliver dentistry at the highest level. Dr. Robert J. Gabriel Jr., DMD is committed to providing patients with precision-driven, comfortable, and efficient treatment using the most modern innovations available in dentistry today.

Our office is distinguished by its investment in cutting-edge technology designed to improve accuracy, safety, and patient outcomes. We utilize advanced 3D digital X-ray imaging to obtain highly detailed, low-radiation diagnostic views that allow for earlier detection and more precise treatment planning.

We also incorporate AI-powered CAD/CAM technology to digitally design and fabricate restorations with exceptional accuracy and aesthetics. This technology enables us to create crowns, inlays, onlays, and other restorations that fit more precisely and blend naturally with your smile.

Where Precision Technology Meets Personalized Dental Treatment

In addition, our in-office 3D printing capabilities allow us to custom-fabricate night guards and occlusal guards with superior fit and comfort often with greater speed and precision than traditional methods.

By integrating digital workflows, artificial intelligence, and advanced imaging, we are able to provide dentistry that is more efficient, more comfortable, and more predictable. Our commitment to innovation ensures that every patient receives care that reflects the highest standards of modern dental science.
