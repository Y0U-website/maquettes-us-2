Meet Dr. Gabrielle

Dr. Robert K. Gabrielle is the founder and lead dentist at Gabrielle Dental, where he is committed to providing exceptional, patient-centered care in a welcoming and comfortable environment. With a passion for dentistry and a dedication to clinical excellence, Dr. Gabrielle combines advanced techniques with a gentle, personalized approach to help patients achieve healthy, confident smiles.

Dr. Gabrielle earned his dental degree from the prestigious New York University College of Dentistry, where he received comprehensive training in restorative, cosmetic, and preventive dentistry. He continues to pursue ongoing education to stay at the forefront of modern dental advancements, ensuring his patients benefit from the latest technology and evidence-based treatments.

Throughout his career, Dr. Gabrielle has built a reputation for taking the time to truly listen to his patients. He believes that clear communication and education are the foundation of great dental care, empowering patients to make informed decisions about their oral health. His focus on precision, comfort, and long-term results has made Gabrielle Dental a trusted practice in the community.

Whether providing routine cleanings, cosmetic enhancements, or comprehensive restorative treatments, Dr. Gabrielle approaches every case with attention to detail and a commitment to excellence.

Outside the office, Dr. Gabrielle enjoys spending time with family and staying active in the community he’s proudly served for over 60 years.
