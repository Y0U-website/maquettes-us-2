Quality dental care for the entire family.

We pride ourselves on taking the time to get to know our patients and understand their needs.

Our mission is to give our patients the time, information, and attention they need to make the best decisions possible. It is our priority to help you fully understand your dental care choices. Our treatment recommendations are guided by one question: Would I want this treatment for myself or my family?

Our office provides general dental services for adults and children. These services include: fillings, cleanings and periodontal services, patient education, root canal treatment, restorations, both fixed (implants, crowns, bridges, veneers) and removable (partial dentures and complete dentures). Advances in technology have made many of these treatment options simpler, stronger, and more attractive than ever before.

Some situations may require more advanced care than we provide in our office. We have established professional relationships with many excellent dental specialists in the Quad City area and work closely with them to give you the best possible care.

Please contact us with any questions regarding our services.

  • Exams Cleanings We want to help you keep your smile good-looking and functional. A healthy mouth and gums begin with regular cleanings and check-ups. We offer digital x-rays and photography, exams, periodontal services, fluoride treatments and more. Together, we’ll create a treatment plan for you and your family to keep everyone healthy!
  • Restorative Procedures When teeth need to be restored, our skilled and caring team is here to discuss which options and procedures are right for you. We provide fillings, crowns, implants, partials, dentures, and a variety of other restorative services to help you restore your smile.
  • Root Canal Treatment We want you to be pain free, and it’s our goal to make every procedure as comfortable as possible. A root canal isn’t as scary as it sounds , and our team will be here every step of the way to help you and to answer your questions.
