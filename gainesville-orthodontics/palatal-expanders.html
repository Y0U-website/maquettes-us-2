Palatal Expanders

Office Directions Request an Appointment 571-261-9117 Palatal Expanders Palatal expanders are a valuable orthodontic tool designed to address issues related to narrow upper arches in children. This non-invasive device works by gradually widening the palate, creating additional space for proper dental alignment and optimal oral development.

Benefits of Palatal Expansion

Our orthodontist may recommend palatal expanders to correct various orthodontic issues such as crowding, crossbites and impacted teeth. By expanding the upper jaw, we can guide teeth into their proper positions, preventing future complications and enhancing the overall functionality of the bite.

Customized Treatment Plans

Our orthodontic team understands that every child is unique, and treatment plans must be tailored to their specific needs. Dr. Peter Yi specializes in creating personalized palatal expansion strategies, ensuring the most effective and comfortable experience for each young patient.

Timely Intervention for Lifelong Benefits

Early detection and intervention with palatal expanders can significantly impact a child’s oral health. Addressing narrow arches promptly can prevent more complex orthodontic issues, reducing the need for extensive treatments in the future.
