Orthodontics

At Galleria of Smiles, we offer Six Month Smiles®, an advanced clear bracket and tooth-colored wire system engineered for esthetic short-term alignment.

This system leverages proven orthodontic principles in combination with modern materials to achieve precise and rapid results while maintaining a discreet appearance throughout the treatment.

If you are seeking an efficient and aesthetically pleasing orthodontic solution, short-term orthodontics may be the ideal treatment path. Schedule a consultation to determine candidacy and explore your personalized treatment plan.
