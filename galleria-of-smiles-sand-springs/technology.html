CAD/CAM Dentistry

This advanced system allows us to design and create custom dental prosthetics, including crowns, veneers, and bridges, using computer-guided precision and high-resolution 3D scans of your teeth.

Each restoration is digitally sculpted with meticulous accuracy to ensure a flawless fit, optimal function, and lifelike aesthetics. Schedule your appointment today and let us restore your smile with efficiency, precision, and care, all in one appointment.

Digital Impressions

Digital impression technology allows for highly precise, three-dimensional imaging of the teeth and surrounding oral structures, significantly improving the accuracy and fit of dental restorations such as crowns, bridges, and veneers.

At Galleria of Smiles, we utilize this advanced scanning method to eliminate the discomfort and variability often associated with conventional impression materials.

The process is efficient, reduces the risk of distortion or retakes, and enhances clinical outcomes by enabling immediate visualization and adjustments.

By involving patients in the digital workflow, we also support more transparent communication and more informed decision-making throughout the treatment process.

  • Is swift, allowing us to promptly review the images and make necessary adjustments.
  • Reduces the likelihood of errors and distortions.
  • Allows us to better include you in the process so you have a deeper understanding of your oral health and make well-informed decisions.

Oral Cancer Screening

  • Persistent sore throat
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Chronic pain in the mouth or ear.
  • White or red patches on the gums, tongue, tonsils, or lining of the mouth.
  • Unexplained bleeding in the mouth or throat.
  • Numbness or tingling in the mouth or lips.
  • Persistent bad breath that doesn’t improve with oral hygiene.
  • Difficulty chewing, moving the jaw, or speaking.
  • Swelling, lumps, or bumps in the mouth, neck, or throat.
