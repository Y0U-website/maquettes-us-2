Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Ouida Marine

Dr. Ouida Marine was born and raised just outside of Charlotte, NC, in the small town of York, SC, near her family’s farm. She attended Clemson University, where she earned her degree in Animal and Veterinary Science with a minor in Business Administration, graduating in 2020. Driven by a passion for healthcare and serving others, she went on to earn her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the Medical University of South Carolina in the spring of 2025.

Dr. Marine is dedicated to providing compassionate, patient-centered care and values the meaningful relationships she builds with every patient. Her goal is to create a welcoming, comfortable environment where patients feel truly cared for and confident in their dental experience.

Outside the dental office, Dr. Marine enjoys gardening, baking, discovering new music, and traveling to new places.
