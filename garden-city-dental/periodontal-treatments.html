Scaling and Root Planing

At Garden City Dental in Orangeburg, SC, we provide scaling and root planing as essential periodontal treatments aimed at improving gum health and preventing the progression of gum disease. This non-surgical procedure is often recommended for patients experiencing signs of gum disease, such as redness, swelling, or bleeding gums.

Scaling involves the meticulous removal of plaque and tartar buildup from both above and below the gum line. Our experienced dental team uses specialized instruments to thoroughly clean the surfaces of your teeth and the pockets between your gums and teeth. This process not only helps to eliminate harmful bacteria but also promotes healthier gums.

Root planing follows scaling and focuses on smoothing the roots of the teeth, making it more difficult for plaque and tartar to accumulate in the future. By removing rough spots on the tooth roots, root planing helps the gums reattach to the teeth, which is crucial for healing and reducing pocket depths.

The benefits of scaling and root planing extend beyond just cleaning; they significantly contribute to the overall health of your gums and teeth. By addressing gum disease in its early stages, we can help prevent more serious issues, such as tooth loss and bone damage.

If you’re experiencing gum issues or have concerns about your periodontal health, contact us today to schedule a consultation. Our dedicated team is here to provide effective scaling and root planing treatments that will help restore and maintain your healthy smile!

Periodontal Maintenance

At Garden City Dental in Orangeburg, SC, we emphasize the importance of periodontal maintenance as a vital component of your overall oral health. After initial treatment for gum disease, ongoing periodontal maintenance is essential for preventing recurrence and ensuring that your gums remain healthy and stable. Our dedicated team is here to provide personalized care that supports your long-term dental health.

Periodontal maintenance visits are typically scheduled every three to four months, depending on the severity of your gum condition. During these appointments, we perform comprehensive cleanings that go beyond regular dental check-ups. Our experienced hygienists will meticulously remove plaque and tartar buildup from above and below the gum line, using advanced techniques and tools designed for thorough cleaning.

In addition to scaling and root planing, we also assess your gum health by measuring pocket depths and checking for any signs of inflammation or infection. These evaluations are crucial for monitoring your progress and ensuring that your gums are healing properly. We will provide tailored recommendations for at-home care, including proper brushing and flossing techniques, to help you maintain your gum health between visits.

Our goal is to help you achieve optimal oral health while preventing further issues related to gum disease. With regular periodontal maintenance, you can enjoy the benefits of a healthier smile, reduced risk of tooth loss, and overall improved well-being.

At Garden City Dental, we understand that each patient’s needs are unique. If you have a history of gum disease or are due for a maintenance appointment, contact us today to schedule your visit. Let us help you keep your gums healthy and your smile vibrant!
