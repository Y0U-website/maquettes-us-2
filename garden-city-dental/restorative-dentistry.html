Dental Crowns

At Garden City Dental in Orangeburg, SC, we provide high-quality dental crowns as an effective solution for restoring the strength and appearance of damaged or compromised teeth. Dental crowns, also known as caps, are custom-made restorations that cover the entire tooth, providing protection and support while enhancing its shape and color. They are an excellent option for various dental issues, including severe decay, cracks, fractures, or after root canal therapy.

The process of getting a dental crown typically involves two visits. During your first appointment, our experienced team will assess your tooth and discuss the best treatment options for your specific situation. If a crown is determined to be the right choice, we will prepare the tooth by removing any decayed or damaged material. We’ll then take precise impressions to create a custom crown that fits seamlessly with your natural teeth.

While you wait for your permanent crown, a temporary crown will be placed to protect your tooth and maintain aesthetics. Once your custom crown is ready, you’ll return for the second appointment. We will carefully fit and bond the crown to your tooth, ensuring it aligns comfortably with your bite.

At Garden City Dental, we offer a variety of crown materials, including porcelain, ceramic, and metal, allowing us to choose the best option based on your needs and preferences. Our goal is to provide durable, aesthetically pleasing crowns that restore both function and confidence in your smile.

If you’re experiencing tooth damage or decay, don’t hesitate to reach out. Contact us today to schedule a consultation and learn how dental crowns can help restore your smile and dental health at Garden City Dental!

Dental Bridges

At Garden City Dental in Orangeburg, SC, we offer dental bridges as an effective solution for replacing missing teeth and restoring your smile. A dental bridge is a prosthetic device that spans the gap left by one or more missing teeth, utilizing adjacent teeth for support. This treatment not only enhances your smile’s appearance but also helps maintain the structure of your mouth and prevents other teeth from shifting.

The process of getting a dental bridge begins with a thorough consultation. Our experienced team will evaluate your dental health and discuss your specific needs, ensuring that a bridge is the right option for you. We will take precise measurements and impressions of your mouth to create a custom bridge that fits comfortably and securely.

During the first appointment, we will prepare the adjacent teeth by gently reshaping them to accommodate the crowns that will anchor the bridge. After preparing the teeth, we will take impressions to send to our dental lab, where your custom bridge will be crafted. In the meantime, you may receive a temporary bridge to protect the exposed area and maintain your smile.

Once your permanent bridge is ready, you will return for a follow-up appointment. Our team will carefully fit the bridge, ensuring it aligns with your bite and matches the color of your natural teeth. We will securely bond it in place, providing you with a functional and aesthetically pleasing solution.

Dental bridges are durable and can last for many years with proper care, including regular dental check-ups and good oral hygiene practices. At Garden City Dental, we are dedicated to helping you achieve a healthy, beautiful smile. If you’re interested in learning more about dental bridges, contact us today to schedule your consultation!

Dentures

At Garden City Dental in Orangeburg, SC, we provide high-quality dentures as a reliable solution for individuals facing tooth loss. Dentures are custom-made prosthetic devices that replace missing teeth, restoring both functionality and aesthetics to your smile. Whether you need complete dentures to replace all your teeth or partial dentures for specific gaps, our experienced team is here to guide you through the process.

The journey to getting dentures begins with a comprehensive consultation. During this visit, we’ll evaluate your oral health and discuss your specific needs and preferences. We’ll take precise measurements and impressions of your mouth to ensure a perfect fit. Our goal is to create dentures that not only restore your smile but also look and feel natural.

Once your custom dentures are designed, we’ll conduct fittings to make any necessary adjustments for optimal comfort and appearance. For complete dentures, the process involves replacing an entire arch of teeth, while partial dentures are designed to fill in gaps while still allowing you to keep your remaining natural teeth. Both options are made from durable materials that mimic the look of natural teeth and gums.

At Garden City Dental, we understand that adjusting to new dentures can take time. Our compassionate team will provide guidance and support throughout the transition, offering tips on care and maintenance to ensure longevity. We are committed to your comfort and satisfaction, and we strive to make the process as seamless as possible.

If you’re experiencing tooth loss and seeking a solution, contact us today to schedule a consultation. Let us help you regain your confidence and enjoy the benefits of a complete smile with our high-quality dentures at Garden City Dental!

Inlays and Onlays

At Garden City Dental in Orangeburg, SC, we offer dental inlays and onlays as a durable and aesthetic solution for restoring damaged teeth. These restorations are ideal for cases where traditional fillings may not provide the necessary strength or where a tooth has sustained more extensive damage that doesn’t require a full crown.

Inlays and onlays are custom-made from materials such as porcelain, composite resin, or gold, allowing us to choose the best option based on your needs and preferences. Inlays are used to fill the grooves of a tooth, while onlays cover the cusp tips and provide additional support. This versatility makes them an excellent choice for treating decay, fractures, or large fillings.

The process for getting inlays or onlays begins with a comprehensive consultation, where our skilled team will evaluate your tooth and discuss your treatment options. If an inlay or onlay is recommended, we will prepare the affected tooth by removing any decay or damaged structure. Next, we will take precise impressions to create a custom restoration that fits seamlessly with your natural tooth.

During the initial visit, a temporary restoration may be placed to protect the tooth while your custom inlay or onlay is being fabricated in a dental lab. Once it’s ready, you’ll return to our office for the final fitting. We’ll ensure that the restoration aligns properly with your bite and is securely bonded in place, restoring both function and aesthetics.

Inlays and onlays not only enhance the appearance of your smile but also provide a long-lasting solution for tooth restoration. At Garden City Dental, we prioritize your comfort and satisfaction. If you’re interested in learning more about dental inlays and onlays, contact us today to schedule your consultation!
