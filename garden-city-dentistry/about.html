Our dentists

Dr. Jae Pak

Dr. Jae Pak is a skilled and compassionate dentist licensed in New York State, known for his precision and calm, professional demeanor. With a focus on both general and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Pak combines clinical expertise with a patient-centered approach to ensure every individual receives personalized, high-quality care.

Whether he’s performing a routine exam or crafting a full smile transformation, Dr. Pak is dedicated to delivering lasting results in a comfortable, stress-free setting. Patients consistently praise his ability to explain treatment options clearly and make them feel at ease throughout their visit.

Driven by a passion for excellence and a commitment to oral health, Dr. Pak is proud to serve the Garden City community with integrity and care.

Dr. Peter Vastardis

Dr. Vastardis is a highly respected and compassionate dentist with decades of experience serving the Garden City community. Known for his gentle chairside manner and commitment to clinical excellence, Dr. Vastardis has built a loyal patient base by combining advanced dental techniques with genuine, patient-first care.

He focuses on general, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry, with a special interest in smile makeovers, dental implants, and long-term oral health strategies. Dr. Vastardis takes the time to get to know each patient, ensuring that their treatment plans are personalized, efficient, and fully understood.

Patients appreciate not only his expertise, but also his calming presence and dedication to making every visit a positive experience.

Whether you’re here for a routine cleaning or a complete smile transformation, you’re in trusted hands with Dr. Vastardis.
