Porcelain Inlays/Onlays

Porcelain inlays and onlays are custom-crafted restorations used when a filling isn’t enough and a full crown isn’t necessary. Inlays fit within the grooves of the tooth, while onlays extend over one or more cusps. These restorations are bonded to the tooth to restore strength, function, and appearance preserving more natural tooth structure than a crown.

Dental implant restorations are the final step in your implant journey bringing function, aesthetics, and confidence back to your smile. Once your implants are placed, we create custom-designed restorations such as crowns, bridges, or full-arch prosthetics that attach securely to the implants.
