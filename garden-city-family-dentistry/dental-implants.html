The Dental Implant Journey

Comprehensive Evaluation

We perform a thorough exam, including 3D imaging, to assess your bone structure and determine if you are a candidate for implants.

Surgical Placement

Under local anesthesia (and sedation if desired), the titanium implant post is precisely placed into your jawbone.

Healing (Osseointegration)

Over the next 3 to 6 months, the jawbone naturally fuses with the titanium post, creating a permanent, rock-solid foundation.

Abutment Placement

Once healing is complete, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached to the implant.

Final Restoration

Your custom-crafted crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached to the abutment, completing your beautiful new smile.
