Implant-Supported Dentures

  • The Connectors: Once the implants have healed, special attachments (often ball-and-socket or bar-clip mechanisms) are placed on top of them.
  • The Denture: Your custom-crafted denture has corresponding receptacles underneath. When you put the denture in, it snaps firmly onto the implants.
  • The Result: A rock-solid smile that you can easily remove yourself for daily cleaning, but that will never slip while you are wearing it.

The Treatment Process

3D Imaging Planning

We take a 3D Cone Beam CT scan to evaluate your jawbone density and precisely plan the optimal placement for your implants.

Implant Placement

Under local anesthesia (and sedation if requested), the implants are surgically placed into your jawbone. You will wear a temporary denture while you heal.

Healing (Osseointegration)

Over 3-6 months, your jawbone naturally fuses with the titanium implants, creating incredibly strong anchors.

Denture Fabrication

We take precise impressions of your healed gums and implants to craft your final, custom-fitted denture.

Final Fitting (The Snap )

We attach the final denture, ensuring the bite is perfect and the snap mechanism holds securely. You leave with a confident, fully functional smile!
