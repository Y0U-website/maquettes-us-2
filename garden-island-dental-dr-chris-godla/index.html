The practice

Dr. Godla began with a simple yet clear vision: to provide high-quality dental care at a reasonable cost in a relaxed atmosphere. With advanced training and state-of-the-art technology, we offer the ultimate convenience and high-quality dentistry you deserve in a welcoming atmosphere.

With a physician father and a natural passion for helping others, Dr. Godla always knew he wanted to pursue a career in healthcare. While an undergraduate at the University of Notre Dame, he had the opportunity to shadow a local dentist. He discovered that the profession perfectly blended his passion for science with a hands-on approach and the ability to make a meaningful difference to help…

After earning his dental degree in 2013, Dr. Godla completed an advanced education in general dentistry residency before opening Garden Island Dental on Haleko Road in 2020. Today, he finds it incredibly rewarding when a patient mentions how grateful they are to be out of pain or how much they love their new smile.

Whether you need a simple cleaning, emergency care, cosmetic improvements, or complex implant restoration, we have the technology and expertise to provide excellent results. With same-day CEREC technology , you can even receive your custom crown in a single comfortable visit.

At Garden Island Dental, we believe in honest assessments and transparent pricing. We understand the role that dental costs play in decision-making and do everything we can to make care accessible and affordable .

We are pleased to work with major insurance plans, including HDS, HMSA, HMAA, Humana, and United Healthcare , and offer flexible payment options, including CareCredit and health savings accounts.

If you re looking for a Kauai dentist who genuinely listens and makes recommendations in your best interest, we d love to welcome you to Garden Island Dental. Dr. Godla and our team are committed to providing the same quality of care we would want for our loved ones.

Dr. Chris Godla brings over a decade of dental experience to our team, combining a strong academic foundation with a genuine passion for patient care.
