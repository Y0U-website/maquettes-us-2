Our Services

Preventative Care & Cosmetic Dentistry

Preventative care is the key to a healthy smile. Regular checkups allow us to treat issues before they become a problem. Sometimes, cosmetic dentistry is the best course of action in these cases. But whether it’s elective or for treatment, we’ll make sure your smile is healthy and beautiful!

Emergency Care & Oral Surgery

Our office provides treatments appropriate for most majority of emergencies. Broken or fractured teeth, broken existing fillings, infections, severe toothaches, emergency root canals and extractions are examples of emergencies we treat at our office. Occasionally, if or when the emergency is of a more complicated nature, we still assist our patients by referring them to an appropriate local specialist.
