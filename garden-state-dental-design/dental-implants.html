What Are The Different Types Of Dental Implants Available At Garden State Dental?

At Garden State Dental Design, a wide range of dental implant options are available, each tailored to meet diverse patient needs and preferences:

Single Tooth Replacement

Ideal for replacing a single missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth.

Porcelain Implant Bridges: A durable and aesthetic solution for replacing several missing teeth in a row.

Permanent Dentures

Designed for patients missing most or all of their teeth, providing a secure and permanent alternative to traditional dentures.

Full Arch Replacement

Ideal for patients who require a complete arch restoration , offering a more stable and comfortable solution than traditional full dentures.

Same-Day Permanent Tooth/Teeth

A quick and effective way to replace missing teeth in just one visit.

Bone Grafting and Sinus Lift

Procedures to enhance the jawbone’s structure, essential for successful implant placement in patients with insufficient bone mass.

PRGF Endoret Treatments

Advanced technology used to enhance healing and promote bone growth around the implant.

Putting It All Together

What To Expect During A Dental Implant Procedure

You begin with a consultation discussing your ideal outcome to help us gain a full understanding for desiring such treatment. The process of getting dental implants in Millburn & Clark at Garden State Dental Design involves several key steps:

Each step is performed with the utmost care, ensuring comfort and optimal results throughout the procedure.

  • Initial Consultation : A thorough examination and discussion about your dental history and implant options.
  • Treatment Planning : Personalized planning using advanced imaging technology to ensure precise implant placement.
  • Implant Placement : The implant, a small titanium post, is surgically inserted into the jawbone.
  • Healing Period : A period of healing (osseointegration) follows, where the implant fuses with the jawbone
  • Abutment Placement : After healing, an abutment is attached to the implant to hold the new tooth.
  • Artificial Tooth Attachment : Finally, a crown designed to match your natural teeth is attached to the abutment.

After Your Consultation

After which, a comprehensive exam is performed to gather all the necessary information to develop a customized treatment plan. During this exam, photos of your smile, digital impressions of your teeth and mouth, along with a 3D Ct-scan x-ray is taken to evaluate the health of your bone to better formulate a comprehensive treatment plan. A follow-up appointment is set up to go over treatment options for your desired transformation.

Bone-Grafting

In the event where inadequate bone volume exists where teeth have been missing for quite some time, you might have to undergo a procedure of bone regeneration known as “ bone-grafting ” to rebuild the bone in anticipation of implant placement. At Garden State Dental Design, we use a homeopathic approach utilizing your own blood, in the form of PRGF Endoret® (Plasma Rich in Growth Factors) . This is performed at the time of hopeless tooth removal or at the time of implant surgery to help with the regeneration of bone and soft tissue ensuring successful healing.

Once the bone has had time to heal, then in a single visit at the site of the missing tooth, Your biocompatible titanium screw is inserted into the jaw bone (about an hour with local numbing or sedation, if necessary), and that implant is left to heal and integrate for six-to-eight weeks.

Dental Implant Recovery

Following implant placement, you may experience some swelling and discomfort. Dr. Szabela will provide specific instructions on managing pain and caring for your implant site during healing.

During your follow up appointment, we evaluate the implant and determine when to start the process of preparing the site for receiving a tooth. Once the gums are prepared, a digital impression is taken to custom design the abutment and crown at our office. At your final visit, your new tooth is permanently placed onto the implant. Your new tooth looks, feels, and functions just like a natural tooth, and requires only regular good oral hygiene to last a lifetime.

Dental Implant Frequently Asked Questions How long does the dental implant process take? The dental implant process can vary depending on several factors, such as the number and location of implants needed and your individual healing rate. It can generally take several months, with healing time required between implant placement and restoration placement. Dr. Szabela will provide a detailed timeline during your consultation.

How much do dental implants cost?

The cost of dental implants can vary depending on the complexity of your case, the number of implants needed, and the type of restorations used. Our Garden State Dental Design team is happy to provide you with a personalized cost estimate after a consultation with Dr. Szabela. We understand that cost is a concern, and we offer flexible payment options to help make dental implants more affordable.

What are the risks associated with dental implant surgery?

As with any surgical procedure, some potential risks are associated with dental implant surgery, such as infection or bleeding. Dr. Szabela is a highly experienced and skilled dentist who prioritizes patient safety. During your consultation, he will discuss the risks and benefits of dental implants in detail.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Maintaining good oral hygiene is essential for the long-term success of your dental implants. Brush and floss your teeth regularly, and remember to clean around the implant site gently. Regular dental checkups and cleanings are crucial for monitoring your implants and ensuring their longevity .
