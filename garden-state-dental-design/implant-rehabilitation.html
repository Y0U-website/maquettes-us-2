What is the dental implant procedure like?

The implant rehabilitation procedure typically involves several stages:

Fix What’s Missing What conditions does Dental Implant Rehabilitation address? With implant rehabilitation Millburn & Clark patients can get a variety of dental issues addressed, including:

  • Consultation : Dr. Szabela will discuss your dental history, examine your mouth, and take 3D X-rays to determine if you are a candidate for dental implant surgery.
  • Planning : A personalized treatment plan for dental implant surgery will be created, including the number and location of implants needed and the type of restorations.
  • Dental Implant Placement : Dr. Szabela will surgically place the dental implants in your jawbone.
  • Healing : The jawbone will heal and integrate with the dental implants over several months.
  • Abutment Placement : Small connectors called abutments will be placed on the implants.
  • Restoration Placement : The final dental restorations , such as crowns, bridges, or dentures, will be attached to the abutments.
  • Single Missing Tooth : A single dental implant can replace a single missing tooth with a natural-looking crown, restoring function and protecting the remaining teeth.
  • Multiple Missing Teeth : Implant-supported bridges can replace several missing teeth in a row.
  • Full Tooth Loss : Implant-supported dentures provide a stable and comfortable solution for replacing all teeth in an arch.

What are the benefits of implant rehabilitation?

Dental implant rehabilitation offers a multitude of benefits for patients with missing teeth:

  • Improved Function : Dental implants allow you to chew and speak normally, enhancing oral health.
  • Enhanced Appearance : Implants provide natural-looking replacements for missing teeth, boosting your confidence in your smile.
  • Durability : Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Prevents Bone Loss : Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss that can occur with traditional dentures.
  • Improved Quality of Life : Regaining a complete and functional smile can significantly improve your quality of life.

Am I a candidate for implant rehabilitation?

If you are missing one or more teeth and are in good general health, you may be a candidate for implant rehabilitation . During a consultation, Dr. Szabela will evaluate your situation and medical history and determine if implant rehabilitation is the right treatment option to preserve your remaining natural teeth and restore your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the implant rehabilitation process take?

The implant rehabilitation process can vary depending on several factors, such as the number and location of implants needed and your healing rate. It can generally take several months, with healing time required between implant placement and restoration placement. Dr. Szabela will provide a detailed timeline during your consultation.

How much does implant rehabilitation cost?

The cost of implant rehabilitation can vary depending on the complexity of your case, the number of implants needed, and the type of restorations used. After a consultation with Dr. Szabela, our Garden State Dental Design team is happy to provide you with a personalized cost estimate. We understand that cost is a concern, and we offer flexible payment options to help make implant rehabilitation more affordable .

What are the risks associated with dental implant surgery?

As with any surgical procedure, dental implant surgery involves some potential risks, such as infection or bleeding. Dr. Szabela is a highly experienced and skilled dentist who prioritizes patient safety. During your consultation, he will discuss the risks and benefits of implant rehabilitation in detail.
