Dr. Joel P. Gardner is a board-certified orthodontist and proud member of the American Association of Orthodontists. With more than 25 years of experience, he is dedicated to providing precise, personalized orthodontic care designed to create healthy, confident smiles.

“Creating confident, healthy smiles is not just my profession — it’s my passion.”

Dr. Gardner earned his Bachelor of Science degree with high honors from Gonzaga University before completing his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at Oregon Health Sciences University School of Dentistry. He later completed advanced specialty training in orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics and became a diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontists — a distinction that reflects his…

Throughout his career, Dr. Gardner has remained committed to continuing education and modern treatment techniques, ensuring that patients benefit from the latest advancements in orthodontics.

He has proudly provided orthodontic treatment to families in Mount Vernon, Oak Harbor, Stanwood, and surrounding communities throughout Skagit County and Whidbey Island. Many patients return years later with their own children — something he considers one of the greatest privileges of his career.

Beyond the office, Dr. Gardner is deeply invested in the local community, supporting schools, organizations, and events. His dedication to both clinical excellence and community involvement defines the culture of Gardner Orthodontics.

Our Mount Vernon office serves families from throughout Skagit County and surrounding areas, offering advanced technology, flexible payment options, and a free initial consultation.
