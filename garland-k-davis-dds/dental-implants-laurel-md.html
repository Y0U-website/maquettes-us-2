See How We Transform Smiles With Dental Implants

  • Long-Term Solution: With proper care, dental implants are a long-lasting option for tooth replacement.
  • Stability: Dental implants have a secure anchoring in the jawbone. This eliminates any concerns of slipping or shifting.
  • Dietary Freedom: Enjoy all of your favorite foods with no restrictions. Dental implants provide the stability to chew different textures.
  • Easy Maintenance: Dental implant care is as simple as brushing your teeth and flossing.

What to expect every step of the way

Our Dental Implants Process

We use cutting-edge computerized imaging systems and highly trained staff members to ensure that every step of the process goes smoothly. In addition, we know how important it is for our patients to feel comfortable during their treatment, so we strive to provide a calming environment where they can be assured they are receiving the best care.

  • 1. Step One: Examination The dental implant process begins with a thorough examination and consultation to review your health history to replace missing teeth. Next, we will determine the number of teeth that need replacing and discuss your treatment goals.
  • 2. Image Mapping If a dental implant is the best option for you, Dr. Davis will use advanced computer imaging technology to map out the placement of the implants to maximize results.
  • 3. Implant Creation and Placing Once our team approves your plan, we will create an abutment (connecting piece) attached to each implant post in the jaw bone. This part helps ensure the strength of each implant and prevents any shifting or loosening over time. Once everything is finished, you can enjoy improved stability and comfort when chewing or speaking!
  • 4. Osseointegration This is the healing period of allowing the implant to fuse with your jawbone. The process typically takes several months. During this time, it is important to follow all instructions provided by Dr. Davis to ensure successful integration and long-term success with your new implants!

Precision Implant Placement

A key element in the long-term success of an implant is exact placement in healthy bone. When the implant is placed, a sophisticated, computerized imaging system guides its exact location in healthy bone to ensure its success. If you have lost bone mass, Dr. Davis can first build up the bone with a bone graft before proceeding with an implant. There are many steps to providing a strong, healthy implant, but with Dr. Davis’ extensive training and experience, you are in very good hands.
