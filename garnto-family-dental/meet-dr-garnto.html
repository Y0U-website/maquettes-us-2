Meet Dr. Garnto

Dr. Travis Garnto

Dr. Travis Garnto grew up in Wrightsville, Georgia. He received both a bachelor of science and a master of science in biology from Georgia College and State University. Before attending dental school, Dr. Garnto worked as an Environmental Consultant. He then completed his doctorate at the Dental College of Georgia at Augusta University. During his education at the Dental College of Georgia, Dr. Garnto earned coveted awards for distinction in research, ethics, and for his artistic achievements in cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Garnto has a passion for dentistry and loves giving his patients a beautiful smile. He enjoys listening to and collaborating with his patients of all ages to provide the best care, and always having the patient’s best interest at heart.

Dr. Garnto is currently a member of the Georgia Dental Association, American Dental Association, Eastern District Dental Society, Clarke County Dental Society, Georgia Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, Academy for Sports Dentistry, and the American Academy of General Dentistry. Dr. Garnto believes that learning is a lifelong commitment and routinely attends advanced continuing education courses.

Dr. Garnto resides in Oconee County with his wife, Susan, and their daughter, Laney. He acquired the practice of J. David Brown, D.M.D. in May of 2019. This practice has been serving the Watkinsville-Oconee community since 1976. Dr. Garnto loves being a part of this great community, and continuing Dr. Brown’s traditions of providing the highest level of service and quality care to his patients. In his free time, he enjoys fishing, hunting, and being with his family.
