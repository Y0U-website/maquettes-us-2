Dental Crowns

A crown functions as a protective shell, securely covering the visible part of your tooth while restoring strength, function, and aesthetics.

  • Severely Decayed – Restore structure and function after deep cavities.
  • Worn Down or Weakened – Reinforce teeth affected by grinding or large fillings.
  • Discolored or Misshapen – Improve the appearance of stained or misshaped teeth.
  • Following a Root Canal – Protect and preserve a tooth after treatment.
  • Supporting a Dental Bridge or Implant – Provide stability for missing tooth replacements.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

With proper care, dental crowns can last 10-15 years or longer. Their longevity depends on factors such as oral hygiene habits, regular dental check-ups, and avoiding excessive pressure from grinding or chewing hard foods.

At Garrett B. Lyons, DDS, we offer several crown materials to fit your needs:

Porcelain Crowns – Natural-looking and ideal for front teeth. Zirconia Crowns – Extremely durable, great for molars. Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal (PFM) Crowns – A strong, long-lasting blend of aesthetics and durability. Same-Day Crowns (if applicable) – A convenient option for patients needing a crown in a single visit.

The process typically involves two appointments:

Tooth Preparation & Impressions – The tooth is reshaped, and digital impressions are taken. Temporary Crown Placement (if needed) – A temporary crown protects the tooth while the custom crown is being made. Final Crown Placement – The permanent crown is bonded securely for a natural, long-lasting fit. For same-day crowns, the entire process may be completed in just one visit.

To ensure the longevity of your crown, follow these care tips:

  • Brush and floss daily to prevent decay around the crown.
  • Avoid chewing on hard objects like ice, pens, or hard candy.
  • Wear a nightguard if you grind your teeth.
  • Schedule regular dental check-ups to monitor your crown and overall oral health.
