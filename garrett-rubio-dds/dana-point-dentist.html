Dental Implants for Missing Teeth

Missing teeth can impact your appearance, confidence, and ability to chew comfortably. Dental implants are considered one of the most predictable and durable solutions for replacing missing teeth.

Unlike traditional tooth replacement options, implants replace both the visible tooth and the underlying tooth root. This helps stimulate the jawbone, reduce future bone loss, and preserve facial structure.

Dental implants function similarly to natural teeth, allowing patients to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. During your consultation, Dr. Rubio will discuss available treatment options and determine whether implants are the right solution for your needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How Often Should I Visit the Dentist?

Most patients should schedule a dental exam and cleaning every six months. Regular visits help prevent cavities, detect problems early, and support long-term oral health.

What Should I Do If I Have a Dental Emergency?

If you experience severe tooth pain, swelling, a broken tooth, or dental trauma, contact our office immediately. Prompt emergency dental care can help relieve discomfort, prevent complications, and improve the chances of saving a damaged tooth.

Are Dental Implants a Good Solution for Missing Teeth?

Yes. Dental implants are among the most effective solutions for tooth loss because they replace both the visible part of the tooth and the root. Implants help preserve bone structure, reduce the risk of bone loss, and function similarly to natural teeth.

What Are the Signs of Gum Disease?

Common signs include bleeding gums, swollen gum tissue, persistent bad breath, gum recession, and loose teeth. When gum disease is left untreated, it can lead to bone loss and eventual tooth loss. Treatment may include scaling and root planing or other treatment options, depending on the severity of the condition.

Do You Offer Invisalign Treatment and Other Cosmetic Dental Services?

Yes. We offer Invisalign treatment, professional teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, and other cosmetic procedures as part of our comprehensive dental care services. During your consultation, we will create a personalized treatment plan to help you achieve your smile goals.
