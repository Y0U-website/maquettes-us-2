Dentistry That Starts with Understanding

We believe good dentistry starts with taking the time to understand the full picture.

The practice

For some patients, that means keeping things simple routine care, small repairs, and long-term maintenance. For others, it means stepping back, evaluating carefully, and building a more comprehensive plan.

Either way, our approach stays the same: listen first, evaluate thoroughly, and explain everything clearly so you know exactly where you stand.

Welcome to Garrison Dental ​ ​ A Practice Built on Relationships ​ We’re a father-and-son team, and over time that’s shaped the way we approach care.

We take the time to get to know the people who come through our doors what’s going on, what they’re comfortable with, and what makes sense for them.

It’s something we work through together, based on what’s actually right for the person in front of us.

Many of the people we see have been coming here for decades, which allows for a more thoughtful, individualized approach to care.
