Dr. Michael Gershanok is a distinguished New York dentist with over 30 years of experience providing exceptional, patient-centered care. Patients seek him for his depth of experience, leading-edge techniques, and commitment to delivering dentistry of the highest standard.

Dr. Gershanok is a long-standing member of the American Dental Association and the Ninth District Dental Society and is highly regarded by his peers. In addition to his private practice, he has spent decades teaching and mentoring dental residents at Montefiore Hospital and Jacobi Hospital, and he regularly lectures nationally and internationally.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Gershanok enjoys racing BMWs, teaching performance driving, skiing, and practicing pottery, pursuits that reflect his precision, discipline, and appreciation for craftsmanship.
