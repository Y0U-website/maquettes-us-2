Special Needs Pediatric Dentist

We invite you to request an appointment and see how gentle, understanding dental care can make a difference for your child.

  • Down Syndrome : Our team is experienced in managing oral health concerns common with Down syndrome, such as gum sensitivity and bite issues.
  • Cerebral Palsy : We accommodate mobility challenges and tailor treatments to ensure comfort and accessibility.
  • Special Healthcare Needs and Developmental Delay: We offer flexible appointment times and gradual acclimation to dental routines.
