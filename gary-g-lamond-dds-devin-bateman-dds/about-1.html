Meet Our Staff

Each member of our staff has an important role in serving our patients.

Our Office Manager heads the front desk. You know them as the folks that schedule your appointment. Far beyond that is a mountain of detailed paperwork that keeps our office humming. They also do their best to help our patients navigate the confusing world of dental insurance.

Our Hygienists are talented, skilled, and well-trained professionals. The team consists of registered Dental Hygienists. Our Hygienists have a combined 32 years of experience with Dr. LaMond. At each hygiene appointment, they detect early changes in your mouth, provide education, and personalize your home care. They are dedicated to provide the best care possible to each patient.

Our Chairside Dental Assistants are dedicated members of our team. You may know the assistant as the individual who removes saliva and water from your mouth during dental procedures. Dental Assistants go far beyond that to keep the office running smoothly. They prepare the operatory, make sure all instruments are properly sterile, take radiographs, review health history, take impressions, and much more. Our Chairside Assistants are top-notch, dedicated professionals that help assure excellent patient care.
