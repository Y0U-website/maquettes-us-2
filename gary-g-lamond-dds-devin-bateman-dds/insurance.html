Insurance & Financing

Not sure about your insurance benefits or how your plan works in our office?

If you don’t have insurance or you’re unsure what is covered don’t worry!

Our office is pleased to offer several different financing options to allow you to spread your payments over several months & still receive the care that you need to make your smile bright.

We know the world of dental insurance and benefits can be quite tricky and confusing, so feel free to call and speak to our trusted insurance coordinator. We will work with you & your insurance to find which options & solutions fit your needs, as well as help with any dental insurance & benefit questions that you might have.

Thank you for choosing Bateman & Lamond family dentistry in Milford, we look forward to seeing you soon!

We will gladly process your insurance claims for you after treatment in our office. There are certain plans, however, that we do not participate in, such as HMO’s. Please provide your insurance information to our office when you call so that we can verify your benefits before your first visit.

We accept checks, cash and all major credit cards. We also offer flexible in-house payment plans. Please talk to our insurance coordinator for details.

CareCredit is available to help you pay for treatments and procedures your insurance doesn’t cover. We offer No Interest* financing or low minimum monthly payment options. It ’s easy to apply for CareCredit online or by phone.

For more information or to apply online, visit carecredit.com
