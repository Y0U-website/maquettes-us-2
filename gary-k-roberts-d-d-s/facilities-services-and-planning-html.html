Facilities Services

Facilities Services is comprised of business units that manage all aspects of our nonclinical operations, planning & construction, and general services such as patient transport, food services, security, property/facility management, and transportation. Each business unit plays a unique, important role and is key to our success. Business units include:

Mission Statement Facilities Services enables high-quality healthcare by ensuring environments are safe, reliable, and aligned with operational and clinical needs—planning for tomorrow, managing for today.

Vision Statement To lead in delivering safe, efficient, and responsive environments that support the future of healthcare.

Business Units Facilities Services create and maintain safe, highly functional, and welcoming environments across all sites while advancing innovation in construction, sustainability, and operations.

Our Mission Facilities Services is a shared service that provides non-clinical operational support and construction to Stanford Health Care (SHC) & other Stanford Medicine entities.

Our Vision Facilities Services’ mission is to ensure safe and successful non-clinical operations 24/7 and plan for the organization’s future facility needs.

&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbspBusiness Units

Hospitality Services plays a critical role in shaping the overall experience within healthcare environments. The program focuses on delivering comfort, convenience, and a sense of welcome for patients, families, and staff. By integrating thoughtful amenities and support systems, Hospitality Services enhances the quality of care and strengthens the connection between clinical excellence and the human experience.​

The business unit provides a comprehensive range of services designed to enhance the patient and visitor journey. These include concierge assistance, guest accommodations, and coordination of resources for families during extended stays. Hospitality Services also supports staff by maintaining environments that promote well-being and operational efficiency. Each service is developed with attention to detail, ensuring alignment with organizational standards and regulatory requirements.​

Hospitality Services operates through collaboration with clinical teams, facilities management, and design professionals to create spaces that are functional, welcoming, and safe. Processes emphasize consistency, responsiveness, and adaptability to meet diverse needs. Continuous evaluation and feedback loops are embedded to maintain service quality and identify opportunities for improvement.​

A strong hospitality program contributes directly to patient satisfaction, staff engagement, and organizational reputation. By prioritizing comfort and accessibility, Hospitality Services supports healing and fosters trust. The integration of hospitality principles into healthcare operations reflects a commitment to excellence beyond clinical care, reinforcing the organization’s mission to deliver a holistic experience.

Facilities Infrastructure & Safety ensures that the built environment supports operational excellence, regulatory compliance, and long-term sustainability. This function encompasses the planning, maintenance, and protection of critical building systems, creating spaces that are safe, reliable, and aligned with healthcare standards.​

The program manages essential infrastructure components, including mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems, as well as life safety systems such as fire protection and emergency power. Preventive maintenance strategies and rigorous inspection protocols are implemented to minimize risk and maintain uninterrupted operations. Compliance with local, state, and federal regulations is a foundational priority, ensuring that facilities meet or exceed all safety requirements.​

Facilities Infrastructure & Safety operates through a proactive model that emphasizes resilience and adaptability. Collaboration with design and construction teams during new projects and renovations ensures that infrastructure is integrated seamlessly and supports future growth. Continuous monitoring and data-driven decision-making enable rapid response to emerging issues while optimizing performance and resource utilization.​

Reliable infrastructure and robust safety programs are essential to delivering high-quality care and maintaining trust within the healthcare environment. By safeguarding building systems and prioritizing life safety, Facilities Infrastructure & Safety contributes to operational continuity, patient well-being, and staff confidence. This commitment reflects industry best practices and reinforces the organization’s dedication to excellence in every aspect of facility management.

Ambulatory Facilities & Activations supports the development and readiness of outpatient environments, ensuring that spaces are operationally efficient, patient-centered, and aligned with organizational standards. This function bridges planning, design, and activation processes to deliver facilities that meet the evolving needs of ambulatory care.​

The program oversees the coordination of new clinic openings, expansions, and relocations, managing timelines, resources, and stakeholder engagement. Responsibilities include space planning, infrastructure readiness, and integration of technology to support clinical workflows. Activation efforts encompass equipment installation, signage, and wayfinding, ensuring that each location is fully prepared for patient care on day one.​

Ambulatory Facilities & Activations employs a structured methodology that emphasizes collaboration across design, construction, and operational teams. Early involvement in project planning enables alignment with clinical requirements and regulatory standards. Detailed activation checklists and phased readiness reviews reduce risk and support seamless transitions from construction to operational use.​

Effective ambulatory facility planning and activation enhance access to care, improve patient experience, and optimize staff efficiency. By delivering functional, welcoming environments, this program supports organizational growth and reinforces the commitment to high-quality outpatient services. The approach reflects industry best practices and ensures that every new space contributes to operational excellence and patient satisfaction.

The Sustainability Program Office provides strategic leadership and operational direction to advance environmentally responsible practices across Stanford Health Care (SHC). This function ensures that sustainability is embedded in organizational decision‑making, aligning daily operations with long‑term climate goals, regulatory expectations, and SHC’s commitment to patient and community well‑being.​

Facilities Planning & Design provides strategic oversight and creative direction for the development of functional, efficient, and aesthetically engaging environments. This function ensures that every space aligns with operational requirements, regulatory standards, and the organization’s vision for patient-centered care.​

The program manages all phases of planning and design, including project initiation, schematic development, and construction documentation. Responsibilities include space programming, interior design coordination, and integration of wayfinding systems to support intuitive navigation. Facilities Planning & Design also incorporates art and environmental elements into projects, ensuring dedicated budgets and infrastructure for commissioned works and installations.​

A collaborative process drives planning and design efforts, engaging architects, engineers, and operational stakeholders from concept through construction. Regular design reviews and site visits verify alignment with project goals and prevent costly changes. Attention to detail during critical milestones—such as wall rough-in and pre-drywall phases—ensures proper placement of integrated features, including art and signage.​

Thoughtful planning and design create environments that enhance patient experience, support staff efficiency, and reflect the organization’s commitment to excellence. By balancing functionality with aesthetics, Facilities Planning & Design delivers spaces that are adaptable, sustainable, and aligned with long-term strategic objectives. This approach reinforces industry best practices and elevates the quality of care through the built environment.
