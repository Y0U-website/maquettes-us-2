Modern Care, Personal Attention Discover the philosophy, experience, and patient-first approach behind Gary L. Torres D.M.D.

Meet Dr. Gary L. Torres Since 1994 Dr. Gary L Torres has been committed to providing consistent, high-quality dental care to the Titusville community.

His approach is simple-treat people the right way, take the time to do things properly and deliver care patients can trust for the long term.

I’m proud to provide my patients with the best in dental technology, treatment options, and patient comfort. it is my pleasure to address all of your questions and concerns.

A Personalized Approach to Dental Care In today’s world of high-volume, corporate dentistry, Dr. Torres has built his practice differently.

Patients are never rushed, treatment is never one-size-fits-all, and every decision is made with long-term health in mind.

The goal isn’t just to complete procedures-it’s to create an experience where patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident in their care.

Experience You Can Trust. Technology You Can Feel. Dr. Torres is a graduate of the University of Florida College of Dentistry and brings decades of hands-on experience to every patient he treats.

He combines that experience with advanced dental technology including 3D imaging, digital impressions, and same-day CEREC restorations to deliver care that is both precise and efficient.

Through ongoing education and training, Dr. Torres and his team stay current with modern techniques, continually refining their skills to provide the highest level of care.

This commitment to growth and technology ensures a better, more consistent experience for every patient.

A Practice Built on Trust and Relationships This is a privately owned, family-involved practice built on long term relationships.

Dr.Torres works alongside his wife and daughter-in-law, with a team that has developed strong loyalty and pride in the care they provide.

That consistency matters, patients know who they’re seeing, who they’re speaking with, and what level of care to expect every time they walk in.

Beyond The Office Dr. Torres is known for his steady, patient-first approach. He takes pride in treating people fairly, explaining care clearly, and doing what’s right regardless of the situation.

His personality reflects that balance professional, approachable and often lighthearted, with a sense of humor that helps patients feel more at ease.

Outside the office Dr. Torres is actively involved in the local community and supports events throughout Titusville.

He enjoys golfing, ballroom dancing, and spending time at the range, bringing the same focus and discipline to his hobbies as he does to his work.

Experience the Difference of Personalized Dental Care Care you can trust. A team that knows you. Results that last.
