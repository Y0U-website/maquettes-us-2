The practice

Our practice has been part of the New Rochelle community for 70 years. It was founded by Dr. Donald Scharoff in 1956, and Dr. Gary Scharoff joined his father in practice in 1985. Dr. Donald Scharoff retired after 51 years of dentistry, but the philosophy he established—caring for every patient as if they were a member of our own family—continues today.

Our philosophy is to keep patients informed and involved in decisions about their dental health. We take the time to explain treatment options, answer questions, and make our patients partners in their care.

Dr. Gary Scharoff was born and raised in New Rochelle and knew from a very early age that dentistry was what he wanted to do. A second-generation dentist, he grew up around dentists and dentistry. Almost from the day he could walk, he was looking over or under his father’s shoulder to see what was going on in the office. When he was finally tall enough, he would observe and assist his father…

A 1980 graduate of Hobart College with a Bachelor of Science degree in Chemistry, Dr. Scharoff went on to New York University College of Dentistry, where he received his D.D.S. degree in 1984—30 years after his father received his D.D.S. degree from the same university. Following graduation, he completed a General Practice Residency at Maimonides Medical Center in Brooklyn, New York.

Dr. Scharoff served as Director of Dentistry at the United Hebrew Geriatric Center in New Rochelle for 30 years. In 1987, after 31 years on Pelham Road, the family dental practice was relocated to its present location at 1255 North Avenue in the Wykagyl section of New Rochelle.

Dr. Scharoff has completed more than 1,000 hours of continuing dental education, with particular emphasis on advances in cosmetic, restorative, and implant dentistry.

Les soins

We provide a wide variety of dental services for patients ranging in age from three to 100. Our services include comprehensive preventive and restorative care, cosmetic dentistry, implant dentistry, and many other aspects of general dental care.
