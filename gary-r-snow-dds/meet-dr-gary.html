DR. GARY

I always use in magnification, not because I need it, but I do not believe optimal treatment can be provided without it. Dentistry is microsurgery- there is a reason Operating room doctors wear magnification, we use it for the same reason. We do not use city water in our water lines, due to the likelihood of bacterial growth. We only use distilled water with anti-bacterial treatment.

I Graduate from Neshannock High School in 1976. After completion of a B.S. in biology at the University of Pittsburgh I attended West Virginia University School of Dentistry, where I earned my D.D.S. degree in 1984.

Returning to New Castle to join my father and brothers in his family practice, I now enjoy life with my two beautiful daughters. I’m an ordained elder at Northminster Presbyterian Church and support Lawrence County Habitat for Humanity and several other local charities.
