ClearCorrect Invisible Braces

ClearCorrect offers a swift and inconspicuous orthodontic solution designed to rapidly transform your smile without the inconvenience of traditional braces. Consisting of a series of clear plastic aligner trays, ClearCorrect gradually and efficiently delivers lasting results, making it an ideal choice for busy adults seeking seamless orthodontic care that seamlessly integrates into their daily routines.

During your visit, we will conduct a thorough assessment of your mouth, capturing impressions and photos of your teeth to customize your aligners for optimal teeth straightening. The virtually invisible aligners require replacement every two weeks to ensure the best results, and their gentle pressure means you can comfortably enjoy orthodontic treatment without the discomfort associated with metal braces.
