A neuromuscular orthotic is a specialized dental appliance designed to position the jaw in a relaxed, physiological position, addressing muscle imbalances and TMJ dysfunction. It’s a custom-made, removable device that fits over the lower teeth and aims to relieve pain and improve function by promoting a more comfortable and functional jaw position.
