Need a dental crown without waiting weeks for your final restoration? Gary Silva DDS, Inc. uses advanced CEREC technology to provide same-day dental crowns right here in Lodi. Digital technology allows our team to design and produce your final crown in-office, making it possible to restore your tooth in a single appointment in many cases.

State-of-the-art digital X-rays enhance our diagnostics to ensure accurate and thorough assessments of your dental health. We provide personalized treatment plans to match your unique dental needs.

Our advanced milling technology from Cermill allows us to craft crowns, bridges, custom implant abutment, and more with unparalleled precision. With the ability to fabricate low profile bite guards in just 2 hours, you can have it ready for a comfortable fit the very next day.

For optimal hygiene and effective treatment of periodontal disease, we utilize soft tissue lasers to ensure your oral health is in top condition.
