Signature Office Cookie Recipe

The key to this recipe is to under bake because they need time to set after they are pulled out of the oven. Preheat oven to 350.

Cream: - 2 cups of butter (Plugar or European only) - 2 cups of sugar - 2 cups of brown sugar Add: - 4 eggs (extra large or jumbo) - 1 tablespoon of vanilla - 4 cups of flour (unbleached) - 5 cups of oatmeal (ground fine in food processor) Mix together, then add: - 1½ teaspoons of salt - 2 teaspoons of baking powder - 2 teaspoons of baking soda Finally, add: - 24 ounce bag of dark or bittersweet chocolate chips - 8 ounce (grated) milk chocolate bar - 3 cups of chopped nuts, pecans or walnuts - Measure dough to 2.5 oz. balls and bake for 12 minutes
