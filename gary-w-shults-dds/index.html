The practice

Our practice provides a wide variety of dental services and procedures to address all of our patient’s dental health needs. By combining quality patient care with up-to-date technology, we are able to treat patients of all ages in a comfortable and relaxing setting.

Dental implants are a popular choice for patients seeking a permanent restorative solution to missing teeth, or to help secure dentures in place.

Both a denture and partial will help transform your entire smile. If you&#x27;re missing your natural teeth, we can make you a customized denture in our in-house lab.

Clear aligners are a discrete orthodontic treatment that gently move teeth into place over time. This is a popular alternative to traditional braces, giving patients beautiful straight smiles.

Years of neglect, stains and discoloration can affect the way your smile looks. We offer an array of cosmetic options to enhance and correct your existing smile.

Dr. Shults is an experienced oral surgeon who offers many up-to-date surgical options to meet your dental needs, including the use of N20 and conscious sedation.

Regular dental exams and cleanings are vital to maintaining your oral health. We offer comprehensive services to keep your teeth and gums healthy

Dental implants function like our natural teeth, and allow patients to speak and eat normally. They are comfortable in the mouth, and do not cause gum or mouth irritation like with other restorative solutions. Implants provide long lasting results, and if cared for properly they can last a lifetime.
