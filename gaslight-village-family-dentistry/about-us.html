Dr. Joshua Wansten

Education and Experience

Dr. Wansten earned his Bachelor of Science in Biopsychology from the University of Michigan in 2004, followed by his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from U of M s School of Dentistry in 2009. He completed his General Practice Residency at Loyola University Chicago in 2010, providing him with advanced training in complex dental procedures. Dr. Wansten joined Gaslight Village Family Dentistry in 2022.

Patient Care Philosophy

Dr. Wansten was drawn to dentistry because it combines his fascination with science with the opportunity to help people. He appreciates the blend of scientific knowledge, compassion, artistic vision, problem-solving skills, and the commitment to excellence required for every procedure.

His favorite cases are those involving transformative journeys. Whether helping a patient overcome years of dental anxiety, restoring smiles through complex dentistry, or watching someone learn to value their health and themselves. Dr. Wansten finds great fulfillment in working alongside patients to achieve life-changing results.

Team Leadership

Dr. Wansten feels fortunate to be surrounded by an exceptional team who continuously support each other while sharing the common goal of helping patients achieve their healthiest, happiest smiles. He takes pride in leading a group of professionals who strive for excellence and regularly go the extra mile for both patients and colleagues.

Community Connection and Personal Interests

A true local, Dr. Wansten grew up in Kentwood and is thrilled to have returned to West Michigan after spending several years in Chicago. He and his wife have made East Grand Rapids their home, drawn by the strong sense of community and proximity to family and Lake Michigan s beautiful beaches.

Outside the office, Dr. Wansten enjoys an active lifestyle that includes tennis, pickleball, basketball, and mountain biking on local trails. He s also a devoted University of Michigan football season ticket holder. At home, he loves experimenting with new recipes in the kitchen and tending to his garden. Dr. Wansten enjoys music, whether listening to his growing vinyl record collection or attending live concerts. He s also an avid reader and lifelong learner, currently teaching himself guitar and harmonica.
