We agree with the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry guidelines that recommend children have their first dental checkup by age one, or within six months of getting their first baby tooth, whichever comes first.

We are in network with Delta Dental, but file all claims on our patients’ behalf and accept payments from PPO insurances with which we are out of network. Our dental team will do everything to help you understand your coverage and make sure you receive the maximum benefit under your policy.

A crown is a porcelain or ceramic covering that completely surrounds a tooth to restore comfortable chewing and a natural-looking appearance.

We commonly recommend dental crowns for teeth that are too decayed for a filling, weakened by cracks or damage, or that already have a large filling, providing support and strength so you can chew comfortably again.
