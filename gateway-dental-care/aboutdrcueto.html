0 Dr. Cueto Dr. Cueto’s journey to becoming your go-to dental expert started at the prestigious Universidad Iberoamericana in the Dominican Republic, after getting his undergraduate degree at Florida International University in Miami (sun, fun, and good vibes!). After dental school, Dr. Cueto didn’t stop there—he completed TWO years of General Practice Residency, first at Akron City Hospital and then at Carolinas Medical Center in Charlotte, NC. That’s some serious hands-on experience!

What sets Dr. Cueto apart is his dedication to keeping his skills top-notch. He’s always learning and growing, diving into advanced topics like dental implants, oral surgery, cosmetic dentistry, and sedation, all to give YOU the best care possible. He truly values the trust and respect his patients show him and loves staying up to date on the latest dental trends.

A proud member of the American Dental Association , Ohio Dental Association , and Stark County Dental Society , Dr. Cueto is also passionate about giving back to the community. He’s involved in initiatives like Give Kids A Smile , helping kids in need get the dental care they deserve.

Dr. Cueto feels incredibly lucky to work with a team that’s as caring and knowledgeable as they are fun! While he might have grown up in sunny South Florida, he’s found his forever home in Northeast Ohio—there’s no place like it to work, live, and raise a family. When he’s not transforming smiles, you’ll catch him out and about—playing sports, hitting the slopes to ski, camping under the stars, or cheering on the local college and high school football teams!
