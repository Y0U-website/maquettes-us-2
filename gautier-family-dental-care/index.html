Dr. Taylor DeHaven earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Medical University of South Carolina in 2026 after completing her undergraduate studies at University of South Carolina. Originally from Northern Virginia, she is excited to now call the Gulf Coast home as she begins her career in dentistry.

Dr. DeHaven is thrilled to begin this new chapter and serve the Gautier community. She is passionate about providing compassionate, high-quality dental care in a comfortable and welcoming environment and values building long-term relationships with her patients. Her clinical philosophy is centered on patient education, trust, and helping each individual feel confident and at ease in their care.

During her training, Dr. DeHaven participated in international dental mission trips as well as domestic RAM (Remote Area Medical) clinics, experiences that deepened her passion for service and providing care to underserved communities. Her faith is an important part of her life and motivates her commitment to serving others both in and outside of the dental office.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. DeHaven enjoys traveling, reading, and crafting, as well as cheering on hockey and college football. She also enjoys spending time with family and friends and exploring her new home along the Gulf Coast.
