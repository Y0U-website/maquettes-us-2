Our Service Area

Our office has sat at the same address on Milltown Road for more than forty years, a few hundred feet from where Newport Gap Pike splits off toward Prices Corner . That puts us within a short drive of most of western New Castle County. Families come to us from Pike Creek and Hockessin in about ten to fifteen minutes, and from Greenville and North Star by way of Limestone Road and Kennett Pike.

Closer in, we are the neighborhood practice for Marshallton , Stanton , Newport , and Elsmere all of them close enough that patients tend to schedule around school pickup rather than around traffic. Across the rest of Wilmington , and further out in Brookside , Ogletown , and New Castle , people generally choose us on purpose: because we are independent and family-owned rather than part of a chain, or because someone in their family already comes here.
