The practice

Our Mission At Reliance Dental Care, we strive to always give our patients the best care. Our clinic is created around family centered values, which allows families to share a perfect and healthy smile with each other.

We were established in 2005, with the aim of providing comprehensive dental care for the entire family.

We take pride in offering high quality individualized affordable dental care that meets your exact needs and expectations.

Our clinic is centered around family values, we make sure that every family has perfect and healthy smiles.

Every one of our staff members values family and aims to create a safe and healthy environment here at our clinic.

We offer comprehensive evaluations and treatments to all ages; from ages 2-100, we provide the necessary treatment.

Our treatments include general dentistry, restorative treatment, periodontal treatment, and more!
