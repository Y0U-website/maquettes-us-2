Insurance / Payment Information

If you are an existing patient, please check with our front desk to make sure that we accept that insurance

If you are unsure if we accept your insurance, please contact our office and we will try our best to assist you

Please scroll to the bottom for more questions regarding insurances!

Note: We do not accept any discounted insurance plans

Payments Currently, we only accept payments either by cash, check, or credit/debit/HSA card

You may mail us a check or pay over the phone for your bill

We accept the following as payment options:

Online Payments We are currently trying to implement a system that allows our patients to pay online, however, the project is still in the works.. Please reach out to our office for any questions regarding billing and payments. We apologize for the inconvenience

In-Network Insurances Please make sure that we are in network with your insurance before calling us to set up an appointment.

You may refer to this guide on insurances that we accept. If you have more questions, please contact our office. Thanks!
