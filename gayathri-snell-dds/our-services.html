Periodontal Treatment

Periodontal treatment helps prevent gum disease. A typical periodontal treatment included scaling and root planning (deep cleaning) and in more advanced situations, surgery. At our clinic, Dr. Dempsey, a board certified Periodontist, helps patients treat and manage their Periodontal condition.

Restorative Care

A healthy smile will make a significant difference in your life. At our clinic, we offer all aspects of restorative dental care, including fillings, crowns, bridges, and implants.

Partial and Complete Dentures

If the natural teeth cannot be saved (or if they are already lost), we can improve your function by fabricating partial or complete dentures.

Extractions

When the teeth cannot be saved due to tooth decay, periodontal disease, or other conditions, they may need to be extracted. At our clinic, we offer some extraction services. If we feel as if the extraction is better done in an oral surgeon’s clinic, we would refer you to their office.

Emergency Care

Whether you are an existing patient or a new patient, we will try our best to accommodate you on any emergencies that happen with your teeth.
