Meet Our Staff!

After moving to Boston, she worked as a General Dentist at Dorchester House Multi-Service Health Center and at Cambridge Dental Associates.

Dr. Snell has been providing dental care at Reliance Dental Care since it’s inception in 2005. She is a member of the American Dental Association and the Massachusetts Dental Society.

Outside of her dental life, she enjoys spending time with her husband and two daughters.

Dr. Raymond Dempsey (DMD) - Periodontist Dr. Raymond Dempsey practices Periodontics and Implant dentistry at our office. Dr. Dempsey earned his Bachelor’s degree in Biology from Trinity College in Connecticut and received his Doctorate in Dental Medicine from Tufts University.

He completed his residency in General Dentistry at Mount Sinal Medical Center in New York City. He then completed his training in Periodontics and Dental Implants at Case Western Reserve University in Ohio

Hernande - Dental Hygienist Hernande graduated from Middlesex Community College with an Associate’s in Dental Hygiene on the Dean’s list. She also holds a Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration. In her free time, she loves traveling and relaxing with family.

Mona - Dental Hygienist Mona graduated from Middlesex Community College in 2011 with an Associate’s of Science in Dental Hygiene with the highest honor. Upon graduating from Hygiene School, she has been inducted into Phi Theta Kappa, the national honor society. She also holds a bachelor’s degree in Economics and Business Administration.

CheChe - Dental Assistant CheChe is one of Dr. Snell’s assistant. She helps Dr. Snell with various procedures and ensures that all patients go through a smooth procedure. In her free time, she loves traveling and spending time with family.

Khanh - Dental Assistant Khanh is one of Dr. Snell’s and Dr. Dempsey’s assistant. She has been at the office since 2012. She helps patients with different treatments ranging from general dentistry to periodontal treatment. In her free time, she loves to cook and spend time with her family, especially her dogs.

Prisha - Front Desk Receptionist Prisha is currently in college completing her B.S. in Mathematics. Working in a dental office allows her to practice her problem solving skills, which is a huge part of Math. Outside of work, she loves traveling and baking.
