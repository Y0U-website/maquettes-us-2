Your First Visit

Expect to feel welcomed and not anxious about your first visit to Geise Dental. Expect an inviting atmosphere and a friendly staff. While waiting in the lobby, enjoy a cup of coffee from the Keurig or a cold water from the cooler: Make yourself at home.

Once the paperwork is ready, we will perform your cleaning and exam in one of our modern patient rooms. During your cleaning, you can enjoy watching the flat-screen TVs streaming our favorite shows (HGTV is our favorite) or choose to listen to Pandora music.

Following the cleaning, one of our doctors will greet you and then do a thorough and gentle exam. Our doctors will then inform you of any treatment, if there is any to be done, that needs to be completed. We use state-of-the-art digital x-rays and intraoral cameras to diagnose and effectively inform our patients.

At Geise Dental, we educate our patients on proper oral hygiene and stress preventative measures to preserve your healthy smile for a lifetime.

When our patients require treatment, we strive to be as informative as possible. Providing different treatment options, when possible, allows our patients to decide which treatment method suits their needs the best.

Dental anxiety can be greatly alleviated by educating our patients on what to expect during their dental procedure and by using advanced techniques to make the procedure comfortable and pleasant.
