Implants / Extractions

Precise dental procedures, including tooth extractions and dental implant placement, designed to restore your oral health, function, and confidence.

Each procedure is carefully planned using digital 3D imaging to support accuracy, safety, and predictable treatment. Whether removing a problematic or damaged tooth or replacing missing teeth with modern dental implants, Dr. Atallah takes a personalized, minimally invasive approach whenever appropriate.

Our goal is to provide stable, natural-looking results that restore comfort, function, and confidence while supporting your long-term oral health.

Aligners / Orthodontics

Achieving a straight, balanced smile goes beyond appearance. Proper tooth alignment can improve comfort, bite function, and long-term oral health.

At GEM Dental, we offer modern orthodontic solutions, including clear aligners and traditional braces , designed to align your teeth discreetly and effectively. Using digital 3D scanning and precise treatment planning, Dr. Atallah creates personalized treatment plans designed for controlled, comfortable tooth movement and predictable results.

Whether you are looking to correct crowding, spacing, crooked teeth, or bite issues , our goal is to create a smile that looks natural, feels comfortable, and supports long-term dental health.

Serving patients in Longwood, Altamonte Springs, and surrounding Central Florida communities , Gem Dental provides personalized orthodontic care in a comfortable, modern dental environment.

Root canals

Root canal treatment is designed to save infected or injured teeth by removing damaged pulp tissue, relieving pain, and preserving the natural tooth.

At GEM Dental, we use modern rotary instrumentation, digital imaging, and advanced techniques to provide precise, efficient, and comfortable root canal treatment. By treating infection within the tooth and carefully sealing the root canals, we work to preserve your natural tooth and restore its function.

Whether you are experiencing tooth pain, sensitivity, swelling, or a damaged tooth , Dr. Atallah provides personalized root canal care focused on comfort, function, and long-term oral health.

The goal is to help you keep your natural tooth and return to a healthy, comfortable smile.
