The cost of treatment depends on the severity of the patient’s problem. We will always explain our findings to you fully. If you do not understand something please ask. There are usually several ways to obtain good dental treatment results. All available treatment modalities will be explained to you along with the pros and cons of each. Investment in dental treatment can be expensive. We will work with your dental insurance to make sure you get the maximum benefits you are entitled to. Dental treatment is however between you and the dentist.

In an effort to keep dentistry fees down while maintaining the highest level of professional care, we have established this financial policy:

Treatments paid for in full by the onset of treatment will be given a discount.

To fit your individual needs, financial arrangements can be made to extend your payments over a period of months. In-office financing is offered, with no finance charge.

Third party financing is available, many plans of which are interest free.

For your convenience and the portion not covered by your insurance, we accept payment by Visa, MC, Am Express, Discover, Cash or Personal Check.

We will be happy to accept whichever works best for you.
