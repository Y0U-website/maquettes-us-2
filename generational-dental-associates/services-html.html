Our Services

Comprehensive dental care for every member of your family — from first teeth to lifelong smiles. Give us a call or stop in to schedule.

Routine Cleanings & Exams Professional cleanings and thorough exams for patients of all ages, using modern equipment for a comfortable, stress-free visit. Regular checkups are the foundation of a healthy smile — we recommend two per year.

Invisalign & Clear Aligners A clear and comfortable alternative to traditional braces. We use Invisalign to straighten teeth discreetly, including Invisalign Teen. Custom aligners are virtually invisible and removable for easy eating and cleaning.

Crowns Durable, natural-looking crowns crafted with modern materials to restore strength and appearance to damaged or weakened teeth. Our crowns are designed to be attractive, long-lasting, and indistinguishable from your natural teeth.

Dentures Custom complete or partial dentures designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth. We take precise impressions to ensure a comfortable, secure fit that restores your ability to eat and smile with confidence.

Tooth Extractions

Gentle extractions when a tooth can’t be saved, focused entirely on your comfort and a smooth recovery. We use modern techniques and local anesthetic to make the process as easy as possible, and discuss replacement options afterward.

Root Canal Therapy Gentle, effective root canal treatment using modern technology to save your natural tooth and relieve pain. Today’s root canal procedures are nothing to fear — most patients report feeling surprisingly comfortable throughout.

Dental Implants Permanent solutions for missing or damaged teeth, with a personalized consultation and restoration plan. Implants look and function like natural teeth and are designed to last a lifetime with proper care.

Teeth Whitening Safe, professional-grade whitening using the latest technology, tailored to your smile for noticeably brighter, longer-lasting results. A simple way to boost your confidence and refresh your appearance.

Veneers & Smile Makeovers Cosmetic veneers and full smile makeovers using top-quality materials and the latest techniques. Whether you want to correct chips, stains, or gaps, we’ll craft a smile that looks completely natural and uniquely yours.

Pediatric Dentistry Compassionate dental care for children, helping them feel comfortable and build healthy habits early. We take extra care to ensure young patients have a positive, fear-free experience that sets them up for a lifetime of good oral health.

Emergency Dental Care Same-day care for dental emergencies — call us right away so we can see you as soon as possible. Whether it’s a cracked tooth, severe pain, or a knocked-out tooth, we’re here to help when you need us most.
