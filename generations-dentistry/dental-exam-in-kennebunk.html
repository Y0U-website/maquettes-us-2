Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the difference between plaque and tartar?

Plaque refers to the bacteria that gather around your teeth as they eat stuck food particles that collect in your mouth. Plaque is unavoidable, as there will always be bacteria in your mouth, but regular oral hygiene can easily remove it.

Tartar, however, is a different story. Tartar is a hard substance that forms on your teeth when plaque is left to accumulate. Tartar causes cavities and gum disease over time. Brushing alone cannot reliably remove tartar, so it’s important for you to visit us for your dental exam in Kennebunk. We will remove any built-up tartar.

Are there any risks to a dental exam?

Dental exams carry very little risk for most patients. You may experience some mild discomfort from the cleaning, depending on the sensitivity of your teeth and gums, but we don’t use any devices or tools that carry any notable risks when operated by our trained dental team.

When should I visit you for my exam?

We generally recommend a dental visit every 6 months for routine cleaning, but for some patients, we recommend more frequent visits depending on your oral health.

If you notice dark red, bleeding gums, or excessive sensitivity, you shouldn’t wait to schedule your appointment. We’ll help clean your teeth and work towards improving your overall oral health.

What happens if you find a problem during my exam?

If we discover areas of concern during your exam, such as cavities, broken teeth, or infections, we may need to schedule a follow-up appointment to perform restorative work as needed. On occasion, you may need a referral to one of our vetted specialists. We will guide you through the process and make sure you understand the reason for the referral. We will make sure you get the best treatment available.
