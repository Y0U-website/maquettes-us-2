After Your Dental Implant

  • After we place the implant, take it easy for the next 24 hours. Avoid any exercise and heavy lifting.
  • If we give you prescription pain medication or antibiotics, complete your entire medication regimen to promote healing.
  • For the next few days, treat the area around your implant gently. Brush and floss carefully to avoid disrupting clotting and healing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to recover from dental implants?

Restoration with dental implants in Kennebunk consists of two major steps. Placing the implant itself requires about 3 months of recovery time for the implant to properly integrate with your bone tissue.

Once your jaw is ready, we can complete the implant with a crown restoration, which doesn’t require any recovery time. You’ll enjoy your new, natural-looking implant immediately.

Do dental implants need special care?

For the most part, your dental implants require the same brushing and flossing routine as the rest of your teeth. You’ll need to take extra care in flossing around the implant and under the crown’s edges to clear out any debris and prevent gum disease, but this extra effort will add a minimal amount of time to your normal dental routine.

Leading up to your dental implant procedure, we will talk you through the entire process, answer any questions, and go over any special care guidelines.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

You are a candidate for dental implants in Kennebunk if you are missing one or more teeth and have sufficient bone mass in your jaw to support the implants. The process of installing your dental implants will take several months, but once complete, your restoration can last for decades with proper care.
