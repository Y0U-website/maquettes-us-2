Taking Care of Your Clear Aligners

  • For best results, make sure to wear your clear aligners for about 22 hours a day.
  • Take your aligners out before eating, drinking, flossing, and brushing your teeth.
  • Clean your aligners every night with a soft-bristled toothbrush and gentle soap. Use specialized solutions periodically for a deeper clean.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Generations Dentistry correct teeth using braces?

Using clear aligners, we can address minor issues like spacing, gaps, crookedness, and minor cases of overbite and underbite. However, for more extensive orthodontics, such as braces, we will refer you to an orthodontist in Kennebunk who can provide you with the highest levels of care.

Can I take off my aligners when I need to?

Yes. One of the benefits of clear aligners is that they are entirely removable. You should take them off before eating and drinking, while you brush and floss your teeth, and to clean them regularly.

You can also take them off for special occasions, like a wedding or graduation. However, you should wear them as much as possible in order to get the best results from treatment.

Will my results from clear aligners be permanent?

We always strive to give you permanent results from our treatments. However, as your dental health evolves and changes, it’s possible that your teeth may want to shift back. To prevent this, we will provide you with a final set of clear aligners to wear at night, which will help keep your teeth in place and maintain your results indefinitely.

If we determine that another orthodontic treatment will give you better long-term results, we will refer you to an orthodontist in Kennebunk who can help you achieve a permanently improved smile.
