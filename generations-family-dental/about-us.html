About Us

Our legacy of quality, personal dental care in Lincoln, NE

We’re your dad’s dentist and your grandkids’ dentist too!

Dentistry is a family tradition for the Asbjornsons. Our practice was originally founded in 1962 by Don. Soon, his son Dr. Jon Asbjornson joined the practice as well. Today, Generations Family Dental is led by Jon’s daughter, Dr. Katie Asbjornson and her team of dental care providers. As established and trusted members of the Lincoln healthcare community, we’ve earned the trust and loyalty of three generations of patients.
