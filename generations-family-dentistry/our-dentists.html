Our Dentists

Abby L. King, D.D.S.

Dr. Abby L. King, D.D.S., earned a Bachelor of Science degree at Hope College in Holland, Mich., and a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor. She joined Linda G. Goltz, D.D.S. at Generations Family Dentistry in 2012.

“I grew up around the dental practice, and I had an exposure to all that the profession offers since I was young,” she said. “I always wanted to work in a health profession, and dentistry was the best option for me. It allows me to have a career as well as a family.” King most likes dentistry because it allows her to treat patients of all ages and to get to know them like family. “We truly care about our patients, and we strive to provide the best comprehensive care possible,” she said.

In 2009, King married her sweetheart, Brandon. “We enjoy spending time with family up north all year round, and we really enjoy cooking and eating good food together,” she said. They now have three children. Their son, Lincoln, was born in 2014; a daughter, Piper, was born in 2016; and son, Ellis, was born in March of 2020.

“I will provide exceptional dental treatment to all of my patients with a caring and compassionate manner,” King pledges to her patients.

Linda G. Goltz, D.D.S.

Dr. Linda G. Goltz, D.D.S., has recently retired from Generations Family Dentistry. Goltz is enjoying life spending time with family and traveling with Bob, her husband since 1976. They have three grown children, and four beloved grandchildren: Gabriel was born in 2011, Elijah was born in 2013, Lincoln was born in 2014, and Piper was born in 2016.

A native of Iowa, Goltz graduated with a Bachelor of Arts degree from Augustana College in Sioux Falls, S.D., and a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Iowa in Iowa City in 1981. She founded Generations Family Dentistry (originally Linda Goltz Dentistry) in 1982. Her daughter Dr. Abby King carries on her dedication to treating patients like she would want to be treated.
