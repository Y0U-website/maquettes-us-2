About Us

Genesis Dental offers a wide range of services. Whether it’s popular cosmetic treatments like whitening, veneers, and smile makeovers, or restorative procedures like dental implants, root canal therapy, or crowns and bridges, Genesis Dental is a trusted provider of safe and effective dental care.

At Genesis Dental, the patient is the team’s top priority. The dentists and the staff approach patient care by putting themselves in their patients’ shoes. They strive to make personal connections and build long-term relationships so that their patients can enjoy a lifetime of excellent care and consistently healthy smiles.

Here at Genesis Dental, we want you to be confident and relaxed, and smile from the beginning. Give us a call today to schedule a consultation.
