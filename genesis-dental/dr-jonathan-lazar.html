Dr. Jonathan Lazar

Dr. Jonathan Lazar, DMD

Dr. Jonathan Lazar has devoted his entire professional career to public and private dental healthcare practice. As the clinical manager of a large multi-disciplinary dental healthcare facility in Rockland County, he learned that every dental procedure can be both comfortable and affordable for the patient, when professionals listen to and work with their patients. This lesson has continued to inform his practice in the local community and inspired him to launch Genesis Dental.

A graduate of the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey, Dr. Lazar was awarded the prestigious Anthony R. Volpe Award for Outstanding Clinician in Periodontics, during his clinical years. He subsequently completed his residency at the highly-acclaimed VA Harbor Healthcare-Brooklyn Hospital campus, and was brought in as a consultant for clinical management for The Center of Discovery.

Dr. Lazar is a member of the American Dental Association, New Jersey Dental Society, and Essex County Dental Society, as well as a member of the Passaic Park Dental Study Club, which focuses on implant dentistry.

Born and raised in the heart of nearby Passaic, New Jersey, Dr. Lazar and his wife, a native of West Orange, New Jersey, are maintaining their “homegrown Jersey” roots. Along with their children, the Lazars live in northern New Jersey, where they are active and respected members of their community.

He continues to serve the public sector and offers his services and expertise through Genesis Dental.
