Parent Involvement and Comfort

At Genesis Smiles Pediatric Dentistry, we understand the importance of a comforting and supportive environment for both children and parents. We warmly welcome parents to remain with their child during appointments, fostering a sense of security and trust. We believe that your presence not only helps alleviate anxiety but also allows us to address any concerns or questions you may have firsthand. Our team strives to create a partnership with parents, ensuring a positive dental experience for your child. Together, let’s build healthy smiles and lasting relationships.
