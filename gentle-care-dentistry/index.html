Dr. Luu is highly trained in all areas of cosmetic and general dentistry. She has the knowledge and experience to provide for you and your family the care you need to enjoy long-term smiles that are healthy and beautiful.

She also takes the time to educate patients on the importance of oral health care and the benefits of cosmetic dentistry for patients to get the most out of their visits.

"Care" isn’t just in our name - we practice it every day! Dr. Brianne Luu safeguards her patients, her staff, the environment, and herself by practicing only mercury free dentistry. She stays up to date with the latest advances in biological dental care through active membership in the IAOMT (International Academy of Oral Medicine and Toxicology).

If you are looking for a dental office that cares about you and your family, call Dr. Luu and the staff at Gentle Care Dentistry of San Gabriel Valley. You will be glad that you began treatment with those who truly care about your family’s oral and dental health needs.

From general dentistry to cosmetic dentistry, we focus on providing you with a beautiful, healthy smile! Call today for a consultation appointment!

Dr. Brianne Luu graduated from Tufts Dental School in 2001. She practiced in private practice for two years, then went back to school for an additional degree. While still practicing full-time dentistry, she attended the Executive Master of Public Health Program at the University of California, Los Angeles.
