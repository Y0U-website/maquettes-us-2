Same-Day Crowns in Vestal, NY

If you’re considering a dental crown and want to save time without compromising on quality, ask your dentist about the benefits of same-day crowns and discover how this advanced treatment can restore your smile quickly and comfortably. Request an Appointment FAQs Who Can Benefit from Same-Day Crowns? Same-day crowns are an excellent choice for patients with busy schedules, dental anxiety, or anyone seeking a faster, more comfortable dental experience. They are especially helpful for those who want to restore a damaged or weakened tooth without the inconvenience of multiple visits.

  • Digital Impressions: Instead of uncomfortable impression trays, a digital scanner captures precise 3D images of the tooth and surrounding bite. This process is quick, accurate, and far more comfortable for patients.
  • Crown Design: Using advanced software, the dentist designs a crown that matches the patient’s bite and tooth shape. The crown is also color-matched to blend seamlessly with surrounding teeth.
  • On-Site Milling: Once the design is complete, a milling machine in the dental office carves the crown from a solid ceramic block. This process typically takes 30-35 minutes.
  • Placement and Bonding: The dentist checks the fit and bite before bonding the crown permanently in place. Adjustments are made as needed to ensure comfort and natural appearance.

What are same-day crowns?

Same-day crowns are full-coverage restorations that are designed and milled in our office. The tooth preparation and cementation are all done in the same visit, saving you time.

How long does the same-day crown process take?

The entire procedure is generally completed in two hours.
