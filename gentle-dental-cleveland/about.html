Gentle Dental in Cleveland, Ohio

Building on over 40 years of practice, Dr. Jin Hee Choi took over Gentle Dental from Dr. Nicholas Graor in 2022. She has been continuing his legacy of gentle dentistry and the family culture he developed! ‍ Gentle Dental in Cleveland, Ohio puts an emphasis on being gentle. High quality dental care and patient comfort is very important to us. We are committed to helping you achieve the best possible dental experience in a comfortable, friendly atmosphere.

What Procedures we offer

We are always happy to welcome new patients. We have emergency appointments and we do our best to get you an appointment as timely as possible. We will file any insurance paperwork for you, just bring in your current dental insurance information.

Diagnostic Services

- 3-Dimensional X-rays (CBCT) - Digital X-rays (Lower dose radiation with higher resolution) - Oral Cancer Screenings

Preventive Care

- Fluoride Treatments - Mouth guards for TMJ jaw and headache pain, Bruxism (grinding) and Sports - Sealants (Protective tooth coating against decay)

Restorative Dentistry

- Bonding - Bridges - Crowns (Caps) - Dentures - Implants - Partial Dentures - Replacement of amalgam (silver) fillings with Composite Resin - Veneers

Emergency Services

- Emergency dentistry - Nitrous Oxide Sedation (laughing gas) - Extractions - Endodontics (Root Canal Therapy)

Cosmetic Procedures

- Cosmetic dentistry - One hour in-office Opalescence Teeth Whitening System and home whitening

Periodontal Services

- Periodontal Disease (Gum disease) Prevention, early diagnosis, and treatment. - Strengthens teeth against decay and tooth sensitivity.

Educational Video Library

Discover expert insights, tips, and step-by-step guides to help you maintain a healthy smile and understand dental treatments. Our videos make oral care easy and accessible for all ages.
