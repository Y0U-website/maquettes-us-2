Combining Advanced Training with Compassionate Care

Dr. Travis Perkins has dedicated his career to helping patients achieve healthier, more confident smiles through comprehensive, patient-centered dentistry. With more than two decades of experience and advanced training in implant, cosmetic, restorative, and sedation dentistry, he is committed to delivering high-quality care while ensuring every patient feels comfortable and informed.

After earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine (D.M.D.) degree from Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine, Dr. Perkins attended a hospital-based General Practice Residency, during which he received advanced training in trauma care, emergency medicine, anesthesia, and internal medicine. This additional experience provided a strong foundation for treating patients with a wide range of dental and medical needs.

Throughout his career, Dr. Perkins has remained committed to continuing education and staying at the forefront of modern dentistry. He completed advanced implant training through the Medical College of Georgia and the Atlanta Institute for Advanced Dental Education, and has accumulated more than 5,000 hours of continuing education in implant dentistry. He has also received advanced training in conscious sedation and cosmetic dentistry, studying with some of the most respected educators in the profession.

Dr. Perkins’ passion for education extends beyond treating patients. He founded the Nevada Institute for Advanced Dental Education, where he helped train fellow dentists in implant procedures and advanced treatment techniques. He has also served in numerous leadership positions within organized dentistry and remains actively involved in professional organizations dedicated to advancing patient care.

His career has taken him across the country, from private practice in Nevada, Utah, and Arizona to leadership roles in Alaska, including serving as Dental Director for the Kodiak Area Native Association. These diverse experiences have strengthened his ability to care for patients from all backgrounds while maintaining a personalized and compassionate approach.

Patients appreciate Dr. Perkins for his attention to detail, commitment to excellence, and genuine concern for their well-being. Whether helping someone restore missing teeth, improve their smile, or maintain long-term oral health, he takes the time to understand each patient’s goals and create treatment plans tailored to their individual needs.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Perkins enjoys reading, learning languages, spending time outdoors, and pursuing new adventures with his family.
