Our provider will clean, polish and floss your teeth too, all while providing feedback and suggestions for your oral care. Schedule your appointment today!

Dental Defense

Brushing is the best defense against dental issues. Brush twice a day for two minutes each time. Adding a daily floss and mouth rinse only helps more! Combine that with visits to us every six months and you’ll be the winner with great oral health!

Dental Detectives

During our examinations, we take X-rays and exam your entire mouth, not just your teeth. This helps us detect infections, gum diseases or even oral cancers. We can also indicate markers for diabetes, lung and heart disease.

Family Friendly

Adults are not our only patients, we love treating children ages 13+ too!

Remember, your entire family should brush for two minutes, twice daily as soon as the first tooth buds. Flossing and rinses are good too!
