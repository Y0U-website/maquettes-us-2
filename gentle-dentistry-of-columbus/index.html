The practice

We offer services ranging from routine cleanings, fillings, crowns, bridges, cosmetic dentistry, and more . Dr. Devon Paris and her dental team strive to use the latest technologies and techniques to give our patients the best possible experience. From checkups to more complicated treatments, we are committed to providing excellent care tailored specifically to your needs.

We want you to feel at ease each time you visit the dentist’s chair.

When you come in for an appointment at Gentle Dentistry of Columbus, you can expect personalized attention and compassionate service. We understand the importance of ensuring our patients feel comfortable, safe, and confident throughout their dental experience.

We enjoy educating our patients on proper oral health habits for a lifetime of beautiful smiles!

We believe in preventive care and patient education, so you can look forward to discussing your oral health needs with us. Our team is always happy to answer any questions you may have about treatments or procedures, and we work hard to ensure you obtain the treatments you need for a healthy and beautiful smile every day!

We are one of the few genuinely locally and hometown owned and operated practices here in town that have not been acquired by a private equity firm and we intend to remain that way, offering and continually improving our customer service in our to serve our community with consistency.

At Gentle Dentistry of Columbus, we are passionate about helping our patients maintain optimal oral health. We want to provide the highest quality care in a comfortable and welcoming environment.
