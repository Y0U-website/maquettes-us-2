Meet The Doctor

Dr. Ronald D. Jessup, D.M.D., P.C.

Dr. Ronald Jessup DMD, PC is the owner of Gentle Family Dentistry, located off Lee Highway in Bristol, Virginia. He received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the Medical College of Georgia in 1996 and opened Gentle Family Dentistry in early 2000. Dr Jessup is widely recognized for his knowledge of industry products, new technology methods, devices and procedures. He specializes in all areas of family dentistry, pediatric dentistry, endodontics and oral surgery.

Dr Jessup also has a dedicated commitment to organized dentistry. He is a member of the executive council of ASDA at the Medical College of Georgia and served as President from 1992-1996. Dr. Jessup served as the President of the Bristol Dental Society from 1997-1998. He is very active in his role with the Virginia Dental Association on committees and as a delegate representative from 2000 to present. Dr. Jessup has also served as President of Component 6 from 2010-2011 and served as a Reference Committee Chair in 2010.
