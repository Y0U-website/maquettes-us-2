General & Preventive Care

Preventive dentistry is the foundation of a healthy smile. At Gentle Family Dentistry, we provide comprehensive exams, professional cleanings, digital X-rays, fluoride treatments, and dental sealants to help patients of all ages maintain optimal oral health. Dr. Bei Li recommends twice-yearly visits for most patients, catching small problems early before they become costly or painful. Consistent preventive care is the single most effective investment you can make in your long-term oral health.

Fillings

When decay damages a tooth, a filling restores its strength and function while stopping further deterioration. We use tooth-colored composite resin that blends seamlessly with your natural enamel, so your restored tooth looks and feels completely natural. The procedure is straightforward and comfortable — Dr. Li removes the decayed tissue, cleans the area, and bonds the composite material directly to the tooth, typically in a single appointment.

Crowns

A dental crown is a custom cap that covers a damaged, weakened, or heavily restored tooth, restoring it to full strength and appearance. At Gentle Family Dentistry, Dr. Li crafts custom porcelain crowns designed to blend seamlessly with your natural smile. Whether your tooth has been fractured, worn down, or treated with a root canal, a crown provides lasting protection and a beautiful result.

Bridges

A dental bridge is a fixed restoration that fills the gap left by one or more missing teeth, using the neighboring teeth as anchors. Bridges restore your ability to chew and speak comfortably, prevent surrounding teeth from shifting, and bring back the natural appearance of your smile. Dr. Li crafts each bridge from high-quality porcelain to match your natural tooth color, providing a durable, lifelike result that can last for many years with proper care.

Dentures

Dentures are a time-tested solution for replacing multiple missing teeth, restoring function, aesthetics, and confidence. We offer both full dentures for patients who have lost all of their teeth and partial dentures for those missing several teeth in an arch. For patients seeking a more stable and permanent option, implant-supported dentures anchor firmly to dental implants, eliminating slipping and improving chewing efficiency significantly compared to traditional removable dentures.

Extractions

While our goal is always to save natural teeth, there are situations where an extraction is the best path forward — severe decay, advanced gum disease, a cracked tooth beyond repair, or a tooth that needs to be removed before orthodontic treatment. Dr. Li performs both simple and surgical extractions with a gentle technique and, when appropriate, nitrous oxide sedation to ensure you remain calm and comfortable throughout the procedure. Post-extraction care instructions are provided to support a smooth, uneventful recovery.

Root Canals

Root canal therapy has an undeserved reputation — with modern techniques and anesthesia, most patients are surprised at how comfortable the procedure actually is. When the inner pulp of a tooth becomes infected or inflamed due to deep decay or trauma, a root canal removes the infected tissue, cleans and shapes the canals, and seals the tooth to prevent reinfection. Saving the natural tooth with a root canal is almost always preferable to extraction, preserving your bite and avoiding the need for a replacement.

Veneers

Porcelain veneers are ultra-thin shells of dental ceramic bonded to the front surfaces of teeth to correct a wide range of cosmetic concerns — chips, cracks, permanent stains, uneven sizing, slight gaps, and more. The result is a dramatic smile transformation that looks completely natural. Veneers are custom-crafted to fit your facial features and desired aesthetic, and with proper care they can last a decade or more. If you are unhappy with your smile, veneers may be the most elegant and long-lasting solution available.

Inlays & Onlays

Inlays and onlays — sometimes called indirect fillings or partial crowns — are precision restorations used when a tooth is too damaged for a filling but does not require a full crown. An inlay fits within the cusps of the tooth, while an onlay extends over one or more cusps. Both are fabricated from durable porcelain and bonded in place for a precise, long-lasting fit. Because they preserve more natural tooth structure than a crown while providing greater strength than a filling, inlays and onlays are often the ideal middle-ground restoration.

Invisalign

Invisalign clear aligners offer a discreet, comfortable alternative to traditional braces for straightening teeth and correcting bite issues. A series of custom-made, removable plastic trays gradually shift your teeth into proper alignment without any brackets or wires. Because the aligners are nearly invisible and removable for eating and brushing, Invisalign fits seamlessly into adult and teen lifestyles. Dr. Bei Li is a trained Invisalign provider and will guide you through every step of your treatment from the initial scan to your final result.

Nitrous Oxide

Nitrous oxide — commonly known as laughing gas — is a safe, effective, and widely used sedation option that helps anxious patients relax during dental treatment. Administered through a small mask worn over the nose, nitrous oxide takes effect within minutes, producing a calm, comfortable feeling while keeping you fully conscious and able to respond. The effects wear off quickly after the mask is removed, so most patients can drive themselves home afterward. Nitrous oxide is available for both adults and children at Gentle Family Dentistry.
