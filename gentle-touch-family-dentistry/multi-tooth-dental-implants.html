Multi-Tooth Dental Implant Procedure

The process of restoring multiple missing teeth with multi-tooth dental implants involves several key steps:

  • Initial Consultation: Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation at Gentle Touch Family Dentistry. During this appointment, our experienced dental team will assess your oral health, take necessary X-rays, and discuss your goals and options for multi-tooth dental implants.
  • Treatment Planning: A personalized treatment plan will be created based on your specific needs and preferences. This plan will outline the number of implants required, the type of prosthetic (bridge or partial denture), and the timeline for the procedure.
  • Implant Placement: The next phase involves the surgical dental implant after insertion into the jawbone. We shall administer a local anesthetic for your pain relief during this minimally invasive procedure. These implant posts will serve as the stable foundation for your multi-tooth restoration.
  • Healing and Osseointegration: Following implant placement, a healing period is necessary to allow the implants to integrate with the surrounding bone tissue. This process, known as osseointegration, typically takes a few months.
  • Abutment and Restoration: Once osseointegration is complete, abutments are attached to the implant posts. These abutments act as connectors between the implants and the final multi-tooth restoration. The custom bridge or partial denture is then securely affixed to the abutments.
  • Follow-Up and Maintenance: Regular follow-up appointments at Gentle Touch Family Dentistry are essential to monitor the health and function of your multi-tooth dental implants. Our team will also guide you through maintaining a high standard of oral hygiene to lengthen the life of your restoration.

Success Rate?

The success rate of multi tooth dental implants is generally high when performed by experienced professionals and when proper oral hygiene is maintained after the procedure. With proper care and regular visits to your dentist, these implants can last a lifetime.

How About the Cost?

In terms of cost, multi tooth dental implants may be more expensive than other restorative options initially; however, they can be more cost-effective in the long run due to their durability and longevity.
