Serving Patients in Clarksville, TN & Surrounding Communities

Since 2009, Gentry Orthodontics has provided comprehensive orthodontic care in Clarksville, TN. Our single-doctor, private practice serves patients of all ages. We are committed to helping patients improve and straighten their smile. Our goal is to help you improve the aesthetics and health of your smile, while boosting your confidence. We are a team of friendly and knowledgeable orthodontic professionals, committed to the highest level of patient care. We use modern technology to help diagnose and develop individual treatment plans.

Every member of our team cares about your health and well-being. We work to provide timely services and to keep every patient well-informed about their treatment options and progress.
