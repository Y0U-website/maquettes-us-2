What You Need to Know

Before you schedule with Genuine Care Dentistry, the office of Dr. John Bruinsma, here are a few items that we would like you to know about our dental practice that will assist in your decision for choosing us as your dental provider.

Experience

Implants

If implants are needed, you will be referred to a trusted Oral Surgeon for the implant placement and Dr. Bruinsma will then place the new crown that attaches to the implant. Implant procedures require a minimum of three months for placement and healing, sometimes longer depending on circumstances related to each implant.

Dental Insurance

We are a Delta Dental Insurance Premier Provider. That means we are in-network with Delta Dental Insurance. All other dental insurance claims will be sent for payment and patients will be responsible for any balance not covered by your dental insurance. Patients with Blue Cross and Blue Shield insurance will be expected to pay at the time of their dental appointment, since payment from BCBS will be sent to your home. We are not enrolled in Medicaid.
