The practice

Dr. Bruinsma is a compassionate listener and caring dentist who strives to educate you regarding all aspects of your dental care.

Dr. Bruinsma will always give you his honest opinion, using his years of experience and knowledge to deliver the best personal solution for your dental care.

Dr. John Bruinsma and his wife Debra, a dental hygienist, have devoted their dental careers to helping people achieve their best dental health. Whether educating patients, researching and developing products that prevent decay, or innovating treatments that save teeth as long as possible, their experience and knowledge are highly valued by patients who desire to improve their dental health.
