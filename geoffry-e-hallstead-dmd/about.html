Treatments

At our practice, we prioritize a holistic approach to dental care, incorporating several key health screenings and treatments:

  • Oral Cancer Screening As part of your regular exam, we conduct quick screenings for early signs of oral cancer, recommending biopsies if necessary. Prevention includes avoiding tobacco, limiting alcohol, getting sun protection, and eating a healthy diet. Early detection is vital for successful treatment.
  • Periodontal disease Gum disease, a leading cause of tooth loss, often goes unnoticed. Regular check-ups involve measuring the spaces between teeth and gums to detect plaque buildup, which can lead to gingivitis and periodontitis. Symptoms include swollen gums and bad breath. Early intervention with good oral hygiene is essential.
  • Sleep Apnea Treatment We offer comprehensive sleep apnea treatments, including custom oral appliances that help maintain an open airway during sleep. Our approach focuses on improving your overall health and quality of life through personalized dental solutions.
  • Snoring Prevention Chronic snoring affects many and can indicate underlying health problems. It&#x27;s often caused by relaxed throat muscles. Solutions may include a mandibular advancement device (MAD) to improve airway space or other tailored treatments.
  • TMD Treatments Temporomandibular Disorder (TMD) can cause chronic pain and headaches due to issues with the jaw joint. Symptoms include jaw pain and clicking sounds. Dentists can diagnose TMD and suggest treatments, while preventive measures include avoiding teeth grinding and maintaining good posture.
