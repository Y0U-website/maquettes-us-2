Ideal Candidates: Non-smokers with good oral health, healthy gums, and sufficient jawbone. What is a Dental Implant? A dental implant is a synthetic tooth root, typically made of titanium, surgically placed into the jawbone, with a replacement tooth attached. Benefits of Dental Implants: Look and feel natural.
  • Stronger biting force compared to dentures.
  • Do not compromise neighboring teeth.
  • Permanent solution, often outlasting other treatments like dentures or bridges.

Post-Treatment Care

Implants require regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups, similar to natural teeth.

Implant Services

  • Implant Site Development Bone grafting to prepare for implant placement after tooth removal.
  • Computer Enhanced Treatment Planning 3D scans to ensure optimal implant placement.
  • Sinus Lift Augmentation A procedure to restore bone loss from enlarged sinuses before implant placement.

Maintenance

Dental implants need regular cleaning and professional check-ups to prevent plaque buildup and infection, similar to natural teeth.
