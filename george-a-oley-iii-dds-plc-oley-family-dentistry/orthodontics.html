Post-Orthodontics Care

After your braces are removed, the retention phase begins to help maintain your newly aligned teeth. This phase is crucial for ensuring that your teeth stay in their proper position, and it typically lasts for at least 24 months. Consistently wearing your retainer as instructed is essential for preserving the results you’ve worked hard to achieve.
