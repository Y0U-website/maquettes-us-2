Dr. Debra George, DMD

Dr. Debra George has been setting the highest standards of excellence in orthodontics since 1988. She attended Wheaton College , then continued on to Tufts University School of Dental Medicine , where she completed both her dental education and her post-doctoral education in orthodontics.

She has had the honor of working with Dr. Raymond George Sr., Dr. Raymond George Jr., and now her favorite doctor of all, her daughter, Dr. Kelsey George. Dr. Kelsey s knowledge of the newest and latest techniques, combined with Dr. Debbie s years of experience, makes for the perfect patient experience.

In her spare time, Dr. George enjoys spending time with her three children, her grandchildren and close friends . She loves to travel, and she is an avid Patriots fan. She has a little rescue dog from the Seekonk Animal Shelter named Baxter. Dr. Debbie believes in giving back to her community and has volunteered in countless local, school and dental organizations. She works out occasionally, hates to cook and will join almost anyone for a nice dinner out. Most of all, she loves her work and all the happy smiles she creates.
