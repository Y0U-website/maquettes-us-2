My practice has been dedicated to the comprehensive diagnosis and management of Temporomandibular Disorders (TMD) and Orofacial Pain for the last 25 years. I also have been treating Obstructive Sleep Apnea patients using oral appliances, being able to help thousands of patients to improve their overall health.

I will be offering the following services:

  • Comprehensive diagnosis and treatment related to Temporomandibular Disorders (TMD), orofacial pain, headache, neuropathic pain. The complex therapeutic approach includes self-care, physical therapy exercises and modalities (laser, iontophoresis, therapeutic Botox, PRF), individualized behavioral therapeutic approach, pharmacotherapy, oral appliances.
  • The management of snoring and obstructive sleep apnea with oral appliances.
