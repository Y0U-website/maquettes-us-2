A dental emergency can occur without warning.

Regular patients can have problems between routine visits. Many people not receiving routine care still have dental concerns. If you are experiencing a dental emergency, we are prepared to help. Contact our front office to schedule an appointment. We can focus on your specific concern, deliver treatment plan options, and, if possible, begin treatment on the same day. We do recommend calling ahead to schedule an appointment. We can not guarantee same day service to walk in patients.
