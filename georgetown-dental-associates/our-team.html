Our Dentists

Dr. Patrick B. Ryan

Dr. Ryan was raised in Columbia, Missouri. A graduate of Hickman High School and the University of Missouri, Dr. Ryan graduated from Creighton University School of Dentistry (Omaha, Nebraska) in 2006. Dr. Ryan has practiced in Columbia since graduation and busies himself by raising 4 children with his wife, Sara. He is a member of the American Dental Association, Missouri Dental Association, Columbia Dental Society and Central Dental Society.

Dr. Gregory Stevens

Dr. Stevens grew up in Columbia, Missouri and became associated with the firm in 2003. He graduated from the University of Missouri-KC School of Dentistry with honors in 2002 and went on to a one-year residency at Truman Lakewood Hospital in Kansas City, Missouri, focusing on comprehensive care for patients, especially children. He is a member of the American Dental Association, Missouri Dental Association, Columbia Dental Society and Central Dental Society.

Dr. Caleb Murray

Hello! I’m Dr. Caleb Murray, born and raised right here in Columbia. I completed my bachelor’s degree at Missouri State University in Springfield, Missouri. Following graduation, I headed up to Kansas City where I attended UMKC School of Dentistry, completing my doctorate in 2022. Embarking on my next transition, I chose to refine more skills and knowledge at Wichita State University’s AEGD residency program. Beginning in July of 2023, I am thrilled to return to Columbia with my wife, Claire, and son, Joshua.

In my free time, I enjoy spending time with my family, snow and water skiing, scuba diving, playing golf, and spending time outdoors. I’m very eager to begin working alongside the team at Georgetown to provide quality dental care and begin building relationships with the patients I am blessed with the opportunity to serve.
