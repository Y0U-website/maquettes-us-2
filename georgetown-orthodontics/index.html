The practice

Clear aligners are transparent orthodontic devices that aim to straighten your teeth without the use of metal wires or brackets commonly associated with traditional braces. Clear aligners are custom-made to fit your teeth – from the first set of aligners to the last to gradually move your teeth to their desired position.

When it comes to orthodontic treatments, one of the most popular and effective options available for both children and adults are traditional braces. In fact, traditional braces have been around for decades. Fortunately, they have undergone a lot of changes and improvements over the years.
