Common Treatments for Full Mouth Reconstruction

Dental Implants

Implants are titanium posts we place into your jawbone to replace missing teeth. They can support crowns, bridges, or dentures and restore your ability to eat and speak. Since they also fuse to your jaw, they prevent your other teeth from shifting.

Same-Day Crowns

When decay or trauma wears down some of your teeth, we cover them with same-day crowns. Using our Planmeca® mill, we create these tooth-shaped caps in a single visit and attach them where needed.

Bridges

Bridges are made up of crowns on either side and replacement teeth in between. They’re placed over natural teeth or dental implants to stay in place.

Veneers

Thin porcelain shells bonded to the front surface of your teeth to correct color, shape, or size imperfections. With veneers , we can improve your smile&#x27;s appearance without reshaping your teeth too much.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are invisible trays used to straighten misaligned teeth and correct bite issues. Patients need to wear them from 20 to 22 hours a day and remove them only to eat or brush their teeth. They slowly move teeth toward their correct positions.
