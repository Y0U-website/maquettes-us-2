Entrance requirements Admission is open to any qualified applicant. No qualified person may be excluded from enrollment in GDEI training program based on age, race, gender, disability, or national origin.

Applicants must meet the following requirements: 1. Provide proof of at least a high school diploma or GED. 2. Have paid or have made school-approved arrangements to pay the tuition in full. 3. Have executed an enrollment agreement.
