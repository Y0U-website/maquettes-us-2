Adolescents / Teens (Ages 10-17)

Orthodontics in this age group is particularly effective as full treatment can be timed to coincide with the natural “growth-spurt”. This special timing of treatment can be very effective for certain types of bite correction. Also, in this age group all permanent teeth become present in the mouth so everything can be fully aligned at this stage. Smile options for Adolescents / Teens:

  • Braces
  • Clear Braces
  • Invisalign Teen
