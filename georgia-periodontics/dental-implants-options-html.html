What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are designed to be secured within the jawbone, underneath the gumline. Due to their biocompatible nature, they’re able to fuse with the bone through a process called osseointegration, which allows them to provide stimulation and blood flow to the area to keep it from eroding over time, as well as offer an unwavering foundation for a restoration to be attached to.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

Dental implants are the best tooth replacement option out there. They are the only solution that replaces the roots of the missing teeth, allowing for a wide range of benefits. To achieve this, getting dental implants requires a multi-step process that takes place over the course of several months. Continue reading to learn more about these steps so you know what to expect.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

The first step of every dental implant treatment is a consultation with a dentist. During this visit, they will help you determine whether dental implants are right for you. Some patients require preliminary treatments, like bone grafting or gum disease treatment, before they can get dental implants. If this applies to you, we will help to get those scheduled. Once your jaw and gums are in good condition, we can move on to the next step of your treatment.

Dental Implant Surgery

Once you decide you want to move forward with getting dental implants, our team will schedule your surgery at one of our Georgia Periodontics locations. Dr. Litterer and Dr. Scher are specially trained in this procedure, allowing them to provide the best results and most comfortable experience possible.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

For the next three to six months, your gums will heal, and the implants will undergo a process called osseointegration, where they fuse with the jawbone. This is what makes your implants so stable and long-lasting. Once the area has healed, you will need to undergo another minor procedure in which the abutments are attached. Impressions are taken and sent to the dental lab so your final restoration can be made.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restoration(s)

When your final restorations are ready, they will be sent to your dentist. Then, you’ll return to your general dentist so they can place your new teeth on your dental implants via the abutments. If everything looks and feels great, you can leave showing off your new, complete smile!

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants in Decatur are such a popular tooth replacement treatment because they offer several benefits that other traditional solutions don’t. We can discuss the advantages more in-depth during your consultation, but feel free to read on if you want to learn a little more on the topic in the meantime.

Day-to-Day Benefits

Fortunately, you won’t have to wait long to enjoy the benefits of dental implants in Georgia. After you’ve healed, you’ll notice several improvements to your daily life, including:

  • The Ability to Eat a Varied Diet Since dental implants are inserted directly into your jawbone, they can restore up to 80 % of your original bite power. So, you can say goodbye to the days of eating applesauce, yogurt, and other extremely soft foods on repeat.
  • Easy Maintenance While some restorations require special cleaning products, dental implants do not! To keep them in pristine condition, all you need to do is brush, floss, and rinse with mouthwash consistently.
  • Improved Self-Esteem With noticeable gaps in your smile, it can be difficult to smile confidently. Fortunately, dental implants can replace any number of missing teeth. Plus, the restorations secured on top are custom-made, ensuring they look and feel incredibly lifelike.

Health Benefits

There are a few ways that dental implants can benefit your oral and overall health, including:

  • Allowing You to Eat Nutrient-Dense Foods With a strong bite, you can eat seasonal fruits, fresh vegetables, and lean proteins with ease. Not only is this more satisfying than a soft-foods-only diet, but it also ensures your body has the nutrients it needs to stay healthy.
  • Preventing Dental Drift Dental drift is when the teeth surrounding the gap move out of position. With dental implants, we can fill the gaps in your smile, helping preserve the alignment of your bite in the process.
  • Preserving More of Your Healthy Smile Dental bridges can replace one tooth or several teeth in a row, but they require the teeth surrounding the gap to be altered, even if they are healthy. That’s not the case with dental implants because they are self-supporting.

Long-Term Benefits

Finally, dental implants offer several long-term benefits. Here are a few noteworthy ones:

  • High Success Rate Dental implants have a success rate of over 95 % with non-smoking patients.
  • Preserve Your Youthful Face Shape Dental implants are inserted into your jawbone. Not only does this result in unmatched stability, but it also helps prevent bone loss and the alteration of your facial shape.
  • Long Lifespan With proper care and maintenance, like scheduling a dental checkup and cleaning every six months and exercising caution with crunchy foods, dental implants can easily last for 30+ years.

Who Dental Implants Can Help

In addition to being one of the most advanced answers to tooth loss, dental implants are also incredibly versatile, with the ability to help patients in a variety of situations.

Missing One Tooth

For a single missing tooth, our periodontists can place one dental implant below the gumline and into the jawbone. After it has fused with the bone and the abutment has been attached to it, your general dentist will secure a free-standing, custom-crafted dental crown to it to fill the space in your arch.

Missing Multiple Teeth

If you have several missing teeth in a row or throughout your arch, we can secure an implant bridge as well as implant crowns to complete your smile. An implant bridge is anchored within the mouth by placing two dental implants at either side of the gap. This will allow the bridge to be supported by the implants instead of your natural existing teeth, making the procedure more conservative.

Missing All of Your Teeth

To rebuild a smile completely, our team can secure anywhere between four and eight dental implants throughout the jawbone. This will anchor a full denture without the fear of having it slip or shift whenever you eat or speak. Our team is happy to partner with local general dentists to offer All-on-4 and Teeth in a Day, which can bring back an entire row in a single appointment.

Dental Implant Failure & Salvage

Dental implants don’t fail very often, but it’s good to know that your periodontists in Georgia can help if you ever experience complications! Fortunately, failure doesn’t necessarily mean that you’ll have to go without dental implants. Rest assured, we’ll do everything we can to salvage your restoration and save your smile.

Preliminary Treatments & Dental Implant Surgery

The Parts of Your Dental Implant

The total cost of your procedure can also vary based on a few different considerations, such as:

  • The number of implants you’re receiving. Depending on how many teeth went missing, you might need multiple posts to support your replacements, which can increase the total amount due.
  • Type of restoration . Dental crowns, bridges, and dentures can all be used to rebuild your smile with the support of dental implants, each with its own pricing structure.
  • The materials used. Different options, such as titanium or zirconia, can have different associated fees.

How Dental Implants Help Pay for Themselves

Dental implants may cost more initially than other options, like dentures or bridges. However, they also tend to last longer and offer several unique advantages that pay off over time.

For example, because they’re so firmly rooted in your jaw, they’re less likely to become destabilized or dislodged. That means you’re less likely to require expensive repairs or replacements than are often required for other treatments.

Not only that, but there’s no need to regularly invest in additional accessories, such as special brushes, cleaners, soaking solutions, or adhesives to help maintain your restorations, which can help reduce out-of-pocket expenses over the years.

Dental Implant FAQs

Does Getting Dental Implants Hurt?

Many experts consider this treatment the ideal way to rebuild smiles after tooth loss because, unlike the alternatives, implants are embedded directly into your jawbone. However, many patients hesitate to proceed with this service because they’re afraid their procedure will hurt.

Fortunately, our compassionate team in Georgia understands you may be anxious, and will do everything we can to help ease your frayed nerves. For instance, before starting your procedure, we’ll numb your mouth with a local anesthetic to help you feel more comfortable. We also offer IV sedation to help you get through even the most complex procedures.

Are Dental Implants Removable?

Are you curious whether your new teeth are intended to be frequently removed and reinserted, like dentures? It’s natural to have questions about their functionality, but one of the reasons so many patients choose this treatment is because it’s a much more permanent and reliable solution for tooth loss.

For example, once the support posts positioned in your jaw fuse with the bone material, they are essentially considered a permanent part of your mouth, and can’t be removed. It’s capped with a lifelike restoration made of resilient, tooth-colored ceramic, which is cemented in place and also shouldn’t be taken out except by a professional.

How Successful are Dental Implants?

If you’re worried that you’ll invest time and resources into getting dental implants just for them to fail, you can set your concerns aside. This procedure has an incredibly high success rate of more than 90 % when administered by a qualified provider, which means most people won’t develop complications as long as they follow the provided post-operative care instructions. These directions are intended to help you mend as quickly and effectively as possible while avoiding potential problems.

How Long Do Dental Implants Typically Last?

Those seeking a longer-term solution for tooth loss might benefit from getting dental implants thanks to their enhanced longevity. Once your jaw fuses with the support rod in a process called osseointegration, it’s essentially a permanent part of your mouth and can potentially remain in place for the remainder of your natural life.

But the ceramic, tooth-colored restoration attached to your implant might not last quite as long. These resilient replacement teeth are less porous than natural enamel, so they are more resistant to chips, cracks, and discoloration, but they’re not invincible. Although they can last 15+ years if cared for correctly, it’s also possible for them to be damaged and eventually require repair or replacement.
