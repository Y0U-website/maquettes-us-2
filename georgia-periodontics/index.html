The practice

Our team of dental specialists brings decades of experience and has helped countless patients reclaim their health following tooth loss and gum disease, establishing long-standing relationships built on trust, expertise, and outstanding outcomes.

Dr. Mark T. Litterer is widely regarded as one of the most accomplished and skilled periodontists in Atlanta. After displaying remarkable academic prowess at Furman University, where he graduated with the highest honors in chemistry in 1999, he pursued his dental education at the Medical University of South Carolina, where he quickly distinguished himself.

His three-year residency at the University of Kentucky’s prestigious periodontics program honed his abilities in placing dental implants and rebuilding soft/hard tissues for patients with gum disease. Today, his innovative approach to minimally invasive periodontal procedures continues to set the standard in the field.

In 2002, Dr. Litterer joined Georgia Periodontics, where his protocols have consistently led to improved patient outcomes. His membership in the American Academy of Periodontology and various other influential organizations reflects his standing as a thought leader in the field.
