Dr. Kim’s Expertise in Prosthodontics: Specializing in Implant Rehabilitation and Advanced Restorative Procedures

Dr. Kim enjoys helping patients with a range of prosthodontic procedures, particularly full arch implant rehabilitation, surgical implant placements, fixed restorations, and both complete and partial dentures. He is highly skilled in using advanced materials like monolithic zirconia and metal/acrylic dentures. His expertise extends to both guided and non-guided implant placements, as well as soft and hard tissue grafting, providing comprehensive care to patients with complex restorative needs.
