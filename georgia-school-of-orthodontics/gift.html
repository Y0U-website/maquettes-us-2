Gift Of A Smile Contest

Give the Gift of a Smile

In celebration of Georgia School of Orthodontics’ 10th anniversary, GSO is expanding its Gift of a Smile initiative through a special campaign. Between June and December, 10 deserving children will receive complimentary orthodontic treatment.

For the past 10 years, GSO’s giveback efforts have been rooted in its complimentary care programs, which provide orthodontic treatment to children and community heroes in need. Through Gift of a Smile , Gift of a Smile Heroes , and Purple Heart Smiles , GSO has donated more than $290,000 in care and helped deserving individuals achieve healthy, confident smiles.

Who Can Be Nominated

This campaign is open to children in need of orthodontic treatment whose families may not otherwise be able to afford care. Eligibility now includes dependent children up to age 26 living in the household. Dependent nominees ages 18 to 26 must live in the same household as their legal guardian.

How to Nominate a Child

To nominate a child, please click the nomination form button below. Once submitted:

Nominees will receive an email with instructions to complete the application.

The application will include digital new patient paperwork and instructions for submitting smile photos.

Finalists will be selected each month throughout the anniversary campaign.

Selected finalists must complete a complimentary orthodontic evaluation.

Recipients will be selected between June and December 2026.

Gift of a Smile 2026

Lia Stone

Lia is a sweet 11-year-old girl who suffers from bullying and self-esteem issues because of her teeth. She has asked for braces several times, but her family has had to put off orthodontic treatment for financial reasons. Gift of a Smile wants to make her wish for braces come true and help Lia see how beautiful she is!

Dana De La Parra

Dana is a kind-hearted 12-year-old girl who began experiencing crowding when her permanent teeth came in. Despite being teased over her teeth, she never let others take her smile away. Gift of a Smile is excited to give her a smile as bright and beautiful as her heart!

Eli Apted

Eli is a bright 14-year-old with a great sense of humor who loves to make others smile. However, because of his insecurities over his teeth, he often hides his own smile. Financial strain for Eli’s family made it seem like orthodontic treatment was not an option, but thanks to Gift of A Smile, soon he’ll be able to laugh with confidence!

Damian Soler

Free braces will soon also help Damian Soler, 13. Soler lives in Decatur, but comes from Gwinnett. His teeth prevent him from being able to close his mouth while he sleeps. As a result, he suffers from constant sore throats. Soon, he’ll be able to sleep peacefully.

Savien Cox

Savien Cox, a fifth-grader from Lawrenceville, will also be able to fix his orthodontic problems thanks to GSO. Cox is a happy kid who loves to smile, but suffers from low-self esteem over his teeth. Soon, braces will help him overcome those insecurities.

Sierra Henson

Dacula resident Sierra Henson, 14, was one of the children selected to receive free orthodontic care, which will include appliances, braces, retainers and more at no cost. Henson is a straight A student enrolled in AP and gifted classes. Because of a family member’s expensive medical costs, it seemed like Henson wouldn’t be able to receive orthodontic care.

After a recent and sudden loss of Jennifer’s close family member, the family has sought counseling and free grief support camp opportunities for her. Logan Leftenant

Logan is a bright, compassionate 11-year-old who has faced more hardship than most his age. Since losing his father in 2015, he has been lovingly raised by his grandmother. Logan comes from a large family with nine siblings, ranging in age from 15 to 26. He’s an avid puzzler - tackling 3,000-pieces now. He loves gardening, and expresses himself through his creativity.

Receiving a healthy, straight smile would bring Logan renewed confidence and joy to a boy who truly deserves to smile more.
