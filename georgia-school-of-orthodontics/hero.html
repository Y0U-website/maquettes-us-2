Francisco Monreal, from Marietta, has been a patient of Georgia Smiles since 2013. We have watched him grow into the hero he has become today by being a firefighter for Cobb County Fire. He has been such a hero for us and the community while working in the front line during the pandemic. He always has a smile every time he comes into our dental office, what a better opportunity than to have a better smile when he comes in.

Yasmin who resides in Buford was nominated by her husband who calls his wife a hero. She currently is a frontline worker, a Certified Nursing Assistant and caregiver at The Oaks at Hampton in Cumming, GA. Going to school while being a wife and mom of a three-year old takes courage, but she wants to continue in the medical profession and is attending Altierus Career College in Surgical Technology. She never gives up and is working hard to achieve the goals & dreams for her and her family!

Romaldo “Tony” Hughes – Tony is a Dekalb County police officer who lives in Ellenwood. Not only has he served as a police officer for over twenty years, but he also supports his community. Tony has spent his free time and personal money mentoring youth and providing life coaching for adults. In addition, he has had to postpone his orthodontic treatment to fund his daughter’s college education. Tony is excited for his new smile, which he says will enhance his self-esteem as he remains on the front lines serving his community.

Isabel Nogues – Isabel, who lives in Flowery Branch, has served her community during the pandemic by working as an EKG Technician and Urgent Care Assistant. Her mother, who nominated Isabel, explains that she is a kind and compassionate person who pulls extra hours every week to help with the surge in COVID-19 cases. She was supposed to receive orthodontic treatment at the age of twelve, but financial circumstances prevented her from moving forward with her care. She is thrilled to receive the gift of a new smile from GSO.

Ashika Payne – Ashika is a teacher who resides in the East Point suburb of Atlanta. She works as a fourth-grade teacher at Gideons Elementary, and has worked with youth for over twenty-five years. Before serving as a teacher, she was Program Director at the Douglas County Boys and Girls Club. She has dedicated her life to helping her students, and is so grateful for the opportunity to have her teeth straightened.

We are excited to announce GSO’s March Gift of a Smile Heroes winner! Congratulations to Education Consultant, Alisha McGlawn. Alisha is an everyday Wonder Woman, mother of two wonderful boys, an educator, a parent/child advocate, and a budding entrepreneur who’s sense of community and fellowship is a true gift to all those she encounters! We are honored to provide her with the gift of a confident new smile!

We are excited to announce GSO’s July Gift of a Smile Heroes winner! Congratulations to Tashika Allen! Tashika was nominated by her daughter Keyana who calls her mom her personal hero who does what she can for anyone, whether she knows them or not. A survivor, Tashika has overcome many obstacles in her life all while putting her family first. We are honored to provide Tashika with the healthy, confident smile she deserves!

Congratulations to our quarterly Gift of a Smile Heroes winner Stephen McDowell! Stephen served in the US Military for 13 years and retired in 2008 with the rank of E6 Staff Sergeant. Stephen has three children: Dylan, 17, Bella 13, and Jay 7. His hobbies include coaching his youngest son’s baseball team and his daughter’s softball team. When he’s not coaching, Stephen also enjoys archery hunting. A hero for his service to our country, Stephen lists his father as his personal hero for instilling in him at a young age that when you work hard anything is possible.
