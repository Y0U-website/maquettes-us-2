Advanced Care for Complex Needs

Dr. Begley performs a full scope of oral and maxillofacial surgery, including extractions, bone grafting, facial trauma repair, and surgical collaboration with referring dentists. While many surgeons offer All-on-Four dental implants, Dr. Begley is one of the few in New Jersey trained to place zygomatic implants, which provide a stable, long-term solution for patients with significant bone loss.

Every procedure is carefully tailored to the individual, and every treatment plan reflects a deep understanding of how oral health supports overall wellness. “We take the extra time to explain procedures to patients and families so they understand how treatment decisions will positively impact their lives,” says Dr. Begley.

Trusted by Dentists, Hospitals, and Professional Boards

He is a proud examiner for the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, helping to shape the future of the profession by evaluating and certifying new generations of surgeons.

Supporting Our Chester Community

Educator. Innovator. Lifelong Learner.

Dr. Begley earned his dental degree from the University of Pennsylvania and completed his oral surgery residency at Rutgers and Temple Universities, where he remains on staff as an Assistant Professor. His passion for continuous growth is evident in his involvement with Spear Study Club and the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (AAOMS), where he regularly attends national courses and seminars.
