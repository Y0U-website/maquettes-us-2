Implant Restoration

Dental implants can be a bit of a confusing procedure. That&#x27;s because it comes in two parts: the implant and the dental crown. The main thing to remember is that one cannot go without the other. Implants are needed to replace a tooth&#x27;s root, while dental crowns, bridges, or dentures replace the part you can actually see (your smile). To restore your smile to its full potential and beauty, implant restorations are essential. You deserve to have a set of teeth that you feel comfortable in and we are experts in keeping you smiling for decades to come.

Platelet-Rich Fibrin

We utilize Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) as an advanced, innovative treatment to enhance healing in various dental procedures. PRF is derived from your own blood, concentrated with platelets, growth factors, and cytokines, which promote rapid healing and tissue regeneration. This natural approach minimizes recovery time and improves outcomes in procedures such as dental implants, bone grafting, and periodontal therapy. Embrace the cutting-edge benefits of PRF to ensure faster, more effective healing.

Dental Implant Complications

Think you might have an implant complication? We&#x27;re here to help. Our skilled team treats implant issues, restoring both function and aesthetics. If you’re feeling pain while chewing, noticing swollen or bleeding gums, experiencing changes in your bite, or sensing looseness around your implant, don’t wait—schedule a checkup today. Early detection is key to successful treatment, so the sooner you act, the better your results will be.
