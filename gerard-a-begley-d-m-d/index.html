The practice

Dr. Begley performs a full scope of oral and maxillofacial surgery, including extractions, bone grafting, facial trauma repair, and surgical collaboration with referring dentists. While many surgeons offer All-on-Four dental implants, Dr. Begley is one of the few in New Jersey trained to place zygomatic implants, which provide a stable, long-term solution for patients with significant bone loss.

Every procedure is carefully tailored to the individual, and every treatment plan reflects a deep understanding of how oral health supports overall wellness. “We take the extra time to explain procedures to patients and families so they understand how treatment decisions will positively impact their lives,” says Dr. Begley.

He is a proud examiner for the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, helping to shape the future of the profession by evaluating and certifying new generations of surgeons.

Dr. Begley earned his dental degree from the University of Pennsylvania and completed his oral surgery residency at Rutgers and Temple Universities, where he remains on staff as an Assistant Professor. With cone-beam computed tomography, or CBCT technology, we can provide our patients peace of mind with more effective and informative treatment planning. This reliable technology creates 3D images that ensure precise placement for increased patient comfort and better results.

Long Lifespan With proper care, dental implants can last decades — often a lifetime. While the bridge attached to your implants may eventually need updating, it&#x27;s far more durable compared to removable dentures that typically wear out every 5 years.

Lifelike Results All-on-X bridges are designed from high-quality materials like porcelain or zirconia to closely match the look, feel, and function of natural teeth. Plus, because they&#x27;re securely anchored to implants, you&#x27;ll never experience the slipping or irritation that can happen with dentures.

Better Oral Health Implants help stimulate and preserve your jawbone, preventing the bone loss that typically happens after tooth loss. All-on-X supports long-term oral health and helps maintain your facial structure over time.
