Zygomatic implants are a special type of dental implant designed for patients with significant bone loss in the upper jaw. Unlike traditional implants that are placed into the jawbone, zygomatic implants anchor into the cheekbone (zygoma), which is naturally denser and more supportive. This allows us to restore your upper teeth even if you’ve been told you’re not a candidate for implants in the past.

Why Choose Zygomatic Implants?

Skip the Bone Graft Zygomatic implants eliminate the need for bone grafting or sinus lifts, which means fewer surgeries and faster results.

Secure, Permanent Results These implants provide a solid foundation for full upper arch restorations that look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Ideal for Advanced Bone Loss Even if your jawbone is too thin or soft for traditional implants, zygomatic implants may still be an option for you.

The Zygomatic Implant Process Consultation & 3D Imaging We’ll begin with a comprehensive consultation, including advanced 3D scans to assess your bone structure. If zygomatic implants are right for you, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan and walk you through every detail.

Implant Placement

Zygomatic implants are placed under sedation for your comfort. The implants are anchored into the cheekbone for maximum support. Depending on your case, we may also place additional implants in the front of your upper jaw.

Healing & Restoration

You may receive a temporary set of teeth on the same day as your surgery. Once the implants have fully integrated with your bone (typically over a few months), we’ll replace the temporary bridge with your final, custom-made restoration.
