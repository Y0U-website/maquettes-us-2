Dr. Gerard Boquel, DDS

Board Certified in Periodontology and Dental Implant Surgery

Dr. Gerard Boquel is a board-certified Periodontist and Implant Surgeon. He has provided premier dental implant surgery and periodontal treatment to the metro Washington DC area for more than 35 years.

Dr. Boquel graduated from Marietta College in 1981 with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. He then earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Georgetown University School of Dentistry in 1986. He continued his dental education with a Periodontics Residency at Rutgers University School of Dental Medicine, which he completed in 1988.

In 2018, Dr. Boquel earned the important distinction as a Diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology. This designation represents Dr. Boquel’s personal and professional commitment to the standards of periodontics and continued professional growth through ongoing education and continuous certification. His accreditation as a Diplomate assures his patients that they will receive the highest quality of periodontal and dental implant surgery.

On a personal note, Dr. Boquel has extensive local roots. He was raised in Rockville, Maryland, and attended St. Jude’s School, graduating from Rockville High School. He enjoys cooking and has recently discovered oil painting on canvas and panels.

Dr. Alyssa M. Dierkes, DDS, MS

Dr. Alyssa M. Dierkes is a board-certified periodontist and implant surgeon. She was born and raised in Fairfax, Virginia, where she graduated from W.T. Woodson High School. She attended and graduated from Virginia Tech with a Bachelor of Science in Nutrition.

She attended New York University College of Dentistry, where she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS). She was awarded the Ramsdorf Scholarship for merit based on her academic performance.

For periodontal studies, Dr. Dierkes attended the University of Maryland in Baltimore, where she was awarded a post-graduate periodontic scholarship. There, she completed her residency in Periodontics and Implant Surgery.

At the same time she pursued her specialty education in periodontics, Dr. Dierkes obtained a Master’s in Biomedical Sciences at the University of Maryland in Baltimore. She successfully defended her thesis on the interaction of diabetes and peri-implant disease.

Dr. Dierkes has authored two research publications on dental and periodontal issues. While at NYU, she published an article on the use of cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) in the diagnosis of dental conditions. At the University of Maryland, she authored a textbook chapter on diabetes as a systemic factor in peri-implantitis.

Dr. Dierkes is highly trained in all aspects of periodontal therapy and has a special interest in cosmetic and minimally invasive periodontal-plastic surgery.

She is a diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology and Implant Surgery, which requires her to maintain an elite level of standards through lifelong learning and maintaining contemporary skills. She is a member of the American Academy of Periodontics and a member of Spear Education. Her focus is to provide the highest level of care, combining technical excellence, an eye for esthetics, and compassionate care.

In her free time, Dr. Dierkes enjoys cooking, playing golf, doing Pilates, and spending time with her Pomeranian, Cashew.
