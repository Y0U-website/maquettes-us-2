Dental Services

We offer a variety of general adult dental and pediatric dental services including exams, fillings, crowns, dentures, root canals, extractions (including wisdom teeth), implants, invisalign, laser drill-free dentistry and more. Options for sedation in the office and at Fairbanks Memorial Hospital are also available. By providing a wide variety of quality services to Fairbanks, North Pole, and all of the other many communities Alaska s interior, we strive to save our patients time, money, and insurance confusion, and multiple trips to multiple offices. Root Canals

Root canal therapy involves carefully cleaning and removing necrotic or dying nerve and tissue inside a tooth and filling/sealing it off to keep bacteria out. Modern 3D imaging with file, irrigation, and bio-ceramic sealer technology allows us thoroughly clean and seal a tooth better, faster and more predictably better than ever.

Extractions

We can extract virtually any tooth in adults and children, including 3rd molars ("wisdom teeth"). If you are considering replacing the missing tooth with an implant, bone grafting is also available to preserve the bone at the extraction site in preparation for placement. Sedation options are available for patients with anxiety.

Laser Dentistry- drill-free, anesthetic free

Not a fan of dental drill noise and vibration? Laser dentistry has finally advanced to a level of practical, fast, conservative, and in some cases anesthetic free dentistry. Our Fotona Lightwalker hard/soft tissue dental laser can carefully and quickly remove decay or defective tooth colored fillings in a manner that is safer and more controlled than a traditional dental drill, while also providing a better surface for bonding filling materials. This option has been especially popular with children and adults with dental anxiety.
