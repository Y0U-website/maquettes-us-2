Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Gerken was raised in Waurika, Oklahoma, and his wife, Mika (Moore) was raised in Ringling. They knew they wanted to raise their children, MiKaylyn, Gage, and Matlyn in a smaller, active community close to family and friends, and found Ardmore was perfect! Why Gerken Orthodontics

At Gerken Orthodontics, we believe every smile is as unique as the person behind it. That’s why we provide personalized care tailored to meet your individual needs and goals. From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll experience a warm, welcoming environment where courteous service and patient-centered care are our top priorities. With a highly experienced doctor and dedicated team, we’ve built a reputation for delivering exceptional results—and it shows in our glowing online reviews from happy patients and families.

We offer a wide range of advanced treatment options, including traditional braces and Invisalign, a nearly invisible solution using clear, custom-made aligners. To make your orthodontic journey as easy as possible, we offer affordable, customizable payment plans designed to fit your budget, and we accept most insurance plans. At Gerken Orthodontics, we’re committed to delivering outstanding care with cutting-edge technology, all while ensuring your experience is seamless and stress-free. Let us help you achieve the confident, healthy smile you deserve!
