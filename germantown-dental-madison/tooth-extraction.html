When Might a Tooth Need to Be Removed?

Tooth extractions are typically recommended when a tooth cannot be saved through restorative treatments such as fillings, crowns , or root canals. Common reasons for extractions include:

● Severe tooth decay or infection that has reached the root

● Advanced periodontal disease affecting the supporting bone

● Cracked or fractured teeth that cannot be repaired

● Impacted wisdom teeth causing pain or shifting

● Crowding , especially before orthodontic treatment

● Dental trauma that results in non-restorable damage

At Germantown Dental, we take a conservative approach to dental care—meaning we’ll always explore every option to save your tooth before recommending removal.

What to Expect from the Extraction Process

Though we don’t perform the procedure in-house, we educate our patients on what to expect so you can approach your extraction with confidence. Typically, a tooth extraction involves:

● Local anesthesia to ensure you’re comfortable

● Gently removing the tooth with minimal pressure

● Instructions for recovery , including tips to avoid dry socket or discomfort

● Follow-up planning for tooth replacement, if needed

We remain available throughout your treatment journey to answer questions and assist with post-extraction decisions, such as tooth replacement options.

Replacing a Tooth After Extraction

If a permanent tooth is removed, replacing it is often important for maintaining alignment, chewing ability, and appearance. Germantown Dental offers several modern solutions, including:

● Dental Implants – Long-lasting, natural-looking results

● Dental Bridges – Fixed appliances to fill one or more missing teeth

● Partial Dentures – Removable solutions for multiple missing teeth

Once healing is complete, our team will help you choose the right option to restore your smile’s function and appearance.
