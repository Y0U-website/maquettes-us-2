Dr. Tuan Nhu attended Sherwood High School and has since made Germantown his hometown. It has been his dream to serve the families and professionals in this historic community.

He earned his dental degree from University of Maryland, Baltimore College of Dental Surgery. In his senior year, Dr. Nhu completed an oral surgery clerkship at the University of Maryland, Baltimore. After dental school, he completed his postdoctoral residency in general dentistry at the SUNY, Stony Brook Dental School.

Dr. Nhu has completed advanced training and certification in a broad range of dental services including rotary endodontics, esthetic dentistry, oral surgery, clear braces (Invisalign), laser dentistry, and implant dentistry.
