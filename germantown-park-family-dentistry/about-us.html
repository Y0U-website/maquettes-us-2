ABOUT US

Reliable. Professional. Friendly.

Dr. Mike Pulido graduated from the University of Tennessee at Knoxville and received his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Tennessee in Memphis in 1985. He has previously served as President of the Memphis Dental Legion. He has enjoyed membership in the Academy of General Dentistry since 1986.

Dr. Pulido is committed to the delivery of excellent family dentistry by continuous education to stay abreast of the latest techniques available. His involvement in modern technology includes orthodontic correction of minor tooth movement, cosmetics, and implantology.

Mike Pulido speaks Spanish fluently and is actively involved in the community as a coordinator with adult and youth soccer associations.

Dr. Ben Addison graduated from the University of Alabama and received his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Tennessee Health Science Center in Memphis in 2021.

Dr. Addison brings compassion and commitment to our patients dental care and is dedicated to helping patients maintain healthy, confident smiles through his expertise in cosmetic, restorative, and implant dentistry.

Dr. Addison was the inaugural coach of the local water polo team, the MHEA Eagles, which he led to back-to-back state championships in 2017, 2018, and 2019. He is now the head youth coach for the Bluff City Blues water polo team.
