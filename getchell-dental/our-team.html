Our Team

0 The people who make this practice feel like home. Our team has been serving the greater Seacoast community for over 20 years. We’re not a revolving door. Many of our staff have been with this practice for a decade or more, and some of our patients have been seeing the same hygienist or assistant for just as long.

Meet the team

Portsmouth has always been home for Dr. Chris Getchell, whose family has had roots in this community for generations. He earned his DMD from Tufts University and spent time practicing in Florida and Maine before making his way back to the Seacoast, where he and his wife Jamie are raising their two children, Miles and Margo. Outside the office he’s most at home on a mountain, whether he’s skiing, hiking, fishing, or shooting photography. He took over this practice with one goal: to provide the kind of personal, unhurried care that this community has always deserved.

Dr. Chris Getchell, DMD — Owner & Dentist
