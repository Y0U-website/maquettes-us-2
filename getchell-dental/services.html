Services

  • Digital X-Rays Digital X-rays let us see what the naked eye cannot: cavities forming between teeth and changes in bone structure. With modern digital imaging, we get high-quality diagnostic images quickly and with minimal radiation.
  • Scaling & Root Planing (Deep Cleaning) When gum disease has progressed beyond what a regular cleaning can address, scaling and root planing provides a thorough, targeted treatment. This procedure cleans below the gumline, removing the buildup that has caused the gums to pull away from the teeth. We’ll walk you through exactly what to expect before, during, and after.
  • Laser Gum Treatment (Gingivectomy) Using a precise diode laser, we can treat excess or diseased gum tissue with minimal discomfort and faster healing than traditional methods. This approach is used to address gum disease, uneven gum lines, and overgrowth. It can also improve the appearance of a "gummy" smile.

Preventive Care/ Gum Health

  • Composite Fillings (Tooth-Colored) When decay or tooth structure is damaged, composite fillings restore its function and appearance using a material that’s color-matched to your natural teeth. No more silver. The entire procedure is typically completed in a single visit, and the result looks and feels completely natural.
  • Zirconia Crowns & Bridges Zirconia is one of the strongest, most durable ceramic materials available in modern dentistry. We use zirconia crowns to protect and restore damaged or decayed teeth that can’t be repaired with a filling alone. Bridges use the same technology to replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to the healthy teeth on each side.

Restorative Dentistry

  • Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography) is an advanced 3D imaging technique. It rotates around your head to capture highly detailed, 3D views of your teeth, jawbones, nerve pathways, and soft tissues in a single, quick scan.
  • Intraoral Scanner The Dentsply Sirona Primescan is a high-performance, intraoral scanner that is used to take highly accurate 3D digital impressions of a patient’s mouth in under a minute, eliminating the need for messy, physical impression molds

Diagnostic Technology

  • Tooth Extractions Whether it’s a wisdom tooth, a tooth too damaged to save, or one causing crowding or infection—we’ll make sure you’re fully comfortable before we begin, and we’ll give you clear post-operative instructions so healing goes smoothly. We perform extractions with care and precision.
  • Dental Implants Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. A small titanium post is placed directly into the jawbone, where it integrates with the bone over time to create a stable, permanent foundation. Once healed, a crown, bridge, or denture is attached. The result looks, feels, and functions just like a natural tooth. We handle the full process right here, from consultation through final restoration.
  • Bone Grafting Bone Grafting (also called Socket Preservation) is a procedure performed immediately after tooth extraction to minimize the natural bone resorption and soft tissue collapse that occurs in the empty socket. This procedure is primarily indicated when future implant placement is planned, as it helps preserve sufficient bone dimensions to support successful implant osseointegration.

Oral Surgery

Root Canal Therapy

  • Learn More When a tooth’s nerve becomes infected or inflamed, a root canal cleans out the damaged tissue, relieves pain, and saves the tooth. The procedure itself is typically no more uncomfortable than having a filling placed. We take our time, we communicate every step, and we won’t move forward until you’re ready.
  • Nightguards If you grind or clench your teeth at night (a condition called bruxism), a custom nightguard can protect your teeth, your restorations, and your jaw joint from long-term damage. Custom-fitted nightguards are far more effective and comfortable than over-the-counter alternatives.
  • Retainers After orthodontic treatment, retainers keep your teeth in their new positions. We fabricate custom retainers that fit precisely and are easy to wear consistently.
  • Dentures & Partial Dentures For patients missing multiple teeth or a full arch, dentures and partial dentures restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. We design removable appliances that fit comfortably and look natural. Partial dentures are a great option when some natural teeth remain and can help anchor the restoration in place.

Removable Devices

Clarity Aligners — Orthodontics

  • Learn More Clarity Aligners offer a discreet, comfortable alternative to traditional braces. Using a series of clear, removable trays, your teeth gradually shift into their ideal positions, without wires, brackets, or the dietary restrictions that come with them. We’ll assess your smile and let you know if you’re a good candidate, and then walk you through exactly what treatment will look like.

Curodont — Biomimetic Remineralization

  • Learn More Curodont is a breakthrough in preventive dentistry. Using biomimetic technology that mimics the body’s own processes, Curodont can actually remineralize early enamel lesions, stopping the progression of a cavity in its tracks. This is the kind of forward-thinking, science-backed care we’re proud to offer. In the right situations, it means we can treat a problem without a drill.
