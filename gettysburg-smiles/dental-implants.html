Do I need to replace all of my missing teeth with dental implants?

When it comes to implant restorations, every patient has different needs. Some patients only need one or two implants to replace their missing teeth. Other patients may have full rows of lost teeth.

We offer All-On-Four, or full arch restorations if patients have upper or lower arches of missing teeth. Full arch restorations use four to six dental implants to secure a denture. If you have missing teeth and want an implant restoration, we will help you find the right option to fit your specific needs.

How soon can you get an implant after a tooth extraction?

We can sometimes place implants the same day as a tooth extraction, depending on the health of the gums and bone. Same-day dental implants are possible for some Gettysburg patients.

Other times, the bone and gums need to heal before an implant is placed. Dr. Tempel will recommend how to proceed based on your oral health for the most successful outcome.

What is the recovery process like after getting dental implants?

Everyone will recover from a surgical procedure differently. After getting dental implants, you should expect discomfort and swelling for the first few days. Medications and ice packs will help alleviate the pain. You will need to follow a soft food diet, maintain good oral hygiene, and avoid strenuous activities during the initial healing period.

After healing from surgery, maintain oral hygiene for several months until the implant fully integrates with the jawbone. At that point, we can attach the final dental prosthetic.

Are dental implants safe?

Yes, dental implants are very safe and have a high success rate. They’re a well-established and reliable solution for replacing missing teeth. However, individual health factors and proper oral care are vital to their success.

Every patient must consult Dr. Tempel to see if they qualify for teeth implants. She will make sure you are healthy enough to receive them and ensure your treatment is safe and effective.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The dental implant process can vary depending on individual factors. On average, it takes several months to complete. The process includes planning, a surgical procedure, healing and integration of the implant with the jawbone, abutment placement, and placement of the final prosthetic.

Some patients may need a bone graft or teeth extractions. These extra procedures can prolong treatment time. However, it’s vital for a successful and lasting result. After your consultation, Dr. Tempel can give you an accurate treatment timeline, whether it is a few months or up to a year.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Good candidates for dental implants typically have good oral health, ample jawbone density, and the drive to maintain good oral hygiene for a lifetime. Patients with missing teeth or those facing tooth extraction may be good candidates. Dr. Tempel will determine if you are eligible based on your overall health, oral health, and specific dental conditions.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants can last a lifetime with proper care and maintenance. They are designed to be a long-term solution for missing teeth, offering stability, functionality, and durability comparable to natural teeth. Regular dental check-ups and oral hygiene practices can help prolong the lifespan of dental implants.
