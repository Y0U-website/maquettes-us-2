Dental Services and Technology

Our dental office provides patients with comprehensive, quality dental treatments and technology. We can provide customized, effective care using modern dental technology and proven solutions. Some of our dental services and dentist office technology include:

Digital Smile Design

This computer software can show patients a mock-up of their potential smile. We use digital smile design to educate patients about their treatment. Using this dental technology , we take images of patients’ smiles in their natural and retracted form. Then, we take reference points in these photos, which the software uses to create a balanced, straighter smile.

Invisalign

Clear Invisalign aligners provide a discreet and comfortable alternative to metal braces. Each pair of Invisalign aligners is different and shifts teeth into a straighter position over time. Patients can also remove their Invisalign trays to eat without restrictions and thoroughly clean their teeth and gums. Invisalign is a great modern orthodontic treatment for adults.

Sleep Apnea Treatment

Did you know that your local dentist office can help you treat your sleep apnea ? Dr. Tempel offers oral appliances for patients with obstructive sleep apnea. These appliances cover the upper and lower teeth and open up the airway. During sleep, the soft tissues in the back of the mouth collapse.

Patients with sleep apnea can encounter apnea multiple times a night. A custom oral appliance can prevent snoring, sleeping difficulty, and other signs and symptoms of sleep apnea.
