Privacy Policy

Kannan Komandur Dental Inc., located at 525 N Cleveland Massillon Rd, Sute 106, Akron OH 44333, ("we," "us," or "our"), respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website and in the course of providing dental care, how we use it, and the choices you have.

This Privacy Policy applies to our website, www.ghentdental.com, and to the contact and communication information we collect from patients. It does not replace our **HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices**, which separately describes how we handle your protected health information (PHI) as your dental care provider. A copy of that notice is available at our office or at [link/insert location].

We may collect the following types of information:

- **Contact information** you provide to us, such as your name, phone number, email address, and mailing address (e.g., when you call, email, fill out a contact form, or schedule an appointment).

- **Appointment and treatment-related information** needed to provide dental care, communicate with you about your visits, and send appointment reminders.

- **Website usage information**, such as pages visited or general browser/device information, if our website uses analytics or cookies (see Section 5).

We do **not** knowingly collect sensitive information through our website beyond what is necessary to schedule appointments or respond to inquiries.

We use the information we collect solely to:

- Communicate directly with you, including confirming, scheduling, or rescheduling appointments, sending reminders, and following up on your care.

- Respond to questions or requests you submit through our website or by phone/email.

- With your consent, ask you to consider leaving a review of your experience on Google or similar platforms (see Section 3).

We appreciate when patients share feedback about their experience with us, including on our Google Business page. If we ask you to leave a review, we will do so directly for example, in person after your visit, or through a follow-up email or text message sent to you individually. We do not publicly disclose that you are a patient of this practice, and leaving a review is always entirely optional. You are never required to leave a review, and declining will not affect your care in any way.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. We do not share your information with anyone except:

- Staff and contractors who need it to help run our practice (e.g., scheduling software, billing services), who are bound by confidentiality obligations.

- As required by law, or to protect the safety of our patients or staff.

- As otherwise described in our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices for treatment, payment, and healthcare operations purposes.

Our website may use cookies or similar tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site. These tools may collect information like your IP address, browser type, and pages viewed. This information is used only to improve our website and is not linked to your health records. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information you share with us, including limiting access to authorized staff and using secure systems for storing patient information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100 % secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

- Ask us to stop contacting you for non-treatment purposes (e.g., review requests) at any time.

- Request access to or correction of your contact information.

- Contact us with any questions about how your information is used.

Requests regarding your medical/dental records specifically should be directed to us in accordance with our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

If we provide care to minor patients, information is collected and used only as described above, with parental or guardian consent as required, and solely for treatment and communication purposes.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

By opting into SMS from a web form or other medium, you are agreeing to receive SMS messages from Kannan Komandur Dental. This includes SMS messages for customer care. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. See privacy policy at https://ghentdental.com/privacy-policy-1. Message HELP for help. Reply STOP to any message to opt out.
