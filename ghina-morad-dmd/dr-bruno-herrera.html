Awards & RECOGNITIONS

Dr. Herrera is a Diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology. He completed his DDS at the Detroit Mercy Dental; Masters and residency training in Periodontics at University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill; PhD in Pharmacology at the University of Sao Paulo, Brazil; and Post-Doc training at the Forsyth Institute (Harvard affiliate) funded by the US Army.

Dr Herrera has published several research articles and book chapters, has served as peer reviewer for scientific journals in his field, and has received several awards, including AAP Educator Award from the AAP Foundation.

Dr. Herrera’s patients can expect a comprehensive care using the best evidence-based dentistry in a humane and ethical way. You will quickly notice his passion for dentistry and patient care. In his spare time, he enjoys hiking, watching sports, going to concerts, cooking, and traveling.
