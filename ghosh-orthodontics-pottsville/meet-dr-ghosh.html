Meet Orthodontist Dr. Sam Ghosh

Dr. Sam Ghosh has always had a passion for serving and giving back to his local communities, which is why he has been crafting beautiful smiles for over 25 years in Allentown and Pottsville.

“My goal is to achieve the best result in the most efficient and comfortable manner,” Dr. Ghosh says.

Education

Dr. Ghosh received his D.M.D from the University of Pennsylvania. He graduated at the top of his class and was awarded the prestigious International Award for academic excellence and clinical orthodontics excellence.

He went on to receive his two-year post-doctoral graduate training from the University of Rochester’s Eastman Dental Center with a specialty in orthodontics. This program is recognized as one of the country’s top residency programs.

Dr. Ghosh’s education did not stop at graduation. He is extensively involved in furthering his education by taking hundreds of hours of continuing education courses and seminars throughout the world. There are rapid advances in orthodontic technology and techniques, and it is important to Dr. Ghosh that his knowledge continues to grow with the industry.

Career

After he graduated in 1995, Dr. Ghosh began serving his community as an orthodontist and has since opened two successful practices. He first established his practice in Pottsville, PA in 1997 and went on to open a second Ghosh Orthodontics location in Allentown, PA in 2006.

Both practices are extremely successful today and Dr. Ghosh is recognized as one of the area’s best orthodontists. Learn more about Dr. Ghosh’s state-of-the-art practice where he offers the most advanced orthodontic treatment in the area.

He is dedicated to providing the very best orthodontic care to his patients. Throughout his career, Dr. Ghosh has received many accolades, including being voted as the Top Orthodontist in the county by Schuylkill Living magazine.

Memberships and Associations

Dr. Ghosh is actively involved in the orthodontic community. He is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) and the American Dental Association (ADA) .

He was the former President of the Schuylkill County Dental Society and is still a member. He was also the former President of the 4th District Dental Society.

Currently, Dr. Ghosh is a member of the Lehigh Valley Society of Orthodontics, the Angle Orthodontic Society, the American Cleft Palate Craniofacial Association, and the Lehigh Valley Dental Society, where he serves on the Board of Directors.

Dr. Ghosh is actively involved in the community through his services and philanthropy. He served through the Rotary Club, where he also served as the President.

Dr. Ghosh connects with his community by sponsoring sports teams and actively participating in school programs and charity outreach. He is also involved with the local Chambers of Commerce.

Personal Life

When Dr. Ghosh isn’t crafting beautiful smiles, he enjoys spending time with his family. He and his wife, Zubeda, have three children: Alisha, Sabrina, and Roy.
