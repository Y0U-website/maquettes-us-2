Dr. Adam Brantley

Originally from Columbia, Dr. Brantley moved to Spartanburg three years ago to be closer to his children. After 20 years of running his own practice in Columbia, he genuinely enjoys the change of pace here at Skylyn Dental. His desire to work with his hands and help others has led to 21 years in the dental industry, and he looks forward to many more! When Dr. Brantley is not strengthening your smile, he enjoys spending time with his family (his dog is family), biking, playing music, and being with friends.
