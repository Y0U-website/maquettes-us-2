Common Cosmetic Care Services

Professional Teeth Whitening Brighten Your Smile with Zoom! Whitening

Professional teeth whitening is one of the fastest and most popular cosmetic dental treatments available. Over time, teeth can become stained from coffee, tea, wine, tobacco, and natural aging. Professional whitening safely lifts these stains and restores a brighter, more youthful smile.

At Gibson & Heath Family Dentistry, we offer professional teeth whitening in Gulf Breeze and Pensacola with options designed to fit your schedule and goals.

Zoom! Whitening is a powerful in-office treatment that delivers dramatic results in a single visit.

During the procedure, your lips and gums are protected while a specialized whitening gel is applied to your teeth. The gel is then activated with the Zoom! light system, helping break up stains and discoloration quickly and effectively.

In just over an hour, many patients see their teeth become six to ten shades whiter.

After treatment, a fluoride application helps reduce sensitivity and strengthen enamel. Custom trays may also be provided to help maintain your results at home.

Zoom! is an excellent option for patients who want fast, noticeable whitening results before a wedding, event, vacation, or special occasion.

Looking for professional teeth whitening in Gulf Breeze or Pensacola?

Schedule your Zoom! whitening appointment today.

Custom Take-Home Whitening Trays

For patients who prefer whitening gradually at home, we also offer custom professional whitening trays.

These trays are created from impressions of your teeth to ensure a comfortable fit and even whitening results. A professional-strength whitening gel is placed inside the trays and worn daily for several hours or overnight.

Most patients see their desired results within three to four weeks of consistent use.

Custom trays are also ideal for maintaining your whitening results over time.

Patients throughout Pensacola, Gulf Breeze, and Pensacola Beach appreciate the flexibility of this convenient take-home option.

Porcelain Veneers

Transform Your Smile with Natural-Looking Results

Porcelain veneers are one of the most effective cosmetic dentistry treatments for dramatically improving the appearance of your smile.

Veneers are thin, custom-made shells designed to cover the front surface of your teeth. They are crafted from durable porcelain to match the natural color and translucency of your teeth, resulting in a beautiful, natural-looking appearance.

Most veneer treatments involve two appointments. During the first visit, the teeth are gently prepared, and impressions are taken so your custom veneers can be created in a dental laboratory. Temporary veneers may be placed while your permanent ones are being crafted.

At your second appointment, your veneers are carefully bonded to your teeth—revealing a refreshed, confident smile.

Compared to crowns, veneers often require less removal of natural tooth structure while still delivering stunning cosmetic improvement.
