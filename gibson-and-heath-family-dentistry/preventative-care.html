Common Preventative Care Services

Fillings Fillings are used to remove tooth decay and restore a tooth’s natural structure. When a cavity forms, the damaged portion of the tooth is carefully removed and replaced with a durable material that fills the space left behind.

At Gibson & Heath Family Dentistry, we use tooth-colored composite fillings that blend naturally with your teeth while providing long-lasting strength and protection.

When cavities are detected early, treatment is typically quick, comfortable, and minimally invasive. Left untreated, however, decay can spread deeper into the tooth, leading to pain, infection, or the need for a root canal or extraction.

Routine dental checkups help us catch cavities early and keep your smile healthy.

Contact our Gulf Breeze dental office to schedule an exam.

Dental Bonding

Dental bonding is a versatile treatment that improves both the function and appearance of teeth using a natural-looking composite resin.

During the procedure, the tooth surface is gently prepared so the material bonds securely. The composite resin is then applied, shaped, and hardened with a specialized light before being polished for a smooth, natural finish.

For many patients in Pensacola , Gulf Breeze , and Pensacola Beach , bonding offers a simple and effective way to restore confidence in their smile.

Schedule a consultation with our dental team today.

  • Repair chipped or cracked teeth
  • Restore minor decay
  • Improve the shape or alignment of teeth
  • Close small gaps between teeth
  • Enhance the overall look of your smile

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants provide an added layer of protection against cavities, especially for molars with deep grooves and pits.

Some teeth naturally have tiny crevices that toothbrush bristles cannot fully reach. These areas can trap plaque and bacteria, increasing the risk of tooth decay.

Sealants work by applying a thin protective coating over the chewing surface of the tooth. This smooth layer seals out bacteria and makes the tooth easier to clean.

Sealants are especially beneficial for children and teens , but many adults can benefit from them as well.

Protect your family’s smiles before cavities start.

Ask our team about dental sealants at your next appointment.

Non-Surgical Gum Disease Treatment

Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile. The gums, ligaments, and bone that support your teeth—known collectively as the periodontium—play a critical role in long-term oral health.

When gum disease is detected early, it can often be treated with non-surgical periodontal therapy , such as scaling and root planing. This deep-cleaning procedure removes plaque and bacteria beneath the gumline, allowing the gums to heal.

Early treatment can prevent more serious complications, such as bone or tooth loss.

If you are noticing symptoms of gum disease, don’t wait.

Schedule a periodontal evaluation with our team today.

  • Red, swollen, or bleeding gums
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Receding gums
  • Loose teeth
  • Changes in the way your teeth fit together
