We borrow from Montessori principles to make every visit feel like a place your child wants to explore, not one they’re dragged into.

The practice

A Prepared, Calm Space Soft colors, low noise, and everything sized for small hands and shorter attention spans.

Child-Led Visits Kids get to touch the tools, ask questions, and set the pace — with plenty of "show, tell, do" before anything happens.

Real Answers for Parents No jargon. We explain what we see, why it matters, and what you can do at home in plain language.

Growing Independence We teach kids to care for their own teeth, building habits and confidence that last well past graduation from our office.

Well-Child Checkups & Cleanings Gentle routine exams and cleanings, twice a year — including extra-oral and intraoral screenings, a TMJ (jaw joint) evaluation, and a sleep apnea / airway screening, covering the things parents are asking about most these days.

First Tooth, First Visit Infant oral exams starting around age one, so we can guide teething, feeding, and early habits together.

Dental Sealants A thin protective coating on molars that dramatically lowers the risk of cavities in tricky-to-brush spots. Traditional and fluoride-free sealant options are both available.

Gentle by design BPA-free materials Mercury-free — no amalgam Latex-free environment Fluoride-free option available Learn more Minimal Intervention Dentistry For early, incipient lesions, we offer needle-free options like silver diamine fluoride (SDF) and Curodont, which can arrest or even remineralize a cavity before it needs a filling.

Les soins

Recommended as both toothpaste and periodic professional treatments to strengthen enamel and prevent decay, tailored to each child’s cavity risk.

Dental Crowns Durable stainless steel crowns, tooth-colored Bioflx crowns, and natural-looking zirconia crowns to protect teeth with extensive decay or after a root canal.

From your baby’s very first tooth to teenage orthodontic screenings, our team covers the full journey.
