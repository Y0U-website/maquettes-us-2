Outside the Office

Dr. Snow is invested in the community, both professionally and personally. He is the Founder and President of the Hi Desert Children’s Dental Clinic, which provides free dental care to kids form low income homes. A sports and health enthusiast, Dr. Snow enjoys water skiing, tennis, running, paintball and snow skiing. Dr. Snow has 6 children, and 22 grandchildren.
