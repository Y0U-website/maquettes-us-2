Early orthodontic treatment for children also known as phase one treatment, refers to orthodontic interventions that are performed on children at a younger age, typically between the ages of 7 and 11.

Why early treatment at Gilbert Orthodontics?

As a parent, it’s not always easy to know if your child will need orthodontic treatment. While there is no exact age for children to begin orthodontics treatment, the American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first orthodontic visit at around the age of seven.

By this age, most children have a mix of both adult and baby teeth. This will allow the orthodontist to assess whether or not early intervention is needed to correct problems with the teeth and jaws, and potentially avoid surgery down the road.

Reasons why early orthodontic treatment may be recommended for kids

Jaw and Bite Development

During the early years, a child’s jaw is still growing and developing. Early orthodontic treatment can help guide the growth of the jaw, ensuring that it develops properly and aligns correctly with the upper and lower teeth. This can prevent or address issues such as underbites, crossbites, and overbites.

Space Maintenance

Early orthodontic treatment can create or preserve space for the permanent teeth to erupt properly, reducing the likelihood of future crowding and the need for more extensive orthodontic treatment later on.

Correcting Habits

Certain oral habits, such as thumb-sucking, tongue-thrusting, or prolonged pacifier use, can impact the alignment and development of the teeth and jaws.

Early orthodontic treatment can help address these habits and guide proper oral development.

Speech and Airway Concerns

Early intervention can address these concerns, helping children achieve proper speech patterns and maintain clear airways for optimal breathing.

Psychological and Social Well-being

Straighter teeth and a more balanced smile can boost confidence and contribute to improved overall psychological well-being.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Why should my child see an orthodontist by age 7?

By age 7, most children have a mix of baby and adult teeth. An early visit allows Dr. Gilbert to spot potential issues with jaw growth or emerging teeth while they are easier to guide, often preventing more invasive treatments later.

Does an early evaluation mean my child will get braces immediately?

Not necessarily. In many cases, we simply “monitor” the child’s growth. Our goal is to find the ideal time to start treatment, not the earliest.

What is ’’Phase 1’’ treatment?

Phase 1 is a limited orthodontic treatment (like an expander or partial braces) designed to correct a specific skeletal or dental issue, such as a crossbite or severe crowding, while a child is still growing.

How do I know if my child needs an expander?

If your child has a narrow upper jaw or a crossbite, a 3D-printed expander might be recommended. At Gilbert Orthodontics , we use digital scanning to ensure these appliances fit perfectly and are as comfortable as possible.

Will my child still need braces later if they have early treatment?

Most children who have Phase 1 treatment will eventually need a “Phase 2” (full braces or aligners) to fine-tune the position of all their permanent teeth, but the process is usually much faster and simpler.
