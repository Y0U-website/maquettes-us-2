Clear Aligner and Braces Options for Glencoe Patients

At Gilbert Orthodontics, we offer a variety of treatments designed to meet the needs of patients of all ages, including:

Invisalign®: The Invisible Solution for a Confident Smile

Our Invisalign treatment is a popular choice for Glencoe patients seeking a nearly invisible, comfortable way to straighten teeth. Using a series of custom-made, clear aligners, Invisalign gradually moves teeth into alignment without the need for traditional braces.

Clear Aligners & Braces for Flexible Options

We also offer clear aligners like Go Smile Custom Aligners, ideal for those who want a nearly invisible alternative to braces. Additionally, we provide traditional metal and clear braces for patients looking for a tried-and-true approach.

The Importance of Orthodontic Care

Investing in orthodontic treatment isn’t just about aesthetics; it’s also key to maintaining good oral health. Straight teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of decay and gum disease, while an aligned bite can prevent jaw strain and uneven wear on teeth. By choosing Gilbert Orthodontics as your trusted orthodontist near Glencoe, you’re enhancing both your smile and your long-term dental health.
