Clear Aligner Solutions: Invisalign, and Go Smile Custom Aligners

At Gilbert Orthodontics , we offer three clear aligner systems to provide flexible solutions for different patient preferences and orthodontic needs:

Invisalign®: A Trusted Choice for a Nearly Invisible Treatment

Invisalign is a popular clear aligner system known for its comfort and discreet appearance. Custom-designed to gradually move your teeth into alignment, Invisalign is ideal for patients who want effective treatment without the appearance of traditional braces.

Go Smile Custom Aligners: Tailored for Precision

Go Smile Custom Aligners use detailed 3D imaging to create a truly personalized fit, resulting in precise tooth movements and effective outcomes. Go Smile is ideal for patients who value a custom-fit approach to clear aligners.

Additional Treatment Options: Metal and Clear Braces

For patients who prefer or need traditional braces, we offer both metal braces and clear ceramic braces . Clear braces offer the same effectiveness as metal braces but use tooth-colored brackets for a more subtle look. These options are particularly beneficial for treating complex alignment cases, making them ideal for patients of all ages who want reliable, proven results.
