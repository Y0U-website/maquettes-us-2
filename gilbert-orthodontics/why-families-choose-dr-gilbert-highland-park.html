What age should my child first visit an orthodontist in Highland Park?

In line with the American Association of Orthodontists, we recommend that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7 . At this stage, Dr. Gilbert can monitor the relationship between the erupting permanent teeth and the growing jaw. While most children won’t need braces immediately, early detection of issues like crossbites or severe crowding allows us to intervene at the optimal biological window, often preventing more invasive procedures in the future.
