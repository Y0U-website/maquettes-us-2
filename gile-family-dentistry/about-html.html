Meet the Doctor

NATALIE GILE, DDS

Dr. Gile is a passionate general dentist who is committed to providing patients with the very best in modern dentistry and hometown care. She is a Kearney native and feels very fortunate to be able to treat and give back to the community that she loves.

Dr. Gile believes in helping her patients improve their oral health and achieve a smile to light the world with. Her goal is to create a welcoming and family oriented environment where each patient feels at home. When asked why she enjoys being a dentist, she explains that she loves to build long-term relationships with her patients and treat the person, not just the tooth.

After completing high school at Kearney Catholic, Dr. Gile moved to Lincoln to attend college at Nebraska Wesleyan University and completed her dental education at University of Nebraska Medical Center College of Dentistry. While attending UNMC Dr. Gile was highly involved. She was president of their Christian Dental Association chapter and organized their Simulation Clinic for pre dental students. She intends to continue her love for community involvement as she establishes long-term roots in Kearney.

When she isn’t treating smiles Dr. Gile, along with her business partner and husband Nathan, loves spending time with their family and friends and raising their baby girls Navy Mae and Nellie Lou. Together they enjoy playing golf, attending concerts of all genres, and spending time in the great outdoors. Dr. Gile and her team look forward to providing you and your family with excellent dental health care in a professional and friendly manner.
