Dr. Marc DeLorenzo

A graduate of the University of Alabama for his undergraduate studies, Dr. DeLorenzo earned his doctorate from the prestigious Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. He was inspired to enter the dental field by a local dentist he met while working at his parents convenience store during high school. Impressed by the dentist s joy in his profession and the positive impact he had on people s lives, Dr. DeLorenzo found his calling.

What Dr. DeLorenzo loves most about his work is helping patients regain confidence in their smiles, whether it s repairing chipped front teeth they ve hidden for years or alleviating pain. He takes great satisfaction in ensuring his patients are comfortable both emotionally and physically throughout their dental experience. As a practice owner, he is committed to fostering a positive work environment that promotes a healthy work-life balance and opportunities for professional growth for his team.

Outside of the office, Dr. DeLorenzo enjoys an active lifestyle. He can often be found hiking with his two dogs, Jenny and Enzo, hitting the slopes for a ski run, or playing tennis or pickleball. While he modestly claims not to have any unique hobbies, his passion for outdoor activities and sports is evident.

A recent and exciting development in Dr. DeLorenzo s life is the arrival of his first child, Audrey, born in September 2024. This new chapter as a father adds another dimension to his already fulfilling life and career.

At Gilford Dental, Dr. DeLorenzo combines his professional expertise with a genuine care for his patients and team, creating a dental practice that prioritizes excellent care and a positive experience for all.

Stevie

Stevie brings decades of experience, dedication, and enthusiasm to Gilford Dental. Stevie joined Gilford Dental in January 2026 and quickly became a valued part of the team. She received her education and training through Mattatuck Community College in Waterbury, Connecticut, and Eli Whitney Regional Vocational Technical School in Hamden, Connecticut.

One of Stevie’s favorite parts of working in dentistry is helping restore a patient’s smile. Stevie’s passion for dentistry began memorably. On New Year’s Eve in 1973, her mother developed an abscess, and their family dentist came in, wearing a tuxedo, to treat her. As he talked Stevie through performing a root canal, she became fascinated by the field and has loved dentistry ever since.
