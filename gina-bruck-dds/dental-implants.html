Dental Implants in Shorewood, IL

Like natural teeth, dental implants require good oral hygiene and regular dental care to keep the surrounding gums and tissues healthy.

  • Improved stability when eating and speaking.
  • Support for maintaining jawbone in the area of a missing tooth.
  • No need to rely on neighboring teeth to support a single-tooth implant.
  • A durable tooth replacement option with proper care.

Replacing One or Multiple Missing Teeth

Dental implants aren’t limited to replacing a single tooth. Depending on the number and location of missing teeth, implants may be used to support different types of restorations.

A single dental implant can replace one missing tooth without relying on adjacent teeth for support. For patients missing several or all of their teeth, implant-supported treatment may provide additional stability compared with traditional removable options.

Our team can explain the available tooth replacement options and help you compare dental implants, bridges and dentures based on your oral health, goals and individual needs.
